Official or import

Of the 12 Xiaomi phones in stock, 1 has an official unit on sale somewhere we read, 10 we could only find as imports and on 1 no shop said which it was selling.

What the two channels mean here

  • Official — brand warranty in Bangladesh. Brought in by the brand's own distributor and sold with the manufacturer's Bangladesh warranty, serviced at the brand’s own centres.
  • Unofficial import — shop warranty only. Brought in outside the distributor, usually a good deal cheaper. The cover is whatever the seller offers, honoured by the seller; the brand’s service centre is not obliged to take it. Confirm the handset’s IMEI and BTRC status with the shop before you pay.
  • Not stated. The shop published a price and never said which it was selling. We do not guess, so the table says so.
