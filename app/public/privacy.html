Privacy

Privacy

Bhalo Phone has no accounts, no logins and no ads. You can use every page without telling us who you are. This is the full list of what the site records.

What gets recorded

  • How pages are used. Microsoft Clarity records clicks, scrolling and anonymous session replays so we can see where a page confuses people. It masks text you type, and it does not get your name or phone number.
  • How fast pages load. Vercel Speed Insights measures load times in the phone picker. It records timings, not who you are.
  • What you ask the phone picker. The budget and needs you enter are sent to our server to rank phones, and the answer is cached so the next person asking the same thing gets it faster. The cache holds the question, not who asked it.
  • Two settings in your own browser. Your language choice and the phones you shortlist are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device, and clearing site data removes them.

The Facebook page

An app called BhaloPhone Poster publishes price cards to our own Facebook page and posts a link under each one. It reads only that page's posts and comments, and only so it does not post the same phone twice. It does not read anyone's profile, friends or messages.

What never happens

  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared with shops, brands or advertisers.
  • No shop pays for a position, and no click you make is passed to a shop.
  • No email list, no phone list, no marketing messages.

Questions, or want something removed? message the Facebook page. Last updated 29 October 2026.
