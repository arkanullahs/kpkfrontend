Why it matters

Three things your support actually protects.

Rankings that answer to you

The moment a shop can pay for the top slot, the list stops being advice and starts being an ad. Donations are the only funding model that does not create that pull.

A costly decision, done once

A phone bought on a shopkeeper's recommendation and regretted for two years is a real loss for a household here. Getting that call right is the entire job of this site.

Free for the people who need it most

The buyers who can least afford a mistake are the least likely to pay for advice. Nothing here sits behind a wall, so that stays true.

Where your taka actually goes

  • Servers and the domain — the boxes that crawl shops overnight and serve these pages all day.
  • The data crawl — every shop we track gets re-read, so the price you see is the price today, not last month.
  • AI ranking calls — reading reviews and re-ranking phones is metered per call, and it is the single biggest running cost.
  • Phone photos and data storage — every image is self-hosted so no page depends on a shop's server staying up.

Nothing here pays a salary. It is one person's side project, and any surplus goes straight back into covering the next month.

Does donating move a phone up the list?

No. The order comes from live shop prices and what reviewers actually found, recalculated every day or two. Nobody can buy a spot — not a shop, not a brand, not you. The day that stops being true, the list is worthless to you anyway.

What if I can't spare anything?

Then don't send anything. Every guide, every price and the personal pick work exactly the same for you — nothing here is locked and nothing will be. Sending a guide to a friend who is about to buy helps just as much.

Is this a charity?

No, it is one person's project — built and paid for by Arkanullah Saad. What you send keeps the servers and the data runs going. It is not tax-deductible, and it does not buy you anything.

I sent it by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes. Message the Facebook page with the transaction ID and it goes straight back to you. No questions.
