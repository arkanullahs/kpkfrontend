Can I compare any two phones?

Yes. Pick any two -- or three -- of the 720 phones we track, and the table fills in from the same live shop data every other page on this site is built from. Nothing is pre-written, so there is no list of “approved” pairs to stay inside.

What does the tick mean?

A tick means that phone is measurably ahead on that row -- ahead by more than the margin we treat as noise for that spec. Two phones within that margin both stay unticked, because a 100 mAh battery difference is not a difference. Rows we cannot measure the same way on both phones -- screen size, the chipset name, the software -- are never ticked at all.

How current are these prices?

Prices and stock were last checked 11 Sep 2026, across the Bangladeshi shops we track. Each price says whether it is an official-warranty listing or a gray import, because in Bangladesh those are two different products at two different prices.

Which phones can I pick from?

Only phones that have their own page here, which means a spec sheet we could match and confirm and a shop actually holding stock. A phone missing from the list is one we would not publish a page about yet.
