Xiaomi 15 Ultra Is it worth buying?

If you can live without a Bangladeshi manufacturer warranty and tolerate occasional software glitches, the Xiaomi 15 Ultra gives flagship performance at a gray import price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs

What works well4

  • 3200 nit peak brightness
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite speed
  • 50MP main camera with optical zoom
  • 80W fast charging

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official Bangladeshi warranty
  • Thermal throttling under prolonged load
  • HyperOS can lose photos and Bluetooth
  • Curved edges block glass protectors

Why we say that

The 6.73 inch LTPO AMOLED panel reaches a peak brightness of 3200 nit and runs at 120 Hz, making outdoor use easy. The Snapdragon 8 Elite processor with 16 GB RAM handles most tasks quickly and scores 8.8 for gaming, though long stress tests show noticeable thermal throttling. Battery endurance is strong with more than 16 hours of active use on the 5410 mAh cell, and 80W charging refills it fast, but the charger must match the phone's specifications.

The 50 MP main camera with OIS and 3x telephoto delivers world class stills, yet moving subjects can blur in low light. Software runs HyperOS 3 on Android 15 and promises up to four major Android upgrades, but users report photo loss and Bluetooth pauses. Curved screen edges make glass protectors hard to fit.
