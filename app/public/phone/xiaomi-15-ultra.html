Buying verdict

Xiaomi 15 ULTRA Is it worth buying?

The Xiaomi 15 Ultra is a powerful device that delivers world-class camera performance and exceptional battery endurance, but it comes with significant software and hardware caveats for Bangladeshi buyers.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize top-tier camera performance and battery life and are comfortable managing potential software bugs on an imported device.
Consider alternatives if
You require a reliable manufacturer warranty, need a stable software experience, or rely on glass screen protectors.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Massive battery endurance with 16 hours of active use
  • World-class camera stills and zoom capabilities
  • Stunning 3200 nits peak brightness display
  • Blazing peak performance from Snapdragon 8 Elite

Reported trade-offs4

  • HyperOS software is currently unstable with bugs
  • Significant thermal throttling under prolonged CPU load
  • Curved edges make glass screen protectors nearly impossible
  • No manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh

Why we say that

Owners report that HyperOS is currently unstable, with bugs causing photos to disappear and Bluetooth to pause unexpectedly. While the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset offers blazing peak speeds, the phone suffers from significant thermal throttling during prolonged stress. The curved display is visually stunning, yet it makes finding a compatible glass screen protector nearly impossible.

Because these units are gray imports, you will not receive any manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh. Repairs are entirely at your own expense through the shop. Before purchasing, you must confirm whether you are getting a China or Global ROM, as the China version lacks Google services and the Bangla language pack.

Does this phone come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every listing is a gray import, meaning you must rely on whatever limited promise the individual shop provides. All repairs are paid out of your own pocket.

How does the battery perform during daily use?

Owners report a massive endurance jump with this device. It is very efficient and capable of providing over 16 hours of active use on a single charge. It currently holds a high owner rating of 9.3 for battery life.

Is this device suitable for heavy gaming?

While the phone features the powerful Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset, it is not ideal for long gaming sessions. Owners have observed significant thermal throttling during prolonged CPU stress tests, which may impact performance during extended play.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 15 and is promised up to 4 major Android upgrades. You can also expect 4 to 5 years of security updates. However, be aware that current HyperOS versions are reported to be unstable.
