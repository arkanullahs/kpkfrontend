Motorola G85 Is it worth buying?

The Motorola G85 is a stylish device that struggles with basic system stability and performance reliability.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize a high-quality curved display and a clean software interface over raw performance and gaming capabilities.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable phone for gaming, heavy multitasking, or require an official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Premium curved 120Hz P-OLED display
  • High 1600 nits peak brightness
  • Clean near-stock Hello UI software
  • Main camera includes OIS

Reported trade-offs4

  • Frequent system hangs and app crashes
  • Proximity sensor fails during calls
  • Slow 33W charging speed
  • Limited to 1080p 30fps video recording

Why we say that

While the 6.67 inch P-OLED display is a standout feature with 1600 nits peak brightness, the user experience is frequently marred by system hangs and app crashes. Owners report that the phone often stutters during daily tasks, and the proximity sensor frequently fails to turn off the screen during calls, leading to accidental touches. The camera setup includes OIS, but users find the output prone to over-processing and heavy saturation.

Video recording is limited to 1080p at 30fps, which is disappointing for the price. Battery life is generally considered good, but the 33W charging speed feels slow compared to other options. Because these units are gray imports, you will not receive any manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh.

Read full reasoning

You must rely entirely on the shop for repairs and coverage. Given the reported instability and the availability of better alternatives like the Motorola G100 at a similar price point, this device requires careful consideration before purchase.

Does the Motorola G85 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you must rely on the specific shop for any coverage or repairs. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

Is the Motorola G85 suitable for gaming and heavy tasks?

Owners report that the phone struggles with heavy tasks and gaming. There are frequent complaints about system hangs, stuttering, and app crashes. With a performance rating of 5.8 and a gaming rating of 5.5, it is not recommended for users who need a powerful device.

How long will the Motorola G85 receive software updates?

The device is expected to receive between 1 and 3 OS updates. However, Motorola has a history of weak update support. You should also be aware that recent updates have introduced stability and app-loading issues for some users.

How is the battery life and charging speed of this phone?

The 5000 mAh battery provides good endurance for daily use, earning a rating of 7.2 from owners. However, the 33W charging speed is considered slow for this price point, which may be a drawback if you need to charge your phone quickly.
