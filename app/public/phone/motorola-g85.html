Is the Motorola G85 worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 1600 nits P-OLED display
  • Clean near-stock Hello UI software
  • 50MP main camera with OIS
  • Smooth 120Hz refresh rate

What is not

  • Proximity sensor fails during calls
  • Only unofficial gray-import stock available
  • Historically weak software update support

Buy it if You want a clean software experience and a high-quality display and are comfortable managing the risks of an unofficial gray-import device.

Skip it if You rely on a reliable proximity sensor for calls or require the peace of mind that comes with an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Living with this device means dealing with a persistent proximity sensor issue where the screen fails to turn off during calls, which often leads to accidental touches. While the Hello UI provides a near-stock Android 14 experience with minimal bloatware, Motorola has a history of weak software support, offering only 1 to 3 OS updates. You are getting a 50MP camera with OIS and a 120Hz P-OLED screen, but the lack of an official brand warranty is a significant trade-off.

Since you are buying unofficial stock, you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your money. It is a capable device for daily tasks, provided you can tolerate the sensor glitches and the risks associated with shop-only warranties.
