Is the Apple iPhone 15 Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • Exceptional battery life for daily use
  • Premium matte glass build feels solid
  • 48MP main camera with optical image stabilization
  • A16 Bionic delivers smooth performance

What is not

  • 60Hz display feels outdated
  • Overheating during charging and heavy use
  • No telephoto lens on camera
  • High price compared to Android alternatives

Buy it if Buy it if you value iOS stability, strong battery and a premium feel over the latest screen refresh rate.

Skip it if Skip it if you need the latest software updates, a high‑refresh display, or a lower total cost of ownership.

Why we say that

The device runs iOS 17 smoothly on the A16 Bionic, and owners rate its performance and video quality highly. However, the lack of a telephoto lens, the outdated refresh rate and the reports of warmth while charging or gaming are common complaints. At an unofficial price of 85,490‑95,490 taka it is far cheaper than the official warranty price of 179,999 taka, yet it is already three years old, so software updates will likely stop soon.

Consider the price gap to the newer iPhone 16 Plus, which sells for about the same in Bangladesh. Overall, it feels like a high‑end iPhone with some compromises that matter depending on your priorities.
