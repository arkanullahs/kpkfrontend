Is the Samsung Galaxy A17 4G worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.7 inch Super AMOLED display
  • 50MP main camera with OIS
  • 8GB RAM with up to 256GB storage
  • Six years of Android updates

What is not

  • Helio G99 struggles with UI animations
  • 25W charging feels slow for price
  • Waterdrop notch and thick chin look dated
  • No 3.5mm headphone jack

Buy it if Buy it if you value a big AMOLED screen, solid camera and long software support and can tolerate occasional lag.

Skip it if Skip it if you need fast gaming, smooth multitasking, or a 3.5mm audio jack.

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch Super AMOLED panel looks bright and smooth at 90Hz, yet the waterdrop notch and thick chin feel dated. The 50MP main camera with OIS delivers reliable photos in good light, while the ultrawide lens is limited to daylight. Battery life is average for a 5000 mAh cell and the 25W charger is slower than expected for the price.

The Helio G99 chipset struggles with modern UI animations and multitasking, leading to noticeable lag. Build quality is decent with Victus+ glass, but the missing 3.5mm jack disappoints many. Software updates are a strong point, offering up to six major Android upgrades, but only shop warranty is available for gray‑import stock, so confirm IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

Will the 5000 mAh battery last a full day and how fast does it charge?
