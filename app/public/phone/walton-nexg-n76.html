Walton NEXG N76 Is it worth buying?

Even at the cheapest official warranty price, the Walton NEXG N76 feels like a compromise phone that will let you browse and call but will frustrate you with heat and lag.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a large screen and long battery for light daily tasks and can tolerate heat and limited performance.
Consider alternatives if
You expect smooth gaming, good low light photos, or regular software updates.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 6000 mAh battery gives long endurance
  • 6.9 inch IPS display with 120 Hz refresh
  • 8 GB RAM handles basic multitasking
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included

Reported trade-offs3

  • Camera sensor quality is poor
  • Phone overheats severely during use
  • Software is ad heavy and gets no updates

Why we say that

The 6.9 inch IPS panel offers a large workspace and a 120 Hz refresh rate, but the panel is dim and the colors are muted, so outdoor reading is hard. The 6000 mAh battery does keep the device alive for basic tasks, yet heavy use quickly drains it and the phone gets noticeably hot, sometimes to the point of shutting down. Performance is limited by the Unisoc T7250; 8 GB RAM helps with light multitasking, but gaming and demanding apps stutter, overheat, and may crash.

Camera specs sound good on paper with a 52 MP sensor, but real world photos are blurry and low light shots are noisy, matching the low owner rating. Software runs a light Android skin that is cluttered with medium level ads, and the brand rarely provides updates, leaving the device vulnerable over time.

How long does the battery last on normal use?

The 6000 mAh cell can keep the phone alive for a full day of calls, messaging and web browsing, but heavy video streaming or gaming will drain it faster and raise the temperature.

Can this phone handle mobile games smoothly?

The Unisoc T7250 chipset with 8 GB RAM can run simple games, but demanding titles often stutter, overheat and may close, so smooth high end gaming cannot be expected.

Is the camera usable in low light conditions?

The 52 MP main sensor looks sharp in bright light, but in low light the pictures are noisy and lack detail, matching the low owner rating for camera performance.

Will I receive software updates and is the warranty official?

The phone comes with the brand’s official warranty and can be serviced through Walton’s network, but the Android skin receives rarely any updates and the company is known for not providing long term support.
