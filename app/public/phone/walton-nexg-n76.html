Is the Walton NEXG N76 worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6000 mAh battery lasts 1.5 to 2 days
  • Rugged IP64 rated build quality
  • Comes with Android 15
  • iPhone inspired aesthetic design

What is not

  • Low HD+ resolution lacks sharpness
  • Performance issues with heavy apps and gaming
  • Slow 18W charging for a large battery
  • Poor camera performance in low light

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a rugged device with exceptional battery life for basic daily tasks.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you play games, use heavy applications, or care about display sharpness and camera quality.

Why we say that

Owners report that the 6000 mAh battery is the standout feature, easily lasting up to two days on a single charge. However, the experience is hampered by a low HD+ resolution on the large 6.9 inch screen, which makes the display look less sharp. Performance is a major pain point, as the UNISOC T7250 chipset and 4 GB of RAM struggle with heavy apps and gaming, often leading to crashes.

While the rugged IP64 build and Android 15 software are appreciated, the camera quality is inconsistent, particularly in low light. Users feel the device is overpriced at ৳12,499, noting that the 18W charging speed is quite slow for such a large battery. If you prioritize battery longevity over speed and screen sharpness, this phone might serve your basic needs.

How long does the battery actually last?

The 6000 mAh battery is highly rated by owners. It easily lasts between 1.5 to 2 days on a single charge, making it one of the most reliable aspects of the device.

Is this phone good for gaming?

No, this phone is not recommended for gaming. Owners report that gaming is limited to low settings and heavy applications often crash due to the chipset and insufficient RAM.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The camera performance is poor in low light conditions. While daylight photos are usable, they often appear oversharpened, and the artificial bokeh effect does not perform well.
