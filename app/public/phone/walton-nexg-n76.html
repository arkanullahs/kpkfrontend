What works well4

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • 6.9‑inch IPS display with 120 Hz refresh
  • 52 MP main camera works in daylight
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included

Reported trade-offs4

  • Low HD+ resolution feels blurry
  • 18 W charging is slow for large battery
  • Heavy apps often crash
  • Low‑light camera performance poor

Why we say that

With a 6000 mAh cell it can stay alive for one and a half to two days on normal use, which owners rate nine out of ten. The 6.9‑inch IPS panel offers a smooth 120 Hz motion but its HD+ resolution leaves the picture looking soft, especially when you look closely. The Unisoc T7250 chipset with 8 GB RAM handles basic apps, yet heavy multitasking often crashes and games run only at low settings, reflected in low performance and gaming scores.

The 52 MP main sensor produces decent daylight shots but becomes oversharpened and struggles in low light, while the 8 MP selfie camera offers no special features. Android 15 runs on a light skin that lacks a gallery app and receives updates rarely. Charging at 18 W feels sluggish for such a large battery.

Read full reasoning

Overall the phone feels rugged and includes a headphone jack and FM radio, but it falls short on display sharpness, gaming, and camera versatility.
