Is the OPPO A6c worth buying?

What is good

  • Huge 7000 mAh battery
  • IP64 splash resistance
  • 120 Hz smooth display
  • Headphone jack

What is not

  • 720p resolution on large screen
  • 15W charging is very slow
  • Unisoc T7250 struggles with apps
  • Camera poor in low light

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize long battery life and basic daily tasks over gaming or high‑resolution media.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect fast charging, high‑end performance, or sharp display quality.

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh battery scores 9.6 out of 10 and easily lasts beyond a full workday even with heavy use. The polycarbonate body is sturdy and carries an IP64 splash rating, so occasional rain won’t ruin it. The 6.75‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, giving smooth scrolling, but the 720p resolution looks soft on such a large panel and many owners call it “funny”.

The Unisoc T7250 chipset with 4 GB RAM handles basic tasks but stalls on games or multitasking, reflected in low gaming and performance scores. Charging at 15 W is painfully slow; a full charge can take close to two hours. Camera performance is average in daylight and drops sharply at night or during video calls.

Software runs Android 15 with ColorOS 15, which works but includes noticeable bloatware. Overall the phone feels overpriced for 2026 standards, offering strong battery life but weak performance and display quality.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The OPPO A6c has a 7000 mAh battery that owners rate 9.6 out of 10. In real use it can easily exceed a full workday, even with moderate browsing, social media and video playback, without needing a recharge.

Can the phone run mobile games smoothly?

The device uses a Unisoc T7250 chipset with 4 GB RAM. Owner ratings give gaming a 3.0 and performance a 4.2, and reviews note the chip struggles beyond basic tasks. Expect low frame rates in demanding games.

Is the camera good for low‑light photos or video calls?

The main camera is 13 MP f/2.2 and owners rate it 3.8. Daylight shots are average, but reviews describe night photos and video call quality as poor, with users reporting blurry images and low brightness.
