Buying verdict

OPPO A6c Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run for several days without charging, the OPPO A6c delivers that with its massive 7,000 mAh battery.

Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life and a big screen over fast charging, sharp display, and high‑end gaming performance.
Consider alternatives if
You need quick charging, a crisp high‑resolution display, or smooth gaming experiences.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Huge 7,000 mAh battery lasts days
  • 120 Hz refresh feels smooth
  • Headphone jack for wired audio
  • Large 6.75‑inch display

Reported trade-offs

  • 720p screen feels low for size
  • 15W charging is painfully slow
  • Unisoc chipset struggles with games
  • Camera performs poorly in low light

Why we say that

The 6.75‑inch IPS LCD screen runs at 120 Hz, so scrolling feels smooth, but the 720p resolution looks dull at this size. Charging is a bottleneck; the included 15W charger takes almost two hours to fill the huge cell. The Unisoc T7250 chipset struggles with most games and everyday multitasking feels sluggish.

Daylight photos are acceptable at 13 MP, but night shots are poor and video calls often look grainy. ColorOS 15 is clean but carries medium‑level bloatware, and you can expect only a few major Android upgrades. Official units priced at ৳17,499 include a brand warranty, while cheaper gray‑import listings at ৳18,299 lack manufacturer support and require paid repairs.

How long does the battery last on the OPPO A6c?

The 7,000 mAh battery is rated as industry‑leading and can easily last two to three days with moderate use. Owner ratings give the battery a 9.8 out of 10, confirming its endurance.

Gaming on the OPPO A6c is not smooth. The Unisoc T7250 chipset is sluggish and owners rate gaming at 3.0 out of 10. Most modern games will lag or stutter.

The 13 MP main sensor struggles in low‑light conditions. Reviews describe night photos as poor quality, and the camera rating is only 4.0 out of 10. Daylight shots are mediocre.
