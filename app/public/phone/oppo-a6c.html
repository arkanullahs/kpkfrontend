Is the OPPO A6c worth buying?

What is good

  • Huge 7,000 mAh battery lasts days
  • 120 Hz refresh makes scrolling smooth
  • Headphone jack for wired audio
  • Large 6.75‑inch display

What is not

  • 720p screen feels low‑resolution
  • 15W charging is very slow
  • Unisoc chip struggles with games
  • Camera poor in low light

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize long battery life and can accept modest performance and a low‑resolution display.

Skip it if Skip it if you need fast gaming, a sharp high‑resolution screen, or a strong low‑light camera.

Why we say that

The 7,000 mAh battery truly lasts multiple days on moderate use, which is the biggest draw for most owners. The 6.75‑inch IPS LCD screen runs at 120 Hz, giving smooth scrolling, but its 720p resolution feels blurry and cheap at this price point. The Unisoc T7250 chipset struggles with everyday tasks and makes gaming almost unplayable, and the 15W charger needs nearly two hours to fill the huge battery, which many find painfully slow.

Camera performance is mediocre in daylight and drops sharply in low‑light scenes, so night photos are disappointing. ColorOS 15 is relatively clean but still carries medium‑level bloatware. Official units priced at ৳17,499 (64 GB) or ৳21,999 (128 GB) include a brand warranty, while cheaper gray imports lack manufacturer support and require paid repairs.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The OPPO A6c has a 7,000 mAh battery that can easily last two to three days with moderate use. Owners report multi‑day endurance, making it suitable for heavy daily phone use without frequent charging.

Can I play games smoothly on this phone?

Gaming on the OPPO A6c is not smooth. The Unisoc T7250 chipset is sluggish and struggles with most games, so the experience is essentially non‑viable for serious gamers.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 13 MP main camera produces poor quality photos at night. Owners describe night photography as low‑detail and noisy, so the phone is not reliable for low‑light shooting.

Official listings at ৳17,499 (64 GB) and ৳21,999 (128 GB) include the brand warranty. Gray‑import units have no manufacturer cover and only paid repairs. ColorOS 15 receives a few Android updates, similar to other mid‑range devices.
