Why we say that

Since this phone was released in 2023, you should expect very limited remaining software support. It runs on the Dimensity 7020 chipset and features a 120Hz IPS LCD display, providing a near-stock software experience with low bloatware. Because all units currently found in the market are gray imports, you will not receive any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh.

Any repairs required will be paid out of your own pocket, and you must rely solely on the limited coverage provided by the individual shop. Always verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before finalizing your purchase to ensure the device is legitimate. While the 50MP camera with OIS is a functional inclusion, the overall value proposition is weakened by the existence of cheaper, newer alternatives.
