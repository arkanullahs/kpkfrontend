Google Pixel 9 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you want a Pixel with the brightest screen and solid camera but can tolerate slower charging and modest gaming, the Pixel 9 Pro fits the bill.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value a clean Android experience, top‑tier photography and long‑term software support over raw gaming power.
Consider alternatives if
You need fast charging or high‑performance gaming on a daily basis.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 120 Hz LTPO OLED with 3000‑nit peak
  • Google Tensor G4 AI and clean Android
  • Excellent camera detail and natural colours
  • Seven‑year software updates

Reported trade-offs4

  • 27 W charging feels slow for flagship
  • Gaming performance weak, low sustained FPS
  • UFS 3.1 storage slower than typical flagships
  • 5× telephoto lacks portrait mode

Why we say that

The 6.3" LTPO OLED reaches a peak of 3000 nits and runs at 120 Hz, making everyday use feel smooth. The Tensor G4 chip handles AI tasks well and Android 14 stays clean and ad‑free. Battery life is reliable for a day, yet the 27 W charger feels sluggish for a flagship.

Gaming performance is weak and sustained frames drop, and the UFS 3.1 storage feels a bit slower than newer flagships. The 50 MP main camera delivers elite detail and natural colours, but the 5× telephoto lens lacks a portrait mode. Overall, it offers premium build and long‑term software support, but the price and performance trade‑offs matter.
