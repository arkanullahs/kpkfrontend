Why we say that

The 6.55 inch P OLED panel reaches 1000 nits peak and feels smooth, yet users report intermittent touch failures that need a restart. The 108MP main camera is versatile on paper, but low light performance is weak and processing inconsistent. Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 handles daily tasks, but gaming feels underwhelming for the price.

Battery life is poor with a standby loss of 2 to 3 percent per hour and some units arrive with faulty cells. Software runs Android 14 and can be upgraded to Android 15, but bugs and delayed updates are common. Repairs are paid and only the shop’s limited warranty applies.

Marques Brownlee · Every Phone Should Copy This! ↗

Marques Brownlee highlighted the Skyline as an inspiring mid-ranger bringing two rare features to Android: genuine user repairability via an iFixit partnership and built-in magnetic Qi2 wireless charging. While he praised the nostalgic Lumia design and customizable action button, he noted that the Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 chip occasionally drops frames, the haptics feel subpar, and the cameras are merely passable.

JerryRigEverything · The $300 smartphone they don't want you to know about ↗

JerryRigEverything strongly commended HMD for prioritizing sustainability and consumer rights by designing an exceptionally durable, repair-friendly phone that keeps its warranty intact after at-home disassembly. Through hands-on durability and teardown testing, he praised the cheap replacement parts on iFixit, the solid aluminum frame, and how easily the back glass and screen detach with minimal tools.

Gyan Therapy · How to Make - A Flop Phone in India ! ↗

Gyan Therapy delivered a harsh assessment, deeming the phone an uncompetitive flop in the Indian market given its steep pricing against far superior alternatives. He was particularly frustrated that the advertised self-repair spare parts were completely unavailable locally, and criticized the sluggish chipset, inconsistent cameras, very poor video stabilization, and mediocre battery capacity.

Tech Spurt · Lumia Reborn! | HMD Skyline Unboxing & Full Tour ↗

Tech Spurt enjoyed the retro Nokia Lumia styling, bright 144Hz panel, and handy customizable side button, noting that the simple single-screw DIY repair process was genuinely impressive. However, he warned that competitors from OnePlus, Poco, and Samsung offer much better processing speed, faster charging, and significantly longer software support than the two OS updates promised here.

GSMArena Official · HMD Skyline review ↗

GSMArena concluded that while the Skyline provides genuine novelty through its easy home repairability, distinct retro aesthetics, and wireless charging, it struggles to deliver the best value for money. Benchmark performance from the Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 falls short of rivals, the battery active-use score is uninspiring, and the cameras lack electronic stabilization when recording in 4K resolution.

Oscarmini · HMD Skyline Review: The $500 Mistake? (3 Months Later) ↗

Oscarmini found the phone difficult to recommend at its $500 price point after three months of use, pointing out that cheaper competitors like the Galaxy S23 FE or Redmi Note series offer superior processing power and longer software lifespans. Although he appreciated the smooth 144Hz display, magnetic wireless charging, and repairability, he found the camera quality inconsistent and the battery life underwhelming.

Eric Okafor · HMD Skyline Unboxing and Review ↗

Eric Okafor was broadly positive about the Skyline, praising its solid metal build reminiscent of the Nokia N9, its crisp 144Hz display, and its clean stock Android software. He found the 108MP camera capable of capturing excellent detail and skin tones in daylight, recommending the phone to buyers who prioritize standout design, fluid scrolling, and unique lifestyle features over hardcore mobile gaming.

Will the phone get Android updates and for how long?

The Skyline ships with Android 14 and can be upgraded to Android 15. HMD promises up to two major OS updates, so after the second update you should not expect further official upgrades.

How is the battery life in daily use?

Battery endurance is reported as poor. Users see a standby drain of about 2 to 3 percent per hour and some have received faulty battery batches, so you may need to replace it sooner.

Is the camera good in low light?

The 108MP triple lens system is versatile, but low light shots are inconsistent and the processing is weak. Expect noise and loss of detail when shooting in dark environments.

What warranty or support does the phone have in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop may offer its own limited warranty, but any repair will be paid by you.
