Buying verdict

Infinix Hot 30i Is it worth buying?

The Infinix Hot 30i feels like a budget phone that can get you through calls, texts and light web use, but it shows its age in many daily tasks.

Choose it if
You need a cheap phone for basic calls, messaging and occasional web use.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on gaming, high‑quality photos or fast software updates.

What works well

  • 5000 mAh battery lasts a day
  • 90 Hz IPS display is smooth
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • Affordable official warranty price

Reported trade-offs

  • Fingerprint sensor often stops working
  • Camera produces poor, low‑detail photos
  • 18 W charging is slow
  • Heavy bloatware and ads

Why we say that

Its 5000 mAh battery holds up for basic activities, yet the 18 W charger fills it slowly. The 6.56‑inch 90 Hz IPS panel is smooth but struggles with outdoor brightness. The 13 MP main camera often produces blurry, low‑detail pictures, especially in dim light.

Users report the fingerprint sensor stops working after a short time and performance drops noticeably with heavier apps. The phone ships with Android 12 and XOS 10.6 loaded with pre‑installed apps that push ads and cannot be removed. Being a 2023 model, it has already received only one or two OS updates, so future software support is limited.
