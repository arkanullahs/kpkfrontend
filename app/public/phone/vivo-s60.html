Vivo S60 Is it worth buying?

The Vivo S60 is a powerful gray-import device that offers exceptional battery life but requires careful consideration regarding its software and lack of official support.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize raw battery endurance and gaming performance and are comfortable managing a gray-import device without official support.

Why we say that

With a massive 7200 mAh battery, it provides industry-leading endurance that owners rate highly. The Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset ensures smooth performance for heavy tasks and gaming. However, buyers must be aware that all units in Bangladesh are gray imports with no manufacturer warranty.

You are limited to whatever repair promise the individual shop provides. The software experience is a major point of friction for many users. If you purchase a unit running OriginOS, you will face challenges with Chinese services, no preinstalled Google apps, and a lack of Bangla language support.

Owners also report that the ultrawide camera is under-spec'd for this price point and the software contains a noticeable amount of ads and bloat. Always confirm the specific software version and IMEI status with your seller before paying the ৳67,499 asking price.

Does the Vivo S60 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import. You must rely solely on the repair promise provided by the specific shop where you purchase the device.

How does the battery perform during daily use?

The battery performance is exceptional. Owners rate the battery 9.8 out of 10, noting that the industry-leading 7200 mAh capacity provides endurance that lasts through heavy usage. It is one of the strongest features of this device.

Is the Vivo S60 suitable for mobile gaming?

Yes, the phone is well-suited for gaming. It is powered by the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset, which owners report handles heavy tasks and gaming smoothly. The device receives an 8.6 rating for gaming performance from users.

What are the risks of buying the OriginOS version?

The OriginOS version is built for the China market and lacks Google services preinstalled. It does not support the Bangla language and receives updates only from Chinese servers. This version is intended for users who can manage the friction of a Chinese-market software environment.
