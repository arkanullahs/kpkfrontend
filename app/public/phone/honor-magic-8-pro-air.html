Is the Honor Magic 8 Pro Air worth buying?

What is good

  • High performance Dimensity 9500 chipset
  • Bright 120Hz LTPO OLED display
  • Efficient 5500 mAh battery life
  • Versatile 50MP camera system

What is not

  • Medium build quality with real thickness
  • Camera performance trails expensive flagships
  • Only gray-import stock available
  • Medium levels of bloat and ads

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize raw performance and a high-quality display for gaming and media consumption.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you require an official brand warranty or demand the absolute best camera performance available on the market.

Why we say that

Owners rate the display highly, yet they note that the camera quality is not quite on par with more expensive flagships. While the battery offers real endurance and efficiency, the physical build is described as having real thickness and medium quality. You will find the software experience includes medium levels of bloat and ads.

Because this phone is currently only available as gray-import stock, you must rely on a shop warranty rather than a brand warranty. It is essential to confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before you finalize your purchase. Living with this phone means accepting that you are getting a high-performance chipset and a bright screen, but you are sacrificing the premium build and top-tier camera performance found in more expensive alternatives.
