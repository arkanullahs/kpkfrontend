  • Dim 480‑nit display hard to see in sunlight
  • Slow 18 W charging for a large battery
  • Blurry, noisy camera especially in low light
  • Only 3 GB RAM limits multitasking and gaming

Why we say that

The 5000 mAh battery gives reliable endurance for everyday calls, messages and light web browsing, and the 6.56‑inch IPS panel is large enough for media, though its 480‑nit brightness makes outdoor use uncomfortable. The plastic body feels sturdy for the price and the official warranty covers service through the brand’s network. Under the hood, the Unisoc T603 with 3 GB RAM handles Android basics, but multitasking and modern games like PUBG are unplayable and the system feels sluggish.

Charging at 18 W is noticeably slow for a battery of this size. The single camera produces blurry, noisy images, especially in low light, and the Android skin is plain with occasional ads and rare updates. Reviewers consider the ৳9,999 price high, noting that the newer Max 60 offers better specs for the same amount.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery provides reliable endurance for basic daily tasks such as calls, messaging and light web use, usually lasting a full day. Heavy usage will reduce that time noticeably.

Can I play games like PUBG on this phone?

The device runs on a Unisoc T603 with only 3 GB RAM, so demanding games such as PUBG are unplayable. It is limited to simple apps and basic navigation.

How good are the photos in low‑light conditions?

The single camera produces blurry and noisy images, and low‑light performance is especially poor, resulting in lack of detail and significant grain. It is not suitable for night photography.

What warranty and software updates does the phone get?

Because it is sold through the official channel, the phone carries the brand’s warranty and can be serviced at any authorized BD unit. Software updates are rare and the Android skin includes moderate ads.
