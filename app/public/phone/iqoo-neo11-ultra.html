What works well

  • 144Hz AMOLED display with high peak brightness
  • Dimensity 9500M delivers flagship level speed
  • 9100 mAh battery with 100W fast charge
  • 50MP main camera with optical image stabilization

Reported trade-offs

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Chinese OriginOS includes medium ads
  • Limited software updates, only three OS upgrades
  • Large size may feel heavy in hand

Why we say that

The 6.83 inch AMOLED panel runs at 144Hz and can peak at 4500 nits, so bright outdoor use feels comfortable. The Dimensity 9500M chipset with 12GB RAM handles games and multitasking without stutter. The 9100 mAh battery lasts a full day of heavy use and refills in minutes thanks to 100W charging.

The 50MP main sensor with OIS captures detailed photos in good light, while the 16MP selfie camera is adequate for video calls. Owners often complain about the lack of an official brand warranty and the Chinese OriginOS software that shows medium level ads and receives only three major updates. The phone also feels bulky because of the large battery and the charger plug follows the Chinese standard, which may need an adapter.
