Vivo Y200 5G Is it worth buying?

The Vivo Y200 5G is a 2023 device that relies on a Snapdragon 4 Gen 1 chipset and a 6.67 inch AMOLED display to handle daily tasks.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize a high-quality AMOLED display and 5G connectivity on a budget and are comfortable managing a gray-import device.
Consider alternatives if
You require a manufacturer warranty or need a device that will receive long-term software updates.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Bright 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Capable 64MP main camera
  • Reliable Snapdragon 4 Gen 1 chipset
  • Support for 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs4

  • Only gray-import stock available
  • No manufacturer warranty
  • Limited remaining software update life
  • Contains medium levels of bloatware

Why we say that

At ৳22,490, you are paying for a screen that hits 800 nits of peak brightness and a 120Hz refresh rate. Because this model is now three years old, you should expect very little remaining software support from the manufacturer. You will likely encounter medium levels of bloatware and advertisements within the Funtouch 13 interface.

Since only gray-import stock is available, you must verify the BTRC status and IMEI of the unit with the seller before handing over your money. There is no manufacturer warranty provided for these units, so you are limited to whatever shop warranty the seller offers. The 4800 mAh battery is standard for this class, but the age of the device means you should be cautious about long-term performance expectations.
