What works well

  • 6.78" AMOLED 144Hz display
  • 6,000 mAh battery with 45W fast charge
  • Dimensity 7300 Ultimate chipset for decent performance
  • Headphone jack and IR blaster

Reported trade-offs

  • Camera quality is rubbish and lags
  • Software adds a lot of ads
  • No major Android updates promised
  • Official warranty costs more

Why we say that

The 6,000 mAh battery is praised as efficient and the 45W charger refills it quickly. The Dimensity 7300 Ultimate chipset delivers decent performance and the phone includes a headphone jack and an IR blaster. Owners, however, complain that the 64MP camera feels rubbish, often lags, and struggles in low light.

Software runs Android 15 with HiOS 15, but updates are slow, only one or two OS upgrades are expected, and the system shows a lot of ads. Overall the device feels solid for media consumption but falls short for photography and long‑term software support.
