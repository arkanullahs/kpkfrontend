Is the Realme C71 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts over two days
  • 45 W fast charging fills quickly
  • 50 MP main camera captures decent photos
  • Headphone jack and IP64 protection

What is not

  • 720p display looks dim in sunlight
  • Unisoc T7250 struggles with games
  • Fake secondary camera sensors
  • Software filled with bloatware and ads

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, fast charging and basic daily performance on a tight budget.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect smooth gaming, high‑resolution display or reliable camera performance.

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh battery and 45 W fast charging are genuinely impressive, keeping the device alive for over two days of normal use. The 6.67‑inch IPS LCD offers a smooth 120 Hz refresh, but its 720p resolution looks dim and pixelated under bright sunlight. The 50 MP main camera produces decent pictures in good light, yet the extra sensors are fake and the 5 MP selfie camera is disappointing.

Performance suffers from the Unisoc T7250 chipset, leading to lag in multitasking and poor gaming scores. Realme UI on Android 15 brings medium‑high bloatware and ads, further slowing the experience. The phone is built from plastic, carries an IP64 rating and a headphone jack, and the official version costs ৳19,949 with a brand warranty, while gray‑import models lack any cover.
