Buying verdict

Infinix Note 12 Is it worth buying?

Avoid this phone unless you only need a basic device for calls and light browsing.

Choose it if
You need a very cheap phone with a good screen and long battery life for light tasks.
Consider alternatives if
You want to play heavy games or need a reliable device that does not crash.

What works well

  • Bright and punchy AMOLED display
  • Excellent battery endurance
  • Decent 33W charging speed
  • Includes a headphone jack

Reported trade-offs

  • Frequent system crashes and restarts
  • Heavy bloatware and notification ads
  • Poor gaming performance
  • Plastic build with no protection

Why we say that

While the AMOLED screen is bright and the battery lasts up to two days, the actual experience is unstable. Owners report critical system issues including random crashes, auto restarts, and failures to show call notifications. The software is filled with about 20 bloatware apps and notification ads.

Performance is weak because the Helio G88 chipset stutters during heavy gaming. It is a 2022 model running Android 11, so you should expect little to no remaining software update life. Reliability issues make it a risky choice for long term use despite the low entry price.
