Is the Infinix Note 12 worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 1000 nits AMOLED display
  • Excellent battery endurance
  • Decent 33W charging speed
  • Acceptable main camera in light

What is not

  • Critical system crashes and restarts
  • Heavy bloatware and notification ads
  • Outdated 60Hz refresh rate
  • Poor gaming performance

Buy it if You need a secondary device primarily for media consumption on a bright screen and can tolerate frequent software bugs.

Skip it if You need a reliable phone for daily calls, gaming, or long-term use without system crashes.

Why we say that

While the 6.7 inch AMOLED display is bright and punchy, the software experience is marred by heavy bloatware and notification ads. Users frequently complain about call notification failures where the phone vibrates but fails to show the caller on screen. The Helio G88 chipset struggles with stutters, making heavy gaming barely playable.

Although the battery endurance is excellent, the build quality feels cheap despite its premium look. Since this device was released in 2022, you should expect little to no remaining software support. Reliability issues significantly impact the long-term value of this phone, as many owners struggle with the device freezing or restarting without warning.

If you prioritize a stable phone for daily communication, the reported system bugs make this a difficult device to recommend for your primary handset.
