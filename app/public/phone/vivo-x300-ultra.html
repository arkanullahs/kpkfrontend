Is the Vivo X300 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • 200MP main camera with OIS
  • 6.82 inch 144Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • 6600 mAh battery with 100W fast charge
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance

What is not

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Chinese OriginOS lacks Google and Bangla
  • Shop warranty only, repairs cost extra
  • Build quality only moderate

Buy it if Buy it if you want top‑tier camera and display and can accept a gray‑import warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need official service support or a lightweight UI.

Why we say that

The 200MP main sensor with OIS and a 3.7x tele lens delivers sharp photos and strong detail, while the 6.82‑inch LTPO AMOLED panel runs at 144Hz for smooth scrolling and gaming. Powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and 12 GB RAM, everyday tasks feel quick and heavy games run without stutter. The 6600 mAh battery holds a full day for most users and the 100W charger refills it in under an hour.

Owners praise the camera and screen but note the lack of an official warranty and the heavier Chinese OriginOS UI if they pick the CN version. The build feels solid but not premium, and the software includes medium‑level bloat. Expect three Android version updates on the global Funtouch model, fewer on the budget variants.
