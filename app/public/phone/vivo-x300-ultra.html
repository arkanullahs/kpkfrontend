Is the Vivo X300 Ultra worth buying?

Buying this phone means choosing raw power over comfort and software ease. It delivers a bright, smooth 144Hz display and high performance for gaming, but the physical experience is lacking. Owners describe the flat design as feeling like holding a brick. The biggest daily struggle is the software. Since these are gray imports from China, you get OriginOS instead of Funtouch OS. This means no preinstalled Google apps and no Bangla language support. While the 6600 mAh battery sounds large, owners report it is not efficient and provides only average battery life. You are paying a premium for a device that lacks brand warranty and requires you to verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

How is the battery life on the Vivo X300 Ultra?

Despite the 6600 mAh capacity, owners report that the battery is not efficient. It will get a user through a day, but the overall battery life is described as average.

Is this phone good for gaming?

Yes, it is very strong for gaming. It uses the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset and owners have given it a gaming rating of 8.0 and a performance rating of 9.0.

What software does this phone use and are there issues?

The units sold here use the China ROM with OriginOS. This version has no preinstalled Google apps and no Bangla language. Updates come only from Chinese servers, which creates friction for Google users.
