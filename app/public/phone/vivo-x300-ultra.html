Is the Vivo X300 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • 200MP main camera with OIS
  • 6600 mAh battery with 100W fast charge
  • 144 Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance

What is not

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Chinese OriginOS lacks Google services
  • Medium bloat and ads in software
  • Ease of use rated low by owners

Buy it if Buy it if you need top‑tier camera and battery and can live with a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you rely on official warranty or need a Bangla‑friendly UI out of the box.

Why we say that

The 6.82" LTPO AMOLED panel runs at 144 Hz and shows vivid colours, while the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset with 12 GB RAM handles everyday tasks and heavy gaming without stutter. Battery life is solid thanks to a 6600 mAh cell and 100W fast charging, though owners rate overall ease of use low. The main camera scores high in daylight, but low‑light performance can suffer on the Chinese OriginOS version that lacks Google services.

Software includes medium bloat and ads, and updates are limited to three Android versions on global builds. Expect to pay a shop‑only warranty and cover repair costs yourself.

Before you buy

Some sellers here list the China-market version (OriginOS (CN)), others the global one. Check which build you are buying
