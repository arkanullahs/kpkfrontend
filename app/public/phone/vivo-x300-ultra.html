Buying verdict

Vivo X300 Ultra Is it worth buying?

If you can live without a Bangladeshi manufacturer warranty, the Vivo X300 Ultra feels like a flagship in daily use.

Choose it if
You want flagship speed, a top camera and can accept a gray import without brand warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need official after‑sales support or a lightweight, simple user interface.

What works well

  • 200MP camera captures fine detail
  • 6600 mAh battery lasts a day
  • 144 Hz AMOLED display looks smooth
  • 100 W charging refills quickly

Reported trade-offs

  • No official warranty in Bangladesh
  • Chinese UI lacks Google services
  • UI can feel heavy
  • Build quality only average

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 delivers smooth performance and the 144 Hz AMOLED screen stays bright and fluid. The 200 MP main sensor records detailed photos and the 6600 mAh battery usually lasts a full day, while 100 W charging tops it up quickly. Owners praise the gaming scores and video quality but complain about the average build feel and a heavy UI that can be confusing, especially on the China version that lacks Google services.

The lack of official after‑sales support means any repair will cost extra, so be ready for that. Check whether the unit runs the global Funtouch UI or the Chinese OriginOS before buying, as the experience differs significantly.

Will the battery last a full day with heavy use?

The 6600 mAh cell is rated 8.2 out of 10 for endurance and usually lasts a day of mixed use. Heavy gaming or video streaming may require a top‑up before nightfall, but the 100 W charger can restore charge in under an hour.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 200 MP sensor with a f/1.9 aperture and optical image stabilization scores 9.6 for photography. It captures bright, detailed images even in dim settings, though the software UI may add processing lag on the Chinese version.

Does the phone come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray imports, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop you buy from may offer its own limited warranty, but any repair will be paid by the owner.

Which software will I get and how many updates are promised?

Global units run Funtouch OS on Android 16 and receive about two major OS upgrades. Chinese units run OriginOS, have no Google apps, and receive OTA updates only from Chinese servers. Choose the version that matches your app needs.
