Is the Nubia A56 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.75 inch IPS LCD with 90 Hz refresh
  • 5000 mAh battery provides full day use
  • Headphone jack and FM radio are still present
  • Official warranty at the lowest price

What is not

  • No microSD card slot
  • Front microphone sounds weak
  • Gaming performance is limited

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large display, decent battery life and official warranty at a price under ৳15,000.

Skip it if Skip it if you need high end gaming, expandable storage or a strong front microphone.

Why we say that

The 6.75 inch IPS LCD runs at 90 Hz and the 5000 mAh cell keeps the phone alive through a full day of browsing, video and social media. Performance is modest; the Unisoc T7200 and 4 GB RAM handle everyday apps smoothly but struggle with demanding games. The 13 MP rear camera captures decent daylight shots, yet low light results are average and video quality is modest.

Users appreciate the headphone jack, FM radio and the official warranty that comes at the cheapest price of ৳14,999. The main complaints are the lack of a microSD slot, a weak front microphone and limited gaming capability. Overall the phone feels like a solid entry level device for users who value screen size and battery life more than high end performance.
