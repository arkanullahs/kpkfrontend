OPPO Find X8s Is it worth buying?

The OPPO Find X8s is a powerful flagship that delivers top-tier performance and a compact design, but it comes with significant software hurdles for local users.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You are a power user who prioritizes raw performance and compact hardware over software convenience and official support.

  • Massive 5700 mAh battery in compact body
  • Industry-leading 1.25mm display bezels

Reported trade-offs4

  • Chinese ROM with untranslated system apps
  • Outdated USB 2.0 port speed
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Poor PD charging support

Why we say that

Owners praise the Dimensity 9400+ chipset for its gaming stability and the 5700 mAh battery for lasting a full day. However, living with this device requires patience because it is a gray import running a Chinese ROM. You will encounter untranslated Chinese text in the App Market, AI widgets, and Breeno features.

While the hardware is premium with an IP69 rating and thin bezels, the inclusion of a slow USB 2.0 port is a frustrating limitation for a phone at this level. Since there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh, you must rely entirely on the shop for support. The software experience is hindered by update friction and forced AI elements that may not suit everyone.

Does the OPPO Find X8s have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every listing is a gray import, meaning you must rely on the specific shop for any repair promises. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

How does the battery perform during daily use?

The phone features a massive 5700 mAh battery that owners report easily lasts a full day. It is highly efficient for a compact device, though you should note that the 80W charging is proprietary and has poor support for standard PD chargers.

Is this phone good for heavy gaming?

Yes, the Dimensity 9400+ chipset is incredibly fast and provides a stable 60fps experience in games like Genshin. Owners rate the gaming performance highly, though you may notice some warmth during extended video calls or heavy recording sessions.

What are the software limitations of this Chinese ROM version?

The software includes Chinese language remnants in the App Market, AI widgets, and Breeno. You will also face update friction and forced AI features. It is not a fully localized experience, which may impact ease of use for some buyers.
