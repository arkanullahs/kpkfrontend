Why we say that

It is not a smartphone and lacks support for modern apps. Owners report that the performance is very low and the camera is poor. The 2.8 inch screen has low resolution and basic visibility.

While it is easy to use for calls, the plastic build feels lightweight. You cannot play games on this device because the hardware does not support them. The battery is adequate for basic calling but has a small capacity.
