Samsung Galaxy M35 5G Is it worth buying?

If you can tolerate a heavy body and slow charging, the Galaxy M35 5G gives you a bright 120 Hz Super AMOLED screen, a large 6000 mAh battery and a 50 MP main camera with OIS for decent daylight shots.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large bright screen, solid battery life and decent daylight camera without needing fast charging or a light handset.
Consider alternatives if
You need a lightweight phone, fast charging or reliable low‑light photography.

What works well4

  • 120 Hz Super AMOLED display
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • 6000 mAh battery lasts a day
  • Exynos 1380 handles daily tasks

Reported trade-offs4

  • Heavy and bulky design
  • Slow 25 W charging, no charger included
  • Night photos noisy and distorted
  • Wi Fi range drops

Why we say that

The Exynos 1380 handles everyday apps smoothly, but sustained gaming can stutter and the phone feels bulky in the hand. Battery lasts a full day under normal use, yet recent software updates have introduced noticeable drain and the 25 W charger is not included, making a full charge take about two hours. Wi Fi range can drop despite the Wi Fi 6 label, and night photography shows noise and colour distortion.

The device runs Android 14 with One UI 8.5 and promises four years of OS updates, but there is no official Samsung warranty in Bangladesh, only the shop’s limited cover. Overall it is a capable mid‑range phone if you accept its compromises.
