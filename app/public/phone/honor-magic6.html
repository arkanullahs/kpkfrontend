Honor Magic 6 Is it worth buying?

The Honor Magic 6 is a powerful device that delivers top-tier performance but comes with significant software and camera compromises.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize raw processing power and gaming performance above all else and are comfortable managing a gray-import device.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on a stable, bug-free software experience or require a reliable camera for low-light photography.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Fast Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 performance
  • Reliable all-day battery endurance
  • Bright 120Hz LTPO OLED display
  • Durable metal chassis with IP68/69 rating

Reported trade-offs4

  • Inconsistent camera colors and poor shadow handling
  • MagicOS software bugs and intrusive ads
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Slippery build and surface heating during gaming

Why we say that

Owners consistently praise the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset for its speed and lack of throttling during intense gaming sessions. However, the user experience is hampered by MagicOS, which includes ads, language bugs that sometimes display Chinese text, and restrictive split-screen multitasking. While the 5000 nits peak brightness on the 6.78 inch display is impressive, the camera system struggles with inconsistent colors and poor shadow processing in low-light conditions.

The metal chassis feels premium with IP68/69 protection, though the finish is quite slippery. You should expect the camera area to get warm during long gaming sessions. Because this is a gray import, you will not receive any manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh.

Does the Honor Magic 6 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import. You must rely solely on the specific coverage promised by the shop where you purchase the device, and all repairs will be paid.

How does the camera perform in low-light conditions?

Owners report that the camera struggles significantly in low light. The processing often makes shadows look darker than they appear in reality, and the color reproduction is frequently inconsistent across different shots.

Is this phone suitable for heavy gaming?

The phone is excellent for gaming because the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset maintains high speeds without throttling. You should be aware that the camera area can become quite toasty during extended gaming sessions.

How long will the Honor Magic 6 receive software updates?

This device is expected to receive 3 to 4 OS updates as part of the flagship support cycle. Please note that the current MagicOS software includes medium levels of bloatware and ads, along with some reported language bugs.
