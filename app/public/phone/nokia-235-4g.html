Nokia 235 4G Is it worth buying?

The Nokia 235 4G is a basic feature phone that serves strictly for calling and texting.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs

What works well4

  • Durable and classic Nokia plastic build
  • Long battery standby time
  • Simple and easy to use
  • Includes headphone jack and FM radio

Reported trade-offs4

  • Extremely low resolution camera
  • Poor display quality
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • No gaming capability

Why we say that

Living with this device means accepting a very low resolution 2.8 inch TFT display and a 2MP camera that owners consistently rate poorly. While the build quality feels durable and classic, the performance is limited to simple tasks. You will not find any gaming capability here, and the software experience is basic.

Because every unit currently available in Bangladesh is a gray import, you must be aware that there is no manufacturer warranty support. Any repairs required will be paid out of your own pocket, and you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before handing over your money. It is a functional tool for communication, but it lacks the modern features or support that many buyers expect today.

Does the Nokia 235 4G come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every listing currently found is a gray import. You will have to rely on whatever limited coverage the individual shop provides, and all repairs will be paid.

Can I play games on this phone?

No, the Nokia 235 4G has no gaming capability. Owners report that the device is strictly for basic calling and texting. The performance is not designed to handle games or demanding applications.

How is the camera quality for taking photos?

The camera quality is very poor. Owners report an extremely low resolution from the 2MP main camera. It is not suitable for capturing clear or detailed images.

The brand is currently in transition following the HMD split. While the phone runs on S30+ software, there is no information regarding future software updates for this specific model.
