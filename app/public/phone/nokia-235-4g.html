Why we say that

It feels durable thanks to the classic Nokia plastic build. Owners find it very easy to use, but the hardware is very limited. The display is a small, low resolution TFT panel that users dislike.

The 2MP camera is a major point of complaint, with owners reporting extremely low resolution photos. You cannot play games on this device as it has no gaming capability. Performance is slow and only suits basic tasks.
