Is the OPPO Reno 13F worth buying?

What is good

  • Exceptional battery life lasting over a day
  • Bright 2100 nit AMOLED display
  • Fluid and intuitive ColorOS 15 software
  • Premium build quality with IP69 rating

What is not

  • Outdated processor with poor gaming performance
  • Grainy low light photos and shaky video
  • Random network drops affecting connectivity
  • Slow 45W charging for the price

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize long battery life and a bright display for daily tasks over gaming or high-end photography.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a mobile gamer or a photography enthusiast who needs reliable low light performance and stable video.

Why we say that

While the 5800 mAh battery provides exceptional endurance that easily lasts over a day, owners frequently report frustration with random network drops that disrupt calls and gaming. The display is a highlight, offering excellent sunlight visibility with its 2100 nits peak brightness. However, the camera system is a major disappointment for many, producing grainy and noisy images in low light while suffering from shaky video stabilization.

Gamers will find the Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 chipset limiting, as it requires low settings to avoid frame drops. Although the ColorOS 15 software is fluid and intuitive, the presence of bloatware and the slow 45W charging speed make it difficult to recommend at this official price point. Owners feel the device is overpriced for the hardware provided, especially given the reports of screen fragility despite the premium build and IP69 rating.

Does the OPPO Reno 13F have a good battery for long days?

Yes, the phone features a 5800 mAh battery that provides exceptional endurance. Owners report that it easily lasts over a full day of use, making it a reliable choice for those who need a phone that does not require frequent charging.

Is the OPPO Reno 13F suitable for playing heavy games?

The phone is not ideal for gaming because it uses the dated Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 chipset. Owners report that it requires low settings to run games, and you may still experience frame drops on medium settings during extended sessions.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The camera performance in low light is poor. Owners note that images often appear grainy and noisy, and the lack of effective stabilization makes video footage quite shaky, which may disappoint those looking for a strong camera experience.
