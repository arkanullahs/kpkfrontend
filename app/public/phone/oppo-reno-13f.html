Buying verdict

OPPO Reno 13F Is it worth buying?

This phone is a mixed bag that prioritizes battery and screen over raw power.

Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life and a high quality screen over gaming performance.
Consider alternatives if
You are a gamer or need a reliable camera for low light and video.

What works well

  • Exceptional 5800 mAh battery endurance
  • Bright 2100 nit AMOLED display
  • Premium feel with IP69 rating
  • Fluid ColorOS 15 software

Reported trade-offs

  • Dated processor with poor gaming performance
  • Random network drops affecting calls
  • Grainy low light photos and shaky video
  • Slow 45W charging for the price

Why we say that

You get a very bright AMOLED display and a massive 5800 mAh battery that easily lasts over a day. However, owners report significant issues with network instability and random drops during calls and gaming. The Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 chipset feels dated, leading to frame drops on medium settings and a need for low settings when gaming.

While the build feels premium and has an IP69 rating, some users have reported screen fragility. The cameras struggle in low light with grainy and noisy images, and video footage is often shaky due to a lack of effective stabilization. Many users feel the device is severely overpriced given the outdated processor and average camera performance.
