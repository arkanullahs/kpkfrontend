Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

The brand warranty applies to the official unit only. Cheaper listings are gray imports with no manufacturer cover. If you take a gray import to a service centre, they will only accept it as a paid repair.

Can I play games on the Nokia 150 DS 2023?

No, this phone is not suitable for gaming. Owners report that the device freezes or lags when trying to play games, and it received a gaming rating of 2.0 out of 10.

How is the battery life for daily use?

The battery life is poor. Users describe the 1450 mAh battery as small and inefficient. Some owners report that the battery drains quickly and lasts only a few hours.

Will I get software updates for this phone?

You should expect short or no remaining software update life. The device is 3 years old and the brand is currently in transition following the HMD split.
