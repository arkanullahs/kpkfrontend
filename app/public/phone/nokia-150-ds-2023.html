Is the Nokia 150 DS 2023 worth buying?

What is good

  • Simple ease of use
  • Includes headphone jack
  • Built-in FM radio
  • No bloatware or ads

What is not

  • Battery drains very quickly
  • Slow and unresponsive performance
  • Low resolution display
  • Unsuitable for gaming

Buy it if Buy this phone only if you need a very basic device for simple calling and do not mind charging it frequently throughout the day.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you expect reliable battery life, decent display quality, or the ability to play games without the device freezing.

Why we say that

Owners report that the battery is small and inefficient, often draining in just a few hours of use. The performance is consistently slow and unresponsive, making even simple tasks feel sluggish. Users are particularly disappointed with the display, noting that colors appear washed out and the brightness is insufficient for outdoor visibility.

While the device is easy to use, the hardware limitations are significant. You should be aware that this model is 3 years old, meaning it will likely receive no further software updates. The camera is basic with no extra features, and the phone is entirely unsuitable for gaming due to frequent freezing and lag.

If you need a device that lasts through the day or handles more than basic calls, this phone will likely frustrate you.
