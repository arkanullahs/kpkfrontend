Why we say that

The 6.83 inch AMOLED screen delivers 1900 nits peak brightness and smooth 120Hz motion, making outdoor viewing comfortable. The 200MP main sensor captures detailed photos for social media, though it lacks a telephoto lens and struggles in low‑light scenes. Performance feels adequate for everyday apps but the Dimensity 7360 chipset falls short in demanding games, a point many owners note as overpriced for this segment.

The phone runs Android 16 with OriginOS 6, which is smooth but includes noticeable bloatware. Build quality is protected by IP68/69 rating, yet the plastic frame feels cheap to some users. Overall, it suits users who value endurance and display quality more than top‑tier gaming or night photography.
