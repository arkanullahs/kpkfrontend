Buying verdict

Honor 500 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can game for hours on a single charge, the Honor 500 Pro delivers that and more.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life, high‑end gaming and a bright display and can accept gray‑import risks.
Consider alternatives if

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 8000 mAh battery with 80 W fast charge
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite performance for gaming
  • 6.55‑inch AMOLED display with 120 Hz refresh
  • 200 MP main camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official brand warranty
  • Front camera quality is disappointing
  • Speaker volume is weak
  • 5G band compatibility issues

Why we say that

The 8000 mAh battery paired with 80 W fast charging keeps the device alive through a full day of heavy use, and the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset provides flagship‑level performance in games and multitasking. The 6.55‑inch AMOLED panel with 120 Hz refresh and a peak brightness of 6000 nits makes the screen bright and smooth, even in sunlight. The 200 MP main camera with OIS captures detailed photos, though the 50 MP selfie shooter falls short of expectations.

Owners praise the durability thanks to an IP69K rating, but they complain about weak speaker output, inconsistent 5G band support and the lack of an official warranty because only gray‑import units are available. Software runs MagicOS 10 on Android 16, offering decent updates but medium bloat and uncertain Google services on some Chinese builds.

Will the battery really last a whole day?

The 8000 mAh cell is rated for exceptional endurance and owners give it a 9.7 rating, so most users see a full day of heavy use. Some users question the exact capacity, but real‑world tests confirm long life.

How does the phone perform in games?

Powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite and 12 GB RAM, the device scores a 9.6 rating for gaming. It runs flagship titles smoothly at high settings and handles multitasking without lag.

Is the camera good in low‑light situations?

The 200 MP main sensor with a large 1/1.4" size and OIS captures detailed images, and it performs reasonably well in low light. The 50 MP selfie camera, however, is reported as disappointing in similar conditions.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

Only gray‑import stock is available, so there is no official brand warranty; you must rely on shop warranty and verify IMEI status. MagicOS 10 on Android 16 typically receives three to four major OS upgrades, with medium bloat and occasional update delays.
