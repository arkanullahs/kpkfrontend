ZTE V80 Max Is it worth buying?

What works well4

  • 6000 mAh battery gives excellent endurance
  • 50 MP main sensor provides decent photos
  • Headphone jack allows wired audio
  • IP64 rating offers water and dust resistance

Reported trade-offs4

  • Unisoc T7250 chipset causes noticeable lag
  • eMMC storage creates slow data handling
  • Display viewing angles are poor
  • HD+ resolution feels low on 6.9‑inch screen

Why we say that

The 6.9‑inch IPS LCD panel runs at 120 Hz but suffers from poor viewing angles and only HD+ resolution, so it looks fine up close but loses clarity from the side. The Unisoc T7250 processor paired with eMMC storage creates noticeable lag during multitasking and gaming, and owners report stutter when many apps stay open. The 50 MP main camera is the only useful sensor; auxiliary lenses add no real benefit.

Battery life is the standout feature, easily lasting a full day of mixed use thanks to the 6000 mAh cell. The phone includes a headphone jack and an IP64 rating, rare at this price. Official warranty is only for the genuine unit; cheaper gray imports lack manufacturer cover and require paid repairs.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 6000 mAh battery provides excellent endurance and can easily last a full day of mixed usage such as browsing, social media and video playback without needing a charge.

Can I play modern games smoothly on this phone?

The Unisoc T7250 chipset and eMMC storage are weak for gaming, so you will notice lag and stutter in most modern titles, especially when graphics settings are high.

Is the 50 MP camera good in low‑light conditions?

The phone only has one usable 50 MP sensor and no dedicated low‑light hardware, so photos in dim environments will be average at best and lack detail.

What does the official warranty cover compared to gray imports?

The official warranty applies only to the genuine unit sold by the brand and covers manufacturer defects. Gray‑import listings have no manufacturer cover and any repair at a service centre is paid.
