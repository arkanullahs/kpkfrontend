Is the ZTE V80 Max worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • Large 6.9‑inch 120 Hz screen
  • Headphone jack included
  • Durable plastic body

What is not

  • 720p resolution feels blurry
  • Unisoc T7250 struggles with games
  • Main camera lacks low‑light ability
  • No dedicated gallery app

Buy it if If you need a big screen phone with long battery life and can accept modest performance and camera quality, this model is worth considering.

Skip it if If you expect smooth gaming, a high‑resolution display, or a fully featured software experience, look for another device.

Why we say that

At ৳17,299 you get a 6.9 inch IPS LCD panel that refreshes at 120 hertz, but the resolution stays at HD plus which makes the picture look soft and the viewing angles are not great. The 6000 mAh cell easily lasts a full day of browsing, video and calls and even supports 10 watt reverse charging, yet the 22.5 watt charger needs about one and a half hours to fill it completely. The Unisoc T7250 processor with 8 gigabytes of RAM can handle everyday apps but it stutters with heavy multitasking and cannot run demanding games smoothly.

The 50 megapixel main shooter captures decent daylight shots but struggles in low light and the extra lenses do not add any real benefit. Android 16 runs without heavy bloat but you will miss a built‑in gallery app. Overall the phone feels like a budget‑friendly media device that sacrifices performance and visual sharpness.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?
