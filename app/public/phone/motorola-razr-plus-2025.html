Why we say that

Living with this device means enjoying a bright 3000 nits screen and fast 100W charging, but you must accept the reality of gray-market ownership. Because every unit is a gray import, there is no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh. You are entirely dependent on the specific shop for any repair promises, and you will have to pay for all maintenance costs yourself.

The software experience is clean thanks to the near-stock Hello UI, though Motorola has a history of weak update support. You should be prepared for the fact that this phone will only receive between 1 and 3 OS updates. Does the Motorola Razr Plus 2025 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, so you must rely solely on the warranty provided by the specific shop where you purchase the device.

How many years of software updates will this phone receive?

Motorola has a history of weak update support for this model. You can expect between 1 and 3 OS updates throughout the lifespan of the device.

Is the 4000 mAh battery enough for daily use?

The phone features a 4000 mAh battery. While this capacity powers the foldable display, you should consider your usage habits as the device also supports fast 100W charging to help you get back to full power quickly.

What should I check before buying this unofficial phone?

You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before completing your purchase. Since these are gray-import units, verifying the registration status is essential to ensure the phone will function correctly on local networks.
