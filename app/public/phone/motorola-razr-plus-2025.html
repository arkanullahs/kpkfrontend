Motorola Razr Plus 2025 Is it worth buying?

If you can live without an official warranty, the Razr Plus offers flagship performance in a foldable form at a relatively low price.

Reported trade-offs3

  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Software updates historically weak
  • Battery may drain fast at high brightness

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset delivers smooth multitasking and gaming scores around eight out of ten from owners. The 6.9 inch LTPO AMOLED panel refreshes at 165 Hz and can reach 3000 nits, making bright outdoor use easy. Battery capacity is 4000 mAh but the high brightness and fast 100 W charger keep daily use realistic, though heavy screen use will drain it faster.

The 50 MP main sensor and 32 MP selfie camera capture detailed photos, and the near‑stock Hello UI adds low ads and bloat. Software updates have been weak historically, with only one to three major Android versions expected. Because the phone is a gray import, the shop warranty is the only protection and any repair will cost extra.

Is there an official warranty for the Motorola Razr Plus 2025 in Bangladesh?

How long will the 4000 mAh battery last in everyday use?

The 4000 mAh cell can comfortably last a day with moderate screen use and balanced settings. If you keep the 3000‑nit brightness and 165 Hz refresh active, the battery will drain noticeably faster.

Will the Razr Plus handle high‑end mobile games smoothly?

Yes, the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 4nm processor provides enough power for most high‑end games. Owner ratings give the gaming experience an eight out of ten, indicating smooth performance at typical settings.
