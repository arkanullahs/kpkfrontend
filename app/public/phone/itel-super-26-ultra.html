Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Stunning 144Hz curved AMOLED display
  • Massive 6000 mAh battery endurance
  • Premium slim feel with Gorilla Glass 7i
  • Bright 4500 nits peak brightness

Reported trade-offs4

  • Very slow 18W charging speed
  • SIM detection and connectivity failures
  • Heavy ads and bloatware
  • Below average single speaker audio

Why we say that

Owners love the 1.5K curved AMOLED display and the 6000 mAh battery endurance. However, the experience is marred by heavy ads and bloatware in the software. There are serious reports of SIM detection failures after restarting in low signal areas, which requires reinserting the card.

The thin chassis causes the phone to heat up quickly under load. You also have to deal with a single bottom firing speaker that produces below average sound. While daily tasks are smooth, the 18W charging is very slow for such a large battery.

How is the gaming performance on this phone?

The Unisoc T7300 chipset handles daily tasks smoothly and supports consistent gaming on mid settings. However, the thin chassis leads to faster heat generation when you play games for a while.

Does the battery last long and charge quickly?

The 6000 mAh battery provides massive endurance for users. However, the 18W charging is very slow and is considered the lowest charging speed for this price point.

Will I get regular software updates?

The phone runs Android 15 with itel OS 15. You should expect very few updates as the evidence indicates that updates are rarely provided for this software.
