Buying verdict

Realme Narzo 90 Is it worth buying?

This phone is a battery powerhouse that comes with a very bright screen.

Choose it if
You need a long lasting battery and a bright screen for outdoor use.
Consider alternatives if
You want an official brand warranty or need a 256GB storage option.

What works well

  • Large 7000 mAh battery
  • Very bright 120Hz AMOLED display
  • IP69 rated build materials
  • Up to 3 major Android upgrades

Reported trade-offs

  • No 256GB storage variant
  • Medium to high bloatware and ads
  • DND mode notification issues
  • Poor video quality ratings

Why we say that

Owners appreciate the 7000 mAh battery and the IP69 rated build quality. However, it is not perfect for everyone. Some users report a bug where notifications still appear even when DND mode is turned on.

The software comes with medium to high levels of bloatware and ads. Video quality is a weak point according to owner ratings. You should also be aware that there is no 256GB storage option available.

Read full reasoning

Since only gray imports are available in the market, you will get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty. You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying.
