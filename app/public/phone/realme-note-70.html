Buying verdict

Realme Note 70 Is it worth buying?

The Realme Note 70 will sit in your pocket for a full day without hunting for a charger, but you will feel the lag whenever you open several apps or try to play a game.

Choose it if
Buy if you need long battery life and basic media use.
Consider alternatives if
Skip if you want fast gaming or high‑quality photos.

What works well

  • Huge 6300mAh battery
  • Large 6.74 inch LCD
  • Headphone jack included
  • Slim design with armor shell

Reported trade-offs

  • Very slow 15W charging
  • Laggy performance and poor gaming
  • Mediocre camera quality
  • Display brightness low and 90Hz inconsistent

Why we say that

The 6300mAh battery is the strongest point, yet the 15W charger needs two to three hours to fill it. The 6.74 inch LCD shows a water‑drop notch and its 90Hz refresh can drop, making scrolling feel uneven. The 13MP rear sensor and 5MP front camera produce soft images that lack detail, especially in low light.

Realme UI 5.0 adds many pre‑installed apps that slow the system further. If you accept occasional freezes and modest photo quality, the phone offers a large screen and a headphone jack at a low price.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 6300mAh battery gives industry‑leading endurance for light users, easily lasting a full day of calls, messaging and video streaming. Heavy gaming or bright screen use will reduce that time noticeably.

Will this phone handle games smoothly?

The Unisoc T7250 chipset causes frequent lag and low frame rates, barely reaching thirty to forty fps in most games. Expect choppy performance even on modest titles.

Is the camera good for low‑light photos?

The 13MP rear sensor and 5MP front camera are outdated and lack sharpness. In low‑light conditions the images become soft and noisy, so they are not suitable for serious photography.
