Is the Realme Note 70 worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6300 mAh battery lasting 2‑3 days
  • Slim design with military grade armor shell
  • Headphone jack for wired audio
  • Android 15 with Realme UI 5.0

What is not

  • 15 W charging takes over 2.5 hours
  • Dim 450 nit display struggles in sunlight
  • Laggy performance and heating during gaming
  • Poor selfie camera and outdated low light shots

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize long battery life and need a headphone jack.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect smooth gaming, fast charging, or strong camera performance.

Why we say that

Its 6300 mAh battery can keep light users alive for two to three days, and the device still includes a headphone jack for wired audio. The design is slim and wrapped in a military grade armor shell, which gives a solid feel in hand. It runs Android 15 with Realme UI 5.0, so the software is up to date, though medium high bloatware and occasional system freezes are reported after a month of use.

The 6.74 inch LCD panel runs at 90 Hz but often drops to lower refresh rates and only reaches about 450 nits, making outdoor visibility dim. Charging at 15 W is painfully slow, taking over two and a half hours. The 13 MP main camera is acceptable in daylight, but the 5 MP selfie shooter feels outdated, and low light shots are weak.

Gaming performance is laggy and the phone heats up after thirty minutes of play. Overall the phone feels overpriced for the hardware when compared with similar models from Tecno or Infinix.
