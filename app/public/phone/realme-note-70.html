Realme Note 70 Is it worth buying?

The Realme Note 70 will keep you powered all day but will frustrate you with slow performance and charging.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need long battery life, a big display and can accept slow charging and modest performance.
Consider alternatives if
You expect smooth gaming, fast charging or high‑quality photos.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Huge 6300 mAh battery lasts all day
  • Headphone jack for wired audio
  • Attractive design with notification light
  • Large 6.74‑inch screen

Reported trade-offs4

  • 15 W charging takes 2‑3 hours
  • Unisoc chip causes frequent lag
  • 13 MP camera produces soft images
  • Refresh rate drops to 60 Hz

Why we say that

Its 6.74‑inch LCD screen is large enough for media, yet the 720p panel looks dim and the 90Hz refresh often falls back to 60Hz. The Unisoc T7250 chipset struggles with everyday multitasking and games, leading to noticeable lag and occasional freezes. Battery life is the strongest point, with a 6300 mAh cell that easily lasts through basic use, but the 15 W charger needs two to three hours to fill it.

Camera results are soft and lack detail, especially in low light, and the pre‑installed Realme UI apps add to the feeling of bloat. Build quality feels cheap despite an attractive look and a handy headphone jack. Expect a paid repair if you buy a gray‑import unit.
