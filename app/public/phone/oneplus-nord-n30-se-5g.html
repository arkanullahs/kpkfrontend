Why we say that

The OnePlus Nord N30 SE 5G runs Android 13 with OxygenOS 13.1 and offers a 6.72 inch IPS LCD, 5G, a 50MP main sensor and a headphone jack at ৳16,990. The Dimensity 6020 chipset with 4GB RAM and 128GB storage feels sluggish when more than one app is open and the camera app hangs on every launch. Owners rate the battery at 6.0 but report barely six hours of screen on time with regular use.

The display brightness is often insufficient and the build quality scores only 4.0 out of 10. Gaming performance is low with a rating of 3.0, and the phone lacks any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh, leaving repairs to the shop’s own policy. If you can accept these limits, the phone still provides decent photos in good light and basic 5G connectivity.
