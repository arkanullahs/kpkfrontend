Buying verdict

Infinix Smart 8 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Infinix Smart 8 Pro gives you a big, bright screen and a large battery but you will feel the lag in everyday use.

Choose it if
You need a large screen, decent camera and long battery at a low price and can tolerate slow charging and occasional lag.
Consider alternatives if
You want smooth gaming, fast charging or a stable, low‑ad software experience.

What works well

  • 6.6 inch 90Hz IPS LCD display
  • 50MP main camera
  • 5000 mAh battery capacity
  • Durable build with headphone jack

Reported trade-offs

  • Very slow 10W charging
  • Severe lag in apps and games
  • Frequent software crashes and heavy bloat

Why we say that

It sports a 6.6 inch IPS LCD panel that can refresh at 90Hz and reach 500 nits at peak, which makes scrolling and video watching pleasant. The 50MP main sensor can capture decent photos in good light, and the 5000 mAh cell provides enough juice for a full day of moderate use. However, the Helio G36 chipset struggles with basic apps, causing noticeable stutter on TikTok, YouTube and simple games.

Charging is limited to 10W, so refilling the battery takes a long time. Software runs Android 13 Go with XOS 13, but it is heavy on pre‑installed ads and crashes often. Build quality is solid and the phone includes a headphone jack and FM radio, which many users appreciate.

How long does the battery last on the Infinix Smart 8 Pro?

The phone has a 5000 mAh battery which can easily last a full day of normal use such as browsing, messaging and occasional video. Charging is limited to 10W, so refilling the battery takes considerably longer than on faster‑charging phones.

Can I play games smoothly on the Infinix Smart 8 Pro?

Gaming performance is poor because the Helio G36 SoC lags badly with basic titles. Users report severe lag on TikTok, 720p video and simple games, and the base Smart 8 with a T606 chip runs smoother.

Is the 50MP camera good in low light?

The 50MP sensor captures decent photos in bright conditions, but reviews describe overall camera quality as mediocre. Low‑light performance is not strong, and some users still prefer the base Smart 8 for better results.

Does the phone come with an official warranty and software updates?

Yes, the official Bangladeshi unit includes the brand’s warranty and access to its service network. It runs Android 13 Go with XOS 13 and is expected to receive one to two OS updates, though updates are generally slow.
