Is the Tecno Phantom V Fold2 worth buying?

What is good

  • Large 7.85 inch foldable LTPO AMOLED display
  • Strong gaming performance with Dimensity 9000+ chipset
  • Capable 50MP main camera with OIS
  • Generous 12GB RAM and 512GB storage

What is not

  • Poor battery life and overheating issues
  • Lack of consistent software updates
  • High levels of bloatware and ads
  • Disappointing camera zoom quality

Buy it if You are a mobile enthusiast who prioritizes a large foldable screen and gaming performance above all other concerns.

Skip it if You need a reliable daily driver with long battery life, consistent software support, and a polished camera system.

Why we say that

While the large 7.85 inch foldable display and Dimensity 9000+ chipset offer decent performance for gaming, owners are deeply frustrated by the device. The most significant complaints center on poor battery endurance and excessive heat, with users reporting that the 5750 mAh capacity fails to last through the day. Software support is another major pain point, as owners feel abandoned by the lack of timely updates beyond Android 14.

The camera system, despite featuring a 50MP main sensor with OIS and 2x telephoto capabilities, is frequently criticized for needing significant quality and zoom improvements. With high levels of bloatware and ads in HiOS, the daily user experience is often described as disappointing. Prospective buyers should weigh these functional shortcomings carefully against the 130,990 taka price point before committing to this foldable.
