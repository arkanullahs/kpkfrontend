Is the OPPO Find X8 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • 6100 mAh battery with 100 W fast charge
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite delivers top‑tier speed
  • 50 MP main camera with periscope zoom
  • 6.82‑inch LTPO AMOLED 120 Hz display

What is not

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Chinese ROM shows language gaps and notification bugs
  • Device can overheat under prolonged load
  • Peak brightness lower than rival flagships

Buy it if Buy it if you want flagship speed, great camera and long battery and accept a gray‑import warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need official brand support or cannot tolerate occasional overheating.

Why we say that

The 6100 mAh silicon‑carbon battery lasts well beyond a day of normal use and refills in half an hour with the 100 W charger. The Snapdragon 8 Elite processor paired with up to 16 GB RAM makes apps open instantly and games run at high frame rates, though the device can become warm during long sessions. The 50 MP main camera with a large sensor and dual periscopes delivers sharp photos and good zoom, and the 6.82‑inch LTPO AMOLED panel shows crisp 1440p images at 120 Hz, though its peak brightness trails some rivals.

ColorOS 16 on Android 15 offers the usual features but the Chinese ROM shows language gaps, occasional notification glitches and reports usage to Chinese servers. There is no official manufacturer warranty; only the shop’s limited coverage applies, so verify IMEI status before buying.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 6100 mAh silicon‑carbon battery is rated for excellent endurance, easily lasting a full day of mixed use such as browsing, video streaming and moderate gaming, and it can be refilled from zero to full in about thirty minutes with the 100 W charger.

Is the phone suitable for heavy gaming?

The Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset together with up to 16 GB RAM makes the phone very fast in games, delivering high frame rates on the 120 Hz screen. Some users notice the device heating after long sessions, but performance stays strong.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with a large 1/1.56‑inch size and f/1.8 aperture captures bright images at night, and the periscope zoom adds detail. Owners rate the camera highly, though the Chinese ROM may affect some software features.

Will the phone receive official updates and is there any warranty?

The device runs ColorOS 16 on Android 15 and is promised three to four major OS upgrades like other flagships, but it arrives as a gray‑import so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s limited warranty applies.
