Is the OPPO Find X8 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • 6100mAh battery with 100W fast charging
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite gives top performance
  • 50MP main camera with periscopes
  • 6.82 inch LTPO AMOLED display

What is not

  • China ROM shows unwanted Chinese text
  • Overheating during long gaming sessions
  • No eSIM support
  • Brightness lower than rival flagships

Buy it if Buy it if you want flagship hardware at a gray‑import price and are comfortable managing a Chinese ROM and shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need official brand warranty, eSIM, or a phone that stays cool under marathon gaming.

Why we say that

The 6.82 inch LTPO AMOLED screen shows sharp 1440p images but peaks at 2500 nits, which some users say feels dimmer than rival phones. The Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset delivers fast apps and games, yet it can heat up and throttle during long sessions. The 6100mAh silicon‑carbon battery lasts through a full day and refuels in minutes with 100W charging, though the 100W claim is real only with the supplied charger.

Camera reviewers love the 50MP main sensor and dual periscopes, but the China ROM adds unwanted Chinese text and no eSIM support. Overall it feels like a high‑end device softened by software quirks and warranty uncertainty.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 6100mAh silicon‑carbon battery is rated very high by owners and usually lasts a full day of mixed activities. When it runs low, the 100W charger can refill it in a few minutes, which many users find convenient.

Will the phone overheat while gaming?

The Snapdragon 8 Elite provides excellent gaming speed, but owners report the device tends to heat up and may throttle when play is prolonged. Short sessions feel smooth, while long sessions require occasional cooling breaks.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50MP main sensor with a 1/1.56 inch size, f/1.8 aperture and optical image stabilization delivers strong low‑light results. Dual periscopes add zoom capability, and reviewers label the stills as elite for this price range.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

The phone comes with a shop warranty, not the official OPPO guarantee, so verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller. ColorOS 16 on Android 15 will receive about three to four major Android upgrades, but the China ROM may show Chinese text and occasional notification bugs.
