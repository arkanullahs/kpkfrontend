Vivo X300 Is it worth buying?

The Vivo X300 is a powerful, compact device that excels in performance and battery life but requires careful consideration regarding its unofficial status in Bangladesh.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a high-performance, compact flagship and are comfortable managing a gray-import device without official brand support.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on Google services and need a hassle-free experience with official warranty coverage and local language support.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Outstanding 6040 mAh battery life
  • Top-tier performance and gaming
  • Compact and comfortable build
  • Bright 4500 nits peak display

Reported trade-offs4

  • Aggressive skin smoothing in portraits
  • Oversaturated camera color profile
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Weak ultrawide camera hardware

Why we say that

Owners report that the 6040 mAh battery is outstanding for daily use, and the Dimensity 9500 chipset provides top-tier gaming responsiveness while running cooler than Snapdragon alternatives. However, the camera system has notable quirks, including aggressive skin smoothing in portraits and oversaturated colors. The ultrawide lens is also a hardware step down from the main sensors.

Because these units are gray imports, you must verify if you are receiving the global Funtouch OS version or the China-market OriginOS, as the latter lacks preinstalled Google services and Bangla language support. You will not receive any manufacturer warranty, so you must rely entirely on the specific shop for any repair needs.

Does the Vivo X300 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you must rely on the specific shop for any coverage or repairs. You should confirm the status of your purchase directly with the seller.

How does the battery perform during daily use?

Owners report that the 6040 mAh battery is outstanding. It delivers exceptionally solid real-world battery life, making it one of the most reliable aspects of the device according to user ratings.

Is the Vivo X300 suitable for heavy gaming?

Yes, the phone is excellent for gaming. It is powered by the Dimensity 9500 chip, which provides top-tier responsiveness and smoothness. Owners also note that the device runs significantly cooler under load compared to modern Snapdragon counterparts.

What is the difference between the global and China-market versions?

The global version runs Funtouch OS, which is light and includes Google services. The China-market version runs OriginOS, which is prettier but lacks preinstalled Google services, Bangla language support, and requires OTA updates from Chinese servers, creating daily friction for many users.
