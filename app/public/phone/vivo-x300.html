Buying verdict

Vivo X300 Is it worth buying?

If you can live without an official warranty, the Vivo X300 gives you flagship‑level speed, a 90 W fast charge and a display that stays bright even in sunlight.

Choose it if
You want flagship performance, a bright display and long battery life and you are comfortable with a shop‑only warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need an official brand warranty or a phone that works with Google services out of the box.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • 4500‑nit LTPO AMOLED display
  • 6040 mAh battery with 90 W fast charge
  • Dimensity 9500 gives top‑tier gaming performance
  • 200 MP main camera with OIS and 3× telephoto

Reported trade-offs

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Chinese OriginOS version lacks Google services
  • Portrait mode skin smoothing can look artificial
  • Ultrawide camera is noticeably weaker

Why we say that

The 6.31‑inch LTPO AMOLED panel reaches a peak of 4500 nits, making outdoor viewing comfortable. The 6040 mAh battery easily lasts a full day of mixed use and the 90 W charger refills it in under an hour, while a 40 W wireless pad is also supported. Dimensity 9500 delivers smooth gaming and high performance scores, and the 200 MP main camera with OIS, 3× telephoto and ultrawide lens produces detailed photos, though portraits may look overly smoothed and colors can be oversaturated.

The ultrawide sensor is weaker than the main lenses. The phone arrives as a gray import, so only the shop’s limited warranty applies and repairs are paid. Choose the global version if you need Google services, otherwise the Chinese OriginOS version will lack them.
