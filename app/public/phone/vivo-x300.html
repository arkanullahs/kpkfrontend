Is the Vivo X300 worth buying?

What is good

  • Compact 6.31‑inch LTPO AMOLED display
  • 4500‑nit peak brightness
  • 90W fast wired charging
  • 200MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Gray‑import only, no official Vivo warranty
  • CN OriginOS lacks Google and Bangla
  • Portrait mode adds aggressive skin smoothing

Buy it if Buy it if you value high performance, fast charging and a solid camera and can accept a shop warranty and possible software quirks.

Skip it if Skip it if you need an official brand warranty, Google services preinstalled or a fully balanced ultrawide lens.

Why we say that

The 6.31‑inch LTPO AMOLED screen reaches a dazzling 4500 nits peak and stays smooth at 120Hz. Its Dimensity 9500 processor with 12GB RAM gives top‑tier performance and stays cooler than most Snapdragon rivals. The 6040 mAh battery lives through heavy daily use and refuels quickly with 90W wired charging and 40W wireless.

The 200MP main shooter with OIS, 3× telephoto and ultrawide covers most scenes, though the ultrawide sensor is a step down and colors can feel oversaturated. Portrait mode adds noticeable skin smoothing that not everyone likes. The phone arrives as a gray‑import, so you get a shop warranty instead of Vivo’s brand warranty and, in the CN version, no Google apps or Bangla support.

Overall it feels premium and powerful but requires careful checking of the software build before buying.

Before you buy

Some sellers here list the China-market version (OriginOS (CN)), others the global one. Check which build you are buying

THE classic trap: global Vivo runs Funtouch (plain, light-ish), CN runs OriginOS (prettier, heavier, deeply integrated Chinese services, NO Google preinstalled, no Bangla, OTA updates only from CN servers). OriginOS units in BD = gray CN imports; gorgeous UI but daily friction for Google-centric users.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 6040 mAh cell is rated as outstanding and owners report solid real‑world endurance even with heavy use. Combined with 90W charging it can easily last a full day of mixed tasks.

Is the Vivo X300 suitable for gaming?

Yes, the Dimensity 9500 chipset and 12GB RAM earn a 9.5 gaming rating. It stays cooler than many Snapdragon phones, so long sessions remain smooth without throttling.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 200MP main sensor with OIS handles low light well and delivers deep shadows, but some owners note that colors may become oversaturated and the ultrawide lens is weaker than the main unit.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

Only gray‑import stock is available, so you get a shop warranty rather than Vivo’s official coverage. The CN OriginOS version receives OTA updates from Chinese servers only, and upper models get about three Android upgrades.
