POCO X7 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you want flagship‑class speed at a sub‑50 k taka price, the POCO X7 Pro delivers that but you must accept a gray‑import warranty and some compromises.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need high performance gaming and a bright display and can live with a shop‑only warranty.
Consider alternatives if

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 120 Hz AMOLED display with 3200 nit peak
  • Dimensity 8400 Ultra gives smooth gaming
  • 6000 mAh battery with 120 W fast charge
  • 12 GB RAM and up to 512 GB storage

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Battery health drops quickly after ~150 cycles
  • Selfie camera produces soft unfocused images
  • Low call volume and occasional 3G fallback

Why we say that

The Dimensity 8400 Ultra with 12 GB RAM runs games smoothly and the 6.67" AMOLED screen stays bright even in sunlight. Battery life is adequate for a day of normal use, yet health drops to 98 % after about 150 cycles and charging stops at 80 % to protect it. The main camera is usable, but the ultrawide is soft and the selfie lens struggles in low light.

Call volume is low and the phone can fall back to 3G in a 5G area. The build feels solid plastic with IP68 rating, but the software is heavy on ads and the warranty is limited to the shop’s promise.

Will the battery last a full day of heavy use?

The 6000 mAh battery usually lasts a full day of moderate use, but heavy use shortens it and health can drop to 98 % after roughly 150 cycles; fast charging reaches about 90 W but the included charger is not EU type and charging stops at 80 %.

How does the phone handle gaming and performance?

The Dimensity 8400 Ultra chipset together with 12 GB RAM gives flagship‑class speed, so most games run at high frame rates without stutter; occasional heating may cause mild throttling but it remains playable.

Is the camera good in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.5 aperture and OIS captures decent low‑light shots, though details are not as sharp as premium flagships; the ultrawide lens is softer and the 20 MP selfie camera struggles in dim environments.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

All units are gray‑import, so there is no official brand warranty in Bangladesh; the shop offers its own limited warranty and any repairs are paid. Software will receive about two to three Android version updates, but the HyperOS skin is heavy on ads and bloat.
