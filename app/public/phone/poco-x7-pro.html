Is the POCO X7 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Strong performance with Dimensity 8400 Ultra
  • Bright 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Solid IP68 build quality
  • Large 6000 mAh battery

What is not

  • Battery drains quickly
  • Poor selfie and ultrawide cameras
  • High amount of bloatware and ads

Buy it if You want a high performance device for gaming and value a bright screen.

Skip it if You need a reliable camera system or a long lasting battery.

Why we say that

Owners report that the battery drains quickly and health can drop to 98 percent after 150 cycles. You get a very bright AMOLED screen and strong processing power from the Dimensity 8400 Ultra. However, the software comes with high levels of bloatware and ads.

Some users notice occasional overheating, though they describe it as normal. The build quality is solid with an IP68 design. You should be aware that the selfie and ultrawide cameras are poor.

Since only gray imports are available, you will rely on shop warranties rather than brand support. You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before paying.
