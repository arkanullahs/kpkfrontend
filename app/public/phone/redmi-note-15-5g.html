Redmi Note 15 5G Is it worth buying?

If you want a large bright screen and a 108 MP camera without paying flagship prices, the Redmi Note 15 5G can deliver, but expect ads and limited warranty on cheap imports.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value a bright large screen and high resolution photos and can accept ads and a non official warranty on the cheapest listings.
Consider alternatives if
You need a phone with a guaranteed manufacturer warranty, stable 4K video, or a headphone jack.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 6.77 inch 120 Hz AMOLED display
  • 108 MP main camera with OIS
  • 5520 mAh battery with 45 W charging
  • Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 performance

Reported trade-offs4

  • System shows full screen pop up ads
  • Gray import units lack official warranty
  • 4K video is shaky without stabilization
  • No 3.5 mm headphone jack

Why we say that

The 6.77 inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can reach a peak of 3200 nits, making outdoor use easy. The Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 chipset handles everyday tasks and moderate gaming, though occasional lag is reported. Battery life is reliable for a full day and the 45 W charger refills it quickly, but it is not as fast as premium fast charging.

The main camera is strong thanks to OIS, while the ultrawide lens and selfie sensor feel average and 4K video is shaky without stabilization. HyperOS 2 runs Android 15 and promises up to four major upgrades, yet full screen pop up ads are common. The phone lacks a headphone jack and the cheapest listings are gray imports without official warranty.

Will the Redmi Note 15 5G receive Android updates?

The phone ships with Android 15 and HyperOS 2 and promises up to four major Android upgrades. Budget devices still receive many ads, and the timing of updates may vary compared to flagship models.

How is the battery life and charging speed?

The 5520 mAh battery gives reliable all day endurance and the included 45 W charger refills it in about an hour. The charging speed is decent but not as fast as flagship fast charging solutions.

Is the camera good for low light and video?

The 108 MP main sensor with OIS captures detailed photos even in low light, but the ultrawide lens is weak and selfies are average. 4K video lacks stabilization, so footage can appear shaky.
