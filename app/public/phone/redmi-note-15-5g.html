Buying verdict

Redmi Note 15 5G Is it worth buying?

The Redmi Note 15 5G offers a stunning curved AMOLED display that stands out in its budget segment, but living with the device reveals significant software frustrations.

Choose it if
You prioritize a high-quality display and a slim design for daily tasks and media consumption.
Consider alternatives if
You dislike intrusive system ads or require a reliable ultrawide camera and a headphone jack.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Stunning 3200 nits peak brightness AMOLED display
  • Reliable all-day battery endurance
  • Strong 108MP main camera sensor
  • Impressively slim and light build

Reported trade-offs

  • System-embedded full-screen pop-up ads
  • Shaky 4K video without stabilization
  • No 3.5mm headphone jack
  • Weak ultrawide camera performance

Why we say that

Owners frequently report system-embedded full-screen pop-up ads that disrupt the user experience. While the 108MP main camera captures strong photos, the ultrawide lens is weak and 4K video footage remains shaky due to a lack of stabilization. The phone is impressively slim and light, yet the curved glass design increases potential repair costs.

Performance is generally stable for gaming, though some users experience occasional lag. Battery life provides reliable all-day endurance, but the 45W charging speed is only decent rather than fast. You must be careful when choosing between official and gray-market units, as some imported versions lack Google services and Bangla language support.

The removal of the 3.5mm headphone jack is another point of contention for many buyers.

Redmi Note 15 5G costs ৳23,499 to ৳36,499 in Bangladesh on unofficial listings, in stock at 6 shops we track as of 11 Sep 2026. The official price starts at ৳35,499.

Does the Redmi Note 15 5G provide reliable battery life for a full day?

Owners report that the 5520 mAh battery offers reliable all-day endurance. While the 45W charging is decent, it is not considered fast by modern standards.

Is the Redmi Note 15 5G suitable for gaming?

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 chipset provides stable thermals and consistent frames during gaming sessions. However, some users have reported instances of lag during use.

What is the difference between official and gray-market units in Bangladesh?

Official units come with brand warranty support. Gray-market units have no manufacturer cover, and service centers will only accept them for paid repairs. Some gray-market units are built for China and may lack Google services and Bangla language support.

How long will the Redmi Note 15 5G receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 15 and HyperOS 2. It is promised to receive up to 4 major Android upgrades, though the amount of bloatware and ads remains high on this budget line.
