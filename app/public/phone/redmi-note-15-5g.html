Is the Redmi Note 15 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.77‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED
  • 108 MP main camera
  • 45 W fast charging
  • 5G connectivity

What is not

  • Full‑screen system ads
  • No 3.5 mm headphone jack
  • 4K video shaky without stabilization
  • Gray‑import units lack official warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large bright screen and high‑resolution photos without spending a premium.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a guaranteed manufacturer warranty or cannot stand intrusive ads.

Why we say that

The 5.5‑inch display reaches a peak of 3,200 nits, making outdoor use easy. Battery life is solid with a 5,520 mAh cell and 45 W charging, though the charge speed is not class‑leading. Performance is decent on the Snapdragon 6 Gen 3, but some users notice occasional lag in heavy games.

The camera captures detailed photos in good light, yet the ultrawide is weak and 4K video lacks stabilization. HyperOS 2 runs smoothly but is filled with full‑screen ads that many find intrusive. Build quality feels premium, but the curved glass can raise repair costs.

Is the battery enough for a full day of use?
