Buying verdict

Motorola G100 Is it worth buying?

The Motorola G100’s massive 7000 mAh battery is its biggest advantage, easily covering two days of typical use.

Choose it if
You need long battery life, solid build and a clean Android experience without needing top‑tier gaming or camera performance.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on official brand warranty, need strong low‑light photography, or expect frequent major OS updates.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • Huge 7000 mAh battery lasts two days
  • 120 Hz bright display with 1050 nits peak
  • IP68/69 water and dust protection

Reported trade-offs

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Camera colors can be oversaturated
  • Gaming performance is modest
  • Software updates may be limited

Why we say that

The 6.72‑inch panel refreshes at 120 Hz and can reach 1050 nits, but owners notice colors sometimes look oversaturated. Build quality feels solid with IP68/69 rating and military‑grade 810H protection, giving confidence against drops and water. The Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 chipset with up to 12 GB RAM handles daily apps smoothly, yet gaming performance is only modest; Genshin Impact runs at low settings and 60 fps.

The 50 MP Sony LYT600 main sensor with OIS records 4K60 video, but low‑light shots can be noisy and color‑biased. Android 15 with Hello UI feels clean and light on bloat, though past updates have been limited to one or two versions. The phone includes a headphone jack and 5G, but it arrives as a gray‑import with only the shop’s warranty, so any repair will cost extra.
