Is the Honor X7a worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts 2‑3 days
  • 22.5 W charger is adequate for price
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • Large 6.75‑inch bright display

What is not

  • Very slow performance and gaming
  • Low‑resolution 720p screen lags
  • Poor low‑light camera, no night mode
  • Touch delay and UI lag

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life and basic phone functions at a low price.

Skip it if Skip it if you want smooth gaming, fast performance, or good night‑mode photos.

Why we say that

The Honor X7a offers a massive 6000 mAh battery that most owners say lasts two to three days on normal use, and the 22.5 W charger refills it at a reasonable pace for the price. The 6.75‑inch TFT LCD is bright enough for outdoor reading, and the 50 MP main camera can produce decent daylight shots. However, the Helio G37 chipset with 6 GB RAM struggles with multitasking, and gaming feels very slow.

The 720p display shows occasional lag, and users notice touch delay and UI stutter. Low‑light photography is poor and there is no night‑mode. The build feels plastic and attracts fingerprints.

At ৳19,499 you get an official warranty, but the phone is already three years old, so software updates are likely near the end of their cycle.

How long does the battery last on the Honor X7a?

The Honor X7a has a 6000 mAh battery that owners report can last two to three days on a single charge under normal use. This endurance is one of its strongest points.

Can the Honor X7a handle games and multitasking?

The phone uses a Helio G37 chipset with 6 GB RAM, but owners find it very slow for multitasking and basic games. Gaming performance is rated low and the UI often lags.

How good is the camera in low light on the Honor X7a?

The 50 MP main sensor captures decent photos in daylight, but in low‑light conditions the images are poor and there is no night‑mode feature. Users report unsatisfactory results after dark.
