Is the Vivo S19 Pro 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Very bright 4500 nits AMOLED display
  • Powerful Dimensity 9200+ chipset
  • Large 5500 mAh battery
  • High resolution 50MP selfie camera

What is not

  • No official brand warranty
  • Medium amount of bloatware and ads
  • Only available as gray import

Buy it if You want a high brightness screen and a powerful processor for ৳55,990.

Skip it if You require an official manufacturer warranty and a clean software experience.

Why we say that

You get a powerful Dimensity 9200+ chipset and a very bright 4500 nits AMOLED screen. The 5500 mAh battery and 80W charging provide strong endurance. However, you will not find a manufacturer warranty.

You must rely on shop warranties instead. The software experience is mixed. You will encounter a medium amount of bloatware and ads.

Depending on the model tier, you can expect 2 or 3 OS updates. The 50MP cameras on both the front and back are strong points for photography.

Does this phone have an official brand warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only found as gray import stock. You will get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

How long will the software be updated?

The update policy varies by model. Upper models receive 3 OS updates, while budget models receive 2 OS updates. The phone runs on Android 14 with either OriginOS 4 or Funtouch OS.

Is the battery life and charging speed good?

The phone features a 5500 mAh battery. It supports 80W charging, which allows for faster power refills compared to standard chargers.

What are the camera specifications for photos?

The main camera is 50MP with a 1/1.56 inch sensor, OIS, and a 2.0x telephoto lens. It also has an ultrawide lens and a 50MP selfie camera.
