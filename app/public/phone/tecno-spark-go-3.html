Buying verdict

Tecno Spark Go 3 Is it worth buying?

If you need a cheap phone with a big screen and decent battery, the Tecno Spark Go 3 will get the job done, but expect many compromises.

Choose it if
You only need basic apps, media playback and long battery on a tight budget.
Consider alternatives if
You want fast charging, good gaming or reliable low‑light photos.

What works well

  • 120 Hz smooth display
  • 5000 mAh battery lasting a day
  • headphone jack and FM radio
  • official warranty at ৳16,499

Reported trade-offs

  • Charging takes over three hours
  • Fingerprint sensor often fails
  • Low‑light photos are very poor
  • Games lag and open slowly

Why we say that

The 6.74" IPS LCD offers a smooth 120 Hz experience, yet sunlight visibility is weak and colors shift. Battery life is a highlight, lasting over a day with basic use, but charging is painfully slow, taking more than three hours with the supplied 10 W charger. The Unisoc T7250 chipset handles everyday apps, but games lag and apps open slowly.

Camera quality is acceptable in daylight but fails in low light, and the fingerprint sensor is unreliable. Heavy HiOS bloatware and ads add to the frustration. Official units come with a brand warranty at ৳16,499, while gray imports lack manufacturer support.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

How long does the battery last on the Tecno Spark Go 3?

Owners report that the 5,000 mAh battery easily lasts more than a day with basic tasks such as messaging, browsing and media playback. Heavy use will shorten that time, but overall endurance is a strong point.

Can I play games like BGMI on the Tecno Spark Go 3?

Performance is limited by the Unisoc T7250 chipset; games like BGMI stutter and show noticeable lag. The phone is suited only for casual or turn‑based games, not for graphics‑intensive titles.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 13 MP main sensor captures acceptable photos in daylight, but in low‑light conditions the images become grainy and resemble CCTV quality, making it unsuitable for night photography or poorly lit environments.

Does the phone come with an official warranty and will it get Android updates?

Only the official units sold at ৳16,499 include the brand warranty; gray‑import listings do not and repairs are paid. The phone receives about one to two Android version updates, and HiOS remains heavy with ads.
