Is the Tecno Spark Go 3 worth buying?

What is good

  • Smooth 120Hz display refresh rate
  • Battery endurance lasts over a day
  • Includes headphone jack and IR blaster
  • Acceptable daylight camera performance

What is not

  • Painfully slow charging over 3 hours
  • Heavy bloatware and ad-heavy software
  • Unreliable and slow fingerprint sensor
  • Poor low light camera quality

Buy it if You only need a phone for very basic daily tasks and prioritize a high refresh rate display over gaming or camera quality.

Skip it if You want a reliable device for gaming, photography, or a clean software experience without constant ads.

Why we say that

While the 120Hz display offers smooth scrolling, users frequently complain about poor sunlight visibility and noticeable color shifts. The Unisoc T7250 chipset is strictly for basic tasks, as owners report lag in games like BGMI and slow app opening times. Living with this phone means dealing with a heavy, ad-filled software experience and a fingerprint sensor that often fails to detect your touch.

Charging is a major pain point, with the included charger taking over 3 hours to fill the 5000 mAh battery. Many owners feel the device is a downgrade, citing the dated teardrop notch and the lack of dual speakers as reasons to consider alternatives like older flagships or the Samsung A06 instead.

What is the Tecno Spark Go 3 price in Bangladesh?

Tecno Spark Go 3 costs ৳16,999 in Bangladesh on official listings, in stock at 3 shops we track as of 9 Sep 2026. The unofficial price starts at ৳16,999.

Is the battery life good enough for a full day?

Yes, owners report that the 5000 mAh battery provides good endurance. It typically lasts over a day for basic use, though you should be prepared for the extremely slow charging speed that takes over 3 hours.

Can I play games like BGMI on this phone?

The phone is not recommended for gaming. Owners report that it lags in BGMI and is generally only suitable for basic tasks. With a gaming rating of 2.5, it is not built for performance-heavy applications.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The camera is not suitable for low light conditions. Owners describe the quality as unusable and comparable to CCTV footage when the lighting is poor. It is only considered acceptable for daylight photography.

Why should I choose the official unit over a gray import?

The official unit is the only version that comes with a brand warranty. Gray imports have no manufacturer cover, and any service centre that accepts them will treat the device as a paid repair.
