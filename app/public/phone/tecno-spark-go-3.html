Tecno Spark Go 3 Is it worth buying?

Expect a big screen and decent battery but be ready for slow charging and weak performance.

What works well4

  • Smooth 120Hz display for scrolling
  • Large 5000 mAh battery lasts a day
  • Headphone jack, FM radio and IR blaster
  • Cheapest official‑warranty price at ৳16,330

Reported trade-offs4

  • Charging slower than 10W, over 3 hours
  • Fingerprint sensor often misses prints
  • Low‑light camera produces poor images
  • Gaming and heavy apps lag noticeably

Why we say that

The Spark Go 3 gives you a 6.74 inch 120Hz display that feels smooth for scrolling, and a 5000 mAh cell that can last over a day with basic use. The phone runs Android 15 with Tecno's HiOS layer, which adds a lot of pre‑installed apps and ads that cannot be removed. Performance is limited to everyday tasks; opening apps feels sluggish and games like BGMI lag noticeably.

The 13MP rear camera captures acceptable daylight shots but turns into a grainy CCTV‑style image in low light. Charging uses a 10W charger and takes more than three hours to fill, which owners find painfully slow. Build quality feels dated with a teardrop notch and no dual speakers.

Read full reasoning

The fingerprint sensor is unreliable and sometimes fails to read prints. Overall it is a budget‑friendly device for simple use, but it shows its limits in speed, photography and gaming.
