Tecno Spark Go 3 Is it worth buying?

Expect a large, 120 Hz screen and a 5000 mAh battery, but be ready for slow charging and a lot of pre‑installed ads.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a big screen and long battery on a tight budget and can tolerate ads and slower charging.
Consider alternatives if
You care about low‑light photos, smooth gaming, or a premium design without bloatware.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 120 Hz refresh rate
  • 5000 mAh battery
  • Headphone jack
  • FM radio and IR blaster

Reported trade-offs4

  • Low screen brightness
  • Slow 15 W charging
  • Heavy bloatware with ads
  • Poor low‑light camera

Why we say that

The 6.74‑inch IPS LCD looks smooth thanks to the high refresh rate, yet its brightness is low and viewing angles are limited. Daily tasks run without trouble on the Unisoc T7250 and 4 GB RAM, but gaming often drops frames and the device feels laggy in demanding apps. The 13 MP main camera captures decent daylight shots, while low‑light photos are noisy and portraits become over‑exposed.

Owners frequently mention a red line appearing on the display and a charging time of more than three hours with the supplied 10‑15 W charger. The phone includes a headphone jack, FM radio and IR blaster, but the design has reverted to a teardrop notch and lacks stereo speakers. Software runs Android 15 with HiOS 15, but heavy bloatware and ads reduce the overall experience.
