What works well4

  • 6.78 inch AMOLED with 120Hz refresh
  • 1300 nit peak brightness for outdoor use
  • 108MP main camera with f/1.8 lens
  • 5000 mAh battery with 33W fast charging

Reported trade-offs4

  • Slow performance in heavy apps
  • Heating reaches 37°C under load
  • Camera app cannot set SD default
  • XOS shows many ads

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch AMOLED panel runs at 120Hz and can reach 1300 nit peak brightness, making outdoor viewing easy. The 108MP main sensor with an f/1.8 lens captures detailed photos, though the software lacks tuning and low‑light results are modest. A 5000 mAh battery offers average endurance and refills with 33W fast charging, but efficiency is not exceptional.

Under load the Dimensity 7020 chipset shows an AnTuTu score around 500k, leading to slow performance in demanding apps and noticeable heating around 37°C. The XOS 14 skin on Android 14 brings many preinstalled apps and frequent ads, and only a shop warranty is available, so verify the IMEI and BTRC status before purchase. Overall the phone feels solid but falls short for power users.
