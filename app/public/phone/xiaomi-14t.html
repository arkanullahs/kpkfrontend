Buying verdict

Xiaomi 14T Is it worth buying?

The Xiaomi 14T gives you a flagship class screen and gaming speed but its battery and software quirks make daily use uneven.

Choose it if
You want a top tier display and gaming power and can accept a gray import warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need long battery life or official brand support.

What works well

  • 144Hz AMOLED display with 4000 nit peak brightness
  • Dimensity 8300 Ultra provides strong gaming performance
  • Leica tuned 50MP camera delivers good night shots
  • 67W fast charging refills 5000mAh battery quickly

Reported trade-offs

  • Battery drains quickly and overall life is short
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Software includes bloatware and frequent ad popups
  • Plastic frame feels less durable than metal

Why we say that

The 6.67 inch AMOLED panel runs at 144Hz and can reach 4000 nit peak brightness, delivering vivid colors and smooth motion. The Dimensity 8300 Ultra chipset provides strong performance in heavy games and everyday apps, and the Leica tuned 50MP camera with OIS captures reliable night shots. Battery life is a weak point; the 5000 mAh cell drains quickly and owners rate it only 6 out of 10, with no sign of a fix.

HyperOS adds many features but also brings noticeable bloatware, ads and occasional update instability. The phone arrives as a gray import, so there is no official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh, only the shop's limited cover. At ৳61,990 it is pricier than the newer Xiaomi 15T, which sells for less and may be a better overall value.

How long does the battery last in real use?

Is the Xiaomi 14T good for gaming?

The Dimensity 8300 Ultra chipset handles heavy games smoothly, and the 144Hz AMOLED panel shows fast motion without lag. Gaming scores are around 8, though the battery may run out faster during long sessions.

The 50MP main sensor with a 1/1.56 inch size, f/1.7 aperture, OIS and Leica colour profiles delivers reliable night shots and good colour accuracy, earning an 8.6 rating from owners.
