What works well

  • Industry-leading video quality
  • Highly capable A15 Bionic performance
  • Clean and intuitive iOS software
  • Reliable main camera stills

Reported trade-offs

  • Unacceptable 60Hz display refresh rate
  • Mediocre battery life and health degradation
  • Frame paint is prone to chipping
  • Lacks telephoto camera versatility

Why we say that

Living with this device reveals a mix of high performance and frustrating hardware limitations. While the A15 Bionic chipset remains capable for daily tasks and gaming, owners frequently report that the 60Hz display refresh rate makes swiping and scrolling feel choppy. Battery life is a significant concern, with users noting mediocre endurance and rapid health degradation.

Build quality is premium, but the frame paint is prone to chipping if used without a cover. You get industry-leading video quality and a reliable camera system, but the lack of a telephoto lens limits versatility. Given that this model is 4 years old, you should expect short or no remaining software update life.
