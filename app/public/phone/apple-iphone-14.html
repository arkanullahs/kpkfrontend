Apple iPhone 14 Is it worth buying?

The iPhone 14 is a device that feels premium in the hand but reveals significant frustrations once you start using it daily.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
Buy this phone only if you prioritize video recording quality and iOS stability over display smoothness and battery longevity.
Consider alternatives if
You want a modern display refresh rate or a device that will receive software updates for many more years.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Industry-leading video quality
  • Highly capable A15 Bionic performance
  • Clean and intuitive iOS software
  • Premium build materials

Reported trade-offs4

  • Mediocre battery endurance and health
  • Unacceptable 60Hz display refresh rate
  • Frame paint is prone to chipping
  • Lacks telephoto camera versatility

Why we say that

While the A15 Bionic chipset remains highly capable for gaming and daily tasks, the user experience is hampered by a 60Hz display that makes swiping and scrolling feel choppy. Owners frequently report that the battery health degrades quickly, often dropping from 94% to 88% in just a few weeks, and the endurance struggles under heavy usage. You must also be careful with the physical build, as the frame paint is prone to chipping if you use the phone without a cover.

While the video quality is industry-leading and the software is intuitive, the phone is now four years old. You should consider that the newer iPhone 15 is available for about ৳73,999, which is usually the better buy for your money. If you choose the cheaper ৳68,990 gray-import unit, remember that you get no manufacturer warranty and any service center visit will be a paid repair.

Does the iPhone 14 have a good battery life for daily use?

Owners report mediocre battery endurance and note that the device struggles with heavy usage. Many users have experienced rapid battery health degradation, with some reporting a drop from 94% to 88% in just a few weeks of ownership.

Is the iPhone 14 still a good choice for mobile gaming?

The A15 Bionic chipset is still highly capable for gaming and daily tasks. Owners have rated the gaming performance at 7.5 out of 10, suggesting it remains a reliable option for those who play games on their phone.

What is the difference between the official and gray-import units?

The official unit comes with a brand warranty, while gray-import units have no manufacturer cover. If you buy a gray-import unit, any service center that accepts the phone will treat it as a paid repair rather than a warranty claim.

How long will the iPhone 14 receive software updates?

The phone is already four years old, so you should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point. While iOS is generally known for long-term support, the age of this specific model limits its future update potential.
