What works well

  • Fast A15 Bionic chipset
  • 120Hz Super Retina XDR OLED display
  • No bloatware or ads
  • Versatile camera with 3.0x telephoto

Reported trade-offs

  • Widespread green or white screen reports
  • Short remaining software update life
  • No official brand warranty available

Why we say that

While the A15 Bionic chip and 120Hz Super Retina XDR OLED display provide a high quality experience, there is a serious hardware risk. Many users report the AMOLED screen suddenly turning green or white. This green screen or white screen issue is widespread.

You also need to be aware that this phone is 5 years old. This means the remaining software update life is likely short or non existent. Since only gray import stock is available, you will get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty.
