Is the Samsung Galaxy M17e worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000mAh battery lasts all day
  • 50MP camera produces good photos
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • 25W fast charging

What is not

  • 720p screen feels low
  • Dimensity 6300 is outdated
  • Some 5G bands missing
  • Phone gets hot in summer

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize long battery life, basic photography and need a headphone jack without caring about high‑resolution screens or top‑tier gaming.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect a crisp display, smooth gaming performance or the peace of an official Samsung warranty.

Why we say that

It runs Android 16 with One UI 8 and promises up to six major Android upgrades, so the software will stay current for several years. The 50MP main sensor gives decent snapshots, though you will notice shutter lag and slower processing in low light. The 6.7‑inch 720p PLS LCD panel feels cheap and the Dimensity 6300 chipset struggles with modern games and can heat up in summer heat.

Network performance is mixed because some 5G bands are missing. The phone includes a headphone jack, FM radio and 25W fast charging, which are rare in this price range. Overall it is a solid battery‑first device, but you should not expect a sharp display or high‑end performance.
