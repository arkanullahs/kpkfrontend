Walton NEXG N27 Is it worth buying?

If you want a big screen and big battery for under ৳12,000, the Walton NEXG N27 will give you those basics but expect a sluggish software experience.

Why we say that

The 6.7‑inch IPS panel runs at a smooth 120 Hz, which makes scrolling and video look pleasant. The 5000 mAh cell can get you through a day of moderate use, though heavy apps will drain it faster. The 50 MP main sensor captures detailed photos in good light, but the Go edition skin is poorly optimized and adds noticeable lag.

Ads and bloat appear in the UI, and the 18 W charger feels slow for a large battery. Network signal drops in rural or riverside areas, a common complaint among owners. The phone ships with an official warranty and a headphone jack, but software updates are rare.

Overall it feels like a budget device with a few high‑end specs that are held back by software and connectivity issues.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 5000 mAh battery is rated as a 7 out of 10 by owners and can last a full day with moderate browsing, messaging and video. Heavy gaming or bright screen use will reduce that time noticeably.

Will this phone run games smoothly?

Performance scores sit at 5 out of 10 and the Unisoc T615 chip struggles with demanding titles. Light games run okay, but you will notice lag and stutter in newer, graphics‑intensive games.

Are the photos good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor captures sharp images in bright light, but the Go edition software lacks advanced processing. In low‑light situations the pictures become noisy and lack detail, so results are mixed.

Does the phone have an official warranty and will it receive updates?

Yes, the device is sold with the brand’s official warranty and can be serviced through Walton’s network. However, software updates are rare and the Android 15 Go skin receives little optimisation over time.
