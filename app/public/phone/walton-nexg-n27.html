Buying verdict

Walton NEXG N27 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that lasts a full day on a single charge, the Walton NEXG N27 delivers that.

Choose it if
You want a large battery, decent daylight camera and official warranty at a low price.
Consider alternatives if
You need strong gaming performance or good low‑light photography.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • 5000 mAh battery lasts over a day
  • 120 Hz IPS display feels smooth
  • 50 MP main camera shows daylight detail
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included

Reported trade-offs

  • Low‑light photos are noisy and lack HDR
  • Heavy games lag and are unusable
  • Display feels like 90 Hz and low resolution
  • Thick lower chin looks outdated

Why we say that

The 5000 mAh battery scores 8.5 out of 10 and easily gets you through typical daily use, while the 18 W charger refills it at a decent pace. The 6.7" IPS InCell panel runs at 120 Hz but many owners feel it behaves more like 90 Hz and the HD+ resolution struggles under direct sunlight. The 50 MP main sensor provides good detail in daylight, yet HDR, white balance and low‑light performance are weak, making night shots noisy.

The Unisoc T615 handles basic apps fine, but heavy games suffer noticeable lag and become unplayable. Android 15 Go is lightweight but updates are rare and the system carries a moderate amount of ads. Build quality feels premium with a frosted finish, but the thick lower chin looks outdated.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery is rated 8.5 out of 10 by owners and easily lasts over a day with typical use, and the 18 W charger refills it at a decent speed, though not ultra‑fast.

Can I play modern games on this phone?

For basic apps and browsing the Unisoc T615 handles tasks fine, but owners report heavy games run with significant lag and are essentially unusable, so the phone is not suited for serious mobile gaming.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor captures good detail in daylight, yet reviewers note HDR and white balance are weak and low‑light images become noisy, making night photography disappointing.

Will the phone receive regular Android updates and is the warranty reliable?

The phone comes with the brand’s official Bangladesh warranty and can be serviced through Walton’s network, but Android 15 Go receives rare updates and the software includes a moderate amount of ads, so long‑term support is limited.
