Is the Google Pixel 9 worth buying?

What is good

  • Industry-leading still photography
  • Extremely bright 2700 nits OLED display
  • Clean, bloat-free Android software
  • Seven years of major Android updates

What is not

  • Recurring network signal drops
  • Thermal throttling during heavy gaming
  • Slow 27W charging speeds
  • Lack of telephoto lens

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a clean software experience, long-term update support, and high-quality still photography in a compact form factor.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a heavy gamer, require reliable network connectivity for calls, or need fast charging capabilities.

Why we say that

While the display is excellent and the software is clean, owners frequently report frustrating issues with network connectivity and call drops. The Tensor G4 chipset struggles under heavy gaming loads, leading to thermal throttling that impacts performance and battery health. Charging speeds are notably slow at 27W, which feels outdated for a phone in this price range.

While the still photography is industry-leading, the front camera lacks detail compared to cheaper alternatives. You are paying for a specific software experience and a high-quality build, but you must be prepared for potential signal instability and heat management issues during intensive tasks. If you prioritize a bloat-free interface and long-term support over raw gaming power or fast charging, this device may suit your needs.

What is the Google Pixel 9 price in Bangladesh?

Google Pixel 9 costs ৳68,499 to ৳74,499 in Bangladesh on unofficial listings, in stock at 6 shops we track as of 4 Sep 2026. The official price starts at ৳84,999.

Does the Google Pixel 9 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

Official units come with a brand warranty, but these are priced at ৳84,999. Cheaper units starting at ৳68,499 are gray imports. These imports have no manufacturer cover, and service centers will only accept them for paid repairs.

How long will the Google Pixel 9 receive software updates?

The Google Pixel 9 provides industry-leading software support. It comes with Android 14 and is eligible for up to 7 major Android upgrades, ensuring the device remains current through Android 16 and beyond.

Is the Google Pixel 9 suitable for heavy gaming?

The Tensor G4 chipset struggles with heavy gaming and sustained thermal loads. Owners report that the phone experiences thermal throttling under load, which results in subpar performance and increased heat that can degrade the battery faster.

What are the common complaints regarding the Google Pixel 9 battery and charging?

Owners report that the battery endurance is average. The charging speed is a major point of contention because it maxes out at 27W, which many users find slow and outdated for the price of the device.
