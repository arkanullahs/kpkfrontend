Buying verdict

Google Pixel 9 Is it worth buying?

If you want a phone with a brilliant OLED screen and top‑tier still photography, the Pixel 9 delivers that but you must accept slow charging and occasional network drops.

Choose it if
You value a bright screen and excellent still photos and don’t need fast charging or heavy gaming performance.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable network calls, fast charging, or strong gaming performance.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • 120 Hz OLED display
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • 7‑year Android update promise
  • Clean stock Android experience

Reported trade-offs

  • 27 W charging is slow
  • Frequent signal drops
  • Heavy gaming throttles quickly
  • Front camera lacks detail

Why we say that

The 6.3‑inch 120 Hz OLED is extremely bright and responsive, making outdoor use easy. The 50 MP main sensor with OIS produces industry‑leading photos, though it lacks a telephoto lens and advanced video controls. Battery life is average and the 27 W charger feels slow for a premium device.

Gaming performance suffers from thermal throttling, and owners report signal drops and call interruptions. The front 10 MP camera shows less detail than cheaper rivals. Software is clean and will receive seven years of updates, but only the official ৳84,999 unit carries a manufacturer warranty; gray‑import models have no cover and repairs are paid.

How long will the Pixel 9 receive Android updates?

Google promises seven years of Android updates for the Pixel 9, starting with Android 14 and upgradeable to Android 16, plus up to seven major version upgrades. This is longer than most Android phones on the market.

Is there a manufacturer warranty for the Pixel 9 in Bangladesh?

Only the official unit sold for ৳84,999 carries the Google brand warranty. The cheaper gray‑import models at ৳68,499 have no manufacturer cover and any repair at a service centre is paid.

What is the battery life and charging speed like?

Battery endurance is rated average with a 7.0 owner score and a 4,700 mAh cell. Charging tops out at 27 W, which owners describe as slow and outdated for the price.

Does the Pixel 9 have network or call‑drop problems?

Several owners report modem‑related signal drops and call interruptions, especially during longer conversations. Network stability can be inconsistent, so if reliable connectivity is critical you may want to consider another model.
