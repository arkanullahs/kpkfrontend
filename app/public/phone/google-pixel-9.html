Is the Google Pixel 9 worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz OLED display with 2700 nits peak
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • Clean Android 14 and seven year updates
  • Tensor G4 chipset with 12 GB RAM

What is not

  • Thermal throttling limits sustained performance
  • Battery drains quickly for some users
  • Heavy and brick like build
  • 128 GB base storage feels small

Buy it if Buy it if you value camera quality and long term software support more than raw gaming power.

Skip it if Skip it if you need strong gaming performance or a larger starter storage.

Why we say that

The stock Android experience stays clean and promises up to seven major updates, so the software feels future proof. Daily tasks run smoothly on the Tensor G4 and 12 GB RAM, but heavy games or long video sessions quickly heat the device and trigger throttling that lowers performance. Battery life is mixed; some users get a full day while others notice rapid drain, especially when network issues appear.

The phone feels solid but many describe it as heavy and brick like. At an unofficial price of 68,499 taka for the 128 GB model it is still pricey, and the base storage may feel limited for heavy users.

What is the Google Pixel 9 price in Bangladesh?

Google Pixel 9 costs ৳68,499 to ৳73,990 in Bangladesh, in stock at 8 shops we track as of 2026-08-29.

How long will the Pixel 9 receive Android updates?

Google promises up to seven major Android upgrades for the Pixel 9, starting with Android 14 and moving to Android 16. This means the phone will stay officially supported for several years, far longer than most Android devices.

Is the battery life good for everyday use?

Battery life varies; many owners get a full day of normal use, but some report rapid drain especially when network problems occur. The 4700 mAh cell with 27 W charging is competitive, yet not guaranteed for heavy usage.

How does the camera perform in low light or nature photography?

The 50 MP sensor with optical image stabilization delivers DSLR like nature shots and good low light detail. Users praise the photo quality and the 10 MP selfie lens also captures clear self portraits.

Is there an official warranty available in Bangladesh?

The lowest official warranty price for the Pixel 9 in Bangladesh is 84,999 taka and includes Google’s standard coverage. Unofficial listings start at 68,499 taka but do not provide the official warranty protection.
