Redmi 14C Is it worth buying?

The Redmi 14C gives you a big screen and a 50MP camera at a very low price, but expect frequent lag, overheating and fast battery drain.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a large display and decent photos while accepting occasional slowdowns.
Consider alternatives if
You want smooth gaming, reliable battery life, or a clean software experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 6.88 inch IPS LCD with 120Hz refresh
  • 50MP main camera with f/1.8 aperture
  • Headphone jack and FM radio
  • 5160 mAh battery capacity

Reported trade-offs3

  • Severe overheating during use
  • Battery drains quickly
  • Laggy performance after a few months

Why we say that

Its 6.88 inch IPS LCD runs at 120Hz and reaches 600 nits, which looks smooth in normal lighting. The Helio G81 Ultra chipset with up to 8GB RAM handles everyday tasks, yet owners notice the phone becomes sluggish after a few months and heats up badly during gaming or heavy use. The 5160 mAh battery sounds generous, but real‑world tests show it empties quickly and may overheat.

Camera performance is praised for still photos, but low‑light results are only average. Software runs Android 14 with HyperOS, but the budget version includes many ads and bloatware. Network connectivity is reported as poor, and charging issues have been mentioned.

Read full reasoning

Overall, it suits users who need a large display and decent photos without demanding high performance or long endurance.
