Is the Google Pixel 9a worth buying?

What is good

  • Clean stock Android with 7 years of updates
  • Strong battery endurance with 8-9 hours SOT
  • Great main camera sensor and HDR performance
  • Bright and accurate 120Hz P-OLED display

What is not

  • Severe overheating and thermal throttling during gaming
  • Painfully slow 23W wired charging speed
  • Connectivity issues with 5G and VoLTE
  • Dated build with thick bezels and old glass

Buy it if You prioritize long-term software support and a clean interface over gaming performance and fast charging.

Skip it if You are a mobile gamer or someone who needs reliable 5G connectivity and fast charging speeds.

Why we say that

Living with this phone means enjoying a clean, bloatware-free Android 15 experience with the promise of 7 major updates. However, owners frequently report significant frustrations regarding thermal management, as the device reaches temperatures up to 44°C during heavy tasks. Gaming is particularly poor, with users noting severe throttling after only 15 to 20 minutes of play.

While the 5100 mAh battery provides strong endurance with 8 to 9 hours of screen-on time, the 23W charging speed is painfully slow. Connectivity is another pain point, with reports of 5G and VoLTE issues linked to the modem. The build quality feels dated due to the use of Gorilla Glass 3 and thick display bezels.

Ultimately, the phone struggles to justify its price when compared to newer alternatives like the Pixel 10a.

How long will the Google Pixel 9a receive software updates?

This phone ships with Android 15 and is upgradable to Android 16. Google provides up to 7 major Android updates for this device. This ensures the software remains current for a long time, provided the hardware continues to function well.

Is the Google Pixel 9a suitable for heavy gaming?

The phone is not recommended for gaming. Owners report severe thermal throttling and overheating, with temperatures reaching 41 degrees in just 15 to 20 minutes. The gaming performance rating is very low at 4.0 out of 10.

What is the battery life like on the Google Pixel 9a?

The 5100 mAh battery offers strong endurance for daily tasks. Users report achieving 8 to 9 hours of screen-on time on a single charge. However, you must be prepared for very slow 23W wired charging, and note that no charger is included in the box.
