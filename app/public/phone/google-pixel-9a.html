Is the Google Pixel 9a worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 120Hz P‑OLED display
  • Excellent 48MP camera with OIS
  • Large 5,100 mAh battery
  • Seven years of Android updates

What is not

  • Slow 23W charging, no charger included
  • Modem issues cause 5G and VoLTE drops
  • Plastic back and thick bezels feel dated
  • Gaming performance lags in heavy titles

Buy it if Buy if you value a vivid screen, solid camera and long software support more than fast charging or top gaming performance.

Skip it if Skip if you need reliable 5G connectivity, fast charging or a phone that handles demanding games without lag.

Why we say that

Its 6.3 inch 120Hz P‑OLED screen stays bright even in direct sun, and the 48MP main camera delivers clear photos with optical image stabilization. The 5,100 mAh battery gives decent endurance, yet the Tensor G4 modem can drain power faster in some regions and struggles with 5G and VoLTE signals. Software is a strong point with Android 15, a promise of up to seven major updates and a clean, bloat‑free experience.

However, the 23W charger is slow, no charger comes in the box, and gaming performance feels laggy compared with the price. Build quality is solid with IP68 protection, but the plastic back and thick bezels look dated. Overall, it is a solid daily driver if you can tolerate its connectivity and charging limits.
