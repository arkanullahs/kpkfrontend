Why we say that

The 6.59 inch AMOLED panel runs at 120Hz and reaches a bright 5000 nits peak, making outdoor use easy. Under the hood sits a Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 that handles everyday apps smoothly and can manage most games, though long sessions raise the temperature and cause occasional frame drops. The 6500mAh cell provides excellent endurance and the 90W charger refills it in under an hour, but there is no wireless charging option.

Photo quality is good with the 50MP main and selfie sensors, yet the ultrawide lens is underwhelming and low‑light performance suffers. Software runs on Android 16 with OriginOS 6, offering a polished UI but medium ads and some bloatware. The phone lacks eSIM and HDR video features, and the official warranty only covers the retail version, not gray imports.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

The 6500 mAh cell gives excellent endurance, easily lasting a full day of mixed use such as browsing, video streaming and moderate gaming. Owners report they can go 15‑20 hours without needing to recharge.

Will the phone overheat while gaming and does it affect performance?

During extended gaming sessions the device gets warm and occasional frame drops have been noted. The heat can cause minor throttling, but most day to day games run smoothly and the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 remains responsive.

How does the camera perform in low light situations?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures decent photos in good light, but the ultrawide lens struggles in dim environments and the telephoto detail can be softer than expected. Low light selfies are also average.

What warranty and software update support does the official model have?

Official units sold through the tracked shops carry the brand warranty, but it only applies to the official retail version. The global model receives three major Android updates and two security patches, while gray imports have no manufacturer cover.
