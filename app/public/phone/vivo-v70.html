Is the Vivo V70 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.59 inch 120Hz AMOLED display
  • 5000 nit peak brightness
  • 50MP selfie camera
  • 90W fast charging

What is not

  • No eSIM support
  • WhatsApp video calls look waxy
  • Gaming performance mediocre
  • No 4K 60fps video recording

Buy it if Choose this phone if you want a bright screen, strong battery and good selfie quality without needing eSIM or top tier gaming power.

Skip it if Skip it if you need high end gaming performance, 4K 60fps video or eSIM capability.

Why we say that

The 6.59 inch AMOLED panel shines with a 5000 nit peak and smooth 120Hz refresh, making media consumption pleasant. The 50MP main sensor with OIS and 3x tele delivers decent photos and excellent selfies, though low light detail can be inconsistent. Performance feels adequate for daily tasks but the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 uses older cores and the GPU struggles with demanding games.

Software runs on OriginOS 6 with a polished feel, yet some users notice update delays and medium level bloat. The lack of eSIM support and the waxy quality of WhatsApp video calls are common complaints, as is the absence of 4K 60fps video recording. Overall it feels like a premium device with a few notable compromises.
