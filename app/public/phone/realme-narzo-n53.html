Is the Realme Narzo N53 worth buying?

What is good

  • 90 Hz IPS LCD screen
  • 5000 mAh battery capacity
  • 50 MP main camera sensor
  • headphone jack for wired audio

What is not

  • HD resolution instead of full HD
  • low speaker volume
  • no official warranty or 5G

Buy it if Buy if you want a large display, long‑lasting battery and a cheap 50 MP camera and can accept limited updates and no 5G.

Skip it if Skip if you need official warranty, 5G connectivity, high‑quality audio or a true FHD display.

Why we say that

The 6.74‑inch IPS LCD runs at 90 Hz, giving smooth scrolling, yet the panel is only HD (720 × 1600) instead of full‑HD, so sharpness is limited. A 5000 mAh cell provides decent capacity, but owners note it is not very efficient and battery ratings are modest. The 50 MP main sensor captures detailed photos in good light, though the software tuning is weak and low‑light results suffer.

The phone lacks 5G, FM radio and an official brand warranty; you only get a shop warranty and must check IMEI and BTRC status. Software runs Android 13 with Realme UI T, but bloatware is noticeable and the three‑year‑old model is near the end of its update life. Audio from the speaker is quiet, and gaming performance is only average.

Is there any official warranty for the Realme Narzo N53 in Bangladesh?

There is no official brand warranty for this model in Bangladesh; the units found are gray‑import and only come with a shop warranty. You should verify the IMEI and BTRC registration with the seller.

How long will the phone receive software updates?
