Samsung Galaxy A36 5G Is it worth buying?

If you want a bright 6.7 inch AMOLED display but can tolerate fast battery drain and UI lag, the Galaxy A36 5G may fit your needs.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs

What works well4

  • 120 Hz Super AMOLED display
  • IP67 water dust resistance
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • 45 W fast charging

Reported trade-offs4

  • Rapid battery drain
  • Frequent UI lag and stutter
  • Thermal throttling during video or games
  • No microSD expansion

Why we say that

The phone feels solid, has an IP67 rating and a 50 MP main sensor with optical image stabilization. The 120 Hz Super AMOLED panel reaches 1900 nits, making outdoor use easy. However, owners report needing to charge the phone twice a day and seeing the device heat up during video recording or light gaming.

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 chipset struggles with multitasking and shows frequent UI stutters. One UI 8.5 brings many features but still suffers from animation bugs. The lack of a microSD slot limits storage flexibility.

Official warranty applies only to the higher‑priced listings; cheaper gray imports have no manufacturer cover. Compared with the newer Galaxy A54 5G, which sells for less, the A36 offers fewer advantages for the same or higher cost.
