Is the Samsung Galaxy A36 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Super AMOLED display with 120Hz refresh
  • 1900 nits peak brightness
  • IP67 rating and Gorilla Glass
  • 50MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Rapid battery drain needing multiple daily charges
  • Severe system lag and stuttering
  • No microSD card slot
  • Gaming performance is weak

Buy it if Buy if you care more about a bright, smooth screen and premium feel than top‑tier speed or long gaming sessions.

Skip it if Skip if you need strong gaming performance, reliable battery life, or expandable storage.

Why we say that

The 50MP main sensor with optical image stabilization captures decent shots but does not stand out in low light. Owners constantly mention that the 5000 mAh battery empties far faster than expected, forcing charges two or three times a day. System sluggishness is another regular complaint; simple actions lag and the phone heats up during any intensive task.

Gaming suffers from noticeable stutter and low frame rates, making the device unsuitable for serious players. The lack of a microSD slot eliminates any chance of expanding storage beyond the built‑in 128 GB or 256 GB. Software support is solid with Android 15 and up to six major upgrades, yet early One UI 8.5 builds feel buggy.

In short, the phone shines in display and build but falls short in performance, battery endurance and gaming.
