Reported trade-offs

  • Rapid battery drain
  • System UI lag and stutter
  • Heats up during video and games
  • No microSD expansion slot

Why we say that

The Galaxy A36 5G offers a 6.7‑inch Super AMOLED panel that reaches 1900 nits and runs at 120 Hz, making everyday use feel smooth and vivid. Build quality is solid with an IP67 rating, so it survives rain and dust without worry. The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures decent social‑media photos, and the 45 W charger can refill the 5000 mAh battery quickly.

However, owners report the battery drains fast, often needing multiple charges per day, and the One UI 8.5 interface feels sluggish with frequent stutters. The device also heats up during video recording or light gaming, leading to thermal throttling that hurts performance. There is no microSD slot, so storage is fixed.

How long does the battery last in normal daily use?

Owners say the 5000 mAh battery drains quickly, often requiring a charge twice a day even with moderate use. Fast charging helps, but you should expect to plug it in frequently.

Is the Galaxy A36 5G suitable for gaming?

The phone heats up during light gaming and shows UI stutters, resulting in poor sustained performance. It may handle casual games, but serious gamers will find it disappointing.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures adequate photos for social media, but it lacks flagship‑level processing, so low‑light images can be noisy and lack detail.
