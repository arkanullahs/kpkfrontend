Why we say that

The Galaxy A36 5G offers a 6.7 inch Super AMOLED panel that reaches 1900 nits and runs at 120 Hz, making everyday browsing and media consumption very pleasant. The body feels solid, carries an IP67 rating and the 50 MP main camera with OIS delivers decent social media photos in good light. Under the hood, the Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 with 6 GB RAM struggles with the One UI 8.5 interface; users report noticeable lag, stutters and heating even during simple video recording.

Battery life is the biggest disappointment, with many owners charging the 5000 mAh cell twice a day. The phone also lacks a microSD slot, limiting storage flexibility. Software will receive up to six Android version upgrades, but current bugs affect smoothness.

Will the battery last a whole day with normal use?

The phone packs a 5000 mAh cell, but owners repeatedly report severe drain and the need to charge twice a day, so a full day without topping up is not reliable.

Is the phone suitable for gaming?

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 with 6 GB RAM handles casual titles, but users notice UI stutter, heating and poor sustained performance, making it unsuitable for demanding or long gaming sessions.

How good is the camera in low light?

The 50 MP sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures decent pictures in daylight, and it is adequate for casual social‑media posts, but low‑light processing is not on par with flagship phones.
