Is the Samsung Galaxy A36 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 6.7 inch Super AMOLED display
  • IP67 water and dust resistance
  • 50MP main camera with OIS
  • 45W fast charging

What is not

  • Rapid battery drain, multiple charges daily
  • Frequent UI lag and stuttering
  • Thermal throttling during video or light gaming
  • No microSD card slot

Buy it if Buy it if you value a large, bright screen and solid build over long battery endurance and high‑end gaming performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need all‑day battery life, smooth gaming, or expandable storage.

Why we say that

The Galaxy A36 5G offers a 6.7 inch Super AMOLED panel that reaches 1900 nits, making outdoor use easy. Build quality feels solid and the IP67 rating protects against dust and water. The 50MP main sensor with OIS captures decent social media photos, and 45W charging refills the 5000 mAh cell quickly.

However, owners report the battery empties within a few hours, forcing multiple charges. The One UI 8.5 layer feels heavy, causing noticeable lag and occasional UI stutter. Even light gaming or video recording makes the device warm and slows performance.

The lack of a microSD slot limits storage flexibility. Official units start at ৳44,499 and include Samsung’s warranty, while cheaper gray import listings lack manufacturer support and may require paid repairs.

Why does the battery drain so quickly?

The phone has a 5000 mAh battery but owners report severe drain that forces charging two or more times a day. The fast‑charging 45W adapter refills it quickly, but the underlying consumption remains high.

Can I play games smoothly on this phone?

Gaming performance is limited. Users notice UI stutter, sluggish multitasking, and thermal throttling even during light games or video recording, which reduces frame rates and makes the experience feel laggy.

How good is the camera in low‑light situations?

The 50MP sensor with OIS captures acceptable photos for casual social media, but it lacks flagship‑level processing. Low‑light results are adequate, not outstanding, and the phone is not aimed at professional photography.
