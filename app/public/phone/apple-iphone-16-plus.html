Is the Apple iPhone 16 Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • A18 chip gives top performance
  • 48MP main camera with OIS
  • 4674 mAh battery lasts all day
  • iOS 18 smooth and ad‑free

What is not

  • 60 Hz display feels less smooth
  • Camera button glitches on hold
  • Charging 15 W is slow
  • Phone heats during heavy gaming

Buy it if Buy it if you want flagship speed, excellent battery life and are okay with a slower refresh rate and modest charging speed.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a high‑refresh display, fast wired charging, or a glitch‑free camera button.

Why we say that

The A18 processor delivers flagship speed and the 4674 mAh battery easily lasts a full day of mixed use, even outlasting the Pro Max models. The 6.7‑inch Super Retina XDR OLED shines at 2000 nits, though the 60 Hz refresh makes scrolling feel less fluid than expected for the price. The 48 MP main sensor with OIS captures sharp photos, yet the new camera button can freeze the screen when held.

Gaming runs fast thanks to the chip, but long sessions turn the device into a heating machine. iOS 18 runs smooth and will receive several major updates, but Apple Intelligence features remain limited in Bangladesh. Overall, it offers top‑tier performance and battery life, but compromises on display smoothness, charging speed, and button reliability.
