Is the Apple iPhone 16 Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • 2‑day battery life
  • A18 chipset handles AAA games
  • 48 MP main camera with OIS
  • 2000‑nit peak brightness

What is not

  • 60 Hz refresh feels laggy
  • 15 W wired charging is slow
  • Camera control button glitches
  • Gray‑import lacks official warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you want iOS stability, strong performance and premium build and can accept a 60 Hz display.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a fast charger, high refresh rate or official warranty at the lowest price.

Why we say that

The 6.7‑inch Super Retina XDR OLED reaches 2000 nits, giving excellent outdoor visibility, but the 60 Hz panel feels laggy compared with newer high‑refresh screens. A18 with 8 GB RAM scores 9.5 in performance and runs AAA games, yet long sessions make the phone a heating machine. The 48 MP main camera with OIS and macro mode produces sharp images, but no telephoto lens and occasional camera‑control button glitches hurt low‑light work.

Battery life is praised, often close to two days for light use, while the 15 W wired charger is slow; wireless charging is faster but needs extra gear. Official‑warranty models start at ৳1,35,000, gray‑import listings as low as ৳93,499 lack manufacturer cover and require paid repairs. iOS 18 runs smoothly, receives five to six major updates, and has no ads, though some AI features are delayed or region‑locked.
