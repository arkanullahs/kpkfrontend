Is the Apple iPhone 16 Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • 48 MP main camera with OIS
  • 2000‑nit peak brightness
  • A18 chipset handles AAA games
  • Long battery endurance for light use

What is not

  • 60 Hz refresh feels laggy
  • No telephoto lens
  • Camera control button glitches
  • Slow wired charging

Buy it if Buy it if you value top‑tier performance, bright display and a strong camera and are comfortable paying a premium for those strengths.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect a high refresh‑rate screen, telephoto zoom or a low‑cost device with official warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.7‑inch Super Retina XDR OLED can reach 2000 nits, so outdoor visibility is excellent, yet the low refresh rate shows lag in scrolling and gaming. The A18 processor provides smooth performance and can handle AAA titles, though long sessions turn the device into a heating machine. Battery life is a strong point; most light users report nearly two days between charges, but wired charging is slow and wireless charging needs extra accessories.

The 48 MP main camera with OIS produces clear images and a new macro mode, but the lack of a telephoto lens and a glitchy camera control button disappoint some users. Software updates are reliable, with iOS 18 upgradable to iOS 26.6 and no bloat. Official warranty only covers brand‑new units, while cheaper gray imports have no manufacturer support.

How long does the iPhone 16 Plus battery last in daily use?

The 4674 mAh battery is rated 9.0 by owners and often lasts close to two days for light users, while moderate use typically reaches a full day before needing a charge. Charging is slow on wired, faster with wireless accessories.

Will the iPhone 16 Plus get hot while gaming?

The A18 chipset delivers flagship performance and can run AAA titles smoothly, but during extended high‑intensity sessions the phone becomes a heating machine. Most users notice heat only when gaming for long periods.

Is the iPhone 16 Plus camera good in low light?
