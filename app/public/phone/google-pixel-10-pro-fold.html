Buying verdict

Google Pixel 10 Pro Fold Is it worth buying?

The Pixel 10 Pro Fold feels like a high end tablet you can slip into your pocket, but you pay for that flexibility with weight, a fragile crease and no official service support.

Choose it if
If you want a premium foldable with a strong camera and clean software and you are comfortable with a shop warranty, this phone fits your needs.
Consider alternatives if
If you need reliable gaming performance or official service support, look for another device.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • 8 inch 120 Hz LTPO OLED display
  • 48 MP main camera with OIS and 5× telephoto
  • Clean Android 16 with long term updates
  • Strong multitasking and AI tools

Reported trade-offs

  • No official Google warranty in Bangladesh
  • Heavy 258 g and prone to scratches
  • Screen crease deepens over time
  • Gaming performance weak

Why we say that

The 8 inch LTPO OLED screen is bright and smooth, reaching 3000 nits and 120 Hz, which makes everyday use pleasant. The 48 MP main camera with optical image stabilization and 5× telephoto captures sharp stills, though video quality lags behind top competitors. Android 16 runs cleanly and promises up to seven major updates, and the Tensor G5 handles daily tasks without lag.

Battery life is adequate for normal use but drops quickly when the screen is active or gaming. The hinge is sturdy and the device is IP68, yet owners notice micro scratches, a deepening crease and a heavier feel at 258 g. Because every unit is a gray import, you rely on the shop’s warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

Is the battery enough for a full day of heavy use?
