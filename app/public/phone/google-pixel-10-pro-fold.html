Google Pixel 10 Pro Fold Is it worth buying?

If you want a premium foldable with Google’s pure Android, expect a beautiful screen but be ready for warranty gaps and durability worries.

What works well4

  • 8‑inch 120 Hz LTPO OLED display
  • 48 MP main camera with OIS and 5× telephoto
  • Stock Android 16 with up to seven major updates
  • 30 W fast charging

Reported trade-offs4

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Screen hinge can develop deep creases
  • Battery life may drop after software updates
  • High‑end games run poorly

Why we say that

The 8‑inch 120 Hz LTPO OLED folds smoothly and shows vivid colors, while the 48 MP camera with OIS delivers natural stills and a strong selfie experience. Stock Android 16 feels clean and promises up to seven major OS updates, and the 30 W charger refills the 5,015 mAh battery reasonably fast. Real‑world use shows the phone handles daily tasks well, yet owners complain about a deep crease forming on the hinge, occasional black‑liquid leaks on the display, and battery drain after certain software updates.

Without an official Bangladeshi warranty, any repair falls to the shop’s limited coverage, so consider the risk before spending a month’s salary.

How good is the gaming performance on this phone?
