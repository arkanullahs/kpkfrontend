Is the Google Pixel 10 Pro Fold worth buying?

What is good

  • 8‑inch 120 Hz LTPO OLED with 3000‑nit peak
  • 48 MP rear camera with OIS and 5× telephoto
  • Stock Android 16 with up to seven major updates
  • IP68 rating and sturdy hinge

What is not

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Heavy at 258 grams and easy to scratch
  • Screen crease deepens with use
  • Gaming performance struggles on intensive titles

Buy it if Buy it if you want a premium foldable display, stock Android and can accept gray‑import warranty risk.

Skip it if Skip it if you need strong gaming performance or official service support.

Why we say that

The 120 Hz LTPO OLED delivers vivid colors and peak brightness of 3000 nits, making media consumption a joy. The 48 MP main sensor with OIS and 5× telephoto captures excellent stills and rear‑camera selfies, though video falls short of iPhone standards. Android 16 runs cleanly with up to seven major updates and useful AI multitasking tools.

Battery life is adequate for typical daily use but drops quickly when the phone is stressed by gaming or video. The hinge is durable and the phone is IP68 rated, yet the 258‑gram weight feels noticeable and the screen develops deeper creases and micro‑scratches over time. Repairs rely entirely on the shop’s warranty, so any hardware issue is at the buyer’s risk.

No official warranty is offered in Bangladesh. Every listing is a gray import, so the phone only has the shop's warranty and any repair costs are paid by the buyer. Confirm IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

How many Android version upgrades will the phone receive?

The device ships with Android 16 and promises up to seven major Android upgrades, matching the update schedule of newer Pixel models. This means you can expect several years of software improvements.

What is the battery life like for everyday use?

The 5015 mAh battery with 30 W charging provides solid endurance for typical daily tasks, but it drains quickly under heavy load such as gaming or video playback, according to owner reviews.

Can I play demanding games smoothly on this device?

The Tensor G5 chipset handles everyday apps well, but owners report lag and low frame rates in intensive games. If gaming performance is a priority, this phone may not meet expectations.
