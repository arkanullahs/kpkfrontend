Why we say that

Performance feels smooth for everyday tasks thanks to the Snapdragon 6 Gen 1, yet the phone may lag after a day and need a restart. The 50 MP camera can take decent pictures but struggles with consistency, especially in low light. The 6.72 inch IPS LCD offers a 120 Hz refresh rate and bright peaks, but the basic LCD panel feels cheap for media consumption.

Build quality is thin and not very durable, and there is no official manufacturer warranty, only the shop's limited cover. Overall it works for heavy use if you can tolerate the quirks and lack of brand support.

Will the battery last all day with heavy use?

The 6000 mAh battery is rated efficient and can last a full day for most users, but owners report occasional overheating and faster drain, especially when the CPU and GPU run hot.

How does the phone perform for gaming?

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 provides good overall performance and a smooth UI, yet the gaming rating is modest and some users notice lag after a day of use, requiring a restart.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor with f/1.8 aperture can take decent photos, but consistency drops in low light and owners describe the results as mixed rather than reliable.

Does the iQOO Z9X come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?
