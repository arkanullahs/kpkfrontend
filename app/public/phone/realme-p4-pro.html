Why we say that

The 6.8‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 144 Hz and reaches 6,500 nits peak, making everyday use bright and smooth. Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 delivers solid performance for most apps and games, and the 80 W charger fills the huge cell in about 75 minutes. The phone feels cheap because of its plastic chassis, and the camera produces oversaturated colours and weak night shots.

Realme UI 6.0 runs Android 15 but is packed with bloatware that you will need to disable manually. There is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s limited guarantee and any repair will cost extra. Overall it is a large, fast‑charging device that works well for media and gaming, but it falls short on build quality, camera fidelity and after‑sales support.

How long does the battery last on a normal day?

Will the phone handle modern games smoothly?

Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 with 8 GB RAM runs most games at good frame rates and stays cool, though the LPDDR4X memory may limit peak performance in the most demanding titles.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor struggles in low light, producing noisy images with inaccurate colours and low contrast. Night shots are average at best.
