Is the Realme P4 Pro worth buying?

The Realme P4 Pro offers a high-performance experience with a bright display, but owners are reporting significant issues with battery longevity. While the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset provides strong performance for daily tasks, the 7000 mAh battery drains quickly, leading to a low owner satisfaction rating of 4.5 for battery life. The 6.8 inch AMOLED screen is a highlight, earning an 8.5 rating for its brightness and 144Hz smoothness. However, the camera system is described as good but not exceptional, with users giving it a 7.0 rating and video performance a 6.0. You should be aware that this device is currently only available as gray-import stock, meaning you will receive a shop warranty rather than a brand warranty. Buyers should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing. With medium to high levels of bloatware and ads in the software, this phone is best suited for those who prioritize screen quality and raw performance over battery endurance.
