Redmi K100 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Redmi K100 Pro is a battery powerhouse that forces you to compromise on software experience and camera quality.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if

What works well4

  • Industry-leading 8580 mAh battery capacity
  • Extremely smooth 185Hz AMOLED display
  • Strong sustained performance for gaming
  • Solid IP68/69 build quality

Reported trade-offs4

  • HyperOS is heavily burdened by ads and bloatware
  • Shrunken main camera sensor limits photo quality
  • USB port downgraded to 2.0 speed
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

With an industry-leading 8580 mAh battery, it is built for users who prioritize longevity over everything else. Owners report that the device is heavily burdened by ads and bloatware, which significantly impacts the ease of use. While the 185Hz display is extremely smooth and bright, the main camera suffers from a shrunken sensor size and recycled optics that limit real-world performance.

You should also be aware that the USB port is limited to 2.0 data transfer speeds. Because this is a gray-import device, you will not receive any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Repairs are handled solely by the shop you purchase from, and you must confirm the BTRC status of your specific unit before paying.

Does the Redmi K100 Pro come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as a gray import. There is no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh regardless of the brand. You must rely on the specific shop for any repair promises, and all repairs will be paid services.

How does the camera perform for daily photography?

Owners rate the camera at 6.5 and video at 6.0. While the pixel count is high, the sensor size has actually shrunk and the optics are recycled. These hardware limitations restrict the real-world photographic quality you can expect from the device.

Is this phone suitable for heavy gaming?

The phone features a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 V Series chipset and owners rate gaming at 8.6. While there is a reduction in GPU compute units compared to the standard version, the device still provides strong sustained performance for mobile games.

What should I check before buying a gray-import unit?

You must confirm the BTRC status of the device with the seller. Additionally, verify if the unit is a China-market version or a global version. China-market units may lack Google services and Bangla language support, and converting them can be difficult.
