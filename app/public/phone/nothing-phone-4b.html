Is the Nothing Phone 4b worth buying?

What is good

  • Clean Nothing OS interface
  • Bright 2000 nits AMOLED display
  • Three major Android OS upgrades
  • No bloatware or ads

What is not

  • Aggressive and unfixable camera post-processing
  • Underpowered chipset for the price
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Heavy and bulky build quality

Buy it if You are a fan of the Nothing OS interface and do not require high-end gaming performance or reliable camera output.

Skip it if You want a reliable device with official warranty support, good gaming performance, or a camera that produces natural-looking photos.

Why we say that

Owners report that the camera suffers from aggressive, unfixable post-processing that ruins photos with unnatural colors and distortion. While the software offers a clean interface, recent updates have degraded system reliability and camera quality. The Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 chipset struggles with modern app demands, making it a poor choice for gaming or heavy multitasking.

Users find the device heavy and bulky, noting the absence of essential features like a microSD card slot or an auxiliary port. Because this is a gray-import unit, you have no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh. If the phone breaks, you are entirely at the mercy of the shop where you purchased it.

With 33W charging considered slow for 2026 standards and unremarkable display specs, the device is widely viewed as poor value for the money.

Marques Brownlee · Nothing Phone 4b: They Can't Say It! ↗

Marques concludes that the Phone 4b is a solid budget device that cleverly cuts corners without ruining the core Nothing experience. He notes that while the processor is lower-end, the plastic build wears easily, and haptics are mushy, the incredibly clean software and stellar battery life make it a bargain for those who can live with its compromises.

C4ETech English · Nothing Phone (4b) – The Budget Nothing We Wanted? ↗

Ash feels the Phone 4b successfully brings Nothing's unique aesthetic to a more affordable segment. He highlights the bright display, smooth software, and surprisingly functional simplified Glyph bar, concluding that the sensible price makes it a budget phone that buyers should not ignore.

Beebom · Nothing Phone (4b) Review: Should You Buy? ↗

Beebom finds the Phone 4b to be a well-balanced mid-range option during a tough year for smartphone pricing. They praise the excellent stereo speakers, bright display, and great thermal management, though they acknowledge the slow charging and weak low-light camera performance as notable drawbacks.

ATC Android ToTo Company · Nothing Phone (4b) : ঠিক জমলো না! ↗

ATC views the Phone 4b more like a rebranded budget CMF device equipped with Glyph lights, heavily criticizing its thick bezels, slower storage, and average performance. They feel it is difficult to recommend fully at its current price unless it gets a significant discount, suggesting it is merely an average phone in a tough market.

Tech Spurt · Nothing Phone 4b Review | What's Changed vs 4a? ↗

Tech Spurt appreciates the budget-friendly approach but expresses frustration over Wi-Fi connectivity dropouts and a smudge-prone plastic back. Ultimately, they suggest that buyers who can afford to spend a little more should strongly consider upgrading to the Phone 4a for its slicker design, better chip, and superior cameras.

Sillycorns · Nothing Phone 4b: Should You Buy? ↗

Sillycorns describes the Phone 4b as a highly practical device born out of current market limitations, delivering incredible battery life and remarkably stable performance. While the thick chin and slower charging speed are mildly annoying, they still consider it a very balanced all-rounder for anyone shopping on a budget.

Trakin Tech English · We Tested Nothing’s Budget Phone for 2026! ft Phone 4b ↗

Arshad loves the phone's design and overall value, pointing out that it handles temperatures remarkably well under gaming loads. Despite the large bottom bezel and a basic ultra-wide camera, he highly recommends the device for its reliable battery, vibrant screen, and premium software experience.

Tech to the Point - TTP · Nothing ফোনের এ কি হাল? Nothing 4B Review in Bangla ↗

Does the Nothing Phone 4b come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit is a gray import, meaning you must rely solely on the specific shop's promise for repairs. There is little to no official service presence for this brand locally.

Is the Nothing Phone 4b good for gaming?

The phone is not recommended for gaming. Owners report that the Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 chipset is underpowered for the price and struggles to handle modern app demands. It currently holds a low rating of 4.0 out of 10 for gaming performance.

How long will the Nothing Phone 4b receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 16 and Nothing OS 4.1. It is promised to receive up to 3 major Android upgrades. However, users have noted that recent updates have negatively impacted system reliability and camera performance.

What is the battery life and charging speed like?

The phone features a 5200 mAh battery that provides average endurance. It supports 33W charging, which is considered slow by 2026 standards. Owners have rated the battery performance as a 7.0 out of 10.
