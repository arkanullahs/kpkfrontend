Honor X7e Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day without charging, the Honor X7e delivers that.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want long battery life and a solid, affordable phone for basic use.
Consider alternatives if

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 7500 mAh battery with 45 W fast charging
  • 120 Hz smooth display
  • 50 MP main camera for daylight shots
  • IP64 rated sturdy build with headphone jack

Reported trade-offs3

  • Helio G81 struggles with lag
  • Low light photos are noisy and lack detail
  • TFT panel shows gray blacks

Why we say that

Its 7500 mAh battery and 45 W fast charging give you exceptional endurance for a budget device. The 6.61" TFT LCD runs at 120 Hz, which makes scrolling feel smooth, but the panel shows gray blacks and poor contrast. The 50 MP main camera captures decent daylight photos, yet low light shots become noisy and lack detail, and there is no stabilization.

Performance is held back by the Helio G81 Ultra, leading to noticeable lag in everyday use and during intensive gaming. The build is sturdy with IP64 protection, a headphone jack and FM radio, and MagicOS 10 runs Android 16 with some bloatware. Owners appreciate the battery and durability, but they complain about lag, low‑light camera quality and the dimmer display.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The Honor X7e packs a 7500 mAh battery that owners rate 9.8 out of 10, giving you well over a full day of mixed use and even longer with moderate use. Fast 45 W charging restores most of the charge in about an hour.

Will the phone handle mobile games well?

The Helio G81 Ultra chipset struggles with daily tasks and intensive games, and owners report noticeable lag. Expect lower frame rates in demanding titles, so the phone is better suited for casual gaming rather than high performance play.

How does the camera perform in low light?

In bright daylight the 50 MP sensor produces decent photos, but in low light situations the images become noisy and lose detail, and there is no optical stabilization. Users note a sharp drop in quality when lighting falls.

Does the phone come with an official warranty and how many updates will it get?

Only the listings that show the official warranty give you manufacturer coverage; gray import versions have no brand warranty and repairs are paid. The device runs Android 16 with MagicOS 10 and may receive three to four major OS updates, similar to other Honor flagships.
