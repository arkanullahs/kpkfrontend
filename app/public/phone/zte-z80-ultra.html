ZTE Z80 Ultra Is it worth buying?

The ZTE Z80 Ultra is a high-performance powerhouse that demands you accept significant compromises in software and support for its raw speed.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You are a power user who prioritizes raw gaming performance and battery life above all else and can handle manual software configuration.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable daily driver with stable software, good selfie quality, and the peace of mind provided by an official brand warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Elite Snapdragon 8 Elite performance
  • Massive 7200 mAh battery endurance
  • Beautiful notchless 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Unbeatable price to performance ratio

Reported trade-offs4

  • Poor under-display selfie camera quality
  • Unreliable software and short update promises
  • Cheap haptics and vibration feedback
  • No official brand warranty support

Why we say that

Living with this device means dealing with a China-ROM experience that includes clunky translations, no preinstalled Google services, and messy OTA updates. While the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset and massive 7200 mAh battery provide elite gaming and endurance, the user experience is hampered by unreliable software support and cheap haptics. Owners frequently report that the under-display selfie camera produces poor results and the build, while featuring a unique holographic design and IP69 rating, is prone to scratches.

Because every unit in Bangladesh is a gray import, you are entirely at the mercy of the shop if the device breaks. You are paying ৳1,02,999 for flagship silicon, but you must be prepared to troubleshoot software quirks and accept that official brand-backed service does not exist here.

ZTE Z80 Ultra: what buyers ask us

What is the ZTE Z80 Ultra price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for ZTE Z80 Ultra in Bangladesh was ৳1,02,999, checked 24 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does the ZTE Z80 Ultra have official warranty support in Bangladesh?

No, there is no official brand-backed warranty for this phone in Bangladesh. Every unit currently available is a gray import, meaning you must rely solely on the shop for any service or support if the device breaks.

How long will the ZTE Z80 Ultra receive software updates?

Software support is considered unreliable for this device. Owners report that ZTE provides a maximum of one year of updates, and the software experience is further complicated by the use of China-ROM variants that lack Google services.

Is the ZTE Z80 Ultra good for heavy gaming?

Yes, the phone is excellent for gaming. It features the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset which delivers elite speed and stable high frame rates. While the device stays generally cool, some heat is expected during hardcore gaming sessions.

How is the battery life on this device?

The battery life is exceptional. It features a massive 7200 mAh silicon-carbon battery that provides industry-leading endurance. Owners rate the battery performance very highly at 9.7 out of 10, making it one of the strongest features of the phone.
