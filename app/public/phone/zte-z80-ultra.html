Buying verdict

ZTE Z80 Ultra Is it worth buying?

The Z80 Ultra gives you a flagship class gaming experience and a battery that can last two days, but you must live without official warranty or long software support.

Choose it if
You want flagship level performance, massive battery life and can accept a gray import unit with no official warranty or long software support.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable software updates, official service centers or high quality selfie photos.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • 7200 mAh battery with 90 W fast charging
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 delivers top gaming performance
  • 6.85 inch 144 Hz AMOLED display with 2000 nit peak brightness
  • 16 GB RAM and up to 1 TB storage options

Reported trade-offs

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Software updates limited to about one year and can be unstable
  • Under display selfie camera quality is weak
  • Haptics and speakers feel cheap

Why we say that

It packs the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset, 16 GB RAM and up to 1 TB storage, so apps and games run smoothly at high frame rates. The 6.85 inch AMOLED panel refreshes at 144 Hz and can reach 2000 nits peak brightness, making bright outdoor use easy. The 7200 mAh silicon carbon battery charges at 90 W and easily survives heavy use.

The main 50 MP camera delivers good detail, though the under display selfie camera and telephoto mode are disappointing. The build looks holographic and is IP69 rated, but the haptics feel cheap and the screen lacks HDR support. Software runs Android 16 with Nebula AIOS 2, but updates stop after about a year and can be unstable.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 7200 mAh silicon carbon battery can easily last two full days of mixed use and even longer with moderate use. It refills in about 45 minutes with the 90 W charger.

Is the Z80 Ultra good for gaming?

The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and 16 GB RAM keep frame rates high even in demanding titles. Owners report stable performance with only occasional heat during very long sessions.

How is the camera performance in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor with a large 1/1.3 inch size and f/1.7 aperture captures decent low light shots, but the under display selfie camera remains weak and the telephoto mode shows fuzzy detail.

Will I get official warranty or software updates?

The phone is sold as a gray import, so there is no official ZTE warranty in Bangladesh. Repairs depend on the shop’s warranty and software updates are expected for only about one year.
