What works well

  • Bright 1800 nits AMOLED display
  • Fast 80W charging
  • Strong gaming performance
  • OIS on the 50MP main camera

Reported trade-offs

  • No official brand warranty
  • Ads on the power button
  • Weak video quality
  • Medium amount of bloatware

Why we say that

It offers a bright 120Hz AMOLED screen that works well in sunlight and a Dimensity 7200 chipset for decent gaming. Owners give gaming and battery a score of 8.0. However, the software experience is mixed.

Some users report an ad for bluelm copilot when long pressing the power button. You must be careful about which version you buy. Some sellers offer the China market version with OriginOS, while others have India units with Funtouch.

The camera has OIS but owners note it does not reach flagship quality. Video performance is the weakest point with an owner rating of 5.5.
