Is the Redmi Note 15 4G worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6000 mAh battery endurance
  • Stunning 3200-nit peak brightness AMOLED display
  • Slim and lightweight premium feel
  • Up to 4 major Android upgrades

What is not

  • HyperOS 2 contains intrusive system ads
  • Frequent and unstable Wi-Fi disconnections
  • Lack of an ultrawide camera lens
  • Struggles with heavy gaming performance

Buy it if You prioritize multi-day battery life for social media and light tasks and are comfortable managing a budget-tier software experience.

Skip it if You are a mobile gamer, require a reliable ultrawide camera, or need a phone that works perfectly out of the box without software bugs.

Why we say that

Owners report that the massive 6000 mAh battery is the standout feature, often lasting several days for light users. However, the user experience is heavily impacted by HyperOS 2, which is described as buggy and filled with intrusive system ads. While the 3200-nit AMOLED display is visually impressive, it suffers from accidental touches.

Performance is strictly basic, as the Helio G100 chipset is not suitable for heavy gaming. The camera setup is also limited by the absence of an ultrawide lens. Buyers must be cautious regarding the software version, as many gray-market units are China-specific builds that lack Google services and Bangla language support.

Frequent Wi-Fi disconnections remain a significant reported issue for many users.

What is the Redmi Note 15 4G price in Bangladesh?

Redmi Note 15 4G costs ৳24,490 to ৳32,699 in Bangladesh on unofficial listings, in stock at 5 shops we track as of 9 Sep 2026. The official price starts at ৳26,999.

Does the Redmi Note 15 4G have a reliable battery?

Yes, the phone features a massive 6000 mAh battery that owners rate highly. Light users report that the device can last between two to four days on a single charge.

Is this phone good for playing heavy games?

No, the device is not recommended for heavy gaming. It uses a basic Helio G100 chipset that is fine for social media usage but struggles significantly when running demanding games.

What is the difference between official and unofficial units?

Official units come with a brand warranty and are built for the local market. Unofficial units are gray imports that lack manufacturer cover, and some may be China-market versions that lack Google services and Bangla language support.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The device is promised to receive up to 4 major Android upgrades. However, users should be aware that the software experience includes high levels of bloatware and frequent system advertisements.
