Is the Redmi Note 15 4G worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6000 mAh battery endurance
  • Bright and vibrant 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Excellent thermal management stays cool
  • Strong 108MP main camera sensor

What is not

  • Intrusive system and full-screen ads
  • Sluggish performance in heavy games
  • Slow 33W charging speed
  • Missing ultrawide camera lens

Buy it if You are a basic user who prioritizes long battery life and a premium-looking display over gaming performance.

Skip it if You are a heavy gamer, a multitasker, or someone who finds system ads and software lag frustrating.

Why we say that

Owners report that the 6000 mAh battery is the standout feature, often lasting up to four days. However, the experience is marred by a heavy layer of HyperOS that includes intrusive full-screen ads and performance issues following system updates. While the 120Hz AMOLED display is vibrant and bright, the curved edges lead to frequent accidental screen touches.

The Helio G100 Ultra chipset handles basic tasks well, but it feels sluggish during multitasking and heavy gaming. Build quality is another concern, as the plastic back scratches easily. Buyers must be extremely cautious regarding the software version, as many gray-market units are China-market versions that lack Google services and Bangla language support.

With slow 33W charging and a lack of an ultrawide camera, this phone is a mixed bag that requires careful consideration before spending your money.

How long will the Redmi Note 15 4G receive software updates?

This device is equipped with Android 15 and is promised up to 4 major Android upgrades. However, owners have reported that system updates can cause lag and performance degradation, so you should consider the potential impact on your phone's speed.

The phone is fine for basic tasks, but it is not recommended for heavy gaming. Owners rate the gaming performance at 4.2 out of 10, noting that the device feels sludgy and struggles with multitasking during intensive use.

You must verify if the unit is a global version or a China-market version. Many gray-market units are China-market versions that lack Google services and Bangla language support, and converting them to a global ROM is difficult.
