Redmi Note 15 4G Is it worth buying?

If you need a massive battery and a bright AMOLED screen, the Redmi Note 15 4G can keep you going, but expect software ads and limited performance.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value long battery life and a vivid screen over high‑end gaming.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable Wi‑Fi, ad‑free software, or a strong gaming phone.

What works well4

  • 6000 mAh battery lasting up to four days
  • 3200‑nit AMOLED display
  • 108 MP main camera
  • IR blaster for remote control

Reported trade-offs4

  • HyperOS shows intrusive ads
  • Wi‑Fi disconnects frequently
  • No ultrawide or telephoto lens
  • Heavy games lag on Helio G100

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh cell gives two to four days of use for light users, and the 6.77‑inch 120 Hz panel reaches a peak brightness of 3200 nits, making outdoor viewing easy. The 108 MP main sensor captures decent photos, yet the lack of an ultrawide lens hurts versatility. Helio G100 Ultra handles social media fine but struggles with demanding games, and HyperOS 2 adds Android 15 with up to four major upgrades but is cluttered with pop‑up ads.

Wi‑Fi drops are reported often, and the device has no 3.5 mm jack. Official warranty applies only to the higher‑priced listings; cheaper gray imports are China or Global builds without manufacturer cover.
