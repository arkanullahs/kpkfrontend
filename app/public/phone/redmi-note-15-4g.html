Redmi Note 15 4G Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can sit on the charger for a few minutes and run for days, the Redmi Note 15 4G delivers that.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value long battery life and a bright display more than top‑tier gaming or camera versatility.
Consider alternatives if
You need 5G, a smooth gaming experience, or a warranty‑free purchase.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 6000 mAh battery lasting 2‑4 days
  • 6.77‑inch AMOLED with 3200‑nit peak
  • 108 MP main sensor
  • IR blaster for remote control

Reported trade-offs4

  • No ultrawide or telephoto camera
  • HyperOS shows intrusive ads
  • Wi‑Fi disconnects frequently
  • Heavy games lag on Helio G100

Why we say that

The 6.77‑inch AMOLED screen is bright enough for outdoor use and the 120 Hz refresh feels smooth. The 6000 mAh battery easily reaches two to four days for light users, and the 33 W charger refills it quickly. The 108 MP main camera produces decent photos but the lack of an ultrawide lens limits versatility.

Performance is adequate for social media and everyday apps, yet the Helio G100 struggles with demanding games. HyperOS brings Android 15 and up to four major updates, but it is packed with ads that many find intrusive. Wi‑Fi drops are reported by several owners.

Read full reasoning

Build quality feels premium but the rear panel scratches easily. Overall it is a solid budget option if you can tolerate the software and camera compromises.
