What works well

  • 6000 mAh battery lasting up to four days
  • 3200 nit AMOLED display with 120 Hz refresh
  • 108 MP main camera sensor
  • IR blaster for remote control

Reported trade-offs

  • HyperOS shows intrusive pop up ads
  • Wi Fi disconnects frequently
  • No ultrawide or telephoto camera
  • Heavy games lag on Helio G100

Why we say that

The 6.77 inch AMOLED panel reaches a peak brightness of 3200 nits and refreshes at 120 Hz, making outdoor use easy. The 6000 mAh cell is rated 9.2 by owners and often lasts two to four days for light users, though heavy browsing shortens that. The Helio G100 Ultra handles social media and light apps without trouble, but heavy games feel laggy and frame drops are common.

The 108 MP main sensor captures decent photos in good light, yet the lack of an ultrawide lens and modest low light performance disappoint many. HyperOS 2 brings Android 15 and up to four major updates, but it is loaded with pop up ads and can disconnect from Wi Fi repeatedly. The phone arrives without a 3.5 mm jack and many gray import units run a China ROM that lacks Google services.
