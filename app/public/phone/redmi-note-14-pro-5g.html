Buying verdict

Redmi Note 14 Pro 5G Is it worth buying?

The Redmi Note 14 Pro 5G gives you a flagship level camera and a very bright AMOLED screen, but the software and lack of official warranty make daily use a mixed bag.

Choose it if
You want a high resolution camera and a very bright display and can tolerate software quirks and the lack of an official warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need smooth gaming, reliable software, or an official Xiaomi warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • 200MP main camera with OIS
  • 6.67 inch 120Hz AMOLED display
  • 5110 mAh silicon carbon battery
  • 45W fast charging

Reported trade-offs

  • HyperOS is ad heavy and unstable
  • Severe UI lag and stutters
  • Charging limited to 45W
  • Curved screen causes touch issues

Why we say that

It runs on a Dimensity 7300 Ultra chipset with 8 GB RAM and up to 512 GB storage, delivering decent performance for everyday tasks but many owners report UI lag, stutters and occasional restarts. The 200 MP main sensor with f/1.7 aperture and optical image stabilization captures sharp night shots, while the ultra wide and macro lenses struggle in low light. The 6.67 inch AMOLED panel reaches a peak of 3000 nits and supports 120 Hz refresh, yet the curved edges can cause touch inaccuracies.

Battery life is strong thanks to a 5110 mAh silicon carbon cell, but the 45 W charger is slower than previous models. The phone ships as a China ROM with HyperOS, which is ad heavy and prone to bugs. A newer Note 15 Pro 5G sells for a similar price and is often a better choice.

Will the Redmi Note 14 Pro 5G receive Android updates?

The phone runs Android 14 with HyperOS and promises up to three major Android upgrades. Updates may arrive slower because the device is a China ROM and the software is heavily customized.

How is the battery life and charging speed?

The 5110 mAh silicon carbon battery provides great endurance for daily use. However, the 45W charger is slower than previous models, so charging takes longer than some users expect.

Is the camera good for low light photos?

The 200MP main sensor with f/1.7 aperture and OIS performs well at night, delivering sharp images. The ultra wide and macro lenses are weaker and produce poorer results in low light.

What warranty and support can I expect?

All units are gray import China ROM, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Only the shop’s limited warranty applies and any repairs will be paid by the owner.
