Is the Samsung Galaxy S24 worth buying?

What is good

  • Excellent performance from Snapdragon 8 Gen 3
  • Bright and smooth 120Hz display
  • Sturdy and durable build quality
  • Long-term software support up to 7 years

What is not

  • Average battery life
  • Only gray-import stock available
  • Potential for green line display issues

Buy it if Buy this phone if you want a compact flagship with high-end performance and long-term software support.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you require an official brand warranty or need a battery that lasts through heavy usage.

Why we say that

Living with this device means enjoying a smooth 120Hz experience and a powerful Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset that handles gaming well. However, owners report that the 4000 mAh battery life is only average and could be better. While the build quality is sturdy and durable, users have raised concerns regarding the potential for green line issues on the OLED panel.

Since you are limited to shop warranties rather than official brand support, you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your money. The software experience is clean with low bloat and ads, and the promise of up to 7 major Android upgrades adds long-term value. If you prioritize raw speed and screen quality over battery endurance, this compact flagship remains a strong contender.
