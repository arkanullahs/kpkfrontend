Buying verdict

Helio 47 Is it worth buying?

The Helio 47 gives you a massive battery that can easily last a full day, but everything else feels budget‑level.

Choose it if
If you need long battery life, a cheap officially‑warranted phone and are fine with basic performance, this device fits your needs.
Consider alternatives if
If you want fast charging, high‑end gaming, a bright high‑resolution screen or a camera that handles low light, look elsewhere.

What works well

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 120 Hz refresh makes scrolling feel smooth
  • Clean Android UI without bloatware
  • Official warranty at ৳17,399

Reported trade-offs

  • Charging is very slow in practice
  • 720p display struggles in bright sunlight
  • Camera blur due to slow shutter
  • No major Android OS updates

Why we say that

At the cheapest official‑warranty price of ৳17,399 you get a 6.67‑inch IPS LCD with a 120 Hz refresh, 6 GB RAM, 128 GB storage and a 50 MP main camera. Real‑world use shows the 6000 mAh cell is strong, yet charging is painfully slow despite the 100 W spec. The display’s 720p resolution and 500‑nit brightness struggle under direct sunlight, and the camera often produces blurry shots because of a slow shutter.

Software is clean and free of bloat, but you will only receive security patches, no major Android updates. Gaming performance is low, with PUBG or COD playable only on the lowest settings. Build quality is plastic with an IP64 rating, but the back attracts fingerprints.

Overall it is a decent basic phone for the price, but not for power users.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 6000 mAh battery received an 8 out of 10 rating from owners and can easily last a full day of mixed use, but you should expect a slow recharge because real‑world charging is much slower than the advertised 100 W.

Can I play games like PUBG or Call of Duty on this phone?

The Helio G81 chipset with 6 GB RAM handles basic tasks well, but gaming ratings are low and owners report that PUBG and COD run only on the lowest graphics settings, so it is not suited for serious mobile gaming.

Is the camera good for low‑light or night shots?

The 50 MP main sensor often produces blurry photos because the shutter speed is slow, and owners note poor dynamic range and exposure, especially in low‑light conditions, making it unreliable for night photography.

Will the phone receive Android OS updates?

The software is clean and free of bloatware, but owners say only security patches are provided and no major Android version updates are expected, so the OS will stay on its original version.
