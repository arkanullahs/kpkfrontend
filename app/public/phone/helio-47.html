Is the Helio 47 worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz smooth display
  • Huge 6000 mAh battery
  • Fast 100W charging
  • 50MP main camera

What is not

  • Helio G81 lags in heavy games
  • No 5G support
  • Newer Helio 50 cheaper
  • Camera struggles in low light

Buy it if Buy it if you want long battery life, fast charging and a smooth screen at a modest price.

Skip it if Skip it if you need the latest chipset, 5G connectivity or the best low‑light camera.

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh battery easily powers a full day of browsing, video streaming and moderate gaming, and the 100W charger refills it in minutes. The 6.67‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120Hz, so scrolling and most games feel smooth. The 50MP main sensor captures sharp photos in good light, and Android provides a familiar interface.

The Helio G81 chipset with 6GB RAM handles everyday apps well but can stumble in the most demanding games, and the lack of 5G means slower data speeds where networks are available. Owners often note that the camera struggles in low‑light scenes and that a newer Helio 50 model is priced lower, making it a more attractive option for budget‑conscious buyers. Overall the phone feels solid for its price, but it is not the most future‑proof choice.
