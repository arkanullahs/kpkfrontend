Is the Helio 47 worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6000 mAh battery endurance
  • Clean and bloat-free software UI
  • Official warranty support in Bangladesh
  • IP64 rated plastic build

What is not

  • Slow shutter speed causes blurry photos
  • Low 720p display resolution and brightness
  • No major Android OS updates
  • Single speaker lacks stereo sound

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a device with a very long battery life for basic daily tasks and prefer a clean software interface.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you enjoy mobile gaming, take frequent photos, or want a device that will receive major Android OS updates.

Why we say that

While the 6000 mAh battery provides solid longevity, owners frequently struggle with the slow shutter speed that often results in blurry photos. The 720p display lacks sufficient brightness for outdoor use, making it difficult to see in direct sunlight. Performance is strictly limited to basic tasks, and gamers will find that titles like PUBG or COD only run on low settings.

The software experience is clean and bloat-free, but the lack of major Android OS updates is a significant drawback for long-term users. Additionally, the single speaker setup is a disappointment for media consumption. Given that the newer Helio 90 is available for ৳16,499, this device faces stiff competition.

If you prioritize a massive battery and a clean interface, it might serve your needs, but be prepared for compromises in camera reliability and overall speed.
