Buying verdict

Google Pixel 10 Is it worth buying?

The Google Pixel 10 is a problematic device that struggles with thermal management and battery efficiency, making it a difficult recommendation for most users.

Choose it if
You are a dedicated fan of stock Android who prioritizes long-term software support over gaming performance and battery endurance.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable phone for gaming, heavy multitasking, or all-day battery life without the risk of thermal issues.

What works well

  • Clean and fluid Android 16 software
  • Excellent 3000 nits peak display brightness
  • Industry-leading 7 years of Android updates
  • Premium build materials

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe thermal throttling under load
  • Disappointing battery life and slow charging
  • Camera hardware sensor downgrades
  • Thick bezels and top-heavy design

Why we say that

Owners report that the phone gets hot under moderate load and suffers from severe thermal throttling, where performance drops to less than 30 percent of its maximum capacity. The battery life is a regression from the previous model, lasting only 9 hours and 38 minutes in active use tests. While the software experience is fluid and clean, the hardware choices, including sensor downgrades in the camera system, have left many users disappointed.

Charging is also slow, taking 1 hour and 38 minutes for a full cycle. Given that the newer Google Pixel 11 is available for ৳94,999, most buyers will find that the newer model offers better value for their money. This phone is an unofficial gray import, meaning there is no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh, and all repairs must be paid for out of pocket.

Does the Pixel 10 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is an unofficial gray import. There is no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh regardless of the brand's service score. Any repairs are paid, and coverage is limited to whatever the specific shop promises.

Is the Pixel 10 good for mobile gaming?

The phone is not recommended for gaming. Owners report persistent thermal management issues and heavy throttling. The GPU performance lags significantly behind flagship competitors, and the device gets hot under moderate load.

How long will the Pixel 10 receive software updates?

The device runs Android 16 and is backed by industry-leading support. You will receive up to 7 major Android upgrades, ensuring the software remains current for a long period.

How does the battery life perform in daily use?

Battery performance is a regression compared to the Pixel 9. Owners report poor efficiency, with an active use score of only 9 hours and 38 minutes. Additionally, a full charge takes 1 hour and 38 minutes.
