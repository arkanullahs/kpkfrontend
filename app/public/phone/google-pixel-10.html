Is the Google Pixel 10 worth buying?

What is good

  • 3000‑nit OLED display
  • Clean stock Android with 7‑year updates
  • Premium build material
  • 30W fast charging

What is not

  • Very weak battery life
  • Heavy thermal throttling under load
  • Camera sensor smaller than previous
  • eSIM only, no physical SIM

Buy it if Buy it if you value an exceptional display and long‑term software support over raw performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need solid battery endurance, strong gaming performance, or a physical SIM slot.

Why we say that

The Pixel 10 offers a 6.3 inch OLED panel that can peak at 3000 nits, making outdoor use easy. The Tensor G5 chip delivers the familiar Pixel smoothness, yet owners notice heavy thermal throttling when the phone is stressed, which lowers gaming and overall performance scores. The 4970 mAh battery with 30W charging still provides weak endurance; many see inconsistent discharge and a rating of 5.5 out of 10.

Camera fans will miss the larger sensors of earlier Pixels because the 48MP sensor is smaller, leading to disappointing low‑light detail. Build quality feels premium, though the device feels top‑heavy and dense. Software stays a highlight with stock Android 16, no bloatware and up to seven years of updates.

Buying unofficially keeps the price between 73,990 and 94,990 taka, but the phone comes without official warranty protection.

The phone ships with Android 16 and promises up to seven major Android upgrades, giving it about seven years of software support from Google.

Is the battery life good for everyday use?

Owners report very weak battery endurance and inconsistent discharge. The battery rating is 5.5 out of 10, so it may not last a full day under heavy use.

Will the Pixel 10 handle gaming well?

Gaming performance is modest with a rating of 6.0. The Tensor G5 shows some gains but heavy thermal throttling under load can cause frame drops.

Can I use a regular SIM card in this phone?

The Pixel 10 is eSIM only, meaning it does not have a physical SIM slot. Many buyers consider this a deal breaker if they need a physical SIM.
