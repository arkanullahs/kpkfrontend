Is the Google Pixel 10 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.3 inch OLED 120 Hz display
  • Clean Android 16 with seven years updates
  • 12 GB RAM for smooth multitasking
  • 48 MP main camera with optical image stabilization

What is not

  • Battery lasts under ten hours of active use
  • Device gets hot and throttles quickly
  • Charging takes more than an hour and a half
  • Camera softness and lower low‑light quality

Buy it if Buy if you want a bright phone with pure Android and long software support and can charge it daily.

Skip it if Skip if you need all‑day battery, heavy gaming or top‑tier camera performance.

Why we say that

The 6.3 inch OLED panel hits 3,000 nits at peak, making outdoor use easy, and the 120 Hz refresh feels fluid. Android 16 runs cleanly and promises seven years of major updates, so the software stays fresh. The 12 GB of RAM handles multitasking without stutter, and the 48 MP main sensor still captures decent photos in good light.

Owners, however, report the battery drops to about nine and a half hours of active use, far below the Pixel 9, and a full charge now takes an hour and a half. The device runs hot under moderate load and throttles to less than 30 % of its CPU power, which hurts gaming and heavy apps. Camera softness and lower low‑light quality also disappoint compared with earlier models.
