  • Soft low light photos

  • Slow 30 W charging

Why we say that

The Pixel 10 shows a vivid 6.3 inch OLED panel that can reach 3000 nits at peak, making outdoor use easy. Android 16 runs without bloat and promises up to seven major OS updates, so the software stays fresh for years. The Tensor G5 chipset delivers smooth everyday performance, yet owners report severe thermal throttling that cuts CPU speed during long sessions.

The 48 MP main camera uses a smaller sensor, so images appear soft and low light shots lack sharpness. Battery endurance is modest and the 30 W charger fills the 4970 mAh cell slowly, taking over an hour and a half for a full charge. The phone feels top heavy and the bezels are thick, which may affect comfort.

How long will the phone receive Android updates?

The Pixel 10 ships with Android 16 and is promised up to seven major OS updates, similar to the Pixel 8+ line, so you can expect software support for about seven years.

What is the battery life and charging speed like?

The 4970 mAh battery is rated weak by owners and endurance is low. The included 30 W wired charger is slow, taking around one hour and 38 minutes for a full charge and only reaching 46 percent in half an hour.

Is the camera good in low light conditions?

The main 48 MP camera uses a smaller sensor, which results in soft detail and struggles in low light. Photos may lack sharpness and clarity when lighting is poor.
