Reported trade-offs

Why we say that

While it has a large 108MP camera and a 120Hz screen, owners report the display is dim. Real world use reveals significant issues with overheating and battery drain, even when just scrolling through social media. Gaming is a major weak point.

Users report rapid overheating that causes frames to drop to 5. The software experience is hindered by high amounts of bloatware and ads. Since this phone was released in 2023, you should expect very little to no remaining software update life.

What is the Tecno Spark 20 Pro price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Tecno Spark 20 Pro in Bangladesh was ৳19,990, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Is this phone good for gaming?

No, it is not for heavy gamers. Owners report the phone overheats rapidly during games with good graphics, which causes the fps to drop to 5.

How is the battery life on this device?

The battery life is described as average. Some owners have reported issues with battery drain even when performing simple tasks like scrolling through social media.

Will I get software updates for a long time?

You should expect short or no remaining software update life. The phone was released in 2023 and the software is reported to be slow with high bloatware.

How is the camera quality?

The phone has a 108MP main sensor and a 32MP selfie camera. However, reviews describe the photos as mediocre.
