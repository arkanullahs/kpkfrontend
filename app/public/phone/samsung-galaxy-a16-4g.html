Why we say that

The 6.7 inch Super AMOLED panel runs at 90Hz, giving vivid colours and smooth scrolling for everyday apps. A 5000 mAh cell paired with 25W charging easily carries you through a typical workday without hunting for a charger. The Helio G99 chipset handles basic tasks well, yet owners report lag, freezes and thermal throttling when gaming or using navigation together.

Camera performance is mixed; the 50MP main sensor captures decent daylight shots, while the ultrawide lens and video stabilization fall short. Software runs One UI 8.5 on Android 14 and promises up to six major Android upgrades, though some preinstalled apps remain. Build quality is solid plastic with IP54 protection, but there is no headphone jack and the water drop notch looks dated.
