What works well

  • 7200 mAh battery with strong endurance
  • 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED 144 Hz display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite performance
  • 50 MP dual camera system

Reported trade-offs

  • No official brand warranty
  • Medium bloat and ads in MagicOS
  • Large size may feel bulky

Why we say that

The 7200 mAh battery gives strong endurance and quick charging, so most owners report getting through a day of heavy use. The 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED panel reaches a peak of 6000 nits, making the screen bright and smooth at 144 Hz. Snapdragon 8 Elite paired with up to 16 GB RAM provides flagship level performance that owners rate as nine out of ten.

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS and a 3× tele lens captures good photos, and the same resolution selfie camera satisfies most users. MagicOS 9 runs Android 15 with Google services, but it carries medium bloat and ads. Updates are expected for three to four major versions, which is typical for flagships.
