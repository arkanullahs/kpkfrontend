Is the Motorola S50 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Very bright 3000 nits peak display
  • High 12GB RAM
  • Clean software with low ads
  • Fast 68W charging

What is not

  • Small 4310 mAh battery
  • Historically weak software updates
  • No official brand warranty

Buy it if You want a compact phone with a high quality screen and clean software.

Skip it if You need a long battery life or an official manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

You get a 6.36 inch LTPO P-OLED display that hits 3000 nits peak brightness. The Dimensity 7300 chipset and 12GB RAM handle daily tasks well. However, the 4310 mAh battery is smaller than many competitors.

You should be aware that this phone is only available as gray import stock. This means you get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty. You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying.

Software updates are a known weak point for this brand. You might only get 1 to 3 OS updates over the life of the device. The Hello UI is near stock with low bloatware and ads.
