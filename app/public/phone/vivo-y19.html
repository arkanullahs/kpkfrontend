Is the Vivo Y19 worth buying?

What is good

  • 5G connectivity
  • Large 5500 mAh battery
  • Android 15 software
  • Includes headphone jack and FM radio

What is not

  • Slow eMMC storage
  • Low resolution 720p display
  • Poor build quality
  • Weak gaming performance

Buy it if You need a cheap 5G phone for basic tasks and want the latest Android version.

Skip it if You play games or want a high quality screen and fast storage.

Why we say that

Owners report that the Dimensity 6300 chipset is wasted because the phone uses eMMC storage instead of UFS 2.2. This makes the performance feel slow, and gaming is rated poorly at 3.5 out of 10. The 720p screen with an ultrasonic notch is described as terrible by users.

While the 5500 mAh battery provides average endurance, the overall build quality is rated very low. You are getting a modern version of Android 15, but the hardware holds it back. Since only gray import stock is available, you must check the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying.
