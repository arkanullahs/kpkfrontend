Infinix Zero 40 Is it worth buying?

The Infinix Zero 40 offers a powerful performance and a high-quality display, but it comes with significant software and hardware frustrations that potential buyers must consider.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize high-end display and performance specifications and are comfortable managing software ads and gray-market warranty terms.
Consider alternatives if
You want a reliable, hassle-free experience with a manufacturer warranty and no heating or software bloat issues.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • High performance Dimensity 8200 Ultimate chipset
  • Bright 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Capable 108MP main camera with OIS
  • Generous 12GB RAM and large storage options

Reported trade-offs4

  • High levels of ads and bloatware
  • Heating issues during gaming and charging
  • Camera settings fail to save automatically
  • Only gray-import stock available without manufacturer warranty

Why we say that

While the Dimensity 8200 Ultimate chipset handles tasks well, owners frequently report that the device gets hot during gaming and charging. The software experience is hindered by high levels of bloatware and notification ads, which detract from the overall ease of use. Camera performance is another point of contention, as users have noted that specific settings like 4K 60 fps video resolution do not save, requiring manual adjustment every time the app is opened.

Battery life is described as average, and some users have reported charging issues. Because only gray-import stock is currently available, you will not receive a manufacturer warranty, and you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing. This phone is a mixed bag that prioritizes raw specifications over a polished, reliable daily experience.

What is the Infinix Zero 40 price in Bangladesh?

Does the Infinix Zero 40 come with an official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there is no listing that states a manufacturer warranty for this device. You will only find gray-import stock, which means you will have to rely on a shop warranty instead. You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before you make a purchase.

How long will the Infinix Zero 40 receive software updates?

The phone runs on Android 14 and XOS 14.5 out of the box. It is promised to receive up to 2 major OS updates. However, users have noted that software updates can be slow to arrive.

Is the Infinix Zero 40 good for gaming?

The phone features a Dimensity 8200 Ultimate chipset and owners report good performance for gaming. However, you should be aware that the device gets hot during gaming sessions, which may impact your overall experience.

What is the battery life like on the Infinix Zero 40?

Owners report that the battery life is average. While the phone includes a 5000 mAh battery, some users have reported charging issues and noted that the device gets hot while it is charging.
