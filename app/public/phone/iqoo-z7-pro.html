iQOO Z7 Pro Is it worth buying?

The iQOO Z7 Pro is a gray-import device that requires careful consideration regarding its lack of official brand support in Bangladesh.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a high-performance display and fast charging and are comfortable using an unofficial device without brand warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable 5G connectivity, official brand warranty support, or high-quality camera performance.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Bright 1300 nits AMOLED display
  • Efficient 4600 mAh battery performance
  • Fast 66W charging capability
  • Strong Dimensity 7200 chipset performance

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official brand warranty support
  • 5G network is unavailable
  • Poor owner ratings for camera and video
  • Limited remaining software update life

Why we say that

Owners rate the performance and display highly, but the experience is hampered by significant software and network limitations. While the Dimensity 7200 chipset handles tasks well, users report that 5G connectivity is unavailable on this device. The camera system, despite having OIS, receives low marks from owners for both photo and video quality.

Since this phone was released in 2023, you should expect very limited remaining software update support. You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before purchasing, as there is no official warranty available. The device relies entirely on shop-provided warranties, which carry different risks than standard brand coverage.

Read full reasoning

If you prioritize a bright AMOLED screen and fast charging, this phone offers those features, but the overall package is held back by its unofficial status and reported network issues.

Does the iQOO Z7 Pro come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there is no official brand-backed warranty for this phone in Bangladesh. All units found in the market are gray imports, meaning you only receive a shop warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before making a purchase.

How long will the iQOO Z7 Pro receive software updates?

This phone was released in 2023 and is currently running Android 13 with a planned upgrade to Android 14. Given its age, you should expect very short or no remaining software update life at this point in time.

Is the iQOO Z7 Pro good for gaming and daily performance?

Owners rate the performance of this phone at 8.5 out of 10 and gaming at 7.5 out of 10. The Dimensity 7200 chipset is generally considered efficient for these tasks, though individual experiences may vary based on your specific usage.

Can I use 5G networks on this phone in Bangladesh?

No, you cannot use 5G on this device. Owners have specifically reported that 5G network is unavailable on this phone, which is a significant limitation for users looking for modern connectivity speeds.
