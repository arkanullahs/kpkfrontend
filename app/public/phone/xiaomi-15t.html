Is the Xiaomi 15T worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz AMOLED with 3200‑nit peak brightness
  • Dimensity 8400 Ultra delivers flagship gaming
  • 50MP Leica main camera with OIS
  • 67W fast charging

What is not

  • Heavy pre‑installed bloatware
  • Telephoto lens lacks OIS
  • Fake always‑on display limited to 10 seconds
  • Virtual proximity sensor only

Buy it if Buy it if you want flagship‑level performance and smooth gaming without needing top‑tier camera features or an official warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable low‑light photography, a true always‑on display, or brand‑backed warranty support.

Why we say that

The 6.83 inch AMOLED screen delivers smooth 120Hz motion and peaks at 3200 nits, making outdoor use easy. Dimensity 8400 Ultra with 12GB RAM runs Android 15 smoothly and handles most games at high settings, thanks to IceLoop cooling, though you may notice minor dips at the very top. The 5500 mAh battery gives decent day‑long endurance for most users, yet some owners report mixed daily life results and the 67W charger, while reliable, is slower than newer 120W options.

The Leica‑branded 50MP main sensor captures good photos, but the 2× telephoto lacks OIS, the ultrawide is weak, and selfies are unimpressive. The phone arrives as a gray‑import with no official Xiaomi warranty; only the shop’s limited warranty applies and any repair will cost you. Heavy bloatware and a fake always‑on display that works for only ten seconds add to the compromises.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 5500 mAh battery usually lasts a full day for moderate use, but owners report mixed endurance and occasional early drain, so you may need to charge before the evening if you are a heavy user.

Is the Xiaomi 15T good for gaming?

Yes, the Dimensity 8400 Ultra with 12GB RAM provides flagship‑like smoothness and good thermals thanks to IceLoop cooling. You may see minor frame drops at the highest settings, but most games run well.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50MP Leica main sensor handles low‑light decently with f/1.7 aperture, but the ultrawide is weak and the 2× telephoto lacks OIS, so overall low‑light results are not as strong as true flagship cameras.

Will I get an official warranty and how many updates?

No official Xiaomi warranty is provided because the phone is a gray‑import. The shop offers its own limited warranty and any repair will be paid. Software will start on Android 15, upgrade to Android 16, and receive about three to four OS updates and four to five security patches.
