Is the Xiaomi 15T worth buying?

What is good

  • Elite 3200 nits peak brightness display
  • Very smooth performance for heavy gaming
  • Capable 50MP main camera for daylight shots
  • Stable thermals during daily use

What is not

  • Excessive bloatware and pre-installed apps
  • Plastic frame and back feel less premium
  • Weak selfie and low-light camera performance
  • No OIS on the telephoto camera

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize raw gaming performance and a high-quality display over build materials and official warranty support.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you want a premium build, reliable low-light photography, or the peace of mind that comes with an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Owners report that the display is truly elite, offering sharp visuals and impressive brightness that stands out in this price range. However, the experience is marred by excessive bloatware and pre-installed apps that many users feel compelled to remove manually. While the performance is stable, the build quality feels less premium due to the plastic frame and back.

Battery life is highly polarizing, with some users managing over a day while others struggle to get through the afternoon. You should also be aware that the camera setup lacks OIS on the telephoto lens and struggles in low-light conditions. Since this device is only available through unofficial gray-import channels, you will rely on shop warranties rather than official brand support.

Given that the newer Xiaomi 17T is available for a similar price, you should consider if the savings are worth the trade-offs.

Is the battery life reliable for a full day of use?

Battery performance is polarizing among owners. Some users report getting 1.5 days of usage on a single charge, while others find themselves needing to charge the device twice daily. Your experience will likely depend on your specific usage patterns.

How does the phone perform for heavy gaming?

The Xiaomi 15T is considered a beast for gaming. Owners report that the Dimensity 8400 Ultra chipset provides very smooth performance for both daily tasks and heavy gaming sessions. The device maintains great stability with generally stable thermals during use.

Does this phone come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is currently only available through unofficial gray-import channels. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty. It is important to confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before making a purchase.

How long will this device receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 15 and is upgradable to Android 16. It is expected to receive 3 to 4 OS updates and 4 to 5 years of security updates. Note that the software includes pre-installed apps and ads that can be disabled.
