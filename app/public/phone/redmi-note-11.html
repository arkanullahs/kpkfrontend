Redmi Note 11 Is it worth buying?

If you need a large, bright screen and a big battery without spending much, the Redmi Note 11 fits the bill.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a bright screen, solid battery and decent camera at a low price and can accept limited software support.
Consider alternatives if
You need official warranty, long‑term Android updates or a phone for heavy gaming.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 90 Hz AMOLED display
  • 5000 mAh battery with 33W fast charge
  • 50 MP main camera
  • headphone jack and FM radio

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Software updates likely ended
  • Gray‑import may have IMEI issues
  • High amount of preinstalled ads

Why we say that

The 6.43 inch AMOLED panel runs at 90 Hz and can peak at 1000 nits, making media consumption comfortable even in sunlight. The 5000 mAh cell paired with 33W fast charging keeps the phone alive through a full day of browsing, streaming and occasional gaming. The Snapdragon 680 handles everyday tasks smoothly, though it is not a powerhouse for demanding games.

The 50 MP main camera captures detailed photos in good light, but low‑light performance is modest. The device includes a headphone jack, FM radio and IR blaster, which are rare these days. Owners often complain about the lack of an official warranty, the gray‑import status that may affect IMEI registration, and the fact that software updates have largely stopped after four years on the market.

Is there any official warranty for the Redmi Note 11 in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray‑import, so the phone has no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop you buy from may offer its own limited warranty, but any repair work will be paid by you. Confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchase.

Will the phone receive Android updates in the future?

The device launched in 2022 and is now four years old, so the remaining software‑update life is short or none. It runs HyperOS and may get two to four OS upgrades depending on the tier, but further updates are unlikely.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 5000 mAh battery provides a full day of moderate use such as browsing, social media and video playback. Fast charging at 33W refills the cell quickly, but using the bright 90 Hz screen at high brightness can reduce endurance.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor with an f/1.8 aperture captures detailed images in good lighting. In low‑light situations the results are acceptable for a budget phone but may show noise and less sharpness compared to newer flagships.
