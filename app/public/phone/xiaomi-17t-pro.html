Buying verdict

Xiaomi 17T Pro Is it worth buying?

Expect a phone that can run games for hours without charging, thanks to its 7,000 mAh battery and Dimensity 9500 chipset.

Choose it if
Buy if you need massive battery life, high refresh display, and can accept a gray import warranty.
Consider alternatives if
Skip if you require official brand warranty, a newer USB port, or strong low light ultrawide photos.

What works well

  • 7,000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 6.83 inch AMOLED reaches 3,500 nits peak
  • Dimensity 9500 delivers top gaming performance
  • 100 W fast charging fills quickly

Reported trade-offs

  • No manufacturer warranty, only shop warranty
  • USB 2.0 port feels outdated
  • Optical fingerprint scanner is slower
  • Ultrawide camera struggles in low light

Why we say that

The 6.83 inch AMOLED panel reaches a peak brightness of 3,500 nits and refreshes at 144 Hz, making outdoor use easy to read. Fast 100 W charging refills the large cell in minutes, and the 50 MP main camera with optical image stabilization and 5 x telephoto delivers sharp photos. Owners praise the massive battery, smooth display and gaming performance.

Complaints focus on the lack of an official warranty, a USB 2.0 port on a premium priced device, an optical fingerprint sensor and an ultrawide lens that struggles in low light. Software is polarising with noticeable bloat, and the price feels high for a gray import.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

Owners report that the 7,000 mAh cell gives exceptional endurance, often lasting a full day of heavy use and still having charge left for evening activities. Real world tests confirm the battery rating is accurate.

Is the phone good for gaming?

The Dimensity 9500 chipset is described as a ‘giant killer’ and delivers blazing responsiveness in demanding titles. Reviewers give the gaming experience a 9.4 rating, noting smooth frame rates at 144 Hz.

How does the camera perform in low light situations?

The main 50 MP sensor with OIS performs well, but the ultrawide lens lacks autofocus and produces noisy results in dim environments. Users say low light ultrawide videos are pretty bad.

What warranty and after sale support does a gray import have?

All listings are gray imports, so there is no official Xiaomi warranty in Bangladesh. The shop you buy from may offer its own limited warranty, but repairs are paid and you must verify IMEI and BTRC status.
