Is the Xiaomi 17T Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Exceptional 7000 mAh battery endurance
  • Blazing fast Dimensity 9500 performance
  • Bright 3500 nits peak display
  • Great main and telephoto camera quality

What is not

  • Slow USB 2.0 data transfer speeds
  • Disappointing optical fingerprint scanner
  • Fixed-focus ultrawide camera lens
  • Bloated HyperOS with locked bootloader

Buy it if You are a mobile gamer who prioritizes raw performance and battery life above all other features.

Skip it if You value a clean software experience, fast data transfer speeds, or the security of an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Owners are thrilled with the Dimensity 9500 chipset and the massive 7000 mAh battery, which provides industry-leading endurance. However, the user experience is marred by a polarizing HyperOS that includes bloatware and a locked bootloader. While the display is bright and high-quality, the inclusion of an optical fingerprint scanner and slow USB 2.0 data speeds feels out of place for a device costing up to 86,500 taka.

The camera system is capable for daylight photography, yet the fixed-focus ultrawide lens and poor low-light video performance leave much to be desired. Because this phone is only available through gray-import channels, you must be prepared to rely on shop warranties rather than official brand support. It is a device built for raw power, but it requires you to overlook several frustrating hardware and software limitations.

What reviewers said

The Xiaomi 17T Pro delivers outstanding battery life, powerful performance, and a superb telephoto camera, but a steep price increase ruins the value proposition the series is known for. It is an excellent smartphone held back by minor upgrades over the previous generation, annoying software quirks, and a cost that puts it too close to true premium flagships.

Samuel Nam · Xiaomi 17T Pro Review - Best bang for buck? ↗

Samuel Nam found the Xiaomi 17T Pro to be an absolute battery monster with a fantastic camera setup that outpaces competitors in many scenarios. Despite a noticeable price hike and some minor hardware cutbacks like an older charging port, he concluded that the massive battery capacity, incredibly fast charging, and superior zoom capabilities make it a great deal for tech enthusiasts willing to look past the Apple logo.

GSMArena Official · Xiaomi 17T Pro review: Premium power at a premium price ↗

GSMArena praised the phone for its outstanding battery endurance, top-tier processor performance, and flagship-grade main and telephoto cameras. However, they noted that heavy processor throttling under load, underwhelming stereo speakers, and a weak ultra-wide camera hold it back. Ultimately, they felt the high launch price puts it too close to true premium phones, making it a device better bought after a discount.

Tech Spurt · Xiaomi 17T Pro Review | Camera Beast, or Overhyped? ↗

Tech Spurt appreciated the smooth gaming performance, solid battery life, excellent display, and a smudge-resistant design. However, he had mixed feelings about the occasional software glitches, imbalanced audio, and the camera's slight struggle to focus on moving subjects. He concluded that while it is a very solid smartphone overall, the steep price tag makes slightly older or cheaper alternatives look like much better deals.

Flossy Carter · Xiaomi 17T Pro "Real Review" - Almost Perfect ↗

Flossy Carter loved the phone's durable build quality, stunning screen, included fast charger, and impressive reverse wireless charging feature. However, he was severely disappointed by the steep launch price for the global version, arguing that it is simply too expensive compared to imported alternatives with better specs. He firmly stated that unless you absolutely need a global device right away, you should look elsewhere or stick to the older model.

TechMishka · Xiaomi 17T Pro Review: The Best Xiaomi You Shouldn't Upgrade To?! ↗

TechMishka highlighted the phenomenal battery life, powerful processing chip, and professional-grade video recording features as major selling points that punch above the phone's weight. On the downside, he heavily criticized the slow data transfer port, average ultra-wide camera, and the lack of meaningful hardware upgrades over the previous generation. He concluded that while the phone is fantastic on its own, the high price makes it almost impossible to recommend upgrading to if you already own last year's model.

Does the Xiaomi 17T Pro have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, the device is currently only available through gray-import stock. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, so you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before making your purchase.

How long will the Xiaomi 17T Pro receive software updates?

The phone runs on Android 16 and is upgradable to Android 17. It is supported by HyperOS 3, which includes a commitment of 3 to 4 OS updates and 4 to 5 years of security updates.

Is the Xiaomi 17T Pro good for gaming?

Yes, the Dimensity 9500 chipset is considered a giant killer by owners. It provides blazing fast responsiveness and holds a high owner rating of 9.4 for gaming performance, making it an excellent choice for demanding mobile games.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

While the main and telephoto cameras are great, the camera system is let down by poor low-light video performance. Additionally, the fixed-focus ultrawide lens struggles to capture sharp images when lighting is not ideal.
