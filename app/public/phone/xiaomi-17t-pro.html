Is the Xiaomi 17T Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 7,000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 3,500 nit AMOLED screen is extremely bright
  • Dimensity 9500 delivers flagship level gaming
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS and 5x telephoto

What is not

  • No official Xiaomi warranty in Bangladesh
  • USB C port limited to USB 2.0 speeds
  • Optical fingerprint sensor feels slower
  • Ultrawide camera struggles in low light

Buy it if Buy if you need massive battery life, a high refresh display and top tier gaming performance and you are comfortable with a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip if you rely on an official manufacturer warranty, need fast USB data transfer, or prioritize low‑light ultrawide photography.

Why we say that

Its 7,000 mAh cell gives owners real world endurance that scores 9.6 out of 10, and the Dimensity 9500 chipset delivers flagship level responsiveness for demanding games. The 6.83 inch AMOLED panel shines at a peak 3,500 nits and runs a smooth 144 Hz refresh rate, though the high PWM may bother sensitive eyes. Photography is strong on the 50 MP main sensor with OIS and 5x telephoto, yet the fixed focus ultrawide struggles in low light and video quality drops.

Software runs HyperOS 3 on Android 16, upgradeable to Android 17, but it carries noticeable bloat. The device arrives as a gray import, so only the shop’s warranty applies and repairs are paid. USB C is limited to USB 2.0 and the fingerprint reader is optical, which some users find slow.

Samuel Nam · Xiaomi 17T Pro Review - Best bang for buck? ↗

Samuel Nam found the Xiaomi 17T Pro to be an absolute battery monster with a fantastic camera setup that outpaces competitors in many scenarios. Despite a noticeable price hike and some minor hardware cutbacks like an older charging port, he concluded that the massive battery capacity, incredibly fast charging, and superior zoom capabilities make it a great deal for tech enthusiasts willing to look past the Apple logo.

GSMArena Official · Xiaomi 17T Pro review: Premium power at a premium price ↗

GSMArena praised the phone for its outstanding battery endurance, top-tier processor performance, and flagship-grade main and telephoto cameras. However, they noted that heavy processor throttling under load, underwhelming stereo speakers, and a weak ultra-wide camera hold it back. Ultimately, they felt the high launch price puts it too close to true premium phones, making it a device better bought after a discount.

Tech Spurt · Xiaomi 17T Pro Review | Camera Beast, or Overhyped? ↗

Tech Spurt appreciated the smooth gaming performance, solid battery life, excellent display, and a smudge-resistant design. However, he had mixed feelings about the occasional software glitches, imbalanced audio, and the camera's slight struggle to focus on moving subjects. He concluded that while it is a very solid smartphone overall, the steep price tag makes slightly older or cheaper alternatives look like much better deals.

Flossy Carter · Xiaomi 17T Pro "Real Review" - Almost Perfect ↗

Flossy Carter loved the phone's durable build quality, stunning screen, included fast charger, and impressive reverse wireless charging feature. However, he was severely disappointed by the steep launch price for the global version, arguing that it is simply too expensive compared to imported alternatives with better specs. He firmly stated that unless you absolutely need a global device right away, you should look elsewhere or stick to the older model.

TechMishka · Xiaomi 17T Pro Review: The Best Xiaomi You Shouldn't Upgrade To?! ↗

TechMishka highlighted the phenomenal battery life, powerful processing chip, and professional-grade video recording features as major selling points that punch above the phone's weight. On the downside, he heavily criticized the slow data transfer port, average ultra-wide camera, and the lack of meaningful hardware upgrades over the previous generation. He concluded that while the phone is fantastic on its own, the high price makes it almost impossible to recommend upgrading to if you already own last year's model.
