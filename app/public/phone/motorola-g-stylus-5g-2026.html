Is the Motorola G Stylus 5G 2026 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.7 inch AMOLED 120Hz display
  • 5000 mAh battery with 68W fast charge
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • Headphone jack for wired audio

What is not

  • Shop warranty only, no brand warranty
  • Update support historically weak
  • Must verify IMEI and BTRC status

Buy it if Buy if you want a large bright screen, solid camera and can handle a shop issued warranty.

Skip it if Skip if you need an official manufacturer warranty or guaranteed long term updates.

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch AMOLED panel runs at 120Hz and can peak at 3000 nits, so bright outdoor use feels comfortable. The Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 paired with 8GB RAM handles everyday apps and moderate gaming without stutter. The 5000 mAh cell lasts a full day for most users and the 68W charger refills it in under an hour.

Photos are captured with a 50 MP main sensor that has OIS and an f/1.8 lens, giving decent low light results, while the 32 MP selfie camera is sharp enough for video calls. The phone runs Android 15 with a near stock Hello UI, low bloat and promises up to two major Android upgrades, though past Motorola devices have been slow to deliver updates. Owners often mention the need to double check the IMEI and BTRC registration because the device is a gray import and only comes with a shop warranty.

Is there an official Motorola warranty for this phone in Bangladesh?

How many Android updates will this phone receive?

Will the battery last a full day with heavy use?

The 5000 mAh battery combined with the Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 and a 120Hz display should last a full day for most heavy users. The 68W charger can refill the battery quickly when needed.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor has an f/1.8 aperture and optical image stabilization, which helps in low light situations. Results are decent for a mid range phone but will not match flagship level quality.
