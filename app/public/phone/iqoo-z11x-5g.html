Is the iQOO Z11x worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 7200 mAh battery life
  • Stable 60FPS gaming performance
  • Excellent thermal management
  • Durable IP68 and IP69 rating

What is not

  • Outdated IPS LCD with large bezels
  • Slow 44W charging speed
  • Poor low-light camera performance
  • Persistent notification delays

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize multi-day battery life and stable gaming performance above all else.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you want a high-quality AMOLED display, fast charging, or a reliable camera system.

Why we say that

Owners report that the massive 7200 mAh cell easily lasts 1.5 to 2 days, making it a reliable choice for those who hate carrying a charger. However, the 44W charging speed is slow for such a large battery, requiring 80 to 100 minutes for a full cycle. You will notice significant display bezels that some users compare to the width of a highway.

While the Dimensity 7400 Turbo chipset handles gaming well with stable 60FPS in PUBG, the camera system struggles with oversaturated daylight shots and soft low-light performance. Software remains a point of frustration due to notification delays and bloatware. Since this is an unofficial gray-import device, you must accept that there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh.

Repairs are entirely at your own expense through the shop you choose.

Does the iQOO Z11x have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you must rely on the specific shop for any coverage. All repairs are paid services.

How does the battery perform in real-world usage?

Owners rate the battery highly with a 9.7 score. The 7200 mAh cell provides 1.5 to 2 days of real-world use. It also supports bypass charging to help manage thermals during long sessions.

Is the camera good for low-light photography?

The camera performance is generally weak, with owners giving it a 5.2 rating. Low-light photos appear soft, and the main sensor struggles with oversaturated colors in daylight. There is also a persistent exposure issue in video recording.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The global or India units running Funtouch OS are promised 3 OS updates. If you purchase a China-market unit with OriginOS, you should confirm the specific update policy with your seller as it may differ.
