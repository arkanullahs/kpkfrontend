Honor X7c Is it worth buying?

The Honor X7c gives you a big battery and decent everyday speed for a price well below the official warranty level.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want long battery life, solid everyday performance and a headphone jack at a budget price.
Consider alternatives if
You need a high resolution display, strong low light photos or flagship level gaming power.

Why we say that

At ৳18,999 it arrives as a gray import, cheaper than the cheapest official warranty listing of ৳22,999. It packs a Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 chipset, 8 GB RAM and 256 GB storage, which feels smooth for browsing, social media and routine apps. The 5,200 mAh cell and 35 W charger keep the phone alive for one and a half to two days with a full charge in about ninety minutes.

The 6.8 inch TFT LCD runs at 120 Hz but only offers HD+ resolution, which many owners call disappointing for the cost. The 50 MP main sensor captures good daylight photos, yet low light shots are weak and there is no ultrawide lens. Gaming performance drops on demanding titles, though daily use remains fine.

Honor X7c: what buyers ask us

What is the Honor X7c price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Honor X7c in Bangladesh was ৳18,999, checked 17 Oct 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5,200 mAh battery usually lasts between one and a half and two days with normal use such as browsing, social media and video playback, and it recharges fully in about ninety minutes with the 35 W charger.

Will the phone handle mobile games well?

Everyday games run smoothly thanks to the Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 and 8 GB RAM, but demanding titles cause frame drops and lower performance, so it is not suited for heavy gaming sessions.

Is the camera good in low light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor captures clear daylight photos, but low light images are noisy and lack detail, and the phone does not include an ultrawide lens, which limits versatility in dim environments.

Does the phone come with an official warranty or software updates?

There is no listed official warranty for this gray import model, so buyers rely on the seller’s policy, while MagicOS 8 on Android 14 is expected to receive about three to four major OS updates over its lifespan.
