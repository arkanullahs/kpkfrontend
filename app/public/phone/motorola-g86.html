Motorola G86 Is it worth buying?

If you want a cheap 5G phone with a big screen and a solid battery, the Motorola G86 delivers basic performance at a low price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a cheap 5G phone with a big battery and decent camera.
Consider alternatives if
You need long software support or a solid manufacturer warranty.

What works well4

  • 120Hz IPS LCD display
  • 5G connectivity
  • 50MP main camera
  • 5000 mAh battery

Reported trade-offs4

  • Ghost touches reported in humid conditions
  • Software updates likely ended
  • Shop warranty only, no brand warranty
  • 15W charging is slow

Why we say that

The 6.5 inch IPS LCD runs at 120Hz which makes scrolling feel smooth, and the Snapdragon 480+ handles everyday apps without lag. The 50MP main camera captures decent photos in good light, while the 16MP selfie shooter is adequate for video calls. Battery life is strong thanks to the 5000 mAh cell, but the 15W charger refills it slowly.

Owners report occasional phantom touches on the display, especially in humid weather, and the phone is already four years old so software updates are likely finished. The device arrives as gray import stock, meaning only a shop warranty is offered and you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

Motorola G86: what buyers ask us

What is the Motorola G86 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Motorola G86 in Bangladesh was ৳48,990, checked 21 Oct 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Will the phone receive Android updates?

The phone ships with Android 12 and can be upgraded to Android 13. It was released in 2022 and is now four years old, so further major updates are unlikely.

Is there a manufacturer warranty?

No manufacturer warranty is listed. The phone is sold as gray import stock, so only a shop warranty is offered and you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

How long does the battery last?

The 5000 mAh battery can easily last a full day of mixed use. Charging is limited to 15W, so refilling the battery takes longer than on fast‑charging phones.

Does the display have any known problems?

Some units experience phantom or ghost touches, especially in humid conditions. The issue is not universal but has been reported by several owners.
