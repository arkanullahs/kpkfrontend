Is the Motorola G86 worth buying?

What is good

  • Smooth 120Hz display refresh rate
  • Large 5000 mAh battery capacity
  • Clean near-stock Hello UI software
  • Includes a dedicated headphone jack

What is not

  • Widely reported display ghost touches
  • Dated Snapdragon 480+ chipset
  • No official manufacturer warranty available
  • Limited remaining software update life

Buy it if Buy this phone only if you are a collector or a light user who specifically needs a near-stock Android experience and does not mind the risks of gray-market hardware.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you want a reliable daily driver with official warranty support, modern performance, or guaranteed long-term software updates.

Why we say that

While the 120Hz display and 5000 mAh battery offer decent utility, the phone is plagued by reports of ghost touches, particularly in the humid weather common across Bangladesh. Because this model is four years old, you should expect little to no remaining software update life beyond the Android 13 upgrade. You will only find gray-import stock in the market, meaning you must rely on shop-provided warranties rather than official manufacturer support.

The Snapdragon 480+ chipset is dated, and the overall experience is hampered by the lack of long-term software maintenance. Before handing over ৳48,990, you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller to ensure the device is legitimate. This phone is a relic of a previous generation that struggles to compete with current market standards.
