Vivo S50 Is it worth buying?

The Vivo S50 is a powerful China-ROM import that offers flagship performance but requires significant technical effort to use daily in Bangladesh.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if

What works well4

  • Massive 6500 mAh battery life
  • Flagship-class gaming performance
  • Bright 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Fast 90W charging speeds

Reported trade-offs4

  • No Google services pre-installed
  • Weak 8MP ultrawide camera
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • OriginOS software has ads

Why we say that

Because these units run OriginOS, you will not find Google Play pre-installed, and you must navigate a system designed for Chinese services. Owners appreciate the massive 6500 mAh battery and the smooth Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset, which handles gaming without throttling. However, the experience is marred by a weak 8MP ultrawide camera and a generic design that feels uninspired.

You are buying a gray-market device with no manufacturer warranty, meaning any repairs are paid and your only support comes from the shop. While the 120Hz AMOLED display is bright and responsive, the software friction of OriginOS makes this a difficult recommendation for anyone who relies on standard Google apps or expects a simple out-of-the-box experience.

Does the Vivo S50 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import built for the Chinese market. You must rely on whatever limited coverage the individual shop provides, and all repairs will be paid.

How does the battery perform for daily use?

Owners report that the huge 6500 mAh battery lasts very well throughout the day. When you do need to recharge, the 90W fast charging system provides a quick way to get back to full power.

Is this phone good for gaming?

Yes, the phone is excellent for gaming. It features the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset, which provides flagship-class smoothness. Owners have reported that the device does not suffer from performance throttling during long gaming sessions.

Will I have trouble using Google apps on this device?

Yes, you will likely face friction. The device runs the Chinese version of OriginOS, which does not have Google Play pre-installed. Owners have noted that Google apps may not work as expected compared to standard global mobile versions.
