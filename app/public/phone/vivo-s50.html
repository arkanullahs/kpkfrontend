Is the Vivo S50 worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz AMOLED display with vivid colors
  • 6500 mAh battery with 90 W fast charging
  • Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 delivers strong performance
  • IR blaster for remote control

What is not

  • Heavy bloatware slows down notifications
  • 8 MP ultrawide camera feels low‑end
  • Phone gets hot under sustained load
  • No official Vivo warranty in Bangladesh

Buy it if If you want a high‑end display, fast charging and strong main camera and can live with a shop warranty, it suits power users who game and stream.

Skip it if If you rely on Google services, need an official warranty, or want a reliable ultrawide camera, look elsewhere.

Why we say that

The 6.59‑inch AMOLED panel at 120 Hz looks vivid and bright, and the 90 W charger refills the 6500 mAh battery in under an hour. Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 with 12 GB RAM handles games at 120 fps, though the metal chassis can get warm after long play. The 50 MP main camera with OIS and 3× telephoto produces solid photos, yet the 8 MP ultrawide feels low‑end.

Software runs OriginOS or Funtouch depending on the version, both loaded with bloatware that slows notifications. No official Vivo warranty is available; you rely on the shop’s limited coverage and pay for any repairs. Overall it feels premium, but the trade‑offs matter for users who need reliable updates and a strong ultrawide lens.
