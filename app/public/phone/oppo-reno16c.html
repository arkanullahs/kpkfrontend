OPPO Reno16c Is it worth buying?

What works well4

  • 6.57 inch 120Hz AMOLED display
  • 7,000 mAh battery with 80W fast charge
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS and telephoto
  • Dimensity 7300 handles gaming smoothly

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Must verify IMEI and BTRC registration
  • Medium amount of pre installed ads
  • Indian charger plug may need adapter

Why we say that

The 6.57 inch AMOLED panel reaches 1400 nits and runs at 120Hz, so scrolling and media feel smooth. The 7000 mAh cell paired with an 80W charger keeps the phone alive through a full day of heavy use, though the bright display can drain it faster. The Dimensity 7300 chipset handles games and multitasking without stutter.

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS, 3.5x telephoto and ultrawide lens captures detailed photos in good light, and the same resolution selfie camera is sharp. Owners complain about the lack of an official warranty, the need to confirm IMEI and BTRC registration, and the medium amount of pre installed ads. The charger plug follows the Indian market, so an adapter may be required.

How many Android updates will the Reno16c receive?

The phone ships with Android 16 and ColorOS 16. Flagship devices from the brand typically get three to four major OS upgrades, so you can expect a similar update cadence for this model.

Will the 7,000 mAh battery last a full day of typical use?

The 7,000 mAh cell is large enough to power a day of browsing, video streaming and moderate gaming. Brightness at 1400 nits and a 120Hz screen can shorten runtime, but overall battery life is strong.

Is there any official warranty for this phone in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray import units built for India, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop provides its own warranty and any repair costs are paid by the owner.

Will the phone work on Bangladeshi 5G networks and is the IMEI safe?

The Reno16c supports 5G and the global band list includes the frequencies used in Bangladesh. You should still confirm the IMEI and BTRC registration status with the seller before purchase.
