Is the Tecno Pova 7 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6000 mAh battery capacity
  • Extremely bright 4500 nits AMOLED display
  • Powerful Dimensity 8350 Ultimate chipset
  • Smooth 144Hz refresh rate

What is not

  • High levels of bloatware and advertisements
  • Only shop warranty available
  • Slow software update support
  • Limited to 1 to 2 OS updates

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize raw hardware performance and battery life over software cleanliness and official brand support.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you require a reliable official warranty or dislike dealing with pre-installed advertisements and bloatware.

Why we say that

Because this phone is currently only available through gray-import channels, you will not receive a brand warranty. Instead, you are limited to a shop warranty, making it essential to verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your 32,990 taka. Daily usage is defined by the HiOS 15 software, which comes with a high amount of bloatware and advertisements.

While the Dimensity 8350 Ultimate chipset provides strong performance, the software experience is hampered by slow update cycles, with only 1 to 2 OS updates promised. This phone is a hardware-first device that requires a buyer who is comfortable managing software clutter and navigating the risks of unofficial imports.
