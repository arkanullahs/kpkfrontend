Is the Tecno Pova 7 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • Very bright 4500 nits peak display
  • Large 6000 mAh battery
  • Fast 70W charging
  • High 144Hz refresh rate

What is not

  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • Slow software updates
  • No official brand warranty

Buy it if You want a 5G phone with a huge battery and a very bright screen for gaming.

Skip it if You hate seeing ads in your software or want an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

You get a very bright 144Hz AMOLED screen and a large 6000 mAh battery for long days. The Dimensity 8350 Ultimate chipset handles heavy tasks well. However, the user experience is hampered by high levels of bloatware and ads.

Owners will find the software updates to be slow. You must be careful with the purchase because only gray import stock is available. This means you get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty.

You need to verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying ৳32,990.
