Is the Motorola G86 Power worth buying?

What is good

  • 6720 mAh battery lasts two to three days
  • 4500 nit bright 6.67 inch P OLED display
  • 50 MP main camera bright daylight photos
  • Rugged IP68/69 vegan leather build

What is not

  • 30 W charging takes about one and a half hours
  • Low light camera and video audio perform poorly
  • Only one guaranteed Android OS upgrade
  • 5G use heats device and drains battery

Buy it if If you want a big battery, bright screen and rugged build at a low gray import price, this phone fits.

Skip it if If you need fast charging, strong low light camera or long software support, look elsewhere.

Why we say that

Its 6.67 inch P OLED panel reaches 4500 nits and runs at 120 Hz, so outdoor visibility is excellent even in bright sunlight. The 6720 mAh battery feels massive and holds up well, though the 30 W charger needs about one and a half hours to fill it. The Dimensity 7300 chipset with 8 GB RAM handles everyday apps smoothly, but games need low settings to stay stable and the phone can heat up when 5G is active.

The 50 MP main sensor captures vibrant daylight photos, yet low light shots suffer from weak AI processing and video audio is often too quiet. Build quality is solid with IP68/69 rating and a vegan leather back, but the device only receives one guaranteed Android upgrade and the warranty is from the gray import seller, not Motorola.

How long will the battery last in daily use?

The 6720 mAh cell can keep most users alive for two to three days of normal use, but after system updates some owners notice faster drain, especially when 5G is turned on.

Can I play games smoothly on this phone?

The Dimensity 7300 with 8 GB RAM runs everyday apps fine, but games need low graphics settings to stay stable and the device may heat up during intense sessions or when 5G is active.
