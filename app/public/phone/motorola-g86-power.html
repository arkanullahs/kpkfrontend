Is the Motorola G86 Power worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.67 inch 120Hz P‑OLED with 4,500‑nit peak brightness
  • 6,720 mAh battery lasting two to three days
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS for daylight photos
  • Rugged IP68/69 build with vegan leather back

What is not

  • 30W charging takes about one and a half hours
  • Low‑light camera performance is poor
  • Only one guaranteed Android OS upgrade
  • 5G use causes heat and extra battery drain

Buy it if Buy it if you want a bright, large‑battery phone and can accept a gray‑import warranty with limited updates.

Skip it if Skip it if you need strong low‑light camera performance, fast charging, or an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Daily use feels smooth thanks to the Dimensity 7300 and 8 GB RAM, and the 50 MP main camera produces vibrant daylight photos. Low‑light shots are disappointing and the AI tuning feels mediocre. Charging at 30 W takes about one and a half hours, which feels slow for such a large battery.

When you enable 5G the phone can heat up and drain faster, a point many owners note after recent system updates. The build is solid with IP68/69 protection and a vegan‑leather back, yet the promised software upgrades are weak, with only one guaranteed Android version upgrade. Overall it offers excellent value at the unofficial price of ৳30,999 if you can live with these compromises.
