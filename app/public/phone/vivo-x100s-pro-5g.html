Vivo X100s Pro 5G Is it worth buying?

The Vivo X100s Pro 5G is a powerful device that relies on the Dimensity 9300+ chipset to handle demanding tasks.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs

  • Very bright 3000 nits display
  • Large 5400 mAh battery capacity
  • Capable 50MP main camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs3

  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Medium levels of bloat and ads

Why we say that

You get a large 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED display that reaches 3000 nits of peak brightness, making it very visible outdoors. The 5400 mAh battery provides enough capacity for heavy daily use. Photography is handled by a 50MP main sensor with OIS and a 4.3x telephoto lens.

You should be aware that this phone is only available as a gray import in Bangladesh. This means there is no manufacturer warranty support regardless of what you might expect from the brand. You must rely entirely on the specific shop for any repair promises, and all service work will be paid.

Users should also check the IMEI and BTRC status with their seller before finalizing the purchase. The software experience includes medium levels of bloat and ads, which is a common point of frustration for owners.

Does the Vivo X100s Pro 5G come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as a gray import. There is no manufacturer warranty provided by the brand in Bangladesh. You must rely on the warranty promised by the specific shop, and all repairs will be paid.

How is the battery life on this device?

The phone is equipped with a 5400 mAh battery. This capacity is designed to support the high-performance Dimensity 9300+ chipset and the bright 120Hz display throughout your daily usage.

What should I know about the software and updates?

The phone runs Android 14 with OriginOS 4 or Funtouch OS for global models. You can expect medium levels of bloat and ads. Updates are limited to 3 OS versions on upper models and 2 on budget models.

Is this phone suitable for mobile photography?

Yes, it features a 50MP main camera with a 1/0.98 inch sensor and OIS. It also includes a 4.3x telephoto lens and an ultrawide camera, making it a versatile tool for various lighting conditions.
