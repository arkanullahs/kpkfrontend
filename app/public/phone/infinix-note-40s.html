Is the Infinix Note 40S worth buying?

What is good

  • Stunning 6.78 inch curved AMOLED display
  • 1300 nits peak brightness
  • Large 108MP main camera sensor
  • 5000 mAh battery lasting over a day

What is not

  • Unnatural camera colors and soft details
  • Gaming drops frames and throttles
  • XOS adds many ads and bloat
  • No 3.5 mm headphone jack

Buy it if If you want a big bright screen and plenty of storage at a low price.

Skip it if If you need smooth gaming, accurate camera colors, or a headphone jack.

Why we say that

At ৳28,199 it is the cheapest official warranty option for the 8 GB 256 GB variant, and the 6.78 inch display really shines with 1300 nits peak brightness and a smooth 120 Hz refresh. The Helio G99 chipset handles everyday tasks without lag, but under sustained gaming the phone heats up and frame rates drop, especially in titles like PUBG. Camera reviewers note the 108 MP sensor captures detail but the colors look overly boosted and exposure can be inconsistent, making low light shots look unnatural.

The 5000 mAh battery comfortably lasts a day of mixed use, yet the 33 W charger is only average and the included charger does not support wireless charging. Software runs Android 14 with XOS 14, which adds pre installed apps and frequent ads, and the device promises up to two major Android upgrades. Overall the phone feels light at 176 g and includes FM radio, but it lacks a 3.5 mm headphone jack, which some users miss.

How long does the battery last in real use?

The 5000 mAh battery typically gives you a little more than one full day of mixed usage, including browsing, social media and video playback. Charging with the supplied 33 W charger restores the charge in about an hour, but it is not exceptionally fast.

Will this phone handle heavy games like PUBG?

The Helio G99 can run PUBG at low settings, but during long sessions the device heats up and the frame rate becomes choppy. Expect occasional drops in stability and reduced performance after extended play.

Are the camera colors realistic in low light?

The 108 MP sensor captures detail, but reviewers report that colors appear overly boosted and exposure handling is weak, especially in low light scenes. Pictures may look unnatural without post processing.

What warranty and software updates does it get?

An official Bangladesh unit comes with the brand’s warranty and can be serviced through Infinix’s network. The phone runs Android 14 with XOS 14 and promises up to two major Android version upgrades.
