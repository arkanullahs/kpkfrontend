Is the Infinix Note 40S worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.78 inch curved AMOLED with 1300 nits
  • 120Hz refresh rate for smooth scrolling
  • 5000 mAh battery lasting over a day
  • 33W fast charging

What is not

  • Camera colors look overly boosted
  • Gaming gets choppy after long sessions
  • No 3.5mm headphone jack
  • Wireless charger not included

Buy it if Buy it if you value a large, bright display and solid day‑long battery at a low official‑warranty price.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable gaming performance or accurate camera colors.

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch curved AMOLED shines at 1300 nits and runs smooth at 120Hz, making media consumption pleasant. The 8GB RAM and 256GB storage feel generous for everyday apps and media. Battery life is reliable for more than a day, and the 33W charger refills it quickly, though the charger itself is not included.

The phone runs Android 14 with XOS 14, but expect pre‑installed apps and ads that can feel intrusive. Camera results are mixed; the 108MP sensor produces bright pictures but colors are overly boosted and details can be soft, especially in low light. Gaming is acceptable for casual titles but heavy games like PUBG become choppy after a while due to thermal throttling.

The lack of a headphone jack and the presence of wireless charging without a supplied pad are additional trade‑offs.
