Infinix Note 40S Is it worth buying?

Display quality is the only thing that truly shines on the Infinix Note 40S.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
If you want a large bright display and big storage at a low price and can tolerate average performance and camera quirks, this phone may suit you.
Consider alternatives if
If you need reliable gaming, accurate camera colours, or an official warranty without extra risk, look elsewhere.

What works well4

  • 6.78 inch curved AMOLED with 120 Hz
  • 1300 nits peak brightness
  • 108 MP main camera sensor
  • 5000 mAh battery lasting over a day

Reported trade-offs4

  • Unnatural camera colours and soft details
  • Gaming drops frames and heats up
  • Wireless charger not included
  • No headphone jack

Why we say that

The 6.78‑inch curved AMOLED with 120 Hz refresh and 1300‑nit peak makes media consumption pleasant, and the 5000 mAh battery usually lasts a little over a day. The phone feels light at 176 g and offers a massive 108 MP main sensor, but owners complain about colour that looks overly boosted, soft details and poor exposure handling. Daily performance is acceptable, yet gaming such as PUBG becomes choppy and the device throttles when it gets hot.

Wireless charging works but the charger is not included, and the lack of a 3.5 mm jack is noticeable. Software runs Android 14 with XOS 14, but it brings typical Infinix bloat and ads, and only two major Android upgrades are promised. Official warranty applies only to the ৳28,199 listing; cheaper gray‑import options have no manufacturer cover and repairs cost extra.

Only the unit sold for ৳28,199 includes the brand warranty. The cheaper ৳27,999 listings are gray imports that have no manufacturer cover and any repair at a service centre will be charged as a paid service.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery provides reliable more than one day endurance for most users. It charges at 33 W, which owners describe as average, and it also supports wireless charging, though the charger is not supplied in the box.

Is the phone good for gaming like PUBG?

Daily use feels smooth, but PUBG becomes choppy and the device shows thermal throttling on long sessions. Owners report that gaming stability drops and the phone cannot maintain high frame rates for extended periods.

Will the camera work well in low light?

The phone has a 108 MP sensor with an f/1.9 lens, but users say the colours look overly boosted, details are soft and exposure handling is poor, making low‑light photos disappointing.
