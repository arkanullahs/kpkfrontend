Is the Vivo Y21d worth buying?

What is good

  • Long lasting 6500 mAh battery
  • 44W fast charging
  • IP68/69 water and dust resistance
  • 90Hz refresh rate

What is not

  • Frequent lag and stuttering
  • HD+ low resolution display
  • Outdated 5 MP front camera

Buy it if If you need a rugged phone with a huge battery and can accept slow performance, this model may suit you.

Skip it if If you expect smooth gaming, a high resolution screen, or a good selfie camera, look elsewhere.

Why we say that

Its 6500 mAh cell can easily last two days with moderate screen time and the 44W charger brings it back to full in under an hour. The phone is built to survive drops and water thanks to IP68/69 protection, and the 90Hz panel makes scrolling feel smoother even though the resolution stays at HD+. The Unisoc T7225 chipset struggles with multitasking and games, causing noticeable lag and stuttering.

The 50MP main sensor produces average pictures and the 5MP selfie camera is far behind current standards. Funtouch OS 15 runs clean but still shows removable ads and bloatware. In Bangladesh the cheapest gray import costs ৳18,490 but carries no official warranty, while the official version with warranty starts at ৳25,899.

Overall the phone offers great endurance and ruggedness but falls short on performance, display sharpness and camera quality.
