Vivo Y21d Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can stay on for days without charging, the Vivo Y21d delivers that.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize battery life and ruggedness over speed and selfie quality.
Consider alternatives if

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 6500 mAh battery lasting multiple days
  • IP68/69 water and dust resistance
  • 44 W fast charging
  • 50 MP main camera sensor

Reported trade-offs4

  • Frequent system lag and stuttering
  • HD+ low‑resolution display
  • 5 MP front camera is outdated
  • No 3.5 mm headphone jack

Why we say that

The 6500 mAh battery and 44 W fast charging let you go from zero to full in under an hour, and the IP68/69 rating protects it from water, dust and drops. However, the Unisoc T7225 chipset struggles with everyday tasks, causing noticeable lag and stuttering that owners repeatedly mention. The 6.68‑inch IPS LCD runs at 90 Hz but only offers an HD+ resolution, which looks blurry compared with modern screens at this price.

The 50 MP rear sensor captures decent photos in good light, yet reviewers call it mediocre and the 5 MP front camera is far behind current standards. Overall the phone feels solid and lasts long, but its performance, display sharpness and selfie quality make it a poor value, especially when a newer Y31d is cheaper.

How long will the battery last in daily use?

The 6500 mAh cell is rated as industry‑leading, giving you multi‑day endurance on moderate use. Heavy gaming or bright screen settings will reduce that time, but most users report lasting two to three days without a charge.

Can the Vivo Y21d handle games smoothly?

The phone uses the Unisoc T7225 chipset, which owners say causes frequent lag and stuttering. Gaming performance is rated low, so expect choppy frames in most modern titles.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The rear 50 MP sensor can capture decent images in good light, but reviews call its overall quality mediocre. In low light the pictures become noisy, and the 5 MP front camera is far too weak for selfies.

What warranty and software updates does the phone receive?

An official Bangladesh unit means the brand’s warranty and service network apply. Budget models like this receive two major Android updates, and the Funtouch OS includes removable bloatware with medium ad frequency.
