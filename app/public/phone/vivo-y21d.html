Is the Vivo Y21d worth buying?

What is good

  • 6500 mAh battery lasts multiple days
  • IP68/69 water and dust resistance
  • 44 W fast charging
  • FM radio support

What is not

  • Frequent system lag and stuttering
  • HD+ low‑resolution display
  • 5 MP front camera is outdated
  • No 3.5 mm headphone jack

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, rugged build, and are okay with a gray‑import without manufacturer warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you want smooth gaming, high‑resolution screen, or a strong selfie camera.

Why we say that

The 6500 mAh cell is rated as industry‑leading and owners give it a 9.8 rating, so you can expect the phone to stay alive through two or three days of typical use. Build quality is a strong point, with IP68/69 protection and military‑grade drop resistance that many users praise. The 44 W charger refills the large battery quickly, and the 6.68‑inch 90 Hz panel is bright thanks to a 1000‑nit peak, though its HD+ resolution feels low for the price.

Software runs Android 15 with Funtouch 15, but removable bloatware and medium ads add to the feeling of sluggishness.
