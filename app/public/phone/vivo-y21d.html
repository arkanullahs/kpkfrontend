Vivo Y21d Is it worth buying?

The Vivo Y21d can stay alive for days on a single charge, but you will constantly fight lag and a blurry selfie camera.

What works well4

  • 6500 mAh battery lasts multiple days
  • 44 W fast charging refills quickly
  • IP68/69 water and dust resistance
  • 50 MP main camera captures detailed photos

Reported trade-offs3

  • Frequent lag and stuttering in daily use
  • HD+ display feels low resolution
  • 5 MP front camera is outdated

Why we say that

Its 6500 mAh battery and 44 W fast charger give you multi‑day endurance and quick top‑ups. The phone is sealed to IP68/69 standards, so water, dust and drops are not a daily worry. The 50 MP main sensor can capture detailed pictures in good light, yet the HD+ 6.68‑inch screen looks soft compared with the price.

The Unisoc T7225 chipset struggles with everyday apps and games, leading to noticeable stutter. Software runs on Android 15 with Funtouch 15, which is clean but still carries removable bloatware. The cheapest listing at ৳18,490 is a gray import that lacks any manufacturer warranty, while the official‑warranty version costs ৳25,899.

Overall the phone suits users who need rugged durability and long battery life and can tolerate slow performance and a low‑resolution display.
