Why we say that

The 6.56 inch IPS LCD is adequate for watching videos and browsing, while the Unisoc T606 chipset with 4GB RAM handles everyday apps without lag. Owners like the large 5000 mAh battery and the convenience of a headphone jack and FM radio. The camera scores only five out of ten and feels just okay for the price, and some users notice a small gap around the rear camera module.

Software updates are rare and the phone is already three years old, so future updates are unlikely. Overall it is a budget‑friendly device that works well for basic tasks but shows its age in performance and software support.

How many Android updates will the Benco V91 receive?
