OnePlus Nord CE3 Is it worth buying?

If you can live with a weak battery and no official warranty, the OnePlus Nord CE3 gives you a large 120Hz display and fast charging for a low price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a smooth large screen and quick charging at a low price and can accept limited battery life and no official warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need long‑lasting battery life or official after‑sales support.

Reported trade-offs4

  • Battery drains quickly
  • No official brand warranty
  • Software updates likely ended
  • Green line rumors exist

Why we say that

The 6.7‑inch Fluid AMOLED screen is bright and smooth, making media and scrolling pleasant. Performance from the Snapdragon 782G feels solid for everyday apps and moderate gaming, and the 80W charger refills the 5000 mAh cell quickly. The 50MP main sensor with OIS delivers decent photos in good light, and the phone supports 5G and an IR blaster.

Owners complain that the battery does not last a full day, especially after Android updates, and that software updates are likely finished after three years. There is no brand‑backed warranty; you must rely on the shop’s warranty and verify IMEI/BTRC status before buying. Overall it is a budget‑friendly option if you prioritize display and speed over endurance and official support.

OnePlus Nord CE3: what buyers ask us

What is the OnePlus Nord CE3 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for OnePlus Nord CE3 in Bangladesh was ৳27,990, checked 24 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery feels weak in real use. Owners report it often cannot last a full day, especially after Android updates, so you may need to charge it before evening.

Will the phone receive Android updates?

The phone was released in 2023 and is now three years old. Expect short or no remaining software‑update life, as OnePlus usually stops updates for older models.

Is there an official OnePlus warranty in Bangladesh?

No official brand warranty is available. Only shop warranty is offered for gray‑import units, so you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchase.

Is the camera good for low‑light photos?

The 50MP sensor with OIS provides decent image quality in good light. In low‑light situations the results are acceptable but not as strong as flagship cameras, so expect moderate performance.
