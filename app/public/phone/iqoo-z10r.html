iQOO Z10R Is it worth buying?

The iQOO Z10R is a gray-import device that offers a premium display and build quality at the cost of long-term software support and storage speed.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize a high-quality display and rugged water resistance over long-term software updates and fast storage speeds.
Consider alternatives if
You need a phone with official brand warranty support or require fast storage for heavy multitasking and gaming.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Exceptional IP68/69 water and dust resistance
  • Bright 1800 nits quad-curved AMOLED display
  • Solid 8 hour plus screen on time
  • Capable 50MP main camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs4

  • Slow UFS 2.2 storage impacts performance
  • Limited two year Android update support
  • Slow 44W charging for a 5700 mAh battery
  • Useless depth sensor and no ultrawide lens

Why we say that

Owners appreciate the 6.77-inch quad-curved AMOLED screen and the exceptional IP68/69 water resistance, which is rare at this price point. However, living with the phone reveals frustrations regarding the outdated UFS 2.2 storage, which causes slow app loading and background rendering times. The 44W charging speed feels sluggish for a 2025 release, and the 5700 mAh battery, while providing solid 8-hour screen-on time, takes longer to top up than competitors.

Users are particularly vocal about the disappointing two-year OS update limit and the inclusion of a useless depth sensor. Because every unit in Bangladesh is a gray import, you must verify if you are receiving the OriginOS China version or the Funtouch global version before paying. The lack of official brand warranty means you are entirely dependent on the shop for support.

iQOO Z10R: what buyers ask us

What is the iQOO Z10R price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for iQOO Z10R in Bangladesh was ৳26,490, checked 17 Oct 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does the iQOO Z10R come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there is no official brand-backed warranty for this phone in Bangladesh. Every listing we have recorded is a gray import, meaning you must rely solely on the shop for any warranty claims.

How long will the iQOO Z10R receive Android software updates?

The phone is limited to only two major Android OS upgrades. Many owners have expressed disappointment with this short support window, as they believe a device released in 2025 should receive at least three years of updates.

Is the iQOO Z10R suitable for heavy gaming?

While the Dimensity 7400 chipset can maintain a stable 60fps in BGMI, the outdated UFS 2.2 storage slows down app loading and background rendering. It is not the best choice if you require high-speed performance for intensive tasks.

What should I check before buying this phone from a local seller?

You must confirm whether the unit is the China-market version with OriginOS or the global version with Funtouch OS. Additionally, always verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller to ensure the device is legitimate.
