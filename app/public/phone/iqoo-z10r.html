Is the iQOO Z10R worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.77 inch 120 Hz AMOLED display
  • 5,700 mAh battery with 44 W charging
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • UFS 2.2 storage slows app loading
  • Only two years of OS updates
  • Depth sensor adds no value

Buy it if Buy if you want a large bright screen and decent camera at a low price.

Skip it if Skip if you need fast storage, long software support or an official warranty.

Why we say that

The 5,700 mAh battery easily lasts a full day of mixed use, but the 44 W charger takes longer than newer fast chargers to refill. The Dimensity 7400 chipset delivers decent performance, keeping BGMI at a stable 60 fps, yet the UFS 2.2 storage makes app launches feel slower than expected. The 50 MP main camera with optical image stabilization captures clear photos, but the lack of an ultrawide lens and a filler depth sensor limit versatility.

Software runs Android 15 with Funtouch 15, includes medium bloat and promises only two major OS upgrades, which many owners find short. Because every unit is a gray import, there is no official brand warranty, only the shop’s limited cover and paid repairs.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 5,700 mAh cell can keep the phone running for more than eight hours of screen on time under mixed usage, and it usually lasts a full day without charging. The 44 W charger refills the pack, but it is slower than newer 80 W chargers.

Is the phone suitable for gaming?

The Dimensity 7400 processor keeps games like BGMI at a steady 60 fps and the 120 Hz screen shows smooth motion. However, the UFS 2.2 storage can cause longer loading times, so heavy titles feel a bit laggy.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP sensor with a 1/1.95 inch size and f/1.8 aperture plus optical image stabilization captures usable shots in dim environments. The lack of an ultrawide lens and a filler depth sensor means you cannot rely on extra angles in low light.

What warranty and software support does the phone have?

All units are gray imports, so there is no official brand warranty in Bangladesh. Shops offer their own limited warranty and any repair is paid. The phone ships with Android 15 and Funtouch 15 and will receive up to two major OS upgrades, giving about two years of software support.
