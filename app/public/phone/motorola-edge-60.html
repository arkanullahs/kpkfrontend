Is the Motorola Edge 60 worth buying?

What is good

  • Very reliable all day battery life
  • Exceptional IP68 and IP69 durability
  • High 4500 nits peak brightness
  • Clean software with low bloat

What is not

  • Inconsistent and unreliable camera processing
  • No Always On Display support
  • Underwhelming gaming performance for the price
  • No wireless charging

Buy it if You want a durable phone with a great screen and clean software.

Skip it if You need a reliable camera or high performance for gaming.

Why we say that

This phone feels great in the hand and has a very bright screen, but the actual experience is mixed. Owners are frustrated with the camera software, reporting that nearly half of their photos are blurry, soft, or noisy. The camera app also suffers from lag.

While the battery is reliable for a full day, the Dimensity 7300 chipset feels underwhelming for the price, especially for gaming. You also lose wireless charging compared to the Edge 50 and there is no Always On Display. Since these are Indian imports, you have no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh.

You must rely on whatever the shop promises for repairs.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone has no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh because every listing is a gray import. You only get the warranty provided by the shop, and all repairs are paid.

How is the battery life and charging?

The 5200 mAh battery provides solid all day performance and is considered very reliable by owners. It supports 68W charging, but wireless charging has been removed from this model.

Is the camera good for photography?

The hardware is decent, but the software is unreliable. Owners report that the camera app lags and many photos come out blurry, soft, and noisy.

How long will this phone get software updates?

The phone comes with Android 15 and is promised up to 3 major Android upgrades. However, the brand has historically been weak with providing updates.
