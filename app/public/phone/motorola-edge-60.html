Is the Motorola Edge 60 worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 4500 nits P-OLED display
  • Strong 5200 mAh battery
  • Clean near‑stock Android 15
  • 12GB RAM for smooth daily use

What is not

  • Camera software produces blurry noisy photos
  • No always‑on display support
  • No wireless charging
  • Gaming performance below price

Buy it if Buy if you want a bright screen, solid battery life and a clean Android experience at a mid‑range price.

Skip it if Skip if you need reliable low‑light camera quality or top‑tier gaming performance.

Why we say that

The 6.67 inch P-OLED panel reaches 4500 nits, making outdoor use easy, but the lack of an always‑on display feels dated. The 12GB RAM and Dimensity 7300 chipset handle everyday apps smoothly, yet gaming performance falls short of the price. Camera hardware is solid with a 50MP main sensor, OIS and 3x tele, but software processing often yields blurry, noisy shots and the camera app can lag.

Software is near‑stock Android 15 and promises up to three major upgrades, though past updates have been slow. The phone arrives as a gray‑import, so warranty is from the shop, not Motorola, and you must verify IMEI and BTRC status. Compared to the Edge 70 at a similar price, the Edge 60 offers fewer strengths for the cost.
