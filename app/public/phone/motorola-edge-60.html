Motorola Edge 60 Is it worth buying?

If you need a bright, durable phone that lasts all day, the Motorola Edge 60 can deliver that, but expect camera and gaming compromises.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value a bright, rugged screen and long battery life more than top‑tier gaming or flawless camera results.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable low‑light photography, strong gaming performance, or an official manufacturer warranty.

What works well4

  • 4500‑nit peak brightness on a 120 Hz OLED
  • 5200 mAh battery with 68 W fast charging
  • IP68/69 water and dust resistance
  • 12 GB RAM with ample storage options

Reported trade-offs4

  • Inconsistent camera processing and app lag
  • No wireless charging or always‑on display
  • Gaming and performance feel underwhelming
  • Only shop warranty, no official support

Why we say that

The 6.67‑inch P‑OLED panel reaches 4500 nits and runs at 120 Hz, making media consumption easy even in sunlight. The 5200 mAh battery paired with 68 W fast charging gives solid all‑day endurance, and the IP68/69 rating means it survives rain and dust. Performance feels adequate for everyday apps thanks to the Dimensity 7300 and 12 GB RAM, but heavy games and high‑frame titles feel sluggish.

The camera hardware is respectable with a 50 MP main sensor, OIS and 3× tele, yet software processing often produces blurry or noisy shots and the camera app can lag. Software is near‑stock Android 15 with up to three major upgrades, though past updates have been slow. The phone arrives as a gray‑import with only the shop’s warranty, so repairs are out‑of‑pocket.

Does the Motorola Edge 60 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, every listing is a gray‑import built for India, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop may offer its own limited warranty, but any repairs will be paid by you.

How many Android updates will the phone receive?
