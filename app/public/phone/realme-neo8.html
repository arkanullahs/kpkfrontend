Is the Realme Neo8 worth buying?

What is good

  • 8000 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • 165 Hz AMOLED display with 6500 nits peak
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 delivers fast performance
  • 50 MP main camera with 3.5× telephoto

What is not

  • Chinese ROM causes notification delays
  • Phone gets very hot during gaming
  • No official brand warranty in Bangladesh
  • Ultrawide camera quality is poor

Buy it if Buy it if you want flagship‑level specs at a sub‑60 k price and can accept a gray‑import warranty and occasional software annoyances.

Skip it if Skip it if you need official after‑sales support, a cool device during gaming, or a clean software experience.

Why we say that

Its Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chip and 12 GB RAM make everyday tasks feel instant and gaming runs smoothly until the metal body starts to feel hot. The 6.78‑inch 165 Hz AMOLED screen is bright enough for outdoor use and the 8000 mAh battery easily lasts a day and a half to two days, while 80 W charging refills it quickly. The main camera produces vibrant photos and the 3.5× telephoto works well, yet the ultrawide lens is soft and selfies look over‑processed.

Owners praise the display and battery but complain about notification delays, heavy bloatware, and noticeable heat during long gaming sessions. The phone arrives without an official Realme warranty in Bangladesh, so any repair will be paid and covered only by the shop’s own promise.
