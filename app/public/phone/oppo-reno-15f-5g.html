Is the OPPO Reno 15F 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 7,000 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • 6.57‑inch AMOLED display with 120 Hz refresh
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • IP68/69 water resistance and aluminum frame

What is not

  • Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 is outdated for gaming
  • Lockscreen ads and bloatware in ColorOS
  • Price is high for 2023 chipset

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize long battery life and solid build over cutting‑edge performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need top gaming performance or want the best price‑to‑spec ratio.

Why we say that

Its 7,000 mAh battery and 80 percent fast charge are praised, while daily tasks run smoothly on the Snapdragon 6 Gen 1. The 6.57‑inch AMOLED panel looks bright thanks to 1,400 nits peak brightness, and the 50 MP main sensor with OIS provides decent photos for the price. However owners complain about the outdated chipset for gaming, lockscreen advertisements, and a price that seems high for 2023 hardware.

Water resistance and a sturdy aluminum frame are real positives, yet the overall value is questioned compared with newer rivals.
