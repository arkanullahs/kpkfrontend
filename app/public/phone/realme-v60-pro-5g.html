Is the Realme V60 Pro 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 5600 mAh battery with fast 45 W charging
  • IP68 water and dust resistance
  • 120 Hz refresh rate on a large screen
  • 12 GB RAM with up to 512 GB storage

What is not

  • 720p resolution display feels low
  • Dimensity 6300 struggles in hot weather
  • Bootloader restrictions limit custom testing
  • No official brand warranty, only shop warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you want long battery life, solid build and 5G at a mid‑range price.

Skip it if Skip it if you need top‑tier gaming performance, high‑resolution display or an official manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.67" IPS LCD offers a smooth 120 Hz experience but only at 720p, which many find underwhelming. Performance from the Dimensity 6300 and 12 GB RAM is adequate for everyday tasks, yet owners report the chipset struggles when it gets hot and the cooling system feels weak. The 50 MP main camera captures decent photos, but tuning is average and low‑light results are only moderate.

Software runs Android 14 with Realme UI 5.0, bringing medium‑high bloat and ads, and you can expect roughly two to three major OS upgrades. Warranty is limited to a shop guarantee, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The V60 Pro carries a 5600 mAh cell and owners rate its endurance at 7.5 out of 10. In daily mixed use it can comfortably last a full day, and the 45 W charger refills it quickly.

Is the Realme V60 Pro 5G suitable for gaming?

The phone uses a Dimensity 6300 chipset with 12 GB RAM, which handles casual games fine. However owners give gaming a 5.0 rating and report cooling problems in hot weather, so demanding titles may lag.
