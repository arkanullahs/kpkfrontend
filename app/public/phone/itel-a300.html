itel A300+ Is it worth buying?

The itel A300+ is a budget device that prioritizes massive battery capacity over overall system speed and software quality.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a device with exceptional battery endurance for basic tasks and do not mind dealing with software ads.
Consider alternatives if
You require a device for gaming, photography, or a clean software experience without constant advertisements.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Massive 7000 mAh battery capacity
  • Smooth 120Hz display refresh rate
  • Includes a dedicated headphone jack
  • Features a convenient IR blaster

Reported trade-offs4

  • High volume of ads and bloatware
  • Poor sustained performance and throttling
  • Mediocre 13MP single camera output
  • Plastic build with no IP rating

Why we say that

Living with this phone means dealing with a high volume of ads and bloatware pre-installed on the Android 16 Go edition software. While the 7000 mAh battery provides excellent endurance, owners report that the Helio G81 chipset struggles with sustained loads, leading to noticeable throttling during gaming or heavy tasks. The 13MP main camera is described as mediocre, and the 120Hz IPS LCD display is considered modest in its output.

Build quality is entirely plastic with no IP rating for water or dust resistance. Owners have given low ratings for ease of use and build quality, reflecting a user experience that feels unrefined. If you prioritize battery life above all else, this device may serve your needs, but those expecting a smooth, ad-free experience will likely find the software and performance limitations frustrating.

Does the itel A300+ have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

Yes, the itel A300+ is available as an official BD unit. This means you are covered by the brand's own warranty and their service network when you purchase from official channels.

Is the itel A300+ suitable for mobile gaming?

The phone is not recommended for gaming. Owners report that the Helio G81 chipset is slow and suffers from poor sustained performance, which leads to throttling when the device is under load.

How is the camera performance on this device?

The camera performance is considered mediocre. The device features a single 13MP main camera and an 8MP selfie camera, which owners have rated poorly for both photography and video recording.

Will the itel A300+ receive regular software updates?

The device runs on itelOS based on Android 16 Go edition. According to the available information, the phone rarely receives any software updates, and users should expect a high amount of pre-installed bloatware and ads.
