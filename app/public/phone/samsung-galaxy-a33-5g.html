Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy A33 5G Is it worth buying?

The Samsung Galaxy A33 5G is a 2022 device that now faces significant limitations due to its age.

Choose it if
You specifically need a budget Samsung device with an AMOLED screen and do not mind the lack of future software updates.
Consider alternatives if
You want a device that will receive long-term software support or if you need high performance for modern mobile gaming.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • Vibrant 6.4 inch Super AMOLED 90Hz display
  • Main camera includes OIS for stability
  • Clean software experience with low bloat
  • Large 5000 mAh battery capacity

Reported trade-offs

  • Four years old with limited update life
  • Slow 25W charging speed
  • Exynos 1280 chipset shows its age

Why we say that

While the Super AMOLED display and OIS camera provide a decent experience, the hardware is four years old. You should be aware that this device is nearing the end of its software support cycle, meaning you should expect short or no remaining major Android updates. The Exynos 1280 chipset powers the device, but it is not designed for modern heavy tasks.

Battery life is supported by a 5000 mAh cell, though charging is limited to 25W. Because this phone was released in 2022, it is important to consider that newer models offer better longevity for your money. The software experience remains clean with low bloat and ads, but the lack of future-proofing is a major concern for anyone planning to keep their phone for several years.

Read full reasoning

If you choose this device, you are buying older technology that may struggle to keep up with current app requirements and security standards in the coming months.
