Vivo X500 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you can live without Google apps preinstalled, the Vivo X500 Pro gives a bright screen and fast charging at a low gray import price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a high refresh display, strong camera and fast charging and can accept a Chinese UI without Google.
Consider alternatives if
You need official brand warranty or a Google ready Android experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 6.36 inch 144Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • 90W fast charging for large battery
  • 50MP main camera with OIS
  • Dimensity 9600 Pro handles games well

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • OriginOS lacks Google services and Bangla
  • Updates come only from Chinese servers
  • Shop warranty may require paid repairs

Why we say that

The 6.36 inch LTPO AMOLED panel reaches 4500 nits peak and refreshes at 144Hz, making everyday scrolling feel smooth. The Dimensity 9600 Pro handles most games at high settings without stutter, and the 90W charger fills the 6510 mAh battery in under an hour. The 50MP main sensor with f/1.6 aperture, OIS and a 3.5x tele lens captures detailed photos even in dim light.

Owners complain about the heavy Chinese OriginOS that lacks Google services, Bangla language support and receives OTA updates only from Chinese servers. The lack of any official manufacturer warranty means you rely on the shop warranty and must pay for any repairs. Overall the phone feels fast and capable but requires patience with software and support.
