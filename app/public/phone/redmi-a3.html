Is the Redmi A3 worth buying?

What is good

  • Premium aesthetic design
  • 90Hz display refresh rate
  • Good battery endurance
  • Includes headphone jack and FM radio

What is not

  • Severe system lag and app crashes
  • Painfully slow 10W charging
  • Poor camera detail and freezing
  • 32-bit architecture causes app incompatibility

Buy it if You only need a basic phone for simple tasks and prioritize a premium-looking design over performance.

Skip it if You want to play games, use modern apps, or need a phone that does not lag during daily use.

Why we say that

While it features a premium aesthetic design, owners report that the phone feels like a budget device in hand. The 32-bit architecture causes widespread app incompatibility, leading to frequent crashes and an inability to run modern applications. Users experience significant frustration with the 10W charging speed, which takes between 2 to 3 hours to reach a full charge.

The camera performance is consistently rated poorly, with owners noting low detail and frequent freezing during operation. Although the 90Hz display is a nice touch, the low brightness and resolution hinder the overall experience. With high levels of bloatware and ads, the software environment is cluttered.

Owners have given the phone very low ratings for gaming, performance, and ease of use, making it a difficult device to recommend for anyone expecting a smooth experience.

Does the Redmi A3 support gaming?

No, the phone is not suitable for gaming. Owners report severe lag and app crashes, noting that even basic modern apps struggle to run. The gaming performance rating is extremely low at 1.5 out of 10.

How long does the battery take to charge?

The phone uses 10W charging, which owners describe as painfully slow. You should expect it to take between 2 to 3 hours to fully charge the 5000 mAh battery.

Will this phone receive software updates?

The device comes with Android 14 or Android 14 Go edition and is upgradable to Android 15. It runs on HyperOS, which provides 2 to 4 OS updates depending on the tier.

Is the camera good for photography?

The camera performance is rated very poorly by owners. It features an 8MP main sensor that produces low detail, lacks stabilization, and frequently freezes during operation.
