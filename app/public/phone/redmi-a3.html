Is the Redmi A3 worth buying?

What is good

  • 5000 mAh battery gives strong endurance
  • 90 Hz refresh on a large 6.71" screen
  • headphone jack and FM radio are present
  • official warranty covered by brand network

What is not

  • 10 W charger needs two to three hours
  • 32‑bit processor blocks many modern apps
  • 8 MP camera struggles in low light
  • severe system stutter makes scrolling painful

Buy it if Buy it if you need basic daily use, long battery and cheap price.

Skip it if Skip it if you want smooth gaming, heavy apps or fast charging.

Why we say that

The 5000 mAh cell can keep the phone alive through a full day of typical use, yet the 10 W charger needs two to three hours to refill. The 6.71" IPS LCD offers a 90 Hz refresh, but its 720p resolution and 500‑nit brightness make it look dim, especially with the large bezels. The Helio G36 chipset is 32‑bit, causing many newer apps to refuse installation and resulting in severe stuttering when scrolling social feeds.

The 8 MP rear camera produces usable shots only in bright light, and video is shaky. Software runs HyperOS with Android 14 (Go) and can be upgraded to Android 15, but it is packed with bloatware, ads and storage‑reporting bugs. Overall, the phone feels like a budget device with a premium look but struggles to deliver smooth, modern performance.

What is the Redmi A3 price in Bangladesh?

Redmi A3 costs ৳10,999 in Bangladesh on official listings, in stock at 1 shop we track as of 9 Sep 2026.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery provides strong endurance and can easily last a full day of normal usage. However, when it runs out you will need two to three hours on the 10 W charger to refill it.

Can I play modern games like Genshin Impact on this phone?

No, the phone uses a 32‑bit Helio G36 processor which makes many modern games incompatible. Owners report extreme lag and that titles such as Genshin Impact or Asphalt 9 cannot run at all.

Is the camera usable in low‑light conditions?

The 8 MP rear camera with f/2.0 aperture works only in bright light. In low‑light situations the photos are dim and video footage is shaky, so it is not reliable for night shooting.

What warranty and software updates does the Redmi A3 receive?

The phone comes with the brand’s official warranty and can be serviced through the official Bangladesh network. It ships with Android 14 (Go edition) on HyperOS and can be upgraded to Android 15, though updates often bring bugs and ads.
