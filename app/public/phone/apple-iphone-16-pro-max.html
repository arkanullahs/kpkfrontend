Is the Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max worth buying?

What is good

  • Powerful A18 Pro chipset performance
  • Massive and bright 6.9 inch display
  • Top-tier camera and video results
  • Minimal throttling during gaming

What is not

  • Severe battery drain after iOS 26.3
  • Buggy software with animation stutters
  • Prone to scratches on frame and display
  • Clumsy camera control button ergonomics

Buy it if You prioritize raw gaming performance and a large display and are willing to wait for future software updates to fix current bugs.

Skip it if You want a reliable device that does not require multiple charges per day or you prefer a phone that is not prone to physical scratches.

Why we say that

Owners report that the A18 Pro chipset handles gaming with minimal throttling, yet the experience is often interrupted by animation stutters that make the screen feel like it is running at 60Hz. While the camera produces excellent results, the new physical control button is ergonomically clumsy, often causing the phone to shake during use and resulting in blurry photos. Battery life is a major concern for many, with reports of severe drain following the iOS 26.3 update that forces some users to charge twice a day.

The build quality feels premium, but users frequently note that the frame and display are prone to scratches, and the OIS mechanism can rattle. Given that the newer iPhone 17 Pro Max is available for ৳1,54,999, most buyers will find that model to be the better investment.
