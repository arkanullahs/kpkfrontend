Is the Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max worth buying?

What is good

  • Powerful A18 Pro chipset performance
  • Massive and bright 6.9 inch display
  • Top-tier camera and video results
  • Minimal throttling during heavy gaming

What is not

  • Severe battery drain after software updates
  • Buggy iOS 18 animations and crashes
  • Clumsy camera control button ergonomics
  • Slow charging speeds

Buy it if You are a dedicated Apple user who prioritizes raw processing power and display quality over current software stability.

Skip it if You want a reliable phone that does not require frequent charging or suffer from UI glitches.

Why we say that

While the A18 Pro chipset delivers top-tier performance for gaming and daily tasks, owners report that iOS 18 is plagued by animation stutters and frequent app crashes. The new physical camera control button is a major point of frustration, as its placement often causes the phone to shake during use, resulting in blurry photos. Battery life is another major concern, with many users experiencing severe drain issues following the iOS 26.3 update.

The build quality feels premium, yet the frame and display are prone to scratches, and the OIS mechanism can rattle. Charging speeds remain slow compared to modern Android flagships. Before purchasing, consider that the newer iPhone 17 Pro Max is available for ৳1,54,999 and is generally considered the better buy.

Does the iPhone 16 Pro Max come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

How long will the iPhone 16 Pro Max receive software updates?

Is the iPhone 16 Pro Max good for heavy gaming?

Why are owners complaining about the camera on this phone?

While the camera produces top-tier results, the new physical control button is difficult to use. Owners report that pressing the button causes the phone to shake in their hand, which often leads to shaky and blurry photos.
