Is the Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.9 inch LTPO display with 2000 nits
  • A18 Pro chipset delivers top gaming performance
  • 48MP camera with OIS and 5x telephoto
  • Premium titanium build

What is not

  • 15W charging is noticeably slow
  • Battery drain after recent iOS updates
  • Camera control button makes photos shaky
  • UI animation stutter reduces smoothness

Buy it if Buy if you prioritize the best display, camera and gaming power and can stretch to the premium price.

Skip it if Skip if you need fast charging, long battery endurance or a lower price.

Why we say that

The 6.9 inch LTPO OLED panel reaches 2000 nits and keeps 120Hz refresh, yet animation stutters can make it feel slower. The A18 Pro chip gives top performance for games and multitasking, while the 48MP main sensor with OIS captures elite photos, though the new camera control button often introduces shake. Battery capacity is solid at 4685 mAh, but recent iOS updates have caused noticeable drain and the 15W charger needs almost 45 minutes for a full charge.

Heavy tasks like 4K recording and high‑end gaming generate heat. Overall the device feels premium, but the incremental upgrades over the 15 Pro Max and the similar price of the newer iPhone 17 Pro Max make it a hard sell for many.

What is the Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max price in Bangladesh?

Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max costs ৳1,38,490 to ৳2,02,000 in Bangladesh, in stock at 11 shops we track as of 2026-08-30.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 4685 mAh battery offers solid endurance, but owners report that after the iOS 26.3 update the phone drains noticeably faster. Charging is limited to 15W, so reaching full charge can take close to 45 minutes.

Is the iPhone 16 Pro Max good for gaming?

Yes, the A18 Pro processor and 8GB RAM give the device top gaming performance, reflected in a 9.0 rating. Some users notice animation glitches and UI stutter, which can make the 120Hz panel feel like 60Hz.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 48MP sensor with a large 1/1.28 inch size, f/1.8 aperture and optical image stabilization captures high‑quality low‑light shots. However, the dedicated camera control button is reported to cause shaky photos for many owners.

Will the phone receive software updates for several years?

The iPhone 16 Pro Max ships with iOS 18 and is upgradable to iOS 26.6. Apple typically provides 5 to 6 major version updates, though early iOS 18 builds showed bugs and animation stutters.
