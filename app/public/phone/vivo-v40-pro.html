Vivo V40 Pro 5G Is it worth buying?

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official warranty in Bangladesh
  • USB 2.0 limits fast data transfer

  • Low‑light edge detection is weak

Why we say that

The 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel reaches a peak 4500‑nit brightness and runs at a smooth 120 Hz, making everyday scrolling and media consumption feel fluid. Under the hood, the Dimensity 9200+ chipset paired with up to 12 GB RAM delivers responsive multitasking and decent gaming scores, though the USB 2.0 port limits fast data transfers. The 5500 mAh battery provides good endurance and fast charging, but owners notice a rapid drain of about 4‑5 % every 15 minutes when the camera is active.

Photo quality is solid in daylight and for portraits, yet low‑light edge detection can be weak. Build quality feels premium and durable, but the lack of brand‑backed warranty means you must rely on shop warranty and verify IMEI/BTRC status before purchase.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 5500 mAh battery gives good overall endurance and fast charging, but owners report it loses about 4‑5 % every 15 minutes when the camera is active, so heavy camera use shortens the day.

Will the phone handle gaming smoothly?

Powered by the Dimensity 9200+ and up to 12 GB RAM, the phone scores well in performance and gaming ratings, and the 120 Hz display helps keep motion fluid, making most mobile games run comfortably.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

Day and night photos are generally good, especially portraits, and the 50 MP front camera is praised. However, users note the camera struggles with edge detection in low‑light scenes, and using it drains the battery quickly.

Is there an official warranty or reliable after‑sales support in Bangladesh?

No official Vivo warranty is recorded for Bangladesh. The phone is sold as a gray‑import with shop warranty only, so buyers should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.
