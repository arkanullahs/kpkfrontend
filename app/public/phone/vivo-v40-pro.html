Why we say that

At ৳59,999 the Vivo V40 Pro offers a large 6.78 inch display that reaches peak brightness of 4500 nits, making outdoor use easy. The Dimensity 9200+ chipset with up to 12 GB RAM handles most apps and games smoothly, and the 5500 mAh battery gives solid endurance for everyday tasks. Owners praise the premium build and the 50 MP main and selfie cameras for clear day photos and good night video, yet they note that heavy camera use cuts the charge by about 4‑5% every fifteen minutes and low light edge detection is weak.

The phone arrives as a gray import, so there is no official Vivo warranty; only the shop’s limited promise applies and any repair cost is paid by you. Data transfer is limited by a USB 2.0 port and an external SD card can be lost during resets. Software runs Funtouch OS 14 with medium bloat and only a couple of major Android updates expected.
