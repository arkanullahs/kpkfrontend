Is the Honor 600 Lite worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz AMOLED screen with 6500‑nit peak brightness
  • 6520 mAh battery lasting all day with slow drain
  • Dimensity 7100 Elite chipset for smooth everyday use

What is not

  • Camera produces blur and noise
  • Gaming suffers slow loading and GPU stutters
  • Update promises viewed as unreliable

Buy it if Buy if you value a bright fast display and long battery more than camera quality or guaranteed long‑term software support.

Skip it if Skip if you need reliable photography, smooth gaming, or assured multi‑year updates.

Why we say that

The 6.6‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can hit a 6500‑nit peak, making everyday use a visual treat. The 6520 mAh cell delivers the kind of endurance owners describe as extremely slow percentage drop, easily lasting a full day of mixed use. The metal frame feels solid, though the overall design looks cheap to some buyers.

Under the hood, the Dimensity 7100 Elite chipset handles routine tasks without hiccups, but games load slowly and the GPU stutters, which matches the low gaming rating. The 108 MP main sensor is technically impressive, yet real‑world photos are blurry, noisy and often over‑ or under‑exposed, and video is capped at 30 fps. Software runs fairly smoothly on MagicOS 10, but promises of six years of updates are met with skepticism.

At a price between 40,990 and 44,499 taka, many consider it overpriced for what it actually delivers.

What is the Honor 600 Lite price in Bangladesh?

Honor 600 Lite costs ৳40,990 to ৳44,499 in Bangladesh, in stock at 11 shops we track as of 2026-08-29.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 6520 mAh battery is rated very highly by owners. Reviews say the phone shows an exceptionally slow percentage drop, allowing it to comfortably last through a full day of mixed usage without needing a charge.

Is the phone good for mobile gaming?

The Dimensity 7100 Elite chipset handles daily animations well, but owners notice slow game loading and occasional GPU stutters. The gaming rating is low, so it may not satisfy heavy gamers.

How does the 108MP camera perform in low‑light conditions?

Although the sensor is large, users report blurry and noisy pictures with over‑ or under‑exposure in dim settings. Video recording is limited to 30 fps, and overall camera quality is considered poor.

Will Honor really provide six years of Android updates?

Honor promises up to six major Android upgrades on paper, but owners express doubt and say the brand has a history of misleading update claims. Expect fewer guaranteed updates than advertised.
