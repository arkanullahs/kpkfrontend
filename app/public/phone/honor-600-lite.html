Buying verdict

Honor 600 Lite Is it worth buying?

If you need a bright screen and a battery that can survive a full day without charging, the Honor 600 Lite delivers that.

Choose it if
You value a bright, high‑refresh display and long battery life and can accept limited gaming and camera features.
Consider alternatives if

What works well

  • 120 Hz AMOLED display with very high peak brightness
  • 6520 mAh battery lasting up to 1.5 days
  • 108 MP main camera gives decent daylight photos
  • 45 W fast charging refills battery quickly

Reported trade-offs

  • Missing gyroscope sensor
  • 5 MP ultrawide camera is unusable
  • Video limited to 1080p 30 fps
  • Gaming heats device to about 40 °C

Why we say that

Its 6.6‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 6500 nits, making outdoor use easy. The 6520 mAh cell typically lasts one to one‑and‑a‑half days, and the 45 W charger refills it quickly. Daily performance feels smooth thanks to the Dimensity 7100 Elite and 8 GB RAM, and the 108 MP main camera captures decent daylight shots.

However, owners repeatedly note the missing gyroscope, which hurts gaming and motion‑based apps, and the device heats to about 40 °C under load. The 5 MP ultrawide lens is practically useless, and video recording stops at 1080p 30 fps, which many find disappointing. Software is clean MagicOS with Google services, but only the official unit priced at ৳44,499 carries a brand warranty; cheaper gray‑import versions lack manufacturer support.
