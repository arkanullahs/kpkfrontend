POCO M8 5G Is it worth buying?

If you need a bright, water resistant phone with a big battery and decent performance, the POCO M8 5G can fit the role.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a bright screen, strong battery and can live without official warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need a low light camera, smooth gaming or official brand support.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 120 Hz AMOLED display with 3200 nits peak brightness
  • 5520 mAh battery with 45 W fast charging
  • Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 handles everyday tasks
  • IP65/66 water and dust resistance

Reported trade-offs4

  • HyperOS heavy with ads and bloatware
  • Low light camera struggles without ultrawide
  • Mid range games get a little laggy
  • No eSIM and no headphone jack

Why we say that

The 6.77 inch AMOLED screen reaches 3200 nits at peak and runs at 120 Hz, making outdoor use comfortable. The Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 handles everyday apps smoothly but shows lag in mid range games like Roblox. Battery life is strong, lasting all day, and 45 W fast charging refills quickly, though the phone gets warm while charging.

The camera captures decent daylight photos but struggles in low light and lacks an ultrawide lens, while video features such as HDR and stabilization are questionable. HyperOS 2 brings many features but is heavy with ads and bloatware, causing occasional lag. The device is IP65/66 rated but offers no official warranty in Bangladesh, only shop warranty, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.

POCO M8 5G: what buyers ask us

What is the POCO M8 5G price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for POCO M8 5G in Bangladesh was ৳26,299, checked 21 Oct 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Will the phone receive Android updates?

The POCO M8 5G ships with Android 15 and HyperOS 2 and the manufacturer says it will receive up to four major Android version upgrades. You can expect two to three OS updates during its life.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 5520 mAh battery is rated to last a full day of typical use and the 45 W charger can refill it in about an hour. It also stays warm while charging but does not throttle.

Is the camera good in low light?

Daylight photos are decent with the 50 MP sensor but the camera loses detail in low light and there is no ultrawide lens. HDR video support is reported as unreliable and 4K video lacks stabilization.

Is there an official warranty in Bangladesh?

There is no official POCO warranty in Bangladesh and the units found are gray imports. You will only get a shop warranty and you should verify the IMEI and BTRC registration before buying.
