Is the itel A80 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.7 inch 120Hz IPS LCD
  • 50MP main camera
  • 5000mAh battery
  • Headphone jack

What is not

  • Unisoc T603 causes overheating
  • Frequent app and UI crashes
  • Severe lag in games
  • Fast battery drain despite large capacity

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large display and high‑resolution photos while staying within a tight budget.

Skip it if Skip it if you need smooth gaming, reliable performance, or long battery endurance.

Why we say that

The 6.7" 120Hz IPS LCD looks sharp and bright, and the 5000 mAh battery sounds generous, yet the Unisoc T603 chipset drains power quickly and runs hot. Users complain about the phone heating like an oven, the system UI freezing, and apps crashing often. Gaming feels laggy and the performance feels outdated for a 2024 release.

The software layer adds many ads and rarely receives updates, so stability is a constant issue. If you can tolerate these flaws, the device still offers a large display, decent camera hardware and a headphone jack at a very low official price.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The phone has a 5000 mAh battery, but the Unisoc T603 chip is inefficient, so owners notice fast drain even with moderate use. In real life the battery often needs charging before the day ends.

Can I play games smoothly on the itel A80?

Gaming performance is poor. The device scores 2.0 out of 10 for gaming and 3.2 for overall performance. Users report severe lag, frequent crashes and the phone heating up like an oven during play.

How good is the camera in low‑light conditions?

The A80 carries a 50MP main sensor which captures detailed photos in bright light, but the software is unstable and lacks good low‑light processing. Owners rate the camera around 4.5 out of 10.

Does the phone come with official warranty and software updates?

Yes, the itel A80 is sold with the brand’s official Bangladesh warranty and can be serviced through its network. It runs Android 14 with itelOS 14, but updates are rare and the system includes many ads.
