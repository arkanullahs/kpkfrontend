Is the itel A80 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.7 inch IPS LCD with 120 Hz refresh
  • 50 MP main camera for bright‑light photos
  • 5000 mAh battery capacity
  • Headphone jack for wired audio

What is not

  • Severe overheating during regular use
  • Frequent app crashes and UI freezes
  • Outdated Unisoc T603 processor limits performance
  • Poor gaming experience with lag

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize a large display and high‑resolution photos over speed and stability.

Skip it if Skip it if you need smooth gaming, reliable performance, or long‑lasting battery life.

Why we say that

The 6.7" IPS LCD offers smooth 120 Hz visuals, and the 50 MP sensor can capture detailed shots in good light. However, the Unisoc T603 chipset is inefficient, draining the 5000 mAh battery quickly and heating the device like an oven. Users report system UI freezes, storage notifications that are wrong, and frequent app crashes.

Gaming performance is especially poor, with severe lag and frequent crashes. Software updates are rare and the OS is loaded with ads. While the phone looks attractive and includes a headphone jack, the overall experience feels frustrating for daily use.
