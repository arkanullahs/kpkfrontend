Is the Samsung Galaxy A04 worth buying?

What is good

  • 5000 mAh battery life
  • 50 MP main camera in daylight
  • headphone jack included
  • large 6.5‑inch display

What is not

  • Severe lag with basic apps
  • No fingerprint sensor
  • Poor low‑light and selfie camera
  • Gaming almost impossible

Buy it if Buy it if you only need basic calls, texts and occasional photos.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect smooth gaming or frequent software updates.

Why we say that

The 6.5‑inch PLS LCD gives you a big screen but the 720p resolution feels soft and lacks sharpness. The 50 MP main sensor captures decent daylight photos, yet low‑light performance and the 5 MP selfie shooter are disappointing. Performance is a major pain point; the Helio P35 struggles with basic apps and makes gaming virtually impossible.

One UI Core runs stably on Android 12, but it misses useful features like Secure Folder and the device shows no fingerprint reader. At four years old, expect little to no future software updates, and the newer A05 model is cheaper and newer.
