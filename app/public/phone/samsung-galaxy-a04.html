What works well

  • Excellent battery endurance
  • Durable against drops
  • Decent 50MP daylight photos
  • Includes a headphone jack

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe lag with basic apps
  • No fingerprint sensor
  • Low resolution display
  • Poor video stabilization

Why we say that

Owners report severe lag when using basic apps and social media like Facebook. Gaming is nearly impossible on the Helio P35 chipset. You will find the lack of a fingerprint sensor to be a major inconvenience in daily use.

The display lacks sharpness due to its low resolution. Video quality is poor because there is no stabilization. While the build is durable against drops, the plastic feels cheap.

Read full reasoning

The software is stable but lacks features like Secure Folder. Since this device is 4 years old, you should expect little to no remaining software update life. The newer Galaxy A05 is usually the better buy as it sells for less at ৳14,799.
