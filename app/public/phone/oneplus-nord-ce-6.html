Is the OnePlus Nord CE6 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.78 inch 144Hz AMOLED display
  • 80W fast charging under 90 minutes
  • 7500 mAh battery lasting 12‑13 hours screen‑on
  • IP68/69 and MIL‑STD‑810H durability

What is not

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • No ultrawide camera
  • Performance drops compared to CE5
  • No microSD slot

Buy it if Buy if you want a bright high‑refresh screen, fast charging and a rugged build at a gray‑import price.

Skip it if Skip if you need official warranty, strong low‑light camera or expandable storage.

Why we say that

The 6.78‑inch screen reaches a peak brightness of 3600 nits, making it easy to read under direct sunlight. The Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset handles everyday tasks smoothly, yet benchmarks show a noticeable drop from the CE5, especially in raw gaming power. Charging at 80W fills the massive battery in under 90 minutes and even supports reverse charging.

The 50MP main sensor with OIS delivers decent photos in good light, but low‑light shots are weak and the missing ultrawide lens limits versatility. Build quality feels solid with IP68/69 rating and MIL‑STD‑810H durability, though the plastic frame may not impress everyone. No manufacturer warranty is available; you rely on the shop’s limited cover and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.

What is the OnePlus Nord CE6 price in Bangladesh?

OnePlus Nord CE6 costs ৳39,999 to ৳45,499 in Bangladesh on unofficial listings, in stock at 4 shops we track as of 4 Sep 2026.

Is there an official OnePlus warranty for the Nord CE6 in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray‑import units built for India and global markets, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop may offer its own limited warranty, but repairs are paid and you should confirm IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

How long will the Nord CE6 receive software updates?

The phone runs OxygenOS based on Android 16 and follows OnePlus' policy of four major OS updates for flagships, but owners report only two years of updates for this model, which is shorter than many competitors.

What kind of battery life can I expect in daily use?

The 7500 mAh battery is described as a monster capacity and provides about 12 to 13 hours of screen‑on time. Combined with 80W fast charging, you can refill the battery in less than 90 minutes.

Will the phone handle modern games smoothly?

The Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset runs daily apps smoothly, but owners note a clear performance regression from the CE5. Gaming is acceptable at lower settings, yet raw power feels weak for demanding titles.
