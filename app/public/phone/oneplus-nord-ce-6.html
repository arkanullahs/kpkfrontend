OnePlus Nord CE6 Is it worth buying?

The OnePlus Nord CE6 is a battery-focused device that sacrifices raw performance and camera versatility compared to its predecessor.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize extreme battery life and a durable, water-resistant build over gaming power or camera versatility.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable camera system with an ultrawide lens or if you expect long-term software support and high-end gaming performance.

Why we say that

While the 7500 mAh battery provides best-in-class endurance with 12-13 hours of screen-on time, owners are frustrated by the significant performance regression and the removal of the microSD card slot. The phone runs cool under load and features a bright 144Hz AMOLED display, but the lack of an ultrawide camera and a limited 2-year software update policy make it a difficult recommendation. Because this is a gray-import device, you will not receive any manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh.

Repairs are entirely at your own expense, and you must rely solely on the shop's promise for coverage. The device is built for global or Indian markets, meaning the charger and box contents reflect those regions rather than local standards.

Does the OnePlus Nord CE6 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available through gray-import channels. There is no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh regardless of the brand's service reputation. You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller, and any repairs will be paid out of your own pocket.

How does the battery life perform in daily usage?

The battery life is excellent, with owners rating it a 9.7 out of 10. It features a 7500 mAh capacity that provides 12-13 hours of screen-on time. It is considered the best-in-class for endurance and supports 80W fast charging that fills the battery in under 90 minutes.

Is the OnePlus Nord CE6 good for gaming?

Gaming performance is a weak point for this device. Owners report a noticeable downgrade compared to the previous CE5 model, noting that the raw power is insufficient for demanding tasks. While it runs cool, it is not recommended for users who prioritize high-end gaming performance.

How long will the OnePlus Nord CE6 receive software updates?

The software support window is limited to only 2 years of updates. This is a major point of frustration for owners who expected a longer commitment from the manufacturer. You should consider this short support cycle before making your purchase.
