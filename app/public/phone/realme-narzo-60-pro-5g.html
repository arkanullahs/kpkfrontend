Is the Realme Narzo 60 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 100 MP main camera for bright photos
  • 6.7 inch AMOLED display with 120 Hz
  • 5000 mAh battery with 67W fast charge
  • Premium vegan leather back finish

What is not

  • No official warranty, only shop warranty
  • Heavy pre installed apps and ads
  • Excessive heating under load
  • Inconsistent OIS and video stabilization

Buy it if If you want a large bright screen, fast charging and a high resolution camera at a sub ৳34,000 price and can live with a gray import warranty, this phone fits.

Skip it if If you need reliable video recording, long term software updates or a brand backed warranty, look elsewhere.

Why we say that

The 5000 mAh battery easily lasts a full day and refills in about half an hour with 67W fast charging, which owners call exceptional. The vegan leather back feels premium and the curved display shows vivid colors and smooth motion. Realme UI 4.0 runs Android 13 but is packed with pre installed apps and ads that can feel intrusive, and the phone is three years old so only a few more software updates are expected.

Performance handles daily tasks and casual games, yet the Dimensity 7050 throttles when the device gets hot and users report excessive warmth. The main camera captures detailed photos in good light, but optical image stabilization is inconsistent and video stabilization is weak. In short, the phone offers a large bright screen, fast charging and a high resolution sensor at a low price, but the lack of official support, heating and heavy bloatware are real drawbacks.
