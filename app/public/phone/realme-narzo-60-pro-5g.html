Is the Realme Narzo 60 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 100MP main camera with OIS
  • 120Hz curved AMOLED display
  • 67W fast charging to 50% in twenty minutes
  • 5000 mAh battery lasting multiple days

What is not

  • Heavy bloatware and notifications
  • No ultrawide or macro lenses
  • No headphone jack or SD slot
  • Shop warranty only and limited software updates

Buy it if Buy it if you need massive internal storage, a bright fast screen and can live with a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect long software support, a clean OS or a dedicated camera module for selfies and ultrawide shots.

Why we say that

The 5000 mAh battery gives exceptional endurance and can last several days with light use, while the 67W charger pushes it to half charge in about twenty minutes. The curved AMOLED screen is bright with 950 nits peak and smooth motion, making media consumption pleasant. Build quality feels like premium vegan leather, though some decorative parts may peel over time.

Daily performance is smooth and the phone handles BGMI okay, but it is not a pro level gaming device. The 100MP main sensor captures detail but the lack of ultrawide and macro lenses limits versatility. Realme UI 4.0 brings Android 13 but is loaded with pre installed apps and frequent notifications.

Only a shop warranty is offered and the device is three years old, so remaining official updates are likely short or none.
