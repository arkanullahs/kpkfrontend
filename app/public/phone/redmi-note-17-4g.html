Redmi Note 17 4G Is it worth buying?

The Redmi Note 17 4G gives you a huge, very bright screen and a massive battery at a price that can be under ৳30,000.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
If you want a big bright screen, long battery life and a decent camera without needing 5G or a fully stock Android experience, this phone fits well.
Consider alternatives if
If you need guaranteed manufacturer warranty, Google apps out of the box, or 5G connectivity, look for another model.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 6.99 inch AMOLED 120Hz display
  • 7700 mAh battery with 45W fast charge
  • 50 MP main camera with f/1.8 lens
  • IR blaster for remote control

Reported trade-offs4

  • China ROM lacks Google services
  • High ads in HyperOS
  • No official warranty on cheap imports
  • No 5G support

Why we say that

Its 6.99 inch AMOLED panel runs at 120Hz and can reach 1800 nits, so outdoor use feels comfortable. The Snapdragon 6s 4G Gen 2 chipset handles everyday apps and casual gaming without stutter, though heavy 3D titles may show its limits. The 7700 mAh cell lasts well beyond a day even with video streaming, and the 45W charger refills it quickly.

The 50 MP main camera captures decent photos in good light, but low‑light performance is average and the CN software version lacks Google services, which many buyers find inconvenient. The phone ships with HyperOS 3 on Android 16, receiving two to four major updates, but it shows a lot of built‑in ads. Official warranty is only on the higher‑priced listings; cheaper imports are gray and need paid repairs.

Overall it feels like a large‑screen, long‑lasting device with some software compromises.
