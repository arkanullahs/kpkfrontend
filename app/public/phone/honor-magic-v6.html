Honor Magic V6 Is it worth buying?

If you can live without an official warranty and accept occasional performance drops, the Honor Magic V6 offers a large, bright foldable with excellent battery life.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large foldable with strong battery and can handle paying for repairs yourself.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 7.95‑inch bright foldable AMOLED display
  • 6660 mAh battery lasts all day
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 with 16 GB RAM
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs4

  • CPU throttles heavily under load
  • Wi‑Fi connectivity issues common
  • Screen crease visible at certain angles
  • No manufacturer warranty, repairs are paid

Why we say that

The 7.95‑inch LTPO2 AMOLED screen reaches 5,000 nits peak and runs at 120 Hz, making outdoor use easy. A 6,660 mAh cell and 80 W charger give all‑day endurance even with heavy apps. The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 paired with 16 GB RAM delivers flagship‑level speed, but owners report the CPU halves its performance under load unless the aggressive performance‑battery mode is used, which adds heat.

Wi‑Fi connections can be unstable and the fold line may show a visible crease at certain angles. The 50 MP main camera with OIS produces solid photos but does not match top‑tier flagships. The phone arrives as a gray import, so there is no manufacturer warranty; any repair must be paid for through the seller’s limited service.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 6660 mAh cell is rated for all‑day use even with heavy apps, and the 80 W charger refills it quickly. Users report the phone easily lasts a full day without needing to recharge.

Will the phone handle gaming without lag?

The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and 16 GB RAM give strong initial performance, but owners say the CPU halves its speed under sustained load unless the performance‑battery mode is used, which adds heat and can cause lag.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor with a 1/1.56″ size, f/1.6 aperture and OIS captures solid stills, but reviews note it is not flagship‑level in low light, scoring around 8.6 out of 10.

All listings are gray imports, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s limited coverage applies and repairs are paid. The device runs Android 16 with MagicOS 10 and promises up to seven major OS updates.
