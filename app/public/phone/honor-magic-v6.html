Is the Honor Magic V6 worth buying?

What is good

  • 7.95‑inch foldable LTPO2 AMOLED display
  • 5000‑nit peak brightness
  • 6660 mAh battery with 80 W fast charging
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance

What is not

  • Bluetooth connectivity drops frequently
  • Push notifications are sometimes delayed
  • Thermal throttling without Performance mode
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Buy it if Buy it if you want a high‑end foldable with flagship specs and can accept a gray‑import warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a reliable official warranty or cannot tolerate software glitches.

Why we say that

The 7.95‑inch LTPO2 AMOLED screen reaches 5000 nits at peak and stays bright even in sunlight. The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chip delivers smooth performance and scores 9.4 in gaming, though you must enable Performance mode to avoid noticeable throttling. Battery life is strong thanks to a 6660 mAh cell and 80 W fast charging, and owners rate endurance at 9.6.

Photos are sharp with a 50 MP main sensor, f/1.6 aperture and 3× optical zoom, earning an 8.7 camera rating. Build quality is thin, lightweight and carries an IP69 rating, and the device includes an IR blaster. Real‑world use shows Bluetooth sometimes disconnects, push notifications can be delayed, and the software layer is buggy and feels like iOS.

There is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; any repair must be paid to the shop that sold it.
