Is the Honor Magic V6 worth buying?

What is good

  • Industry-leading 6660 mAh battery capacity
  • Incredibly thin and lightweight build
  • Bright 5000 nits peak display
  • Flagship-level camera stills and zoom

What is not

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Buggy software with delayed notifications
  • Thermal throttling issues during gaming
  • Bluetooth connectivity bugs requiring reboots

Buy it if You want a premium, thin foldable with the best battery life available and are comfortable managing software bugs without official support.

Skip it if You need a reliable phone for work notifications or expect official brand service and warranty coverage in Bangladesh.

Why we say that

While the 6660 mAh battery and 5000 nits peak brightness display are impressive, owners frequently report bugs that hinder the experience. You will likely encounter delayed notifications and Bluetooth connectivity issues that require a full phone reboot to resolve. Performance is powerful thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chip, yet you must enable Performance Mode to avoid throttling, which then leads to heat issues.

The software experience is often described as unpolished and overly similar to iOS. Because these units are unofficial gray imports, you have no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh. Any repairs will be paid out of your own pocket, and the shop providing the device is your only point of contact for service.

Does the Honor Magic V6 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit is a gray import, meaning you receive no brand-backed support. Any repairs are paid, and you must rely solely on the promises made by the specific shop where you purchase the device.

How does the battery perform on this foldable?

The battery performance is exceptional with an industry-leading 6660 mAh capacity. Owners rate the battery life very highly, noting that it provides excellent endurance for a foldable device. It is one of the strongest features of this phone.

Is the Honor Magic V6 good for gaming?

The phone features a powerful Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chip, but gaming is not straightforward. You must use Performance Mode to maintain high speeds, which causes heavy thermal throttling. Without this mode, CPU performance is halved, impacting your gaming experience.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The Honor Magic V6 supports up to 7 major Android upgrades. While the hardware is capable, keep in mind that the current MagicOS software is reported to be buggy and unpolished, which may affect your daily usage despite the long update promise.
