Buying verdict

Honor Magic V6 Is it worth buying?

The Honor Magic V6 is a luxury foldable that delivers industry-leading battery life and a remarkably thin build, but it demands patience due to significant software and thermal quirks.

Choose it if
You want the thinnest foldable with the largest battery and are comfortable managing software bugs and thermal settings yourself.
Consider alternatives if
You require a reliable device for work notifications or need the peace of mind provided by an official manufacturer warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • Industry-leading 6660 mAh battery capacity
  • Incredibly thin and lightweight foldable design
  • Bright 5000 nits peak display
  • Flagship-level camera stills and zoom

Reported trade-offs

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Buggy software with delayed notifications
  • Heavy thermal throttling during gaming
  • Bluetooth connectivity issues requiring reboots

Why we say that

While the 6660 mAh battery and 5000 nits display are impressive, owners frequently struggle with buggy software that mimics iOS too closely and suffers from delayed notifications. You must enable Performance Mode to get the most out of the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chip, though this choice triggers heavy thermal throttling. Bluetooth connectivity is another common frustration, often requiring a full device reboot to resolve.

Because these units are gray imports, you have no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh. Repairs are entirely at your own expense, and the shop-provided coverage is limited. It is a powerful device for those who prioritize hardware specs over a polished, reliable user experience.

Does the Honor Magic V6 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as a gray import in Bangladesh. There is no manufacturer warranty support regardless of the brand's service score. Any repairs are paid, and you must rely solely on the limited coverage promised by the specific shop where you purchase the device.

How does the battery perform on the Honor Magic V6?

The phone features an industry-leading 6660 mAh battery. Owners report exceptional endurance for a foldable device, giving it a high rating of 9.6 out of 10. It is currently one of the best options for users who prioritize long-lasting power in a foldable form factor.

Is the Honor Magic V6 suitable for heavy gaming?

While it uses a powerful Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chip, gaming performance is inconsistent. You must use Performance Mode to avoid throttling, but this leads to heavy thermal issues. Owners give the gaming experience a rating of 8.8 out of 10, noting that performance is halved without specific settings.

How long will the Honor Magic V6 receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 16 and is promised up to 7 major Android upgrades. While the software is feature-rich, users report that the current MagicOS 10 experience is unpolished and buggy, with specific issues regarding Bluetooth connectivity and delayed notifications that persist even on the global ROM.
