Buying verdict

Huawei Nova 15 Max Is it worth buying?

Expect a phone that can run for days on a single charge but will feel slow and lack essential Google apps.

Choose it if
You value long battery life and a bright screen more than fast performance or Google apps.
Consider alternatives if
You need 5G, smooth gaming, or native Google services.

What works well

  • 8500 mAh battery lasting multiple days
  • 6.84 inch OLED display with 4000 nits peak
  • 256 GB internal storage for apps and media
  • IR blaster for TV remote control

Reported trade-offs

  • No Google Play services or GMS
  • No 5G connectivity
  • Kirin 8000 chipset feels outdated
  • 40 W charging is slow for huge battery

Why we say that

The Nova 15 Max packs an 8500 mAh battery that easily lasts two to three days for typical Bangladeshi usage, and its 6.84 inch OLED panel reaches a peak brightness of 4000 nits, making outdoor viewing comfortable. Storage is generous at 256 GB and the device includes an IR blaster for TV control. However, the Kirin 8000 chipset is built on an old 7 nm process and scores only average in performance and gaming tests.

40 W charging struggles to refill the massive battery quickly. The 50 MP main camera is acceptable in daylight but many users find it dated and weak in low light. The phone runs EMUI 14.2 without native Google Mobile Services, so popular apps need workarounds.

How long will the battery last on daily use?

The 8500 mAh battery is rated 9.7 by owners and can easily last two to three days of typical use, even with the large OLED screen. Charging is 40 W, which is slower than expected for such capacity.

Can I play modern games smoothly on this phone?

Gaming performance is limited; the Kirin 8000 built on 7 nm is considered outdated for 2026 and owners rate gaming at 4.5 out of 10. Expect low frame rates in demanding titles and occasional lag.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The main 50 MP sensor with a 1/1.56” size and f/1.9 aperture delivers decent daylight shots, but many find it dated and performance drops noticeably in low light.

Does the phone come with an official warranty and will it receive updates?

Official listings at ৳47,999 include the brand warranty, while gray‑import versions do not and any repair will be charged. Software updates are irregular; EMUI 14.2 may receive occasional patches but no guarantee of long‑term support.
