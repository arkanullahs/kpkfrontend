Huawei Nova 15 Max Is it worth buying?

The biggest draw is the massive 8500 mAh battery that can stretch a day‑plus without a charge, but owners constantly complain about the slow 40 W top‑up for such a huge cell.

What works well4

  • 8500 mAh battery lasts days
  • 6.84‑inch OLED with 4000‑nit peak
  • 40 W charger included
  • IR blaster for remote control

Reported trade-offs4

  • No Google services out of the box
  • No 5G connectivity
  • Kirin 8000 feels outdated
  • Side‑mounted fingerprint feels old

Why we say that

The 6.84‑inch OLED shines with 4000‑nit peaks, yet the Kirin 8000 chipset feels dated and struggles with modern games. Lack of 5G and the absence of Google Mobile Services mean you must rely on workarounds for essential apps. Build quality is solid and heavy at 230 g, but the camera design looks old and photo quality is mixed.

Software runs EMUI 14.2 with low bloat, but updates are uncertain. Overall, it suits users who value endurance over speed, and who can live without Google services.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 8500 mAh cell can easily cover two to three days of moderate use, but heavy browsing or video streaming will drain it faster and may require a charge before the day ends.

Will this phone run modern games smoothly?

The Kirin 8000 chipset is a 7 nm part from 2022, so it handles casual games okay but struggles with demanding titles, giving a gaming rating of around four and a performance score of five out of ten.

Can I use Google apps like Gmail and YouTube?

The device ships without Google Mobile Services, so apps from the Play Store are not pre‑installed. You must install Gbox or MicroG to run Gmail, YouTube and other essential Google apps, which adds extra steps.
