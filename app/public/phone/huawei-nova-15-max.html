Is the Huawei Nova 15 Max worth buying?

What is good

  • 8500 mAh battery lasting multiple days
  • 6.84 inch OLED display with 4000 nits peak
  • 256 GB internal storage for apps and media
  • IR blaster for TV remote control

What is not

  • No Google Play services or GMS
  • No 5G connectivity
  • Kirin 8000 chipset feels outdated
  • 40 W charging is slow for huge battery

Buy it if Buy it if you value long battery life and a bright screen more than fast performance or Google apps.

Skip it if Skip it if you need 5G, smooth gaming, or native Google services.

Why we say that

The Nova 15 Max packs an 8500 mAh battery that easily lasts two to three days for typical Bangladeshi usage, and its 6.84 inch OLED panel reaches a peak brightness of 4000 nits, making outdoor viewing comfortable. Storage is generous at 256 GB and the device includes an IR blaster for TV control. However, the Kirin 8000 chipset is built on an old 7 nm process and scores only average in performance and gaming tests.

40 W charging struggles to refill the massive battery quickly. The 50 MP main camera is acceptable in daylight but many users find it dated and weak in low light. The phone runs EMUI 14.2 without native Google Mobile Services, so popular apps need workarounds.

Lack of 5G and a side mounted fingerprint sensor add to the feeling of an outdated flagship.
