Is the Samsung Galaxy A05 worth buying?

What is good

  • Excellent battery endurance lasting up to two days
  • Solid plastic build that survives drops well
  • Decent main camera performance in daylight
  • Low levels of bloatware and advertisements

What is not

  • Frequent screen freezing and unresponsive touch
  • Severe lag reported after software updates
  • Dim display with poor sunlight visibility
  • No fingerprint sensor included

Buy it if You need a basic phone for simple tasks and prioritize long battery life over performance or gaming.

Skip it if You play games like COD, require a responsive touch screen, or want a device that will receive long-term software updates.

Why we say that

While the 5000 mAh battery provides excellent endurance for one to two days, the user experience is frequently marred by screen freezing and unresponsive touch issues. Owners have expressed significant frustration regarding severe lag following software updates, particularly after moving to One UI 0.8. The display is dim and difficult to see in sunlight, and the lack of a fingerprint sensor is a major disappointment for many buyers.

While the plastic build is solid and survives drops well, the internal performance is not suitable for gaming. You should be aware that this model is three years old, meaning software support is nearing its end. Many users feel that the A05s offers better value for your money than this specific model.
