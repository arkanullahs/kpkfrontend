Buying verdict

Samsung M07 Is it worth buying?

If you need a large screen phone with decent camera and long software support at a low price, the Samsung M07 can fit the bill.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a big screen, decent camera and long software support without spending much.
Consider alternatives if
You need strong warranty, premium build or high‑end gaming performance.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 50MP main camera for bright photos
  • 25W fast charging
  • Six guaranteed Android updates
  • Large 6.7" 90Hz display

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official Samsung warranty
  • Unclear storage type (eMMC vs UFS)
  • Generic plastic build feel
  • Limited performance for heavy games

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch PLS LCD with 90Hz refresh works fine for YouTube and news, and the 50MP main sensor captures clear daylight shots. Android 15 arrives with One UI 7 and promises up to six major updates, which owners praise as a rare advantage for a budget device. The 5000 mAh battery and 25W charger give a day of moderate use without rushing to plug in.

Daily tasks and light games run smoothly on the Helio G99, though heavy games feel throttled. Users complain about the generic plastic feel, the unclear eMMC versus UFS storage claim, and the lack of any official Samsung warranty, meaning repairs must be paid out of pocket. Overall it feels like a practical, no‑frills phone for basic needs.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh cell usually provides a full day of moderate use such as browsing, messaging and watching videos, and the 25W charger refills it quickly. Heavy gaming will reduce the endurance noticeably.

Can I play games smoothly on this phone?

The Helio G99 handles basic tasks and light games without lag, and owners rate gaming around 4.8 out of 10. More demanding titles will show frame drops and may not run at high settings.

How good is the camera in low‑light conditions?

The 50MP sensor has an f/1.8 aperture which helps in daylight, but owners give the camera a modest 5 out of 10 rating, indicating low‑light performance is average and may produce noisy pictures.

Will I get any official warranty or support for this device?

All listings are gray‑import, so Samsung does not provide a manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop you buy from may offer its own limited warranty, but any repair will be paid by you.
