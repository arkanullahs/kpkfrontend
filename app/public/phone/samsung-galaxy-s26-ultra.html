Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra Is it worth buying?

If you want flagship speed and a solid camera without paying premium, the S26 Ultra can work.

Choose it if
You need top gaming, fast performance and can handle a gray import warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on long battery life or need official Samsung warranty.

What works well

  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance
  • 200MP main camera with reliable results
  • 6.9 inch LTPO AMOLED display with privacy mode
  • 60W fast charging

Reported trade-offs

  • 5000 mAh battery feels outdated
  • Privacy display cuts resolution and brightness
  • S Pen only fits one orientation
  • Phone rocks on flat tables

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 delivers top tier performance and keeps temperatures low, which makes gaming feel smooth. The 200MP main sensor with f/1.4 aperture and OIS gives consistent photos, though it lacks the novelty of newer Chinese rivals. The 6.9 inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X screen is bright, reaches 2600 nits peak and offers a privacy mode, but activating it dims the display and lowers resolution.

Battery life is a mixed bag; the efficient chip helps, yet the 5000 mAh cell feels old for a 2026 flagship and owners rate it 7.2. The S Pen now only slides in one way, which many find annoying, and the phone tends to wobble on flat surfaces. Software runs Android 16 with One UI 8.5, promising up to seven major updates and low bloat.
