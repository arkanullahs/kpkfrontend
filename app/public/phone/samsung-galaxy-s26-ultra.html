Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 keeps frame rates high and stays cool even in long sessions. The 6.9 inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED screen shines at 2600 nits peak and the Privacy Display adds a useful layer of security, though it dims the panel and cuts some resolution. The 200MP main sensor captures detailed photos, but the camera hardware feels unchanged compared with newer rivals.

Battery life is acceptable thanks to the efficient chip, yet the 5000mAh cell feels small for a 2026 flagship and many owners notice the phone rocks on flat tables. The S Pen now only slides in one direction, which many find annoying. Software is stable with One UI 8.5 and promises up to seven Android upgrades.
