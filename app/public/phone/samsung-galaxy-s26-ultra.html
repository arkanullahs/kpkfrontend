Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 delivers top speed
  • 60W fast charging fills the 5000 mAh battery quickly
  • 200 MP main sensor captures detailed photos
  • Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X display offers smooth 120 Hz motion

Reported trade-offs4

  • Camera hardware lags behind Chinese flagships
  • Privacy Display halves resolution and brightness
  • CPU throttles under long heavy loads
  • S Pen lost Bluetooth support

Why we say that

The phone feels fast thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and the 120 Hz LTPO display, and the 200 MP sensor captures detailed shots in good light. Battery life is decent because the chip is efficient, but the 5000 mAh cell is unchanged. Owners complain that the camera hardware falls behind Chinese rivals, that the privacy display cuts resolution in half, and that the CPU throttles under long heavy loads.

The S Pen no longer has Bluetooth, which disappoints long‑time users. Overall it is a powerful device with some compromises that matter for photography and sustained performance.
