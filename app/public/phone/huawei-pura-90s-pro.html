Huawei Pura 90s Pro Is it worth buying?

If you can live without Google Play services, the Pura 90s Pro feels like a solid all‑rounder thanks to its massive 6000 mAh battery, fast 66 W charge and a bright 6.6‑inch LTPO OLED panel that runs at 120 Hz.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large battery, high‑refresh screen and strong camera and can manage without Google Play services.
Consider alternatives if
You need official warranty support or rely on Google apps out of the box.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 6000 mAh battery with 66 W fast charge
  • 6.6‑inch LTPO OLED display at 120 Hz
  • 12 GB RAM with Kirin 9030S performance
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS and 4× telephoto

Reported trade-offs3

  • No Google Play services, apps need workarounds
  • No manufacturer warranty, only shop warranty
  • Limited official service network in Bangladesh

Why we say that

The Kirin 9030S chipset paired with 12 GB RAM delivers smooth performance in everyday tasks and most games, while the 50 MP main camera with OIS and 4× telephoto produces decent photos in good light. Owners praise the long battery life and the premium glass build, but they often complain about the lack of official warranty and the need to find workarounds for apps that rely on Google services. Service support is limited to the shop’s own warranty and any repair will cost you.

Overall the phone offers high specs for its price, but you must accept the gray‑import trade‑offs.
