Why we say that

It offers a bright 144Hz P-OLED display and a battery that owners describe as efficient and long lasting. The Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 chipset handles daily tasks well, but gaming performance is rated lower at 6.0. You should be aware of reports regarding heating during gaming and charging, though owners say it is minor.

A serious concern is the Android 16 update, which some users say causes screen flickering and random restarts. Since these are unofficial units, you only get shop warranty. You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying.

Motorola Edge 50 Fusion: what buyers ask us

What is the Motorola Edge 50 Fusion price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Motorola Edge 50 Fusion in Bangladesh was ৳26,999, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there are no confirmed official units in stock. The listings we track are imports, which means you get a shop warranty instead of brand backed support. You should confirm the BTRC status with your seller.

How is the battery life and charging speed?

The phone has a 5000 mAh battery that owners rate 8.5 for efficiency and longevity. It supports 68W charging, although some users have reported minor heating while the device is charging.

Is this a good phone for gaming?

Gaming is one of the weaker points with an owner rating of 6.0. Users have reported that the device heats up during gaming sessions, though they noted it does not reach overheating levels.

How many software updates will this phone get?

The phone launched with Android 14 and is upgradable to Android 15. It is promised up to 3 major Android upgrades, but some owners report restarts and flickering after the Android 16 update.
