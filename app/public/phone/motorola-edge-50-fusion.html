Motorola Edge 50 Fusion Is it worth buying?

If you want a bright 144Hz OLED screen and a solid camera without spending a premium, the Edge 50 Fusion fits the bill.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value a high refresh OLED screen and a good camera while accepting a gray import warranty.
Consider alternatives if

What works well4

  • 6.7 inch 144Hz OLED display
  • 50MP main camera with OIS
  • 68W fast charging
  • Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 chipset

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Heating during charging and gaming
  • Android 16 update can cause restarts
  • Gaming performance only average

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch P‑OLED panel reaches 1600 nits at peak, making outdoor use easy. The 50MP main sensor with OIS captures good photos and videos, and the 32MP selfie camera is also respectable. Powered by Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 and 8GB RAM, everyday tasks feel smooth and the 5000 mAh battery lasts long according to owners.

Fast 68W charging refills the battery quickly. The phone runs near‑stock Android 14 and can be upgraded to Android 15, with up to three major OS upgrades promised. However, it arrives as a gray‑import with only the shop’s warranty, so any repair will cost you.

Read full reasoning

Some users notice heating while charging or gaming, and the Android 16 update has caused occasional restarts and screen flicker. Gaming performance is average, not a flagship level.
