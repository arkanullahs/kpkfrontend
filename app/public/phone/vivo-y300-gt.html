Is the Vivo Y300 GT worth buying?

What is good

  • 7,620 mAh battery with 90 W fast charge
  • 6.78‑inch AMOLED 144 Hz panel with 5,500 nits peak
  • Dimensity 8400 provides strong gaming performance
  • 256 GB or 512 GB storage options

What is not

  • Average photo quality in most lighting
  • Heavy and thick glass body
  • Medium amount of pre‑installed ads
  • No official brand warranty, only shop warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you value long battery life, smooth display and gaming power and are comfortable with a gray‑import that lacks brand warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a lightweight phone, top‑class camera or official warranty support.

Why we say that

The 7,620 mAh battery paired with 90 W fast charging gives you more than a day of use and a quick top‑up. The 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel refreshes at 144 Hz and can peak at 5,500 nits, so bright outdoor viewing and smooth scrolling feel natural. Dimensity 8400 delivers strong performance for gaming and multitasking, reflected in high owner ratings for gaming and overall speed.

Storage options of 256 GB or 512 GB keep large media libraries handy. The glass‑back design looks premium but adds noticeable weight and thickness, making the phone feel bulky in the hand. Camera results are average; the 50 MP sensor does not stand out in low‑light scenes.

Software runs Android 15 with OriginOS 5, bringing medium‑level ads and bloat. The device is only available as a gray‑import, so you must rely on shop warranty and verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
