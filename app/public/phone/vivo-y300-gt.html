Buying verdict

Vivo Y300 GT Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day and handle games smoothly, the Vivo Y300 GT delivers that at a low gray‑import price.

Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life, high refresh‑rate display, and solid gaming performance on a limited budget.
Consider alternatives if
You need a top‑tier camera or an official manufacturer warranty.

What works well

  • Massive 7620 mAh battery with 90 W fast charge
  • 144 Hz AMOLED display with 5500 nits peak brightness
  • Dimensity 8400 offers strong gaming performance
  • 8 GB RAM with up to 512 GB storage

Reported trade-offs

  • Camera quality is only average
  • Heavy and thick design
  • No official brand warranty
  • Medium software bloat and ads

Why we say that

Its 7620 mAh battery paired with 90 W fast charging gives exceptional endurance, often lasting more than a full day of mixed use. The 6.78" AMOLED panel runs at 144 Hz and can peak at 5500 nits, making bright outdoor viewing comfortable. Under the hood, the Dimensity 8400 and 8 GB RAM provide strong performance for gaming and multitasking, reflected in high owner scores for gaming and overall speed.

The camera setup, however, is only average; photos are decent in good light but struggle in low‑light scenes. The glass‑back design feels premium but adds noticeable weight and thickness, which some owners find bulky. There is no official Vivo warranty; you must rely on shop warranty and verify IMEI/BTRC status.

Read full reasoning

OriginOS 5 on Android 15 brings medium‑level ads and bloat, and budget models typically receive two major OS updates.
