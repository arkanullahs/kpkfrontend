Buying verdict

Realme C85 Is it worth buying?

The Realme C85 is a specialized device that prioritizes extreme battery longevity over display quality and gaming performance.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize a rugged build and multi-day battery life over display clarity and gaming power.
Consider alternatives if
You want a sharp Full HD or AMOLED display and a clean software experience without bloatware.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Massive 7000 mAh battery lasts two days
  • Rugged IP69 and military standard build
  • Smooth 144Hz display refresh rate
  • Reliable performance for daily tasks

Reported trade-offs4

  • Low HD+ screen resolution
  • Excessive bloatware and ads
  • Poor low light camera performance
  • Single down-firing speaker

Why we say that

Living with this phone means you will rarely reach for a charger, as the massive 7000 mAh battery easily lasts two days and supports reverse charging. However, owners frequently express frustration with the HD+ resolution and the lack of an AMOLED panel, which makes the screen feel outdated for the price. While the Android 15 software is smooth, the experience is marred by excessive bloatware that users find intrusive.

The build quality is a standout feature, boasting an IP69 rating and military standard certification, making it rugged enough for harsh environments. Performance is reliable for daily tasks, but the chipset is not suited for heavy gaming. You should be prepared for average camera results, particularly in low light where edge detection struggles.

Read full reasoning

Ultimately, this phone is a tool for those who value endurance and durability above all else, provided you can tolerate the low resolution and the pre-installed software clutter.

Does the Realme C85 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

Yes, the Realme C85 is available as an official BD unit. This means you are covered by the brand's own official warranty and their service network across the country.

Is the Realme C85 good for playing heavy games?

The phone is not recommended for dedicated gaming. While the Dimensity 6300 chipset is reliable for daily tasks and runs cool, owners report that it is not suitable for demanding gaming sessions.

How long will the Realme C85 receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 15 with Realme UI 6.0. It is scheduled to receive between 2 to 3 OS updates during its lifecycle, though users should expect a medium to high amount of bloatware.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The 50MP main camera is considered average by owners. It struggles significantly with low light photography and often faces issues with edge detection when capturing subjects.
