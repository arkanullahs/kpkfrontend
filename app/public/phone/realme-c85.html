Realme C85 Is it worth buying?

The Realme C85’s biggest advantage is its massive 7000 mAh battery that can easily last two days of normal use.

  • 7000 mAh battery with multi‑day endurance
  • 6.8‑inch 144 Hz IPS display

  • 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs4

  • HD+ resolution feels soft on large screen
  • Heavy bloatware and ads
  • Basic Dimensity 6300 limits gaming
  • No stereo speakers

Why we say that

At 22,499 to ৳25,499 the phone sits in the budget segment and still offers 5G, a 6.8‑inch 144 Hz IPS panel and an IP69 rugged rating. The display is only HD+ and feels soft on the large screen, and the bottom chin is noticeably thick, which many owners point out. Realme UI 6.0 runs Android 15 but comes with medium‑high bloatware and ads that affect the smoothness of everyday tasks.

The 50 MP camera scores 5.5 out of 10, delivering decent daylight shots but soft low‑light images and no ultrawide option. Performance is driven by a Dimensity 6300 chipset, enough for casual apps but not for demanding games, and the phone lacks stereo speakers. Overall it feels like a solid battery‑first device with several compromises that keep it in the entry‑level range.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 7000 mAh battery is rated for multi‑day endurance, and reviewers call it exceptional. With moderate use you can expect more than a full day without charging, and the 45 W charger refills it quickly.

Can the phone handle modern games smoothly?

Gaming performance is limited by the Dimensity 6300 chipset and 6‑8 GB RAM. Owners rate gaming at 5.5 out of 10 and say it works for casual titles but struggles with demanding games.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor is rated 5.5 for camera and reviewers call low‑light photos soft. It lacks an ultrawide lens and struggles when lighting is poor, so expect average results in dim environments.

Does the phone receive official warranty and software updates?

The phone is sold through official channels, so it carries Realme’s own warranty and can be serviced at the brand’s network in Bangladesh. Realme promises two to three major Android updates for this model.
