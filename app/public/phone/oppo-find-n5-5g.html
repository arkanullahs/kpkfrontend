Is the OPPO Find N5 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 8.12 inch 2100 nits LTPO display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite performance
  • 5600 mAh battery with 80W fast charge
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • 7 core CPU instead of full 8 core
  • 8 MP ultrawide camera is poor
  • Very thin body snaps easily
  • No official manufacturer warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large bright foldable with strong gaming performance and can accept a gray import warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a reliable ultrawide camera or official brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 8.12 inch LTPO OLED panel reaches 2100 nits at 120Hz, making outdoor use easy. The Snapdragon 8 Elite drives 12 to 16 GB RAM smoothly, and gaming feels fast even though the chip runs a 7 core configuration that can heat up under load. Battery life is praised; the 5600 mAh cell and 80 W charger let you run health monitoring apps all day without a charge.

Camera wise the 50 MP main sensor with OIS and 3× telephoto is solid, but the 8 MP ultrawide is far below what competitors offer. The phone is very thin and owners report it can snap like a dorito. It arrives as a gray import, so only the shop’s warranty applies and repairs are paid.
