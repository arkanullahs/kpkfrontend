Samsung Galaxy S23 Ultra Is it worth buying?

The Samsung Galaxy S23 Ultra is a three-year-old device that currently struggles to justify its ৳1,79,999 price tag when newer models like the S25 Ultra are available for significantly less.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Stunning 6.8 inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display
  • Versatile 200MP primary camera sensor
  • Powerful Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset
  • Polished One UI software experience

Reported trade-offs4

  • Recurring green or pink vertical display lines
  • Significant battery degradation and drain
  • Overheating and throttling under load
  • Persistent camera shutter lag

Why we say that

While the 200MP camera and 120Hz display remain impressive, living with this phone involves managing significant hardware risks. Owners frequently report frustrating issues including green or pink vertical lines appearing on the screen and noticeable shutter lag when taking photos. Battery performance is a major concern, with long-term users experiencing significant drain and degradation.

The device also suffers from overheating during gaming or prolonged use, which often leads to performance throttling. Because the phone is three years old, you should expect very short or no remaining software update life. While the build quality feels premium, reports of adhesive failure after two years suggest long-term durability concerns.

Given that the newer S25 Ultra sells for ৳99,999, this older model is difficult to recommend for any buyer looking for reliable performance.
