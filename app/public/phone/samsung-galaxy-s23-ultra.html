What works well

  • 200MP main camera with optical zoom
  • 6.8 inch 120Hz Dynamic AMOLED 2X display
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 performance
  • 45W wired charging

Reported trade-offs

  • Battery degrades quickly after two years
  • Overheating during gaming causes throttling
  • Green line display issue after updates
  • Shutter lag in camera

Why we say that

The 6.8 inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X panel shines at 1750 nits and 120Hz, making media consumption a pleasure. The Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset provides smooth performance in apps and games, though prolonged load often triggers noticeable heat and occasional throttling. The 200MP main sensor captures detailed photos, yet many owners report a time gap between pressing the shutter and the picture being taken.

Battery life starts strong but degrades noticeably after two years, with some users seeing only five hours of use. Charging at 45W is adequate but feels slow compared to newer fast‑charging standards. The phone runs Android 13 with One UI 8.5 and will receive up to four major Android upgrades, but being three years old means little update window remains.

How is the battery life on the Galaxy S23 Ultra after a couple of years?

The 5000 mAh battery feels strong when new, but many owners report significant degradation after two years, with daily use dropping to around five hours. Expect reduced endurance compared to when the phone launched.

Will the phone overheat while gaming and does it affect performance?

Yes, the device is widely reported to get hot during extended gaming sessions. The heat can trigger throttling, which lowers frame rates and may affect smoothness, even though the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 is still powerful.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions and is there any lag?

The 200MP sensor with f/1.7 aperture captures detailed low‑light shots, but many users notice a noticeable delay between pressing the shutter button and the photo being taken, which can be frustrating.

Is the official warranty valid in Bangladesh and how many Android updates will I get?

The phone is sold as an official Bangladesh unit, so Samsung’s brand warranty and service network apply. It runs Android 13 with One UI 8.5 and will receive up to four major Android upgrades, but being three years old means few updates remain.
