Buying verdict

Realme C100x Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run for days without charging, the Realme C100x delivers that and nothing more.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize battery endurance and a rugged, cheap large‑screen device over speed and camera quality.
Consider alternatives if
You need fast gaming, low‑light photography, or a premium display.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 8000 mAh battery lasting 2‑3 days
  • 45 W fast charging in about 1.5 hours
  • Military‑grade shock resistance with IP64 rating
  • Smooth 120 Hz refresh on a large screen

Reported trade-offs4

  • Sluggish Unisoc T7250 and eMMC 5.1 storage
  • 50 MP camera gives over‑saturated, oil‑painting results
  • 720p water‑drop notch screen feels outdated
  • Hybrid proximity sensor misreads causing accidental touches

Why we say that

Its 8000 mAh battery easily gives 13 hours of screen‑on time and can stretch to two or three days for most users. The 45 W charger refills the huge cell in about one and a half hours, which is a relief when you finally find a power outlet. The device feels solid, with military‑grade shock resistance, an IP64 rating and a large 6.8‑inch panel that runs a buttery smooth 120 Hz refresh, even though the resolution stays at 720p.

The software runs Android 16 with a relatively clean UI but is weighed down by medium‑high ads and only two to three major OS updates. The main camera produces bright but over‑saturated, oil‑painting style photos and struggles in low light. Performance feels sluggish due to the Unisoc T7250 and eMMC 5.1 storage, and the hybrid proximity sensor can misread, causing accidental touches during calls.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 8000 mAh battery gives about 13 hours of screen‑on time and can comfortably last two to three days for typical daily use. With the included 45 W charger the phone refills in roughly one and a half hours.

Can the Realme C100x handle mobile games smoothly?

Gaming performance is limited. The Unisoc T7250 chipset together with eMMC 5.1 storage makes games feel laggy and frame drops are common, especially in graphics‑intensive titles. Casual games run okay but expect sluggishness.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor produces bright images but they look over‑saturated and have an oil‑painting effect. In low‑light situations the camera struggles to capture detail, resulting in noisy and blurry photos.

What warranty and software updates does the phone get?

Official units sold at ৳20,599 include the brand warranty; gray‑import listings do not and repairs are paid. Realme promises two to three Android OS upgrades, so you can expect updates through Android 17 at most.
