Is the Realme C100x worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 8000 mAh battery capacity
  • Large 6.8 inch 120Hz display
  • Decent 900 nits peak brightness
  • Includes a headphone jack

What is not

  • Weak chipset with poor sustained performance
  • Medium-high bloatware and ads
  • Low owner ratings for camera and gaming
  • Poor build quality

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize extreme battery endurance above all else and do not plan to play games or run demanding applications.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you want a device that handles gaming, multitasking, or heavy sustained loads without performance drops.

Why we say that

Owners report that the massive 8000 mAh battery provides extreme longevity, which is the standout feature of this 2026 release. However, the user experience is hampered by a weak Unisoc T7250 chipset that struggles with sustained loads and gaming. Owners have given the phone low ratings for performance, gaming, and ease of use.

You will also encounter medium-high levels of bloatware and ads within the Realme UI. While the 6.8 inch 120Hz display offers decent brightness, the build quality and camera output receive poor feedback from those who have used the device daily. If you need a phone that stays powered for a very long time and you do not care about speed or high-end photography, this might be a consideration.

Otherwise, the poor sustained performance and software clutter make it a difficult recommendation for most users.
