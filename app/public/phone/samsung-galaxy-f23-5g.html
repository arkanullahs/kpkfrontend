Is the Samsung Galaxy F23 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz refresh rate display
  • 5000 mAh battery with 25 W fast charge
  • 50 MP main camera
  • headphone jack and FM radio

What is not

  • TFT LCD with poor viewing angles
  • Frequent motherboard and boot‑loop failures
  • Wi‑Fi and hotspot may overheat

Buy it if If you need an inexpensive 5G phone with a big battery and can accept occasional hardware glitches, this model may suit you.

Skip it if If you expect a durable build, reliable Wi‑Fi, or long software support, look elsewhere.

Why we say that

The Galaxy F23 5G offers a 6.6‑inch 120 Hz TFT LCD, a 50 MP main sensor and a 5,000 mAh battery that can be topped up with 25 W fast charging. Android 12 runs One UI 6 and can be upgraded to Android 14, giving a clean and well‑optimised software feel. Everyday tasks and casual games run okay thanks to the Snapdragon 750G and 6 GB RAM.

However owners report frequent motherboard failures that cause boot loops, and Wi‑Fi or hotspot can overheat and stop working. The TFT panel shows dull colours and narrow viewing angles, and the battery loses noticeable capacity after a few years. At ৳33,499 the phone is cheap, but the newer Galaxy F36 5G is cheaper and more reliable.

Long‑term software updates are unlikely as the device is already four years old.

Pratima Adhikari · Samsung Galaxy F23 5G Full Review! ↗

Pratima Adhikari concluded that the Samsung Galaxy F23 5G is a decent budget phone led by good daylight primary cameras, an ergonomic lightweight design, and capable everyday performance from the Snapdragon 750G processor. However, she was let down by the transition from an AMOLED panel to a dimmer LCD screen, the lack of stereo speakers, poor night photography, and the missing wall charger in the box, noting that users focused on multimedia consumption might prefer competitors with AMOLED displays.

Gadgets 360 · Samsung Galaxy F23 5G Review: A Worthy Contender? ↗

Gadgets 360 praised the Galaxy F23 5G for its excellent multi-day battery endurance, dependable daylight image capture, and strong commitment to future software updates. On the other hand, the reviewer criticized the display for washing out under bright sunlight, pointed out the absence of stereo speakers or an IP water-resistance rating, and encountered noticeable UI sluggishness and gaming stutter on the 4GB RAM configuration, strongly advising prospective buyers to choose the 6GB variant instead.
