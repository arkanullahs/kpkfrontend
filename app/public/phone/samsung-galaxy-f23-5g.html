Samsung Galaxy F23 Is it worth buying?

The Samsung Galaxy F23 is a risky purchase due to widespread reports of motherboard failures and boot loop issues that often render the device unusable.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You are looking for a very cheap secondary device for casual tasks and are fully aware of the significant risks regarding hardware failure.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable primary phone that will last for several years without suffering from motherboard or connectivity issues.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Clean and well-optimized One UI software
  • Capable performance for casual gaming
  • Fast 120Hz display refresh rate
  • Includes a headphone jack and FM radio

Reported trade-offs4

  • Widespread motherboard and boot loop failures
  • Dated TFT display with poor viewing angles
  • Weak selfie camera and mediocre main sensor
  • Wi-Fi and hotspot hardware failure issues

Why we say that

While the phone offers a 120Hz display and 5G connectivity, owners frequently struggle with hardware longevity. The TFT LCD panel suffers from poor viewing angles and brightness, which makes outdoor use difficult. Users report that the Wi-Fi and hotspot hardware can fail, often accompanied by overheating.

Although the One UI software is clean and well-optimized, the device is now 4 years old, meaning it is nearing the end of its software support life. The camera performance is mediocre, with a particularly weak selfie sensor. Many owners find that the battery degrades significantly after 3 years of use.

Read full reasoning

Given the high cost of motherboard repairs and the questionable build quality, this phone represents poor long-term value for anyone looking for a reliable daily driver.

Is the Samsung Galaxy F23 good for gaming?

The Snapdragon 750G chipset is capable of handling casual gaming. However, owners report that hardware longevity is highly questionable. You should consider the risk of motherboard failure and overheating before relying on this device for extended gaming sessions.

How long will the Samsung Galaxy F23 receive software updates?

The phone was released in 2022 and is now 4 years old. While it runs on Android 12 and is upgradable to Android 14, you should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point in its lifecycle.

What are the common hardware problems reported by owners?

Owners frequently report motherboard failures and persistent boot loops. There are also reports of Wi-Fi and hotspot hardware failing, which is often linked to overheating. Additionally, the battery is known to show significant degradation after 3 years of use.
