Is the iQOO Z9s 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Excellent 5500 mAh battery endurance
  • Bright 1800 nits AMOLED display
  • Smooth daily performance with Dimensity 7300
  • Includes FM radio functionality

What is not

  • No 4K 60fps video recording support
  • Debated build quality and average camera
  • Only gray-import stock with shop warranty
  • Reports of post-update software bugs

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize long battery life and a bright display for daily use at a competitive price point.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you require official brand warranty support or need high-end video recording features like 4K 60fps.

Why we say that

Owners consistently praise the 5500 mAh battery for its longevity, making it a reliable companion for long days. While the 6.77 inch AMOLED display is generally well-regarded for its 1800 nits peak brightness, some users have noted a lack of sharpness. Performance is smooth for daily tasks, though gamers should be aware of reports regarding post-update stutters.

The camera setup is considered average, and users are often disappointed by the lack of 4K 60fps video recording. Because only gray-import stock is currently available, you must rely on shop warranties rather than official brand support. You should also be prepared for Funtouch OS to include some bloatware and potential bugs following software updates.

It is a functional device for those who value battery life above all else, provided you are comfortable with the risks associated with unofficial imports.

Does the iQOO Z9s 5G have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there is no official brand warranty available for this device in Bangladesh. You will only find gray-import stock, which means you must rely on a shop warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before making a purchase.

How long will the iQOO Z9s 5G receive software updates?

The phone runs on Android 14 and is promised to receive up to 2 major Android upgrades. It uses Funtouch 14, though users have reported experiencing some bugs after installing software updates.

Is the iQOO Z9s 5G good for gaming?

The phone features a Dimensity 7300 chipset that handles daily tasks smoothly. While it is capable, some owners have reported experiencing stutters during gaming sessions after recent software updates.

What is the camera quality like on this phone?

Owner feedback regarding the camera is mixed, with many describing it as average. While the 50MP main sensor includes OIS, users have noted that it lacks advanced video features like 4K 60fps recording, limiting it to 30fps.
