What works well

  • Efficient 6000 mAh battery
  • Aluminum frame with no antenna lines
  • Fast 45W charging
  • Android 15 software

Reported trade-offs

  • No audio jack or NFC
  • Medium to high bloatware
  • No official brand warranty
  • No FM radio

Why we say that

Owners appreciate the efficient 6000 mAh battery and the aluminum frame without antenna lines. However, the daily experience is marred by the total absence of an audio jack, NFC, and FM radio. You will deal with medium to high levels of bloatware and ads.

The software is Android 15 with Realme UI 6.0. Performance is decent for the price, but the build and camera quality only receive average ratings from users. Be aware that this is a gray import.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No official brand backed warranty is available for this model. The units found in shops we track are gray imports. You will only receive a shop warranty, so you should confirm the BTRC status with the seller.

How is the battery life and charging speed?

The battery life is a strong point with a 6000 mAh capacity that owners describe as efficient. It supports 45W charging to help refill the large battery faster.

Can I use this phone for gaming?

Owners give gaming a rating of 7.0 out of 10. It uses the Dimensity 6400 chipset and a 120Hz IPS LCD display to handle tasks.

How many software updates will I get?

The phone runs on Android 15 with Realme UI 6.0. You can expect 2 to 3 OS updates over the life of the device.
