Buying verdict

OPPO Reno15 Pro Mini Is it worth buying?

The OPPO Reno15 Pro Mini is a compact powerhouse that excels in battery endurance but suffers from severe thermal management issues.

Choose it if
You prioritize a compact device with massive battery life and a high-quality display for daily tasks.
Consider alternatives if
You are a mobile gamer or require professional camera features like RAW mode.

What works well

  • Exceptional 6200 mAh battery endurance
  • Very bright 1800 nits AMOLED display
  • Premium build with IP68/IP69 rating
  • Great detail and zoom from 200MP camera

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe GPU thermal throttling during gaming
  • Missing RAW and 50MP camera modes
  • No wireless charging support
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

Owners report that the Dimensity 8450 chipset experiences drastic GPU throttling under load, dropping performance by as much as 35 percent. While the 6200 mAh battery provides exceptional longevity, the device is not suitable for gaming due to this heavy heat throttling. The 200MP camera captures great detail and zoom, though users are frustrated by the current lack of RAW and 50MP shooting modes in ColorOS 16.

You get a very bright 1800 nits AMOLED display and a premium build with IP68/IP69 protection. However, the absence of wireless charging and the presence of pre-installed bloatware are notable drawbacks. Since all units in Bangladesh are gray-import models built for the Indian market, you must rely entirely on shop-provided support rather than manufacturer coverage.
