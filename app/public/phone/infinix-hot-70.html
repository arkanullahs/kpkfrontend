Buying verdict

Infinix Hot 70 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day without charging, the Infinix Hot 70 delivers that and more.

Choose it if
You value long battery life, fast charging and an official warranty over a high‑resolution display.
Consider alternatives if
You need a sharp screen, good low‑light photos or a headphone jack.

What works well

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts long
  • 45 W fast charging
  • 120 Hz refresh rate
  • IP65 water resistance

Reported trade-offs

  • 720p screen looks dull
  • Low‑light camera is poor
  • Bloatware and ads
  • Missing headphone jack

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh battery and 45 W fast charging keep you online for many hours and top up quickly. Daily tasks feel smooth thanks to the Helio G100 Ultimate chipset and 120 Hz refresh, but the 720p LCD looks dull in bright sunlight and struggles with fine detail. Daylight photos are acceptable with the 50 MP sensor, yet low‑light shots are noisy and portrait blur looks artificial.

The device comes with an official Bangladeshi warranty and a service network, but it is packed with pre‑installed apps that push ads and it lacks a headphone jack that many budget users expect. Overall it feels like a modest upgrade of the Hot 60 and may feel overpriced for the specifications.

How long will the battery last with heavy daily use?

The 6000 mAh cell is rated very high by owners and can easily cover a full day of heavy browsing, video streaming and gaming, especially with the optional bypass charging mode for gamers.

Can I play games like Free Fire smoothly?

The Helio G100 Ultimate chipset and 120 Hz panel handle casual gaming well, but owners notice occasional frame drops in Free Fire at 90 fps and light throttling in PUBG, so performance is decent but not flawless.

Will the phone receive Android updates for how many years?

Infinix promises Android 16 with up to three major Android upgrades, meaning you can expect at least three years of major OS updates, plus security patches during that period.

Yes, the phone is sold through official channels in Bangladesh, so it carries the brand’s own warranty and can be serviced at any authorized Infinix service center listed by the company.
