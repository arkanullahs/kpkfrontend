Infinix Hot 70 Is it worth buying?

The Infinix Hot 70 is a device defined by its massive battery life, but it is held back by significant compromises in display quality and software experience.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life above all else and do not mind a lower resolution display for basic daily tasks.
Consider alternatives if
You are a gamer, a photography enthusiast, or someone who expects a clean software experience without ads.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Exceptional 6000 mAh battery endurance
  • Fast 45W charging support
  • Sturdy build with IP65 rating
  • Up to 3 major OS updates

Reported trade-offs4

  • Low 720p display resolution
  • Heavy bloatware and notification ads
  • Lack of 3.5mm headphone jack
  • Poor camera dynamic range

Why we say that

Owners consistently report that the 720p resolution is unacceptable for a phone at this price point, making the screen feel outdated. While the 6000 mAh battery provides exceptional endurance, the daily experience is marred by heavy bloatware and persistent notification ads within XOS 16. Performance is adequate for basic tasks, yet the Helio G100 chipset struggles with throttling during gaming sessions.

Users are particularly frustrated by the lack of a 3.5mm headphone jack, which many consider a standard requirement for budget devices in 2026. The camera system is basic, often failing to capture sufficient detail in low-light conditions or manage dynamic range effectively. While the official warranty and sturdy IP65 build offer some peace of mind, the base model with 4GB of RAM suffers from noticeable lag during simple games and multitasking.

How long will the Infinix Hot 70 receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 16 and is promised to receive up to 3 major OS updates. However, users should be aware that the XOS 16 software remains heavily bloated with preloaded apps and notification ads.

Is the Infinix Hot 70 good for gaming?

The Helio G100 chipset is adequate for daily use, but it throttles under heavy gaming loads. Owners have reported that the 4GB RAM base model experiences lag even during simple games, resulting in a low gaming rating of 5.5 out of 10.

Does the camera perform well in low light?

The 50MP main camera is considered a basic shooter. Owners report that it struggles significantly with dynamic range and fails to capture sufficient detail when shooting in low-light conditions.
