Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Elite drives smooth gaming and the 120 W charger refills the 7060 mAh cell in minutes. The LTPO OLED screen stays bright up to 6800 nits and shows vivid colors. The 200 MP sensor captures detailed shots, yet default colors look funky and stitching stays at 24 MP despite the high resolution.

The built‑in gimbal adds creative options but can lose calibration after a drop and saps noticeable battery power. The phone arrives as a China‑ROM gray import, so there is no manufacturer warranty and Google services are missing unless you install them yourself. Repairs are paid and you must verify IMEI status with the seller.

How long will the battery last in everyday use?

The 7060 mAh battery gives long endurance for regular tasks, but the gimbal motor consumes noticeable power when active. Users report solid day‑long use, yet heavy gimbal shooting can shorten the run time noticeably.

Will the phone run high‑end games smoothly?

Yes, the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 delivers smooth frame rates in demanding titles. Owner ratings show gaming performance at 8.8 out of 10 and no severe throttling, making it suitable for mobile gamers.

Is the camera good in low light and does it need Google services?

The 200 MP sensor captures detailed images, but default colors appear funky and low‑light output can be inconsistent. The China‑ROM version lacks Google apps, so you must install GMS yourself if you rely on Google services.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

All units are gray imports with no manufacturer warranty; the shop provides its own limited warranty and any repairs are paid. Honor typically offers three to four Android updates on flagships, but update timing may vary.
