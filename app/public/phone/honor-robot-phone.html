Buying verdict

Honor Robot Phone Is it worth buying?

If you can tolerate a fragile, hot, and software‑heavy phone, the Honor Robot offers unmatched camera stabilization and raw performance.

Choose it if
You need top video stability and can handle extra maintenance.
Consider alternatives if
You want a reliable daily driver with official support.

What works well

  • 200 MP gimbal‑stabilized camera
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 speed
  • 7060 mAh battery capacity
  • 6800 nits peak brightness

Reported trade-offs

  • Mechanical gimbal prone to damage
  • Phone gets very hot under load
  • Chinese ROM lacks Google services
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

The 200 MP sensor paired with a built‑in gimbal delivers video that stays steady even when you move, and the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 keeps games running at high frame rates without stutter. The 6.31‑inch LTPO OLED screen reaches a peak of 6800 nits, so outdoor viewing is clear, and the 7060 mAh battery gives a long baseline, though the gimbal motors drain it quickly and the device can become uncomfortable to hold when it heats up. Build quality feels premium, but the moving gimbal adds a point of failure and a single drop could damage the mechanism.

The software runs MagicOS 10 on a Chinese ROM, so Google services are not pre‑installed and some bloat remains. Updates are expected for three to four generations, and the only warranty is the shop’s limited cover.

Will the battery last a full day with normal use?

The 7060 mAh cell gives a long baseline, but the gimbal motors draw a lot of power. Owners rate battery 8.2, and many notice the phone drains faster when the stabilization is active, especially during video shooting.

How does it perform for gaming?

Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 delivers top‑tier speed and sustained performance. Gaming ratings are 8.8 and performance scores 9.6, so most titles run smoothly at high settings, though the device may become warm after long sessions.

Is the camera good in low light?

The 200 MP sensor has a large 1/1.28‑inch size and f/1.6 aperture, which helps in dim environments. Video stays steady thanks to the gimbal, but still photos can show color shifts that need post‑processing.

Does it come with an official warranty and how many Android updates will it receive?

All units are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; the shop provides its own limited cover and any repair costs are paid by the owner. Honor promises three to four major Android upgrades for flagships.
