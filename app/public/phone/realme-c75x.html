Is the Realme C75x worth buying?

What is good

  • Exceptional tank-like build quality
  • Superb battery endurance
  • Efficient 45W fast charging
  • Impressively cool operating temperatures

What is not

  • Underwhelming performance and laggy software
  • Low 720p screen resolution
  • Poor camera quality in high-contrast lighting
  • Lack of a 3.5mm headphone jack

Buy it if You need a highly durable phone that can survive drops and water exposure and you prioritize battery life above all else.

Skip it if You care about display sharpness, gaming performance, or taking high-quality photos.

Why we say that

While the build quality is exceptional, owners report that the phone feels sluggish due to the weak Helio G81 Ultra chipset. You will notice lag during app switching and poor performance in games like Free Fire. The display is a major point of contention, as users find the 720p resolution and thick bottom chin unacceptable for a phone at this price point.

The camera system also falls short, producing grainy zoom shots and soft selfies while struggling in high-contrast lighting. Despite these flaws, the battery life is a standout feature, offering over 8 hours of screen-on time and lasting 1.5 days on a single charge. The device remains impressively cool under pressure, but many owners feel the phone is overpriced for the hardware provided.

If you prioritize extreme durability over speed and display quality, this might be a consideration, but most users will find the performance limitations frustrating for daily use.
