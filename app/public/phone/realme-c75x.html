Is the Realme C75x worth buying?

What is good

  • Exceptional tank-like build quality
  • Superb battery life with 1.5 days endurance
  • Efficient 45W charging under 40 minutes
  • Impressively cool operating temperatures

What is not

  • Unacceptable 720p display resolution
  • Underwhelming performance and laggy software
  • Poor camera quality in high-contrast lighting
  • Lack of a 3.5mm headphone jack

Buy it if You need a highly durable device that can survive extreme physical drops and water exposure.

Skip it if You care about display sharpness, gaming performance, or a smooth software experience.

Why we say that

Owners praise the extreme durability, noting the IP69 rating allows it to survive two-story drops and long periods in water. However, the actual experience of using the phone is marred by a sluggish Helio G81 Ultra chipset and a low-resolution display that users find unacceptable. While the battery life is superb, lasting 1.5 days with over 8 hours of screen time, the software feels slow and unresponsive.

The camera quality is another major point of contention, with users reporting grainy zoom and poor performance in high-contrast lighting. Many owners feel the phone is overpriced for the hardware provided, as the impressive build quality does not compensate for the laggy app switching and poor gaming performance. If you prioritize a phone that can survive physical abuse over one that runs apps smoothly, this might be a consideration, but most users will find the hardware limitations frustrating for daily use.

How long does the battery last on the Realme C75x?

The battery life is considered a major strength of this device. Owners report superb endurance with the 5600 mAh battery, typically lasting 1.5 days of usage. You can expect over 8 hours of screen-on time before needing to recharge.

Is the Realme C75x good for playing games like Free Fire?

The phone is not recommended for gaming. Owners report that the Helio G81 Ultra chipset provides underwhelming performance. Specifically, users have noted poor performance when playing Free Fire and general lag when switching between applications.

How many years of software updates will this phone receive?

The Realme C75x comes with Android 15 and Realme UI 6.0 out of the box. The manufacturer provides support for 2 to 3 OS updates. Be aware that the software can feel sluggish due to the weak hardware.

Does the camera perform well in low light conditions?

The camera system is a weak point for this device. Owners report that the 50MP main camera struggles significantly with high-contrast lighting. Additionally, users have complained about grainy zoom and soft selfies, making it a poor choice for photography enthusiasts.
