Google Pixel 7 Is it worth buying?

The Google Pixel 7 is a compact, premium device that excels at photography but struggles with thermal management and network reliability.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a premium, compact camera phone for photography and do not mind using a cooler for gaming.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable network connectivity, long-lasting battery life, or a phone that stays cool during heavy tasks.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Best-in-class main camera performance
  • Clean and bloat-free stock Android
  • Premium, compact, and durable design
  • IP68 water and dust resistance

Reported trade-offs4

  • Prone to overheating during gaming
  • Very slow charging speeds
  • Inconsistent battery endurance
  • Recurring network connectivity failures

Why we say that

Living with this phone means enjoying a clean, bloat-free Android experience, yet you must be prepared for inconsistent battery life and slow charging speeds. Owners frequently report that the Tensor G2 chipset causes the device to overheat during gaming, often requiring a cooler to maintain performance. Network connectivity is a significant concern, with reports of signal failures in certain regions and rapid battery drain caused by the modem when searching for a signal.

While the camera remains best-in-class for point-and-shoot photography, the hardware is now 4 years old. You should be aware that software support is nearing its end, and the device may face thermal throttling during heavy use. It is a specialized choice for those who prioritize camera quality over gaming performance and battery endurance.

Does the Pixel 7 get official warranty support in Bangladesh?

The brand warranty applies to the official unit only, which is priced at ৳45,999. The cheaper gray-import units, starting at ৳35,490, have no manufacturer cover. If you take an imported unit to a service centre, they will only accept it as a paid repair.

Is the Pixel 7 good for gaming?

The phone is smooth for daily tasks, but owners report that it is prone to overheating during sustained gaming sessions. You will likely experience thermal throttling, and some users suggest using a cooler to manage the heat while gaming.

How long will the Pixel 7 receive software updates?

The device was released in 2022 and is upgradable to Android 16. However, because the phone is now 4 years old, you should expect short or no remaining software-update life at this point.

Why do users complain about the battery and network?

Owners report mediocre battery endurance with inconsistent drain. The Tensor G2 chipset has a known modem weakness that causes rapid battery drain in areas with weak signals. Additionally, some users have reported complete network connectivity failures in certain regions.
