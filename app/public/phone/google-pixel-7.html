Buying verdict

Google Pixel 7 Is it worth buying?

If you want a phone that delivers excellent photos and a clean Android experience, the Pixel 7 can still satisfy you, but expect compromises on battery and network reliability.

Choose it if
You value camera quality and a bloat‑free Android experience more than top‑tier performance or flawless network connectivity.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable 5G, long battery endurance, or future‑proof software support.

What works well

  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • 6.3 inch 90 Hz AMOLED display
  • Premium compact design with IP68
  • Clean stock Android with long‑term updates

Reported trade-offs

  • Battery life mediocre and slow charging
  • Frequent overheating under load
  • 5G and VoLTE often fail regionally
  • Modem drains battery on weak signal

Why we say that

The 6.3 inch AMOLED screen feels bright with 1400‑nit peaks and smooth 90 Hz motion, while the 50 MP main sensor produces sharp, well‑colored stills even in low light. The compact body feels premium and the IP68 rating adds peace of mind against water. Software updates will continue for a few more months, but the four‑year‑old device is already near the end of its update cycle.

Owners report that the 4355 mAh battery feels mediocre, charging at 20 W is noticeably slow, and the phone can become warm during gaming or heavy use. Regional 5G, VoLTE and modem performance are also inconsistent, especially outside Dhaka. Consider these trade‑offs before spending a month’s salary on the unit.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 4355 mAh battery gives a rating of 6.0 for endurance, which many owners describe as mediocre. With 5G active and moderate use you will likely need to charge daily, and the 20 W charger is considered very slow by current standards.

Is the Pixel 7 good for gaming?

The phone handles everyday apps smoothly, but gaming performance is rated 6.0. Under heavy load the Tensor G2 tends to throttle and become warm, so extended gaming sessions may feel throttled and uncomfortable.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor with a 1/1.31" size, f/1.9 aperture and OIS delivers industry‑leading point‑and‑shoot stills. Reviewers praise its color science and contrast, and it maintains a high camera rating of 9.2 even in dim environments.
