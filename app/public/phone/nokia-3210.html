Is the Nokia 3210 worth buying?

What is good

  • No bloatware or ads
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • Has FM radio
  • Official warranty available

What is not

  • Low resolution 2MP camera
  • Small TFT display
  • No 5G connectivity

Buy it if You want a simple 2024 feature phone without ads.

Skip it if You need a smartphone with 5G or a high quality camera.

Why we say that

It is a 2024 release that uses a small 2.4 inch TFT display and a Unisoc T107 chipset. You get a headphone jack and FM radio for basic entertainment. There are no ads or bloatware to slow it down.

Be very careful with where you buy it. The cheapest listings are gray imports with no manufacturer cover. If you take a gray import to a service centre, they will only treat it as a paid repair.

The official unit costs ৳6,600 and includes a brand warranty. This is a simple feature phone and not a modern smartphone.
