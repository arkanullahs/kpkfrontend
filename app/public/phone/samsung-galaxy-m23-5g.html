Samsung Galaxy M23 5G Is it worth buying?

If you need a 5G phone with a big battery and a 50 MP camera for under ৳20,000, the Galaxy M23 5G can deliver that basic experience.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a cheap 5G phone with a big battery and decent camera for everyday use.
Consider alternatives if
You need the latest software updates or high end gaming performance.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 120Hz smooth display
  • 5000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 50 MP main camera captures detailed photos
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included

Reported trade-offs4

  • Thermal throttling causes lag in games
  • Software updates likely stopped after four years
  • Gray import may lack Samsung brand warranty
  • TFT LCD is less vibrant than AMOLED

Why we say that

The 6.6" 120Hz TFT LCD is smooth enough for scrolling and video, while the 5000 mAh cell keeps you going through a typical day. The Snapdragon 750G handles everyday apps well, but it heats up and throttles during longer gaming or multitasking, leading to noticeable lag. The 50 MP main sensor captures detailed shots in good light, yet low‑light performance suffers due to the small sensor.

Being a four‑year‑old gray import, it lacks a Samsung brand warranty and is unlikely to receive further major Android updates. Overall it feels solid for casual use, but power users will notice its limits.

Samsung Galaxy M23 5G: what buyers ask us

What is the Samsung Galaxy M23 5G price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Samsung Galaxy M23 5G in Bangladesh was ৳19,990, checked 24 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

How long will the battery last on a full charge?

The 5000 mAh battery can easily last a full day of typical use such as browsing, social media and video playback. Heavy gaming or continuous 5G streaming will reduce the time, but you should still get around 8 to 10 hours.

Will this phone handle gaming without lag?

The Snapdragon 750G chipset can run many casual games smoothly, but the device is known for thermal throttling that causes noticeable lag and stuttering during longer gaming sessions. Expect performance to drop after about 15 minutes of intensive play.

How good is the camera in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor has an f/1.8 aperture which helps in dim environments, but the small 1/4.0" sensor size limits light intake. Photos will be usable but may show noise and reduced detail compared to brighter scenes.

Does the phone come with an official Samsung warranty?

The listings found are gray import stock, so they only offer a shop warranty rather than a Samsung brand warranty. You should verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.
