What works well4

  • 50MP main camera
  • 5200 mAh battery with strong endurance

Reported trade-offs4

  • Screen freezes on some apps
  • Gaming performance is very weak
  • Camera struggles in low light
  • No official brand warranty

Why we say that

The 6.67 inch IPS LCD with 90Hz refresh feels smooth for browsing and video, and the 50MP main sensor captures decent daylight shots. Android 15 runs near stock with low bloat, so the system feels clean. Owners praise the sturdy rear feel and the convenience of a headphone jack.

The 5200 mAh cell truly endures, but the Helio G81 Extreme chipset shows its limits in gaming and heavy multitasking. Screen freezes have been reported by several users, and the camera loses detail in low light. There is no official brand warranty; only shop warranty is available and buyers must verify IMEI and BTRC status.

Read full reasoning

Software updates are expected to be few, with only one to three OS upgrades historically. Overall the phone suits budget buyers who value screen size and battery over performance and official support.

Motorola G05: what buyers ask us

What is the Motorola G05 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Motorola G05 in Bangladesh was ৳12,499, checked 25 Oct 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

How long does the battery last on everyday use?

The 5200 mAh battery is praised for lasting all day on basic tasks such as browsing, messaging and video playback. Users report it feels like it can run forever when the phone is used lightly.

Can I play games smoothly on this device?

The Helio G81 Extreme chipset is described as unsuitable for gaming. Owners say the performance is very weak and games often lag, so it is not a good choice for heavy mobile gaming.

How does the camera perform in low‑light situations?

The 50MP sensor captures decent photos in daylight, but reviews note it struggles in low light. Details become soft and noise increases, making it a basic utility rather than a strong low‑light performer.

Is there an official warranty or reliable after‑sales support?

No official brand warranty is recorded for this model in Bangladesh. Only shop warranty is offered on gray‑import stock, so buyers should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchase.
