Is the Motorola G05 worth buying?

What is good

  • 5200 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 90Hz IPS display scrolls smoothly
  • 50MP main camera in bright light
  • headphone jack for wired audio

What is not

  • screen can freeze unexpectedly
  • gaming performance is poor
  • budget build feels cheap
  • software updates are limited

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, simple Android and are comfortable with a budget feel.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect high frame gaming, official brand warranty or frequent OS upgrades.

Why we say that

The 5200 mAh cell paired with 18W charging lets the phone run all day and still have charge left for evening use. The 6.67 inch IPS LCD runs at 90Hz, giving smooth scrolling for browsing and media. The 50MP main sensor produces decent photos in good light but struggles in low light and the front camera has microphone quirks.

The phone feels solid for its price, though the overall build is clearly budget. Users report occasional screen freezes that seem common across this model. Gaming runs only basic titles and feels sluggish.

Software is a near stock Android 15 experience with low bloat, but past updates have been weak and you will only receive a few OS upgrades. The device arrives as a gray‑import unit, so you only get a shop warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.
