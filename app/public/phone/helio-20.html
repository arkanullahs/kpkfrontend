Is the Helio 20 worth buying?

What is good

  • Battery lasts all day
  • Bright 120Hz display
  • Efficient battery performance

What is not

  • Poor gaming performance
  • Low build quality
  • Disappointing camera results

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a basic device with a long-lasting battery and a bright screen for simple tasks.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you enjoy mobile gaming or expect high-quality photography from your camera.

Why we say that

While the 120Hz display is bright, owners rate the overall build quality and performance quite low. You will find the battery lasts all day, but the camera quality is frequently criticized by users who find it merely decent rather than impressive. Gaming is a significant pain point, with owners reporting poor performance during sessions.

The software experience is rated below average, and the build quality feels lacking to those who use it daily. If you prioritize a smooth interface or high-end gaming, this phone will likely disappoint you. It is a basic tool for those who only need a screen that lasts through the day, provided they have very low expectations for photography and speed.

Does the Helio 20 have good battery life?

Yes, the 5000 mAh battery is considered efficient by owners. Users report that the phone successfully lasts all day on a single charge, which is one of the few areas where the device performs reliably.

Is the Helio 20 suitable for gaming?

No, the phone is not recommended for gaming. Owners have specifically reported poor gaming performance, and the device receives a very low rating of 3.0 out of 10 for its gaming capabilities.

How is the camera quality on this device?

The 50MP main camera is described as decent but not great. Owners have expressed dissatisfaction with the overall camera quality, and it receives a low rating of 4.0 out of 10 for its photographic output.

Is the Helio 20 a good choice for a primary phone?

It depends on your needs, but most users rate the build, performance, and ease of use quite low. With ratings around 4.0 out of 10 for most categories, it is better suited for light, basic usage rather than heavy daily tasks.
