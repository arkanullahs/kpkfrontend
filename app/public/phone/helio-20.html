Buying verdict

Helio 20 Is it worth buying?

The Helio 20 is a budget device that struggles to meet modern expectations, as reflected by low owner ratings across almost every category.

Choose it if
Buy this phone only if you prioritize basic daily tasks and battery life over camera quality or gaming performance.
Consider alternatives if
You want a device that handles games well or provides high quality photography.

What works well

  • Efficient 5000 mAh battery
  • Bright 120Hz display
  • Official warranty support

Reported trade-offs

  • Poor gaming performance
  • Disappointing camera quality
  • Low build quality ratings

Why we say that

While the 120Hz display is bright, users report significant dissatisfaction with the build quality, camera performance, and gaming capabilities. Owners specifically highlight concerns regarding the camera output and the phone's inability to handle gaming tasks effectively. With an average rating of only 3.0 for gaming and 4.0 for performance, this device is not suited for demanding users.

The battery is described as efficient and capable of lasting all day, which is the only area where users find some consistency. Potential buyers should be aware that the newer Helio 46 model is currently available for ৳13,499, making it a more logical choice for your money. Given the feedback from current owners, this phone requires careful consideration before purchase.

Does the Helio 20 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

Yes, the Helio 20 is listed as an official BD unit. This means you are covered by the brand's own warranty and their official service network.

Is the Helio 20 good for playing mobile games?

No, the phone is not recommended for gaming. Owners have rated the gaming performance at a low 3.0 out of 10, indicating it struggles with modern mobile games.

How is the battery life on the Helio 20?

The battery life is one of the few strengths of this device. Owners report that the 5000 mAh battery is efficient and lasts all day on a single charge.

What do users say about the camera quality?

Users describe the camera as decent but not great. Owners have expressed concerns about the overall quality, and the camera received a low rating of 4.0 out of 10.
