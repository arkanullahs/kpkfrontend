Why we say that

It runs a stockish Android feature phone system with no ads and a tiny 2.4 inch TFT screen. The Unisoc SC6531E chipset provides only basic performance, which owners rate as two out of ten. Battery life is modest at 1,750 mAh and earned a five out of ten score, so you will likely charge it every day.

The camera is very weak with a one point five rating and struggles in low light. Fast charging is a bright spot, delivering 100W power despite the small battery. Because every unit is a gray import, there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh and you must rely on the shop's limited coverage.
