Is the Nokia 123 Shield worth buying?

What is good

  • Very low price
  • 100 W fast charging
  • Simple feature‑phone UI
  • Easy to use

What is not

  • Poor camera quality
  • Weak battery life
  • Dull small display

Buy it if Buy it if you only need basic calls, texts and fast charging on a tight budget.

Skip it if Skip it if you want a good camera, gaming performance or official warranty.

Why we say that

It sells for between 4,300 and ৳4,599 on the cheapest listings, making it one of the cheapest options on the market. The 2.4‑inch TFT screen is tiny and dull, and owners rate the display only two out of ten. The 1750 mAh battery barely lasts a day of moderate use, earning a five‑point rating, but the 100 W charger can refill it in minutes.

Performance is limited by the Unisoc SC6531E chip, so apps open slowly and gaming scores are at the bottom of the scale. The camera is almost unusable, with a rating of 1.5, especially in low light. Because every unit is a gray import, there is no official warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited promise and must pay for any repair.

Users complain most about the weak camera, short battery life and the lack of brand support. The phone runs a stock‑ish Android feature‑phone UI without ads, which some find simple to navigate.

Is there an official manufacturer warranty for the Nokia 123 Shield in Bangladesh?

No official manufacturer warranty is provided for the Nokia 123 Shield in Bangladesh because every unit is a gray‑import. The shop you buy from may offer its own limited warranty, but any repair will be charged. Always verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

How long will the software receive updates?

Software updates are uncertain because the brand is in transition after the HMD split. The phone runs a stock‑ish Android feature‑phone interface and does not carry extra bloat, but you should not expect regular major upgrades.

Will the 1750 mAh battery last a full day of typical use?

The 1750 mAh battery is small and owners rate its endurance at five out of ten. With basic calls and occasional messaging it may stretch close to a day, but any screen or network activity will drain it quickly despite the 100 W fast‑charge.

Can I play games on this phone?

Gaming performance is very weak. The Unisoc SC6531E chipset and the low‑rated performance score of two out of ten mean most games will lag or not run at all. It is best used only for basic phone functions.
