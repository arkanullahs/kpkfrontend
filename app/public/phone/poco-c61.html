What works well4

  • 5000 mAh battery gives long unplugged time
  • 6.71‑inch 90 Hz IPS LCD feels spacious
  • Headphone jack and FM radio are still included
  • Helio G36 handles everyday apps smoothly

Reported trade-offs4

  • 10 W charger charges very slowly
  • OS is cluttered with ads
  • Lag appears during simple tasks
  • 8 MP camera struggles in low light

Why we say that

The POCO C61 offers a 6.71‑inch 90 Hz display and a 5000 mAh cell that can keep the phone alive through a day of typical use. The Helio G36 chipset handles everyday apps without crashing, yet owners notice noticeable slowdown even when making a simple call. The 10 W charger struggles to refill the large battery quickly, and the MIUI‑based HyperOS is filled with advertisements that distract from the experience.

The 8 MP rear camera captures basic shots but fails in low‑light conditions, and gaming performance stays around 25 fps with no game‑boost feature. The phone arrives as a gray‑import, so there is no official brand warranty; only the shop’s warranty applies after confirming IMEI and BTRC status. Overall it suits users who need a big, cheap screen and can tolerate software bloat and modest performance.
