Why we say that

The 6.87‑inch TFT LCD panel runs at 120 Hz and can reach 1020 nits, but reviewers note the display feels flat and lacks deep blacks, reflected in a low 5.5 rating. The phone feels solid with an IP69K water and dust seal and a sturdy build that scores 8.0. Software runs Android 16 with MagicOS 10, includes Google services, but carries a moderate amount of ads and bloat, and you can expect about three to four major OS upgrades like other flagships.

The device is a gray‑import, so there is no official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s own warranty applies and any repairs are paid. Confirm the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
