Honor 600s Is it worth buying?

The Honor 600s will keep you powered for days but will feel sluggish in most tasks.

What works well4

  • Massive 8,100 mAh battery
  • 120 Hz refresh rate
  • IP69K water‑dust protection
  • 45 W fast charging

Reported trade-offs4

  • Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 underpowered for price
  • 720p low‑resolution display
  • Mediocre 50 MP camera with no ultrawide
  • 5 MP selfie camera is weak

Why we say that

Its 8,100 mAh battery easily lasts through a full day of basic media use and the 45 W charger restores charge quickly. The 6.87‑inch TFT LCD offers bright 1,020 nit peaks and a smooth 120 Hz refresh, yet the low 720p resolution and thick bezels make the screen look dated. Performance suffers because the Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 chipset is underpowered for the price, leading to lag in apps and games.

The 50 MP main camera lacks an ultrawide lens and delivers only average results, while the 5 MP selfie shooter is far below expectations. Build quality is a bulky plastic shell with IP69K protection but no headphone jack. Software runs Android 16 with MagicOS 10, includes Google services, and receives about three to four major updates, but it carries medium bloat and ads.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 8,100 mAh cell provides exceptional endurance for basic media consumption and can easily last a full day of light to moderate use. Heavy gaming or video streaming will reduce that time noticeably.

Will this phone run modern games smoothly?

The Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 chipset is severely underpowered for this price segment, so most modern games run with low frame rates and occasional stutter. Gaming performance is rated poorly by owners.

Is the camera good for low‑light photography?

The 50 MP main sensor lacks an ultrawide lens and delivers only average results in low light. Owner ratings for camera quality are low, and pictures often appear noisy in dark conditions.

Does the phone come with an official warranty and how many Android updates will it receive?

All units are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s warranty applies and repairs are paid. The device runs Android 16 with MagicOS 10 and typically receives three to four major OS updates.
