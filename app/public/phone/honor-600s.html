Is the Honor 600s worth buying?

What is good

  • 8,100 mAh battery lasting two days
  • Very bright 1,020 nit display with eye‑care mode
  • IP69K water and dust resistance
  • Fast wired charging up to 80 W

What is not

  • Heavy preinstalled bloatware
  • No telephoto lens for zoom
  • Fingerprint sensor low and sometimes unreliable
  • Gaming performance only average

Buy it if Buy it if you value long battery life, bright screen and rugged build more than top‑tier gaming or camera zoom.

Skip it if Skip it if you need smooth high end gaming, a clean software experience or an official manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

The battery rating of 9.7 shows it really holds up, and fast wired charging around 80 W gets you back in use quickly. The 6.87‑inch display reaches 1,020 nits, making it easy to see outdoors, and the screen includes eye‑care features. Build quality is solid with IP69K protection, though the design mimics an iPhone and some owners find the fingerprint sensor low and occasionally unreliable.

The Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 chipset provides smooth everyday performance, but high‑end gaming frames drop noticeably. The 50 MP main camera (owners note a 200 MP effective sensor) captures decent shots, yet the lack of a telephoto lens limits zoom and low‑light results are only average. MagicOS 10 runs on Android 16 but is packed with preinstalled AI apps that many consider excessive.

Overall the phone feels durable and long‑lasting, but it is priced higher than the performance it offers.

How long will the battery last in daily use?

The 8,100 mAh cell is rated as a monster battery and owners report about two days of normal use before needing to recharge, even with the 120 Hz screen active.

Will this phone handle modern games well?

Daily tasks run smoothly, but the Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 chipset struggles with demanding titles, delivering lower frame rates than premium gaming phones. It stays cooler than the Pro model during play.

What is the camera quality in low light and does it have zoom?

The 50 MP (reported as 200 MP effective) main sensor captures decent photos, but without a telephoto lens digital zoom is weak and low‑light performance is only average, reflected in a 6.5 camera rating.

Is there an official warranty and how many Android updates will it receive?

All units are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s limited warranty applies and repairs are paid. The device runs Android 16 with MagicOS 10 and is expected to receive three to four major OS updates like other Honor flagships.
