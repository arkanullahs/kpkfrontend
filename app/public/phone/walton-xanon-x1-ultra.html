Buying verdict

Walton XANON X1 Ultra Is it worth buying?

The Walton XANON X1 Ultra feels solid but its low‑resolution screen and weak camera hold it back.

Choose it if
Buy if you need a sturdy phone with long battery life and can accept a low‑resolution display.
Consider alternatives if
Skip if you prioritize a sharp FHD+ screen, strong low‑light camera, or smooth gaming performance.

What works well

  • Durable marble‑like plastic body
  • 5000 mAh battery gives long endurance
  • 120 Hz refresh rate feels smooth
  • Fast charging advertised at 100 W

Reported trade-offs

  • HD+ resolution screen feels low at this price
  • Camera struggles in low light and bokeh looks fake
  • Software bugs cause accidental touch issues
  • Gaming performance is only average

Why we say that

Owners appreciate the marble‑like plastic build and the 5000 mAh battery that easily lasts a full day thanks to the HD+ panel. The 120 Hz refresh rate makes scrolling feel smooth, and the advertised 100 W fast charge does refill the battery quickly, though real‑world tests show about 33 W is sufficient. Daily tasks run fine on the Helio G99, but the chipset feels dated for 2025 and gaming performance is only average.

The 50 MP main sensor suffers from exposure problems, artificial bokeh and poor low‑light results, while video recording lacks any stabilization. The light Android skin is clean but is plagued by occasional bugs and accidental touch glitches, and software updates are rare. Overall the phone is overpriced for what it offers, especially when competitors provide FHD+ displays and better cameras at similar prices.
