Is the Walton XANON X1 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • Extremely fast 100W charging
  • Smooth 120Hz IPS LCD display
  • Capable Helio G99 chipset
  • Large 5000 mAh battery

What is not

  • Medium level of bloat and ads
  • Rarely any software updates
  • Basic 8MP selfie camera

Buy it if You want the fastest charging speed available at 15,999 taka and a smooth 120Hz screen.

Skip it if You prefer a clean software experience without ads or expect regular system updates.

Why we say that

Living with the Walton XANON X1 Ultra means you get a smooth 120Hz display and a reliable Helio G99 chipset for daily tasks. However, the software experience is not perfect. Owners often find the medium level of bloat and ads intrusive during daily use.

You should also be aware that software updates are rarely provided, which might affect long-term usability. The 50MP main camera performs adequately for the price, but the 8MP selfie camera is basic. While the 5000 mAh battery is standard, the extremely fast charging helps you get back to full power quickly.

It is a functional device for those who prioritize charging speed and display fluidity over software polish or frequent system updates.
