Is the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 7 FE worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz foldable AMOLED with 2600‑nit peak brightness
  • 50 MP main camera captures good daylight photos
  • Solid aluminum‑glass build with IP48 rating
  • One UI 8 offers many features and up to seven Android upgrades

What is not

  • 4000 mAh battery struggles to last a full day
  • 25 W charger takes about 1.5 hours for full charge
  • Heavy thermal throttling cuts CPU performance in half
  • Cover screen is dim, small and only 60 Hz

Buy it if Buy it if you love a stylish flip with a vivid main display and can accept slower charging and modest battery life.

Skip it if Skip it if you need long gaming sessions, fast charging, or an official Samsung warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.7" foldable Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X panel reaches 2600 nits and runs at 120 Hz, making media consumption enjoyable. The 50 MP main camera is pleasant in daylight, yet night shots turn grainy. Battery life is mediocre; the 4000 mAh cell often needs topping up before the day ends, and the 25 W charger takes about 1.5 hours for a full charge.

Under demanding apps the Exynos 2400 heats up, throttling CPU performance by up to 50 percent, which hurts gaming and heavy multitasking. The cover screen is small, dim and limited to 60 Hz, and the glossy back attracts fingerprints. Software is feature‑rich with One UI 8 and a promise of up to seven Android upgrades, but the phone arrives as a gray‑import with only a shop warranty, so confirming IMEI and BTRC status is essential.
