Is the iQOO Z9 Turbo worth buying?

What is good

  • Efficient 6000 mAh battery
  • Bright 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Strong Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 performance
  • Solid build quality

What is not

  • Severe performance throttling
  • Pink-green horizontal line display defect
  • Average camera quality
  • No official brand warranty

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize raw performance and battery life and are comfortable managing a gray-import device with a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you need reliable camera performance, a stable display without potential line defects, or official brand warranty support.

Why we say that

While the 6000 mAh battery is efficient and lasts about 11 hours, owners have reported a concerning pink-green tint horizontal line appearing in the center of the display panel. You are getting a 144Hz AMOLED screen with 4500 nits peak brightness, but the camera quality is only average, with the front camera performing even worse. Because this phone is only available as a gray-import in Bangladesh, you will rely on shop warranties rather than brand support.

You must clarify if you are receiving the China-market version with OriginOS or the India-market version with Funtouch OS before paying 30,990 taka. The build quality holds up well for 1.5 years, but the overall experience is marred by software bloat and inconsistent hardware reliability.

Does the iQOO Z9 Turbo have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is currently only available as a gray-import. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before making your purchase.

How is the gaming performance on this device?

While the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset is powerful, the phone suffers from widely reported aggressive throttling. Owners note that severe performance drops occur during gaming or heavy multitasking, which may impact your experience despite the high 8.5 rating for gaming.

What software version should I expect on this phone?

It depends on the unit you buy. Some sellers provide the China-market version with OriginOS 4, while others provide India-market units with Funtouch OS. You must ask the seller which version they are selling, as the China version has specific caveats regarding software.

Is the battery life good enough for daily use?

The 6000 mAh battery is considered efficient by owners. It typically lasts about 11 hours on a single charge. This makes it a reliable choice for users who need their phone to stay powered throughout a busy day.
