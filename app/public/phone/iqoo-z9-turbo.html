Buying verdict

iQOO Z9 Turbo Is it worth buying?

This is a high performance machine that comes with significant risks.

Choose it if
You want a high performance gaming phone and can accept the risks of a gray import.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable camera or the security of an official brand warranty.

What works well

  • Fast Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset
  • Bright 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Large 6000 mAh battery
  • High performance ratings from owners

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe performance throttling
  • Reports of screen tint lines
  • Average camera quality
  • No official brand warranty

Why we say that

Owners love the speed and the 144Hz AMOLED screen, but the experience is inconsistent. Many users report severe performance throttling during heavy multitasking or gaming. There are also reports of a pink green tint horizontal line appearing in the center of the panel.

The battery is efficient and lasts about 11 hours. You must be careful with the software version because most units in Bangladesh are imports. Some have OriginOS from the China market while others have Funtouch OS.

Read full reasoning

Since these are gray imports, you only get shop warranty and must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

iQOO Z9 Turbo: what buyers ask us

What is the iQOO Z9 Turbo price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for iQOO Z9 Turbo in Bangladesh was ৳30,990, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No official brand backed warranty is available for this phone. All recorded listings are imports. You will only get a shop warranty, so you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

How is the gaming performance on this device?

Owners give gaming a rating of 8.5 and performance a 9.0. However, there are widespread reports of aggressive and severe performance throttling during gaming or heavy multitasking.

How long does the battery last?

The phone has a 6000 mAh battery. Reviews describe it as efficient and state that it lasts about 11 hours.

Which software version will I get?

It depends on the import. China market units use OriginOS 4. India units use Funtouch OS. You must ask the seller which build you are buying because most units in Bangladesh are imports.
