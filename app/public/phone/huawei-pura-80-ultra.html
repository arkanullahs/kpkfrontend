Huawei Pura 80 Ultra Is it worth buying?

If you can live without Google services and accept a shop only warranty, the Pura 80 Ultra gives a bright, smooth screen and solid telephoto photos.

Why we say that

The 6.8 inch LTPO OLED panel reaches 3000 nits peak and runs at 120Hz, making everyday scrolling feel fluid. The 50MP main sensor with 3.7x telephoto delivers good quality pictures, especially when you need zoom. Performance feels mid range; the Kirin 9020 throttles when the device gets hot, which gamers notice.

Battery life is below average despite a 5170 mAh cell, and owners rate it 5.5 out of 10. Charging is fast at 100W, so a short top‑up can get you back on the road. The phone is water resistant with IP68 and IP69 ratings, but there is no official Huawei service in Bangladesh and any repair will be paid to the shop.

Will the phone run bKash and other Bangladeshi apps?

The device does not have Google Play services, so many local apps need HarmonyOS compatibility or manual sideloading. The international EMUI version may support some apps, but the China version drops Android compatibility entirely.

How long will the software receive updates?

The phone ships with EMUI 15 for international units or HarmonyOS 5.1 for China units. Update frequency varies and there is no official commitment from Huawei for long term support in Bangladesh.

Is the battery life good for daily use?

Owners rate battery life at 5.5 out of 10, describing it as below average. The 5170 mAh cell can last a day with light use, but heavy tasks drain it quickly.

Can I rely on warranty and service if it breaks?

All listings are gray imports, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. You must rely on the shop's warranty, and any repair will be paid to the shop, as official Huawei service is scarce.
