Buying verdict

Apple iPhone 12 Mini Is it worth buying?

If you can find a reliable shop, the iPhone 12 Mini gives a compact premium experience for under ৳43,000.

Choose it if
Buy if you want a small phone with premium camera and can accept limited battery and warranty.
Consider alternatives if
Skip if you need long battery life, large storage, or official Apple support.

What works well

  • Bright 1200 nit OLED display
  • A14 Bionic performance
  • Good 12MP main camera
  • 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs

  • Small 2227 mAh battery
  • Only 4GB RAM limits heavy multitasking
  • No official Apple warranty
  • Software updates may end soon

Why we say that

The 5.4 inch Super Retina XDR OLED screen reaches 1200 nit peak brightness, so outdoor viewing is clear. The A14 Bionic chip and 4GB RAM handle most apps and games smoothly, and the 12MP main camera with f1.6 aperture and optical image stabilization captures decent photos even in modest light. 5G support lets you use the newest networks where available.

Owners often complain that the 2227 mAh battery drains quickly, especially with 5G and bright screen use. The 64GB storage can fill fast if you keep many apps or media. Because the phone is six years old, software updates are likely to stop soon and there is no official Apple warranty, only whatever shop warranty is offered.
