Apple iPhone 12 Mini Is it worth buying?

The iPhone 12 Mini is a compact device that offers a powerful A14 Bionic chipset in a small frame, but it is now a six-year-old model that faces significant limitations.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a compact Apple device for light usage and are comfortable managing shop-level warranty support.
Consider alternatives if
You need a phone with long battery life or guaranteed long-term software updates.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Powerful A14 Bionic chipset
  • Bright 1200 nits OLED display
  • Compact and portable design
  • Capable 12MP camera system

Reported trade-offs4

  • Small 2227 mAh battery capacity
  • No manufacturer warranty available
  • Limited remaining software update life
  • Only gray-import stock available

Why we say that

Living with this phone means managing a small 2227 mAh battery that may struggle to last through a full day of heavy use. Because this device is only available as gray-import stock, you will not receive a manufacturer warranty and must rely on shop-provided coverage instead. You should verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your money.

While the Super Retina XDR OLED display is bright and sharp, the age of the hardware means you should expect little to no remaining software support. It remains a capable performer for basic tasks, but the lack of official support and the aging battery make it a risky purchase for anyone looking for a long-term daily driver.

Apple iPhone 12 Mini: what buyers ask us

What is the Apple iPhone 12 Mini price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Apple iPhone 12 Mini in Bangladesh was ৳42,990, checked 24 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does the iPhone 12 Mini have an official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there is no manufacturer warranty for this device. The units currently available in the market are gray-import stock, meaning you will only receive a shop warranty. You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

Will the iPhone 12 Mini receive future software updates?

This phone was released in 2020 and is now six years old. You should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point, as the device has already reached the later stages of its support cycle.

Is the battery life on the iPhone 12 Mini sufficient for daily use?

The device features a 2227 mAh battery. Given the age of the phone and the small capacity, you may find that the battery life is limited and might not sustain heavy usage throughout an entire day.

Who is the ideal user for this compact iPhone?

This phone suits someone who prioritizes a small, pocketable form factor and high-quality display over battery longevity. It is best for users who perform basic tasks and are aware of the risks associated with buying gray-import hardware without a manufacturer warranty.
