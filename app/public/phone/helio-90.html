Why we say that

While the hardware specifications look strong on paper, real-world usage reveals a mixed experience. Owners report that the camera tuning falls short when compared to rivals like Samsung, and the software interface is not particularly clean. There is also significant concern regarding the long-term software update roadmap, as users are skeptical about receiving future support.

Performance is smooth for basic daily tasks, but the device is only modest when handling modern games. The build quality feels average with its plastic construction. While the 5000 mAh battery is decent, users have expressed that the price feels slightly high for this segment, with many suggesting it would be a better deal if it were cheaper.
