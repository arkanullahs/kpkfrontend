Is the Helio 90 worth buying?

What is good

  • Excellent 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Solid 5000mAh battery endurance
  • Clean user interface
  • Bright screen for indoor use

What is not

  • Fragile plastic build quality
  • No Widevine L1 certification
  • Struggles with heavy gaming
  • Single speaker setup

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a vibrant AMOLED display for daily tasks and indoor media consumption.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a heavy gamer or someone who needs high-definition streaming on OTT platforms.

Why we say that

Living with this phone means enjoying a vibrant 6.7-inch AMOLED screen that makes indoor viewing a pleasure, though you must handle the plastic body with care as it feels fragile and attracts fingerprints. While the 5000mAh battery reliably lasts a full day, the camera performance is inconsistent due to software softness and highlight clipping. Owners frequently point out that the lack of Widevine L1 certification prevents high-definition streaming on platforms like Netflix.

The Helio G99 chipset handles daily tasks well enough, but it is not built for heavy gaming sessions. You will also have to manage with a single speaker setup and some pre-installed bloatware. Since there are no long-term update promises, this phone is best suited for users who want a sharp screen for media consumption rather than a durable device for intensive performance or high-end photography.

Does the Helio 90 support high-definition streaming on Netflix?

No, the phone lacks Widevine L1 certification. This means you cannot watch full HD+ video content on Netflix or other OTT platforms.

Is the Helio 90 suitable for playing heavy games?

The Helio G99 chipset is adequate for daily tasks, but it struggles with heavy gaming. Owners have rated the gaming performance at 5.0 out of 10.

How long will the battery last on a single charge?

The phone features a 5000mAh battery that provides solid endurance for a full day of use. It supports 33W charging, which is considered standard.
