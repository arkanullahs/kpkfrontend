Buying verdict

Helio 90 Is it worth buying?

If you want an AMOLED screen and a 64MP camera without breaking the bank, the Helio 90 delivers that at ৳16,499.

Choose it if
You value a large AMOLED screen and decent photography while staying within a tight budget.
Consider alternatives if
You need top‑tier gaming performance or guaranteed Netflix HD streaming.

What works well

  • 120Hz AMOLED display with vibrant colors
  • 64MP main camera for detailed photos
  • 5000mAh battery lasts a full day
  • 33W charging refills quickly

Reported trade-offs

  • Glossy plastic attracts fingerprints easily
  • No Widevine L1 limits full HD streaming
  • Low‑light photos appear soft and noisy
  • High‑end games run with noticeable lag

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch 120Hz panel looks vibrant in indoor lighting but struggles under direct sunlight. Battery life is average, lasting a day for most users, and the 33W charger refills it in about an hour. Performance feels smooth for everyday apps and 2D games, yet high‑end titles like PUBG show lag.

The main camera is sharp in daylight but produces soft, noisy pictures in low light. The glossy plastic body attracts fingerprints and the fingerprint sensor feels magnetically sticky. Lack of Widevine L1 means you cannot watch full HD Netflix or OTT streams.

Read full reasoning

Software is close to stock but includes some bloatware and has no clear update promise. Overall it offers good value for its price but has notable compromises.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

With a 5000mAh cell the phone typically gets you through a normal workday and light evening use before needing a recharge, and the 33W charger can top it up in about an hour.

Can I play high‑end games like PUBG on this phone?

Daily tasks and 2D titles run smoothly, but demanding 3D games such as PUBG show frame drops and reduced graphics, so the device is not suited for high‑end mobile gaming.

How good is the camera in low‑light conditions?

The 64MP main sensor captures sharp daylight shots, yet in low‑light environments the images become soft, noisy and lose detail, making night photography a noticeable weakness of this phone.

Does the phone come with an official warranty and future Android updates?

The unit sold in Bangladesh includes the brand’s official warranty and can be serviced at the listed network, but the software currently shows some bloatware and has no clear update schedule, so future Android upgrades are uncertain.
