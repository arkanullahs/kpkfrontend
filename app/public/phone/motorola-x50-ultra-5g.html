Motorola X50 Ultra 5G Is it worth buying?

The Motorola X50 Ultra 5G is a powerful device that relies on the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset to deliver high performance for its ৳70,990 price tag.

What works well4

  • Extremely fast 125W charging
  • Bright 2500 nits P-OLED display
  • Clean near-stock Hello UI software
  • Versatile 50MP camera system

Reported trade-offs3

  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Historically weak software update support
  • Modest 4500 mAh battery capacity

Why we say that

You get a bright 2500 nits display and a versatile camera setup that includes a 50MP main sensor with OIS and a 3.0x telephoto lens. Living with this phone means using a near-stock Hello UI that keeps bloat and ads to a minimum. However, buyers must be aware that this device is only available as gray-import stock in the market.

This means you will not receive a manufacturer warranty, so you must rely on the shop warranty provided by the seller. You should also verify the IMEI and BTRC status before finalizing your purchase. While the 125W charging is very fast, the 4500 mAh battery capacity is relatively modest for a phone with a 144Hz screen.

Read full reasoning

Motorola has a history of weak software support, so you should expect between 1 to 3 OS updates over the life of the device.

Motorola X50 Ultra 5G: what buyers ask us

What is the Motorola X50 Ultra 5G price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Motorola X50 Ultra 5G in Bangladesh was ৳70,990, checked 24 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does the Motorola X50 Ultra 5G come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there is no official manufacturer warranty for this device. It is currently only available as gray-import stock, meaning you will have to rely on a shop warranty provided by the seller.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

Motorola has a history of weak software support for its devices. You should expect to receive between 1 to 3 OS updates throughout the lifespan of this phone.

Is the battery life sufficient for heavy daily usage?

The phone features a 4500 mAh battery. While this capacity is standard, it powers a 144Hz display which may consume significant energy during heavy use. Fortunately, the 125W charging allows for very quick power top-ups.

What should I check before buying this gray-import phone?

You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status of the device with the seller before you pay. Since there is no manufacturer warranty, verifying these details is essential to ensure the phone is legitimate and can be registered properly.
