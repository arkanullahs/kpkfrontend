OPPO A6 4G Is it worth buying?

The OPPO A6 4G will keep you powered all day but will disappoint you with its camera and heat.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize marathon battery life and can accept a weak camera and occasional heating.
Consider alternatives if

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 7000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 45 W fast charging refills quickly
  • 120 Hz refresh makes scrolling smooth
  • Durable body feels solid

Reported trade-offs4

  • Camera produces blurry, low‑clarity photos
  • HD+ LCD screen feels outdated
  • Phone gets very hot under load
  • No headphone jack

Why we say that

Its 7000 mAh battery and 45 W fast charger give you more than a day of use without hunting for an outlet. The 6.75" IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, which feels smooth for scrolling, yet the HD+ resolution looks dated next to newer panels. The Snapdragon 685 struggles under heavy gaming and often becomes very hot, leading to lag.

The 50 MP rear camera produces blurry, low‑clarity photos, especially in low light, and the lack of a headphone jack limits accessories. Build quality feels solid and water‑resistant claims are present, though some users doubt them. Overall the phone feels overpriced for the performance and display it offers, but its battery life remains its strongest point.

How long does the battery actually last in daily use?

The 7000 mAh battery is the main selling point. Users report exceptional endurance, easily lasting a full day of heavy use and often more. With 45 W charging it can be topped up from zero to full in about an hour.

Will the phone handle gaming without lag or overheating?

The Snapdragon 685 is an older chipset. Owner ratings give performance a 5.0 and gaming a 4.5. Many report overheating and lag during demanding games, so expect occasional slowdowns when the phone gets very hot.

How does the rear camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The rear camera is a 50 MP sensor with f/1.8 aperture, but users criticize blurriness and lack of clarity. In low‑light situations the images become especially soft and noisy, making it unsuitable for serious photography.
