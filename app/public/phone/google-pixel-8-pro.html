Buying verdict

Google Pixel 8 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you care most about photo quality and a bright screen, the Pixel 8 Pro delivers that but struggles with battery and gaming.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want top‑tier photography and a vivid display and can accept modest battery life.
Consider alternatives if
You need strong gaming performance or reliable long‑lasting battery.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 50MP camera with optical image stabilization
  • 2400‑nit LTPO OLED display
  • Stock Android with long‑term updates
  • Flat screen improves one‑hand use

Reported trade-offs4

  • Battery drains quickly on 5G and hotspot
  • Gaming performance is mediocre
  • 5G connection can be unstable
  • Low‑light photos may show purple tint

Why we say that

Owners love the 50MP camera with OIS and the 2400‑nit LTPO OLED panel that stays vivid even in sunlight. The flat screen design feels comfortable for one‑hand use and the stock Android experience is clean, with up to seven years of updates promised for the series. However, the 5050 mAh battery shows poor standby on 5G and drains fast when hotspot is active, earning a low battery rating.

Gaming feels mediocre because the Tensor G3 lacks raw power, and several users report 5G instability and moderate heating. A purple tint can appear in low‑light Night Sight photos, and a green line on the display has been seen after six months. The phone is three years old, so remaining major Android upgrades are limited.
