Is the Google Pixel 8 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 50MP camera with optical image stabilization
  • 6.7 inch LTPO OLED display with 2400 nits peak
  • Clean stock Android with up to seven years of updates

What is not

  • Battery drains quickly on 5G and hotspot
  • Frequent green line on screen after months
  • Unstable 5G connection in Bangladesh
  • Tensor G3 struggles with gaming performance

Buy it if Buy it if you want a top tier camera and display and can tolerate short battery life and occasional network glitches.

Skip it if Skip it if you need strong gaming performance or a phone that lasts all day on 5G without heating.

Why we say that

The 50 MP sensor with optical image stabilization and 5 x telephoto produces sharp photos that many reviewers call spectacular. The 6.7 inch LTPO OLED panel reaches 2400 nits at peak, delivering bright and colour accurate images. Google’s Tensor G3 chip runs Android 14 cleanly and promises up to seven major Android upgrades, but three years after launch the remaining update window is short.

Real world use shows poor standby, high drain on 5G and hotspot, and a noticeable green line on some screens after months. Network stability on 5G is inconsistent in Bangladesh, and the chipset struggles with sustained gaming loads, giving a modest performance rating. The phone ships with 12 GB RAM, storage options up to 1 TB, and 30 W charging, but you should expect heating during heavy use.
