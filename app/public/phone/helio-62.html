Helio 62 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can stay on for a full day without charging, the Helio 62 delivers that.

What works well4

  • 7000 mAh battery gives all‑day use
  • 120 Hz IPS LCD feels smooth
  • 33 W fast charging
  • Headphone jack and FM radio

Reported trade-offs4

  • Helio G81 struggles with performance
  • Gaming drops frames and throttles quickly
  • Software updates are limited
  • No AMOLED display despite high brightness

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh battery easily lasts through heavy social media use and video streaming, and the 33 W charger brings it back to full in about two hours. The 6.78‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, giving smooth scrolling and decent brightness of 800 nits. The 50 MP main camera and 32 MP selfie sensor capture usable photos, but the sensor is under‑tuned and struggles in low light, resulting in average image quality.

Performance is the weakest area; the Helio G81 chipset feels slow, gaming frames drop, and the device throttles quickly. Software updates are limited and the UI is not well optimized. At ৳18,300 the price feels high for a device with entry‑level SoC and only an IPS panel, especially when a newer Helio 90 model is cheaper.

Overall it suits users who value battery life and a smooth display more than raw speed or premium photography.

How long does the 7000 mAh battery last in daily use?

The 7000 mAh battery can easily last more than a full day of typical browsing, social media and video streaming, often reaching 14‑16 hours of mixed use before needing a recharge. Charging at 33 W refills it in about two hours.

Will this phone run modern games smoothly?

The Helio G81 chipset is entry‑level, so modern games often drop frames and throttle after a short period. Casual titles may run okay, but demanding games will feel laggy and unstable on this device.

How good are the cameras in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor is under‑tuned, producing average photos and struggling in low‑light scenes. Images may appear noisy and lack detail, so you should not expect strong performance in dim environments.

Does the phone come with official warranty and will it get software updates?

Yes, the phone is sold as an official Bangladesh unit, so the brand’s warranty and service network apply. However, owners report only a few software updates and a UI that is not well optimized.
