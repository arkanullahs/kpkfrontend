Realme 14 5G Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day on a single charge and handle 5G gaming, the Realme 14 5G delivers that but compromises on camera and build quality.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want long battery life, 5G connectivity and solid gaming performance at a mid‑range price.
Consider alternatives if
You need a premium build, strong low‑light camera or a clean software experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 2000 nits peak brightness
  • Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 smooth gaming
  • 120 Hz AMOLED display

Reported trade-offs4

  • Plastic back feels cheap
  • No ultrawide or low‑light camera performance
  • No headphone jack
  • Realme UI has bloatware and ads

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh battery lives through heavy use and the Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 with 12 GB RAM keeps games smooth at 90 fps. The 6.67‑inch AMOLED screen is bright and fast but some users notice eye strain from the 120 Hz panel. The phone feels cheap because of its plastic back even though it has an IP69 rating.

The 50 MP main sensor works in good light but struggles in low‑light and there is no ultrawide lens. Realme UI 6.0 adds many features but also brings bloatware and ads. Official units cost ৳39,099 and include a brand warranty, while cheaper gray imports lack any manufacturer cover.
