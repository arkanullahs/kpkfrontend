Buying verdict

Realme 14 5G Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day without hunting for a charger, the Realme 14 5G delivers.

Choose it if
You need long battery life, fast charging and solid gaming on a budget.
Consider alternatives if
You care about camera versatility, low light quality or a clean software experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 120 Hz AMOLED screen stays bright
  • Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 handles games smoothly
  • 45 W fast charging refills quickly

Reported trade-offs

  • No 3.5 mm headphone jack
  • No ultrawide camera limits versatility
  • Low light photos are noisy
  • Realme UI shows bloatware and ads

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh battery lives through heavy use and the 45 W charger brings it back to full in under an hour. The 6.67‑inch AMOLED panel stays bright even in sunlight and the 120 Hz refresh makes scrolling feel smooth. The Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 chipset gives solid performance for games and multitasking, and the 12 GB RAM keeps apps responsive.

Owners love the durability rating but complain about the plastic back feeling cheap. The single 50 MP sensor struggles in low light and the lack of an ultrawide lens limits photography options. Realme UI adds many features but also shows bloatware and ads that annoy some users.

Read full reasoning

The missing 3.5 mm headphone jack is a deal breaker for many. Overall it is a capable daily driver if you can overlook the camera and software quirks.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 6000 mAh cell is rated to last a full day of heavy use and can survive long gaming sessions thanks to its efficient 4 nm chipset. Users report it easily reaches a full charge after a day of normal activity.

Is the Realme 14 5G good for gaming and does it stay cool?

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 paired with 12 GB RAM delivers smooth 90 fps gameplay in supported titles. The device stays reasonably cool during extended play, and the 45 W charger helps you get back into action quickly.

How does the camera perform in low light and does it have an ultrawide lens?

The phone uses a single 50 MP sensor with OIS, but low light images are noisy and lack detail. There is no ultrawide camera, so you cannot capture very wide scenes without moving closer.
