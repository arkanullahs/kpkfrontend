Realme Neo8 Is it worth buying?

If you can tolerate heat and software quirks, the Neo8 gives flagship specs for under 64 k taka.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want flagship hardware at a gray‑import price and can accept a non‑official warranty.
Consider alternatives if

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 8000 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • 165 Hz AMOLED screen is extremely bright
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 delivers strong performance
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS and 3.5× telephoto

Reported trade-offs4

  • Chinese ROM causes notification delays
  • Device gets very hot during gaming
  • Back panel feels cheap
  • No wireless charging support

Why we say that

The 8000 mAh battery easily lasts a day and a half, and the 80 W charger tops it up quickly. The 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 165 Hz and reaches peak brightness that makes outdoor use comfortable. Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 delivers fast performance in apps and games, though the metal chassis can feel scorching under heavy load.

The 50 MP main camera with OIS and 3.5× telephoto is solid, but the ultrawide struggles and selfies feel over‑processed. Realme UI 7.0 runs on a Chinese ROM that shows notification delays and carries noticeable bloatware. No official warranty is available; you must rely on the shop’s warranty and confirm IMEI/BTRC status before buying.

Realme Neo8: what buyers ask us

What is the Realme Neo8 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Realme Neo8 in Bangladesh was ৳63,999, checked 17 Oct 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

How long does the battery really last?

The Neo8 uses an 8000 mAh silicon‑carbon battery that typically lasts 1.5 to 2 days with regular use. Even with heavy browsing it can get through a full day, and the 80 W charger refills it in roughly an hour.

Will the phone overheat while gaming?

Owners report the metal body gets very hot during intensive gaming and the phone can overheat. Performance stays strong but thermal management is inconsistent, so you may need to pause long sessions to let it cool.

Is the camera good in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures decent low‑light shots, but the ultrawide lens struggles in darkness. The 3.5× telephoto remains usable, while selfies are processed and not as sharp.

What warranty and software support does it have?

The phone is only available as a gray‑import, so there is no official Realme warranty. You must rely on the shop’s warranty and verify IMEI and BTRC status. The Chinese ROM runs Realme UI 7.0, receives about two to three Android updates and includes medium‑high bloatware.
