Buying verdict

Realme Neo8 Is it worth buying?

The Realme Neo8 gives you flagship level specs at a gray import price, but you must accept software quirks and no official warranty.

Choose it if
You need top hardware and can tolerate a gray import with limited software polish.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable software updates or an official manufacturer warranty.

What works well

  • 8000 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • 165 Hz AMOLED screen with 6500 nits peak
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 delivers fast performance
  • 50 MP main camera with optical zoom

Reported trade-offs

  • Chinese ROM causes notification delays
  • Phone gets very hot during gaming
  • Ultrawide camera quality is poor
  • No official warranty, only shop warranty

Why we say that

Its 8000 mAh silicon carbon battery easily lasts one and a half to two days and refills in minutes with 80 W fast charging. The 6.78 inch AMOLED panel runs at 165 Hz and can peak at 6500 nits, making scrolling smooth and outdoor viewing bright. Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 paired with up to 16 GB RAM handles apps and games quickly, though the metal body can become very hot during long sessions and thermal management is uneven.

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS and 3.5× telephoto captures vibrant photos, while the ultrawide lens is noticeably weaker and selfies look processed. Realme UI 7 on Android 16 brings Chinese ROM bloatware and occasional notification delays. The phone ships without a manufacturer warranty; only the shop’s limited guarantee applies, and any repair will cost extra.

Read full reasoning

If you can live with these compromises, the hardware value is hard to ignore.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 8000 mAh silicon carbon battery usually lasts one and a half to two days with normal use. It can be charged from zero to full in a few minutes using the 80 W fast charger.

Will the phone overheat while gaming?

Owners report that the metal body becomes very hot during long gaming sessions and thermal management can be inconsistent. The phone may feel hot to the touch, especially under heavy load.

How good is the camera in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures vibrant images and the 3.5× telephoto works well. Low‑light performance is decent, but the ultrawide lens struggles and selfies appear heavily processed.

Does the Realme Neo8 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No manufacturer warranty is provided for this gray import. Only the shop’s limited warranty applies and any repair will be paid by the owner. Verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.
