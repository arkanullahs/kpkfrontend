Honor X5b Is it worth buying?

If you want a big screen phone that will stay alive through a day without breaking the bank, this model delivers that basic promise.

What works well4

  • 90Hz smooth display
  • 5200 mAh battery
  • headphone jack included
  • 13 MP main camera

Reported trade-offs3

  • TFT LCD not as vivid as AMOLED
  • 4 GB RAM may lag
  • Helio G36 not for heavy gaming

Why we say that

The 6.56 inch TFT LCD runs at 90Hz, giving smoother scrolling than standard panels but it lacks the deep blacks of an AMOLED. Brightness peaks at 530 nits, which is fine indoors but can look washed out under strong sunlight. The Helio G36 chipset paired with 4GB RAM handles everyday tasks and casual games, yet heavy titles may feel sluggish and occasional lag can appear in the Magic OS environment.

Battery capacity of 5200 mAh usually carries you through a normal day, though gaming or screen‑on time will cut that down. The 13 MP main camera with f/1.8 aperture records decent daylight photos, but low light scenes turn out noisy and lack detail. A 5 MP selfie sensor and a headphone jack are useful extras.

Read full reasoning

Software includes Android 14 with Magic OS 8, medium bloat and ads, and it follows the usual Honor update cadence of three to four Android versions. No official warranty information is provided, so buyers should be comfortable with that uncertainty.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5200 mAh battery is sized to last a full day with moderate use such as browsing, messaging and occasional video. Heavy gaming or constant screen brightness will reduce that time noticeably.

Can the Honor X5b handle modern mobile games smoothly?

The Helio G36 processor together with 4 GB RAM can run casual and less demanding games without major issues. More demanding titles may show lower frame rates and occasional stutter, especially on higher settings.

The 13 MP sensor with an f/1.8 aperture captures acceptable daylight photos, but in low light the images become noisy and lack fine detail because the camera and processing are modest.
