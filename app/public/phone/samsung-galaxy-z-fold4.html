Samsung Galaxy Z Fold4 Is it worth buying?

If you can tolerate a visible crease and a hinge that may stiffen, the Fold4 still offers a large, fast, and versatile experience.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a premium foldable with strong performance for multitasking and gaming, and you accept the aging update support.
Consider alternatives if
You need the latest software updates, a flawless screen, or a phone that will stay fresh for many more years.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 7.6 inch 120Hz Dynamic AMOLED 2X display
  • Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 delivers flagship performance
  • 50MP main camera with OIS and 3x telephoto
  • Efficient battery gives good everyday endurance

Reported trade-offs4

  • Prominent crease on the inner foldable screen
  • Hinge may loosen, click or become stiff over time
  • Standby drain can lose 10‑15% battery overnight
  • Software updates likely ending soon for this 2022 model

Why we say that

The 7.6" 120Hz AMOLED screen feels premium and bright, while the Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 chipset delivers flagship‑level performance for multitasking and gaming. Battery life is efficient for daily use, though standby drain can be noticeable. The 50MP main camera with OIS produces good photos but falls short of top‑tier low‑light quality.

Owners report moderate heating during long gaming sessions and some hinge looseness after months of use. Software updates are likely ending soon, so the device may not receive the latest Android features. Overall, it feels like a high‑end device with a few aging drawbacks that matter if you expect a flawless long‑term experience.

How is the battery life and standby drain on the Galaxy Z Fold4?

Owners say the 4400 mAh battery feels efficient for daily use and can last a full day with moderate tasks. However many report a standby loss of about ten to fifteen percent overnight, so you may need to charge each morning.

Can the Fold4 handle gaming smoothly?

With the Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 and 12 GB RAM the Fold4 runs games fast and smooth, and the 120 Hz AMOLED screen adds fluid visuals. Some users notice moderate heating during long sessions, but it does not usually cause lag.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture, OIS and a 3× telephoto lens captures decent photos, but it is not top‑tier in low‑light. Images are good overall, yet noise can appear in very dark scenes.

What warranty and software update support does the official Bangladeshi unit have?

The Bangladeshi official unit comes with Samsung’s brand warranty and can be serviced through the local network. It launched with Android 12L and is eligible for up to four major Android upgrades, but being a 2022 model, future updates may soon stop.
