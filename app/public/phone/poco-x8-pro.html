Is the POCO X8 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6500 mAh battery capacity
  • Excellent 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Fast 100W charging support
  • Powerful Dimensity 8500 Ultra chipset

What is not

  • Severe camera processing lag
  • Excessive heating during gaming
  • No official brand warranty support
  • High levels of bloatware and ads

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a large battery and display quality over camera performance and are comfortable managing a gray-import device.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you need a reliable camera, stable gaming performance, or official brand warranty support.

Why we say that

While the 6500 mAh battery and 120Hz AMOLED display provide a strong foundation, owners report that the Dimensity 8500 Ultra struggles with basic tasks. The most critical issue is the camera processing, which can take up to 10 seconds per photo and causes the entire system to lag, stuttering background music and disabling haptic feedback. Gamers should be particularly cautious, as the device reaches temperatures as high as 69 degrees Celsius during intensive sessions.

Because every unit currently available in Bangladesh is a gray import, you will not receive brand-backed warranty support. You must rely entirely on the shop warranty provided by the seller. High levels of bloatware and ads further complicate the user experience on HyperOS 3.

How is the gaming performance and thermal management?

Owners report that the camera is very laggy. The processor often struggles to handle images, sometimes taking 10 seconds to process a single photo, which can cause the entire system to stutter.
