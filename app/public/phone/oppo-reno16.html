Why we say that

The Reno 16 arrives as a gray‑import unit built for China and India, so the shop only offers its own limited warranty and any repair costs are out of pocket. The 6700 mAh cell gives owners a battery rating of 9.4 and can easily survive a full day of mixed use. The 6.32‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 3600 nits, yet sunlight readability is still a problem for some.

The 200 MP main sensor with OIS and 3.5× telephoto produces natural tones, but the camera app is frequently laggy and has crashed for several users. Performance on the Dimensity 8500 is modest, with gaming and UI feeling sluggish. ColorOS 16 offers smooth animations but is packed with bloatware and intrusive notifications.

Does the OPPO Reno 16 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

All Reno 16 units listed are gray imports, so they do not carry any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Only the shop you buy from may offer a limited warranty and any repairs will be paid by you.

How long does the battery last on normal daily use?

The phone uses a 6700 mAh cell that owners rate 9.4 for battery life. It provides exceptional endurance and can easily last a full day of mixed usage, often extending into a second day with light use.

Is the Reno 16 suitable for gaming and heavy apps?

Performance scores are low, with the Dimensity 8500 feeling sluggish in demanding games. UI stutters are common and the gaming rating is 5.2, so it is not ideal for heavy gaming or intensive multitasking.

Will the camera work well in low‑light and is the app stable?

The 200 MP sensor with OIS and 3.5× telephoto delivers good natural tones, but many owners report lag, crashes and even the camera app dying. Low‑light shots can be decent, but software instability is a frequent complaint.
