Is the OPPO Reno16 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6700 mAh battery with all‑day endurance
  • 200 MP main camera with natural tones
  • 80 W wired fast charging
  • Premium compact build with IP68/69K rating

What is not

  • Camera app lags and can crash
  • Gaming and UI feel sluggish
  • No wireless charging support
  • Heavy bloatware with intrusive notifications

Buy it if Buy it if you value long battery life, high‑resolution photos and a premium compact design and can tolerate software quirks.

Skip it if Skip it if you need smooth gaming, reliable camera software or an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Owners love the 6700 mAh cell that easily lasts a full day of mixed use and appreciate the premium compact design with IP68/69K protection. The 6.32‑inch AMOLED panel is bright and colorful, though sunlight legibility can be tricky. Fast 80 W wired charging refills the large battery quickly, but the lack of wireless charging feels like a step back.

The Dimensity 8500 Super chipset handles everyday tasks fine, yet demanding games and heavy UI interactions feel stuttery. Camera hardware is strong, but the native app is laggy and sometimes crashes, making reliable photo shooting a hassle. ColorOS 16 looks smooth but is packed with bloatware and ads that many find intrusive.

Overall, the phone feels overpriced for the performance it delivers.
