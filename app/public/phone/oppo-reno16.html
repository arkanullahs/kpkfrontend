Buying verdict

OPPO Reno 16 Is it worth buying?

The OPPO Reno 16 is an extremely overpriced device that struggles with basic performance and software stability despite its premium build.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You are a collector who prioritizes a compact, high-capacity battery phone and are willing to manage heavy bloatware and potential software instability.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable daily driver for gaming, photography, or professional use where performance consistency and official warranty support are required.

  • Extremely overpriced for the provided hardware
  • Sluggish performance and frequent UI stutters
  • Camera app suffers from lag and crashes
  • Heavy bloatware and intrusive notifications

Why we say that

While the massive 6700 mAh battery offers exceptional endurance, the user experience is marred by a sluggish chipset and frequent UI stutters. Owners report that the camera app is prone to lag and crashes, with some users even experiencing total hardware failure. The software is heavily burdened by bloatware and intrusive notifications that require constant management.

You are paying a premium price for a device that lacks official manufacturer support in Bangladesh, as all units are gray imports. The combination of poor gaming performance and unreliable camera software makes this a difficult phone to recommend for daily use. If you prioritize a stable, reliable device, the current owner feedback suggests this model will likely cause more frustration than satisfaction.

Does the OPPO Reno 16 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you must rely on the specific shop for any repair promises. All repairs are paid services.

How does the battery life perform during daily usage?

The phone features a massive 6700 mAh cell that provides exceptional endurance. Owners rate the battery performance very highly at 9.4 out of 10, making it one of the most reliable aspects of the device.

Is this phone suitable for heavy gaming?

The device is not recommended for gaming. Owners report that the chipset feels sluggish and struggles with demanding games. The gaming performance is rated poorly at 5.2 out of 10, with users noting frequent UI stutters.

What should I know about the software before buying?

The phone runs ColorOS 16, which offers smooth animations but comes with heavy bloatware and intrusive notifications. Some units are China-market versions that lack Google services and Bangla language support, so you must confirm the build version with your seller.
