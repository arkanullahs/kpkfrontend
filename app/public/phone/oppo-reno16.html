OPPO Reno 16 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day on a single charge, the OPPO Reno 16 delivers that, but expect compromises elsewhere.

Choose it if

What works well

  • 6700 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 200 MP main camera with telephoto
  • 80 W wired fast charging
  • 120 Hz AMOLED display

Reported trade-offs

  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Gaming and UI feel sluggish
  • Camera app lags and crashes
  • Heavy bloatware and ads

Why we say that

The 6700 mAh battery truly lasts through a full day of mixed use and the 80 W wired charger tops it up quickly. The 200 MP main sensor captures bright, natural colours and the 3.5× telephoto works well in daylight, while the 6.32‑inch AMOLED panel offers vivid colours and a smooth 120 Hz feel. However, the device is a gray‑import with no official OPPO warranty in Bangladesh, so any repair will be paid out of pocket and you must rely on the shop’s limited guarantee.

The Dimensity 8500 struggles with demanding games and the UI feels sluggish, especially when the camera app crashes or lags. Heavy bloatware and intrusive ads add to the annoyance, and the lack of wireless charging feels outdated for the price range.
