Is the ZTE 11 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Unbeatable sustained gaming frames
  • Massive 7500 mAh battery endurance
  • Immersive 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Bypass charging prevents battery degradation

What is not

  • Unpolished software with poor translations
  • Poor low light selfie quality
  • No official brand warranty in Bangladesh
  • Chassis gets hot in max mode

Buy it if You are a hardware enthusiast who prioritizes gaming performance and battery life over software polish.

Skip it if You need official brand warranty support or a high quality camera for low light selfies.

Why we say that

You get unbeatable gaming performance and a massive battery that lasts two days. However, living with it means dealing with poor English translations and missing quality of life features. The chassis gets uncomfortably hot in max mode.

Owners report that the fingerprint sensor stops working if you use a privacy screen protector. If you unlock the bootloader, the fingerprint scanner fails completely. The software feels shockingly unpolished for the price.

You are buying a gray import, which means you have no brand support in Bangladesh. If the hardware breaks, you are entirely dependent on the shop that sold it to you.

What is the ZTE 11 Pro price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for ZTE 11 Pro in Bangladesh was ৳1,11,999, checked 9 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, every listing we have recorded is a gray import. There is little to no official ZTE service presence in Bangladesh. You will get a shop warranty instead of a brand backed warranty.

How is the gaming performance and heating?

Gaming performance is excellent with unbeatable sustained frames. Liquid cooling handles Diablo mode without throttling. While internals stay cool, the chassis can get uncomfortably hot when using max mode.

How long does the battery last?

The massive 7500 mAh battery provides exceptional endurance. Owners report that it lasts for 2 days of normal use.

Is the selfie camera good for night photos?

The under display selfie camera is poor in low light. Owners report that night video literally shows screen pixels.
