Is the Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • 200MP versatile photo system
  • 2600 nits peak screen brightness
  • Titanium frame feels premium
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 performance

What is not

  • 45W charging slower than rivals
  • Throttles and heats during long gaming
  • Settings UI now harder to navigate
  • Official warranty price high

Buy it if You should buy it if you want a flagship that excels at photography, display quality and raw performance and you can handle a slower charger.

Skip it if Skip it if you need the cheapest warranty coverage or cannot tolerate occasional throttling and a more complex settings menu.

Why we say that

The S24 Ultra feels solid with its titanium frame and delivers flagship performance thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 and 12 GB RAM. Daily photography is versatile, the 200 MP main camera covers wide, tele and ultra wide shots, though the zoom step can feel inconsistent. Battery life is strong, easily covering a full day of heavy use, but the 45 W charger is slower than competing fast charge solutions and can be picky about the adapter used.

Gaming runs smooth, yet prolonged sessions cause the device to heat up and throttle, reducing frame rates. Software support is a highlight with up to seven Android upgrades, but the One UI settings have become harder to reach, frustrating users who liked the simpler layout of previous versions. Overall the phone feels premium, but the cooling and UI quirks are the main sources of complaint.
