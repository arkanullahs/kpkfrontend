Why we say that

The 6.61 inch TFT LCD runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 1,010 nits, so scrolling and gaming feel smooth and the screen stays visible outdoors. The Helio G81 Ultra handles casual games well, though demanding titles may show occasional frame drops. Photos are sharp in daylight with the 50 MP f/1.8 sensor, but low light shots are noisy and the 5 MP selfie camera lacks detail.

The phone keeps a headphone jack and FM radio, which many users still appreciate. Software runs Android 16 with MagicOS 10, includes Google services, and carries medium level bloatware and ads that some find intrusive. Owners often complain about the lack of 5G and that cheaper gray import listings do not include the official warranty, forcing paid repairs.

How many Android updates will the Honor X6e receive?

The phone ships with Android 16 and MagicOS 10 and, like other Honor flagships, is expected to get about three to four major Android versions, so you can expect software support for a few years.

Will the 7,500 mAh battery last through a heavy day of use?

The 7,500 mAh cell can easily last two days of normal use and will handle a heavy day with screen on and apps running, especially with the 45W charger that refills it quickly.

How does the 50MP camera perform in low light?

In daylight the 50MP f/1.8 sensor captures sharp detail, but in low light the images become noisy and lack the clarity of higher end sensors, so results are acceptable but not outstanding.

What warranty do I get if I buy the cheapest listing?

The cheapest listings are gray imports that do not carry the official Honor warranty, so any repair will be treated as a paid service at an authorized centre.
