Is the Tecno Spark 40C worth buying?

What is good

  • Large 6000 mAh battery capacity
  • Smooth 120Hz IPS LCD display
  • Includes a headphone jack and IR blaster
  • Affordable 11,799 taka price point

What is not

  • Frequent lags on the home screen
  • High amount of ads and bloatware
  • Reported heating and signal issues
  • Poor gaming and general performance

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a basic device with a large battery for simple tasks and do not mind occasional software advertisements.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you play games, require a fast interface, or need a reliable device that does not suffer from signal or heating issues.

Why we say that

Owners report significant frustration with the user experience, specifically noting that the phone lags on the home screen even when opening simple apps. While the 6000 mAh battery is a highlight, users have observed it draining by 6 percent overnight while in standby mode. The software experience is hindered by a high amount of bloatware and advertisements.

Performance is a major concern, with owners rating it poorly for gaming and general speed. You will also need to be aware of reported signal issues and heating problems during use. While the 120Hz display offers a smooth visual experience on paper, the underlying hardware struggles to keep up with the interface.

This phone is best suited for those who prioritize a large battery and basic features over speed or gaming performance.
