What works well4

  • 6.67 inch 120Hz IPS LCD
  • 6000 mAh battery capacity
  • Headphone jack, FM radio and IR blaster
  • 128 GB or 256 GB storage options

Reported trade-offs4

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Home screen lag and performance lags
  • Battery drains quickly in standby
  • Heating and signal issues

Why we say that

The 6.67 inch IPS LCD runs at 120Hz which makes scrolling feel smooth, and the 6000 mAh cell can power a day of moderate use. Under heavy use the Helio G81 chipset shows heating and the home screen often stutters, especially when opening daily apps. Battery percentage can drop several percent overnight even in standby, so you may need to charge more often than the capacity suggests.

The 13 MP rear camera is basic and struggles in low light, while the 8 MP front shooter is adequate for video calls. Software runs Android 15 with HiOS 15.1, but it adds many ads and bloat, and only one or two OS upgrades are expected. Network signal can be weak and gaming performance is poor.

How long does the battery really last?

The phone packs a 6000 mAh cell which feels long lasting during light use, but owners report a noticeable drain overnight and faster depletion under heavy tasks, so you may need to charge daily.

Can I play games smoothly on this phone?

Gaming performance is weak. The Helio G81 chipset and limited RAM cause lag on the home screen and in games, earning a low gaming rating, so it is not suited for demanding titles.

Is the camera good in low light?

The rear camera is a 13 MP sensor with f/1.8 aperture, but reviews call it basic and it struggles in low‑light scenes, delivering modest results compared to higher‑end phones.
