Vivo S2 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can stay on for days and looks bright even in sunlight, the Vivo S2 delivers that.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if

Consider alternatives if

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 7,050 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 3,000 nits AMOLED display is very bright
  • IP68/69 water and dust resistance
  • 44 W fast charging refills quickly

Reported trade-offs4

  • No ultra‑wide camera lens
  • OriginOS includes bloatware and ads
  • Gray‑import means no official warranty
  • Price is high for the hardware

Why we say that

The 6.83‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 3,000 nits, making outdoor viewing comfortable. The 7,050 mAh battery easily covers a full day of mixed use and the 44 W charger refills it quickly. Performance is stable for everyday tasks and casual gaming thanks to the Dimensity 7360 Turbo, though it won’t push the highest graphics settings.

The 50 MP main camera captures decent low‑light photos but the lack of an ultra‑wide lens limits versatility. OriginOS 6 offers many AI tools but also brings medium‑level bloatware and ads that some users find annoying. The phone is a gray‑import, so there is no official manufacturer warranty; only the shop’s limited warranty applies.
