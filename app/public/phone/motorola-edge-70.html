Is the Motorola Edge 70 worth buying?

What is good

  • High grade IP68 and IP69 durability
  • Industry leading battery endurance
  • Very bright and color accurate P OLED
  • Lightweight and ergonomic build

What is not

  • Software bugs and random restarts
  • No telephoto lens
  • Throttles under heavy gaming loads
  • Shallow speaker quality

Buy it if You want a thin, durable phone with great battery life and a bright screen.

Skip it if You need a stable software experience or a device for heavy gaming.

Why we say that

Owners appreciate the battery endurance and the ergonomics of the thin chassis. However, living with this phone comes with software frustrations. Users report launch day bugs, bloatware, and serious instability where units shut down and restart on their own.

The speakers sound shallow in the mids and vocals. While the main camera performs well, you will miss a telephoto lens and strong video stabilization. Since these are Indian imports, you have to deal with shop warranties and check the IMEI status yourself.

It feels like a premium piece of hardware held back by inconsistent software and a lack of certain display features like Always On Display.
