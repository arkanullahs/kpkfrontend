Is the OPPO Find X9 worth buying?

What is good

  • Industry-leading 7025 mAh battery life
  • Excellent sustained gaming performance
  • Bright 3600 nits peak AMOLED display
  • Premium aluminum build with IP69 rating

What is not

  • Selfie camera lacks autofocus
  • Slow USB 2.0 transfer speeds
  • Portrait mode focus hunting issues
  • Bloatware and occasional UI bugs

Buy it if You are a power user who prioritizes battery life and gaming performance above all else and can manage the risks of unofficial gray-market imports.

Skip it if You rely on a reliable selfie camera for social media or require the peace of mind that comes with an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Owners report that the massive 7025 mAh battery is industry-leading, providing over 10 hours of screen-on time. While the Dimensity 9500 chipset handles thermal management well, the user experience is hampered by software bugs and bloatware in ColorOS 16. Photographers should be aware of persistent issues with portrait mode focus hunting and a selfie camera that lacks autofocus.

The hardware build is premium with an IP69 rating, yet the device suffers from table wobble and slow USB 2.0 data transfer speeds. Because this phone is only available through unofficial gray-import channels, you must verify if you are purchasing a China-market unit, which lacks Google services and Bangla language support. You will also rely on shop warranties rather than official brand support, so confirm the BTRC status of your unit before paying.

Does the OPPO Find X9 provide long-term software support?

Is the battery life on this phone actually good for heavy users?

Will I face issues with the camera when taking portraits?

Yes, owners report that the camera struggles with portrait mode focus hunting. Specifically, shifting from the 1x to the 2x lens often results in soft images, and the selfie camera lacks autofocus.
