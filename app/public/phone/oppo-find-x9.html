Is the OPPO Find X9 worth buying?

What is good

  • 7025 mAh battery lasts up to 1.5 days
  • Dimensity 9500 runs cool in heavy games
  • 50 MP Hasselblad‑tuned main camera
  • 6.59‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED display

What is not

  • No official OPPO warranty in Bangladesh
  • Selfie camera lacks autofocus
  • USB 2.0 limits transfer speed
  • Exposure inconsistency on main camera

Buy it if Buy if you want flagship speed, long battery life, and can live with a shop‑provided warranty.

Skip it if Skip if you need a reliable selfie focus, fast USB transfers, or an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Its 7025 mAh cell easily reaches 10+ hours of screen‑on time and can stretch to about one and a half days of normal use, while the 80 W charger refills it quickly. Dimensity 9500 stays cool and stable, handling long gaming sessions without throttling, which matches the high owner scores for gaming and overall performance. The 6.59‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED panel is bright and sharp, though it lacks LTPO efficiency.

The 50 MP main sensor with Hasselblad tuning delivers strong photos, yet owners report occasional exposure misses and focus hunting on the 2× zoom. The selfie camera struggles with autofocus, and the phone ships with USB 2.0, limiting transfer speeds. All units are gray‑import, so only the shop’s limited warranty applies and any repair costs are out‑of‑pocket.
