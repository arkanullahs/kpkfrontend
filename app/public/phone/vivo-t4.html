Why we say that

The 6.77‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can reach 5000 nits, so bright outdoor use feels comfortable. Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 delivers smooth everyday performance and 90 W charging refills the huge cell in under an hour. Android with Funtouch OS brings familiar apps but includes medium‑level bloat and only two major OS updates for budget models.

Owners praise the long‑lasting battery and vivid display, yet they miss an ultra‑wide lens and complain about the mono speaker that makes media feel thin. The glass back feels decent but the overall build is described as medium, and the lack of any official Vivo warranty means repairs must be paid through the shop’s policy. Gaming scores sit at six out of ten, so heavy gamers may feel limited, while video recording also trails behind competitors.

How long will the battery last on a typical day of use?

The Vivo T4 carries a 7300 mAh cell that owners rate 8.5 out of 10, so most users can expect a full day of mixed browsing, social media and video playback without needing to recharge. Heavy gaming will reduce that time.

Can the Vivo T4 handle modern mobile games smoothly?

Powered by the Snapdragon 7s Gen 3, the phone scores a 6 out of 10 for gaming, indicating decent performance on casual titles but occasional frame drops on demanding AAA games. If gaming is a priority, you may want a higher‑rated device.

How good is the camera in low‑light without an ultra‑wide lens?

The rear camera setup receives a 7 out of 10 rating, and the 32 MP selfie sensor performs adequately in daylight. In low‑light situations the lack of an ultra‑wide lens and modest video rating of 6 mean images can be noisy and less detailed.
