Why we say that

The Camon Air gives you a 5600 mAh cell and an AMOLED panel that can refresh at 144 Hz, yet the screen is only HD Plus so videos stop at 720p. The Helio G200 chip keeps power use low, but it struggles with heavy games and multitasking, and the Android 9 based HiOS feels cluttered. Build quality is solid plastic but fingerprints show easily.

Camera shots are decent in bright light, but low‑light scenes are noisy and lack stabilization. Overall the phone feels basic and best suited for users who value endurance over speed or high‑end performance.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 5600 mAh cell gives good endurance because the Helio G200 is low‑power, and owners rate battery life around six out of ten. It can comfortably last a full day of moderate use, but heavy gaming will drain it faster.

Is the charging speed fast enough for daily use?

Although the phone is listed with 60 W charging, owners report it actually charges at about 10 W. It took almost three hours to go from zero to full, so you should plan for a long charging period.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor captures decent daylight photos, but low‑light images are noisy and lack detail because there is no stabilization or dedicated night mode. Expect basic results in dim environments.

Will the phone receive software updates and is the warranty reliable?

The device runs HiOS 16.3 based on Android 9 and is expected to get only one or two OS upgrades. An official Bangladeshi unit is listed, so the brand’s warranty and service network apply.
