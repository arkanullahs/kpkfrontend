Is the Tecno Camon Air worth buying?

What is good

  • Large 6.78 inch 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Long lasting 5600 mAh battery
  • Fast 60W charging support
  • High resolution 50MP main camera

What is not

  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • Slow software performance
  • Limited 1 to 2 OS updates

Buy it if Buy this phone if you want a large high refresh rate screen and a big battery for media consumption.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you prefer a clean software experience without ads or need long term OS update support.

Why we say that

Living with HiOS 16.3 requires patience because the interface feels slow during daily tasks. While the 144Hz AMOLED screen makes scrolling look smooth, the high volume of pre-installed applications and advertisements can be frustrating for users who prefer a clean interface. You get a decent 50MP main camera and a 32MP selfie shooter, which perform adequately for standard photography.

The 5600 mAh battery is a highlight, providing enough power to last through long days, and the 60W charging helps you get back to full capacity quickly. However, owners should be prepared for limited software support, as the device only promises 1 to 2 OS updates. If you prioritize hardware specifications like screen refresh rate and battery capacity over a refined software experience, this phone might suit your needs.

Just be aware that the heavy software skin is the primary complaint for most users.
