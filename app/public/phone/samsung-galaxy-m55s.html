Samsung Galaxy M55s Is it worth buying?

At ৳27,977 the Galaxy M55s gives you a 6.7‑inch 120 Hz Super AMOLED+ screen that stays bright even in sunlight, but everyday use is marred by heating and a finicky under‑display fingerprint sensor.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
If you want a bright 120 Hz screen, fast charging and decent performance at a sub‑30 k taka price and can accept a gray‑import warranty, this phone fits your needs.
Consider alternatives if
If you need reliable battery endurance, official Samsung service or a flawless fingerprint sensor, look elsewhere.

What works well4

  • 120 Hz Super AMOLED+ with 1000‑nit brightness
  • Snapdragon 7 Gen 1 delivers smooth performance
  • 45W fast charging fills 5,000 mAh quickly
  • 50 MP main and selfie cameras

Reported trade-offs4

  • Inconsistent under‑display fingerprint sensor
  • Heating can cause apps to close
  • Battery life feels short for 5,000 mAh
  • No official Samsung warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 7 Gen 1 paired with 8 GB RAM feels smooth for most apps and casual gaming, though heavy games can push the device hot enough to close apps. Battery life feels short despite a 5,000 mAh cell, with owners reporting average efficiency and quick drain under load. The 50 MP main camera with OIS and a 50 MP selfie shooter capture decent photos, but tuning is only average and low‑light performance is modest.

The phone arrives as a gray‑import, so there is no official Samsung warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited warranty and paid repairs. Software runs Android 14 with One UI 8.5, promises up to four major Android upgrades and low bloat, which is a plus for long‑term use.
