Asus ROG Phone 7 Ultimate 5G Is it worth buying?

The Asus ROG Phone 7 Ultimate 5G is a specialized gaming device that prioritizes raw performance over camera versatility.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You are a dedicated mobile gamer who prioritizes high refresh rates and battery life over professional-grade photography.
Consider alternatives if

What works well4

  • Excellent 165Hz AMOLED display
  • Long-lasting 6000 mAh battery
  • Top-tier gaming performance
  • Includes a headphone jack

Reported trade-offs4

  • Basic camera setup lacks 8K video
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Limited software update support
  • Gray-import stock carries high service risk

Why we say that

Owners rate the gaming experience and display highly, noting the 165Hz AMOLED screen and 6000 mAh battery provide a long-lasting experience. However, the camera setup is considered basic compared to other 2023 flagships and lacks 8K video recording. Because this is a gray-import unit, you must rely entirely on the shop for support since Asus has little to no official service presence in Bangladesh.

The software support is limited to 2 major OS updates, which is weak for a phone at this price point. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing. If the device breaks, you are at the mercy of the shop warranty rather than a manufacturer guarantee.
