Asus ROG Phone 7 Ultimate 5G Is it worth buying?

If you want a flagship gaming phone and can live without official warranty, the ROG Phone 7 Ultimate gives you raw power at a steep price.

What works well4

  • 6000 mAh battery with 65 W fast charging
  • 165 Hz AMOLED display with 1500 nits peak brightness
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset with 16 GB RAM
  • Headphone jack for wired audio

Reported trade-offs3

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Only two major OS updates left
  • Camera setup is basic compared to 2023 flagships

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chip paired with 16 GB RAM delivers smooth performance in every game and multitasking scenario. The 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 165 Hz and can hit 1500 nits, making fast‑paced titles look vibrant even in bright light. Battery life is strong thanks to a 6000 mAh cell and 65 W fast charging, and the headphone jack lets you use wired audio without lag.

Storage is generous at 512 GB, so you won’t run out of space for large game files. However, the camera system feels basic for a 2023 flagship, lacking 8K video and struggling in low‑light scenes. Being a gray‑import, the phone carries no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh and Asus has little official service presence, so any repair will depend on the shop’s policy.

Read full reasoning

Software updates are limited to two major Android releases, which is modest for a three‑year‑old device.

How long does the battery last on the Asus ROG Phone 7 Ultimate?

The phone uses a 6000 mAh battery that owners rate 9 out of 10 for endurance. It lasts a full day of heavy use and can be topped up from zero to full in about an hour with the 65 W charger.

Is the Asus ROG Phone 7 Ultimate good for gaming?

Yes, it is built for gaming. The Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 processor, 16 GB RAM and 165 Hz AMOLED screen deliver high frame rates. Owner ratings give gaming a 9 out of 10 and performance an 8.5, and the headphone jack adds low‑latency audio.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The main camera is a 50 MP sensor with an f/1.9 aperture, but reviewers call the setup basic. It lacks 8K video and struggles in dim environments, earning a 7.5 rating for camera quality.

Will I get official warranty and service in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray‑import units, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop may offer its own limited warranty, but any repair will be paid for and Asus has little official service presence locally.
