Is the Honor Magic 6 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.8 inch LTPO OLED with 5000 nits brightness
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 gives top performance
  • 5600 mAh battery with 80W fast charge
  • 50MP main camera with OIS and telephoto

What is not

  • Photos often look oversaturated
  • Software updates arrive inconsistently
  • Ease of use feels average
  • Cheaper Magic 7 Pro now available

Buy it if Buy it if you need flagship speed, a very bright screen and strong battery for gaming and media.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable colour accuracy in photos or guaranteed fast software updates.

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 and 12GB RAM make every app feel instant and games run at high frame rates on the 6.8 inch LTPO OLED that can reach 5000 nits. The 5600 mAh battery lasts through a full day and refills quickly with 80W charging, which reviewers praised. The 50MP main sensor captures detailed stills, though many owners complain the colour tone is too bright and oversaturated.

Software updates arrive on an irregular schedule, leaving some users waiting for promised upgrades. A newer Magic 7 Pro now sells for less, so consider that if price is the main factor.
