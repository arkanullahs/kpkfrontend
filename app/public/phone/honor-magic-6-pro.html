Buying verdict

Honor Magic 6 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Honor Magic 6 Pro is a powerful flagship that delivers elite performance and display quality, but you must accept the risks of an unofficial import.

Choose it if
You want a high-performance device with a top-tier display and battery life and are comfortable managing an unofficial import without brand-backed warranty support.
Consider alternatives if
You prioritize consistent software updates, professional-grade video recording, or the security of an official manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

Owners praise the massive 5600 mAh battery and the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset for gaming, yet the software experience remains a point of frustration. While the hardware feels premium, users report that the MagicOS interface feels like an iOS clone and suffers from inconsistent update schedules. The camera system captures excellent still photos, though you may notice over-saturated colors and softness in 4K video footage.

Because this is a gray import, you have no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh. Repairs are entirely at your own expense, and you are reliant on the shop for any limited coverage. With the newer Magic 7 Pro available for less, this device is a niche choice for those who prioritize raw performance over software polish and official support.

Does the Honor Magic 6 Pro have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every listing is a gray import, meaning you have no brand-backed support. Any repairs are paid, and you must rely solely on whatever limited coverage the individual shop provides.

How is the battery life for daily use?

Owners report massive endurance from the 5600 mAh battery. It easily lasts all day even with high screen-on time. You also get fast 80W wired charging and very impressive 66W wireless charging to keep you powered up.

Is this phone good for heavy gaming?

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset makes this a beast for gaming. It maintains stable high frame rates in demanding titles like Genshin Impact. The device keeps cool during gaming, though you may experience gradual throttling if you use high-performance modes.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

Honor promises up to 4 major Android upgrades for this flagship. However, owners report significant frustration regarding software update inconsistency. Users have noted waiting for updates with zero information provided by the manufacturer.
