Is the Huawei Nova Y74 worth buying?

What is good

  • Large 6620 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 90 Hz IPS LCD with bright 850 nits peak
  • 8 GB RAM handles everyday multitasking
  • Solid build quality for the price

What is not

  • Obsolete Kirin 710A chipset slows gaming
  • No native Google Play services
  • Camera struggles in low light
  • Display limited to 720p resolution

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life and can manage without Google Play apps.

Skip it if Skip it if you want smooth gaming or a reliable low‑light camera.

Why we say that

The 6620 mAh cell delivers the kind of endurance owners call ‘never dies’, and the 40 W charger refills it quickly. The 6.67‑inch IPS LCD is bright enough with 850 nits peak, and the 90 Hz refresh makes scrolling feel smoother despite the 720p panel. Multitasking is decent thanks to 8 GB RAM, but the Kirin 710A chipset feels dated and struggles with modern games, a point many reviewers highlight.

The 50 MP main sensor produces acceptable daylight shots but falters in low light, and the lack of native Google Play services forces you to use GBox for essential apps. Software runs EMUI 12 on an Android 10 base, so you won’t get the latest features or security patches. Official units carry a brand warranty, while cheaper gray imports lack manufacturer support and require paid repairs.
