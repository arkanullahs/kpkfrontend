Is the Huawei Nova Y74 worth buying?

What is good

  • Excellent 6620 mAh battery endurance
  • Bright 6.67" 90 Hz display
  • Generous 8 GB RAM and 256 GB storage
  • Solid build for the price

What is not

  • Obsolete Kirin 710A chipset
  • No native Google Play services
  • 720p screen limits sharpness
  • Camera only decent in daylight

Buy it if Buy it if you need a long‑lasting battery and a large screen and can live with workarounds for Google apps.

Skip it if Skip it if you want smooth gaming, a strong camera, or the latest Android experience.

Why we say that

The 6620 mAh cell really does not die quickly, and the 40 W charger brings it back to full in about an hour and a half. The 6.67‑inch IPS LCD is bright enough for outdoor use and the 90 Hz refresh feels smoother than many cheap phones. Build quality feels solid for the price and the 8 GB RAM with 256 GB storage handles everyday apps without lag.

However the Kirin 710A chipset is three generations old and struggles with modern games or heavy multitasking. The 50 MP rear sensor produces only basic daylight shots and the 720p screen limits sharpness. The software runs on Android 10 under EMUI 12, so Google Play services are missing and you need the GBox workaround for most apps.

Updates are irregular and the overall experience feels dated despite the low price.
