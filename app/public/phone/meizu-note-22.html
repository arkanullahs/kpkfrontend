What works well4

  • 6600 mAh battery lasts a full day of heavy use
  • 50 MP main camera captures detailed photos in daylight
  • 6.78‑inch IPS LCD with 120 Hz refresh feels smooth
  • 40 W charger refills the large battery quickly

Reported trade-offs3

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Unisoc T8200 chipset may lag in demanding games
  • Software updates limited to one or two versions

Why we say that

The 6.78‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz and reaches a peak brightness of 5,000 nits, making outdoor use comfortable. The Unisoc T8200 chipset provides acceptable performance for everyday tasks but may feel sluggish with demanding games. Flyme AIOS 2 offers a familiar Android feel with medium bloat and about one to two OS updates during its life.

The 50 MP main sensor records sharp daylight photos, while the 8 MP selfie camera is adequate for video calls. The 6,600 mAh battery lasts most of the day and refills quickly with 40 W charging. The biggest drawback is the lack of any manufacturer warranty; you rely on the shop’s promise and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 6,600 mAh cell can easily cover a full day of moderate browsing, video streaming and social media. Heavy gaming will shorten that time, but the 40 W charger brings it back to full in about an hour and a half.

Will this phone run modern games smoothly?

The Unisoc T8200 is a mid‑range processor, so casual titles run fine and frame rates feel acceptable on the 120 Hz screen. High‑end games with heavy graphics may drop frames and feel less responsive.

How good is the camera in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor with an f/1.8 aperture captures clear images in bright light. In low‑light situations the lack of large sensor size and optical stabilization means photos can become noisy and lose detail.

What warranty and service can I expect for a gray‑import Meizu?

All listings are gray‑import, so there is no official Meizu warranty in Bangladesh. You rely on the shop’s warranty promise, and any repair will be paid by you. Confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchase.
