Is the Meizu Note 22 worth buying?

What is good

  • Very large 6600 mAh battery
  • High 5000 nits peak brightness
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • Fast 120Hz refresh rate

What is not

  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Medium amount of bloatware and ads
  • Limited official service presence in BD

Buy it if You need a long battery life and a bright screen for ৳19,999.

Skip it if You want official brand warranty and reliable after sales service.

Why we say that

It is a gray import with no manufacturer warranty, so you are entirely dependent on the shop for any repairs. If the hardware fails, you are at the mercy of the seller. The software is Flyme AIOS 2 and it comes with a medium amount of bloatware and ads.

You can expect 1 to 2 OS updates over its life. On the positive side, the battery is very large and the screen is bright. However, the risk of owning a device with no official service presence is the main point of concern for most users.

ATC Android ToTo Company · 20 হাজারে এমন ফোন কীভাবে সম্ভব? Meizu Note 22 ↗

ATC found the Meizu Note 22 extremely attractive on paper, especially for its AMOLED display, stereo speakers, under-display fingerprint reader, battery life, and hardware offered around ৳20,000. In actual use, however, software optimization became the major problem: apps lagged or crashed, gaming froze, the camera app was slow, Wi-Fi performance was inconsistent, and the cameras failed to deliver results matching their specifications. The reviewer ultimately struggled to recommend it over less impressive-looking alternatives that provide a smoother experience, suggesting it mainly for basic users who prioritize the display and media consumption.

SamZone · Meizu Note 22 Overview ! AMOLED, Ultrawide, G99 for less than 20K. ↗

SamZone's first impression was that the Meizu Note 22 provides an impressive overall package below ৳20,000 led by its AMOLED display, stereo speakers, under-display fingerprint reader, and adequate everyday performance. However, the reviewer found it less smooth than Meizu's Lucky 08, noticed some warming and problematic auto-brightness behavior, and was not impressed by the cameras, particularly their contrast, sharpening, exposure, and skin-tone handling. The lack of future major Android updates and potentially poor availability of cases, protectors, and spare parts were also highlighted as important compromises buyers should consider.
