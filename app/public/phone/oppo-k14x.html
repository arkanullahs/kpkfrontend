Is the OPPO K14x worth buying?

What is good

  • Huge 6500 mAh battery
  • 45 W fast charging
  • 50 MP main camera
  • IP64 water resistance

What is not

  • Low resolution 720p display
  • Outdated Dimensity 6300 processor
  • Unstable gyroscope for gaming
  • Heavy bloatware and ads

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize long battery life and fast charging and can accept a low‑resolution screen.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a high‑resolution display, smooth gaming performance, or an official warranty.

Why we say that

Its 6500 mAh battery and 45 W fast charger give you more than a day of use and quick top‑ups. The 50 MP main camera sounds impressive, but owners say image quality is below average and the 5 MP selfie lens is outdated. The 6.75‑inch IPS LCD is a 120 Hz panel, yet the resolution stays at 720p, resulting in a yellowish, soft picture that many find a deal breaker.

Performance is held back by the recycled Dimensity 6300 processor, poor RAM handling and an unstable gyroscope, which together make gaming feel laggy and cause 5G to drop to 4G. The phone runs ColorOS 15 with medium bloatware, and because it is a gray import there is no official OPPO warranty, only the shop’s limited cover.

Will the battery last a full day with heavy use?

The 6500 mAh battery gives the phone a very long runtime, and most owners report it easily lasts a full day of heavy use. It also supports 45 W charging, so you can refill the charge in under an hour.

How does the phone perform for gaming?

Gaming performance suffers because the phone uses the older Dimensity 6300 chip and shows poor RAM management. Reviewers note an unstable gyroscope and frequent 5G drops to 4G, resulting in a low gaming rating.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor is advertised as high resolution, but owners describe the photos as below average, especially in low light, and the 5 MP selfie camera is also outdated. Expect modest results.

Does the phone come with an official warranty and software updates?

All units are gray imports, so there is no official OPPO warranty in Bangladesh; you only get the shop’s limited warranty and paid repairs. The phone runs ColorOS 15 and may receive three to four major Android updates, but it includes medium level bloatware and ads.
