Is the OPPO K14x worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6500 mAh battery
  • 45W fast charging
  • 120Hz refresh rate
  • Headphone jack included

What is not

  • 720p low resolution display
  • Cheap plastic build feel
  • Outdated Dimensity 6300 processor
  • Unstable gyroscope for gaming

Buy it if If you need long battery life, fast charging and can accept a low‑resolution screen, this phone may fit your budget.

Skip it if If you care about camera quality, smooth gaming or an official warranty, look elsewhere.

Why we say that

It also supports 45W fast charging, so you can refill the large cell in under an hour. The 6.75 inch IPS LCD panel runs at 120Hz, giving smooth scrolling, but the 720p resolution makes images look soft and the yellow tint hurts sharpness. Build quality feels like cheap plastic despite an IP64 rating, and the grip is comfortable.

The Dimensity 6300 chipset is the same older processor found in K13x and K12x, leading to sluggish performance and unstable gyroscope during gaming. Camera results are below average; the 50MP main sensor and 5MP selfie lens struggle in low light. Software runs ColorOS 15 on Android 15 with noticeable bloatware and medium ads.

The phone is a gray‑import, so there is no official manufacturer warranty; only the shop’s limited warranty applies and repairs are paid.
