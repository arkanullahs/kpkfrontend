Is the iQOO Neo 10 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 144 Hz LTPO AMOLED screen
  • 6100 mAh battery lasts all day
  • Dimensity 9400 handles games smoothly
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Only gray‑import stock with shop warranty
  • Network issues reported in Dhaka
  • Build feels solid but not premium

Buy it if Buy it if you want a fast gaming phone with long battery and can tolerate a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need an official brand warranty or flawless network connectivity.

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED panel reaches 4500 nits peak brightness and runs at 144Hz, making scrolling and games feel smooth. The 6100 mAh cell is rated excellent by owners and easily covers a full day of mixed use. Dimensity 9400 paired with up to 16GB RAM delivers solid performance and a gaming score of 8.8.

The 50MP main sensor with OIS captures decent photos, though it is not class‑leading in low light. Build quality feels good but not premium, and the phone ships with a shop warranty only. Software runs Android 15 with OriginOS 5, receives three OS updates and carries medium bloat and ads.
