Is the Apple iPhone Air worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz LTPO OLED display with 3000‑nit peak
  • Apple A19 Pro chip with 12 GB RAM
  • Thin and light body with solid build
  • 48 MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Battery life weaker than other iPhones
  • Charging limited to USB 2.0 and slow
  • Speaker sound weaker than iPhone 13 Pro Max

Buy it if Buy it if you value a top‑class display and fast performance and can accept modest battery endurance and slower charging.

Skip it if Skip it if you need long battery life, fast charging, or premium audio quality.

Why we say that

Daily use feels smooth thanks to the Apple A19 Pro chip and 12 GB of RAM, and the thin light body feels premium. The 48 MP main camera with OIS captures clear photos in good light, though owners note it struggles in low‑light and lacks the depth of a multi‑lens system. Battery life is modest; the 3149 mAh cell does not last as long as older iPhones and charging is limited to USB 2.0, making top‑ups slower.

Audio output is quieter than the iPhone 13 Pro Max, which some users notice during media playback. Software remains clean and receives five to six major iOS updates, keeping the device secure. Overall the phone feels fast and beautiful on screen, but you must accept weaker endurance and slower charging.
