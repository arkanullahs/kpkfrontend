Why we say that

The display is stunning and the software support is industry leading with 7 major Android upgrades. However, you must be aware of the reported green line defect on the AMOLED screen. While daily tasks are snappy, the phone suffers from aggressive thermal throttling under heavy load.

Gaming performance drops as the CPU and GPU throttle down. You will also find that the included cable cannot actually support the maximum 45W charging speed. Since these are gray imports, you have no manufacturer warranty and must rely on the shop for any issues.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is a gray import. It has no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Any warranty provided is from the shop itself, and all repairs are paid.

How is the gaming performance and heating?

Daily use is snappy, but the phone throttles aggressively under sustained load. Owners report that the CPU and GPU both throttle down in performance, and battery drains fast during gaming.

How long will this phone get software updates?

This phone runs Android 14 and is promised up to 7 major Android upgrades. It uses a polished One UI with low bloatware and useful AI features.

Is the battery life good for daily use?

The 4900 mAh battery is solid for daily use. However, some users find it inferior to the S23+ and note that 45W charging is slow compared to rivals.
