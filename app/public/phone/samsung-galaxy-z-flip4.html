Is the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip4 worth buying?

What is good

  • Stunning 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Strong main camera with OIS
  • Premium build materials
  • Smooth One UI for foldables

What is not

  • Hinge and flex cable failures cause blackouts
  • Battery barely lasts a full day
  • Severe thermal throttling during gaming
  • Hard to open with one hand

Buy it if You should buy it if you want a stylish foldable with good selfies and can handle occasional hinge issues.

Skip it if Skip it if you need all day battery life or plan heavy gaming without overheating.

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch foldable screen shows vivid colors at 120Hz and the crease is visible but not distracting. The 12 MP main camera with OIS delivers sharp photos and the cover screen selfie camera produces high quality images. Battery life is modest; the 3700 mAh unit lasts a full day for most users but may need charging by evening.

The Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 chip feels snappy for daily tasks yet throttles heavily during gaming, dropping performance to about 40 percent. Owners frequently report hinge and flex cable failures that cause screen blackouts or power offs, and opening the device often requires two hands. Software is smooth with One UI and updates may continue for a few more years, but the phone is four years old and future support is uncertain.
