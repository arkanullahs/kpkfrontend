Reported trade-offs

  • Hinge and flex cable prone to failure
  • Battery lasts less than a full day
  • Heavy heat throttles gaming performance

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch Foldable Dynamic AMOLED 2X panel delivers 120Hz smoothness and peaks at 1200 nits, making outdoor viewing easy. Under the hood sits the Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 paired with 8GB RAM, which feels quick for everyday apps but drops performance after a few minutes of gaming because of heat and throttling. The 3700 mAh cell is an improvement over the previous model but still struggles to last a full day of mixed use.

Camera quality is solid with a 12MP main sensor and a 10MP selfie shooter that benefits from the cover screen. One UI 8 runs Android 12 and can be upgraded to Android 16, though at four years old the phone may receive few more updates. Official warranty and Samsung service network are available in Bangladesh, but the reported hinge and flex cable failures remain a concern.

How long does the battery last on normal use?

The 3700 mAh cell is an improvement over the Flip 3, but owners report it struggles to reach a full day of mixed use. Expect a day of light tasks and need a charge by evening.

Can I play games without the phone heating up?

The Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 and 8 GB RAM feel fast for everyday apps, but during gaming the device heats quickly and throttles after about five minutes, dropping performance to roughly forty percent of its peak.

Is the camera usable in low light?

The main 12 MP sensor with a 1/1.76″ size, f/1.8 aperture and optical image stabilization captures decent low light shots, though it is not class leading. The 10 MP selfie camera benefits from the cover screen for better portraits.

What warranty and software support does the phone have in Bangladesh?

An official Samsung unit sold in Bangladesh carries the brand’s warranty and can be serviced through Samsung’s local network. At four years old the phone is near the end of its software update window, so only a few more Android versions are expected.
