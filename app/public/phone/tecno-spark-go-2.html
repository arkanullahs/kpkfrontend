Is the Tecno Spark Go 2 worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz IPS LCD display
  • Includes an IR blaster
  • Has a headphone jack
  • Runs Android 15

What is not

  • Battery drains quickly when idle
  • Slow software with high ads
  • Basic 13MP camera
  • Low build quality ratings

Buy it if You need a very cheap phone with a high refresh rate screen and an IR blaster.

Skip it if You want a phone with reliable battery life and clean software.

Why we say that

Owners report that the battery drops even when the device is idle. This is a major concern for a 5000 mAh battery. The software experience is also frustrating because it is slow and contains high amounts of bloatware and ads.

While the 120Hz screen is a nice touch, the overall build and display quality receive low ratings from users. The 13MP camera is basic and does not offer much. You get a device with an IR blaster and a headphone jack, but the daily usability is hampered by software issues and power drain.

It feels like a budget device where the hardware and software are not well optimized.
