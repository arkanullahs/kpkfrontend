Is the Tecno Spark Go 2 worth buying?

What is good

  • Smooth 120Hz IPS LCD display
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • Features an IR blaster
  • Built-in FM radio

What is not

  • Battery drains quickly while idle
  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • Poor overall owner satisfaction ratings
  • Slow software update support

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a budget device with an IR blaster and a high refresh rate display for basic tasks.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you need reliable battery life or a clean software experience without excessive advertisements.

Why we say that

Owners report that the phone drains power quickly, which is a major concern for daily reliability. While the 120Hz display offers a smooth visual experience, the overall user satisfaction remains low across all categories including build quality and camera performance. You will encounter high levels of bloatware and advertisements within the HiOS 15 interface.

The software experience is further hampered by slow updates, with only 1 to 2 OS upgrades expected over the life of the device. Performance is rated poorly by current users, making it difficult to recommend for anything beyond basic tasks. If you prioritize a phone that stays powered throughout the day, this model will likely cause frustration due to its rapid power consumption.
