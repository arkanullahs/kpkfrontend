What works well

  • Bright and smooth 120Hz Dynamic AMOLED 2X display
  • Strong general performance and ease of use
  • Good camera quality across the board
  • Low amount of bloatware and ads

Reported trade-offs

  • Average battery life
  • Gaming performance affected by heat
  • Limited remaining software update life
  • Reports of green line issues

Why we say that

It offers a bright and smooth display and strong general performance. However, living with it means dealing with average battery life that may require a power adapter. Some owners report that gaming performance drops when the device gets hot.

There are also mentions of potential green line issues common to S Series phones. While it launched with promises of updates, the device is now 3 years old. This means you should expect short or no remaining software update life.
