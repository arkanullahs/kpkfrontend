Why we say that

The 6.6 inch IPS LCD is bright enough with 500 nits, and the 5000 mAh battery can easily last a day of browsing, calls and media. Everyday use feels smooth for basic apps, but the Helio A22 chipset and 3‑4 GB RAM show their limits when you open many tabs or try demanding games. The Android 12 based XOS skin is heavy with pre‑installed apps and frequent notification ads, which many owners find annoying.

Software updates are almost finished after three years, so you should not expect new features. The phone arrives as gray‑import stock, so only a shop warranty may be offered and you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery can easily power the phone through a full day of normal use such as browsing, calls and media playback. Charging is limited to 10W, so it refills slowly compared to fast‑charge models.

Can I play games smoothly on this phone?

The Helio A22 processor with 3‑4 GB RAM handles casual games without major lag, but heavier titles will feel sluggish. Expect lower frame rates and occasional stutters during demanding gaming sessions.

Will I get an official warranty or software updates?

The phone is sold as gray‑import stock, so only a shop warranty may be offered and you must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller. At three years old, it has little to no remaining software‑update life.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 13 MP main sensor and 5 MP selfie lens are adequate in daylight, but they lack advanced low‑light features. Photos in dim environments will be noisy and lack detail.
