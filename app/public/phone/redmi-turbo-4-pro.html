Is the Redmi Turbo 4 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz AMOLED display with 3200‑nit peak
  • Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 delivers strong performance
  • 7550 mAh battery provides long endurance

What is not

  • China ROM lacks Google services and Bangla
  • Thermal throttling and overheat shutdowns in heavy games
  • Camera struggles in low light and ultrawide
  • Fake chargers and notification delays reported

Buy it if If you want flagship‑level speed, a huge battery and a bright screen at a sub‑50 k taka price, this phone fits your needs.

Skip it if If you need a reliable camera, smooth gaming without heat, or official software support, look elsewhere.

Why we say that

Its Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset makes everyday apps feel instant and gaming can reach 90 FPS in titles like PUBG. The 6.83‑inch AMOLED panel shines even in bright sunlight thanks to a 3200‑nit peak and 120 Hz refresh. The 7550 mAh cell lets you stay unplugged for days, yet heavy use can drain it faster than expected and the 90 W charger needs about 75 minutes for a full top‑up.

Owners report delayed notifications, especially on WhatsApp, and the software is loaded with ads and bloatware. The camera produces darker pictures in low light and the ultrawide lens is weak, while the phone may overheat and throttle during long gaming sessions. Build quality feels premium but the glass can dent easily.

Buying from gray‑import channels means you get a shop warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

The 7550 mAh cell can keep the phone running for two to three days with moderate use, but heavy gaming or video streaming will drain it faster and may trigger the occasional high‑drain warning.

Will the phone overheat while playing games?

Yes, the device can become very hot during long gaming sessions and may throttle performance or show an overheat warning, especially in demanding titles like Genshin Impact.

Is the camera usable in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor produces darker images in low light and the ultrawide lens is weak, so night photography will be disappointing compared to other phones in this price range.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

Gray‑import units come with a shop warranty, not the official brand warranty, and run a China ROM that lacks Google services. Updates are limited to 2‑4 OS versions and the software includes a lot of ads and bloatware.
