Is the Redmi Turbo 4 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz AMOLED display with 3200 nit peak
  • Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 performance
  • 7550 mAh battery with 90W fast charge
  • 50MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • China ROM lacks Google services
  • Severe notification delays
  • Gets very hot during gaming
  • Shop warranty only, no brand warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you need top gaming power and can accept a China ROM without Google apps.

Skip it if Skip it if you rely on Google services or need an official Xiaomi warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.83 inch AMOLED panel refreshes at 120Hz and can reach 3200 nits, so bright outdoor use feels smooth. Under the hood sits a Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 paired with up to 12 GB RAM, giving a performance rating of 8.9 and gaming scores of 9.1. The 7550 mAh cell is rated 9.2 for battery and users see more than ten hours of basic use, while 90W charging refills it quickly.

The main camera uses a 50 MP 1/1.95 inch sensor with f/1.5 aperture and OIS, but owner ratings sit at only 6.2, indicating average low‑light results. The frame is Gorilla Glass 7i but dents easily and the device gets very hot during screen recording or long gaming sessions. Network users report occasional 5G drops in some regions.

The phone arrives as a China ROM with HyperOS, missing Google apps, showing severe notification delays, and cannot be flashed outside China. Only a shop warranty is offered, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.
