Is the Realme 12 4G worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 2000-nit 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Fast 80W charging included in the box
  • Elegant luxury watch design
  • Capable 50MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Noticeable system lag and app switching delays
  • Unsuitable for modern gaming
  • Hybrid SIM slot limits flexibility
  • Lacks an ultrawide camera lens

Buy it if Buy this phone if you primarily watch media and need a very bright, high-refresh-rate display for outdoor visibility.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a gamer or a power user who needs a device that handles multitasking without lag.

Why we say that

While the 2000-nit AMOLED screen is excellent for visibility, owners frequently report noticeable lag when switching between apps. The design mimics a luxury watch, but the inclusion of a hybrid SIM slot limits your storage and connectivity options. Photography is limited to a decent main sensor that lacks an ultrawide lens, making it less versatile for group shots or landscapes.

Battery life is polarizing, with some users satisfied while others describe it as the worst they have experienced. You will also encounter a significant amount of bloatware within the Realme UI 5.0 software. If you prioritize a bright, smooth screen over gaming power or system speed, this phone might suit your needs.

However, those expecting a smooth multitasking experience or a reliable gaming device will likely find the Snapdragon 685 chipset disappointing.
