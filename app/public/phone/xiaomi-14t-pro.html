Xiaomi 14T Pro Is it worth buying?

The Xiaomi 14T Pro is a powerful device that currently only exists as a gray-import unit in Bangladesh, meaning you will not receive any manufacturer warranty support.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize raw performance and gaming power above all else and are comfortable managing a device without official brand warranty support.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable all-day battery life or want the best value for your money, as the newer Xiaomi 15T Pro is cheaper.

Why we say that

While the Dimensity 9300+ chipset delivers strong performance and gaming capabilities, real-world usage is hampered by significant battery concerns. Owners frequently report that the 5000 mAh battery drains very quickly, particularly when using the Chrome browser. You are paying ৳90,990 for this device, yet the newer Xiaomi 15T Pro is available for ৳77,999, making this model a difficult recommendation.

You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before purchasing, as you are relying entirely on the shop for any repair promises. The software experience includes high levels of bloatware and ads in system apps, though these can be disabled.

Does the Xiaomi 14T Pro come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit currently available is a gray import. You will have to rely on the specific shop for any repair promises, and all repairs will be paid services.

How is the battery life on this device?

Owners report that the battery drains very fast. Users specifically note that the drain is noticeable when using the Chrome browser. Despite the 5000 mAh capacity, the real-world experience is rated at only 6.5 out of 10 by owners.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 14 and HyperOS. It is scheduled to receive 3 to 4 OS updates and 4 to 5 years of security updates. Be aware that the system comes with high levels of bloatware and ads.

Is this phone good for gaming?

The phone is quite capable for gaming, with owners giving it a performance rating of 8.5 and a gaming rating of 8.0. It is powered by the Dimensity 9300+ chipset, which handles demanding tasks well.
