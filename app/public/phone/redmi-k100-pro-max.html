Redmi K100 Pro Max Is it worth buying?

The Redmi K100 Pro Max gives you a gigantic 9,070 mAh battery that can last a full day of heavy use, but you pay for it with heat and a Chinese software experience.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want top tier speed, a massive battery and can live with a Chinese ROM and shop provided warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable camera in low light, official warranty, or a smooth ad free software experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 9,070 mAh battery with 100 W fast charging
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance
  • 6.9 inch 185 Hz AMOLED with 4,500 nits brightness
  • 200 MP main camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs4

  • Chinese ROM lacks Google services and Bangla
  • Thermal throttling causes heating during gaming
  • Aggressive AI smoothing makes faces look plastic
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

Powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, it delivers flagship level speed in apps and games, yet under sustained load the chip reaches about 46 °C and throttles, reducing performance. The 6.9 inch AMOLED panel shines with 4,500 nits peak brightness and a buttery 185 Hz refresh, though the lack of LTPO means the display sips more power than comparable flagships. The 200 MP main camera is technically impressive, but the HyperOS CN ROM applies aggressive AI smoothing that can make faces look plastic and struggles with white balance in low light.

The phone ships as a gray import Chinese unit, so there is no official Xiaomi warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited coverage and must handle repairs yourself. Overall, it suits power users who accept these compromises.

How long will the battery last with daily heavy use?

The 9,070 mAh cell is rated as industry leading, and owners report it easily covers a full day of heavy browsing, gaming and video playback, often lasting beyond 12 hours before needing the 100 W fast charge.

Will the phone overheat while gaming?

Yes, the Snapdragon 8 Elite can hit around 46 °C under intense gaming, and owners note the device throttles core speeds to manage heat, which can lower frame rates after long sessions.

How good is the camera in low light and does AI affect photos?

The 200 MP sensor and OIS are strong on paper, but the Chinese ROM applies aggressive AI smoothing that can make skin look plastic and the white balance can shift, especially in low light scenes.

Is there any official warranty and how many Android updates will I get?

All units are gray import Chinese models, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; the shop offers its own limited coverage and any repairs are paid. Software updates are limited to two to four major Android versions depending on the tier.
