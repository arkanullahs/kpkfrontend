Is the Nokia 5310 2024 worth buying?

What is good

  • Weeks of standby battery life
  • Headphone jack and FM radio
  • Clicky T9 keyboard
  • Lightweight plastic build

What is not

  • VGA camera nearly unusable
  • Only 16 MB internal storage
  • No 4G or Wi‑Fi connectivity
  • Low‑resolution display can be bright

Buy it if Buy if you want a nostalgic feature phone with long battery life and a simple keypad for calls and texts.

Skip it if Skip if you need a smartphone for apps, good camera, fast internet or official warranty support.

Why we say that

The 1450 mAh battery can sit idle for weeks on a single charge, which owners love. The device feels light and the clicky T9 keyboard works well for quick typing. It still has a headphone jack and FM radio, so you can listen to music without extra accessories.

The 2.8‑inch IPS LCD shows bright colours but the low resolution can be harsh on the eyes. The Unisoc 6531F chipset only handles simple games like Snake and basic arcade titles. The VGA 0.3 MP camera is almost useless, especially in low light.

Storage is limited to a meagre 16 MB, so you cannot keep many files. There is no 4G or Wi‑Fi support and the phone comes as a gray import with only a shop warranty, not an official one.
