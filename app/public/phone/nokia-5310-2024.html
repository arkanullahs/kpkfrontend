Buying verdict

Nokia 5310 2024 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can sit on a shelf for weeks without charging, the Nokia 5310 2024 delivers that.

Choose it if
You want a nostalgic feature phone with ultra‑long battery life and you do not need apps, modern camera or internet connectivity.
Consider alternatives if
You expect a smartphone experience, decent camera, ample storage, or official manufacturer warranty.

What works well

  • Weeks of standby on a single charge
  • Lightweight plastic with clicky T9 keyboard
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • Clean ad‑free simple interface

Reported trade-offs

  • VGA 0.3 MP camera is practically unusable
  • Only 16 MB internal storage
  • No 4G or Wi‑Fi connectivity
  • Low‑resolution display can be blindingly bright

Why we say that

The 1450 mAh battery truly lasts for weeks in standby, which is the biggest plus for users who only need calls, texts and FM radio. The lightweight plastic body feels cheap but the clicky T9 keypad is satisfying for nostalgic typing. However, the phone offers only 16 MB of internal storage, no 4G or Wi‑Fi, and a VGA 0.3 MP camera that is practically unusable even in daylight.

The 2.8‑inch IPS LCD is bright but low‑resolution and can be harsh on the eyes. Performance is limited to simple Snake‑style games and the S30+ interface has no ads but also no modern apps. Because it is a gray‑import, there is no official manufacturer warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited warranty and must pay for any repairs.

How long does the battery last on the Nokia 5310 2024?

The 1450 mAh battery gives an impressive standby time that can stretch to several weeks on a single charge. Heavy use such as frequent calls or FM listening will reduce that time, but everyday standby remains very long.

Can I play games on this phone?

The device runs a basic S30+ platform and can only handle simple titles like Snake and other arcade games. Gaming performance is rated low and it cannot run modern mobile games or graphics‑intensive apps.

Is the camera usable in low light?

The phone carries a VGA 0.3 MP sensor that is nearly useless by today’s standards. In low‑light conditions the image is very dark and lacks detail, making it unsuitable for any serious photography.

Will I get any official warranty or software updates?

All listings are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. You only receive the shop’s limited warranty and must pay for any repairs. Software updates are uncertain as the brand is in transition after the HMD split.
