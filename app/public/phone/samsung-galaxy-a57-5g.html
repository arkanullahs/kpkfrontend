Samsung Galaxy A57 5G Is it worth buying?

The Samsung Galaxy A57 5G offers a long-term software commitment but struggles to justify its price against the S-series FE models.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize long-term software support and a bright display for daily tasks over high-end gaming performance.
Consider alternatives if
You are a heavy gamer or expect premium charging speeds and consistent hardware quality for your money.

What works well4

  • Industry-leading 6-year Android update support
  • Bright and vivid 120Hz Super AMOLED+ display
  • Reliable main and selfie camera performance
  • Solid battery endurance from 5000 mAh cell

Reported trade-offs4

  • Slow 45W charging speed is outdated
  • Struggles with heavy gaming and heat
  • Inconsistent display panel quality concerns
  • Lacks flagship-tier AI camera features

Why we say that

Living with this device means enjoying a bright 1900 nits Super AMOLED+ display and clean One UI 8.5 software, yet owners frequently report frustration with the slow 45W charging speed and the lack of wireless charging. While the Exynos 1680 chipset handles daily tasks, it is not built for heavy gaming and tends to heat up. Users have also raised concerns regarding inconsistent display panel quality, with reports suggesting some units use Chinese OLED panels rather than Samsung's own.

The camera system is reliable for standard shots, though it lacks the advanced AI features found in flagship devices. Ultimately, the phone provides solid endurance, but the overall value proposition remains a point of contention for many buyers.

Does the Samsung Galaxy A57 5G get long-term software support?

Yes, the device runs on Android 16 and includes industry-leading support with up to 6 major Android upgrades. This ensures the phone remains current with software features for several years after your purchase.

Is the Samsung Galaxy A57 5G suitable for heavy gaming?

The Exynos 1680 chipset is stable for daily tasks, but it struggles with heavy gaming. Owners report that the device tends to heat up during intensive sessions, resulting in a gaming rating of 6.2 out of 10.

What is the difference between official and gray-import units?

Official units come with a brand warranty, while gray imports have no manufacturer cover. If you take a gray-import unit to an official service centre, they will only accept it as a paid repair.

How does the camera perform in real-world usage?

The 50MP main camera and 12MP selfie camera provide reliable performance for everyday photography. However, the system lacks the flagship-tier AI features that some users might expect at this price point.
