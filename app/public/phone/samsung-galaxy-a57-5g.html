Is the Samsung Galaxy A57 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 1900‑nit Super AMOLED+ display
  • 5000 mAh battery with good endurance
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • Android 16 with up to six OS upgrades

What is not

  • Slow 45 W charging, no wireless charger
  • Possible electric tingling on frame while charging
  • Inconsistent washed‑out CSOT panel
  • No charger or case in box

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large bright screen, solid camera and long software support at a mid‑range price.

Skip it if Skip it if you need fast gaming performance or a reliable fast‑charging experience.

Why we say that

Its 6.7‑inch Super AMOLED+ panel can reach 1900 nits, yet many owners notice a washed‑out look because Samsung uses Chinese CSOT panels. The 5000 mAh battery lasts well through a day and editorial reviews confirm improved endurance, but the 45 W charger feels sluggish and there is no wireless option. The phone feels slim, light and IP68 protected, though the glossy back and missing charger or case in the box disappoint some buyers.

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures sharp photos and decent video, while the 12 MP selfie camera is also good. Performance is smooth for everyday tasks, but gaming scores low and heavy titles may lag. Samsung promises Android 16 with up to six major OS upgrades and low bloat, giving decent long‑term software value despite the higher price tag.
