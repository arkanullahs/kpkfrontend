Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy A57 5G Is it worth buying?

If you want a large 5G phone with a bright 6.7 inch Super AMOLED+ screen and solid daily performance, the Galaxy A57 5G can deliver, but expect a few real‑world annoyances.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a big 5G screen, solid daily performance and long software support.
Consider alternatives if
Fast charging, top‑tier gaming or a low price are your top priorities.

What works well4

  • 6.7 inch Super AMOLED+ 120Hz display
  • IP68 water and dust resistance
  • Six years of Android updates
  • 50MP main camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs4

  • 45W charging feels slow
  • CSOT panel can look washed out
  • Aluminum frame leaks current while charging
  • Gaming performance is modest

Why we say that

The Exynos 1680 and 8GB RAM keep apps smooth, the 50MP main sensor with OIS is reliable for social media, and the IP68 rating protects against water and dust. Owners praise the six‑year Android update promise and the 5000 mAh battery that lasts through a typical day. However, the 45W charger feels sluggish, the CSOT panel can appear washed out, and the aluminum frame may give a tingling sensation due to current leakage while charging.

Gaming performance is modest and the price, even at the cheapest gray‑import, is seen as high compared with flagship models. Choose this phone only if you value long‑term software support and a premium display over fast charging and high‑end gaming.
