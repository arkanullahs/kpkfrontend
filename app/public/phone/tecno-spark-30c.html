Is the Tecno Spark 30C worth buying?

What is good

  • Smooth 120Hz display
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • Features an IR blaster
  • Decent 50MP main camera

What is not

  • Frequent crashing and lagging
  • Boring software with high ads
  • Poor performance and gaming
  • Overheating issues reported

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a basic device with an IR blaster and a high refresh rate display for a low price.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you need a reliable device for multitasking or gaming without constant system crashes.

Why we say that

While the 120Hz display is a highlight, owners report significant frustration with the phone crashing and lagging when trying to open simple applications like the gallery. The user experience is further hampered by a boring operating system that comes with high levels of bloatware and advertisements. Performance ratings are low across the board, with owners specifically noting issues with gaming and general ease of use.

While the camera quality is considered average, the overall build quality and software optimization leave much to be desired. Owners have also reported issues with overheating during use. If you prioritize a smooth experience without constant system crashes, this device may not meet your expectations for a daily driver.
