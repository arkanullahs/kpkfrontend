Buying verdict

Tecno Spark 30C Is it worth buying?

Expect significant performance issues and software frustration with this device.

Choose it if
You need a basic phone with a 120Hz screen and an ir blaster.
Consider alternatives if
You want a smooth experience without lagging, crashing, or heavy ads.

What works well

  • 120Hz IPS LCD display
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • Has an ir blaster
  • 50MP main camera

Reported trade-offs

  • Frequent crashing and lagging
  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • Reports of overheating
  • Boring software experience

Why we say that

While it has a 120Hz display and a 50MP camera, owners report that the phone crashes and lags frequently. Some users cannot even open two apps or access the gallery without the system crashing. The HiOS software is described as boring and contains high levels of bloatware and ads.

Overheating is also a reported problem. While the display is viewed as good by some, the actual user experience is marred by poor ease of use and low performance ratings. It is a device that struggles with basic multitasking despite the RAM options.
