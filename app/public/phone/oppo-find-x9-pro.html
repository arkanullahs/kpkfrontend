Is the OPPO Find X9 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 7500 mAh battery lasts two full days
  • Snappy performance and reliable gaming frame rates
  • Beautiful color processing in camera photos
  • Convenient 80W fast charger included in the box

What is not

  • Frequent voice drops and muffled call quality
  • Telephoto camera fails to focus at medium zoom
  • Slow USB 2.0 data transfer speeds
  • Unbalanced stereo speaker audio

Buy it if You prioritize extreme battery longevity and raw gaming performance above all else and are comfortable managing an unofficial gray-import device.

Skip it if You rely on your phone for clear voice calls or need a reliable camera for zoom photography and official brand warranty support.

Why we say that

While the Dimensity 9500 chipset delivers snappy performance and reliable gaming frame rates, the user experience is marred by widely reported issues. Owners frequently complain about voice drops and muffled audio during calls, which is attributed to the dual SIM antenna design. The camera system also struggles with focus failures at medium zoom levels when Auto Night mode is disabled.

You will also face limitations with slow USB 2.0 data transfer speeds and unbalanced stereo speakers. Because this is only available through unofficial gray-import channels, you are limited to shop warranties rather than official brand support. Always verify if you are purchasing the China-market version, as it lacks Google services and Bangla language support.

The device runs on Android 16 and is promised up to 5 major Android upgrades. You should expect a clean and stable experience with ColorOS 16, though you may encounter a medium amount of bloatware and ads depending on the software version.

Is the battery life actually good for heavy users?

Owners rate the battery performance at 9.8 out of 10. The massive 7500 mAh capacity is highly efficient and easily lasts two full days of moderate use, making it one of the strongest features of this phone.

Does the camera perform well in low light conditions?

The 50MP main camera features a large 1/1.28 inch sensor and OIS, which helps with beautiful color processing. However, be aware that the telephoto lens often fails to focus at 6x to 10x zoom unless you keep Auto Night mode enabled.
