OPPO Find X9 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you can live without a Bangladeshi manufacturer warranty and tolerate a few hardware quirks, the Find X9 Pro gives you a massive battery and flagship performance for around ৳95,000.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want long battery life, fast charging and top‑tier performance and are okay with a gray‑import warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need official brand support, reliable call quality or a true LTPO display.

What works well4

  • 7500 mAh battery lasts two days
  • 80 W fast charger included
  • Dimensity 9500 provides flagship speed
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs4

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Telephoto focus fails at medium zoom
  • Display uses LTPS, not true LTPO
  • USB 2.0 limits data speed

Why we say that

The 7500 mAh cell easily lasts two full days of moderate use and the included 80 W charger refills it in under an hour. Dimensity 9500 paired with 12 GB RAM delivers snappy app launches and steady gaming frame rates, which owners rate highly. The 6.78‑inch AMOLED is bright with 3600‑nit peaks, but it uses LTPS rather than true LTPO, so the 120 Hz refresh feels less fluid.

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures vivid colors and accurate portraits, yet the 3× telephoto often fails to focus at medium zoom and can blur in Auto Night mode. Call quality suffers from voice drops on dual‑SIM use, and the USB port is limited to 2.0 speeds. Speakers are unbalanced and heat can concentrate on the back during heavy tasks.

All units are gray imports, so there is no official OPPO warranty; only the shop’s limited warranty applies, and you must verify the IMEI and whether the build is the global or China version.
