Buying verdict

OPPO Find X9 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you can live without a Bangladeshi warranty and tolerate a few hardware quirks, the Find X9 Pro gives you massive battery and flagship performance.

What works well4

  • 7500 mAh battery lasts two days
  • 80 W charger included in the box
  • Dimensity 9500 gives strong gaming performance
  • 50 MP main camera with vivid color processing

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official Bangladeshi warranty
  • Display uses LTPS not true LTPO
  • Telephoto focus fails at medium zoom
  • Call quality drops with dual SIM

Why we say that

The 7,500 mAh cell easily pushes past two days of moderate use, and the included 80 W charger refills it in under an hour. Dimensity 9500 paired with 16 GB RAM keeps apps and games running smoothly, and the 120 Hz AMOLED stays bright with a 3,600‑nit peak. The 50 MP main sensor delivers vivid colors and reliable portrait edges, while the 50 MP selfie camera is also sharp.

However, the phone arrives as a gray‑import unit, so there is no brand‑backed warranty in Bangladesh and you rely on the shop’s limited cover. The display uses LTPS instead of true LTPO, which some users notice as less fluid. Medium‑zoom telephoto often fails to focus, and dual‑SIM calls can drop or sound muffled.

Is the phone suitable for heavy gaming?

Yes, the Dimensity 9500 chipset with 16 GB RAM delivers snappy launches and sustained frame rates, and the 120 Hz screen helps, though heat may feel localized on the back during long sessions.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.5 aperture and OIS produces bright colors and accurate portraits, but the telephoto lens often fails to focus at medium zoom and Auto Night mode can be unreliable.
