Is the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 7 worth buying?

What is good

  • Stunning 2600 nits foldable display
  • Exynos 2500 delivers strong raw power
  • 12 GB RAM with up to 512 GB storage
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Glacial 25 W charging speed
  • Frequent overheating during heavy use
  • Hinge may develop wobble over time
  • Inner screen easily dents

Buy it if Buy it if you love a premium flip design, need a bright screen and can tolerate slower charging and occasional heat.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a reliable gaming device, fast charging or an official Samsung warranty in Bangladesh.

Why we say that

The Exynos 2500 chipset and 12 GB RAM give you smooth everyday performance and quick app launches. The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures clear photos, though low‑light detail can fall short without a telephoto lens. Battery life is modest; the 4300 mAh cell rates 6.5 out of 10 and charging at 25 W feels glacial.

Users report the hinge loosening after months and the inner display picking up dents from normal handling. Overheating appears during wireless Android Auto, video calls, or long gaming sessions, and the device throttles heavily under sustained load. Software is feature‑rich with One UI 8 and up to seven Android upgrades, but the cover screen needs GoodLock for full use.

Trakin Tech · Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 7 Unboxing & Quick Review ⚡ The Perfect Flip! ↗

Trakin Tech is highly enthusiastic about the Z Flip 7, noting that it brings significant upgrades over the previous generation, particularly in battery life and screen size. The reviewer praises the phone's pocketability and the new 4.1-inch cover display, which allows for most tasks to be done without opening the phone. The inclusion of the new Exynos 2500 chip is seen as a positive for performance and efficiency, and the promise of seven years of updates makes it a future-proof investment. While acknowledging it's meant for a niche audience, the reviewer feels it's the most refined and durable Flip yet.

GSMArena Official · Samsung Galaxy Z Flip7 full review ↗

GSMArena offers a more measured take, describing the Z Flip 7 as a refined rather than revolutionary update. They appreciate the engineering that made the phone thinner while housing larger screens and a bigger battery, and they praise the new Dex support and the overall solid performance of the Exynos 2500 chip. However, they strongly criticize the lack of meaningful improvements to the cover screen's software functionality, noting the ongoing absence of full app support. They also point out that the camera hardware is unchanged and that the 25W charging remains frustratingly slow, concluding that while it's a great phone, it might not justify an upgrade from the Z Flip 6.

How long will the battery last in daily use?

The 4300 mAh battery scores 6.5 out of 10, meaning it will get you through a typical day but not much more. Charging is limited to 25 W, which feels glacial, and heavy use can cause the phone to heat up, reducing endurance further.

Can I play games smoothly on this phone?

The Exynos 2500 and 12 GB RAM give strong raw performance, yet gaming ratings sit at 6 out of 10 because the device throttles heavily when it gets hot. Short sessions feel fine, but long play will see frame drops.

How does the camera perform in low‑light situations?

The 50 MP sensor with a 1.57 inch size, f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures decent photos, but the lack of a dedicated telephoto lens limits reach. Low‑light results are acceptable but not as bright as flagship competitors.
