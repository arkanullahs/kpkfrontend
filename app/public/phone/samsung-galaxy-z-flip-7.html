Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 7 Is it worth buying?

If you want a stylish foldable with a brilliant screen and strong performance, the Galaxy Z Flip 7 delivers that but you must accept several practical compromises.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value a premium foldable screen and strong performance over fast charging and long‑term durability.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable battery endurance, rapid charging, or an official manufacturer warranty.

  • 2600 nit 120 Hz foldable display
  • Exynos 2500 with 12 GB RAM
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs4

  • Glacial 25 W charging speed
  • Overheating and heavy thermal throttling
  • Hinge can develop wobble over time
  • Inner screen prone to dents

Why we say that

The 6.9 inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X panel reaches 2600 nits and runs at 120 Hz, making everyday use feel smooth and bright. The Exynos 2500 paired with 12 GB RAM handles apps and multitasking well, and the 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures decent photos for a flip form factor. Battery life sits at a modest 6.5 rating and the 25 W charger feels glacial, so long sessions will need a charger nearby.

Users report the hinge developing a wobble, the inner screen denting easily, and the phone heating during heavy tasks or wireless Android Auto. The device arrives as a gray import with only the shop’s warranty, so any repair will be out of pocket.

GSMArena offers a more measured take, describing the Z Flip 7 as a refined rather than revolutionary update. They appreciate the engineering that made the phone thinner while housing larger screens and a bigger battery, and they praise the new Dex support and the overall solid performance of the Exynos 2500 chip. However, they strongly criticize the lack of meaningful improvements to the cover screen's software functionality, noting the ongoing absence of full app support. They also point out that the camera hardware is unchanged and that the 25W charging remains frustratingly slow, concluding that while it's a great phone, it might not justify an upgrade from the Z Flip 6.

Will the phone get software updates and for how long?

The Z Flip 7 ships with Android 16 and One UI 8 and is promised up to seven major Android version upgrades, giving it roughly seven years of software support, similar to other Samsung flagships.

How is the battery life and charging speed in daily use?

The 4300 mAh cell scores about 6.5 out of 10 for endurance, lasting a day with moderate use. The included 25 W charger is described as glacial, so topping up takes longer than many competitors.

Is the camera good for low‑light and does it have a telephoto lens?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS performs reasonably in low light for a flip, but the phone lacks a dedicated telephoto lens, limiting zoom quality.
