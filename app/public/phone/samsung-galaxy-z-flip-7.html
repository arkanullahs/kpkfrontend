Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 7 Is it worth buying?

If you can tolerate a slow charger and a battery that rarely lasts a day, the Galaxy Z Flip 7 offers a striking foldable display and premium camera.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You love a compact foldable with premium looks and can accept limited battery life and a shop‑only warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable all‑day endurance, fast charging, or an official Samsung warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Stunning 6.9‑inch LTPO AMOLED display with 2600 nits peak
  • 12 GB RAM delivers smooth everyday performance
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS captures detailed photos
  • 5G connectivity and Android 16 support

Reported trade-offs4

  • 25W charging is very slow
  • Battery often fails to last a full day
  • Hinge can become loose and noisy
  • Inner screen prone to dents and scratches

Why we say that

The 6.9‑inch LTPO AMOLED panel is bright enough for outdoor use and the 50 MP sensor delivers good photos in decent light. Daily tasks feel snappy thanks to the Exynos 2500 and 12 GB RAM, but sustained gaming or heavy apps trigger heavy throttling. Owners frequently mention the hinge developing a wobble and a faint noise, and the inner screen is easily dented or scratched.

Overheating appears during wireless Android Auto, video calls, or long recordings. All units are gray‑import, so there is no Samsung manufacturer warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited cover and must pay for any repairs. The phone promises up to seven Android upgrades, but the slow 25W charging and modest battery endurance remain major drawbacks.

GSMArena offers a more measured take, describing the Z Flip 7 as a refined rather than revolutionary update. They appreciate the engineering that made the phone thinner while housing larger screens and a bigger battery, and they praise the new Dex support and the overall solid performance of the Exynos 2500 chip. However, they strongly criticize the lack of meaningful improvements to the cover screen's software functionality, noting the ongoing absence of full app support. They also point out that the camera hardware is unchanged and that the 25W charging remains frustratingly slow, concluding that while it's a great phone, it might not justify an upgrade from the Z Flip 6.

Will the Galaxy Z Flip 7 last a full day on a single charge?

The 4300 mAh battery with 25W charging is modest; owners report it often does not survive a full day of mixed use and charging feels glacial. Expect to charge nightly.

How does the phone perform in games and heavy apps?

Daily tasks feel snappy thanks to the Exynos 2500 and 12 GB RAM, but under sustained gaming loads the chip throttles heavily, leading to frame drops and reduced performance after a short period.

Is the 50MP camera good in low‑light situations?

The 50 MP sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures detailed photos in good light, but reviewers note low‑light performance suffers without a telephoto lens and some apps fail to use the main sensor fully.

Does the phone come with an official Samsung warranty in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray‑import, so there is no Samsung manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; the shop may offer its own limited cover and any repair will be paid. The device promises up to seven major Android upgrades and four to seven years of security patches.
