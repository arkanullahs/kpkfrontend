Apple iPhone 13 Is it worth buying?

What works well

  • Bright 6.1 inch OLED display
  • A15 Bionic chipset handles games
  • 12MP camera with OIS
  • No bloat or ads in iOS

Reported trade-offs

  • Software updates may end soon
  • Official warranty costs more
  • Battery life modest for heavy use

Why we say that

The 6.1 inch display can reach 1,200 nits at peak, so it stays readable outdoors. The 12MP main sensor with f/1.6 aperture and optical image stabilization handles everyday shots well, though low light scenes may not match the very latest models. Battery capacity is 3,240 mAh, which most users find lasts a normal day but will feel short if you push heavy gaming or video streaming.

The phone supports 5G and runs iOS 15, upgradable to iOS 26.6, but being five years old means Apple may stop major updates soon. Owners complain mainly about the limited future software support and the higher cost if they want an official warranty.

Will the iPhone 13 still receive iOS updates?

The phone launched with iOS 15 and can be upgraded to iOS 26.6. Because it is five years old, Apple is likely to stop major updates soon, so expect short remaining update life.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The device has a 3,240 mAh battery. Most owners say it comfortably lasts a typical day of mixed usage, but intensive gaming or video streaming can drain it faster.

Is the camera good in low light conditions?

The main camera uses a 12MP 1/1.9 inch sensor with f/1.6 aperture and optical image stabilization, which gives decent low light performance, though it may not match newer higher‑resolution sensors.
