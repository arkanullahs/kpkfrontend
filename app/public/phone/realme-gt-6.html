Is the Realme GT 6 worth buying?

What is good

  • Stunning 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED display
  • Excellent battery life with 14 plus hours active use
  • Blazing 120W charging speed
  • Strong main camera performance

What is not

  • Significant GPU throttling during gaming
  • Plastic build materials feel cheap
  • Poor ultrawide and selfie camera quality
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a high-quality display and long battery life for daily tasks over heavy gaming.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a serious gamer or if you require a reliable manufacturer warranty for your device.

Why we say that

While the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset handles daily tasks with ease, owners report noticeable GPU throttling when pushing the phone with heavy graphics. The build quality feels underwhelming due to the plastic frame and back, which also attracts smudges easily. Photography is a mixed bag, as the main sensor performs well, but the ultrawide and selfie cameras are consistently unimpressive.

You should be aware that this is a gray-import unit, meaning there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Repairs must be handled through the shop, and you should verify the BTRC status before purchasing. The inclusion of a USB 2.0 port is a disappointing limitation for a phone at this price point.

What is the Realme GT 6 price in Bangladesh?

Realme GT 6 costs ৳49,999 in Bangladesh on unofficial listings, in stock at 1 shop we track as of 9 Sep 2026.

Does the Realme GT 6 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. All units currently available are gray imports. You must rely on the specific shop for any repair promises, and all repairs will be paid services.

How does the phone perform during long gaming sessions?

While the phone is snappy for daily use, owners report significant GPU throttling during games. The device tends to get slightly warm, and this heat leads to noticeable drops in graphics performance.

How long will the Realme GT 6 receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 14 and is promised to receive up to 3 major Android upgrades. You can expect the standard Realme UI experience, though users should be aware of the medium to high levels of bloatware.

Is the camera system good for photography?

The main camera is strong, but the overall experience is inconsistent. Owners report that the ultrawide and selfie cameras are unimpressive, making the camera system less versatile than expected for this price range.
