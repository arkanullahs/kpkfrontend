Is the Realme GT 6 worth buying?

What is good

  • Stunning 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED display
  • Excellent battery life with 14 plus hours usage
  • Blazing 120W charging reaches full in 28 minutes
  • Strong main camera performance

What is not

  • Significant GPU throttling during gaming
  • Plastic build materials feel cheap
  • Poor ultrawide and selfie camera quality
  • Only available as gray-import with shop warranty

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a bright, high-quality display and long battery life for daily tasks over heavy gaming.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a serious gamer who needs consistent performance or if you require an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Owners report that while the 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED screen is stunning with its 6000 nits peak brightness, the plastic frame and back feel underwhelming for the price. You will get excellent battery endurance lasting over 14 hours of active use, and the 120W charging is incredibly fast, hitting a full charge in 28 minutes. However, the phone struggles with heat management.

Users frequently complain about significant GPU throttling during gaming sessions, which leads to performance drops. The camera system is also inconsistent, with a strong main sensor paired with disappointing ultrawide and selfie performance. Since this is only available as gray-import stock at ৳49,999, you must rely on shop warranties rather than official brand support.

Be prepared for bloatware in the software and a slow USB 2.0 connection that limits data transfer speeds.

How long will the Realme GT 6 receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 14 and is promised to receive up to 3 major Android upgrades. You should expect the standard Realme UI experience, though users have noted that the software includes a medium to high amount of bloatware.

Is the Realme GT 6 suitable for heavy gaming?

While the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset is snappy for daily use, it is not ideal for long gaming sessions. Owners report significant GPU throttling when the device gets warm, which leads to noticeable drops in graphics performance.

Does this phone come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, the Realme GT 6 is currently only available through gray-import channels at ৳49,999. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, so you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before purchasing.

How is the camera performance for photography?

The main camera is strong and capable of good results. However, the secondary cameras are unimpressive, with owners reporting poor quality from both the ultrawide and selfie lenses. The overall camera tuning is also described as inconsistent.
