Buying verdict

OPPO Find X8 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you can live without a Bangladeshi manufacturer warranty and are okay with a premium price, the Find X8 Pro delivers flagship speed and a solid all round experience.

Choose it if
You want flagship speed, a large battery and are comfortable with a shop only warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need high frame rate gaming or an official Bangladeshi warranty.

What works well

  • 12 to 16 GB RAM with up to 1 TB storage
  • 50 MP main camera with 3× telephoto
  • 80 W fast charging in about 55 minutes
  • 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED display with 120 Hz

Reported trade-offs

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Gaming limited to 60 FPS
  • 6× zoom images hazy in bright light
  • Peak brightness lower than some rivals

Why we say that

The Dimensity 9400 keeps apps and games buttery smooth and stays cool even during long sessions. Battery life is strong thanks to a 5910 mAh cell and silicon carbon tech, and 80 W charging fills the phone in about 55 minutes. The 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED panel offers 120 Hz refresh and good colour accuracy, though its peak brightness of 4500 nits trails some rivals.

The 50 MP main camera with 3× telephoto is versatile, but the 6× zoom can look hazy in bright light and skin tones may appear reddish. Gaming is capped at 60 FPS so high frame rate titles are limited. ColorOS 15 feels polished but includes medium bloatware.
