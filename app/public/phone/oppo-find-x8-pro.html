Is the OPPO Find X8 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • 5910 mAh battery with 80 W fast charge
  • Dimensity 9400 delivers smooth performance
  • Premium build with IP69 rating

What is not

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Gaming capped at 60 FPS
  • 6× zoom hazy in bright light
  • Peak brightness lower than some rivals

Buy it if Buy it if you value performance, battery life and premium feel over official warranty and high‑fps gaming.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a phone with official brand warranty or high‑fps gaming support.

Why we say that

The Dimensity 9400 paired with 12‑16 GB RAM makes everyday tasks feel instant and multitasking smooth. The 6.78‑inch LTPO AMOLED screen runs at 120 Hz and shows vibrant colors, though its 4500‑nit peak is lower than some rivals. Battery endurance is exceptional; owners report more than eight hours of screen‑on time and the 80 W charger fills the 5910 mAh cell in about 55 minutes.

The camera package is versatile with a 50 MP main sensor, 3× telephoto and ultrawide, but the 6× zoom can look hazy in bright light and skin tones may appear reddish. Gaming is limited to 60 FPS, which some gamers find restrictive. Thermals stay cool even in demanding titles.

The phone feels premium, has IP69 protection and an IR blaster, but it arrives as a gray‑import with only the shop’s warranty and is considered pricey for the Bangladeshi market.
