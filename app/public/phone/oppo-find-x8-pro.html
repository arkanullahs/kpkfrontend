Is the OPPO Find X8 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • 80 W fast wired charging
  • 50 MP main camera with 3× optical zoom
  • 12 to 16 GB RAM with Dimensity 9400

What is not

  • Only gray import stock with shop warranty
  • No gaming above 60 FPS
  • Optical fingerprint scanner placed low
  • Price higher than Vivo or OnePlus alternatives

Buy it if Buy if you want a flagship experience with strong performance, fast charging and a high brightness display and can accept a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip if you need high frame rate gaming or an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED screen stays smooth at 120 Hz and can reach 4500 nits peak, making outdoor use easy. Battery endurance is excellent with 8 to 9 hours screen‑on time and a 80 W wired charger fills the 5910 mAh cell in about 55 minutes. The Dimensity 9400 and up to 16 GB RAM deliver strong performance and keep the device cool even in demanding games, though the phone caps everything at 60 FPS which hurts competitive gamers.

The 50 MP main sensor with 3× optical zoom covers most scenes, but low light shots can look hazy and the 6× zoom adds a green tint. ColorOS 15 offers smooth animations but adds medium level bloat. Only gray import stock is available, so you get a shop warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
