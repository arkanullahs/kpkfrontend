Is the Vivo Y31d worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 7200 mAh battery
  • Rugged tank-like build quality
  • High 1250 nits peak brightness
  • Includes convenient IR blaster

What is not

  • Unacceptable 720p display resolution
  • Outdated chipset with poor gaming
  • Weak low light camera performance
  • No memory card expansion slot

Buy it if You prioritize multi-day battery endurance and a durable, rugged build above all other features.

Skip it if You care about display sharpness, gaming performance, or high-quality photography in low light conditions.

Why we say that

Owners report that the 7200 mAh battery provides industry-leading multi-day endurance, which is the primary reason to consider this phone. However, the experience is hampered by a 720p resolution display that many users find unacceptable for a device costing over ৳23,999 in 2026. The Snapdragon 6s 4G Gen 2 chipset is considered outdated, leading to poor performance in gaming and general tasks.

While the build quality is rugged and tank-like with an IP rating, the camera system struggles significantly in low light and lacks detail. Users also express frustration regarding the lack of memory card expansion and the presence of pre-installed bloatware in the software. Ultimately, owners feel the phone is severely overpriced given the significant limitations in screen resolution and chipset capability.
