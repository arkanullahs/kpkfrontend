Buying verdict

Vivo Y31d Is it worth buying?

Expect a phone that can sit on the charger for a few minutes and then run for several days without hunting for an outlet.

What works well

  • 7200 mAh battery lasts multiple days
  • 44 W fast charging refills quickly
  • IR blaster works as remote control
  • Rugged build with IP rating

Reported trade-offs

  • 720p display feels low for price
  • Snapdragon 6s Gen 2 is outdated
  • No memory card expansion slot
  • Low‑light camera performance poor

Why we say that

The Vivo Y31d, released in 2026, carries a 7,200 mAh battery that owners rate 9.8 out of 10 for endurance. It pairs the battery with 44 W fast charging, so a short top‑up gets you back to full quickly. The 6.75‑inch IPS LCD offers a 120 Hz refresh rate but only 720p resolution, which many buyers find disappointing for a price that starts at ৳23,999.

It uses a Snapdragon 6s Gen 2 4G chipset with 6 GB RAM and 128 GB or 256 GB storage, but the chipset is considered outdated for gaming and heavy apps. The 50 MP main camera produces decent shots in bright light yet struggles in low‑light scenes, and the 8 MP selfie sensor is modest. Build quality is solid with an IP‑rated rugged feel, and an IR blaster adds remote‑control convenience.

Read full reasoning

The phone runs Android 16 with OriginOS 6, which is clean but includes some pre‑installed bloatware. Official Vivo warranty and service network apply in Bangladesh. Overall owners praise battery life and durability but criticize the low‑resolution display, limited performance, and price that feels high for the offered specs.
