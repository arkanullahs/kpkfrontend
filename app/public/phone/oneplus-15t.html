Buying verdict

OnePlus 15T Is it worth buying?

The OnePlus 15T is a high-performance device that struggles with severe thermal management in its compact frame.

Choose it if
You prioritize extreme battery longevity and compact design over gaming performance and camera versatility.
Consider alternatives if
You are a heavy gamer, rely on timely notifications for work, or require a reliable manufacturer warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Unprecedented 7500 mAh battery life
  • Extremely bright 3600 nits peak display
  • Durable ceramic-like scratch resistant coating
  • Smooth 165Hz display refresh rate

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe overheating and thermal throttling
  • Missing ultra-wide camera lens
  • Chinese ROM notification delays
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

While the 7500 mAh battery provides an unprecedented three to four days of use, the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset generates excessive heat that makes the phone barely holdable during heavy gaming. Owners report that the device reaches temperatures near 50°C, leading to significant performance throttling. You must be aware that these units are gray imports built for the Chinese market, meaning they run ColorOS 16 rather than OxygenOS.

This results in bloatware and critical notification delays for apps like WhatsApp and Messenger. The camera setup is also polarizing, as it lacks an ultra-wide lens and the front camera does not support 4K video recording. Since these are gray imports, there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh, and you must rely solely on the shop for any repair support.

Does the OnePlus 15T have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, so you must rely on the specific shop for any repair promises. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

How does the battery perform on the OnePlus 15T?

The battery performance is exceptional for a compact device. Owners report an unprecedented 7500 mAh capacity that allows the phone to last for three to four days on a single charge. It is currently one of the strongest features of this model.

Is the OnePlus 15T good for heavy gaming?

While the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset offers instant daily speed, it is not ideal for heavy gaming. Owners report severe thermal throttling and temperatures reaching 50°C. The device becomes so hot during intense sessions that it is barely holdable.

What are the limitations of the Chinese ROM on this phone?

The Chinese ROM, ColorOS 16, does not include Google services or Bangla language support out of the box. Users frequently report critical notification delays for apps like WhatsApp and Messenger, meaning messages may not appear on your screen immediately.
