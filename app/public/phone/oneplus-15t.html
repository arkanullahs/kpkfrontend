Is the OnePlus 15T worth buying?

What is good

  • Unprecedented 7500 mAh battery life
  • Extremely durable IP69K build quality
  • Ultra-slim 1.1mm display bezels
  • Smooth 165Hz display refresh rate

What is not

  • Severe overheating and thermal throttling
  • Missing ultra-wide camera lens
  • Chinese ROM notification delays
  • Front camera lacks 4K video support

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize extreme battery life and compact dimensions above all else and are comfortable managing a Chinese ROM.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a heavy gamer, need a reliable ultra-wide camera, or require a device that stays cool during use.

Why we say that

While the 7500 mAh battery provides industry-leading longevity, the phone becomes barely holdable during heavy use as temperatures climb toward 50°C. Owners report that the device suffers from significant thermal throttling, which hampers gaming performance despite the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset. You must be prepared for the realities of a Chinese ROM, including notification delays on apps like WhatsApp and Messenger, as well as the lack of an ultra-wide camera.

The build quality is exceptionally durable with an IP69K rating, but the absence of a manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh means you are entirely dependent on the shop for any repairs. This is a high-performance machine that demands compromises on software stability and thermal comfort.

What is the OnePlus 15T price in Bangladesh?

OnePlus 15T costs ৳74,999 in Bangladesh on unofficial listings, in stock at 2 shops we track as of 9 Sep 2026.

Does the OnePlus 15T come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit is a gray import, meaning you only receive whatever limited coverage the specific shop provides. All repairs must be paid for out of your own pocket.

How does the battery perform on the OnePlus 15T?

The battery performance is exceptional due to the 7500 mAh capacity. Owners report that the device can last between 3 to 4 days on a single charge, which is unprecedented for a phone of this compact size.

Is the OnePlus 15T suitable for heavy gaming?

While the chipset is powerful, the phone is not ideal for heavy gaming. Owners report severe thermal throttling and temperatures reaching 50°C, which makes the device uncomfortable to hold and negatively impacts the gaming experience.

What are the drawbacks of the Chinese ROM on this device?

The Chinese ROM often causes critical notification delays for apps like WhatsApp and Messenger. Additionally, the software lacks Google services and Bangla language support, and you may face issues with network connectivity in areas where B20 bands are required.
