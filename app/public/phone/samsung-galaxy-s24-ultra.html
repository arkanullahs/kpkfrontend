Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra Is it worth buying?

If you want a phone with the brightest display and a 200 MP camera, the S24 Ultra delivers, but expect throttling and other quirks.

Choose it if
You value top‑tier display and camera and can handle occasional throttling.
Consider alternatives if
You need consistent performance for long gaming sessions or rely on strong indoor signal.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • 2600‑nit peak brightness
  • 200 MP versatile camera
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 performance
  • 5 G connectivity

Reported trade-offs

  • Throttles under sustained heavy load
  • Grainy at minimum brightness
  • 10× zoom image drop
  • Indoor signal can be weak

Why we say that

The 6.8‑inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X panel reaches a peak of 2600 nits, making outdoor viewing effortless. The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset provides fast everyday performance and strong gaming scores, yet the larger vapor chamber can still let the device heat up and drop speed during long intense tasks. Battery life is rated 8.6 out of 10 and lasts a full day for most users, though the 45 W charger is slower than some competitors.

The matte titanium build feels premium but shows scuffs easily, and the anti‑reflective coating can look grainy at the lowest brightness. Network signal may be weak indoors, and the software adds aggressive Samsung services and occasional UI regressions. Overall it feels like a flagship with a few noticeable compromises.
