Samsung Galaxy A18 Is it worth buying?

The Samsung Galaxy A18 is a difficult recommendation because owners report severe multitasking lag and poor performance that makes the device feel sluggish.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize a high-quality display and long-term software support for basic tasks and are willing to pay for the 6GB RAM variant.
Consider alternatives if
You need a phone for gaming, heavy multitasking, or high-quality video recording, as this device struggles with performance and camera stability.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Reliable and efficient 5000 mAh battery
  • Best-in-class 90Hz Super AMOLED display
  • Clean and feature-rich One UI 9 software
  • Long-term support with 6 major OS updates

Reported trade-offs4

  • Severe multitasking lag on 4GB RAM model
  • Poor low-light and zoom camera performance
  • Slow 25W charging speed
  • Poor value compared to other available options

Why we say that

While the 6.7 inch Super AMOLED display is excellent for media consumption, the 4GB RAM variant is described by users as unusable for daily tasks. You will find that the battery life is highly reliable and efficient, but the slow 25W charging speed is a frustration for many. The camera setup struggles significantly in low light, and video recording is limited to 1080p30 with ineffective stabilization.

Build quality is solid with an IP64 rating, yet the downgrade to Gorilla Glass 3 is a notable step back. Given the high launch price, many buyers feel that better alternatives exist for less money, including the Samsung Galaxy A24 which is available for ৳38,199.
