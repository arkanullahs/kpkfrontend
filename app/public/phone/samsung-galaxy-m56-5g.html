Is the Samsung Galaxy M56 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.74 inch 120 Hz Super AMOLED display
  • 45 W fast charging
  • One UI with low bloat
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • No official IP rating
  • Mono speaker only
  • Screen may flicker after a year
  • No charger included

Buy it if Buy if you want a large smooth display and fast charging at a mid range price.

Skip it if Skip if you need strong gaming performance or a waterproof phone.

Why we say that

The display is thin bezel and bright, earning the highest owner rating of 8.5. Battery capacity is 5000 mAh and supports 45 W charging, yet owners report the capacity feels lower than previous M series and the screen can start flickering within a year. Performance is smooth for everyday apps thanks to the Exynos 1480 and 8 GB RAM, but the phone lacks thermal headroom for long gaming sessions and scores only 6 out of 10 in gaming.

Build quality suffers from the absence of an IP rating, a single mono speaker and no charger in the box, which many buyers find disappointing. Software runs Android 15 with One UI 7, low bloat and up to six major Android upgrades, a point that keeps some users loyal. As a gray import you will get shop warranty only, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.
