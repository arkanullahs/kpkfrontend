Why we say that

The 5000 mAh battery gives you about one day of use, yet owners note it feels smaller than the advertised 6000 mAh. The Exynos 1480 with 8 GB RAM handles daily apps smoothly, but gaming at high frame rates often drops and the device warms up. The 50 MP main camera produces decent photos in good light, while the ultrawide lens is muddy and low‑light performance is only average.

The phone arrives without a charger, with a single mono speaker and a slow optical fingerprint sensor. Software runs One UI 7 on Android 15 and promises up to six Android upgrades, with low bloat. All units are gray imports built for India, so there is no Samsung manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s warranty applies and repairs are paid.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery usually lasts about one day with normal use, but owners say it feels smaller than the earlier 6000 mAh models, so heavy users may need to charge before the day ends.

Can the phone handle high‑fps gaming without issues?

The Exynos 1480 and 8 GB RAM keep everyday apps smooth, but sustained high‑fps gaming often drops frames and the device gets warm, so it is not ideal for intensive gaming sessions.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor performs okay in bright light, but low‑light shots lack detail and the ultrawide lens appears muddy, making the overall camera experience average for this price range.
