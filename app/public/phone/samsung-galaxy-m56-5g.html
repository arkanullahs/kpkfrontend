Is the Samsung Galaxy M56 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.74 inch Super AMOLED+ 120 Hz display
  • 45W fast charging
  • 50MP main camera with OIS
  • One UI with low bloat

What is not

  • Mono speaker only
  • No official IP rating
  • Display may flicker after a year
  • Battery capacity drop concerns

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large 120 Hz AMOLED screen, decent camera and fast charging at a mid‑range price and can accept a gray‑import warranty.

Why we say that

The Exynos 1480 handles everyday apps smoothly, yet it runs hot under extended gaming, and a few users report screen flicker within a year. One UI on Android 15 feels clean and updates are promised for up to six years, which many cite as a key reason to choose Samsung. The gray‑import model arrives without a charger and only a mono speaker, and warranty is limited to the shop’s policy, so checking IMEI and BTRC status before purchase is essential.

At 35,999 taka the price sits between the cheapest gray‑import listing and official offers, making it a value pick for those who can live with the trade‑offs.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 5,000 mAh cell is rated lower than previous M models, and owners report a noticeable capacity drop, so daily screen‑on time may fall short of a full day for power‑hungry users. Fast 45W charging can restore charge quickly.

Is the phone good for gaming?

The Exynos 1480 delivers smooth performance for casual games and the 120 Hz panel makes motion feel fluid, but owners note limited thermal headroom, so extended heavy gaming can cause throttling and lower frame rates.

Does the phone have an official warranty or water‑proof rating?

The device is sold as a gray‑import, so it comes with the shop’s warranty rather than Samsung’s official coverage and it lacks any IP water‑resistance rating. Verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How many Android updates will it receive?

It ships with Android 15 and Samsung promises up to six major Android upgrades, translating to roughly four to six years of software support, with One UI 7 keeping the interface light on bloat.
