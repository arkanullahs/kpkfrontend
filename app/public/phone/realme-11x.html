What works well

  • Smooth 120Hz display
  • Matte finish resists fingerprints
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs

  • Heavy bloatware and intrusive ads
  • Poor low light photography
  • Lags in high graphic games
  • Low brightness in sunlight

Why we say that

Owners frequently complain about the software, describing the Realme UI as being stacked with bloatware and intrusive ads. While the 120Hz screen feels smooth, it is difficult to see under direct sunlight due to poor brightness. Gaming is a weak point because the phone lags heavily in high graphic games and gets noticeably hot during PUBG.

The camera fails in dark areas and lacks an ultrawide lens. Since this is a 2023 model, you should expect very little remaining software update life. It is a lightweight plastic phone that resists fingerprints, but the overall experience is marred by software clutter and mediocre hardware performance.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is a gray import built for the Indian market. It has no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Any warranty provided is from the shop itself, and all repairs are paid.

How is the gaming performance on the Dimensity 6100+?

It is smooth for daily work but lags heavily in high graphic games. Owners report noticeable heating during high load gaming like PUBG, and it received a gaming rating of 4.0 out of 10.

Can I use this phone for photography in low light?

The camera performs poorly in dark areas. Owners report that the 64MP main sensor lacks detail and the low light photography is very bad.

How long will I get software updates for this device?

This phone was released in 2023. Because it is already 3 years old, you should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point.
