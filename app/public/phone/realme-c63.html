Buying verdict

Realme C63 Is it worth buying?

Prioritize battery and charging over everything else with this device.

Choose it if
You need a phone with fast charging and a large battery for basic tasks.
Consider alternatives if
You want a device for gaming or a reliable multi camera system.

What works well

  • Fast 45W charging
  • Large 5000 mAh battery
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • 90Hz refresh rate display

Reported trade-offs

  • Slow performance and gaming
  • Fake second camera
  • Plastic build quality
  • Bluetooth connectivity issues

Why we say that

It features a 5000 mAh battery and 45W charging that owners generally like. However, the actual experience is mixed. Users report slow performance and Bluetooth issues.

The build is plastic and feels cheap to some. A major concern is the camera setup, as owners claim only the first camera is real and the second one is fake. Gaming is a weak point with a very low owner rating.

You will also deal with medium to high levels of bloatware and ads. It runs Android 14, but the hardware struggles to keep up with daily tasks according to user feedback.

Realme C63: what buyers ask us

What is the Realme C63 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Realme C63 in Bangladesh was ৳14,990, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Is the Realme C63 good for gaming?

No, it is not good for gaming. Owners gave it a very low rating of 2.5 for gaming and 3.0 for general performance. It uses the Unisoc Tiger T612 chipset which users describe as slow.

How is the battery life and charging?

The battery life is a strong point with a 5000 mAh capacity and an owner rating of 8.5. Users specifically mentioned that the 45W charging is good.

Are the cameras on this phone real?

Owners report that only the first 50MP camera is real. They claim the second camera is fake. The overall camera rating from users is low at 3.0.

How many software updates will it get?

The phone comes with Android 14 and realme UI. The evidence indicates it will receive 2 to 3 OS updates, though budget users should expect medium to high amounts of bloatware and ads.
