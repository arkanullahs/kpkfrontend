Samsung Galaxy F15 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can stay alive for two days without charging, the Galaxy F15 delivers that, but expect compromises elsewhere.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
If you prioritize long battery life, a large AMOLED screen, and 5G on a tight budget, this phone can fit your needs.
Consider alternatives if
If you need smooth gaming, reliable low‑light photos, or an official manufacturer warranty, look for another model.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 6000 mAh battery lasting up to two days
  • Vibrant 6.6‑inch Super AMOLED display
  • 5G connectivity and headphone jack
  • 50 MP main camera good in daylight

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official Samsung warranty, only shop cover
  • Slow 25 W charging without included charger
  • UI lag and stutter in everyday use
  • Poor low‑light camera performance

Why we say that

The 6.6‑inch Super AMOLED panel is bright and colorful, though the teardrop notch feels dated and some users notice PWM flicker. The 6000 mAh battery indeed stretches to 1.5‑2 days for most daily routines, but the 25 W charger is slow and the box does not include a charger, so you must buy one separately. Performance feels sluggish; the Dimensity 6100+ chipset struggles with basic UI tasks and PUBG is only playable on low settings.

The 50 MP main sensor captures decent daylight shots, yet low‑light photos are noisy and processing is inconsistent. Build quality is solid plastic but the phone feels bulky and heavy. Software runs Android 14 with One UI 8.5, promising upgrades to Android 16, yet bloat and ad‑heavy lock‑screen recommendations add annoyance.

How long does the battery last in real use?

The 6000 mAh cell typically gives you between one and a half to two days of normal use, so you can go through a workday and still have charge left for the evening. Heavy streaming may reduce that time.

Can I play games like PUBG on this phone?

The Dimensity 6100+ chipset struggles with demanding games; PUBG runs only on the lowest graphics settings and you will notice noticeable lag. Casual games work, but expect a choppy experience with high‑intensity titles.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

In daylight the 50 MP sensor produces sharp images, but in low‑light the phone becomes noisy and the processing is inconsistent, resulting in blurry or over‑processed photos. You may need a dedicated night‑mode app for better results.

Is there an official Samsung warranty and how long will software updates be provided?

All listings are gray‑import, so there is no official Samsung warranty in Bangladesh; you only get the shop’s limited cover and any repair costs are paid by you. Samsung promises Android updates for about four to seven years for this series, with upgrades to Android 16 expected.
