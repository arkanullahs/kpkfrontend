Buying verdict

Apple iPhone 12 Is it worth buying?

The iPhone 12 is a 2020 device that now faces significant limitations regarding its long-term software support and battery longevity.

Choose it if
You want an affordable entry into the Apple ecosystem and are comfortable managing a device without official brand warranty support.
Consider alternatives if
You require long-term software updates, a large battery, or the security of an official manufacturer warranty.

What works well

  • Powerful A14 Bionic chipset
  • Bright 1200 nits OLED display
  • Capable 12MP main camera with OIS
  • Supports 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Small 2815 mAh battery capacity
  • Limited remaining software update life
  • Only available as gray-import stock

Why we say that

While the A14 Bionic chipset remains capable for daily tasks, the 2815 mAh battery is small by modern standards and may struggle to last through a full day of heavy use. You are purchasing a gray-import unit, which means you must rely entirely on the shop for any repair needs rather than official brand support. Because this model is 6 years old, you should expect little to no remaining software update life.

The display offers a bright 1200 nits peak brightness, but the hardware is aging compared to newer releases. Buyers must be diligent about checking the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before handing over ৳50,999. This is a device for someone who specifically wants the Apple ecosystem on a budget but understands the risks of buying an older, unofficial import.

Does this phone come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every listing is a gray import, so you must rely on the specific shop for any repair promises. You will have to pay for all repairs yourself.

How long will the iPhone 12 continue to receive software updates?

This phone was released in 2020 and is now 6 years old. You should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point in time.

Is the battery life sufficient for a full day of use?

The device features a 2815 mAh battery. Given the age of the hardware and the capacity provided, you may find the battery life insufficient for heavy daily usage compared to newer smartphones.

What should I check before buying this gray-import unit?

You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before completing your purchase. Since these are gray-import units, verifying the registration status is essential to ensure the device is compliant for use in Bangladesh.
