Is the Apple iPhone 12 worth buying?

What is good

  • Super Retina XDR OLED display
  • A14 Bionic performance
  • 5G connectivity
  • Accurate bright‑light camera

What is not

  • Shop warranty only, no Apple warranty
  • Battery capacity limited at 2815 mAh
  • Software updates may end soon
  • Only gray‑import stock available

Buy it if Buy it if you want a proven iPhone experience at a lower price and can live with a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need long term software support or an official Apple warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.1 inch Super Retina XDR OLED screen stays bright even in sunlight, and the A14 Bionic chip handles apps and games without lag. Camera quality is good in daylight with a 12MP main sensor, though low light performance is not as strong as newer models. Battery life is modest, lasting a full day with light use but draining faster with intensive tasks.

Owners often mention the need to verify IMEI and BTRC status before purchase, and many wish the device had a longer software‑support window. Since only gray‑import stock is available, you will rely on the seller’s shop warranty rather than Apple’s official coverage. Overall, it offers a premium iPhone feel at a lower price if you are comfortable with these trade‑offs.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 2815 mAh battery usually lasts a full day with light use such as messaging and browsing. Heavy gaming or video streaming will reduce the time and may require a charge before evening.

Can the iPhone 12 handle modern mobile games smoothly?

Yes, the A14 Bionic chip provides smooth performance in most current games. You may notice lower frame rates in the most demanding titles, but overall the experience remains responsive.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The 12MP sensor with f/1.6 aperture and OIS captures decent photos in low light, but images may be noisier compared to newer phones that have larger sensors or night‑mode software.

Will the phone receive iOS updates for many more years?

Released in 2020, the iPhone 12 has already received five to six major iOS versions. At six years old, the remaining update window is short, and future major updates may no longer be provided.
