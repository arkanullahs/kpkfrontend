What works well

  • Large 7000 mAh battery
  • Smooth 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Fast 80W charging
  • 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs

  • Poor low light camera performance
  • Reports of battery drain
  • No official brand warranty
  • Medium amount of bloatware and ads

Why we say that

It features a 7000 mAh battery and a 120Hz AMOLED display that owners rate highly for brightness and smoothness. However, the real world experience is mixed. Some owners report battery drain issues despite the large capacity.

The camera is a major weak point, with users describing the low light performance as the worst. Gaming and general performance are mediocre, as shown by owner ratings of 5.0 and 6.5. You must be careful with the purchase because no official BD units are recorded.

OPPO K13: what buyers ask us

What is the OPPO K13 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for OPPO K13 in Bangladesh was ৳25,290, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No official brand backed warranty is available. All recorded listings are imports. You will only get a shop warranty, so you must confirm the BTRC status and IMEI with the seller.

How is the camera performance in low light?

The camera performs poorly in low light. Owners have described the low light performance as the worst, and the camera received a low owner rating of 3.5.

Is the battery life good for heavy use?

The 7000 mAh battery is efficient and lasts all day for many. However, some owners have reported facing battery drain issues.

Is this a good phone for gaming?

Gaming performance is mediocre. Owners gave it a rating of 5.0 out of 10, and the general performance rating is 6.5.
