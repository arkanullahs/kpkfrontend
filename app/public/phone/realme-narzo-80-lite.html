Is the Realme Narzo 80 Lite worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz smooth display
  • 6000 mAh long‑lasting battery
  • 5G capable Dimensity 6300
  • headphone jack included

What is not

  • 15 W slow charging
  • single camera low‑light performance
  • low build‑quality rating
  • medium‑high bloatware

Buy it if Buy it if you need a large, smooth screen and a day‑plus battery while staying within a tight budget.

Skip it if Skip it if you rely on fast charging, strong low‑light photography, or premium build quality.

Why we say that

The 6.67‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, making scrolling and basic games feel fluid, and the 6000 mAh cell keeps the phone alive through heavy messaging, video streaming and occasional gaming. The Dimensity 6300 chipset provides decent 5G connectivity and everyday performance, though owners note occasional lag in demanding titles and a modest 5.5 rating for overall speed. The 32 MP single rear lens captures decent daylight shots but struggles in low light, and the three‑circle camera island can be confusing.

Charging is a clear weak spot; 15 W tops out, so refilling the large battery takes a long time. The phone ships with a shop warranty only, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
