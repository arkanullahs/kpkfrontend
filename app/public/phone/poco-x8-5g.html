POCO X8 5G Is it worth buying?

The POCO X8 5G gives you a massive 8340 mAh battery that easily lasts a full day of heavy use.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Huge 8340 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 67W fast charging refills quickly
  • Bright 6.83 inch AMOLED with 120Hz
  • Smooth performance for daily tasks

Reported trade-offs3

  • Ad heavy HyperOS with intrusive promotions
  • Moderate heating during long gaming
  • No manufacturer warranty, only shop cover

Why we say that

Its 6.83 inch AMOLED screen is bright and runs at 120Hz, making scrolling and media playback feel smooth. The 67W charger refills the battery quickly, so you spend less time plugged in. Performance is adequate for daily tasks and casual gaming, though the Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 is not a top tier chipset.

The 50MP main camera captures clear detail but its tuning is average, and low light results are modest. Owners complain about the ad heavy HyperOS interface that pushes intrusive promotions, and some notice warmth during long gaming sessions. Because it is a gray import, there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only shop cover applies, and repairs are paid.
