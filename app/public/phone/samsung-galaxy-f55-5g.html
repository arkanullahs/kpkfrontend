What works well

  • Bright and smooth 120Hz display
  • Low amount of bloatware and ads
  • Decent general performance
  • High resolution 50MP selfie camera

Reported trade-offs

  • Short battery life and overheating
  • Green tint on AMOLED display
  • Faulty proximity sensor
  • Grainy photos and slow shutter speed

Why we say that

While the 6.7 inch Super AMOLED+ display is bright and smooth, many owners report a greenish tint in low light. The battery life is a major problem. Users report it lasts only 12 to 14 hours of mixed use and the device is prone to overheating.

You will also find a proximity sensor issue that turns off the display unexpectedly. The cameras have a slow shutter speed and produce grainy or oversharpened photos. It runs on Android 14 and the Snapdragon 7 Gen 1 provides decent performance, but the hardware flaws make it a risky purchase for the money.

Samsung Galaxy F55 5G: what buyers ask us

What is the Samsung Galaxy F55 5G price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Samsung Galaxy F55 5G in Bangladesh was ৳28,990, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No official brand backed warranty is available. The units found in shops we track are imports. You will only get a shop warranty, so you must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

How is the battery life and charging?

The phone has a 5000 mAh battery with 45W charging. However, owners report short battery life and overheating. Some users only get 12 to 14 hours of mixed use on a good day.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The phone comes with Android 14 and is upgradable to Android 16. Based on Samsung patterns, A series devices typically receive updates for 4 to 6 years.

Is the camera good for photography?

The 50MP main camera has OIS, but reviews mention poor shutter speed. Users have complained that the resulting photos are often grainy or look oversharpened.
