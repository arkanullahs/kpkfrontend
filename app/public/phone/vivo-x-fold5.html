Buying verdict

Vivo X Fold5 Is it worth buying?

If you can live without an official Bangladeshi warranty and tolerate occasional software hiccups, the Vivo X Fold5 gives you a large, bright foldable with excellent battery life and a solid camera.

Choose it if
You want a premium‑size foldable with strong battery, bright screen and good camera and are okay with gray‑import warranty and occasional software quirks.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable official warranty, the latest chipset, or a flawless software experience.

What works well

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 8.03‑inch bright foldable LTPO display
  • 50 MP Zeiss‑tuned camera with telephoto
  • 80 W fast wired and 40 W wireless charging

Reported trade-offs

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Software bugs in multitasking and YouTube
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 feels dated for price
  • Hinge not as rigid as Samsung

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh cell easily powers a full day of mixed use and refills quickly with 80 W wired or 40 W wireless charging. The 8.03‑inch LTPO AMOLED panel reaches up to 4500 nits, making outdoor viewing comfortable and the crease barely noticeable. Photography benefits from a 50 MP Zeiss‑tuned sensor, f/1.6 aperture, OIS and a 3× telephoto lens, delivering good low‑light shots and portraits.

Performance is competent thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 and 12 GB RAM, though heavy gaming can cause the device to warm up. Owners report software bugs in multitasking and YouTube, and the hinge feels less rigid than Samsung’s. The phone runs Android 15 with Funtouch OS or OriginOS, offering up to four major Android upgrades but still showing some UI immaturity.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.
