Honor X5c Is it worth buying?

The Honor X5c is a budget device that prioritizes battery endurance over raw performance.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize an official warranty and long battery life for basic daily tasks.
Consider alternatives if
You play games, need a reliable camera, or require a smooth multitasking experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Exceptional battery life lasting over a day
  • Sturdy plastic build quality
  • Clean MagicOS 9 software experience
  • Includes a headphone jack and FM radio

Reported trade-offs4

  • System freezes requiring hard reboots
  • Very slow 15W charging speed
  • Outdated TFT LCD with thick bezels
  • Poor low-light camera performance

Why we say that

Owners report that the 5260 mAh battery easily lasts 1 to 1.5 days, which is the phone's strongest feature. Living with this device requires patience because the system freezes frequently, often forcing users to perform a hard reboot to regain control. While the MagicOS 9 software is clean, the hardware struggles with multitasking and gaming.

The display is outdated with thick bezels, and the camera lacks a night mode, making it unsuitable for low-light photography. You will also notice a lack of video stabilization when recording. While the build is sturdy, the design mimics an iPhone.

Read full reasoning

If you choose this phone, you are paying for the official warranty and battery life rather than speed or display quality.

Does the Honor X5c perform well for gaming?

The phone struggles with performance and multitasking. Owners report that it is only suitable for light games like Free Fire. With a low gaming rating of 3.2, it is not recommended for users who prioritize mobile gaming.
