Is the Honor X5c worth buying?

What is good

  • Long lasting 5260 mAh battery
  • Headphone jack and FM radio
  • Large 6.74 inch 90Hz display
  • 50MP main camera works okay in daylight

What is not

  • Very slow 15W charging
  • Low light photos are poor
  • TFT LCD looks dated with thick bezels
  • System can freeze and need reboot

Buy it if Buy it if you need a cheap phone with solid battery and official warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you want fast charging, smooth gaming or good night mode photos.

Why we say that

The 6.74 inch TFT LCD shows colors okay but the chin and thick bezels make it look old. Battery endurance is the highlight, lasting one to one and a half days on moderate use, though the 15W charger refills it very slowly. Performance is limited to light apps and games like Free Fire, and multitasking often leads to freezes that require a hard reboot.

Camera delivers decent daylight shots with its 50 MP sensor, but low light pictures are noisy and there is no night mode or video stabilization. Software runs a clean MagicOS 9 on Android 15, yet it lacks AI features and split screen, and updates are expected to be fewer than flagship devices.
