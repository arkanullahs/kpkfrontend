POCO M6 5G Is it worth buying?

Avoid this phone if you need a reliable daily driver.

3 strengths · 4 trade-offs

What works well3

  • Includes a headphone jack
  • Has FM radio
  • 5G connectivity available

Reported trade-offs4

  • Laggy OS and frequent crashes
  • Poor build quality
  • Intermittent 5G data
  • Short battery life

Why we say that

Owners report a frustrating experience with a laggy OS and frequent crashes. The HyperOS 2 skin causes bottlenecks due to RAM issues, leading to poor performance and laggy gaming. You will likely face overheating and a battery that drains quickly despite the 5000 mAh capacity.

The 5G data is intermittent, which means videos and feeds often stop loading. Build quality is poor and the display is dim. Camera performance is very bad, lacking gyro support and producing poor photo and video quality.

Read full reasoning

Since this is a 2023 model, it has very little software update life remaining. You are buying an old device that struggles with basic tasks.

What is the POCO M6 5G price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for POCO M6 5G in Bangladesh was ৳12,290, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No official brand backed warranty is available. Only gray import stock has been found in shops we track. These units come with shop warranty instead of brand warranty. You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

How is the gaming performance on this device?

Gaming performance is poor. Owners report laggy gaming and overheating issues. The OS itself lags because of RAM bottlenecks, and the gaming rating from owners is only 2.5 out of 10.

Will the battery last a full day?

Many owners report short battery life. Even with a 5000 mAh battery, users say it drains quickly. Charging is also described as slow by those who use the device.

How long will I get software updates?

This phone was released in 2023. Because it is 3 years old, you should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point.
