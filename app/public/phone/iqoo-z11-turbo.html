Is the iQOO Z11 Turbo worth buying?

What is good

  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 delivers flagship performance
  • 7600 mAh battery lasts all day and more
  • 100 W charger refills in minutes
  • 6.59‑inch AMOLED panel with 144 Hz refresh

What is not

  • OriginOS shows notification delays
  • Bloatware clutters the system
  • No telephoto lens for zoom shots
  • Camera video upload bug

Buy it if Buy it if you need top‑tier speed, massive battery and a large, fast screen for gaming and media.

Skip it if Skip it if you rely on an official warranty or need a reliable telephoto camera.

Why we say that

Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 keeps apps and games running without lag, and the 6.59‑inch AMOLED panel with 144 Hz refresh feels smooth for scrolling and gaming. The 7600 mAh cell holds enough charge for heavy use and the 100 W charger tops it up quickly, so you rarely worry about power. The 200 MP main sensor captures detailed photos in good light, yet the lack of a telephoto lens limits zoom and the camera app sometimes fails to upload videos.

OriginOS adds a lot of pre‑installed apps and can delay notifications, which many owners find annoying. The phone is built with premium metal and glass and has an IP68/69 rating, but any repair will be paid out of pocket because there is no official brand warranty in Bangladesh.
