Is the iQOO Z11 Turbo worth buying?

What is good

  • Exceptional 7600 mAh battery endurance
  • Flagship performance with Snapdragon 8 Gen 5
  • Bright 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Durable IP68/69 rated build

What is not

  • Persistent notification delays
  • Excessive software bloatware
  • Missing telephoto camera lens
  • Gray-import status lacks official brand warranty

Buy it if Buy this phone if you are a heavy gamer who prioritizes raw performance and battery life above all else.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you rely on timely notifications for work or prefer a clean software experience without bloatware.

Why we say that

With a massive 7600 mAh battery and the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset, it handles heavy gaming like Genshin Impact with ease. Owners consistently praise the class-leading battery life and flagship-level performance. However, living with the device reveals significant frustrations.

The most prominent issue is notification delays, which can be a dealbreaker for daily communication. Users also report that the OriginOS software is cluttered with unnecessary bloatware. While the 200MP main camera captures sharp images, the lack of a telephoto lens and occasional video upload bugs limit its versatility.

Because this is currently only available as a gray-import, you must be prepared to rely on shop warranties rather than official brand support. Always confirm whether you are receiving the China-market version with OriginOS or the India-market version with Funtouch OS before paying.

Does the iQOO Z11 Turbo have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, the device is currently only available through gray-import channels. This means you will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, so you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before purchasing.

How does the battery perform for heavy users?

The phone features an exceptional 7600 mAh battery that provides class-leading endurance. Owners rate the battery performance at 9.8 out of 10, making it a reliable choice for users who spend long hours gaming or using their phone heavily throughout the day.

Is the camera good for photography?

The 200MP main camera is strong and capable of high-quality shots. However, it lacks a telephoto lens and the ultrawide sensor is basic. Some users have also reported software bugs where default camera videos fail to upload properly.

What is the difference between the China and India versions?

The China version runs OriginOS, which is known for notification delays and bloatware. The India version runs Funtouch OS, which is generally preferred by users. You should ask your seller specifically which version you are buying to avoid software issues.
