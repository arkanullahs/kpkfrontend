iQOO Z11 Turbo Is it worth buying?

The iQOO Z11 Turbo is a powerhouse for gamers and heavy users, but it requires patience with its Chinese software quirks.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You are a power user or gamer who wants the fastest performance and longest battery life available and can manage software tweaks.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable camera system, a local manufacturer warranty, or a phone that works perfectly out of the box without notification issues.

What works well4

  • Exceptional 7600 mAh battery endurance
  • Flawless Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 gaming performance
  • Premium metal and glass build with IP68/69 rating
  • Bright 144Hz AMOLED display

Reported trade-offs4

  • Persistent notification delays and bloatware
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Lacks a dedicated telephoto lens
  • Camera software bugs affecting video uploads

Why we say that

Living with this device means dealing with the OriginOS interface, which comes with significant bloatware and persistent notification delays that owners find frustrating. While the 7600 mAh battery offers industry-leading endurance, the camera experience is inconsistent due to software bugs and the absence of a telephoto lens. You are buying a gray-import unit built for the Chinese market, meaning there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh.

Repairs are entirely at your own expense, and you must rely on the specific shop for any coverage. Performance is undeniably flagship-grade, yet the ease of use score is low because of the software setup. If you prioritize raw speed and battery life over a polished, localized software experience, this phone delivers, but be prepared to troubleshoot notification settings daily.

Does the iQOO Z11 Turbo come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you only receive whatever limited coverage the specific shop provides. All repairs are paid, and you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

How does the battery perform for heavy daily usage?

The battery performance is exceptional. With a 7600 mAh capacity, owners rate it 9.8 out of 10, describing it as providing industry-leading endurance for heavy users. It is one of the strongest features of this device.

Is the camera good for photography and video?

The 200MP main sensor is capable, but the overall camera experience is limited. Owners report a lack of a telephoto lens, a basic ultrawide camera, and software bugs that can prevent video uploads. It is not recommended for users who prioritize photography.

What software issues should I expect with the Chinese variant?

The China-ROM variant runs OriginOS, which includes plenty of unnecessary bloatware. The most prominent problem reported by owners is recurring notification delays. You may need to adjust settings manually to ensure apps function as expected compared to a global software version.
