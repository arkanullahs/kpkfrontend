Is the Tecno Spark 20C worth buying?

What is good

  • Good display performance
  • 90Hz refresh rate
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • Has FM radio

What is not

  • Slow processor performance
  • Poor photo and video quality
  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • Design issues with the build

Buy it if You need a very basic phone for light use and value a 90Hz display.

Skip it if You want to play games or take clear photos.

Why we say that

Owners report that the processor is not good enough and the phone is not designed for games. You will likely face frustration with gaming performance and a slow interface. The camera quality is a major pain point, with users describing photos as a blur.

While the display performance is rated as good, the build quality has design issues. The software is filled with ads and bloatware. Since this phone was released in 2023, it is now 3 years old.

You should expect very little to no remaining software update life. It is a basic device that fails to deliver in performance and photography.
