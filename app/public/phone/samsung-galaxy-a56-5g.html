Samsung Galaxy A56 5G Is it worth buying?

The Samsung Galaxy A56 5G offers a premium build that feels like a flagship, but recent software updates have introduced significant performance and battery issues.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize a sturdy, flagship-feeling build and long-term software support, and you are willing to wait for future updates to resolve current performance issues.
Consider alternatives if
You want a phone that works perfectly out of the box without heating or lag, or if you prefer the better value offered by the newer Galaxy A57 5G.

  • Excellent 120Hz Super AMOLED display

Reported trade-offs4

  • Inconsistent virtual proximity sensor
  • Noticeable camera shutter lag
  • High launch price

Why we say that

While the 6.7-inch Super AMOLED display is excellent, owners report that the One UI 8.5 update causes noticeable heating, system lag, and increased battery drain. The camera setup provides good selfies and video quality, yet it lacks a telephoto lens and suffers from frustrating shutter lag. Users also note that the virtual proximity sensor is inconsistent during calls compared to physical sensors.

Given that the newer Galaxy A57 5G is available for ৳47,499, many buyers find the current launch price of the A56 5G too high. If you choose to purchase this device, be aware that unofficial units lack manufacturer warranty support and may require paid repairs at service centers.

Does the Samsung Galaxy A56 5G come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

Official warranty is only provided for units purchased through official channels. If you buy a cheaper gray-import unit, it has no manufacturer cover. Any service center that accepts an unofficial unit will treat it as a paid repair.

How long will the Samsung Galaxy A56 5G receive software updates?

This device is part of the Samsung A-series, which typically receives between 4 to 6 years of software updates. While the long support window is a benefit, current users have reported that the One UI 8.5 update has caused significant problems.

Is the battery life on the Samsung Galaxy A56 5G reliable?

The phone features a 5000 mAh battery that provides decent endurance. However, recent software updates have introduced noticeable battery drain for some users. You should consider this potential impact on daily usage before making your purchase.

Is the Samsung Galaxy A56 5G good for gaming?

The Exynos 1580 chipset is capable of handling tasks, but owners have reported that recent software updates have caused significant heating and lag during use. These performance issues may affect your gaming experience until further software improvements are released.
