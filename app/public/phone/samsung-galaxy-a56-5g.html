Samsung Galaxy A56 5G Is it worth buying?

The Samsung Galaxy A56 5G is a premium-feeling device that struggles with significant software instability and thermal management issues following the One UI 8.5 update.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize a premium aluminum build and long-term software support and are willing to wait for potential future patches to fix current thermal issues.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable phone for gaming or heavy multitasking, or if you prefer a device with expandable storage and faster charging.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Premium aluminum frame build
  • Vibrant 120Hz Super AMOLED display
  • Great video and selfie quality
  • Up to 6 major Android upgrades

Reported trade-offs4

  • Significant thermal and lagging issues
  • Slow 45W charging speeds
  • Main camera stills underperform
  • No microSD card slot

Why we say that

While the hardware features a high-quality aluminum frame and a vibrant 120Hz Super AMOLED display, real-world usage is hampered by reports of lagging and overheating. Owners frequently express frustration with the slow 45W charging speeds and the lack of a microSD slot, which feels like a downgrade from older models. The camera system produces great video and selfies, but the main stills often underperform relative to the high price point.

Because the software update has introduced performance regressions, the phone currently offers a polarizing experience. Prospective buyers should weigh the premium build quality against the reported instability and the fact that older, more reliable models like the Galaxy A73 5G are available for less money.

How long will the Samsung Galaxy A56 5G receive software updates?

Is the Samsung Galaxy A56 5G good for gaming?

While the Exynos 1580 chipset is capable, owners report that recent software updates have introduced significant thermal issues and lagging. These performance regressions make the device less ideal for gaming compared to its hardware potential.

Owners report average battery endurance and describe the 45W charging speed as slow. Many users find the charging process not very fast, which is a major drawback for a phone at this price point.
