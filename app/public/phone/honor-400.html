Is the Honor 400 worth buying?

What is good

  • 5000 nit peak brightness
  • 200 MP main camera with OIS
  • 12 GB RAM with 80 W fast charging
  • 6.55 inch AMOLED 120 Hz display

What is not

  • Frequent notification failures
  • Low hand free call volume
  • Overheating and battery drain during heavy use
  • No telephoto lens

Buy it if Buy it if you value a bright screen and high resolution photos and can tolerate occasional software hiccups.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable notifications, strong call audio, or smooth heavy gaming.

Why we say that

The 6.55 inch AMOLED panel reaches a peak of 5000 nits, making outdoor use easy. The Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 with 12 GB RAM handles everyday apps well, yet heavy games often stall or lag as the chipset throttles. The 200 MP main sensor captures detailed daylight photos, though the lack of a telephoto lens limits zoom options.

Battery capacity is 6000 mAh and supports 80 W charging, but owners report uneven battery drain and overheating, which can shorten real world endurance. MagicOS 10 runs on Android 15 and includes an IR blaster, but notification failures are common and call audio from the hand free speaker is low. The phone arrives as a global model, so the official warranty only applies to the higher priced listings; cheaper gray imports rely on paid repairs.

Will the 6000 mAh battery last a full day with normal use?

The large battery can last a day for many users, but owners report mixed experiences. Some find it great, while others see faster drain and overheating, which can reduce practical endurance.

How does the phone handle heavy gaming?

The Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 and 12 GB RAM run daily apps smoothly, but heavy games often throttle or lag. Owner ratings show gaming performance around 5.8 out of 10, indicating limited heavy gaming capability.

Is the camera good in low light conditions?

The 200 MP main sensor delivers excellent daylight photos with optical image stabilization. Low light performance is not highlighted in reviews, and the lack of a telephoto lens means zoomed night shots may be weak.
