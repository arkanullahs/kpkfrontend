Buying verdict

Honor 400 Is it worth buying?

The Honor 400 offers a stunning display but suffers from significant software bugs that impact daily reliability.

Choose it if
You primarily value a high-brightness display for media consumption and do not rely on instant notifications for work.
Consider alternatives if
You are a heavy gamer or require reliable, real-time notifications for messaging apps like WhatsApp and Gmail.

What works well

  • Industry-leading 5000-nit peak brightness
  • Great 200MP main camera daylight shots
  • Smooth 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Fast 80W charging capability

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe notification failures for apps
  • Overheating and lag during heavy gaming
  • Low call volume on hands-free
  • Plastic frame construction

Why we say that

While the 5000-nit screen is impressive, owners frequently report severe notification failures where Gmail and WhatsApp alerts disappear after the screen locks. The device feels premium and lightweight, yet the plastic frame and reports of overheating during heavy tasks suggest compromises in build and performance. Camera performance is strong for daylight photography, but the lack of a telephoto lens limits versatility.

Users have also highlighted concerns regarding low call volume and inconsistent battery life, which some find non-competitive. With a MagicOS interface that includes medium levels of bloat and ads, the phone requires patience to manage. If you prioritize a vibrant screen over consistent software performance, this device might suit you, but the reported bugs make it a risky choice for those who rely on their phone for critical communication.

Does the Honor 400 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

The brand warranty applies only to the official unit. Cheaper listings are gray imports with no manufacturer cover, and any service centre that accepts an unofficial unit will treat it as a paid repair.

How is the battery life on the Honor 400?

Owner reports on battery life are mixed. Some users find the 6000 mAh battery great, while others describe it as non-competitive. There are also specific reports of battery drain and overheating issues.

Is the Honor 400 good for gaming?

While the Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 chipset handles daily tasks smoothly, the phone is not ideal for heavy gaming. Owners report that the device throttles and lags when pushed with demanding games.

How many years of software updates will this phone receive?

The Honor 400 is a flagship model that is expected to receive 3 to 4 OS updates. It currently runs on Android 15 with MagicOS 10.
