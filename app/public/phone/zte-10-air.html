Is the ZTE 10 Air worth buying?

What is good

  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 delivers flagship performance
  • 6.8 inch AMOLED with 120Hz and 1600‑nit peak
  • 80W wired charging refills 6000 mAh quickly
  • Generous 12‑16 GB RAM and up to 512 GB storage

What is not

  • Device gets hot after 30‑40 minutes gaming
  • IP54 water resistance is low for 2025
  • Under‑display selfie camera looks murky
  • No 3.5 mm headphone jack

Buy it if Buy if you want flagship speed, a large bright screen and fast charging at a gray‑import price and can accept limited warranty and occasional overheating.

Skip it if Skip if you need reliable water resistance, a headphone jack, or official service support in Bangladesh.

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset feels fast in everyday tasks and games, and the 6.8‑inch AMOLED with 120 Hz refresh and 1600‑nit peak looks impressive even under bright sunlight. The 6000 mAh battery together with 80W wired charging sounds generous, yet many owners notice the charge runs out sooner than expected in real use. Gaming runs smoothly for the first half hour, but the phone becomes noticeably hot after 30 to 40 minutes and may throttle performance.

The premium build feels good in hand, but the IP54 rating offers only basic splash protection and there is no 3.5 mm headphone jack. The under‑display selfie camera produces murky selfies and the software, while packed with gaming features, suffers from awkward English translations and medium‑level ads. Because it is a gray‑import unit, you rely on the shop’s warranty and must verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
