Why we say that

The phone runs Android 14 with Realme UI 5.0 and supports 5G, so everyday tasks feel responsive. Battery life is average, lasting a day for typical use, but several owners notice faster drain that may indicate an older unit. Software feels heavy with noticeable bloat and ads, which can affect the overall experience.

The 50MP main camera captures decent shots in good light, yet it falls short of the pro version and struggles in low light. Build quality is glass back and 200g, giving a premium feel but also making it prone to fingerprints. Fast 45W charging helps recover quickly, and the headphone jack adds convenience.

Overall it is a capable midrange phone if you can tolerate the battery and software quirks.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 5000mAh battery provides average endurance, lasting a full day with moderate use. Some owners report faster drain, especially if the unit is older, so real world life may vary.

Is the phone good for gaming?

With the Dimensity 7050 chipset and 6GB RAM it handles most games at medium settings smoothly. Owner rating for gaming is 6.5, indicating decent but not top tier performance.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50MP sensor with f/1.8 aperture captures decent photos in good light, but low light detail is limited and it lacks the extra lenses of the pro variant.

Will I get official warranty and software updates?

The gray import stock only offers shop warranty, not a manufacturer guarantee. Realme promises about two to three Android version updates, but the UI includes medium high bloat and ads.
