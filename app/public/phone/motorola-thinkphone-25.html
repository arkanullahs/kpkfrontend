Why we say that

Living with this phone means relying on the shop for support rather than the manufacturer, as there is no official warranty coverage in Bangladesh. The device features a bright 3,000 nits P-OLED display and a capable Dimensity 7300 chipset that handles daily tasks well. While the near-stock Hello UI provides a clean experience, users should be aware that Motorola has a historically weak track record for software updates despite the promise of up to 5 major OS updates.

The 4,310 mAh battery is modest, so heavy users may find themselves reaching for the 68W charger more often than expected. Because these are global units, you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before finalizing the purchase to ensure the device is registered for use on local networks.

Does the Motorola Thinkphone 25 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit currently available is a gray import, meaning you must rely solely on the warranty provided by the specific shop where you purchase the device.

How long will the Motorola Thinkphone 25 receive software updates?

The device is promised up to 5 major OS updates. However, you should be aware that Motorola has a historically weak track record regarding the consistency and speed of these software updates.

Is the battery life on the Motorola Thinkphone 25 sufficient for heavy users?

The phone is equipped with a 4,310 mAh battery. While it supports 68W fast charging to help you top up quickly, the capacity is relatively modest, which may require frequent charging for users with heavy daily demands.

What should I check before buying this unofficial unit?

You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before you pay. Since these are gray-import units, verifying the registration status is essential to ensure the phone will function correctly on local mobile networks.
