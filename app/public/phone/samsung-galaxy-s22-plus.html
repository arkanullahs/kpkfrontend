Is the Samsung Galaxy S22 Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.6 inch 120 Hz AMOLED display
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS and 3x telephoto
  • 45 W fast charging
  • 5G connectivity

What is not

  • Thermal throttling at 120 Hz reduces performance
  • Battery life only average and drains quickly
  • Software updates likely ending soon
  • No official warranty on this listing

Buy it if Buy it if you value a premium screen and camera at a low price and can accept limited future software support.

Skip it if Skip it if you need long term updates, a phone that stays fast at high refresh rates, or an official warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.6 inch AMOLED panel reaches 1750 nits and runs at 120 Hz, making scrolling and media smooth. The 50 MP main sensor with OIS and 3x telephoto delivers sharp photos in good light and decent results in low light. Battery life is average; the 4500 mAh cell lasts a day for most users but drains faster with the high refresh rate.

Fast charging at 45 W helps recover quickly. The Exynos 2200 chipset feels snappy in everyday tasks, yet performance drops when the display stays at 120 Hz because of thermal throttling. Software runs Android 12 with One UI 6.1.1 and promises up to four major Android upgrades, but being four years old the update window is closing.

No official warranty is listed for this price, so you rely on the seller’s return policy.
