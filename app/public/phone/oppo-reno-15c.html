Is the OPPO Reno 15c worth buying?

What is good

  • 6500 mAh battery lasts two days
  • Premium aluminum frame with IP68/69 rating
  • 50 MP main and selfie cameras in daylight
  • 120 Hz AMOLED display with 1200 nits peak

What is not

  • Low‑light camera performance is poor
  • Gaming and performance feel weak for price
  • No microSD slot for storage expansion
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Buy it if Buy it if you need a big battery, solid build and can accept a gray‑import warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you prioritize low‑light photography, high‑end gaming or official warranty support.

Why we say that

The 6.59 inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can reach 1200 nits, though owners say brightness feels only average and HDR is missing. The aluminum frame is premium and carries an IP68/69 rating, giving it a solid feel. In bright light the 50 MP main sensor and the same‑resolution selfie camera produce clear pictures, but low‑light performance is noisy and edge detection is weak.

The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset provides smooth everyday use, yet gaming and heavy workloads feel underpowered for the price, reflected in low owner scores. Fast 80 W charging refills the large battery quickly. All units are gray‑import from India, so there is no official OPPO warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s limited warranty applies and repairs cost extra.

Software runs ColorOS 16 on Android 16, but pre‑installed bloatware is noticeable.
