What works well

  • 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Fast 90W charging
  • Solid primary camera for closeups
  • Includes an IR blaster

Reported trade-offs

  • Frequent system lags and crashes
  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • Battery drains quickly
  • Poor gaming performance

Why we say that

While the 144Hz AMOLED display is a highlight for viewing, the actual experience is marred by software and hardware instability. Owners report that the device overheats, lags, and crashes during regular use. The camera hardware is solid for closeups and zoom, but the app itself often freezes for the first three seconds or crashes entirely.

Battery life is only average and drains quickly. You also have to deal with high amounts of bloatware and notification ads within XOS. It is a risky choice because the hardware specs on paper do not match the reported user experience of lagging and instability.

No, it is not recommended for gaming. Owners gave it a very low gaming rating of 2.5 and reported that the phone overheats and lags.

How many software updates will it get?

The phone comes with Android 15 and XOS 15. It is expected to receive up to 2 major Android upgrades.
