Infinix Note 30i Is it worth buying?

The Infinix Note 30i is a budget device that relies on its AMOLED display and large storage capacity to attract buyers at ৳18,990.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize a large AMOLED screen and massive storage for media consumption on a strict budget.
Consider alternatives if
You want long-term software updates, a clean user interface, or the security of an official manufacturer warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Large 6.66 inch AMOLED display
  • Generous 256GB internal storage
  • Reliable 5000 mAh battery capacity
  • Includes a dedicated headphone jack

Reported trade-offs4

  • High volume of bloatware and ads
  • No official manufacturer warranty available
  • Limited future software update support
  • Aging Helio G85 chipset performance

Why we say that

Living with this phone means navigating a software experience heavily cluttered with preloaded apps and notification advertisements. Because the device was released in 2023, you should expect very little in terms of future software support, as it is already reaching the end of its typical update cycle. The Helio G85 chipset is aging, which impacts performance during daily tasks.

You must be aware that this device is only available as gray-import stock, meaning you will not receive a manufacturer warranty. Instead, you are limited to whatever shop warranty the seller provides. Always verify the IMEI and BTRC status before handing over your money to ensure the device is legitimate.

Read full reasoning

While the 64MP camera and 5000 mAh battery offer decent utility, the overall experience is hampered by the heavy XOS interface and the lack of official support channels in the country.

Infinix Note 30i: what buyers ask us

What is the Infinix Note 30i price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Infinix Note 30i in Bangladesh was ৳18,990, checked 24 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does the Infinix Note 30i come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there is no official manufacturer warranty for this device. All units currently found in the market are gray-import stock, which means you only receive a limited shop warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before purchasing.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The phone was released in 2023 and is already three years old. You should expect very little or no remaining software update life for this device. It typically receives only one or two OS updates, which are often slow to arrive.

Is the battery life good for daily use?

The device is equipped with a 5000 mAh battery. This capacity is standard for the price point and should provide sufficient power for a full day of typical usage, though actual performance depends on your specific apps and screen brightness settings.

Is this phone suitable for heavy gaming?

The phone uses the Helio G85 chipset. While this processor can handle basic tasks and light applications, it is an older chip. You may experience performance limitations if you attempt to play demanding games or run multiple heavy applications simultaneously.
