Buying verdict

OPPO A3x Is it worth buying?

If you just need a cheap 5G phone with a bright screen, the A3x will work but expect noticeable lag in everyday use.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want an inexpensive 5G phone with a bright screen and quick charging for basic apps and browsing.
Consider alternatives if

What works well4

  • Bright 120Hz display
  • Fast 45W charging
  • Large 5100 mAh battery
  • Headphone jack included

Reported trade-offs4

  • Laggy overall performance
  • Weak and slow camera
  • Battery feels short lived
  • Poor gaming experience

Why we say that

The 6.67 inch IPS LCD shows 120Hz refresh and can reach 1000 nits, making it easy to see outdoors. The 5100 mAh battery and 45W charger sound good on paper, yet owners say it feels short lived and the phone drains quickly under load. The 8MP main camera and 5MP selfie sensor are slow and produce blurry pictures, especially in low light.

Performance is hampered by the Dimensity 6300 chipset, leading to stutter in games and apps. The build feels cheap and the device does not receive the frequent OS upgrades seen on flagship models. Overall it is a basic device for light tasks, but it falls short for power users who need smooth performance and decent photography.
