Is the OPPO A3x worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz bright display
  • 45W fast charging
  • 5100 mAh battery
  • headphone jack

What is not

  • Laggy performance
  • Weak camera
  • Short battery life
  • Poor gaming experience

Buy it if Buy it if you value screen quality and quick charging over gaming and photography.

Skip it if Skip it if you need strong gaming performance or a high quality camera.

Why we say that

It comes with a 6.67 inch IPS LCD panel that reaches 120Hz and can hit 1000 nits, so the screen stays clear even in sunlight. The 5100 mAh battery supports 45W charging, which fills the cell quickly, but owners report the battery feels short lived during daily use. Its Dimensity 6300 chipset with 4GB RAM struggles with speed, and users describe the phone as laggy when opening apps or playing games.

The main camera is an 8MP sensor with f/2.0 aperture, yet reviewers find it slow and underperforming, especially in low light. Selfies come from a 5MP lens that also feels laggy, so photo quality is not a strong point. The phone runs Android 14 with ColorOS 14, which brings medium bloat and occasional ads, and it may receive three to four major OS upgrades like other flagships.

Overall the device feels suitable for basic tasks and media consumption, but it falls short for gamers and photography enthusiasts.
