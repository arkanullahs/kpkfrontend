Is the Motorola G77 Power worth buying?

What is good

  • 5000‑nit peak brightness on AMOLED
  • 5200 mAh battery lasting a full day
  • 120 Hz smooth display
  • 108MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Dimensity 6400 feels underpowered for price
  • 30 W charging is slow
  • Plastic frame shows quality concerns
  • Heavy games run juddery

Buy it if Buy if you value a bright high‑refresh display and long battery life and can accept modest gaming performance and a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip if you need fast charging, top‑tier gaming, or an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Daily use feels smooth thanks to 8 GB RAM and a 5200 mAh battery that easily lasts a full intensive day. The 108 MP main sensor with OIS delivers decent photos in good light but struggles with motion and low‑light scenes. Build feels slim and MIL‑STD durable yet the plastic frame raises quality concerns.

Gaming is acceptable for casual titles but heavy games become juddery and the phone gets slightly warm. Charging at 30 W is noticeably slower than many modern phones and there is no wireless option. Software runs a near‑stock Hello UI on Android 16 but includes some pre‑installed crapware and historically weak update support.

As a gray‑import you only get a shop warranty, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying. Overall it offers a great display and battery but feels overpriced for the Dimensity 6400 chipset.
