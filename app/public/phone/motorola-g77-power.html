Buying verdict

Motorola G77 Power Is it worth buying?

The Motorola G77 Power feels like a big‑screen, long‑lasting daily driver that still shows its limits under pressure.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large AMOLED display, big battery and 5G at a sub‑35 k taka price and can accept a gray‑import without official warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable gaming performance, strong low‑light camera or an official brand warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 108MP main camera for bright scenes
  • 6.78 inch AMOLED with 120Hz refresh
  • 5200 mAh battery lasting full day
  • 30W fast charging

Reported trade-offs4

  • Dimensity 6400 throttles under heavy load
  • Low‑light photos are noisy and lack detail
  • Gaming and multitasking feel sluggish
  • Stereo speakers are unbalanced

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch AMOLED with 120 Hz refresh and 108 MP main sensor look impressive in bright conditions, and the 5200 mAh battery easily survives a heavy day. However the Dimensity 6400 chipset often throttles, making demanding games and multitasking feel sluggish. Low‑light photos quickly reveal noise and loss of detail despite the high‑resolution sensor.

The plastic frame keeps the weight down but feels cheap, and the stereo speakers are noticeably unbalanced. Software is near‑stock Android with useful Moto gestures, but update history is weak and you will only get one to three major OS upgrades. The phone arrives as a gray‑import built for India, so there is no official Motorola warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s limited guarantee and pay for any repairs.
