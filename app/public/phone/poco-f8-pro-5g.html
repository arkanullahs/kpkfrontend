What works well

  • 120Hz AMOLED display with 3500‑nit peak
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite delivers flagship performance
  • 100W fast charging refills huge battery quickly
  • 6210 mAh cell provides over 17 hours active use

Reported trade-offs

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Thermal throttling reduces performance in long games
  • HyperOS shows intrusive ads in core apps
  • Charger brick often missing from the box

Why we say that

The 6.59‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 3500 nits, so outdoor visibility and smooth scrolling feel premium. Under the hood sits the Snapdragon 8 Elite with 12 GB RAM, delivering fast app launches and strong gaming frames until the device heats up. The 6210 mAh battery lasts around 17.5 hours of active use and refills in minutes with 100 W charging, although the charger brick is often omitted.

Camera quality is mixed; the 50 MP main sensor is solid, but the ultrawide is weak and the telephoto adds little. Software runs HyperOS 3 on Android 16, packed with features but plagued by intrusive ads and irregular updates. During long gaming sessions the phone throttles, causing noticeable FPS drops.

Overall it feels powerful for daily tasks but requires patience with software quirks and no official service support.

Is there an official warranty for the POCO F8 Pro 5G in Bangladesh?

No. All listings are gray‑import units, so the phone comes with only the shop’s own warranty and any repairs must be paid. The brand does not provide official service support in Bangladesh.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 6210 mAh cell delivers about 17.5 hours of active use, which translates to a full day of typical browsing, social media and video playback before needing a recharge.

Will the phone overheat while gaming?

During heavy gaming the device gets hot and thermal throttling appears, causing noticeable FPS dips. Short gaming sessions feel fine, but long sessions may suffer performance drops.

How many Android updates will the POCO F8 Pro 5G receive and are they timely?

The phone runs HyperOS 3 on Android 16 and has received only two to three OS upgrades so far. Owners report updates are irregular and sometimes delayed by several months.
