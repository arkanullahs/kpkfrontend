What works well

  • Bright 120Hz OLED display
  • Strong performance for the price
  • Good night camera quality
  • No bloatware or ads

Reported trade-offs

  • Poor cooling for high graphics gaming
  • Battery life degrades over time
  • Subpar video quality
  • Limited remaining software updates

Why we say that

It offers strong performance and a bright OLED screen that works well in sunlight. However, this device is 4 years old. You should expect very little remaining software update life.

Owners report that battery drain gets worse after major updates and the battery degrades over time. While photos are acceptable, the video quality is not good enough for many users. Gaming is limited because the phone lacks sufficient cooling for long sessions with high graphics.

How is the battery life and charging?

It has a 4500 mAh battery with 33W charging. Owners say the battery life is good, but it degrades over time and drain increases after major software updates.

Can I use this phone for heavy gaming?

The Snapdragon 778G+ 5G provides good performance. However, owners report it lacks sufficient cooling for longer gaming sessions with high graphics.

The phone launched with Android 12 and is upgradable to Android 15. Because it is 4 years old, you should expect short or no remaining software update life.
