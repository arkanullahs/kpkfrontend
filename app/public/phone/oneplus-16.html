OnePlus 16 Is it worth buying?

If you can live without a manufacturer warranty, the OnePlus 16 gives you flagship power at a gray‑import price.

What works well4

  • 200 MP main camera with OIS
  • 185 Hz LTPO AMOLED display up to 2000 nits
  • 9000 mAh battery with 100 W fast charging
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 performance

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • China version may lack Google services
  • No IP water‑resistance rating disclosed
  • Shop warranty only, repairs are paid

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED screen reaches 2000 nits and refreshes at 185 Hz, making everyday use feel smooth and bright. The Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 paired with 12 GB RAM delivers fast performance in apps and games, reflected in the 9.5 gaming rating. A massive 9000 mAh battery lasts all day and refuels in minutes with 100 W charging, which owners rate 9.6.

The 200 MP main sensor with OIS captures detailed stills, though low‑light performance is not highlighted. Build feels premium but lacks an IP rating and the only warranty comes from the shop, not the brand. Some listings ship the China‑market ColorOS version without Google services, which can be a hassle to convert.
