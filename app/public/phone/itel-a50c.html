What works well

  • Very low official price
  • Headphone jack included
  • Looks more premium than its cost
  • Light Android Go system

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe overheating and shutdowns
  • Very slow 5W charging
  • Constant lag and freezing
  • Dim HD+ display

Why we say that

The 6.56 inch IPS LCD is dim and only HD+, and the Unisoc T603 chipset struggles with anything beyond simple browsing. Owners report frequent temperature warnings, sudden shutdowns in sunlight, and a 5W charger that needs four hours for a full charge. The Android 14 Go system is light but filled with bloatware, and the 2GB RAM cannot keep up with games like Free Fire, which freeze or lag constantly.

The 8MP rear camera and 5MP selfie lens are usable in good light but perform poorly in low‑light conditions. The phone looks more premium than it feels, and the official warranty is the only safety net. Overall, it is a budget device for minimal use, not for performance or heavy multitasking.
