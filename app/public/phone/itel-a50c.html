Is the itel A50C worth buying?

What is good

  • Very low price
  • Headphone jack included
  • Large 6.56 inch screen
  • Android 14 Go edition

What is not

  • Extreme overheating and shutdowns
  • Very slow 5W charging
  • Severe lag in games
  • Cheap low quality display

Buy it if Buy it if you only need calls, texts and occasional apps and can accept frequent slowdowns.

Skip it if Skip it if you want smooth gaming, fast charging or reliable performance.

Why we say that

The device runs Android 14 Go on a Unisoc T603 chipset with 2 GB RAM and a 4000 mAh battery, yet owners report fast drain when it gets hot and charging often stops at about 57 percent. The 5W charger needs roughly four hours to fill the battery. The 6.56 inch IPS LCD is cheap and low quality, and the phone overheats severely, showing temperature warnings and sometimes shutting down in sunlight.

Gaming is almost impossible because of severe lag and freezing. The camera setup of 8 MP rear and 5 MP front receives low ratings, especially in low light. Software updates are rare and the system is loaded with banking bloatware, making the overall experience feel like a waste of money for most users.

How long does the battery last in normal use?

The 4000 mAh battery can last a day with light use, but owners report fast drain when the phone gets hot and charging often stops at about 57 percent, so real world endurance is inconsistent.

Can I play games on the itel A50C?

Gaming on the A50C is problematic; the Unisoc T603 with 2 GB RAM struggles, causing severe lag, freezing and sudden shutdowns, so even basic games are not playable smoothly.

Is the camera good in low light?

The phone has an 8 MP rear and 5 MP front camera, but reviews rate them low and the cheap IPS LCD does not help in low light, so pictures are often dull.

Will the phone receive software updates?

The A50C runs Android 14 Go with itelOS and comes with an official warranty at ৳8,399, but updates are rare and the system includes a lot of banking bloatware, limiting long term software support.
