Is the Motorola A300 worth buying?

What is good

  • Very low price
  • Simple near stock interface
  • Easy to navigate menus
  • Decent build for the cost

What is not

  • 1 MP camera produces blurry photos
  • Battery drains quickly
  • Performance lags in everyday use
  • Gaming is unplayable

Buy it if Buy it only if you need a very cheap phone for basic calls and texts.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect good photos, long battery life or smooth gaming.

Why we say that

It runs a MediaTek 6261D processor with a 2.0 inch TFT LCD that is clear enough for simple menus but feels tiny compared with modern smartphones. The Hello UI is close to stock Android, so there is little bloat and ads are rare, but software updates stop after one to three versions. Owners complain that the 1 MP camera produces blurry pictures even in daylight and video quality is almost unusable.

The 1,000 mAh battery lasts only a few hours of mixed use and drains faster when the screen is on. Performance scores are low, apps open slowly and gaming is practically impossible on this hardware. The build feels plastic and the display brightness is low, which makes outdoor reading difficult.

Because every listing is a gray import there is no official Motorola warranty in Bangladesh, only the shop’s limited promise and any repair will cost extra.
