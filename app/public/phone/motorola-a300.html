Buying verdict

Motorola A300 Is it worth buying?

At ৳3,300 the Motorola A300 offers the lowest entry price you can find, but it struggles with almost every other aspect.

Choose it if
You only need calls, texts and occasional simple apps and want the cheapest possible device.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable battery, decent camera, smooth gaming or official warranty support.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • very low price
  • near stock Hello UI
  • minimal ads and bloatware
  • easy to navigate

Reported trade-offs

  • battery dies quickly
  • camera produces blurry images
  • gaming is unplayable
  • no manufacturer warranty

Why we say that

The 2.0 inch TFT screen is tiny and dim, making reading and media uncomfortable. The Hello UI is close to stock and contains very little bloat, so the phone feels simple to use. Battery life is poor; the 1,000 mAh cell drops quickly and owners rate it only 2.5 out of 10.

Performance is sluggish with the M৳6,261D chipset, and gaming is essentially unplayable. The 1 MP camera produces blurry pictures even in good light, and video quality is rated at 1 out of 10. There is no official manufacturer warranty because every unit is a gray import, and any repair will cost extra.
