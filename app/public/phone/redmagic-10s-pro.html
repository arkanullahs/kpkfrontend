What works well

  • Snapdragon 8 Elite with active fan
  • Massive 7050 mAh battery
  • True full screen 144Hz AMOLED
  • Fast 80W GaN charger included

Reported trade-offs

  • Blurry and hazy selfie camera
  • Frame gets uncomfortably hot
  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Complex software with some bugs

Why we say that

You get the Snapdragon 8 Elite and an active fan that prevents throttling, making it a beast for gaming. The 7050 mAh battery is massive and lasts a long time even under heavy loads. However, living with it has drawbacks.

The frame becomes uncomfortably hot to touch during use. The under display selfie camera is very poor, with owners describing images as soft, grainy, and hazy. The software is feature dense but complex and lacks Google ARCore support.

Read full reasoning

Since this is a gray import, you have no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. If the hardware fails, you are entirely dependent on the shop that sold it to you for paid repairs.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as a gray import. It has no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Any warranty provided is from the shop itself, and all repairs are paid.

How is the gaming performance and heating?

Gaming performance is excellent due to the Snapdragon 8 Elite and active fan. While internal temperatures stay stable, the frame can become uncomfortably hot to touch during use.

Is the battery life good for heavy users?

Yes, the 7050 mAh battery provides exceptional endurance even under extreme gaming loads. The included 80W GaN charger can refill the battery in under an hour.

How is the camera quality for selfies and photos?

The main camera is average. The under display selfie camera is poor, with owners reporting that images look soft and hazy, as if smothered in Vaseline.
