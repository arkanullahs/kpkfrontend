Redmi Note 15 Pro Plus 5G Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that lasts all day without charging, the Redmi Note 15 Pro Plus 5G delivers.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value long battery life, bright screen and decent main photos and can tolerate limited gaming and ads.
Consider alternatives if
You need high end gaming, 4K 60 fps video or a guaranteed official warranty.

  • 6500 mAh battery endurance

  • UFS 2.2 storage limits speed
  • Ultrawide and zoom quality poor
  • No 4K 60 fps video
  • Heavy bloatware and ads

Why we say that

Its 6500 mAh silicon carbon battery gives real world endurance that most users rate 9.7 out of 10. The 6.83 inch AMOLED panel reaches a 3200 nit peak, so outdoor visibility is excellent. The 200 MP main sensor captures detailed photos in good light, though the ultrawide lens and digital zoom are noticeably weaker.

Performance feels smooth for daily tasks, but the UFS 2.2 storage and Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset limit high frame rate gaming and 4K 60 fps video recording. HyperOS 2 runs Android 15 with up to four major upgrades, yet it carries a lot of pre installed apps and ads that many find intrusive. The phone is water and dust resistant with an IR blaster, but the value is only attractive after price drops, especially if you need an official warranty.
