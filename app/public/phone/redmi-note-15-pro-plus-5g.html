Redmi Note 15 Pro Plus 5G Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day on a single charge and still look bright outdoors, the Redmi Note 15 Pro Plus 5G delivers that but you will feel its compromises in speed and software.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want long battery life, a bright screen and a high‑resolution main camera and can tolerate slower gaming and extra software clutter.
Consider alternatives if
You need top‑tier gaming speed, 4K video or a clean Android experience without ads.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Massive 6500 mAh battery with excellent endurance
  • Bright 6.83‑inch AMOLED display with 3200 nits peak
  • 200 MP main camera captures detailed images
  • IP68/IP69K water and dust resistance

Reported trade-offs4

  • UFS 2.2 storage limits gaming performance
  • No 4K 60fps video recording
  • Heavy bloatware and ads in HyperOS
  • China ROM lacks Google and Bangla apps

Why we say that

The 6500 mAh silicon‑carbon battery lives through heavy use and the 6.83‑inch AMOLED panel reaches 3200 nits, making it easy to see in sunlight. The 200 MP main sensor captures detailed photos in good light, while the ultrawide and zoom are noticeably weaker. The Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset feels smooth for daily tasks but the UFS 2.2 storage and 100 W charger (slower than the previous 120 W) show the phone’s budget limits.

HyperOS 2 runs Android 15 and promises up to four major updates, yet it is packed with pre‑installed apps and ads that many owners find annoying. Official warranty only covers the retail version; cheaper gray imports lack manufacturer support and may run a China‑only ROM without Google services.
