What works well

  • 6500 mAh battery with 100 W fast charge
  • 3200 nits peak brightness on 6.83‑inch AMOLED
  • 200 MP main camera with OIS
  • IP68/IP69K water and dust resistance

Reported trade-offs

  • UFS 2.2 storage limits gaming performance
  • HyperOS pre‑installed apps and ads
  • Ultrawide and zoom quality disappoint
  • Gray‑import units lack official warranty

Why we say that

The 6500 mAh silicon‑carbon cell gives real‑world endurance that most users praise, and the 100 W charger refills it quickly. The 6.83‑inch AMOLED panel reaches a peak of 3200 nits, making outdoor use easy. The 200 MP main sensor with OIS captures detailed photos, though the ultrawide lens and digital zoom fall short of expectations.

Performance feels smooth for everyday apps, but the UFS 2.2 storage and Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset limit high‑frame‑rate gaming. HyperOS 2 runs Android 15 and promises up to four major OS upgrades, yet it is loaded with pre‑installed apps and ads that many find intrusive. The phone is IP68/IP69K rated and includes an IR blaster, but only official listings carry a manufacturer warranty; gray‑import units rely on paid repairs.

Read full reasoning

Overall it feels premium in battery and display, but compromises in gaming, camera versatility and software cleanliness keep the value modest until price drops.
