Is the Redmi Note 15 Pro Plus 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Exceptional 6500 mAh battery endurance
  • Stunning and bright 3200 nits AMOLED display
  • Durable build with IP68 and IP69K rating
  • Fast 100W charging capability

What is not

  • Bloated software with high ad presence
  • Slow UFS 2.2 storage bottlenecks performance
  • Unusable secondary camera lenses
  • Lack of 4K 60fps video recording

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize extreme battery life and a high-quality display for media consumption over gaming performance.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a mobile gamer, a serious videographer, or someone who wants a clean software experience without ads.

Why we say that

While the 6500 mAh battery offers exceptional endurance that can last light users up to 5 days, the actual experience is hampered by a bloated HyperOS 2 interface that is complex to navigate and filled with ads. Owners report that the Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset is held back by slow UFS 2.2 storage, making it a poor choice for gaming or heavy performance tasks. The 200MP main camera is dependable for general photography, yet the secondary lenses are largely unusable and the lack of 4K 60fps video recording is a major disappointment for a phone at this price point.

You must also be cautious of radio noise during calls and the potential for the curved screen to break if mounted on a bike holder. Many gray market units are China-market versions that lack Google services and Bangla language support, making them difficult to use for the average buyer.

Is the battery life really as good as people say?

Yes, the 6500 mAh battery provides exceptional endurance. Owners have rated the battery performance at 9.4 out of 10, with light users reporting that the phone can last up to 5 days on a single charge.

Can I play high-end games on this device?

Gaming performance is not competitive in this segment. Owners have rated the gaming experience at 5.8 out of 10, noting that the UFS 2.2 storage acts as a major bottleneck for performance.

How do I know if I am buying the right version?

You must check if the unit is a global or China-market version. Many gray market units are China-market builds that lack Google services and Bangla language support, and these are difficult to convert.

How many years of software support will this phone receive?

The device runs on Android 15 and is promised up to 4 major Android upgrades. However, be aware that the HyperOS software is described by users as bloated and complex.
