Is the Redmi Note 15 Pro Plus 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.83 inch 120Hz AMOLED display
  • 6500 mAh battery lasting up to five days
  • 200 MP main camera with OIS
  • IP68/IP69K water and dust protection

What is not

  • UFS 2.2 storage slows gaming
  • HyperOS is ad‑heavy and complex
  • No 4K 60fps video recording
  • Curved screen can crack in bike holder

Buy it if Buy if you value long battery life, a bright display and a high‑resolution main camera and can tolerate software bloat.

Skip it if Skip if you need smooth gaming, reliable 4K video or a clean, ad‑free Android experience.

Why we say that

The 6.83‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 3200 nits, making media consumption a pleasure. The 200 MP main sensor with optical image stabilization captures sharp photos in good light, and the IP68/IP69K rating protects against water and dust. However, the secondary lenses are largely unusable and the device cannot record 4K video at 60 fps, which many rivals already offer.

Performance feels held back by UFS 2.2 storage, so games do not run as smoothly as the chipset alone suggests. HyperOS adds many ads and a steep learning curve, reducing overall ease of use. The fast 100 W charger works, but the smart‑charging feature is inconsistent.

The curved glass is attractive but can crack if mounted on a bike holder. Missing L5 GPS and occasional radio noise on calls also bother some owners.
