Buying verdict

OPPO Reno14 F Is it worth buying?

If you want a large, bright screen and long battery at a mid range price, the OPPO Reno14 F fits the bill.

Choose it if
You need a bright large screen, strong battery and decent camera without spending a premium.
Consider alternatives if
You require a clear manufacturer warranty or want minimal software bloat.

What works well

  • 6.57 inch AMOLED 120 Hz display
  • 6000 mAh battery with 45W fast charge
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 performance

Reported trade-offs

  • Medium amount of preinstalled bloat and ads
  • Official warranty details not clearly listed

Why we say that

The 6.57 inch AMOLED panel reaches 1400 nits and runs at 120 Hz, so everyday use feels smooth and visible even in sunlight. Under the hood sits a Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 chipset paired with 8 GB RAM, giving reliable performance for apps and most games. The 6000 mAh cell combined with 45 W fast charging keeps the phone alive through a full day and can be topped up quickly.

Photography is handled by a 50 MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture, OIS and an ultrawide lens, while the 32 MP selfie camera is sharp for video calls. Software runs Android 15 with ColorOS 15, offering three to four major OS upgrades, but it does include a medium level of preinstalled bloat and ads. Official warranty information is not clearly listed, which some buyers find unsettling.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 6000 mAh cell is large enough to power a full day of mixed use, including browsing, social media and video playback. With 45 W fast charging you can refill the charge in about half an hour.

Is the phone good for gaming?

Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 together with 8 GB RAM and a 120 Hz AMOLED panel delivers smooth frame rates in most modern games. It handles high settings well, though very demanding titles may need a slight dip.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor uses a 1/1.95 inch size, f/1.8 aperture and optical image stabilization, which helps gather light and reduce blur. Results are clear for a mid range phone, but not as bright as flagship models.

ColorOS 15 runs on Android 15 and promises three to four major OS upgrades for flagships, so you can expect several years of software support. The warranty is not clearly listed, so you may need to verify it with the seller.
