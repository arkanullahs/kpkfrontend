Buying verdict

Redmi Note 14 5G Is it worth buying?

The Redmi Note 14 5G gives you a very bright AMOLED screen and a big battery but suffers from weak performance and no official warranty.

Choose it if
You need a bright screen and large battery at a low gray import price and can live without official warranty or smooth gaming.
Consider alternatives if
You want reliable gaming performance, consistent camera quality, or official Xiaomi support in Bangladesh.

What works well

  • 120 Hz AMOLED display
  • 2100 nit peak brightness
  • 108 MP main camera sensor
  • 45 W fast charging

Reported trade-offs

  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • China ROM lacks Google services
  • Dimensity 7025 struggles with heavy games
  • Camera colors inconsistent and laggy

Why we say that

The 6.67 inch display reaches 2100 nit at peak and runs at 120 Hz, making outdoor use easy. The 5110 mAh cell easily lasts a full day for most users, and the 45 W charger refills it in a reasonable time. The 108 MP main sensor captures detailed shots but colors can shift and the camera often lags, especially in low light.

The Dimensity 7025 Ultra chipset handles everyday tasks but stutters in heavy games and many modern titles are not supported. HyperOS adds many ads and occasional animation hiccups, and the China ROM lacks Google services and Bangla input, making app conversion difficult. Repairs are paid and only the shop’s limited warranty applies, so keep the IMEI status clear before buying.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

Owners report strong endurance and the 5110 mAh cell easily lasts a full day for most daily usage. Heavy gaming or bright screen settings will reduce the time but it still outlasts many rivals in the same price range.

Will the phone run modern games smoothly?

The Dimensity 7025 Ultra chipset can handle casual games but stutters appear in heavy titles. GPU compatibility issues mean some popular games have been dropped, so expect limited performance for demanding gaming sessions.

Is the camera good in low light?

The 108 MP sensor captures detail in good light, but low‑light colors are inconsistent and the camera often lags. Users notice slower focus and occasional frame drops, making it less reliable for night photography.

Does the phone get official warranty and Android updates?

All units are gray imports, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop provides its own limited warranty and repairs are paid. The device runs HyperOS on Android 14 and promises up to two major Android upgrades.
