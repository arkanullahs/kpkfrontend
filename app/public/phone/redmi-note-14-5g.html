Is the Redmi Note 14 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz AMOLED display with 2100‑nit peak brightness
  • 5110 mAh battery that lasts a full day
  • 108 MP main camera with OIS
  • Headphone jack, FM radio and IR blaster

What is not

  • Weak chipset causes stutter in heavy apps
  • HyperOS is bloated with ads and animation lag
  • Camera colors are inconsistent and focus lags
  • No manufacturer warranty, only shop warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you want a bright large screen, long battery life and can accept a China ROM with no official warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need smooth gaming, reliable camera performance or official brand support.

Why we say that

The 6.67‑inch panel reaches 2100 nits, so outdoor visibility is excellent, and the 5110 mAh cell usually lasts from morning to night without a charge. The 108 MP main camera captures detailed shots, yet colors can shift and the secondary lenses struggle, especially in low light, and users report noticeable lag when opening the camera app. HyperOS is loaded with ads and animation stutters, which adds to the feeling of bloat.

Gaming performance suffers; the Dimensity 7025 and PowerVR GPU stumble in heavy titles and some newer games are not supported. The phone arrives as a China‑ROM gray import, so there is no official Xiaomi warranty and you must rely on the shop’s limited coverage. Overall, it feels like a bright, long‑lasting device that falls short in speed, software polish and after‑sales support.

Is there an official Xiaomi warranty for the Redmi Note 14 5G in Bangladesh?

No official Xiaomi warranty is available for the Redmi Note 14 5G in Bangladesh. The phone is a gray‑import unit, so only the shop’s warranty applies and any repair costs are paid by the owner.

Can I use Google services and Bangla language on this phone?

The phone ships with the China ROM of HyperOS, which lacks Google Play Services and Bangla input. Users must flash a different ROM, which is difficult and not guaranteed to work reliably.

How long does the battery last and how fast does it charge?

The 5110 mAh battery easily lasts a full day for most users, and the 45 W charger refills it in about an hour, though charger availability can vary.

Is the Redmi Note 14 5G suitable for gaming and heavy apps?

The Dimensity 7025 chipset and PowerVR GPU cause stutters in demanding games, and some modern titles are not supported, so the phone is not ideal for heavy gaming.
