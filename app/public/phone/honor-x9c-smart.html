Honor X9c Smart Is it worth buying?

If you can live with a gray import phone that lacks official warranty, the Honor X9c Smart offers a 6.8 inch 120 Hz screen and a 108 MP sensor at a low price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large 5G phone on a tight budget and can accept shop only warranty.
Consider alternatives if

Why we say that

It runs on a Dimensity 7025 Ultra chipset, provides 8 to 12 GB RAM, 256 GB storage and supports 5G. Owners give the battery a 4 out of 10 rating and say it drains quickly despite the 5800 mAh cell, so a full day of heavy use is rare. The 108 MP camera uses a large sensor but real world photos are described as mediocre, especially in low light, and the 16 MP selfie camera does not impress.

The 6.8 inch TFT LCD panel refreshes at 120 Hz but reviewers note low brightness, which can be a problem under sunlight. Software is Android 14 with Magic OS 8, includes Google services and should get three to four major OS upgrades, though it carries medium bloat and ads. Because it is a gray import, the cheapest listing at ৳24,990 comes with only the shop’s warranty and any repair will be charged, so verify the IMEI and BTRC status.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

In real life the 5800 mAh battery does not last a full day for most users. Owners report the charge drops quickly, especially when the 120 Hz screen and 5G are active, so you may need to recharge by evening.

Is the gaming performance smooth?

The Dimensity 7025 Ultra with 8 to 12 GB RAM handles most games at medium settings without major lag. Gaming scores are around 4.5 out of 10, but the battery may drain fast during long sessions.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 108 MP sensor is large but owners describe the pictures as mediocre, especially in low light scenes. Colors may be washed out and detail is lost, so it is not a strong point for night photography.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

The phone is a gray import, so it comes with only the shop’s warranty and any repair will be paid by you. Magic OS 8 on Android 14 should receive three to four major OS upgrades, but bloat and ads are medium.
