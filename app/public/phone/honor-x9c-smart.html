What works well

  • 12GB RAM and 256GB storage
  • 5G connectivity
  • 120Hz refresh rate
  • Android 14 with Google

Reported trade-offs

  • Low brightness TFT LCD display
  • Quick battery drain
  • Poor camera quality
  • No official brand warranty

Why we say that

Owners report that the battery drains quickly despite the 5800 mAh capacity. The TFT LCD panel is a major point of contention for a 2024 device, with users complaining about low brightness. Camera quality is described as mediocre and poor.

One owner mentioned the phone began to hang after two months and required a format. While the performance rating is a 6.0, the overall build and display quality feel dated. You are getting a device with a 108MP camera and 12GB of RAM, but the actual daily usage does not match these numbers.

What is the Honor X9c Smart price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Honor X9c Smart in Bangladesh was ৳24,999, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, every listing we have recorded is a gray import. You will only get a shop warranty instead of brand backed support. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

How is the battery life on the Honor X9c Smart?

Despite having a 5800 mAh battery, owners report that the battery drains quickly. The battery received a low owner rating of 4.0 out of 10.

Is the camera good for photography?

The camera quality is described as mediocre and poor by owners. While it has a 108MP main sensor, the actual photo quality is a common complaint.

What is the display quality like?

The phone uses a TFT LCD panel which is considered outdated for 2024. Users have complained about low brightness and poor overall display quality.
