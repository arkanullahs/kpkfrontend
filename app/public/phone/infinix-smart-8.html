  • 5000 mAh battery lasts all day

  • headphone jack and FM radio included

Reported trade-offs4

  • 10W charging takes about two hours
  • Android Go limits apps and shows ads
  • Frequent freezing and lag on simple tasks
  • Camera shows shutter lag and low light weakness

Why we say that

The 6.6 inch IPS LCD runs at 90Hz which feels smooth for scrolling, yet the panel only reaches 500 nits and looks dim in bright light. A 5000 mAh battery gives solid endurance for light use, but the 10W charger needs about two hours to fill the cell. The Unisoc T606 with 4 GB RAM handles basic apps, but Android Go strips many features and adds preinstalled ads that annoy.

The 13 MP rear sensor captures acceptable photos in good light but suffers shutter lag and performs poorly in low light or social media posts. The plastic body is lightweight and shatter resistant, but it scratches easily and feels cheap. System heating appears under direct sunlight and gaming triggers “not responding” errors.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh cell can keep the phone alive through a full day of light use such as calls, messaging and occasional web browsing. Heavy streaming or gaming will drain it faster, but for basic tasks it is reliable.

Can I play games smoothly on this device?

The phone uses a Unisoc T606 and 4 GB RAM, which is enough for very light games. More demanding titles often cause lag and not responding errors, so it is not suited for serious gaming.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 13 MP rear sensor captures decent pictures in good lighting, but it suffers noticeable shutter lag and produces dark, noisy images in low light conditions, making it unsuitable for social media posts after dark.

Is there an official warranty and will I get software updates?

The phone is sold as an official Bangladesh unit, so the brand’s warranty and service network apply. However, being three years old, it is unlikely to receive further Android updates beyond the one or two already provided.
