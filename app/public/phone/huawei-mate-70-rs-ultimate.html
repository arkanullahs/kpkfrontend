Huawei Mate 70 RS Ultimate Is it worth buying?

If you can live without Google Play services, the Mate 70 RS Ultimate offers a large, bright screen and strong battery at a steep price.

Why we say that

The 6.9 inch dual layer LTPO OLED panel reaches a peak of 3500 nits and runs at a smooth 120 Hz, making videos and scrolling feel fluid. Its Kirin 9020 chipset paired with 16 GB RAM delivers solid performance, reflected in an 8.5 display rating and an 8.5 performance rating from owners. Battery life is praised for endurance, with the 5700 mAh cell lasting a full day of typical use without a charge.

Storage options of 512 GB or 1 TB give plenty of room for apps and media, and the phone includes an IR blaster for remote control. Owners note the camera is decent with a 50 MP main sensor, but it does not match flagship level quality and low light results are modest. The biggest drawback is the lack of Google Play services, meaning many Bangladeshi apps need workarounds, and the gray import status means you rely on the shop for warranty and repairs.
