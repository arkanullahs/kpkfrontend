Is the Samsung Galaxy M47 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts a full day
  • Bright 6.7‑inch Super AMOLED 120 Hz display
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • 45 W fast charging

What is not

  • Heavy One UI causes noticeable lag
  • Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 struggles with modern games
  • No stereo speakers
  • Ultra‑wide camera is grainy

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize long battery life and a large AMOLED screen and can accept occasional UI lag and limited gaming performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need smooth gaming, reliable multi‑lens camera performance, or an official Samsung warranty in Bangladesh.

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh cell easily gets you through a full day of heavy browsing, video streaming and social media without hunting for a charger. The 6.7‑inch Super AMOLED panel delivers bright colours and a smooth 120 Hz motion, though the bezels are thick and there is no always‑on display. The 50 MP main sensor with optical image stabilisation captures decent photos, but the ultra‑wide lens is grainy and struggles in low light.

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 chipset feels underpowered; One UI 8.5 adds noticeable lag and games like BGMI crash or drop frames. Charging at 45 W is acceptable but you must buy a charger separately. The phone arrives as a gray‑import, so Samsung’s official warranty does not apply and any repair will cost extra.

Overall it works for basic tasks but heavy users will feel the performance and software shortcomings.
