Is the Samsung Galaxy M47 worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz Super AMOLED screen
  • 6000 mAh battery with 45W fast charge
  • Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 chipset
  • 50 MP OIS main camera

What is not

  • Only gray import stock, no brand warranty
  • Shop warranty may be limited
  • IMEI and BTRC status must be verified

Buy it if Buy it if you need a big, smooth phone with strong battery and are comfortable with a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you require an official Samsung warranty or want guaranteed IMEI clearance.

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch Super AMOLED panel runs at 120Hz, making scrolling and gaming feel fluid. The Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 paired with 8GB RAM handles everyday apps and most games without stutter, though long sessions can warm the device. A 6000 mAh battery easily lasts more than a day of typical use and the 45W charger refills it in roughly an hour.

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures clear photos in good light, but low‑light results are decent rather than spectacular. The phone runs Android 16 with One UI 8.5 and promises up to six major Android upgrades, keeping it secure for years. The main drawback is that only gray‑import units are available, so you receive a shop warranty instead of Samsung’s brand warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 6000 mAh battery can easily last more than a day with moderate use such as browsing, social media and video playback. When the screen is set to 120Hz and brightness is high, you may need to charge once daily, but the 45W charger refills the battery in about an hour.

Can the Galaxy M47 handle heavy gaming?

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 processor together with 8GB RAM and a 120Hz display runs most popular games smoothly at medium settings. Extended high graphics sessions may cause the phone to become warm, but performance remains playable for typical gaming sessions.

How does the 50 MP camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP sensor with a 1/2.76 inch size, f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures clear images in good lighting. In low light the photos are decent, though they may lack the brightness and detail of premium flagship cameras and can show some noise.

Will I get an official Samsung warranty and how many updates?

Only gray import units are available, so the phone comes with a shop warranty rather than an official Samsung brand warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller. Software will receive up to six major Android upgrades and the One UI skin has low bloat and ads.
