What works well

  • 6500 mAh battery with 90 W fast charge
  • 6.77‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED display
  • 50 MP main camera with good daylight portraits
  • 12 GB RAM and up to 512 GB storage

Reported trade-offs

  • Plastic body with only IP65 rating
  • Mid‑range chipset throttles and heats in games
  • Camera app opens slowly
  • Charging limit bug can stop at 100 %

Why we say that

The 6500 mAh cell paired with 90 W fast charging easily gets you from zero to full in under an hour. The 6.77‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and stays bright outdoors, making media consumption pleasant. Daily tasks feel smooth thanks to 12 GB RAM and ample storage, and the 50 MP main sensor delivers decent portraits and daylight shots.

However, the plastic chassis only offers an IP65 rating despite some marketing claiming IP69, and the Dimensity 7360 chipset starts to throttle and warm up during long gaming sessions. The camera app also feels a bit sluggish at launch, and a charging limit bug can stop the charge at 100 % unexpectedly. Overall the phone feels like a solid battery‑first device with a few compromises in build quality and high‑performance use.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 6500 mAh battery is rated very highly by owners and can easily last a full day of mixed usage. Fast 90 W charging refills the cell in under an hour, so you rarely run out of power.

Can the Vivo V60 Lite 5G handle heavy gaming?

The phone manages casual games well but the Dimensity 7360 chip tends to throttle and become warm during long sessions. Gaming scores are low, so it is not ideal for demanding titles.

Is the camera good in low light and does the app respond quickly?

The 50 MP sensor shines in daylight and portrait mode, and the Aura light feature helps in bright conditions. In low light the results are modest, and the camera app launch feels a bit slow for some users.

What warranty and software updates does the official model receive?

An official Bangladeshi unit comes with the brand's warranty and can use the local service network. The device runs Funtouch 15 on Android 15 and receives two major OS updates for budget models, with occasional bug fixes such as the charging limit issue.
