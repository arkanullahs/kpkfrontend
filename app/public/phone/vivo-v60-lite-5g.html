Vivo V60 Lite 5G Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can last all day on a single charge, the Vivo V60 Lite 5G delivers that, but expect compromises in gaming and high‑end performance.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want long battery life, a bright display and decent photos without needing top‑tier gaming performance.
Consider alternatives if
You need a phone that can handle heavy gaming or you expect an IP69 water‑dust rating.

What works well4

  • 6.77 inch AMOLED display with 120 Hz refresh
  • 50 MP main camera with strong portrait colours
  • 90 W fast charging fills 6500 mAh battery quickly
  • 12 GB RAM and up to 512 GB storage

Reported trade-offs3

  • Dimensity 7360 overheats and drops frames in games
  • Camera app feels sluggish
  • Plastic body only has IP65 rating

Why we say that

The 6.77‑inch AMOLED panel with 120 Hz refresh feels smooth and bright, while the 50 MP main camera produces good portrait colours. The 12 GB RAM and up to 512 GB storage keep daily apps responsive, and 90 W fast charging refills the 6500 mAh battery quickly. Owners praise the battery endurance and display quality, but many note that the Dimensity 7360 Turbo chipset overheats and drops frames during extended gaming.

The camera app is also reported as sluggish, and the plastic body only carries an IP65 rating, not the higher IP69 some expected. Software runs Android 15 with Funtouch 15, stable but with some bloatware and occasional ads. At the official price of ৳41,499, the phone feels overpriced for its performance level.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 6500 mAh battery gives industry‑leading endurance, easily lasting a full day of normal use such as browsing, social media and video streaming. Heavy gaming will drain it faster, but most users will still get through the day without charging.

Will the phone handle mobile games smoothly?

The Dimensity 7360 Turbo chipset handles everyday apps well but shows its limits in demanding games. Users report heat buildup and occasional frame drops, so the phone is okay for casual play but not for intensive gaming sessions.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor delivers strong portrait colours and decent detail, and the 32 MP selfie camera is sharp. However, the camera app feels sluggish and low‑light performance is not exceptional, so it may fall short for night photography.
