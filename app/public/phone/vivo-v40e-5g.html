Is the Vivo V40e 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.77 inch AMOLED 120Hz display
  • 80W wired fast charging
  • 50MP main and selfie cameras
  • Dimensity 7300 handles games well

What is not

  • Shop warranty instead of brand warranty
  • Gray import stock may lack official support
  • Medium amount of pre installed ads
  • Only two major OS updates expected

Buy it if Buy it if you need a big, fast screen and decent camera at a mid range price.

Skip it if Skip it if you require official brand warranty or the latest software updates.

Why we say that

The 6.77 inch AMOLED panel runs at 120Hz, making scrolling and gaming feel fluid. The 5500 mAh battery easily lasts a full day of mixed use and the 80W charger refills it in about half an hour. The Dimensity 7300 chipset delivers solid performance for most games and everyday apps.

The 50MP main sensor with OIS captures sharp photos in good light, while the 50MP selfie camera is also respectable. The software runs Android 14 with Funtouch 14, but it includes a medium amount of pre installed ads and only promises two major OS upgrades. The biggest practical issue is that only gray import stock is available, so you get a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty and you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5500 mAh battery usually lasts a full day with moderate use such as browsing, social media, and occasional video playback. Heavy gaming or constant video streaming will reduce the endurance but it still reaches most of the day.

Can the Vivo V40e 5G handle heavy gaming smoothly?

The Dimensity 7300 chipset together with 8GB RAM provides smooth performance in most modern games at medium settings. The 120Hz display adds fluidity, though the most demanding titles may need lower graphics options to stay stable.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The 50MP main sensor has optical image stabilization, which helps in low light, but the small 1/1.95 inch size limits how much light it can gather. Results are acceptable but not as bright or detailed as premium flagships.

Only gray import stock is found, so the phone comes with a shop warranty rather than an official Vivo brand warranty. The device is expected to receive two major Android version updates, and you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchase.
