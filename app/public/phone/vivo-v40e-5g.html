Vivo V40e 5G Is it worth buying?

If you want a big, fast 5G phone with a strong camera without paying flagship prices, the Vivo V40e 5G delivers that.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large, fast 5G phone with a strong camera at a mid‑range price.
Consider alternatives if
You need a guaranteed brand warranty or want minimal software clutter.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Smooth 120 Hz AMOLED display
  • Fast Dimensity 7300 performance
  • High‑resolution 50 MP camera with OIS
  • Large 5500 mAh battery

Reported trade-offs3

  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Potential bloatware and ads
  • Updates may stop after two Android versions

Why we say that

The 6.77‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz, making scrolling and gaming feel fluid. The Dimensity 7300 paired with 8 GB RAM handles everyday apps and most games without stutter. The 50 MP main sensor with OIS produces sharp photos in good light and acceptable results in low‑light scenes.

The 5500 mAh battery easily lasts a full day of mixed use, and the 5G modem keeps you connected. Owners often note the lack of an official manufacturer warranty because the phone is only sold as gray‑import stock, so you rely on a shop warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status. Some complain about medium‑level bloatware and ads in Funtouch OS, and budget models may stop receiving updates after two Android versions.
