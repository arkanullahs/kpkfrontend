Motorola Edge 50 Is it worth buying?

The Motorola Edge 50 is a stylish device that prioritizes build quality and display performance over raw processing power.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a durable, lightweight phone with a high-quality display and do not mind the risks of a gray-import device.
Consider alternatives if
You are a heavy gamer or a mobile photographer who needs a lag-free camera and consistent performance under pressure.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Excellent 6.7 inch 120Hz P-OLED display
  • Durable build with IP68 rating
  • Fast 68W charging support
  • Lightweight and thin design

Reported trade-offs4

  • Noticeable heating and thermal throttling
  • Camera app lag and slow lens switching
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Limited OS update support

Why we say that

Owners frequently report that the phone gets noticeably hot under load and suffers from thermal throttling. While the 6.7 inch P-OLED display is vivid and bright, the camera experience is hampered by app lag and slow lens switching. You will find a near-stock software experience, but users have noted occasional bugs and limited OS update support compared to expectations.

The 5000 mAh battery provides about 8 hours of screen-on time, which is reliable for daily tasks. Because this device is only available as a gray-import in Bangladesh, you must accept that there is no manufacturer warranty. Repairs are paid and depend entirely on the specific shop where you purchase the unit.

Does the Motorola Edge 50 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as a gray-import in Bangladesh. There is no manufacturer warranty provided by the brand. Any coverage is limited to what the individual shop promises, and all repairs must be paid for by the owner.

How long will the Motorola Edge 50 receive software updates?

Owners report that the device is limited to 2 major OS updates and 3 years of security updates. This is considered a weak point for long-term software support compared to other modern smartphones.

Is the Motorola Edge 50 good for gaming?

The phone is not ideal for gaming, as owners rate its gaming performance at 5.5 out of 10. Users report that the device gets noticeably hot under load and suffers from thermal throttling during intensive tasks.

How is the battery life on this device?

The 5000 mAh battery is rated 7.5 out of 10 by owners. In real-world usage, it provides approximately 8 hours of screen-on time. You can recharge the battery quickly using the 68W fast charging feature.
