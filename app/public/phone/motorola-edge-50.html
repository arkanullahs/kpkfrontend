Motorola Edge 50 Is it worth buying?

If you want a big, bright screen and a decent battery without paying a premium, the Motorola Edge 50 gray import fits the bill.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large bright screen, solid battery and water resistance at a low gray import price and can accept limited camera speed and no brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch OLED panel reaches 1600 nits peak and runs at 120Hz, making everyday use feel smooth. Battery life is generally good, with many users seeing more than half a charge after light browsing, though some report gradual capacity loss over months. The phone is IP68 rated, so it survives water and dust, and the Snapdragon 7 Gen 1 handles routine apps without trouble.

Owners love the display and the sturdy feel, but they are disappointed by a laggy camera app, weak telephoto performance, and sluggish gaming and overall performance scores. The lack of an official manufacturer warranty means you rely on the shop’s limited cover and must confirm IMEI and BTRC status before purchase. Software is near stock Android 14 with up to five major updates, but past update speed has been slow.

Will the battery last a full day of normal use?

The 5000 mAh battery generally gives good endurance and many owners report more than half a charge left after light browsing and video calls. Some users notice gradual capacity loss, so heavy use may not stay full day.

How does the phone perform in gaming?

Gaming scores are modest with owners rating gaming around 4.5 out of 10. The Snapdragon 7 Gen 1 runs casual titles smoothly, but demanding games feel laggy and frame rates drop, reflecting the lower performance rating.

Is the camera reliable in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor captures bright photos in daylight, but owners complain about a laggy camera app and poor telephoto results. Low‑light shots are usable, yet the slow response and weak zoom make it less reliable for night photography.

What warranty and support can I expect for a gray import?

All listings are gray imports, so there is no official Motorola warranty in Bangladesh. The shop you buy from may offer its own limited warranty, but repairs are paid and you should verify the IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.
