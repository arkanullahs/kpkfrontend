Is the POCO C55 worth buying?

What is good

  • Efficient 5000mAh battery life
  • Good 50MP main camera tuning
  • Solid build quality
  • Includes a headphone jack and FM radio

What is not

  • Gyroscope is not supported
  • Poor gaming and general performance
  • Low display quality
  • Only shop warranty available

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a basic device for calls and photography on a strict budget and do not care about gaming or advanced sensors.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a gamer, need a reliable gyroscope for navigation or apps, or want the security of an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

While the build quality is considered good by owners, the user experience is hampered by significant performance limitations. Owners report a low gaming score of 2.5 and a display rating of 4.0, noting that the screen lacks a high refresh rate. You must be aware that the gyroscope does not work, which affects certain apps and games.

Since this is a 2023 release, you should expect little to no remaining software support. All units currently available are gray-import stock, meaning you will receive a shop warranty rather than a brand warranty. You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before finalizing your purchase to ensure the device is legitimate.

Does the POCO C55 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there is no official brand warranty available for this device. All units currently found in the market are gray-import stock, which means you will only receive a shop warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

Is the POCO C55 suitable for playing mobile games?

The phone is not recommended for gaming. Owners have rated the gaming performance at 2.5 out of 10. The Helio G85 chipset struggles with demanding tasks, and the lack of a working gyroscope further limits your ability to play many popular titles.

How long will the POCO C55 receive software updates?

This phone was released in 2023 and is already three years old. You should expect a very short or non-existent remaining software update life at this point. It currently runs on Android 12 and MIUI 13 for POCO, with some units featuring HyperOS.

How is the battery performance of this phone?

The 5000mAh battery is considered efficient by users. It holds a rating of 7.0 out of 10 from owners. However, keep in mind that the device only supports 10W charging, so it will take a significant amount of time to reach a full charge.
