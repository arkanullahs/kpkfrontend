Why we say that

It offers a decent 50MP main camera and a build that owners describe as good. However, the daily experience is mixed. Owners rate the display and performance poorly, with a performance score of 4.0 and a display score of 4.0.

Gaming is a major struggle as it only scores 2.5 in that category. You should know that the gyroscope does not work on this phone. The software is heavy with ads and bloatware.

Read full reasoning

Since it was released in 2023, it is now 3 years old. You should expect very little to no remaining software update life. You must check the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying.

What is the POCO C55 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for POCO C55 in Bangladesh was ৳10,490, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official brand warranty in Bangladesh?

No, every listing we have recorded is a gray import. You will only get a shop warranty instead of brand warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

Is the POCO C55 good for gaming?

No, it is not good for gaming. Owners have given it a very low gaming rating of 2.5 out of 10. The performance rating is also low at 4.0.

How is the battery life on this device?

The battery life is a strength of this phone. It has a 5000 mAh battery that reviews describe as efficient. Owners gave the battery a rating of 7.0.

Will I get software updates for a long time?

Probably not. The phone is 3 years old. You should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point.
