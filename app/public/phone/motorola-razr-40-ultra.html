Buying verdict

Motorola Razr 40 Ultra Is it worth buying?

Buying this phone is a high risk due to reports of critical hardware failures.

Choose it if
You want a foldable with a top tier display and can risk the hardware issues.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable device with official warranty and long term software support.

What works well

  • Excellent 165Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • Strong performance with Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1
  • Low bloatware and ads
  • Durable build quality

Reported trade-offs

  • Reports of burnt charging IC
  • Faulty capacitors causing total shutdown
  • Slow charging and average battery
  • No official brand warranty

Why we say that

While the 165Hz LTPO AMOLED display is excellent and performance is strong with the Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1, owners have reported serious motherboard issues. Some users experienced burnt charging ICs that made the phone useless. Others reported faulty capacitors causing the device to shut off completely within days of purchase.

The battery life is only average and charging is slow. Since this is a 2023 model, you should expect very little remaining software update life. You will only find gray import stock in Bangladesh, which means you get shop warranty instead of brand support.

The phone was released in 2023 and is now 3 years old. Security updates are expected to end in June 2027, and the last OS update is Android 15.
