Is the Samsung Galaxy F07 worth buying?

What is good

  • 5000 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • 50 MP main camera captures decent daylight photos
  • 25 W fast charging refills quickly
  • 90 Hz display smooth for basic use

What is not

  • Plastic build feels cheap
  • Storage speed is slow
  • Only 4 GB RAM limits updates
  • Outdated notch design

Buy it if Buy it if you need a large‑battery phone on a tight budget and can accept basic performance and a plastic body.

Skip it if Skip it if you rely on heavy gaming, high‑quality video, or an official Samsung warranty.

Why we say that

Owners love the 5000 mAh cell that can stretch to two days thanks to the efficient Helio G99, and they appreciate the 25 W charger that refills the phone quickly. The 6.7‑inch 90 Hz PLS LCD works fine for everyday scrolling, yet the outdated notch and modest panel quality feel dated. The 4 GB RAM and slow storage struggle with the promised six Android upgrades, and the basic plastic chassis is often called a copy of older models.

Gaming runs okay on light titles but heavy games feel throttled. Video stabilization is weak, and the camera, while bright in daylight, does not excel in low light. There is no official Samsung warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited guarantee and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
