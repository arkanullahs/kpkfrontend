Why we say that

The 6.78 inch LTPO OLED can reach a peak of 5000 nits, making outdoor visibility easy. Performance feels flagship thanks to Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 and up to 16 GB RAM, and gaming stays smooth for long sessions. Build quality is premium with a metal frame and IP68 rating, but the phone arrives as a gray import so there is no official brand warranty and any repair will cost you.

The main camera captures sharp daylight photos, yet low‑light scenes appear darker and colors shift, and the 4K video output feels soft. MagicOS runs Android 14 and works, but occasional Chinese language prompts and medium bloat remain. Overall the device offers high-end specs at a lower price, but you must accept limited after‑sales support and camera compromises in dim light.
