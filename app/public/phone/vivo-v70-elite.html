Is the Vivo V70 Elite worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6500 mAh battery with 90 W fast charge
  • Bright 6.59‑inch AMOLED display with 120 Hz refresh
  • Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 delivers strong performance
  • Zeiss‑tuned 50 MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Heavy bloatware and ads in OriginOS
  • No gyroscope limits gaming sensors
  • Ultrawide video capped at 1080 p 30 fps
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large battery, fast charging and a premium display and can tolerate software bloat and the lack of an official warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable gaming performance, eSIM support or a clean Android experience.

Why we say that

Its 6500 mAh battery easily lasts 1.5‑2 days and refills in half an hour with 90 W fast charging. The 6.59‑inch AMOLED panel shines brightly at a 120 Hz refresh rate, making media consumption pleasant even under sunlight. Under the hood, the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 provides snappy everyday performance, and the Zeiss‑tuned 50 MP main camera produces good portraits, though the ultrawide lens is weak in low light and capped at 1080 p 30 fps.

Owners complain about heavy bloatware and ads in OriginOS, the absence of a gyroscope and eSIM, and noticeable heating during long gaming sessions. The phone arrives as a gray‑import with only the shop’s limited warranty, so any repair costs are out‑of‑pocket. Overall it feels premium but its software quirks and lack of official support may deter some buyers.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The Vivo V70 Elite packs a 6500 mAh battery that typically lasts between one and a half to two days of normal use, and it can be refilled from zero to full in about 30‑40 minutes with the included 90 W charger.

Is the phone good for gaming?

Performance is snappy thanks to the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3, but long gaming sessions cause noticeable heating and the lack of a gyroscope means motion‑based games feel limited, making it less comfortable for serious gamers.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor with Zeiss tuning and OIS captures good portraits even in dim light, but the ultrawide lens struggles in low‑light conditions and the video is limited to 1080 p at 30 fps.

Will I get official warranty and software updates?

All units are gray‑imported, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s limited warranty and paid repairs. The phone ships with Android 16 and OriginOS 6 and promises up to four major Android version upgrades.
