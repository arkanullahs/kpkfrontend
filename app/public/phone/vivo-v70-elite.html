Is the Vivo V70 Elite worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6500 mAh battery capacity
  • Fast Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 performance
  • Bright 5000 nits peak AMOLED display
  • High quality 50MP main camera

What is not

  • Excessive and frustrating bloatware
  • Significant heating during gaming
  • AI-induced camera shutter lag
  • No official brand warranty

Buy it if You prioritize raw battery life and performance above all else and are comfortable managing an unofficial device.

Skip it if You want a clean software experience, official brand support, or a phone that does not overheat during gaming.

Why we say that

Owners consistently praise the 6500 mAh battery life, which is the standout feature of the device. However, living with the phone is difficult due to excessive bloatware in OriginOS 6, which many users describe as a deal-breaker. While the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset provides fast raw performance, gamers report significant heating issues during sessions of COD.

The camera system captures high-quality images, yet users frequently complain about shutter lag caused by AI processing. Because this phone is only available through unofficial gray-import channels, you will not receive a brand warranty, only a shop warranty. You must also be prepared for the lack of eSIM support and an aesthetic that some find unappealing.
