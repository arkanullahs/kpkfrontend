Buying verdict

OPPO A5 Pro Is it worth buying?

The OPPO A5 Pro is a durable device with exceptional battery life, but it suffers from significant performance and display limitations that make it hard to recommend at ৳22,799.

Choose it if
You prioritize extreme physical durability and long battery life over display sharpness and gaming performance.
Consider alternatives if
You want a sharp screen for media consumption or need a phone that handles multitasking and gaming without lag.

What works well

  • Massive 5800 mAh battery lasts two days
  • Extremely durable IP69 shock-proof build
  • Premium vegan leather back design
  • Efficient 45W SuperVOOC charging included

Reported trade-offs

  • Unacceptable 720p display resolution
  • Laggy performance during multitasking
  • Grainy low-light camera quality
  • Poor value for the price

Why we say that

Owners praise the tank-like build quality, noting the IP69 rating and shock-proof design as standout features. However, the user experience is marred by a 720p display that lacks sharpness, which many find unacceptable for a 2025 release. While the 5800 mAh battery easily lasts up to two days, the Dimensity 6300 chipset struggles with multitasking and heavy gaming.

Users frequently report system lag during video calls and grainy low-light photography that resembles poor webcam quality. Although ColorOS 15 provides a smooth interface, the presence of bloatware and the outdated screen resolution lead many owners to feel the phone offers poor value for the price.

Does the OPPO A5 Pro have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

Yes, the phone is available as an official BD unit. This means you are covered by the brand's own warranty and their official service network when you purchase it for ৳22,799.

Is the battery life on the OPPO A5 Pro actually good?

The battery life is excellent according to owner ratings. The massive 5800 mAh battery is reported to last between 1.5 to 2 days on a single charge, making it one of the strongest features of the device.

Can I use the OPPO A5 Pro for heavy gaming?

The phone is not suitable for heavy gaming. Owners report that the Dimensity 6300 chipset struggles with performance, and the device experiences lag during multitasking, which makes it a poor choice for demanding games.

How is the camera performance in low light?

The camera performance in low light is poor. Owners describe the output as grainy, noting that shadows often contain visible noise and the overall quality is comparable to a low-end laptop webcam.
