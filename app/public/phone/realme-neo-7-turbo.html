Buying verdict

Realme Neo7 Turbo Is it worth buying?

Battery life is the phone’s biggest advantage, with a 7200 mAh cell that most owners say lasts between one and a half and two days of normal use.

Choose it if
You need massive battery life, a bright fast screen, and can tolerate a China ROM and no official warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on Google apps, need reliable low‑light photos, or cannot accept overheating and lack of brand warranty.

What works well

  • 7200 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • 6.8‑inch AMOLED with 120 Hz and 6500‑nit peak
  • Dimensity 9400e with 12 GB RAM delivers fast performance
  • 100 W wired charging fills the huge cell quickly

Reported trade-offs

  • China ROM drops Google services and causes notification glitches
  • Phone overheats above 50 °C and may crash
  • Camera struggles in low light and lacks telephoto
  • No official brand warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

The 6.8‑inch AMOLED screen is bright, fast and has narrow bezels, making media consumption pleasant. Under the hood, the Dimensity 9400e paired with 12 GB RAM delivers fast app launches and good gaming scores, but the chipset runs hot, often exceeding 50 °C and causing occasional crashes. The camera produces decent daylight shots but lacks depth sensors and struggles in low‑light scenes, delivering only average results.

Software runs a China‑based Realme UI that omits Google services, leading to frequent notification failures and noticeable bloatware. There is no official brand warranty; only the shop’s limited coverage applies, and any repair will cost extra. Overall, the phone feels powerful and long‑lasting but requires tolerance for overheating, software quirks, and the lack of official support.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.
