Is the OPPO Find X9s worth buying?

What is good

  • 7025 mAh battery lasting up to two days
  • Dimensity 9500s delivers smooth gaming
  • 6.59 inch AMOLED with 3600 nit peak brightness
  • 80 W fast charging refills the large battery quickly

What is not

  • No wireless charging
  • Missing LTPO and stereo speakers
  • Camera sensors downgraded, warm tones
  • Shop warranty only, not brand

Buy it if Buy it if you need a huge battery and solid gaming performance and can accept compromises on camera quality and warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect flagship camera quality, wireless charging, or an official OPPO warranty.

Why we say that

It packs a massive 7025 mAh cell that most owners say lasts between one and a half to two days of normal use, and the 80 W charger fills it quickly. The Dimensity 9500s chipset gives smooth performance in games and everyday apps, though it is a bit underclocked compared with the older X9. The 6.59 inch AMOLED screen is bright enough for outdoor use with a 3600 nit peak, but it uses PWM dimming and lacks LTPO and stereo speakers, which many buyers notice.

The camera hardware is a step down from flagship level, producing warm tones and missing a colour lens, so low‑light shots are only passable. Software runs ColorOS 16 on Android 16 with medium bloat and about three to four major updates expected, but update longevity worries remain. The phone is sold as a gray import, so warranty comes from the shop, not OPPO, and you must verify IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.
