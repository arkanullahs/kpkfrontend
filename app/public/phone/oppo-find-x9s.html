Why we say that

The Find X9s feels solid in hand and its 7025 mAh silicon‑carbon battery really lasts, matching the high owner rating of 9.8. The 6.59‑inch AMOLED screen can hit 3600 nits, so outdoor visibility is excellent, though the LTPS panel does not have LTPO efficiency and can drain more in always‑on mode. Performance is smooth thanks to the Dimensity 9500s, and the 80W wired charger refills quickly, but the lack of any wireless charging is noticeable.

The camera delivers consistent 50 MP results with natural tones, yet it falls short of the flagship X9 hardware and struggles in very low light. The phone arrives as a gray‑import built for India, so there is no official brand warranty, only the shop’s limited promise, and the Gorilla Glass 7i surface scratches more easily than expected.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 7025 mAh silicon‑carbon battery gives about two days of normal use, and owners report genuine endurance that matches the 9.8 rating. Heavy gaming or bright screen settings will reduce that time.

Is the Find X9s suitable for gaming?

Yes, the Dimensity 9500s chip and its GPU keep frame rates steady in most games, earning an 8.5 rating from owners. The phone may not hit the absolute top speeds of the X9, but it feels smooth for most titles.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP triple camera produces natural tones in good light, but in low‑light scenes it lacks the top‑tier hardware of the X9, so images can be softer and noise may appear.

What warranty and software update support does the Find X9s have in Bangladesh?

All units are gray‑imports built for India, so there is no official OPPO warranty in Bangladesh. Shops we track offer their own limited warranty and repairs are paid. ColorOS 16 will receive three to four Android version updates, with medium bloat and ads.
