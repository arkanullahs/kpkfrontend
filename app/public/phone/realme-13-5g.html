Realme 13 5G Is it worth buying?

Battery life will be the first thing you notice missing, because the 5000 mAh cell empties faster than most users expect.

Reported trade-offs4

  • Battery drains quickly
  • Display brightness only 580 nits
  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Realme UI shows medium‑high ads

Why we say that

The phone feels solid with a 6.72‑inch IPS LCD that refreshes at 120 Hz, but its typical peak brightness of 580 nits makes outdoor use dim. The Dimensity 6300 chipset delivers smooth everyday performance and the 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures decent photos, though low‑light results are only average. The 16 MP selfie camera is adequate and the headphone jack is a rare convenience.

Android 14 with Realme UI 5.0 runs fine, but you will see medium‑high ads and only two to three major OS updates. All listings are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited cover and must pay for any repairs. Overall the phone offers good speed and camera for its price, but expect to carry a charger or power bank.

Is there an official Realme warranty for the Realme 13 5G in Bangladesh?

No official Realme warranty is available because every unit is a gray import. The phone is covered only by the shop’s limited warranty and any repair costs must be paid by the buyer. Verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

How long will the battery last in daily use?

The phone has a 5000 mAh battery, but owners report it drains quickly and often needs a power bank after a few hours of moderate use. Expect less than a full day of heavy usage without charging.

Is the Realme 13 5G good for gaming?

The Dimensity 6300 chipset and 8‑12 GB of RAM give decent performance and a 120 Hz screen helps smooth gameplay, but the battery’s fast drain limits long gaming sessions and you may need to recharge frequently.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures decent photos, but owners describe the low‑light results as only average and not as good as higher‑end models.
