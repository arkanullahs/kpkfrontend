Is the Realme 14T 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts two days
  • 2100 nits AMOLED screen
  • IP68/IP69 water resistance
  • Large 6.67 inch display

What is not

  • No 3.5 mm headphone jack
  • Thick bottom bezel
  • Poor low light camera performance
  • Gaming slows due to budget chipset

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, a bright rugged screen and can accept modest performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you want fast gaming, excellent night photos or a sleek bezel free design.

Why we say that

Its 6000 mAh battery easily lasts one to two days and the 45 W charger refills it in under two hours. The 6.67 inch AMOLED panel reaches a peak brightness of 2100 nits, making outdoor viewing easy, though the bottom bezel is noticeably thick. The phone is rated IP68/IP69, so water and dust do not pose a problem.

Under the hood sits a Dimensity 6300 chipset with 8 GB RAM, which delivers acceptable everyday speed but creates bottlenecks when the system is taxed, especially in games. The 50 MP main sensor lacks optical image stabilization and shows noise in dim environments, and the software adds noticeable bloatware and ads. Realme promises Android 15 and up to three major OS upgrades, but the overall value feels stretched for the price.
