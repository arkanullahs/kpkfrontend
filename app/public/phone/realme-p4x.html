Is the Realme P4x worth buying?

What is good

  • 7000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 45 W fast charging
  • Dimensity 7400 Ultra smooth mid range performance
  • 6.72 inch 144 Hz high refresh display

What is not

  • Thick bezels look dated
  • IPS LCD lacks deep blacks
  • No NFC support
  • Shop warranty only

Buy it if Buy if you need massive battery life and decent performance at a budget price.

Skip it if Skip if you want a sleek design, AMOLED screen, or NFC for mobile payments.

Why we say that

Owners love the massive battery and the 45 W charging that refills it quickly, and the Dimensity 7400 Ultra chipset feels smooth for everyday apps and casual games. The 6.72‑inch 144 Hz IPS LCD is bright with 1000‑nit peak, yet the lack of AMOLED contrast and the thick bezels draw criticism. Camera scores are modest at 5 out of 10, and the 8 MP selfie shooter does not impress in low light.

Realme UI 6.0 runs Android 15 but includes typical budget‑tier ads and bloatware. The phone arrives as a gray‑import unit, meaning you get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty and you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller. Overall it suits users who prize stamina and price over polish.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 7000 mAh battery is rated 9.6 out of 10 and is described as a battery monster. In typical daily use it can easily cover a full day of screen time, social media, and video streaming, and the 45 W charger tops it up in about an hour.

Is the Realme P4x suitable for gaming?

The Dimensity 7400 Ultra built on a 4 nm process delivers smooth mid range performance. Owner ratings give gaming a 6.8 out of 10, meaning casual and some demanding titles run well, though you may not hit the highest frame rates on very graphics‑intensive games.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The main sensor is a 50 MP f/1.8 lens but camera owners rate it only 5 out of 10. Low‑light shots are usable but lack detail and suffer from noise, while the 8 MP selfie camera does not brighten dark scenes effectively.
