Realme P4x Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run games at 90 frames per second and last two days on a single charge, the Realme P4x delivers that.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life and high frame gaming over NFC and camera polish.

What works well4

  • 7000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • Dimensity 7400 Ultra handles high frame gaming
  • 45 W fast charging refills quickly
  • Bright 1000 nit display is visible outdoors

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Software shows medium high ads
  • Thick bezels reduce screen immersion
  • Camera lacks advanced processing

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh battery gives industry leading endurance and the 45 W charger refills it in under two hours. The Dimensity 7400 Ultra chipset provides smooth performance for everyday apps and keeps high frame rates stable thanks to its VC cooling system. The 6.72 inch IPS LCD shines at 1000 nits and refreshes at 144 Hz, but the panel is LCD and the bezels are noticeably thick, which reduces the premium feel.

The 50 MP main sensor captures decent photos in good light but lacks advanced processing, so low light results are average. Realme UI 6.0 runs Android 15 but includes medium high ads and bloatware that some users find intrusive. The phone arrives as a gray import built for India, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s limited warranty applies and any repair will cost extra.

How long does the battery last on normal daily use?

The 7000 mAh cell is rated for industry leading endurance. Most owners report it easily covers a full day of heavy use and can stretch to two days with moderate usage before needing a recharge.

Can the Realme P4x run demanding games smoothly?

The Dimensity 7400 Ultra chipset together with VC cooling keeps frame rates stable. Owners see 90 FPS gaming without major throttling, and the 144 Hz LCD refresh helps keep motion fluid.

Is the camera good in low light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor captures decent detail in bright scenes but it does not have advanced processing. In low light the pictures become soft and lack the clarity seen on higher priced rivals.

Will I get any official warranty or regular software updates?

All units are gray imports built for India, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop offers its own limited warranty and repairs cost extra. Realme UI 6.0 promises two to three major Android updates.
