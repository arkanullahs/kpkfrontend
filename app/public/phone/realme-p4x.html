Is the Realme P4x worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 7000 mAh battery capacity
  • Stable 90FPS gaming performance
  • Fluid 144Hz IPS LCD display
  • Bypass charging for gamers

What is not

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Large and unattractive bottom bezels
  • Lack of NFC support
  • Budget-tier bloatware and ads

Buy it if Buy this phone if you are a dedicated gamer who prioritizes long battery life and stable frame rates over display technology and premium design.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you require NFC, prefer the vibrant colors of an AMOLED screen, or need the security of an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

With a massive 7000 mAh battery and bypass charging, it is a reliable companion for long gaming sessions, though the experience is marred by a plastic build and thick bottom bezels that users find quite unattractive. Performance is stable for gaming, yet the device lacks NFC and relies on an IPS LCD panel rather than the AMOLED screens found in competitors. Because this is an unofficial gray-import device, you will not receive any manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh.

Repairs are entirely at your own expense, and you must rely solely on the promises made by the individual shop. Owners frequently express frustration regarding the chunky bezels and the absence of NFC, which limits its utility for some users. While the 144Hz refresh rate is smooth, the overall package feels like a niche choice for gamers who value battery life above all else.

Does the Realme P4x come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as a gray-import device. There is no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh regardless of the brand's service reputation. You must rely on the specific shop for any coverage, and all repairs will be paid.

How does the battery perform for heavy users?

The battery is the standout feature of this device, earning a 9.7 rating from owners. It features a massive 7000 mAh capacity and includes bypass charging, which allows gamers to power the phone directly without stressing the battery during long sessions.

Is the Realme P4x suitable for playing games like BGMI or COD?

Yes, the phone is well-suited for gaming. It is powered by the Dimensity 7400 Ultra chipset and includes VC cooling, which allows for a stable 90FPS experience in titles like BGMI and COD. Owners rate the gaming performance at 8.0.

How many software updates will this phone receive?

The Realme P4x runs on Android 15 with Realme UI 6.0. You can expect to receive between 2 to 3 OS updates over the life of the device. Be aware that the software includes a medium to high amount of bloatware and ads.
