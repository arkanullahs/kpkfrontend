What works well

  • Massive 6000 mAh battery capacity
  • Clean near-stock Hello UI software
  • 50MP main camera with OIS
  • Smooth 120Hz display refresh rate

Reported trade-offs

  • No official brand warranty support
  • Historically weak software update track record
  • Only available as gray-import stock

Why we say that

It runs on the Dimensity 7025 chipset and offers a clean software experience with Hello UI, which is very close to stock Android. You get a 120Hz IPS LCD display that makes scrolling feel smooth. The 50MP main camera includes OIS, which is a helpful addition for steady shots.

However, you must be careful because this phone is only available as a gray-import unit in the market. There is no official brand warranty available for this device in Bangladesh. You will have to rely on shop warranties provided by the seller.

Read full reasoning

Always check the IMEI and BTRC status before you pay any money. The software support history for this brand is historically weak, even though it is planned to receive an upgrade to Android 15. It is a functional choice for those who prioritize battery life and a clean interface over official support.

Motorola G64: what buyers ask us

What is the Motorola G64 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Motorola G64 in Bangladesh was ৳18,490, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does the Motorola G64 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there is no official brand-backed warranty for this phone in Bangladesh. All units currently found in the market are gray-imports. You will only receive a shop warranty from the seller, so you must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status before purchasing.

How long will the Motorola G64 receive software updates?

The phone currently runs on Android 14 and has a planned upgrade to Android 15. Historically, the brand has a weak track record for long-term software updates, with support typically ranging between one to three operating system versions.

Is the Motorola G64 good for taking photos in low light?

The phone features a 50MP main camera with an f/1.8 aperture and OIS. Optical Image Stabilization helps keep shots steady, which is beneficial for low light photography, though performance will depend on the specific lighting conditions you encounter.

Who is the Motorola G64 best suited for?

This phone is suited for users who want a device with a 6000 mAh battery and a near-stock software experience. It is a practical choice for those who do not mind using gray-import units and are comfortable verifying the device status with their seller.
