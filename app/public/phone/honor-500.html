Honor 500 Is it worth buying?

If you can live with a Chinese‑ROM phone that may miss some local 5G bands, the Honor 500 gives you a massive battery and a smooth display.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 8000 mAh battery with excellent endurance
  • 6.55‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED with 6000‑nit peak brightness
  • Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 delivers strong performance
  • Premium aluminum‑glass build with IP69K protection

Reported trade-offs4

  • No manufacturer warranty, only shop warranty
  • China‑ROM may lack Google services
  • Missing key 4G/5G bands for Bangladesh
  • Low‑light camera performance is weak

Why we say that

The 8000 mAh cell provides all‑day endurance and the 80 W charger refills it quickly. The 6.55‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED is bright enough for outdoor use and protects the eyes. Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 keeps performance and gaming stable, and the premium aluminum‑glass body feels solid with IP69K water resistance.

Owners love the battery and screen but complain about missing Google services, network band gaps, low‑light camera weakness and the lack of an official warranty. The phone feels like a flagship in many ways, yet the software quirks and band issues can be frustrating in daily Bangladeshi use.

How long will the battery last in daily use?

The Honor 500 packs an 8000 mAh cell, and reviewers note industry‑leading endurance that easily covers a full day of mixed use. You can recharge with the included 80 W charger in under an hour.

Is the phone suitable for heavy mobile gaming?

Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 powers the Honor 500 and owners rate performance at 8.7 and gaming at 8.6. The chipset stays cool and delivers stable frame rates, making it comfortable for most demanding games.

How does the camera perform in low‑light situations?

The 200 MP main sensor captures good detail in daylight, but reviewers say low‑light shots are noisy and lack sharpness. The phone also omits a telephoto lens, limiting zoom options in dim environments.

Will the phone have Google services and an official warranty?

All units sold here are gray‑import China‑ROM models that do not include Google Mobile Services by default. You must verify GMS works, and the only warranty is the shop’s limited coverage, not a brand warranty.
