Is the Honor 500 worth buying?

What is good

  • 8000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 6.55 inch AMOLED with 120 Hz refresh
  • Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 gives flagship performance
  • 200 MP main camera records 4K video

What is not

  • No official brand warranty
  • Missing 4G band 20 limits some networks
  • China only ROM may lack Google services
  • No eSIM support

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize long battery life, high refresh display and flagship gaming performance and can handle a gray import without official warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need guaranteed Google apps, eSIM or full 4G band coverage.

Why we say that

The 6.55 inch AMOLED screen shines at 120 Hz and can reach a peak of 6000 nits, making outdoor use easy. Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 paired with 12 GB RAM gives fluid performance in games and everyday apps, and the 200 MP main sensor records 4K video at 60 fps. Owners praise the battery life and display brightness, but they often complain about the lack of an official warranty, the missing 4G band 20, and the fact that many units run a China only ROM without Google services.

The phone also does not support eSIM and software updates are limited to three or four major versions. Check the IMEI and confirm Google Play works before buying a gray import unit.
