Is the Honor 500 worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 8000 mAh battery capacity
  • High-end Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 performance
  • Bright 6.55 inch AMOLED display
  • Capable 200MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Missing 4G Band 20 support
  • No eSIM functionality
  • China-exclusive software requires manual Google setup
  • Only gray-import stock with shop warranty

Buy it if You are a tech-savvy user who prioritizes raw battery life and gaming performance and knows how to manage China-market software.

Skip it if You need reliable network connectivity, official brand warranty, or a phone that works perfectly with Google services out of the box.

Why we say that

While the 8000 mAh battery is a genuine powerhouse that owners rate highly, the experience is marred by the reality of gray-import stock. You are buying a device that lacks official brand warranty support in Bangladesh, meaning you must rely on shop-provided coverage. Owners report significant frustration with the China-exclusive firmware, which requires extra effort to install Google services.

The absence of the B20 network band and eSIM support are major functional drawbacks for local users. While the Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset delivers fluid gaming and performance, the software interface and connectivity limitations make this a device for power users who are comfortable troubleshooting technical hurdles rather than someone looking for a plug-and-play experience.

Does the Honor 500 support official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is currently only available as gray-import stock. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, so you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before purchasing.

How does the battery perform for daily use?

The battery is a standout feature with an 8000 mAh capacity. Owners rate it very highly at 9.7 out of 10, describing it as a true powerhouse that easily handles heavy usage throughout the day.

Will I face issues with Google apps on this phone?

The experience depends on the specific build you purchase. Some units are China-exclusive and require manual installation of Google services, which can be difficult. You must verify that Google Mobile Services work on your specific unit before paying.

Is this phone suitable for mobile gaming?

Yes, the Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset provides high-end and fluid operation for gaming. Owners have given the gaming performance a solid rating of 8.5 out of 10, making it a strong choice for performance-focused users.
