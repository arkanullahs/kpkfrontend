What works well

  • Efficient 5800 mAh battery
  • Bright 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Includes an ir blaster
  • 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs

  • Reports of green and pink screen lines
  • Poor gaming and performance ratings
  • Limited remaining software updates
  • Medium amount of bloatware and ads

Why we say that

While the AMOLED display is bright and the 5800 mAh battery is efficient, the actual user experience is poor in key areas. Owners gave performance a 3.0 and gaming a 2.0 out of 10. You will likely struggle with lag during heavy tasks.

The camera is another weak point with a 5.0 rating and reports of line issues. Since this phone was released in 2023, it is now 3 years old. You should expect very little to no remaining software update life.

It is a risky choice for someone who wants a reliable long term device.
