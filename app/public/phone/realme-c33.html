Buying verdict

Realme C33 Is it worth buying?

Expect a big battery but very slow charging and frequent lag.

Choose it if
You need a cheap phone with a big battery and can tolerate slow charging and occasional performance hiccups.
Consider alternatives if
You want smooth gaming, reliable software updates, or an official warranty.

What works well

  • 50 MP main sensor
  • 5,000 mAh battery
  • Headphone jack
  • Large 6.5 inch screen

Reported trade-offs

  • 10 W Micro USB charging is very slow
  • Frequent lag and app freezes
  • No hardware gyroscope for games

Why we say that

The Realme C33 sells for around ৳13,000 on the cheapest listings, but it arrives without any manufacturer warranty, only the shop's promise of repair at your cost. Its 5,000 mAh cell can keep the screen on for most of the day, yet the 10 W Micro USB charger refills it at a snail's pace. The 6.5‑inch IPS LCD is bright enough for indoor use but struggles under direct sunlight and stays at a basic 60 Hz refresh rate.

The Unisoc Tiger T612 chipset with 3‑4 GB RAM feels sluggish, especially when opening apps or playing games that need a gyroscope, such as PUBG. The 50 MP rear camera sounds impressive on paper but suffers from poor software tuning and occasional app freezes. Software updates have added bloat and more lag, and at four years old the phone is unlikely to receive further official updates.
