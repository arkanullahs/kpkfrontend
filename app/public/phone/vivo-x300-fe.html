Is the Vivo X300 FE worth buying?

What is good

  • Phenomenal 6500mAh battery endurance
  • Stunning 5000 nit LTPO AMOLED display
  • Powerful Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 performance
  • Fast 90W wired and 40W wireless charging

What is not

  • Pathetic 8MP ultrawide camera
  • Outdated USB 2.0 port
  • Cluttered software with complex menus
  • Aluminum frame gets noticeably warm

Buy it if You prioritize raw performance and battery endurance above all else and are comfortable managing a gray-import device without official brand support.

Skip it if You need a reliable ultrawide camera for photography or expect a modern, bloat-free software experience with official manufacturer warranty coverage.

Why we say that

While the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset handles gaming with ease, the aluminum frame gets noticeably warm during heavy use. Owners are particularly vocal about the outdated USB 2.0 port, which feels like technology from the year 2000. The camera system is a mixed bag, pairing an excellent main sensor with a pathetic 8MP ultrawide lens that cannot even record in 4K.

Software is another pain point, as OriginOS 6 is described as cluttered with bloat and complex menus. Because this is strictly a gray-import device, you must accept that there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Repairs are entirely at your own expense, and you are relying solely on the shop for any support.

Does the Vivo X300 FE come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as a gray-import unit. There is no manufacturer warranty provided by the brand in Bangladesh. Any coverage is limited to what the specific shop promises, and all repairs must be paid for by the owner.

How does the battery perform during daily use?

Owners report phenomenal endurance from the 6500mAh battery. It is capable of lasting for 1.5 days on a single charge, even within the compact body design of the phone.

Is this phone suitable for heavy gaming?

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset is a beast that provides high frame rates for gaming. However, the aluminum frame does get noticeably warm during extended sessions, even though the internal vapor chamber works well to manage heat.

How long will the software be supported?

The device runs Android 16 and is promised up to 5 major Android upgrades. While the hardware is capable, users should be aware that the OriginOS 6 software is reported to be cluttered with bloat and complex menus.
