Is the Vivo X300 FE worth buying?

What is good

  • 5,000‑nit LTPO AMOLED display
  • 6,500 mAh battery lasting 1.5 days
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 flagship performance
  • 50 MP main camera with Zeiss tuning

What is not

  • USB‑C 2.0 port is outdated
  • Only 8 MP ultrawide camera
  • Gray‑import warranty only from shop
  • Heavy UI without Google preinstalled

Buy it if Buy it if you want a bright screen, long battery and flagship speed and can accept a gray‑import warranty and a heavier software experience.

Skip it if Skip it if you need official Vivo warranty, Google services ready, or a strong ultrawide camera.

Why we say that

The 6.31‑inch LTPO AMOLED can hit 5,000 nits, making outdoor reading easy and gaming vivid. The 6,500 mAh cell often lasts about one and a half days even with heavy use, and 90 W wired charging restores most of the charge in half an hour. Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 delivers smooth performance in apps and games, though the aluminum frame can become warm after long sessions.

The main 50 MP camera with Zeiss tuning produces sharp photos, while the 8 MP ultrawide is noticeably weak and cannot record 4K. The phone runs OriginOS 6 on gray imports, which looks attractive but adds heavy bloat and lacks Google apps out of the box. Owners frequently mention the outdated USB‑C 2.0 port and the need to verify shop warranty and IMEI before buying.

How long does the battery really last in everyday use?

The 6,500 mAh cell is rated 9.6 out of 10 and owners report it often lasts about one and a half days even with moderate gaming and video streaming, so you will usually charge every night.

Will the phone overheat while gaming?

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 and a vapor‑chamber cooling keep performance stable, but the aluminum frame does become noticeably warm after long gaming sessions, which some users find uncomfortable.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.6 aperture and OIS performs well in low light, especially with Zeiss tuning, but the 8 MP ultrawide struggles and cannot capture 4K video.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

Only a shop‑provided warranty is available for gray‑import units, and you must verify IMEI and BTRC status. OriginOS 6 promises up to five major Android upgrades, though updates come from Chinese servers and may not include Google services.
