Buying verdict

Redmi A7 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run two days on a single charge, the Redmi A7 Pro delivers that at a budget price.

Choose it if
You want long battery life and a cheap, large screen for everyday tasks.
Consider alternatives if
You need fast charging, high‑resolution display, or strong gaming performance.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • 6000 mAh battery lasting two days
  • 120 Hz smooth display
  • Headphone jack and FM radio
  • Official warranty at ৳15,499

Reported trade-offs

  • HD+ resolution looks soft on 6.9‑inch screen
  • 15 W charging takes over two hours
  • Gaming limited to low settings
  • Camera struggles in low light

Why we say that

The 6.9‑inch IPS LCD screen runs at 120 Hz, giving smooth scrolling, but the HD+ resolution looks soft on such a large panel. The 6000 mAh battery is the star, lasting about two days of moderate use, yet the 15 W charger needs over two hours for a full top‑up. Performance is adequate for social media and light games, but heavy titles will struggle.

The 13 MP main camera produces decent daylight shots and a usable night mode, though low‑light quality is limited. Software runs on Android 16 with HyperOS 3, offering four years of updates but comes with noticeable ads. Build quality is plastic with a frosted finish and good weight balance.
