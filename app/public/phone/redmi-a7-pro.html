Redmi A7 Pro Is it worth buying?

Expect a phone that can sit on the table for days without needing a charge, but you will wait over two and a half hours for it to fill up again.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need long battery life, a big screen, and can tolerate slow charging and modest performance.
Consider alternatives if
You want fast charging, sharp display, or smooth gaming.

What works well4

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts multiple days
  • 6.9‑inch 120 Hz display feels spacious
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • Official warranty available at ৳16,499

Reported trade-offs4

  • 15 W charger needs over 2.5 hours
  • HD+ screen looks blurry on large panel
  • Gaming causes thermal throttling
  • Plastic build feels dated

Why we say that

It has a large 6.9‑inch 120 Hz IPS LCD, but only HD+ resolution, so the picture looks soft on the big panel. The 6000 mAh battery gives multi‑day endurance for light users and scores 9 out of 10, yet the 15 W charger is painfully slow, taking more than 2.5 hours for a full charge. Performance is driven by the Unisoc T7250, adequate for browsing and social apps, but gaming triggers thermal throttling and low frame rates.

The 13 MP camera is acceptable in daylight but struggles with detail and low‑light scenes. Software runs Android 16 with HyperOS 3, functional but filled with bloatware and ads. Build is plastic with a water‑drop notch, no IP rating and no stereo speakers.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 6000 mAh battery can easily last two to three days for light users who browse, chat and watch videos moderately. Reviews give the battery a 9 out of 10 rating, so you won’t need to charge it daily.

Can the Redmi A7 Pro handle mobile games without lag?

The phone uses the Unisoc T7250 chipset which is designed for basic tasks. In gaming it often thermal‑throttles, leading to noticeable lag and lower frame rates, so demanding games are not smooth.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 13 MP main sensor works okay in daylight but struggles to capture detail when light is low. Reviews note the camera is basic and low‑light photos appear noisy and lack sharpness.
