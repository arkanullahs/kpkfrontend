Buying verdict

Redmi A7 Pro Is it worth buying?

You will get two days of moderate use from a single charge, but you will notice a blurry picture on the big screen.

What works well4

  • 6000 mAh battery lasting two days
  • 120 Hz smooth scrolling on 6.9‑inch screen
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • Frosted plastic body with balanced weight

Reported trade-offs4

  • HD+ resolution looks soft on 6.9‑inch display
  • 15 W charger needs over two hours for full charge
  • Low‑light photos only usable with night mode
  • Budget software shows many ads

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh battery truly shines, keeping the phone alive for about 48 hours with average apps. The 6.9‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, so scrolling feels fluid, yet the HD+ resolution makes text and images look soft on such a large panel. The Unisoc T7250 chipset handles social media and light games like Free Fire on low settings, but it will struggle with demanding titles.

The 13 MP main camera captures decent daylight shots, and night mode helps in low light, though details remain limited. Software runs HyperOS 3 on Android 16, offering 2‑4 OS upgrades and a promised four‑year support window, but it is packed with ads. Build quality is plastic with a frosted finish that feels solid, and you still get a headphone jack and FM radio.
