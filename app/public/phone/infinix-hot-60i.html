Is the Infinix Hot 60i worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 50 MP main camera with AI object removal
  • 120 Hz smooth scrolling display
  • 45 W fast charging

What is not

  • eMMC storage slows apps and games
  • Heavy bloatware and ads
  • Outdated notch design
  • Poor software update support

Buy it if Buy it if you need a big battery and basic 5G at a low price.

Skip it if Skip it if you want fast gaming or a future proof camera.

Why we say that

Its 6000 mAh battery and 45 W charger give real world endurance that most users praise. The 6.75 inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, making scrolling feel smooth even though the resolution is modest and the notch looks dated. The Dimensity 6400 paired with 6 GB RAM handles everyday apps, but the eMMC storage causes slow launches and noticeable hiccups in demanding games such as eFootball.

The 50 MP main sensor produces usable photos and AI object removal helps a bit, yet low light results are average. XOS 15.1 on Android 15 is loaded with preinstalled apps and ads, and only one or two OS updates are expected. Overall the phone is a budget 5G option for users who value battery life over performance.
