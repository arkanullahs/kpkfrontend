Is the Infinix Hot 60i worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts long
  • 120 Hz display feels smooth
  • 50 MP camera works in daylight
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included

What is not

  • eMMC storage slows apps
  • Notch looks outdated
  • Gaming stutters on demanding titles
  • Heavy bloatware and ads

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large 5G phone with a strong battery and can tolerate slower performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need fast gaming, smooth software updates, or a premium display.

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh battery and 45W charger give you enough juice for heavy use, though the charging speed feels only normal. The 6.75 inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, providing smooth scrolling, but the resolution is low and the notch looks dated. Performance is held back by eMMC storage, causing slow app launches and occasional hiccups in demanding games such as eFootball.

The 50 MP main sensor captures decent daylight shots and AI object removal helps a bit, yet low light results are modest. Software runs XOS 15.1 on Android 15, but heavy bloatware, ads and only one or two OS updates make the experience feel stale. Overall the phone feels like a budget 5G device with a strong battery and large screen, while you must accept slower performance and limited future support.
