Infinix Hot 60i Is it worth buying?

The Infinix Hot 60i is a budget device that relies heavily on its massive battery while struggling with dated hardware choices.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life above all else and only need a device for basic daily tasks.
Consider alternatives if
You are a gamer or someone who expects a modern, clean software experience without ads.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Massive 6000 mAh battery capacity
  • Smooth 120Hz display refresh rate
  • Useful AI object removal feature
  • Includes headphone jack and IR blaster

Reported trade-offs4

  • Slow eMMC storage causes app lag
  • Heavy bloatware and notification ads
  • Outdated screen notch design
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

Owners consistently report that the 6000 mAh battery provides excellent real-world endurance, which is the phone's strongest asset. However, the experience is hampered by the use of eMMC storage instead of faster UFS, leading to slow app loading times and poor gaming stability. Users are frustrated by the outdated screen notch design and the heavy presence of bloatware and notification ads within the XOS 15.1 software.

While the 120Hz display offers smooth scrolling, the low resolution and mediocre build quality leave much to be desired. The camera performance is basic, with owners noting that while the 50MP main sensor is usable, video quality is particularly weak. Because this is an unofficial import, you will not receive any manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh, and all repairs must be paid for out of pocket.

Is the Infinix Hot 60i good for gaming?
