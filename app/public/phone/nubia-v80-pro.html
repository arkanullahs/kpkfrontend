Why we say that

The Nubia V80 Pro ships with a 6.75 inch IPS LCD panel that only reaches HD+ resolution, so the pixel density feels soft despite a 120 Hz refresh and 1000 nit peak brightness. The Unisoc T7280 processor shows its age, giving low scores in gaming and overall speed, and owners report frequent lag in demanding apps. The 108 MP rear sensor sounds impressive but reviewers say the pictures lack real detail and look like a marketing trick.

Battery life is decent thanks to a 5000 mAh cell, yet the 22 W charger is modest for such a large pack. Build quality resembles an iPhone shape but feels cheap, and the bottom bezel is noticeably large. FM radio is missing and the software includes medium level ads and bloat.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The phone has a 5000 mAh battery which owners rate 6.5 out of 10. In everyday use it can last most of the day with moderate screen time, but heavy gaming or bright display settings will drain it faster.

Will this phone run games smoothly?

The device uses the Unisoc T7280 chipset, which users describe as outdated. Gaming scores are low and many report lag in demanding titles, so it is not suited for high‑performance gaming.

Is the 108MP camera good for low‑light photos?

Although the sensor is 108 MP, reviewers call it a marketing gimmick. Low‑light images lack detail and show noise, so the camera does not deliver the quality its megapixel count suggests.

Does the phone come with an official warranty and software updates?

Yes, the official Bangladeshi unit includes the brand’s warranty and service network. It runs Android 16 with Nebula AIOS / MyOS and is expected to receive one to two OS updates, though the software contains medium level ads and bloat.
