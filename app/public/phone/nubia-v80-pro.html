Is the Nubia V80 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz refresh rate
  • 5000 mAh battery capacity
  • headphone jack included
  • Android 16 operating system

What is not

  • HD+ low‑resolution display
  • slow eMMC 5.1 storage
  • mediocre 108 MP camera performance
  • single bottom speaker

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize long battery life, a headphone jack, and a smooth refresh rate over high‑resolution display and gaming performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a sharp screen, strong gaming capability, or a high‑quality camera for low‑light shots.

Why we say that

The 5000 mAh cell lasts through a day of moderate use, yet owners note it falls short of 2026 standards and offers no reverse charging. The 6.75‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz but only delivers an HD+ resolution, making text and videos look soft on a large panel. The premium iPhone‑like design feels solid but attracts fingerprints quickly.

Under the hood, the Unisoc T7280 paired with eMMC 5.1 storage struggles with multitasking and gaming, and the single bottom speaker provides limited audio depth. The 108 MP main camera is marketed heavily but produces poor detail and dynamic range, especially in low light. Android 16 brings the latest OS, but the Nebula AIOS/MyOS layer adds medium‑level bloatware and ads.

Overall the phone feels overpriced for the hardware it offers.
