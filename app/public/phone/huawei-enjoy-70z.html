Huawei Enjoy 70z Is it worth buying?

The Huawei Enjoy 70z is a device that prioritizes battery longevity over actual performance.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
Buy this phone only if you need a secondary device strictly for long battery life and do not mind the lack of Google services.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable daily driver for gaming, photography, or apps that depend on Google Play services.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Efficient and long-lasting 6000 mAh battery
  • Optimized HarmonyOS 4.0 software
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • Generous 8GB RAM and 128/256GB storage

Reported trade-offs4

  • Terrible camera quality
  • Slow and unresponsive performance
  • Low resolution display
  • No Google Play services

Why we say that

Owners report that the Kirin 710A chipset is slow and unresponsive, performing similarly to older Helio G80 series chips. While the 6000 mAh battery is efficient and long-lasting, the rest of the experience is hampered by significant limitations. The camera quality is described as terrible, and the display resolution is low.

Because this phone lacks Google Play services, you will need to find workarounds for most apps essential to daily life in Bangladesh. You are also buying a gray-import unit, meaning there is no official manufacturer warranty and you are entirely dependent on the shop for any support if the device breaks. The build quality is basic, and the overall speed is often described as abysmal by those who use it daily.

What is the Huawei Enjoy 70z price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Huawei Enjoy 70z in Bangladesh was ৳19,990, checked 24 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does the Huawei Enjoy 70z have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

There is no official manufacturer warranty for this phone in Bangladesh. All units found are gray-import stock, meaning you only receive a shop warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

Is the Huawei Enjoy 70z suitable for gaming?

No, this phone is not suitable for gaming. Owners report that the performance is slow and unresponsive. The Kirin 710A chipset is comparable to older Helio G80 series chips, which struggle with modern gaming demands.

How does the battery perform on this device?

The 6000 mAh battery is a major strength of this phone. Owners rate the battery life highly at 8.5 out of 10, describing it as efficient and long-lasting for daily use.

Can I use all my regular apps on this phone?

You cannot use all apps easily because the phone lacks Google Play services. Most apps essential for users in Bangladesh require manual workarounds to function, which may be difficult for some users.
