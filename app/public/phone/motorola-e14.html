Why we say that

The 6.56 inch IPS LCD runs at 90Hz, giving smooth scrolling for social media and video. Android 14 Go edition keeps the system light, but the 2 to 4 GB RAM and Unisoc T606 chipset feel sluggish when many apps are open. The 13 MP main camera captures decent daylight shots but struggles in low light and the 5 MP selfie lens is modest.

Battery life is solid thanks to the 5000 mAh cell, often lasting a full day of mixed use. The phone arrives as a gray import, so there is no manufacturer warranty and repairs must be paid after the shop’s limited coverage. Software updates are expected for only one to three Android versions, which is weaker than many competitors.

Owners often complain about the slow performance under load and the lack of official support, especially when a newer Motorola E22 is cheaper.

Will the battery last a full day with regular use?

The 5000 mAh battery is large enough to last most of a day when you use social media, messaging and occasional video. Heavy gaming or bright screen for many hours will drain it faster, but typical mixed use reaches a full day.

Can I play demanding games on this phone?

The Unisoc T606 processor with 2 to 4 GB RAM can handle simple games, but demanding titles will run slowly or lag. Expect lower graphics settings and occasional frame drops on heavier games.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 13 MP main sensor with an f/2.2 aperture captures acceptable photos in daylight, but in low light the small aperture and basic processing produce noisy and dark images. The 5 MP selfie camera shows similar limitations.

Does this phone get official warranty and regular software updates?

All listings are gray imports, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop may offer its own limited warranty, but repairs are paid. Android 14 Go will receive only one to three OS updates, which is less than many rivals.
