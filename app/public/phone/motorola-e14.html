Is the Motorola E14 worth buying?

At its price the Motorola E14 gives a smooth 90 Hz screen and a full‑day battery, but the low‑end chipset and limited software support keep it from feeling future‑proof. The 6.56‑inch IPS LCD shows colors clearly and the 5000 mAh cell easily lasts through heavy messaging, social media and video streaming without needing a charge. Android 14 Go with Hello UI runs light, so basic apps open quickly even with 2 GB RAM, though multitasking can stutter when several tabs are open. The 13 MP rear camera captures decent daylight shots but struggles in low light, and the 5 MP selfie lens is only adequate for casual snaps. Owners appreciate the headphone jack and the cheap 14990 taka price, yet they often complain about the shop‑only warranty and the fact that Motorola has historically delivered only one to three Android upgrades. Overall it feels like a competent entry‑level device for everyday use, as long as you accept its compromises.

How long will the 5000 mAh battery last with normal use?

Is the 13 MP camera usable in low‑light conditions?

The 13 MP rear sensor with f/2.2 aperture records acceptable photos in good daylight, but in low‑light it produces noisy and underexposed images, so it is not reliable for night photography.
