Buying verdict

Apple iPhone 17 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you want a flagship with a brilliant display and strong performance but can tolerate software bugs and a fragile aluminum body, the iPhone 17 Pro may fit you.

Choose it if
You value a top‑tier screen and fast processor and are okay with paying extra for an official warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable low‑light photos, long gaming sessions, or a phone that will receive updates longer than the next iPhone generation.

What works well

  • 120 Hz LTPO OLED display
  • Apple A19 Pro performance
  • 48 MP main camera sensor
  • 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs

  • iOS 26 buggy with UI glitches
  • Aluminum frame scratches easily
  • Telephoto lens low resolution and needs distance

Why we say that

The 6.3‑inch LTPO Super Retina XDR OLED panel hits 3000 nits peak and runs at 120 Hz, making scrolling and media consumption feel smooth. The Apple A19 Pro chip with 12 GB RAM delivers fast app launches and high scores in performance tests, though gaming can trigger noticeable thermal throttling. Battery life sits at a modest 7.2 rating; the 3998 mAh cell lasts a day for most users but can feel inconsistent after iOS updates, and fast‑charging requires a 40 W charger that is not always included.

The 48 MP main camera captures detailed photos, yet night shots suffer from lens flares and the 4× telephoto struggles with close subjects and drops resolution to near 1 MP. iOS 26 brings new features but is plagued by UI glitches and occasional notification‑center swipe failures. Official‑warranty units cost at least ৳1,85,999, while gray‑import models start at ৳1,41,499 but lack manufacturer support.

What reviewers said on YouTube

4 reviews · 15.6M views

The iPhone 17 Pro is a thicker, performance-focused device that delivers meaningful upgrades to battery life, cooling, and charging speeds. However, its new aluminum body scratches easily, and the overall package feels like it is laying the hardware groundwork for future software features.

Best for Power users and mobile gamers

  1. MMarques Brownlee

    iPhone 17 Pro Review: Paradox in a Box! (opens YouTube)

    6.8M views

    Marques Brownlee concludes that Apple leaning into a thicker, heavier, and completely aluminum design has successfully freed the Pro line to be more maximalist. He highly praises the inclusion of a vapor chamber for sustained performance, the massive battery improvements, and reliable cameras, though he is disappointed by the lack of an 8K video option and persistent lens flares. Overall, he believes making the Pro physically larger and more robust ultimately benefits the mainstream audience who just want better battery and a great screen.

  2. MMrwhosetheboss

    iPhone 17 Pro Review - Something’s Missing (opens YouTube)

    3.7M views

    Mrwhosetheboss finds the iPhone 17 Pro to be a somewhat strange mix of hardware upgrades that seem designed for heavy AI features that have not been released yet. While he loves the reliable cameras, wider selfie lens, and improved thermals, he heavily criticizes the new aluminum body's susceptibility to scratching and the unpolished state of the iOS 26 software. He suggests that unless you desperately need the larger battery or plan to use demanding future features, most buyers would be better off saving their money and buying the base iPhone 17.

  3. GGSMArena Official

    Apple iPhone 17 Pro review (opens YouTube)

    162K views

    GSMArena notes that while the iPhone 17 Pro brings welcome additions like a larger battery, substantially faster charging, and a bright anti-reflective display, it is not quite a slam dunk. They appreciate the stellar video stabilization and excellent two times zoom, but point out that the new four times telephoto lens fails at close focusing and the aluminum frame is too easily scuffed. Ultimately, they view it as a solid but incremental device that forces buyers to weigh its practical battery gains against its reduced physical durability.

How many iOS updates will the iPhone 17 Pro get?

Apple promises 5 to 6 major iOS versions for this model. The device ships with iOS 26 and can be upgraded to iOS 26.6, but the current software is reported to have UI glitches and feature regressions.

Is there an official warranty for the iPhone 17 Pro in Bangladesh?

Only units sold as official warranty listings receive Apple’s manufacturer cover, starting at ৳1,85,999. Gray‑import phones, which start at ৳1,41,499, have no manufacturer warranty and any repair at a service centre is charged as a paid service.

Will the battery last me through a typical day?

The 3998 mAh battery earned a 7.2 rating, meaning average endurance. Owners report it lasts a day under normal use but can become inconsistent after software updates. Fast charging is 40 W, though the charger is not always included.

How does the camera perform in low light and with zoom?

The 48 MP main sensor is reliable, but night shots show lens flares around bright lights. The 4× telephoto lens cannot focus on close subjects, drops resolution to just above 1 MP, and the phone still lacks 8K video recording.
