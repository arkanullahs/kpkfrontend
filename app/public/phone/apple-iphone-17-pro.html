Is the Apple iPhone 17 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 3000‑nit LTPO OLED display
  • Apple A19 Pro chipset with 12 GB RAM
  • All‑day battery life
  • Strong short‑burst performance

What is not

  • Aluminum body scratches easily
  • Telephoto lens cannot focus close
  • iOS 26 software instability
  • 15 W charging is slow

Buy it if Buy it if you value a premium screen, fast performance and can tolerate software quirks and a scratch‑prone body.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable close‑up zoom, 8K video, or a phone that charges quickly.

Why we say that

The LTPO OLED screen stays crisp at any angle and the A19 Pro chip with 12 GB RAM makes apps feel instantaneous. Battery life is solid and usually lasts a full day even with heavy use. However, the unibody aluminum chassis scratches and chips easily, and the telephoto lens loses focus on close subjects, which hurts portrait work.

iOS 26 feels buggy and can freeze during routine actions. Charging is limited to 15 W, so refilling the 3998 mAh cell is slower than many Android competitors. Gaming spikes in power but the device throttles after a while, and the phone can become warm under load.

In bright daylight the display shines, but low‑light shots show flare and the lack of 8K video may disappoint video creators.

What reviewers said

The iPhone 17 Pro is a thicker, performance-focused device that delivers meaningful upgrades to battery life, cooling, and charging speeds. However, its new aluminum body scratches easily, and the overall package feels like it is laying the hardware groundwork for future software features.

Best for Power users and mobile gamers

Praised

  • Better thermal performance A new internal cooling system keeps the phone from getting excessively hot during gaming or heavy tasks. Marques Brownlee · iPhone 17 Pro Review: Paradox in a Box! ↗
  • Noticeably longer battery life A physically larger battery provides enough power to comfortably last through a full day of heavy use. GSMArena Official · Apple iPhone 17 Pro review ↗
  • Much faster charging speeds The phone recharges significantly faster than previous models, reaching half capacity in about twenty minutes. GSMArena Official · Apple iPhone 17 Pro review ↗
  • Screen reduces outdoor glare A new anti-reflective coating on the display significantly cuts down on harsh reflections when used outside. GSMArena Official · Apple iPhone 17 Pro review ↗
  • Wider selfie camera view The front-facing camera captures a broader scene, making it much easier to fit multiple people into one frame. Mrwhosetheboss · iPhone 17 Pro Review - Something’s Missing ↗
  • Excellent two times zoom shots Images taken at two times magnification look incredibly sharp and detailed despite not having a dedicated lens for it. Marques Brownlee · iPhone 17 Pro Review: Paradox in a Box! ↗

Criticised

  • Aluminum body scratches easily The new exterior finish is highly prone to permanent scuffs and scratches, especially around the camera bump. Mrwhosetheboss · iPhone 17 Pro Review - Something’s Missing ↗
  • Telephoto lens cannot focus close The new zoom camera struggles with close-up subjects, forcing the phone to switch back to the main lens. Mrwhosetheboss · iPhone 17 Pro Review - Something’s Missing ↗
  • Persistent night video flares Bright light sources still cause annoying dots and reflections when recording video in the dark. Marques Brownlee · iPhone 17 Pro Review: Paradox in a Box! ↗
  • Software feels quite unpolished The newly redesigned operating system currently suffers from several visual glitches and stuttering animations out of the box. Mrwhosetheboss · iPhone 17 Pro Review - Something’s Missing ↗

Marques Brownlee · iPhone 17 Pro Review: Paradox in a Box! ↗

Marques Brownlee concludes that Apple leaning into a thicker, heavier, and completely aluminum design has successfully freed the Pro line to be more maximalist. He highly praises the inclusion of a vapor chamber for sustained performance, the massive battery improvements, and reliable cameras, though he is disappointed by the lack of an 8K video option and persistent lens flares. Overall, he believes making the Pro physically larger and more robust ultimately benefits the mainstream audience who just want better battery and a great screen.

Mrwhosetheboss · iPhone 17 Pro Review - Something’s Missing ↗

Mrwhosetheboss finds the iPhone 17 Pro to be a somewhat strange mix of hardware upgrades that seem designed for heavy AI features that have not been released yet. While he loves the reliable cameras, wider selfie lens, and improved thermals, he heavily criticizes the new aluminum body's susceptibility to scratching and the unpolished state of the iOS 26 software. He suggests that unless you desperately need the larger battery or plan to use demanding future features, most buyers would be better off saving their money and buying the base iPhone 17.

GSMArena Official · Apple iPhone 17 Pro review ↗

GSMArena notes that while the iPhone 17 Pro brings welcome additions like a larger battery, substantially faster charging, and a bright anti-reflective display, it is not quite a slam dunk. They appreciate the stellar video stabilization and excellent two times zoom, but point out that the new four times telephoto lens fails at close focusing and the aluminum frame is too easily scuffed. Ultimately, they view it as a solid but incremental device that forces buyers to weigh its practical battery gains against its reduced physical durability.
