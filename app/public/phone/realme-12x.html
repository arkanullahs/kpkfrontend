Buying verdict

Realme 12x Is it worth buying?

If you need a cheap 5G phone that can survive a day of heavy use, the Realme 12x will do that but it comes with several compromises.

Choose it if
You want an affordable 5G phone with decent day‑long battery and can tolerate a modest display and some software clutter.
Consider alternatives if
You expect a high‑resolution screen, fast charging, or official brand warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • 5000 mAh battery lasts over a day
  • 8 GB RAM handles everyday apps
  • 120 Hz refresh feels smooth
  • IP54 splash resistance

Reported trade-offs

  • 720p screen feels low for 2024
  • Charging often limited to 15 W
  • Low‑light photos are noisy
  • Heavy bloatware and ads

Why we say that

The 5000 mAh battery easily lasts a full day or more, and the 8 GB RAM keeps everyday apps responsive. The 6.67" IPS LCD panel runs at 120 Hz, yet its 720p resolution feels dated and the mono speakers are unimpressive. Daylight photos from the 50 MP main sensor are acceptable, but low‑light shots are noisy and oversharpened.

Charging often falls back to 15 W despite a 45 W claim, and the device can become uncomfortably hot during mobile data or gaming. Realme UI 5.0 brings many features but also a noticeable amount of bloatware and ads. The phone arrives as a gray‑import unit with no official brand warranty, only a shop‑provided guarantee that may require paid repairs.

Read full reasoning

Overall it offers solid value for entry‑level 5G users who can tolerate these trade‑offs.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

Owners report excellent endurance, with the 5000 mAh battery easily lasting a full day of mixed use and often more. The battery rating of 8.0 reflects this strong performance.

Can the Realme 12x handle mobile games without lag?

The phone delivers smooth performance for basic games thanks to the Dimensity 6100+ and 8 GB RAM, but it can get hot during extended gaming sessions. Heavy titles may show occasional lag.

Is the camera usable in low‑light conditions?

Daylight photos are adequate, but low‑light images suffer from noise and oversharpening. Reviewers rate the camera around 5 out of 10, indicating limited usefulness in dim environments.

Will I get an official warranty and regular software updates?

All units are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s warranty applies and repairs are paid. Realme UI promises about two to three major OS updates, but bloatware remains medium to high.
