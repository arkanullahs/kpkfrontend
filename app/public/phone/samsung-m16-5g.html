Is the Samsung M16 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.7 inch Super AMOLED with 800 nits brightness
  • 5000 mAh battery lasting up to two days
  • 50 MP main camera works well in daylight
  • Large screen with smooth 90Hz refresh

What is not

  • Plastic build feels cheap
  • 4 GB RAM lags with multitasking
  • 25W charger often missing
  • Low light and ultra wide camera poor

Buy it if Buy it if you need a large bright screen and decent daylight photos while staying under 20,000 taka.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect smooth gaming, strong low light photos, or a premium feel.

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch Super AMOLED panel reaches 800 nits and runs at 90Hz, making everyday use pleasant even in sunlight. Battery life stretches to one and a half or two days for moderate users, but the 25W charger is slow and often missing from the box. The plastic body feels light but cheap, and owners notice the lack of an always‑on display and screen recording.

The 50 MP main sensor captures decent daylight shots, yet ultra wide and low light photos are disappointing. With 4 GB RAM the phone struggles to keep three apps open, and gaming feels laggy. Android 15 arrives out of the box, but the promised six‑year update window feels uncertain on this hardware.

The only available stock is a gray‑import with shop warranty, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery usually lasts between one and a half to two days for moderate use. Heavy gaming or many background apps will reduce that time noticeably.

Will this phone handle mobile games well?

Casual games run okay, but the 4 GB RAM version shows lag in more demanding titles. Expect occasional stutter and slight warmth near the camera area during longer sessions.

Is the camera usable in low light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor performs acceptably in daylight, but low light photos are grainy and the ultra wide lens is especially weak. Do not rely on it for night photography.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

Only gray‑import stock is available, so you receive a shop warranty, not a Samsung brand warranty. Samsung promises up to six years of Android upgrades, but the hardware may struggle to support the full update cycle.
