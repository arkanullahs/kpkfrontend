Is the OPPO Reno13 F 4G worth buying?

If you need a phone that can stay on all day and show vivid colors, the OPPO Reno13 F 4G delivers that at a modest price. The 6.67‑inch AMOLED panel is bright, smooth and comfortable for media and browsing. The 5800 mAh battery easily lasts a full day of normal use, even with the 120 Hz refresh rate. The 50 MP main sensor captures decent photos in good light, and the 32 MP selfie camera is useful for social media. However, the camera does not have the promised optical image stabilization, so low‑light shots can be blurry. Performance feels average; the Helio G100 handles everyday tasks but struggles with demanding games and can become hot. Build quality feels moderate, not premium. Overall the phone is a solid choice for media consumption and long battery life, but it falls short for serious photography or heavy gaming.
