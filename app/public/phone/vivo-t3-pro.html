Is the Vivo T3 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Elite battery endurance with 8 to 12 hours SOT
  • Very bright 6.77 inch curved AMOLED display
  • Efficient 4nm Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 performance
  • Includes OIS for the 50MP main camera

What is not

  • Fragile curved glass design raises durability concerns
  • Notification bugs and battery drain after software updates
  • No microSD card slot for storage expansion
  • Video recording limited to 4K30

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a bright display and long battery life for daily tasks and are comfortable managing an unofficial gray-import device.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you require a reliable official brand warranty or need advanced video features like 4K60 recording.

Why we say that

Owners report that the 5500 mAh battery provides elite endurance, though recent software updates have caused noticeable battery drain. While the Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 chipset handles daily tasks and gaming smoothly, the build quality is a point of contention due to the fragile nature of the curved glass. You will face limitations with the camera, specifically the lack of 4K60 video recording and average selfie performance.

Software reliability is another concern, with users highlighting notification bugs in apps like Microsoft Teams. Because this device is only available through unofficial channels, you will rely on a shop warranty rather than a brand warranty. Always verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before finalizing your purchase.

Does the Vivo T3 Pro come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, the device is currently only available as unofficial gray-import stock. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, so you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

How long will the Vivo T3 Pro receive software updates?

The phone runs on Android 14 and Funtouch 14 out of the box. It is promised to receive up to 2 major Android upgrades during its lifecycle.

Is the Vivo T3 Pro suitable for heavy gaming?

The phone features a 4nm Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 chipset that provides smooth daily responsiveness. Owners have rated its gaming performance at 6.5 out of 10, suggesting it handles tasks well but may not be the top choice for intensive gaming.

Can I expand the storage on this phone?

No, the Vivo T3 Pro does not include a microSD card slot. You are limited to the internal storage capacity of either 128GB or 256GB that you choose at the time of purchase.
