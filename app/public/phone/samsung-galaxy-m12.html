Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy M12 Is it worth buying?

This is a 2021 device that feels dated for the current market.

Choose it if
You need a basic Samsung phone with a large battery and a headphone jack.
Consider alternatives if
You want a device with long term software support or fast charging.

What works well

  • Large 5000 mAh battery
  • Smooth 90Hz refresh rate
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • Low amount of bloatware

Reported trade-offs

  • Very old 2021 release date
  • Slow 15W charging speed
  • Limited remaining software updates

Why we say that

You get a large battery and a smooth 90Hz screen, but the Exynos 850 chipset is basic. The most critical issue is the age of the phone. Since it is 5 years old, you should expect very little or no remaining software update life.

This means you will miss out on new features and security patches. The 15W charging is slow for a battery this size. It handles basic tasks, but it is not built for heavy use.
