Is the iQOO 15T worth buying?

What is good

  • 200MP main camera with OIS
  • 8000 mAh battery with 100W fast charge
  • Dimensity 9500 Monster chipset for smooth gaming
  • 6.82‑inch LTPO AMOLED with 144 Hz refresh

What is not

  • Only shop warranty, no official brand coverage
  • China ROM may show medium ads
  • Software updates limited to three Android versions
  • Potentially higher bloat compared to global ROM

Buy it if Buy it if you want top performance and are okay with a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need an official brand warranty or a clean global ROM.

Why we say that

The Dimensity 9500 Monster chipset and 12GB RAM handle games and multitasking without lag. The 6.82 inch LTPO AMOLED screen stays bright even in sunlight thanks to a 4500 nit peak and 144Hz refresh. The 8000mAh battery lasts well beyond a day of normal use and the 100W charger refills it in under an hour.

The 200MP main sensor with OIS captures detailed photos, though low‑light results depend on the China ROM software. The main drawback is the lack of an official brand warranty and the presence of medium level ads in the OriginOS CN version. Owners also note that software updates are limited to three Android releases.

How long does the 8000 mAh battery last in daily use?

The 8000 mAh cell can easily last more than a day with moderate browsing, video streaming and social media. Heavy gaming or continuous video playback will drain it faster, but the 100W charger refills it in under an hour.

Is the iQOO 15T suitable for high‑end mobile gaming?

Yes, the Dimensity 9500 Monster processor together with 12GB RAM and a 144Hz LTPO AMOLED panel delivers smooth frame rates in demanding games. The device may become warm during long sessions, but performance remains consistent.

How does the 200MP camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The large 1/1.56 inch sensor, f/1.9 aperture and optical image stabilization help capture brighter images at night. The China ROM software adds medium level processing, so results are good but not as refined as premium night‑mode phones.

What warranty and software update support does the gray‑import version have?

The phone comes with a shop warranty rather than an official iQOO brand warranty, so you must verify IMEI and BTRC status with the seller. Software updates are limited to three Android versions and the OriginOS CN build includes medium ads.
