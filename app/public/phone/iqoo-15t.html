Buying verdict

iQOO 15T Is it worth buying?

The iQOO 15T is a powerhouse for mobile gamers that demands you accept significant compromises in software and camera quality.

Choose it if
You are a dedicated mobile gamer who values raw performance and battery life above all else.
Consider alternatives if
You want a reliable camera, a clean software experience, or the security of an official brand warranty.

What works well

  • Massive 8000 mAh battery with two-day endurance
  • Stunning 144Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • Powerful Dimensity 9500 Monster chipset
  • Premium metal frame with IP68/69 rating

Reported trade-offs

  • Poor selfie camera quality
  • Chinese bloatware and notification bugs
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Lack of detail in ultra-wide camera

Why we say that

Living with this device means managing Chinese bloatware and notification bugs inherent to the OriginOS 6 interface. While the 8000 mAh battery provides exceptional two-day endurance, the camera system fails to match the premium price tag. Owners specifically report that the 16MP selfie camera produces results comparable to a budget phone, and the ultra-wide lens lacks necessary detail.

You are buying a gray-import unit built for the Chinese market, which means you get no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Repairs are entirely at your own expense, and you must rely on the specific shop for any support. Performance is undeniably strong with the Dimensity 9500 Monster chipset, but you should be prepared for minor stutters during intense gaming sessions.

Read full reasoning

This is a niche device for those who prioritize raw hardware specs over a polished, worry-free user experience.

Does the iQOO 15T come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is sold as a gray import. There is no manufacturer warranty available in Bangladesh regardless of the brand's service score. You must rely on the promise of the specific shop where you purchase the device, and all repairs will be paid.

How does the battery perform during daily use?

The battery performance is excellent with a massive 8000 mAh capacity. Owners report that the device provides two-day endurance on a single charge. It also supports 100W fast charging to help you get back to full power quickly.

Is this phone suitable for heavy gaming?

The phone is built for gaming with the Dimensity 9500 Monster chipset. While it offers monster raw power, owners note that heavy games like Genshin Impact can cause the device to reach 40°C and may experience minor stutters during gameplay.

What software issues should I expect with the Chinese ROM?

The Chinese ROM comes with OriginOS 6, which includes pre-installed apps and notification handling issues. You may find the software experience less smooth than global versions due to this bloatware, and you should confirm with your seller if you are receiving a China or India variant.
