Realme 12 Lite Is it worth buying?

If you need a big screen and a high resolution camera without breaking the bank, the Realme 12 Lite delivers decent performance for everyday tasks.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large screen and strong camera on a tight budget.

  • 6.72 inch 90Hz display
  • 108MP main camera
  • 5000 mAh battery
  • headphone jack

Reported trade-offs3

  • Shop warranty only no brand warranty
  • Medium to high ads in UI
  • Snapdragon 685 not for heavy gaming

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 685 chipset handles social media, video streaming and light gaming smoothly, while the 90Hz IPS LCD makes scrolling feel fluid. The 5000 mAh battery easily lasts a full day of moderate use and the 108MP main sensor captures detailed photos in good light. Realme UI runs Android 14 but includes medium to high ads that some users find intrusive.

The phone arrives as a gray‑import unit, so only a shop warranty is offered and you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying. There is no confirmed 5G support and the chipset is not aimed at heavy gaming. Overall it feels solid for its price but the software and warranty situation are the main complaints.

Realme 12 Lite: what buyers ask us

What is the Realme 12 Lite price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Realme 12 Lite in Bangladesh was ৳28,990, checked 24 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Will the battery last a full day of heavy use?

The 5000 mAh battery is large enough to get through a day of typical usage such as browsing, video playback and occasional gaming. Heavy gaming may reduce the endurance but it should still last most of the day.

How good is the camera in low light?

The 108MP sensor has an f/1.8 aperture which helps in brighter conditions, but low‑light performance may show noise and less detail because the phone does not have a large sensor or advanced night mode.

Does the phone come with an official Realme warranty?

Only a shop warranty is provided for the gray‑import stock. Realme does not list a manufacturer warranty for this unit, so you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchase.

How many Android updates will the phone receive?

Realme promises about two to three major Android version updates for this model. The device ships with Android 14 and Realme UI, and you can expect a few years of software support.
