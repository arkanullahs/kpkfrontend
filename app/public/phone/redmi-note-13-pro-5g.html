Redmi Note 13 Pro 5G Is it worth buying?

At ৳27,990, this gray-import device offers a high-resolution 200MP camera and a bright 1.5K AMOLED display, but it comes with significant software and reliability trade-offs.

What works well4

  • Bright 1.5K AMOLED display
  • Capable 200MP main camera
  • Includes 3.5mm headphone jack
  • Fast 67W charging support

Reported trade-offs4

  • Heavy bloatware and UI ads
  • Frequent software lag and freezing
  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Weak ultrawide and macro cameras

Why we say that

Owners frequently report frustrating lag and freezing issues, particularly when using mobile data, Bluetooth, and location services simultaneously. The user experience is further hampered by heavy bloatware and persistent ads within the interface. Because these units are gray imports, you receive no manufacturer warranty, and the China-ROM variants often lack Google services and Bangla language support.

While the hardware includes useful features like a 3.5mm headphone jack and an IR blaster, the virtual proximity sensor is notably slow. Given that the newer Redmi Note 14 Pro 5G is available for a similar price, this older model is difficult to recommend for most buyers. You must confirm the specific ROM version and BTRC status with your seller before parting with your money.

Does this phone come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every listing is a gray import, meaning you only receive whatever limited promise the individual shop provides. All repairs must be paid for out of your own pocket.

Is this device suitable for mobile gaming?

Gaming performance is limited to medium settings on this device. While the Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 chipset is adequate for basic daily use, owners have rated the gaming experience at 6.0 out of 10, suggesting it is not ideal for demanding titles.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

This phone was released in 2023 and is now three years old. You should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point, despite the theoretical support for up to three major Android upgrades.

What should I check before buying this gray-import unit?

You must verify if the unit is a China-ROM variant, as these often lack Google services and Bangla language support. Additionally, you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller to ensure the device is not restricted.
