Buying verdict

Redmi Note 13 Pro 5G Is it worth buying?

If you can tolerate heavy software bloat and no official warranty, the Redmi Note 13 Pro 5G gives a bright 120Hz screen and a 200MP camera for under ৳30,000.

Choose it if
You want a large, bright screen and a high‑resolution camera without spending a premium and you are okay with gray‑import limitations.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable software performance, official warranty support, or heavy gaming capability.

What works well

  • 200MP main camera with OIS
  • 6.67 inch 120Hz AMOLED display
  • 67W fast charging
  • Headphone jack and IR blaster

Reported trade-offs

  • Heavy bloatware and ads
  • Software lag after updates
  • Limited gaming performance
  • No official manufacturer warranty

Why we say that

The 6.67 inch AMOLED panel reaches 1800 nits peak brightness, making media consumption pleasant even in sunlight. The Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 chipset with 12GB RAM handles everyday tasks without trouble, but heavy games and multitasking feel sluggish. Battery life is average; the 5100 mAh cell lasts a day with moderate use, and the 67W charger refills quickly though some owners notice inconsistent reliability.

The phone arrives as a gray‑import Chinese model, so it may run the HyperOS CN ROM without Google services and only the shop’s limited warranty applies. Software updates are limited to a few more Android versions, and recent HyperOS upgrades have introduced noticeable lag and ads. Overall it feels like a premium‑looking device that falls short on software polish and long‑term support.

Does the Redmi Note 13 Pro 5G come with a warranty in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray‑import units built for China, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop you buy from may offer its own limited warranty, but any repair will be paid by you.

How long does the battery last and how fast does the charger work?

The phone has a 5100 mAh battery that provides average endurance for a full day of moderate use. The 67W charger refills the battery quickly, but some owners report inconsistent charging reliability.

Can I play demanding games smoothly on this device?

The Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 with 12GB RAM handles daily apps well, but performance ratings show it struggles with heavy gaming and multitasking. Expect occasional frame drops in demanding titles.

Will the phone receive future Android updates?

It ships with Android 13 and HyperOS 3 and is eligible for up to three major Android upgrades, reaching Android 16. Being three years old, the remaining update window is short and may end soon.
