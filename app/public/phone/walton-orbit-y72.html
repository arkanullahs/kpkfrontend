Buying verdict

Walton Orbit Y72 Is it worth buying?

The Walton Orbit Y72 is a budget device that prioritizes battery longevity over performance.

Choose it if
You need a long-lasting battery for basic daily tasks and do not care about gaming or photography.
Consider alternatives if
You want a device that charges quickly, performs well in games, or takes decent photos in low light.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Massive 6000 mAh battery capacity
  • Lasts 1.5 to 2 days easily
  • Surprising IP64 water and dust resistance
  • Large 90Hz display panel

Reported trade-offs

  • Extremely slow 10W charging speed
  • Poor performance and gaming capability
  • Dim display visibility outdoors
  • Mediocre camera quality in low light

Why we say that

Living with this phone means accepting that you will rarely need to reach for a charger, but when you do, the process is frustratingly slow. Owners report that the 6000 mAh battery easily lasts up to two days, which is the primary reason to consider this model. However, the experience is hampered by a weak chipset that struggles with anything beyond basic apps.

Gaming is not a viable option, as users report significant lag in titles like PUBG. The display is large and features a 90Hz refresh rate, yet it suffers from poor outdoor visibility and an outdated notch design. While the IP64 rating provides a surprising level of durability for a plastic build, the camera performance remains basic at best.

Read full reasoning

You should expect mediocre results in low light and selfies. The Android 15 Go Edition software is a necessary inclusion to keep the hardware functional, though you should not expect frequent updates.

How long does the battery actually last on a single charge?

The Walton Orbit Y72 features a massive 6000 mAh battery. Owners report that it easily lasts between 1.5 and 2 days on a single charge, making it a reliable choice for users who prioritize battery life over other features.

Is the Walton Orbit Y72 suitable for playing games like PUBG?

This phone is not recommended for gaming. Owners report that the device struggles with performance and experiences significant lag when playing games like PUBG. It is intended only for basic applications.

Does this phone come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

Yes, the Walton Orbit Y72 is listed as an official BD unit. This means you are covered by the brand's own official warranty and their service network across the country.

How does the camera perform when taking photos at night?

The camera system is quite basic. While it can handle daylight photos, owners report that it performs poorly in low-light conditions. You should also expect mediocre results from the 5MP selfie camera.
