Reported trade-offs4

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Heavy and slippery chassis
  • Camera quality low in dim light
  • Occasional software bugs from China ROM

Why we say that

The 6.85‑inch AMOLED screen delivers bright, 144 Hz visuals and the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset keeps even the most demanding titles running without throttling thanks to Aqua Core liquid cooling and an active fan. Battery life is the standout, with a 7500 mAh cell that lasts through long sessions and a full 80 W charge in just over an hour. Software is clean Android 16 with low bloat and useful gaming tools, though it runs a China ROM that may show occasional bugs.

The camera setup is adequate for everyday snaps but falls short of flagship quality, especially in low light and video stabilization. The chassis, while premium, is heavy at 230 g and can get greasy in the hand. Repairs rely on the shop’s warranty and any breakage is their responsibility.

How long will the battery last during heavy gaming?

The 7500 mAh cell is praised for exceptional endurance, letting most users play for many hours before needing a charge. In real‑world tests heavy gaming sessions can stretch beyond five hours, and a full 80 W charge tops up in about 68 minutes.

Will the phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray‑import units built for China, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop provides its own limited warranty and any repair costs are paid by the buyer if the phone fails.

Is the camera suitable for low‑light photography?

The 50 MP main sensor is decent for everyday shots but owners note it lacks flagship tuning and struggles in low light. Video stabilization is also inconsistent, making dim‑environment photos look softer than expected.

How many Android updates will this phone receive?

RedMagic OS 11.5 runs on Android 16 and the company promises one to two major OS upgrades. Updates are low on bloat and ads, but the China ROM may still show occasional software bugs.
