Is the RedMagic 11S Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Industry-leading gaming performance
  • Exceptional 7500 mAh battery life
  • True full-screen 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Effective liquid cooling and fan

What is not

  • Extremely poor selfie camera quality
  • Heavy and slippery chassis
  • Inconsistent rear video stabilization
  • No official brand warranty in Bangladesh

Buy it if You are a hardcore gaming enthusiast who prioritizes sustained performance and battery life above all other smartphone features.

Skip it if You want a reliable daily driver with a high-quality camera, official service support, and a comfortable, lightweight design.

Why we say that

Owners report industry-leading performance and gaming capabilities, but the experience is marred by significant compromises in camera quality and physical handling. The under-display selfie camera produces poor results, and rear video stabilization is often inconsistent. While the 7500 mAh battery provides exceptional endurance, the device is heavy, slippery, and prone to smudges.

Because this is an unofficial gray-import device, you are reliant on shop-level support rather than official brand service. Software bugs are an accepted reality of this China-ROM variant, which is common in the local market. This phone is a high-risk, high-reward purchase that demands a specific commitment to mobile gaming at the expense of a balanced flagship experience.
