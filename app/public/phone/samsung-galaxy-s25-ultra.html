Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra Is it worth buying?

The Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra is a powerful device that struggles with significant thermal and battery stability issues following recent software updates.

Choose it if
You prioritize a high-quality display and camera versatility and are comfortable managing potential thermal and battery issues.
Consider alternatives if
You are an S24 Ultra user or require consistent performance without thermal throttling and standby battery drain.

What works well

  • Elite anti-reflective 120Hz LTPO display
  • Versatile 200MP camera system
  • Fluid One UI 7 software experience
  • Strong 9 hour screen on time

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe standby battery drain and overheating
  • Slow 45W charging takes 60 minutes
  • Visible camera banding in night mode
  • Heavy thermal throttling under load

Why we say that

While the display and camera performance remain top-tier, owners frequently report severe standby battery drain and idle overheating. The removal of S Pen Bluetooth functionality and the lack of Qi2 magnetic charging are notable regressions for a flagship device. Users also face uncertainty regarding future AI feature costs, as the subscription model remains undefined.

While the hardware is capable, the experience is marred by heavy thermal throttling during intense gaming sessions and visible camera banding in specific lighting conditions. It is an iterative update that offers little incentive for those already using the previous generation model.

Does the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

The official unit comes with a brand warranty, but the cheaper gray import units do not. If you purchase an unofficial unit, any service center that accepts it will treat the repair as a paid service rather than a warranty claim.

How long will the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra receive software updates?

As a flagship device, the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra is supported with software updates for up to 7 years. This ensures long-term software maintenance compared to the 4 to 6 years provided for the A-series models.

Is the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra good for heavy gaming?

While the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset provides snappy daily performance, owners report heavy thermal throttling during prolonged stress tests. This suggests that the phone may struggle to maintain peak performance during extended gaming sessions.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The camera is versatile with zero shutter lag, but low light performance is considered suboptimal. Additionally, users have reported visible horizontal or vertical streaks known as camera banding when shooting in night mode or high-contrast lighting.
