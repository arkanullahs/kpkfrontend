Is the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • 200MP main camera with OIS
  • 6.9 inch 120Hz LTPO display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite performance
  • 45W fast charging

What is not

  • Severe standby battery drain
  • Thermal throttling under load
  • Night mode banding issue
  • No Qi2 magnetic charging

Buy it if Buy if you need top camera specs and a large display and can tolerate battery quirks.

Skip it if Skip if you expect long standby life, stable cooling, or magnetic wireless charging.

Why we say that

The 6.9 inch 120Hz LTPO display shines with 2600‑nit peak brightness, and the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset feels snappy for most tasks. The 200MP main camera delivers sharp photos and zero shutter lag, though low‑light shots show banding and are not ideal. Battery endurance is decent for active use, giving about nine hours of screen‑on time, but the 45W charger needs an hour for a full charge and feels slow.

Software runs fluidly on One UI 7, yet the AI subscription model remains unclear. Build quality feels premium, but IP68 protection and lack of magnetic Qi2 charging may disappoint power users. Overall, it feels like an incremental upgrade that may not justify the price for current S24 Ultra owners.
