Is the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • Extremely smooth gaming and performance
  • Premium 6.9 inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED display
  • Generally beats Apple and Google in camera quality
  • Improved handling and premium build feel

What is not

  • Severe standby battery drain and idle overheating
  • Visible banding streaks in night mode photos
  • S Pen connectivity and remote features removed
  • Suboptimal low-light camera performance

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize raw performance and a high-quality display for gaming and media consumption.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you rely on consistent battery life or need reliable low-light photography.

Why we say that

While the 200MP camera system generally outperforms competitors, owners frequently report suboptimal low-light results and visible banding streaks in night photos. The device feels premium in the hand, yet the removal of S Pen connectivity and the forced shift to Google Messages have frustrated long-time users. Gaming is extremely smooth, though the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset may throttle during long sessions.

You are paying for a high-end experience that is currently hindered by significant software bugs and hardware limitations. Prospective buyers should weigh the high cost against these widely reported technical flaws before committing to such a large investment.

What is the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra price in Bangladesh?

Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra costs ৳94,490 to ৳1,49,990 in Bangladesh, in stock at 20 shops we track as of 2026-08-29.

Does the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra have good battery life?

Owners rate the battery at 6.8 out of 10. While it offers solid endurance, the capacity is small and charging is slow-ish at 45W. Users also report severe standby battery drain and idle overheating, which became significantly worse after the July 2026 software update.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The low-light photo experience is suboptimal in both behavior and end results. Owners report visible horizontal or vertical streaks appearing in photos taken in Night Mode or high-contrast lighting, an issue that has been recognized by Samsung.

How long will the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra receive software updates?

As a flagship device, the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra is supported with software updates for up to 7 years. The phone currently runs on Android 15 and One UI 7, which users describe as fluid despite complaints about the forced transition to Google Messages.

Is the S Pen still useful on this model?

The S Pen has been stripped of its connectivity and remote features. Owners report that these capabilities have been removed, meaning the stylus no longer functions as it did on previous Ultra models.
