Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra Is it worth buying?

The S25 Ultra feels like a premium flagship but its real‑world reliability is shaky.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want top tier camera and display and can accept a gray‑import warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need an official Samsung warranty or a phone with reliable battery endurance.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 200 MP main camera with optical image stabilization
  • 6.9 inch 120 Hz LTPO AMOLED with 2600 nits peak
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite delivers strong performance
  • 12 GB RAM with up to 1 TB storage

Reported trade-offs4

  • Severe standby battery drain after July 2026 update
  • 45 W charging takes about an hour
  • Thermal throttling under prolonged load
  • Night mode photos show banding

Why we say that

The 6.9 inch LTPO AMOLED shines with 2600 nits peak and smooth 120 Hz motion, while the Snapdragon 8 Elite and 12 GB RAM keep daily tasks snappy. The 200 MP main sensor delivers detailed photos in good light, though night shots suffer banding and low‑light performance is only average. Battery life is decent on paper but severe standby drain and overheating have been reported after the July 2026 software update.

Charging at 45 W is slower than expected, taking about an hour for a full charge. The device arrives as a gray import built for Vietnam and India, so only the official unit carries Samsung’s warranty; the cheaper listings rely on paid repairs. Owners also miss the S Pen Bluetooth feature, magnetic Qi2 charging and are uncertain about future AI subscription costs.

The newer S26 Ultra costs about the same and usually offers a better overall package.
