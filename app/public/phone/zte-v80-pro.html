Buying verdict

ZTE V80 Pro Is it worth buying?

The ZTE V80 Pro is a device that struggles to justify its ৳21,000 price tag due to significant performance and display limitations.

Choose it if
You prioritize a large screen and high storage capacity for basic tasks and do not mind the lack of official warranty support.
Consider alternatives if
You need a phone for gaming, multitasking, or high-quality photography, as the hardware limitations will likely frustrate you.

What works well

  • Large 6.75 inch 120Hz display
  • High resolution 108MP main camera
  • Generous 8GB RAM and 256GB storage
  • Includes a dedicated headphone jack

Reported trade-offs

  • Sluggish performance and multitasking
  • Low display resolution and thick bezels
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Lack of stereo speakers

Why we say that

While it offers a large 6.75 inch screen and a 108MP camera, owners report that the Unisoc T7280 chipset and eMMC 5.1 storage lead to noticeable sluggishness during daily tasks and multitasking. The display resolution is a major point of contention, with users describing it as a poor experience that leads to instant rejection. You will find the build quality feels premium at first glance, but the plastic frame is prone to micro-scratches.

The camera is usable in daylight but fails to impress in low-light conditions or with dynamic range. Software is current with Android 16, yet the presence of bloatware requires manual cleanup. Since this is an unofficial import, you receive no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh, and any repairs must be paid for out of pocket at the shop level.

Does the ZTE V80 Pro come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

Is the ZTE V80 Pro suitable for mobile gaming?

Owners rate the gaming performance very low at 3.0 out of 10. The combination of the Unisoc T7280 chipset and eMMC 5.1 storage results in sluggishness that makes it unsuitable for demanding games.

The 108MP main sensor is usable in daylight, but it struggles significantly with dynamic range and low-light photography. Users have rated the camera performance at 5.0 out of 10.
