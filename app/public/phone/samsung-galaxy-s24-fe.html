Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy S24 FE Is it worth buying?

If you want a big, bright screen and a solid camera for everyday snaps, the Galaxy S24 FE delivers that but it shows its age in charging and gaming.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value a large, vivid screen, solid daytime camera and long software updates for everyday use.
Consider alternatives if
You need fast charging, thin bezels, or top‑tier gaming performance.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Bright 1900 nits Dynamic AMOLED display
  • Strong daylight and portrait camera
  • Premium glass metal build with IP68
  • Long software support up to seven years

Reported trade-offs4

  • Slow 25W wired charging
  • Thick uneven screen bezels
  • Thermal throttling in demanding games
  • Battery drains quickly under heavy use

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X panel reaches 1900 nits and runs at 120Hz, making media consumption pleasant. The 50MP main sensor produces sharp daylight photos and good portraits, while the premium glass‑metal build with IP68 feels sturdy. Android 14 with One UI 8.5 brings up to seven years of updates, which is a strong point for long‑term owners.

However, the 25W wired charger is slow for a flagship price, the bezels are noticeably thick and uneven, and the Exynos 2400e heats up quickly in demanding games, causing throttling. Battery life is average for light use but drops fast under heavy tasks. Overall it feels like a capable daily driver that struggles with power‑hungry scenarios.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 4700 mAh battery gives about a day of light use, but owners report it drains quickly when gaming or using heavy apps, earning a rating of 6.4 out of 10 for battery life.

Will the phone overheat while playing games?

During 3D or demanding games the Exynos 2400e heats up fast and the phone throttles, so performance drops and the device can feel uncomfortable to hold.

How good is the camera in low light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor captures sharp photos in daylight and does well with portraits, but in low light scenes and at high zoom the results are noisy and lack detail.

What warranty and software updates does the Galaxy S24 FE receive?
