Buying verdict

Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max Is it worth buying?

The Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max delivers top-tier performance and industry-leading video quality, but it comes with notable build and software frustrations.

Choose it if
You need a high-performance device for intensive gaming and professional-grade video recording.
Consider alternatives if
You want a durable build that resists dents or a bug-free software experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Industry-leading video recording quality
  • Excellent sustained gaming performance
  • Bright 3000 nits peak display
  • Reliable all-day battery endurance

Reported trade-offs

  • Aluminum frame prone to dents
  • iOS 26 has UI stutters
  • Green tint at low brightness
  • Aggressive image processing for stills

Why we say that

Owners report that the aluminum frame is softer than previous titanium models, making it prone to dents and scratches if dropped. While the 6.9 inch display is bright and accurate, some users notice a green tint at low brightness levels. The software experience is currently marred by UI stutters and a chaotic menu design in iOS 26.

Despite these issues, the vapor chamber cooling system keeps the A19 Pro chipset running well during heavy gaming sessions. You get reliable all-day battery life, though the physical SIM variant has a slightly lower capacity than the eSIM-only version. It is a powerful device for those who prioritize raw performance and video recording, provided you can overlook the current software bugs and the delicate frame.
