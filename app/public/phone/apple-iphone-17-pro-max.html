Is the Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max worth buying?

What is good

  • 3000 nits peak brightness display
  • 12GB RAM delivers smooth performance
  • 48MP main camera with optical image stabilization
  • 15W fast charging support

What is not

  • Aluminum frame scratches and dents easily
  • iOS 26 UI stutters in menus
  • Green tint visible at very low brightness
  • No charger included in the box

Buy it if Buy if you want a premium screen, strong gaming performance and can afford the high price.

Skip it if Skip if you need long charging speed, a flawless software experience or a more durable frame.

Why we say that

The 6.9 inch LTPO Super Retina XDR OLED panel reaches 3000 nits peak brightness and shows accurate colors, though a slight green tint appears at very low brightness. The Apple A19 Pro chip with 12GB RAM delivers buttery smooth gaming and handles everyday tasks without lag, and the vapor chamber keeps the device cooler than the previous model. Battery endurance is rated excellent with a 4823 mAh cell, yet charging is limited to 15W and no charger is included in the box.

The 48MP main camera with OIS is reliable for most scenes, but some Android rivals still outperform it in low light. Owners complain about the aluminum frame scratching more easily than titanium and about iOS 26 UI stuttering in menus. Overall it feels like an incremental upgrade that costs a lot.
