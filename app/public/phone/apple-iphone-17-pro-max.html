Is the Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max worth buying?

If you can afford a premium flagship, the iPhone 17 Pro Max delivers flagship performance with a large, bright display and solid battery life, but iOS 26 feels chaotic. The 6.9 inch LTPO Super Retina XDR OLED panel runs at 120Hz and can peak at 3000 nits, making outdoor use easy. Under the hood the Apple A19 Pro 3 nm chip paired with 12GB RAM scores 9.2 for performance and 8.8 for gaming, so heavy games run smoothly. The 48MP main camera with f/1.8 aperture, OIS and 4.0x telephoto produces reliably great photos, though it is not class‑leading in low light. Battery capacity of 4823 mAh gives excellent endurance, confirmed by owners reporting 113 cycles at full health. Build quality is good with aluminum body, water resistance and a vapor chamber for heat. The main complaints are the chaotic iOS 26 experience and difficulty moving WhatsApp chats from Android.
