Is the Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max worth buying?

If you can afford a premium price, the iPhone 17 Pro Max gives you a large, bright display and strong performance for daily tasks. The 6.9 inch LTPO OLED screen reaches up to 3000 nits at peak, making outdoor use easy. The Apple A19 Pro chip with 12GB RAM handles apps, multitasking and games without lag. Battery life is praised as excellent, and the 4823 mAh cell stays healthy after more than a hundred full cycles. Camera quality is solid for most scenes, though it does not lead the class in low‑light shots. Owners love the smooth iOS experience without ads, but many complain that iOS 26 feels chaotic and that moving WhatsApp chats from Android is difficult. Overall the phone feels premium, but the software quirks and price may deter some buyers.
