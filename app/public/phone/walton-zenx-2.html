Is the Walton ZENX 2 worth buying?

What is good

  • Very solid and rugged build quality
  • Great battery endurance of 1.5 days
  • Large 6.8 inch 120Hz display
  • Light Android 14 software skin

What is not

  • Painfully slow 10W charging speed
  • Poor multitasking and app performance
  • Insufficient 64GB internal storage
  • Average camera with poor edge detection

Buy it if You should buy this phone if you prioritize a rugged, durable build above all other technical specifications.

Skip it if You should skip this phone if you need a device for gaming, heavy multitasking, or fast charging.

Why we say that

While the 6.8 inch 120Hz display offers a large viewing area, owners report that it struggles in direct sunlight and features an outdated notch design. Living with this phone requires patience, as the Unisoc Tiger T615 chipset frequently causes app delays and multitasking crashes. Owners specifically complain that the 64GB storage is insufficient for 2025 and that the device cannot handle more than three or four applications at once.

The battery life is a highlight, lasting up to 1.5 days, but the 10W charging speed is a major frustration. Users report that a full charge takes about three hours, which is painfully slow by modern standards. While the software is a light Android 14 skin, you should expect very few updates and some UI bugs.

This phone is built for those who need a tough device that survives drops, but it is not designed for users who demand speed or high-end camera performance.

How long does the battery last on the Walton ZENX 2?

The battery provides great endurance and can last up to 1.5 days on a single charge. However, you must be prepared for the charging process, as the 10W charger is extremely slow and takes about three hours to reach a full charge.

Can I play heavy games on this phone?

This device is not suitable for heavy gaming. Owners report that the performance is limited, and the phone can only handle 2D or light games. You will likely experience noticeable app delays and crashes if you try to push the hardware too hard.

How is the camera performance in low light?

The camera performance is generally average in daylight conditions. When you move to low light, the images become soft. Additionally, the camera struggles with portrait photography, specifically regarding poor edge detection around the subject.

Will this phone receive regular software updates?

The phone runs on Android 14 with a light skin and low bloat. However, owners should not expect frequent support, as there are rarely any software updates provided for this device. You may also encounter occasional UI bugs during daily use.
