Why we say that

The build feels very solid and rugged, which matches the brand’s durability claim. The 6.8 inch IPS LCD screen runs at 120Hz, giving smooth scrolling, yet the resolution is low and the notch looks dated, especially under bright sunlight. Battery life is a strong point, but the 10W charger is painfully slow.

Performance from the Unisoc Tiger T615 with 4GB RAM feels sluggish; multitasking often crashes after opening three apps and only simple games run without lag. The 5MP selfie camera is average in daylight but produces soft images in low light and struggles with portrait edges. Storage of 64GB quickly fills up with apps and media.

How long does the battery last and how fast does it charge?

The 5000 mAh battery can keep the phone alive for about one and a half days under normal use. Charging is limited to 10W, so a full charge takes roughly three hours, which many owners find very slow.

Will this phone handle games and multitasking well?

Performance is driven by the Unisoc Tiger T615 with 4 GB RAM. Owners report noticeable app delays, crashes when more than three apps run, and only simple 2D games run smoothly, so heavy gaming is not realistic.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

In daylight the 5 MP selfie camera produces average pictures, but in low light the images become soft and the portrait edge detection fails, making night selfies look blurry and lacking detail.

Does the phone come with an official warranty and will it receive updates?

The phone is sold through official channels at ৳9,999 and carries the brand’s warranty, so you can claim service at any authorized Walton centre. Android 14 updates are rare and major upgrades are unlikely.
