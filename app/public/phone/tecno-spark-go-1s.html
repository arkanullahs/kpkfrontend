Is the Tecno Spark Go 1S worth buying?

What is good

  • Excellent display quality
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • Has an ir blaster
  • 90Hz refresh rate

What is not

  • Poor build quality
  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • Incompatible apps
  • Slow performance

Buy it if You only need a cheap phone with a good screen for basic viewing.

Skip it if You need a durable phone for gaming or installing many different apps.

Why we say that

While the display is praised as excellent, owners report significant issues with build quality and performance. Many users find the device defective or broken shortly after purchase. You will deal with high amounts of bloatware and ads on a slow software system.

Some apps are completely incompatible and will not install. Basic features like double tap to wake or raise to wake are missing. There is also no internal radio.

Since only gray import stock is available, you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller because you will get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty.
