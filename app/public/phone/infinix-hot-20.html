Is the Infinix Hot 20 worth buying?

What is good

  • 50 MP main camera for daylight shots
  • 6.82‑inch 90 Hz IPS LCD display
  • 5000 mAh battery capacity
  • headphone jack and FM radio

What is not

  • Slow charging and sudden shutdowns
  • Frequent UI glitches and ad‑heavy XOS
  • Overheating even when idle
  • Fingerprint sensor often fails

Buy it if Buy it if you need a large display, long battery life and official warranty at a low price and can accept performance limits.

Skip it if Skip it if you need smooth gaming, reliable software or future Android updates.

Why we say that

The 6.82‑inch 90 Hz panel makes videos and scrolling feel smooth, yet the low resolution hurts visibility in bright sunlight. The 5000 mAh cell gives decent endurance, but slow charging and occasional sudden shutdowns are reported. The 50 MP main sensor can capture acceptable daylight photos, but dynamic range suffers and low‑light shots are weak.

Helio G85 handles basic tasks, but multitasking and modern games feel laggy and the device can overheat even when turned off. XOS 12 is loaded with ads and UI glitches such as unresponsive keyboards and home‑screen freezes. The fingerprint sensor is known to fail hardware‑wise, requiring replacement.

Official warranty is available at the cheapest price of ৳16,699, yet the phone is four years old so future Android updates are unlikely. Overall it works for casual use if you can tolerate the quirks.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 5000 mAh cell provides decent endurance for a full day of moderate use, but slow charging means you cannot quickly top up. Some owners report sudden shutdowns, so keep a charger handy.

Can I play games like Roblox on this phone?

The Helio G85 struggles with multitasking and modern games. Users notice lag and frame drops in Roblox and similar titles, so the device is not suited for demanding gaming sessions.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor captures acceptable photos in daylight, but reviews mention poor dynamic range and slow app response. In low light the images become noisy and lack detail.

Will I receive software updates and is the warranty official?

The phone comes with the brand’s official warranty, valid through the listed official channels. However, it is four years old, so expect only one or two more OS updates and limited future support.
