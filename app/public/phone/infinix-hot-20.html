Infinix Hot 20 Is it worth buying?

Living with the Infinix Hot 20 is challenging because owners report significant hardware and software reliability issues.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a basic device for simple tasks and can tolerate significant software ads and potential hardware maintenance.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on your phone for gaming, multitasking, or stable performance without constant system errors.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Large 6.82 inch 90Hz display
  • Frosted back resists fingerprints
  • Decent 5000 mAh battery capacity
  • 50MP main camera for daylight

Reported trade-offs4

  • Frequent system UI and keyboard glitches
  • Overheating issues even when powered off
  • Fingerprint scanner hardware failure
  • Ad-heavy and bloatware-filled XOS software

Why we say that

While the 5000 mAh battery offers decent capacity, users frequently experience slow charging and sudden shutdowns. The Helio G85 chipset struggles with modern apps and multitasking, leading to lag in games like ML and Roblox. Owners are particularly frustrated by the XOS software, which is described as ad-heavy and prone to UI glitches like unresponsive keyboards or frozen home screens.

Hardware concerns are also prevalent, with reports of fingerprint scanners failing entirely and requiring physical replacement. The device suffers from overheating, even when powered off, and some users note weak Wi-Fi signal strength. Given that this 2022 model is four years old, you should expect little to no remaining software support.

Read full reasoning

The large 90Hz display is hampered by low resolution and poor sunlight visibility, making it difficult to use outdoors. Overall, the combination of performance stutters and recurring system errors makes this a difficult device to recommend for daily use.
