Is the Apple iPhone 13 Mini worth buying?

What is good

  • Compact 5.4 inch Super Retina XDR OLED display
  • Powerful Apple A15 Bionic chipset
  • Capable 12MP main camera with OIS
  • Supports 5G connectivity

What is not

  • Poor battery life due to small capacity
  • Limited software update lifespan remaining
  • Only gray-import stock with shop warranty

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a small, pocketable device and are willing to carry a power bank.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you need reliable all-day battery life or long-term software support.

Why we say that

Living with this phone means accepting that the 2438 mAh battery will struggle to last through a full day of heavy use. While the A15 Bionic chipset remains capable for daily tasks, the device is now 5 years old, meaning you should expect little to no remaining software support. Owners frequently report poor battery life because the small physical size cannot keep up with the power draw of the hardware.

Since only gray-import stock is available in Bangladesh, you will rely on a shop warranty rather than an official brand warranty. You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your 58,990 taka. This phone is a niche choice for those who specifically want a small screen, but it requires compromises on longevity and battery reliability.

Will the battery last through a full day of heavy usage?

No, the battery life is widely reported as poor. The 2438 mAh battery is physically small and struggles to manage the power draw of the device, so you will likely need to charge it multiple times throughout the day.

Does this phone come with an official Apple warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there is no official brand warranty available for this device. You will only find gray-import stock, which means you must rely on a shop warranty. Always confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

How many more years of software updates will this phone receive?

This phone was released in 2021 and is now 5 years old. You should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point, despite the device being upgradable to iOS 26.6.

Is this device suitable for mobile gaming?

The phone features the Apple A15 Bionic chipset, which provides strong performance. However, the small battery capacity and the tendency for poor endurance mean that intensive gaming sessions will drain the power very quickly.
