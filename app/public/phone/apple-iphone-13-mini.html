Is the Apple iPhone 13 Mini worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 1200 nits OLED display
  • No bloatware or ads
  • Powerful A15 Bionic chipset
  • Compact 5.4 inch size

What is not

  • Poor battery endurance
  • Short remaining software update life
  • No official brand warranty

Buy it if You want a very small phone with a high quality screen and no bloatware.

Skip it if You need a battery that lasts all day or want official brand warranty.

Why we say that

The small physical battery cannot keep up with the power draw, leading to poor endurance. It is a compact phone from 2021 that still packs the A15 Bionic chip and a bright Super Retina XDR OLED screen. You get a clean iOS experience without ads or bloatware.

However, this phone is now 5 years old. You should expect very little remaining software update life. Since only gray import stock is available in Bangladesh, you will get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty.

You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before paying ৳58,990.
