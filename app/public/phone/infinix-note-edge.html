Buying verdict

Infinix Note Edge Is it worth buying?

The Infinix Note Edge is a polarizing device that prioritizes a massive battery and a bright display over consistent performance and software quality.

Choose it if
You prioritize a very bright display and a large battery for basic daily tasks and do not mind the presence of ads.
Consider alternatives if
You are a mobile gamer or someone who expects a clean software experience without heating issues.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Massive 6500 mAh battery capacity
  • Stunning 1.5K AMOLED 120Hz display
  • Very slim 7.2mm build
  • Official warranty and service network

Reported trade-offs

  • Heavy bloatware and notification ads
  • Recurring heating during charging and usage
  • Unplayable performance in heavy games
  • No microSD card slot

Why we say that

While the 6500 mAh battery is a standout feature, owners frequently report erratic drain and significant heating issues during both charging and daily use. The 1.5K AMOLED screen is visually impressive with its 4500 nits peak brightness, yet the limited Always-On Display functionality frustrates many users. You will find the software experience hampered by heavy bloatware and persistent notification ads.

Gaming performance is notably weak, with heavy titles like Wuthering Waves proving unplayable. The camera setup lacks an ultrawide lens and struggles with grain in low-light conditions. Many buyers feel the device is overpriced compared to alternatives from Poco or Redmi.

Read full reasoning

If you prioritize a slim 7.2mm build and official warranty support, this phone might catch your eye, but the recurring heat complaints and software clutter make it a difficult recommendation for power users.

Does the Infinix Note Edge support expandable storage?

No, the phone does not include a microSD card slot. Owners have expressed frustration regarding this design choice, as there is no way to increase the internal storage capacity beyond the 128GB or 256GB variants.

How long will the Infinix Note Edge receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 16 and is promised up to 3 major Android upgrades. However, users should note that the XOS 16 software is known for being slow and heavily loaded with preinstalled apps and ads.

Is the Infinix Note Edge suitable for heavy gaming?

The phone is not recommended for heavy gaming. While it handles basic daily use, owners report that demanding titles like Wuthering Waves are unplayable. The device also suffers from recurring heating issues during intensive tasks.

What is the quality of the camera in low light?

The main 50MP camera provides decent results in good lighting, but it struggles significantly in darker environments. Owners report that low-light performance is grainy, and the lack of an ultrawide lens limits your photography options.
