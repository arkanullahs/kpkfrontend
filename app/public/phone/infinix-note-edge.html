Infinix Note Edge Is it worth buying?

If you want a big, bright screen and a massive battery and can live with heating and ads, the Infinix Note Edge will get the job done.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a big screen, long battery life and can accept ads and occasional heating.
Consider alternatives if
You want smooth heavy gaming or a clean, ad‑free software experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Large 6,500 mAh battery
  • Bright 1.5K AMOLED display
  • 120 Hz refresh rate
  • 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs4

  • 45W charging feels slow
  • Phone gets hot while charging
  • Low‑light camera is grainy
  • No microSD card slot

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch AMOLED panel shines with 120 Hz refresh and peaks at 4500 nits, making outdoor use easy. The 6500 mAh cell gives long endurance, though some owners notice occasional rapid drain. Charging at 45W fills the huge battery in about an hour and a half, which feels slow for the size.

The Dimensity 7100 handles daily tasks fine, but heavy games such as Wuthering Waves stumble and the device gets noticeably hot while charging. The 50 MP main sensor captures decent photos in daylight but struggles with grain in low light and lacks an ultrawide lens. XOS 16 brings Android 16 and up to three major upgrades, but it is packed with ads and preinstalled apps.

Overall the phone feels premium in some areas but falls short in performance and software cleanliness.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 6,500 mAh battery is rated high by owners and can easily last a full day of mixed use. Some users report occasional rapid drain, but overall endurance is one of the phone’s strongest points.

Can I play heavy games like Wuthering Waves on this phone?

Heavy games such as Wuthering Waves are unplayable on the Infinix Note Edge. The Dimensity 7100 handles everyday apps well, but performance drops and the device heats up during demanding gaming sessions.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor captures decent daylight photos, but low‑light images are grainy and lack detail. There is no ultrawide lens, so versatility in dim environments is limited.

Will the phone receive official warranty and software updates?

Yes, the phone comes with the brand’s official warranty and can be serviced through the official BD network. It runs Android 16 with XOS 16 and is promised up to three major Android upgrades, though updates may be slower than on some competitors.
