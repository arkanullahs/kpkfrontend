Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy F15 5G Is it worth buying?

The Samsung Galaxy F15 5G will keep you online for two days, but expect noticeable lag in everyday use.

Choose it if
You need long battery life, 5G connectivity, and a large vibrant screen at a budget price.
Consider alternatives if
You expect flagship speed, smooth gaming, or an official Samsung warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • 6.6‑inch Super AMOLED with 90 Hz refresh
  • 50 MP main camera works well in daylight
  • Headphone jack for wired audio

Reported trade-offs

  • Slow 25 W charging without charger in box
  • UI stutters and apps reload frequently
  • Low‑light camera performance is poor
  • Heavy bulky plastic build feels massive

Why we say that

Its 6000 mAh battery truly lasts between one and a half and two days with moderate use, and the 6.6‑inch Super AMOLED panel offers vivid colours and a smooth 90 Hz refresh. The Dimensity 6100+ chipset combined with 4‑8 GB RAM handles basic apps, yet the UI feels stuttery and apps often reload, making multitasking feel sluggish. Gaming is limited to low graphics settings; PUBG runs only on the lowest frame rate.

The 50 MP main sensor captures decent daylight photos, but low‑light shots are noisy and processing is inconsistent. Charging is slow at 25 W and the box does not include a charger, so you need to buy one separately. The phone arrives as a gray‑import, so there is no official Samsung warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited guarantee and any repair costs are out of pocket.

Read full reasoning

Software runs Android 14 with One UI 8.5 and promises updates up to Android 16, but the system includes ads and lacks a native screen recorder.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 6000 mAh cell can keep the phone alive for about one and a half to two days with normal usage such as browsing, social media and video playback. Heavy gaming will reduce that time noticeably.

Can I play demanding games like PUBG on this phone?

The Dimensity 6100+ chipset with 4‑8 GB RAM can run PUBG only on the lowest graphics settings. You will notice lag and occasional frame drops, so the device is not suited for high‑performance gaming.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

In daylight the 50 MP sensor produces bright images, but in low‑light the pictures become noisy and the processing is inconsistent. Expect reduced detail and colour accuracy when shooting at night.
