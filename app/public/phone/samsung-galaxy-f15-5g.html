Samsung Galaxy F15 Is it worth buying?

The Galaxy F15 will keep you online for two days but will test your patience with lag and ads.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
If you need long battery life, a bright AMOLED screen and can tolerate some lag, this phone may suit you.
Consider alternatives if
If you expect flagship performance, strong low‑light photography or an official Samsung warranty, look elsewhere.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • 6.6‑inch Super AMOLED with 90 Hz refresh
  • 50 MP main camera captures bright daylight shots
  • Headphone jack for wired audio

Reported trade-offs4

  • Slow 25 W charging without included charger
  • UI stutters and apps reload often
  • Low‑light photos are noisy and dark
  • Heavy plastic body feels bulky

Why we say that

Its 6000 mAh cell easily stretches to 1.5‑2 days of mixed use, and the 6.6‑inch Super AMOLED with 90 Hz looks vibrant. The 50 MP main sensor snaps decent daylight pictures, while the 13 MP selfie camera is adequate. However, the plastic chassis feels heavy and the UI often stutters, making simple tasks feel slower.

Gaming is limited to low settings and the 25 W charger is slow, especially since no charger comes in the box. Software promises Android 14 upgradable to 16, but the setup is ad‑heavy and lacks a native screen recorder. There is no official Samsung warranty; you must rely on shop warranty and verify IMEI status before buying.
