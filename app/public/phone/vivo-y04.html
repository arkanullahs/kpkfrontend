Buying verdict

Vivo Y04 Is it worth buying?

The Vivo Y04 will get you through a full day without charging, but expect sluggish performance and a very slow camera app.

Choose it if
If you need an inexpensive phone that lasts a day and you only use basic apps, it may suit you.
Consider alternatives if
If you want smooth gaming, fast charging, or reliable navigation sensors, skip it.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • large 5,500 mAh battery
  • 90 Hz refresh rate
  • IP64 dust and splash protection
  • headphone jack and FM radio

Reported trade-offs

  • 15 W charging takes about ninety minutes
  • slow 720p LCD lacks sharpness
  • no gyroscope or magnetometer
  • keyboard lag and system stutter

Why we say that

At ৳14,999 it offers a large 5,500 mAh battery, a 6.74‑inch 90 Hz LCD and IP64 protection, which many users find useful for basic media consumption. The plastic build feels cheap and the phone lacks a gyroscope and magnetometer, so navigation apps are unreliable. The 13 MP rear sensor produces mediocre pictures and the camera app opens slowly, while the 5 W charger needs about ninety minutes for a full charge.

The 720p display is bright enough at 570 nits but looks soft and lacks detail. Under heavy use the device heats quickly and the keyboard feels laggy, making gaming and multitasking uncomfortable. Software runs Android 14 with medium bloat, and updates are limited.

Overall it is a budget‑friendly device for simple tasks, but it falls short for power users.
