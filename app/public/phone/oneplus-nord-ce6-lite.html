Is the OnePlus Nord CE6 Lite worth buying?

What is good

  • 7,000 mAh battery lasting multiple days
  • 45 W fast charging refills quickly
  • Dimensity 7400 Apex gives smooth performance
  • 144 Hz IPS LCD is PWM‑friendly

What is not

  • No NFC support
  • No 3.5 mm headphone jack
  • Heavy plastic build feels cheap
  • Camera quality disappointing

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, fast charging, and a smooth Android experience without needing NFC or a top‑tier camera.

Skip it if Skip it if you rely on NFC, demand a high‑quality camera, or prefer an AMOLED display.

Why we say that

Its 7,000 mAh cell easily lasts two to three days of average use, and the 45 W charger refills it quickly. The Dimensity 7400 Apex chip paired with UFS 3.1 storage delivers smooth Android 16 performance and decent 144 Hz IPS LCD responsiveness, which many PWM‑sensitive users appreciate. Build quality is IP64 rated yet feels heavy and plastic, and the lack of NFC and a 3.5 mm jack disappoints many.

The 50 MP main sensor underperforms in low light, leading to poor camera scores. Software runs OxygenOS 16 with low bloat and receives about four Android version updates, but only shop warranty is available for gray‑import units.
