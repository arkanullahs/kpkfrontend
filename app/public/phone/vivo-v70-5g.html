Is the Vivo V70 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 6500 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 6.59‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED display
  • 50 MP main and selfie cameras with OIS
  • IP68/IP69 water and dust resistance

What is not

  • Ultrawide camera is soft and low detail
  • Gaming performance throttles on Snapdragon 7 Gen 4
  • No eSIM support
  • Gray import units lack official warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you value long battery life, solid main camera and water resistance over top gaming performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need strong ultrawide shots, eSIM or a fully supported warranty.

Why we say that

Its 6500 mAh battery and 90 W charger keep you online from sunrise to night even with gaming and video streaming. The 6.59‑inch AMOLED panel shines at 5000 nits peak and scrolls smoothly at 120 Hz, making media consumption pleasant. The 50 MP main sensor with OIS and the same resolution selfie camera capture clear photos in good light, while the 3× telephoto adds flexibility.

Build quality feels premium with an aluminum frame and IP68/IP69 protection. However, the 8 MP ultrawide lens is soft and lacks detail, and the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chip throttles during demanding games. OriginOS 6 feels polished but carries medium bloat and ads.

The phone does not support eSIM and gray import units have no official warranty, requiring paid repairs if issues arise.
