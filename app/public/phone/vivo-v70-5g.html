Is the Vivo V70 worth buying?

What is good

  • Exceptional 6500 mAh battery endurance
  • Premium aluminum frame with IP68/IP69 resistance
  • Bright 1.5K AMOLED display with narrow bezels
  • Strong main and telephoto camera performance

What is not

  • Ultrawide camera lacks sharpness and detail
  • Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 throttles during gaming
  • Software includes bloatware and ad-heavy apps
  • No eSIM support or pro video features

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a device that lasts all day on a single charge and offers a high-quality display for media consumption.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a mobile gamer or a photography enthusiast who relies heavily on ultrawide shots and professional video tools.

Why we say that

Owners consistently praise the massive 6500 mAh battery for lasting through heavy usage, yet they report that the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset throttles when pushed. While the 50MP main and telephoto cameras perform well, the ultrawide lens is a significant disappointment due to a lack of detail and sharpness. The build quality feels premium with its aluminum frame and high water resistance, but the software experience is marred by bloatware and ad-heavy applications.

Users also express frustration over the lack of eSIM support and the absence of professional video features. If you prioritize battery life and a bright display over gaming power and ultrawide photography, this device serves as a reliable daily companion.
