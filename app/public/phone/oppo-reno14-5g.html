Is the OPPO Reno14 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts a full day
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • 120 Hz AMOLED display with high brightness
  • 80 W fast charging fills the battery in about an hour

What is not

  • Official price far above market value
  • ColorOS includes noticeable bloatware
  • Device gets hot during 4K recording
  • Ultrawide camera is weak

Buy it if Buy it if you need a large battery, solid main camera and can accept the high official price or a gray import with paid warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect top gaming performance, low light ultrawide shots or a clean software experience without extra ads.

Why we say that

The 6.57 inch AMOLED panel runs at 120Hz and reaches 1400 nits, so outdoor visibility is good but it is not LTPO. The Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 paired with 12GB RAM handles daily tasks smoothly, yet gaming shows occasional frame drops and the phone gets hot during 4K recording. The 50MP main sensor with OIS captures strong portraits, while the ultrawide lens is weak and struggles in dim settings.

ColorOS 15 feels snappy but is filled with bloatware and ads that many owners find annoying. Official units cost at least ৳56,990 and include a brand warranty, but the price is far above similar specs in the market, and the newer Reno15 sells for about the same price. Gray import models are cheaper at ৳48,990 but lack manufacturer warranty and require paid repairs.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

The 6000 mAh battery typically survives a full day of heavy use, and owners report it can handle a day of mixed browsing, streaming and gaming without needing a charge. Fast 80 W charging restores it in about an hour.

Will the phone overheat while gaming or recording video?

During 4K video capture and intensive games the phone becomes noticeably warm and the heat can be felt through the metal frame. The issue does not stop the device from working but it is a common complaint.

Is the camera good in low light and for ultrawide shots?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS delivers strong portraits and good low light results, but the 8 MP ultrawide lens is weak and struggles in dim environments. Auto mode often adds unwanted artifacts.

What warranty coverage do I get with the official and gray import listings?

The official listing includes the brand warranty but only for the genuine unit, while the cheaper gray import does not carry manufacturer cover and any repair at a service centre is charged. Choose the official version if warranty matters.
