Realme Narzo 100 Lite Is it worth buying?

The Realme Narzo 100 Lite is a massive battery device that prioritizes longevity over camera quality or charging speed.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a phone with a massive battery for long hours of use and do not mind slow charging or unofficial warranty status.
Consider alternatives if
You want a reliable manufacturer warranty, fast charging, or high quality cameras for photography.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Massive 7000 mAh battery capacity
  • Smooth 144Hz IPS LCD display
  • Includes a dedicated headphone jack
  • Supports 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs4

  • Very slow 15W charging speed
  • Basic 13MP and 5MP camera sensors
  • No official manufacturer warranty support
  • High amount of bloatware and ads

Why we say that

Living with this phone means carrying a heavy device that lasts a long time, but you will spend a significant amount of time waiting for it to charge at 15W. The 144Hz display makes scrolling feel smooth, though the 13MP main camera and 5MP selfie camera are basic and struggle to capture high detail. Users often find the software experience cluttered with medium to high levels of bloatware and ads.

Because this is an unofficial import, you must accept that there is no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh. Any repairs required will be paid out of your own pocket, and you are limited to whatever limited support the individual shop provides. It is a functional device for those who value screen size and battery capacity above all else, provided you are comfortable with the risks of gray market hardware.

Does this phone come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available through unofficial gray import channels. There is no manufacturer warranty support available for this device in Bangladesh. You must rely solely on the limited warranty provided by the specific shop where you purchase the unit.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The Realme Narzo 100 Lite is expected to receive between 2 to 3 OS updates. It currently runs on Android 16 with Realme UI 7.0 out of the box. You should expect a medium to high amount of pre-installed bloatware and advertisements in the software.

Is the battery life good for heavy usage?

The phone features a 7000 mAh battery, which is designed to provide extensive usage time. However, keep in mind that the device only supports 15W charging. This means that while the battery lasts a long time, it will take a significant amount of time to recharge fully.

How does the camera perform for daily photography?

The device is equipped with a 13MP main camera and a 5MP selfie camera. These are basic sensors that are not designed for high-end photography. You should expect standard image quality that may struggle in challenging lighting conditions compared to more expensive models.
