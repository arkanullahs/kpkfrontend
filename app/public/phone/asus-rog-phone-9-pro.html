Buying verdict

Asus ROG Phone 9 Pro Is it worth buying?

Expect a phone that delivers desktop level gaming power but demands extra cooling and accepts limited software support.

Choose it if
You are a hardcore mobile gamer who values raw performance and a high refresh display above all else.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable camera, long term software support, or official warranty coverage.

What works well

  • 185Hz LTPO AMOLED display with 2500 nit peak brightness
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset with 16 GB RAM
  • 5800 mAh battery with true bypass charging
  • Headphone jack and dual USB C ports

Reported trade-offs

  • Only two years of OS updates
  • Thermal throttling without external cooler
  • Software bugs and unstable gaming UI
  • 65W charging feels slow versus 100W rivals

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED panel runs at 185 Hz and can reach 2500 nits, making games look smooth and bright. Under the hood sits a Snapdragon 8 Elite processor paired with 16 GB RAM and up to 1 TB storage, so frame rates stay high. Battery life is strong thanks to a 5800 mAh cell and true bypass charging, and the 65 W charger refills it in a reasonable time.

The device keeps a headphone jack and two USB C ports, which many gamers appreciate. However the phone throttles when it gets warm, and without the AeroActive Cooler performance drops. Software can be buggy and only two major Android updates are promised.

How many major Android updates will the phone receive?

The ROG Phone 9 Pro is promised only two major Android updates after launch, which means it will receive Android 15 and one subsequent version before support ends.

Is there any official warranty for this phone in Bangladesh?

No official Asus warranty exists for this model in Bangladesh because it is a gray import unit; only the shop’s limited warranty applies and any repair will be paid by the buyer after confirming IMEI and BTRC status.

How does the battery perform during long gaming sessions?

The 5800 mAh battery gives excellent endurance and true bypass charging keeps the charge level stable during long games, but the device can become warm and may throttle performance if it gets too hot.

Is the camera suitable for low light photography?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS and a 3x telephoto lens is decent for a gaming phone, but low light shots often show reddish skin tones and the overall rating is only moderate.
