Asus ROG Phone 9 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you need raw gaming power and can tolerate a fragile support situation, the ROG Phone 9 Pro delivers.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You are a hardcore mobile gamer who values top‑tier performance above all else.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable daily camera or long‑term software support.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 185 Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset
  • 58 Wh battery with true bypass charging
  • 24 GB RAM with up to 1 TB storage

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official Bangladeshi warranty
  • Software bugs and limited updates
  • Thermal throttling without external cooler
  • Charging feels slow compared to rivals

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset and 24 GB RAM make every game feel instant, while the 185 Hz LTPO AMOLED panel stays bright even in sunlight. Battery life is excellent with a 5,800 mAh cell and true bypass charging, though the 65 W charger feels slower than newer 100 W+ options. Owners love the premium glass‑aluminum build and dual USB‑C ports, but the phone runs hot and throttles without the optional AeroActive Cooler.

Software is feature‑rich for gamers but buggy, and only two major Android updates are promised, which many consider too few for the price. The camera is serviceable for a gaming device but struggles with skin tones and low‑light scenes. No official Bangladeshi warranty exists, so you rely on the shop’s warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
