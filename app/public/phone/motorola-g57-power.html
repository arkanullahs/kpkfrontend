Motorola G57 Power Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day without hunting for a charger, the G57 Power delivers that endurance.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value long battery life and solid performance over camera excellence and official warranty.
Consider alternatives if

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 7000 mAh battery lasts over two days
  • Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 runs daily apps smoothly
  • 120 Hz IPS LCD is bright and responsive
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official Motorola warranty in Bangladesh
  • 33 W charge is slow for the large battery
  • Low light photos are weak
  • Phone feels big and viewfinder lags

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh battery easily lasts more than two days, which owners praise as industry leading. Charging at 33 W refills the large cell but feels slow compared with smaller batteries. The 6.72 inch 120 Hz IPS LCD is bright, though it lacks the deep blacks of an AMOLED.

Daily performance is smooth thanks to the Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 and 8 GB RAM, and 60 fps gaming runs stably. Camera images are decent in daylight, but low light shots are weak and the viewfinder shows lag. The phone is bulky and uncomfortable for one hand use.

Read full reasoning

It comes with a headphone jack, FM radio, Gorilla Glass 7i and IP64 protection. All units are gray imported, so there is no official Motorola warranty; only the shop’s warranty applies and repairs are paid. Confirm the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long will the battery last on typical use?

The 7000 mAh cell gives more than two days of use for most users. Reviews note industry leading endurance, so you can get through a workday and still have charge left for evening activities.

Can the phone handle gaming at 60 fps?

Yes, the Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 and 8 GB RAM keep 60 fps games stable. Owners report smooth daily usage and reliable performance in most titles, though very demanding games may show occasional dips.

Is the camera good in low light conditions?

Daylight shots are decent, but low light performance suffers. The 50 MP sensor and f/1.8 lens struggle with noise and processing, and users notice lag in the viewfinder, making night photography less reliable.

What warranty and software support can I expect?

All units are gray imported from India, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Shops we track offer their own limited warranty and paid repairs. Software receives only one major Android 16 update and security patches end June 2028, with limited long term support.
