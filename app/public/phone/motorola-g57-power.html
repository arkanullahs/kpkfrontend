Buying verdict

Motorola G57 Power Is it worth buying?

The Motorola G57 Power is a battery-focused device that prioritizes longevity over display quality or camera performance.

Choose it if
Your primary requirement is extreme battery life and you prefer a clean, near-stock software interface.
Consider alternatives if
You want a compact device, advanced camera features, or long-term software update support.

What works well

  • Industry-leading 7000 mAh battery capacity
  • Clean near-stock Hello UI experience
  • Durable vegan leather with military-grade certification
  • Capable Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 performance

Reported trade-offs

  • Hefty and uncomfortable physical size
  • Limited software support with only one major update
  • Camera viewfinder lag and lack of 4K video
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

With its massive 7000 mAh battery, users can expect 1.5 to 2 days of real-world usage, which is the standout feature of this phone. However, living with the device reveals significant compromises. Owners frequently report that the phone is physically uncomfortable due to its hefty size.

The camera experience is hindered by viewfinder lag and a lack of 4K video recording or advanced night processing. While the Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 chipset handles daily tasks and 60fps gaming well, the software support is limited to a single major update to Android 17. The display lacks the deep blacks and contrast found in AMOLED panels.

Read full reasoning

Since these units are gray imports, you must rely on shop-provided coverage rather than manufacturer support. Given that the newer Motorola G67 Power is available for a similar price, many buyers may find that model to be a better overall investment.

Does the Motorola G57 Power come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit currently available is a gray import built for the Indian market. You must rely on the specific warranty terms promised by the individual shop, and all repairs will be paid services.

How long will the Motorola G57 Power receive software updates?

The software support for this device is limited. It currently runs on Android 16 and is planned for only one major upgrade to Android 17. Owners have expressed concerns regarding this short update policy compared to other devices.

Is the Motorola G57 Power good for gaming?

The Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 chipset performs well for daily tasks and handles 60fps gaming. While it is not a high-end gaming phone, owners generally rate the gaming performance at 7.0 out of 10, suggesting it is sufficient for most casual users.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The camera is functional for daylight photography but struggles in challenging conditions. It lacks advanced night processing and 4K video recording capabilities. Owners have also reported noticeable viewfinder lag when using the camera app.
