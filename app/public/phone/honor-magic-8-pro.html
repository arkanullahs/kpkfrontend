Honor Magic 8 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Honor Magic 8 Pro is a powerful device that offers exceptional battery life but suffers from significant thermal management issues and a cluttered software experience.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life and a bright display for media consumption and are willing to manage a cluttered software interface.
Consider alternatives if
You are a serious mobile gamer who needs consistent performance without thermal throttling or you require a high-quality selfie camera.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Excellent battery endurance lasting over two days
  • Bright 6000 nits peak LTPO OLED display
  • Strong low light camera performance
  • Fast 100W wired and 80W wireless charging

Reported trade-offs4

  • Heavy CPU throttling and thermal heating issues
  • Selfie camera lacks autofocus and creates artifacts
  • Plastic back feels less premium
  • MagicOS software is messy with ads and bloat

Why we say that

Owners report that the phone provides over two days of endurance, which is a standout feature for heavy users. However, the device struggles with heavy CPU throttling and gets hot when gaming while charging. While the display is bright and the main camera performs well in low light, the selfie camera lacks autofocus and produces odd artifacts.

The plastic back feels less premium than expected for a flagship, and the MagicOS interface is often described as messy with a noticeable amount of ads and bloatware. Potential buyers must be careful to verify if they are purchasing a global unit with Google services or a China-market version, as the market in Bangladesh currently mixes both.

Does the Honor Magic 8 Pro have a reliable battery for long days?

Yes, owners report excellent battery endurance with this device. You can expect the 7100 mAh battery to last for more than two days on a single charge, making it one of the most reliable options for longevity.

Is the Honor Magic 8 Pro suitable for heavy gaming?

While the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset is powerful, owners report that the phone experiences heavy CPU throttling. The device also gets hot when gaming while charging, which may impact your overall gaming experience.
