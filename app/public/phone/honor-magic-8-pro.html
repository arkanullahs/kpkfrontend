Is the Honor Magic 8 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Industry-leading 6000 nits peak brightness
  • Exceptional 7100 mAh battery endurance
  • Top-tier Snapdragon 8 Elite performance
  • Excellent low-light camera stills

What is not

  • Plastic back feels less premium
  • Significant thermal throttling under stress
  • Soft video quality and selfie artifacts
  • Cluttered and occasionally buggy software

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a massive battery and a display that remains visible under the brightest sunlight.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you record a lot of video or expect a premium glass build for your money.

Why we say that

Living with this device means enjoying industry-leading screen brightness and exceptional battery endurance, yet you will notice the plastic back feels less premium than expected for this price. While the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset provides top-tier performance, owners report significant thermal throttling during intense use, dropping performance to 55 percent of its peak. The camera system captures excellent stills and performs well in low light, but you should be aware of softness in video recordings and odd artifacts in selfie shots.

MagicOS 10 is feature-rich but can feel cluttered and occasionally buggy. If you prioritize screen quality and battery life over premium materials and perfect video, this phone is a powerful companion.

Does the Honor Magic 8 Pro have a good battery for long days?

Is the Honor Magic 8 Pro suitable for heavy gaming?
