Is the Honor Magic 8 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Industry leading 6000 nits peak brightness
  • Exceptional 7100 mAh battery endurance
  • Excellent low light still photography
  • Fast 100W charging

What is not

  • Plastic back feels less premium
  • Video quality lacks sharpness
  • Performance drops during thermal throttling
  • Selfie camera lacks autofocus

Buy it if You want a phone with a massive battery and one of the brightest screens available.

Skip it if You prioritize premium glass build materials or high quality video recording.

Why we say that

Owners love the 6000 nits brightness and the endurance of the 7100 mAh battery. However, the plastic back feels cheap for a device at this price. Performance is high with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, but it suffers from thermal throttling that can drop performance to 55 percent during stress tests.

Camera users report excellent still photos and low light results, but video quality is soft on the main and ultrawide lenses. The selfie camera lacks autofocus and produces odd artefacts. MagicOS 10 is feature rich but feels cluttered and buggy at times.
