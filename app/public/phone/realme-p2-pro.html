Why we say that

The Realme P2 Pro uses a Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 chipset that delivers strong day‑to‑day speed and high frame rates in games, reflected in an 8.5 rating for gaming. Its 6.7‑inch OLED panel runs at 120 Hz, yet owners report peak brightness of 2000 nits only in short bursts and typical brightness around 600 nits, leading to complaints that the display feels dull outdoors. Battery life is decent with a 5200 mAh cell and 80 W fast charging, though the rating of 7.5 suggests it won’t last a full day under heavy use.

The 50 MP main camera with OIS and a 32 MP selfie shooter scores about 7 out of 10, performing adequately in good light but not excelling in low‑light scenes. Software runs Android 14 with Realme UI 5.0, but medium‑high bloatware and ads are common. Because every unit is a gray import, there is no manufacturer warranty; only the shop’s limited warranty applies and repairs are paid out of pocket.
