Is the Motorola Edge 70 Max worth buying?

What is good

  • Stunning 2K 144Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • Massive 7100mAh battery with 90W fast charging
  • Powerful Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset
  • Rugged aluminum frame with IP68/69 rating

What is not

  • No telephoto lens despite premium price
  • 8MP ultrawide cannot record 4K
  • Thermal throttling makes device hot
  • Camera ratings below 6 out of 10

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize a bright, fast display, long battery life, and rugged build over camera versatility.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a versatile camera system or an official Motorola warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.82" LTPO AMOLED panel reaches a peak brightness of 7000 nits and runs at a smooth 144 Hz, making outdoor use easy to read. Under the hood sits a Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 processor paired with 8 GB RAM, delivering strong day‑to‑day performance and decent gaming scores, though heavy titles can cause frame drops and the device becomes uncomfortably hot. The 7100 mAh silicon‑carbon battery lasts well beyond a full day and refuels in minutes thanks to 90 W wired and Qi2 magnetic wireless charging.

The main 50 MP sensor is solid, yet the 8 MP ultrawide and lack of any telephoto lens draw criticism, reflected in low camera and video ratings. The phone arrives as a gray‑import with a shop warranty only, so confirming IMEI and BTRC status is essential before purchase.
