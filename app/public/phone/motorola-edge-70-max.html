Why we say that

The Edge 70 Max runs on Snapdragon 8 Gen 5, delivering smooth multitasking and high frame rates in most games, though occasional software glitches can cause lag. Its 6.82‑inch LTPO AMOLED panel offers 2K resolution, 144 Hz refresh and peak brightness up to 7000 nits, making outdoor viewing easy. The 7100 mAh cell with 90 W charging and Qi2.2 wireless support lets you run all day and top up quickly.

Photography is a mixed bag: the 50 MP main sensor with OIS performs reasonably in low light, but the 8 MP ultrawide is weak and there is no telephoto lens. Build quality feels premium with metal‑glass design and IP68/69 rating, but the device arrives as a gray‑import with only the shop’s warranty and a USB 2.0 port. Users appreciate the display and battery, yet they complain about software bugs, missing telephoto capability and the lack of official after‑sales support.
