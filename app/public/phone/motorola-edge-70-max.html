Buying verdict

Motorola Edge 70 Max Is it worth buying?

The Motorola Edge 70 Max is a polarizing device that pairs a massive battery and industry-leading display with significant thermal and camera compromises.

Choose it if
You prioritize raw battery life and display quality above all else and do not mind the lack of a telephoto camera.
Consider alternatives if
You are a mobile gamer who hates thermal throttling or a photography enthusiast who requires a versatile camera system.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • Exceptional 7100 mAh battery endurance
  • Stunning 2K 144Hz LTPO display
  • Industry-leading 7000 nits peak brightness
  • Rare Qi2 magnetic wireless charging

Reported trade-offs

  • Unacceptable lack of telephoto lens
  • Thermal throttling and uncomfortable heat
  • Low resolution 8MP ultrawide camera
  • Pre-installed bloatware and crapware

Why we say that

Living with this phone means enjoying exceptional endurance from the 7100 mAh cell and a stunning 7000 nits peak brightness screen, but you will frequently deal with the device becoming uncomfortably hot during heavy use. Owners report that the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset suffers from frame drops and performance dips when the phone gets toasty. The camera system is a major point of contention, as users feel the lack of a telephoto lens and the inclusion of a low-resolution 8MP ultrawide sensor are unacceptable at this price point.

While the Hello UI is near-stock, the experience is marred by pre-installed bloatware like Temu and random games. Because this is an unofficial gray-import unit, you must accept that there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh and all repairs are paid.

Does the Motorola Edge 70 Max come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every listing is a gray import, meaning you only receive whatever warranty the specific shop promises. All repairs are paid, and you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

How does the battery perform during daily use?

The phone features a massive 7100 mAh silicon-carbon cell that provides exceptional endurance. Owners rate the battery performance very highly at 9.7 out of 10, making it one of the most reliable aspects of the device for long-term usage.

Is this phone suitable for heavy gaming?

While the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset is fast, the phone gets uncomfortably hot under load. Owners report thermal throttling and frame drops in heavy titles, which may impact your gaming experience. The gaming performance is rated at 7.2 out of 10.

How many years of software updates will this phone receive?

The Motorola Edge 70 Max runs Android 16 and is promised up to 3 major Android upgrades. However, the brand has a historically weak reputation for updates, and the current software experience is already cluttered with pre-installed bloatware.
