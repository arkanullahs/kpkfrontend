Motorola Razr 60 Ultra Is it worth buying?

The Motorola Razr 60 Ultra is a powerful foldable device that offers a high-end experience, but it is currently only available as a gray-import unit in Bangladesh for ৳94,990.

Choose it if
You want a high-performance foldable device and are comfortable managing the risks associated with gray-import units.

What works well

  • Bright 7.0 inch foldable LTPO AMOLED display
  • Efficient battery life from 4700 mAh cell
  • Fast performance with Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset
  • Solid build quality with good materials

Reported trade-offs

  • Front camera distortion during video calls
  • Only gray-import stock available
  • Historically weak software update support
  • No manufacturer warranty provided

Why we say that

Living with this phone means enjoying a bright 7.0 inch foldable LTPO AMOLED display and the fast Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset. Owners appreciate the efficient battery life and the solid build quality. However, you must be aware that this is not an official unit, meaning you will rely on shop warranty rather than brand support.

You should also confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before purchasing. Some users have reported specific issues with the front camera during video calls, noting that faces can appear distorted compared to other devices. While the Hello UI provides a near-stock Android 15 experience with low bloat, the historical update support remains a point of concern for long-term users.
