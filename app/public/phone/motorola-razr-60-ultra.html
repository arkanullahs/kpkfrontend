Is the Motorola Razr 60 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 165Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • Efficient battery life
  • Solid build materials
  • Low bloatware and ads

What is not

  • Front camera distortion during video calls
  • Historically weak software updates
  • No official brand warranty

Buy it if You want a powerful foldable with a high quality screen and near stock software.

Skip it if You rely heavily on video calls or want a guaranteed official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Owners appreciate the bright display and efficient battery life. It runs on the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset and Android 15. However, there is a specific issue with the front camera during video calls.

One owner reported that their face appeared distorted to the other person, a problem they did not experience with a Samsung phone. You should also be aware that only gray import stock is available. This means you get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty.

You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying. Software updates have been historically weak for this brand, with only 1 to 3 OS updates expected.
