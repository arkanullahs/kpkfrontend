Is the Motorola Razr 60 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 7.0 inch foldable LTPO AMOLED display
  • Efficient 4700 mAh battery life
  • Powerful Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset
  • Solid build quality with good materials

What is not

  • Front camera distortion during video calls
  • Only gray-import stock with shop warranty
  • Historically weak software update support

Buy it if Buy this phone if you want a high-performance foldable device and are comfortable managing the risks of a gray-import purchase.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you require a reliable brand warranty or depend on high-quality front camera performance for frequent video calls.

Why we say that

Living with this phone means enjoying a bright 7.0 inch display and efficient battery life, though the software support remains a concern. Owners report that the device feels solid and performs well for daily tasks. However, you must be aware of specific issues with the front camera during video calls, where users have reported distorted facial images.

Because this phone is currently only available through gray-import channels, you will not receive a brand warranty. You must rely on a shop warranty instead. It is essential to verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your money.

While the hardware is impressive, the combination of weak update history and potential video call glitches makes it a device for those who prioritize the foldable form factor over long-term software reliability.
