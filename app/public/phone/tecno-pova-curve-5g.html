What works well

  • 8000 mAh battery lasting up to three days
  • 6.78 inch curved AMOLED display with 144 Hz refresh
  • 45 W fast charging
  • 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs

  • poor camera quality with no OIS
  • no expandable storage
  • Dimensity 7100 feels outdated for price
  • heavy bloatware and slow updates

Why we say that

The 8000 mAh battery is the standout feature and can keep the phone alive for up to three days of normal use. The 6.78 inch curved AMOLED screen looks beautiful and runs at 144 Hz, making scrolling and media consumption pleasant. However the Dimensity 7100 processor feels dated and does not deliver the speed owners expect for the price.

Camera results are poor, lacking OIS or an ultrawide lens, and the 13 MP selfie sensor is also underwhelming. Storage cannot be expanded beyond the built‑in 128 or 256 GB, which many find limiting. The software includes a lot of bloatware and updates are slow, with only one or two major OS upgrades promised.

Build quality is average and the IP64 rating is seen as insufficient for heavy use.
