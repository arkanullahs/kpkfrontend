Realme GT8 Is it worth buying?

The Realme GT8 gives you a massive 7000 mAh battery that easily stretches beyond 20 hours of active use, and a 100 W charger that tops up the pack in under an hour.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need long battery life, a large bright display, and can accept a gray‑import warranty with shop‑only support.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on an official warranty or need strong secondary cameras for versatile photography.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 7000 mAh battery lasts over 20 hours
  • 6.79‑inch 2K AMOLED with 144 Hz refresh
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite delivers strong performance
  • Fast 100 W wired charging

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • GPU throttles and phone gets hot
  • Ultrawide and telephoto cameras are weak
  • Software shows frequent nagging notifications

Why we say that

The 6.79‑inch 2K AMOLED screen shines at 144 Hz and reaches a peak brightness that stays visible even in bright sunlight. Snapdragon 8 Elite provides strong day‑to‑day performance, but under heavy gaming the device heats up to nearly 50 °C and the GPU throttles, which some owners find disappointing. The main 50 MP camera captures good photos, yet the 8 MP ultrawide and the telephoto lens are weak, especially in low‑light scenes.

Realme UI on Android 16 brings a few useful features but also constant nagging notifications and noticeable bloatware. Because every unit is a gray‑import, there is no official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s limited warranty and pay for any repairs. Overall, it suits power users who value battery life and display quality and can tolerate the warranty limitation and occasional heat.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The GT8 packs a 7000 mAh battery that most owners report lasting more than 20 hours of active use. When it runs low, the 100 W charger can refill it in under an hour.

Will the phone overheat while gaming?

During extended gaming sessions the phone can reach nearly 50 °C and the GPU may throttle, which reduces frame rates. It still runs games, but you may notice heat and occasional slowdowns.

Does the Realme GT8 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

All GT8 units sold here are gray‑imports, so there is no manufacturer warranty. You only get the shop’s limited warranty and must pay for any repairs yourself.

How many Android updates will this phone receive?

Realme promises up to four major Android updates for the GT8, running Realme UI 7.0 on Android 16. In practice, owners see two to three OS upgrades, with medium‑high bloatware.
