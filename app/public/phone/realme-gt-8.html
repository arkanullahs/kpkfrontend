Realme GT8 Is it worth buying?

If you need a massive battery and a bright 144 Hz screen, the Realme GT8 delivers that but you must accept a gray‑import warranty and some performance quirks.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value battery endurance and a high‑refresh display over official warranty and flawless gaming performance.

Why we say that

The 7,000‑nit peak brightness makes outdoor use easy and the 100 W charger fills the 7,000 mAh cell in minutes. Snapdragon 8 Elite with 12 GB RAM feels fast for everyday apps and the 6.79‑inch AMOLED panel scores high on color and smoothness. Owners praise the premium IP68/69 build and the IR blaster for TV control.

However the phone lacks a brand warranty in Bangladesh, so repairs are paid and you rely on the shop’s promise. GPU throttles under continuous load, making long gaming sessions feel sluggish. The ultrawide and selfie lenses miss autofocus, and video stabilization is weak.

How long does the battery last with heavy daily use?

The 7,000 mAh battery scores a 9.3 rating from owners and easily lasts a full day of heavy browsing, video streaming and social media. Fast charging at 100 W restores most of the charge in under ten minutes.

Is the phone good for extended gaming sessions?

Performance feels strong at first thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Elite, but owners report heavy GPU throttling when the processor is under continuous load. Gaming scores drop after about thirty minutes of nonstop play.

How does the camera perform in low light and does it have autofocus?

The 50 MP main sensor captures good stills, but the ultrawide lens and the 16 MP selfie camera lack autofocus, which hurts low‑light detail. Video stabilization is also below expectations.

What warranty and software update support does the GT8 have in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray‑import units, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop provides its own limited warranty and any repairs are paid. Realme promises up to four major Android updates and two to three OS upgrades, but the UI includes medium‑high ads and frequent nagging notifications.
