Is the Apple iPhone XS worth buying?

What is good

  • Super Retina OLED display
  • No bloatware or ads
  • Upgradable to iOS 18.7

What is not

  • Significant battery degradation
  • No brand warranty
  • Very old hardware from 2018

Buy it if You want a basic iOS device with an OLED screen on a budget.

Skip it if You need a phone with long term software support and strong battery life.

Why we say that

Many users report a significant drop in capacity and performance throttling over time. This phone is from 2018, which means it is 8 years old. You should expect very short or no remaining software update life.

It uses a Super Retina OLED display and the A12 Bionic chip. Since only gray import stock is available, you will get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty. You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying.

It runs iOS without bloatware or ads. It is a very old device that may struggle with modern demands despite the OLED screen.
