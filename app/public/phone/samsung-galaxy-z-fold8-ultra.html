Is the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • 8.0 inch 120 Hz LTPO AMOLED 2X display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance
  • 200 MP main camera with OIS and 3× telephoto
  • 45 W fast charging

What is not

  • Only gray‑import stock, no official Samsung warranty
  • Battery may need daily charge with heavy use
  • Price is high for unofficial channel

Buy it if If you want a premium foldable with flagship performance and accept a shop warranty instead of an official Samsung warranty, this model fits you.

Skip it if If you need an official Samsung warranty or a lower price, look for another device.

Why we say that

Its 8.0 inch LTPO AMOLED 2X screen hits 3000 nits and runs at 120Hz, delivering vivid colors and smooth scrolling. The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 with 12GB RAM makes multitasking and gaming feel effortless, reflected in the 9.8 performance and 9.7 gaming ratings. The 200MP main camera with OIS, f/1.7 aperture and 3× telephoto captures detailed shots, though low‑light performance is solid rather than spectacular.

Battery life is decent at 5000 mAh and 45W fast charging, but power‑hungry use can require daily charging. Owners praise the build quality and display but complain about the lack of an official Samsung warranty and the higher price of unofficial imports. If you can handle shop warranty and confirm IMEI, the fold offers a premium experience at 199,999‑219,999 taka.

However, its tendency to run hot and the stubbornly outdated telephoto lens hold it back from true perfection.

MrMobile [Michael Fisher] · Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra Review: Is It Still Hip To Be Square? ↗

MrMobile appreciated the Fold8 Ultra as the most refined book-style foldable to date, specifically highlighting its usability as a portable pocket tablet. He praised the new anti-reflective main display and the much-improved main and ultrawide cameras, which performed beautifully on his trip to Alaska. However, he noted that the phone still runs a bit hot, the color options are uninspired, and the telephoto lens remains disappointingly weak for a device with such a massive price tag.

TechSci Guy · Samsung this time surprised me | Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 series Launched! Specs, Price & First Look ↗

TechSci Guy highlighted the highly practical aspect ratio and robust multi-window capabilities of the new Fold 8 series. He noted key improvements like the protective anti-reflective display and the incredibly bright screen, alongside the durable titanium design and the brand new ultra-wide angle lens that enhances the overall camera experience.

Tech Spurt · Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra Review | Worth it vs Z Fold 7? ↗

Tech Spurt found the Z Fold8 Ultra to be incredibly durable, easily surviving an accidental drop into a hard ceramic tub. He lauded the improved battery life from the new silicon-carbon technology and praised the upgraded anti-reflective screen that excels in bright sunlight. On the downside, he criticized several frustrating software bugs, the poor screen dimming technology that could cause eye strain, the phone's tendency to overheat during intensive gaming, and the fact that the telephoto camera hasn't received a desperately needed upgrade.
