Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra Is it worth buying?

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra is a premium foldable that offers a stunning 8.0 inch display but suffers from significant thermal management issues.

Choose it if
You want a large foldable display for productivity and multitasking and are comfortable managing a device without official brand support.
Consider alternatives if
You prioritize gaming performance, camera versatility, or a reliable manufacturer warranty for your expensive investment.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • Industry-leading 8 inch LTPO display
  • Impressively thin and durable hinge design
  • Elite multitasking with One UI 9
  • Seven years of major Android upgrades

Reported trade-offs

  • Missing vapor chamber causes thermal throttling
  • Outdated 3x telephoto lens without periscope
  • No S-Pen support for the folding screen
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

Living with this device means enjoying elite multitasking on One UI 9, yet owners frequently report lagging and heating during heavy use because the phone lacks a vapor chamber. While the build quality is impressively thin and durable, the 5000 mAh battery struggles to power the large screen efficiently. Photography enthusiasts are often disappointed by the outdated 3x telephoto lens that lacks periscope capabilities.

You must also accept that this device is only available as a gray import in Bangladesh, meaning you get no manufacturer warranty and must rely solely on the shop for repairs. Many users feel the S26 Ultra provides much better value for the money given the high price tag and the lack of S-Pen support on this folding model.

Does this phone come with an official Samsung warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as a gray import in Bangladesh. There is no manufacturer warranty provided by the brand, and you must rely on the specific shop for any repair promises or support.

How long will the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra receive software updates?

This device is supported by seven years of major Android upgrades. You will receive consistent software support through the Android 17 platform and One UI 9 interface for that duration.

Is the battery life sufficient for heavy daily usage?

Owners report that the 5000 mAh battery is tight for an 8 inch panel. While the Si/C battery technology improves efficiency, the large display consumes power quickly, leading to a lower owner rating of 7.2 for battery life.

Can I use this phone for high-end gaming?

While the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset offers peak power, owners report lagging and heating during gaming sessions. This is primarily due to the lack of a vapor chamber, which leads to thermal throttling under heavy loads.
