Buying verdict

Infinix Smart 10 HD Is it worth buying?

Expect a slow experience that struggles with basic tasks.

Choose it if
You only need a basic phone for calls and very light use.
Consider alternatives if
You want to play games or hate seeing ads in your software.

What works well

  • 90Hz IPS LCD display
  • Includes an IR blaster
  • Has a headphone jack
  • Includes FM radio

Reported trade-offs

  • Frequent lag and poor gaming
  • Ad heavy software with bloatware
  • Battery drains quickly
  • Misleading RAM and storage claims

Why we say that

Owners report significant lag and a poor gaming experience, with one user calling it a tortoise. The software is a major pain point because it is ad heavy and filled with bloatware. While it has a 5000 mAh battery, users say it drains quickly.

There are also serious complaints about misleading RAM and storage claims. You get a 90Hz screen and an IR blaster, but the actual performance is very low. It runs Android 14 Go edition, which is meant for low end hardware, but it still feels sluggish to those using it daily.
