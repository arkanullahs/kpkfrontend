Why we say that

The 6.83 inch AMOLED panel runs at 120Hz and can reach 1500 nits, making outdoor use comfortable. The Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 paired with up to 12GB RAM delivers smooth performance in games and everyday tasks. The 6000 mAh cell is praised for lasting through a full day of mixed use without needing a charge.

The 50MP main shooter has optical image stabilization, yet owners miss an ultra wide lens and report poor results in low light situations. The phone runs Android 15 with Realme UI 6.0, which includes medium high ad content and about two to three OS updates. Build quality feels solid, but the lack of an official brand warranty means any repair will be paid for by you.
