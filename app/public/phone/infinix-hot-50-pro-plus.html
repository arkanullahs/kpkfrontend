Is the Infinix Hot 50 Pro Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.78 inch AMOLED with 120Hz refresh
  • 50MP main camera sensor
  • 33W fast charging
  • Large 5000 mAh battery

What is not

  • Battery drains quickly
  • Camera quality limited
  • Heavy bloatware and ads
  • Poor gaming performance

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large, bright screen and a decent camera while staying within a tight budget.

Skip it if Skip it if you need smooth gaming, fast software updates, or a high‑performing camera.

Why we say that

The 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel with 120 Hz refresh feels smooth for everyday scrolling and media. The 50 MP main sensor captures decent photos in good light, yet owners note the single camera is mediocre and struggles in low‑light scenes. The 5,000 mAh battery sounds generous, but real‑world use shows it drains quickly, delivering only average day‑long life.

Android 14 runs on XOS 14.5 with a lot of pre‑installed apps and frequent notification ads, and the device is promised only one major Android upgrade, making software feel slow. Gaming is hampered by the Helio G100 chipset and low performance scores, so demanding games feel laggy. Overall, it works for basic tasks but falls short for power users.
