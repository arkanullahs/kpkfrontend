Is the Google Pixel 8a worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz OLED display with 2000‑nit peak
  • 64 MP main camera with OIS
  • Stock Android and up to seven years of updates
  • 5G connectivity

What is not

  • Battery lasts only one day under normal use
  • Thick bezels make the phone look dated
  • Thermal throttling reduces performance under load
  • No official Google service or brand warranty in Bangladesh

Buy it if Buy it if you value a sharp display and clean software more than long battery life.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a phone that lasts two days on a charge or rely on official service centers.

Why we say that

The 6.1‑inch OLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 2000 nits, making outdoor use easy. The 64 MP main sensor with optical image stabilization captures clear pictures and video, and the stock Android experience stays free of bloat and promises up to seven major updates. However, owners notice the battery drains quickly, especially with heavy use, and the phone feels warm with performance throttling under load.

The gray‑import unit arrives with a shop warranty, not a Google brand warranty, and official service centers are practically absent in Bangladesh. Thick bezels also make the design look older than newer budget rivals.

What is the Google Pixel 8a price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Google Pixel 8a in Bangladesh was ৳45,999, checked 3 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

Owners report the 4492 mAh battery gives about one day of use under normal conditions. If you keep the screen off most of the time it may stretch to two days, but heavy use drains it quickly.

Will the Pixel 8a handle mobile games well?

The phone runs games at decent frame rates thanks to the 120 Hz display, but performance ratings are moderate and the Tensor G3 chipset throttles when it gets hot, so demanding titles may lag.

Is the camera good for low‑light photography?

The 64 MP sensor with f/1.9 aperture and optical image stabilization captures clear low‑light shots and video, though it lacks dedicated macro or telephoto lenses, so extreme zoom or close‑up detail is limited.

What warranty and service options are available in Bangladesh?

Only gray‑import units are sold, offering a shop warranty instead of an official Google warranty. Google has little official service presence in Bangladesh, so any repair depends on the seller and you should verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
