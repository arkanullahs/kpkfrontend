Is the Realme Narzo N63 worth buying?

What is good

  • Fast 45W charging
  • Large 6.75 inch 90Hz display
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • 5000 mAh battery

What is not

  • Medium to high bloatware and ads
  • No official brand warranty
  • Basic Unisoc Tiger T612 chipset

Buy it if You need a cheap phone with fast charging and a large screen for basic tasks.

Skip it if You want an official brand warranty or a device for heavy gaming.

Why we say that

You get a large 90Hz screen and a 5000 mAh battery to last the day. However, the Unisoc Tiger T612 chipset is not for heavy tasks. Owners will likely deal with medium to high levels of bloatware and ads.

A major concern is that only gray import stock is available. This means you get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty. You must check the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying.

The 560 nits peak brightness is modest for outdoor use. It is a simple tool for those who need a phone for calls and basic apps.
