Is the ZTE Blade V70 Design worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz refresh rate on a large screen
  • 5000 mAh battery for all‑day use
  • Generous 8 GB RAM and 256 GB storage
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included

What is not

  • 22.5 W charging is slow
  • Camera detail is weak despite 50 MP
  • Gaming and multitasking lag noticeably
  • Unexpected silent‑mode activation

Buy it if Buy it if you need a big screen and lots of storage on a tight budget.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect smooth gaming or high‑quality photos.

Why we say that

The 6.7‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, which feels smooth, yet the 720p resolution looks dated. The Unisoc T606 chipset with 8 GB RAM can handle basic apps, but multitasking and modern games often stutter and lag. The 50 MP rear sensor promises detail, but owners report blurry images and poor low‑light results because the processing is basic.

Battery life is decent thanks to a 5000 mAh cell, though the 22.5 W charger refills it slowly. The phone includes a headphone jack and FM radio, and the design looks attractive, though the surface attracts fingerprints and there is no water resistance. Software runs Android 14 with MyOS, but it carries medium bloatware, translation glitches and only one or two OS updates.

Users also complain about random silent‑mode activation and a messy file manager.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery can usually last through a full day of moderate use such as browsing, social media and video playback, but heavy gaming or bright screen settings will drain it faster. Charging with the supplied 22.5 W charger takes over two hours.

Can I play modern games smoothly on this phone?

Modern games run with noticeable lag because the Unisoc T606 and the RAM struggle with demanding graphics. Simple casual games work, but you should not expect high frame rates or smooth performance in newer titles.

Is the 50 MP camera good for low‑light photos?

Despite the 50 MP sensor, low‑light pictures are often blurry and lack detail because the image processing is basic. The f/1.8 aperture helps a little, but results remain far from professional quality.

Does the phone come with an official warranty and how many Android updates will I receive?

Yes, the phone is sold through official channels and includes the brand’s warranty and service network in Bangladesh. Software updates are limited to one or two major Android versions, so long‑term support is minimal.
