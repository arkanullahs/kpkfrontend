Buying verdict

Vivo Y27s Is it worth buying?

Battery life and fast charging are the standout strengths of the Vivo Y27s.

Choose it if
If you need a budget phone with good battery life, fast charging and a decent camera and you do not mind basic gaming, this model fits well.
Consider alternatives if
If you want smooth heavy gaming or long‑term software updates, look for a newer device.

What works well

  • 44W fast charging
  • 5000 mAh battery with strong endurance
  • 50 MP main camera for price
  • IP54 protection and attractive colors

Reported trade-offs

  • IPS LCD with outdated notch
  • Heavy games freeze or lag
  • Software updates likely ending soon

Why we say that

The 5000 mAh cell gives strong endurance and the 44W charger refills the phone quickly, which owners repeatedly praise. The 50 MP main sensor delivers decent photos for the price, and the IP54 rating adds some protection against dust and splashes. However, the 6.64‑inch IPS LCD feels dated, especially with its notch, and the display brightness is modest.

Performance is smooth for everyday apps, but heavy games such as CODM often freeze, reflecting low gaming scores. The device runs Android 13 with Funtouch 13 and has received a few OS updates, yet being three years old means future updates are unlikely. Overall, it feels like a solid everyday phone if you accept basic gaming and an older display, but it may disappoint if you need a future‑proof, high‑performance handset.
