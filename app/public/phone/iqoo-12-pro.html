Buying verdict

iQOO 12 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you want a flagship‑level gaming phone with a brilliant 144 Hz display and strong performance, the iQOO 12 Pro delivers that at a gray‑import price, but it comes with notable compromises.

Choose it if
You prioritize high‑refresh gaming performance and a bright, fast screen at a gray‑import price.
Consider alternatives if
You need an official warranty or reliable low‑light telephoto photography.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • 6.78" 144Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 performance
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • 5100 mAh battery with decent endurance

Reported trade-offs

  • No official warranty in Bangladesh
  • USB 2.0 port limits fast charging
  • Overheats under heavy gaming
  • Telephoto struggles in low light

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset runs games like Genshin at 2K 60 FPS without stutter, and the 6.78" LTPO AMOLED panel reaches 3000 nits peak brightness, making outdoor use easy. Battery life is solid for everyday tasks, though intensive gaming drains it faster. The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures clear photos, while the telephoto lens loses detail in dim environments.

Build quality feels premium with IP68 protection, yet the USB 2.0 port feels outdated and the phone can get hot under load, sometimes throttling. Software runs Android 14 with up to two major updates, but being three years old means limited future updates. There is no manufacturer warranty; only the shop’s limited warranty applies, and any repairs are paid by the owner.

Will the iQOO 12 Pro receive software updates in Bangladesh?

The phone launched in 2023 and supports up to two major Android upgrades, but being three years old means little update life remains. It does not have an official warranty, only the shop’s limited coverage.

How does the battery perform for daily use and gaming?

The 5100 mAh battery offers good endurance for normal daily tasks, but intensive gaming draws more power and reduces runtime. Owners report solid overall endurance but notice higher drain during heavy gaming sessions.

Is the camera reliable in low‑light, especially the telephoto lens?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures strong images, and the ultrawide works well. However, the 3× telephoto lens crops heavily in low light, making it unsuitable for night‑time zoom shots.
