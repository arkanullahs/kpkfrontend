Is the Google Pixel 11 Pro XL worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.8 inch LTPO OLED with 3600 nit peak brightness
  • 50 MP main camera with excellent ultra wide and 5x zoom
  • Clean Pixel Android with Gemini AI and 7 years updates
  • Battery easily lasts a full day in real use

What is not

  • 45W charging feels slow for a flagship
  • Only 16 GB RAM makes price feel high
  • GPU struggles with AAA games and shows pink screen
  • No manufacturer warranty, only shop warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you value top tier camera, bright display and long battery more than high end gaming performance and official service support.

Skip it if Skip it if you need strong AAA gaming capability or a reliable official warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.8 inch LTPO OLED screen reaches a peak brightness of 3600 nits and runs at 120Hz, making everyday use pleasant. The 50 MP main camera with 5x tele and ultra wide lens produces top tier stills and natural skin tones, though the camera app feels cluttered. Battery endurance has jumped and comfortably lasts a full day, earning an 8.4 rating.

Build quality feels tank like with good ergonomics. Software is clean Pixel Android with Gemini AI and promises up to 7 major OS updates. Performance is smooth for daily tasks, but the GPU is outdated for AAA gaming and can show a pink screen in Fortnite.

The phone arrives as a gray import with only a shop warranty, so any repair will be paid by you. Value feels overpriced because RAM is cut to 16 GB while price stays high.
