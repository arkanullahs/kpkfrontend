Is the Motorola G06 Power worth buying?

What is good

  • 7000 mAh battery lasts days
  • Large 6.88 inch display
  • 50 MP main camera on paper
  • Headphone jack included

What is not

  • Performance feels juddery and slow
  • Gaming struggles on COD Mobile
  • Charging only 18 W, very slow
  • Camera blurry with motion and low light

Skip it if Skip it if smooth gaming or reliable low‑light photos are a priority.

Why we say that

The 6.88 inch IPS LCD display is big and comes with a 120 Hz claim, yet most owners feel the refresh is not smooth and the panel looks dim outdoors. The Helio G81 Ultra chipset with 4 GB RAM manages basic apps but trips over heavier games like Call of Duty Mobile, giving inconsistent frame rates. The 50 MP primary sensor sounds impressive on paper but produces blurry shots on moving subjects and in low light, and the camera app feels heavy with unnecessary cruft.

Charging is limited to 18 W, so a full top‑up takes a long time and there is no wireless option. The phone arrives as a gray‑import unit, so you only get a shop warranty and must verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying. Despite the drawbacks, many users call it a steal for the price range of 16,799 to 17,490 taka.

How long will the battery actually last in daily use?

Owners report the 7000 mAh cell can stretch to two or three days with moderate use. Heavy browsing or video playback will shorten that, but it still outlasts most phones in this price range.

Will this phone handle games like Call of Duty Mobile well?

The Helio G81 Ultra and 4 GB RAM struggle with demanding titles. Users say Call of Duty Mobile runs with frequent frame drops and feels unplayable, so expect limited gaming performance.

Is the camera usable in low light or when subjects move?

The 50 MP sensor captures detail in good light, but moving subjects often come out blurry and low‑light shots lack clarity. The app also feels heavy with extra bloat.

What warranty and software support does the gray‑import version have?

Only a shop warranty is offered, not the official Motorola brand warranty. Software runs Android 15 with Hello UI and receives limited updates, roughly one to three OS versions, and a short security‑patch window.
