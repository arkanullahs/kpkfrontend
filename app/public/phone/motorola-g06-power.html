Motorola G06 Power Is it worth buying?

This phone is essentially a massive battery in a plastic shell.

Choose it if

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Massive 7000 mAh battery
  • Textured back feels reasonably premium
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • Good value for the price

Reported trade-offs

  • Sluggish and juddery performance
  • Poor gaming experience
  • Blurry motion shots
  • Slow 18W charging

Why we say that

Owners love the 7000 mAh cell because it lasts for days, but almost everything else feels basic. Day to day use is often sluggish and juddery. The 120Hz screen does not feel smooth in practice and is dim when you take it outdoors.

Gaming is a struggle, with titles like Call of Duty Mobile showing inconsistent frame rates. The camera is basic and often produces blurry shots of moving subjects. You also have to deal with preinstalled bloatware and a slow 18W charging speed.

Read full reasoning

Since these are Indian imports, you get no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. You are relying entirely on the shop for any coverage, and all repairs will be paid.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone has no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every listing is a gray import built for the Indian market. Any warranty provided is from the shop itself, and all repairs are paid.

Can I play Call of Duty Mobile on this device?

Gaming performance is poor. Owners report that Call of Duty Mobile really struggles on this device with inconsistent frame rates. It is not recommended for gaming.

How is the camera performance in low light or with movement?

The camera is basic and struggles with moving subjects. You should expect occasional blurry pictures when shooting moving objects. The camera app is also described as sludgy.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The phone comes with Android 15. However, owners suggest not expecting more than a couple of years of security patching due to historically weak update support.
