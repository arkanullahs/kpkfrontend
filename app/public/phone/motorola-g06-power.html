Is the Motorola G06 Power worth buying?

This phone is a battery champion that makes serious compromises everywhere else to hit its low price. Owners praise the massive 7000 mAh battery that can last for days on a single charge, making it a reliable device for basic use. However, daily performance is described as sluggish and juddery, with poor gaming performance even on optimized titles like Call of Duty Mobile. The camera is basic and struggles with moving subjects, while the 120Hz display is dim outdoors and not smooth in practice. Be aware it is only available as a gray import, meaning you get a shop warranty instead of an official Motorola one.

What is good

  • Massive 7000 mAh battery lasts for days
  • Near stock Android software with Hello UI
  • Textured plastic back feels reasonably premium

What is not

  • Sluggish and juddery day to day performance
  • Poor camera struggles with motion and low light
  • Gaming performance is weak even on optimized titles
  • Gray import means no official brand warranty

Buy it if You are a very light user who needs a phone with an incredible battery life for basic calls and messaging and are comfortable with a gray import.

Skip it if You play mobile games, take a lot of photos, or need smooth and reliable performance for daily apps.
