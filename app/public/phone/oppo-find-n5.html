Is the OPPO Find N5 worth buying?

What is good

  • Brilliant and large 8.12 inch foldable display
  • Excellent battery endurance for daily tasks
  • Very fast performance from Snapdragon 8 Elite
  • Includes a useful IR blaster

What is not

  • Significant fears of structural fragility
  • Abysmal 8MP ultrawide camera quality
  • Overheating concerns with the 7-core CPU
  • Medium levels of bloat and ads in software

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a large, bright foldable display and fast performance for high-data apps and are comfortable with the risks of unofficial imports.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you need a reliable ultrawide camera, a durable build that can withstand daily handling, or the security of an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Owners report that the device is impressively thin, yet this design choice leads to widespread fears that the phone is fragile and could snap easily. While the Snapdragon 8 Elite provides fast performance, users are concerned about overheating, which has led to the use of a 7-core CPU configuration instead of the standard 8-core version. The 8MP ultrawide camera is widely considered abysmal and not competitive for a device at this price point.

You will also encounter medium levels of bloat and ads within ColorOS 15. Because this phone is only available through unofficial gray-import channels, you must rely on shop warranties rather than official brand support. Always verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your money.

Is this phone suitable for mobile gaming?

The phone is very fast, but there are concerns regarding heat. Because the device is so thin, the 7-core CPU variant may overheat during intense gaming sessions, despite its 8.2 rating for gaming performance.
