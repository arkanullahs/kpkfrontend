Is the OPPO Find N5 worth buying?

What is good

  • 2100 nits foldable OLED screen
  • 5600 mAh battery with 80W fast charge
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite delivers fast gaming
  • 50MP main camera with telephoto lens

What is not

  • 7 core CPU instead of 8
  • Thin chassis prone to cracking
  • 8MP ultrawide camera is weak
  • Only shop warranty, no brand warranty

Buy it if Buy if you value a premium display and performance and accept a thin body with limited warranty.

Skip it if Skip if you need a strong ultrawide camera or official brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 8.12 inch OLED can reach 2100 nits, making outdoor use easy. The 5600 mAh cell supports 80W fast charge and survives heavy health monitoring apps without trouble. Snapdragon 8 Elite delivers fast gaming scores, though the 7 core configuration can get warm during long sessions.

The 50MP main sensor with optical image stabilization and 3x telephoto produces solid photos, yet the 8MP ultrawide is far below expectations. ColorOS 15 runs on Android 15 with medium bloat and about three to four major OS updates. The phone arrives as a gray import, so only a shop warranty is available and you must verify IMEI and BTRC status.

At 159,990 taka it sits in the ultra premium range, making value hard to justify for some.

Mrwhosetheboss · Oppo Find N5 Review - Samsung just got Demolished!? ↗

Mrwhosetheboss is overwhelmingly impressed by the Find N5's hardware, framing it as a complete demolition of Samsung's current foldable offerings. He praises the unbelievably thin design, the significantly larger battery that charges much faster, and the superior Snapdragon 8 Elite performance. While he loves the multitasking capabilities, he notes that Oppo's software aesthetics feel a bit cheap, their AI features lag behind Samsung's, and the camera system—particularly the ultrawide—suffers noticeably from the device's extreme thinness.

The Tech Chap · OPPO Find N5 Real World Review ↗

The Tech Chap considers the Find N5 the best foldable phone available, praising its incredibly thin profile, subtle crease, and excellent battery life powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite. He highlights the utility of the O+ Connect feature for Mac users and the strong performance of the main and telephoto cameras. However, his review is heavily overshadowed by the last-minute realization that the phone will not launch in Europe or the UK, a decision he finds hugely disappointing and one that severely limits the phone's actual market impact.

MrMobile [Michael Fisher] · Oppo Find N5 Review: Canary In The Coal Mine ↗

MrMobile views the Find N5 as a technological marvel that serves as a bittersweet 'canary in the coal mine' for the foldable industry. He marvels at its lack of compromises—retaining fast wireless charging, an alert slider, and incredible battery life within a ridiculously thin chassis. He praises the crease and the anti-reflective inner screen. Yet, he is deeply frustrated by the abysmal 8MP ultrawide camera and the phone's limited regional availability, concluding that while it's the best foldable ever made, its high price and lack of a global launch mean it will likely remain an obscure curiosity rather than a market disruptor.

Tech Spurt · Best Foldable Phone For 2025! | OPPO Find N5 Review ↗

Tech Spurt enthusiastically crowns the Find N5 the best foldable of 2025, heavily emphasizing its brilliant 'skinny' design that still manages to be highly durable and water-resistant. He loves the battery life, the gaming performance of the Snapdragon 8 Elite, and the seamless multitasking software. While he praises the main and telephoto cameras (and the fun macro mode), he acknowledges minor software bugs, like Android Auto failing to connect, and the frustration of the screen becoming unresponsive when even slightly wet.

How long will the battery last with heavy use?

The 5600 mAh battery scores an 8.8 rating and owners report excellent endurance, even when running high data health monitoring apps, so a full day of intensive use is generally achievable before recharging.

Will the phone get hot while gaming?

Performance ratings are high at 8.8 and gaming scores reach 8.2, but reviewers note the Snapdragon 8 Elite runs only seven cores and can overheat during long gaming sessions, so occasional heat is expected.

Is the camera usable in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.9 aperture and optical image stabilization delivers solid photos, but the 8 MP ultrawide lacks light gathering ability and is considered weak, making low light ultrawide shots disappointing.

Will I receive official OPPO warranty and software updates?

The device is only available as a gray import, so it comes with a shop warranty rather than an official OPPO guarantee; however ColorOS 15 is expected to receive three to four major Android updates like other flagships.
