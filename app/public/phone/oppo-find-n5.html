Buying verdict

OPPO Find N5 Is it worth buying?

The OPPO Find N5 gives you a huge 2100 nits foldable screen and a long lasting battery, but you pay for a gray import with no official warranty and a few notable compromises.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a bright large foldable with strong performance and can accept a shop warranty instead of an official one.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable ultrawide camera or an official brand warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 2100 nits foldable OLED display
  • 5600 mAh battery with strong endurance
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite fast gaming performance
  • 50 MP main camera with optical image stabilization

Reported trade-offs4

  • 7 core CPU instead of full 8 core
  • 8 MP ultrawide camera is weak
  • Very thin body feels fragile
  • Can overheat under heavy load

Why we say that

The 8.12 inch LTPO OLED panel is bright and smooth at 120Hz, making everyday use and media consumption a pleasure. The 5600 mAh cell easily lasts a full day even with health monitoring apps, and the 80W charger refills it quickly. Performance feels very fast thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Elite, yet owners report the chip runs a 7 core CPU and can get hot during intensive tasks.

The 12 GB RAM and 256 GB storage feel generous, but the 8 MP ultrawide lens is far below expectations. The chassis is impressively thin, but many say it snaps like a dorito. Because it is a gray import, only the shop warranty applies and any repair will cost extra.

What reviewers said on YouTube

4 reviews · 7.9M views

The Oppo Find N5 represents the current pinnacle of foldable hardware, achieving a breathtakingly thin, light design while packing a massive battery and top-tier processor. However, a genuinely terrible ultrawide camera, minor software quirks, and lack of a global release stop it from being an unqualified recommendation for everyone.

Best for Foldable enthusiasts outside Europe willing to import a phone for its hardware superiority.

  1. MMrwhosetheboss

    Oppo Find N5 Review - Samsung just got Demolished!? (opens YouTube)

    7.2M views

    Mrwhosetheboss is overwhelmingly impressed by the Find N5's hardware, framing it as a complete demolition of Samsung's current foldable offerings. He praises the unbelievably thin design, the significantly larger battery that charges much faster, and the superior Snapdragon 8 Elite performance. While he loves the multitasking capabilities, he notes that Oppo's software aesthetics feel a bit cheap, their AI features lag behind Samsung's, and the camera system—particularly the ultrawide—suffers noticeably from the device's extreme thinness.

  2. MMrMobile [Michael Fisher]

    Oppo Find N5 Review: Canary In The Coal Mine (opens YouTube)

    231K views

    MrMobile views the Find N5 as a technological marvel that serves as a bittersweet 'canary in the coal mine' for the foldable industry. He marvels at its lack of compromises—retaining fast wireless charging, an alert slider, and incredible battery life within a ridiculously thin chassis. He praises the crease and the anti-reflective inner screen. Yet, he is deeply frustrated by the abysmal 8MP ultrawide camera and the phone's limited regional availability, concluding that while it's the best foldable ever made, its high price and lack of a global launch mean it will likely remain an obscure curiosity rather than a market disruptor.

  3. TTech Spurt

    Best Foldable Phone For 2025! | OPPO Find N5 Review (opens YouTube)

    134K views

    Tech Spurt enthusiastically crowns the Find N5 the best foldable of 2025, heavily emphasizing its brilliant 'skinny' design that still manages to be highly durable and water-resistant. He loves the battery life, the gaming performance of the Snapdragon 8 Elite, and the seamless multitasking software. While he praises the main and telephoto cameras (and the fun macro mode), he acknowledges minor software bugs, like Android Auto failing to connect, and the frustration of the screen becoming unresponsive when even slightly wet.

  4. TThe Tech Chap

    OPPO Find N5 Real World Review (opens YouTube)

    349K views

    The Tech Chap considers the Find N5 the best foldable phone available, praising its incredibly thin profile, subtle crease, and excellent battery life powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite. He highlights the utility of the O+ Connect feature for Mac users and the strong performance of the main and telephoto cameras. However, his review is heavily overshadowed by the last-minute realization that the phone will not launch in Europe or the UK, a decision he finds hugely disappointing and one that severely limits the phone's actual market impact.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 5600 mAh battery scores 8.8 out of 10 for endurance, and owners say it easily lasts a full day even when running high data health monitoring apps. Fast charging at 80W also reduces downtime.

Is the phone good for gaming?

Gaming feels very fast thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Elite and 12 GB RAM, earning an 8.0 rating. However, the chip runs a 7 core CPU and can overheat during long sessions, which may affect comfort.

How does the camera perform in low light, especially the ultrawide lens?

The 50 MP main sensor with optical image stabilization captures decent low‑light shots, but the 8 MP ultrawide lens is considered weak and non‑competitive, so expect poorer results in dim environments.

Will I get an official warranty and software updates?

All listings are gray imports, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop provides its own limited warranty and any repair will be paid for. The device runs ColorOS 15 on Android 15 and typically receives three to four major OS updates on flagship models.
