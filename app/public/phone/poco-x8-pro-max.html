POCO X8 Pro Max Is it worth buying?

The POCO X8 Pro Max is a massive device built for endurance, but it requires significant compromises regarding software and thermal management.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize extreme battery life above all else and are comfortable managing a device with heavy software bloat.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable camera for zoom photography or require a phone that maintains peak performance during long gaming sessions.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Industry-leading 8500 mAh battery capacity
  • Bright 1.5K AMOLED display
  • Powerful Dimensity 9500s chipset
  • Premium IP68/69K build quality

Reported trade-offs4

  • Heavy bloatware and intrusive ads
  • Significant thermal throttling under load
  • Lack of bypass charging
  • Muddled speaker audio quality

Why we say that

With an 8500 mAh battery, it offers industry-leading longevity that easily powers through heavy usage. However, the Dimensity 9500s chipset suffers from thermal throttling under sustained load, dropping to half its performance in stress tests. Living with the phone means dealing with HyperOS 3, which is heavily burdened by bloatware, intrusive ads, and mandatory account requirements.

Owners also report frustration with the always-on display, which turns off after ten seconds, and a muddled speaker quality. The camera system is capable for general shots but lacks a telephoto lens and tends to over-sharpen images. Because this is a gray-import device, you must rely entirely on the shop for support rather than the manufacturer.

Read full reasoning

It is a heavy phone at 218g, and the lack of bypass charging is a notable drawback for those who game while plugged in.

Does the POCO X8 Pro Max come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as a gray-import unit. There is no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh, so you must rely on the specific warranty terms provided by the shop where you purchase the device.

How does the battery perform for heavy users?

The 8500 mAh battery provides industry-leading endurance. It is designed to last through heavy usage, making it one of the strongest features of the device for users who need a phone that does not require frequent charging.

Is this phone suitable for serious mobile gaming?

While the Dimensity 9500s is a powerful chipset, it is prone to thermal throttling during prolonged gaming sessions. The device also lacks bypass charging, which may be a disadvantage for gamers who prefer to play while the phone is connected to power.

How long will the POCO X8 Pro Max receive software updates?

The device is supported for 2 to 3 OS updates. You should be aware that the software experience is heavily impacted by pre-installed bloatware and intrusive advertisements that are part of the HyperOS skin.
