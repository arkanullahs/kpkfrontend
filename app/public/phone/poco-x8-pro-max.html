Is the POCO X8 Pro Max worth buying?

What is good

  • 8500 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 6.83‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED with 3500 nits peak
  • Dimensity 9500s gives near‑flagship performance
  • 50 MP main sensor with OIS

What is not

  • Heavy software bloat and ads
  • Thermal throttling under sustained load
  • Mediocre speaker quality
  • Only shop warranty, no brand warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you need massive battery life, a bright display and strong performance for gaming and can tolerate software bloat and a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you want a clean OS, high‑quality audio, or an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.83‑inch AMOLED panel shines with 120 Hz refresh and a 3500‑nit peak, making outdoor viewing easy. Under the hood the Dimensity 9500s and 12 GB RAM deliver near‑flagship speed, so most apps feel snappy and games run well until the chip heats up and throttles performance. The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures decent photos, but the lack of a telephoto lens and a tendency to over‑sharpen can be noticeable in low light.

HyperOS is loaded with ads and bloatware, requiring time to clean up, and the always‑on display turns off after ten seconds. Audio from the speakers is muddled, and the phone arrives only as a gray‑import with shop warranty, so you must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
