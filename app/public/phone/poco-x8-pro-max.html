POCO X8 Pro Max Is it worth buying?

The POCO X8 Pro Max is a massive device that prioritizes raw battery endurance and display brightness over a refined user experience.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize massive battery life and a very bright screen for media consumption and are willing to spend time debloating the software.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable manufacturer warranty, require bypass charging for gaming, or dislike heavy devices with aggressive thermal throttling.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Industry-leading 8500 mAh battery endurance
  • Extremely bright 3500 nits peak display
  • Powerful Dimensity 9500s chipset performance
  • Durable IP68/69K build quality

Reported trade-offs4

  • Heavy software bloatware and intrusive ads
  • Significant thermal throttling under sustained load
  • Mediocre speaker audio quality
  • Always-on display times out after 10 seconds

Why we say that

Living with this phone means dealing with a heavy 218g chassis and a software environment that is heavily bloated with ads and pre-installed applications. While the Dimensity 9500s chipset offers near-flagship speed, owners report significant thermal throttling during long gaming sessions. The camera setup is capable for general shots, but the lack of a telephoto lens and aggressive over-sharpening limit its versatility.

You will also need to manage expectations regarding the always-on display, which times out after 10 seconds, and the lack of bypass charging for gamers. Because this is a gray-import device, you must rely entirely on the shop for any repairs, as there is no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh.

Does the POCO X8 Pro Max come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you must rely on the specific shop for any repair promises. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

How does the battery perform during daily use?

The battery performance is industry-leading according to owner ratings. The massive 8500 mAh cell provides exceptional endurance, making it one of the strongest features of the device. However, keep in mind that the phone lacks bypass charging, which may affect long-term power management for heavy users.

Is this phone suitable for heavy gaming?

While the Dimensity 9500s chipset offers near-flagship speed, it is prone to significant thermal throttling. Owners report that the device can throttle down to half of its peak performance during prolonged CPU stress tests, which may impact your gaming experience over time.

How long will the POCO X8 Pro Max receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 16 and HyperOS 3 out of the box. It is scheduled to receive between 2 and 3 OS updates. Be aware that the software comes loaded with bloatware and ads that require manual effort to remove.
