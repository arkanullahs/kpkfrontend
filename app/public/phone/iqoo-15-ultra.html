Is the iQOO 15 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • 7400 mAh battery with 100 W fast charge
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance
  • 6.85‑inch 144 Hz AMOLED screen
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Shop warranty only, no brand warranty
  • China‑market OS may have extra ads
  • Software updates limited to three OS versions

Buy it if Buy it if you want flagship speed, a large screen and long battery for gaming and media.

Skip it if Skip it if you need an official brand warranty or a completely clean OS.

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset and 144 Hz AMOLED panel make every game feel smooth and responsive. The 7400 mAh battery paired with 100 W fast charging means you can charge from zero to full in under an hour and still have enough juice for a full day of heavy use. The 50 MP main camera with OIS and a 32 MP selfie sensor capture clear photos in good light, though low‑light performance is average.

The phone runs Android 16 with OriginOS 6 or Funtouch OS depending on the import, and you will get up to five major Android upgrades but only three OS version updates. The main complaints are the lack of an official brand warranty, the possibility of a China‑market OS with extra ads, and medium bloatware.
