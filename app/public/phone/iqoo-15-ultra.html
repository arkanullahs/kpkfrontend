Buying verdict

iQOO 15 Ultra Is it worth buying?

The iQOO 15 Ultra is a powerful gray-import device that relies entirely on shop-provided support rather than official brand backing.

Choose it if
You are an enthusiast who understands how to manage gray-import devices and wants top-tier hardware performance.
Consider alternatives if
You require official brand warranty support or want a device that is guaranteed to work perfectly with local network services out of the box.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • Massive 7400 mAh battery capacity
  • Extremely bright 8000 nits peak display
  • Powerful Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset
  • Fast 100W charging support

Reported trade-offs

  • No official brand warranty support
  • Potential software issues with China-ROM units
  • Only available as gray-import stock

Why we say that

Living with this phone means navigating the complexities of China-ROM software if you purchase the OriginOS variant, which requires careful setup compared to the Funtouch OS version found on India imports. The massive 7400 mAh battery provides significant endurance, while the 8000 nits peak brightness display remains visible in harsh outdoor conditions. Owners must be aware that every unit currently available is a gray import, meaning you do not get official manufacturer service.

You should verify the BTRC status of your specific unit with the seller before handing over your money. The software experience varies significantly depending on whether you receive the China-ROM or the India-ROM version, so clarify this with your seller to avoid unexpected bloatware or language barriers.

What is the iQOO 15 Ultra price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for iQOO 15 Ultra in Bangladesh was ৳1,15,999, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does the iQOO 15 Ultra come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there is no official brand warranty for this phone in Bangladesh. Every unit currently available is a gray import, so you must rely solely on the warranty provided by the specific shop where you purchase the device.

How long will the iQOO 15 Ultra receive software updates?

The update policy depends on the software version. The China-ROM OriginOS 6 variant supports up to 5 major Android upgrades, while the India-ROM Funtouch OS variant is limited to 3 major OS updates.

What is the difference between the China and India versions of this phone?

The China version runs OriginOS 6, which may have specific regional limitations. The India version runs Funtouch OS, which is generally more familiar to global users but comes with a medium amount of pre-installed bloatware and ads.

Is the 7400 mAh battery enough for heavy daily usage?

The 7400 mAh battery is very large and should provide substantial longevity for heavy users. You can recharge the device quickly using the 100W charging support, which helps minimize downtime during your busy day.
