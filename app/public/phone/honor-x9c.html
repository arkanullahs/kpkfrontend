Is the Honor X9c worth buying?

What is good

  • 6600 mAh battery with 66W fast charging
  • 6.78 inch AMOLED display with 120Hz refresh
  • 108MP main camera with OIS for stills
  • Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 chipset

What is not

  • Screen and back crack easily
  • Wi‑Fi signal often weak
  • Video stabilization lacks functional OIS
  • Performance feels underpowered for price

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize long battery life and a bright, smooth display above top‑tier gaming performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable build quality, strong gaming power, or solid video stabilization.

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch AMOLED panel is bright, vivid and runs at a smooth 120Hz refresh rate, making everyday scrolling pleasant. Still images look sharp thanks to the 108MP main sensor with optical image stabilization. However, owners constantly report that the glass back and screen can crack with a simple drop despite marketing claims of drop resistance.

Wi‑Fi connectivity is often weak, and the Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 chipset feels underpowered for the price when gaming or running heavy apps. Video recording suffers because the OIS does not work, leading to shaky footage. The software runs Android 14 with MagicOS 8 and includes medium bloat and ads.

In real use the phone shines in endurance and display quality but falls short in durability, connectivity and performance.
