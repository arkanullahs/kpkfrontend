Is the Honor X9c worth buying?

What is good

  • Large 6.78 inch 120Hz AMOLED display
  • 6600 mAh battery lasting up to two days
  • Fast 66W charging in about 70 minutes
  • 108 MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Screen cracks easily despite tough chassis
  • Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 struggles with heavy games
  • 5 MP ultra‑wide camera is outdated
  • Video recording shows severe instability

Buy it if Buy it if you need a big, bright screen and long battery life and can accept modest gaming and camera performance.

Why we say that

The 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 4000 nits, making outdoor use easy. The 6600 mAh battery really lasts 1.5 to 2 days for most owners and the 66 W charger fills it in about 70 minutes. The 108 MP main camera with OIS is decent for everyday shots, though the 5 MP ultra‑wide lens feels outdated and video recording can shake like an earthquake.

Performance is held back by the Snapdragon 6 Gen 1, so heavy games and multitasking feel sluggish. The curved glass is the biggest practical complaint; it cracks from short drops despite a tough chassis. Software runs on MagicOS with Android 14 and includes some pre‑installed apps, but it is stable.

Overall the phone is usable if you value battery and display over gaming and premium camera features.

How long does the battery last in real use?

The 6600 mAh battery typically lasts between one and a half to two days for most daily routines. Owners rate battery performance very high and the 66 W charger refills the phone in roughly seventy minutes.

Will the Honor X9c receive Android updates?

The phone runs MagicOS 8 on Android 14 and follows the brand’s update pattern of three to four major OS upgrades for flagship models. It also includes some pre‑installed apps but remains stable.

Is the display prone to cracking if dropped?

The chassis is described as tough, yet the curved glass screen cracks easily in real‑world drops. Users report cracks from falls of less than one metre, so extra care is needed.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 108 MP main sensor with OIS handles low‑light situations decently, but overall camera ratings are modest. The 5 MP ultra‑wide lens is outdated and video recording can be unstable, limiting night‑time results.
