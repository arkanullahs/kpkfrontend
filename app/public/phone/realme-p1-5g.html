Realme P1 5G Is it worth buying?

The Realme P1 5G is a budget device that offers a strong display and gaming performance but comes with significant trade-offs regarding battery life and software stability.

Why we say that

Owners frequently report that the battery drains fast and the device experiences heating issues during use. You must be aware that this phone is only available as a gray import in Bangladesh.

There is no manufacturer warranty provided by the brand, so you are entirely dependent on the specific shop for any repair promises. Does the Realme P1 5G have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every listing currently available is a gray import. You will only receive whatever limited coverage the specific shop promises, and all repairs must be paid for out of your own pocket.

How is the battery life on the Realme P1 5G?

Owners report that the battery drains fast on this device. Users have specifically noted that the battery is draining drastically during daily use. If you are looking for a phone that lasts a long time on a single charge, this may not be the right choice.

Is the Realme P1 5G good for gaming?

The phone offers good performance for gaming according to owner ratings. It is powered by the Dimensity 7050 chipset, which handles games well. However, be aware that some users have reported heating issues while using the device.

How many software updates will this phone receive?

The Realme P1 5G is expected to receive 2 to 3 OS updates. Please note that the software experience includes a medium to high level of bloatware and advertisements, and some users have found the Realme UI 5.0 to be not fully optimized.
