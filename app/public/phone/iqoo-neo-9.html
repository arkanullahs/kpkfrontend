What works well

  • Fast Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 performance
  • Smooth 144Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • High 1400 nits peak brightness
  • Includes an ir blaster

Reported trade-offs

  • Overheats and throttles during gaming
  • Battery drains quickly
  • No official brand warranty
  • Limited remaining software updates

Why we say that

Owners report that the phone overheats and reduces performance, which is a known issue called thermal throttling. The 144Hz LTPO AMOLED display is a highlight, and the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset provides strong raw power. However, the battery life is a mixed bag.

While the capacity is 5160 mAh, users say it drains quickly. The cameras are decent but not top tier, and some owners have specifically questioned the camera quality. Since this phone was released in 2023, you should expect very little software update life remaining.

iQOO Neo 9: what buyers ask us

What is the iQOO Neo 9 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for iQOO Neo 9 in Bangladesh was ৳34,499, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there are no confirmed official units in stock. All available listings are gray imports, so you will only get a shop warranty. You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

How is the gaming performance and heating?

The performance is strong with a Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chip. However, it is widely reported that the device overheats and reduces performance during sustained gaming due to thermal throttling.

How long will the battery last?

The phone has a 5160 mAh battery. While some find it good, many reviews state that the battery drains quickly in real world use.

Will I get Android updates for a long time?

This phone was released in 2023. Because it is already 3 years old, you should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point.
