Buying verdict

Vivo V50 Is it worth buying?

The Vivo V50 offers exceptional battery endurance and a bright display, but it falls short in performance and video capabilities for its price.

Choose it if
You prioritize a long-lasting battery, a highly durable build, and a bright display for daily tasks.
Consider alternatives if
You are a serious mobile gamer or a content creator who requires 4K 60fps video recording.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Exceptional 6000 mAh battery life
  • Extremely bright 4500 nits AMOLED display
  • Professional portraits with Zeiss optics
  • Durable IP69 rated build quality

Reported trade-offs

  • Outdated UFS 2.2 storage speed
  • Video recording limited to 4K 30fps
  • Virtual proximity sensor issues during calls
  • Struggles with heavy gaming performance

Why we say that

Owners report that the 6000 mAh battery easily lasts up to 2 days, which is a major highlight. However, the experience is hampered by the use of outdated UFS 2.2 storage, which feels out of place in a phone at this price point. Gamers should be aware that the Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 chipset struggles during extended sessions, and the device tends to get warm.

Users are also frustrated by the lack of 4K 60fps video recording and a virtual proximity sensor that often fails to turn off the screen during calls. While the Zeiss optics perform well for portraits and low-light stills, the software includes a noticeable amount of bloatware. You are paying a premium for the build and screen, but the internal hardware does not always match the cost.

Does the Vivo V50 have a reliable battery for long days?

Yes, the phone features an exceptional 6000 mAh BlueVolt battery. Owners report that it provides enough endurance to last up to 2 days on a single charge.

Is the Vivo V50 suitable for heavy gaming?

The phone is not ideal for heavy gaming. While daily tasks remain smooth, the Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 chipset and UFS 2.2 storage struggle with demanding games, and the device often gets warm during extended sessions.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The camera system uses Zeiss optics which provide strong low-light stills. It is well-regarded for professional-looking portraits, though users should note that video recording is capped at 4K 30fps.

What is the difference between official and unofficial units?

Official units come with a brand warranty, while cheaper gray-import listings have no manufacturer cover. If you buy an unofficial unit, any service centre that accepts it will treat the repair as a paid service.
