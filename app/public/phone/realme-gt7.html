Realme GT7 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that lasts all day and charges in minutes, the Realme GT7 delivers that.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 7000 mAh battery with 100 W fast charging
  • 6000 nits peak brightness on a 6.78‑inch LTPO AMOLED
  • Dimensity 9400e gives strong performance for games
  • IP69 protection and solid build

Reported trade-offs4

  • Camera quality falls behind Xiaomi 14T
  • Realme UI shows medium‑high ads
  • Only shop warranty, no brand warranty
  • Battery drains faster when hotspot is on

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh battery paired with 100 W fast charging keeps you online with minimal downtime. The Dimensity 9400e chipset and 120 Hz LTPO AMOLED screen provide smooth performance for games and media. Build quality is solid with IP69 protection, and the 6000‑nit peak brightness makes outdoor use easy.

Owners praise the battery life and charging speed, but they note the camera falls short of premium rivals and the UI includes noticeable ads. The only warranty available is from the shop, so verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying. Overall it feels like a capable mid‑range device that excels in endurance and performance while compromising on photography and official support.
