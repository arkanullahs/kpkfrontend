Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Strong performance with Snapdragon 8s Gen 3
  • Excellent AMOLED display with 120Hz
  • Fast 90watt charging
  • High peak brightness of 2400 nits

Reported trade-offs

  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • Back panel scratches and dents easily
  • Average battery life
  • Occasional overheating and lagging

Why we say that

Owners appreciate the high performance and display quality, but the daily experience has some flaws. You will notice a high amount of bloatware and ads in the software. Some users report that the phone overheats and lags occasionally.

The build quality is decent, but the back panel scratches easily and can dent from falls. Battery life is described as average, and some users experience battery drain. There are also concerns regarding software updates, with one owner reporting a lack of OTA updates since mid 2025.

POCO F6: what buyers ask us

What is the POCO F6 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for POCO F6 in Bangladesh was ৳34,999, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, every listing recorded is a gray import. You will get a shop warranty instead of a brand backed warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

How is the gaming performance on the POCO F6?

Gaming is a strong point with an owner rating of 8.0. It uses the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset. Some users report that it can overheat and lag a bit during use.

Is the battery life good enough for a full day?

Reviews describe the battery life as average. Some owners report battery drain, though they find the 90watt fast charging helpful to manage the 5000 mAh battery.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The phone launched with Android 14 and is promised up to 3 major Android upgrades. However, some owners have complained about not receiving OTA updates since mid 2025.
