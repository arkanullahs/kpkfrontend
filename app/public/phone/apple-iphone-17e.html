Is the Apple iPhone 17e worth buying?

What is good

  • Powerful A19 processor
  • Premium aluminum and glass build
  • 48 MP daylight camera
  • Clean iOS with long‑term updates

What is not

  • 60 Hz screen feels outdated
  • Notch and thick bezels
  • Noisy low‑light photos
  • Slow 20 W charging

Buy it if Buy it if you value iOS performance, solid build and can accept a 60 Hz display.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a high‑refresh screen, strong night photography or fast charging.

Why we say that

The A19 chip and 8 GB RAM keep apps and games responsive, yet the 6.1" Super Retina XDR panel stays at 60 Hz and its bezels are thick, making scrolling feel less fluid than newer phones. Battery capacity stays at 4005 mAh, so day‑long use is possible but not generous, and the 20 W charger feels slow compared with higher‑watt options. The 48 MP main camera captures sharp daylight photos, but night shots are noisy and have a yellow tint, and there is no ultrawide lens.

Build quality is solid with aluminum, glass and Ceramic Shield 2, and MagSafe works for wireless charging. Software stays clean and receives up to six major iOS updates, which is a strong point for long‑term support.
