Is the Xiaomi 17 Pro Max worth buying?

What is good

  • Class-leading 7500 mAh battery endurance
  • Stunning 3500 nits peak brightness display
  • Premium build quality with IP68 rating
  • Powerful Snapdragon 8 Elite performance

What is not

  • Significant thermal throttling under load
  • China-only software lacks Google services
  • Inconsistent telephoto camera performance
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Buy it if You are an advanced user who knows how to sideload Google services and does not mind the lack of a local warranty.

Skip it if You need a reliable phone with full Google integration, Bangla language support, and a standard manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

While the 7500 mAh battery offers class-leading endurance, the software experience is a major hurdle for local users. You will face limitations like the lack of Google services, no Bangla language support, and aggressive Chinese cloud services. Owners report that the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset suffers from significant thermal throttling during heavy use, dropping to 50 percent of its peak performance.

The camera system is also inconsistent, with the telephoto lens often producing blurry images and the software applying skin tone processing tuned specifically for China. Since there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh, you are entirely dependent on the shop for any repairs. This device is a powerful piece of hardware that feels unfinished for anyone outside of China.

Does the Xiaomi 17 Pro Max have a manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you only receive whatever limited coverage the specific shop provides. All repairs must be paid for out of your own pocket.

How does the battery perform during daily use?

The battery performance is excellent. Owners rate the battery at 9.8 out of 10, noting that the 7500 mAh capacity provides class-leading endurance that is unmatched by most other devices currently on the market.

Is this phone good for heavy gaming?

While the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset is powerful, it is prone to thermal throttling. During prolonged stress tests, the performance can drop to 50 percent of its peak, which may impact your gaming experience over long sessions.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The device is scheduled to receive 3 to 4 operating system updates and 4 to 5 years of security updates. However, keep in mind that the China-only ROM lacks global features like Android Auto and Google services.
