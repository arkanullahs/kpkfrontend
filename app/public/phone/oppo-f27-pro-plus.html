OPPO F27 Pro Plus Is it worth buying?

Overall the OPPO F27 Pro Plus feels like a bright, fast charging phone that can last a day, but its sluggish chipset makes everyday use feel stilted.

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch AMOLED panel shines at 950 nits and runs at a smooth 120Hz, which owners rate highly. The 5000 mAh battery paired with 67W charging gives good endurance and quick top‑ups. However the Dimensity 7050 struggles with heavy apps and games, leading to noticeable lag.

The 50MP camera produces average photos and video playback often misses background music, which users find disappointing. Software runs ColorOS with medium bloat and receives about three to four major Android upgrades, similar to other flagships. The phone arrives as a gray import, so there is no official manufacturer warranty, only the shop’s limited cover.

Read full reasoning

Repairs will be paid out of pocket. If you can tolerate occasional lag and lack of brand warranty, the device offers a solid display and fast charge for its price.
