Buying verdict

OnePlus Ace 6 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run games for hours without hunting a charger, the OnePlus Ace 6 delivers that.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life, high refresh rate display and gaming power over camera quality and official warranty.

What works well4

  • 7800 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 165 Hz AMOLED screen feels very smooth
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite gives top gaming performance
  • 120 W fast charging refills huge battery quickly

Reported trade-offs4

  • Camera lacks telephoto and ultrawide is weak
  • Notification delays are noticeable
  • Thermal throttling after long gaming
  • No official OnePlus warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

Its 7800 mAh cell is among the largest you will find and easily survives a full day of heavy use, while 120 W charging refills it in roughly half an hour. The 6.83 inch AMOLED panel runs at 165 Hz and reaches 1800 nits, making scrolling and media look very smooth and bright. Powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite, the device scores near perfect marks for performance and gaming, and the built in IR blaster adds a handy remote function.

Owners, however, complain about a camera that feels basic; the 50 MP main lens is decent but the 8 MP ultrawide is weak and there is no telephoto option. ColorOS on the China version shows Chinese text, includes bloatware and often delays notifications. Thermal throttling appears after long gaming sessions, and the phone arrives without any official OnePlus warranty, only the shop’s limited cover.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

The 7800 mAh cell is rated as industry leading and owners report it easily survives a full day of heavy use, even with gaming and video playback, without needing a recharge. Fast 120 W charging restores most of the charge in about half an hour.

Will this phone handle heavy gaming without overheating?

Snapdragon 8 Elite provides top tier sustained performance and owners rate gaming at 9.2 out of 10. Under extreme stress the phone can reach 40‑45 °C and thermal throttling may appear after thirty minutes of continuous heavy gaming.

Is the camera good for low‑light photos?

The main sensor is a 50 MP unit with f/1.8 aperture and OIS, but the accompanying 8 MP ultrawide is weak and there is no telephoto lens. Reviewers give the camera a rating of five out of ten, indicating low‑light performance is modest at best.

Will I get official OnePlus warranty and regular software updates?

All units sold in Bangladesh are gray imports, so they come without OnePlus’s official warranty. The shop you buy from may offer its own limited warranty, but repairs are paid. Software updates follow the global schedule for ColorOS or OxygenOS, but CN builds may need manual conversion and can be update fragile.
