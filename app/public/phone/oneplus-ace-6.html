OnePlus Ace 6 Is it worth buying?

If you can live without a brand warranty, the OnePlus Ace 6 gives you flagship performance and a massive 7800 mAh battery for under ৳60 k.

  • 1800‑nit 165 Hz AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite delivers smooth gaming
  • 7800 mAh battery lasts multiple days

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official OnePlus warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset and 12 GB RAM make everyday tasks feel instant and gaming runs at a smooth 90 fps, though PUBG Mobile is capped at that level. The 6.83‑inch AMOLED panel shines with 1800‑nit peak brightness and a 165 Hz refresh rate, so scrolling and media look fluid. Charging at 120 W fills the huge cell in less than an hour, and owners say the battery can last several days of normal use.

Build quality is solid with an aluminium frame and IP68 rating, and the phone includes an IR blaster. The main 50 MP camera is respectable, but low‑light shots, ultra‑wide and selfie quality are disappointing. The device can warm to about 40 °C during long gaming sessions, and the CN version lacks Google services and Bangla language support.

Read full reasoning

Software is clean ColorOS 16 with low ads, but a button‑hold glitch has been reported. Overall it feels like a high‑spec device at a gray‑import price, as long as you accept the limited warranty and occasional network quirks.

Will the OnePlus Ace 6 receive official Android updates?

The phone ships with Android 16 and ColorOS 16 and, like other OnePlus flagships, is promised four major OS updates. Because it is a gray‑import, the update path can be fragile and may depend on the specific software build you receive.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 7800 mAh cell provides exceptional endurance, with owners reporting it lasts several days of normal use. The 120 W charger refills the battery in less than an hour, making long‑term use practical despite the lack of a brand warranty.

Is the camera good for low‑light and ultra‑wide photos?

The main 50 MP sensor performs decently in good light, but low‑light shots are weak and the ultra‑wide lens lacks detail. Selfie quality is also below expectations, so the phone is not ideal for those photography needs.

All listings are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. You rely on the shop’s limited warranty and must pay for any repairs. Verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchase.
