Is the Redmi 17 worth buying?

What is good

  • 7500 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 6.9 inch bright 120 Hz display
  • 45 W fast charging
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Heavy ad clutter in HyperOS
  • Software feels slow to navigate
  • No 5G support

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, high refresh display, and can tolerate ads.

Skip it if Skip it if you require a clean UI or 5G connectivity.

Why we say that

The massive 7500 mAh battery earns a 9 out of 10 rating and many owners say it easily powers a full day of mixed use. The 6.9 inch IPS LCD screen reaches 825 nits and refreshes at 120 Hz, making scrolling and gaming feel smooth and bright. Performance is strong thanks to the Helio G91 Ultra chip, 4 to 6 GB RAM and a 9.5 rating for gaming and overall speed.

The 50 MP main camera includes optical image stabilization, giving decent results in low light, though the selfie camera is only 8 MP. Software runs Android 16 with HyperOS 3, but the UI is packed with ads and bloat, which lowers the ease of use score to 5. Owners appreciate the headphone jack, FM radio and IR blaster, but the lack of 5G may limit future connectivity.

Uzzal Tech Studio found the Redmi 17 to be an attractive option for buyers looking for an expansive 6.9-inch screen, stylish design, and exceptional multi-day battery endurance from its 7500 mAh cell. However, the reviewer expressed disappointment over the slow 5-to-6-second camera launch time and the inclusion of only 4GB of physical RAM at its price point, concluding that while it is great for casual media consumption, users looking for gaming power or quick photography should choose an alternative.

Jacky YBT · Xiaomi Redmi 17 5G First Look & Hands On! ↗

Jacky YBT concluded that the Redmi 17 5G is a functional entry-level phone packed with practical utility, highlighting its massive 7500 mAh battery, reverse power-bank charging, stereo speakers, and customizable rear RGB lighting. On the other hand, he noted major budget compromises including a low 720p display resolution with a thick chin, a very basic camera setup limited to 1080p 30fps recording, and a low-end processor that requires lowest graphical settings in games.
