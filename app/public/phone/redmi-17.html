Is the Redmi 17 worth buying?

What is good

  • 7,500 mAh battery with 45 W fast charging
  • 50 MP main camera
  • 120 Hz refresh rate on large screen
  • Headphone jack, FM radio and IR blaster

What is not

  • 720p resolution on 6.9‑inch display
  • Obsolete Helio G91 chipset slows everything
  • Heavy HyperOS bloatware and ads
  • Considered a downgrade from earlier Redmi models

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize battery life and basic photography over speed.

Skip it if Skip it if you need smooth gaming, high‑resolution display, or a clean Android experience.

Why we say that

Its 7,500 mAh battery and 45 W charger give you more than a full day's use without hunting for an outlet. The 50 MP main camera can capture detailed shots in good light, yet low‑light pictures lack punch. The 6.9‑inch panel runs at 720p, so the display looks soft despite a 120 Hz refresh rate.

Under the hood, the Helio G91 Ultra chipset struggles with modern apps and games, and HyperOS adds a lot of ads and pre‑installed software that tax the RAM. Owners rate battery life near perfect but give performance, gaming and video scores below four. In short, you get massive endurance and basic features at a low price, but you also inherit outdated hardware and a cluttered software experience.

Uzzal Tech Studio found the Redmi 17 to be an attractive option for buyers looking for an expansive 6.9-inch screen, stylish design, and exceptional multi-day battery endurance from its 7500 mAh cell. However, the reviewer expressed disappointment over the slow 5-to-6-second camera launch time and the inclusion of only 4GB of physical RAM at its price point, concluding that while it is great for casual media consumption, users looking for gaming power or quick photography should choose an alternative.

Jacky YBT · Xiaomi Redmi 17 5G First Look & Hands On! ↗

Jacky YBT concluded that the Redmi 17 5G is a functional entry-level phone packed with practical utility, highlighting its massive 7500 mAh battery, reverse power-bank charging, stereo speakers, and customizable rear RGB lighting. On the other hand, he noted major budget compromises including a low 720p display resolution with a thick chin, a very basic camera setup limited to 1080p 30fps recording, and a low-end processor that requires lowest graphical settings in games.
