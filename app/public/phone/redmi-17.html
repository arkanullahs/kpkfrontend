Buying verdict

Redmi 17 Is it worth buying?

The Redmi 17 will keep you powered for two days, but you will constantly fight with a sluggish system and a blurry screen.

Choose it if
Buy if you need a phone that lasts all day and you are fine with basic performance and a low‑resolution display.
Consider alternatives if
Skip if you want smooth gaming, a sharp screen, or a clean software experience.

What works well

  • Huge 7500 mAh battery
  • 45 W fast charging
  • Headphone jack and FM radio
  • IR blaster for remote control

Reported trade-offs

  • 720p screen on 6.9‑inch panel
  • Outdated Helio G91 chipset lags
  • HyperOS heavy with ads

Why we say that

Its 7500 mAh battery and 45 W charger give you unmatched endurance, and the phone still includes a headphone jack, FM radio and an IR blaster. The 6.9‑inch IPS LCD is large but only 720p, so the picture looks soft and pixelated. The Helio G91 Ultra chipset is old and struggles with modern apps, especially games, and HyperOS adds a lot of ads and bloat that eat the limited 4 GB RAM.

Build quality feels cheap and the 50 MP camera does not shine in low light. Official units at ৳21,299 carry a brand warranty, while cheaper gray imports lack manufacturer cover and need paid repairs. Overall, you get massive battery life at the cost of performance, display sharpness and software polish.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 7500 mAh battery is rated as industry‑leading, so it can easily last more than a full day with moderate use and may stretch to two days if you keep screen brightness low and avoid heavy apps.

Is the Redmi 17 good for gaming?

The phone uses the Helio G91 Ultra chipset, which owners describe as obsolete and laggy. Gaming scores are low, and demanding titles will stutter, making it unsuitable for serious mobile gaming.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor receives a low rating and struggles in dim environments, producing noisy and less detailed photos. It is not comparable to higher‑end Redmi models for night shots.
