Why we say that

The Unisoc T612 chipset handles basic apps and social media but struggles with heavy games. The 8 MP rear camera and 5 MP selfie lens capture acceptable photos in good light, yet low light performance is weak. Realme UI adds medium high bloatware and ads that can feel intrusive over time.

The phone received about two to three Android version updates since its 2022 launch, so future updates are unlikely. No official manufacturer warranty is listed; only a shop warranty may apply and you should verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

Realme C30: what buyers ask us

What is the Realme C30 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Realme C30 in Bangladesh was ৳11,990, checked 24 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

How long will the battery last on a normal day?

The 5,000 mAh battery is sized for a full day of typical use such as browsing, social media and occasional video. Heavy gaming or screen on time will reduce the endurance, but most users report it lasting until evening.

Is the camera good in low light?

The phone uses an 8 MP rear sensor and a 5 MP front sensor. In good lighting the pictures are acceptable, but in low light the images become noisy and lack detail, so it is not a strong point.

Does the phone come with an official warranty?

No official manufacturer warranty is listed for this gray import model. The seller may offer a shop warranty, so you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status and understand the terms before purchase.

Will the phone receive future Android updates?

The Realme C30 launched in 2022 and received about two to three Android version updates. Being four years old, it is unlikely to get many more updates, so software support is limited.
