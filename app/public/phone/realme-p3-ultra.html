What works well

  • Efficient 6000 mAh battery
  • Bright 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Fast 80W charging
  • Up to 512GB storage options

Reported trade-offs

  • Prone to thermal throttling
  • Scratch prone curved display
  • Hazardous battery wear reports
  • Medium to high bloatware

Why we say that

Owners report that the quad curved display scratches easily. While the Dimensity 8350 Ultra provides good performance, the device is prone to thermal throttling under load. There are also concerning reports from owners about battery wear happening twice as fast, which some say can be hazardous.

You will deal with medium to high levels of bloatware and ads. Since only gray imports are available, you must rely on shop warranties rather than brand support. You should verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before paying.

Realme P3 Ultra: what buyers ask us

What is the Realme P3 Ultra price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Realme P3 Ultra in Bangladesh was ৳36,990, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No official brand backed warranty is available. All recorded listings are imports. You will only get a shop warranty, so you must confirm the BTRC status and IMEI with the seller.

How is the gaming performance on this device?

The phone uses the Dimensity 8350 Ultra chipset and gets a performance rating of 8.0. However, owners report that the device is prone to thermal throttling when under load.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The phone launched with Android 15 and Realme UI 6.0. It is expected to receive up to 3 major Android upgrades.

Is the battery life good for daily use?

The 6000 mAh battery is described as efficient and lasts long. However, some owners have reported that the battery wears down twice as fast and can be hazardous.
