Honor Magic V5 Is it worth buying?

If you want a foldable with strong performance and fast charging, the Honor Magic V5 delivers that but feels bulky and shows a visible crease.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a high end foldable for gaming and multitasking and can accept a thicker body.
Consider alternatives if
You prioritize a slim profile, excellent selfie photos, or a completely ad free experience.

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset and 16 GB RAM make multitasking and gaming run smoothly, and the 66W wired charger refills the 5820 mAh battery in under an hour. The 7.95 inch LTPO AMOLED panel reaches a 5000 nit peak, so outdoor visibility is excellent, though owners notice a minor crease on the inner screen. The triple camera setup offers a 50 MP main sensor with optical image stabilization and a 3× telephoto lens, yet colour rendering appears harsh and the 20 MP selfie camera performs poorly in low light.

Software runs MagicOS 10 on Android 15 with medium ads and a timer bug, and the phone receives up to seven major Android updates. Build quality feels premium with IP58/59 protection, but the 16.25 mm thickness is noticeably thicker than competing foldables.
