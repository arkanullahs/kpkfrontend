Is the Samsung Galaxy M14 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6000 mAh battery capacity
  • Smooth 90Hz display refresh rate
  • Includes a dedicated headphone jack
  • Capable 50MP main camera sensor

What is not

  • Widespread lag and overheating issues
  • Poor camera performance in low light
  • Very limited remaining software support
  • Significant price gap for official warranty

Buy it if Buy this phone only if you need a basic device for light tasks and are comfortable with the risks of an unofficial unit.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you want a smooth gaming experience, reliable low-light photography, or long-term software security updates.

Why we say that

While the 6000 mAh battery provides a large capacity, the Exynos 1330 chipset is widely reported to suffer from lag, stuttering, and overheating during daily use. Since the phone was released in 2023, it is now three years old, meaning you should expect little to no remaining software update life. Users frequently complain about the camera performance, noting that photos appear grainy and noisy in low-light conditions despite the 50MP sensor.

You must decide if the unofficial price of 16,990 taka is worth the risk of these hardware and software issues compared to the official warranty price of 31,990 taka. This device is not a modern performer, and the combination of aging software and reported chipset instability makes it a difficult recommendation for anyone needing a reliable daily driver.

Will the Samsung Galaxy M14 5G get more software updates?

Because this phone was released in 2023 and is now three years old, you should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point. While some Samsung series receive four to seven years of support, this specific model is nearing the end of its cycle.

Is the Samsung Galaxy M14 5G good for gaming?

The phone is not recommended for gaming. There are widespread reports of lag, stuttering, and overheating caused by the Exynos 1330 chipset. These performance issues make it difficult to maintain a stable experience during demanding tasks or extended use.

How does the camera perform at night?

Users report poor low-light camera performance on this device. Despite the 50MP main camera, photos taken in dim conditions are widely reported to be grainy and noisy. You should not expect high-quality results when shooting in low light.

The unofficial version is available for 16,990 taka, while the official version costs 31,990 taka. Buying the official version provides the security of a warranty, whereas the unofficial version is significantly cheaper but lacks that official support.
