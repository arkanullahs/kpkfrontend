Samsung Galaxy M14 5G Is it worth buying?

If you can live with lag and no official warranty, the Galaxy M14 5G gives you a big screen and huge battery for under ৳17,000.

What works well4

  • 6.6 inch 90Hz PLS LCD display
  • 6000 mAh battery with 25W charge
  • 50 MP main camera
  • Headphone jack

Reported trade-offs4

  • Exynos 1330 causes lag and overheating
  • Low light photos are grainy
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Software updates likely ended

Why we say that

The 6.6 inch PLS LCD runs at 90Hz, which makes scrolling feel smooth. The 6000 mAh cell easily lasts a full day of moderate use and the 25W charger refills it quickly. The 50 MP main sensor captures sharp daylight shots, but low light pictures turn grainy and noisy.

The Exynos 1330 chipset is known for occasional stutter, overheating and slower gaming performance. The phone arrives as a gray import, so Samsung’s standard warranty does not apply and any repair will be paid for. Software updates are unlikely because the model is three years old.

Overall it is a budget‑friendly 5G option if you value battery and display over speed and long‑term support.

Will the phone receive Android updates?

The phone launched in 2023 and belongs to the A series which normally gets four to six years of updates. Being three years old, it is unlikely to receive any new Android version from Samsung.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 6000 mAh battery combined with a 6.6 inch display typically lasts a full day of moderate use and can stretch to two days with light usage. The 25W charger refills it in about an hour and a half.

The 50 MP sensor performs well in daylight, but many users report grainy and noisy results when shooting in low light despite the f/1.8 aperture.
