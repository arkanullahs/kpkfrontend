Is the Samsung Galaxy A14 worth buying?

What is good

  • Excellent battery endurance
  • Reliable 5000 mAh capacity
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • 1080p display resolution

What is not

  • Severe system lag and multitasking issues
  • Extremely slow 15W charging
  • Poor low light camera performance
  • Heavy bloatware and ads

Buy it if You prioritize long battery life above all else and only need a basic device for light tasks.

Skip it if You need a phone that can handle multitasking, gaming, or modern software demands without lagging.

Why we say that

While the 5000 mAh battery provides excellent endurance that easily lasts a full day, the rest of the experience is hampered by poor performance. Users frequently complain that the phone heats up when opening more than two apps and suffers from incorrect storage reporting. The 15W charging is extremely slow, taking around two and a half hours for a full refill, and the box does not include an adapter.

With a dim PLS LCD screen, thick bezels, and a dated waterdrop notch, the hardware feels behind the times. Owners also note that the camera struggles in low light and the software comes with heavy bloatware and ads. Given that this model is three years old, you should expect very little remaining software support.

Most buyers feel this phone offers poor value compared to other options available at the 23,499 taka price point.

Does the Samsung Galaxy A14 perform well for mobile gaming?

No, the phone is not suitable for gaming. Owners report severe lag and poor sustained performance when playing games. The Helio G80 chipset struggles with multitasking, and the device often heats up during use.

How long does the battery last on a single charge?

The 5000 mAh battery offers excellent endurance. Owners report that it easily lasts for a full day or more on a single charge, making it one of the few reliable aspects of the device.

Is the camera good for taking photos at night?

The camera struggles significantly in low light conditions. While the 50MP main sensor is considered okay for basic photography, it lacks an ultrawide lens and does not perform well when the lighting is dim.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

This phone was released in 2023 and is already three years old. You should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point, despite the device being upgradable to Android 15.
