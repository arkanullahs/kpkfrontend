Is the Samsung Galaxy A14 worth buying?

What is good

  • Large 5,000 mAh battery lasts two days
  • 50 MP main camera captures decent shots
  • Solid plastic build resembles flagship look
  • Headphone jack for wired audio

What is not

  • 15 W charging is painfully slow
  • 4 GB RAM struggles with One UI
  • LCD display is dim with thick bezels
  • Limited software updates for a three‑year‑old phone

Buy it if Buy if you need a cheap phone with a big battery and acceptable camera and can tolerate slow charging and modest performance.

Skip it if Skip if you want smooth gaming, fast charging, or long‑term software support.

Why we say that

Its 5,000 mAh battery truly lasts about 48 hours with moderate use, yet the 15 W charger needs around two and a half hours for a full charge. The 6.6‑inch PLS LCD shows sharp 1080p images but appears dim and is surrounded by thick bezels. The Helio G80 with 4 GB RAM struggles with Samsung's One UI, causing lag in multitasking and very low frame rates in games like PUBG.

The 50 MP main sensor captures decent photos for a budget phone, but users report no usable ultrawide mode and a useless macro lens. Plastic build feels solid and mimics flagship styling, though it feels cheap. Software comes with Android 13 and can be upgraded to Android 15, but at three years old the update window is closing.

Bloatware appears during setup, and storage can falsely show full.

How long does the battery last on the Galaxy A14?

The 5,000 mAh battery is rated 8.7 out of 10 and typically lasts about two days on moderate daily use. Users report that it can comfortably survive a full day of mixed activities without needing a recharge.

Can I play games like PUBG on the Galaxy A14?

Gaming performance is very limited. The Helio G80 with 4 GB RAM often drops to around 30 fps in PUBG, heats up quickly, and struggles with multitasking, making it unsuitable for demanding games.

How good is the camera in low light on the Galaxy A14?

The 50 MP main sensor delivers decent budget‑level photos, but low‑light results are average and the phone lacks a functional ultrawide lens. The macro camera is considered useless by owners.

Does the Galaxy A14 have official warranty and software updates in Bangladesh?

Yes, the phone comes with Samsung's official Bangladesh warranty and can be serviced through the brand's network. It runs Android 13, is upgradeable to Android 15, but being three years old, further updates may be limited.
