Samsung Galaxy A14 Is it worth buying?

The Galaxy A14 will give you a full day and a half of screen time, but expect sluggish performance and slow charging.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a big screen, solid battery and can accept slow charging and modest performance.
Consider alternatives if
You want fast gaming, smooth multitasking, or a guaranteed manufacturer warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Long‑lasting 5000 mAh battery
  • 50 MP main camera for a budget phone
  • Large 6.6‑inch 1080p display
  • Headphone jack for wired audio

Reported trade-offs4

  • 15 W charging is very slow
  • 4 GB RAM causes noticeable lag
  • Display dim with thick bezels
  • Cheap imports have no official warranty

Why we say that

Its 5000 mAh battery often reaches two days on moderate use, which owners rate highly. The 50 MP main sensor captures decent photos in good light, though the lack of a true ultrawide lens and a useless macro mode limit versatility. The 6.6‑inch 1080p PLS LCD is sharp but appears dim and is surrounded by thick bezels, and the plastic body feels solid yet cheap.

The Helio G80 paired with 4 GB RAM struggles with multitasking and games such as PUBG, dropping to around 30 fps and heating up. Charging at 15 W takes about two and a half hours, which many find painfully slow. Software runs One UI Core with many pre‑installed sponsored apps, and at three years old the phone is close to the end of its update cycle.

How long does the battery last on the Galaxy A14?

Can I play games like PUBG on the Galaxy A14?

Gaming performance is weak; the Helio G80 with 4 GB RAM struggles to keep above 30 fps in PUBG, heats up quickly, and multitasking feels sluggish, so expect a sub‑par experience.

Does the Galaxy A14 receive software updates?

The phone launched in 2023 and is already three years old, so it is near the end of the typical 4‑6 year update window for Samsung A‑series devices, meaning few, if any, future Android upgrades are expected.
