Tecno Spark 50 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Tecno Spark 50 Pro is a device defined by a massive battery that struggles to overcome its dated performance and disappointing display quality.

3 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a phone with a massive battery for basic daily tasks and value water resistance over display quality.
Consider alternatives if
You enjoy mobile gaming, care about screen sharpness, or dislike intrusive ads and pre-installed software.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well3

  • Exceptional 6000 mAh battery endurance
  • Solid build with IP68/69 water resistance
  • Includes FM radio and IR blaster

Reported trade-offs3

  • Low resolution HD+ display
  • Heavy bloatware and ad-heavy software
  • Poor performance for modern gaming

Why we say that

Owners frequently report that the HD+ resolution on a large 6.78-inch screen feels like a significant regression for a 2026 release. While the build quality is solid and includes IP68/69 water resistance, the user experience is hampered by heavy software bloatware and persistent ads. The Helio G100 chipset handles basic daily tasks, but it is widely considered poor for modern gaming.

Camera performance is basic at best, with users noting that it struggles significantly with dynamic range and low-light detail. Many buyers feel the phone is a downgrade from the previous model, making it a difficult recommendation for anyone who values screen clarity or gaming capability. If you prioritize battery life above all else, this phone might serve you, but most users will find the overall package lacking in modern standards.

How does the battery perform on this device?

The phone features an exceptional 6000 mAh battery that provides excellent endurance. Owners rate the battery performance very highly at 9.5 out of 10, making it the strongest feature of the device.

Is this phone suitable for playing modern games?

No, the Helio G100 chipset is dated and considered poor for modern gaming. Owners have given the gaming performance a low rating of 4.5 out of 10, suggesting it is only sufficient for basic tasks.

The device runs on Android 16 and HiOS 16. It is expected to receive between 1 and 2 OS updates, though the update process is described as slow by users.
