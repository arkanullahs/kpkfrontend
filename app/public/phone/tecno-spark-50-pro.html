Is the Tecno Spark 50 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Large 6000 mAh battery capacity
  • Premium build with IP69 rating
  • Includes FM radio and IR blaster

What is not

  • Downgraded IPS LCD display with poor resolution
  • Stagnant Helio G100 performance
  • Heavy bloatware and intrusive ads
  • Regression in charging speed

Buy it if Buy this phone only if you prioritize a large battery and an IP69 rating over display quality and gaming performance.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you expect a modern display, fast charging, or a clean software experience without excessive ads.

Why we say that

Owners are particularly frustrated by the shift from AMOLED to an IPS LCD display with low resolution, which feels outdated for a modern smartphone. While the 6000 mAh battery provides a large capacity, users have noted a disappointing regression in charging speeds compared to previous models. Performance remains stagnant as the Helio G100 chipset offers no meaningful upgrades over older versions.

The software experience is frequently criticized for being cluttered with heavy bloatware, intrusive ads, and pushy AI features that hinder daily usability. Although the phone features a premium look and an IP69 rating, the camera system is widely considered mediocre and deceptive in its design. With low owner ratings across display, performance, and gaming categories, this phone fails to deliver a balanced experience for the price.
