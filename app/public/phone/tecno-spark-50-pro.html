Buying verdict

Tecno Spark 50 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can sit on a charger for a few minutes and run for two days, the Tecno Spark 50 Pro delivers that basic endurance.

Choose it if
You want long battery life and basic photography at a low price.
Consider alternatives if
You need high end gaming, low light camera or reliable fast charging.

What works well

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts around two days
  • 50 MP main camera captures decent daylight photos
  • 120 Hz IPS LCD gives smooth scrolling
  • FM radio and IR blaster for extra utility

Reported trade-offs

  • 720p LCD screen feels low resolution
  • HiOS adds heavy bloat and ads
  • 4 GB RAM struggles with multitasking
  • Charging speed sometimes drops to 18 W

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh battery easily reaches two days of mixed use, and the 120 Hz IPS LCD scrolls smoothly despite its 720p resolution. Daylight photos are acceptable with the 50 MP sensor, but low light shots become noisy and lack detail. The Helio G100 Ultimate handles everyday apps, yet multitasking and modern games feel sluggish, especially with only 4 GB of RAM.

Charging claims range from 60 W to 18 W, so real‑world speed can be slow. HiOS 16 brings AI tools but also heavy bloat and ads, and software updates are limited to one or two versions. The phone feels solid with IP69 rating, but the downgrade from previous AMOLED panels and the lack of official warranty on gray imports are common complaints.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The Spark 50 Pro packs a 6000 mAh cell that owners report can easily stretch to two days of mixed use, including calls, messaging and occasional video playback. Heavy streaming will shorten that time but it still outlasts many rivals.

Will the phone handle modern games smoothly?

With the Helio G100 Ultimate and 4 GB of RAM, the phone manages everyday apps fine but struggles when several modern games run together. Simple titles run okay, yet demanding titles may lag or drop frames.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor produces clear pictures in bright daylight, but owners note that low light scenes turn noisy and lack detail. There is no optical zoom, so night shots remain a weak point.
