Is the OPPO Reno15 F 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 7,000 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • Fast 80 W wired charging
  • Premium aluminum frame with IP68/69 rating
  • 50 MP front ultrawide selfie camera

What is not

  • Outdated Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 slows gaming
  • Unstable Wi‑Fi and 5G connectivity
  • No eSIM support
  • No HDR10+ on display

Buy it if Buy it if you value long battery life and a premium build more than cutting‑edge performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need smooth high‑end gaming or future‑proof specs.

Why we say that

Its 7,000 mAh battery paired with 80 W charging gives you long endurance and quick top‑ups, which owners rate as phenomenal. The premium aluminum frame with glass back and IP68/69 rating feels solid and safe from rain. The 6.57‑inch AMOLED panel runs smooth 120 Hz but lacks HDR10+ and typical brightness is modest, so outdoor viewing can feel dim.

The 50 MP front ultrawide camera captures decent selfies, yet the main camera, though capable of 4K video, scores only average in low‑light. Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 is already three years old, making gaming sluggish and causing occasional Wi‑Fi or 5G drops. Software runs ColorOS 16 with Android 16, but lockscreen ads and medium bloat linger.

Overall, the phone feels overpriced for its dated processor and missing eSIM, but its battery and build quality may still appeal to some.

How long does the battery last in daily use?
