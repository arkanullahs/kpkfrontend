Buying verdict

Honor X7d Is it worth buying?

The Honor X7d is a device defined by its massive battery life, but it falls short in display quality and camera performance.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a phone that lasts all day on a single charge and do not mind a basic display.
Consider alternatives if
You care about high-resolution content consumption, 5G speeds, or taking quality photos in low light.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Exceptional 6500 mAh battery endurance
  • Sturdy build with IP64 rating
  • Smooth multitasking with UFS 2.2 storage
  • Includes 35W fast charger in box

Reported trade-offs4

  • Disappointing low-resolution TFT LCD display
  • Poor low-light camera and video stabilization
  • Lacks 5G connectivity
  • Slow charging speed for the battery size

Why we say that

Owners consistently praise the 6500 mAh battery for its exceptional endurance, making it a reliable companion for long days. However, the experience is marred by a disappointing TFT LCD panel that suffers from low resolution and a thick lower chin. Users are particularly unhappy with the camera, citing poor low-light details and weak video stabilization.

While the Snapdragon 685 chipset handles daily multitasking well, the lack of 5G connectivity at this price point is a significant drawback for many buyers. The build is sturdy with an IP64 rating, yet the overall value proposition is questionable given the performance limitations. If you prioritize battery longevity above all else, this phone delivers, but those seeking a sharp display or high-end photography will likely find themselves frustrated by the compromises made here.

Honor X7d costs ৳22,999 to ৳27,999 in Bangladesh on official listings, in stock at 7 shops we track as of 13 Sep 2026. The unofficial price starts at ৳23,490.

Is the battery life actually good for heavy users?

Yes, the phone features a massive 6500 mAh battery that offers exceptional real-world endurance. Owners have rated the battery performance very highly at 9.3 out of 10, making it one of the strongest features of this device.

Does the Honor X7d support 5G networks in Bangladesh?

No, the device does not support 5G connectivity. This is a major point of concern for many owners who feel that a phone at this price point should include modern network capabilities.

Should I buy the official version or the gray import?

You should choose the official unit to receive the brand warranty. Gray imports have no manufacturer cover, and service centers will only accept them for paid repairs if they accept them at all.

How does the camera perform for night photography?

The camera performance is disappointing, especially in low-light conditions. Owners report poor details in dark environments and weak stabilization when recording 1080p video, leading to significant dissatisfaction with the camera system.
