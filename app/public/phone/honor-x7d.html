Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • Exceptional 6500mAh battery endurance
  • Sturdy build with SGS drop resistance
  • IP64 rating for protection
  • Smooth day to day multitasking

Reported trade-offs

  • Low resolution TFT LCD display
  • Poor low light camera details
  • No 5G connectivity
  • Slow charging for the battery size

Why we say that

The massive 6500mAh battery provides exceptional endurance for real world use. However, owners are unhappy with the display and camera. The TFT LCD panel has a low resolution that is noticeable when watching content, and the lower chin is quite thick.

Camera performance is disappointing, especially with poor low light details and weak video stabilization. While day to day multitasking is smooth, the Snapdragon 685 chipset lacks 5G connectivity. Many users feel the device is overpriced for a 4G phone in the Bangladesh market.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

Official warranty applies only to official units. The cheapest listings are gray imports with no manufacturer cover. If you take a gray import to a service centre, they will only accept it as a paid repair.

How is the camera performance in low light?

The camera performance is disappointing. Owners report poor details in low light and weak stabilization for 1080p videos. Many users state they are not happy with the camera.

Is the battery life and charging speed good?

The 6500mAh battery offers exceptional real world endurance. However, the included 35W fast charger is considered slow for a battery of this size.

Can I use this phone for heavy gaming?

The phone uses a Snapdragon 685 chipset. While it handles multitasking well, owners gave gaming a low rating of 4.5 out of 10.
