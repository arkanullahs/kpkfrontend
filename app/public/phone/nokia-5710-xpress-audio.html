Nokia 5710 Xpress Audio Is it worth buying?

This device is a basic feature phone designed for simple communication rather than modern smartphone tasks.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if

Consider alternatives if

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Strong standby battery life
  • Sturdy plastic build quality
  • Simple and easy to use
  • No bloatware or advertisements

Reported trade-offs4

  • Very poor camera quality
  • Low resolution display
  • Incapable of modern gaming
  • Extremely basic performance

Why we say that

Owners report that the build quality is sturdy despite the basic plastic construction. You will find the battery life reliable with strong standby time typical of this category. However, the user experience is limited by a small, low-resolution display that performs poorly by modern standards.

Performance is extremely basic, and the phone is incapable of running modern apps or games. The camera quality is also rated very low by users. Because the brand is currently in a transition phase following the HMD split, you should not expect significant software updates.

Read full reasoning

If you need a secondary device for calls or a simple handset that does not require constant charging, this phone serves that purpose well. Do not expect it to function like a smartphone or handle any media-heavy tasks.
