Nokia 5710 Xpress Audio Is it worth buying?

The Nokia 5710 Xpress Audio is a basic feature phone that struggles to meet modern expectations for daily reliability.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a simple, no-frills device for basic calling and listening to the radio.
Consider alternatives if
You expect to play games, use apps, or need a device with long battery life.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Easy to use interface
  • Includes FM radio
  • No bloatware or ads
  • Official warranty support

Reported trade-offs4

  • Poor sustained performance
  • Unusable for gaming
  • Small and dim display
  • Short battery endurance

Why we say that

Owners report that the device suffers from poor performance under sustained loads and is essentially unusable for gaming due to the lack of a dedicated GPU. The 2.4 inch TFT LCD display is small and dim, leading to poor visibility in many lighting conditions. Battery life is a significant concern, as the 1450 mAh capacity is considered tiny and drains quickly according to user feedback.

While the interface is easy to use, the hardware limitations make it difficult to recommend for anything beyond the most basic communication tasks. Since the phone was released in 2022, you should expect little to no remaining software update life. The build quality is plastic and offers only basic durability.

Read full reasoning

If you prioritize a simple device for calls and radio, this might function, but do not expect a smooth experience for any other tasks.
