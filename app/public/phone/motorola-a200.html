Motorola A200 Is it worth buying?

The Motorola A200 is a basic 2G device that serves as a simple communication tool rather than a modern smartphone.

Why we say that

Living with this phone means accepting significant limitations, as owners report that it lacks Bluetooth connectivity and cannot support 4G networks like Jio. The 1.77-inch screen is functional for reading text but lacks the clarity found in modern devices. While the hard plastic build feels durable, the performance is rated quite low by users.

You must be aware that this is an unofficial gray-import device, meaning there is no manufacturer warranty available in Bangladesh. Any repairs will be at your own expense, and you should verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing. Since the newer Motorola A300 is available for ৳3,300, it is generally considered the better value option for those looking for a budget device.

Does the Motorola A200 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit is a gray import, so you must rely on the specific warranty terms provided by the shop where you purchase the device.

Can I use this phone for UPI payments or scanning QR codes?

No, the Motorola A200 does not have camera hardware. Because it lacks a camera, it is impossible to scan QR codes for UPI payments, though the software does include UPI via Gupshup.

How long does the battery last on a single charge?

The device features an 800mAh battery. Owners report that it offers reliable multi-day standby time, which is typical for 2G devices of this category.

Will this phone work with my 4G SIM card?

No, this is a 2G-only device. It is incompatible with 4G-only networks, and owners specifically report that Jio SIM cards will not work in this phone.
