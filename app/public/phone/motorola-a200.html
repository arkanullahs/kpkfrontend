Why we say that

The device runs Hello UI which feels close to stock Android and does not load you with heavy ads. It is cheap enough for basic messaging, browsing and light apps. Owners often complain about the lack of any official Motorola warranty, meaning any repair will cost extra and you must rely on the shop’s promise.

Software updates are sparse, usually only one to three OS upgrades over the phone’s life, so you may stay on an older version for a while. The newer Motorola A300 is listed for ৳3,300 and generally offers a better value, so many buyers end up choosing that instead. Because it is a gray‑import you should verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying.
