Buying verdict

Tecno Spark 50 4G Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day without charging, the Spark 50’s massive battery is its biggest advantage.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life and a large screen over gaming power and high‑resolution visuals.
Consider alternatives if
You need fast charging, strong gaming performance, or a crisp display.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Huge 7,000 mAh battery
  • 50 MP main camera
  • 120 Hz refresh rate
  • Headphone jack and FM radio

Reported trade-offs4

  • Very slow 18 W charging
  • Low 720p resolution display
  • Weak Helio G81 performance
  • Mediocre low‑light camera

Why we say that

The 7,000 mAh cell easily stretches to two days for most users, and the 120 Hz panel feels smooth despite its low 720p resolution. Daily tasks run without major hiccups, but the Helio G81 struggles with demanding games and the 18 W charger takes a long time to refill the huge battery. The 50 MP main sensor captures decent daylight shots, yet low‑light photos appear soft and the camera software feels under‑tuned.

Build quality is solid plastic with an IP64 rating, giving a premium look that hides some cheap details. Software includes HiOS 16 with noticeable bloatware and a dark‑mode glitch, and only one or two Android updates are expected. Overall, the phone feels overpriced for the performance and display it offers.
