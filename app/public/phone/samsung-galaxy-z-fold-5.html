Is the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 5 worth buying?

What is good

  • 7.6 inch 120Hz foldable Dynamic AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 delivers flagship performance
  • Strong gaming performance with sustained frame rates
  • Multitasking features in One UI 8.5

What is not

  • Visible crease on inner screen
  • Battery life drops below five hours
  • Wi Fi and Bluetooth may fail after two years
  • Hinge can lead to screen cracks

Buy it if Buy it if you value a large, high refresh rate foldable screen and can accept limited battery life and warranty risk.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable long term battery, solid warranty, or a phone that will receive many more software updates.

Why we say that

The Dynamic AMOLED 2X panel delivers vivid colors and a smooth 120Hz experience that feels premium in both folded and unfolded modes. Under the hood the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 and 12GB of RAM give flagship level speed, so multitasking and gaming run without noticeable lag. One UI 8.5 adds useful split screen tools, though recent updates have introduced occasional bugs.

Real world owners complain about a visible crease on the inner display, rapid battery wear that can drop below five hours, and Wi Fi or Bluetooth failures after the two year mark. The hinge, while innovative, has been linked to screen cracks and black spots. At three years old the phone is nearing the end of its software update window, with only a few major Android upgrades left.

Buying the cheap gray import saves money but forfeits Samsung’s official warranty, leaving repairs to be paid out of pocket.

mobiscrub · Galaxy Z Fold 5 - The 6 Things No One is Telling You! ↗

Mobiscrub focuses heavily on how the Fold 5 changes user behavior, particularly praising the new silent, gapless hinge and the 25% stronger inner display. He emphasizes the phone's massive potential for productivity, highlighting the taskbar and unique split-view app experiences like WhatsApp and YouTube in Flex mode. While he acknowledges drawbacks like the high price, narrow cover screen, and lack of dust resistance, he concludes it is the absolute best foldable on the market for serious work and multitasking.

Gyan Therapy · Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 5 - New Refinement ! ↗

Gyan Therapy highlights the physical refinements of the Fold 5, specifically the new gapless hinge that makes the device lighter and slimmer. He praises the software improvements, demonstrating how easy it is to drag and drop images between apps and utilize multiple windows for tasks like trading or note-taking. While noting that the cameras provide typical, slightly boosted Samsung colors that look good but aren't the absolute best available, he considers the phone a highly refined, premium device for those willing to pay the high price tag.

GSMArena Official · Samsung Galaxy Z Fold5 review ↗

GSMArena's review positions the Fold 5 as a refined but highly iterative update over the Fold 4. They commend the new flat-folding hinge, the brighter displays, and the significant performance bump from the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2. However, they are highly critical of Samsung for carrying over the exact same camera hardware, battery capacity, and slow 25W charging from the previous year. They conclude that while it's an excellent device, most buyers would be better off saving money by purchasing the older Fold 4 instead.

Everyday Legends · Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 5 Review in 2025: Everything You Need to Know ↗

Everyday Legends reviews the Fold 5 from a 2025 perspective, arguing it represents incredible value on the used market. He praises the dual-rail hinge for finally eliminating the gap, making the device feel highly durable and premium. While he heavily criticizes the outdated cameras and agonizingly slow 25W charging, he believes the top-tier Snapdragon performance, bright screens, and unmatched multitasking software make it the perfect entry point for anyone wanting to try a foldable without spending $2,000.
