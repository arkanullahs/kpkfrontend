Why we say that

The 6.6 inch IPS incell LCD with 90Hz refresh feels smooth for videos and scrolling. The 50MP main sensor captures clear daylight shots, while the 8MP selfie camera is adequate for social media. The 5000 mAh cell paired with an 18W charger usually lasts a full day of mixed use.

The Unisoc Tiger T606 chipset handles everyday apps but can stutter with heavy games. Android 12 runs on a light skin that shows medium ads and rarely receives new versions. Owners often note the lack of future software support and that a newer N27 model is cheaper.
