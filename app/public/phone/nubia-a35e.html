Nubia A35e Is it worth buying?

The Nubia A35e is a basic entry-level device that struggles with daily tasks due to its limited hardware.

  • Very slow 10W charging
  • Weak Unisoc SC9863A1 chipset

Why we say that

While some users label it a budget king because of the ৳10,999 price tag, the real-world experience is defined by significant lag. The 2GB of RAM is insufficient for modern apps, leading to sluggish system performance that makes even simple navigation feel slow. Owners report very low satisfaction scores for gaming and general performance.

The 5000 mAh battery provides a large capacity, but the 10W charging speed is frustratingly slow for a full recharge. You get a 6.52 inch 90Hz display, but users rate the screen quality poorly. The cameras are strictly for basic needs, with the 8MP main sensor and 5MP selfie camera offering limited utility.

Read full reasoning

If you choose this phone, you are accepting a device that is not built for heavy usage or multitasking. It is a bare-bones Android 14 Go experience that prioritizes low cost over speed or reliability.

TechHUNT24 concludes that this is an incredibly basic smartphone aimed purely at buyers with an absolute minimum budget, particularly those transitioning from feature phones to Android. The reviewer heavily criticizes the inclusion of only 2GB of memory, noting that it causes apps to open slowly and heavily bottlenecks the device's overall speed. However, they appreciate the large 5000 mAh battery, the spacious display for indoor media consumption, and the security of a one-year official warranty. Ultimately, they advise that if a buyer can afford to increase their budget even slightly, they will find much better options on the market.

Does the Nubia A35e come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

Is the Nubia A35e suitable for playing mobile games?
