Is the Nubia A35e worth buying?

What is good

  • Large 5000 mAh battery capacity
  • Official warranty support in Bangladesh
  • Includes a headphone jack and FM radio
  • Affordable ৳9,699 price point

What is not

  • Significant lag due to 2GB RAM
  • Very slow 10W charging speed
  • Weak chipset unsuitable for gaming
  • Poor camera and display quality

Buy it if Buy this phone only if you need an extremely cheap device for basic communication and have very low expectations for speed.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you plan to play games, use multiple apps, or expect a smooth experience without system lag.

Why we say that

While it is marketed as a budget option, the 2GB of RAM leads to significant lag and sluggish system performance that owners find frustrating. The Unisoc SC9863A1 chipset is very weak and entirely unsuitable for gaming or running heavy applications. You get a large 5000 mAh battery, but the experience is hampered by the very slow 10W charging speed.

The display quality is rated poorly by users, and the 8MP camera produces only basic results. While some users appreciate the low price point, the overall user experience is defined by slow speeds and performance limitations. If you need a phone for anything beyond the most simple tasks, this device will likely cause you significant annoyance in your daily routine.

TechHUNT24 concludes that this is an incredibly basic smartphone aimed purely at buyers with an absolute minimum budget, particularly those transitioning from feature phones to Android. However, they appreciate the large 5000 mAh battery, the spacious display for indoor media consumption, and the security of a one-year official warranty. Ultimately, they advise that if a buyer can afford to increase their budget even slightly, they will find much better options on the market.
