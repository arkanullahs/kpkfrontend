Is the Nubia A35e worth buying?

What is good

  • Large 5000 mAh battery
  • 90 Hz IPS LCD display
  • Headphone jack and FM radio
  • Very low official price

What is not

  • Only 2 GB RAM causes lag
  • 10 W charging is very slow
  • Basic 8 MP camera
  • No 5G support

Buy it if Buy it if you need an inexpensive phone for calls, messaging and light apps.

Skip it if Skip it if you want smooth gaming, heavy multitasking or strong low‑light photos.

Why we say that

The 6.52‑inch 90 Hz IPS LCD is bright enough for daily browsing and the 5000 mAh battery can last through a full day of light usage. Android 14 Go runs on the Unisoc SC9863A1 chipset with 2 GB RAM, which feels sluggish when opening several apps or playing games. The 8 MP rear camera captures decent photos in good light but struggles in low‑light conditions, and the 5 MP selfie lens is only marginally better.

Charging at 10 W is noticeably slow, so a full charge can take several hours. The phone includes a headphone jack and FM radio, which are rare these days. Overall, it is a budget‑focused device that works for simple tasks but will frustrate anyone expecting smooth performance or strong photography.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery can easily last a full day of light usage such as calls, messaging and occasional browsing. Heavy screen time will reduce endurance, but the large capacity still provides decent standby time.

Can I play games on the Nubia A35e?

The phone uses a Unisoc SC9863A1 chipset with only 2 GB RAM, which makes gaming very weak. Simple casual games may run, but any demanding titles will lag or stutter.

Is the camera good for low‑light photos?
