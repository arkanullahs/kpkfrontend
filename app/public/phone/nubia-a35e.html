What works well

Reported trade-offs

  • Only 2 GB RAM causes lag

Why we say that

The 5000 mAh cell keeps the phone alive through calls, messaging and light browsing, but the 10 W charger fills it slowly. The 6.52 inch IPS LCD runs at 90 Hz, giving smoother scrolling than many budget screens. The Unisoc SC9863A1 chipset with only 2 GB RAM makes the system feel laggy, especially when opening several apps or playing games.

Camera performance stays basic, an 8 MP rear lens that struggles in low light and a 5 MP front lens that some users find acceptable for selfies. Software runs Android 14 Go with medium bloat and about one to two OS updates. Overall the phone feels like a budget device that works for essential tasks but will frustrate anyone expecting fast performance or strong photography.

TechHUNT24 concludes that this is an incredibly basic smartphone aimed purely at buyers with an absolute minimum budget, particularly those transitioning from feature phones to Android. The reviewer heavily criticizes the inclusion of only 2GB of memory, noting that it causes apps to open slowly and heavily bottlenecks the device's overall speed. However, they appreciate the large 5000 mAh battery, the spacious display for indoor media consumption, and the security of a one-year official warranty. Ultimately, they advise that if a buyer can afford to increase their budget even slightly, they will find much better options on the market.
