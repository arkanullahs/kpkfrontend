Infinix Note 12 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you need a big screen, high‑resolution camera and lots of storage, this phone will feel comfortable but you must accept a shop warranty and limited software support.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large screen, high‑resolution camera and can accept a shop warranty with limited software updates.
Consider alternatives if
You need an official brand warranty, regular OS upgrades or guaranteed 5G connectivity.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 108MP main camera with large sensor
  • 6.7 inch AMOLED display
  • 5000 mAh battery with 33W fast charge
  • 8GB RAM and 256GB storage

Reported trade-offs4

  • Only shop warranty, no brand warranty
  • Software updates likely ended
  • XOS has many preloaded apps and ads
  • No confirmed 5G support

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch AMOLED panel shows colors well and the 108MP sensor captures detailed photos in good light. Daily use feels smooth thanks to 8GB RAM, yet the XOS skin feels slow and shows many ads. Battery lasts a full day and refills quickly with 33W charging.

The lack of an official brand warranty means you should verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying. Being four years old, future Android updates are unlikely and the device may feel dated soon.

What is the Infinix Note 12 Pro price in Bangladesh?

Is there an official manufacturer warranty for the Infinix Note 12 Pro?

The phone does not come with a manufacturer warranty. It is sold as gray‑import stock and only a shop warranty is offered. You should verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

Will the phone receive future Android updates?

The device was released in 2022, making it about four years old. Expect only one or two more Android version upgrades and XOS updates are slow with many preinstalled apps. Official support is likely ending soon.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 5000 mAh battery provides solid endurance for a full day of typical use such as browsing, video streaming and social media. When it runs low the 33 W charger refills it in about an hour.

How does the 108MP camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 108 MP rear camera uses a 1/1.67 inch sensor with an f/1.8 aperture, which helps gather light in dim environments. Results are decent but may be affected by the heavy XOS skin and processing limitations.
