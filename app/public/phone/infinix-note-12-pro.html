Infinix Note 12 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you want a big AMOLED screen and a 108MP camera without spending a fortune, the Infinix Note 12 Pro can deliver that, but expect compromises.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a large screen and high‑resolution photos and are okay with shop‑only warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on official warranty, want the latest software, or need strong 5G gaming performance.

  • 6.7 inch AMOLED display
  • 108MP main camera
  • 5000 mAh battery
  • Headphone jack and FM radio

Reported trade-offs4

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • XOS pre‑installed apps show many ads
  • Software updates likely ended
  • Helio G99 not for high end gaming

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch AMOLED screen is bright for media, and the 5000 mAh battery typically lasts a day, though heavy gaming shortens it. Helio G99 with 8 GB RAM runs everyday apps smoothly, but it falters on demanding games and XOS 10.6 adds many ads. The 108MP main camera is sharp in daylight, yet low light photos are noisy; the 16MP selfie camera is decent.

Because it is a gray import, there is no official warranty; you depend on the shop’s warranty and must pay for repairs, so check IMEI and BTRC status. At four years old software updates are almost finished, and the newer Note 30 Pro costs about the same with better specs.

Will the Infinix Note 12 Pro receive Android updates?

The phone launched in 2022 with Android 12 and XOS 10.6. Being four years old, it is expected to get at most one or two more OS upgrades, and official updates are unlikely to continue.

Is there an official warranty for this phone in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray imports, so the device has no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. You only get the shop’s limited warranty, and any repair work will be charged, so verify the IMEI and BTRC status first.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery usually provides a full day of mixed use such as browsing, social media and video playback. Heavy gaming or high screen brightness will reduce the endurance to around eight to ten hours.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 108MP sensor with f/1.8 aperture captures detailed images in daylight, but in low light the pictures become noisy and lose sharpness. The 16MP selfie camera also struggles to produce clear shots without additional lighting.
