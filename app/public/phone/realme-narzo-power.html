Buying verdict

Realme Narzo Power Is it worth buying?

The Realme Narzo Power will keep you online for two days on a single charge, but expect heat and bloatware.

Choose it if
You need long battery life, fast charging, and can accept a gray‑import warranty.
Consider alternatives if

What works well

  • 10,001 mAh battery lasts 1.5‑2 days
  • 80W fast charging with reverse charge
  • 144Hz bright AMOLED display
  • Dimensity 7400 Ultra handles gaming

Reported trade-offs

  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Noticeable heating during gaming
  • Heavy pre‑installed bloatware
  • Fingerprint scanner placed too low

Why we say that

Its 10,001 mAh battery and 80W fast charger mean you can go long without hunting for a plug. The 6.8‑inch 144Hz AMOLED screen is bright and smooth, and the Dimensity 7400 Ultra handles daily apps and occasional 90FPS PUBG sessions. Owners love the long battery life and quick charging, but they complain about noticeable heating during gaming, a low‑position under‑display fingerprint sensor, and a lot of pre‑installed apps that slow the experience.

The camera is decent in daylight but struggles with low‑light contrast and portrait realism. The phone arrives as a gray‑import unit, so there is no official brand warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited warranty and paid repairs. Software runs Android 16 with Realme UI 7.0 and promises up to three major OS upgrades, though bloatware remains medium‑high.
