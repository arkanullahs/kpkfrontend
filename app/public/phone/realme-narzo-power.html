Is the Realme Narzo power worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 10001 mAh battery
  • 144 Hz AMOLED display
  • 80W fast charging with reverse support
  • Dimensity 7400 Ultra performance

What is not

  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Heavy pre‑installed bloatware
  • Low under‑display fingerprint sensor
  • Heat and video jitter during gaming

Buy it if If you want a huge battery, fast charging and a smooth high‑refresh screen and you are comfortable with a gray‑import warranty, this phone is a good fit.

Skip it if If you need an official warranty, accurate low‑light photos or a clean software experience, look for another model.

Why we say that

The 10001 mAh cell pairs with 80W fast charging and 27W reverse charging, so you can top up quickly and even charge accessories. The 6.8‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 144 Hz, giving smooth scrolling and bright outdoor visibility, though colors are not perfectly accurate. The Dimensity 7400 Ultra chipset handles everyday apps well and can push PUBG to around 90 FPS, but you will feel noticeable heat and occasional stutters during long sessions.

The camera produces decent daylight shots, but low‑light scenes suffer from poor contrast and artificial portrait rendering. Realme UI on Android 16 brings many pre‑installed apps and ads that feel intrusive, and the under‑display fingerprint sensor sits lower than most users prefer. The device arrives as a gray‑import with only the shop’s warranty, so any repair will cost you.

Overall it feels like a high‑capacity, high‑refresh phone that trades off polish and official support for price.
