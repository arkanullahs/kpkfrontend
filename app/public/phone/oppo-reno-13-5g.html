Buying verdict

OPPO Reno 13 5G Is it worth buying?

The OPPO Reno 13 5G is a premium feeling device that excels in battery endurance and build quality but suffers from significant software frustrations.

Choose it if
You prioritize a thin, durable metal build and long battery life for daily tasks.
Consider alternatives if
You dislike excessive bloatware or want the peace of mind provided by an official manufacturer warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Stellar 5600 mAh battery endurance
  • Premium thin metal frame with IP69 rating
  • Fast 80W charging under 50 minutes
  • Vibrant 120Hz AMOLED display

Reported trade-offs

  • Extreme amount of pre-installed bloatware
  • Fingerprint sensor struggles with moist hands
  • No wireless charging support
  • Basic ultra-wide camera performance

Why we say that

Owners report that the phone is absolutely stacked full of bloatware and crapware, which detracts from the experience despite the buttery smooth animations of ColorOS 15. While the 5600 mAh battery lasts a long time even during heavy gaming, the metal frame tends to get warm during intense sessions or outdoor use. The 50MP main camera captures natural colors and great portraits, though the ultra-wide lens is basic and lacks detail.

You should be aware that the optical fingerprint sensor struggles if your hands are moist. Since this is an unofficial gray import, you will have no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh, and all repairs must be paid for at your own expense. Given that the newer OPPO Reno 14 5G is available for ৳48,990, many buyers find this model overpriced for what it offers.

Does the OPPO Reno 13 5G come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is sold as an unofficial gray import in Bangladesh. It does not carry any manufacturer warranty, and any repairs required will be at your own expense.

How does the battery perform during heavy gaming?

The 5600 mAh battery offers stellar endurance. Owners report that it lasts a long time even when the device is used for heavy gaming sessions.

Is the OPPO Reno 13 5G good for gaming?

The phone features a Dimensity 8350 chipset and supports 90fps in BGMI. While daily use is snappy, some users report frame drops during heavy gaming sessions.

How many software updates will this phone receive?

The device runs on Android 15 and ColorOS 15. As a flagship model, it is expected to receive 3 to 4 OS updates over its lifespan.
