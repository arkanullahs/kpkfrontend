Is the OPPO Reno 13 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Large 5600 mAh battery capacity
  • Strong performance from Dimensity 8350
  • Exceptional 5 years OS update promise
  • Impressive video HDR quality

What is not

  • Serious concerns regarding screen fragility
  • Low owner rating for build quality
  • Medium level of bloatware and ads

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize long-term software support and battery life over physical durability.

Why we say that

Living with this device means enjoying a smooth 120Hz AMOLED display and strong performance from the Dimensity 8350 chipset, yet you must be extremely careful with handling. Users report that the screen is prone to breaking easily, with one owner noting that a drop to a flat floor left the display completely dead. While the camera performance is better than expected and video HDR is a highlight, the build quality rating is quite low at 5.5.

You should also consider that the newer OPPO Reno 14 5G is available for 48,990 taka, which is often the better buy. With official warranty units starting at 59,990 taka and unofficial options ranging from 44,490 to 48,990 taka, you need to weigh the risks of unofficial hardware against the high cost of official support.
