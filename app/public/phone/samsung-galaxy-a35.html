Is the Samsung Galaxy A35 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz Super AMOLED display
  • 5000 mAh battery with 25 W fast charge
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • Premium glass feel with IP67 rating

What is not

  • Display flickers at low brightness
  • No charger in the box
  • Quarterly software updates only
  • Gaming performance is weak

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large bright screen, solid camera and battery and can live with occasional flicker and slower updates.

Skip it if Skip it if you need strong gaming performance, frequent updates, or a lower price than the A53 5G.

Why we say that

The phone feels premium with glass back and IP67 protection, though the size makes it feel a bit bulky. Battery capacity is 5000 mAh and the 25 W charger fills it quickly, giving solid day long endurance that most users find satisfactory. The 50 MP main sensor with OIS delivers clear photos and video for a midrange device, while the 13 MP selfie camera is adequate.

Performance is fine for everyday apps, but the Exynos 1380 struggles with demanding games and can become warm. Software runs One UI 7 on Android 14 and will get up to four major upgrades, yet updates have been cut to a quarterly cadence since early 2025. No charger is included in the box.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 5000 mAh cell gives solid day long endurance for mixed usage such as browsing, social media and video playback. Owners say it is reliable but not class leading, so a full charge will usually last you through a typical day.

Is the Galaxy A35 good for mobile games?

The phone handles daily tasks well, but the Exynos 1380 is a noted weakness for gaming. Performance drops in demanding titles and the device can become warm, which is why owners rate its gaming experience low.

Will the camera work well in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor with f/1.8 aperture and optical image stabilization provides reliable photos for a midrange phone. In low light the results are decent, though not as good as flagship cameras, and video quality remains acceptable.

How many Android updates will I receive and how often?

The device ships with Android 14 and One UI 7 and is promised up to four major Android upgrades. Since February 2025 Samsung reduced update frequency to a quarterly schedule, so new features and security patches arrive every three months.
