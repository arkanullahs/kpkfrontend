Is the Symphony ATOM 5 worth buying?

What is good

  • Long battery life for daily use
  • Large 6.74 inch screen
  • Official warranty at ৳9,499
  • Affordable price for a new phone

What is not

  • Slow charging with included charger
  • Camera blurry and laggy in low light
  • Performance lags in games
  • Software updates are rare

Buy it if Buy it if you need a big display and long battery on a tight budget and only use basic apps.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect smooth gaming or high‑quality photos in low light.

Why we say that

At ৳9,499 it comes with an official warranty and a glossy plastic build that looks decent for the price. The 6.74 inch IPS LCD is large enough for media, though its 720p resolution feels modest. The Unisoc T606 chipset and 4GB RAM handle everyday tasks like messaging and browsing without trouble, but games and heavy apps will lag.

The 5MP selfie camera produces blurry pictures and shows noticeable shutter lag in low light. Android 14 runs on a light skin, but updates are rare and some bloatware remains. Overall it is a budget phone that works for basic use but falls short for demanding users.
