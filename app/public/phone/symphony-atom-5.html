Is the Symphony ATOM 5 worth buying?

What is good

  • Long battery endurance
  • Large 6.74 inch IPS display
  • Official warranty support
  • Decent glossy plastic design

What is not

  • Painfully slow charging speed
  • Significant camera shutter lag
  • Lags during gaming or heavy use
  • Rare software updates

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a budget device strictly for basic daily tasks and prioritize battery life over camera or gaming performance.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you enjoy mobile photography, play games, or require a device that charges quickly.

Why we say that

Living with this phone means accepting that it is strictly for simple tasks, as the Unisoc T606 chipset struggles and lags under heavy use or gaming. Owners report that the 5000 mAh battery lasts a long time, but the included 10W charger is painfully slow for such a large capacity. While the 6.74 inch IPS display is adequate for the price, the camera experience is a major point of frustration.

Users frequently complain about poor image clarity and significant shutter lag, especially when shooting in low light conditions. The software is based on Android 14 and includes some useful features, but you should expect medium levels of bloatware and very rare software updates. The build quality consists of a basic glossy plastic design that looks decent for a budget phone.

If you need a device for light daily usage and can tolerate slow charging and limited camera performance, this official unit provides a standard warranty experience at ৳9,499.

What is the Symphony ATOM 5 price in Bangladesh?

Symphony ATOM 5 costs ৳9,499 in Bangladesh on official listings, in stock at 1 shop we track as of 4 Sep 2026.

Does the Symphony ATOM 5 have good battery life?

Yes, the phone features a 5000 mAh battery that provides long endurance. This is largely due to the low-power Unisoc T606 chipset. However, keep in mind that the charging process is very slow because the included 10W charger takes a long time to fill the battery.

Can I use this phone for gaming?

This phone is not recommended for gaming. Owners report that the device is strictly for basic tasks and it lags significantly under heavy use or gaming. The performance rating for gaming is quite low at 2.5 out of 10.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The camera performance in low light is poor. Owners report significant shutter lag and processing issues when taking photos in dim environments. Users have noted that the images often lack clarity despite the high megapixel count.

Will this phone receive regular software updates?

You should not expect regular software updates for this device. The phone runs on Android 14 with a light skin, but updates are rarely provided by the manufacturer. Additionally, the software experience includes a medium amount of bloatware and ads.
