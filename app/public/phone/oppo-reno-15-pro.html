Is the OPPO Reno 15 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 6200 mAh battery with 9.6 rating
  • 200 MP main camera with OIS
  • 1800 nit AMOLED display at 120 Hz
  • 80 W fast wired charging

What is not

  • Heavy throttling under sustained load
  • No wireless charging
  • Software includes bloat and ads
  • No official manufacturer warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you value long battery life, high‑resolution photos and can accept a gray‑import warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable gaming performance without throttling or an official warranty.

Why we say that

Its 6200 mAh battery earned a 9.6 rating and reviewers call the endurance massive for a compact device. The 6.32 inch AMOLED panel reaches 1800 nits peak brightness and runs at 120 Hz, making everyday use bright and fluid. Photography is a strong point with a 200 MP main sensor, f/1.8 aperture, OIS and a 3.5× telephoto lens, plus a 50 MP selfie shooter.

Performance is driven by the Dimensity 8450 and 12 GB RAM, which feels smooth for daily apps but throttles heavily during long gaming sessions. Charging is fast at 80 W, though there is no wireless option. The phone runs ColorOS 16 on Android 16, offering fluid animations but also medium level bloat and ads.

At ৳66,499 for the 12 GB/256 GB model and ৳73,499 for 512 GB, it feels pricey compared with similar offerings. All units are gray imports built for India, so there is no official OPPO warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s limited coverage and must confirm IMEI and BTRC status.
