Is the OPPO Reno 15 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 200MP main camera with OIS
  • 6200 mAh battery with 80W fast charge
  • Bright 3600 nit AMOLED display
  • Premium compact build with IP68/69 rating

What is not

  • Heavy throttling under sustained load
  • No wireless charging
  • Shop warranty only, not brand
  • Overpriced compared to similar flagships

Buy it if Buy if you want a high resolution camera and long battery in a compact premium body and can accept shop only warranty.

Skip it if Skip if you need reliable gaming performance, wireless charging, or an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 200MP main sensor with OIS and a 50MP selfie lens produce sharp images in good light, while the 6200 mAh battery paired with 80W wired charging lets you go many hours without a plug. The 6.32‑inch AMOLED panel reaches up to 3600 nit and runs at 120 Hz, making the experience bright and fluid. The compact premium chassis is IP68/69 rated, but the device is a gray‑import, so you only get a shop warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status.

Owners praise the camera and battery but complain about heavy throttling under sustained load, lack of wireless charging and a price that feels high compared with similar flagships.

How long will the battery last in daily use?

Can the phone run heavy games without slowdown?
