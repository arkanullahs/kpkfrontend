Buying verdict

OPPO Reno 15 Pro Is it worth buying?

The OPPO Reno 15 Pro is a compact powerhouse that excels in battery endurance and camera quality but suffers from significant thermal management issues.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a compact, high-end camera phone with exceptional battery life and do not mind the lack of official warranty support.
Consider alternatives if
You are a heavy gamer who needs sustained performance or you require the peace of mind provided by an official brand warranty.

  • Bright 1800 nits AMOLED display

Reported trade-offs4

  • Heavy throttling under load
  • No wireless charging support
  • Contains bloatware and ads

Why we say that

Owners report that the Dimensity 8450 chipset throttles heavily under heavy load, which impacts gaming performance despite the smooth daily experience. While the 6200 mAh battery is a standout feature, the lack of wireless charging is a deal breaker for many users. The software experience is fluid with great AI, though you will have to deal with pre-installed bloatware and ads.

Because this device is only available as a gray-import, you must accept that there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Repairs are paid and depend entirely on the specific shop where you purchase the unit. Users often feel the phone is overpriced compared to alternatives from Xiaomi or the Find X series.

Does the OPPO Reno 15 Pro have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit currently available is a gray-import, meaning you only receive whatever limited promise the individual shop provides. All repairs are paid and not covered by the brand.

Is the OPPO Reno 15 Pro good for heavy gaming?

While the phone handles daily tasks smoothly, it is not ideal for long gaming sessions. Owners report that the device throttles heavily under heavy load, which negatively impacts performance during demanding games.

How long will the OPPO Reno 15 Pro receive software updates?

As a flagship model, this device is expected to receive 3 to 4 OS updates. You should be aware that the global version of ColorOS 16 comes with some pre-installed bloatware and advertisements.

How does the battery perform on the OPPO Reno 15 Pro?

The battery performance is highly rated by owners, scoring a 9.6 out of 10. The massive 6200 mAh capacity provides incredible endurance, making it one of the most reliable aspects of the phone for daily use.
