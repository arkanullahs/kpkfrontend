Why we say that

You get a bright LTPO P-OLED screen and a versatile camera setup with a 3.0x telephoto lens. The Hello UI is close to stock and has very few ads. However, you must rely on shop warranties instead of a brand warranty.

Motorola has a history of weak software updates. While the box mentions up to 5 major Android upgrades, the actual track record is inconsistent. It runs Android 15 and uses the Dimensity 7400 chipset.

What is the Motorola Edge 60 Neo price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Motorola Edge 60 Neo in Bangladesh was ৳63,990, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official brand warranty in Bangladesh?

No, only gray import stock has been found. You will get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

How long will the software be supported?

The phone comes with Android 15 and promises up to 5 major Android upgrades. However, Motorola has been historically weak with providing updates on time.

What are the camera specifications?

It has a 50MP main camera with OIS and a 1/1.56 inch sensor. The setup also includes a 3.0x telephoto lens and an ultrawide camera. The selfie camera is 32MP.

What is the battery life and charging speed?

The phone features a 5000 mAh battery. It supports 68W charging to refill the battery.
