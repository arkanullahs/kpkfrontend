Motorola Edge 60 Neo Is it worth buying?

If you want a bright 6.36 inch screen and fast charging without a brand warranty, the Edge 60 Neo fits the bill.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value a vivid display and quick charging and can accept a shop warranty.
Consider alternatives if

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 3000 nits peak brightness
  • 120Hz LTPO OLED display
  • 68W fast charging
  • Dimensity 7400 handles games

Reported trade-offs3

  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Software updates historically weak
  • Low light camera performance modest

Why we say that

The LTPO OLED panel can reach 3000 nits at peak and runs at a smooth 120Hz, making everyday scrolling and video watching feel premium. The Dimensity 7400 chipset handles most games at high settings, and the 68W charger fills the 5000 mAh battery in roughly half an hour, so you spend less time tethered to a wall. Camera quality is solid in daylight with a 50MP main sensor, OIS and a 3x tele lens, but low‑light shots lack the punch of higher‑end flagships.

Software runs Android 15 with a near stock Hello UI, yet the brand has a history of delivering only one to three OS upgrades despite promising up to five. The biggest complaint is the lack of an official manufacturer warranty; you must rely on the shop’s own policy and pay for any repairs. Verify the IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.

How long will the Motorola Edge 60 Neo receive Android updates?

Is the battery enough for a full day of use and how fast does it charge?
