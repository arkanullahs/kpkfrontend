Is the Vivo V40 Lite worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 1300‑nit AMOLED display
  • Smooth 120 Hz refresh rate
  • Large 5500 mAh battery with 80 W charge
  • Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 delivers decent performance

What is not

  • Feels like a cheap rebrand
  • Funtouch OS adds ads and bloat
  • Price high for the specs

Buy it if Buy if you want a vivid screen, fast charging and acceptable performance and can tolerate ads and a budget‑grade build.

Skip it if Skip if you expect a premium feel, top‑tier camera results or a clean software experience.

Why we say that

The 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel reaches 1300 nits and runs at 120 Hz, making everyday use pleasant. The 5500 mAh battery lasts through a full day for most users, though occasional drain can appear. Performance is decent thanks to the Snapdragon 6 Gen 1, handling day‑to‑day tasks and casual games without major hiccups.

The camera hardware is respectable with a 50 MP main sensor, yet tuning is average and low‑light shots are unimpressive. Software runs Android 14 with Funtouch 14, which adds medium‑level ads and bloatware that some find intrusive. At 28,599 taka the price feels high for the spec level, especially since a newer V50 Lite is priced similarly and offers a better overall package.
