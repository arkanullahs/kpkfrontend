Redmi Turbo 4 Is it worth buying?

If you want flagship‑level speed and a huge battery at a gray‑import price, the Redmi Turbo 4 can deliver, but expect software clutter and warranty gaps.

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Heavy ad‑bloat in HyperOS 2
  • Inconsistent low‑light camera
  • Slow optical fingerprint sensor

Why we say that

The Dimensity 8400 Ultra keeps performance high and temperatures low, so gaming stays smooth at 60 fps for hours. The 6.67‑inch AMOLED screen runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 3200 nits, making outdoor viewing bright, though colors are not perfectly calibrated. The 6550 mAh cell paired with 90 W charging refills quickly, yet heavy use still drains it after seven to eight hours.

The 50 MP main sensor is decent in daylight, but night shots vary and the 8 MP ultrawide adds little value. HyperOS 2 brings many ads and occasional notification delays, especially on the Chinese ROM that lacks Google services. The plastic frame is IP68/69 rated but feels less premium, and the optical fingerprint sensor feels slow.

Will the Redmi Turbo 4 get Android updates and for how long?

The phone runs HyperOS 2 on Android 15 and receives two to four major OS upgrades depending on the tier. Updates arrive slower on the budget line and the Chinese ROM may miss Google services, so long‑term support is limited.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 6550 mAh battery lasts about seven to eight hours with heavy use such as gaming or video playback. With moderate use it can stretch to one and a half to two days. The 90 W charger refills the phone in roughly thirty minutes.

Is the camera reliable for night photography?

The 50 MP main sensor captures decent daylight photos, but night shots are inconsistent and can appear washed out. The 8 MP ultrawide sensor is weak and adds little value, and 4K video may show focus hunting and jitter.

What warranty and after‑sales support are available in Bangladesh?

All units are gray imports, so there is no official Xiaomi warranty in Bangladesh. Shops we track offer their own limited warranty and repairs are paid. Always verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchase.
