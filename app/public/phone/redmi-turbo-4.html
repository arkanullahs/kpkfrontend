Is the Redmi Turbo 4 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6550 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 120 Hz AMOLED stays bright outdoors
  • Dimensity 8400 Ultra runs games without throttling
  • 90 W charger refills in under an hour

What is not

  • Plastic frame feels bulky and cheap
  • Camera processing is inconsistent
  • HyperOS is ad‑heavy and bloated
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, high gaming performance and can accept a plastic build with software ads.

Skip it if Skip it if you want a premium feel, reliable camera quality or official warranty support.

Why we say that

The 6550 mAh battery lives through heavy use and the 90 W charger brings it back to full in under an hour. The Dimensity 8400 Ultra paired with 12 GB RAM delivers smooth 120 Hz gaming without noticeable throttling, and the bright 3200‑nit AMOLED stays readable outdoors. HyperOS 2 runs Android 15 but is packed with ads and requires manual tweaks to stop notification delays.

The 50 MP main sensor offers good daylight shots, yet processing is uneven, the ultra‑wide is weak and video stabilization jitters. The plastic frame is bulky and the camera bump is prominent. Because every unit is a gray‑import, there is no manufacturer warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited guarantee and must verify IMEI and BTRC status.

Overall it suits power users who can tolerate software clutter and a non‑premium build.
