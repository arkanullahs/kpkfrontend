Buying verdict

Redmi Turbo 4 Is it worth buying?

The Redmi Turbo 4 is a performance-focused device that prioritizes raw power over build quality and camera reliability.

Choose it if
You are a mobile gamer who prioritizes raw performance and battery life above all else.
Consider alternatives if
You want a reliable camera, a premium build, or a device that works perfectly out of the box without software tweaks.

What works well

  • Exceptional 6550 mAh battery endurance
  • Top-tier sustained gaming performance
  • Bright 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Fast 90W charging capability

Reported trade-offs

  • Bloated software with excessive ads
  • Inconsistent camera processing and jittery video
  • Bulky plastic frame feels less premium
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

With the Dimensity 8400 Ultra chipset, it delivers top-tier gaming performance without thermal throttling, making it a strong choice for heavy users. However, living with this phone involves significant compromises. Owners report that HyperOS 2 is bloated with ads and requires manual tweaks to fix notification delays.

The build feels bulky and plastic, while the camera system suffers from inconsistent color processing and jittery video stabilization. Because these units are gray imports built for the China market, you must verify if the software includes Google services and Bangla language support before purchasing. There is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh, so you are entirely dependent on the specific shop for any repairs or support.

Does the Redmi Turbo 4 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you only receive whatever limited support the individual shop provides. All repairs must be paid for out of pocket.

Is the battery life good for heavy users?

Yes, the massive 6550 mAh battery provides exceptional endurance. Owners rate the battery performance highly at 9.4 out of 10, confirming it is well-suited for those who use their phones heavily throughout the day.

How does the phone perform for gaming?

The phone is excellent for gaming. It features the Dimensity 8400 Ultra chipset, which provides top-tier sustained performance. Owners rate the gaming experience at 9.5 out of 10, noting that it handles heavy titles without thermal throttling.

Are there any issues with the software or notifications?

Yes, owners report that HyperOS 2 is bloated with ads and often suffers from notification delays. You may need to perform manual notification tweaks to ensure apps work correctly, and some Chinese variants may lack Google services and Bangla language support.
