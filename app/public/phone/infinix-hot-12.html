Is the Infinix Hot 12 worth buying?

What is good

  • Reliable 5000 mAh battery endurance
  • Large 6.82 inch 90Hz display
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • Official warranty support

What is not

  • Intrusive system-level ads and bloatware
  • Fingerprint sensor hardware failure
  • Low 720p display resolution
  • Poor camera image quality

Buy it if You prioritize basic battery endurance over performance and can find no other options within your budget.

Skip it if You want a reliable phone with modern software support, a working fingerprint sensor, and a clean interface without intrusive ads.

Why we say that

Living with this phone involves dealing with significant software frustrations, including heavy bloatware and intrusive system-level ads that appear across apps. Owners report serious hardware issues, most notably the fingerprint sensor failing or disappearing entirely from settings. While the 5000 mAh battery provides reliable endurance, the overall experience is hampered by a low-resolution 720p display and poor camera quality that produces dull images.

Performance is limited by the Helio G85 chipset and slow eMMC 5.1 storage, making it unsuitable for graphics-intense gaming. Call quality is another concern, with reports of very low earpiece volume. Given that this model is four years old, you should expect no further software support or security patches.

Most owners rate the ease of use and camera performance quite poorly, reflecting the general dissatisfaction with the device's longevity and build quality.
