Is the Samsung Galaxy A23 4G worth buying?

What is good

  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • 5000 mAh battery lasting all day
  • 25 W fast charging support
  • headphone jack and FM radio

What is not

  • Dim 90 Hz display hard to read outdoors
  • UI lags and stutters in everyday use
  • Missing proximity and ambient light sensors
  • Gaming frame rates are poor

Buy it if Buy if you need long battery life, a decent main camera and can accept a modest, dim screen.

Skip it if Skip if you want smooth gaming, bright outdoor display or fast software updates.

Why we say that

The 50 MP main sensor with optical image stabilization gives decent photos in good light, but low light and the ultrawide lens are weak. The 6.6 inch 90 Hz PLS LCD is dim and struggles outdoors, and the lack of proximity and ambient light sensors makes auto brightness sluggish. The Snapdragon 680 and 6 GB RAM cause UI lag and stutter, and gaming frames drop noticeably.

A 25 W fast charging capability exists but the included charger is only 15 W, so full charge takes longer. The phone feels solid in hand, yet the glossy plastic attracts fingerprints and there is no water‑resistance rating. Software runs One UI 6.1, upgradable to Android 14, but the hardware feels sluggish and software updates are likely near their end after four years on the market.

How long will the battery last in real use?

The 5000 mAh cell typically gives six to eight hours of screen on time, which most owners call excellent endurance. Standby time is much longer, so the phone can easily last a full day of normal use.

Will this phone run games like PUBG or COD well?

The Snapdragon 680 and 6 GB RAM struggle with demanding titles. Users report low frame rates and frequent stutters, so the experience is far from smooth. Casual games run okay, but heavy gaming is not recommended.

How does the camera perform in low light situations?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures decent photos in good light, but low light results are mixed. Noise appears and the ultrawide and macro lenses are noticeably poorer, so night shots are not a strong point.
