Honor X9d Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can stay alive for two to four days on a single charge, the Honor X9d delivers that.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value long battery life and a solid, water resistant build more than top tier gaming performance.
Consider alternatives if
You need smooth high frame rate gaming or a high resolution ultrawide camera.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 8300 mAh battery lasts up to four days
  • 6.79 inch 120 Hz AMOLED display
  • IP69K water and dust protection
  • Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 handles daily tasks

Reported trade-offs4

  • 66 W charger needs 1.5 hours for full charge
  • 108 MP main camera lacks low light detail
  • 5 MP ultrawide is very low resolution
  • Heavy games drop below 30 fps

Why we say that

The 8300 mAh battery is the strongest point and owners report it can last up to four days with moderate use. The 6.79 inch AMOLED screen with 120 Hz refresh feels smooth and bright, and the IP69K rating makes it resistant to water, dust and drops. Daily apps run fine on the Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 processor, but heavy games often fall below 30 fps and feel sluggish.

The 108 MP main camera captures decent photos but struggles in low light, and the 5 MP ultrawide sensor is very low resolution. Charging at 66 W fills the huge battery in about one and a half hours, which many consider slow for such a large cell. MagicOS 9 works but includes noticeable bloat and medium ads.

Read full reasoning

Overall the phone feels overpriced for its performance level, so weigh the battery and build benefits against the gaming and camera compromises.
