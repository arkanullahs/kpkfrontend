Buying verdict

Honor X9d Is it worth buying?

If you value a phone that can sit on a charger once a week and survive a rainy commute, the Honor X9d delivers that endurance and durability.

Choose it if
You need long battery life, solid build and can accept modest gaming and camera compromises.
Consider alternatives if
You expect flagship gaming performance or a high‑resolution ultrawide camera.

What works well

  • 8300 mAh battery lasts up to four days
  • IP69K water and dust protection
  • 108 MP main camera with OIS
  • 6.79‑inch AMOLED display with 120 Hz

Reported trade-offs

  • 5 MP ultrawide sensor gives low resolution
  • Fingerprint sensor placed too low
  • 66 W charging is slow for 8300 mAh
  • Heavy games run below 30 fps

Why we say that

The 8,300 mAh battery routinely lasts two to four days, and the IP69K rating protects against water and dust. The 6.79‑inch AMOLED panel offers crisp colors and a smooth 120 Hz refresh, while the 108 MP main sensor with OIS captures decent photos in good light. However, owners complain that the 5 MP ultrawide lens produces low‑resolution shots and the video stabilization wobbles during panning.

The fingerprint sensor sits low on the frame, which many find inconvenient. Charging at 66 W fills the large battery in about 1.5 hours, slower than expected for such capacity. Gaming performance is weak, with demanding titles dropping below 30 fps.

Reviewers also note the device feels overpriced for its Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 chipset.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 8,300 mAh battery typically lasts two to four days with normal usage, giving you industry‑leading endurance. Heavy screen time or gaming will reduce that span, but most owners report it easily covers a full work week without recharging.

Can the Honor X9d run demanding games smoothly?

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 handles daily tasks well, but heavy games such as Wuthering Waves often fall below 30 fps. If smooth high‑frame‑rate gaming is a priority, this phone may feel underpowered.

Is the camera suitable for everyday photos and video?

The 108 MP main sensor with OIS captures decent images in good light, but the 5 MP ultrawide lens produces low‑resolution shots and video stabilization shows noticeable wobble when panning. There is no telephoto option.
