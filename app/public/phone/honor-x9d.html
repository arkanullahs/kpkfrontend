Buying verdict

Honor X9d Is it worth buying?

The Honor X9d will keep you powered for up to four days, but you will notice slow charging and average gaming.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
If you need long battery life, a rugged build and are okay with modest gaming and camera quirks, this phone fits your needs.
Consider alternatives if
If you expect flagship gaming performance, a high‑quality ultrawide camera, or fast charging, look elsewhere.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 8300 mAh battery lasts 2‑4 days
  • 6.79‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED with high peak brightness
  • IP69K dust and water resistance
  • 108 MP main camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs4

  • 5 MP ultrawide sensor is very low resolution
  • 66 W charging takes 1.5 hours for full charge
  • Heavy games run below 30 fps
  • Fingerprint sensor placed low

Why we say that

Its 8,300 mAh battery truly stands out, lasting two to four days for most users. The 6.79‑inch AMOLED screen offers 120 Hz refresh and a 6,000‑nit peak, making everyday use pleasant. The phone feels solid with an IP69K rating and a sturdy frame.

The 108 MP main camera captures detailed shots, yet the 5 MP ultrawide sensor is a clear weak spot and video stabilization can wobble. Gaming performance drops below 30 fps on demanding titles, and the fingerprint sensor sits lower than many prefer. Software runs Android 15 with MagicOS 9, providing useful AI features but also some bloat.

Read full reasoning

Overall, it suits those who value endurance and build quality more than top‑tier gaming or a perfect camera suite.
