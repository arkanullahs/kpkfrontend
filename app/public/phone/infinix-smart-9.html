Buying verdict

Infinix Smart 9 Is it worth buying?

Expect a frustrating experience if you need a reliable daily driver.

Choose it if
You need a very basic device for simple tasks and want an IR blaster.
Consider alternatives if
You want to play games or need a phone that does not lag.

What works well

  • Includes a headphone jack
  • Has an IR blaster
  • Includes FM radio

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe lagging and ghost touches
  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • Fast battery drain and heating

Why we say that

Owners report severe lagging and ghost touches that force them to restart the phone multiple times. The device suffers from heating issues because the CPU works too hard. You will deal with high amounts of bloatware and notification ads.

While it has a large screen, users claim it does not actually feel like 120Hz. The battery drains fast and heats up quickly during use. Many games are not compatible and VR cannot be played.

It runs Android 14 Go edition which is slow. This phone struggles with basic stability and performance.

Infinix Smart 9: what buyers ask us

What is the Infinix Smart 9 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Infinix Smart 9 in Bangladesh was ৳9,999, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Is this phone good for gaming?

No, it is not good for gaming. Owners report that many games are not compatible and you cannot play VR. The performance rating is 4.0 and the phone suffers from lagging and heating issues.

How is the battery life on the Infinix Smart 9?

The battery life is disappointing for many. Although it has a 5000 mAh battery, owners report that it drains fast and heats up quickly during use.

Will I get software updates for this phone?

The phone comes with Android 14 Go edition and XOS 14. You can expect 1 to 2 OS updates, but the software is reported to be slow with many preloaded apps.

What is the camera quality like?

The phone has a 13MP main camera and an 8MP selfie camera. Despite these specs, user reviews describe the photos as low quality.
