What works well

  • Very low price
  • Large 5000 mAh battery
  • Headphone jack and FM radio
  • Light Android Go system

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe UI lag and slow touch
  • Very slow 5‑10 W charging
  • Basic camera works only in bright light
  • Frequent network drops

Why we say that

At ৳8,599 the Symphony G27 offers a 6.56 inch IPS LCD screen, a 5000 mAh battery that lasts a day, and a headphone jack with FM radio. Android 14 Go keeps the system light, yet 120 pre‑installed games and heavy UI cause noticeable lag and delayed touch response. The 3 GB RAM and 32 GB storage feel cramped for multitasking, and the 8 MP rear camera only works well in bright sunlight.

Users also report occasional network loss after a few days. Charging is very slow, limited to 5‑10 W, and the glossy plastic body attracts fingerprints. Overall it is usable for basic communication but struggles with any demanding app.
