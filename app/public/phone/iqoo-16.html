iQOO 16 Is it worth buying?

If you can live without a brand backed warranty the iQOO 16 feels like a flagship on a gray import price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want flagship speed, a huge screen and long battery life at a gray import price.
Consider alternatives if
You need an official brand warranty or guaranteed long term software support.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 performance
  • 6.85 inch 165Hz AMOLED display
  • 8400 mAh battery with 100W fast charge
  • 50MP main camera with OIS and telephoto

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Software includes medium ads
  • Updates limited to three Android versions
  • Must verify IMEI and BTRC status

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 chipset delivers smooth performance in games and heavy multitasking. The 6.85 inch AMOLED panel runs at 165Hz and can flash a peak of 10000 nits, so bright outdoor use is easy. Twelve gigabytes of RAM and up to a terabyte of storage keep apps and media from slowing down.

The 8400 mAh battery lasts a full day of mixed use and the 100W charger refills it in under an hour. The 50MP main sensor with OIS and 3x telephoto gives decent low light shots, while the 32MP selfie camera is sharp. Owners complain about the lack of an official warranty, the need to verify IMEI status, and medium level ads in the Funtouch OS build.
