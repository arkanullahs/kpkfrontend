Buying verdict

Realme P4 Power Is it worth buying?

The Realme P4 Power will keep you powered for three days of light use thanks to its 10,001 mAh battery.

Choose it if
You need massive battery life, a bright high‑refresh screen, and 5G for everyday media and casual gaming.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on a strong low‑light camera, stereo sound, or official warranty support.

What works well

  • 10,001 mAh battery lasts days
  • 144 Hz AMOLED display with extreme brightness
  • 80 W fast charging and 27 W reverse charge
  • 5G connectivity and IR blaster

Reported trade-offs

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Single mono speaker limits audio depth
  • Camera blurs moving subjects and low light
  • Gaming heats up heavily during long sessions

Why we say that

It fills from zero to full in about forty minutes with the 80 W charger and can reverse charge other devices at 27 W. The 6.8‑inch AMOLED screen runs at 144 Hz and can peak at 6,500 nits, so outdoor viewing is clear and gaming feels fluid. The Dimensity 7400 Ultra chipset with 8 GB RAM handles daily apps well and can push BGMI to 144 FPS, though it heats up after extended play.

The 50 MP main camera with OIS produces decent stills, but the ultrawide lens is weak and any movement or low light yields blurry photos. Audio comes from a single mono speaker, limiting sound depth. The phone is a gray‑import built for India, so there is no official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh and repairs are paid.

Read full reasoning

Realme UI 7 on Android 16 offers up to three major updates, but some bloatware and minor bugs remain.
