Is the OPPO Find X9 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • 7,050 mAh battery with 100W fast charging
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 powerhouse performance
  • 200MP main camera with Hasselblad tuning
  • 6.82 inch LTPO AMOLED display with 3,600 nit peak brightness

What is not

  • Heavy and bulky to hold
  • ColorOS loaded with bloatware
  • Thermal throttling during extended gaming
  • Aggressive battery management may close apps

Buy it if Buy it if you need top camera quality, long battery life, and flagship gaming power and can tolerate the weight.

Skip it if Skip it if you prefer a lightweight phone, clean software, or official brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 7,050 mAh cell gives industry leading endurance and the 100W charger refills it in about half an hour, which many owners praise. The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 drives smooth gaming and apps, though long sessions trigger thermal throttling that can lower frame rates. The 6.82 inch LTPO AMOLED panel reaches a peak 3,600 nit brightness and shows crisp colors, yet the 144 Hz rate only works in selected games.

The 200MP main sensor, paired with Hasselblad tuning, delivers impressive photos and a 3× telephoto lens, but AI processing can look over done and low light video may appear soft. ColorOS 16 offers many features but is layered with medium level bloatware and aggressive battery management that can close background apps unexpectedly. The phone is IP69 certified but its weight makes it bulky to hold for long periods.

Only gray import units are found, so the warranty comes from the shop rather than Oppo and you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.
