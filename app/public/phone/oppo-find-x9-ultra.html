What works well

  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 fast performance

Reported trade-offs

  • No official brand warranty in Bangladesh
  • Camera can fog in humid conditions
  • Thermal throttling under long gaming
  • Heavy and bulky for one‑hand use

Why we say that

The 6.82‑inch LTPO AMOLED screen reaches a peak 3,600 nits and can run at 144 Hz in supported games, making visuals vivid and smooth. Under the hood sits the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 with 16 GB RAM, delivering fast app launches and strong gaming performance, though long sessions may cause thermal throttling. The 7,050 mAh battery is praised for lasting a full day of heavy use, and the 200 MP Hasselblad‑tuned camera provides detailed photos and versatile zoom, but some owners see fogging on the lens and softer video than expected.

The phone is heavy and large, and it comes with pre‑installed bloatware that can affect the experience. It runs global ColorOS 16, promises up to five Android upgrades, and includes an IR blaster. Repairs are paid and only the shop’s limited warranty applies.

How long does the battery last on the OPPO Find X9 Ultra?

The 7,050 mAh battery is rated 9.4 by owners and is described as industry‑leading endurance. In real use it can comfortably last a full day of heavy browsing, video streaming and gaming before needing a recharge.

Will the phone handle heavy gaming without lag?

The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 gives a performance rating of 9.5 and gaming score of 8.8, so most titles run smoothly. However owners notice thermal throttling during long sessions and the 144 Hz panel only works in selected games.

Is the camera good in low light and are there any issues?

The 200 MP main sensor with 1/1.12″ size, f/1.5 aperture and OIS delivers natural Hasselblad colours and good low‑light detail. Some owners report fogging on the lens and video output can appear softer than expected.

Does the OPPO Find X9 Ultra come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray‑import units, so there is no official OPPO warranty in Bangladesh. The shop provides its own limited warranty and any repair costs are paid by the buyer. The phone ships with ColorOS 16 and will receive up to five Android upgrades.
