Is the Xiaomi 17 worth buying?

What is good

  • Class-leading 6330 mAh battery endurance
  • Stunning 3500 nit LTPO AMOLED display
  • Excellent premium compact design and feel
  • Fast 100W charging capabilities

What is not

  • Heavy CPU throttling under load
  • High levels of system bloatware and ads
  • Downgraded ultrawide camera with no autofocus
  • Stock camera app overexposes skin tones

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a compact form factor with exceptional battery life and a high-quality display for media consumption.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a heavy gamer who needs sustained performance or if you want a clean software experience without ads.

Why we say that

Owners report that the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset suffers from heavy CPU throttling under load, which impacts gaming performance. While the 6.3 inch LTPO AMOLED display is stunning and reaches 3500 nits, the user experience is hampered by HyperOS 3, which comes with high levels of bloatware and system ads. The camera system is a mixed bag, featuring a capable 50MP main sensor but a disappointing ultrawide lens that lacks autofocus and has a narrow field of view.

Users also note that the stock camera app tends to overexpose skin tones. Because this device is only available through unofficial channels, you must rely on shop warranties rather than a brand warranty. You should verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before making a purchase, as the high price point makes this a significant investment.

Does the Xiaomi 17 come with an official brand warranty in Bangladesh?

No, the Xiaomi 17 is currently only available through unofficial gray-import channels. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, so you must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before buying.

How does the battery life perform for daily usage?

The battery life is considered class-leading for a compact device. Owners report insane endurance from the 6330 mAh battery, making it one of the strongest features of the phone for users who need their device to last throughout the day.

Is this phone suitable for high-end mobile gaming?

While the phone uses top-tier silicon, it is not ideal for long gaming sessions. Owners report that the device suffers from heavy thermal throttling under load, which significantly impacts performance during intensive tasks.

How many years of software updates will this device receive?

The Xiaomi 17 running HyperOS 3 is scheduled to receive 3 to 4 OS updates. Additionally, the manufacturer provides 4 to 5 years of security updates to keep the device protected over its lifespan.
