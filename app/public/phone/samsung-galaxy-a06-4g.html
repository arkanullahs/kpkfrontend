Is the Samsung Galaxy A06 4G worth buying?

What is good

  • Strong 5,000 mAh battery
  • Large 6.7 inch screen
  • 50 MP main camera sensor
  • Headphone jack and FM radio

What is not

  • Noticeable UI lag and freezes
  • Dim low resolution display
  • Slow charging without included charger
  • Mediocre video and low light camera

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, basic photography and prefer a cheap device with a headphone jack.

Skip it if Skip it if you want smooth gaming, a bright high resolution screen or reliable low light photos.

Why we say that

The Galaxy A06 4G holds a 5,000 mAh cell that often lasts through two full days of normal use. The 6.7 inch PLS LCD is low resolution and can feel dim and reflective, especially in bright light. The Helio G85 processor with 4 GB RAM shows frequent freezes and UI stutters, and owners report that the 4 GB version is particularly unstable.

The 50 MP main sensor can produce decent stills in good light but overall camera performance is mediocre with poor dynamic range and shaky video. Charging is limited to 25 W and the box does not include a charger, so real‑world charging may feel slow. Android 14 with One UI 8 brings the latest software but feels heavy on this hardware, leading to sluggish UI and poor sustained gaming.

The phone includes a headphone jack, FM radio and a solid plastic build that feels thin and sturdy. If you can tolerate the performance limits, the low price and strong battery make it a usable budget option.
