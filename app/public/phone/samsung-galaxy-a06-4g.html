Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy A06 4G Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that will stay alive all day without charging, the Galaxy A06 4G delivers that.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a cheap phone with long battery life, basic camera and are okay with modest performance.
Consider alternatives if
You need a smooth gaming experience, a sharp display or reliable low light photography.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 5000 mAh battery lasts a full day
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • 50 MP main camera sensor
  • 25 W fast charging

Reported trade-offs4

  • 720p display is dim and ghosty
  • Helio G85 struggles with UI and games
  • Low light photos are poor
  • Touch responsiveness can be laggy

Why we say that

The 5000 mAh battery easily gets you through a full day and the 25 W charger refills it quickly. The plastic build feels solid and the fingerprint sensor works reliably, while the headphone jack and FM radio add useful extras. However the 6.7 inch 720p panel is dim, shows ghosting and smears when you swipe, and the Helio G85 chipset feels outdated, causing UI lag and poor gaming performance.

The 50 MP main sensor sounds impressive but photos are mediocre, especially in low light, and there is no video stabilization. Touch responsiveness can be inconsistent and the retail box often arrives without a charger. The cheapest official‑warranty unit costs ৳17,399, while gray imports start at ৳16,499 but lack Samsung’s cover.
