What works well

  • Durable plastic build
  • Good AI for daily photos
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • 90Hz refresh rate display

Reported trade-offs

  • Frequent software crashes and restarts
  • Overheating problems
  • Poor gaming performance
  • Slow 10W charging

Why we say that

While the plastic build is durable and the camera works for daily use, owners report frequent software crashes and the phone restarts often. Overheating is another common problem reported by users. The Unisoc Tiger T612 chipset does not provide strong performance, and gaming is rated poorly at 3.0.

You will deal with medium to high levels of bloatware and ads. The UI has limitations that affect the overall experience. It is a budget device that struggles with reliability, making it a risky choice if you need a stable phone for your primary tasks.
