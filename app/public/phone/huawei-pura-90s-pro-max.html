Is the Huawei Pura 90s Pro Max worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.9 inch LTPO OLED display with anti‑reflective coating
  • 50MP main camera with strong low‑light HDR
  • 6000 mAh battery with 100 W fast charging
  • Industry‑leading 5G signal recovery in dead zones

What is not

  • No Google Play services at all
  • Side fingerprint sensor placed too high
  • EMUI UI feels dated and lacks modern gestures
  • No in‑display fingerprint reader on a flagship

Buy it if Buy it if you value a large OLED screen, strong camera and long battery and can manage without Google Play services.

Skip it if Skip it if you need official Huawei service, a modern UI or top‑tier gaming performance.

Why we say that

The 6.9‑inch LTPO OLED panel shows vivid colours and keeps reflections low thanks to its anti‑reflective coating. The 50 MP main sensor delivers sharp photos and the reviews praise its low‑light HDR performance, while the 13 MP selfie camera is adequate. A 6000 mAh cell paired with 100 W charging lets you run a full day and refill in minutes.

The Kirin 9030S handles daily tasks smoothly, but gaming feels a step behind newer 3 nm flagships and the performance rating is modest. EMUI runs fast animations but the UI looks dated and lacks modern gestures. The side‑mounted fingerprint reader sits high and the lack of an in‑display sensor feels odd for a 2026 flagship.

Huawei does not ship Google Play services, so you will need workarounds for many Bangladeshi apps. The phone arrives as a gray‑import unit, so warranty is from the shop and official service is scarce in Bangladesh.
