Buying verdict

Huawei Pura 90s Pro Max Is it worth buying?

The Huawei Pura 90s Pro Max is a powerful camera-centric device that demands significant compromises for its ৳1,24,999 price tag.

Choose it if
You are a photography enthusiast who values battery life and signal strength above all else.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on Google apps or expect a modern, hassle-free flagship experience with official service support.

What works well

  • Massive 6000 mAh battery endurance
  • Elite low-light and HDR camera performance
  • Durable Kunlun Glass build quality
  • Industry-leading 5G signal recovery

Reported trade-offs

  • No Google Play services available
  • Dated EMUI interface and icons
  • High-placed side fingerprint scanner
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

While the 6000 mAh battery and elite low-light camera performance are standout features, the lack of Google Play services remains a major hurdle for daily use in Bangladesh. Owners frequently report frustration with the side-mounted fingerprint scanner, which is positioned too high for comfortable access. The EMUI software feels dated compared to modern standards, and the Kirin 9030S chipset struggles to keep pace with 3nm flagships during intensive gaming sessions.

Because this is an unofficial gray-import unit, you have no manufacturer warranty support in the country. You are entirely dependent on the individual shop for any repairs or hardware issues. If you prioritize photography and battery endurance over software convenience and official support, this device offers a unique experience, but it is not a hassle-free flagship for the average user.

Does the Huawei Pura 90s Pro Max have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit currently available is a gray import, meaning you must rely solely on the specific shop for any repair promises or support.

How does the battery perform on this device?

The phone features a massive 6000 mAh battery that provides excellent endurance. Owners rate the battery performance highly at 9.2 out of 10, and it supports very fast 100W charging to keep you powered throughout the day.

Is this phone suitable for heavy gaming?

While the phone handles daily tasks smoothly, it is not ideal for high-end gaming. The Kirin 9030S chipset uses a 5nm node that lags behind modern 3nm flagships, resulting in a gaming rating of only 6.8 out of 10.

How is the camera performance in low light?

The camera system is a major strength of this device, earning an elite rating of 9.6 out of 10 from owners. It excels in low-light conditions and HDR photography, though some users note that daylight white balance can lean too blue or white.
