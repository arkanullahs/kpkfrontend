Reported trade-offs3

  • No Google Play services, need workarounds
  • Side fingerprint sensor placed too high, no in‑display option
  • EMUI UI feels dated and lacks modern gestures

Why we say that

The phone feels solid in the hand thanks to a dual‑tone metal frame and Kunlun glass, and the 6.9″ LTPO OLED panel delivers smooth 120 Hz motion with an anti‑reflective coating. Camera scores are high, especially the 200 MP telephoto and macro lenses that handle low‑light scenes with good HDR. 5G connectivity is strong even in typical dead zones.

Daily performance is smooth for most tasks, but the Kirin 9030S on a 5 nm process does not match the speed of newer 3 nm flagships, which shows in demanding games. The EMUI interface runs fast but looks dated and lacks modern gestures, and the side‑mounted fingerprint sensor sits too high for comfortable reach. The biggest drawback is the complete absence of Google Play services, meaning many Bangladeshi apps need workarounds.
