Why we say that

The 12 GB RAM and Kirin 9030S keep most tasks fluid, but the lack of Google Play services means many local apps need manual installation or alternative stores. Owners praise the long‑lasting 6000 mAh battery and crisp 50 MP photos, yet they often complain about the gray‑import status, which limits warranty to the selling shop and leaves repairs at the shop’s discretion. Software updates are not guaranteed and can vary between units, adding uncertainty for long‑term owners.

In short, the phone delivers strong performance and display quality, but the service and app ecosystem constraints make it a niche choice for users comfortable with workarounds.
