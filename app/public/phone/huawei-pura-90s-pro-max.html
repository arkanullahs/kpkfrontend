Huawei Pura 90s Pro Max Is it worth buying?

The Huawei Pura 90s Pro Max is a premium hardware device that struggles significantly with modern software expectations and daily usability.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You are a dedicated Huawei enthusiast who is comfortable sideloading apps and managing workarounds for Google services.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on Google apps for daily tasks or expect the performance and software fluidity of a modern flagship.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Large 6000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • Bright 120Hz LTPO OLED display
  • Premium metal frame with Kunlun glass
  • Strong telephoto and variable aperture camera

Reported trade-offs4

  • No Google Play services support
  • Dated EMUI software with ad suggestions
  • Performance lags behind current flagships
  • Inconsistent 100W fast charging speeds

Why we say that

While the 6000 mAh battery provides reliable all-day power, the actual user experience is hampered by the dated EMUI interface, which owners describe as feeling three years old and cluttered with ad suggestions. Performance is a major point of contention, with users noting it feels slower than current flagships and comparable to older mid-range hardware. The lack of native Google Play services remains a critical hurdle, requiring workarounds like MicroG or GBox that can introduce lag.

Physical design choices also frustrate owners, specifically the hard-to-reach side-mounted fingerprint sensor and a camera bump so large it causes wireless charging issues. You are paying ৳1,14,999 for a device that requires constant technical maintenance to function like a standard smartphone.

Does the Huawei Pura 90s Pro Max come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you must rely entirely on the shop's own promise for coverage. Repairs are paid, and there is little to no official service presence for this brand.

How does the battery perform during daily use?

Owners report that the 6000 mAh battery is a strong point, lasting all day on a single charge. While the phone supports 100W charging, users note that this speed is not consistently delivered and often requires using a specific turbo mode to get the best results.

Can I play high-end games on this device?

Gaming performance is rated at 6.5 out of 10 by owners. The Kirin 9030S chipset provides performance that users describe as slower than current flagships, feeling more like a three-year-old mid-range phone, which may limit your experience with demanding modern titles.

Is the camera system good for photography?

The camera system is well-regarded, with owners giving it an 8.2 rating. It features large sensors, a variable aperture, and a strong telephoto lens. However, the physical size of the camera bump is so large that it can interfere with wireless charging by causing the phone to tip over.
