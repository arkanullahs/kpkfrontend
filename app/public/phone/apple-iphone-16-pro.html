  • 120Hz LTPO OLED display with 2000 nit peak

  • 48MP main camera with 5× optical zoom
  • Long software support up to iOS 26.6

Reported trade-offs4

  • Charging takes over an hour
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Camera Control button is unintuitive
  • Titanium frame attracts fingerprints

Why we say that

The 6.3 inch LTPO Super Retina XDR OLED panel shines at 2000 nits and adapts to 120 Hz, making scrolling and media look smooth. Under the hood, the A18 Pro processor and 8 GB of RAM delivers top tier performance, and owners rate gaming at 9.4 with consistent frame rates. The 48 MP main camera with 5× optical zoom captures sharp stills, and the 12 MP selfie sensor is adequate.

Battery life is respectable for the size, though the 3582 mAh cell takes over an hour to fill because charging is unusually slow. The titanium frame feels solid but attracts fingerprints, and the new Camera Control button confuses many users. Some report occasional refresh rate hiccups and mild heating under heavy outdoor data use.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?
