Buying verdict

Apple iPhone 16 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you can tolerate very slow charging, the iPhone 16 Pro feels premium in daily use.

Choose it if
You value a smooth iOS experience, strong performance and long‑term resale value.
Consider alternatives if
You need fast charging, easy Android data migration, or a lower price.

What works well

  • 120 Hz LTPO OLED display
  • Apple A18 Pro delivers top performance
  • 48 MP camera with 5× optical zoom
  • iOS receives 5‑6 major updates

Reported trade-offs

  • Charging takes over an hour
  • Camera control button is unintuitive
  • Titanium frame attracts fingerprints
  • Transferring data from Android is painful

Why we say that

The 6.3" LTPO Super Retina XDR OLED display shines at 2000 nits and adapts its 120 Hz refresh rate smoothly. The A18 Pro chip and 8 GB RAM deliver top‑tier performance, scoring 9.6 in benchmarks and handling heavy games with consistent frames. Photography is strong with a 48 MP main sensor, 5× optical zoom and solid low‑light results, though the new camera control button confuses many.

Build quality is high thanks to a titanium frame, but it attracts fingerprints and the frame can feel slippery without a case. Battery life is decent for the size, yet charging takes over an hour, far slower than most Android flagships. Software runs iOS 18 smoothly and will receive five to six major updates, with no ads or bloat.

Overall, owners love the display, speed and camera, but they repeatedly mention the sluggish charging, the unintuitive button and the fingerprint‑magnet frame.
