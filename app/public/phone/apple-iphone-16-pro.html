Is the Apple iPhone 16 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Exceptionally powerful A18 Pro chipset
  • Industry-leading video and 5x zoom
  • Beautiful 120Hz LTPO OLED display
  • Sustained 120fps gaming performance

What is not

  • Slow 15W charging speed
  • Titanium body attracts fingerprints and scratches
  • Camera Control button ergonomics feel complex
  • Lacks split-screen and has clunky file management

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize top-tier gaming performance and professional-grade video recording capabilities.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you need fast charging, split-screen multitasking, or a device that does not require a protective case.

Why we say that

Owners consistently praise the A18 Pro chipset for sustained 120fps gaming and the 9.8 rating for video recording. Living with the device reveals a few frustrations. The titanium build is a major fingerprint magnet that feels incomplete without a protective cover.

Charging is objectively slow, with users reporting that a full charge takes a couple of hours. While the display is beautiful with thin bezels, some users have encountered refresh rate bugs. The new Camera Control button is polarizing, as many find it complicates simple photography tasks.

Migrating data from Android, specifically WhatsApp, remains a significant pain point for new users. Despite these issues, the phone remains a powerhouse for those who prioritize raw performance and camera capabilities over convenience features like fast charging or split-screen multitasking.
