Is the Apple iPhone 16 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz OLED display with 2000 nit brightness
  • A18 Pro chip delivers top gaming performance
  • 48MP camera with 5x optical zoom
  • iOS receives five to six major updates

What is not

  • 15W charging is noticeably slow
  • Titanium body attracts fingerprints and scratches
  • Camera control button feels awkward
  • Some users report refresh rate bugs

Buy it if Buy it if you want a premium iPhone with excellent display, camera and gaming capability and are comfortable using a case and slower charging.

Skip it if Skip it if you need fast charging, want a hassle free Android to iPhone data move, or prefer a lower price with official warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.3 inch LTPO OLED screen reaches 2000 nit brightness and runs at 120 Hz, though a few owners have seen occasional refresh rate glitches. The A18 Pro chip keeps games running at a steady 120 fps and the 48MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture and 5x optical zoom delivers clear photos and video, even in low light, but the new camera control button feels awkward to many. Battery life is decent for a 3582 mAh cell, earning a 7.8 rating, yet the 15W charger needs a couple of hours for a full charge.

iOS 18 runs smoothly and will receive five to six major updates, but it lacks split screen and has clunky file management. A case is recommended to protect the titanium finish.
