Is the Apple iPhone 16 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz LTPO Super Retina XDR OLED display
  • Apple A18 Pro chip with 8 GB RAM
  • 48 MP main camera with 5× optical zoom
  • Battery lasts a day with moderate use

What is not

  • Charging exceeds one hour for full charge
  • Titanium frame attracts fingerprints
  • Camera Control button feels unintuitive
  • Refresh‑rate may behave inconsistently

Buy it if Buy it if you want a premium build, top performance for gaming, and can accept slow charging or a gray‑import warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need fast charging, a fingerprint‑free surface, or a fully covered manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.3" LTPO OLED screen shines at 2000 nits and 120 Hz, though some users notice occasional refresh‑rate hiccups. The A18 Pro chip with 8 GB RAM handles heavy gaming and multitasking without stutter, and the 48 MP camera with 5× optical zoom produces excellent stills, even in low light. Battery life is decent for the size, yet charging takes over an hour, which many find frustrating.

The titanium body feels solid but attracts fingerprints, and the new Camera Control button is considered unintuitive by most. Software updates will keep the phone current for several years, and the device holds its resale value well. Official warranty only applies to the higher‑priced official unit; gray imports lack manufacturer cover.
