Buying verdict

OPPO Reno12 F 4G Is it worth buying?

If you value a bright, smooth display and long battery more than gaming speed or low‑light photos, the OPPO Reno12 F 4G fits that niche.

Choose it if
You want a large bright screen, solid battery life, and basic performance at the lowest official‑warranty price.
Consider alternatives if
You need flagship gaming performance, reliable low‑light photography, or a clean software experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • 6.67‑inch AMOLED panel with 120 Hz refresh rate
  • 5000 mAh battery with 45 W fast charging
  • 32 MP selfie camera
  • IR blaster for remote control

Reported trade-offs

  • Snapdragon 685 struggles with gaming and high FPS
  • Main camera oversaturated, poor low‑light, no stabilization
  • Heavy bloatware and occasional overheating

Why we say that

The 6.67‑inch AMOLED panel with 120 Hz refresh feels fluid, and the 5000 mAh cell lasts well beyond a day for most users. Charging at 45 W is decent, topping up quickly when needed. The phone looks premium with a minimalist design and solid grip, and the IR blaster adds a handy remote function.

However, the Snapdragon 685 chipset shows its age, struggling with games and causing noticeable lag. The main camera produces vivid but oversaturated images and fails in low‑light without any stabilization. Software carries medium‑level bloatware and occasional AOD quirks.

Read full reasoning

Overheating under prolonged load can throttle performance. Overall, it offers a good visual and battery experience at the cheapest official‑warranty price, but compromises on performance and camera quality.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery receives an 8.8 rating from owners and is described as having excellent endurance, easily lasting a full day of mixed use such as browsing, video streaming, and moderate app activity without needing a recharge.

Will this phone run modern games smoothly?

Owners report severe lag and low frame rates with the Snapdragon 685, especially in demanding titles like Genshin Impact, where the device struggles to reach 30 FPS even on the lowest settings. Gaming performance is therefore limited.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor is praised for bright colors but owners note oversaturation and poor low‑light results, with no optical or electronic image stabilization to help, making night shots appear noisy and blurry.

Does the phone come with an official Bangladeshi warranty and how many Android updates will it receive?

The listed unit is an official Bangladesh model, so it carries the brand’s warranty and can be serviced at OPPO’s local network. It ships with Android 14 and is promised an upgrade to Android 15, typical for mid‑range ColorOS devices.
