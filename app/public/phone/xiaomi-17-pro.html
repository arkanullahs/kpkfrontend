Buying verdict

Xiaomi 17 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you can live with a gray‑import and handle software quirks, the Xiaomi 17 Pro feels like a flagship in daily use.

Choose it if
You want flagship speed and display and are comfortable with a gray‑import that has only a shop warranty.

What works well

  • 6.3 inch 120Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • 6300 mAh battery with 100W fast charging
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 flagship performance
  • 50 MP main camera with 5× optical zoom

Reported trade-offs

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • China ROM lacks Google apps and Bangla language
  • Back panel is plastic and QC varies
  • Side heating under heavy load

Why we say that

The 6.3 inch LTPO AMOLED screen reaches 3500 nits and stays smooth at 120Hz, making media and scrolling pleasant. The 6300 mAh battery lasts well beyond a day and refills in minutes thanks to 100W wired charging, while the 50 MP main sensor with OIS and 5× telephoto delivers detailed photos and decent zoom. Performance stays high with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, and games run at top frame rates, though the sides of the phone can become hot under long sessions.

The front glass is solid but the back is plastic, and some units show quality inconsistencies. Software runs HyperOS 3 on Android 16, but the China ROM lacks Google apps, Bangla language and includes ads, making the experience less user‑friendly. There is no official Xiaomi warranty; only the shop’s limited guarantee and paid repairs are available.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 6300 mAh cell gives a full day of mixed use and more for light tasks. Owners rate battery life 9.6 out of 10, and the 100W charger refills it in under an hour.

Will the phone run games smoothly?

Yes, the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 scores 9.5 for gaming and 9.7 for performance. Games run at high frame rates, and although the sides heat up, the device stays stable during long sessions.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor with f/1.7 aperture and OIS captures detailed images and the 5× telephoto works well. Some owners notice skin‑tone over‑exposure, but overall low‑light detail is strong.

What warranty and software support does the phone have?

There is no official Xiaomi warranty; only the shop’s limited guarantee and paid repairs apply. Software updates cover about three to four Android versions and four to five security patches, and the China ROM lacks Google services.
