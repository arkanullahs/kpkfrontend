Xiaomi 17 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you can live without Google services and a brand warranty, the Xiaomi 17 Pro gives flagship performance at a gray import price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want flagship level performance and display and can accept a Chinese software version and shop only warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable Google services, Bangla language support, or an official brand warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 120 Hz LTPO AMOLED display with 3500 nits peak
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 delivers top gaming performance
  • 6300 mAh battery with 100 W fast charging
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS and 5× telephoto

Reported trade-offs4

  • Chinese ROM lacks Google apps and Bangla language
  • Heavy bloatware and system ads
  • Wide angle camera has no optical stabilization
  • No official manufacturer warranty, only shop warranty

Why we say that

The 6.3 inch LTPO AMOLED panel is bright and smooth, and the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chip keeps games and apps running fast. Battery life is solid thanks to a 6300 mAh silicon carbon cell, and the 100 W charger refills it quickly. The main 50 MP camera captures decent photos, but the processing is not as refined as top flagships and the wide angle lens has no optical stabilization.

The phone runs HyperOS, which comes with a lot of preinstalled ads and limited language options, and the Chinese ROM lacks Google apps and Bangla support. There is no official Xiaomi warranty, only the shop's limited coverage, so any repair will cost extra. Overall it feels like a high‑end device with some software compromises.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 6300 mAh silicon carbon battery gives excellent endurance and easily lasts a full day of mixed use such as browsing, video streaming and gaming. When it does run low, the 100 W wired charger refills it in about half an hour.

Is the Xiaomi 17 Pro good for mobile gaming?

The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset delivers top tier sustained performance, earning a 9.2 gaming rating. It handles demanding titles and eSports sessions smoothly, though prolonged play may raise device temperature.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.7 aperture and OIS captures decent low light photos, but the image processing is not as refined as premium flagships, giving a camera rating of 7.2. The 50 MP ultra wide lens lacks OIS, so its low light results are weaker.

Will I get an official warranty and regular software updates?

There is no official Xiaomi manufacturer warranty for gray import units; you only receive the shop’s limited warranty and any repair will be paid. Software updates are limited to three or four Android versions and four to five security patches, and the Chinese ROM lacks Google apps and Bangla language support.
