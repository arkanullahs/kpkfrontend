Is the Infinix Note 60 worth buying?

What is good

  • Exceptional battery endurance
  • Stunning 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Excellent thermal management
  • Premium aluminum frame design

What is not

  • Outdated UFS 2.2 storage
  • Excessive bloatware and ads
  • Disappointing IP64 water resistance
  • Poor camera dynamic range

Buy it if You prioritize a bright, high-refresh-rate display and long battery life above all other technical specifications.

Skip it if You expect modern storage speeds, a clean software experience, or high-end camera performance for your ৳44,999.

Why we say that

Owners frequently highlight the massive battery life as a standout feature, yet the overall experience is hampered by outdated UFS 2.2 storage that feels unacceptable at this cost. While the aluminum frame looks and feels high-end, the IP64 rating is considered disappointing for a device in this segment. You will find the software experience frustrating because XOS 16 comes preloaded with excessive bloatware and persistent notification ads that require manual removal.

Camera performance is limited to usable daylight shots, often suffering from dull colors and poor dynamic range. Although the thermal management is excellent for gaming, the combination of slow storage and intrusive software makes it difficult to recommend for power users who expect modern standards at this price point.
