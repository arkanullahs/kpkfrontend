Infinix Note 60 Is it worth buying?

The Infinix Note 60 offers a stunning display and massive battery life, but it struggles to justify its high price tag due to outdated hardware choices.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize a bright, high-refresh-rate display and long battery life for daily tasks.
Consider alternatives if
You expect modern storage speeds, a high-end camera, or a clean software experience without ads.

  • Stunning 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Excellent thermal management

  • Heavy bloatware and notification ads
  • Disappointing IP64 water resistance
  • Poor camera dynamic range

Why we say that

Owners report that the 6150 mAh battery provides exceptional endurance, making it a reliable companion for long days. However, the experience is marred by significant software issues, including heavy bloatware and persistent notification ads in XOS 16. While the aluminum frame provides a premium aesthetic, users are disappointed by the low IP64 rating, which feels inadequate for a device at this price point.

The most common complaint involves the use of UFS 2.2 storage, which owners find unacceptable for a phone costing up to ৳44,999. While the Dimensity 7400 Ultimate chipset handles daily tasks well, the camera performance is limited by dull colors and poor dynamic range. You are paying for a bright screen and a large battery, but you must be prepared to manage preloaded apps and accept slower storage speeds.

How long will the Infinix Note 60 receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 16 and is promised up to 3 major Android upgrades. However, owners note that the software experience is currently hindered by high levels of preloaded apps and notification ads.

Is the Infinix Note 60 suitable for heavy gaming?

While the Dimensity 7400 Ultimate chipset and large vapor cooling chamber provide smooth daily performance, the gaming experience is limited by the use of outdated UFS 2.2 storage. Owners have rated the gaming performance at 6.0 out of 10.

The 50MP main camera is capable of usable daylight shots, but users report dull colors and poor dynamic range. The camera is rated 5.2 out of 10 by owners, suggesting it may not meet the expectations of photography enthusiasts.
