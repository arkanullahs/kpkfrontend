HTC Wildfire E7 Life Is it worth buying?

The HTC Wildfire E7 Life is a budget device that struggles to keep up with modern expectations despite its 6GB of RAM.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a basic device for simple tasks and prefer a clean, ad-free software experience.
Consider alternatives if
You play games, care about display quality, or need fast charging to get through your day.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Clean and ad-free Android 16 software
  • Attractive textured back design
  • Generous 6GB RAM capacity
  • Lightweight build for daily handling

Reported trade-offs4

  • Painfully slow 10W charging speed
  • Dim display with massive bezels
  • Poor camera sharpness and selfie quality
  • Lags during long gaming sessions

Why we say that

Living with this phone means dealing with a display that feels outdated due to massive bezels and low brightness levels. While the clean Android 16 software provides a pleasant experience with smooth animations, the hardware often lags during daily use. Owners are particularly frustrated by the ancient 10W charging speed, which takes over two hours to reach a full charge.

The camera performance is limited to acceptable daylight shots, with poor sharpness and disappointing selfies. Build quality is a mixed bag, featuring an attractive textured back that is lightweight but noticeably thick. The fingerprint scanner is accurate but slow to respond.

Read full reasoning

If you prioritize a clean software interface over raw performance or display quality, this phone might serve basic needs, but heavy users will likely find the experience underwhelming.
