Is the Vivo S30 worth buying?

What is good

  • Huge 6500 mAh battery lasts over a day
  • Fast 90 W charger fills in under an hour
  • Vibrant 6.67‑inch AMOLED with 5000 nits peak
  • Strong 50 MP main camera with OIS and 3× telephoto

What is not

  • Plastic frame feels cheap
  • Heating noticeable during gaming
  • UFS 2.2 storage feels slow
  • China ROM delays notifications and lacks Google

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large battery, fast charging and a bright display at a mid‑range price and can tolerate a plastic body and Chinese software quirks.

Skip it if Skip it if you need an official warranty, latest UFS storage, or reliable notification performance for daily messaging.

Why we say that

Its 6.67‑inch AMOLED screen shows vivid colours and can hit a 5000‑nit peak, making outdoor use easy, while the 120 Hz refresh keeps scrolling smooth. The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 with 12 GB RAM handles everyday apps without lag, and the 90 W charger fills the 6500 mAh battery in under an hour, giving roughly a day and a half of use. The 50 MP main sensor with OIS and 3× telephoto produces good photos in good light, and the IR blaster adds a remote‑control perk.

However, the plastic frame feels cheap and the device gets noticeably hot during long gaming or high‑brightness sessions. Storage runs on older UFS 2.2, which feels slower than newer phones, and the Chinese OriginOS ROM lacks Google apps, Bangla language, and often delays notifications. Only a shop warranty is available, so confirm IMEI status before buying.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 6500 mAh battery gives roughly one and a half days of normal use and can stretch to two days with light tasks. When it runs out, the included 90 W charger refills the phone in less than an hour.

Will the phone get hot while gaming or using high brightness?

The phone reaches high temperatures during extended gaming sessions or when the screen is set to its 5000‑nit peak brightness. Users report noticeable warmth that can affect comfort, although it does not shut down the device.

Does the phone come with Google apps and Bangla language support?

Gray‑import units run the Chinese OriginOS ROM which does not ship with Google services or Bangla language support. You will need to install Google apps manually and may face occasional notification delays in messaging apps.

Is there an official Vivo warranty for the S30 in Bangladesh?

Only a shop warranty is offered for the gray‑import S30, not Vivo’s official Bangladeshi warranty. Verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchase, because official service may be unavailable.
