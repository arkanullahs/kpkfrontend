What works well

  • Bright and colorful 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Thin and lightweight build
  • High peak brightness of 4500 nits
  • Includes an IR blaster and FM radio

Reported trade-offs

  • Overheating under minimal usage
  • Underwhelming battery performance
  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • Mediocre low light photos

Why we say that

Owners describe the screen as bright, colorful, and smooth. However, living with this phone comes with several frustrations. Many users report that it overheats even under minimal usage.

There are also reports of network issues where callers may think the user is muted. The software experience is hindered by high amounts of bloatware and ads. Some users find the contact search to be very slow.

Read full reasoning

While the performance rating is decent, the battery life is described as underwhelming. You get a thin and lightweight build, but the internal software and thermal issues make it a risky choice for some.
