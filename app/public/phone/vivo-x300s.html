Is the Vivo X300s worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 7100 mAh battery capacity
  • Elite 144Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • Excellent sustained performance from Dimensity 9500
  • High detail 200MP main camera

What is not

  • Broken Gmail badge notifications
  • Concerns over weight and IP rating
  • Inconsistent skin texture processing
  • Only gray-import stock with shop warranty

Buy it if Buy this phone if you are a power user who prioritizes battery life and gaming performance above all else.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you rely on consistent Gmail notifications or require the security of an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Owners rate the performance and battery at a stellar 9.7, making it a top choice for heavy users who prioritize raw power. However, the experience is marred by OriginOS bloat and broken Gmail badge notifications, which some users feel undermines the premium nature of the device. While the 200MP camera captures high detail, there are reports of inconsistent skin texture processing.

The build quality is rated at 7.5, with users noting concerns regarding the weight and the lack of a clear IP rating. Since this is only available as gray-import stock, you will rely on a shop warranty rather than a brand warranty.
