Why we say that

The 7100 mAh battery easily powers a full day of mixed use and charges quickly with the included 90 W charger. The 6.78‑inch LTPO AMOLED panel runs at 144 Hz and stays bright even in sunlight, while the 200 MP main sensor delivers natural skin tones and good zoom. The Dimensity 9500 chipset handles most apps smoothly, but it does heat up during long gaming sessions and the performance drops after seven to eight minutes.

OriginOS 6 feels modern but still shows bloatware and ads. There is no official manufacturer warranty, so you rely on the shop’s limited coverage and must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
