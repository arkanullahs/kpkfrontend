POCO X6 Is it worth buying?

If you can live without a brand warranty, the POCO X6 gives you a bright 120Hz AMOLED screen, decent Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 performance and a large 5100 mAh battery for the price.

4 strengths · 2 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a bright, fast screen and good performance at a low price and you are okay with shop‑only warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need official brand support or a camera that works instantly in low light.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 120Hz AMOLED display with 1800‑nit peak brightness
  • Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 delivers solid performance
  • 5100 mAh battery gives decent endurance
  • Headphone jack and IR blaster included

Reported trade-offs2

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Camera app opens slowly on HyperOS

Why we say that

The phone feels solid with Gorilla Glass Victus and IP54 protection, and it includes a headphone jack and IR blaster that many mid‑range phones lack. Owners enjoy the daylight photos from the 64 MP main sensor but complain that the camera app opens slowly and that HyperOS adds noticeable bloat and ads. Software updates are promised for a few years, yet the UI can feel sluggish.

Overall it is a capable everyday device if you accept the gray‑import warranty limits.
