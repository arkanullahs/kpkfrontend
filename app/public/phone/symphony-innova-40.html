Why we say that

With a 6000 mAh cell the device can run all basic apps and media for many hours without hunting for a charger. The 6.75" IPS LCD runs at 120Hz, so scrolling feels smooth, but the low resolution and dim outdoor brightness make it hard to read in sunlight. The Unisoc T615 chipset handles everyday tasks but stalls on games and heavy apps, and the 18 W charger feels too slow for such a large battery.

The 50 MP main sensor captures decent daylight photos, yet low light scenes lose detail and dynamic range. The phone feels bulky with thick bezels and a waterdrop notch that looks dated. Android 15 runs clean with little bloat, but updates are rare.

How long does the battery last on daily use?

Can I play games on this phone?
