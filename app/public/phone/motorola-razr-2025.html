Why we say that

You get a bright 6.9 inch foldable screen and a clean software experience with low bloatware. However, the 30W charging is slow for a device at this price. Owners often struggle with Motorola's history of weak software updates, as you might only get 1 to 3 OS versions.

Since no official channel is listed, you will rely on shop warranties rather than brand support. You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying ৳89,990. It is a stylish device, but the long term software support is a gamble.

What is the Motorola Razr 2025 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Motorola Razr 2025 in Bangladesh was ৳89,990, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official brand warranty in Bangladesh?

No listing states an official channel. Only gray import stock is available, which means you get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty. You should confirm the BTRC status with the seller.

How long will I get software updates for this device?

Motorola has a historically weak update record. You can expect between 1 and 3 OS updates for this phone.

Is the battery and charging fast enough for heavy use?

The phone has a 4500 mAh battery. It supports 30W charging, which is relatively slow compared to other devices in this price range.

What are the camera specifications for photos?

The main camera is 50MP with an f/1.7 aperture and a 1/1.95 inch sensor. For selfies, it uses a 32MP front camera.
