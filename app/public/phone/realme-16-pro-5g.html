Realme 16 Pro Plus Is it worth buying?

The Realme 16 Pro Plus is a massive device that prioritizes battery longevity over raw gaming power.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize extreme battery life and a bright display for daily tasks over gaming performance.

What works well4

  • Class-leading 7000 mAh battery life
  • Bright 6500 nits 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Strong 200MP main camera performance
  • Durable IP69 rated build quality

Reported trade-offs4

  • Thermal throttling during heavy gaming
  • Ad-heavy software requiring manual cleanup
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Average low-light video performance

Why we say that

Living with this phone means managing a heavy 7000 mAh battery that easily lasts heavy users over a day, but you must be prepared to deal with a software experience cluttered by ads and bloatware. Owners report that the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset struggles under pressure, with temperatures reaching 43 degrees Celsius and causing noticeable thermal throttling during gaming sessions. While the 200MP main camera performs well for portraits, the ultrawide and low-light video capabilities are merely average.

The curved 144Hz AMOLED display is bright and impressive, yet the physical design remains polarizing. Because these units are gray-import stock built for India, you will not receive any manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh. You must rely entirely on the shop for repairs and confirm the BTRC status of your specific unit before purchasing.

Does the Realme 16 Pro Plus come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray-import model built for India. You must rely on the specific shop for any repair promises, and all repairs will be paid services.

How does the phone perform for gaming?

The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset is adequate for daily tasks but is not ideal for heavy gaming. Owners report that the device suffers from thermal throttling when pushed, with temperatures reaching 43 degrees Celsius during intense sessions.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The device is supported by Realme UI and is expected to receive 2 to 3 OS updates. Users should be aware that the software comes with a medium to high amount of pre-installed bloatware and ads that require manual cleanup.

Is the camera good for low light photography?

The 200MP main sensor provides strong performance for standard shots and portraits. However, owners report that the ultrawide camera and low-light video capabilities are only average compared to other features on the device.
