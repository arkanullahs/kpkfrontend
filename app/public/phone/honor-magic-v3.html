What works well

  • Unrivaled thinness and weight
  • Industry leading brightness and minimal crease
  • Fast 66W wired and 50W wireless charging
  • High build quality rating

Reported trade-offs

  • Aggressive thermal throttling
  • Unpolished and buggy software
  • Inner screen durability issues
  • Low speaker volume

Why we say that

The display is a highlight with a minimal crease and superior eye care technology. However, living with it reveals several frustrations. Owners report software bugs where interface elements overlap and grammatical errors exist in the system.

Performance is snappy for daily tasks, but the phone throttles heavily under load with a reported performance dip over 40 percent. Heat builds up near the camera module during intensive use. There are also serious concerns regarding inner screen durability, as some users have seen black lines or spots form in the center.

Read full reasoning

The speakers are noted to be too low in volume. While the hardware is impressive, the unpolished software and thermal issues make the premium price hard to justify for some.

How is the gaming performance on this phone?

It is snappy for daily tasks, but it throttles heavily under sustained load. Owners report a performance dip of over 40 percent and noticeable heat buildup near the camera module during intensive use.

Does the battery last a full day?

The 5150 mAh battery uses impressive silicon carbon technology. However, reviews state that the real world endurance is just average.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The phone runs MagicOS based on Android 14 and is upgradable to Android 15. Honor typically provides 3 to 4 OS updates on its flagship devices.

Is the camera good for all situations?

The setup is versatile, but processing is inconsistent. Users have reported subpar performance when using the ultrawide lens or taking photos in backlit conditions.
