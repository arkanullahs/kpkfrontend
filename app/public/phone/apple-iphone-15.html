Buying verdict

Apple iPhone 15 Is it worth buying?

If you can live without a Bangladeshi Apple warranty and accept slower charging, the iPhone 15 feels premium in daily use but shows its age in several key areas.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value iOS smoothness, premium build, and top‑level camera over high refresh rate and fast charging.
Consider alternatives if
You need fast charging, long gaming sessions, or official Apple warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • A16 Bionic delivers snappy everyday performance
  • 48 MP main camera captures detailed photos
  • Premium frosted glass back feels solid
  • iOS provides clean, long‑term software support

Reported trade-offs4

  • 20 W charging is slow and charger not included
  • 60 Hz display feels outdated
  • Battery drains quickly under heavy use
  • GPU throttles heavily during long gaming sessions

Why we say that

The A16 Bionic still feels snappy for apps and iOS 17 runs cleanly, while the 48 MP main camera delivers detailed photos and strong video quality. The frosted glass back and aluminum frame give a solid, premium feel. However, the 6.1‑inch 60 Hz OLED panel looks dim compared with modern high‑refresh screens, and the 20 W charger is not included, making top‑up noticeably slow.

The 3349 mAh battery is enough for light to moderate use but drains quickly under heavy load, especially after iOS upgrades. Gaming sessions generate noticeable warmth and the GPU throttles to about half its original speed, limiting long‑play performance. As a gray‑import, you will rely on the shop’s own repair promise and pay for any fixes.

How long will the iPhone 15 keep receiving iOS updates?

The phone launched in 2023 and is now three years old, so the remaining major iOS updates are expected to be short or may stop soon. It will still get security patches for a while.

What is the battery life like and how fast does it charge?

The 3349 mAh battery is solid for daily tasks but drains faster under heavy use or after iOS upgrades. Charging uses 20 W, which is slower than newer phones and no charger is included in the box.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 48 MP main sensor with f/1.6 aperture and optical image stabilization captures clear low‑light photos and excellent video. The ultrawide lens lacks autofocus, so detail can be softer in dim environments.

What warranty and repair options are available for this gray‑import?

There is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh for this unit. The shop you buy from may offer its own limited promise, but any repair work will be paid out of pocket.
