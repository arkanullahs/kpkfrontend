Is the Apple iPhone 15 worth buying?

What is good

  • Excellent 48MP main camera
  • Premium frosted glass build
  • Clean software with no bloat
  • Top-tier video recording quality

What is not

  • Outdated 60Hz display refresh rate
  • Slow 20W charging speeds
  • Battery drain and heating issues
  • Restricted USB 2.0 data speeds

Buy it if You are upgrading from an iPhone 11 or 12 and want a premium build with a reliable camera system.

Skip it if You are a mobile gamer or someone who expects modern display technology and long-term software support.

Why we say that

While the A16 Bionic chip handles daily tasks with ease, gamers should be aware that the GPU throttles to 50 percent under sustained load. Owners frequently report anxiety regarding the 3349 mAh battery, which struggles with drain and heating issues following the iOS 26 update. The camera system is a highlight, offering excellent 48MP shots and top-tier video quality, though the ultrawide lens is noticeably soft.

You are essentially buying a three-year-old device that is nearing the end of its software support life. With the newer iPhone 16 available for a similar price, this model is a difficult recommendation unless you find a significant discount. The USB-C port is a welcome addition, but the restricted USB 2.0 data speeds remain a frustrating limitation for power users.

Will the iPhone 15 still receive software updates?

This phone is already three years old. You should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point, as it is nearing the end of its support cycle.

Is the iPhone 15 good for heavy gaming?

While the A16 Bionic chip is snappy for daily tasks, it is not ideal for heavy gaming. Owners report that the GPU throttles to 50 percent under sustained load and the device gets warm near the camera.

How does the battery perform in daily use?

The 3349 mAh battery can get you through a day, but the small capacity causes anxiety. Owners report significant battery drain and heating issues specifically after the iOS 26 update.
