Buying verdict

Apple iPhone 15 Is it worth buying?

The iPhone 15 is a premium device that offers a high-end experience but comes with significant compromises for the price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a premium Apple experience and prioritize camera quality and software stability over high-refresh-rate gaming.
Consider alternatives if
You are a heavy gamer or someone who expects modern display features and official warranty support for your investment.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Excellent main camera and video quality
  • Premium frosted glass and aluminum build
  • Clean software with no bloatware
  • High peak brightness of 2000 nits

Reported trade-offs4

  • Outdated 60Hz display refresh rate
  • Significant GPU throttling during gaming
  • Slow 20W charging and USB 2.0 speeds
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

While the A16 Bionic chipset handles daily tasks with ease, owners frequently report that the GPU throttles to half its performance during long gaming sessions. The 60Hz display is a major point of contention, with users calling it the most expensive phone in the world to feature such an outdated refresh rate. Battery life is another concern, as the small 3349 mAh capacity struggles under load and shows increased drainage after recent software updates.

You will also notice warmth near the camera module during heavy background tasks. Because these units are gray imports, you receive no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh, and any repairs must be paid for out of pocket. While the camera captures excellent video and main sensor shots, the lack of autofocus on the ultrawide lens limits its versatility.

Read full reasoning

It remains a capable device for those who prioritize a clean software experience over raw gaming power or modern display standards.

Apple iPhone 15 costs ৳73,999 to ৳89,490 in Bangladesh on unofficial listings, in stock at 7 shops we track as of 18 Sep 2026. The official price starts at ৳1,09,999.

Does this phone come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit is a gray import, meaning you are responsible for all repair costs. The shop you purchase from may offer its own limited coverage, but there is no brand-backed support.

How long will the iPhone 15 receive software updates?

The device runs iOS 17 and is upgradable to iOS 26.6. However, since the phone was released in 2023, it is already three years old. You should expect a limited remaining lifespan for major software updates at this point.

Is the battery life reliable for daily use?

Owners describe the battery as solid for basic daily tasks, but the 3349 mAh capacity is small. Many users report noticeable battery drainage after upgrading to the latest iOS versions, and the battery struggles significantly when the phone is under heavy load.

Can I use this phone for heavy gaming?

While the A16 Bionic chipset is snappy for daily apps, it is not ideal for prolonged gaming. Owners report that the GPU throttles down to around 50 percent of its initial performance, and you will feel noticeable warmth near the camera during intense sessions.
