Apple iPhone 15 Is it worth buying?

If you can live without an Apple factory warranty, the iPhone 15 still feels like a premium daily driver.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a high‑quality iPhone experience and are comfortable handling a shop warranty and buying a separate charger.
Consider alternatives if

What works well4

  • 48 MP main camera is excellent
  • A16 chip runs smoothly
  • Premium glass and aluminum build
  • Bright OLED display up to 2000 nits

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official Apple warranty in Bangladesh
  • 60 Hz display feels outdated
  • USB C limited to USB 2.0
  • Battery drains faster after iOS 26

Why we say that

The A16 Bionic chip delivers smooth performance for everyday tasks and most games, while the 48 MP main camera captures sharp photos in good light. The OLED panel reaches very high brightness, making outdoor use easy, and the glass‑aluminum build feels solid and water resistant. Owners complain about the 60 Hz screen that feels dated compared with newer phones, and the phone gets warm during long gaming sessions.

Battery life is decent for a day but drops after the iOS 26 update, and the lack of a charger in the box means you need to buy a separate fast charger. The USB C port is limited to USB 2.0 speeds, and there is no telephoto lens or autofocus on the ultrawide. Because it is a gray import, you only get the shop’s limited warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

Will the iPhone 15 receive software updates for long?

The phone launched in 2023 and can still be upgraded to iOS 26.6, but being three years old means only a few major updates are likely left. Expect the update window to close soon.

Is there an official Apple warranty for this model in Bangladesh?

How does the battery perform after the iOS 26 update?

Can I play games without the phone overheating?

The A16 chip handles most games well, but heavy gaming or long sessions cause the device to become warm. Expect some heating during extended play.
