Is the Helio 50 worth buying?

What is good

  • Reliable 5000 mAh battery endurance
  • Rare 33W fast charging support
  • Smooth 90Hz FHD+ display panel
  • Clean user interface

What is not

  • Slow eMMC storage bottleneck
  • Camera app unresponsiveness
  • Fragile build prone to scratches
  • Poor camera dynamic range

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a device for basic daily tasks and value a high refresh rate display on a budget.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a heavy gamer or someone who relies on a responsive camera for photography.

Why we say that

While the 5000 mAh battery and 33W fast charging provide solid endurance, the device is held back by an eMMC storage bottleneck that causes the phone to slow down over long periods of use. The 108MP camera sensor is another point of frustration, as it lacks dynamic range and suffers from software optimization issues that make the camera app unresponsive. Users also report that the build quality feels premium but is fragile and prone to scratches.

While the 90Hz FHD+ display is a highlight, the average brightness makes it difficult to see clearly under direct sunlight. If you prioritize a clean interface and basic daily tasks, this phone might suffice, but heavy users will likely find the Helio G88 chipset and software responsiveness lacking for their needs.
