What works well

  • Reliable 5000 mAh battery endurance
  • Rare 33W fast charging in this segment
  • Premium glass like build feel
  • Good FHD+ 90Hz panel with Gorilla Glass

Reported trade-offs

  • eMMC storage causes long term slowdown
  • Camera app is often unresponsive
  • Fragile build prone to scratches
  • Average outdoor brightness

Why we say that

You get a premium glass like feel and a reliable 5000 mAh battery that supports 33W fast charging. However, real world use reveals some frustrating flaws. Owners report that the camera app often becomes unresponsive.

The use of eMMC storage is a major concern because it creates a bottleneck that makes the phone slow down after long term use. While the 90Hz display is decent, the 500 nits brightness is not enough for direct sunlight. The Helio G88 chipset handles basic daily tasks fine, but it is not built for heavy workloads.

Helio 50: what buyers ask us

What is the Helio 50 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Helio 50 in Bangladesh was ৳14,199, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Is this phone good for gaming?

The Helio G88 chipset is adequate for daily tasks. However, it struggles with heavy gaming, and owners have given gaming a low rating of 5.0 out of 10.

How is the battery life and charging?

The phone has a reliable 5000 mAh battery. It features 33W fast charging, which is rare for this price segment, and owners rated the battery 7.5 out of 10.

Does the 108MP camera take good photos?

The 108MP sensor lacks dynamic range and suffers from poor software optimization. Owners also report that the camera app becomes unresponsive at times.

Will the phone stay fast over time?

Owners report that the eMMC storage technology creates a bottleneck. This causes the phone to slow down significantly after a long period of use.
