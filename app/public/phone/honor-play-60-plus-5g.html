Honor Play 60 Plus 5G Is it worth buying?

The Honor Play 60 Plus 5G is a device defined by its massive battery capacity rather than its display or gaming capabilities.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a phone that prioritizes battery longevity over display quality and gaming performance.
Consider alternatives if
You want a sharp screen for media consumption or a device capable of handling demanding games.

What works well4

  • Efficient 6000 mAh battery
  • Large 6.77 inch display
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • Up to 512GB storage

Reported trade-offs4

  • Low resolution 720p TFT screen
  • Poor gaming performance
  • Untuned 50MP camera
  • Medium levels of software ads

Why we say that

While the 6000 mAh battery is efficient and provides long-lasting power, owners are frustrated by the 720p TFT screen which feels outdated for a phone priced at ৳26,990. The Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 chipset struggles to meet expectations for gaming, leading to poor performance ratings from users. You will also encounter medium levels of ads within the Magic OS 8 software.

The 50MP main camera is functional but remains untuned, resulting in mediocre image quality. Owners have expressed significant disappointment regarding the display technology, noting that it lacks the quality found in competing devices. If you prioritize battery life above all else, this phone might hold some appeal, but the overall build and display experience leave much to be desired for the price.

What is the Honor Play 60 Plus 5G price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Honor Play 60 Plus 5G in Bangladesh was ৳26,990, checked 24 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does the Honor Play 60 Plus 5G come with an official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh?

There is no listing that states the channel for this phone, so we cannot confirm a manufacturer warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller, as you will likely only receive a shop warranty.

Is the Honor Play 60 Plus 5G suitable for playing heavy games?

Owners have rated the gaming performance as a 3.0 out of 10. The Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 chipset and the 720p TFT screen do not provide a strong experience for users looking for a gaming device.

How is the battery life on this device?

The phone features an efficient 6000 mAh battery that owners have rated highly with an 8.0 out of 10. While the TFT screen may consume more power than an IPS panel, the battery remains the strongest feature of this phone.

What kind of software experience can I expect?

The phone runs Android 14 with Magic OS 8. Users report that the software includes a medium amount of ads. While it is a global version with Google services, you should be prepared for these advertisements during daily use.
