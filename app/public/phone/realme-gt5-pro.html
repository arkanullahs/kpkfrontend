Realme GT5 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you can accept a gray‑import unit with no brand warranty, the Realme GT5 Pro gives flagship speed and a blisteringly bright 144 Hz screen at a low price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want flagship‑level speed, a bright high‑refresh display and can live with a shop‑provided warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable low‑light camera, official warranty or strong water resistance.

What works well4

  • 144Hz LTPO AMOLED screen
  • Fast 100W wired charging
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 performance
  • Large 5400 mAh battery

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official brand warranty
  • Chinese ROM lacks Google services
  • Ultrawide camera weak
  • IP64 not water‑proof enough

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset delivers smooth performance in apps and games, and the 5400 mAh battery lasts through a full day while the 100W charger refills it in minutes. The LTPO AMOLED panel reaches a peak of 4500 nits, making outdoor use easy to read. However, the phone runs a China‑ROM that lacks Google services, shows medium‑high ads, and may need a paid unlock code.

Camera scores are mixed; the main 50 MP sensor is decent but the ultrawide lens is underwhelming and video stabilization is weak. The IP64 rating does not protect against submersion, and some users report poor network reception. At three years old, software updates are likely to end soon, so consider the limited future support before buying.

Is there an official Realme warranty for the GT5 Pro in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray‑import units, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop may offer its own limited warranty, but repairs are paid and you should verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

Will the phone still receive Android updates?

The phone launched in 2023 and is already three years old. It currently runs Android 14 and can be upgraded to Android 16, but future updates are unlikely, so expect a short remaining software‑support window.

How does the camera perform in low light and video?

The main 50 MP sensor is decent, but owners rate the overall camera at 7.5 out of 10. The ultrawide lens is underwhelming, video stabilization is weak, and videos tend to be softer than expected, especially in low‑light conditions.

What is the battery endurance and charging like?

The 5400 mAh battery is described as long‑lasting, and the 100W charger refills the phone very quickly. Users also note rapid wired and wireless charging, and there is no noticeable throttling during heavy use.
