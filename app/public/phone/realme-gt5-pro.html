Is the Realme GT5 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Powerful Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 performance
  • Stunning 4500 nits peak brightness display
  • Excellent battery endurance with 100W charging
  • Elite main and periscope camera quality

What is not

  • Frustrating region lock and Chinese bloatware
  • Poor ultrawide camera and weak video stabilization
  • Heavy and front-heavy build quality
  • No official brand warranty

Buy it if You are an advanced user who knows how to handle China-ROM software and wants flagship gaming performance at a lower price.

Skip it if You want a hassle-free experience with official warranty, native Bangla support, and reliable video recording capabilities.

Why we say that

While the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset delivers exceptional gaming performance and the 4500 nits display is stunning, the device is sold as a gray-import China-ROM unit. Owners frequently report frustrating region lock issues that may require paying extra fees to unlock. You will also face significant software hurdles, including heavy Chinese bloatware and the lack of native Google or Bangla support.

The build is premium vegan leather, yet it feels heavy and front-heavy due to the camera module. While the main and periscope cameras are elite, the ultrawide lens performs poorly and video stabilization is inconsistent. Because this is a 2023 model, you should expect limited remaining software support.

You are also limited to shop warranties rather than official brand support, making it essential to verify the BTRC status before spending your money.

Does the Realme GT5 Pro have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as a gray-import unit in the local market. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, so you must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before purchasing.

How is the gaming performance on this device?

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset provides buttery smooth and stable high-FPS gaming. Owners rate the gaming experience at 9.2 out of 10, noting that the phone runs cool during most tasks with minimal throttling during sustained sessions.

Will I face issues with the software or language settings?

Yes, the units sold here are China-ROM variants. They lack native Google and Bangla support, and you will encounter heavy Chinese bloatware. Many owners also report being forced to pay extra fees to resolve region lock issues.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The phone was released in 2023 and is now three years old. You should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point, despite the device being capable of running Android 14 and Realme UI 7.0.
