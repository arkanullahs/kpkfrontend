Why we say that

This phone feels premium thanks to Victus 2 glass and has a bright AMOLED screen that is easy to see in sunlight. Battery life is a strong point as the 5500 mAh cell easily lasts a full day. However, the Helio G100 Ultra chipset is underpowered for the price.

Owners complain that it struggles with heavy gaming and high settings. The software experience is frustrating because HyperOS comes with excessive bloatware and unnecessary ads. While the 200MP camera takes good still photos, it lacks sharpness and dynamic range.
