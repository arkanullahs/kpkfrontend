Is the Realme C51 worth buying?

What is good

  • Fast 33W charging support
  • Smooth 90Hz display refresh rate
  • Capable 50MP main camera sensor

What is not

  • High levels of bloatware and ads
  • Limited remaining software update life
  • Low resolution 5MP selfie camera

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a basic device with fast charging for daily tasks and light social media use.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you want long-term software updates or a device capable of handling demanding games.

Why we say that

Living with this phone means managing a device that is now 3 years old, which significantly limits its future software support. While the 90Hz display makes scrolling feel smooth, the Unisoc Tiger T612 chipset is entry-level and will struggle with heavy tasks. Users often report frustration with the medium to high levels of bloatware and advertisements pre-installed on the Realme UI T software.

You should be aware that the 5MP selfie camera produces basic results, and the peak brightness of 560 nits may be difficult to view under direct sunlight. It is a functional tool for basic communication and light usage, but it is not built for long-term performance or heavy gaming. If you choose this device, you are prioritizing immediate charging speed over long-term software security.
