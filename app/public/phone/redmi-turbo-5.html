Is the Redmi Turbo 5 worth buying?

What is good

  • 7540 mAh battery with 100 W fast charging
  • 6.59‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED with 3500 nits peak brightness
  • Dimensity 8500 Ultra delivers flagship‑level performance
  • Solid metal frame with IP68/69 durability

What is not

  • China ROM lacks Google services and Bangla
  • Camera quality is mediocre and over‑processed
  • Software shows notification delays and heavy bloatware
  • Device can get warm under heavy load

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize long gaming sessions, fast charging and a bright display and can accept a Chinese ROM and no official warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a reliable camera, official brand warranty, or a smooth, ad‑free software experience.

Why we say that

Its 7540 mAh battery, rated 9.8 by owners, lasts through heavy use and the 100 W charger refills it in minutes. The Dimensity 8500 Ultra chipset gives flagship‑level speed, and the 6.59‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED shines with up to 3500 nits, making outdoor viewing easy. The metal frame feels premium and the IP68/69 rating protects against water and dust.

However, the phone ships with a China‑only HyperOS ROM that lacks Google services and Bangla support, and users report persistent notification delays and a lot of pre‑installed ads. The 50 MP sensor produces over‑processed photos and struggles in low light, and the device can become warm during intensive gaming, with some titles not reaching high FPS. There is no manufacturer warranty; only the shop’s limited coverage applies, so verify IMEI status before purchase.
