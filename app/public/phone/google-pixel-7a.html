Why we say that

The phone runs Android 13 smoothly on the Tensor G2 chip and offers a bright 6.1 inch OLED panel with 90Hz refresh. The 64MP main camera with optical image stabilization captures good low‑light photos, and the clean stock Android experience stays free of bloat. Owners praise the ease of use and overall speed, but they complain that the 4385 mAh battery drains quickly, often needing two to four charges a day.

There is no official warranty in Bangladesh, so any repair depends on the shop that sold you the device. Being three years old, the phone has only a few software updates left. Consider the trade‑off between price, camera quality and the need for extra charging.

Google Pixel 7a: what buyers ask us

What is the Google Pixel 7a price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Google Pixel 7a in Bangladesh was ৳46,999, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

How long will the Pixel 7a receive Android updates?

The phone launched in 2023 with Android 13 and is promised up to five major Android upgrades, reaching Android 16. Because it is already three years old, only a couple of update cycles remain.

Is there an official warranty for the Pixel 7a in Bangladesh?

The phone has no brand backed warranty in Bangladesh. All listings are gray import units, so you rely on the shop warranty and must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

How is the battery life for daily use?

Owners say the 4385 mAh battery does not last a full day under normal use. Many charge the phone two to four times daily and notice capacity dropping after about a year.

Can the phone handle gaming well?

The Pixel 7a scores 6.5 for gaming and 8.5 for performance, thanks to 8 GB RAM and the Tensor G2 chip. It runs games smoothly but the modest battery may limit long sessions.
