Is the Google Pixel 7a worth buying?

What is good

  • 64MP main camera with good low‑light results
  • 90 Hz OLED screen with solid brightness
  • Google Tensor G2 delivers smooth performance
  • Stock Android with no bloatware

What is not

  • Battery drains quickly, needs multiple charges daily
  • Only shop warranty, no official Google service
  • Software updates may end soon
  • Build not as rugged as flagship

Buy it if Buy it if you want a capable camera and clean Android experience at a mid‑range price and can accept limited warranty and battery endurance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need long battery life or official after‑sales support.

Why we say that

The phone feels fast thanks to the Google Tensor G2 and 8 GB of RAM, and the 64 MP main sensor captures impressive low‑light photos. The 6.1‑inch OLED panel runs at 90 Hz, offering good brightness and smooth scrolling for most apps. Stock Android means no bloatware and regular security patches, and the device supports 5G.

However, the gray‑import unit comes only with a shop warranty, and Google has little official service presence in Bangladesh. Build quality is decent but not as rugged as flagship models. At three years old, the remaining software‑update window is limited, so future Android upgrades may stop soon.

Overall, it is a solid mid‑range phone if you can tolerate frequent charging and limited after‑sales support.
