Is the Tecno Spark Go worth buying?

What is good

  • Large 6000 mAh battery
  • Smooth 120Hz IPS LCD
  • 5G connectivity
  • Includes headphone jack and IR blaster

What is not

  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • Slow software performance
  • Slow 18W charging
  • No official brand warranty

Buy it if You need a 5G phone with a big battery and a high refresh rate screen.

Skip it if You want a clean software experience without ads or a manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

You get a large 6000 mAh battery and a 120Hz screen for ৳18,990. The Dimensity 6400 chipset provides 5G connectivity. However, the software experience is a major pain point.

Owners deal with high levels of bloatware and ads. The HiOS interface is slow. You can expect 1 to 2 OS updates.

Since these are gray imports, you only get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty. This means you are relying on the seller for any repairs.
