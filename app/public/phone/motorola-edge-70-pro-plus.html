Buying verdict

Motorola Edge 70 Pro Plus Is it worth buying?

The Edge 70 Pro Plus gives you a massive battery and a very bright 144 Hz screen, but you must accept a gray‑import warranty and occasional gaming hiccups.

Choose it if
You want a high‑end display, big battery and strong camera and can live with a shop‑provided warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable long‑session gaming or an official brand warranty.

What works well

  • 6500 mAh battery with 10 hour screen‑on time
  • 5200 nits peak brightness on 144 Hz AMOLED
  • 12 GB RAM with Dimensity 8500 Extreme chipset
  • 50 MP periscope camera with 3.5× zoom

Reported trade-offs

  • No official Motorola warranty in Bangladesh
  • Gaming performance drops after a few minutes
  • Netflix HDR badge missing
  • Software updates may be limited

Why we say that

Daily use feels smooth thanks to 12 GB RAM and the Dimensity 8500 Extreme chipset, and the 50 MP periscope camera delivers good zoom and selfie quality. The 6.78‑inch AMOLED stays bright even in sunlight with a 5200‑nit peak, and the 90 W charger refills the 6500 mAh cell quickly. Heat is well managed by a vapor chamber, so most tasks stay cool.

Owners praise the long screen‑on time and the sleek 7.2 mm build, yet they note that sustained gaming frames drop to 25‑30 FPS and the Netflix HDR badge is missing. Software is near‑stock Hello UI, but future Android updates may be limited to three major versions. Overall it feels premium, but the lack of official support and gaming instability are real trade‑offs.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

How long does the battery last in real use?

The phone uses a 6500 mAh silicon‑carbon cell that gives more than ten hours of screen‑on time, earning a 9.4 battery rating from owners. It also supports fast 90 W wired and 15 W wireless charging.

Will this phone handle gaming well?

Daily tasks run smoothly, but sustained gaming is weak. Owners report 3DMark stability at only 45 percent and frame rates dropping to 25‑30 FPS, which reflects the 6.5 gaming rating.

Is the camera good in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS performs well in low light, and the periscope zoom is praised. Some users notice processing lag and shutter delay, giving it a 7.7 camera rating.

Does it come with an official warranty and how many Android updates?

All units are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s warranty applies and repairs are paid. Motorola promises up to three major Android upgrades, but users are wary of long‑term update support.
