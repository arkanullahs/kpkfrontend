Is the Motorola Edge 70 Pro Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • 6500 mAh battery with 10 hour screen‑on time
  • 5200 nits peak brightness on AMOLED display
  • 90 W wired and 15 W wireless charging
  • 12 GB RAM with Dimensity 8500 Extreme

What is not

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Sustained gaming performance drops to 25‑30 FPS
  • Netflix HDR badge missing due to software bug
  • Camera processing lag in low‑light scenes

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large battery, very bright high‑refresh display and fast charging and can live with a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable long‑term gaming performance or an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Daily use feels smooth thanks to 12 GB RAM and the Dimensity 8500 Extreme chipset, and the 6.78‑inch screen stays vivid even in direct sunlight with 5200 nits peak brightness. Charging is quick with 90 W wired and 15 W wireless support, so you rarely wait long for a refill. The 50 MP main sensor and periscope zoom produce good photos and selfies, though some users notice processing lag and slower shutter response.

The phone stays cool under most workloads because of a vapor chamber, but sustained gaming sessions see frame drops to 25‑30 FPS and a low 3DMark stability score. Software is close to stock Hello UI and promises up to three Android upgrades, yet many owners doubt long‑term update reliability. Overall it feels premium, but the lack of official warranty and gaming issues are real trade‑offs.
