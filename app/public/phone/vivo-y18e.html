Buying verdict

Vivo Y18e Is it worth buying?

The Vivo Y18e is a budget device that requires significant compromises for its ৳12,990 price tag.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a basic smartphone for light tasks and prioritize having a headphone jack and FM radio on a tight budget.
Consider alternatives if
You play games, require fast charging, or need the security of a fingerprint scanner and official brand warranty support.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Smooth 90Hz IPS LCD display
  • Large 5000 mAh battery capacity
  • Includes a dedicated headphone jack
  • Features built-in FM radio

Reported trade-offs4

  • No fingerprint reader included
  • Extremely slow charging speeds
  • Poor gaming and general performance
  • Only gray-import stock available

Why we say that

Owners report a frustrating experience due to the complete absence of a fingerprint reader, which is a major drawback for daily security and convenience. While the 90Hz display offers a smoother visual experience than standard screens, the overall performance is rated poorly by users, particularly for gaming. You will also face very long charging times for the 5000 mAh battery.

The software experience includes a medium amount of bloatware and ads, which can clutter your interface. Because only gray-import stock is currently available, you must rely on shop warranties rather than brand support. Always verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before finalizing your purchase to ensure the device is legitimate.

Does the Vivo Y18e support official warranty in Bangladesh?

How is the gaming performance on this device?

Gaming performance is rated quite low by actual owners, scoring only 3.5 out of 10. The Helio G85 chipset struggles with demanding tasks, so it is not recommended for users who prioritize mobile gaming.

How many years of software updates will this phone receive?

This device runs on Android 14 with Funtouch 14. While some upper models receive three OS updates, this budget model is expected to receive two OS updates during its lifecycle.

Is the battery life good for daily use?

Owners have given the battery a rating of 5.5 out of 10. While it features a large 5000 mAh capacity, the charging process takes a very long time, which may impact your daily convenience.
