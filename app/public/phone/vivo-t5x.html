What works well

  • 7200 mAh battery lasts two days
  • 1200 nit bright LCD works in sunlight
  • Dimensity 7400 Turbo gives solid performance
  • IP68/69 water and dust protection

Reported trade-offs

  • LCD not AMOLED, colors less vibrant
  • 44 W charging slow for large battery
  • BGMI frame drops to 30 fps
  • Heavy 219 g chassis feels bulky

Why we say that

The 7200 mAh cell delivers more than nine hours of screen‑on time and even supports reverse charging, which owners praise. Performance feels solid thanks to the Dimensity 7400 Turbo and 8 GB RAM, and everyday apps run without hiccups. The 6.76‑inch IPS LCD reaches 1200 nits, so outdoor visibility is excellent, though the lack of an AMOLED panel means colors are less punchy.

Build quality is rugged with IP68/69 protection, but the 219 g chassis feels bulky in the hand. Gaming such as BGMI can hit 60 fps but often drops to 30 fps, which some gamers notice. The 50 MP main sensor is acceptable in daylight but struggles in low light and has no OIS or ultrawide lens.

Will the Vivo T5x run games like BGMI smoothly?
