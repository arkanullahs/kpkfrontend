Is the Vivo T5x worth buying?

What is good

  • 7200 mAh battery lasts multiple days
  • Dimensity 7400 Turbo gives solid performance
  • 50 MP main camera works well in daylight
  • 44 W fast charging refills quickly

What is not

  • Heavy and thick body at 219 grams
  • IPS LCD panel, not AMOLED
  • Bloatware and ad notifications
  • Inconsistent gaming frame rates

Buy it if If you need long battery life and solid daily performance on a tight budget, this phone fits your needs.

Skip it if If you prioritize a lightweight design, AMOLED screen, or a clean software experience, look elsewhere.

Why we say that

The 7200 mAh cell paired with 44 W fast charging keeps you online even with heavy daily use. The Dimensity 7400 Turbo chipset delivers snappy performance for apps and casual gaming, though frame rates can dip during intense sessions. The 6.76‑inch IPS LCD offers a bright 1200 nit display, but the lack of an AMOLED panel feels cheap at this price.

Camera results are acceptable in daylight thanks to the 50 MP sensor, yet the lack of ultra‑wide lens and optical image stabilization hurts versatility and low‑light shots. The phone feels solid with an IP68/69 rating but its 219‑gram weight and thick chassis make it heavy in hand. OriginOS 6 brings many features but is cluttered with bloatware and ad pop‑ups that need manual disabling.

Warranty is limited to shop coverage for gray‑import units, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
