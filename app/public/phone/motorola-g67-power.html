Motorola G67 Power Is it worth buying?

If you need a massive battery and a 5G phone on a tight budget, the Motorola G67 Power can keep you online for days.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value long battery life and 5G connectivity over premium camera quality.

What works well4

  • 7,000 mAh battery lasting multiple days
  • Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 handles daily apps smoothly
  • 120 Hz IPS LCD is bright and responsive
  • Headphone jack for wired audio

Reported trade-offs4

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Camera often over‑saturated and HDR weak
  • Thick bottom chin looks bulky
  • Rapid battery drain reported by some users

Why we say that

The 7,000 mAh cell delivers up to two days of moderate use and the 30 W charger refills it in a reasonable time. The Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 chipset feels stable for apps, social media and casual games, and the 6.7‑inch 120 Hz IPS LCD reaches 1,050 nits at peak, so outdoor visibility is good. Build quality feels solid with vegan‑leather finish, but the design repeats older Motorola looks and the bottom chin is noticeably thick.

The 50 MP main sensor captures detail but often produces over‑saturated colors and weak HDR, especially in low light. Some owners report occasional rapid drain that shortens the impressive endurance. The phone arrives as a gray‑import unit built for India, so there is no official Motorola warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s limited guarantee applies.

How long will the battery actually last in daily use?

The 7,000 mAh cell can last around two days with moderate screen time and mixed apps. Heavy gaming or bright‑screen video will reduce that time, but most users report a full day without needing a charge.

Will the phone handle gaming without overheating?

Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 stays cool during casual games and runs them smoothly. It is not built for intensive AAA titles, but for most mobile games the device remains comfortable to hold.

Is the camera usable in low‑light situations?

The 50 MP sensor captures detail, but reviewers note over‑saturation and weak HDR, especially when light is limited. Low‑light photos may look bright but lack natural color balance.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

The phone is a gray‑import unit, so there is no official Motorola warranty in Bangladesh. Shops we track offer their own limited warranty and repairs are paid. It ships with Android 15 and is promised an upgrade to Android 16, but update frequency is slower than many competitors.
