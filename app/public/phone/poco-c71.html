Buying verdict

POCO C71 Is it worth buying?

The POCO C71 is a budget device that struggles to deliver a functional experience for daily tasks.

Choose it if
Buy this phone only if you are a very light user who needs a basic device for simple tasks and can accept the risks of a gray-import unit.
Consider alternatives if
You play games, value software features, or expect a reliable device that will receive future Android updates.

What works well

  • Reliable one-day battery endurance
  • Large 6.88 inch display
  • Premium dual-tone plastic finish
  • Includes headphone jack and FM radio

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe lagging and poor gaming performance
  • Slow 15W charging speed
  • Stripped Android Go software features
  • No manufacturer warranty support

Why we say that

While the 5200 mAh battery offers reliable one-day endurance for light users, the device is plagued by severe performance degradation and software limitations. Owners report heavy lagging even in basic games, with the eMMC storage acting as a significant bottleneck. The display is large but suffers from low resolution and inconsistent 120Hz smoothness.

You will find the software experience stripped of features like App Lock and Second Space, and users have expressed frustration over the likely cancellation of promised Android updates. The 15W charging is tedious for such a large battery, and the audio volume is disappointingly low. Because this is a gray-import unit, you receive no manufacturer warranty, meaning any repairs are paid out of pocket.

Read full reasoning

Given that the newer POCO C85 is available for a similar price, this model is difficult to recommend for anyone seeking a reliable smartphone.

Does the POCO C71 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit found in the market is a gray import, meaning you must rely on the shop for any coverage and pay for all repairs yourself.

Is the POCO C71 suitable for gaming?

The phone is not suitable for gaming. Owners report heavy lagging even in basic games, and the eMMC storage creates a bottleneck that leads to the worst performance many users have ever experienced.

How long will the POCO C71 receive software updates?

While there were initial promises of two major Android upgrades, owners report a betrayal regarding software support. It is likely that the promised updates have been cancelled, and the device will not receive Android 16 or 17.

How is the battery life on this device?

The 5200 mAh battery provides reliable one-day endurance for light users. However, the charging process is quite slow, as the 15W charger is tedious for a battery of this size.
