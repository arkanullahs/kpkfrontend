Buying verdict

Infinix Hot 70 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Infinix Hot 70 Pro offers a massive battery and unique hardware features, but it faces significant criticism from owners regarding its display technology and software experience.

Choose it if
You need a phone with exceptional battery life and unique hardware features like an IR blaster and active matrix rear display.
Consider alternatives if
You value a high-quality AMOLED display, clean software without ads, or stereo sound for your media consumption.

What works well

  • Massive 6000 mAh battery endurance
  • Rare IP68 rating for budget
  • Smooth 144Hz refresh rate
  • Capable Dimensity 7100 gaming performance

Reported trade-offs

  • Disappointing IPS LCD screen
  • Heavy bloatware and notification ads
  • Single mono speaker
  • Struggles in low light photography

Why we say that

While the 6000 mAh battery provides excellent endurance, users are frustrated by the choice of an IPS LCD screen instead of an AMOLED panel, with some reports noting a 720p resolution. The Dimensity 7100 chipset handles gaming reasonably well, though the software experience is marred by heavy bloatware and persistent notification ads. Owners have expressed strong backlash regarding the pricing, especially given the inclusion of a mono speaker.

While the build quality is notable for its IP68 rating and active matrix rear display, the overall user experience is hampered by the display limitations and software clutter. If you prioritize battery life and unique hardware over screen quality and clean software, this device might suit your needs, but many buyers find the current price point difficult to justify.

Infinix Hot 70 Pro costs ৳34,299 in Bangladesh on unofficial listings, in stock at 3 shops we track as of 11 Sep 2026. The official price starts at ৳36,999.

Does the Infinix Hot 70 Pro have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

The official unit comes with a brand warranty and costs ৳36,999. Cheaper listings at ৳34,299 are gray imports that do not include manufacturer cover, and service centres will only accept these units for paid repairs.

How long will the Infinix Hot 70 Pro receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 16 and is promised up to 3 major Android upgrades. However, users should note that the XOS software updates are generally slow and remain heavily packed with preloaded apps and notification ads.

Is the Infinix Hot 70 Pro good for gaming?

The Dimensity 7100 chipset handles games like PUBG and COD well. While Free Fire can reach 90fps, owners report that the frame rate fluctuates during gameplay. The device stays relatively cool and only gets slightly warm under heavy loads.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The 50MP main camera uses a Sony IMX882 sensor that takes sharp photos in daylight. However, the camera struggles significantly when shooting in low light environments, which is a common point of concern for users.
