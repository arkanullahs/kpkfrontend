Is the Infinix Hot 70 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 144 Hz display feels smooth
  • 50 MP main camera captures detail
  • 5G connectivity works reliably

What is not

  • XOS UI is loaded with ads
  • Software updates are slow
  • Low‑light camera performance is noisy

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large battery, fast screen and 5G without paying a premium.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a clean Android experience or top‑tier camera quality.

Why we say that

The 6.76‑inch IPS LCD reaches 950 nits, so it stays readable outdoors even in bright sunlight. The Dimensity 7100 paired with 8 GB RAM handles most apps and games smoothly, though heavy titles may show occasional frame drops. The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture captures detailed photos in good light, but low‑light scenes can be noisy.

The phone runs Android 16 with XOS 16, and it will receive up to three major Android upgrades, but the update pace is slow and the UI is loaded with preinstalled apps and notification ads. 5G works reliably and the extra headphone jack, FM radio and IR blaster add practical value. Owners appreciate the long battery and smooth display, but they often complain about the cluttered software and the modest low‑light camera performance.
