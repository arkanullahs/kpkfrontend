OPPO A17k Is it worth buying?

If you only need a cheap phone for calls and occasional apps, the OPPO A17k will work, but expect many compromises.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a very cheap phone for basic calls, messaging and light apps and can accept no warranty and slow performance.
Consider alternatives if
You want smooth gaming, reliable performance, official warranty or regular software updates.

What works well4

  • 600‑nit IPS LCD display
  • 5,000 mAh battery capacity
  • headphone jack for wired audio
  • very low price

Reported trade-offs4

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Very slow charging, full charge takes about ten hours
  • Frequent overheating and lag during games
  • Basic camera, poor low‑light performance

Why we say that

At ৳11,990 it offers a 6.56 inch IPS LCD that reaches 600 nits, a 5,000 mAh battery and a headphone jack. The Helio G35 chipset with 3 GB RAM struggles with multitasking and games, often lagging or crashing. Charging is very slow, taking around ten hours for a full charge, and the battery drains quickly despite its size.

The 8 MP rear camera produces basic pictures and does not perform well in low light. The device runs Android 12 with ColorOS 12.1, which includes ads and bloatware. Overheating is reported during heavy use.

Read full reasoning

Because it is a gray‑import, there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh and software updates are unlikely after four years of age.
