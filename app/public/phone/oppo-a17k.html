Is the OPPO A17k worth buying?

What is good

  • Sturdy build quality
  • Good display
  • Includes a headphone jack

What is not

  • Frequent lagging and crashes
  • Very slow charging speeds
  • Quick battery drain
  • Ads and bloatware

Buy it if You only need a basic phone for light use and value a sturdy build.

Skip it if You want to play games or need a battery that charges quickly.

Why we say that

Owners report significant lagging and crashes, with some stating the screen goes black during games. The software experience is hindered by ads and bloatware. While the build is described as sturdy and the display is good, the internal performance is weak.

Battery life is a major pain point as users report it drains quickly and charges very slowly. One owner mentioned a full charge can take 10 hours. Since this phone was released in 2022, it is now 4 years old.

You should expect very little to no remaining software update life. It is a basic device that struggles to keep up with modern app demands.
