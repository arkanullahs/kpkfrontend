Vivo V80 Lite 5G Is it worth buying?

The Vivo V80 Lite 5G will keep you powered all day, but its camera and performance feel cheap for the 60 k taka price tag.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a massive battery, a bright high‑refresh screen, and can tolerate modest camera and performance for a mid‑range price.
Consider alternatives if
You expect flagship‑level camera, fast charging, or a budget‑friendly price.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 10,000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 2000‑nit 120 Hz AMOLED display
  • IP68/IP69 water and dust resistance
  • IR blaster for remote control

Reported trade-offs4

  • Camera quality is mediocre for the price
  • 44 W charging is slow for such a large battery
  • No microSD slot limits storage expansion
  • Performance feels underpowered for a 60 k taka phone

Why we say that

The 10,000 mAh battery truly acts like a power bank and the 2000‑nit 120 Hz AMOLED panel is bright and smooth. However, the 44 W charger struggles to fill such a large cell, and the 50 MP sensor does not deliver sharp or versatile photos, especially in low light. The Dimensity 7300e chipset is efficient but underwhelming for a device in this price range, making gaming and heavy apps feel sluggish.

Build quality is solid with IP68/IP69 protection, but the phone is heavy and lacks a microSD slot. Official warranty is only available on the listed official price, while cheaper gray imports have no manufacturer cover. Owners feel the overall value is far below expectations.
