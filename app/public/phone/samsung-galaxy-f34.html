What works well

  • Bright 120Hz Super AMOLED display
  • Large 6000 mAh efficient battery
  • Main camera with OIS
  • Includes a headphone jack

Reported trade-offs

  • No official brand warranty
  • Poor gaming performance
  • Reports of display lines and short circuits
  • Limited remaining software updates

Why we say that

You get a strong 6000 mAh battery and a bright 120Hz Super AMOLED screen that owners generally like. However, the build quality is rated poorly and some owners report serious issues like short circuits and lines appearing on the display. Performance is mediocre, especially for gaming.

Since the phone was released in 2023, you should expect very little software update life remaining. You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller because these are not official units. If you buy this, you only get a shop warranty, which may not cover the display or internal faults reported by other users.

What is the Samsung Galaxy F34 5G price in Bangladesh?

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there are no confirmed official units in stock. The listings we track are gray imports, which means you only get a shop warranty instead of brand backed support.

How is the battery life for daily use?

The 6000 mAh battery is described as efficient. One owner reported that charging from 20 to 80 percent allowed them to use the phone as a hotspot for 10 to 12 hours.

Is this a good phone for gaming?

Gaming is one of the weakest points of this device. Owners have given it a low rating of 5.5 out of 10 for gaming performance.

How long will I get software updates?

The phone was released in 2023. Because it is now 3 years old, you should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point.
