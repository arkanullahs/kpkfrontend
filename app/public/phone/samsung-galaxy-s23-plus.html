Why we say that

Owners praise the brightness and colors of the screen, and the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset handles gaming well. However, you should be aware of serious reports regarding pink lines appearing on the display. Many users also report that the device overheats and throttles hard under extended load.

Since only gray imports are available, you will not get brand backed support. You must rely on shop warranties and verify the IMEI and BTRC status yourself. The device is now 3 years old, which means the remaining software update life is likely short.

Samsung Galaxy S23 Plus 5G: what buyers ask us

What is the Samsung Galaxy S23 Plus 5G price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Samsung Galaxy S23 Plus 5G in Bangladesh was ৳79,990, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No official brand backed warranty is available for this phone. The listings we track are gray imports, so you will only get a shop warranty. You should confirm the BTRC status and IMEI with the seller before buying.

How is the gaming performance and heating?

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 provides good performance for gaming. However, it is widely reported to overheat during prolonged use. Some owners say the chipset throttles fast and hard under extended load.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The phone launched with Android 13 and supports up to 4 major Android upgrades. Because the device is now 3 years old, you should expect short or no remaining software update life.

Is the battery life and charging speed good?

The 4700 mAh battery has good endurance due to the efficient 4nm chip. The 45W charging is available, but reviews suggest it is not much faster than 25W charging.
