Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy A04s Is it worth buying?

If you need a cheap phone that can survive a day‑to‑day routine without dying, the Galaxy A04s will do that, but expect sluggish performance and limited durability.

Choose it if
You only need basic apps, calls and occasional photos.
Consider alternatives if
You want smooth gaming, heavy multitasking or a reliable warranty.

What works well

  • 5000 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • 6.5‑inch 90 Hz PLS LCD screen
  • 50 MP main camera captures sharp daylight photos
  • Headphone jack for wired audio

Reported trade-offs

  • Exynos 850 cannot handle demanding games
  • 15 W charger fills battery slowly
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Build feels cheap and may dent

Why we say that

The 5000 mAh battery easily reaches two days for light use, while the 6.5‑inch 90 Hz screen feels smooth despite its 720p resolution. Daylight photos are sharp thanks to the 50 MP sensor, but low‑light shots lack detail and stability. The Exynos 850 processor and modest RAM cause frequent lag, especially in games and heavy apps.

Charging at 15 W is slow, taking over two hours for a full charge. Build quality is glossy plastic that may dent over time, and there is no official warranty in Bangladesh, only the shop’s limited promise.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

How long does the battery last on the Galaxy A04s?

The 5000 mAh battery typically lasts about two days for light users and a full day with moderate use. Owners report excellent endurance, but heavy streaming or gaming will reduce that time.

Can I play games smoothly on this phone?

The Exynos 850 chipset and 3‑4 GB RAM cause severe lag in most games. Reviewers describe frequent stutters and poor frame rates, so smooth gaming is not realistic.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor is sharp in daylight but lacks night mode and stabilization. In low light images become noisy and blurry, so the camera is not reliable after dark.

Is there an official warranty for the Galaxy A04s in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray imports, so the phone has no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop may offer its own limited cover, but any repair will be paid by the owner.
