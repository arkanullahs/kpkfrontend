Samsung Galaxy A04s Is it worth buying?

The Samsung Galaxy A04s is a dated device that struggles with basic tasks, making it a difficult recommendation at ৳19,990.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You only need a basic device for light tasks and prioritize long battery life over performance.
Consider alternatives if
You want a smooth experience, play games, or care about getting the best value for your money.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Excellent battery endurance for light users
  • Smooth 90Hz display refresh rate
  • Solid sharpness in daylight photos
  • Includes a convenient headphone jack

Reported trade-offs4

  • Severe UI lag and frequent slowdowns
  • Inadequate performance for gaming
  • Slow 15W charging speed
  • Poor display brightness and resolution

Why we say that

Owners frequently report severe UI lag and constant stutters, as the Exynos 850 chipset is simply inadequate for modern multitasking. While the 5000 mAh battery offers excellent endurance, the hardware is held back by a poor 720p display that lacks an ambient light sensor for automatic brightness. The build quality is glossy plastic with reports of poor long-term durability.

You will also face slow 15W charging that takes over 2 hours to complete. Because this model was released in 2022, you should expect very short or no remaining software update life. Most importantly, the newer Samsung Galaxy A05s is available for ৳14,490, which provides significantly better value.

Read full reasoning

Given the lack of manufacturer warranty on these unofficial imports, you are paying a premium for an aging phone that performs poorly compared to newer alternatives.

Does this phone come with an official Samsung warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is an unofficial gray import. It does not have any manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh. Any repairs are paid, and you must rely solely on whatever limited promise the specific shop provides.

Can I play heavy games on the Galaxy A04s?

No, gaming performance is very poor. Owners rate the gaming experience at 2.5 out of 10. The Exynos 850 chipset is inadequate for heavy tasks, and you should expect severe lag and frequent stutters during use.

How is the camera performance in low light conditions?

The camera lacks a dedicated night mode and stabilization. While it provides solid sharpness in daylight, it struggles significantly in low light environments. You should not expect high quality results when shooting in dark conditions.

Will this phone receive future software updates?

This phone was released in 2022 and is now four years old. You should expect very short or no remaining software update life at this point. It currently runs Android 12 and is upgradable to Android 14.
