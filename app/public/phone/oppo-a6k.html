OPPO A6k Is it worth buying?

The OPPO A6k will keep you powered all day but will frustrate you with its sluggish charging and modest performance.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need long battery life and a cheap 5G phone and can tolerate slow charging and modest performance.
Consider alternatives if
You expect flagship‑level speed, sharp display, or strong camera performance.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Huge 7,000 mAh battery
  • 120 Hz smooth display
  • Headphone jack
  • ColorOS 15 with useful AI

Reported trade-offs4

  • Very slow 33 W charging
  • Average 50 MP camera
  • Low‑resolution LCD panel
  • Performance lags in heavy apps

Why we say that

Its 7,000 mAh battery truly lasts through heavy use, yet the 33 W charger needs two to two and a half hours for a full top‑up. The 6.75‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, giving smooth motion, but the resolution falls short of Full HD+ and looks soft. The Dimensity 6300 chipset manages everyday tasks but shows lag when multitasking or gaming, and owners report hangs after just ten days.

The 50 MP main camera delivers average results and the 8 MP selfie shooter is weak, especially in low light. ColorOS 15 on Android 15 offers useful AI features and a simple mode, though medium bloat and ads are present. Overall, the phone feels overpriced for the hardware, and a newer OPPO A17k is cheaper and better equipped.
