Is the Tecno Camon 50 Ultra 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Long lasting 6160 mAh battery
  • Fast 45W charging
  • Strong 50MP main camera

What is not

  • Slippery premium look feels cheap
  • Significant heat on long voice calls
  • HiOS 16 is cluttered with ads

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large bright display, solid battery and decent main camera without needing top gaming performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a phone that stays cool during calls or runs games smoothly.

Why we say that

Its 6.78 inch AMOLED panel is vivid and smooth, while the 6160 mAh cell provides exceptional endurance praised by owners. The 50MP main camera with optical image stabilization captures clear photos, though the telephoto lens can be inconsistent. Performance is adequate for daily tasks, but gaming is not a strong point and heat may build up during long voice calls.

The HiOS 16 software is feature rich yet filled with ads and bloat, making the experience feel cluttered. Build feels premium but the surface is slippery and the vibration motor feels cheap. Weight feels heavier than claimed.

Overall it offers good specs for the price, but you should be aware of heating and software annoyances before buying.
