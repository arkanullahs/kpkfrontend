Buying verdict

Tecno Camon 50 Ultra 5G Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can survive a full day of heavy use, the Camon 50 Ultra’s 6160 mAh battery is the strongest point.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large bright screen and long battery at a mid‑range price and can accept some software clutter.
Consider alternatives if
You need top tier gaming performance or a completely clean software experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 6160 mAh battery lasts over a day
  • 6.78 inch AMOLED display with 144 Hz refresh
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • Dimensity 7400 Ultimate chipset

Reported trade-offs4

  • UFS 2.2 storage slower than newer standards
  • Heavy bloatware and ads
  • Overheating on long voice calls
  • Gaming performance limited

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch AMOLED panel delivers bright, 144 Hz visuals that stay comfortable for long sessions. The Dimensity 7400oint Ultimate chipset handles everyday apps without lag, but the UFS 2.2 storage feels slower than newer standards and gaming performance tapers on demanding titles. The 50 MP main camera with OIS produces sharp photos in good light, yet the telephoto is only average and the ultrawide is basic.

Owners praise the premium look and IP68/69K rating, but note the plastic frame can be slippery and the phone may heat up during extended voice calls. Software includes useful AI features but is weighed down by heavy bloatware and ads. Overall the device offers a high hardware‑to‑price ratio for those who accept its software quirks.
