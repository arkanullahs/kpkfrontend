Motorola G96 Is it worth buying?

If you want a large, bright screen and a solid main camera without paying for a brand warranty, the Motorola G96 gray-import fits the bill.

What works well4

  • 144 Hz P‑OLED display with 1600 nits peak
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • IP68 water and dust resistance
  • 5500 mAh battery lasting a full day

Reported trade-offs3

  • 33 W charging is slower than rivals
  • Software bugs cause lag and brightness spikes
  • Flash produces dark patches in low light

Why we say that

The 6.67-inch 144 Hz P‑OLED panel reaches 1600 nits peak, making videos and games look vivid even under sunlight. Battery life is respectable; the 5500 mAh cell usually lasts a full day of mixed use, but the 33 W charger refills slowly compared with rivals. The 50 MP primary sensor with OIS delivers sharp daylight photos, yet the flash often creates dark spots and software processing feels uneven in low light.

Performance is adequate for everyday tasks, but gaming stalls at 60 fps and occasional lag appears, matching the modest 6.2 performance rating. Android 15 runs a near‑stock UI, but users report brightness spikes, UI lag, and a weak update promise of only one major Android upgrade. Because every listing is a gray import, there is no official Motorola warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited coverage and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 5500 mAh cell gives you roughly a full day of mixed use, and many owners report it easily reaches 10‑12 hours of screen‑on time. Charging at 33 W is slower than rivals, so a full charge takes about two hours.

Can I play modern games smoothly?

The Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 and 8 GB RAM handle everyday apps well, but demanding games often cap at 60 fps and show occasional lag. Owners rate gaming performance around 5.8 out of 10, so expect decent but not flagship smoothness.

How does the camera perform in low light and with flash?

The 50 MP sensor with OIS captures clear shots in daylight and moderate low light, earning a 6.8 rating. However users notice dark patches when using the flash, and software processing can be inconsistent, especially in very dark scenes.

Will I get official warranty and future Android updates?

All units are gray‑imported from India, so there is no Motorola brand warranty in Bangladesh. Shops we track offer their own limited warranty and any repair costs are paid. Motorola promises only one major Android upgrade, which many owners find insufficient.
