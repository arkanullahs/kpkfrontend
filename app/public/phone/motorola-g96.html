Is the Motorola G96 worth buying?

What is good

  • Superb 5500 mAh battery endurance
  • High quality 144Hz P-OLED display
  • Near-stock Hello UI experience
  • Capable 50MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Only one major Android upgrade
  • Critical system bugs and lagging
  • No 3.5mm jack or SD slot
  • Poor gaming optimization

Buy it if Buy this phone only if you prioritize a large battery and a high refresh rate display above all other concerns.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you want reliable software, long-term update support, or a stable gaming experience.

Why we say that

While the 5500 mAh battery provides superb endurance for heavy daily use, the overall experience is marred by critical system bugs including brightness spikes, mobile data drops, and calendar alarm failures. Owners are particularly frustrated by the limited software support, as the phone only receives one major Android upgrade. Performance is inconsistent, with many users reporting lagging and poor optimization during gaming sessions.

The curved P-OLED display is high quality, but the lack of a 3.5mm headphone jack and SD card slot limits its utility. Because this is currently only available through unofficial gray-import channels, you will rely on shop warranties rather than official brand support. With the newer Motorola G100 available for 26,490 taka, this model is difficult to recommend for most buyers.

Does the Motorola G96 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, the Motorola G96 is currently only available through unofficial gray-import channels. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, so you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

How long will the Motorola G96 receive Android updates?

The software support for this device is limited to only one major Android upgrade. Many owners have expressed anger regarding this policy, describing the lack of long-term updates as ridiculous for a phone released in 2025.

Is the Motorola G96 good for heavy gaming?

Gaming performance is mixed and generally considered poor by owners. Users report significant lagging and poor optimization compared to competing devices, resulting in a low owner rating of 5.8 for gaming.

How does the battery perform during a full day of use?

The 5500 mAh battery is a standout feature of this phone. Owners report superb endurance, noting that the device easily lasts through a full day of heavy use without needing a charge.
