OPPO Reno 15 Is it worth buying?

If you value a phone that can run all day without charging, the OPPO Reno 15 delivers that with its massive 6500 mAh battery.

Choose it if

What works well

  • Huge 6500 mAh battery lasts all day
  • Bright 1200 nit AMOLED panel with 120 Hz
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS and 3.5× tele
  • Premium aluminum frame with IP68/69 rating

Reported trade-offs

  • Mid‑range chipset throttles in demanding games
  • ColorOS ships with noticeable bloatware
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

The 6.59‑inch AMOLED panel reaches 1200 nits and refreshes at 120 Hz, making everyday use feel smooth and bright. The 50 MP main sensor with OIS and 3.5× telephoto works well for portraits and vlogging, and the premium aluminum frame carries an IP68/69 rating for dust and water resistance. However, the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 is a mid‑range chip that throttles under heavy gaming loads, and owners report noticeable frame drops in titles like Genshin Impact.

ColorOS 16 runs fine but is cluttered with pre‑installed bloatware, and the device arrives as a gray‑import with only a shop‑provided warranty, not an official brand guarantee. Overall, it feels solid for media consumption and photography, but it falls short for power users who need top gaming performance or official after‑sales support.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 6500 mAh battery is rated 9.4 out of 10 for endurance and can easily last more than a full day of mixed usage, including browsing, video playback and moderate gaming, without needing a recharge.

Can the OPPO Reno 15 run heavy games like Genshin Impact smoothly?

Owners report that the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 struggles with demanding titles. In Genshin Impact the frame rate often falls below 50 fps due to thermal throttling, so it is not ideal for heavy mobile gaming.

Is the camera good for low‑light photos and vlogging?

The 50 MP sensor with f/1.8 aperture, OIS and a 3.5× telephoto lens performs well for portraits and vlogging. Low‑light results are decent but not flagship level, reflected in a camera rating of 7.8 out of 10.

Does the phone come with an official warranty and how many Android updates will it receive?

All units are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s limited warranty applies and repairs are paid. The device runs ColorOS 16 and typically receives three to four major Android upgrades on flagship models.
