Is the OPPO Reno15 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz AMOLED screen with 1200‑nit peak brightness
  • 6500 mAh battery with 80 W fast charge
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS and 3.5× telephoto
  • Aluminum frame with IP68/69 water‑dust protection

What is not

  • Mid‑range Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 limits heavy gaming
  • ColorOS pre‑installed bloatware and ads
  • Shop‑only warranty, no official support
  • Thermal throttling drops frame rates in demanding games

Buy it if Buy if you want a large, bright display, long battery life and solid photography for everyday use and can accept a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip if you need top‑tier gaming performance or official manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

Daily use feels smooth thanks to the 12 GB RAM and Snapdragon 7 Gen 4, and the 6.59‑inch AMOLED panel at 120 Hz makes scrolling and media enjoyable. The 6500 mAh cell with 80 W fast charging lets you go a full day or more without hunting for a charger, and the 50 MP main sensor with OIS and 3.5× telephoto delivers decent portraits and vlogs. Build quality feels premium with an aluminum frame and IP68/69 protection, and the IR blaster adds a handy remote function.

However, the mid‑range chipset struggles with demanding games, often throttling and dropping frames. ColorOS is smooth but loaded with pre‑installed apps and ads, and you only get a shop‑provided warranty, not an official OPPO guarantee. If you can live with these compromises, the phone offers a solid all‑round experience for everyday tasks.
