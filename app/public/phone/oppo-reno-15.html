Is the OPPO Reno 15 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.59 inch AMOLED display with 120 Hz refresh
  • 50 MP main and selfie cameras
  • 80 W fast charging
  • Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset

What is not

  • Battery drains quickly and gets hot
  • Network drops make SIMs unreachable
  • No microSD slot and weaker glass

Buy it if Buy it if you need a big, bright screen and fast charging, and you’re okay with occasional battery and network quirks.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable battery life, strong build quality, or a guaranteed brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.59 inch AMOLED panel reaches 1200 nits and runs at 120 Hz, which makes scrolling and media smooth. Under the hood sits a Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 and 12 GB RAM, enough for normal apps and light gaming, yet owners rate performance only 6.8 out of 10 and note a spec downgrade compared with newer models. The 50 MP main sensor with OIS and a 50 MP selfie shooter capture decent photos, though reviewers say Vivo still offers clearer images.

Battery capacity is 6500 mAh with 80 W charging, but real world tests show rapid drain and overheating, reflected in a 6.5 rating. Network stability is a concern as many report SIMs becoming unreachable. The phone arrives as an unofficial gray import, offering only a shop warranty and no brand support.

Consider the Reno 16, which costs similar and addresses many of these issues.

Will the OPPO Reno 15 receive Android updates?

The Reno 15 ships with Android 16 and ColorOS 16. Flagship phones typically get three to four major OS updates, so you can expect about three Android upgrades during the device’s life.

How is the battery life in everyday use?

Owners rate the battery 6.5 out of 10 and report rapid drain and overheating. Even with a 6500 mAh cell and 80 W charging, you may not get a full day of heavy use.

Are there any network reliability problems?

Several users say their SIM cards become unreachable, with drops happening six to seven times in four days. This suggests the Reno 15 can have unstable 5G or 4G connectivity.

Does the phone come with a brand warranty?

Only gray import stock is available, which includes a shop warranty instead of an official OPPO warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.
