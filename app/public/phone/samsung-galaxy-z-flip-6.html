Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 6 Is it worth buying?

The Galaxy Z Flip 6 feels like a high‑end novelty that works well in daily tasks but shows its limits when pushed.

Choose it if
Buy if you want a premium foldable with top camera and performance and accept limited warranty.
Consider alternatives if
Skip if you need long battery life, fast charging, or a guaranteed warranty.

What works well

  • 2600 nits peak brightness
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 performance
  • 50MP main camera with OIS
  • Foldable 6.7 inch LTPO AMOLED

Reported trade-offs

  • Crease visible on main screen
  • Inner display may fail after a year
  • 25W charging is slow
  • Battery lasts only a day for heavy use

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch foldable LTPO AMOLED screen dazzles with 2600 nits peak brightness and 120Hz fluidity, yet the crease is clearly visible and the outer cover stays at 60Hz. Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 and 12GB RAM deliver snappy performance for apps and casual games, but prolonged heavy gaming brings noticeable heat and occasional throttling. The 50MP main sensor with OIS captures sharp photos and works nicely for vlogging on the cover screen, while the 10MP selfie camera is adequate.

Battery endurance is average; light users get a full day, heavy users may need a charge before nightfall, and the 25W charger needs 75 minutes for a full top‑up. The cheapest gray‑import listing at ৳84,999 lacks Samsung’s official warranty, meaning any repair will be a paid service. Official units cost ৳1,97,999 and include brand warranty, but reports of inner screen failure after a year are common.

Overall, it is a stylish flagship that suits those who value design and camera over long‑term durability and fast charging.
