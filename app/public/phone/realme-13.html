What works well

  • Fast 67W charging
  • Strong performance ratings
  • Includes a headphone jack

Reported trade-offs

  • Poor battery life
  • Low display brightness
  • Medium to high bloatware
  • No official brand warranty

Why we say that

Owners report that the battery drains quickly and some say it requires a power bank from the first week of use. The display is a major point of frustration because the peak brightness is only 580 nits. This is a drop from the 950 nits found on the Realme 12 5G.

While the Dimensity 6300 chipset handles tasks well, the overall experience is marred by these hardware shortcomings. You also have to deal with medium to high levels of bloatware and ads. Since only gray imports are available, you will get a shop warranty instead of brand support.

Realme 13 5G: what buyers ask us

What is the Realme 13 5G price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Realme 13 5G in Bangladesh was ৳22,490, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No official brand backed warranty is available. The units found in shops we track are gray imports. You will only receive a shop warranty, so you should confirm the BTRC status with the seller.

How is the battery life on the Realme 13 5G?

Owners report that the battery quality is very bad. Many users say the battery drains quickly and some feel they need to carry a power bank for daily support.

Is the display good for outdoor use?

The display is an IPS LCD with a peak brightness of 580 nits. Owners complain that this brightness is low, especially when compared to the previous model.

How many software updates will this phone get?

The phone runs on Android 14 with Realme UI 5.0. It is expected to receive 2 to 3 OS updates over its lifespan.
