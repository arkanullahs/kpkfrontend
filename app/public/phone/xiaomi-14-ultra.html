The Xiaomi 14 Ultra is a camera-focused device that struggles significantly with daily battery endurance.

Why we say that

While the camera system is nearly flawless and produces excellent results, owners report that the battery drains very fast. Some users find themselves needing to charge the phone three times a day because it drops from 100 percent to 30 percent during a four-hour morning commute. Performance remains strong thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset, but the overall ease of use is rated lower by owners.

You are buying an unofficial gray-import unit, meaning there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Repairs must be paid for, and you are limited to whatever coverage the specific shop provides. Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as a gray-import unit. There is no manufacturer warranty provided by the brand in Bangladesh. You must rely on the specific shop for any coverage, and all repairs will be paid for by you.

How long will the Xiaomi 14 Ultra receive software updates?

This device runs on Android 14 and HyperOS. It is supported for up to 4 major Android OS upgrades. You can also expect 4 to 5 years of security updates for the device.

Is the battery life really as bad as people say?

Owners report that the battery life is below average and drains very fast. Some users experience a drop from 100 percent to 30 percent in just four hours, which necessitates charging the phone three times a day.

Who is this phone best suited for?

This phone is best suited for photography enthusiasts who want a high-performing camera system. However, because it is a last-gen device, you should consider that the newer Xiaomi 15 Ultra is available for ৳1,24,999 and is generally the better buy.
