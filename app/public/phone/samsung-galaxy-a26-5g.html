Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy A26 5G Is it worth buying?

If you want a 5G phone with a big 120 Hz AMOLED screen but can tolerate a dated notch and modest value, the Galaxy A26 5G fits the bill.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need 5G, a large smooth screen and solid daily performance at a mid‑range price.
Consider alternatives if
You expect flagship‑level camera consistency, fast charging or the best value for money.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 120 Hz Super AMOLED display
  • 5000 mAh battery
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • IP67 water‑resistance

Reported trade-offs4

  • 25 W charger not included
  • Water‑drop notch and thick bottom chin
  • Inconsistent colors in main camera
  • Value poor compared with A53 5G

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch Super AMOLED panel is bright and smooth, and the Exynos 1380 with 8 GB RAM handles everyday apps without lag. Battery life is mixed; the 5000 mAh cell can last a full day for light users, yet many owners report faster drain. Charging is limited to 25 W and the box does not include a charger, which adds extra cost.

The 50 MP main sensor has optical image stabilization but colors shift and portrait shots are weak, while selfies are decent. Build quality feels premium with glass back, Victus+ protection and IP67 rating, though the water‑drop notch and thick chin look old. Gaming runs at stable 60 fps in PUBG but will not satisfy hardcore players.

Read full reasoning

Samsung promises up to six Android version upgrades and six years of security patches, which is a strong software point. Overall the phone feels solid but its price and compromises make it a poor value compared with the similarly priced A53 5G.
