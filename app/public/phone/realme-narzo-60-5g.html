Is the Realme Narzo 60 worth buying?

What is good

  • Vibrant 90Hz Super AMOLED display
  • Strong battery endurance
  • Premium leather look design
  • Includes a headphone jack

What is not

  • Weak performance and laggy gaming
  • Missing ultrawide camera lens
  • Slow 33W charging speed
  • Hardware sensor failures reported

Buy it if You prioritize a vibrant AMOLED screen and battery life over gaming performance and do not mind using a device without official brand warranty support.

Skip it if You are a mobile gamer, need a reliable camera system with ultrawide capabilities, or require the security of an official manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

While the 6.43 inch Super AMOLED display provides vibrant visuals and good outdoor visibility, the overall experience is hampered by weak performance. Owners frequently report that the Dimensity 6020 chipset is laggy during gaming, specifically in titles like BGMI. The camera setup is limited by the absence of an ultrawide lens, and users have noted issues with hardware sensors like the gyroscope and accelerometer failing.

You must also contend with medium to high levels of bloatware and ads within the Realme UI 4.0 software. Since this phone is only available as a gray import, you will not receive any manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh. Repairs must be paid for out of pocket, and you are limited to whatever limited coverage the individual shop provides.

Given that the phone was released in 2023, you should expect very little remaining software update support.
