Buying verdict

Huawei Mate X7 Is it worth buying?

Expect a stunning foldable experience but compromise on speed and app compatibility.

Choose it if
You value a bright foldable screen, premium build and flagship‑grade photos and can live without Google services.
Consider alternatives if
You need smooth gaming, reliable app ecosystem or want value for money.

What works well

  • 2500‑nit LTPO OLED display
  • Strong 5600 mAh battery with 66 W fast charge
  • Premium build with IP58 rating
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS and telephoto

Reported trade-offs

  • Kirin 9030 Pro performance is budget‑level
  • Severe thermal throttling under load
  • No Google Mobile Services
  • Outdated Android 12 base

Why we say that

The 8‑inch LTPO OLED panel reaches 2500 nits and runs at 120 Hz, making content look vivid and smooth. The 5600 mAh battery holds up well and refills quickly with 66 W charging, and the 50 MP main camera delivers sharp, stabilized photos with a useful telephoto lens. Build quality feels premium and the IP58 rating protects against dust and water.

However the Kirin 9030 Pro chipset performs like a budget phone and throttles heavily when the device gets warm. Gaming suffers from the same thermal limits and frame rates drop. The lack of Google Mobile Services means many popular Bangladeshi apps need workarounds, and the software is based on Android 12, which feels dated.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 5600 mAh cell gives strong endurance for a full day of typical browsing, video and calls, and the 66 W charger refills it in about an hour. Heavy multitasking or gaming will drain it faster because of throttling.

Will the phone run games smoothly?

Gaming performance is limited; the Kirin 9030 Pro behaves like a mid‑range chip and heats quickly. Thermal throttling cuts frame rates, so fast‑paced titles feel laggy and long sessions become uncomfortable.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with a large 1/1.28″ size and f/1.5 aperture captures decent low‑light shots, and OIS helps keep them steady. The 8 MP selfie lens remains weak in dim environments.
