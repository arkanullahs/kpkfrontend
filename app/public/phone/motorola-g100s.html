Motorola G100s Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day without hunting for a charger, the Motorola G100s delivers that with its massive battery.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a big battery and smooth display without paying a premium price.
Consider alternatives if
You need guaranteed brand warranty or fast charging for a huge battery.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Huge 7,000 mAh battery
  • 120 Hz IPS LCD with 1,050 nit peak
  • Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 handles daily tasks
  • Clean near‑stock Android on global version

Reported trade-offs4

  • Only shop warranty, no official support
  • Updates limited to one or two Android versions
  • China version lacks Google services
  • 30 W charging feels slow for large battery

Why we say that

The 7,000 mAh cell easily lasts beyond a full day of browsing, video streaming and moderate gaming, and the 6.72‑inch IPS LCD shows smooth motion at 120 Hz with a bright 1,050‑nit peak. Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 and 8 GB RAM keep everyday apps responsive, though heavy games may feel limited by the modest 30 W charge speed. The 50 MP main sensor captures clear photos in good light, but low‑light results are average compared to premium flagships.

Software runs Android 15 on a near‑stock Hello UI if you get the global version, while some imports use a Lenovo‑flavored ROM without Google services. Updates have been historically weak, usually only one or two Android versions. Only shop warranty is available, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

Motorola G100s: what buyers ask us

What is the Motorola G100s price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Motorola G100s in Bangladesh was ৳23,990, checked 17 Oct 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 7,000 mAh battery can easily last more than a full day with moderate use such as browsing, video streaming and occasional gaming. Heavy gaming or constant screen-on time will reduce that endurance but it still outlasts most mid‑range phones.

Is the phone good for gaming?

The Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 processor and 8 GB RAM provide smooth performance for most mobile games, and the 120 Hz display makes motion feel fluid. However, the 30 W charger refills the large battery slowly, and long gaming sessions will drain it quickly.

Will the camera work well in low light?

The 50 MP sensor with an f/1.9 aperture captures clear images in good lighting, but low‑light performance is average and not on par with flagship cameras. The ultrawide lens is useful for landscapes, while the 8 MP selfie camera is adequate for casual shots.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

There is no official brand warranty; only a shop warranty is offered, so you must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller. Software updates have been historically weak, typically delivering only one or two Android version upgrades after launch.
