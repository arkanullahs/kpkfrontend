Motorola G100s Is it worth buying?

The Motorola G100s gives you a massive 7,000 mAh battery and a 50 MP camera at a gray‑import price around ৳24,000, but you get no official Bangladeshi warranty and limited software updates.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a big battery, decent camera and 5G without needing an official brand warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on fast charging, official warranty or frequent Android updates.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 7,000 mAh battery lasts two days
  • 120 Hz IPS LCD display
  • 50 MP main camera with large sensor
  • Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 performance

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official warranty in Bangladesh
  • Software updates are infrequent
  • CN ROM lacks Google services
  • 30 W charging is slow for large battery

Why we say that

The 6.72‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz and can reach 1,050 nits peak brightness, so everyday use feels smooth and visible even in bright sunlight. The Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 processor with 8 GB RAM handles typical apps and moderate gaming without stutter, though the 30 W charger refills the large battery slowly. Camera quality is decent; the large sensor and f/1.9 aperture improve low‑light shots, yet the lack of optical stabilization can leave night photos noisy.

Buyers often complain about the uncertainty of warranty coverage and the need to confirm the ROM version, because some imports run a Lenovo‑flavored Chinese UI without Google services. Software updates have been historically weak, with only one to three major Android releases expected.
