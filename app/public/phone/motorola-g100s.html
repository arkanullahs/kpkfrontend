Is the Motorola G100S worth buying?

What is good

  • Huge 7000 mAh battery
  • Clean software with low bloat
  • Smooth 120Hz display
  • Includes a headphone jack

What is not

  • Historically weak OS updates
  • No official brand warranty
  • Gray import stock only

Buy it if You need a phone with very long battery life and clean software.

Skip it if You want an official brand warranty and guaranteed long term updates.

Why we say that

It runs Android 15 with a near stock Hello UI that has very few ads. You get a large 120Hz screen and a headphone jack for your wired earphones. However, you must be careful with the purchase.

Only gray import stock is available. This means you get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty. You need to confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying.

Historically, Motorola has been weak with software updates. You might only see 1 to 3 OS updates over the life of the phone. This makes it a risky choice for those who want long term software support.
