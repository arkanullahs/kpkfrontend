Buying verdict

Motorola G57 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run a day‑plus on a single charge without breaking the bank, the Motorola G57 delivers that.

Choose it if
You want long battery life, decent performance and a bright screen at a budget price.
Consider alternatives if

What works well

  • 120 Hz IPS LCD display
  • 50 MP main camera
  • 5200 mAh battery lasting up to three days
  • 33 W fast charging

Reported trade-offs

  • No official warranty in Bangladesh
  • Network instability reported
  • No microSD slot
  • Low‑light camera performance average

Why we say that

The 6.72 inch IPS LCD shows bright 1080p images at 120 Hz and the 5200 mAh battery can last between one and three days depending on use. Charging at 33 W fills the battery in about 75 minutes, which most owners find acceptable. The Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 chipset handles everyday apps and 60 fps BGMI smoothly, and the near‑stock Android 16 UI feels clean.

The 50 MP main sensor captures decent daylight photos, though low‑light results are only average. Network glitches have been reported and the phone arrives without any official Motorola warranty, only the shop’s limited cover. Overall it offers strong value for its price but requires a buyer comfortable with gray‑import trade‑offs.
