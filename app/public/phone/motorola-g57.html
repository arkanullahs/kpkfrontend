Is the Motorola G57 worth buying?

What is good

  • Excellent battery endurance
  • Clean near-stock Android experience
  • Bright 1050 nits peak display
  • Includes a headphone jack

What is not

  • Poor camera and video performance
  • Struggles with heavy gaming
  • Lacks AMOLED display depth
  • Only unofficial shop warranty available

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a clean software interface and long battery life for daily tasks over gaming or photography.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you want a high-quality camera, a vibrant AMOLED display, or the security of an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Living with this phone means enjoying a near-stock Android 16 interface that feels light and easy to use, though owners report significant frustrations with the camera and display quality. While the 5200 mAh battery provides excellent endurance, the device struggles with heavy gaming and lacks the visual depth found in AMOLED panels. Owners have given the camera and video capabilities low ratings, suggesting it is not the right choice for photography enthusiasts.

You must also consider that this is a last-gen device, as the newer Motorola G100 is available for 26,490 taka and generally offers better value. Because this is only available through unofficial channels, you will rely on a shop warranty rather than a brand warranty, making it essential to verify the IMEI and BTRC status before finalizing your purchase.
