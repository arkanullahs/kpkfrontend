Buying verdict

Huawei Mate XT Ultimate Design Is it worth buying?

If you can live without Google Play services and accept a fragile gray import phone, the Mate XT Ultimate Design feels like a high end showpiece rather than a daily workhorse.

Choose it if
You value the unique design and can manage software workarounds.
Consider alternatives if

What works well

  • Stunning 10.2 inch tri fold OLED display
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • 5600 mAh battery lasting a full day
  • 66 W fast charging

Reported trade-offs

  • No Google Play services, workarounds required
  • Very fragile chassis, no IP rating
  • Gaming struggles on modern titles
  • Telephoto cannot do portrait mode

Why we say that

The 10.2 inch tri fold OLED screen looks stunning but the 90Hz refresh feels low for the price and creases are visible when folded. The 5600 mAh battery surprisingly lasts a full day of mixed use and the 66W charger refills it quickly. The 50 MP main camera captures sharp photos with optical image stabilization, though the telephoto lens cannot do portrait mode and low light performance is average.

Performance is limited by an older 7nm chipset, so modern games such as Warzone struggle to run. HarmonyOS 4.2 runs without Google services, meaning essential Bangladeshi apps need risky sideloads. There is no manufacturer warranty; any repair depends on the shop’s own policy and may cost extra.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

Owners report that the 5600 mAh battery provides a full day of mixed usage such as browsing, social media and video playback. It is considered surprisingly good for a device with a large screen.

Can I play modern games like Warzone on this phone?

The Kirin 9010 chipset is built on an older 7nm process and owners say modern titles like Warzone fail to load or run at low frame rates. It is not suitable for demanding gaming.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor has a large aperture of f/1.4 and optical image stabilization, which helps in low light, but the overall image quality is average and the telephoto lens cannot capture portrait mode shots.

Is there any official warranty or service support in Bangladesh?

All units are gray imports with no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Repairs are handled by the shop that sold the phone and any cost is paid by the owner. Confirm IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.
