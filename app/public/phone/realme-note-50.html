Buying verdict

Realme Note 50 Is it worth buying?

This is a basic device that struggles with stability despite its low price.

Choose it if
You need a durable phone for basic tasks and value battery endurance.
Consider alternatives if
You want a smooth software experience without lag or bloatware.

What works well

  • Efficient battery with good endurance
  • Durable build with IP54 rating
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • 90Hz refresh rate display

Reported trade-offs

  • Lagging on small applications
  • Medium to high bloatware and ads
  • Mediocre camera quality
  • Slow 10W charging

Why we say that

Owners report that it keeps lagging on the smallest applications. Some users have noticed weird noises when scrolling through TikTok. While the battery is generally seen as efficient with good endurance, others have complained about a huge drain.

The screen is a basic IPS LCD that some find too large for comfortable use. You get a build that is rated to last with IP54 protection, but the software experience is hindered by medium to high levels of bloatware and ads. It functions as a simple tool, but the performance inconsistencies make it feel unreliable for those who need a smooth experience.
