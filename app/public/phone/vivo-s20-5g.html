Vivo S20 5G Is it worth buying?

What works well4

  • 6.67 inch AMOLED 120Hz display
  • 6500 mAh battery lasting two days
  • Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 handles everyday tasks
  • 50MP main camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs3

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Low light camera performance is modest
  • Software includes medium amount of ads

Why we say that

The 6.67 inch AMOLED panel runs at 120Hz and feels fluid for scrolling and gaming. Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 handles everyday apps and most games without stutter, though the highest graphics settings may need to be lowered. The 6500 mAh cell easily reaches two days of moderate use, but heavy screen time will drain it by evening.

The 50MP main sensor with OIS captures sharp daylight shots, yet low light scenes still show noise and lack detail. The selfie camera matches the main sensor resolution, giving clear portraits. Android 15 with OriginOS 5 brings the latest features but includes a medium level of preinstalled ads.

Read full reasoning

Software updates are expected for about two major versions. Because the device is a gray import, there is no official manufacturer warranty, only the shop's promise, and any repair will cost extra. Owners often mention the lack of official support as the biggest drawback.

Will the 6500 mAh battery last all day with heavy use?

The 6500 mAh cell can easily cover a full day of heavy use, but you may need to charge it before the night if you keep the screen at high brightness and run games for many hours.

How does the phone perform for gaming?

Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 together with the 120Hz AMOLED screen provides smooth gameplay for most titles. You may have to lower ultra settings on the most demanding AAA games to keep frame rates stable.

How good is the camera in low light conditions?

The 50MP sensor with f/1.9 aperture and OIS captures clear images in daylight, but in low light the pictures show noticeable noise and less detail compared to premium flagships.

Does this phone come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop offers its own warranty and any repair will be paid by the owner after confirming IMEI and BTRC status.
