Is the POCO F7 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery with excellent endurance
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 provides flagship performance
  • 90 W fast charging fills battery in under an hour
  • Bright 3200 nit 2K AMOLED display

What is not

  • Noticeable heat during heavy gaming
  • Excessive pre‑installed bloatware
  • No eSIM support
  • Ultrawide and selfie cameras are weak

Buy it if Buy it if you want flagship speed, massive battery and a bright screen at a gray‑import price.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a clean software experience or a strong all‑round camera.

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset and 12 GB RAM deliver flagship speed in apps and games. The 6.67‑inch AMOLED display shines with 3200 nit peak brightness and 120 Hz refresh, making outdoor viewing clear. 90 W fast charging fills the 6000 mAh cell in about half an hour, which suits busy schedules.

The device runs warm during long gaming sessions, and the heat can be felt on the back. The 50 MP main camera is good, but the ultrawide lens is soft and the 20 MP selfie camera struggles in low light. HyperOS 2 is responsive but comes with a large amount of pre‑installed bloatware that many owners find aggravating.

There is no eSIM support and the front camera records only up to 1080p. As a gray import, the phone only has the shop’s warranty, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
