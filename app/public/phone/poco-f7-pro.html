Is the POCO F7 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz AMOLED display with 3200 nits peak
  • 6000 mAh battery with 90 W fast charge
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 performance
  • IP68 glass‑metal build

What is not

  • In‑OS ads and bloatware
  • Thermal throttling in hot weather
  • Ultrawide camera lacks 4K video
  • Selfie camera mediocre

Buy it if Buy it if you need top‑tier speed, a bright high‑refresh display, and long battery life for gaming and media.

Skip it if Skip it if you require a clean software experience or a consistently strong camera across all lenses.

Why we say that

The 6.67‑inch AMOLED panel shines at up to 3200 nits and runs at 120 Hz, making everyday use feel smooth and media consumption vivid. Under the hood, the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 paired with 12 GB RAM handles apps and games with elite speed, though prolonged gaming in Bangladesh’s heat can trigger thermal throttling that reduces performance. The 6000 mAh battery easily lasts a full day and refuels in minutes thanks to 90 W charging, a point many owners praise.

Build quality feels premium with glass‑metal construction and IP68 protection, yet the phone arrives as a gray‑import, meaning only a shop warranty is offered and you must verify IMEI and BTRC status. The camera excels on the main 50 MP sensor but the ultrawide is weak and selfies are only average. Software runs Android 15 with HyperOS 2, but it is cluttered with ads and bloatware that many users find intrusive.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 6000 mAh cell usually powers the phone through a full day of mixed use, and the 90 W charger can refill it from zero to full in about half an hour, which owners find very convenient.

Will the phone handle heavy gaming without lag?

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 and 12 GB RAM give excellent frame rates, but in Bangladesh’s tropical climate the device can heat up and throttle after extended sessions, causing occasional slowdowns.

Is the camera good in low light and for video?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures decent low‑light shots, but the ultrawide lens lacks autofocus and 4K video, and the 20 MP selfie camera is only average, so overall video scores are modest.

Does the phone come with a brand warranty and how many Android updates will it receive?

Only gray‑import units are available, so you get a shop warranty instead of an official POCO warranty and must confirm IMEI and BTRC status. HyperOS promises about two to three major Android version updates.
