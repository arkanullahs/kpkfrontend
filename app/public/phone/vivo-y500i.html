Is the Vivo Y500i worth buying?

What is good

  • Long lasting 7200 mAh battery
  • Bright 120Hz display
  • Includes an ir blaster
  • 5G connectivity

What is not

  • Reports of fake camera lenses
  • Heavy and large size
  • Poor gaming and performance ratings
  • No official brand warranty

Buy it if You need a phone with a very large battery and do not care about camera quality.

Skip it if You want a lightweight phone with a reliable camera and official brand warranty.

Why we say that

It features a 7200 mAh battery that owners describe as long lasting. However, the actual experience is marred by serious concerns. Users report that the camera lens is fake, which is a major issue for a phone at this price.

It is also described as a huge and heavy device that feels overpriced. Performance and gaming ratings are low, with owners giving them 4.0 and 3.0 respectively. Since only gray import stock is available, you will get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty.

You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying ৳27,990.
