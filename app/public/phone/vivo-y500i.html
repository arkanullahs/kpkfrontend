Buying verdict

Vivo Y500i Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can sit on the table for many hours without charging, the Vivo Y500i’s 7200 mAh battery is the biggest advantage.

Choose it if
You want a big, bright screen, long battery life and can accept a heavy phone with a modest camera.
Consider alternatives if
You need a lightweight device, a reliable camera or an official manufacturer warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • 7200 mAh battery lasts long
  • 6.75" bright 120Hz display
  • Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 chipset

Reported trade-offs

  • Fake camera lens
  • Heavy and large size
  • Overpriced for specs
  • Limited software updates

Why we say that

The 6.75‑inch IPS LCD screen is bright and runs at 120 Hz, making everyday scrolling feel smooth. The Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 chipset delivers decent day‑to‑day performance, but owners note the device feels heavy and the 50 MP camera uses a fake lens, so photo quality suffers especially in low light. The phone arrives as a gray‑import unit, so you only get a shop warranty and must verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

Software runs Android 16 with OriginOS 6, but budget models receive only two major OS upgrades and include medium‑level bloatware. Overall the phone feels solidly built yet overpriced for its specifications, and it is best suited for users who value battery life and a large, bright display over camera performance and light weight.
