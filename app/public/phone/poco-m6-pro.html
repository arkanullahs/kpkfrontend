Buying verdict

POCO M6 Pro 5G Is it worth buying?

Buy this only if you are comfortable with an unofficial import and a risky screen.

Choose it if
You need a cheap 5G phone with fast charging and do not mind unofficial stock.
Consider alternatives if
You want a reliable warranty, a stable screen, or a device for heavy gaming.

What works well

  • Fast 67W charging
  • Strong performance ratings
  • Includes headphone jack and ir blaster
  • 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs

  • Intermittent screen flickering
  • Overheating during gaming
  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • No official brand warranty

Why we say that

Owners report that the display flickers intermittently, especially when brightness is low. While the performance is rated well, gaming can lead to overheating and automatic shutdowns during BGMI. Long term use brings more issues, with some users reporting battery drain and lag after one year.

The software experience is heavy on ads and bloatware. Since this phone is 3 years old, you should expect very little to no remaining software update life. You will not find official brand support in Bangladesh, so you must rely on shop warranties and verify the IMEI status yourself.

POCO M6 Pro 5G: what buyers ask us

What is the POCO M6 Pro 5G price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for POCO M6 Pro 5G in Bangladesh was ৳13,890, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there are no confirmed official units in stock. The listings we track are imports, which means you get a shop warranty instead of brand backed support. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

How is the camera performance in low light?

Reports are mixed. Some reviews call the low light performance decent, but actual owners report that the low light camera performance is relatively poor. Video recording is limited to 1080p.

Can I play heavy games like BGMI on this phone?

Gaming performance is rated 6.0. Some owners report that the phone overheats during gaming and may automatically shut down while playing BGMI.

How long will I get software updates?

This phone was released in 2023 and is now 3 years old. You should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point.
