Redmi K80 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Redmi K80 Pro is a powerful device that relies entirely on gray market imports, meaning you will not receive any brand-backed warranty support.

Choose it if
You are an experienced user comfortable with gray market imports and willing to manage potential software limitations.

What works well

  • Efficient 6000 mAh battery life
  • Bright 3200 nits peak display
  • Strong Snapdragon 8 Elite performance
  • Great portrait camera results

Reported trade-offs

  • Poor heat control and overheating
  • High levels of bloatware and ads
  • No official brand warranty support
  • China ROM lacks Google and Bangla

Why we say that

Living with this phone involves managing significant heat control issues, which owners report as a major concern during heavy use. While the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset delivers strong performance, the software experience is hampered by high levels of bloatware and ads. You must be extremely careful to verify if you are purchasing a China-market version, as these units often lack Google services and Bangla language support.

Conversion of these units has become difficult, and some features like Google Timeline Maps history will not function correctly. Owners generally appreciate the battery efficiency and the bright display, but the build quality receives lower marks. Before spending ৳55,490, confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller to ensure the device is not restricted.

Redmi K80 Pro: what buyers ask us

What is the Redmi K80 Pro price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Redmi K80 Pro in Bangladesh was ৳55,490, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does the Redmi K80 Pro come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there is no official warranty for this phone in Bangladesh. Every listing we have recorded is a gray import, meaning you will only receive shop warranty support instead of brand-backed coverage.

How is the battery performance of this device?

Owners report that the 6000 mAh battery is efficient and lasts a long time. It holds an owner rating of 8.0 for battery performance, making it one of the more reliable aspects of the phone.

Will I face issues with the software on this phone?

You may face significant issues if you purchase a China-market version, as it lacks Google services and Bangla language support. Additionally, the software contains high levels of bloatware and ads, and Google Timeline Maps history will not work.

Is this phone suitable for heavy gaming?

While the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset provides strong performance, owners report concerns regarding poor heat control. This overheating issue makes some users doubt the device for long gaming sessions despite the 7.5 rating for gaming.
