Is the Redmi K80 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 6.67‑inch OLED with 120 Hz smoothness
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS and telephoto
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite delivers strong performance

What is not

  • Chinese ROM shows ads and lacks Google services
  • Overheating during heavy use
  • No official warranty, only shop warranty
  • No SD card slot or headphone jack

Buy it if Buy it if you want flagship speed, a large battery and are okay with a Chinese software experience and shop‑only warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need an official brand warranty or a clean Android system without ads.

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset and 12 GB RAM make everyday tasks feel fast and gaming smooth on the 6.67‑inch OLED with 120 Hz refresh. The 6000 mAh battery is praised for lasting all day and the 50 MP main camera with OIS delivers good colors and portrait quality. Owners complain about the Chinese HyperOS showing ads, missing Google services, and occasional overheating under heavy load.

There is no SD card slot, headphone jack or FM radio, and you must rely on the shop’s warranty and verify the IMEI with BTRC. Updates are limited to two to four Android versions depending on the tier. Overall it feels like a high‑end phone with some software and support compromises.
