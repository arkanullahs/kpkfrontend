Is the Realme GT7 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 6500 nits peak brightness
  • 120 W fast charging
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite performance
  • 12 GB RAM with large storage options

What is not

  • Heavy thermal throttling under load
  • Unstable signal during gaming
  • Bulky and heavy design
  • Mediocre ultrawide and selfie camera

Buy it if Buy it if you want long battery life, fast charging and strong day‑to‑day performance and can accept a gray‑import warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable network stability, a lightweight phone, or an official manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.78‑inch LTPO AMOLED shines with up to 6500 nits peak brightness, making outdoor use easy. Under the hood sits the Snapdragon 8 Elite with 12 GB RAM, giving strong performance in most apps and games, yet sustained heavy loads trigger noticeable thermal throttling that can cut CPU speed by half. Battery life is excellent thanks to a 6500 mAh silicon‑carbon cell and 120 W fast charging, but the phone feels bulky and heavy.

The main 50 MP camera captures decent photos, though the ultrawide lens and 16 MP selfie sensor fall short, especially in low light. Network stability can dip during intense gaming sessions. All units are gray‑import, so you only get the shop’s limited warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status yourself.

Software runs Android 15 with Realme UI 6.0 and promises up to three major OS upgrades, though some bloat remains.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

The 6500 mAh silicon‑carbon battery is rated very high, and owners report it easily lasting a full day of mixed use. Fast charging at 120 W can refill the battery from zero to full in about half an hour.

Will the phone handle heavy gaming without problems?

The Snapdragon 8 Elite and 12 GB RAM give strong peak performance, but sustained gaming triggers heavy thermal throttling. CPU speed can drop to around 55 % of its maximum, which may affect frame rates over long sessions.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS works reasonably well in low light, but the ultrawide lens is disappointing and the 16 MP selfie camera remains mediocre when lighting is poor.
