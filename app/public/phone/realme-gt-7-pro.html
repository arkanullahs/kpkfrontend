Realme GT 7 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Realme GT 7 Pro gives you flagship display and battery but will heat and slow down under long gaming sessions.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
If you want a flagship‑level display, fast charging and a strong main camera and can tolerate a bulky body and occasional throttling, this phone fits you.
Consider alternatives if
If you need an official manufacturer warranty, consistent network performance or a compact phone, look elsewhere.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 6500 mAh battery with 120 W fast charge
  • 6.78‑inch LTPO AMOLED with 6500 nits peak
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite peak performance
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS and 3× telephoto

Reported trade-offs4

  • Heavy thermal throttling under sustained load
  • Unstable 5G signal in some games
  • Bulky and heavy body
  • Ultrawide and selfie camera underperform

Why we say that

Its 6.78‑inch LTPO AMOLED shines even in bright sunlight thanks to a 6500‑nit peak, and the 6500 mAh silicon‑carbon battery lasts well beyond a day with 120 W fast charging. The Snapdragon 8 Elite delivers excellent performance in short bursts, and the 50 MP main sensor with OIS and 3× telephoto captures sharp photos in good light. However, the phone gets noticeably hot during sustained use, causing the CPU to drop to about half its speed.

Some owners report occasional 5G signal drops while gaming. The build is solid with an IP69 rating but feels bulky and heavy. The ultrawide lens and 16 MP selfie camera are weaker than expected.

Read full reasoning

The device arrives as a gray‑import with only the shop’s warranty, so any repair will cost extra. Check the IMEI and whether you are getting the global or China UI before buying.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

The 6500 mAh silicon‑carbon battery is rated very high and most owners report more than a full day of mixed use, even with heavy screen brightness and gaming.

Will the phone handle long gaming sessions without slowdown?

It delivers strong performance at the start, but heavy heating causes the CPU to throttle to about 55 % of its maximum, so frame rates may drop in extended sessions.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS works well in low light, but the ultrawide lens and 16 MP selfie camera struggle and produce mediocre results.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

All units are gray‑imports with only the shop’s warranty, so repairs are paid. Realme UI 6.0 on Android 15 promises up to three major OS upgrades, but bloatware is moderate.
