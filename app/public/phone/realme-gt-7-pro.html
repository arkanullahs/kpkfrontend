Why we say that

The Realme GT 7 Pro brings a Snapdragon 8 Elite processor, a 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED panel that can hit 6500 nits and runs at 120 Hz, and a 6500 mAh battery that charges at 120 W. In daily use the display looks vivid and the performance feels smooth for most apps. The camera delivers a sharp 50 MP main sensor and a 3× telephoto, yet the ultrawide lens and 16 MP selfie camera fall short, especially in low light.

Owners praise the premium glass and metal build with IP69 protection, but many report the mobile data dropping randomly and the device throttling to about half its CPU power under sustained load. The phone arrives as a gray import, so there is no official Realme warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s limited coverage applies and repairs are paid. Check the IMEI and whether the unit runs the global or China UI before buying.

How long does the battery last with heavy use?

The 6500 mAh cell gives class leading endurance, easily lasting a full day of heavy browsing, video streaming and gaming. When the battery runs low the 120W charger refills it from zero to full in about half an hour.

Will the phone overheat or slow down while gaming?

Snapdragon 8 Elite is very powerful and runs most games smoothly, but owners report thermal throttling when the device is stressed for long periods. CPU speed can drop to about half of its maximum, affecting frame rates.

Is the camera good in low light and for selfies?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures clear photos in normal lighting and the 3× telephoto is useful for distant subjects. In low light the ultrawide lens struggles and the 16 MP selfie camera produces only average results.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

All listings are gray imports, so there is no official Realme warranty in Bangladesh. The shop provides its own limited coverage and any repair costs are paid by the buyer. Realme UI 6 will receive two to three major Android upgrades.
