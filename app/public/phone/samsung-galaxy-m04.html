Samsung Galaxy M04 Is it worth buying?

Why we say that

Owners frequently report laggy performance and express frustration with the overall experience, noting that the phone feels inadequate even though it is not yet five years old. While the 5000 mAh battery provides good endurance, other aspects of the hardware fall short. The build quality is frequently criticized for a weak back cover, and the display quality is rated poorly by users.

With a camera rating of only 3.5 and gaming performance rated at 2.5, this device is not suitable for demanding tasks. Since this is an unofficial gray-import unit, you will not receive any manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh. Repairs must be paid for out of pocket, and you are limited to whatever limited coverage the individual shop provides.

Does the Samsung Galaxy M04 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as an unofficial gray-import unit. It does not carry any manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh. You must rely on the specific shop for any limited coverage, and all repairs will be paid for by you.

How is the battery life of the Samsung Galaxy M04?

The phone features a 5000 mAh battery which owners rate highly at 8.5 out of 10. Users report that the device offers good endurance for daily usage, making it one of the few standout features of this specific model.

Can I play games on the Samsung Galaxy M04?

Gaming performance is very poor on this device, with owners giving it a rating of only 2.5 out of 10. The Helio P35 chipset and the overall hardware configuration are not designed for gaming, and users report that the phone feels laggy.

Will the Samsung Galaxy M04 receive software updates for a long time?

This phone was released in 2022 and is now four years old. You should expect very short or no remaining software update life at this point in time. It is not a suitable choice if you require long-term software support.
