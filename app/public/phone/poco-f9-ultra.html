POCO F9 Ultra Is it worth buying?

The POCO F9 Ultra is a massive, high-performance device that prioritizes raw power and battery endurance over user comfort and software refinement.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize massive battery life and display brightness above all else and are comfortable managing a device with significant thermal and software quirks.
Consider alternatives if
You want a lightweight device, a clean software experience, or the peace of mind that comes with an official manufacturer warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Exceptional 8050 mAh battery endurance
  • Extremely bright 185Hz AMOLED display
  • Powerful Snapdragon 8 Elite performance
  • High-resolution 200MP main camera

Reported trade-offs4

  • Heavy thermal throttling under load
  • Excessive bloatware and intrusive ads
  • Bulky and heavy for one-handed use
  • Ultrawide camera lacks autofocus

Why we say that

Living with this phone means managing a device that is physically heavy and bulky, making one-handed use difficult. While the 8050 mAh battery provides industry-leading endurance, the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset struggles with inconsistent thermal management. Owners frequently report that the device runs hot and suffers from major, repetitive performance dips and spikes during heavy gaming sessions.

The software experience is also a significant pain point, as HyperOS 3 comes heavily burdened with bloatware and intrusive ads. While the display is exceptionally bright and smooth, the camera system is a mixed bag, with a strong main sensor hampered by an ultrawide lens that lacks autofocus. Because these units are gray imports, you must accept that there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh, and any repairs will be paid out of pocket.

Does the POCO F9 Ultra come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you only receive whatever limited coverage the specific shop provides. All repairs must be paid for by the owner.

How does the battery life perform in real-world usage?

The phone features an industry-leading 8050 mAh battery that provides exceptional, class-defying endurance. Owners rate the battery performance very highly, making it one of the most reliable aspects of the device for long-term use.

Is this phone suitable for heavy gaming?

While the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset offers top-tier power, the device suffers from inconsistent thermal management. Owners report that the phone runs hot and experiences repetitive performance dips and spikes during prolonged heavy gaming sessions.

How many years of software updates will this phone receive?

The POCO F9 Ultra runs on Android 16 and is promised to receive up to 4 major OS updates. However, users should be aware that the HyperOS software is heavily burdened by bloatware and ads.
