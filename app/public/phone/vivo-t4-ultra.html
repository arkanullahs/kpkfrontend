Buying verdict

Vivo T4 Ultra Is it worth buying?

The Vivo T4 Ultra delivers a bright 120Hz AMOLED screen and a strong 50MP main camera, but you must accept a gray‑import with no official warranty and noticeable heating.

Choose it if
You want a high‑refresh bright screen and a strong main camera and can live with a shop‑only warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable battery life, low‑heat gaming, or official brand support.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • 120Hz 6.67 inch AMOLED display
  • 50MP main camera with periscope zoom
  • 90W fast charging
  • Dimensity 9300+ performance

Reported trade-offs

  • No official warranty in Bangladesh
  • 8MP ultrawide camera is weak
  • Software has bloatware and ads
  • Thermal throttling during gaming

Why we say that

Its 6.67‑inch panel reaches a peak 5000‑nit brightness, making outdoor use easy, while the Dimensity 9300+ chipset provides fast performance for most apps. Battery life feels average; the 5500 mAh cell gets a 6.8 rating and users report inconsistent drain and limited optimization. Gaming runs well at first, yet the phone warms up quickly in humid conditions and throttles, reducing sustained performance.

The software layer, Funtouch OS 15 on Android 15, is cluttered with bloatware and ads, which many owners find irritating. The 8MP ultrawide lens is a clear downgrade compared with the flagship main sensor. As a gray‑import, only the shop’s warranty applies and any repairs are paid.

Read full reasoning

Overall, it suits buyers who prioritize display and main‑camera quality over battery endurance, heat management, and official support.

Does the Vivo T4 Ultra have good battery life for daily use?

The phone uses a 5500 mAh battery and scores 6.8 for battery life, but owners report inconsistent drain and a lack of software optimization, so daily endurance may be average.

How does the phone perform in games and does it heat up?

Powered by the Dimensity 9300+ chip, the device scores 7.5 for gaming, yet users notice noticeable heating in humid conditions and thermal throttling that limits sustained performance.

Is the camera good in low‑light and what about the ultrawide lens?

The 50MP main sensor with OIS and a 3× periscope zoom handles low‑light well, but the 8MP ultrawide lens is a major disappointment and is considered weaker than older mid‑range phones.

Will I get any official warranty or software updates?

All listings are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s warranty applies. The phone runs Funtouch OS 15 on Android 15 and receives limited updates, typically two major OS upgrades.
