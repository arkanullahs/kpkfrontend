Why we say that

The 6000 mAh cell keeps the device alive through a full day of video streaming, social media and occasional gaming. Fast charging at 45 W refills the battery in under an hour, which is handy when you are on the move. The Dimensity 7300e chipset handles casual games well and the high refresh rate makes scrolling feel fluid.

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures clear photos in good light, and the 32 MP selfie camera is sharp enough for social posts. Owners complain about the HiOS layer that pushes many ads and feels bloated, and they note that software updates arrive slowly with only up to three major Android upgrades. The official warranty is only available on the ৳57,999 listing, while the cheaper ৳57,599 gray import has no manufacturer cover and any repair is paid.
