Motorola Razr 60 Is it worth buying?

The Motorola Razr 60 is a foldable device that prioritizes a premium display and build quality over raw performance or battery endurance.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a foldable phone for casual use and prioritize a high-quality display over gaming or photography.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable daily driver with long battery life, strong camera performance, or official brand warranty support.

What works well4

  • Stunning 6.9 inch foldable LTPO AMOLED display
  • High 3000 nits peak brightness
  • Solid 8.5 rated build quality
  • Clean near-stock Hello UI software

Reported trade-offs4

  • Poor battery life and slow 30W charging
  • Mediocre camera and video performance
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Weak long-term software update history

Why we say that

Living with this phone means accepting that it is a niche device with significant compromises in daily utility. Owners report that the camera and video capabilities are underwhelming, and the battery life is a major point of frustration. While the 6.9 inch foldable LTPO AMOLED screen is excellent for media consumption, the Dimensity 7400X chipset struggles to deliver a high-end gaming experience.

You are essentially paying ৳49,999 for the foldable form factor rather than a balanced flagship experience. Because this is an unofficial gray-import unit, you must be prepared for the lack of manufacturer support and the reality that any repairs will be paid out of your own pocket. The software experience is clean, but the history of weak update support from the brand remains a concern for long-term users.

Does the Motorola Razr 60 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as a gray-import unit. There is no manufacturer warranty provided by the brand in Bangladesh. You must rely on whatever limited coverage the individual shop offers, and all repairs will be paid.

Is the Motorola Razr 60 good for heavy gaming?

The phone is not recommended for heavy gaming. Owners have rated the gaming performance at 5.0 out of 10. The Dimensity 7400X chipset does not provide the power needed for demanding titles, and the battery life is also a significant limitation.

How long will the Motorola Razr 60 receive software updates?

The device is expected to receive between 1 and 3 OS updates. Historically, the brand has a weak track record regarding long-term software support, so you should not expect extended update cycles for this model.

What is the battery life like on this foldable phone?

Battery life is a major weakness for this device. Owners have rated the battery performance at 5.0 out of 10. With a 4500 mAh capacity and 30W charging, you may find it difficult to get through a full day of heavy usage.
