Buying verdict

Motorola Razr 60 Is it worth buying?

Prioritize the screen over everything else because the internal hardware is disappointing.

Choose it if
You want a foldable phone with a high quality screen and do not care about gaming or speed.
Consider alternatives if
You need a powerful device for gaming or a battery that lasts all day.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Bright 3000 nits LTPO AMOLED display
  • Smooth 120Hz refresh rate
  • Low bloatware and ads
  • Modern Android 15 software

Reported trade-offs

  • Very poor gaming and general performance
  • Outdated UFS 2.2 storage
  • Below average battery life
  • Stagnant camera quality

Why we say that

Owners report that the Dimensity 7400X chipset performs worse than phones costing 15k. The UFS 2.2 storage is outdated and feels like technology from 5 or 6 years ago. While the foldable LTPO AMOLED display is a highlight with 3000 nits peak brightness, the rest of the experience struggles.

Battery life is described as below average. Camera performance has not improved since last year and was not ideal to start with. Gaming is a major weak point with a very low owner rating.

Read full reasoning

You are paying for the foldable form factor and the display, but the actual speed and power are lacking for a device at this price.

What is the Motorola Razr 60 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Motorola Razr 60 in Bangladesh was ৳51,990, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official brand warranty in Bangladesh?

Only gray import stock has been found. This means you will get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

How is the battery life on the Razr 60?

The battery has a 4500 mAh capacity. Owners report that the battery life is below average based on review units, and it received a rating of 6.5 out of 10.

Is this a good phone for gaming?

No, this phone is not suited for gaming. Owners gave gaming a 3.0 rating and stated that the 7400X chipset is worse than what 15k phones provide.

How long will I get software updates?

The phone runs on Hello UI based on Android 15. Historically, updates have been weak, with a range of 1 to 3 OS updates expected.
