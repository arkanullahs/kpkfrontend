Is the Motorola Razr 60 worth buying?

What is good

  • Stunning 6.9 inch 120Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • High peak brightness of 3000 nits
  • Clean near-stock Hello UI software
  • Compact foldable design

What is not

  • Poor performance from the Dimensity 7400X chipset
  • Below average battery life
  • Outdated UFS 2.2 storage technology
  • Lack of manufacturer warranty on gray-import stock

Buy it if You are a fan of the foldable form factor and prioritize a high-quality display over gaming or heavy multitasking.

Skip it if You need a reliable daily driver with strong performance, long battery life, and official manufacturer support.

Why we say that

While the 6.9 inch LTPO AMOLED screen is excellent, the actual experience is hampered by a chipset that owners describe as underperforming compared to budget devices. You will find that the battery life is below average, and the camera system has not seen meaningful improvements over previous iterations. Owners are particularly frustrated by the use of outdated UFS 2.2 storage, which feels significantly behind current standards.

The software experience is clean thanks to the near-stock Hello UI, but the history of weak update support remains a concern. Because this is currently only available as gray-import stock, you will not receive a manufacturer warranty. You must rely on shop-provided coverage and verify the BTRC status of your specific unit before handing over your money.

If you value a unique folding form factor above all else, this phone offers a distinct experience, but it requires significant compromises in performance and daily reliability.

Does the Motorola Razr 60 have an official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh?

There is no manufacturer warranty available for this device. Because only gray-import stock has been found, you will only receive a shop warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

Is the Motorola Razr 60 suitable for mobile gaming?

Owners rate the gaming performance very poorly at 3.0 out of 10. The Dimensity 7400X chipset is reported to be weaker than what is found in much cheaper phones, making it a bad choice for anyone who plays demanding games.

How long will the Motorola Razr 60 receive software updates?

The phone runs Android 15 and uses the near-stock Hello UI. Historically, Motorola has provided between 1 to 3 OS updates for its devices, but the brand is known for having weak update support.

How is the camera performance on this foldable phone?

The camera system is rated at 5.5 out of 10 by owners. Users report that the performance has not improved since last year and was not ideal to begin with, despite the inclusion of a 50MP main sensor with OIS.
