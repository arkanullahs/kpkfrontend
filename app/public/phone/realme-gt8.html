Is the Realme GT8 worth buying?

The Realme GT8 is a performance-focused device that prioritizes raw power over display technology and camera aesthetics. Owners rate the performance at 8.5 and gaming at 8.0, making it a strong choice for users who value speed above all else. However, the experience is marred by significant compromises. Users frequently criticize the poor camera design and note that the display is LTPS rather than the more efficient LTPO technology. While the 7000 mAh battery provides good life, the overall build quality and camera performance receive mediocre ratings from owners. You must be careful with the software, as some units are China-market versions that lack Google services and Bangla language support. Since only gray-import stock is available, you will rely on shop warranties rather than brand support. Always verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before finalizing your purchase to ensure the device is legitimate.
