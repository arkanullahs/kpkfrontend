ZTE 11 Pro Plus Is it worth buying?

If you want a phone that can game for hours without hunting a charger, the ZTE 11 Pro Plus delivers that with its 7500 mAh battery and 120 W fast charging.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a gaming‑focused phone with massive battery and can accept gray‑import warranty risks.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on official service or need a strong front‑camera for selfies.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 7500 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 120 W fast charging fills quickly
  • 144 Hz AMOLED display is smooth and bright
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 gives top gaming performance

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official warranty in Bangladesh
  • Under‑display selfie camera is weak
  • RedMagic OS includes bloatware and ads
  • No eSIM support

Why we say that

The 6.85‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 144 Hz and can peak at 2000 nits, making games and videos feel smooth and bright. Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 with 12 GB RAM keeps performance steady thanks to active cooling and liquid thermal management, which is why owners rate gaming a perfect 10. The main 50 MP camera is decent, but the under‑display 16 MP selfie sensor is noticeably weak and struggles in low light.

RedMagic OS 11 runs Android 16 but carries medium bloatware and ads, which can be annoying. The phone arrives as a gray import, so there is no official ZTE warranty in Bangladesh and any repair will be at the shop’s cost. Lack of eSIM support also limits future carrier flexibility.

How long does the battery last on daily use?

The 7500 mAh cell provides industry‑leading endurance, so most users can get through a full day of apps, calls and even several gaming sessions before needing to recharge. Fast charging restores the charge in minutes.

Is the ZTE 11 Pro Plus good for gaming?

Yes, the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor, 12 GB RAM and active cooling keep performance high for long sessions. The 144 Hz AMOLED screen and 10/10 gaming rating show it handles demanding titles without lag.

How does the selfie camera perform, especially in low light?

The under‑display 16 MP selfie sensor is noticeably weaker than typical flagship front cameras. It struggles in low‑light conditions and does not compete with phones that have a dedicated front lens.

Will I get any official warranty or service for this phone in Bangladesh?

All units are gray imports, so ZTE provides no manufacturer warranty locally. The shop’s own warranty applies, and any repair costs are paid by the buyer because ZTE has little official service presence in Bangladesh.
