Buying verdict

ZTE 11 Pro Plus Is it worth buying?

This is a niche luxury device built specifically for hardcore gamers.

Choose it if
You are a hardcore gamer who wants the best sustained performance and a huge battery.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable official warranty or a high end camera system.

What works well

  • Unmatched gaming performance with active cooling
  • Massive 7500 mAh battery life
  • Immersive 144Hz AMOLED display without a notch
  • Fast 80W wired and wireless charging

Reported trade-offs

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • No protection against dust
  • No expandable memory slot
  • Soft image textures from under display camera

Why we say that

It delivers unmatched sustained performance thanks to a 24k RPM fan and active liquid cooling that prevents CPU throttling. You get a massive 7500 mAh battery that lasts two days with normal use. However, living with this phone comes with risks.

It is only available as a gray import, meaning there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. If the hardware fails, you are entirely dependent on the shop. Owners complain about the lack of dust protection and the absence of expandable memory.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as a gray import. It has no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. You must rely on the shop warranty and confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

How is the battery life for gaming?

The battery is very large at 7500 mAh. Owners report that it lasts for one full day of heavy gaming or up to two days with normal usage.

Is the camera good for photography?

The camera is decent for a gaming phone but lacks a telephoto lens and high end tuning. The under display selfie camera tends to soften image textures.

How many software updates will it get?

The device runs on Android 16 with Redmagic OS 11. It is expected to receive 1 to 2 OS updates.
