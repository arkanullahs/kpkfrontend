Tecno Phantom V Flip2 5G Is it worth buying?

If you want a large foldable screen with decent performance at under one lakh taka, the Tecno Phantom V Flip2 5G can deliver, but expect limited software support and no official warranty.

Reported trade-offs4

Why we say that

The 6.9 inch LTPO AMOLED panel offers smooth 120Hz visuals and folds comfortably, while the Dimensity 8020 chipset with 8GB RAM handles daily apps without stutter. Photography benefits from a 50MP main sensor with OIS and an ultrawide lens, and the 32MP selfie camera is sharp enough for social media. Battery life is moderate with a 4720mAh cell, lasting a typical day of mixed use but draining faster under heavy gaming.

Software runs Android 14 with HiOS 14, but updates are slow and the interface includes noticeable ads and bloat. After‑sales service relies on the shop’s warranty only, and any repair costs fall on the owner.

Will the battery last a full day with regular use?

The 4720 mAh battery can usually get you through a typical day of browsing, social media and video streaming, but heavy gaming or continuous video playback will drain it faster and you may need to charge before night.

How does it perform for gaming?

For most mobile games the Dimensity 8020 paired with 8 GB RAM delivers smooth frame rates at medium settings, but the phone does not receive frequent performance patches and the HiOS overlay may add occasional lag.

Is the camera good in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor with a 1/1.57 inch size and OIS captures decent detail in low light, yet the ultrawide lens can introduce noise and the software adds noticeable processing artifacts.
