Buying verdict

iQOO Z9s Pro Is it worth buying?

Buy this phone only if you can handle the risks of a gray import.

Choose it if
You want a 5G phone with a great screen and battery and do not mind using gray import stock.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable camera and the security of an official brand warranty.

What works well

  • Excellent battery life
  • Bright and smooth AMOLED display
  • Fast 120Hz refresh rate
  • Large 5500 mAh battery

Reported trade-offs

  • Camera app crashes and bugs
  • Slowdowns in normal usage
  • No official brand warranty
  • Medium amount of bloatware and ads

Why we say that

It offers a very bright 120Hz AMOLED display and excellent battery life that owners appreciate. However, real world usage is inconsistent. Some owners report slowdowns during normal day to day tasks.

There are serious concerns regarding the camera app, with reports of it not opening or failing after a software update. Since every listing we have recorded is a gray import, you will only get a shop warranty instead of brand support. You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying.

It is a gamble between strong hardware specs and unstable software performance.
