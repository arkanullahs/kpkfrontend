  • 7.2 inch 120 Hz IPS panel
  • 50 MP main sensor for daylight shots
  • Clean near stock Android experience
  • Huge 7000 mAh battery

Reported trade-offs4

  • 10 W charging is extremely slow
  • Display has thick bezels and cheap looking notch
  • Indoor photos and selfies are poor
  • Free Fire lags after short play

Why we say that

The 7.2 inch 120 Hz IPS panel makes media consumption comfortable, yet the thick bezels and notch make the front look cheap. The 50 MP main camera can capture decent daylight pictures, but indoor shots and selfies are disappointing. The Helio G81 chipset handles everyday apps without trouble, but gaming like Free Fire suffers from lag after a short time.

Software is a highlight with a clean near stock Android feel and no bloatware. Battery capacity is impressive, but the 10 W charging speed means a full charge can take many hours. Build quality feels mixed, with a matte back that feels premium against a front that looks cheap.
