HTC Wildfire E5 Elite Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run for days without charging, the HTC Wildfire E5 Elite delivers that.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life and a big screen and can accept basic performance and a gray‑import warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need fast charging, high‑quality low‑light photos, smooth gaming, or an official HTC service center.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Huge 7000 mAh battery lasts multiple days
  • Large 7.2‑inch screen for media
  • 50 MP main camera can capture detail
  • Android OS with familiar interface

Reported trade-offs4

  • Very slow 18 W charging
  • Low display sharpness on HD+ panel
  • Inconsistent low‑light camera performance
  • Weak gaming and heavy‑task performance

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh battery gives multi‑day endurance for casual use, but the 18 W charger takes a long time to fill the huge cell. The 7.2‑inch IPS panel is spacious for videos, yet its HD+ resolution feels soft and lacks sharpness. The 50 MP main sensor can capture detail, but it is inconsistent and struggles in low light, often requiring several shots to get a decent picture.

Performance is driven by a Helio G81 chipset, which feels sluggish with modern games and heavy apps. The build is heavy and bulky, with Gorilla Glass and a punch‑hole cutout, but HTC has little official service presence in Bangladesh, so any repair depends on the shop’s goodwill. Overall, the phone is best for users who value battery life over speed, camera quality, or 5G connectivity.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

Owners report that the 7000 mAh cell provides multi‑day endurance for casual users, easily lasting more than a full day of normal browsing, messaging and video watching without needing a recharge.

Can the phone handle modern games smoothly?

The Helio G81 is an entry‑level chipset, and reviews note that it struggles with modern gaming and heavy tasks, so you should expect low frame rates and occasional lag in demanding titles.
