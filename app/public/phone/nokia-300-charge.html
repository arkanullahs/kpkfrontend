Why we say that

The Nokia 300 Charge runs a stock‑ish Android version with the S30+ feature set, so the interface feels familiar and uncluttered. Users appreciate the low price and the lack of preinstalled ads, but they often complain about the absence of an official warranty and the need to verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying. Because the device is a gray import, any repair work must be paid for and the shop’s warranty is the only protection offered.

Updates are uncertain as the brand is still transitioning after the HMD split, and there is no 5G support. Overall, it is a decent basic phone if you can accept these limitations.

How long does the battery last on the Nokia 300 Charge?

Battery life is average for a budget phone and should last a full day with moderate use such as calls, messaging and light browsing. Heavy usage will require a charge before the day ends.

Can I play games on the Nokia 300 Charge?

The phone handles casual games smoothly because it runs a stock‑ish Android version. More demanding titles may experience lag or lower frame rates due to modest hardware, so it is best for light gaming.

Will I get official warranty service for this phone in Bangladesh?

No, the phone is a gray import and has no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Only the shop’s warranty applies and any repairs will be paid for by the owner.
