Why we say that

The 3700 mAh battery paired with 10 W charging keeps the phone alive even with heavy messaging, social media scrolling and occasional video playback. The 2.4 inch TFT screen is clear enough for basic tasks but feels cramped for browsing or watching videos. The Unisoc SC6531E chipset handles Android S30+ and simple apps without lag, yet it struggles with demanding games or multitasking.

The phone ships as a gray import, so there is no official Nokia warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s limited guarantee and any repair costs are out of pocket. Software is close to stock Android with no ads, but future updates are uncertain because the brand is in transition after the HMD split. Overall, it is a decent choice for users who need a cheap, long‑lasting device and do not mind a small display and limited support.

Will the battery last a full day with normal use?

Yes, the 3700 mAh battery can easily get you through a day of calls, messaging, social media and occasional video, especially because the phone uses a modest 2.4 inch display and low‑power chipset.

Can this phone handle mobile games?

The Unisoc SC6531E chipset can run casual games such as puzzle or arcade titles, but it will lag or drop frames in graphics‑intensive games, so expect modest performance.

What warranty do I get with this gray‑import?

All listings are gray imports, so there is no official Nokia warranty in Bangladesh; you only receive the shop’s own limited warranty, and any repair after that must be paid for by you.

How long will software updates be provided?

The phone runs a stock‑ish Android S30+ version with no bloatware, but because the brand is in transition after the HMD split, future Android updates are not guaranteed and may stop within a year.
