What works well

  • A18 chip handles games smoothly
  • 48MP main camera with OIS
  • iOS receives regular updates
  • 5G support

Reported trade-offs

  • 60Hz refresh feels dated
  • 15W charging is very slow
  • Back glass can crack easily
  • Device warms during long gaming

Why we say that

The A18 chip makes apps and games feel fast and smooth, and the 48MP main camera captures detailed photos with optical image stabilization. Battery life is acceptable for daily use, yet the 15W charger refills it slowly, which many owners find frustrating. The OLED panel looks great, but the 60Hz refresh rate feels dated for a 2025 flagship.

Some users report the back glass can chip easily and the phone gets warm during long gaming sessions. Overall, it feels premium, but the slow charging and older display may make the price hard to justify for some buyers.

How long will the iPhone 16 receive iOS updates?

Apple promises about five to six major iOS releases for each device. The iPhone 16 ships with iOS 18 and can be upgraded to iOS 26.6, so you can expect software support for several years.

Is the battery life good and how fast does it charge?

The 3561 mAh battery gives decent endurance for a 6.1‑inch phone, but the 15 W charger fills it slowly. Users note that charging feels painfully slow compared with newer fast‑charging standards.
