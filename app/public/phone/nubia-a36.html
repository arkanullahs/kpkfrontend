Is the Nubia A36 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.75 inch IPS LCD with 90 Hz refresh
  • 5,000 mAh battery lasting up to two days
  • Headphone jack and FM radio
  • Official warranty at the cheapest price

What is not

  • Cannot install unknown APK files
  • No fingerprint scanner
  • Rear camera weaker than selfie
  • Only 10 W charging

Buy it if Buy it if you need a large screen and long battery on a tight budget and can live without biometric security or high‑end camera performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you want fast gaming, reliable app installation, or a fingerprint sensor.

Why we say that

The Unisoc T7200 chipset with 4 GB RAM handles everyday tasks, yet gaming feels sluggish and the phone struggles with heavy apps. Camera results are mixed; the 13 MP rear sensor is reported to be weaker than the 5 MP selfie lens, especially in low light. Software runs Android 15 with Nebula AIOS, but users face trouble installing unknown APKs and see medium bloatware.

Build quality feels cheap and there is no fingerprint scanner, which many consider a major omission. Overall it is a functional device for basic use, but it falls short for power users who need speed, reliable app installation, and stronger photography.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

Owners report that the 5,000 mAh battery can last up to two days for many users, which matches the 7.0 rating for battery life. The phone only supports 10 W charging, so refilling is slow.

Can I play demanding games on this phone?

The Unisoc T7200 chipset together with 4 GB RAM gives a performance rating of four out of ten and a gaming score of three. Casual games run fine but demanding titles will feel laggy.

Is the camera good for low‑light photos?

The rear camera is listed as 13 MP but users say it performs worse than the 5 MP selfie lens, especially in low light. Expect soft details and limited usefulness for night photography.

Will the phone receive Android updates and is the warranty official?

An official Bangladeshi unit means the brand’s own warranty and service network apply, so you are covered by the official warranty. The phone ships with Nebula AIOS based on Android 15 and will receive one to two OS updates, with medium bloatware.
