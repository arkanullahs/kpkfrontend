Buying verdict

Nubia A36 Is it worth buying?

If you only need a cheap phone that can sit on a table for two days without charging, the Nubia A36 will do that.

Choose it if
You need an inexpensive phone with long battery life and basic media features.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable app installation, a good camera, or smooth gaming performance.

What works well

  • Large 5000 mAh battery lasts two days
  • 90 Hz IPS LCD screen feels smooth
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • Very low price with official warranty

Reported trade-offs

  • No fingerprint scanner or other biometrics
  • Cannot install unknown APK files
  • Rear camera performs poorly
  • Software shows ads and limited updates

Why we say that

The 6.75‑inch IPS LCD screen runs at 90 Hz, which makes scrolling feel smoother than many budget phones. The 5000 mAh battery is praised for lasting two days for light to moderate use, though heavy gaming will drain it faster. Performance is modest with a Unisoc T7200 chipset and 4 GB RAM, giving a rating of four out of ten, so demanding games feel sluggish.

The rear 13 MP camera is reported to be worse than the 5 MP selfie shooter, and owners complain about poor low‑light results. Software runs on Nebula AIOS / MyOS, but users face trouble installing unknown APK files and see medium‑level ads and operator bloat. The phone lacks any biometric security such as a fingerprint scanner.
