Samsung Galaxy Z TriFold Is it worth buying?

The Galaxy Z TriFold feels like a massive, high‑end tablet you have to carry around.

Why we say that

Its 10‑inch tri‑fold Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X screen delivers vivid colors and a smooth 120 Hz experience, but the visible creases and reflective surface are noticeable. Powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite and 16 GB RAM, gaming runs buttery smooth and feels like a TV in your hands, which matches the high display rating of 9.2. The 200 MP main camera captures impressive detail, yet the telephoto and ultrawide lenses fall short of expectations.

Battery life is average; the 5600 mAh cell drains quickly when you push the brightness to its 1600‑nit peak, and the 45 W charger, while the fastest for Samsung folds, only mitigates the issue slightly. The device is exceptionally heavy and thick, making pocket use uncomfortable, and it lacks S Pen support. Being a gray‑import, it carries only the shop’s warranty and no official Samsung service, so repairs are out‑of‑pocket.
