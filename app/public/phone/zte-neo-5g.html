Why we say that

The 4500 mAh battery lasts about a day with moderate use and the 45W fast charging helps you top up quickly. The 6.6 inch IPS LCD runs at 120Hz, yet owners rate the display only 4.5 out of 10, so colors and brightness feel average. The 50MP main camera records decent daylight shots but scores a 5 out of 10 overall, and low light photos are noisy.

Android 13 comes with a standard budget skin that includes medium bloat and ads. Gaming is fine for casual titles but the Unisoc T820 cannot handle AAA games smoothly. All units are gray import, so there is no manufacturer warranty and ZTE has little service presence in Bangladesh, leaving repairs to the shop and updates unlikely after three years.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 4500 mAh battery is rated about 6 out of 10 by owners, so you can expect a full day of moderate use but heavy streaming may need a top up. Fast charging at 45W helps recover quickly.

Can I play high end games smoothly?

Daily tasks run fine but the Unisoc T820 struggles with AAA titles. Owner ratings put gaming at 5.5 out of 10, so expect low frame rates and occasional lag in demanding games.

Is the camera good in low light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor has an f/1.8 aperture, but owners give the camera a 5 out of 10 rating. Low light photos are often noisy and lack detail, so expectations should be modest.

Will I get official warranty and service support?

All listings are gray import, so there is no official ZTE warranty in Bangladesh. The shop may offer its own limited warranty, but repairs are paid and ZTE has little service presence locally.
