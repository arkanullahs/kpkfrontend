Is the ZTE Neo 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz 6.6 inch IPS LCD
  • 45W fast charging
  • 8GB RAM with 256GB storage
  • 5G connectivity

What is not

  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Limited software updates
  • AAA games run poorly
  • Average low‑light camera

Buy it if If you want a cheap 5G phone with decent everyday performance and can handle gray‑import warranty risks, this model fits your needs.

Skip it if If you need official brand support, regular software updates, or smooth AAA gaming, look elsewhere.

Why we say that

The phone feels fast for everyday apps thanks to 8GB RAM and a Unisoc T820 chip, and the 45W charger fills the 4,500 mAh battery in under an hour. The 6.6‑inch IPS LCD is bright enough for most lighting, though owners rate the display lower than the specs suggest. The 50MP main camera captures decent daylight shots but struggles in low light, reflected in a modest camera rating.

Gaming is acceptable for casual titles but AAA games lag noticeably. Software runs Android 13 with a budget skin that shows medium ads and only a couple of OS updates left. After‑sales support is limited to the shop’s own warranty, and any repair cost falls on you.

Overall it is a budget‑focused device that works if you can live with limited support and future updates.
