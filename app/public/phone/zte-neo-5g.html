ZTE Neo 5G Is it worth buying?

What works well4

  • 120Hz IPS display smooth
  • 45W fast charging
  • 8GB RAM handles multitasking
  • 50MP main camera in bright light

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official warranty in Bangladesh
  • Low light camera performance poor
  • Battery drains quickly during heavy gaming
  • Software updates limited to one year

Why we say that

The 6.6 inch IPS panel runs at 120Hz, giving smooth scrolling, yet the colors are not as vivid as an AMOLED. The Unisoc T820 with 8GB RAM handles daily tasks and casual gaming, but heavy games drain the 4500 mAh battery quickly. The 50MP main camera captures decent shots in bright light, but low light performance is poor and video is limited to 1080p at 30 fps.

Thermal management is good for the price, and the 45W charger refills the battery fast. The phone includes a headphone jack and a large storage capacity, but it arrives as a gray import with no official warranty and limited software updates. Owners also note the glossy plastic body scratches easily and the water drop notch looks dated.

Is there any official warranty for the ZTE Neo 5G in Bangladesh?

There is no manufacturer warranty for this gray import unit in Bangladesh. The shop may offer its own limited warranty, but any repair will be paid by you.

How many software updates will the phone receive?

ZTE usually provides about one year of OS and security updates. The Neo 5G was released in 2023, so at three years old you should expect little or no further updates.

Will the phone overheat or lose battery quickly while gaming?

Thermal management is praised even for a budget device, so the phone stays cool during games. However owners note the 4500 mAh battery drains quickly when gaming heavily, giving average endurance.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor can capture detailed photos in good light, but low light performance is poor and there is no advanced processing. Video recording is limited to 1080p at 30 fps.
