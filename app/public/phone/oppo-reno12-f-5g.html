Buying verdict

OPPO Reno12 F 5G Is it worth buying?

This phone is a mixed bag that prioritizes durability and screen quality over raw power.

Choose it if
You want a very durable phone with a bright screen for basic daily use.
Consider alternatives if
You play heavy games or want a performance upgrade over the Reno 11F.

What works well

  • Incredible durability and build quality
  • Very bright 2100 nits AMOLED display
  • Reliable full day battery endurance
  • Includes an ir blaster

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe gaming struggles and lag
  • Bloatware and AI coin system
  • Slower 45W charging than Reno 11F
  • Mixed camera quality

Why we say that

Owners praise the incredible build quality and the very bright 2100 nits AMOLED display. However, many users feel it is a hardware downgrade from the Reno 11F. The Dimensity 6300 chipset struggles with heavy tasks, and gamers report severe lag and difficulty hitting 30 FPS in games like Genshin Impact.

Software is another pain point, as users complain about bloatware and AI features limited by a coin system. While the battery lasts a full day for moderate use, the 45W charging is slower than the previous model. Many buyers view the device as overpriced when comparing the actual performance to the price tag.

Can I play heavy games like Genshin Impact on this phone?

No, this phone struggles with heavy gaming. Owners report noticeable lag during app launches and difficulty hitting 30 FPS even on the lowest settings.

How is the battery life and charging speed?

The 5000 mAh battery provides reliable full day endurance for moderate use. However, the 45W charging is considered a downgrade from the 67W charging found on the Reno 11F.

What is the software and update situation?

It runs Android 14 and is upgradable to Android 15 with ColorOS 15. Users have reported issues with bloatware and AI features that use a coin system.
