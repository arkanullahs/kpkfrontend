Is the Samsung Galaxy A15 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Outstanding battery life lasting up to three days
  • Vibrant 6.5 inch 90Hz Super AMOLED display
  • Official warranty support in Bangladesh
  • Long-term software update support

What is not

  • Severe RAM management and app closing issues
  • Poor camera performance in night shots
  • Missing charger in the box
  • Severe lag during gaming

Buy it if You are a light user who prioritizes a bright display and long battery life for basic daily tasks.

Skip it if You are a gamer or a power user who needs reliable multitasking and smooth software performance.

Why we say that

Owners frequently report severe RAM management issues where applications restart themselves, alongside UI stutters and bugs that hinder the user experience. While the Super AMOLED display is a vibrant upgrade, the phone suffers from poor camera performance in low light and lacks essential features like a screen recorder. Users also note frustration with the virtual proximity sensor and inconsistent face unlock functionality.

Although the device runs cool during light tasks, it is not suitable for gaming due to severe lag. Given that the newer Galaxy A17 5G is available for ৳28,999, this older model often feels like a less competitive choice for your money. If you prioritize long battery life for basic tasks, it may suffice, but be prepared for the limitations of its aging software and hardware constraints.
