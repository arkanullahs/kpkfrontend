Buying verdict

POCO F6 Pro 5G Is it worth buying?

It offers a bright 6.67 inch AMOLED display and fast Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 performance, but the software and battery issues can be frustrating.

Choose it if
Buy if you want top gaming speed and a vivid display and can tolerate software ads.
Consider alternatives if
Skip if you need long standby time, low light camera quality, or a clean Android experience.

What works well

  • 120Hz bright AMOLED screen
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 speed
  • 120W fast charging
  • Large 5000 mAh battery

Reported trade-offs

  • HyperOS adds heavy bloatware
  • Overheating causes throttling
  • Rapid battery drain in standby
  • No clear brand warranty

Why we say that

The 120Hz panel reaches a peak brightness of 4000 nits, making outdoor use easy, while the 5000 mAh cell and 120W charger give a full day of use with a quick top up. Gaming feels smooth thanks to the chipset, yet the phone gets hot quickly and may throttle, which some owners find disappointing. The camera delivers 50MP detail in good light, but low light images lack the polish of premium flagships and the software adds ads that can annoy.

Because only gray import stock is available, you will get a shop warranty rather than a brand warranty and should verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

POCO F6 Pro 5G: what buyers ask us

What is the POCO F6 Pro 5G price in Bangladesh?

We have no verified Bangladesh price for POCO F6 Pro 5G. No shop we track has listed it, as of 11 Sep 2026. The specs and ratings below are what we do hold, and this page updates itself the day a price appears.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 5000 mAh cell can comfortably last a full day with moderate use, and the 120W charger refills it in minutes. Users report rapid drain when the phone is idle and the standby time is shorter than expected.

Is the POCO F6 Pro good for gaming?

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset delivers smooth frame rates and the 120Hz display shows motion clearly. However the device heats up quickly during long sessions and may throttle, which can affect performance.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.6 aperture and OIS captures decent detail in bright conditions. In low light the images lack the refinement of flagship phones and the software tuning is not optimal.

What warranty and software updates does the phone get?

Only gray import stock is sold, so you receive a shop warranty rather than a brand warranty and should verify the IMEI and BTRC status. The device runs HyperOS on Android 14 and promises up to three major Android upgrades.
