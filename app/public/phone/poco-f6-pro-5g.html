Is the POCO F6 Pro - 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 performance
  • 120 Hz AMOLED display with 4000‑nit peak
  • 120 W wired fast charging
  • 12‑16 GB RAM and up to 1 TB storage

What is not

  • Overheats quickly during gaming or charging
  • Rapid standby battery drain
  • HyperOS filled with bloatware and ads
  • Gray‑import warranty, not brand warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you need top gaming speed and a bright screen and can accept a gray‑import warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you prioritize long standby time, low‑light photography, or a clean software experience.

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 paired with 12‑16 GB RAM makes everyday tasks and heavy gaming feel instant. The 6.67‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 4000 nits, so the screen stays vivid even in bright sunlight. Battery life is enough for a day, yet many users notice rapid drain when the phone sits idle under HyperOS.

Charging is lightning fast at 120 W, but the device heats up quickly during long gaming sessions or while charging, leading to thermal throttling. The 50 MP main camera produces decent photos in good light but lacks the refinement of true flagships, especially in low‑light conditions. HyperOS adds a lot of pre‑installed apps and ads, which can feel intrusive.

The phone arrives as gray‑import stock, meaning you get a shop warranty and must confirm IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.

GSMArena Official · Poco F6 Pro review ↗

GSMArena finds the POCO F6 Pro to be an excellent flagship killer that packs premium features like a sharp QHD OLED screen, a top-tier Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset, and solid main camera performance. However, they note that the battery life is merely okay, waterproofing is limited to IP54, and it regresses by dropping the wireless charging found on its predecessor.

Adam Lobo TV · POCO F6 Pro After TWO Weeks: We Were WRONG! 😡 ↗

Adam Lobo initially doubted the phone but was won over by its symmetrical premium design, fantastic gaming capabilities, and the best night mode photography POCO has produced to date. Despite the high praise for performance, he strongly criticized the atrocious selfie video exposure and the very poor battery drain when using the display's maximum resolution.

TechSci Guy · বিশাল চাপে পোকো | Poco F6 5g ↗

TechSci Guy provides a quick overview focused on the device's hardware strengths, highlighting the smooth 120Hz display, fast charging capabilities, and strong gaming performance in titles like Call of Duty, wrapped up in a sleek and comfortable design.

Versus · POCO F6 Pro review - $499 Flagship Killer! ↗

Versus highly recommends the phone for delivering a flagship-level experience at a mid-range price, praising the incredibly bright 4000-nit peak display and processor benchmarks that beat out the Samsung Galaxy S23 FE. Their only major reservation is the front-facing camera, which completely washed out skin tones and lacked contrast during their indoor testing.

YugaTech · POCO F6 Pro Review ↗

YugaTech praises the phone as an absolute beast for mobile gamers, noting that the combination of up to 16GB of RAM and the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 maintains high frame rates flawlessly during extended sessions. While they loved the blazing-fast 120W charging and main camera quality, they found the HyperOS bloatware and the inclusion of a low-quality macro lens over a telephoto to be annoying drawbacks.

Will the battery last a full day with normal use?

The 5000 mAh battery can comfortably get you through a typical day, but users report faster‑than‑expected drain in standby and under HyperOS. The 120 W charger restores full charge in about half an hour, so you can top up quickly.

How does the phone handle gaming and does it overheat?

Gaming feels smooth thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 and 120 Hz screen, but many owners notice the device heats up fast during long sessions. The heat triggers thermal throttling that can lower frame rates after a short time.

Is the camera good for low‑light photos?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures clear daylight shots, yet the tuning falls short in low‑light scenes. Expect more noise and less detail compared with true flagship cameras, and the ultrawide lens does not add much versatility.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

The phone is sold as gray‑import stock, so it comes with a shop warranty rather than an official POCO guarantee. Verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying, and expect up to three major Android upgrades under HyperOS.
