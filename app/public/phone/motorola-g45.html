Buying verdict

Motorola G45 Is it worth buying?

The Motorola G45 is a 5G device that relies entirely on gray-market imports, meaning you will not find official brand-backed warranty support in Bangladesh.

Choose it if
You prioritize a clean, ad-free software experience and want a 5G device on a budget while accepting the risks of an unofficial import.
Consider alternatives if
You require official brand warranty support or need guaranteed long-term software updates for your device.

What works well

  • Smooth 120Hz IPS LCD display
  • Clean near-stock Hello UI software
  • Includes a 3.5mm headphone jack
  • Supports 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs

  • No official brand warranty available
  • Historically weak software update support
  • Only gray-import units currently available

Why we say that

Living with this phone means navigating the risks of unofficial units, so you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your money. The device runs on a Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 chipset and features a 120Hz display, which makes the interface feel responsive. You get a near-stock Hello UI experience with very little bloatware or ads, which is a refreshing change from many other phones in this price bracket.

However, Motorola has a historically weak track record for software support, and you should only expect between one and three OS updates. The 5000 mAh battery is standard for the segment, and the 50MP main camera handles basic photography needs. Since all units currently found are imports, you are limited to shop-provided warranties rather than official manufacturer coverage.

Motorola G45: what buyers ask us

What is the Motorola G45 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Motorola G45 in Bangladesh was ৳15,990, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does the Motorola G45 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there is no official brand-backed warranty for this phone in Bangladesh. All units currently found in the market are gray-imports, so you will only receive a shop warranty if the seller provides one.

How long will the Motorola G45 receive software updates?

Motorola has a historically weak track record regarding software support. You should expect between one and three OS updates for this device over its lifespan.

Is the Motorola G45 suitable for heavy gaming?

The phone is powered by the Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 chipset. While it supports 5G and has a 120Hz display, it is a budget-oriented device, so performance in demanding games will depend on the specific requirements of those titles.

What should I check before buying this phone from a local seller?

Since these are unofficial imports, you must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing. Always verify that the device is not blocked and that the shop provides a clear warranty policy.
