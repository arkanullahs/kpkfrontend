Is the Motorola G45 worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz IPS LCD display
  • 50MP main camera
  • 5000 mAh battery
  • headphone jack and FM radio

What is not

  • No official brand warranty
  • Updates are historically weak
  • Only gray‑import stock available
  • 18W charging is modest

Buy it if Buy it if you want a cheap 5G phone with decent camera and long battery.

Skip it if Skip it if you need strong after‑sales support or fast charging.

Why we say that

The 6.5 inch IPS LCD runs at 120Hz, making everyday scrolling feel smooth. The 50MP main sensor captures detailed photos in good light, while the 16MP selfie camera is adequate for video calls. A 5000 mAh battery gives you through‑day endurance, though the 18W charger is modest.

Android 14 with Hello UI feels near stock and the phone includes a headphone jack and FM radio for offline entertainment. The main complaints are the lack of an official brand warranty, a history of slow software updates, and the fact that only gray‑import units are available, so you must verify IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

Will the Motorola G45 get Android updates?

The phone ships with Android 14 and Hello UI, which is near stock. Historically the series receives only one to three major OS updates, so future upgrades may be limited.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery is large enough for most users to get through a full day of mixed use. Charging is limited to 18W, so refilling the battery takes longer than fast‑charging competitors.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50MP sensor with a 1/2.76" size and f/1.8 aperture captures clear photos in bright light. In low light the results are acceptable but not as bright or detailed as premium flagship cameras.

What warranty does the phone have in Bangladesh?

Only gray‑import units are found, and they come with a shop warranty rather than an official brand warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.
