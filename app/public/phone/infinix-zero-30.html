Why we say that

This 2023 model features a 6.78 inch AMOLED display with a 144Hz refresh rate and a Dimensity 8020 chipset that handles daily tasks. While the 108MP main camera includes OIS for stability, the software experience is hindered by high levels of bloatware and notification ads within the XOS 13 interface. Because the phone is now three years old, you should expect very limited or no remaining software update support from the manufacturer.

Every unit sold in the market is a gray import, meaning you must rely entirely on the individual shop for any repair promises. You should verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your money to ensure the device is not flagged. Living with this phone means dealing with a slow update cycle and preloaded applications that cannot be easily removed.
