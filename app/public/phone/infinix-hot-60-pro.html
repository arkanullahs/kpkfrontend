Is the Infinix Hot 60 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Excellent 144Hz AMOLED display
  • High 4500 nits peak brightness
  • Includes headphone jack and IR blaster
  • Up to 3 major Android upgrades

What is not

  • High levels of bloatware and ads
  • Slow video playback at 1080p
  • Battery drains quickly under average use
  • Poor gaming performance

Buy it if Buy this phone if you want a bright, high-refresh-rate display for general tasks and do not mind managing frequent battery charging.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a mobile gamer or someone who watches a lot of high-definition video content.

Why we say that

While the 144Hz AMOLED screen is a standout feature for the price, owners report that the phone struggles with video playback, specifically lagging when playing 1080p content. Battery life is another point of contention, as users report needing to charge the device twice a day to get through their daily usage. The software experience is heavily impacted by high levels of bloatware and notification ads, which can make the interface feel cluttered.

While the Helio G200 chipset handles general performance well, gaming is not a strength of this device. If you prioritize a bright, smooth screen over consistent battery longevity and a clean software experience, this phone might suit your needs. However, those who rely on their phone for heavy media consumption or gaming should be aware of the reported playback and performance limitations.

Does the Infinix Hot 60 Pro have good battery life?

Owners report that the battery drains quickly. Despite the 5160 mAh capacity, some users find they must charge the phone twice a day to make it through their daily usage, which averages about 7 to 8 hours.

Is the Infinix Hot 60 Pro suitable for gaming?

The phone is not recommended for gaming. Owners have rated the gaming performance quite low, giving it a score of 4.0 out of 10. You may experience performance issues if you intend to use this device for intensive mobile games.

How long will the Infinix Hot 60 Pro receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 15 and is promised up to 3 major Android upgrades. However, users should note that the XOS software updates are generally reported to be slow and the system comes with significant preloaded apps and notification ads.

How is the camera quality on this phone?

The main camera features a 50MP sensor with an f/1.6 aperture. Owners generally describe the camera quality as decent, though it received a rating of 5.0 out of 10 for photos and 4.0 out of 10 for video capabilities.
