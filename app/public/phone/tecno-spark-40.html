Is the Tecno Spark 40 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 120 Hz bright IPS LCD
  • 50 MP main camera captures decent photos
  • 45 W fast charging

What is not

  • High ads and bloatware
  • Camera not ultra‑tuned
  • Gaming performance low
  • Build feels cheap

Buy it if Buy it if you need a large battery and smooth display on a tight budget.

Skip it if Skip it if you prioritize high‑end gaming or premium camera performance.

Why we say that

The 6.75 inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz and stays bright under sunlight, making everyday tasks feel smooth. The Dimensity 6400 chipset together with 4 to 8 GB RAM handles basic apps well but struggles with demanding games, which owners rate very low. The 50 MP main camera produces decent photos in good light, yet it is not fine‑tuned for low‑light scenes and many users find it average.

Software runs Android 15 with HiOS 15, but the system includes a lot of ads and bloatware that slow down the experience and limit update speed to one or two major versions. Build quality feels cheap, reflected in low build ratings. Overall the phone is suitable for budget buyers who need long battery life and a smooth display, but it falls short for gamers and photography enthusiasts.
