Is the Tecno Spark 40 worth buying?

What is good

  • Efficient 6000 mAh battery
  • Bright 120Hz IPS LCD display
  • Includes headphone jack and IR blaster
  • Affordable 5G connectivity

What is not

  • High levels of ads and bloatware
  • Poor gaming and system performance
  • Low build quality ratings
  • Slow software update schedule

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a long-lasting battery for basic daily tasks and want an affordable entry point into 5G connectivity.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a mobile gamer or someone who values a clean software experience without intrusive advertisements.

Why we say that

Living with this phone means dealing with a high amount of pre-installed bloatware and frequent advertisements within the HiOS 15 interface. While the 6000 mAh battery is highly efficient and lasts a long time, owners report significant frustrations with the overall build quality and sluggish system performance. The 120Hz display is bright enough for outdoor use, but users find the actual visual experience lacking.

Photography is strictly average, as the 50MP main camera produces decent photos but lacks the fine-tuning found in more expensive devices. Gamers should be particularly cautious, as the Dimensity 6400 chipset struggles with demanding titles. You will receive limited software support, with only 1 to 2 OS updates expected over the life of the device.

It is a functional tool for basic tasks, provided you can tolerate the software clutter and lower build standards.
