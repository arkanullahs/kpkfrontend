Tecno Spark 40 Is it worth buying?

The Spark 40 will keep you online all day thanks to its massive 6000 mAh battery, but you must accept a gray‑import with no official warranty and a software layer full of ads.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a large battery, fast charging and 5G on a tight budget and can live without an official warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on official warranty, need strong gaming or low‑light camera performance.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 120 Hz IPS LCD stays bright outdoors
  • 45 W fast charging refills quickly
  • 5 G connectivity works on local networks

Reported trade-offs4

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • HiOS adds many ads and bloatware
  • Camera not tuned for low light
  • Gaming performance low

Why we say that

The Dimensity 6400 chipset delivers adequate performance for everyday tasks and 5G works on local networks. The 6.75" IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz and stays bright in sunlight, while 45 W fast charging refills the large cell in under an hour. The 50 MP main sensor produces decent photos in good light but struggles in low‑light scenes and lacks advanced tuning.

Gaming scores are low and the HiOS skin adds noticeable bloatware, which many owners find irritating. Build quality is modest and the phone includes a headphone jack, FM radio and IR blaster. Updates are slow, with only one or two Android versions expected.

Overall it suits budget‑conscious users who value battery life over premium camera or gaming performance.
