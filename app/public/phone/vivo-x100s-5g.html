Is the Vivo X100s 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • High performance Dimensity 9300+ chipset
  • Bright 3000 nits LTPO AMOLED display
  • Good battery endurance from 5100 mAh cell
  • Capable 50MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Front camera limited to 1080p video
  • Wide 7.8mm bezels on flat design
  • Only gray-import stock available
  • Medium levels of bloat and ads

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize high performance and a bright display and are comfortable managing a gray-import device with shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you require an official manufacturer warranty or need high-quality front camera video for content creation.

Why we say that

Living with this phone means dealing with a flat design that features 7.8mm bezels, which owners note helps move heat away from the internal components. While the 5100 mAh battery provides good endurance, users have expressed frustration regarding the front camera being limited to 1080p video recording. The software experience includes medium levels of bloat and ads, and you should confirm the BTRC status and IMEI with your seller before purchasing.

Because this is gray-import stock, you will rely on shop warranty rather than a manufacturer warranty. Owners rate the display and performance highly, but the camera and gaming experiences receive lower marks from those who use the device daily.
