Buying verdict

Vivo X100s 5G Is it worth buying?

Overall the Vivo X100s 5G feels like a premium flagship with a bright, smooth screen and a capable camera, but you have to live with a shop only warranty and some thermal quirks.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a bright high refresh screen and a solid main camera and can accept a shop warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need a guaranteed brand warranty or top tier gaming performance.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 120Hz LTPO AMOLED display with 3000 nit peak brightness
  • 50MP main camera with OIS and 3x telephoto
  • 5100 mAh battery supports 100W fast charging
  • 12GB RAM and Dimensity 9300+ chipset deliver strong performance

Reported trade-offs3

  • No official manufacturer warranty only shop warranty
  • Front camera video limited to 1080p resolution
  • Thermal design pushes heat outward may feel warm

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED panel runs at 120Hz and can reach 3000 nit peak brightness, making outdoor use easy. The Dimensity 9300+ chipset together with 12GB RAM delivers strong performance for most apps and games, though gaming scores are only moderate. The 50MP main sensor with OIS and 3x telephoto works well in low light, while the 32MP selfie camera is limited to 1080p video.

Battery life is good thanks to a 5100mAh cell and 100W fast charging, but the flat design pushes heat outward, so the phone can feel warm during heavy use. Software runs Android 14 with OriginOS 4 and includes medium level bloat and ads.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

Is there an official Vivo warranty for this model in Bangladesh?

The device runs Android 14 with OriginOS 4 and receives software updates; upper models typically get three major OS upgrades while budget variants receive two, so expect a limited but reasonable update lifespan.

How does the camera perform in low light and what about front video quality?

The 50 MP main sensor with a 1/1.49 inch size, f/1.6 aperture, OIS and 3× telephoto performs well in low light, but users note the front camera only records video at 1080p, which may be a drawback.
