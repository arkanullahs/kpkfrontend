Vivo X100s 5G Is it worth buying?

Living with the Vivo X100s 5G means managing a powerful device that prioritizes heat dissipation through its flat design and wide 7.8mm bezels.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want high-end performance and a bright display and are comfortable managing repairs through a local shop instead of a brand service center.
Consider alternatives if
You require an official manufacturer warranty or if you frequently record high-quality video using the front camera.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Bright 3000 nits LTPO AMOLED display
  • Strong Dimensity 9300+ performance
  • Good battery endurance from 5100 mAh cell
  • Capable 50MP main camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs4

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Front camera limited to 1080p video
  • Wide 7.8mm bezels
  • Medium level of bloatware and ads

Why we say that

Owners generally appreciate the performance and display quality, but the experience is not without compromises. You will notice that the front camera is limited to 1080p video recording, which is a common point of frustration for users. While the 5100 mAh battery offers good endurance, the device relies on gray-import channels in Bangladesh.

This means you get no manufacturer warranty, and any repairs must be handled through the specific shop where you purchased the unit. You should also be prepared for a medium level of bloatware and ads within the software. It is a capable machine for those who prioritize raw power and display brightness, provided you are comfortable with the risks associated with unofficial imports.
