Why we say that

The 7000 mAh cell paired with an 8000‑nit peak brightness screen means you can watch videos outdoors without draining quickly. The Snapdragon 8 Elite and 12 GB RAM deliver smooth performance in most apps and games, though thermal throttling and UFS 3.1 storage keep the top frame rates just short of flagship levels. The 200 MP main sensor with OIS, 3.5× telephoto and ultrawide lens captures detailed photos, but low‑light results are mixed and MagicOS 10 adds noticeable bugs and a subscription model for AI features.

USB ports are limited to USB 2.0, so file transfers feel slow. Official‑warranty units start at ৳95,999, while gray‑import models as low as ৳78,999 lack manufacturer cover and rely on paid repairs. Overall, it feels like a high‑end device with a few costly compromises.
