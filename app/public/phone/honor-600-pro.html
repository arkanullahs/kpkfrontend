Buying verdict

Honor 600 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Honor 600 Pro is a high-priced device that struggles to justify its cost due to significant software bugs and outdated hardware choices.

Choose it if
You prioritize massive battery life and a high-quality display above all other features.
Consider alternatives if
You want a polished software experience and fast data transfer speeds for your professional work.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Industry-leading 7000 mAh battery endurance
  • Extremely bright AMOLED display with thin bezels
  • Versatile 200MP camera with 3.5x telephoto lens
  • Premium IP68/69K build quality

Reported trade-offs

  • Buggy MagicOS 10 software experience
  • Slow USB 2.0 data transfer speeds
  • Outdated UFS 3.1 storage bottleneck
  • Aggressive paid AI subscription model

Why we say that

While the 7000 mAh battery provides industry-leading endurance, the user experience is hampered by a buggy MagicOS 10 interface that relies on aggressive paid AI subscriptions. Owners are particularly frustrated by the inclusion of slow USB 2.0 transfer speeds and UFS 3.1 storage, which creates a bottleneck for a phone at this price point. The design is also frequently criticized for being an iPhone copy.

Although the display is extremely bright and the camera setup is versatile, the overall performance is limited by thermal throttling. You should be aware that the cheaper unofficial units lack manufacturer warranty support, meaning any repairs will be paid services at a service centre.

Does the Honor 600 Pro have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

The brand warranty applies only to the official unit, which is priced at ৳97,499 for the 12GB/256GB variant. Cheaper gray-import units do not come with manufacturer cover, and service centres will only accept them for paid repairs.

Is the Honor 600 Pro good for heavy gaming?

While the phone features a powerful Snapdragon 8 Elite chip, owners report that thermal throttling limits its peak potential. The use of UFS 3.1 storage also acts as a bottleneck for performance during intensive gaming sessions.

How long will the Honor 600 Pro receive software updates?

As a flagship device, the Honor 600 Pro is expected to receive 3 to 4 OS updates. However, users currently report that the MagicOS 10 software is buggy and requires urgent updates to improve stability.

What is the camera performance like on this phone?

The phone features a versatile triple camera setup with a 200MP main sensor and OIS. Owners particularly highlight the 3.5x telephoto lens and the portrait mode as standout features for photography.
