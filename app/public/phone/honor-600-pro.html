Honor 600 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you can tolerate software bugs and a slower charger, the Honor 600 Pro gives you flagship performance at a lower Bangladeshi price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want flagship speed, a huge battery and a high resolution camera while accepting software quirks and limited warranty.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 12 GB RAM with Snapdragon 8 Elite
  • 200 MP main camera with OIS
  • 7 000 mAh battery lasting a day
  • 6.57 inch 120 Hz AMOLED, 8 000 nits peak

Reported trade-offs4

  • MagicOS 10 bugs affect camera and features
  • GPU throttles to about 50% under load
  • 80 W charging feels slower than expected
  • High refresh rate glitches and no LTPO

Why we say that

The 7 000 mAh battery easily lasts a full day of normal use and the 6.57 inch AMOLED can reach a peak of 8 000 nits, making outdoor viewing very clear. The 200 MP main sensor captures great detail and vibrant colours, though the ultra wide lens can look contrasty and the selfie camera lacks autofocus. Snapdragon 8 Elite with 12 GB RAM feels smooth for most apps, but the GPU drops to about 50% of its power during long gaming sessions.

MagicOS 10 brings medium ads and occasional bugs that can affect the camera and other features. The 120 Hz panel sometimes stutters and there is no LTPO. Charging at 80 W works but feels slower than previous generations.

Official units carry a brand warranty, while cheaper gray imports have no manufacturer cover and repairs are paid.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 7 000 mAh cell provides a full day of typical use such as browsing, social media and video playback. It is not the longest lasting battery in its class but it is more than enough for most users.

Can the phone handle heavy gaming without slowdown?

The Snapdragon 8 Elite and 12 GB RAM run most games smoothly at first. Under sustained load the GPU throttles to about 50 percent, so long gaming sessions may feel slower than on a pure flagship.

Is the camera good in low‑light situations?

The 200 MP sensor captures a lot of detail and colour in good light. In low light the main lens remains usable, but the ultra‑wide lens can appear contrasty and the selfie camera lacks autofocus, which may affect night shots.

What warranty do I get if I buy the cheaper gray‑import version?

Gray‑import listings do not include the brand warranty. The unit is covered only by the seller’s policy and any repair at a service centre will be treated as a paid service, unlike the official unit which carries the manufacturer warranty.
