What works well

  • Bright and smooth LTPO AMOLED display
  • Efficient battery life
  • Large 512GB storage
  • Fast 70W charging

Reported trade-offs

  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • Camera focusing issues
  • Fingerprint sensor black screen bug
  • Blurry WhatsApp videos after updates

Why we say that

Owners generally like the battery efficiency and the screen quality. However, living with this device comes with some frustrating bugs. Some users report a black screen issue after using the fingerprint sensor.

There are also reports of blurry WhatsApp videos following a software update. While the camera performance is described as great, you may encounter focusing issues with the main and telephoto lenses. The software experience is hindered by high amounts of bloatware and ads, and some find the system to be slow.
