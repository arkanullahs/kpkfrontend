Is the OPPO Reno15 F 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Phenomenal 7000 mAh battery life
  • Premium aluminum and glass build
  • Fast 80W charging speed
  • Smooth 120Hz AMOLED display

What is not

  • Outdated Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 processor
  • Unstable WiFi and network connectivity
  • Software includes lockscreen ads and bloatware
  • No eSIM support

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize extreme battery endurance above all else and only use your device for basic daily tasks.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a gamer or expect modern performance, as the processor is three years old and struggles with heavy usage.

Why we say that

While the 7000 mAh battery provides phenomenal endurance lasting up to two days, the Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 chipset struggles with modern performance demands. Owners frequently report that the phone feels overpriced for a 2026 release because it uses hardware from 2023. You will find the build quality impressive with its aluminum frame and IP68/69 rating, but the software experience is hindered by persistent lockscreen ads and bloatware.

Connectivity is another pain point, as users have noted unstable WiFi and poor network performance. While the 80W charging is a welcome upgrade, the lack of eSIM support and mediocre camera results make it difficult to justify the ৳56,990 price tag for power users or mobile gamers.
