What works well

  • 7,000 mAh battery lasting up to two days
  • 80 W fast charging fills the tank quickly
  • 6.57‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED screen
  • Premium aluminum frame with glass back and IP68 rating

Reported trade-offs

  • Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 is three‑year‑old
  • Gaming performance is weak
  • Wi‑Fi and network connectivity are unstable
  • No eSIM support

Why we say that

The 7,000 mAh battery truly shines, giving 1.5‑2 days of use and refilling fast with 80 W charging. The premium aluminum frame and glass back feel solid and the IP68 rating adds peace of mind against water. The 6.57‑inch AMOLED panel runs smooth 120 Hz but lacks HDR10+ and typical brightness is modest.

The 50 MP front camera captures detailed selfies and the main sensor delivers decent 4K video, yet low‑light shots are only average. ColorOS 16 runs smoothly but lock‑screen ads and bloatware are noticeable. Network stability is a weak point with frequent Wi‑Fi drops, and the Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 chipset is three years old, making gaming performance poor.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

Owners report the 7,000 mAh cell gives about 1.5 to 2 days of normal use, thanks to efficient power management and the large capacity. Heavy use may reduce that to a single day.

Can I play modern mobile games smoothly on this phone?

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 chipset is three years old, so basic games run fine but demanding titles suffer low frame rates and occasional lag. Expect a sub‑par gaming experience.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures decent photos in daylight and 4K video, but low‑light performance is only average. Images may appear noisy and lack detail compared to newer flagships.
