What works well

  • 5G connectivity
  • 5000 mAh battery lasting all day
  • 25 W fast charging
  • headphone jack and FM radio

Reported trade-offs

  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Sharp back edges feel like paper cut
  • Erratic scrolling and random app openings
  • Overheating during gaming sessions

Why we say that

The 5000 mAh battery easily gets you through work and travel, and the 25 W charger refills it in under an hour. 5G connectivity works well for streaming and browsing, and the large 6.7 inch 90 Hz LCD gives a smooth feel despite its dim 720p resolution. The 50 MP main sensor can capture decent photos in bright light, but tuning issues and zoom problems show in low light.

Owners report occasional overheating when gaming and a persistent erratic scrolling glitch that survives a factory reset. The back panel has very sharp edges that feel like a paper cut. Because every unit is a gray import, there is no official Samsung warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited cover and pay for any repairs.

How long does the battery really last?

The 5000 mAh cell gives you a full day of typical use, and many owners report it easily covering work, travel and light media playback without needing a charge. Heavy gaming will drain it faster.

Can I play games smoothly on this phone?

Light games run fine, but the 4 GB RAM version struggles with more demanding titles and can lag. Users also notice the device heating up during longer sessions, so expect modest performance for casual gaming only.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP sensor captures decent pictures in bright conditions, but in low light the tuning is mediocre and zoom becomes noisy. Owners describe the results as acceptable for casual snaps but not for serious photography.

Will I get an official warranty and how many Android updates?

All listings are gray imports, so Samsung does not provide an official warranty in Bangladesh. The shop offers its own limited cover and you pay for repairs. The phone promises up to four major Android upgrades.
