Is the Samsung Galaxy A06 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 5000 mAh battery lasts a full day
  • 50 MP main camera in bright light
  • 25 W fast charging
  • 5G connectivity

What is not

  • Sharp back edges feel like paper cut
  • 720p LCD dim and scroll glitches
  • 4 GB RAM struggles with games
  • One UI 8.0 bugs persist

Buy it if Buy it if you need an inexpensive 5G phone for basic tasks and occasional photos.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect smooth gaming, high‑end camera performance, or a brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 5000 mAh cell paired with 25 W charging keeps the device running long and refills quickly, though the charger is not included. The Dimensity 6300 chipset handles basic apps and light gaming, but 4 GB RAM struggles with more demanding titles and some users notice heating. The 6.7" 90 Hz PLS LCD is bright enough for indoor use but its 720p resolution appears dim and occasional scrolling glitches have been reported even after a factory reset.

The 50 MP main sensor captures decent photos in good light, yet tuning issues and poor zoom limit its appeal. Build quality feels sturdy with IP54 protection, but the sharp back edges can cause a paper‑cut feeling. Software runs One UI 8.0, which is buggy, but Samsung promises up to four Android upgrades.

The phone arrives as a gray‑import with shop warranty only, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.
