Apple iPhone 18 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you can stretch your budget, the iPhone 18 Pro gives you a bright, fast, and powerful experience but offers little camera progress and feels heavy.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want top‑tier performance, a bright display and can afford the premium price.

What works well4

  • 120 Hz LTPO OLED display
  • 60 W fast charging
  • A20 Pro performance
  • Long battery life

Reported trade-offs3

  • Device feels heavy

Why we say that

The 6.3 inch LTPO Super Retina XDR OLED screen reaches 3000 nits peak brightness and runs at a smooth 120 Hz, making everyday use feel premium. Under the hood, the Apple A20 Pro chip with 12 GB RAM handles gaming and multitasking without lag, and the 4056 mAh battery lasts longer than most phones, topped up by 60 W fast charging. Camera scores are solid at 48 MP, yet owners note hardly any upgrades over the previous model, especially in low‑light.

The phone is heavier than earlier iPhones and costs more than its predecessor, with official warranty only on the higher‑priced listings. Gray‑import units are cheaper but lack manufacturer coverage and rely on paid repairs.

Dave2D · iPhone 18 Pro Max Review - Apple's Hyping The Wrong Thing ↗

Apple is heavily marketing the new variable camera aperture, but the most impressive engineering feat is actually the face-scanning camera flawlessly hidden beneath the display. Beyond that neat hardware trick, the phone delivers incredible sustained performance and battery life thanks to a massive internal cooling upgrade. However, these tangible improvements come at the literal cost of a heavier device and a frustrating price increase across the entire lineup.

Tyler Stalman · iPhone 18 Pro: A Photographer's Review ↗

From a photographer's perspective, this update offers a fantastic suite of professional tools, even if Apple's new built-in film emulation styles fall a bit short of looking like real analog film. The faster lens draws in more light for sharper low-light photos, and the ability to apply background blur to regular video after recording is a massive workflow upgrade. It remains one of the most reliable and capable pocket cameras available for creative professionals.

The Tech Chap · iPhone 18 Pro | Pro Max REVIEW - The Good & Bad (shot on iPhone) ↗

This is a minor, evolutionary update that brings welcome refinements to an already great phone, but it struggles slightly to justify its increased weight and higher price tag. The new cooling system ensures incredibly consistent top-tier performance for gaming, and the screen colors are more accurately calibrated right out of the box. While the physical variable aperture camera doesn't drastically change daily image quality, the ability to add cinematic blur to everyday videos is a major standout feature.

How long will the iPhone 18 Pro battery last on a typical day?

The 4056 mAh battery is rated for longer daily use than most smartphones, and a 60 W charger can refill it quickly. Users report the phone lasting through a full day of mixed usage without needing a mid‑day top‑up.

Is the iPhone 18 Pro good for gaming?
