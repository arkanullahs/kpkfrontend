Honor Magic 7 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Honor Magic 7 Pro is a powerful device that excels in raw performance but struggles with software reliability and camera consistency.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize raw gaming power and a high-quality display above all else.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on consistent notifications or want a reliable camera and timely software support.

What works well4

  • Flawless Snapdragon 8 Elite performance
  • Stunning 5000 nits peak brightness display
  • Durable build with IP69 rating
  • Excellent 3D face unlock

Reported trade-offs4

  • Inconsistent camera exposure and zoom
  • Persistent post-update battery drain
  • Bloatware and notification management bugs
  • Slow and infrequent software updates

Why we say that

Powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite, it handles gaming and heavy tasks with ease, maintaining excellent thermal management. The 6.8 inch LTPO OLED display is a highlight, offering 5000 nits peak brightness and industry-leading eye-care features. However, living with the phone reveals significant frustrations.

Owners frequently report annoying bloatware and notification bugs that prevent apps from alerting them properly. The camera system, while versatile on paper, often produces inconsistent exposure and pixelated zoom shots. Battery life is generally good with a 5850 mAh capacity, but users report post-update drain issues where the phone loses 10 percent of its charge overnight.

Read full reasoning

Software updates are slow, often arriving late with only minor security patches. Since this is a gray-import unit, you receive no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh, and you must verify if your specific unit includes Google Mobile Services before purchasing.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you only receive whatever limited coverage the individual shop provides. All repairs must be paid for out of your own pocket.

How does the battery perform for daily use?

The 5850 mAh battery offers good endurance for most users. However, owners report significant post-update battery drain issues. Some users have noted losing almost 10 percent of their battery charge overnight after installing software updates.

Is the camera reliable for photography?

The camera hardware is versatile but inconsistent in practice. Owners report that the ultra-wide and regular wide lenses do not match well. Images often suffer from over-processing, and zoom output is frequently pixelated and unreliable.

Will I get Google apps on this device?

The Bangladesh market contains both global and China-market versions. Global units come with full Google services, while China-market units do not. You must verify with the seller that Google Mobile Services works on your specific unit before paying.
