Buying verdict

Honor Magic 7 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you can live with a gray‑import warranty, the Honor Magic 7 Pro feels like a flagship in daily use but shows its cracks after software updates.

Choose it if
You want flagship speed, a premium screen and fast charging and are comfortable with a shop‑only warranty and occasional software quirks.
Consider alternatives if

What works well

  • 120 Hz LTPO OLED display with 5000‑nit peak
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite gives top gaming performance
  • 100 W wired and 80 W wireless fast charging
  • Premium build with IP69 and Nano crystal glass

Reported trade-offs

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Software updates are slow and inconsistent
  • Battery drain reported after Android 16 update
  • Telephoto lens lacks crispness

Why we say that

The 6.8‑inch LTPO OLED screen dazzles with 120 Hz refresh and 5000‑nit peak brightness, while the Snapdragon 8 Elite keeps games buttery smooth and the phone cool. Charging is blistering fast at 100 W wired and 80 W wireless, and the 5850 mAh battery usually lasts a day, though owners report noticeable drain after the Android 16 upgrade. Build quality is premium with IP69 protection and Nano crystal glass, but the telephoto lens lacks sharpness and AI processing can look over‑processed.

Software carries medium bloat and updates arrive slowly, sometimes causing battery issues. Because every unit is a gray import, there is no official Honor warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s limited cover and pay for any repairs. Overall, it delivers high performance and a great display, but the software and warranty situation may deter risk‑averse buyers.
