Honor Magic 7 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Honor Magic 7 Pro is a powerful device that excels in raw performance and display quality, but it comes with significant software and support risks for Bangladeshi buyers.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You are a power user who prioritizes raw gaming performance and display quality over software consistency and official support.
Consider alternatives if
You want a reliable phone with a manufacturer warranty and consistent software updates that do not degrade battery life.

Why we say that

While the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset delivers top-tier gaming and thermal management, owners frequently report frustration with inconsistent software updates and bloatware. The camera system produces a great main image, yet the telephoto lens often lacks the crispness users expect at this price point. Battery life is generally strong, but recent reports indicate significant drain issues following the Android 16 update.

Because every unit in Bangladesh is a gray import, you receive no manufacturer warranty, only whatever limited coverage the individual shop provides. You must also be careful to verify if you are purchasing a global unit with Google services or a China-market version that requires manual installation. Given that the newer Honor Magic 8 Pro is available for ৳1,15,999, this model is a difficult recommendation for most buyers.

Does this phone come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every listing is a gray import, meaning you only receive whatever limited coverage the specific shop promises. All repairs must be paid for out of pocket.

How is the battery life on the Honor Magic 7 Pro?

The 5850 mAh battery is generally strong for daily use. However, many owners report significant battery drain issues after updating the device to Android 16. You should consider this potential software bug before purchasing.

Will I get Google services on this device?

It depends on the specific unit you buy. Some shops sell the global version which includes full Google services, while others sell the China-market version that does not. You must verify that Google Mobile Services works on your specific unit before paying.

Is this phone good for heavy gaming?

Yes, the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset provides top-tier smoothness and sustained gaming frames. Owners rate the gaming performance very highly at 9.5 out of 10, noting that the phone maintains excellent thermal management and stays cool during intensive tasks.
