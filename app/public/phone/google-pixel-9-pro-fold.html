Is the Google Pixel 9 Pro Fold worth buying?

What is good

  • Stunningly bright 8.0 inch foldable display
  • Cleanest foldable software experience
  • Elite 7 year Android update promise
  • Minimal crease on the main screen

What is not

  • Extremely slow 21W charging speed
  • Heavy throttling during prolonged gaming
  • Risk of front screen crashing
  • No official service support in Bangladesh

Buy it if You are a dedicated fan of stock Android who prioritizes a thin, premium foldable design and long-term software support over gaming performance.

Skip it if You need a reliable device for heavy gaming, fast charging, or require an official brand warranty for your peace of mind.

Why we say that

While the display is stunning and the software is clean, owners report serious issues including front screen crashes after updates and heavy throttling during gaming. The 21W charging speed is widely criticized as being far too slow for a device at this price point. You are relying entirely on shop mercy for repairs because there is no official Google service presence in Bangladesh.

The camera processing is good, but the sensors are smaller than those found on Pro slab models, leading to soft details. With the newer Google Pixel 10 Pro Fold available for 149,999 taka, this specific model struggles to justify its 179,999 taka unofficial price tag. Living with this phone means accepting a device that prioritizes a thin, premium feel over consistent performance and reliable charging.

What is the Google Pixel 9 Pro Fold price in Bangladesh?

Google Pixel 9 Pro Fold costs ৳1,79,999 in Bangladesh, in stock at 1 shop we track as of 2026-08-30.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have an official brand warranty. It is only available as gray-import stock, meaning you must rely on the shop's warranty. Google has little to no official service presence in the country, so any repairs are entirely at the mercy of the seller.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

This device is supported by an elite 7-year update promise. It comes with Android 14 and is upgradable to Android 16, with a commitment for up to 7 major Android upgrades to keep the software current for a long time.

Is the Google Pixel 9 Pro Fold good for gaming?

Gaming performance is a weak point for this device. Owners report that it is smooth for daily use, but it suffers from heavy throttling and drops in frame rates during heavy games. Prolonged stress tests show significant heating issues.

What should I know about the battery and charging?

The battery provides decent endurance, but the 21W charging speed is widely hated by owners for being very slow compared to rivals. There are also reports of wireless coil alignment issues and general frustration with the slow charging technology.
