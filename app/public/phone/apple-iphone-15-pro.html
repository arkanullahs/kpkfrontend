Why we say that

The iPhone 15 Pro runs the A17 Pro processor and a 6.1 inch LTPO Super Retina XDR OLED panel that reaches 2000 nits and refreshes at 120 Hz, delivering smooth visuals. The 48 MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture, optical image stabilization and 3× telephoto produces sharp photos, though video can show halo from point lights. Battery capacity is only 3274 mAh and many users report three to four hours of active use after the iOS 26.0.1 update, while the included 27 W charger fills the phone slowly and no charger comes in the box.

USB‑C charging can be unreliable with third‑party cables and the ProMotion display sometimes skips frames. At ৳2,20,000 the phone includes Apple’s official warranty and can be serviced through the local network, but being three years old means only a short software‑update window remains.

Will the iPhone 15 Pro still receive software updates?

The iPhone 15 Pro launched in 2023 with iOS 17 and can be upgraded to iOS 26.6. Being three years old, it is likely to receive only a few more major updates before support ends.
