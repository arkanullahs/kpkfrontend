Is the Apple iPhone 15 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Excellent 24MP default camera photos
  • High performance A17 Pro chipset
  • Bright and color-accurate 120Hz display
  • Lightweight and comfortable titanium frame

What is not

  • Severe battery drain after iOS 26.0.1
  • Unreliable USB-C charging with third-party cables
  • Expensive compared to newer models
  • Occasional display stutter and lens flare

Buy it if You are a dedicated Apple user who prioritizes a lightweight titanium build and high-end camera performance despite the known battery and software limitations.

Skip it if You want a reliable device with good battery life or are looking for better value, as the newer iPhone 16 Pro is cheaper.

Why we say that

Living with this phone involves managing a small 3,274 mAh battery that suffers from severe drain issues following the iOS 26.0.1 update, often resulting in only 3 to 4 hours of active use. While the A17 Pro chipset handles console-grade gaming with high efficiency, users frequently report random overheating and occasional frame skips due to ProMotion display stutter. The titanium frame feels light and comfortable, but the device is plagued by USB-C charging reliability issues where many third-party cables fail to charge the phone properly.

You will also encounter dramatic lens flare when shooting video. Because this model is now 3 years old, you should expect very short or no remaining software update life, making it a difficult recommendation for anyone seeking long-term value.
