Honor Magic Vs3 5G Is it worth buying?

If you want a foldable with a bright 120 Hz screen and strong day‑to‑day performance, the Honor Magic Vs3 5G delivers that at a mid‑range price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a premium foldable with strong performance and can accept average camera results and a shop‑provided warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need top‑tier gaming, a reliable brand warranty, or a standout low‑light camera.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 7.9‑inch 120 Hz foldable LTPO AMOLED
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 delivers smooth performance
  • 5000 mAh battery lasts a full day
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS and 5× telephoto

Reported trade-offs4

  • Camera quality only average
  • Gaming frame rates drop under heat
  • No official Honor warranty
  • Medium level ads in UI

Why we say that

The 7.92‑inch LTPO AMOLED folds smoothly and shows vivid colors thanks to Dolby Vision and a billion‑color palette. Under the hood the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 on a 4 nm process keeps apps and multitasking responsive, and owners rate performance 7.5 out of 10. Battery life is solid; the 5000 mAh cell usually lasts a full day of mixed use.

The camera package includes a 50 MP main sensor with OIS and 5× telephoto, but real‑world photos are only average and the camera island design feels odd to many users. Gaming runs okay but frame rates dip when the device heats up. The software is Android 14 with MagicOS 8.0.1, offering a clean UI but medium‑level ads and about three to four major OS updates.

Read full reasoning

The biggest drawback is the lack of an official Honor warranty; you must rely on the shop’s warranty after checking IMEI and BTRC status.

How long does the battery last on daily use?

Is the phone good for gaming?

Gaming on the device is decent. The Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset delivers solid frame rates, but owners note that sustained performance drops when the phone gets hot, giving it a rating of 6 out of 10 for gaming.
