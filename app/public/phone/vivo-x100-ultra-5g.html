Reported trade-offs

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED panel reaches a peak of 3000 nits and keeps motion smooth at 120 Hz, making scrolling and video look vivid. The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 together with 12 GB RAM handles apps and games without stutter, though the gaming score of 7.5 shows the most demanding titles may not run at maximum settings. The 5500 mAh battery gives good endurance and the 80W charger fills it reasonably fast, but measured charging speed is not very competitive.

The camera setup impresses with a 50 MP main sensor, optical image stabilization and a 3.7× telephoto lens, and selfies are sharp thanks to a 50 MP front unit. Build quality is durable and water resistant, but the device arrives only as a gray import, so you must rely on a shop warranty and confirm IMEI and BTRC status. Software runs Android 14 with OriginOS 5 and will upgrade to Android 15, while medium bloat and ads are present.

Vivo X100 Ultra 5G: what buyers ask us

What is the Vivo X100 Ultra 5G price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Vivo X100 Ultra 5G in Bangladesh was ৳1,20,990, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 5500 mAh battery provides good endurance and owners report it easily lasts a full day of mixed use. Charging is efficient but not the fastest, so you will need several hours to reach a full charge.

Is the phone good for gaming?

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 paired with 12 GB RAM delivers smooth performance and the 120 Hz display keeps motion fluid. Owner ratings give gaming an 7.5, so it handles most titles well but may lag on the most demanding games.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The main camera uses a 50 MP sensor with OIS and a 3.7× telephoto lens and reviewers call the telephoto spectacular. In low light situations the large sensor and f/1.8 aperture give decent results, though noise can appear at very high ISO.

Will I get an official warranty and software updates?

There is no manufacturer warranty listed for the gray import version, so you will rely on the shop warranty and must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller. The device runs Android 14, is upgradable to Android 15 and will receive about two major OS updates.
