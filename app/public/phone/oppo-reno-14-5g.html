OPPO Reno 14 5G Is it worth buying?

The OPPO Reno 14 5G is a premium-built device that struggles to justify its high price tag due to its mid-range chipset.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize a durable build, long battery life, and a premium design over raw gaming performance.
Consider alternatives if
You are a mobile gamer or a power user who expects high-end performance for your money.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Massive 6000 mAh battery capacity
  • Premium aluminum frame with IP68/69 rating
  • Strong main camera and portrait performance
  • Fast 80W charging speed

Reported trade-offs4

  • Extreme official overpricing for the chipset
  • Noticeable heating during gaming and recording
  • Software filled with bloatware and ads
  • Weak 8MP ultrawide camera performance

Why we say that

While the 6000 mAh battery provides reliable power for a full day of heavy use, owners are frustrated by the significant performance gap between the cost and the hardware provided. The phone features a high-quality aluminum frame and a rare IP68/69 rating, making it physically durable. However, users frequently report that the software experience is marred by excessive bloatware, unnecessary games, and useless notifications.

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 processor is widely considered inadequate for the official price point, leading to noticeable heating during gaming and 4K video recording. While the main camera and portrait mode perform well, the weak ultrawide lens and auto-mode ripples disappoint those expecting flagship-level photography. Ultimately, buyers feel the official pricing does not align with the internal specifications, making it a difficult recommendation for those prioritizing raw performance.

Does the 6000 mAh battery really last all day?

Yes, owners report that the massive 6000 mAh battery is capable of lasting through a full day of heavy use. You can also rely on the 80W charging system to reach a full charge in about an hour.

Is the OPPO Reno 14 5G good for gaming?

The phone is not ideal for heavy gaming. While daily tasks remain smooth, the Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 chipset struggles with frame drops in demanding titles like Genshin Impact and generates noticeable heat through the metal frame.

How many software updates will this phone receive?

The device runs on Android 15 and ColorOS 15. As a flagship-tier release, it is expected to receive 3 to 4 OS updates over its lifespan, though users should be prepared for pre-installed bloatware and ads.
