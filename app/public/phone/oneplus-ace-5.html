Why we say that

The OnePlus Ace 5 delivers flagship‑level performance and a vivid LTPO AMOLED screen that reaches 4500 nits in bright sunlight. Multitasking feels smooth with up to 16 GB RAM and storage options up to 1 TB. Gaming runs well thanks to the high refresh rate and strong chipset.

The 50 MP main camera captures detailed shots, though low‑light performance is average. Battery life is a common complaint; owners report the 6415 mAh cell depletes quickly, especially after a year of use. Some users also experience touchscreen unresponsiveness a few days after the Android 15 update.

Read full reasoning

The phone arrives as a gray‑import, so you must rely on shop warranty and verify IMEI/BTRC status before purchase. Overall, it suits power users who can tolerate warranty uncertainty and manage battery habits.

Will the battery last a full day of heavy use?

Owners rate battery life at 6.5 out of 10 and report fast drain, especially after a year. Heavy use will likely require a charge before the day ends, so plan for extra power.

Is there any official warranty for the OnePlus Ace 5 in Bangladesh?

No official brand warranty is recorded for this model in Bangladesh. The phone is only available as a gray‑import, so you must rely on the shop warranty and verify IMEI/BTRC status with the seller.

How many Android updates will this phone receive?

The device ships with Android 15 and ColorOS 15, and OnePlus typically provides four OS updates for its flagships. Expect several years of software support, though update speed may vary.

Is the touchscreen reliable after the latest OS update?

Some owners report the touchscreen randomly stops working a few days after the Android 15 update. This issue appears after 2‑3 days of use, so consider the risk before buying.
