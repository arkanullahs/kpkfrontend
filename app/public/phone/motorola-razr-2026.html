Is the Motorola Razr 2026 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.9" foldable LTPO AMOLED 120Hz
  • 3000‑nit peak brightness
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • 5G support and Dimensity 7450X

What is not

  • Shop warranty instead of brand warranty
  • Historically weak Android updates
  • Only gray‑import stock available
  • 30 W charging is modest

Buy it if Buy it if you want a bright foldable with good camera and can accept a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need fast charging, strong official warranty, or guaranteed long‑term software updates.

Why we say that

The 6.9" LTPO AMOLED panel hits 3000 nits at peak and runs smooth at 120Hz, making media and scrolling pleasant. The 50 MP main sensor with a 1/1.95" size, f/1.7 aperture and optical image stabilization captures clear photos in daylight and reasonable shots in low light. The Dimensity 7450X paired with 8 GB RAM handles everyday apps and moderate gaming without stutter, though the 4800 mAh battery and 30 W charger mean you will need a midday top‑up on heavy use.

Android 16 with near‑stock Hello UI feels clean, but the device historically receives only one to three major OS updates. The biggest practical concern is that only gray‑import stock is available, so you get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.
