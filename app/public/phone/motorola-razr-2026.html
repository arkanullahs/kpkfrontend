Motorola Razr 2026 Is it worth buying?

If you want a large foldable with a bright screen and solid camera, the Razr 2026 delivers that but comes with trade‑offs.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs

Reported trade-offs3

  • Only gray‑import stock, no brand warranty

Why we say that

The 6.9 inch LTPO AMOLED panel shows vivid colours and reaches a peak brightness of 3000 nits, making outdoor use easy. The Dimensity 7450X chipset handles everyday apps smoothly and supports 5G, while the 8GB RAM and up to 256GB storage give enough room for media. Photography is a strong point thanks to the 50MP main sensor with OIS and a 32MP selfie shooter.

Battery life feels average; the 4800 mAh cell lasts a day with moderate use and refills with 30W charging. Software runs Android 16 with a near stock Hello UI, but the brand has a history of delivering only one to three OS upgrades. The phone is only available as gray‑import stock, so you must rely on shop warranty and verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 4800 mAh battery provides about a day of moderate use. Heavy gaming or high screen brightness will drain it faster, but the 30W charger can refill it quickly.

Is the phone good for mobile gaming?

The Dimensity 7450X chipset and 8GB RAM handle most games at smooth frame rates. The 120Hz foldable screen adds visual fluidity, though the battery may need topping up after long sessions.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50MP main sensor with a large 1/1.95" size and f/1.7 aperture captures decent low‑light shots, and optical image stabilization helps reduce blur. The ultrawide lens also works reasonably well in dim conditions.

Will I get a manufacturer warranty and regular updates?

Only gray‑import stock is available, so you rely on shop warranty rather than a brand warranty. The device historically receives one to three Android version updates, which is less than many competitors.
