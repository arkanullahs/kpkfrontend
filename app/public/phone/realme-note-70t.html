Is the Realme Note 70T worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6000 mAh battery capacity
  • Smooth 90Hz display refresh rate
  • Includes a dedicated headphone jack
  • Runs on modern Android 15

What is not

  • Slow 15W charging speed
  • Only available as gray-import stock
  • High levels of bloatware and ads
  • Basic 13MP and 5MP camera sensors

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a long-lasting battery for basic daily tasks and prefer having a headphone jack.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you want an official brand warranty, fast charging, or high-quality camera performance.

Why we say that

Living with this phone means you will rarely reach for a charger thanks to the 6000 mAh capacity, but the 15W charging speed is quite slow for such a large cell. The Unisoc T7250 chipset handles basic tasks, though users should expect medium to high levels of bloatware and ads within the Realme UI 5.0 interface. Since this model is currently only available as gray-import stock, you will have to rely on a shop warranty rather than an official brand warranty.

It is essential to verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your money. While the 90Hz display makes scrolling feel smooth, the 13MP main camera and 5MP selfie shooter are strictly for basic photography. This is a utilitarian device for those who value longevity and a headphone jack over high-end features or official support.

Does the Realme Note 70T come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is currently only found as gray-import stock. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, so you must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

How long will the Realme Note 70T receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 15 with Realme UI 5.0 out of the box. You can expect to receive between 2 to 3 OS updates over the lifespan of the phone.

Is the battery life on the Realme Note 70T good for heavy use?

The phone features a large 6000 mAh battery which is designed to last a long time. However, keep in mind that the charging speed is limited to 15W, which will take a significant amount of time to fully recharge.

Can I use the Realme Note 70T for gaming?

The phone is powered by the Unisoc T7250 chipset. While it is capable of handling basic daily tasks and general usage, it is not designed for high-end gaming or intensive performance requirements.
