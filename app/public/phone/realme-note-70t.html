  • Slow 15W charging

Why we say that

It comes with a 6000 mAh battery that lasts a long time, but the 15W charging is slow. You will deal with medium to high levels of bloatware and ads because it is a budget device. The Unisoc T7250 chipset and 4GB RAM are basic for daily tasks.

Be careful with your purchase because only gray import stock is available. This means you get a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty. You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying.
