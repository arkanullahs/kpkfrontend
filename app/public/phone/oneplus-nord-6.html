Is the OnePlus Nord 6 worth buying?

What is good

  • 10‑12 hour screen on battery
  • 165 Hz AMOLED display with 3600 nit peak
  • 80 W fast charging with 27 W reverse
  • Clean OxygenOS 16 with low bloat

What is not

  • Screen flickers while charging
  • Low light camera performance poor
  • Confusing SIM and microphone hole placement
  • Full plastic build feels cheap

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize long battery life, a high refresh rate display and fast charging and can accept modest camera results.

Skip it if Skip it if you need top tier low light photography, a premium metal feel, or a flawless charging experience.

Why we say that

The 7500 mAh battery gives about ten to twelve hours of screen on time and the 80 W charger fills it in under an hour, while reverse charging at 27 W can top up accessories. The 6.78 inch AMOLED panel runs at 165 Hz and can peak at 3600 nits, making it easy to see in bright sunlight. The Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset provides smooth performance and stable thermals in games like PUBG and Genshin, though it is a step behind the 15R flagships.

Daylight photos are decent thanks to a 50 MP main sensor with OIS, but low light shots are disappointing and the ultrawide lens is only average. The phone is built from full plastic, carries an IP69K rating, but the port layout and SIM‑mic holes are confusing. OxygenOS 16 is clean, low on bloat and promises four OS updates.

At an unofficial price of ৳55,499 the value feels stretched, especially with reported screen flickering while charging.
