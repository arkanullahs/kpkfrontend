What works well3

  • 6.8 inch 120 Hz LTPO OLED display
  • 50 MP main camera with optical zoom
  • Stock Android with seven year updates

Reported trade-offs3

  • Battery drains faster than top flagships
  • Gaming performance throttles with heat
  • Loudspeaker too weak for calls

Why we say that

The 6.8 inch LTPO OLED panel reaches 3000 nits at peak, so outdoor visibility is excellent. The 50 MP main sensor with optical zoom produces sharp stills and the 42 MP selfie camera is also strong. Stock Android runs cleanly and Google promises seven years of updates, with upgrades up to Android 16.

Battery life is decent for normal use but will not outlast the best flagships, and heavy gaming triggers thermal throttling that reduces performance. Video recording and stabilization feel weaker than expected, and the call speaker is too quiet for loud environments. Overall the phone feels premium and reliable for everyday tasks, but it shows its limits when pushed for gaming, long video sessions, or when a strong speaker is needed.
