Is the Google Pixel 9 Pro XL worth buying?

What is good

  • Industry-leading display brightness and color accuracy
  • Clean, bloat-free software with 7 years of updates
  • Excellent still photo quality with natural processing
  • Premium and durable flat panel design

What is not

  • Thermal throttling during heavy gaming tasks
  • Video quality and stabilization issues
  • Weak loudspeaker performance
  • Battery life trails behind flagship competitors

Buy it if You prioritize a clean software experience, long-term update support, and high-quality still photography over gaming performance.

Skip it if You are a heavy mobile gamer or rely on your phone for high-quality video recording and loud media playback.

Why we say that

Living with this device means enjoying a clean software interface and excellent still photography, though you must be prepared for thermal throttling during intensive tasks. Owners report that the video quality and stabilization are not up to scratch, which is a notable drawback for a flagship device. The loudspeaker performance is also a point of frustration, as the call speaker is too weak to function effectively as a secondary loudspeaker.

While the battery life has seen improvements over previous models, it still trails behind other top-tier competitors. You should be aware that the unofficial units are built for US and Canada markets, meaning the charger plug and box contents will reflect those regions rather than local standards.

Does the Google Pixel 9 Pro XL come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

The brand warranty applies only to the official unit, which is priced at ৳1,07,999 for the 16GB/128GB variant. Cheaper gray-import listings have no manufacturer cover, and any service centre that accepts them will treat repairs as paid services.

How long will the Google Pixel 9 Pro XL receive software updates?

This device runs on Android 14 and is eligible for up to 7 major Android upgrades. This industry-leading support ensures your phone remains current with the latest software features for many years.

Is the Google Pixel 9 Pro XL suitable for heavy gaming?

While the Tensor G4 chipset is smooth for daily tasks, owners report that it throttles under gaming pressure. Users have specifically noted that the device tends to overheat during heavy gaming sessions.

How does the camera perform for photography and video?

The camera is excellent for still photos with natural processing, earning an owner rating of 8.8. However, the video quality and stabilization are reported to be not up to scratch, and the portrait mode lacks hardware depth.
