Google Pixel 9 Pro XL Is it worth buying?

The Google Pixel 9 Pro XL offers a premium experience with a standout display, but it falls short for users who prioritize heavy gaming or top-tier video production.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a premium, bloat-free Android experience with long-term software support and a high-quality display.
Consider alternatives if
You are a heavy mobile gamer or someone who relies on high-quality video recording and strong resale value.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Industry-leading display brightness and color accuracy
  • Clean and bloat-free stock Android software
  • Seven years of major OS update support
  • Solid battery endurance for daily tasks

Reported trade-offs4

  • Poor resale value compared to competitors
  • Throttling and lag during heavy gaming
  • Underwhelming video and portrait processing
  • Fingerprint sensor requires a hard press

Why we say that

Living with this device means enjoying a clean, bloat-free software environment that promises seven years of updates. However, owners frequently report that the Tensor G4 chipset struggles with heavy gaming, leading to throttling and lag. While the 5060 mAh battery provides the best endurance in Pixel history, it is not class-leading.

The camera captures great stills, yet users are often disappointed by underwhelming portrait and video processing. You will also need to press the fingerprint sensor quite hard to get it to work. Resale value is another significant concern, as it drops drastically compared to competitors like Samsung or iPhone.

Read full reasoning

If you value a premium build and a clean interface over gaming performance and resale value, this phone is a solid daily companion.
