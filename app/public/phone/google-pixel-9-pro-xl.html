Is the Google Pixel 9 Pro XL worth buying?

What is good

  • 3000‑nit peak brightness display
  • 50 MP main camera with optical zoom
  • Tensor G4 handles daily tasks smoothly
  • Seven years of Android updates

What is not

  • Gaming performance throttles under heat
  • Video quality and stabilization weak
  • Loudspeaker too weak for calls
  • Battery life only average

Buy it if Buy it if you want a premium screen, strong still‑photo camera and long‑term software support.

Skip it if Skip it if you need top gaming performance, strong speaker output or the longest possible battery endurance.

Why we say that

The 6.8 inch LTPO OLED panel reaches a 3000‑nit peak and shows colors that stay true even in bright sunlight. Daily tasks run smooth thanks to the Tensor G4 and 16 GB of RAM, and the stock Android experience stays clean with no ads. Still photos are sharp and natural, especially with the 50 MP main sensor and 5× telephoto lens.

Battery life is decent but not class‑leading; a full day of mixed use is common, yet heavy gaming drains it faster. Gaming performance drops when the device heats up, and the loudspeaker is too weak for loud calls. Video recording lacks the stability and quality expected from a flagship.

Owners appreciate the premium build and the promise of seven years of Android updates, but they note the thermal throttling and weak speaker as real annoyances.
