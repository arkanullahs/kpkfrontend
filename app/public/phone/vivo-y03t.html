Is the Vivo Y03t worth buying?

What is good

  • Includes a dedicated headphone jack
  • Features a built-in FM radio
  • Supports expandable storage via SD card
  • Smooth 90Hz IPS LCD display

What is not

  • Frequent system lags and freezes
  • Poor camera quality
  • Very low gaming performance
  • Slow 15W charging speed

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a basic device for simple tasks and value the inclusion of a headphone jack and FM radio.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you play games, take many photos, or require a fast and responsive interface without lags.

Why we say that

While it handles basic tasks, the low gaming rating of 2.0 and performance score of 3.0 suggest it is not built for heavy lifting. The 13MP camera is a significant point of contention, with users expressing disappointment in its output quality. Owners appreciate the inclusion of a headphone jack and FM radio, finding these features useful for their daily needs.

The software experience includes a medium amount of bloat and ads, which may impact the overall feel of the interface. With a battery rating of 6.0, it provides standard power, but the 15W charging speed is quite slow for a 5000 mAh cell. If you prioritize a smooth experience or high-quality photography, this device will likely fall short of your expectations.
