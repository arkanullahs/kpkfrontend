Why we say that

Owners report that the phone lags and freezes during use. While some users find it does what it needs to do, others find it slow. The camera quality is a major point of disappointment, with one user explicitly stating it is not good.

Performance and gaming ratings are very low, suggesting this device is not for power users. You get a 90Hz screen and a large battery, but the actual day to day speed is lacking. It is a simple device for basic needs, but the hardware struggles to keep up with modern demands.
