What works well

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 50 MP main camera captures detailed photos
  • 6.9‑inch 120 Hz LCD screen feels smooth
  • Headphone jack for wired audio

Reported trade-offs

  • eMMC storage causes noticeable lag
  • Unisoc T7250 processor feels weak
  • Two rear cameras appear to be fake
  • Notch design looks outdated

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh battery is the standout feature and owners rate it very highly. The 6.9‑inch IPS LCD with 120 Hz refresh feels smooth, but the notch design feels dated and many users dislike it. The 50 MP main sensor can capture detailed shots, yet the two extra rear lenses look like fake Samsung copies and perform poorly in low light.

Performance suffers because the Unisoc T7250 chip and eMMC storage create noticeable lag, especially in games. Software runs on Nebula AIOS / MyOS and only receives one or two Android version updates, with a medium amount of bloat and ads. Overall the phone is usable for basic tasks and long media consumption, but it falls short for heavy gaming, fast multitasking, and serious photography.
