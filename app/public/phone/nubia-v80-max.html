Is the Nubia V80 Max worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • Headphone jack for wired audio
  • 50 MP main camera works in daylight
  • 16 MP selfie camera

What is not

  • Low HD+ display looks dim outdoors
  • Unisoc chip and eMMC cause lag
  • Low‑light photos are noisy

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, a big screen and a headphone jack on a tight budget.

Skip it if Skip it if you want smooth gaming, a high‑resolution display or good low‑light photography.

Why we say that

You get a massive 6000 mAh cell that easily lasts a full day of heavy use and even supports reverse charging. The 6.9‑inch screen is huge but only HD+ and looks dim under bright sunlight, and the outdated dewdrop notch feels out of place in 2026. The Unisoc T7250 processor together with eMMC 5.1 storage struggles with multitasking and heavy games, so you will notice lag in demanding apps.

The 50 MP main camera produces decent photos in daylight but becomes noisy and hunts focus in low‑light scenes. Android 16 runs on a clean MyOS skin but only promises one to two major OS updates and includes medium‑level ads. Overall the phone feels rugged with a headphone jack and solid build, yet it falls short on performance, display quality, and modern features for its price.

Tech to the Point - TTP · ১৭ হাজারে কেমন ফোন? Nubia V80 Max Full Bangla Review ↗

Tech to the Point concludes that while the Nubia V80 Max delivers reliable battery life, a gigantic 6.9-inch display, and a sturdy body, its asking price of 17,000 BDT is tough to swallow given the entry-level Unisoc chipset, slow storage, and outdated dewdrop notch. The reviewer noted that performance struggles under heavy multitasking, the single speaker lacks depth, and the cameras suffer badly in low light. Overall, the channel views it as an acceptable option for simple daily use and casual video watching, but advises that it is only good value if purchased closer to 14,000 to 15,000 BDT.

YugaTech · nubia V80 Max Review | Only worth it when on sale? ↗

YugaTech found the Nubia V80 Max to be a durable everyday workhorse with standout 6,000 mAh battery endurance, but determined that it is severely overpriced at its standard retail price compared to the competition. The reviewer pointed out significant compromises, including an HD+ display limited to 720p streaming, slow memory that bogs down when full, unplayable heavy gaming, and soft low-light photography without video stabilization. They ultimately concluded that buyers should only pick up this phone if they can grab it during early-bird discounts or promotional sales.

Myke Soon · The Unstoppable Budget Phone? nubia V80 Max Unboxing & First Impressions with Setup ↗

Myke Soon shares an optimistic first impression of the Nubia V80 Max, highlighting its modern styling, solid drop-resistant construction, and expansive 6.9-inch 120Hz screen. He praises the 6,000 mAh battery and bypass charging feature for long media sessions, while noting the 50MP main sensor captures adequate images under good daylight. The reviewer concludes that the V80 Max makes for an accessible, approachable budget device ideal as a starter phone for students, seniors who need a large display, or anyone needing a dependable secondary handset.
