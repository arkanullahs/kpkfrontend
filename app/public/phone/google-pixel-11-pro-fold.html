Is the Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold worth buying?

What is good

  • 8‑inch 120 Hz LTPO OLED display
  • 48 MP main camera with optical image stabilization
  • 16 GB RAM and up to 1 TB storage
  • Stock Android with up to seven OS updates

What is not

  • Only gray‑import stock with shop warranty
  • No official Google service centers in Bangladesh
  • 30 W charging is modest for a large battery

Buy it if Buy it if you want a high‑end foldable with pure Android and can handle limited official support.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a cheap phone or rely on brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 8.0‑inch LTPO OLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 3600 nits, so outdoor use feels clear even in strong sunlight. Google’s Tensor G6 chipset together with 16 GB of RAM makes multitasking and gaming feel responsive, though the 30 W charger is modest for a 4806 mAh battery. The 48 MP main camera, equipped with OIS and a 5× telephoto lens, produces detailed photos and handles low‑light scenes well.

Stock Android 17 arrives with a promise of up to seven major OS updates, giving long‑term software support. The main drawback is the lack of an official Google warranty or service centre in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s warranty and must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status before buying. At 216,999 taka the phone sits in the high‑end price range, so weigh the premium features against the service uncertainty.
