Buying verdict

Infinix Smart 10 Is it worth buying?

The Infinix Smart 10 gives you a full day and a half of battery for a very low price, but you should not expect fast charging or high‑end gaming.

Choose it if
You need a cheap phone with long battery life and basic daily functions.
Consider alternatives if
You want fast charging, smooth high‑refresh gaming, or strong low‑light photos.

What works well

  • 5000 mAh battery lasting ~1.5 days for light use
  • 6.67‑inch display with 120 Hz capability
  • Clean matte design with IP64 rating
  • Headphone jack, FM radio and IR blaster

Reported trade-offs

  • Slow 10 W charging takes over 1.5 hours
  • 8 MP camera poor in low light
  • Heavy games lag and refresh drops to 90 Hz

Why we say that

At ৳13,999 it arrives with a 6.67‑inch IPS LCD that can reach 120 Hz, although in real use the refresh often falls to 90 Hz, so the smoothness is inconsistent. The matte, iPhone‑like body feels solid and even carries an IP64 rating, which is rare at this price. The 5000 mAh cell easily lasts about 1.5 days for light users, and the 15 W charger is limited to a 10 W adapter that needs more than an hour and a half to fill the battery.

The 8 MP rear sensor captures acceptable daylight shots but struggles with detail and noise in low light. Gaming beyond casual titles feels laggy, and the Go edition of Android 15 brings useful AI tools but also many pre‑installed apps and ads. Overall it is a decent everyday phone if you value battery life and basic features over performance.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.
