Is the Tecno Camon 30 worth buying?

What is good

  • 50MP main camera with OIS
  • 120Hz AMOLED display
  • 70W fast charging
  • headphone jack, FM radio, IR blaster

What is not

  • System updates are slow and sometimes fail
  • HiOS includes a lot of ads
  • Battery endurance is only average
  • Gaming performance is weak

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large screen, good camera and fast charging at a budget price and can tolerate some software bloat.

Skip it if Skip it if you need top tier gaming, smooth software updates or a strong battery for long days.

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch AMOLED panel runs at 120Hz and shows bright colors, which most owners rate as good. The 50MP main sensor with optical image stabilization captures clear photos in daylight and decent shots in low light, while the 50MP selfie camera is sharp. Powered by the Helio G99 Ultimate chipset, the phone handles everyday apps fine but struggles with demanding games, reflected in low gaming and ease of use scores.

Fast charging at 70W refills the 5000mAh battery quickly, yet the battery drains faster than expected during heavy use. The software runs HiOS 14 on Android 14, promises up to two major Android upgrades but updates are slow and the interface is loaded with ads. Extras like a headphone jack, FM radio and IR blaster add value for local users.
