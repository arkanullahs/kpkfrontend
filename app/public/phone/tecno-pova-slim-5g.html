Is the Tecno POVA Slim 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.78 inch 144Hz AMOLED display
  • 45W fast charging
  • 5160mAh battery in ultra‑slim body
  • FM radio and IR blaster

What is not

  • Single bottom‑firing speaker only
  • Multitasking lags on aging CPU cores
  • Low‑light camera performance is poor
  • Only one year of OS updates

Buy it if Buy it if you value a bright, high‑refresh screen and quick charging more than top‑tier gaming or camera performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need stereo sound, smooth multitasking, or long‑term software support.

Why we say that

The 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 144 Hz and can reach 4500 nits peak, making outdoor use easy and colors vivid. The 5160 mAh battery fits in an ultra‑slim 5.95 mm chassis and, with 45 W charging, can go from empty to full in about an hour, giving more than seven hours of screen‑on time. The 50 MP main camera produces decent photos in daylight but struggles in low light, and selfies look over‑smoothed.

Multitasking feels laggy because the Dimensity 6400’s older Cortex‑A78 cores cannot keep up with heavy apps. Audio is limited to a single bottom‑firing speaker, and the phone receives only one year of Android OS updates, which many consider too short. Thermals stay stable, and the device includes FM radio and an IR blaster, but the plastic build feels cheap despite passing bend tests.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5160 mAh cell gives more than seven hours of screen‑on time in normal use, and the 45 W charger refills the battery from zero to full in about an hour, so you can get through a day without hunting for a plug.

Is the phone suitable for gaming?

The 6.78 inch 144 Hz AMOLED panel shows games smoothly, and the Dimensity 6400 can handle most titles at medium settings, but the aging Cortex‑A78 cores cause occasional lag when several apps run together, so heavy gamers may feel the slowdown.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor captures decent detail in bright light, but in low‑light scenes the images become noisy and lack sharpness, and the 13 MP selfie camera looks over‑smoothed, making night photography disappointing.

What warranty and software update support does the phone have?

The device is sold as an official Bangladesh unit, so it carries the brand’s warranty and can be serviced through the official network. However, Tecno promises only one year of Android OS updates, which many owners find insufficient.
