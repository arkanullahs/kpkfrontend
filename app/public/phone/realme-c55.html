Is the Realme C55 worth buying?

What is good

  • Good battery endurance
  • Efficient 33W charging
  • Bright 680 nits display
  • Includes a headphone jack

What is not

  • Fragile and not durable build
  • Poor gaming performance
  • Inconsistent wifi and internet
  • Bad overall camera quality

Buy it if Buy this phone only if you need a very cheap unofficial device for basic tasks and are not concerned about long-term durability or software updates.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a gamer, rely on stable internet connectivity, or want a device that will last for several years.

Why we say that

Owners frequently report that performance is not at a great level, making daily tasks feel sluggish. While the 5000 mAh battery offers good endurance and the 33W charging is efficient, the hardware build is a major concern. Users describe the components as fragile and not durable.

You will also face significant frustrations with connectivity, as owners report bad internet connections and inconsistent wifi. The camera quality is another point of contention, with many users stating it is really bad despite the 64MP sensor. Since this phone was released in 2023, you should expect short or no remaining software update life.

Gaming is particularly poor, as the battery saver limits the frame rate to below 30 fps. If you prioritize reliability and long-term software support, this device will likely disappoint you.
