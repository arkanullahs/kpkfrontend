Is the OPPO A6x worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6500 mAh battery capacity
  • Smooth 120Hz display refresh rate
  • Bright 1125-nit peak brightness
  • Handy built-in Google Gemini shortcut

What is not

  • Extremely slow 15W charging speed
  • Blurry camera details and low-light noise
  • Sluggish performance and gaming issues
  • Low HD+ display resolution

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize multi-day battery life above all other features and only need a device for basic daily tasks.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you play games, care about camera sharpness, or need a device that charges quickly.

Why we say that

With a massive 6500 mAh battery, you can easily get through one to two days of usage on a single charge. However, living with this phone involves significant compromises. Owners frequently report that the 15W charging is extremely slow, taking over two hours to fill the battery.

Performance is another major pain point, as the Snapdragon 685 chipset struggles with gaming and app opening speeds compared to other devices. The camera system is limited, producing blurry details and poor results in low-light conditions. While the 120Hz display is smooth, the HD+ resolution makes text and videos look less sharp than expected.

You will also notice that the single bottom-firing mono speaker lacks stereo balance, which can make audio feel unbalanced. If you value battery life above all else, this phone delivers, but you must be prepared for slow charging and sluggish performance in daily tasks.
