Is the OPPO A6x worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6500 mAh battery life
  • Smooth 120Hz display
  • Updated ColorOS 15 software
  • Includes a headphone jack

What is not

  • Extremely slow 15W charging
  • Blurry camera details
  • Low resolution HD+ screen
  • Sluggish app opening speeds

Buy it if You need a phone with a very long battery life for basic daily tasks.

Skip it if You want to play heavy games or take sharp high quality photos.

Why we say that

The 6500 mAh capacity easily lasts one to two days. However, living with this device means dealing with very slow 15W charging that takes over two hours to fill the battery. Owners frequently complain about sluggish performance when opening apps and struggling with gaming.

The HD+ screen lacks sharpness for the price. You will also find the cameras produce blurry details and struggle significantly in low light with noticeable noise. Audio is limited to a single bottom firing mono speaker which lacks balance.

While it runs the updated ColorOS 15 with a Google Gemini shortcut, the 4GB RAM is considered low for this price bracket.

What is the OPPO A6x price in Bangladesh?

OPPO A6x costs ৳17,499 to ৳21,999 in Bangladesh on official listings, in stock at 5 shops we track as of 9 Sep 2026.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

The official unit comes with a brand warranty and starts at ৳17,499. Cheaper listings are gray imports with no manufacturer cover. If you take a gray import to a service centre, they will only accept it as a paid repair.

How is the gaming performance on the Snapdragon 685?

It handles basic 60fps gaming and casual daily tasks well. However, many users report that the phone struggles with gaming and feels sluggish compared to other devices in this price range.

Can I take good photos in low light?

The 13MP AI camera works fine in daylight but struggles in low light. Owners report that low light photos have noticeable noise and lose sharpness and detail.

How long does the battery take to charge?

Charging is very slow because of the basic 15W charger. It is widely reported that it takes over two hours to fully charge the massive 6500 mAh battery.
