What works well

  • 120 Hz OLED display with 2000‑nit peak brightness
  • 50 MP main camera with natural colour rendering
  • Clean stock Android and long‑term update promise
  • Compact premium design feels comfortable in hand

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe overheating during gaming and heavy use
  • Battery lasts only five to six hours of mixed use
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Green line screen issue reported by owners

Why we say that

The 6.2‑inch OLED panel stays vivid even under direct sun thanks to 2000‑nit peak brightness and 120 Hz refresh. Photography feels natural with the 50 MP sensor and f/1.7 lens, though you will miss a telephoto option and portrait edge detection can stumble. The Tensor G3 chipset handles daily apps well and Android 14 feels clean, yet gaming pushes the phone into noticeable throttling and temperature spikes.

Battery drains after five to six hours of mixed use, and owners report a green line appearing on the screen over time. Because every unit is a gray import, there is no manufacturer warranty; any repair depends on the shop’s policy and may cost extra. Software updates continue for several years, but at three years old the remaining major upgrades are limited.

How long does the battery last on the Pixel 8 in everyday use?

Owners report about five to six hours of screen‑on time with mixed usage, which many consider average to poor. The 4575 mAh cell does not stay full all day, especially when the display is set to 120 Hz.

Can I play games smoothly on the Pixel 8?

The phone runs daily apps smoothly, but during gaming it frequently throttles and overheats, leading to lag and instability. If gaming performance is a priority, the Pixel 8 may feel disappointing.

Is there an official Google warranty or service centre for the Pixel 8 in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray‑import units, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Repairs must be handled by the shop that sold the phone, and any cost is paid by the owner.

How many Android updates will the Pixel 8 receive and for how long?

Google promises up to seven major Android upgrades for Pixel 8, starting from Android 14 and moving to Android 16. At three years old, a few more updates remain, but the update window is closing soon.
