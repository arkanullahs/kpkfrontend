Is the Google Pixel 8 worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz OLED display with 2000‑nit peak brightness
  • 50MP main camera with natural daylight photos
  • Pure Android experience with up to seven years of updates
  • Compact premium build and comfortable rounded design

What is not

  • Severe overheating during gaming
  • Battery lasts only 5‑6 hours screen‑on
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Occasional green‑screen display issue

Buy it if Buy it if you love a bright screen and strong camera and can tolerate limited battery life and no official service.

Skip it if Skip it if you need long gaming sessions, reliable after‑sales support, or all‑day battery endurance.

Why we say that

The Pixel 8 offers a 6.2‑inch OLED panel that reaches 2000 nits peak brightness and runs Android 14 with a promise of up to seven major updates. Its 50 MP main sensor delivers natural‑looking photos in daylight, though it lacks a telephoto lens and portrait edge detection can miss subjects. Daily tasks feel smooth thanks to the Google Tensor G3 and 8 GB RAM, but gaming triggers severe throttling and heat that can make the device unstable.

The 4575 mAh battery provides only about five to six hours of screen‑on time, and it degrades noticeably after a few months. As a gray‑import, the phone carries no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s limited guarantee and must pay for any repairs. Owners also report occasional green‑screen glitches on the display.

How long does the battery last on a Pixel 8 in daily use?

The Pixel 8 has a 4575 mAh battery that typically gives about five to six hours of screen‑on time under moderate use. Owners report average to poor endurance, and the battery degrades noticeably after a few months.

Can the Pixel 8 handle gaming without problems?

For everyday apps the phone feels smooth, but during gaming the Tensor G3 throttles and the device overheats severely. Many owners say the heat makes the phone unstable and limits long gaming sessions.

Is the camera good in low light and does it have a telephoto lens?

The 50 MP sensor with a f/1.7 aperture captures natural‑looking photos in daylight and performs reasonably in low light, but the phone lacks a telephoto lens and portrait edge detection can miss subjects.

Will I get official warranty and service for a Pixel 8 in Bangladesh?

No. All Pixel 8 units available here are gray‑import and carry no manufacturer warranty. You rely on the shop’s limited warranty and must pay for any repairs, as Google has little official service presence in Bangladesh.
