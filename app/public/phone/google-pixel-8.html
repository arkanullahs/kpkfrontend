Google Pixel 8 Is it worth buying?

The Pixel 8 delivers excellent photos and a clean Android experience but suffers from weak battery, occasional overheating, and unreliable network in Bangladesh.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value top‑tier photography, a pure Android UI, and can accept a gray‑import warranty with shop‑only support.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable gaming performance, long battery endurance, or official Google service coverage.

What works well4

  • 50MP main camera with natural colors
  • Bright 2000‑nit OLED display at 120Hz
  • Clean stock Android with long‑term updates
  • Compact premium build with IP68 rating

Reported trade-offs4

  • Battery drains fast, needs daily charge
  • Overheats under heavy use
  • Green or pink line can appear on screen
  • Network instability in Bangladesh

Why we say that

Its 50MP main sensor produces natural‑looking stills, and the 6.2" OLED panel reaches a peak 2000‑nit brightness with smooth 120Hz motion. Daily use feels snappy thanks to the Tensor G3 chip and 8GB RAM, yet heavy gaming or long video sessions can make the device hot. The 4575 mAh cell often needs a charge before the day ends, and owners have reported green or pink lines appearing on the screen under certain conditions.

Network drops are common, especially on local 5G bands, and there is no official Google service centre; any repair depends on the shop’s warranty. Software stays fresh with up to seven major Android updates, but at three years old the remaining update window is shrinking. Overall, it feels premium in hand but requires patience with its quirks.

Will the Pixel 8 battery last a full day of typical use?

The phone has a 4575 mAh battery, but owners consistently report average or underwhelming endurance. Most users need to charge it before the day ends, especially with screen brightness or 5G active.

Is the Pixel 8 suitable for gaming?

Performance is good for everyday tasks, but the device tends to overheat during heavy gaming. Owner ratings give gaming a 5.5 out of 10, so it is not ideal for long gaming sessions.

How does the Pixel 8 camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50MP sensor with f/1.7 aperture and OIS captures natural‑looking stills even in dim light. It lacks a dedicated telephoto lens, but still photography is rated 9 out of 10 by owners.

Does the Pixel 8 have an official warranty or service centre in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray‑import units, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Support relies on the shop’s own warranty and any repairs are paid by the buyer, as Google has little official service presence.
