Why we say that

Its Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset delivers smooth performance for apps and games, and the 6400 mAh cell keeps the phone alive through a full day of heavy use. The 6.78 inch AMOLED panel runs at 144 Hz, making scrolling and gaming feel fluid, while the 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures clear photos in good light. Owners note the lack of any protective glass on the screen and the fact that software includes a moderate amount of bloat and ads.

Video recording is rated lower than other areas, and the phone relies on a shop warranty that requires you to verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
