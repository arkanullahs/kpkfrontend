Why we say that

The 6.81 inch LTPO OLED panel reaches 1800 nits and runs at 120Hz, making everyday scrolling and media consumption smooth and bright. The Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset delivers quick app launches and solid daily performance, while the 12GB RAM keeps multitasking fluid. Battery endurance is strong thanks to the 5100 mAh cell and 66W charger, but the phone is three years old so remaining software updates are limited.

Owners love the 50MP main camera for detailed shots, yet many report that new software updates degrade camera consistency. Gaming feels decent until the GPU overheats and throttles, which lowers frame rates. The device arrives as a gray import with only the shop's warranty, so you must verify IMEI and accept paid repairs.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5100 mAh battery provides excellent endurance for most daily tasks and the 66W charger refills it quickly. Real world use often lasts a full day, though capacity may have dropped slightly after three years.

Will this phone handle heavy gaming without issues?

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 and 12GB RAM handle casual games well, but heavy titles cause the GPU to heat up and throttle, leading to lower frame rates. Gaming scores are around six point eight out of ten.

Is the camera reliable after software updates?

The 50MP sensor captures high detail and versatile shots, but many owners notice inconsistency after new updates, with some reporting noticeable degradation in image quality and focus accuracy.

Does it come with an official warranty and future updates?

All units are gray imports, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop's warranty applies and repairs are paid. At three years old, expect little or no further major OS updates.
