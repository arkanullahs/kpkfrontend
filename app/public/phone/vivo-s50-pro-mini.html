Vivo S50 Pro mini Is it worth buying?

The Vivo S50 Pro mini gives flagship speed and a brilliant display but its Chinese software and lack of official warranty can be daily annoyances.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want flagship performance, fast charging and a premium screen at under 70 k taka and can live without Google apps and official warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need an official manufacturer warranty, Bangla UI or a phone that stays cool during long sessions.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 6.31" LTPO AMOLED 120Hz display
  • 90W wired and 40W wireless charging
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 delivers top gaming performance
  • 6500 mAh battery with fast charge

Reported trade-offs4

  • Chinese OriginOS lacks Google services
  • Notification delays on CN ROM
  • Ultrawide camera weak at 8 MP
  • Phone gets hot under load

Why we say that

The 6.31" LTPO AMOLED panel is bright, smooth and feels premium, while the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 keeps games at 120 fps and apps buttery fast. Battery life feels solid with a 6500 mAh cell, though real‑world screen time hovers around 7‑9 hours and some users notice faster drain. Charging is rapid at 90 W wired and 40 W wireless, so a short plug gets you back in minutes.

Camera performance shines on the 50 MP main sensor with OIS and 3× telephoto, yet the 8 MP ultrawide is a clear weak spot and only records 1080p 30fps. The phone runs a Chinese OriginOS that lacks Google services, Bangla language and suffers notification delays, making everyday use feel less smooth. Heat builds up during long photo sessions or heavy gaming, and any repair must be handled through the shop’s paid warranty.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

Can the Vivo S50 Pro mini run high‑end mobile games smoothly?
