Vivo S50 Pro mini Is it worth buying?

If you can tolerate a Chinese ROM and no official warranty, the Vivo S50 Pro mini gives flagship performance at a mid range price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want top tier speed, a large battery and a vivid screen and you are comfortable using workarounds for Google services.
Consider alternatives if
You need an official Vivo warranty or a reliable ultrawide camera.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 delivers smooth gaming
  • 90W wired and 40W wireless charging fill the 6500 mAh battery fast
  • 6.31 inch LTPO AMOLED display is bright and fluid
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS captures detailed photos

Reported trade-offs4

  • Chinese OriginOS causes notification delays
  • No Google apps or Bangla language out of the box
  • 8 MP ultrawide limited to 1080p30 video
  • Phone gets hot during long gaming or photography

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 paired with 12 GB RAM runs games at 120 FPS and handles multitasking without stutter. The 6.31 inch LTPO AMOLED panel is bright, vivid and refreshes at 120 Hz, making scrolling and media consumption pleasant. A massive 6500 mAh cell combined with 90 W wired and 40 W wireless charging keeps the phone alive for a full day, though heavy use can drain it faster.

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS and 3× telephoto produces sharp photos, while the 8 MP ultrawide is limited to 1080p30 video. Owners complain about notification lag, lack of Google services, and noticeable heat during long gaming or photography sessions. The build feels solid with a metal frame and fiber glass back, but the Chinese software adds bloatware and occasional glitches.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 6500 mAh cell can keep the phone alive for about 7 to 9 hours of screen‑on time, but heavy use such as gaming or video playback will drain it faster, and some owners notice the charge dropping quickly after a full charge.

Is the phone good for gaming?

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 paired with 12 GB RAM easily reaches 120 FPS in PUBG and COD, and the 90 W charger restores the large battery in under an hour, making it a solid choice for mobile gamers.

Will I get Google apps and Bangla language?

The unit sold here runs the Chinese OriginOS, which does not include Google Play services or Bangla language support, so you will need to install them manually or use alternative app stores, and some users report notification delays because of the ROM.

What warranty and software updates does this gray import have?

All listings are gray imports, so there is no official Vivo warranty in Bangladesh; the shop may offer a limited warranty and any repair will be charged, and software updates come only from Chinese servers, typically two major Android versions for budget models.
