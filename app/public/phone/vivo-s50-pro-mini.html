Is the Vivo S50 Pro mini worth buying?

What is good

  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 flagship performance
  • 6.31 inch LTPO AMOLED display
  • 6500 mAh battery with 90W fast charge
  • 50 MP main and selfie cameras

What is not

  • 8 MP ultrawide is weak
  • Notification delays on Chinese ROM
  • Thermal heat under sustained load
  • No official Vivo brand warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you want top‑tier speed, a large battery and premium build in a compact phone and can accept a gray‑import with possible software quirks.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a guaranteed Google experience, an official brand warranty, or a strong ultrawide camera.

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset makes everyday tasks feel instant and gaming runs smoothly, while the 6.31 inch LTPO AMOLED screen shows vivid colors at 120 Hz. The 6500 mAh battery holds a full day of moderate use, and the 90W charger refills it quickly. Build quality feels premium with IP68/69 protection, and the 50 MP main and selfie sensors capture sharp photos in good light.

Owners complain about notification delays on the Chinese OriginOS, noticeable heat when the phone is under sustained load, and a weak 8 MP ultrawide lens. The device arrives as a gray‑import, so you only get a shop warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 6500 mAh cell is rated 8.5 out of 10 and can easily last a full day of moderate use. Heavy gaming or video streaming will drain it faster, but the 90W charger refills it in under an hour.

Is the Vivo S50 Pro mini good for gaming?

Yes, the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 and 12 GB RAM give flagship‑grade gaming performance, reflected in an 8.2 gaming rating. The phone does get noticeably warm during long sessions, which may affect comfort.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.6 aperture and OIS captures decent low‑light shots, and the 3× telephoto works well. The 8 MP ultrawide struggles in dim light, and overall camera rating is 7.8.

Will I receive an official Vivo warranty and regular software updates?

Only gray‑import units are available, so you get a shop warranty, not an official Vivo warranty. The Chinese OriginOS version receives OTA updates only from CN servers, while the global Funtouch version gets about two major Android updates.
