Is the Vivo S50 Pro Mini worth buying?

What is good

  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 performance
  • 90W wired and 40W wireless charging
  • 6.31 inch LTPO AMOLED 120Hz display
  • 50MP main camera with OIS and 3x telephoto

What is not

  • Chinese OriginOS lacks Google apps and Bangla
  • Ultrawide camera weak at 8MP 1080p30
  • Noticeable heating during heavy use
  • No official Vivo warranty in Bangladesh

Buy it if Buy it if you want flagship performance, fast charging and a premium display at a sub‑70k price and can tolerate a Chinese ROM.

Skip it if Skip it if you need an official warranty, Google services or a strong ultrawide camera.

Why we say that

Daily use feels smooth thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 and 12GB RAM, and the 90W wired charger refills the 6500mAh battery in under an hour. The 50MP main sensor with OIS and 3x telephoto captures clear photos in good light, while the 6.31 inch LTPO panel stays vivid even in sunlight. Owners notice the phone gets hot during long gaming sessions or intensive photography, and the 8MP ultrawide lens is limited to 1080p30 video.

The OriginOS CN interface lacks Google apps, Bangla language and shows occasional notification delays, which can be frustrating for users accustomed to standard Android. Repairs are covered only by the shop’s warranty and any service costs are paid out of pocket.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 6500mAh cell is rated 8.4 out of 10, giving most users 7 to 9 hours of mixed usage. Fast 90W charging can refill the battery in under an hour, but heavy gaming can drain it faster.

Is the Vivo S50 Pro Mini good for gaming?

Yes, the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 and 12GB RAM deliver smooth 120FPS gameplay in titles like PUBG and COD. The 120Hz AMOLED screen adds fluid visuals, though long sessions may cause the phone to heat up.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50MP main sensor with f/1.6 aperture and OIS handles low‑light scenes reasonably well, but the 8MP ultrawide lens struggles and is limited to 1080p30 video, so it is not ideal for wide‑angle night shots.

Will I get official Vivo warranty and updates in Bangladesh?

All units are gray‑import Chinese ROMs, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Only the shop’s limited warranty applies and any repairs are paid. OTA updates come from Chinese servers, not from Vivo Bangladesh.
