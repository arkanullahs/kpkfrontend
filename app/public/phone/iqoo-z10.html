Is the iQOO Z10 worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 7300 mAh battery capacity
  • Bright 5000 nits peak AMOLED display
  • 50MP main camera with OIS
  • Fast 90W charging support

What is not

  • Outdated mono speaker setup
  • Only gray-import stock available
  • Average build and camera quality
  • Medium level of bloatware and ads

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize extreme battery life and a bright display for media consumption above all other features.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you value high-quality stereo audio or require an official brand warranty for your peace of mind.

Why we say that

With a massive 7300 mAh battery, it offers an efficient experience for users who prioritize screen time above all else. The 6.77 inch AMOLED display is bright and smooth, reaching 5000 nits peak brightness with a 120Hz refresh rate. However, living with this device reveals some compromises.

Owners are frustrated by the mono speaker setup, which feels outdated in 2025. Some users find the audio output too loud and sharp, leading to an irritating listening experience. While the Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 chipset handles daily tasks and gaming reasonably well, the build quality and camera performance receive average ratings from current owners.

You are limited to shop warranties since only gray-import stock is available in Bangladesh. Ensure you verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before finalizing your purchase to avoid future connectivity issues.
