Is the iQOO Z10 worth buying?

What is good

  • Efficient 7300 mAh battery
  • 5000 nits peak brightness
  • Fast 90W charging
  • 120Hz AMOLED display

What is not

  • Only has a mono speaker
  • No official brand warranty
  • Medium amount of bloatware and ads

Buy it if You need a massive battery and a very bright screen for your daily use.

Skip it if You want stereo speakers or the security of an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 7300 mAh battery is efficient and lasts a long time. You get a very bright 120Hz AMOLED screen and a Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 chipset for decent performance. However, the audio experience is a major letdown.

Owners are frustrated that it only has a mono speaker in 2025. You should also be aware that this is an unofficial import. There is no brand backed warranty available in Bangladesh.

You will have to rely on shop warranties and check the IMEI and BTRC status yourself before paying.

Is the battery life good for heavy users?

Yes, it features a very large 7300 mAh battery that owners describe as efficient. It also supports 90W charging to help refill the battery quickly.
