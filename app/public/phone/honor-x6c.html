Is the Honor X6c worth buying?

This is a phone built for long battery life and smooth everyday performance, but owners report missing features and a basic build. The 5300 mAh battery delivers good endurance for normal use, though it drains faster during gaming. Its 120Hz display is bright but not an AMOLED panel, and the 50MP camera is described as unimpressive and needing tuning. Crucially, some owners find advertised features like NFC are missing from their unit, and it may only connect to standard 4G networks, not 4G+. The software comes with a medium amount of pre-installed apps and ads.
