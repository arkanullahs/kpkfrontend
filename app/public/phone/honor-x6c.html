What works well

  • 5300 mAh battery lasts a full day
  • 120 Hz refresh makes scrolling smooth
  • Helio G81 Ultra handles everyday apps
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included

Reported trade-offs

  • HD+ screen resolution feels low
  • Low‑light photos are noisy
  • No NFC despite spec
  • Gaming shows frame drops

Why we say that

The 5300 mAh cell paired with 35 W fast charging keeps the phone alive and refills it in under an hour. Build quality feels solid, with IP64 rating and SGS drop test approval, so everyday bumps are not a worry. The 6.61‑inch TFT LCD runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 1010 nits, giving smooth scrolling and bright outdoor visibility, though the HD+ resolution feels low compared with full HD screens.

The Helio G81 Ultra chipset handles daily apps without heat, but gaming suffers frame drops and low FPS. The 50 MP main camera captures decent daylight shots, yet low‑light images are noisy and lack detail. MagicOS on Android 15 runs fine but includes medium‑level bloat and ads.

Read full reasoning

Missing NFC and the lack of 5G are common complaints. Overall it is a value‑focused phone for users who prioritize durability and battery over premium media performance.
