Is the Samsung Galaxy F36 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Vibrant 6.7 inch Super AMOLED with 120Hz refresh
  • 50MP main camera with optical image stabilization
  • 5000 mAh battery gives average day‑long use
  • One UI 7 promises up to six Android upgrades

What is not

  • 25W charger feels slow for price
  • Plastic frame feels cheap
  • Phone heats up and throttles under load
  • Waterdrop notch and thick bezels look dated

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large AMOLED display and solid camera without needing heavy gaming performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need fast charging, premium build quality, or reliable gaming performance.

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch Super AMOLED with 120Hz looks vibrant, and the 50MP main sensor with OIS captures good daylight photos. The 5000 mAh battery usually lasts a day, but the 25W charger is slow for the price. The Exynos 1380 chip gets hot during heavy use and throttles, which makes gaming feel laggy.

Build quality is mixed; the Victus+ glass feels nice but the plastic frame feels cheap and the waterdrop notch with thick bezels looks dated. One UI 7 runs Android 15 and promises up to six major Android upgrades, which is a strong software point. The phone arrives as a gray‑import unit, so there is no official Samsung warranty in Bangladesh, only the shop’s own warranty and paid repairs.

How many Android updates will the Galaxy F36 get?

Is there an official Samsung warranty for this phone in Bangladesh?

How is the battery life and charging speed?

The 5000 mAh battery usually lasts a full day of mixed use, but the 25W charger is considered slow for the price and takes longer than newer fast‑charging standards.

Will the phone handle gaming and heavy multitasking?

The Exynos 1380 chipset shows heating and thermal throttling during demanding games or heavy multitasking, leading to lag and lower performance, so it is not ideal for intensive gaming.
