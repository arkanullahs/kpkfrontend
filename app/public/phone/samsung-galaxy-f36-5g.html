Samsung Galaxy F36 5G Is it worth buying?

If you can live without an official Samsung warranty, the Galaxy F36 5G offers a big AMOLED screen and a solid camera for under 30 k taka.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large AMOLED screen, decent camera and long software support at a sub‑30 k price and can tolerate a gray‑import warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need strong gaming performance, fast charging or an official Samsung warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 50MP main camera with OIS for bright photos
  • 6.7 inch Super AMOLED display with 120 Hz
  • Up to six Android version upgrades
  • Leather‑like back feels premium

Reported trade-offs3

  • Waterdrop notch and thick chin look dated
  • No charger included, 25 W charging feels slow
  • Exynos 1380 heats and lags in heavy games

Why we say that

The 6.7‑inch Super AMOLED panel is bright and smooth at 120 Hz, and the 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures good daylight photos. Samsung promises up to six Android version upgrades, which is a rare long‑term software promise for a mid‑range phone. However, the water‑drop notch and thick chin look dated, and the plastic frame feels cheap against the leather‑like back.

The phone does not include a charger and the 25 W charging is slow, so you will need to buy a charger separately. Under heavy use the Exynos 1380 heats up and can lag, making gaming less enjoyable. Battery life is average, lasting a day with moderate use but not exceptional.

Does the Galaxy F36 5G come with a charger?

No, the box does not include a charger. You will need to purchase a compatible 25 W charger separately, as the phone relies on an external charger for fast charging.

How many Android updates will this phone receive?

Samsung promises up to six major Android version upgrades for the Galaxy F36 5G. This longer update window is a key advantage compared with many other mid‑range devices.

Is the battery life good for daily use?

The 5,000 mAh battery provides average endurance, lasting about a day with moderate use. Charging is limited to 25 W and is slower because no charger is supplied in the box.

Will it handle games smoothly?

The Exynos 1380 processor manages everyday tasks well but struggles with demanding games. It tends to heat up and cause lag, so it is not ideal for heavy gaming.
