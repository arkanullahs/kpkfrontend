ZTE A56 Is it worth buying?

The ZTE A56 is a budget device that struggles to keep up with daily tasks due to its limited performance capabilities.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
Buy this phone only if you need a basic device for simple tasks and prioritize a large battery over performance or camera quality.
Consider alternatives if
You play games, care about display sharpness, or want a device that charges quickly.

What works well4

  • Reliable 5000 mAh battery endurance
  • Smooth 90Hz display refresh rate
  • Includes a headphone jack and FM radio
  • Current Android 15 software

Reported trade-offs4

  • Extremely slow 10W charging speed
  • No charger included in the box
  • Low 720p display resolution
  • Weak performance for gaming

Why we say that

While the 5000 mAh battery provides reliable endurance for basic usage, the experience is hampered by extremely slow 10W charging and the fact that no charger is included in the box. Owners frequently report that the Unisoc T7200 chipset is not built for heavy gaming and often lags with anything beyond simple applications. The 6.75 inch display offers a smooth 90Hz refresh rate, but the low 720p resolution results in a lack of sharpness.

You will also find bloatware pre-installed on the Android 15 software. Given that the newer ZTE A76 is available for ৳13,500, this model faces stiff competition from within its own brand. If you choose to purchase this phone, ensure you buy the official unit for ৳13,600 to secure the brand warranty, as gray imports offer no manufacturer cover.

Does the ZTE A56 have a good battery life?

Yes, the phone features a 5000 mAh battery that owners find reliable for daily tasks. You can expect consistent endurance throughout the day, though you must account for the very slow 10W charging speed when the battery eventually runs low.

Is the ZTE A56 suitable for gaming?

No, the device is not built for heavy gaming. The Unisoc T7200 chipset struggles with anything beyond basic apps, and owner ratings for gaming performance are quite low at 4.0 out of 10.

How do I get the official warranty for this phone?

You must purchase the official unit, which is priced at ৳13,600 for the 4GB/128GB variant. Gray imports do not come with manufacturer cover, and service centers will only accept them for paid repairs.

How many software updates will the ZTE A56 receive?

The device runs on Android 15 with the MyOS interface. It is expected to receive between 1 and 2 OS updates during its lifespan, though you should be aware that the software comes with a medium amount of bloatware.
