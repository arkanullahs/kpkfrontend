What works well

  • 90Hz smooth display
  • 5000 mAh battery lasts 1‑2 days
  • Android 15 pre‑installed
  • Headphone jack and FM radio

Reported trade-offs

  • 10W charging is very slow
  • Plastic build feels cheap
  • 13MP camera is basic
  • Heavy games lag

Why we say that

The 6.75 inch IPS LCD runs at 90Hz, giving smooth scrolling, but the resolution stays at HD+ and uniformity can be uneven. The 5000 mAh cell lasts one to two days for typical use, yet charging at only 10W feels very slow and some sellers do not include a charger. Build quality is a plain plastic that mimics the Realme GT7 look, which many find cheap.

The 13 MP camera works for daylight snaps and video calls but struggles in low light. Performance is adequate for messaging and social apps, but heavy games lag noticeably. Android 15 arrives out of the box, though only one or two major updates are expected and the software carries medium bloat and ads.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery can comfortably last one to two days of normal use, including messaging, social media and video playback. Heavy gaming or bright screen settings will reduce that time noticeably.

Will this phone handle mobile games well?

For casual titles like Candy Crush or PUBG Lite the phone is acceptable, but the Unisoc T7200 and 4 GB RAM struggle with demanding games. Frame drops and lag are common in newer releases.

How good is the camera in low‑light conditions?

The 13 MP rear sensor captures clear photos in daylight, but low‑light performance is weak. Images become noisy and lack detail, making it suitable only for quick snapshots or video calls rather than serious photography.

Does the phone come with an official warranty and how many updates will it receive?

Only the official ৳11,999 listing includes the brand warranty; gray‑import units have no manufacturer cover and repairs are paid. The device ships with Android 15 and is expected to receive one or two major OS updates, with moderate pre‑installed ads.
