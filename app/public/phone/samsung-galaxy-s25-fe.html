Is the Samsung Galaxy S25 FE worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.7 inch 120 Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • 1900‑nit peak brightness
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • Premium aluminum body, lighter than S24 FE

What is not

  • Battery drains fast under heavy use
  • Severe heat and throttling in games
  • Optical fingerprint sensor feels less responsive
  • No manufacturer warranty on cheap imports

Buy it if Buy it if you value a bright screen, solid daily performance and can tolerate shorter gaming sessions and a faster charging need.

Skip it if Skip it if you need long gaming stamina, reliable all‑day battery or an official Samsung warranty without extra cost.

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch LTPO AMOLED screen reaches 1900 nits and switches smoothly between 1 and 120 Hz, making media consumption a pleasure. Daily tasks run at flagship speed thanks to the Exynos 2400 and 8 GB RAM, and the 50 MP main camera with OIS captures clear photos in good light, while selfies are sharp with the 12 MP sensor. The aluminum body feels premium and is lighter than the previous FE.

However, the 4900 mAh battery drops noticeably under heavy use, often needing a charge before evening, and the 45 W charger still needs about 70 minutes for a full fill. Gaming sessions generate noticeable heat and the CPU throttles to about half its power, reducing frame rates. The fingerprint sensor is optical, not the faster ultrasonic found on flagships.

Gray‑import units cost ৳59,499 but lack Samsung’s official warranty; only the ৳78,500 listing includes brand coverage.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

With light browsing, messaging and occasional video, the 4900 mAh battery can last a full day. Heavy gaming or video recording drains it much faster, often requiring a charge before evening. Owners note average endurance overall.

Will the phone get hot while gaming and does it affect performance?

During intensive games the device becomes noticeably warm and the Exynos 2400 throttles to about 50 percent of its maximum speed. This leads to lower frame rates after a short period, as reported by owners.

Is the camera reliable in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor performs well in good light, but the ultrawide lens struggles in dim environments and the 3.0× telephoto appears soft. Selfies remain sharp thanks to the 12 MP front camera.
