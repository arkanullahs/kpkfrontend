Samsung Galaxy S25 FE Is it worth buying?

The Samsung Galaxy S25 FE offers a premium experience with a stunning display and polished software, but it suffers from significant thermal throttling and mediocre battery life.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a premium Samsung experience with long-term software support and a great display for daily tasks.
Consider alternatives if
You are a heavy gamer or power user who needs sustained performance and reliable battery life throughout the day.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Stunning 120Hz Dynamic LTPO AMOLED display
  • Premium and lightweight armor aluminum build
  • Polished One UI 8 with 7-year support
  • Dependable 50MP main camera

Reported trade-offs4

  • Severe thermal throttling under sustained load
  • Average battery life with fast discharge
  • Optical fingerprint sensor instead of ultrasonic
  • Ultrawide camera struggles in low light

Why we say that

While the Exynos 2400 provides flagship speed for daily tasks, owners report that the device slows down to 50 percent of its maximum CPU performance during prolonged stress tests. The build is impressively light and thin, yet the use of an optical fingerprint sensor instead of the ultrasonic version found on S25 flagships is a notable downgrade. Battery endurance is average, lasting a full day for light users but dying by evening for those who push the device.

You will also notice heat during heavy gaming or long video recording sessions. While the main camera is dependable, the ultrawide lens struggles in low light and the telephoto lens appears soft. It is a capable device for general use, but heavy gamers and power users may find the performance drops and battery drain frustrating.
