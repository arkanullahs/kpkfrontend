Is the Samsung Galaxy S25 FE worth buying?

What is good

  • Excellent 6.7 inch LTPO AMOLED display
  • Premium build quality with IP68 rating
  • Seven years of software support
  • Dependable main camera performance

What is not

  • Slow charging and fast battery discharge
  • Throttles under sustained gaming load
  • Heats up during heavy usage
  • High price point relative to value

Buy it if You want a premium Samsung experience with long-term software support and a high-quality display for daily tasks.

Skip it if You are a heavy gamer or power user who needs consistent performance without overheating or battery anxiety.

Why we say that

While the display and build quality are top-tier, owners frequently report that the device heats up during heavy gaming and long video recording sessions. The Exynos 2400 chipset provides fast daily responsiveness, yet it throttles under sustained load, leading to underwhelming performance during intensive tasks. Battery life remains a point of contention, with mixed reports regarding whether it lasts a full day or drains too quickly.

Charging speeds are also noted as slow by editorial reviews. The software experience is a major highlight, featuring industry-leading support and Galaxy AI. However, the pricing is often criticized for being too close to the S25+ model, making the value proposition difficult for some buyers.

If you prioritize a high-quality screen and long-term software updates over peak gaming performance, this phone remains a capable choice.
