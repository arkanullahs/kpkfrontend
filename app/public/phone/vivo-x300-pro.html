Buying verdict

Vivo X300 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Vivo X300 Pro is a powerful device that demands careful research before purchase because the experience varies wildly between the official global version and the gray-market Chinese import.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize battery life and camera hardware and are comfortable troubleshooting software settings on an unofficial import.
Consider alternatives if
You want a stable, out-of-the-box experience for gaming or if you rely on consistent push notifications for messaging apps.

What works well4

  • Exceptional two-day battery endurance
  • Stunning LTPO AMOLED display
  • World-class telephoto and portrait camera
  • Premium build with IP69 rating

Reported trade-offs4

  • Heavy thermal throttling during gaming
  • Aggressive AI over-processing in photos
  • OriginOS notification lag and bugs
  • Top-heavy physical design

Why we say that

While the 6510 mAh battery provides exceptional two-day endurance and the display is stunning, daily life with the phone is often frustrating. Owners frequently report heavy thermal throttling during gaming and significant software bugs within OriginOS. The camera system is capable of world-class portraits, yet it suffers from aggressive AI over-processing that creates a watercolor effect on human subjects in indoor lighting.

If you choose an unofficial unit running OriginOS, you will face daily friction including notification delays for apps like WhatsApp and the absence of preinstalled Google services. The hardware is premium and includes an IP69 rating, but the top-heavy design can feel awkward. This is a high-performance machine that requires a user willing to manage complex permission settings and tolerate occasional software instability.

Before you buy

Some sellers here list the China-market version (OriginOS (CN)), others the global one. Check which build you are buying

THE classic trap: global Vivo runs Funtouch (plain, light-ish), CN runs OriginOS (prettier, heavier, deeply integrated Chinese services, NO Google preinstalled, no Bangla, OTA updates only from CN servers). OriginOS units in BD = gray CN imports; gorgeous UI but daily friction for Google-centric users.

Does the Vivo X300 Pro have a good battery life?

Yes, the phone features a massive 6510 mAh battery. Owners report that this capacity provides exceptional endurance, allowing the device to last for two full days on a single charge.

Is the Vivo X300 Pro suitable for heavy gaming?

While the Dimensity 9500 chipset offers high peak speeds, the phone is not ideal for long gaming sessions. Owners report that both the CPU and GPU suffer from heavy thermal throttling when running intensive games.

What is the difference between the official and unofficial warranty?

The official unit comes with a brand warranty, while unofficial units are gray imports with no manufacturer cover. If you take an unofficial unit to a service centre, they will only accept it as a paid repair.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The camera is capable of world-class portraits, but it struggles in low or indoor lighting. The image processing pipeline applies heavy AI smoothing and edge enhancement, which creates a noticeable watercolor effect on human subjects.
