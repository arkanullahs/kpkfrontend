Is the Vivo X300 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.78‑inch LTPO AMOLED with 4500‑nit peak
  • 6510 mAh battery lasting around two days
  • 50 MP main camera with optical image stabilization
  • 90 W fast charging

What is not

  • Heavy thermal throttling during long games
  • OriginOS bugs and notification delays
  • Top‑heavy design feels unbalanced
  • Gray‑import units lack official warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large, bright display, strong battery and high‑resolution camera and can accept occasional software quirks.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable software, consistent gaming performance or an official warranty without extra cost.

Why we say that

The 6.78‑inch LTPO AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 4500 nits, making outdoor viewing easy. Its 6510 mAh cell typically lasts about two days of normal use, and the 90 W charger refills it quickly. Powered by the Dimensity 9500 and 12 GB RAM, the phone feels fast, but owners report heavy CPU and GPU thermal throttling that reduces performance in long gaming sessions.

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS, 3.7× telephoto and ultrawide delivers detailed shots, yet the AI processing can add a watercolor effect in low‑light rooms. Software runs either OriginOS or Funtouch; OriginOS looks polished but suffers from notification lag and stability bugs. Official units priced at ৳1,49,999 include a brand warranty, while cheaper gray‑import listings start at ৳97,499 and rely on paid repairs.

Before you buy

Some sellers here list the China-market version (OriginOS (CN)), others the global one. Check which build you are buying

THE classic trap: global Vivo runs Funtouch (plain, light-ish), CN runs OriginOS (prettier, heavier, deeply integrated Chinese services, NO Google preinstalled, no Bangla, OTA updates only from CN servers). OriginOS units in BD = gray CN imports; gorgeous UI but daily friction for Google-centric users.
