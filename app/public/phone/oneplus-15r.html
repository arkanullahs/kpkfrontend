Buying verdict

OnePlus 15R Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run games for hours without charging, the OnePlus 15R delivers.

Choose it if
You want flagship performance and long battery life and accept a gray‑import without official warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on a telephoto camera or need fast USB‑C data speeds.

What works well

  • 7400 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 handles high‑frame‑rate gaming
  • 165 Hz AMOLED is extremely bright
  • 80 W charger fills the tank quickly

Reported trade-offs

  • No telephoto lens on the rear camera
  • USB 2.0 port limits fast data transfer
  • Charging slows after OxygenOS 16.0.5 update

Why we say that

Its 7400 mAh battery easily stretches to two days of mixed use, and the 80 W charger tops up quickly. The Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset keeps high‑frame‑rate titles smooth, and the 165 Hz AMOLED screen stays bright even in sunlight. OxygenOS 16 feels clean and light, and the device gets four major Android updates.

Owners love the long battery, gaming stamina and vivid display, but they miss the telephoto lens, complain about the USB 2.0 port and notice slower charging after the 16.0.5 software update. The phone arrives as a gray import, so there is no official OnePlus warranty in Bangladesh; any repair work must be paid for or covered by the shop’s own promise.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.
