Why we say that

The 6.9‑inch LTPO OLED screen shines at 3000 nits and 120 Hz, making everyday use feel fluid. The A20 Pro processor and 12 GB RAM handle multitasking and gaming without stutter, while iOS 27 promises five to six major updates and no bloat. Photography is strong thanks to a 48 MP sensor with f/1.5 aperture, OIS and 4× telephoto, and the 18 MP selfie camera is adequate.

Battery life is decent for a day of mixed use, though the high refresh rate can drain it faster. Charging at 60 W refills quickly, but any repair costs fall on you. Owners appreciate performance but often complain about the lack of manufacturer service and the need to verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

What reviewers said on YouTube

12 reviews · 1.9M views

The iPhone 18 Pro Max delivers marathon battery endurance, stellar thermal cooling, and market-leading video capabilities, but it is held back by noticeable bulk, unchanged secondary camera sensors, and a steeper price tag. If you already own a recent Pro iPhone, it is an easy skip, but it is an outstanding leap for anyone upgrading from older models.

Best for Mobile creators and power users upgrading from older generations

  1. DDave2D

    iPhone 18 Pro Max Review - Apple's Hyping The Wrong Thing (opens YouTube)

    Dave2D found that Apple hyped the wrong headline feature: while the variable aperture provides minimal real-world benefit in ordinary daylight shots, the hidden under-display Face ID sensor and massively upgraded cooling are genuinely impressive engineering feats. He observed steady frame rates and improved battery longevity, but cautioned that the device is heavier and noticeably more expensive, concluding that the iPhone 17 Pro series remains a far better value for most consumers.

  2. TThe Tech Chap

    iPhone 18 Pro | Pro Max REVIEW - The Good & Bad (shot on iPhone) (opens YouTube)

    706K views

    The Tech Chap described the phone as a classic, iterative S-year release, highlighting the welcome ability to adjust cinematic mode depth after recording alongside cooler gaming temperatures enabled by the triple-sized vapor chamber. However, because daytime image quality and secondary zoom lenses remain virtually unchanged from the 17 Pro Max, he concluded that the heavier weight and price hike make it difficult to recommend to anyone holding a recent Pro iPhone.

  3. TThe Tech Chap

    iPhone 18 Pro vs 18 Pro Max BATTERY Test - Not What I Expected… (opens YouTube)

While the standard 18 Pro fell short of matching last year's Max, he determined that the 18 Pro Max's efficiency and cell size make it the definitive pick for buyers prioritizing maximum unplugged screen time.

MMrwhosetheboss · iPhone 18 Pro Review - My Last iPhone? ↗

Mrwhosetheboss argued that although the phone feels like an incremental and visually repetitive refresh on day one, the class-leading battery life, improved heat management, and next-generation chip provide tremendous performance headroom for the next several years. He felt the variable aperture is largely a marketing narrative that masks older sensor hardware, but praised the refined image processing and recommended the phone as a rock-solid, future-proof purchase for anyone upgrading from an older device.

JJerryRigEverything · iPhone 18 Pro Durability Test - Perfect Timing ↗

JerryRigEverything verified that the phone retains its rigid, highly durable structural integrity under extreme stress, passing the bend test without flexing thanks to its reinforced frame and expanded vapor chamber. He noted that the scratch-resistant glass holds up well, but warned that the anodized aluminum edges and raised camera plateau remain vulnerable to pocket scuffs and cosmetic chipping, suggesting a clear case to safeguard the new hardware.

HHTX Studio · iPhone 18 Pro Review: I Have Mixed Feelings. ↗

HTX Studio gave a mixed verdict, praising the phone's monstrous sustained graphical power, cooler operating temperatures, and stellar battery endurance, while criticizing prominent lens flare artifacts, a heavy 249-gram chassis, and higher component-driven prices. They also noted that higher storage tiers switch to slower QLC memory, concluding that while it is technically capable, the phone's compromises make it less of a clear-cut upgrade over last year's flagship.

TTrakin Tech English · iPhone 18 Pro Max vs Galaxy S26 Ultra Camera Test - Apple Means Business! ↗

Trakin Tech English concluded that the 18 Pro Max remains the premier smartphone for handheld video creation and natural daylight HDR photography, cleanly beating the Galaxy S26 Ultra in low-light shutter speeds and highlight preservation. Nonetheless, he noted that Samsung still holds the upper hand in extreme zoom range and ultra-wide sharpness, advising shoppers who prioritize versatile zoom lenses to weigh their options carefully.

TTom’s Guide · iPhone 18 Pro & Pro Max Review! (Answering the Internet) ↗

Tom's Guide concluded that the 18 Pro Max is a powerhouse device setting a new benchmark for sustained performance and lab-tested battery endurance, topping more than 18 hours in runtime. While noting that the 249-gram weight and price increase are tough pills to swallow for users of last year's model, they determined it represents an exceptional upgrade for anyone holding an iPhone 14 Pro or 15 Pro.

NNick Ackerman · iPhone 18 Pro Max Unboxing & First Impressions ↗

Nick Ackerman concluded from his first impressions that the 18 Pro Max is a refined, high-end powerhouse whose newly integrated manual camera controls and smaller Dynamic Island will delight creative professionals. However, because the design, chassis weight, and day-to-day handling feel very similar to the 17 Pro Max, he viewed it as an incremental revision that most recent buyers can comfortably pass on.

AAndrew Clare · I Switched to the iPhone 18 Pro Max | Here's What Surprised Me ↗

Andrew Clare concluded that the phone's standout achievement is its thermal stability, showing zero heat buildup or throttling even when shooting and editing 4K video outdoors in intense heat. Although he was put off by how noticeably heavy the phone feels in daily carry, he found the more natural photo tuning and flexible manual video controls to be rewarding upgrades for mobile content creators.

How long does the battery last on the iPhone 18 Pro Max?

The phone packs a 5391 mAh battery and supports 60 W fast charging. In typical mixed use you can expect about a full day, but the 120 Hz screen may reduce endurance under heavy use.

Is the iPhone 18 Pro Max good for gaming?

Yes, the Apple A20 Pro chipset together with 12 GB RAM and a 120 Hz LTPO OLED panel delivers smooth frame rates. iOS optimisation also helps maintain consistent performance during long sessions.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 48 MP main sensor is 1/1.28″ with an f/1.5 aperture and optical image stabilization, which together capture more light. The result is clearer, less noisy photos even after dark.
