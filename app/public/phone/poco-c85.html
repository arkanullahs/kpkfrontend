Buying verdict

POCO C85 Is it worth buying?

If you can live with a low‑resolution screen and some software ads, the POCO C85 gives you a huge battery and cheap 5G connectivity.

Choose it if
You need long battery life, basic camera and cheap 5G without caring about high‑end gaming or a sharp display.
Consider alternatives if
You require a crisp high‑resolution screen, smooth gaming performance, or an official manufacturer warranty.

What works well

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts days
  • 33 W fast charging
  • 5G support
  • headphone jack and FM radio

Reported trade-offs

  • 720p on 6.9" screen looks soft
  • eMMC storage slows apps
  • heats and throttles during gaming
  • HyperOS ads are aggressive

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh cell easily reaches two to three days of use, and the 33 W charger refills it quickly, though the charger is not included. The 50 MP main sensor captures decent daylight photos but struggles in low light, and video quality is basic. Gaming feels sluggish because the eMMC storage limits speed and the phone heats up, causing throttling.

The 6.9‑inch IPS LCD is bright at 810 nits but only 720p, so text and images look soft. HyperOS 2 runs Android 15 and promises two major updates, but it is packed with ads and bloatware. The device arrives as a gray‑import with no official warranty, only the shop’s limited cover, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 6000 mAh battery easily reaches two to three days of normal use, even with 5G enabled. Heavy gaming or video playback will reduce that time, but most owners report excellent endurance.

Will the phone handle modern games without lag?

Gaming is limited by the eMMC storage and heating. The phone can run games at lower settings, but it often throttles and feels laggy at 60 fps, especially during long sessions.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor performs decently in daylight, but low‑light shots are poor with noise and lack of detail. Video recording is basic and not suited for dark environments.

Does the POCO C85 come with an official warranty and updates?

All units are gray‑import and have no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop offers its own limited warranty, and you must confirm IMEI and BTRC status. The phone runs Android 15 with HyperOS 2 and promises up to two major Android upgrades.
