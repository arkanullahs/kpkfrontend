Tecno Spark 50C Is it worth buying?

If you need a big screen and a big battery on a tight budget, the Tecno Spark 50C delivers that basic experience.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Consider alternatives if
You need fast charging, strong low‑light camera or guaranteed manufacturer warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Large 6.78 inch 120Hz display
  • 5200 mAh battery lasts all day
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • Helio G81 handles casual games

Reported trade-offs3

  • Software shows many ads
  • Only 1‑2 Android updates promised
  • 15W charging is slow

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch IPS LCD runs at 120Hz, making everyday scrolling feel smooth. The 5200 mAh cell lasts through a full day of social media, video streaming and light gaming without hunting for a charger. The Helio G81 chipset handles casual games but will struggle with demanding titles at high settings.

Android 16 with HiOS 16 brings the latest UI but is cluttered with ads and receives only one or two major updates, so long‑term software support is limited. Camera performance is adequate in daylight with a 13MP main sensor, yet low‑light shots are noisy and lack detail. Charging at 15W is noticeably slow compared to faster rivals.

Read full reasoning

Owners appreciate the headphone jack, FM radio and IR blaster, but complain about the heavy software bloat and the lack of official warranty on gray‑import listings.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 5200 mAh battery can easily cover a full day of browsing, video streaming and light gaming. Heavy gaming or bright screen use will reduce endurance, but most users report reaching the evening without recharging.

Can the phone handle mobile games smoothly?

The Helio G81 chipset with 4 GB RAM runs casual games without lag. More demanding titles may need lower graphics settings to stay smooth, and the 15W charger will not refill the battery quickly after long sessions.

Is the camera good in low light conditions?

The 13 MP main camera captures decent photos in daylight. In low light the images become noisy and lack detail, so it is not ideal for night photography or dim indoor scenes.

Will the phone receive software updates?

The device ships with Android 16 and HiOS 16 and is expected to receive only one or two major OS updates. Updates are slow and the software includes a lot of ads, so long‑term support is limited.
