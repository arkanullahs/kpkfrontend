Tecno Spark 50C Is it worth buying?

If you need a big screen and a battery that can survive a full day without a charger, the Tecno Spark 50C delivers that at a very low price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large screen, long battery life and a cheap price and can tolerate ads and modest camera quality.
Consider alternatives if
You need fast charging, a high‑end camera or a clean, ad‑free software experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 5200 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 120 Hz display feels smooth
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • Helio G81 handles everyday tasks

Reported trade-offs4

  • 15 W charging is slow
  • HiOS UI shows many ads
  • Camera performance is average
  • LCD quality is modest

Why we say that

The 6.78‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, making scrolling and simple games feel smooth, while the 5200 mAh cell keeps you online for many hours. Performance is adequate for everyday apps thanks to the Helio G81 and 4 GB RAM, but heavy gaming can show lag. Owners love the headphone jack, FM radio and IR blaster, but they complain about the HiOS interface that pushes many ads and the 15 W charger that refills the large battery slowly.

The 13 MP camera produces average pictures, especially in low light, and the LCD panel is only modest in sharpness. There is no 5G and the plastic body, though rated IP69, feels cheap. Official warranty is only on the listed unit, gray imports lack cover and need paid repairs.
