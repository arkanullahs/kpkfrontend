Realme GT Neo 6 5G Is it worth buying?

The Realme GT Neo 6 5G is a high-performance device that relies on the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset to handle demanding tasks.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize raw performance and display quality over official brand support and are comfortable managing a gray-import device.
Consider alternatives if
You require a reliable manufacturer warranty or dislike dealing with pre-installed ads and bloatware on your software.

What works well4

  • Powerful Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset
  • Extremely bright 6000 nits peak display
  • Large 5500 mAh battery capacity
  • High-quality 120Hz LTPO AMOLED screen

Reported trade-offs3

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Medium to high levels of bloatware and ads
  • Repairs are entirely at your own expense

Why we say that

You get a massive 5500 mAh battery and a display that hits 6000 nits peak brightness, making it very usable outdoors. Because this is a gray-import device, you must accept that there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Repairs are paid, and you are limited to whatever support the specific shop provides.

Users should be aware that the software comes with a medium to high amount of bloatware and ads. You are essentially paying for raw hardware power while sacrificing the peace of mind that comes with official brand support. Always verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your money to ensure the device is not restricted.

Does this phone come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you only receive whatever limited support the individual shop provides. All repairs must be paid for by the owner.

How long will this device receive software updates?

The Realme GT Neo 6 5G is expected to receive between 2 to 3 OS updates. It currently runs on Android 14 with Realme UI 5.0 out of the box.

Is the battery life good for heavy usage?

The device is equipped with a 5500 mAh battery. This capacity is designed to support the power demands of the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset and the high-brightness display during daily use.
