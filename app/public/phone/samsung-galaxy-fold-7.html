Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7 Is it worth buying?

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7 is a powerful but deeply flawed flagship that demands significant compromises for its high price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You are a power user who prioritizes a large foldable screen and top-tier performance above all other concerns.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable battery life, fast charging, or the security of an official manufacturer warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Powerful Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset
  • Excellent 8-inch foldable inner display
  • Remarkably thin and light build
  • Improved hinge and IP48 rating

Reported trade-offs4

  • Insufficient 4400 mAh battery capacity
  • Unacceptably slow 25W charging speed
  • Significant thermal throttling during use
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

While the 8.0-inch foldable display and Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset offer top-tier performance and visuals, the experience is marred by persistent hardware and software issues. Owners frequently report thermal throttling during heavy use, which limits the potential of the processor. The 4400 mAh battery is insufficient for the power-hungry screen, and the 25W charging speed is unacceptably slow for a 2025 device.

You will also face frustrations with the outer display flickering at low brightness and the removal of S Pen support. Because this phone is only available as a gray import in Bangladesh, you have no manufacturer warranty support. Repairs are entirely at your own expense through the shop.

With downgraded speakers and unwanted bloatware in One UI 8, the high cost makes this a difficult recommendation for most buyers.

Does the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available through gray-import channels. There is no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh regardless of the brand's service score. Any repair or coverage is limited to what the individual shop promises, and you will have to pay for repairs.

How long will the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7 receive software updates?

This device runs on Android 16 and One UI 8. As a flagship model, it is eligible for up to 7 major Android upgrades. You can expect long-term software support for the duration of the update cycle.

Is the battery life good enough for heavy daily usage?

Owners report that the 4400 mAh battery is insufficient for heavy usage and the power-hungry display. The charging speed is also a major concern, as the phone barely replenishes half of its battery charge in 30 minutes using the 25W charger.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

While the 200MP main camera is great for general photography, the telephoto and low-light performance are considered mediocre. If you rely heavily on night photography, you may find the camera output disappointing compared to other flagship devices.
