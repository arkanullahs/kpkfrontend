Reported trade-offs

  • 15 W charging takes about two hours
  • Camera produces poor pictures

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh battery truly lasts one to two days for most owners, and the 6.74‑inch IPS LCD with 90 Hz refresh feels smooth for everyday scrolling. The device supports 5G, has a headphone jack and FM radio, and comes with an official warranty at the cheapest price of ৳14,499. However, charging is painfully slow at 15 W, taking around two hours to fill.

The 13 MP rear and 5 MP front cameras produce poor pictures, especially in low light, and users report noticeable lag with heavy apps and games. The software is simple but carries medium bloat and ads. Overall, it is a budget‑friendly phone that excels in endurance but falls short in performance and imaging.
