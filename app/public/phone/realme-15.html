Buying verdict

Realme 15 5G Is it worth buying?

The Realme 15 5G offers a massive 7000 mAh battery that provides industry-leading endurance, but the overall experience is hampered by significant software and build quality issues.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize extreme battery life above all other features and do not mind a plastic build.
Consider alternatives if
You want a premium feeling device without software ads or need expandable storage for your files.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Industry-leading 7000 mAh battery capacity
  • Vibrant 144Hz 1.5K OLED display
  • Capable Dimensity 7300+ chipset
  • High resolution 50MP selfie camera

Reported trade-offs4

  • Cheap plastic build quality
  • Aggressive bloatware and ads
  • Video stabilization causes heavy cropping
  • Throttles under heavy loads

Why we say that

Owners frequently report that the plastic construction feels cheap and prone to micro-scratches, which is disappointing for a device at this price point. While the Dimensity 7300+ chipset handles daily tasks, it tends to throttle under heavy loads or while recording video. The software experience is a major pain point, as users are frustrated by aggressive bloatware and ads that feel out of place on a phone costing over ৳42,000.

Camera performance is mixed, with a decent main sensor offset by underwhelming ultrawide results and heavy cropping when video stabilization is enabled. Many buyers in Bangladesh feel the device is overpriced for the specifications provided, especially given the lack of an SD card slot for expandable storage.

Does the Realme 15 5G come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

The brand warranty applies only to the official unit. While you may find cheaper listings, these are gray imports that do not include manufacturer cover. Any service centre that accepts a gray import unit will only perform repairs on a paid basis.

How long will the Realme 15 5G receive software updates?

The device is scheduled to receive between 2 to 3 OS updates. Please note that the software experience includes medium to high levels of bloatware and ads, which owners have reported as a significant drawback for a phone in this price range.

Is the Realme 15 5G suitable for heavy gaming?

The Dimensity 7300+ chip is capable, but owners report that it throttles under heavy loads. If you plan to play demanding games for long sessions, you may experience performance drops as the hardware struggles to maintain peak output.

How does the camera perform for video recording?

The main sensor is decent for photos, but the video performance is underwhelming. Owners specifically report that enabling video stabilization results in a significant crop of the frame, which limits the utility of the camera for steady video recording.
