What works well4

  • 120 Hz LTPO OLED display
  • 48 MP main camera with OIS
  • Apple A19 chipset delivers smooth UI
  • 40 W wired charging

Reported trade-offs4

  • Device gets hot during normal use
  • Battery drains faster after iOS updates
  • USB C limited to USB 2.0 data speeds
  • No telephoto lens

Why we say that

The 6.3 inch LTPO OLED screen reaches 3000 nits peak and refreshes at 120 Hz, making everyday use feel smooth. The A19 chip and 8 GB RAM keep the UI responsive, but the device heats up during browsing or light gaming and may throttle. The 48 MP main sensor with OIS captures good photos in daylight, yet low‑light dynamic range is limited and there is no telephoto lens.

The 3692 mAh battery is efficient on paper but real‑world endurance drops after iOS updates and a power‑on bug has been reported. Charging at 40 W is decent, but USB‑C data speeds stay at USB 2.0 levels. Official warranty starts at ৳1,47,999; cheaper gray imports at ৳1,09,999 lack manufacturer cover and require paid repairs.
