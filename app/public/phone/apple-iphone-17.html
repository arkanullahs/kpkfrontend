Is the Apple iPhone 17 worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz LTPO OLED display
  • Apple A19 performance
  • 48 MP main camera with OIS
  • 256 GB storage at gray‑import price

What is not

  • Battery capacity only 3692 mAh
  • Gets very hot and throttles
  • USB‑C limited to USB 2.0 speeds
  • Boot may fail when charging from zero

Buy it if Buy it if you value iOS stability, a top‑class screen and ample storage over raw gaming performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need long battery life, heavy gaming or a guaranteed manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

The A19 chip and 8 GB RAM keep apps snappy and iOS 26 feels clean, though Apple Intelligence can be glitchy. The 48 MP main sensor with OIS delivers sharp photos, but there is no telephoto lens and low‑light performance is only average. The 3692 mAh battery feels small and many owners report only 4‑7 hours of screen‑on time, with the phone heating up and throttling during long sessions.

Charging at 40 W is fast at first but slows after 80 percent, and a few users say the phone won’t power on if the battery is completely drained. USB‑C works at USB 2.0 speeds, and gray‑import units lack official Apple warranty, meaning repairs are paid. Overall it feels premium for everyday use but struggles with heavy gaming and long battery endurance.
