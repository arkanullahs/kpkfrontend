Apple iPhone 17 Is it worth buying?

If you want an iPhone with a 256 GB base and 120 Hz display at the lowest price, the gray‑import iPhone 17 is the only realistic option.

  • 120 Hz LTPO OLED display

  • 48 MP main camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs4

  • 3692 mAh battery feels short
  • Gets very hot under load
  • 40 W charging slows after 80 percent
  • USB‑C limited to USB 2.0 speeds

Why we say that

The screen is bright, smooth and easy on the eyes, and the A19 chip makes everyday tasks feel instant. Photos are sharp thanks to the 48 MP sensor and optical image stabilization, and iOS 26 stays clean without ads. Owners complain that the 3692 mAh battery feels short, giving about four to seven hours of screen‑on time, and it gets very hot during charging or heavy use, which forces the processor to throttle.

The 40 W charger slows after reaching eighty percent, and data transfer is limited to USB 2.0 speeds. The cheapest listings are gray imports, so they lack Apple’s official warranty and any free repair service.
