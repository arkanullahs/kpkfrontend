Buying verdict

OPPO Find X8 Is it worth buying?

If you can accept an import without official warranty, the Find X8 offers a bright 120Hz AMOLED screen and strong performance for its price.

Choose it if
You want a high end spec phone and can handle an import with shop warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need official brand warranty or fast USB C data speeds.

What works well

  • 120Hz AMOLED display with 4500 nit peak brightness
  • Dimensity 9400 delivers smooth gaming
  • 5630 mAh battery lasts through video streaming
  • 50 MP main camera with good zoom and low light video

Reported trade-offs

  • Only USB 2.0 port slows file transfers
  • No official Bangladeshi warranty, rely on shop warranty
  • ColorOS 16 update not yet available

Why we say that

The Dimensity 9400 chipset keeps games and apps running smoothly, and the 5630 mAh battery easily handles a day of video streaming and browsing. The 50 MP main camera gives decent zoom and low light video, while the 32 MP selfie shooter is adequate for social media. Owners praise the display’s 4500 nit peak brightness and the smoothness of the 120Hz panel.

However, the phone ships with a USB 2.0 port, so moving large files takes longer than expected. There is no brand backed warranty in Bangladesh; you must rely on the shop’s warranty and verify the IMEI and BTRC status. Software updates are on schedule for up to five Android versions, but the ColorOS 16 upgrade has not arrived for many users yet.
