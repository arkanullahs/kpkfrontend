Redmi Note 17 5G Is it worth buying?

The massive 7700 mAh battery is the standout feature of the Redmi Note 17 5G, but you must be extremely careful about which version you purchase in Bangladesh.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize a massive battery and a large display for media consumption and are comfortable managing software settings.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable manufacturer warranty or want a device that works perfectly with Google services out of the box without technical configuration.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Massive 7700 mAh battery capacity
  • Bright 1800 nits AMOLED display
  • Includes a useful IR blaster
  • Smooth 120Hz refresh rate

Reported trade-offs3

  • High levels of bloatware and ads
  • China ROM units lack Google and Bangla
  • No manufacturer warranty on unofficial units

Why we say that

While the 6.99 inch AMOLED display with 1800 nits peak brightness offers a great viewing experience, the software situation is complicated. Many gray market units are China-market versions that lack Google services and Bangla language support, which are difficult to fix. You will also encounter a high amount of bloatware and advertisements, as this is a budget line device.

The Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 chipset is functional for daily tasks, but it is not a powerhouse. If you choose a cheaper unofficial unit, remember that you have no manufacturer warranty and any repairs at a service centre will be paid. Always verify if you are buying a global or China ROM before handing over your money.

Does the Redmi Note 17 5G support Google services and the Bangla language?

It depends on the specific unit you purchase. Many gray market units are China-market versions that lack Google services and Bangla language support. These versions are difficult to convert, so you should check the software build before buying.

Is the official warranty available for all Redmi Note 17 5G units in Bangladesh?

No, the brand warranty applies only to the official unit. The cheaper listings are gray imports with no manufacturer cover. If you take an unofficial unit to a service centre, they will only accept it as a paid repair.

How long will the Redmi Note 17 5G receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 16 and HyperOS 3. You can expect between 2 to 4 OS updates depending on the specific tier of the device.

Is this phone suitable for heavy gaming?

The phone uses a Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 chipset. While it handles daily tasks, it is positioned as a budget line device and is not designed for high-end gaming performance.
