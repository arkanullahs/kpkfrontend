Is the Redmi Note 17 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 8000 mAh battery for all‑day use
  • 7‑inch 120 Hz OLED display
  • Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 efficient chipset
  • 45W fast charging

What is not

  • Heavy software bloat and ads
  • Gaming performance weak
  • Build quality only average
  • Only shop warranty, not brand warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize long battery life and a bright high‑refresh display over gaming and a clean software experience.

Skip it if Skip it if you need strong gaming performance, a clean OS, or an official manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

It ships with a massive 8000 mAh battery that owners rate 9.7 out of 10, and the 45W charger restores most of the charge in under an hour. The 7‑inch OLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can hit 1800 nits, so it looks sharp even in bright sunlight. Performance is decent for everyday tasks thanks to the Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 4nm chip, but the low‑tier cores struggle with demanding games and heavy multitasking.

Software is HyperOS 3, which brings a lot of bloatware and frequent ads, especially on the budget version. The phone arrives as a gray‑import, meaning you get a shop warranty instead of the official Xiaomi warranty and you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

Trakin Tech · Redmi Note 17 5G Unboxing & First Look ⚡ 8000mAh, 6.99" 120Hz AMOLED & More ↗

Trakin Tech highlights the Redmi Note 17 5G's massive 6.9-inch 120Hz display and extraordinary 8,000 mAh battery as its primary selling points, suggesting it can easily last two to three days. While noting the lack of stereo speakers and the fact that the phone is quite heavy, they feel the Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 handles everyday tasks and casual gaming adequately. They praise the inclusion of a 45W charger, four years of software updates, and an IP65 rating, concluding it's a solid choice for those prioritizing screen size and endurance.

ATC Android ToTo Company · Redmi note 17 : note series এর এ কি দশা? 🙂 ↗

ATC provides a highly critical review, arguing the Redmi Note 17 represents a severe downfall for the once-beloved Note series, largely blaming the rising costs of RAM and storage. They sharply criticize the phone for stepping backward in almost every category except battery size, pointing out the downgrade to a Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 processor, the removal of the ultrawide camera, the inexplicable limitation of 1080p 30FPS video, and the lack of stereo speakers. Ultimately, they strongly advise against buying it at its current price, stating it has completely lost the series' reputation for value.

Sillycorns · The New Redmi Note 17 is Here! ↗

Sillycorns takes a measured approach, acknowledging that the Redmi Note 17 is a product of difficult market conditions in 2026. They praise the massive flat display, the impressive battery life that exceeds 10 hours of screen-on time, and the generous update policy. However, they are disappointed by the missing stereo speakers, the downgrade to an entry-level processor, and the lack of 4K video recording. They conclude that while it's heavily compromised compared to past years, it remains an option for buyers whose sole priorities are a giant screen and a battery that never dies.

Is the Redmi Note 17 5G good for gaming?

Gaming performance is considered poor. The Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 uses low‑tier cores that cannot handle demanding titles well, and owners give the gaming experience a rating of 4.5 out of 10.

Will the phone have Google services and Bangla language support?
