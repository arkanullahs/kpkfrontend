Why we say that

At ৳11,299 it is the cheapest phone with an official warranty, though the listing does not confirm any warranty details. Its 7,000 mAh cell paired with a 6.8 inch 120 Hz TFT LCD provides long screen time, yet the display resolution is low and the bezels are noticeable. The Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 chipset with 4 GB RAM handles messaging and video apps smoothly, but it struggles with demanding games such as PUBG.

The 50 MP main camera captures acceptable daylight shots, while low light photos are weak and video quality is described as very weak. Android 15 Go edition on MagicOS keeps the software snappy, though it carries medium bloat and receives only a few major OS updates like most budget devices.

What is the Honor Play 10 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Honor Play 10 in Bangladesh was ৳11,299, checked 15 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 7,000 mAh battery can easily last up to two days for light users, and it provides several hours of screen time even with moderate use. Heavy use will reduce that to a full day.

Can I play heavy games like PUBG on this phone?

The phone runs basic apps and YouTube without trouble, but the Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 with 4 GB RAM struggles with demanding titles such as PUBG. Expect low frame rates and occasional lag.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP sensor delivers acceptable photos in daylight, but in low light situations the images are dark and lack detail. Owners report poor low light performance and weak video quality.

Will this phone receive software updates and does it have a warranty?

It ships with Android 15 Go edition on MagicOS and typically receives three to four major OS updates like other budget models. The listing does not specify any manufacturer warranty, so you cannot rely on official coverage.
