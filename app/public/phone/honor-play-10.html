Buying verdict

Honor Play 10 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can stay on for two days without charging, the Honor Play 10 delivers that at a very low price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want an ultra‑budget 5G phone with long battery life and can accept basic performance and modest camera quality.
Consider alternatives if
You need fast charging, high‑end gaming, or good video and low‑light photography.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 7,000 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • 5G connectivity at a budget price
  • Android 15 Go runs snappy on low specs
  • 50 MP main camera works in daylight

Reported trade-offs4

  • 10 W charging is painfully slow
  • Low‑resolution TFT LCD with noticeable bezels
  • Poor low‑light camera performance
  • Weak video recording quality

Why we say that

The 7,000 mAh battery is the strongest point, giving light users up to 48 hours of use. The 6.8‑inch TFT LCD runs at 120 Hz but its low resolution and thick bezels make the visual experience feel basic. Powered by a Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 and 4 GB RAM, it handles WhatsApp, YouTube and everyday apps smoothly, yet it falters with demanding games like PUBG.

The 50 MP main camera produces acceptable daylight shots, but low‑light photos are noisy and video quality is weak. Charging at only 10 W is painfully slow, often taking many hours to fill the large battery. Android 15 Go with Magic OS keeps the software responsive, though some bloat and ads are present.

Read full reasoning

Overall, it offers solid value for ultra‑budget buyers who prioritize battery life over performance and media quality.
