Reported trade-offs4

  • HyperOS loaded with bloatware and ads
  • Global version missing telephoto lens
  • Performance throttles during extended gaming

Why we say that

It offers a 6.67‑inch curved AMOLED panel that hits 120 Hz and can peak at 3000 nits, making outdoor viewing bright and colors vivid. The 6200 mAh battery paired with 90 W wired charging gives you a full day of use and a quick top‑up, though the global version’s capacity is a bit lower than the Chinese model. Performance is driven by the Snapdragon 7s Gen 3, which handles everyday apps fine but will throttle after about twenty minutes of heavy gaming, and the HyperOS skin adds noticeable bloatware and ads that you must manually remove.

Camera wise, the 50 MP main sensor is solid, but the global variant lost the 2.5× telephoto lens, so zoom quality drops and overall camera scores sit around six out of ten.
