Buying verdict

itel A100C Is it worth buying?

The itel A100C is a budget device that struggles with basic performance, making it a difficult choice for daily use.

Choose it if
Buy this phone only if you need a very basic device for simple communication and media consumption on a strict budget.
Consider alternatives if
You expect smooth performance, decent photography, or a display that remains visible under direct sunlight.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Large 5000 mAh battery capacity
  • Includes a convenient headphone jack
  • Features a useful IR blaster
  • Attractive design mimicking flagship models

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe performance lag and multitasking issues
  • Dim 400 nit display with poor outdoor visibility
  • Slow and frustrating 10W charging speed
  • Software bloated with ads and heavy apps

Why we say that

While it offers a large 5000 mAh battery, owners report significant frustration with severe lag during multitasking and heavy applications. The 6.6 inch display is dim at 400 nits, leading to poor visibility outdoors, and the 90Hz refresh rate is inconsistent, often dropping to 60Hz. The camera performance is basic, with the 8MP sensor failing to capture proper detail or exposure.

Software is another major pain point, as the Android 15 Go edition comes pre-loaded with heavy bloatware and ads that further slow down the Unisoc T7100 chipset. While the design mimics more expensive phones, the plastic build feels budget-grade. Charging is also a major drawback, as the 10W speed is outdated for such a large battery.

Read full reasoning

If you prioritize smooth performance or photography, this phone will likely disappoint you.

Does the itel A100C have a good battery life?

The phone features a solid 5000 mAh battery that provides reliable endurance for light daily tasks. However, you should be aware that the 10W charging speed is quite slow and can be frustrating when you need to recharge the device.

Can I play games on this phone?

Gaming performance is very poor on this device. Owners report severe lag and difficulty running heavy applications, so it is strictly intended for basic communication and media rather than gaming.

How is the camera performance in low light?

The 8MP main camera is a basic sensor that struggles with exposure and detail in varied lighting conditions. You should not expect high quality results, especially when the lighting is not ideal.
