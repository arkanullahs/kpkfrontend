Vivo X500 Pro Max Is it worth buying?

If you can live with a Chinese‑origin software layer and no official brand warranty, the Vivo X500 Pro Max feels like a flagship that never runs out of juice.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a huge battery, high‑refresh display and strong camera and can accept a Chinese software experience.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on Google apps, need official warranty support, or want a Bangla‑friendly UI.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 6.85" LTPO AMOLED with 144 Hz refresh
  • 8000 mAh battery and 90 W fast charging
  • Dimensity 9600 Pro handles gaming well
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS and telephoto

Reported trade-offs3

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • China OriginOS lacks Google and Bangla
  • Updates come only from Chinese servers

Why we say that

The 6.85‑inch LTPO AMOLED screen stays bright even in direct sun thanks to a 4400‑nit peak and 144 Hz refresh, while the Dimensity 9600 Pro keeps games smooth and apps responsive. The 8000 mAh battery lasts well beyond a day and the 90 W charger tops it up in under an hour. Photography is solid with a 50 MP main sensor, OIS and 3.5× telephoto, and the 50 MP selfie camera is sharp.

Daily friction comes from the OriginOS China ROM that lacks Google services, Bangla language support and receives OTA updates only from Chinese servers. Repairs must be paid and the shop warranty replaces the missing manufacturer warranty. Overall it is a powerful, large‑battery phone that suits users comfortable with the software trade‑off.

Does the Vivo X500 Pro Max have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

All units listed are gray‑import China ROMs, so they do not carry any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop you buy from may offer its own limited warranty, but any repair will be paid.

How many Android updates will this phone receive?

The phone ships with Android 17 and OriginOS 7 and is promised up to five major OS updates. The updates are delivered from Chinese servers, not from Vivo Bangladesh.

Will the 8000 mAh battery last through a full day of heavy use?

Yes, the 8000 mAh cell easily exceeds a day of typical browsing, video streaming and gaming. The 90 W charger refills the battery from zero to full in about an hour.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with a large 1/1.28" size, f/1.6 aperture and optical image stabilization captures decent low‑light shots, though the lack of Google processing may affect some software features.
