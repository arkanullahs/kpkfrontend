Buying verdict

Tecno Spark 40 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Tecno Spark 40 Pro offers a high-end display experience, but owners report significant frustrations with long-term reliability and software support.

Choose it if
You primarily want a bright, high-refresh-rate display for media consumption and do not mind heavy software ads.
Consider alternatives if
You are a gamer, care about long-term battery health, or expect timely software and security updates.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Bright 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Includes headphone jack and IR blaster
  • Fast 45W charging support
  • Large 5200 mAh battery

Reported trade-offs

  • Battery health drops after three months
  • Heavy thermal throttling during gaming
  • Ad-heavy software with limited updates
  • Camera lacks proper tuning

Why we say that

While the 144Hz AMOLED screen is a standout feature, the actual daily experience is marred by performance throttling during gaming and a noticeable decline in battery health after just three months of use. Users frequently express disappointment regarding the lack of future Android updates, specifically noting that the device will not receive Android 16. The software environment is also heavily burdened by ads and bloatware, which impacts the overall ease of use.

While the hardware includes useful additions like an IR blaster and a headphone jack, the camera lacks proper tuning despite the 50MP sensor. If you prioritize a bright, smooth screen over consistent software support and long-term battery longevity, this device might catch your eye. However, the combination of thermal throttling and limited update cycles makes it a difficult recommendation for those who keep their phones for more than a year.
