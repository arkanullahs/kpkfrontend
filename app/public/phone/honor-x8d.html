Is the Honor X8d worth buying?

What is good

  • 7000 mAh battery lasting two to three days
  • 6.77 inch AMOLED screen with 3000 nit peak
  • 108 MP main camera produces sharp daylight photos
  • 45W fast charging fills the large battery quickly

What is not

  • Outdated 4G only chipset struggles with heavy games
  • No microSD slot limits storage expansion
  • Low light photos are weak and noisy
  • 120Hz refresh feels jittery on the display

Buy it if Buy it if you mainly use apps, social media and calls and value long battery life over top tier performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need 5G, high frame rate gaming or strong low light photography.

Why we say that

The 6.77 inch AMOLED panel can reach a peak of 3000 nit, making it bright enough for outdoor use, but the 120Hz refresh feels a bit jittery to many owners. The 108 MP main sensor produces sharp pictures in daylight, yet low light results are noisy and the video caps at 1080p. Under the hood sits a Snapdragon 6s 4G Gen 2 processor with 8 GB RAM, which owners rate low for multitasking and heavy games.

The large 7000 mAh battery scores a 9.6 rating and typically lasts two to three days, and the 45W charger refills it quickly. Software runs MagicOS 10 on Android 16, feeling clean but a bit heavy for the chip. Overall the phone feels solid with an IP65 plastic body, though it can feel slippery in hand.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The Honor X8d uses a 7000 mAh cell that most owners say lasts two to three days of normal use, so you can go several days without reaching the charger unless you run power hungry apps constantly.
