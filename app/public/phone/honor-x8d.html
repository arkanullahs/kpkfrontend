Honor X8d Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can sit on the charger for a day and still run for two days, the Honor X8d delivers.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value long battery life and a large bright screen more than top‑tier speed.
Consider alternatives if
You need smooth gaming, strong low light photos, or an official warranty without extra risk.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Huge 7000 mAh battery
  • Bright 120Hz AMOLED display
  • 45W fast charging
  • 108MP daylight camera

Reported trade-offs4

  • 2019 chipset causes lag
  • No microSD slot or 3.5mm jack
  • Weak top speaker
  • Low light camera performance poor

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh battery easily lasts one to two days for most users and the 6.77" AMOLED panel reaches a bright 3000‑nit peak with a smooth 120Hz refresh. The 45W charger refills the large cell quickly. However the Snapdragon 6s 4G Gen 2 chipset is from 2019 and owners describe lag in heavy apps and modern games.

The 108MP main camera is acceptable in daylight but struggles in low light and video quality is low. Build quality feels dated with plastic housing, no microSD slot and no 3.5mm headphone jack, and the top speaker is noticeably weaker than the bottom one. Official warranty only applies to the cheapest official listing, while cheaper gray imports lack manufacturer cover.
