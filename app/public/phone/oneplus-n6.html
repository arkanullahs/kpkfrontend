OnePlus N6 Is it worth buying?

The OnePlus N6 is a device that prioritizes battery capacity over almost every other hardware specification.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize extreme battery life above all other features and do not intend to use the device for demanding gaming or high-end photography.
Consider alternatives if
You want a balanced smartphone with a modern display, strong gaming performance, or official brand warranty support in Bangladesh.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Industry-leading 8000 mAh battery capacity
  • Durable build with IP65 rating
  • MIL-STD-810H military grade certification
  • Clean OxygenOS 16 software experience

Reported trade-offs4

  • Underpowered Dimensity 6360 chipset
  • Outdated IPS display with thick bezels
  • Useless secondary camera sensors
  • Poor value for money compared to competitors

Why we say that

While the 8000 mAh battery provides industry-leading longevity, owners report that the phone is significantly overpriced and fails to compete with alternatives from brands like Samsung or iQOO. The Dimensity 6360 chipset is widely considered underpowered, leading to mediocre gaming performance despite the inclusion of a vapor chamber. Users are particularly disappointed by the outdated IPS display, which features thick bezels and low resolution.

The camera system is another point of contention, as the secondary sensors are described as non-functional marketing filler. With a build that is durable and IP65 rated, the phone feels sturdy, but the overall experience is marred by poor value for money. Owners have expressed frustration regarding the hype surrounding the device, often labeling it as e-waste.

Does the OnePlus N6 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you must rely on the specific shop for any repair promises. You should confirm the status of your device with the seller before paying.

How long will the OnePlus N6 receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 16 with OxygenOS 16 out of the box. It is limited to only two major operating system updates during its lifespan. This is a significant limitation compared to other devices in the market.

Is the OnePlus N6 suitable for mobile gaming?

Gaming performance on this device is mediocre. Although the phone includes a vapor chamber, the Dimensity 6360 chipset is underpowered for modern gaming needs. Owners have rated the gaming experience as a 5.0 out of 10.

How does the camera perform on this phone?

The camera performance is basic and generally disappointing to users. While it features a 50MP main sensor, the secondary sensors are non-functional marketing filler. Owners have rated the camera quality at 4.5 and video quality at 4.0 out of 10.
