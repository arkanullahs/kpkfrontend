Is the Samsung Galaxy A24 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.5 inch Super AMOLED 90Hz display
  • 50MP main camera with optical image stabilization
  • 5000 mAh battery with strong endurance
  • Helio G99 chipset with 8GB RAM

What is not

  • Heavy games lag and cause low frame rates
  • Low light photos are weak and noisy
  • New Android updates bring lag and app crashes
  • No water resistance rating

Buy it if Buy it if you want a bright AMOLED screen and decent main camera without needing high performance gaming or long term software updates.

Skip it if Skip it if you need smooth multitasking, strong low light photography, or a phone that will receive updates for many more years.

Why we say that

The 6.5 inch Super AMOLED screen reaches 1000 nits peak and runs at 90Hz, so colors are vivid and scrolling feels smooth. The 50MP main sensor with optical image stabilization captures clear photos in good light, while the ultrawide lens and low light performance are weak. The Helio G99 chipset paired with 8GB RAM handles everyday tasks, but heavy games stumble and multitasking feels cramped.

Android 13 comes with One UI 8.5 and promises up to four major Android upgrades, yet owners report lag and app crashes after updating to Android 16. The phone keeps a headphone jack and a 5000 mAh battery, but it lacks any IP water resistance rating. At 38199 taka you get an official warranty, but being three years old means software support may end soon.

Overall it suits users who value display and basic photography more than gaming or future proof software stability.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

The 5000 mAh battery gives strong endurance and can last most of a day under normal use. Some owners notice faster drain after installing newer Android updates, so real‑world life may vary.

Will this phone handle modern games well?

The Helio G99 with 8GB RAM is adequate for basic games, but heavy titles often lag and drop frames. Expect limited performance if gaming is a priority.

Is the camera usable in low light situations?

The 50MP main sensor works well in good light thanks to optical image stabilization. The ultrawide lens and overall low light performance are weak, so night shots may appear noisy and lack detail.

How many Android updates will it receive and is the warranty official?

Samsung promises up to four major Android upgrades, and the device can be upgraded to Android 16. It comes with an official warranty at 38199 taka, but being three years old the remaining software‑update window is short.
