Is the Samsung Galaxy A24 worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 1000 nit Super AMOLED display
  • 5000 mAh battery lasts a full day
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • 25 W charging (charger not included)

What is not

  • Quiet single speaker, poor audio
  • Ultrawide camera unusable
  • Plastic chassis creaks, no IP rating
  • Lag and crashes after Android 16 update

Buy it if Buy it if you want a bright screen and long battery life on a tight budget.

Skip it if Skip it if you need smooth gaming or reliable latest‑software performance.

Why we say that

Its 6.5 inch Super AMOLED panel reaches 1000 nit, so the screen stays bright even in sunlight. The 5000 mAh battery paired with a 25 W charger gives excellent endurance, though the box does not include a charger. Photography is anchored by a 50 MP main sensor with optical image stabilization, which works well in good light, while the ultrawide lens is practically unusable.

Performance feels smooth for everyday apps, but gaming is limited to low settings and the device can stutter after moving to One UI 8 and Android 16. The plastic chassis feels cheap and the single speaker is quiet, and there is no water resistance rating. Software support is limited; three years have passed and further major updates are unlikely.

Overall it is a budget friendly phone that suits users who need a bright display and long battery life but do not rely on heavy gaming or the latest software.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh cell provides excellent endurance and reviewers say it easily lasts a full day of heavy use, so you can expect a day long run even with video streaming and browsing.

Can the Galaxy A24 handle mobile games smoothly?

Gaming runs at low settings and stays stable, but the Helio G99 chipset and 8 GB RAM struggle with demanding titles, and owners notice lag after the Android 16 update.

Is the camera good for low light photos?

The 50 MP main sensor with optical image stabilization captures decent low light shots, but the ultrawide lens is poor and unusable, and overall low light performance is limited compared to higher end phones.

Will I get software updates for this phone?

The device launched with Android 13 and is promised up to four major Android upgrades, but at three years old the update window is closing and owners report lag after the latest One UI 8 Android 16 upgrade.
