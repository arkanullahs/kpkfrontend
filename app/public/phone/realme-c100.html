Realme C100 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can survive drops, rain and a full day of heavy use, the Realme C100 will keep you going but you will notice compromises everywhere else.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value battery endurance, rugged build and 5G and can accept modest performance and a blurry screen.
Consider alternatives if
You need a sharp display, fast storage or flagship gaming power.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Huge 7,000 mAh battery with 45 W fast charge
  • IP69 water and dust protection
  • 144 Hz high refresh display
  • 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs4

  • HD+ low resolution screen
  • 50 MP camera weak in low light
  • Dimensity 6300 struggles with heavy games
  • Slow eMMC 5.1 storage

Why we say that

The 7,000 mAh battery (reviewed as 8,000 mAh) and 45 W fast charge let you go several days without plugging in, and the IP69 rating means you can toss it in water without panic. The 6.8‑inch 144 Hz panel feels smooth but the HD+ resolution looks blurry on a screen that size, and the eMMC 5.1 storage feels sluggish when apps load. The 50 MP main camera produces decent daylight shots but struggles with dynamic range and low light, matching the low owner rating.

Gaming performance is limited by the entry level Dimensity 6300, which can’t sustain modern heavy titles. Realme UI 7 is clean but filled with bloatware and you should expect only two to three Android upgrades. Overall the phone lives up to its rugged, long‑lasting promise but feels overpriced for the hardware it offers.
