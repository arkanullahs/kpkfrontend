POCO F7 Is it worth buying?

If you can live with a gray‑import phone that has no official brand warranty, the POCO F7 gives you flagship‑level performance at a mid‑range price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want top‑tier specs for gaming and media and can accept a gray‑import warranty and software bloat.
Consider alternatives if

What works well4

  • Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 performance
  • 6.83 inch 120Hz AMOLED display
  • 90W fast charging
  • IP68 premium build

Reported trade-offs4

  • Battery drains fast on HyperOS
  • Phone overheats under load
  • High ads and bloat in software
  • No official manufacturer warranty

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset and 12 GB RAM make gaming and multitasking feel smooth, and the 6.83‑inch AMOLED panel stays bright even in direct sunlight. The 90W charger refills the 6500 mAh battery quickly, but HyperOS drains power fast and the device gets noticeably hot during heavy use. Owners also complain about intrusive ads and pre‑installed bloat that affect the overall experience.

The build feels premium with an IP68 rating, yet you must rely on the shop’s limited warranty and verify the IMEI before buying. In everyday use the phone shines for media consumption and performance, but battery endurance and software polish are its weak points.

Does the POCO F7 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray‑import units, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop may offer its own limited warranty, but repairs are paid and you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.

How long does the battery last and how fast does it charge?

The 6500 mAh battery charges to full in about 40 minutes with the 90W charger, but HyperOS consumes power quickly and many owners report poor battery endurance and overheating during heavy use.

Is the camera good for low‑light photography?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.5 aperture and OIS performs decently in low light, but the ultrawide and selfie cameras are basic and may not match dedicated flagship cameras in challenging lighting.

Will the phone receive Android updates and for how long?

The device ships with Android 15 and HyperOS 2 and is expected to get two to three major OS updates. However, the software includes a lot of ads and bloat, and many users switch to custom ROMs for better performance and battery life.
