Buying verdict

POCO F7 Is it worth buying?

Expect a powerful machine that struggles with its own software.

Choose it if
You want high gaming performance and a bright screen and do not mind using custom ROMs.
Consider alternatives if
You want a clean software experience without ads or overheating issues.

What works well

  • Bright 3200 nits AMOLED display
  • Strong Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 performance
  • Premium build with IP68 rating
  • Large 6500 mAh battery

Reported trade-offs

  • High amount of ads and bloatware
  • Overheating issues
  • Poor battery life on HyperOS

Why we say that

Owners report that the phone gets easily overheated and battery life is poor on HyperOS. You will deal with high levels of ads and bloatware. Some users suggest that raw performance and battery are better on custom ROMs.

The hardware is strong with a bright screen and a premium build, but the software experience is a major pain point. It is a solid device if you can ignore the ads, but the overheating and software issues make it frustrating for some. You are paying for high specs that the current software cannot fully optimize.

POCO F7: what buyers ask us

What is the POCO F7 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for POCO F7 in Bangladesh was ৳41,500, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No official brand backed warranty is available. All units found are gray imports. You will only get a shop warranty, so you must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

How is the gaming performance on the POCO F7?

Gaming is a strength of this device. It uses the Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset and owners have given gaming a rating of 8.0 and performance a rating of 8.5.

Is the battery life good for daily use?

The phone has a large 6500 mAh battery, but owners report that battery life is poor on HyperOS. Some users mention that the phone gets easily overheated during use.

How many software updates will this phone get?

The phone comes with Android 15 and HyperOS 2. It is expected to receive 2 to 3 OS updates over its lifespan.
