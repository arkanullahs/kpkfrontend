Why we say that

The 6.67 inch AMOLED panel shines with a 4500 nit peak, but it stays at a fixed 120 Hz because it lacks LTPO adaptive refresh. Under the hood, the Dimensity 9400 chipset delivers the performance owners praise, scoring 9.7 in benchmarks and 9.5 in gaming, and recent updates have ironed out earlier heating concerns. The 5800 mAh battery easily reaches one and a half to two days of heavy use, and 90W charging refills it in roughly half an hour.

Camera wise, the 50 MP Zeiss tuned main sensor with f/1.6 aperture and OIS captures bright images, though the sensor is smaller than the Pro version and the phone cannot record 8K video. Software can be confusing: the global model runs Funtouch, while the China version runs OriginOS without Google services, and both carry medium bloat. Because every unit is a gray import, you rely on the shop’s limited warranty and must pay for any repairs.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 5800 mAh semi‑solid battery typically gives one and a half to two days of heavy use and longer with moderate use. Fast charging at 90W restores most of the charge in about half an hour.

Will the phone handle demanding games smoothly?

Dimensity 9400 places the X200 in the top tier for gaming. Benchmarks show high frame rates and the device scores 9.5 in gaming tests. Earlier heating reports have been fixed by recent software updates, so long sessions stay comfortable.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.6 aperture and optical image stabilization captures bright low‑light shots, and Zeiss tuning adds colour accuracy. The sensor is smaller than the Pro model, so extreme darkness may show some noise.

What warranty and software update support does the X200 have in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray imports, so there is no official manufacturer warranty. Shops may give a limited cover and you pay for repairs. The device runs Android 15 and promises up to four major Android upgrades, with two to three OS version updates.
