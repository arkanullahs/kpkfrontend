What works well

  • 5G connectivity at entry price
  • 5000 mAh battery with 25 W charge
  • 50 MP main camera for daylight shots
  • One UI Core receives up to four Android upgrades

Reported trade-offs

  • 4 GB RAM limits multitasking
  • HD+ LCD lacks AMOLED vibrancy
  • Gray import units have no manufacturer warranty

Why we say that

The 6.74" 90 Hz LCD shows HD+ resolution and 800‑nit peak brightness, which is fine for indoor use but lacks the punch of AMOLED. The 50 MP main sensor captures decent daylight shots, yet low‑light detail is limited. 4 GB RAM is a bottleneck; multitasking and future Android versions may strain the device.

Battery life is solid with a 5000 mAh cell and 25 W charge, lasting a day for typical use. Official units start at ৳19,499 and include Samsung’s warranty, while cheaper gray import listings around ৳22,890 have no manufacturer cover and only paid repairs. Software receives Android 15, One UI Core 7.0 and up to four major updates, offering longer support than many rivals.

How many Android updates will the Galaxy M06 5G receive?

The phone ships with Android 15 and One UI Core 7.0 and Samsung promises up to four major Android upgrades, which means you can expect software support for about four to six years, similar to other A series models.

Will the 5000 mAh battery last me a whole day?

A 5000 mAh cell gives the M06 5G roughly a full day of moderate use, and the 25 W charger refills it in about an hour and a half, which owners rate as acceptable for the price.

Can I play games like BGMI on this phone?

The Dimensity 6300 and 4 GB RAM handle basic titles, and games like BGMI or Free Fire run at low settings, but you will notice slowdowns when many apps are open, so heavy gaming is not ideal.
