Buying verdict

Vivo Y500 Is it worth buying?

The Vivo Y500 is a battery-focused device that prioritizes endurance over raw performance or camera quality.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize extreme battery life above all else and do not require high-end gaming performance or advanced video recording.
Consider alternatives if
You are a gamer, a heavy camera user, or someone who needs a clean software experience without ads and bloatware.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Exceptional two-day battery endurance
  • Vibrant 1.5K AMOLED display
  • Durable IP68 and IP69 rating
  • High 5000 nits peak brightness

Reported trade-offs4

  • Unisoc T7300 struggles with heavy loads
  • Video recording limited to 1080p
  • Noticeable bloatware and ads in software
  • No SD card slot for storage expansion

Why we say that

Owners report that the massive 8100 mAh battery provides industry-leading two-day endurance, which is the phone's standout feature. However, the Unisoc T7300 chipset struggles with heavy loads and gaming, leading many users to feel the device is overpriced for its internal hardware. The software experience is mixed, as OriginOS 6 is feature-rich but comes with noticeable bloatware, ads, and pre-installed third-party apps that require manual removal.

Camera performance is basic, with video recording limited to 1080p, which disappoints users expecting higher standards. Buyers must be careful regarding the software version, as gray-market units running the Chinese version of OriginOS lack Google services and Bangla language support. While the display is vibrant and the build quality is durable with an IP68/IP69 rating, the lack of an SD card slot and the presence of ads make this a polarizing choice for daily use.

Does the Vivo Y500 have a good battery life?

Yes, the phone features an industry-leading 8100 mAh battery. Owners rate the battery performance at 9.8 out of 10, noting that it provides exceptional two-day endurance for typical users.

Is the Vivo Y500 suitable for gaming?

The Unisoc T7300 chipset is adequate for daily tasks but struggles with heavy loads. Owners have given the gaming performance a low rating of 4.5 out of 10, suggesting it is not ideal for demanding games.

What is the difference between official and gray-import units?

Official units come with brand warranty and Funtouch OS. Gray-import units are built for China, run OriginOS, lack Google services, do not support Bangla, and have no manufacturer warranty coverage in Bangladesh.

How many years of software updates will this phone receive?

The global Funtouch OS version typically receives 3 OS updates on upper models and 2 OS updates on budget models. Please note that Chinese-market units rely on OTA updates only from Chinese servers.
