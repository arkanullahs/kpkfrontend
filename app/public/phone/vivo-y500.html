Is the Vivo Y500 worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 8100 mAh battery capacity
  • Bright 6.83 inch AMOLED display
  • High 120Hz refresh rate
  • High 5000 nits peak brightness

What is not

  • Unisoc T7300 processor is slow and lags
  • Poor camera video quality and no 4K
  • OriginOS contains ads and bloatware
  • No microSD card slot for storage

Buy it if Buy this phone only if you prioritize a massive battery above all other features and do not mind a slow interface.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you need a reliable camera, smooth gaming performance, or a clean software experience without ads.

Why we say that

While the massive 8100 mAh battery earns high praise from owners, the Unisoc T7300 chipset is widely reported to be slow and prone to lag during daily use. Users are particularly disappointed by the camera system, which is described as untuned and incapable of recording quality video. The software experience is another major pain point, as OriginOS comes with intrusive ads and bloatware.

Buyers must be extremely careful to distinguish between the global Funtouch OS version and the Chinese OriginOS version, as the latter lacks Google services and Bangla language support. Many owners feel the hardware does not match the cost, especially given the lack of a microSD card slot and the absence of 4K video support. It is a phone that looks impressive on paper with its bright display, but fails to deliver a smooth experience for the average user.

Is the battery life on the Vivo Y500 actually good?

Yes, the battery life is a standout feature of this device. Owners have given the battery a rating of 9.0 out of 10, specifically praising the massive 8100 mAh capacity for lasting a long time.

Can I use the Vivo Y500 for gaming?

Gaming performance is quite poor on this device. Owners have rated the gaming capabilities only 5.0 out of 10, noting that the Unisoc T7300 processor is slow and prone to lag during operation.

What is the difference between the official and gray import versions?

Official units run Funtouch OS, which is light and includes Google services. Gray import units often run OriginOS, which is designed for the Chinese market, lacks Google preinstalled, does not support Bangla, and requires updates from Chinese servers.

How many years of software updates will this phone receive?

The update policy depends on the model tier. Upper models receive 3 OS updates, while budget models receive 2 OS updates. You should verify which category your specific purchase falls into before buying.
