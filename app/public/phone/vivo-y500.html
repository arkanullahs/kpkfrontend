Is the Vivo Y500 worth buying?

What is good

  • 8100 mAh battery with two‑day endurance
  • 6.83‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED display
  • IP68/IP69 water‑dust protection
  • 50 MP main camera for decent stills

What is not

  • Unisoc T7300 struggles with heavy games
  • No SD card slot for expansion
  • CN OriginOS version lacks Google services
  • Limited 1080p video recording

Buy it if Buy it if you value long battery life and a large smooth display over top‑tier performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need fast gaming, high‑resolution video or official warranty coverage without extra risk.

Why we say that

The 8100 mAh battery paired with 44 W fast charging gives you genuine all‑day use and often a second day without topping up. The 6.83‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 5000 nits, so media looks bright and smooth even in sunlight. The 50 MP main sensor captures decent stills, but video stops at 1080p and low‑light performance is modest.

Under the hood, the Unisoc T7300 handles everyday apps fine but shows lag in heavy games and multitasking. The phone feels solid with an IP68/IP69 rating, yet the plastic frame and lack of an SD slot may disappoint some. Software runs OriginOS or Funtouch depending on the import version; the CN build brings extra bloatware and no Google apps, while the global build is lighter but still shows ads.

Official warranty applies only to the official‑warranty listings, while cheaper gray‑import units have only paid repair options.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

Will the Vivo Y500 run modern games smoothly?
