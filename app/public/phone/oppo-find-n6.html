Is the OPPO Find N6 worth buying?

What is good

  • 8.12 inch bright foldable display
  • 80W wired and 50W wireless fast charging
  • Thin lightweight build with IP58/59 durability
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance

What is not

  • Camera software oversaturation and shutter lag
  • Thermal throttling during intensive tasks
  • Average real‑world battery endurance
  • Only gray‑import warranty, no brand support

Buy it if Buy if you value a large foldable screen, fast charging and premium build and can accept the high price and limited warranty.

Skip it if Skip if you need reliable low‑light camera performance, long battery life, or an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 8.12 inch LTPO OLED folds with a bright 2500‑nit peak and a smooth 120Hz refresh. Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 delivers strong performance but the device gets noticeably hot near the camera bump during long games, causing occasional throttling. Battery capacity is 6000 mAh, yet real‑world endurance feels average for a phone this size.

Charging at 80W wired and 50W wireless is very quick, but the large battery does not translate into all‑day stamina. The 200 MP main sensor offers versatile lenses, but software processing adds heavy oversaturation and a three‑second shutter lag that frustrates quick shots. ColorOS 16 runs smoothly but includes medium bloat and aggressive background app limits, which can delay updates from apps like Life360.

Build quality is thin, lightweight and rated IP58/59, though only a gray‑import warranty is available, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.
