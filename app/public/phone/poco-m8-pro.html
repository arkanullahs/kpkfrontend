Buying verdict

POCO M8 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day without hunting for a charger, the POCO M8 Pro delivers.

Choose it if
You want long battery life, a bright screen and decent performance for everyday apps and casual gaming.
Consider alternatives if

What works well

  • 6500 mAh battery with 100 W fast charging
  • 6.83‑inch AMOLED panel reaching 3200 nits peak brightness
  • Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 provides solid mid‑range performance
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS and f/1.6 aperture

Reported trade-offs

  • HyperOS adds heavy bloatware and frequent ads
  • Build quality rated only average by owners
  • Camera struggles in low‑light situations
  • Software updates limited to about two to three major versions

Why we say that

The 6500 mAh cell paired with a 100 W charger keeps you online for more than a day of mixed use and refills quickly. Its 6.83‑inch AMOLED screen shines with up to 3200 nits, making outdoor viewing easy, and the 120 Hz panel feels smooth. The Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset with 8‑12 GB RAM offers reliable performance for apps and casual games, though it is not a flagship level.

The 50 MP main camera with OIS captures decent daylight photos, but owners rate low‑light results poorly. Software runs on HyperOS 2, which brings Android 15 and up to four major upgrades, but it is packed with bloatware and ads that many find intrusive. The phone arrives as gray‑import stock, so only a shop warranty is available and you must verify IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 6500 mAh cell, built on a 4 nm process, easily powers a full day of mixed use and often reaches two days with light browsing. When it runs low, the 100 W charger refills it in about half an hour.

Is the POCO M8 Pro good for gaming?

The Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset together with 8‑12 GB RAM gives smooth frame rates in most popular titles. Owners rate gaming at 7.0, noting stable performance during longer sessions, though it is not a flagship‑level experience.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor with f/1.6 aperture and OIS captures decent daylight shots, but owners give the camera a 6.2 rating, saying low‑light scenes appear noisy and lack detail despite the large sensor.

What warranty and software update support does the phone have in Bangladesh?

Only gray‑import units are available, so the phone comes with a shop warranty rather than a brand warranty. Buyers should verify the IMEI and BTRC status. HyperOS promises up to four Android upgrades but includes noticeable bloatware and ads.
