Is the POCO M8 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 6500 mAh battery with 100 W fast charge
  • 6.83 inch AMOLED 120 Hz display with 3200 nits peak
  • Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 delivers solid performance
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • HyperOS includes a lot of bloatware and ads
  • No clear manufacturer warranty listed
  • Build quality rated only average

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, a bright large screen and decent gaming without spending a fortune.

Skip it if Skip it if you require a premium camera, a clean software experience or an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.83 inch AMOLED panel offers bright colors and a smooth 120 Hz rhythm, though the build feels only average. Performance sits comfortably in the mid range thanks to the Snapdragon 7s Gen 4, and the 50 MP main camera with OIS handles most scenes adequately. Owners complain most about the HyperOS skin that adds a lot of bloatware and advertisements, and the lack of a clear manufacturer warranty means you must rely on a shop warranty and verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

The Tech Chap · POCO M8 5G & M8 Pro 5G Hands-On – 2026’s BEST Mid-Range Phones?! ↗

The Tech Chap is completely blown away by the sheer value the POCO M8 Pro offers for its price. He highly praises the flagship-level water resistance, incredibly bright AMOLED display, and the massive battery that charges in just 40 minutes. While he notes the lack of wireless charging and some video recording limits, he concludes it is easily one of the most compelling mid-range smartphones of the year.

Tech Spurt · POCO M8 Pro Review Vs POCO M8 | Best Budget Phone for 2026? ↗

Tech Spurt finds the POCO M8 Pro to be a solid hardware upgrade with impressive battery life, robust cooling, and reliable everyday performance. However, his experience was heavily marred by software bugs, unreliable Wi-Fi, and an operating system absolutely saturated with pre-installed bloatware. He appreciates the improved main camera but warns against buying it for graphically intense gaming.

GSMArena Official · Poco M8 Pro review: Almost perfect ↗

GSMArena considers the POCO M8 Pro an almost perfect mid-range device that shares much of its DNA with more expensive Xiaomi models. The reviewer highlights the durable build, stunning OLED display, and stellar battery endurance as major selling points. However, they strongly criticize the aggressive presence of system-wide ads and bloatware, which noticeably detract from an otherwise highly competitive hardware package.

Lim Reviews · POCO M8 Pro 5G: Even More Value! 🤯 Budget Price, Mid Range Specs! 🔥 ↗

Lim Reviews is highly impressed by the sheer size and capability of the device given its budget-friendly price tag. He focuses on the massive screen and excellent battery life, noting that it even acts as a reliable power bank for other devices. With smooth everyday performance and a cooling system that handles moderate gaming well, he sees it as an exceptional deal for buyers wanting maximum hardware value.
