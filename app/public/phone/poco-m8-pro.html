Why we say that

Living with this device means enjoying a massive 6500 mAh battery that lasts through heavy usage, supported by a bright 3200 nits AMOLED display. Owners report solid performance for gaming and daily tasks thanks to the Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset. However, the user experience is hampered by significant bloatware and advertisements within HyperOS 2.

While the hardware offers strong specifications, the build quality and camera performance receive lower ratings from users.
