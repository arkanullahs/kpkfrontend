Why we say that

The Google Tensor G6 chipset and 12GB RAM keep apps and games smooth, while the 6.3‑inch OLED panel delivers vivid colors at 120Hz and peaks at 3000 nits. The 48MP main sensor with OIS, 5x telephoto and ultrawide lenses captures detailed photos in good light, and the 10MP selfie camera is adequate for video calls. Android 17 arrives clean, and Google promises up to seven major OS updates, which is rare in this price range.

Battery capacity is 4985 mAh and charges at 30W, enough for a full day of moderate use but not as fast as some rivals. The biggest downside is that every unit is a gray import, so there is no official Google warranty in Bangladesh; repairs must be handled by the shop and are paid out of pocket. Confirm the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

Will the Pixel 11 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No. Every listing is a gray import, so the phone has no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop you buy from may offer its own limited warranty, but any repair will be paid by you.

How many Android updates will the Pixel 11 receive?

Google promises up to seven major OS updates for the Pixel 11, starting with Android 17. This is longer than most Android phones in the market and ensures new features for several years.

Is the battery enough for a full day of heavy use?

The phone has a 4985 mAh battery and charges at 30W. For typical heavy use, it should last a day, but power‑hungry tasks like gaming at 120Hz may require a charge before the evening.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 48MP main sensor uses a large 1/1.56" size, f/1.7 aperture and optical image stabilization, which helps capture brighter, clearer shots in low light. Results are good but not as bright as dedicated night‑mode phones.
