Google Pixel 11 Is it worth buying?

If you want a bright screen and a solid main camera but can live without official warranty, the Pixel 11 fits that niche.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value a vivid display, good photos and long software support and don’t need heavy gaming or official service.
Consider alternatives if
You need top‑tier gaming performance, fast charging or a brand‑backed warranty.

What works well4

  • 3000‑nit OLED display
  • 48 MP main sensor with AI
  • Clean Android 17 with 7‑year updates
  • Premium build quality

Reported trade-offs4

  • GPU lags in demanding games
  • Wired charging is slower than rivals
  • Average battery endurance
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

The 6.3‑inch OLED panel reaches a peak of 3,000 nits and runs at 120 Hz, making everyday use pleasant. The 48 MP main sensor with AI delivers good photos, though the 5× telephoto can be soft at times. Android 17 is clean and promises up to seven years of updates, which is a strong point for long‑term owners.

Performance feels snappy for apps and web browsing, but the Tensor G6 GPU falls behind rivals in demanding games. Battery life is average for a 4,985 mAh cell and the 30 W charger is slower than many flagships. The phone feels premium in hand, yet the design shows no major innovation.
