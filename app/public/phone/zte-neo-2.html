Buying verdict

ZTE Neo 2 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can sit on the charger for a short time and then run all day, the ZTE Neo 2 delivers that endurance.

Choose it if
You value long battery life, ample storage and can accept an average camera and occasional software quirks.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable low‑light camera, a headphone jack, or guaranteed timely software updates.

What works well

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 8 GB RAM with 256 GB storage
  • 120 Hz refresh rate on large screen
  • 5G connectivity for fast data

Reported trade-offs

  • No 3.5 mm headphone jack
  • Camera quality is poor in low light
  • Thermal throttling after software updates
  • Region‑locked Android 14 updates

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh battery and 33 W fast charge keep you online for many hours, and the 8 GB RAM with 256 GB storage handles most apps without lag. The 6.72‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, giving smoother scrolling than many budget phones. However, owners complain about the lack of a 3.5 mm headphone jack, which feels odd on a gaming‑styled device.

The 50 MP main sensor is marketed as high‑resolution but produces budget‑tier photos, especially in low light. Software updates to Android 14 are often region‑locked or missing, and the latest update introduced aggressive thermal throttling that can cause noticeable lag. Fingerprint scanner bugs also appear after upgrades.

Read full reasoning

Overall, the phone is usable for basic tasks and long media sessions, but it shows its limits in camera quality, audio convenience, and software stability.
