Is the ZTE Neo 2 worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6000 mAh battery
  • 120 Hz IPS LCD display
  • 8 GB RAM with 256 GB storage
  • 5G connectivity and 33 W fast charging

What is not

  • Camera quality is budget‑tier
  • Aggressive thermal throttling after updates
  • Android 14 updates are region‑locked
  • No 3.5 mm headphone jack

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, large storage and can accept modest camera and gaming performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you rely on high‑quality photos, intensive gaming or need a headphone jack.

Why we say that

The ZTE Neo 2 gives you a huge 6000 mAh battery that can easily last two days of moderate use. The 120 Hz IPS LCD screen feels smooth for scrolling and video, and the 8 GB RAM with 256 GB storage handles everyday apps without lag. 5G works and the 33 W charger refills the big battery in a reasonable time.

Android 13 runs fine but the promised Android 14 update is often locked to certain regions and may not arrive for everyone. The 50 MP main sensor produces images that many owners call poor, especially in low light. After recent software updates the chipset gets aggressive thermal throttling, causing noticeable lag during gaming.

The phone also lacks a 3.5 mm headphone jack, which some users find inconvenient. Overall it suits users who value endurance and storage more than camera or high‑intensity gaming performance.

How long does the battery last on daily use?

The 6000 mAh battery can easily last two full days with moderate use such as browsing, social media and occasional video playback. Heavy gaming or constant streaming will reduce that time but it still outlasts most mid‑range phones.

Will the phone handle gaming without overheating?

The Unisoc T820 provides strong raw power, but owners report aggressive thermal throttling after recent updates. This means the device may heat up and drop frame rates during long gaming sessions, affecting smoothness.

Is the camera good for low‑light photos?

The 50 MP main sensor is advertised as high resolution, yet many users describe the image quality as poor, especially in low‑light conditions. Expect budget‑tier results rather than crisp night shots.

Does the ZTE Neo 2 get official Android updates and have a warranty in Bangladesh?

The phone ships with Android 13 and is eligible for the brand’s official warranty in Bangladesh. However, Android 14 updates are often region‑locked, and some owners have not received them at all.
