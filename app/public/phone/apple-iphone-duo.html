Why we say that

The 7.6 inch LTPO Super Retina XDR OLED screen runs at 120Hz and can peak at 3,000 nits, making everything look bright and smooth. Twelve gigabytes of RAM keep apps and games fluid, while the 5,400 mAh battery lasts a full day for most users and refills quickly with 60W charging. The 48MP main camera with f/1.6 aperture and optical image stabilization captures clear photos in good light and decent detail in dim settings, and the 12MP selfie camera is adequate.

5G connectivity is fast and iOS 27 promises five to six major updates with no bloatware. Owners often complain about the lack of manufacturer warranty, having to pay for repairs, and the need to confirm IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

    iPhone Duo: What We Missed! (opens YouTube)

    8.8M views · Sep 2026

    Marques Brownlee highlighted that the Duo essentially bridges the gap between an iPhone and an iPad Mini, bringing heavily customized software continuity to a folding layout. He concluded that while it is a fun, thoughtfully engineered device that finally lets Apple users experience a folding screen, its extreme price and camera compromises mean it is strictly a niche product for affluent enthusiasts rather than an essential upgrade for everyday consumers.

  2. DDave2D

    iPhone Duo Impressions - They NAILED This (opens YouTube)

    1.7M views · Sep 2026

    Dave2D felt that Apple proved why taking a patient approach to new categories works, delivering a foldable that feels far more refined than years of competing Android devices. He praised the sturdy hinge mechanics, the innovative opening animations, consistent stereo speakers, and seamless app handoffs, concluding that despite high pricing, a heavier frame, and some contrast loss from the matte inner display, Apple successfully nailed the overall user experience.

  3. TThe Tech Chap

    iPhone Duo Hands-On - What You NEED to Know! (opens YouTube)

    1.3M views · Sep 2026

    The Tech Chap admired the Duo as a gorgeous, futuristic hybrid of an iPhone and an iPad Mini, singling out the impressive crease suppression, IP68 protection, and flagship processor. Nevertheless, he questioned whether the heavy 250-gram chassis, matte screen contrast compromises, lack of a telephoto lens, and two-thousand-dollar asking price make it a sensible buy when you could purchase both an iPhone 18 Pro and an iPad Air for the same money.

Will the iPhone Duo receive future iOS updates and for how long?

The phone ships with iOS 27 and Apple typically provides five to six major iOS versions for a device. You can expect software support for several years, but exact end‑of‑support date is not announced.

Is there any official warranty for this phone in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop may offer its own limited warranty, but any repair after that will be paid by you. Verify the shop’s terms.

How long does the battery last and how fast does it charge?

The 5400 mAh battery can last a full day with typical use and supports 60W fast charging. You can refill from zero to about 80 percent in under an hour.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 48MP main sensor has a large 1/1.56 inch size and f/1.6 aperture with optical image stabilization. It captures decent detail in dim environments, though low‑light results are not as bright as the 3000‑nit display suggests.
