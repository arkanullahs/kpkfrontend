Is the OnePlus Nord 4 worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz Fluid AMOLED display
  • 100 W fast charging
  • Snapdragon 7+ Gen 3 performance
  • 5G connectivity and IR blaster

What is not

  • Heavy bloatware and ads
  • Inconsistent refresh rate
  • Thermal throttling in heavy games
  • Mediocre ultrawide and selfie cameras

Buy it if Buy it if you want a high‑end spec phone at a gray‑import price and can tolerate some software bloat.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a clean OS, stable 4K video stabilization, or an official warranty.

Why we say that

Daily use feels smooth and 5G works well, while the main 50 MP camera is reliable for most shots. Owners love the metal unibody and the IR blaster, but they complain about the heavy bloatware and ads that now resemble ColorOS, an inconsistent 120 Hz refresh that sometimes drops, and thermal throttling when gaming for long periods. Video recording in 4K lacks electronic image stabilization, which is a noticeable drawback for vloggers.
