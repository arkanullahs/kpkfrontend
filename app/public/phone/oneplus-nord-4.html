Is the OnePlus Nord 4 worth buying?

What is good

  • Elite metal unibody build quality
  • Excellent gaming and sustained performance
  • Bright 2150 nits peak display
  • Fast 100W charging support

What is not

  • Critical battery instability below 50 percent
  • Poor ultrawide and selfie camera quality
  • No EIS support for 4K video
  • Inconsistent 120Hz display refresh rate

Buy it if You prioritize a premium metal design and high-end gaming performance above all else.

Skip it if You need a reliable battery that does not shut down unexpectedly or a phone with capable cameras.

Why we say that

Owners report that the device can experience unexpected shutdowns or excessive drain once the battery level drops below 50 percent. While the Snapdragon 7+ Gen 3 chipset handles gaming and heavy tasks with excellent smoothness, the camera system is underwhelming for the price. You will find the main sensor acceptable, but the ultrawide and selfie cameras perform poorly.

Video recording is also limited by the lack of EIS in 4K. The display is bright and vivid, yet the 120Hz refresh rate switching is inconsistent. Given that the newer OnePlus Nord 6 is available for 55,999 taka, most buyers will find that model to be the better investment.

If you prioritize build quality and raw speed over camera reliability and battery consistency, this phone might suit you, but the current technical issues are difficult to ignore.
