Is the HMD 130 worth buying?

What is good

  • Excellent 2500 mAh battery life
  • Simple and easy to use interface
  • Includes a standard headphone jack
  • No bloatware or advertisements

What is not

  • Very poor display quality
  • Extremely weak performance for tasks
  • Build quality feels mediocre
  • No manufacturer warranty support

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a reliable secondary device for calls and basic tasks that lasts a long time on a single charge.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you want to play games, use modern apps, or require the security of an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Released in 2025, this device runs on a Unisoc 6531F chipset and features a 2.4 inch TN TFT display. Owners report that the battery is the standout feature, while the display quality and overall performance are significant pain points. You will find the interface easy to use, but do not expect to play games on this device.

Because this is an unofficial gray import, you must be aware that there is no manufacturer warranty available in Bangladesh. Any repairs will be paid out of your own pocket, and you should rely only on the specific promise provided by the shop where you purchase the unit. Always verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your money.

This phone is strictly for those who need a simple communication tool and nothing more.
