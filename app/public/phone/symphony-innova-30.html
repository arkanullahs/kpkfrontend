Is the Symphony Innova 30 worth buying?

What is good

  • 5000 mAh battery lasting up to one and a half days
  • 90 Hz screen feels smooth for daily use
  • Unisoc T616 handles basic apps well
  • Large 6.56 inch display

What is not

  • System UI is buggy and outdated
  • Heavy games lag and multitasking stalls
  • Video stabilization is weak
  • Outdoor visibility of the screen is mediocre

Buy it if Buy it if you need a big screen and long battery on a tight budget.

Skip it if Skip it if you plan to play demanding games or need reliable software.

Why we say that

The 6.56 inch IPS LCD runs at 90 Hz and feels smooth for scrolling and video. The Unisoc T616 chipset handles everyday apps without trouble, yet multitasking and heavy gaming often stutter. The build feels solid, though the design looks like an iPhone copy and the outdoor visibility of the screen is only average.

Camera pictures are bright and sharp for the price, but overexposure happens frequently and video stabilization is poor. Software lacks a custom UI, receives rare updates and shows medium ads, leading to occasional glitches. Overall the phone offers good hardware value at 12,999 taka, but software and performance limits may frustrate power users.

How long will the battery last on normal daily use?

Owners report the 5000 mAh battery gives about one to one and a half days of use for an average user. Light web browsing and messaging can stretch it a little further.

Can I play modern games smoothly on this phone?

The Unisoc T616 chipset manages basic games fine, but heavy titles and multitasking often cause stutters. Expect smooth performance only with less demanding games.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 8 MP selfie camera produces bright images in good light, but in low‑light situations it struggles with overexposure and lacks detail. Video stabilization is also reported as weak.

Will the phone receive Android updates in the future?

The device runs a light Android skin with rarely any updates. Owners have noted that the system UI stays outdated and bugs appear over time.
