Buying verdict

Realme Narzo 90X Is it worth buying?

The Realme Narzo 90X is a specialized device that prioritizes extreme battery endurance and rugged durability over display quality or performance.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize extreme battery life and rugged build quality for outdoor use above all else.
Consider alternatives if
You care about display sharpness, gaming performance, or having an official brand warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Massive 7000 mAh battery provides 2-day endurance
  • Highly durable with rare IP69 water and dust resistance
  • Fast 60W charging included in the box
  • Bright 1200 nits peak brightness

Reported trade-offs4

  • Poor 720p resolution lacks sharpness
  • Significant bloatware and ads in software
  • Slow eMMC 5.1 storage limits performance
  • Missing 3.5mm headphone jack

Why we say that

Living with this phone means carrying a massive 7000 mAh battery that easily lasts two days, but you must accept a low 720p resolution on a large 6.8 inch screen that lacks sharpness. Owners are vocal about their disappointment, specifically calling the 720p display an absolute scam. While the IP69 rating makes it highly durable, the Dimensity 6300 chipset is held back by slow eMMC 5.1 storage, leading to average performance and gaming results.

You will also encounter significant bloatware and ads within the Android 15 software. The camera system is limited to acceptable daylight photos, with weak selfies and average night shots. Because no official units exist in Bangladesh, you are limited to gray-import stock with only shop-provided warranty support.

Realme Narzo 90X: what buyers ask us

What is the Realme Narzo 90X price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Realme Narzo 90X in Bangladesh was ৳18,990, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does the Realme Narzo 90X come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there is no official or brand-backed warranty support for this device in Bangladesh. All units found in shops we track are gray-imports, meaning you only receive a shop warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

How does the battery perform for heavy users?

The phone features a massive 7000 mAh battery that provides exceptional 2-day endurance. Owners rate the battery performance very highly at 9.6 out of 10, making it one of the most reliable aspects of the device for long-term use.

Is the Realme Narzo 90X suitable for gaming?

The Dimensity 6300 chipset is only okay for basic tasks. Gaming performance is rated at 4.5 out of 10 by owners because the slow eMMC 5.1 storage significantly limits the speed and responsiveness of the device during intensive use.

How many years of software updates will this phone receive?

The device runs Android 15 with Realme UI 6.0 out of the box. It is expected to receive between 2 to 3 OS updates during its lifecycle, though you should expect a medium to high amount of bloatware and ads in the software.
