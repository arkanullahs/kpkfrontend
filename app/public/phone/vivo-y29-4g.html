Is the Vivo Y29 4G worth buying?

What is good

  • 6500 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • 120 Hz smooth display despite HD+ resolution
  • 50 MP sensor captures bright daylight photos
  • IP64 dust‑water resistance and sturdy build

What is not

  • 720p screen feels low for the price
  • eMMC 5.1 storage slows app loading
  • Low‑light camera performance is poor
  • No 3.5 mm headphone jack

Buy it if Buy if you prioritize long battery life, a bright screen and basic performance for everyday tasks.

Skip it if Skip if you need high‑resolution display, fast storage, strong gaming or low‑light photography.

Why we say that

Its 6500 mAh battery easily reaches a full day and a half of normal use, and the 44 W charger refills it in about eighty minutes. The 6.68‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, giving smooth scrolling, though the 720p resolution feels soft compared with newer screens. The Snapdragon 685 with 8 GB RAM handles everyday apps fine, but the eMMC 5.1 storage and modest GPU make heavy games and large apps feel sluggish.

The 50 MP main camera produces bright daylight photos, yet low‑light shots are noisy and the built‑in beauty filters can look over‑processed. Build quality is solid with an IP64 rating and an IR blaster, but the lack of a headphone jack and the presence of bloatware may bother some users. Overall, it offers long battery life and a bright display at a mid‑range price, but compromises on performance and screen sharpness.
