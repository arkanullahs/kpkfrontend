Buying verdict

Realme P4 Is it worth buying?

The Realme P4 is a specialized device that prioritizes massive battery life above all other features.

Choose it if
Your absolute priority is extreme battery life and you do not mind using a gray import device.
Consider alternatives if
You care about camera quality, stereo sound, or having an official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • Massive 7000 mAh battery lasts three days
  • Bright 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Stays cool due to massive vapor chamber
  • Functions as a powerbank

Reported trade-offs

  • Weak mono speaker quality
  • Slippery plastic build
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Camera lacks OIS and has weak ultrawide

Why we say that

Owners report that the 7000 mAh battery is industry leading and can last for three days on a single charge. It even functions as a powerbank for other devices. Living with this phone requires accepting significant compromises in build quality and audio.

The plastic body is slippery and feels like it wants to escape your hands. The mono speaker is so poor that owners find it laughable. While the 144Hz AMOLED display is bright at 4500 nits, the camera performance is mediocre with a weak ultrawide lens and no OIS.

Read full reasoning

Gaming performance is limited as titles like Genshin Impact and WuWa struggle significantly. You must also accept that this is a gray import unit with no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Repairs are paid and you rely entirely on the shop for support.

If you need a phone that stays powered for days and stays cool during use, this is a unique choice, but you will sacrifice camera quality and audio fidelity to get it.

Does the Realme P4 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import. You must rely on the specific shop for any support, and all repairs are paid.

How is the gaming performance on the Realme P4?

The phone handles daily tasks smoothly and plays BGMI okay. However, demanding games like Genshin Impact and WuWa struggle significantly. It is not recommended for users who prioritize high end mobile gaming.

How long will the Realme P4 receive software updates?

The device is expected to receive 2 to 3 OS updates. You should be aware that the software experience includes a medium to high amount of bloatware and ads.

Is the battery life actually good for heavy users?

Owners rate the battery 9.9 out of 10. It is described as industry leading and can last for three days. It is highly suitable for users who need a device that acts as a powerbank.
