Is the Realme P4 worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 7000 mAh battery provides multi-day endurance
  • Bright 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Fast 80W charging fills battery in one hour
  • Capable Dimensity 7400 chipset for smooth gaming

What is not

  • Disappointing mono speaker quality
  • Camera lacks OIS and struggles with exposure
  • Downgraded IP water resistance rating
  • Smudge-prone build with no microSD slot

Buy it if You prioritize extreme battery life above all other features and can tolerate a mono speaker and average camera performance.

Skip it if You care about high-quality audio, water resistance, or consistent camera performance for your daily photography needs.

Why we say that

With a massive 7000 mAh battery, it is an absolute champion for users who prioritize multi-day endurance over everything else. However, living with this device reveals frustrating flaws that owners frequently highlight. The mono speaker is so underwhelming that it has become a source of ridicule among users.

You will also notice a downgraded IP rating and a lack of a microSD slot, which feels like a step backward. While the 144Hz AMOLED display is bright, you may encounter HDR color shifts and brightness flickering when switching between apps. The camera setup lacks OIS and struggles with exposure, making it a mediocre choice for photography.

Performance is generally smooth for gaming, but you should be prepared for occasional frame drops and bugs in the game bar. This phone is a raw, budget-focused device that excels in longevity but cuts corners in ways that impact daily usability.

Does the Realme P4 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, the Realme P4 is currently only available through gray-import stock in Bangladesh. This means you will receive a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty, so you must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

How long will the Realme P4 receive software updates?

The device comes with Android 15 and Realme UI 6.0 out of the box. You can expect to receive 2 to 3 OS updates over the lifespan of the phone, though you should be aware that there are mixed reports regarding the amount of pre-installed bloatware.

Is the Realme P4 good for heavy gaming?

The Dimensity 7400 chipset provides smooth performance for games like BGMI. However, some users have reported issues with frame drops and bugs within the game bar, so it may not be perfectly stable for every gaming session.

How does the camera perform in different lighting conditions?

The 50MP main camera is considered decent but it lacks OIS. Owners report that the camera often struggles with exposure, which may affect the quality of your photos in various lighting environments.
