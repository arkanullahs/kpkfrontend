Is the Google Pixel 7 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 50 MP camera with excellent zoom
  • 6.7 inch LTPO AMOLED 120 Hz display
  • Clean stock Android with long term updates
  • Premium IP68 build

What is not

  • 23 W charging feels slow
  • Battery lasts only average time
  • Gaming causes heat and throttling
  • Modem drains on weak signal

Buy it if Buy it if you want top tier photography and a bright fluid screen and can accept slower charging and limited gaming performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need fast charging heavy gaming or a full official warranty in Bangladesh.

Why we say that

The 50 MP main sensor with optical image stabilization and 5x telephoto delivers industry‑leading stills and reliable low‑light performance. The 6.7 inch LTPO AMOLED panel reaches 1500 nits peak brightness and runs at a fluid 120 Hz, making everyday browsing and media consumption a pleasure. The phone runs clean stock Android, currently on Android 13, and can be upgraded to Android 16 with up to five major Android upgrades promised.

However, the 5000 mAh battery provides only average endurance and the 23 W charger feels slow compared with newer fast‑charging standards. Gaming sessions quickly raise the temperature and trigger thermal throttling, especially in hot weather. Modem performance can drop on weak signals, leading to noticeable drain.

The cheapest listings are US gray imports without a Google warranty, so any repair will be a paid service.
