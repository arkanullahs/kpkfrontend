Google Pixel 7 Pro Is it worth buying?

Expect a brilliant camera and a gorgeous 6.7 inch 120 Hz display, but plan for modest battery endurance and heating when you game.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value camera quality and a smooth display above heavy gaming or long battery runs.
Consider alternatives if
You need fast charging, intensive gaming performance, or guaranteed warranty coverage.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 50 MP main camera with 5x optical zoom
  • 6.7 inch LTPO AMOLED 120 Hz display
  • Premium IP68 build with clean Android
  • 12 GB RAM and up to 512 GB storage

Reported trade-offs4

  • Battery lasts only average time
  • Charging at 23 W feels slow
  • Gaming causes heat and throttling
  • Software updates may end soon

Why we say that

The Pixel 7 Pro uses Google Tensor G2, giving smooth daily performance and AI features. The 50 MP main sensor with 5x optical zoom and an ultrawide lens delivers industry leading stills even in low light. The LTPO AMOLED panel reaches 1500 nits peak brightness and stays vivid at 120 Hz.

Build quality feels premium with IP68 rating, though the glass can scratch easily. Battery is 5000 mAh but many owners report average endurance and a slow 23 W charge. Gaming pushes the device hot and triggers thermal throttling, especially in summer.

Read full reasoning

Software stays clean and will receive a few more Android upgrades, but at four years old the update window is closing. Regional limits may affect 5G and VoLTE on some networks.

How is the battery life on the Pixel 7 Pro?

The 5000 mAh battery gives average endurance; many owners notice it needs a full day of moderate use and a period of optimization. Slow 23 W charging means it takes longer to top up compared with newer fast‑charge phones.

Will the phone handle gaming without overheating?

The phone runs daily apps fine but sustained gaming raises temperature and triggers thermal throttling, especially in hot weather. Expect reduced frame rates and possible lag during long sessions.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor with f/1.9 aperture and OIS delivers industry leading stills, and the 5x telephoto works well even in dim light. Reviewers praise low light consistency, though video may show some noise.
