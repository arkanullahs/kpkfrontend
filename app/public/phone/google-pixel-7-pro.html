Is the Google Pixel 7 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.7 inch LTPO AMOLED 120Hz display
  • 50MP main camera with optical image stabilization
  • Google Tensor G2 delivers smooth daily performance
  • Pure stock Android with long update promise

What is not

  • Battery endurance below average
  • Very slow 23W charging
  • Overheating during summer gaming sessions
  • Regional locks on 5G and AI features

Buy it if Buy it if you want a premium screen and strong main camera and can accept average battery life and limited gaming heat.

Skip it if Skip it if you need long gaming sessions, fast charging, or unrestricted 5G and AI features.

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch LTPO AMOLED panel reaches 1500 nits and shows smooth motion at 120Hz. The 50MP main sensor with OIS delivers sharp photos in good light, though the ultrawide and selfie lenses struggle in low light. Daily tasks feel fluid thanks to the Google Tensor G2, but the phone runs hot during intensive gaming, especially in summer, and will throttle performance.

Battery life is below average and the 23W charger refills the 5000 mAh cell slowly. Software stays clean and receives updates, yet regional locks hide some 5G, VoLTE and AI features, and at four years old the update window is closing fast.

What reviewers said

The Google Pixel 7 Pro is an exceptionally smart phone that uses brilliant AI software to deliver top-tier still photography and everyday conveniences. While it trails competitors in raw gaming power and fast charging, its highly polished user experience makes it a phenomenal flagship device.

Best for Photography enthusiasts and fans of clean, smart software

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 5000 mAh battery gets a rating of 5.8 out of 10 and owners report below‑average endurance. Combined with a 23W charger, charging is slower than many newer phones, so you will need to top up more often.

Can I play games on this phone without it overheating?

The device handles casual games well, but reviews note severe overheating and thermal throttling during intensive gaming, especially in hot weather. Expect reduced performance if you game for long periods in summer.

Is the camera good in low‑light situations?
