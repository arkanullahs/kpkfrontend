Why we say that

The 6.75‑inch IPS LCD runs at 90 Hz, making scrolling and video feel smooth. The 5000 mAh battery usually lasts a full day of normal use, though heavy gaming or video will drain it faster. The Unisoc T8300 chipset with 4 GB RAM handles everyday apps well but struggles with demanding games and multitasking.

MyOS adds medium‑level ads and bloat, which some users find annoying after a while. The main camera captures sharp images in daylight thanks to the f/1.8 lens, yet low‑light shots can be noisy. There is no manufacturer warranty; the shop’s own cover may offer limited repair service, but any fix costs you.
