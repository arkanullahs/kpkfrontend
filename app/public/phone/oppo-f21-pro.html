What works well4

  • AMOLED display
  • 5G connectivity
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • 8GB RAM and 128GB storage

Reported trade-offs3

  • Four years old
  • Limited remaining software updates
  • Medium amount of bloatware and ads

Why we say that

You get a decent AMOLED screen and 5G connectivity via the Snapdragon 695 5G chipset. However, the phone is 4 years old. This means you should expect very short or no remaining software update life.

While it can run ColorOS 14, the long term support is likely over. The 600 nits peak brightness is modest for outdoor use. You also have to deal with medium levels of bloatware and ads in the software.
