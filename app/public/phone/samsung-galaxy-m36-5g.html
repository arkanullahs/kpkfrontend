Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy M36 5G Is it worth buying?

If you want a bright 120 Hz AMOLED screen and a solid 50 MP camera without spending a flagship price, the Galaxy M36 5G can fit the bill.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value a vivid screen and decent camera and can accept limited gaming performance and a shop‑only warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need heavy gaming power, official brand warranty, or a larger battery for long days.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 120 Hz Super AMOLED display
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • Clean One UI with long update promise
  • 25 W fast charging support

Reported trade-offs4

  • 5000 mAh battery feels average
  • Exynos 1380 heats during gaming
  • No stereo speakers or water‑resistance
  • No official Samsung warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

The 6.7‑inch panel reaches 1000 nits and shows colors well, but the water‑drop notch feels dated. Battery life is average; the 5000 mAh cell usually lasts a day of moderate use but is a step down from the M35. The Exynos 1380 handles everyday tasks smoothly, yet it heats up during longer gaming sessions and the gaming rating is low.

Audio is limited to a single speaker and there is no IP rating, so you won’t get stereo sound or water resistance. The phone arrives without a charger and carries only a shop‑provided warranty, not an official Samsung guarantee. Software is clean One UI on Android 15 with a six‑year update promise, which is a strong point for long‑term use.

Will the battery last a full day of typical use?

The 5000 mAh battery is considered average and usually gives you about a day of moderate use. Owner ratings give it a 6.5 out of 10, and it is a downgrade from the M35’s 6000 mAh cell.

How does the phone perform in games?

The Exynos 1380 handles casual games but struggles with sustained load, heating up the screen. Gaming ratings are low at 5.0 out of 10, so it is not ideal for demanding titles.
