Is the Samsung Galaxy M36 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 6.7‑inch Super AMOLED 120 Hz display
  • 50 MP main camera with optical image stabilization
  • Clean One UI with up to six years of updates
  • 5G connectivity for fast data

What is not

  • Exynos 1380 heats up during gaming
  • No stereo speakers and no water‑resistance
  • Only shop warranty, no official Samsung support
  • 5000 mAh battery feels average

Buy it if Buy if you want a bright AMOLED screen, decent camera and long software support and can accept a shop‑only warranty.

Skip it if Skip if you need strong gaming performance, stereo sound, or an official Samsung warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.7‑inch Super AMOLED panel delivers 120 Hz smoothness and 1000‑nit brightness, though the water‑drop notch feels dated. The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures reliable daylight and portrait shots, while the 13 MP selfie camera is adequate. Under Android 15 One UI 7 the software feels clean and Samsung promises up to six years of updates.

The Exynos 1380 chipset manages everyday tasks well but warms noticeably during sustained gaming, reflected in a low gaming rating. The 5000 mAh battery is average and, unlike the M35, does not stretch comfortably beyond a day of moderate use. The phone arrives without a charger and carries only a shop‑provided warranty, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

At the cheapest gray‑import price of ৳26,499 it feels like a step down from the M35 in both capacity and value.
