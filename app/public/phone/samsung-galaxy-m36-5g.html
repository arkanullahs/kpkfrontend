Is the Samsung Galaxy M36 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz Super AMOLED display
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • Clean One UI with six‑year updates
  • Thin profile with 5G support

What is not

  • 5000 mAh battery feels average
  • No official Samsung warranty in Bangladesh
  • Device heats up during long gaming
  • Water‑drop notch looks dated

Buy it if Buy it if you value a vivid screen and decent camera and can accept average battery life and a shop‑only warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need long gaming sessions, strong battery endurance, or an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.7‑inch Super AMOLED panel with 120 Hz refresh and 1000‑nit peak brightness looks great in daylight and under indoor lighting. The main sensor captures detailed daylight photos and portrait mode works reliably, thanks to OIS and an ultrawide companion. Software feels clean on Android 15 with One UI 7 and Samsung promises six years of updates, so the phone stays current longer than many rivals.

However, the 5000 mAh battery is a downgrade from the M35 and owners rate it only 6.5, so you may need to charge midday on heavy use. The Exynos 1380 chip shows heat during long gaming sessions, and the water‑drop notch and lack of stereo speakers feel dated. Remember the device arrives without a charger and only a shop‑provided warranty.
