Review sources ↓

What works well

  • 5000 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • 50 MP main camera gives decent natural colours
  • 25 W fast charging (adapter not included)
  • Large 6.7 inch display

Reported trade-offs

  • 60 Hz display is dim and low refresh
  • No fingerprint sensor, only face unlock
  • No official warranty, only shop cover
  • Camera focus can be slow

Why we say that

The 5000 mAh cell paired with 25 W charging keeps the phone usable through a full workday and a night of media. The 6.7 inch PLS LCD is large but only 60 Hz, so the picture looks dim compared with newer rivals and fast‑scrolling feels sluggish. The Helio G85 with 4 GB RAM handles basic apps and runs BGMI on low settings, but heavy games quickly reveal the modest performance rating of four out of ten.

The 50 MP main sensor produces natural colours in good light, yet focusing can be slow and low‑light shots are noisy. Software runs Android 14 with One UI Core 6.0 and promises up to two major Android upgrades, though updates may stretch over several years. The phone arrives as a gray import, so there is no manufacturer warranty; only the shop’s limited cover applies and any repair costs are out‑of‑pocket.

Read full reasoning

Overall it fits buyers who need a big screen and long battery at the cheapest price, but it falls short for those who demand a smooth display, official warranty, or strong camera performance.
