Is the Samsung Galaxy F05 worth buying?

What is good

  • Large 6.7 inch screen
  • 5000 mAh battery
  • 50 MP main camera
  • Headphone jack included

What is not

  • Outdated Helio G85 chipset
  • Basic 60Hz LCD lacks brightness
  • No fingerprint scanner
  • Shop warranty only

Buy it if Buy it if you want a big display and long battery life for everyday tasks and can accept basic performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need fast gaming, strong low light photography, or an official Samsung warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch PLS LCD gives you plenty of room for videos and browsing, but the 60Hz refresh and low brightness make the picture look basic. The 5000 mAh cell provides a full day of normal use and the 50 MP main sensor can capture detailed photos in good light, though owners rate the camera low for low‑light scenes. The Helio G85 chipset handles messaging, social media and light games, but it feels sluggish with newer titles and the gaming rating reflects that.

Build quality is solid plastic and thick, yet it feels cheap and there is no fingerprint scanner. One UI Core 6.0 runs Android 14 and promises up to two major updates with little bloat. The phone comes with a shop warranty, so you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
