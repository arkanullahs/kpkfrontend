Realme 13 Pro Plus Is it worth buying?

If you want a bright 120 Hz AMOLED screen and a strong camera without paying a premium, the Realme 13 Pro Plus delivers that at the cheapest gray import price.

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch panel reaches a peak 2000 nits, making outdoor use easy, and the 50 MP main sensor with OIS and 3× telephoto captures good low light shots. Performance feels solid thanks to the Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 and up to 12 GB RAM, and the 5200 mAh battery usually lasts a full day of mixed use. However, the phone arrives without an official Realme warranty, and the shop’s warranty is limited and repairs cost extra.

Users report the under display fingerprint sensor can be slow, NFC struggles with eToll cards, and a recent software update reduced camera quality. The UI includes medium high ads and bloat, and software updates are expected for only two to three major versions. Overall it feels like a high spec midrange with some reliability trade offs.

How long does the Realme 13 Pro Plus battery last on a typical day?

Owners rate battery 7.0, saying it provides good day long usage but is not as efficient as newer models. The 5200 mAh cell usually lasts around 10‑12 hours of mixed use, but heavy 5G or gaming will drain it faster.

Is the Realme 13 Pro Plus good for gaming?

The Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 paired with 8‑12 GB RAM gives an 8.0 performance rating, so most games run smoothly at medium settings. The 120 Hz display helps, but battery drain is noticeable during long sessions.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP primary sensor with f/1.9 aperture and OIS performs well in low light, and users note the 3× telephoto also holds up. Inconsistencies appear after a recent software update, so results can vary.
