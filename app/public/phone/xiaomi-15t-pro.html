Buying verdict

Xiaomi 15T Pro Is it worth buying?

Fast charging and a blindingly bright 144Hz AMOLED screen make daily use feel smooth, but expect the phone to heat up and slow down during heavy gaming and to struggle with selfie and ultrawide shots.

Choose it if
You value a bright high‑refresh screen, fast charging and can accept gray‑import warranty and occasional throttling.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable gaming performance without heat limits or an official brand warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • 3200‑nit 144Hz AMOLED display
  • 90W fast charging in about thirty‑six minutes
  • 5500 mAh battery with all‑day endurance
  • Leica‑tuned 50MP main camera with 5x zoom

Reported trade-offs

  • Thermal throttling reduces performance
  • Selfie and ultrawide cameras weak
  • Heavy bloatware and cluttered settings
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

The 5500 mAh battery easily lasts a full day and refills from zero to full in about thirty‑six minutes, which owners praise. The premium fiberglass and aluminum body feels solid and the IP68 rating protects it from water, though the device feels a bit bulky in the hand. Camera performance shines with Leica tuning and a 5x telephoto lens, yet the 32MP selfie sensor and ultrawide lens fall short in low light.

Software offers many features on Android 15 with HyperOS, but it is cluttered with bloatware and settings can be unintuitive. There is no official warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited guarantee and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.

How long does the battery really last and how fast does it charge?

The 5500 mAh battery can easily last a full day of typical use and the 90W charger fills the phone from zero to full in roughly thirty‑six minutes, which owners describe as very fast.

Will the phone handle intensive gaming without problems?

The Dimensity 9400+ chipset delivers top‑tier peak power, but owners report significant heat and throttling that can cut CPU performance to about sixty percent during long gaming sessions.

Is the camera good for low‑light selfies and wide‑angle shots?

The 50MP main sensor with Leica tuning and 5x telephoto works well, but the 32MP selfie camera and the ultrawide lens are considered weak, especially in low‑light conditions.

Will I get official warranty support and software updates?

All units are gray imports, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop provides its own limited warranty and repairs are paid. Software receives three to four Android version updates and four to five security patches, but the system includes a lot of bloatware.
