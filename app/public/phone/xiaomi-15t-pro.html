Is the Xiaomi 15T Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.83 inch 144Hz AMOLED display
  • 50MP Leica tuned main camera with 5x telephoto
  • 12GB RAM with Dimensity 9400+ performance
  • 90W fast charging

What is not

  • Severe overheating during gaming
  • Battery life drops after HyperOS updates
  • Only shop warranty, no brand warranty

Buy it if Buy if you value a bright high refresh screen and strong photography and can accept possible heating and limited warranty.

Skip it if Skip if you need reliable gaming performance or an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.83 inch AMOLED panel delivers 144Hz smoothness and peaks at 3200 nits, making outdoor use easy. The 50MP main sensor with Leica tuning and a 5x telephoto lens captures detailed photos, though some users notice softness at full resolution. Under the hood, the Dimensity 9400+ paired with 12GB RAM handles daily apps well, yet intensive gaming triggers severe overheating and thermal throttling, lowering the gaming rating.

Battery life is mixed; the 5500 mAh cell rates 7.2 out of 10, and several owners report faster drain after HyperOS 3.0.304 updates. The phone arrives as a gray import, so only a shop warranty is offered and you must verify IMEI and BTRC status. Software runs on HyperOS 3, praised for functionality and low ads, and will receive three to four Android upgrades and four to five security patches.

Will the Xiaomi 15T Pro battery last a full day of use?

The phone has a 5500 mAh battery rated 7.2 out of 10, but many owners report reduced endurance after updating to HyperOS 3.0.304. Expect a full day with moderate use, but heavy tasks may drain it faster.

Is the Xiaomi 15T Pro good for gaming?

Daily performance feels fast, yet gaming scores only 6.5 out of 10 because the device overheats and throttles under stress. You can play casual games, but demanding titles may suffer from heat and lower frame rates.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The 50MP sensor with f/1.6 aperture and OIS captures decent low light shots, and Leica tuning adds good colour. Some users notice softness at full resolution, so results are solid but not flawless.

Does the phone come with an official Xiaomi warranty?

Only gray import stock is available, which provides a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty. Buyers should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchase.
