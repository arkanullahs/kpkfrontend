Is the Realme C73 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery with strong endurance
  • 120 Hz refresh rate for smoother scrolling
  • 5G connectivity for faster data
  • headphone jack for wired audio

What is not

  • 720p display feels low resolution
  • 15W charging is noticeably slow
  • Medium‑high ads in Realme UI
  • Shop warranty only, not brand warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you want long battery life and a basic camera at a low price.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a high‑resolution screen or fast charging.

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh battery gives solid endurance, and most users say it easily gets through a full day of browsing, social media and video. The 6.67‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, which makes scrolling feel smoother, but the panel is only 720p, so sharpness suffers and the 625‑nit peak brightness is modest for bright outdoor use. Performance sits in the middle; the Dimensity 6300 with 4 GB RAM handles everyday apps fine, yet gaming scores low and the 15 W charger needs over three hours to fill the large battery.

The 32 MP camera is basic and struggles in low light, while the Realme UI shows medium‑high ads and promises two to three OS upgrades. A key caveat is that only gray‑import stock is available with a shop warranty, so confirm IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long does the battery last on typical use?

The 6000 mAh battery can easily last a full day of mixed usage such as browsing, social media and video playback, and many owners report good endurance even with the 120 Hz screen active.

Is the Realme C73 good for gaming?

Gaming performance is modest; the Dimensity 6300 and 4 GB RAM handle casual games well, but demanding titles may lag and the low 720p display does not showcase graphics sharply.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 32 MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture is basic; it captures decent daylight shots but struggles in low‑light conditions, producing noisy images that lack detail.
