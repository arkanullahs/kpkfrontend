Is the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 8 worth buying?

What is good

  • Exceptional performance and gaming capabilities
  • Stunning 120Hz foldable display
  • Long-term software support for seven years
  • High quality 50MP main camera

What is not

  • Disappointing battery life for daily use
  • Only available as unofficial gray-import stock
  • Slow 25W charging speed
  • No official brand warranty provided

Buy it if You want a high-performance foldable phone and are comfortable managing a device without an official brand warranty.

Skip it if You need a phone that lasts all day on a single charge or require the security of an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Owners rate the performance at 9.8 and gaming at 9.0, making it a top choice for users who prioritize speed and a high-quality display. However, the battery life is a significant pain point, receiving a low rating of 6.0 from users. You are getting a 6.9 inch foldable screen that is bright and responsive, yet you must manage your charging habits carefully throughout the day.

Because this device is only available through unofficial channels, you will not receive a brand warranty. You must rely on shop support and verify the IMEI and BTRC status before handing over your money. It is a powerful piece of technology that demands compromises on longevity and official support.
