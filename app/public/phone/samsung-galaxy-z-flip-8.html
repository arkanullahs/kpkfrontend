Is the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 8 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.9‑inch bright foldable display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance
  • 12GB RAM for smooth daily use
  • 50MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • 25W charging is slow
  • Severe thermal throttling during gaming
  • No official warranty in Bangladesh
  • No telephoto lens

Buy it if Buy it if you want a high‑end foldable with excellent display and solid everyday performance and can tolerate slower charging and the lack of a brand warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable gaming power, fast charging, or official manufacturer warranty support.

Why we say that

Daily tasks run smoothly thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and 12GB RAM, and the 50MP main sensor captures decent photos, though it lacks a telephoto lens and relies on AI. Battery life is better than the previous model, yet the 4300 mAh cell combined with a 25W charger means you’ll spend more time plugged in. The biggest complaint is the top half of the phone becoming uncomfortably hot during gaming or 4K video, causing the processor to throttle down to about half its speed.

Add to that the fact that every unit is a gray‑import with only a shop‑provided warranty, and the phone becomes a tough sell for those who need reliable performance under load.

It runs games well at first, but the compact chassis gets very hot during long sessions, causing the processor to throttle to about 40‑50 percent of its speed, which hurts performance.

The 50MP sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures decent low‑light shots, but the lack of a telephoto lens and heavy reliance on AI processing can limit detail and consistency.
