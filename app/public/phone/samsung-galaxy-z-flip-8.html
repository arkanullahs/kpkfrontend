Is the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 8 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.9 inch bright foldable display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance
  • Improved battery endurance
  • Polished One UI 9 software

What is not

  • 25W charging is slow
  • Severe heating during gaming
  • No official Samsung warranty in Bangladesh
  • Thermal throttling cuts performance

Buy it if If you want a stylish foldable with strong daily performance and can accept slower charging and no manufacturer warranty.

Skip it if If you need reliable gaming power, fast charging, or official brand support.

Why we say that

The 6.9 inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED display shines with 2600 nits peak brightness and smooth 120 Hz motion. The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and 12 GB RAM handle apps and web browsing effortlessly, and the 4300 mAh battery lasts a full day of mixed use. However, the phone only charges at 25 W, which feels slow compared to other flagships.

Gaming and 4K video make the top half of the device hot, and the processor throttles down to about 40 to 50 percent, hurting sustained performance. The main 50 MP camera is solid but lacks a telephoto lens and depends on AI. There is no Samsung manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s limited cover and must verify IMEI and BTRC status.

How long will the Galaxy Z Flip 8 receive Android updates?

Is there any official Samsung warranty for this phone in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray import, so the phone arrives without Samsung’s manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop may offer its own limited warranty, but any repair will be paid by you and you must verify IMEI and BTRC status.

Will the battery last through a day of typical use?

The 4300 mAh battery together with the efficient Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 gives better endurance than the previous model. Users report a full day of mixed use, though heavy 4K video or gaming will reduce the time.

How does the phone handle gaming and does it get hot?

During long gaming sessions or 4K video recording the top half of the phone becomes very hot and the processor throttles to about 40 to 50 percent of its speed. This makes sustained gaming performance noticeably lower.
