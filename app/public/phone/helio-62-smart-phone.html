Is the Helio 62 Smart Phone worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 7000 mAh battery lasts two days
  • Clean software interface without bloat
  • Premium design with notification light
  • Smooth 120Hz display refresh rate

What is not

  • Heavy and bulky 226 gram weight
  • Poor low-light camera performance
  • Struggles with gaming and animation delays
  • Low display sharpness with thick bezels

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize extreme battery life and a premium look over gaming performance and camera quality.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a mobile gamer, a photography enthusiast, or someone who prefers a lightweight device.

Why we say that

With a massive 7000 mAh battery, it easily lasts two days for moderate users, making it a reliable companion for those who hate carrying a charger. However, living with this phone requires accepting significant compromises. Owners frequently complain about the bulky 226 gram weight and the low display sharpness caused by the HD+ resolution.

While the 120Hz refresh rate is a nice touch, the Helio G81 chipset struggles with demanding games like PUBG or COD and shows animation delays in the software. The camera system is strictly for daylight use, as it suffers from poor low-light performance and weak dynamic range. You get a clean UI without bloat, but the hybrid SIM slot forces you to choose between extra storage and a second SIM card.

It is a niche device for users who value battery life and design above all else.
