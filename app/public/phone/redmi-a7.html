What works well4

  • Large 6.88 inch 120 Hz display
  • 5200 mAh battery lasts a day
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • 15 W fast charging

Reported trade-offs3

  • Only 3 GB RAM struggles with heavy apps
  • Camera quality worse than predecessor
  • Heavy ad bloat in UI

Why we say that

The 6.88 inch IPS LCD refreshes at 120 Hz and can reach 600 nits, yet many owners find it dim and odd looking. The 5200 mAh cell gives a full day of typical use, though heavy gaming drains it faster. The Unisoc T7250 paired with 3 GB RAM handles basic apps, but multitasking or demanding games feel sluggish and freeze.

The 13 MP main sensor is slower than the A5 and struggles in low light, while the 8 MP selfie camera is only average. Android 15 runs on HyperOS with many ads and the promised two OS upgrades often do not arrive, leaving the device on Android 15 longer than expected. Official units cost ৳16,499 and include a brand warranty, while cheaper gray import phones lack any manufacturer support.

How long does the battery last on the Redmi A7?

The phone packs a 5200 mAh battery that usually lasts a full day of normal browsing, messaging and video playback. Heavy gaming or bright screen use will shorten that time noticeably.

Can I play games smoothly on this device?

The Redmi A7 uses a Unisoc T7250 chipset with 3 GB RAM. Basic games run okay, but more demanding titles show lag and low frame rates, and the gaming rating is only 3.5 out of 10.

Is the camera good for low light photos?

The main camera is a 13 MP sensor with f/2.2 aperture. Owners say it is worse than the A5 and performs poorly in low light, giving dull and noisy pictures.
