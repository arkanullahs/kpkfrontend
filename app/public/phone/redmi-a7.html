Is the Redmi A7 worth buying?

If you need a cheap phone with a large screen and long battery at a budget price, the Redmi A7 will get the job done, but expect slow performance and many ads. The 6.88 inch IPS LCD runs at a smooth 120 Hz and the 5200 mAh cell can keep the device alive through a normal day of calls, messaging and media. However, the Unisoc T7250 processor paired with only 3 GB of RAM struggles with multitasking and most games feel laggy. Owners also complain that the 13 MP camera is a step down from the previous model, delivering dull photos especially in low light. The software layer, HyperOS, adds a noticeable amount of advertising and bloatware, which can be intrusive. You will receive Android 15 and up to two major OS upgrades, and the cheapest official‑warranty option starts at 13,499 taka.
