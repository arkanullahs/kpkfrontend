What works well

  • Large 6.88 inch 120Hz display
  • 5200 mAh battery lasts over two days
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • Android 15 with two major upgrades

Reported trade-offs

  • Slow 15W charging for large battery
  • Basic plastic build feels wide and uncomfortable
  • 13MP camera gives mediocre pictures
  • Performance struggles with apps and games

Why we say that

The 6.88 inch IPS LCD screen offers a smooth 120Hz experience but its width makes holding the phone uncomfortable for small hands. The Unisoc T7250 chipset with 3GB RAM struggles with everyday apps and gaming, leading to noticeable lag and occasional crashes. The 13MP rear camera produces only average pictures and does not improve in low light.

Charging at 15W is painfully slow for the 5200 mAh cell, often taking many hours to reach full. Android 15 is installed and the device promises up to two major OS upgrades, yet the software layer adds a lot of ads and bloat. Official units priced at ৳13,499 include a brand warranty, while cheaper gray imports at ৳14,299 lack any manufacturer cover and repairs are paid.

Overall the phone feels like a budget device stretched thin.
