What works well4

  • Long lasting 5260 mAh battery
  • 50 MP main camera in daylight
  • Headphone jack and FM radio
  • Large 6.74 inch 90 Hz display

Reported trade-offs4

  • Very slow 15 W charging
  • Dim TFT LCD with poor angles
  • Weak performance in heavy games
  • Low‑light camera struggles

Why we say that

The 5260 mAh cell scores 8.8 out of 10 from owners and easily gets you through a full workday. The 6.74‑inch TFT LCD runs at 90 Hz but appears dim and loses colour when you tilt it, which many users find disappointing. Charging is painfully slow at 15 W and takes about two hours to fill, so you cannot top up quickly.

The Helio G81 chipset with 4 GB RAM handles basic apps but struggles with multitasking and heavy games such as Genshin Impact. The 50 MP main camera produces decent daylight shots but falters in low light and focus, earning a low rating. Build quality is a sturdy plastic shell without any water‑resistance rating.

Software feels clean but is plagued by bugs and occasional ads. Overall it offers strong value at the lowest price but comes with noticeable compromises.
