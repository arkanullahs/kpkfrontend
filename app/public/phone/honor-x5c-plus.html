Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 50MP main camera works in daylight
  • 5260 mAh battery gives full‑day endurance
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • 90 Hz display on a large screen

Reported trade-offs4

  • 15W charging takes more than two hours
  • Performance stalls with multitasking and heavy games
  • Low‑light photos are noisy and lack stabilization
  • UI bugs make navigation frustrating

Why we say that

The 6.74‑inch TFT LCD offers a 90 Hz refresh rate but suffers from low brightness and thick bezels. The 50 MP main camera captures decent daylight images yet fails in low‑light and offers no stabilization, while the 5 MP selfie shooter is weak. Performance is limited by the Helio G81 and 4 GB RAM, leading to stutter in multitasking and heavy games.

Software runs on Android 15 with MagicOS 9, providing a clean UI that is marred by bugs, especially in notification bar navigation and predictive text. Charging is very slow; 15 W takes more than two hours to fill the 5,260 mAh battery. The phone includes a headphone jack and FM radio, and it is built from sturdy plastic without an IP rating.

Overall it feels like a budget device with solid battery and basic features but many compromises.
