Honor X5c Plus Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can sit on a charger overnight and still run all day, the Honor X5c Plus delivers that endurance but compromises on everything else.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a big battery and basic media features at a low price.
Consider alternatives if
You expect smooth gaming, bright display or reliable low‑light photos.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Large 5,260 mAh battery lasts over a day
  • 50 MP main camera works in daylight
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • 90 Hz refresh rate on a big screen

Reported trade-offs4

  • Dim TFT LCD with narrow viewing angles
  • Very slow 15 W charging
  • Heavy games lag and heat up
  • Low‑light photos are blurry

Why we say that

The 5,260 mAh battery scores 8.8 out of 10 and owners report it easily lasts over a day of mixed use. The 6.74‑inch TFT LCD runs at 90 Hz but appears dim, has thick asymmetrical bezels and loses colour when tilted, making it hard to watch videos outdoors. The Helio G81 chipset with 4 GB RAM struggles with multitasking and heavy games such as Genshin Impact, often heating up and dropping frames.

Charging is limited to 15 W, taking about two hours for a full top‑up, which feels sluggish compared with modern fast‑charge phones. The 50 MP main camera produces decent daylight shots but falters in low‑light and focus, while the 5 MP selfie sensor is modest. Software runs MagicOS 9 on Android 15, offering a clean UI but occasional bugs and medium‑level ads.
