Motorola ThinkPhone Is it worth buying?

The Motorola ThinkPhone is a powerful device that offers high-end performance for ৳39,999, but buyers must accept the risks of gray-market hardware.

What works well4

  • Fast Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 performance
  • Smooth 144Hz P-OLED display
  • Reliable 5000 mAh battery life
  • Clean near-stock software experience

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Camera quality is not flagship level
  • Limited remaining software update life
  • Gray import status requires careful verification

Why we say that

You get a fast Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 chipset and a smooth 144Hz P-OLED display that makes daily tasks and gaming feel very responsive. Owners rate the performance and battery life highly, noting that the 5000 mAh cell easily lasts through a full day of use. However, the camera system is only decent rather than flagship quality, which may disappoint those looking for top-tier photography.

Because this is a 2023 model, you should expect very limited remaining software support. Most importantly, there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. You are relying entirely on the shop for any repairs or support if the device fails.

Read full reasoning

Before paying, you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller to ensure the device is not blocked. This phone is a specialized choice for users who prioritize raw speed and a clean software experience over camera excellence and official brand support.

Does the Motorola ThinkPhone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you must rely on the specific shop for any coverage. All repairs will be paid out of your own pocket.

How long will the Motorola ThinkPhone receive software updates?

The phone was released in 2023 and supports updates up to Android 15. Because the device is already three years old, you should expect very short or no remaining software update life at this point.

Is the Motorola ThinkPhone good for gaming?

Yes, owners rate the gaming performance at 8.5 out of 10. The Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 chipset provides enough power to handle demanding tasks and games efficiently, making it a strong choice for mobile gamers.

How is the camera performance on this device?

The 50MP main camera includes OIS and is considered decent by users, but it does not reach flagship standards. Owners rate both the camera and video performance at 7.0 out of 10, suggesting it is adequate but not exceptional.
