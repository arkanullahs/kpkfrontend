Is the iQOO Z9 Turbo Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • Powerful Dimensity 9300+ chipset
  • Large 6400 mAh battery
  • Bright 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Strong gaming performance

What is not

  • Plastic build quality
  • Lack of official brand warranty
  • Mixed camera and video performance

Buy it if You want a high-performance gaming phone and are comfortable managing a gray-import device with a shop warranty.

Skip it if You prioritize premium build materials or require the peace of mind that comes with an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Owners rate the performance highly at 8.5, making it a capable machine for heavy tasks. However, the experience is hampered by a plastic build that users have rated at a low 5.5. You must be careful about the software version, as most units in Bangladesh are gray-import China-market versions running OriginOS.

This means you may face the same caveats as other Vivo China-market phones regarding bloatware and regional settings. While the 6400 mAh battery is a standout feature, the camera system receives mixed feedback, with owners rating video quality at only 6.5. Since these are gray-import units, you will receive a shop warranty rather than a brand warranty.

Always confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your money.

Does this phone come with an official brand warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is currently only available as gray-import stock. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, so you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

How is the battery life on the iQOO Z9 Turbo Plus?

The phone features a large 6400 mAh battery which owners have rated at 8.0. It is one of the primary strengths of the device for users who need long-lasting power throughout the day.

What software version should I look for when buying?

Most units in Bangladesh are China-market versions running OriginOS, which comes with specific caveats. If you find an India-market unit, it will run Funtouch OS, which is generally considered fine and includes three OS updates.

Is this phone suitable for heavy gaming?

Yes, the phone is well-suited for gaming. Owners have rated the gaming performance at 8.0 and the overall chipset performance at 8.5, thanks to the inclusion of the Dimensity 9300+ processor.
