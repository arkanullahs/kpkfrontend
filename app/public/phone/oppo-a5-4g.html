Buying verdict

OPPO A5 4G Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can survive drops and stay alive for two days, the OPPO A5 4G delivers that without breaking the bank.

Choose it if
You value durability and long battery life above high‑end performance.
Consider alternatives if
You need smooth gaming, crisp display or fast performance.

What works well

  • 6000 mAh battery lasting over a day
  • 45 W fast charging
  • IP65 dust and water resistance
  • Strong build with military‑grade shock protection

Reported trade-offs

  • HD+ screen resolution feels low
  • Laggy multitasking and slow app loads
  • Poor low‑light camera performance
  • Device can get noticeably hot

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh battery easily reaches a day and a half of use and the 45 W charger refills it quickly. Its IP65 rating and military‑grade shock protection give it a premium feel that many owners appreciate. However the 6.67" screen is only HD+ and looks dull beside modern Full HD panels, and the Snapdragon 6s chipset struggles with multitasking and gaming, often feeling laggy.

Low‑light photos are noisy and lack stabilization, while the device can become warm during extended use. ColorOS 15 runs smoothly but includes noticeable bloatware. Overall it is a solid choice for durability and endurance, but it falls short on performance and visual sharpness.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 6000 mAh battery can easily last about one and a half days for most users, even with moderate use, and the 45 W charger refills it in roughly an hour.

Will this phone run games smoothly?

It can handle light games on low settings, but performance ratings are low, multitasking feels laggy, and heavier games will stutter or drop frames, making it unsuitable for demanding gaming.

How does the camera work in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor is decent in daylight, but low‑light shots are noisy, lack stabilization, and heavy skin‑brightening makes details appear washed out, resulting in poor night photography.

Does the OPPO A5 4G come with an official warranty and how many Android updates will it receive?

Yes, it is sold with the brand’s official Bangladesh warranty and uses the same service network. ColorOS 15 is expected to receive about three to four major Android upgrades like other OPPO flagships.
