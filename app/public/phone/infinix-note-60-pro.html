Is the Infinix Note 60 Pro worth buying?

The Infinix Note 60 Pro offers a high-refresh display and a large battery, but real-world usage reveals significant software and hardware frustrations. While the 144Hz AMOLED screen is bright and the Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset handles tasks without throttling, the user experience is marred by heavy bloatware and intrusive notification ads. Owners report specific issues with sound quality, noting that music playback lacks clarity and sounds mixed up. The build quality is also a point of contention, as the camera island is made of plastic despite the aluminum body. Battery performance is inconsistent, with some users experiencing rapid drops immediately after a full charge. While the main camera performs well, the ultrawide lens is limited and video quality receives low marks from owners. You should be prepared for a software experience that feels slow and requires managing preloaded applications.

What is good

  • Bright 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Efficient 6000 mAh battery
  • Solid Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 performance

What is not

  • Heavy bloatware and notification ads
  • Poor sound quality during music playback
  • Plastic camera island construction
  • Inconsistent battery drain after full charge

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a high-refresh display and a large battery for general tasks and do not mind managing preloaded software.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you value high-quality audio, clean software, or consistent camera performance for video recording.

How long will the Infinix Note 60 Pro receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 16 and is promised up to 3 major Android upgrades. However, users should be aware that the XOS software updates are generally slow and come with a high amount of preloaded apps and notification ads.

Is the Infinix Note 60 Pro good for gaming?

The phone provides adequate performance for gaming without throttling, according to owner feedback. It holds a performance rating of 7.5 and a gaming rating of 6.5, suggesting it is capable but not necessarily a top-tier device for intensive mobile gaming.

Does the Infinix Note 60 Pro have a reliable battery?

While the 6000 mAh battery is described as efficient and long-lasting, some owners report issues where the battery drains quickly to 97 percent within an hour of being fully charged. There are plenty of charging options available for the device.

How does the camera perform on this phone?

The 50MP main camera is considered good, but the ultrawide lens is limited in its capabilities. Owners have given the camera a rating of 6.5 and the video recording a lower rating of 5.5, indicating it may not be the best choice for serious videography.
