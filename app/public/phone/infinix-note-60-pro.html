Buying verdict

Infinix Note 60 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Infinix Note 60 Pro offers a massive battery and a bright display, but owners frequently struggle with poor audio quality and heavy software bloat.

Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life and a bright display for outdoor use and can tolerate pre-installed software.
Consider alternatives if
You are an audiophile or a mobile gamer who requires ultra-high settings and clean software without ads.

What works well

  • Exceptional real-world battery endurance
  • Extremely bright 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Premium aluminum frame and slim design
  • Stable thermal management during use

Reported trade-offs

  • Poor audio quality lacking clarity
  • Heavy bloatware and notification ads
  • Inconsistent camera processing
  • No HDR support for streaming apps

Why we say that

Living with this device means enjoying exceptional real-world endurance from the 6000 mAh battery and a very bright 144Hz AMOLED screen that performs well outdoors. However, the user experience is hampered by a high volume of pre-installed apps and notification ads within the XOS 16 software. While the main camera captures decent photos, the ultrawide sensor is weak and image processing remains inconsistent.

Owners specifically complain that the sound quality lacks bass and clarity, often describing it as muddy. Video stabilization is only mediocre, and the lack of HDR support for streaming apps limits the media experience. While the phone handles daily tasks smoothly, it struggles with ultra-high gaming settings.

Read full reasoning

You are paying for a premium aluminum build and a unique rear matrix display, but the software experience and audio limitations are significant trade-offs for the price.

How does the battery perform for a heavy user?

Owners report that the 6000 mAh battery provides exceptional real-world endurance. It holds a high owner rating of 9.5 out of 10, making it one of the most reliable aspects of the device for daily use.

Is this phone suitable for high-end mobile gaming?

The phone provides stable gaming performance for most titles, but it struggles when pushed to ultra-high settings. The 3D Ice Core VC cooling system helps keep thermals manageable, rarely exceeding 40C during sessions.

How long will the Infinix Note 60 Pro receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 16 and is promised up to 3 major Android upgrades. However, users should be aware that the XOS 16 software is known to be slow with updates and contains a high amount of pre-installed bloatware.
