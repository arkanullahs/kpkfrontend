Infinix Note 60 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can last through a full day of heavy use, the Infinix Note 60 Pro delivers strong battery endurance and a very bright AMOLED screen.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large bright screen, solid battery and 5G at a sub‑50 k taka price.
Consider alternatives if
You need a phone with fast storage, clean software or strong water resistance.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 6.78 inch AMOLED with 4500‑nit peak brightness
  • 6000 mAh battery with strong endurance
  • Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 handles everyday tasks
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs4

  • UFS 2.2 storage slows app loading
  • XOS adds many ads and pre‑installed apps
  • IP64 protection is weak for the price
  • Audio sounds inconsistent during music playback

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset provides decent everyday performance, and the 50 MP main camera with OIS captures usable photos, though processing can feel over‑done and there is no telephoto lens. The 144 Hz display looks smooth but most apps do not reach that rate, so the extra refresh is often unnoticed. Owners love the metal frame and the inclusion of FM radio and an IR blaster, but they complain about the slow UFS 2.2 storage, the ad‑filled XOS software, and the weak IP64 protection.

Audio quality can be uneven when playing music. Overall the phone feels premium for its price, but the software bloat and storage bottleneck hold it back from being a truly high‑end choice.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The phone has a 6000 mAh battery that received a 9.5 rating for endurance. Reviewers note industry‑leading stamina, though charging is limited to 30 W, so a full charge takes a while.

Can the Note 60 Pro run mobile games smoothly?

The Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset gives stable daily performance and a gaming rating of 6.5. However, the UFS 2.2 storage is a major bottleneck that can cause slower load times in games.

Is the camera good in low‑light situations?

The 50 MP sensor with f/1.6 aperture and OIS captures usable images, but owners rate the camera at 6.0 and say processing is inconsistent and often over‑processed. No telephoto lens is provided.

What warranty and software updates are included?

The phone comes with the brand’s official warranty and service network in Bangladesh. It runs Android 16 with XOS 16 and promises up to three major Android upgrades, though updates are slow and XOS includes many ads.
