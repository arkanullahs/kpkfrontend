Infinix Note 60 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Infinix Note 60 Pro offers a massive battery and a bright display, but it struggles to justify its price tag due to significant performance and software limitations.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life above all else and do not mind managing software bloatware.
Consider alternatives if
You expect fast storage performance, clean software, or high-end water resistance for your money.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Industry leading battery endurance
  • Very bright 1.5K AMOLED display
  • Official warranty support in Bangladesh
  • Premium metal frame feel

Reported trade-offs4

  • Slow UFS 2.2 storage bottleneck
  • Heavy software bloat and ads
  • Poor IP64 ingress protection
  • Inconsistent camera processing

Why we say that

Owners report that the UFS 2.2 storage acts as a major bottleneck, making it difficult to accept at this price point. While the 6.78 inch AMOLED screen is impressive, the 144Hz refresh rate is underutilized by most applications. The software experience is a primary pain point, with users complaining about heavy bloatware and intrusive notification ads that feel worse than budget alternatives.

Build quality is also a concern, as the IP64 rating is considered insufficient for a phone costing up to ৳49,999. While the main camera is usable, inconsistent processing and the lack of a telephoto lens limit its versatility. Users also note audio quality issues during music playback.

Does the Infinix Note 60 Pro come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

Yes, the phone is available as an official BD unit. This means you are covered by the brand's own warranty and their established service network in the country.

How long will the Infinix Note 60 Pro receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 16 and is promised up to 3 major Android upgrades. However, users should be aware that the XOS software is known for being slow with updates and contains significant bloatware.

Is the Infinix Note 60 Pro good for gaming?

While the Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset provides stability for daily use, gaming performance is rated at 6.5 out of 10. The slow UFS 2.2 storage acts as a major bottleneck that hinders the overall experience.

How does the camera perform on this phone?

The 50MP main sensor is usable for general photography, but it lacks a telephoto lens. Owners report that the image processing is inconsistent and often over-processed, resulting in a camera rating of 6.0 out of 10.
