Is the Infinix Note 60 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.78 inch 144Hz AMOLED display
  • 6000 mAh battery with 30W fast charge
  • Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset
  • 50MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Audio lacks bass and clarity
  • Video stabilization is mediocre
  • No HDR video support for streaming
  • Heavy preinstalled bloat and ads

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large bright screen, long battery life and decent performance at a mid range price.

Skip it if Skip it if you need top tier camera quality, crystal clear audio or a clean software experience.

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch AMOLED panel reaches 4500 nits peak brightness and runs at 144Hz, making it easy to see in bright Bangladeshi streets. The 6000 mAh silicon‑carbon battery keeps the device alive through heavy use and the 30W wired charger refills it quickly. Daily tasks feel smooth thanks to the Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 and 8GB RAM, and gaming runs fine at medium settings.

The 50MP main sensor with OIS captures decent photos, though the ultrawide lens and video processing are inconsistent. Owners love the long battery life and bright screen but complain about weak audio, mediocre video stabilization and lack of HDR streaming support. The software offers many AI tools but is heavy with preinstalled apps and ads, and updates arrive slowly.
