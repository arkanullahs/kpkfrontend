What works well4

  • 6.78 inch 90Hz IPS LCD screen
  • 64MP main camera for a budget phone
  • 5000 mAh battery capacity
  • Headphone jack and IR blaster included

Reported trade-offs4

  • System lag and frequent hanging
  • Device gets hot during gaming
  • Performance drops in demanding apps
  • Software updates add bloat and ads

Why we say that

The phone packs a Helio G91 chipset, 8 GB RAM and up to 256 GB storage, which feels adequate for everyday apps but slows down under heavy multitasking. Owners praise the 64 MP main sensor and the 5000 mAh battery, yet the battery rating of 6.5 shows it does not last a full day of intensive use. The device heats up during gaming and long sessions, and many report hanging and lag after software updates, which include HiOS ads.

Build quality earned an IP64 rating, and the inclusion of a headphone jack and IR blaster are rare at this price. Overall the phone works for casual media consumption but struggles with demanding games and smooth performance.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The Spark 30 carries a 5000 mAh battery that is rated around 6.5 out of 10 by owners. In normal use such as browsing and messaging it can last most of the day, but heavy video or gaming will drain it noticeably faster.

Will this phone handle mobile games smoothly?

The device uses a Helio G91 chipset with 8 GB RAM, but owners report hanging, slowing down and overheating during gaming. Expect frame drops and occasional lag, especially in graphics‑intensive titles.

Is the 64MP camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 64MP sensor is praised as decent or excellent for a budget phone, but overall camera ratings are low. In low‑light it can capture usable shots, yet noise and detail loss are common.

What warranty and service support does the official unit have?

The Spark 30 sold through official channels comes with the brand’s own warranty and is covered by Tecno’s service network in Bangladesh. This means you can claim repairs at authorized centers under the standard warranty period.
