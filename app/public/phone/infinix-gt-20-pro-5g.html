Infinix GT 20 Pro 5G: what buyers ask us

What is the Infinix GT 20 Pro 5G price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Infinix GT 20 Pro 5G in Bangladesh was ৳32,390, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there are no confirmed official units in stock. The listings we found are gray imports, which means you only get a shop warranty. You should confirm the BTRC status and IMEI with the seller before buying.

How is the battery life for daily use?

Owners report that the battery drains quickly. Some users specifically mentioned that watching Netflix or YouTube drains the battery fast. It has a 5000 mAh battery, but the real world rating from owners is 5.0 out of 10.

Is this a good phone for gaming?

Despite the chipset, owners report gaming performance issues. Users have complained about the phone overheating and experiencing frame drops. The owner rating for gaming is 5.5 out of 10.

How many software updates will I get?

The phone runs on Android 14 with XOS 14. You can expect 1 to 2 OS updates. Be aware that the software is reported to be slow and contains many preloaded apps and notification ads.
