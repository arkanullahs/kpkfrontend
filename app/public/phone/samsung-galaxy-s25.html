Samsung Galaxy S25 Is it worth buying?

The Galaxy S25 delivers a flagship‑class display and speed, but its small battery and slow charging make daily use feel limited.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value a top‑tier screen and fast performance more than long battery life or official Samsung support.
Consider alternatives if
You need all‑day battery endurance, rapid charging, or a manufacturer warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Stunning 2600‑nit LTPO AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite provides flagship speed
  • Lightweight premium build
  • One UI 8 with smooth AI features

Reported trade-offs4

  • 4000 mAh battery drains quickly
  • 25 W charging is slow
  • Thermal throttling reduces performance
  • Sharp edges may need a case

Why we say that

The 6.2 inch LTPO AMOLED screen shines at 2600 nits and the Snapdragon 8 Elite keeps apps and games responsive. Owners praise the lightweight, premium feel and the smooth One UI 8 experience with low ads. However, the 4000 mAh cell runs out quickly, especially with video or browsing, and the 25 W charger cannot refill it fast.

During long gaming sessions or extended camera use the phone gets noticeably hot and throttles performance to about half of its peak. The camera is solid in daylight but does not bring a big generational jump, and low‑light shots are only average. Because it is a gray‑import, there is no official Samsung warranty in Bangladesh; repairs are covered only by the shop’s limited guarantee.

Will the Galaxy S25 last a full day on a single charge?

The phone uses a 4000 mAh battery that owners rate 5.5 out of 10. It can manage moderate use for a day, but heavy browsing, video streaming or gaming will drain it noticeably faster than rivals with larger cells.

Is the S25 good for gaming?

Performance is strong thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Elite, but the device heats up during long sessions. Owners report thermal throttling that cuts performance to about half of its peak, so sustained gaming feels limited.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS handles daylight well, but reviewers note the hardware lacks a major upgrade. Low‑light photos are decent yet not a big step up from the previous model, earning a 7.5 rating.

Does the Galaxy S25 come with an official Samsung warranty in Bangladesh?

No. All listings are gray‑import units built for Vietnam, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop provides its own limited warranty and any repairs are paid by the buyer after confirming IMEI and BTRC status.
