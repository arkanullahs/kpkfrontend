Is the Samsung Galaxy S25 worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite performance
  • 12 GB RAM smooth multitasking
  • Premium lightweight build

What is not

  • 4000 mAh battery drains quickly
  • 25 W charging is slow
  • Thermal throttling cuts performance
  • No official manufacturer warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you value a vivid screen and fast performance and can tolerate limited battery life and a shop‑only warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need long battery endurance, fast charging, or official Samsung warranty support.

Why we say that

The 6.2‑inch LTPO AMOLED screen reaches 2600 nits and runs at 120 Hz, making everyday use look vivid and smooth. Snapdragon 8 Elite with 12 GB RAM handles apps and multitasking effortlessly, and the premium lightweight build feels solid, though the sharp edges may need a case. The 50 MP camera is consistent, yet it does not bring a big generational jump, so low‑light shots are only average.

Battery life is a weak point; the 4000 mAh cell drains quickly and 25 W charging feels outdated for a 2025 flagship. During long gaming or camera sessions the phone heats up and thermal throttling cuts performance roughly in half. The device arrives as a gray import, so there is no official Samsung warranty, only the shop’s limited cover.

Updates are promised for up to seven Android versions, and One UI 8 runs smoothly with low bloat.
