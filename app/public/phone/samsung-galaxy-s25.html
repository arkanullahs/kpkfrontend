Samsung Galaxy S25 Is it worth buying?

The Samsung Galaxy S25 is a compact, premium device that delivers top-tier performance but suffers from significant thermal management issues and dated hardware.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a compact, high-performance device with long-term software support and do not mind using a case to handle the sharp frame.
Consider alternatives if
You are a heavy gamer, require long battery life, or need the security of an official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Blazing fast Snapdragon 8 Elite performance
  • Excellent, bright 6.2 inch LTPO AMOLED display
  • Seven years of major Android updates
  • Premium, compact, and lightweight build

Reported trade-offs4

  • Insufficient 4000 mAh battery capacity
  • Significant thermal throttling during heavy tasks
  • Slow 25W charging speed
  • Stagnant camera hardware compared to competitors

Why we say that

Living with this phone requires careful battery management, as the 4000 mAh capacity is insufficient for heavy users. While the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset is blazing fast, owners report that it throttles significantly during gaming and heavy tasks, sometimes dropping to half of its peak performance. The camera hardware feels stagnant compared to modern competitors, and the sharp frame edges often necessitate the use of a back cover for comfort.

Because these units are gray imports from Vietnam, you will not receive any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. You must rely entirely on the shop's own promise for repairs, and you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status before purchasing. If you prioritize a compact, bright display and long-term software support, this phone is a strong contender, provided you can manage the heat and charging limitations.

Does the Samsung Galaxy S25 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you must rely on the specific shop's promise for repairs. You should always confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

Is the Samsung Galaxy S25 good for heavy gaming?

While the Snapdragon 8 Elite is very fast, the phone is not ideal for heavy gaming. Owners report significant thermal throttling during prolonged sessions, with performance dropping to around half of its peak capacity. The device also generates noticeable heat during these tasks.

How long will the Samsung Galaxy S25 receive software updates?

This device is supported by 7 years of major Android upgrades. It runs on Android 15 out of the box and will continue to receive updates for a long period, ensuring the software remains current and feature-rich.

What is the battery life like on the Samsung Galaxy S25?

The 4000 mAh battery capacity is considered insufficient by many owners. Heavy users will find that the battery requires careful management throughout the day. Additionally, the charging speed is capped at 25W, which is relatively slow by modern standards.
