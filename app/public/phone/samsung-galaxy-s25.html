Is the Samsung Galaxy S25 worth buying?

What is good

  • Stunning 2600‑nit LTPO AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite delivers top performance
  • 12 GB RAM keeps multitasking fluid
  • Premium lightweight build feels solid

What is not

  • 4000 mAh battery drains quickly
  • 25 W charging is noticeably slow
  • Thermal throttling cuts performance in half
  • No manufacturer warranty, only shop cover

Buy it if Buy it if you value a top‑class display and flagship speed and can accept limited battery life and a gray‑import warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need long battery endurance, fast charging, or an official Samsung warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.2‑inch screen delivers smooth 120 Hz motion and vivid colors, making media consumption a joy. Snapdragon 8 Elite paired with 12 GB RAM provides flagship‑level speed for apps and multitasking. However, the 4000 mAh cell struggles to last a full day under heavy use and the 25 W charger refills it slowly.

Owners report noticeable heat during gaming or long camera sessions, and the device throttles performance to about half of its peak. The camera is solid with a 50 MP sensor and OIS, yet it does not bring a major generational leap. Build quality feels premium and lightweight, but sharp edges may need a case.

Without an official Samsung warranty you rely on the shop’s limited cover, so confirm IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
