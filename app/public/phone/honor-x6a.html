Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Exceptional battery life lasting two days
  • Premium shimmery design
  • Good daylight main photos
  • Includes a headphone jack

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe lag in basic apps
  • Dim display with poor viewing angles
  • Weak low light and selfie cameras
  • Difficult file transfers to Windows 11

Why we say that

Owners report exceptional endurance that often lasts two full days. However, daily use is frustrating because the phone lags badly in non gaming apps like Telegram, LinkedIn, and Capcut. The display is a dim 720p TFT panel with poor contrast and viewing angles.

While the shimmery design looks premium, the Helio G36 chipset struggles with basic tasks. You will face stutters in simple apps and poor performance compared to Redmi alternatives. Some users report significant trouble transferring files to Windows 11 laptops.

Read full reasoning

There are also complaints about inconsistent NFC availability. Since the phone was released in 2023, you should expect very little remaining software update life.
