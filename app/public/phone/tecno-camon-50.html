Is the Tecno Camon 50 worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6150 mAh battery endurance
  • Sharp 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Exceptional IP68/69K build quality
  • Solid daylight camera performance

What is not

  • Heavy bloatware and ad-filled software
  • Noticeable camera shutter lag
  • Struggles with heavy gaming performance
  • No microSD card slot for storage

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize long battery life and a high-quality display for daily media consumption.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a mobile gamer or if you prefer a clean, ad-free software experience.

Why we say that

Owners appreciate the 6150 mAh battery for its industry-leading endurance, and the 144Hz AMOLED screen is a genuine standout for media consumption. However, living with the phone reveals frustrations. The Helio G200 chipset struggles with heavy gaming and frame drops, making it a poor choice for performance-focused users.

The camera system produces solid daylight photos, but users frequently report shutter lag and weak low-light performance. Perhaps the most persistent complaint involves the HiOS 16 software, which is heavily burdened by bloatware and aggressive ad folders. While the IP68/69K build quality is exceptional for the price, the lack of a microSD card slot limits storage expansion.

If you prioritize battery life and screen quality over gaming performance and a clean software experience, this phone might suit your needs.
