What works well

  • 6150mAh battery lasts a full day
  • 6.78 inch 144Hz AMOLED display
  • 50MP main camera with OIS in daylight
  • 45W charging fills battery in about 90 minutes

Reported trade-offs

  • Heavy games feel laggy
  • Software filled with ad hubs
  • No microSD card slot
  • Camera shutter lag noticeable

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch AMOLED panel runs at 144Hz, delivering sharp and bright images that stay comfortable for long sessions. Charging at 45W fills the large battery in about an hour and a half, which many owners find convenient. The 50MP main sensor with optical image stabilization captures good daylight photos and portraits, though the camera app shows noticeable shutter lag.

Performance relies on the Helio G200 Ultimate, a budget chipset that struggles with demanding games and can feel laggy. The phone runs Android 16 with HiOS 16, but the software layer adds many advertising hubs and fake folders that many users find intrusive. Build quality is plastic yet the device carries IP68/69K and MIL STD ratings, giving solid protection.

Read full reasoning

Updates are limited to one or two major Android versions and arrive slowly. Overall the device feels balanced for media consumption but shows clear compromises in gaming and software cleanliness.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 6150mAh cell typically lasts through a full day of normal activities such as browsing, social media and video playback, according to owner reports. Heavy use may reduce the time but most users see a full day without charging.

Will this phone handle demanding games?

The device uses the Helio G200 Ultimate, which is a budget chipset. Owners note lag and stutter in heavy games, so it is not ideal for demanding gaming sessions but can manage light titles.

How is the camera performance in low light?

The 50MP main sensor with a large 1/1.56 inch size and f/1.8 aperture captures decent daylight photos, but in low light the shutter lag and slower processing reduce sharpness, making results less impressive.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

An official Bangladeshi unit comes with the brand’s warranty and service network. The phone ships with Android 16 and HiOS 16 and is promised up to three major Android upgrades, though updates have been slow in the past.
