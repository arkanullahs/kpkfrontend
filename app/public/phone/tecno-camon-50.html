Is the Tecno Camon 50 worth buying?

The Tecno Camon 50 is a display-focused device that struggles significantly with gaming and software stability. Owners rate the 144Hz AMOLED screen highly, but the overall experience is hampered by a performance score of 5.0 and a very low gaming rating of 2.5. While the 6150 mAh battery provides decent longevity, the software experience is marred by high levels of bloatware and ads. Users report that the camera performance is average, with a 7.0 rating for photos but a disappointing 4.5 for video. The lack of an SD card slot is a major frustration for those needing extra storage. You will find the interface slow, and the update policy is limited to 1-2 OS upgrades despite the phone shipping with Android 16. If you prioritize a smooth, bloat-free interface or heavy gaming, this device will likely frustrate you daily.
