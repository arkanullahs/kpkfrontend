Is the Motorola Razr Ultra 2025 worth buying?

What is good

  • 7 inch 165Hz foldable display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite performance
  • 50MP dual camera system
  • 68W fast charging

What is not

  • Shop warranty only no brand warranty
  • Historically slow software updates
  • Battery may feel modest for heavy use

Buy it if Buy it if you value a large high refresh foldable and flagship speed and can accept a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need guaranteed brand warranty or rapid OS updates.

Why we say that

The 7 inch LTPO AMOLED screen feels smooth at 165Hz and bright enough for sunlight with a peak of 4500 nits. Snapdragon 8 Elite keeps apps and games fast, and the 16GB RAM makes multitasking feel effortless. The 50MP main sensor and matching selfie camera capture detailed photos, while 68W charging refills the 4700 mAh battery quickly.

Owners appreciate the large display and speed but complain about the modest battery endurance during heavy gaming and the lack of a brand warranty, relying on a shop warranty that may vary. Software updates have been historically slow, with only one to three major Android versions expected. The phone runs Android 15 with a near stock Hello UI and minimal ads, which keeps the experience clean.
