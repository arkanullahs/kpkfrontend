Motorola Razr Ultra 2025 Is it worth buying?

The Motorola Razr Ultra 2025 is a foldable device powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset that prioritizes a clean software experience.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a high-performance foldable with clean software and are comfortable managing gray-import risks.

Reported trade-offs3

  • Historically weak software update support

  • No official manufacturer warranty

Why we say that

Living with this phone means navigating the reality of gray-import stock, as there is no official manufacturer warranty available in Bangladesh. You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before finalizing any purchase to ensure the device is registered correctly. The 165Hz foldable display is bright at 4500 nits, making it usable in direct sunlight.

While the Hello UI offers a near-stock Android 15 experience with minimal bloat, owners should be aware that Motorola has a history of weak software support. You can expect between 1 and 3 OS updates over the life of the device. The 4700 mAh battery paired with 68W charging is standard for this form factor, but heavy users may find the capacity limiting.

Does the Motorola Razr Ultra 2025 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there is no official manufacturer warranty for this device in Bangladesh. You will only receive a shop warranty provided by the seller, so you must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with them before buying.

How many years of software updates will this phone receive?

Motorola has a history of weak software support for this model. You can expect between 1 and 3 OS updates throughout the lifespan of the device.

Is the battery life sufficient for heavy daily usage?

The device features a 4700 mAh battery and supports 68W charging. While this is a standard capacity for a foldable, heavy users should consider that the high-performance Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset and 165Hz display may impact overall endurance.

Who is this foldable phone best suited for?

This phone is suited for users who prioritize a near-stock Android 15 experience and top-tier processing power. It is intended for buyers who are comfortable navigating gray-import stock and the lack of official brand support.
