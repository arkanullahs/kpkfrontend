Realme 16 Pro Plus Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can survive a full day of heavy use without charging, the Realme 16 Pro Plus delivers.

What works well4

  • 7,000 mAh battery lasts over a day
  • 200 MP main camera produces strong portraits
  • 6.8‑inch 144 Hz AMOLED is bright and smooth
  • IP69 rating and vegan leather feel

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Realme UI carries medium to high ads
  • Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 throttles during intense gaming
  • Low light video is only average

Why we say that

The 7,000 mAh battery easily outlasts most daily routines and the 80 W charger tops up quickly. The 6.8‑inch AMOLED panel shines at a peak of 6,500 nits and runs at 144 Hz, making media consumption pleasant even in bright sunlight. The 200 MP main sensor captures strong portraits, though ultrawide and low light video are only average.

The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 handles everyday apps well but heats up and throttles when you push it with demanding games. Realme UI offers many features but you will need to disable several pre‑installed apps and ads manually. The phone arrives as a gray import, so there is no official brand warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited guarantee and any repairs cost extra.

Will the battery last a full day of heavy use?

The 7,000 mAh cell is class‑leading and reviewers say it easily covers heavy usage for more than a day, so you should not need to charge before bedtime.

How does the phone handle gaming and does it overheat?

The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 runs everyday apps smoothly, but owners report temperatures reaching around 43 °C during demanding games, which triggers thermal throttling and reduces frame rates.

Is the camera good in low light video?

The 200 MP sensor delivers strong portraits, but low light video performance is described as average, so you may notice noise and less detail in dark environments.
