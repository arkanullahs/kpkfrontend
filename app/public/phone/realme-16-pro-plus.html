Is the Realme 16 Pro Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 6.8 inch AMOLED with 144 Hz refresh
  • Large 7000 mAh battery gives long endurance
  • 200 MP main camera provides detailed shots
  • 80W fast charging refills quickly

What is not

  • Chipset feels weak compared with claims
  • Camera module design is boring and ugly
  • No official IP rating for water resistance
  • Software contains medium high ads and bloat

Buy it if Buy it if you need a big screen, huge battery and fast charging and can tolerate a mediocre chipset and ad‑laden software.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect strong performance, premium camera tuning or an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

The phone offers a 6.8 inch AMOLED panel that reaches 144 Hz and can get very bright thanks to a 6500‑nit peak. Its 7000 mAh cell is praised for lasting through a day of heavy use and can be refilled quickly with 80W charging. The 200 MP main sensor produces detailed pictures but owners complain about dull tuning and an unattractive camera module.

The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 processor is rated low by users who label it trash and it struggles with demanding games. Realme UI brings medium high ads and bloat, and the device arrives only as gray‑import stock with shop warranty and no official IP rating.
