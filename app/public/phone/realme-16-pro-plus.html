Is the Realme 16 Pro Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • 7,000 mAh battery lasting 1‑2 days
  • 200 MP main camera with good portraits
  • 6.8‑inch 144 Hz AMOLED display
  • 80 W fast charging

What is not

  • No official brand warranty in Bangladesh
  • Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 feels under‑powered
  • Plastic frame feels cheap
  • Weak haptic motor

Buy it if Buy it if you need a huge battery, high‑resolution camera and can accept a gray‑import without official warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you prioritize smooth gaming, strong performance or official after‑sales support.

Why we say that

A 7,000 mAh cell gives you one to two days of use without hunting a charger, and the 80 W charger tops up quickly. The 200 MP main sensor and 50 MP selfie lens produce sharp portraits and good detail in daylight. The 6.8‑inch AMOLED panel is bright and fluid at 144 Hz, though the high refresh rate is limited by the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset.

Daily tasks feel smooth, but gaming shows noticeable frame drops and the phone warms to about 43 °C under load. The build mixes vegan leather with a plastic frame, and the haptic motor feels cheap. Because every unit is a gray import, there is no official Realme warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited guarantee and pay for any repairs.

Software is Realme UI 7 with medium‑high bloat and only two to three major OS updates promised.
