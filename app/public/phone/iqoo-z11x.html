Buying verdict

iQOO Z11x Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day on a single charge, the iQOO Z11x delivers.

Choose it if
You want long battery life, solid gaming performance, and can accept a gray‑import with shop warranty only.
Consider alternatives if

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • 7200 mAh battery gives 1.5‑2 days use
  • Dimensity 7400 Turbo with UFS 3.1 feels snappy
  • IP68/69 and military‑grade drop protection
  • 44 W fast charge, though takes over an hour

Reported trade-offs

  • 44 W charge is slow for the huge battery
  • IPS LCD lacks AMOLED contrast and has large bezels
  • Daylight photos are oversaturated and low‑light is soft
  • OriginOS shows notification delays and medium bloat

Why we say that

The 7200 mAh battery really stretches to 1.5‑2 days of normal use and the 44 W charger refills it in about an hour and a half. Performance is solid thanks to the Dimensity 7400 Turbo chipset and UFS 3.1 storage, keeping games like PUBG at a stable 60 FPS while staying cool thanks to vapor cooling. The 6.76‑inch IPS LCD offers 120 Hz refresh and 1200 nits peak brightness, but the large bezels and lack of AMOLED contrast make the screen feel dated.

Camera quality is mixed; daylight shots are oversaturated, low‑light images are soft, and the 2 MP depth sensor adds little value. OriginOS 6 runs fluidly but brings medium bloat and occasional notification delays. As a gray‑import, there is no official brand warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long will the battery actually last in daily use?

Is the iQOO Z11x good for gaming?

Yes, the Dimensity 7400 Turbo paired with UFS 3.1 storage provides snappy performance. Games like PUBG run at a stable 60 FPS and the phone stays cool thanks to its vapor‑cooling system.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

Low‑light photos are soft and lack detail, while daylight images tend to be oversaturated. The 2 MP depth sensor adds little value and the front camera also suffers from exposure issues.
