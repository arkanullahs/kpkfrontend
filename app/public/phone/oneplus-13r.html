Buying verdict

OnePlus 13R Is it worth buying?

The OnePlus 13R offers a powerful Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 experience for ৳68,999, but it comes with significant compromises that owners frequently highlight.

Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life and raw processing power for a price of ৳68,999.
Consider alternatives if
You are sensitive to screen flicker or eye strain, or if you want a high-quality camera experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Exceptional 6000 mAh battery endurance
  • Fast Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 performance
  • Bright 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED display
  • Four major Android OS upgrades

Reported trade-offs

  • Reports of significant eye fatigue
  • Poor 8MP ultrawide and hazy selfies
  • Includes Oppo and ColorOS bloatware
  • BGMI 120fps is generated, not native

Why we say that

While the 6000 mAh battery provides exceptional endurance, the user experience is marred by reports of significant eye fatigue and strain after short periods of use. Performance is generally fast, though gamers should note that the 120fps mode in BGMI relies on frame generation rather than native output. The camera system is inconsistent, featuring a strong main sensor but a poor ultrawide lens and soft, hazy selfies.

Software now includes Oppo-style bloatware, and some users face issues with delayed notifications. While the build is solid, the downgrade to Gorilla Glass 7i and the slower 80W charging compared to previous models are notable drawbacks. Given that the newer OnePlus 15R is available for ৳72,999, many buyers may find that model to be a better overall investment.
