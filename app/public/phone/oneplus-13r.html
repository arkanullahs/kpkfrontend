Is the OnePlus 13R worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts 1 to 2 days
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 delivers fast performance
  • 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED reaches 4500 nits
  • 80W charging refills quickly

What is not

  • Eye fatigue after short use
  • Ultrawide and selfie cameras are soft
  • Notifications sometimes delayed
  • BGMI 120 fps is generated, not native

Buy it if Buy it if you value performance and battery over camera perfection.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a reliable camera or official warranty.

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset and 12GB RAM give fast performance in apps and games, and the 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED screen can reach a peak brightness of 4500 nits. The 6000 mAh battery easily lasts one to two days, and the 80W charger refills it in a short time. Owners praise the speed and endurance, but many report eye fatigue after ten minutes of use and note that the 8MP ultrawide and 16MP selfie lenses produce soft images.

Some users see delayed notifications and occasional overheating during long video calls. BGMI runs at generated 120 fps rather than native, and a few have seen memory crashes. Software runs OxygenOS 15 with low ads but includes some Oppo bloatware.

How long will the battery last in daily use?

The 6000 mAh battery is rated to last one to two days with typical use, and owners report exceptional endurance even with gaming and video streaming.

Will the phone handle high frame rate gaming like BGMI?

The device runs BGMI at 120 fps, but the frames are generated rather than native, so performance feels fast but may not match true 120 fps support on other flagships.

Is the camera good in low light or for selfies?

The main 50MP sensor performs well, but the 8MP ultrawide and 16MP selfie lenses are soft and hazy, especially in low light, so expect compromises.

Will I receive Android updates and is there an official warranty?

The phone ships with Android 15 and OxygenOS 15 and promises up to four major Android upgrades, but the gray‑import version does not list an official manufacturer warranty.
