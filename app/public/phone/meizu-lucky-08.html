Buying verdict

Meizu Lucky 08 Is it worth buying?

If you can accept a gray‑import with no official warranty, the Meizu Lucky 08 gives you a huge battery and a very bright, fast screen for under ৳27,000.

Choose it if
You want a large, bright display and long battery life at a low price and can live without official service or regular Android updates.

What works well

  • Huge 6000 mAh battery with 45 W fast charge
  • Bright 5000‑nit LTPO AMOLED screen at 144 Hz
  • Sharp 108 MP main camera in daylight
  • Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 handles everyday tasks smoothly

Reported trade-offs

  • No manufacturer warranty, only shop warranty
  • Frequent notification delays in Flyme OS
  • Low‑light photos blurry without OIS
  • No NFC and no Android updates

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh cell combined with LTPO efficiency lets most users get through a full day of mixed use without hunting for a charger. The 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel reaches a peak of 5000 nits and refreshes at 144 Hz, making media consumption and scrolling feel smooth. Daily performance feels snappy thanks to the Snapdragon 7s Gen 2, though heavy gaming can raise the temperature and throttle a bit.

The 108 MP main sensor captures sharp images in good light, but low‑light shots suffer from blur because the camera lacks optical image stabilization. Flyme AIOS runs cleanly but does not receive Android version upgrades, and aggressive background management can delay notifications. Service relies on the shop’s warranty only, so any repair cost falls on you.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 6000 mAh battery together with the LTPO panel usually lasts a full day of mixed use, including browsing, video streaming and some gaming. Heavy gaming or bright‑screen use will reduce endurance noticeably.

Will the phone handle gaming without problems?

The Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 provides smooth performance for casual games, but intensive titles can cause the device to heat up and may lead to occasional throttling, affecting frame rates.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 108 MP main sensor is sharp in daylight, but it lacks optical image stabilization, so low‑light photos often appear blurry and grainy. The 8 MP selfie camera remains entry‑level.

What warranty and service can I expect for this phone in Bangladesh?

All units are gray‑imports, so there is no manufacturer warranty. You rely on the shop’s warranty, and any repair cost is paid by you. Official Meizu service centers are practically unavailable in Bangladesh.
