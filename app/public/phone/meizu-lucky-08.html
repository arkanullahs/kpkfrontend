Why we say that

The 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED panel delivers 144 Hz refresh and peaks at 5000 nits, making everyday use feel fluid and vivid. The 6000 mAh cell easily lasts a full day of browsing, video and gaming, and the 45 W charger refills it in under an hour. Daily tasks run comfortably on the Snapdragon 7s Gen 2, though the chip warms up and may throttle during extended gaming sessions.

The 108 MP main sensor captures decent photos in daylight, but the lack of optical stabilization and a weak 8 MP selfie camera hurt low light results. Flyme AIOS is locked on Android 14 with only one or two future updates and shows noticeable notification delays. Because every unit is a gray import, there is no manufacturer warranty; any repair must be handled by the shop that sold it.

Is there an official warranty for the Meizu Lucky 08 in Bangladesh?

No. All listings are gray import units, so the phone comes with only the shop’s limited warranty. Any repair after the shop’s period is paid out of pocket, and Meizu has no official service centers in Bangladesh.

How many Android updates will the phone receive?

The device ships with Flyme AIOS based on Android 14 and the manufacturer has indicated only one or two major OS upgrades. Updates are not guaranteed and the phone may stay on Android 14 for a long time.

Will the battery last through a full day of heavy use?

The 6000 mAh cell combined with the efficient LTPO panel gives excellent endurance, easily covering a day of browsing, video streaming and gaming. Fast charging at 45 W refills the battery in about an hour, though heavy loads can cause the device to heat.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 108 MP sensor produces acceptable pictures in bright conditions, but it lacks optical image stabilization and the phone has no ultrawide lens. In dim environments photos become blurry and grainy, and the 8 MP front camera is also weak.
