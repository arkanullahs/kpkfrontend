Buying verdict

Realme C65 Is it worth buying?

The Realme C65 is a difficult recommendation because its hardware specifications struggle to justify the price of 18,499 to ৳21,800.

Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life above all else and only need a phone for basic daily tasks.
Consider alternatives if
You want a sharp display, stable camera performance, or a device capable of handling modern gaming.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Excellent battery endurance lasting over a full day
  • Slim and attractive design with IP54 rating
  • Smooth software experience with Realme UI 5.0
  • Includes a convenient headphone jack

Reported trade-offs

  • Low resolution display is a major disappointment
  • Outdated Helio G85 chip struggles with modern gaming
  • Camera lacks stabilization and produces soft images
  • Excessive bloatware pre-installed on the device

Why we say that

While the 5000 mAh battery provides excellent endurance that easily lasts a full day, the rest of the experience is hampered by an outdated Helio G85 chipset and a low resolution display. Owners frequently express frustration that a phone at this price point lacks a sharper screen and modern processing power. The camera system is another point of contention, as it lacks stabilization and produces soft, washed out images.

While the Realme UI 5.0 software is smooth, it is heavily burdened by excessive bloatware that users find intrusive. Because these units are gray imports, you will not receive any manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh, and any repairs must be paid for out of pocket. Given that the newer Realme C71 is available for about ৳19,949, most buyers will find better value elsewhere.

Does the Realme C65 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh. Every listing is a gray import, meaning you are responsible for all repair costs. You should only rely on whatever limited promise the specific shop provides.

Is the Realme C65 good for gaming?

The phone is not recommended for gaming. It uses an outdated Helio G85 chipset that struggles with modern games and heavy tasks. Owners have reported that the specifications do not match the price, leading to poor performance in demanding applications.

How long will the Realme C65 receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 14 with Realme UI 5.0. You can expect to receive between 2 to 3 OS updates over the lifespan of the phone. However, be aware that the software comes with medium to high levels of bloatware and ads.

How does the camera perform for videos and photos?

The 50MP main camera is decent in daylight but often produces soft and washed out images. A major drawback is the complete lack of stabilization on both the camera and video modes, which makes capturing steady footage very difficult.
