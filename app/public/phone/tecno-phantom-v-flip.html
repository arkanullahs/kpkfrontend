Buying verdict

Tecno Phantom V Flip Is it worth buying?

The Tecno Phantom V Flip is a fragile device that struggles with basic reliability and long-term durability.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You are a collector who specifically wants a foldable device and does not rely on the phone for heavy daily tasks or long battery life.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable daily driver that can handle gaming, long hours away from a charger, and requires long-term software security updates.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Large 6.9 inch foldable LTPO AMOLED display
  • Smooth 120Hz refresh rate
  • Capable 64MP main camera sensor
  • Fast 45W charging support

Reported trade-offs4

  • Fragile build with no ingress protection
  • Poor battery endurance under 12 hours
  • High levels of bloatware and ads
  • Screen bleed and overheating issues

Why we say that

While the 6.9 inch foldable display offers a decent visual experience, owners report significant issues including a screen that does not open flat and visible bleed at the folding area after only three months of use. Performance is powered by the Dimensity 8050 chipset, but users note the frame becomes scorching hot during heavy gaming sessions. The software experience is hindered by high levels of bloatware and intrusive ads, and the device is now three years old, meaning you should expect little to no remaining software support.

Battery life is a major pain point, with reports indicating it fails to last 12 hours. Combined with complaints about poor touchscreen quality and a lack of ingress protection, this phone presents a high risk for anyone looking for a daily driver that will last.
