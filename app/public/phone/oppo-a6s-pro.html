Is the OPPO A6s Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz AMOLED display
  • 7000 mAh battery lasting over two days
  • 80W fast charging
  • 50 MP ultra‑wide selfie camera

What is not

  • Outdated Helio G100 4G processor
  • Average rear camera performance
  • Poor gaming capability
  • Overpriced for the specs

Buy it if Buy it if battery life and selfie quality matter more than gaming or 5G.

Skip it if Skip it if you need strong gaming performance, 5G connectivity, or high‑resolution video.

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh battery truly lasts over two days for most users and the 80W charger refills it quickly. The 6.57‑inch AMOLED panel at 120 Hz looks smooth and bright even in sunlight. The 50 MP ultra‑wide selfie lens is the only camera that gets regular compliments.

However the Helio G100 chipset is a 4G part that feels slow with modern games and the rear camera is only average. The device feels premium because of its matte Lumina Forest finish, but many owners feel the price is too high for the performance offered. Software is tidy but includes some bloatware and the update cadence is unclear.
