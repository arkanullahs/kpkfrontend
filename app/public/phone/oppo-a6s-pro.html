OPPO A6s Pro Is it worth buying?

The OPPO A6s Pro is a device defined by its massive battery life, yet it is held back by a chipset that feels out of place at this price point.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize extreme battery life and a durable, rugged build above all else.
Consider alternatives if
You are a gamer or expect modern features like 5G and 4K video recording at this price.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Exceptional 7000 mAh battery endurance
  • Premium build with IP69K rating
  • Vibrant 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Strong 50MP selfie camera

Reported trade-offs4

  • Severely underpowered Helio G100 chipset
  • Lacks 5G connectivity and 4K video
  • Software contains bloatware and ads
  • Display lacks HDR support

Why we say that

Owners are frustrated by the Helio G100 processor, which is widely considered underpowered for a phone costing up to ৳42,999. While the 7000 mAh battery offers industry-leading endurance, the overall experience is marred by the lack of 5G connectivity and the absence of 4K video recording. The build quality is a highlight, featuring an IP69K rating and military-grade durability, but the software experience is cluttered with bloatware and ads.

Users also note that the AMOLED display, while vibrant, lacks HDR support. If you prioritize battery longevity and a rugged design over raw performance or advanced camera features, this phone might serve you, but many buyers feel the current pricing does not reflect the hardware provided.

OPPO A6s Pro costs ৳37,999 to ৳42,999 in Bangladesh on official listings, in stock at 5 shops we track as of 28 Oct 2026. The unofficial price starts at ৳38,199.

Does the OPPO A6s Pro have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

Yes, the official unit comes with a brand warranty. You should be careful because cheaper listings are gray imports that have no manufacturer cover. If you take a gray import to a service centre, they will only accept it as a paid repair.

Is the OPPO A6s Pro good for gaming?

Owners rate the gaming performance at 4.5 out of 10. The Helio G100 chipset is considered severely underpowered for a phone in this price segment. You will likely find it disappointing if you intend to play demanding games.

How is the camera performance on this device?

The 50MP selfie camera is strong and includes auto-wide features. However, the rear camera lacks versatility and optical image stabilization. Owners also complain that the device cannot record 4K video, which is a significant limitation for this price range.

What should I know about the software on this phone?

The phone runs Android 16 with ColorOS 16. While the interface is polished, users report that it comes with pre-installed bloatware, hot apps, and hot games. Additionally, the software lacks advanced AI features that some users might expect.
