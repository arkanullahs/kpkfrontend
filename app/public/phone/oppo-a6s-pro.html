Buying verdict

OPPO A6s Pro Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can stay on for two days without charging, the OPPO A6s Pro delivers that.

Choose it if
You value long battery life, a smooth display and fast charging more than top‑tier performance.
Consider alternatives if
You need 5G, high‑end gaming capability, or a strong rear camera.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • 7000 mAh battery lasts over two days
  • 120 Hz AMOLED display with vivid colors
  • 80 W fast charging fills large battery quickly
  • 50 MP ultra‑wide selfie camera

Reported trade-offs

  • Outdated Helio G100 4G chipset struggles with games
  • Rear camera only average, weak in low light
  • No 5G support

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh battery easily lasts over 48 hours for most users, and the 80 W charger refills it quickly. The 6.57‑inch AMOLED panel runs at a smooth 120 Hz and shows vivid colors even in bright sunlight. However, the Helio G100 chipset is a 4G‑only, outdated processor that cannot handle demanding games and feels sluggish in heavy apps.

The rear camera is only average and struggles in low light, while the 50 MP ultra‑wide selfie lens is the standout. ColorOS 16 looks polished but includes medium‑level bloatware and may not get as many updates as flagship devices. Overall, the phone feels premium in design and battery life but falls short on performance and camera quality for its price.
