Why we say that

The 6.75 inch IPS LCD can reach 1,000 nits, making outdoor use easy. The 5,000 mAh battery lasts a day with moderate use, and the 256 GB internal memory holds many apps and media. The design mimics the iPhone 17 Pro, but owners call it a cheap knockoff and note that the camera modules feel fake, with only one real lens.

The Unisoc T7280 chipset struggles with games and heavy multitasking, and the Android 16 based UI receives only one or two OS upgrades, leaving the software feeling outdated. Overall the phone feels like a budget iPhone look‑alike that delivers basic daily tasks but disappoints on gaming, camera quality, and long‑term software support.

The Nubia V80 Design delivers a striking flagship-inspired aesthetic, modern Android 16 software, and dependable all-day battery life for budget-conscious buyers. However, it compromises with an entry-level processor, slow eMMC storage, a sub-1080p screen, and mediocre low-light camera performance.

    SamZone concluded that the Nubia V80 Design is primarily appealing for shoppers looking for a trendy iPhone-inspired aesthetic, official warranty support in Bangladesh, and up-to-date Android 16 software. On the negative side, he found that the elevated camera island is prone to micro-scratches, the screen resolution falls below Full HD, and performance is limited by older eMMC 5.1 storage and an entry-level Unisoc chipset unsuitable for heavy multitasking or gaming.

    Tech to the Point concluded that while the Nubia V80 Design provides good ergonomics, clean Android 16 software, and adequate basic performance, its price point is difficult to justify given market alternatives. He highlighted notable trade-offs such as the low-resolution HD+ display, slower eMMC storage memory, an inflexible dedicated AI button, lack of stereo speakers, and camera results that degrade significantly in dim lighting.

However, they noted several drawbacks including shaky 1080p video without stabilization, muffled single-speaker audio, occasional navigation stutters, an inconsistent facial recognition sensor, and an entry-level processor that struggles with graphically intensive gaming.

  4. MManila Shaker

    nubia V80 Design Review (opens YouTube)

Nonetheless, they cautioned that it is not built for gamers or photography enthusiasts, pointing to noticeable frame drops in 3D gaming, lack of video stabilization, and photos that lose detail and exhibit noise in low light.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5,000 mAh battery is rated as average by owners and can comfortably last a full day with normal browsing, social media and video playback. Heavy gaming will drain it faster.

Can I play modern games smoothly on this phone?

The Unisoc T7280 SoC is considered weak for gaming. Owner ratings show a low score for gaming and reviews call it unsuitable for heavy tasks, so expect low frame rates in demanding games.

Is the camera reliable in low‑light conditions?

The main 50 MP sensor is praised for resolution but owners report fake camera modules and low scores for camera performance. Low‑light photos are likely to be disappointing.

Will the phone receive regular Android updates?

The device runs Nebula AIOS based on Android 16 and has received only one or two OS upgrades so far. Users complain about poor software support, so future updates are uncertain.
