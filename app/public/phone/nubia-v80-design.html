What reviewers said on YouTube

4 reviews · 154K views

The Nubia V80 Design delivers a striking flagship-inspired aesthetic, modern Android 16 software, and dependable all-day battery life for budget-conscious buyers. However, it compromises with an entry-level processor, slow eMMC storage, a sub-1080p screen, and mediocre low-light camera performance.

Best for Budget buyers seeking an iPhone-like design for casual everyday use

  1. SSamZone

    Nubia V80 Design Review - কি অবস্থা এই আধুনিক ডিজাইনার ফোনের! (opens YouTube)

    53K views · Feb 2026Bangla

    SamZone concluded that the Nubia V80 Design is primarily appealing for shoppers looking for a trendy iPhone-inspired aesthetic, official warranty support in Bangladesh, and up-to-date Android 16 software. On the negative side, he found that the elevated camera island is prone to micro-scratches, the screen resolution falls below Full HD, and performance is limited by older eMMC 5.1 storage and an entry-level Unisoc chipset unsuitable for heavy multitasking or gaming.

  2. TTech to the Point - TTP

    কম দামে আইফোনের ভাবঃ Nubia V80 Design Full Review in Bangla (opens YouTube)

    18K views · Feb 2026Bangla

    Tech to the Point concluded that while the Nubia V80 Design provides good ergonomics, clean Android 16 software, and adequate basic performance, its price point is difficult to justify given market alternatives. He highlighted notable trade-offs such as the low-resolution HD+ display, slower eMMC storage memory, an inflexible dedicated AI button, lack of stereo speakers, and camera results that degrade significantly in dim lighting.

  3. YYugaTech

    nubia V80 Design Review (opens YouTube)

    67K views · Nov 2025

    YugaTech concluded that the Nubia V80 Design is a solid entry-level daily driver that punches above its price with an eye-catching exterior, smooth 120Hz display, and exceptionally long battery life. However, they noted several drawbacks including shaky 1080p video without stabilization, muffled single-speaker audio, occasional navigation stutters, an inconsistent facial recognition sensor, and an entry-level processor that struggles with graphically intensive gaming.

  4. MManila Shaker

    nubia V80 Design Review (opens YouTube)

    15K views · Nov 2025

    Manila Shaker concluded that the Nubia V80 Design serves well as an affordable, stylish handset for casual users focused on social media, video streaming, and all-day battery endurance. Nonetheless, they cautioned that it is not built for gamers or photography enthusiasts, pointing to noticeable frame drops in 3D gaming, lack of video stabilization, and photos that lose detail and exhibit noise in low light.
