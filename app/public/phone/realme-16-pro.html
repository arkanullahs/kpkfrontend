Buying verdict

Realme 16 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can sit on the charger for a few minutes and run for days, the Realme 16 Pro delivers that, but it struggles with speed and stability.

Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life and a high‑refresh screen over raw processing power.
Consider alternatives if
You need smooth gaming or a reliable, fast processor for heavy apps.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • 7000 mAh battery lasts multiple days
  • 144 Hz AMOLED display with high peak brightness
  • 200 MP main camera with OIS
  • 80 W fast charging

Reported trade-offs

  • Dimensity 7300 performance 40 % lower than previous
  • Frequent UI jitters and occasional crashes
  • Slow lens switching on camera
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh battery truly lasts multiple days for most users and the 80 W charger refills it quickly. The 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 144 Hz and can peak at 6500 nits, making everyday use bright and smooth. The 200 MP main sensor with OIS captures detailed photos, though switching to the ultrawide lens feels slow and there is no telephoto option.

Under the hood, the Dimensity 7300 Max is about 40 % slower than the previous generation, leading to noticeable jitters and occasional crashes in games and the UI. The phone arrives as a gray‑import unit, so there is no official Realme warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s limited warranty applies and any repair costs are out of pocket. Value is debated because the high price does not match the weakened performance.
