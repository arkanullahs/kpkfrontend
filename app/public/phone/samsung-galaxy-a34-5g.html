Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy A34 5G Is it worth buying?

Expect a bright, smooth display and solid battery, but plan for possible software related battery drain and a green line on the screen.

Choose it if
If you want a 5G phone with solid build, good battery and a bright display at a mid range price.
Consider alternatives if
If you need heavy gaming performance, long term software updates or a newer model with better value.

What works well

  • 120Hz Super AMOLED display with 1000 nit brightness
  • 5000 mAh battery with 25W fast charging
  • 8 GB RAM and Dimensity 1080 for smooth daily use
  • IP67 water dust resistance at this price

Reported trade-offs

  • Green vertical line may appear on screen
  • Battery drains after security updates
  • Messaging apps crash frequently
  • No charger included in box

Why we say that

The 6.6 inch Super AMOLED panel runs at 120Hz and can reach 1000 nits, making it easy to view outdoors. The Dimensity 1080 chipset with 8GB RAM handles everyday apps and light multitasking without heating up. The 5000 mAh cell lasts a full day, though owners notice a noticeable drop after recent security updates.

The phone feels sturdy and even carries an IP67 rating, a rare find at this price. Camera performance is reliable for daylight shots, and optical image stabilization helps a bit in low light, but it does not stand out. Software is clean One UI 8.5 and promises up to four Android upgrades, yet the device is three years old so future updates are limited.

Read full reasoning

Messaging apps have been reported to crash and the box does not include a charger.
