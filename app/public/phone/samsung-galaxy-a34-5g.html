Is the Samsung Galaxy A34 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 120Hz Super AMOLED display
  • Sturdy IP67 water and dust resistance
  • Strong battery endurance
  • Clean One UI software experience

What is not

  • Risk of vertical green line on display
  • No charger included in the box
  • Mediocre performance for heavy gaming
  • Battery drain issues after security updates

Buy it if You prioritize a sturdy build with water resistance and only need a phone for basic daily tasks.

Skip it if You are a heavy gamer or want a reliable device that avoids common display and software stability issues.

Why we say that

While the 6.6 inch Super AMOLED display is bright at 1000 nits, owners frequently report a vertical green line appearing on the screen over time. Daily performance is smooth for basic tasks, but the Dimensity 1080 chipset struggles with heavy gaming, reflected in a low 5.5 gaming rating. Battery life is generally strong, yet recent security updates have caused significant drain, leading some users to avoid updates entirely to maintain stability.

Messaging apps like Google Messages and Samsung Messages are also reported to crash frequently. Given that the phone is three years old, software support is nearing its end. With the newer Galaxy A53 5G available for 32,999 taka, this device is difficult to recommend at its official price of 53,999 taka.

Does the Samsung Galaxy A34 5G perform well for gaming?

The phone is not ideal for heavy gaming. Owners have given it a low rating of 5.5 for gaming performance, noting that while it handles daily tasks smoothly, it struggles when pushed with demanding games.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

This phone was released in 2023 and is now three years old. You should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point, despite the original promise of four major Android upgrades.

Is the battery life reliable on this device?

While the 5000 mAh battery offers strong endurance, it is currently unreliable due to software issues. Many owners report significant battery drain specifically after installing security updates, with some users claiming the updates are detrimental to battery health.
