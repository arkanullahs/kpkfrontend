If you can live with an older software state, the Find N3 gives a large, bright foldable experience at a low price.

Why we say that

The 7.82 inch LTPO3 OLED screen reaches 2800 nits and runs at 120Hz, making everyday use feel smooth and vivid. Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 and 16GB RAM deliver flagship level performance for most apps and games, though the device does not support high frame rate gaming. The 48MP main camera with f/1.7 aperture, OIS and 3x telephoto is praised as good and handles most lighting conditions well, while the 20MP selfie shooter is considered mediocre.

Battery life is rated 8.5 out of 10 and the 67W charger refills the 4805 mAh cell quickly. The phone lacks wireless charging and may stop receiving software updates soon, as it is three years old. Only shop warranty is available, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.
