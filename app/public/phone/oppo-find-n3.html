Is the OPPO Find N3 worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright and color accurate 120Hz LTPO3 OLED display
  • Strong performance from the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset
  • Good battery life with 67W fast charging
  • Capable rear camera system with OIS

What is not

  • No wireless charging support
  • Mediocre selfie camera performance
  • No high frame rate gaming
  • Outdated software with potential bugs

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a large foldable display and high performance and are comfortable managing a gray import device with a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you require official brand support, long term software updates, or high frame rate gaming capabilities.

Why we say that

Owners rate the performance and display highly, noting the 7.82 inch LTPO3 OLED screen is both bright and color accurate. However, living with this phone involves compromises. Users report that the selfie cameras are mediocre and the device lacks wireless charging.

You should be aware that the software is outdated and some owners have experienced bugs in updates. Because this phone is three years old, you should expect little to no remaining software support. The build quality is rated well, though it only carries an IPX4 splash proof rating.

Since only gray import stock is available, you will rely on a shop warranty rather than an official brand warranty. You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before spending 203,590 taka.
