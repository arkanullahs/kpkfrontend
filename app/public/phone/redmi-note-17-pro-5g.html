Is the Redmi Note 17 Pro 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 9000 mAh battery for multi‑day use
  • IP69K rating gives strong water and dust protection
  • 3500 nits peak brightness on AMOLED display
  • 67 W fast charging refills huge battery quickly

What is not

  • Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 chipset feels weak
  • Camera lacks ultrawide and records only 1080p
  • Heavy 226 g chassis with thick bezels
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Buy it if Buy it if you need an ultra‑large battery and rugged build and can tolerate a modest camera and slower performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect flagship‑level gaming, high‑resolution video, or an official warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.83‑inch AMOLED panel reaches a peak of 3500 nits, so outdoor visibility is excellent, yet the thick bezels and 226 gram weight make the phone feel bulky. Fast 67 W charging refills the massive cell quickly, and the IR blaster adds a handy remote function. Software runs Android 16 with HyperOS 3, but heavy bloatware and noticeable notification delays frustrate smooth interaction.

Gaming scores are low and performance ratings sit at five out of ten, reflecting the chipset regression. Video recording tops out at 1080p 30 fps, and the removal of the ultrawide lens hurts versatility. Because the unit is a gray import, there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh and any repair will cost extra.

How long will the 9000 mAh battery last in daily use?

The 9000 mAh cell is rated to give you several days of normal use, and it also supports reverse charging to power accessories. With 67 W fast charging you can refill it in about an hour.

Is the phone good for gaming?

Gaming performance is limited because the Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 chipset is a step back from previous models. Owner ratings place gaming at four out of ten, so demanding titles will feel laggy.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor is small (1/2.76") and the phone no longer has an ultrawide lens, resulting in weak low‑light photos and only 1080p 30 fps video. Owner camera rating is four out of ten.
