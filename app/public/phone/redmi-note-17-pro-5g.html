Is the Redmi Note 17 Pro 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 9000 mAh battery with industry leading capacity
  • 3500 nit peak brightness AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 chipset
  • IR blaster for remote control

What is not

  • CN ROM lacks Google services and Bangla
  • High amount of ads and bloatware in HyperOS
  • Shop warranty only, not brand warranty
  • Gaming and performance ratings only average

Buy it if Buy it if you want a massive battery, a very bright screen, and can tolerate ads and a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable Google apps, strong gaming performance, or official brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.83 inch AMOLED panel reaches a peak brightness of 3500 nits and runs at 120Hz, making outdoor viewing easy. Under the hood sits a Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 chip with 8GB RAM and up to 512GB storage, which delivers decent but not flagship performance. Owners love the battery and display ratings of 9.7 and 8.8, yet they complain about the heavy ads and bloatware in the HyperOS CN ROM and the lack of Google services.

The phone arrives as a gray‑import unit with only a shop warranty, so checking IMEI and BTRC status is essential. If you can tolerate the software clutter and limited warranty, this phone offers a huge battery and bright screen at a competitive price.

How long will the battery really last in daily use?

The 9000 mAh cell is described as industry leading and paired with a 4nm efficient chipset, so owners report more than a day of endurance and a battery rating of 9.7 out of 10. Expect several days of normal use.

Is the phone good for gaming?

Performance is rated 6.8 and gaming 5.5, indicating average results. The Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 will handle casual titles, but demanding games may feel sluggish compared with higher tier devices.

Will the camera work well in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor is 1/2.76 inch with f/1.8 aperture, which helps in dim environments, but reviews do not mention exceptional low light performance. Expect decent pictures but not studio quality in very dark scenes.

What warranty and software update support does it have?

The units sold are gray import China ROM, so they come with a shop warranty rather than Xiaomi’s official coverage. Software runs HyperOS and receives between two and four OS upgrades depending on the tier.
