Redmi Note 17 Pro 5G Is it worth buying?

The Redmi Note 17 Pro 5G will keep you powered for days thanks to its massive 9000 mAh battery, but you will quickly notice the camera and gaming compromises.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need massive battery life, rugged build and can tolerate a weak camera and slower performance.
Consider alternatives if
You prioritize camera quality, high‑end gaming, or want a clean, fast software experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 9000 mAh battery lasts days
  • 6.83‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED display
  • IP69K water and dust protection
  • 67 W fast charging

Reported trade-offs4

  • Camera lacks ultrawide and low‑light performance
  • Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 is slower than previous
  • HyperOS is ad‑heavy and delays notifications
  • Chinese ROM misses Google services

Why we say that

The 6.83‑inch AMOLED panel with 120 Hz refresh and 3500‑nit peak brightness looks vibrant and protects your eyes, while the IP69K rating and rugged construction survive drops and rain. The Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 chipset feels slower than the Note 15 Pro, making modern games choppy and apps lag. The 50 MP main sensor is tiny, offers no ultrawide lens and caps video at 1080p 30fps, which disappoints low‑light shooters.

HyperOS adds many ads and can delay notifications, especially on the Chinese ROM that lacks Google services. If you buy a gray import you lose the official warranty and must pay for any repairs. Overall, the phone feels like a battery‑first device with solid build, but its software and performance feel dated.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 9000 mAh battery is the phone’s standout feature and can easily last more than a full day of heavy use, giving you unmatched endurance compared to most smartphones.

Is the Redmi Note 17 Pro 5G suitable for gaming?

The Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 chipset is a regression from the previous generation, so modern games run with noticeable lag and lower frame rates, making it a poor choice for serious gamers.

Will the phone receive regular software updates?

It runs HyperOS 3 on Android 16 and receives only 2 to 4 major OS updates depending on the model tier, while the system is heavy with ads and can cause notification delays.

What warranty do I get if I buy a gray‑import unit?

Gray‑import listings do not carry the official Xiaomi warranty. Only the official unit bought from authorized channels gets brand warranty, while gray imports require paid repairs at service centres.
