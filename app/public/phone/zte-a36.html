Buying verdict

ZTE A36 Is it worth buying?

The ZTE A36 is a basic backup device that struggles with daily tasks due to severe performance issues and software limitations.

Choose it if
You need an extremely cheap backup device for basic tasks and prioritize long battery life over performance.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on your phone for daily apps, gaming, or photography, as the software and hardware limitations will cause significant frustration.

What works well

  • Strong two-day battery endurance
  • Affordable ৳10,200 price point
  • Smooth 90Hz display refresh rate
  • Includes headphone jack and FM radio

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe keyboard lag and app crashes
  • Painfully slow 10W charging speed
  • Blocked installation of unknown APKs
  • Misleading RAM marketing claims

Why we say that

While the 5000 mAh battery offers strong endurance that can last up to two days, the user experience is hampered by a bottom-of-the-barrel Unisoc T7200 chipset. Owners frequently report app crashes, severe keyboard lag, and a frustrating inability to install unknown APKs. The build quality feels cheap, and the lack of a fingerprint scanner is a notable omission.

Marketing claims regarding RAM are misleading, as the device does not perform anywhere near the advertised capacity. The camera quality is poor, with users noting that the 5MP selfie camera often outperforms the 13MP main sensor. While the 90Hz display is a welcome feature, the low-resolution panel is dim and lacks clarity.

Read full reasoning

With medium levels of bloatware and ads in the MyOS software, this phone is strictly for those who need a secondary device for very light usage.

Does the ZTE A36 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

Yes, the official unit comes with a brand warranty at the price of ৳10,200. Be aware that cheaper listings are gray imports with no manufacturer cover, and service centers will only accept these units for paid repairs.

How long will the ZTE A36 receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 15 and is expected to receive between one and two OS updates. Users should note that the software experience includes medium levels of bloatware and advertisements.

Is the ZTE A36 suitable for mobile gaming?

No, the phone is not suitable for gaming. Owners rate the gaming performance at 3.0 out of 10, citing a bottom-of-the-barrel chipset that leads to severe keyboard lag and frequent app crashes.

How does the battery perform on the ZTE A36?

The 5000 mAh battery is a strong point for this device, with owners reporting that it can last for two days on a single charge. However, recharging the battery is a slow process due to the 10W charging limit.
