Is the Honor Magic 5 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.81 inch LTPO OLED with 1800‑nit peak brightness
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 delivers strong everyday performance
  • 50 MP main camera with optical image stabilization
  • 66 W wired and 50 W wireless fast charging

What is not

  • GPU throttles heavily during prolonged gaming
  • Camera quality drops after software updates
  • 4K video limited to 15 minutes
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Buy it if Buy it if you want flagship‑level display and camera at a lower price and can handle a shop‑only warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need long‑term software updates or rely on an official manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.81 inch LTPO OLED reaches 1800 nits and offers smooth 120 Hz motion, making media consumption a pleasure. Battery endurance is strong, with most moderate users getting two days before recharging, and the 66 W wired plus 50 W wireless charging refill quickly. Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 provides solid everyday performance, yet reviewers note sharp GPU throttling during prolonged gaming or stress tests.

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures detailed photos, but many owners report camera quality dropping after each software update. Software runs Android 13 with MagicOS 8, but it carries medium bloat and occasional notification delays. At three years old, expect limited future OS updates, and the newer Magic 7 Pro sells for a similar price.

The phone arrives without an official manufacturer warranty, only the shop’s limited coverage.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The Honor Magic 5 Pro packs a 5100 mAh battery and owners rate its endurance at 8.8 out of 10. Moderate users typically get about two days of use before needing to recharge, and the 66 W charger refills it quickly.

Will the phone handle heavy gaming without slowdown?

Everyday tasks run smoothly thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2, but reviewers note sharp GPU throttling during prolonged stress tests. In extended gaming sessions the frame rate can drop, so it is not ideal for marathon play.

Is the camera still good after software updates?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS delivers detailed photos and good portraits, yet many owners report that each new Honor software update reduces camera quality. The issue appears across various lighting conditions, so expect possible degradation over time.

Does the phone come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray‑import units built for the global market, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop you buy from may offer its own limited warranty, but any repair will be paid out of pocket.
