Redmi K90 Pro Max Is it worth buying?

The Redmi K90 Pro Max is a powerful China-ROM import that demands technical patience from its user.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You are a power user comfortable with manual software configuration and prioritize raw battery life and gaming performance above all else.
Consider alternatives if
You want a phone with a local warranty, native Bangla support, and a display that manages power efficiently with LTPO technology.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Massive 7560 mAh battery capacity
  • Powerful Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance
  • Excellent main and 5x periscope cameras
  • Very bright 3500 nits peak display

Reported trade-offs4

  • China ROM lacks Google and Bangla support
  • Fiberglass back is prone to scratches
  • Display lacks LTPO dynamic refresh rate
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

While the massive 7560 mAh battery and Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset provide top-tier performance and endurance, the daily experience is hampered by software friction. Owners report that the HyperOS 3 China ROM requires manual installation of the Google Play Store and lacks native Bangla support. The fiberglass back is unique with its denim texture but scratches much more easily than standard glass.

You will also notice the display lacks LTPO technology, meaning the 120Hz refresh rate is not dynamic. While the main camera and 5x periscope lens perform well, the ultrawide sensor is mediocre. Because this is a gray-import unit, you receive no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh, and repairs are entirely at your own expense.

Does the Redmi K90 Pro Max come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit is a gray import, meaning you must rely on the specific shop for any coverage. All repairs are paid, and you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

Is the battery life on this phone actually good?

Owners rate the battery life very highly at 9.6 out of 10. The device features a massive 7560 mAh capacity that provides exceptional endurance. It also supports rare wireless charging, making it a strong choice for users who need a phone that lasts through heavy usage.

Can I use this phone for gaming without issues?

The phone is excellent for gaming with a performance rating of 9.4 and a gaming rating of 8.8. It offers top-tier speed, though you should be aware that the device can hit 50 degrees Celsius during extreme loads.

How difficult is it to set up the software on this device?

The device runs a China ROM version of HyperOS 3, which is quite difficult to manage. You must install the Google Play Store manually through an APK file. Additionally, the software lacks native Bangla support, which may be a significant hurdle for many local users.
