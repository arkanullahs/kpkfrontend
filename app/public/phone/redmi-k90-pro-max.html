Redmi K90 Pro Max Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run games for hours without charging, the Redmi K90 Pro Max delivers that.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 7560 mAh battery with exceptional endurance
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 gives top‑tier performance
  • 50 MP main camera with 5× periscope zoom
  • 6.9‑inch AMOLED panel reaches 3500 nits peak brightness

Reported trade-offs4

  • Fiberglass back scratches easily
  • No dynamic LTPO refresh, 120 Hz is fixed
  • China ROM lacks Google services and Bangla
  • Only shop warranty, no official support

Why we say that

The 7560 mAh battery easily lasts a full day of heavy use and even supports rare wireless charging. The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset gives flagship‑level speed, though it can climb to 50 °C under extreme loads. The 6.9‑inch AMOLED panel is extremely bright, reaching 3500 nits, but the 120 Hz refresh rate is fixed and not dynamic.

The 50 MP main camera with 5× periscope zoom captures detailed shots, while the ultrawide lens is only average. The fiberglass back has a denim texture but scratches more easily than glass. All units are gray‑import, so there is no official brand warranty and you must verify IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

Read full reasoning

The software runs HyperOS 3, which feels snappy but includes a lot of ads and requires manual installation of Google Play on China ROMs.

Redmi K90 Pro Max: what buyers ask us

What is the Redmi K90 Pro Max price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Redmi K90 Pro Max in Bangladesh was ৳75,499, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 7560 mAh cell provides exceptional endurance and can easily last more than a full day of mixed usage. Owners report that it rarely needs a recharge before evening, and wireless charging is supported but uncommon.

Will the phone run heavy games smoothly?

The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset delivers top‑tier speed and a gaming rating of 8.8. Most games run smoothly, though under extreme loads the device can reach about 50 °C, which is normal for high performance.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.7 aperture and OIS captures bright, detailed images in low light. The 5× periscope zoom also performs well, while the ultrawide lens is only mediocre in dim environments.

Does the phone have an official warranty and regular updates?

All units are gray‑import, so only a shop warranty is available and you must confirm IMEI and BTRC status with the seller. Software updates range from two to four OS versions depending on the tier, and the system includes noticeable ads and bloat.
