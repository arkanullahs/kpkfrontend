What works well

  • 6.7 inch 120Hz OLED display
  • 4500 nit peak brightness
  • 6000 mAh battery with 90W fast charging
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS and 3x telephoto

Reported trade-offs

  • Thermal throttling during heavy gaming
  • Video stabilization jitter in movement
  • No 4K 60fps video recording
  • Plastic frame despite IP69 rating

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch OLED panel runs at 120Hz and can reach 4500 nits, so outdoor use feels comfortable. The 6000 mAh battery paired with 90W charging gives you more than a full day of mixed use and a quick top‑up when you are in a hurry. Daily tasks feel snappy thanks to the Dimensity 8350 Extreme chipset and 8 GB of RAM, but sustained gaming pushes the chip hard and the device will throttle temperature.

Photo quality is strong with a 50 MP main sensor, OIS and 3× telephoto, yet video suffers from jitter and there is no 4K 60fps mode. The phone arrives as a gray‑import built for India, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s limited cover applies. Software is a near‑stock Hello UI on Android 15 with up to three major OS upgrades, though occasional UI jitters are reported.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

Owners rate the battery at 9.3 out of 10 and note that the 6000 mAh cell provides industry‑leading endurance for this segment. Most users report a full day of mixed use without needing to recharge.

Will the phone handle heavy gaming without slowdown?

The Dimensity 8350 Extreme chipset feels snappy for everyday apps, but owners report thermal throttling when the device is under sustained load such as heavy gaming. Expect occasional frame drops during long sessions.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures strong stills, but video stabilization is inconsistent and low‑light video may show jitter. Photo quality remains respectable in dim environments.
