Vivo Y32 Is it worth buying?

The Vivo Y32 is a device defined by its massive battery life at the cost of almost every other performance metric.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a phone that lasts for multiple days on a single charge and can survive extreme physical drops.
Consider alternatives if
You care about display sharpness, gaming performance, or getting good value for your money.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Industry-leading 8000 mAh battery capacity
  • Outstanding military-grade drop resistance
  • Durable build with IP69 rating
  • Bright 1300 nits peak display

Reported trade-offs4

  • Unacceptable HD+ resolution on large screen
  • Severe performance lag during basic tasks
  • Poor gaming performance
  • Lacks camera versatility

Why we say that

While the 8000 mAh battery provides industry-leading multi-day endurance, owners report that the experience is marred by severe performance lag and a display that feels outdated. Users specifically complain that the HD+ resolution on a 6.87-inch screen is unacceptable for a phone costing ৳30,999 or more. The Unisoc T7300 chipset struggles with basic multitasking, and gamers find it impossible to play titles like Free Fire without significant lag.

Owners feel the hardware is vastly overpriced, with many mocking the 4GB/128GB variant price point. While the build quality is excellent with military-grade drop resistance and an IP69 rating, the lack of camera versatility and poor display quality make it difficult to recommend. If you prioritize battery capacity above all else, this phone delivers, but it fails to meet the standards expected at this price range in the current market.

Does the Vivo Y32 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

Is the Vivo Y32 good for playing games like Free Fire?

No, the phone is not suitable for gaming. Owners report that the Unisoc T7300 chipset struggles significantly, and even basic games like Free Fire experience severe lag during gameplay.

How long will the battery last on a single charge?

The 8000 mAh battery is considered industry-leading. Owners report that it provides multi-day endurance, making it the strongest feature of the device for users who prioritize battery life over performance.

The software support depends on the model tier. Upper models receive 3 OS updates, while budget models receive 2 OS updates. The device runs Android 16 and OriginOS 6 out of the box.
