Redmi K70 Ultra Is it worth buying?

If you want a flagship level screen and fast charging but can tolerate a weak battery and a basic camera, the Redmi K70 Ultra may fit your needs.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize smooth gaming and a bright high refresh display over long battery endurance.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable low light photography or official after sales support.

Why we say that

It uses a Dimensity 9300+ chip, 12 to 24GB RAM and up to 1TB storage, so apps open quickly and multitasking feels smooth. The 6.67 inch OLED screen runs at 144Hz and can reach 4000 nits peak brightness, staying clear in bright sunlight. 120W charging fills the 5500mAh cell in about half an hour, yet owners say the battery feels inefficient and often needs a top up before day end.

The 50MP main camera with OIS records decent daylight shots, but without a periscope lens it struggles in low light and offers limited zoom. A metal frame gives an IP68 rating, but the phone is a gray import, so there is no official warranty and repairs are paid. It runs Android 14 with HyperOS, but the budget version shows many ads and will receive only two to four major OS updates.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The phone has a 5500mAh battery, but owners rate its endurance at 5.5 out of 10 and describe it as not efficient, often needing a recharge before the day ends, especially with heavy use.

Is the Redmi K70 Ultra good for gaming?

With the Dimensity 9300+ chipset, 12 to 24GB RAM and a 144Hz OLED panel, the phone scores 8.5 for performance and 8.0 for gaming, delivering smooth frame rates in most titles.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The main sensor is a 50MP unit with f/1.7 aperture and OIS, but it lacks a periscope lens, so low light shots remain average and zoom capability is limited.

Will I get official warranty and software updates?

All listings are gray imports, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s limited warranty and any repair costs. The device runs HyperOS on Android 14 and is expected to receive only two to four major OS upgrades.
