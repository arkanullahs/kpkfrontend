Redmi K70 Ultra Is it worth buying?

If you want a flagship level Android with a 144Hz OLED screen at a gray import price, the Redmi K70 Ultra delivers that but falls short on battery efficiency and camera depth.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize smooth gaming and a high refresh display over long battery endurance.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable low light photography or an official warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 120W fast charging
  • 144Hz OLED display
  • Dimensity 9300+ performance
  • Up to 24GB RAM and 1TB storage

Reported trade-offs4

  • Battery not efficient despite 5500mAh
  • Camera lacks periscope zoom
  • High amount of ads in HyperOS
  • No official brand warranty

Why we say that

The Dimensity 9300+ chipset and up to 24GB RAM make most games run smoothly and the 120W charger refills the 5500mAh cell quickly. The metal frame with IP68 rating feels solid, but owners note the battery drains faster than expected despite the large capacity. The 50MP main sensor lacks a periscope lens, so low light shots are average.

HyperOS runs Android 14 but shows many ads and receives only two to four major OS upgrades. There is no official brand warranty, only a shop warranty, so verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

Redmi K70 Ultra: what buyers ask us

What is the Redmi K70 Ultra price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Redmi K70 Ultra in Bangladesh was ৳41,203, checked 24 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The phone has a 5500mAh battery but owners rate its endurance low because it is not efficient. Expect moderate screen on time and rely on the 120W charger for quick top ups.

Is the Redmi K70 Ultra good for gaming?

Yes, the Dimensity 9300+ chipset, up to 24GB RAM and a 144Hz OLED panel give strong performance. Owner ratings for gaming are eight out of ten, indicating smooth gameplay.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The main camera uses a basic 50MP sensor without a periscope lens, so low light photos are average. Users report that the camera is decent in daylight but struggles when lighting is poor.

Will I get an official warranty and software updates?

There is no official brand warranty for this gray import; only a shop warranty is offered and you must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status. HyperOS will receive two to four major OS updates depending on the model tier.
