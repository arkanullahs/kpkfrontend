Is the Nokia 130 Music 2025 worth buying?

What is good

  • Long lasting battery for basic use
  • Simple ad free interface
  • Headphone jack for private listening
  • Very low price

What is not

  • Dull low resolution screen
  • Cannot run apps or games
  • Poor viewing angles

Buy it if Buy if you need an inexpensive device for calls, texts and simple music playback.

Skip it if Skip if you expect a modern smartphone experience or a good display.

Why we say that

The 2500 mAh battery gives excellent endurance for basic feature phone usage and owners rate it 8.2 out of 10. The interface is simple and completely free of ads, making navigation easy for anyone. The 2.4 inch TN TFT panel is low resolution and has poor viewing angles, which many users find disappointing.

Performance is limited to calls, SMS and music; the Unisoc 6531F chipset cannot handle modern apps or games and the gaming rating is just 1.0. Build quality is average with a rating of 6.5. At ৳3,999 the phone is very cheap and comes with an official warranty.

Overall it is a reliable basic phone but not a smartphone replacement.
