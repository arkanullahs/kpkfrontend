itel City 200 Is it worth buying?

The itel City 200 is a device that struggles with basic daily tasks and suffers from severe system lag.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
Buy this phone only if you prioritize long battery life above all other performance metrics and do not mind occasional system stuttering.
Consider alternatives if
You need a device for gaming, social media scrolling, or reliable wireless connectivity.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Excellent battery endurance up to 48 hours
  • Includes a headphone jack and IR blaster
  • IP65 and military certification for build
  • Large 6.78 inch 120Hz display

Reported trade-offs4

  • Severe system lag and scrolling issues
  • Poor display quality for video playback
  • Weak WiFi and Bluetooth signal strength
  • High amount of bloatware and ads

Why we say that

Owners report that the phone is very laggy, making even simple activities like scrolling through Facebook a frustrating experience. While the battery life is a standout feature, the overall performance is hindered by poor software optimization and high levels of bloatware and ads. The display quality is disappointing, with users noting that even 480p video content looks worse than on older devices.

Connectivity is another concern, as owners frequently report weak WiFi and Bluetooth signal strength. The lack of an auto-brightness sensor further complicates daily use. Despite the inclusion of Android 15, the lack of features like split-screen and the presence of persistent system stuttering make this phone difficult to recommend for anyone expecting a smooth experience.

The build quality includes IP65 and military certification, but this does not compensate for the significant performance issues reported by real users.
