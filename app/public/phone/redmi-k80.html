Is the Redmi K80 worth buying?

What is good

  • Elite Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 performance
  • Massive 6550 mAh battery capacity
  • Very bright 2K flat OLED display
  • Premium IP68 and IP69 water resistance

What is not

  • Notification delays and missed calls
  • Significant video recording jitter
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Difficult software setup with CN ROM

Buy it if You are an advanced user who prioritizes raw gaming performance and battery life over camera quality and software stability.

Skip it if You need a reliable phone for daily communication, professional photography, or a device that works perfectly out of the box.

Why we say that

While the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset delivers elite speed and the 6550 mAh battery provides massive capacity, the user experience is hampered by the HyperOS CN ROM. Owners frequently report notification delays and missed calls on apps like WhatsApp, which makes it unreliable for daily communication. The camera system is another weak point, with users noting average main sensor quality, poor ultra-wide performance, and significant video jittering.

Because these are gray-import units built for China, you will not receive a manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. You must rely on shop-provided coverage and pay for any repairs yourself. The software is filled with bloatware, and the lack of native Google and Bangla support makes the setup process difficult for most users.

What is the Redmi K80 price in Bangladesh?

Redmi K80 costs ৳45,999 to ৳48,999 in Bangladesh on unofficial listings, in stock at 2 shops we track as of 9 Sep 2026.

Does the Redmi K80 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit is a gray import built for the Chinese market. You must rely on the specific shop for any coverage, and all repairs will be paid for by you.

Is the Redmi K80 good for gaming?

The phone features the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset, which provides flagship speed and is great for emulation. While performance is generally high, some owners have reported stability issues when playing PUBG.

How is the battery life on this device?

The phone includes a huge 6550 mAh battery that owners rate highly. However, the high-resolution 2K screen does increase power drain. You can charge the device quickly using the 90W charger, which reaches 50 percent in under 20 minutes.

Will I face issues with the software on this phone?

Yes, the Chinese ROM lacks Google services and Bangla language support. Owners report significant issues including notification delays and missed calls on apps like WhatsApp. The software also contains a high amount of bloatware and ads.
