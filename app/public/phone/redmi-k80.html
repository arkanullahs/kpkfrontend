What works well4

  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 delivers top tier performance
  • 6550 mAh battery lasts all day with fast 90W charge
  • 6.67 inch OLED panel offers bright 2K image at 120Hz
  • Metal glass body with IP68/69 rating feels premium

Reported trade-offs4

  • Heavy bloatware and ads slow the system
  • Notifications often arrive late or are killed
  • Camera over processes skin and ultra wide is weak
  • No official Xiaomi warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset delivers top tier performance for gaming and heavy multitasking. The 6.67 inch OLED panel shines with 2K resolution, 120Hz refresh and a peak brightness of 3200 nits, making it easy to view outdoors. The 6550 mAh cell lasts a full day of intensive use and the 90W charger refills it quickly.

The metal glass body feels solid and carries an IP68/69 rating. However the software is loaded with bloatware, ads and notification delays, and the camera applies heavy skin smoothing and struggles with the ultra‑wide lens. The phone arrives as a gray import, so only the shop’s limited warranty applies and you must verify the ROM version before buying.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 6550 mAh cell gives industry leading endurance, easily covering a full day of heavy use and still leaving reserve for another few hours. The 90W charger refills the battery from zero to full in about an hour.

Is the phone good for gaming and emulation?

Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 places the K80 in the top tier for performance, and owners report excellent results in gaming and emulation. Frame rates stay high and the 120 Hz OLED display keeps motion smooth.

How is the camera performance in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor is average and adds noticeable skin smoothing, while the ultra wide lens is weak and adds distortion. Low light photos are usable but lack detail and show the over processing effect.

Will I get an official warranty and Google services?

All units are gray imports, so Xiaomi does not provide a local warranty. The shop you buy from may offer its own limited coverage, but repairs are paid. Many listings are the China ROM without Google services, so check the build before purchasing.
