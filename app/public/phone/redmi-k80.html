Is the Redmi K80 worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6550 mAh battery capacity
  • Top-tier gaming and performance
  • Highly praised flat 2K OLED display
  • Powerful Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset

What is not

  • Heavy bloatware and ad-heavy system
  • Delayed notifications and background app killing
  • 5G connectivity issues on non-Chinese carriers
  • Lacks native Google and Bangla support

Buy it if Buy this phone only if you are an advanced user who can handle complex software workarounds and prioritize raw gaming performance over system stability.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you need a reliable device with official warranty, native Bangla support, and stable notifications for daily communication.

Why we say that

While the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset delivers top-tier performance and gaming, owners report significant frustration with the software experience. You will face aggressive bloatware, heavy ads, and persistent notification bugs that require painful trial and error to manage. The device often kills background apps, leading to delayed calls and messages.

Connectivity is another major hurdle, as many users report 5G signal failure on non-Chinese networks. Because these are gray-import units, you do not get a brand warranty, only a shop warranty. You must also confirm the BTRC status with your seller before spending your money.

While the 6550 mAh battery provides industry-leading endurance, the software limitations and lack of native Google and Bangla support make this a risky purchase for anyone who needs a reliable daily driver.

Does the Redmi K80 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, the Redmi K80 is only available as a gray-import unit in Bangladesh. You will not receive a brand warranty, only a shop warranty. You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before you make your purchase.

How is the battery life on the Redmi K80?

The battery life is excellent, with owners rating it a 9.2 out of 10. The massive 6550 mAh capacity provides industry-leading endurance, making it one of the strongest features of the device for users who need long-lasting power.

Is the Redmi K80 good for gaming?

Yes, the phone is highly capable for gaming. It features the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset, which provides top-tier gaming and emulation performance. Owners have rated the gaming performance very highly at 9.3 out of 10.

Will I face problems with the software on this phone?

Yes, you will likely face significant software issues. The device runs a China ROM that lacks native Google and Bangla support. Owners report aggressive bloatware, heavy ads, and persistent bugs that cause delayed notifications and background app killing.
