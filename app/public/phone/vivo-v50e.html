Is the Vivo V50e worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz AMOLED display with 1800‑nit peak
  • 90W fast charging fills battery quickly
  • Solid gray‑import build quality
  • 50MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • No telephoto lens option
  • Battery life only average
  • Performance rating low
  • Shop warranty instead of brand

Buy it if Buy it if you want a bright screen and fast charging at a mid‑range price.

Skip it if Skip it if you need strong gaming performance or a reliable brand warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.77‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 1800 nits, making everyday use and media consumption pleasant. Charging at 90 W refills the 5600 mAh cell quickly, but the battery only provides average endurance, lasting roughly a day with moderate use. The Dimensity 7300 processor paired with 6‑8 GB RAM scores low in performance and gaming tests, so heavy games feel laggy.

Build quality feels solid, though the phone arrives as gray‑import stock with only a shop warranty, so you must confirm IMEI and BTRC status. The 50 MP main camera offers good detail in daylight thanks to OIS, but the absence of a telephoto lens and modest low‑light results limit versatility. Software runs Android 15 with Funtouch 15, bringing medium bloat and two future OS updates for this budget tier.
