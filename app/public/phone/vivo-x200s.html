Vivo X200s Is it worth buying?

Why we say that

Its Dimensity 9400+ chipset and up to 16 GB RAM deliver smooth gaming and multitasking, and the 6.67‑inch AMOLED screen stays bright even in sunlight thanks to a 5000‑nit peak. The 6200 mAh cell lets most users get through a full day without charging, while 90 W fast charging refills it quickly enough for daily use. The 50 MP main camera with OIS produces sharp portraits and decent telephoto shots, though the software adds some bloat and notification delays on the Chinese ROM.

The phone is water‑resistant and includes an IR blaster, but the USB port is limited to USB 2.0 speeds and the lack of an official warranty means repairs are paid out of pocket. Overall it feels like a premium device that trades official support for lower price.
