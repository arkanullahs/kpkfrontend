Is the Vivo X200s worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6200 mAh battery with industry-leading endurance
  • Powerful Dimensity 9400+ chipset for stable gaming
  • Exceptional portraits from Zeiss-tuned optics
  • Bright 5000 nits peak brightness display

What is not

  • Outdated USB 2.0 port
  • Notification delays due to Chinese ROM
  • Bloatware and advertisements in the user interface
  • No official brand warranty available

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize raw performance and battery endurance above all else and are comfortable manually tweaking software settings.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you need a reliable official warranty or require a clean software experience without notification issues.

Why we say that

Owners rate the performance at 9.7, highlighting the Dimensity 9400+ chipset as a true beast for sustained tasks. However, living with the device requires patience because the Chinese ROM causes notification delays on apps like Facebook Messenger and WhatsApp. You will also encounter bloatware and Chinese applications that display advertisements.

While the Zeiss-tuned camera system produces sharp, consistent imagery, the hardware is held back by an outdated USB 2.0 port. Since only unofficial gray-import stock is available in Bangladesh, you must rely on shop warranties rather than brand support. Confirming the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller is essential before spending your money on this device.
