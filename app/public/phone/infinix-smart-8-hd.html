Is the Infinix Smart 8 HD worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.6 inch IPS LCD screen
  • 90Hz refresh rate
  • 5000 mAh battery capacity
  • headphone jack and FM radio

What is not

  • short battery life in real use
  • poor camera quality especially low light
  • no further software updates expected
  • heavy preinstalled apps and ads

Buy it if Buy it if you only need basic apps, a large display and can accept slow updates.

Skip it if Skip it if you need good photography, gaming performance or long term software support.

Why we say that

The 6.6 inch IPS LCD with 90Hz refresh looks smooth for scrolling and video, yet the display brightness peaks at only 500 nits. Battery capacity is 5000 mAh but owners complain it runs out quickly, so you will likely charge before the day ends. The 13 MP rear camera and 8 MP selfie lens are marketed as decent, but real‑world photos are blurry and noisy, especially in low light.

Performance sits in the mid range thanks to the Unisoc T606 and 3 to 4 GB RAM, but gaming scores are low and the device slows over time. Software runs XOS 13 on Android 13, but updates are rare, bloatware is heavy and ads appear frequently. Expect the phone to work for basic tasks, but not for heavy use or long term ownership.

Will the Infinix Smart 8 HD get future Android updates?

The phone launched in 2023 and is already three years old. Owners report that it will receive at most one or two more OS updates, and the update speed is slow. Expect little support after that.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

It has a 5000 mAh cell, but users say the battery drains quickly. In everyday use you may need to charge before the day ends, especially if you browse or stream video.

Is the camera usable in low light conditions?

The main sensor is 13 MP with f/1.8 aperture, but owners describe the pictures as poor and the camera as a potato in low light. Expect blurry and noisy results without flash.

Can I play games smoothly on this device?

The Unisoc T606 chipset with 3 to 4 GB RAM gives mid range performance. Review scores show gaming at 2.5 out of 10, so demanding games will lag and frame rates will be low.
