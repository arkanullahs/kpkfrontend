OPPO Reno12 5G Is it worth buying?

Expect a solid daily phone with a bright screen and fast charging, but don’t count on flagship‑level gaming or night photography.

What works well4

  • 120Hz AMOLED display with 1200‑nit peak brightness
  • 80W charging fills 5000 mAh battery in under 47 minutes
  • Metallic All Round Armor build feels very durable
  • Battery often lasts one and a half to two days

Reported trade-offs4

  • Dimensity 7300 lags behind rivals in gaming
  • Low‑light photos lack detail and zoom is weak
  • ColorOS ships with noticeable bloatware
  • Gray imports have no official OPPO warranty

Why we say that

The 6.7" AMOLED panel offers 120Hz refresh and 1200‑nit peak brightness, making most content look vivid. The 5000 mAh battery often lasts one and a half to two days, and the 80W charger fills it in under 47 minutes. Build quality is high with a metallic All Round Armor finish that feels very durable.

Performance is adequate for everyday tasks, yet the Dimensity 7300 processor shows its limits in demanding games and the device heats little. Camera performance is mixed; the 50 MP main sensor works in daylight but struggles in low light and zoom, and video stabilization is weak. Software runs on ColorOS 14.1 with medium bloatware and promises up to three major Android upgrades.
