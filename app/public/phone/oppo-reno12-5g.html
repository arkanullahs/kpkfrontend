Is the OPPO Reno12 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 5000 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • 120 Hz AMOLED screen is bright and colorful
  • 80 W charger fills the battery in under 47 minutes
  • Durable metallic All Round Armor finish

What is not

  • Dimensity 7300 feels under powered for its price
  • Low light photos are blurry and lack detail
  • Video stabilization is weak when walking
  • Bloatware and ads are noticeable

Buy it if If you want a solid daily driver with fast charging and a sturdy build and can accept average low light photos, this phone fits you.

Skip it if If you need top tier gaming performance, excellent night photography, or a clean software experience, look elsewhere.

Why we say that

Its 5000 mAh cell often reaches one and a half to two days of use before needing a charge. The 6.7 inch AMOLED panel is bright and colorful, though the 120 Hz refresh sometimes does not scale smoothly. Charging is very fast, reaching full in under 47 minutes with the 80 W charger.

Build quality feels premium thanks to the metallic All Round Armor finish. Performance feels adequate for daily tasks but the Dimensity 7300 falls short against rivals in the same price range. Camera quality is decent in daylight but night shots are blurry and lack definition, and video stabilization does not compensate for movement.

Software includes useful AI tools but also noticeable bloatware. Overall it feels overpriced for the compromises.
