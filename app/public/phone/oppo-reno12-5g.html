Is the OPPO Reno12 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz AMOLED display with 1200‑nit peak brightness
  • 5000 mAh battery praised for all‑day endurance
  • Dimensity 7300 provides smooth daily performance
  • 12 GB RAM with up to 512 GB storage options

What is not

  • Poor low‑light camera detail and artificial AI looks
  • Missing infrared blaster despite specifications
  • Occasional GPS and Bluetooth failures reported

Buy it if Buy it if you want a bright high‑refresh screen, strong battery life and plenty of storage at a mid‑range price.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable low‑light photography or a guaranteed infrared remote function.

Why we say that

Owners love the sleek, lightweight build and the smooth everyday performance from the Dimensity 7300 chipset. The 12 GB RAM and up to 512 GB storage keep multitasking painless. However, the camera falls short in dim environments and the AI tweaks look unnatural.

Some users also experience occasional GPS or Bluetooth drops. The phone does include an IR blaster on paper, but many owners report it missing, adding to the disappointment.
