Is the Nokia 225 4G 2024 worth buying?

What is good

  • Excellent standby and talk time
  • Durable Nokia plastic build
  • Very easy to use
  • No bloatware or ads

What is not

  • Dim and low resolution display
  • No app capability
  • Poor camera quality

Buy it if You need a durable secondary phone for basic calling and long battery life.

Skip it if You want to use apps, play games, or take photos.

Why we say that

Owners appreciate the classic durable Nokia plastic build and the ease of use. You will find the battery life impressive with excellent standby and talk time. However, the screen is a major weak point.

Users complain that the 2.4 inch TFT display is small, dim, and has low resolution. Performance is limited to basic calling only. There is no app capability, so you cannot use modern social media or tools.

The camera is virtually non existent and rated very poorly by owners. It serves its purpose as a simple communication device, but it offers nothing for entertainment or photography.
