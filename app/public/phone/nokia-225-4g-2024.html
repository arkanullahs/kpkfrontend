Is the Nokia 225 4G 2024 worth buying?

What is good

  • Excellent standby and talk time
  • Durable classic Nokia plastic build
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • Very low official price

What is not

  • Tiny dim low resolution screen
  • No camera functionality
  • Cannot run apps or games
  • Very weak performance

Buy it if Buy it if you only need a cheap device for calls, texts and FM radio.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect a smartphone with apps, camera or decent gaming.

Why we say that

The 1450 mAh cell gives excellent standby and talk time, which owners rate highly. The device feels solid thanks to classic Nokia plastic, but the 2.4 inch TFT screen is tiny, dim and low resolution, making reading difficult. There is no camera at all, so you cannot capture photos or video.

The Unisoc T107 chipset and stockish Android / S30+ software limit the phone to basic calling and texting; it cannot run typical apps or games. Gaming and performance scores are very low, confirming that it is not a smartphone for multimedia. It does include a headphone jack and FM radio, which some users still enjoy.

Official warranty is available at the cheapest price of 6,100 taka, but future software updates are uncertain because the brand is in transition after the HMD split.
