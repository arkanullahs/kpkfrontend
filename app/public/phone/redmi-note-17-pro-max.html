What works well4

  • 6.83 inch AMOLED 120Hz display
  • 100W fast charging
  • 9210 mAh battery for long use
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs4

  • High ads in the software
  • Official warranty only on official units
  • Gray imports need paid repairs
  • Updates limited to 2‑4 Android versions

Why we say that

The 6.83 inch AMOLED panel runs at 120Hz and can reach a peak brightness of 3500 nits, so outdoor use is clear. Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 handles modern games smoothly and the 100W charger fills the 9210 mAh battery in minutes, giving a full day of heavy use. The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.5 aperture and OIS captures sharp photos in good light and decent detail in low light, while the 32 MP selfie camera is adequate for video calls.

The software runs Android 16 with HyperOS and shows a lot of ads, which many owners find annoying. Official warranty only covers the official unit, so the cheapest gray‑import listings have no manufacturer cover and any repair is paid. Updates are limited to two to four Android versions depending on the tier, so long‑term software support is modest.
