Redmi Note 17 Pro Max Is it worth buying?

If you need a big screen, fast chip and huge battery, the Redmi Note 17 Pro Max delivers that at a mid range price.

Why we say that

The 6.83 inch AMOLED panel runs at 120Hz and can reach a peak brightness of 3500 nits, so outdoor use feels clear. Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 keeps games smooth and apps responsive, though the system is loaded with ads that appear in many pre‑installed apps. The 9210 mAh cell gives two days of heavy use and the 100W charger fills it in under half an hour, but the phone feels heavy in the hand.

The 50MP main sensor with OIS captures sharp daylight shots, yet low light scenes still show noticeable noise. Software runs Android 16 with HyperOS and promises two to four OS upgrades depending on the model. Official warranty covers only units sold with the brand warranty; gray imports receive only paid repair service.
