Buying verdict

Motorola G77 Is it worth buying?

The Motorola G77 feels like a premium‑looking phone that can survive a day, but its outdated processor makes everyday use sluggish.

Choose it if
You want a large bright screen, solid daytime photos and can accept slower performance with no official warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need smooth gaming, fast charging, or a brand‑backed warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Bright 6.78‑inch AMOLED display
  • 5200 mAh battery lasting a full day
  • Premium build with IP64 and MIL‑STD‑810H
  • 108 MP sensor captures detailed daylight photos

Reported trade-offs

  • Dimensity 6400 chipset bottlenecks performance
  • 30W charging feels slow
  • Upscaled display resolution reduces sharpness
  • Low‑light camera noisy and blurry

Why we say that

The 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel shines with 120 Hz refresh and peaks at 5000 nits, though the resolution is upscaled, so sharpness is not native. Battery life is a strong point; the 5200 mAh cell easily lasts a full intensive day according to owners. Build quality is solid with IP64 rating and MIL‑STD‑810H durability, giving a premium feel.

The 108 MP main sensor captures detailed photos in daylight, but low‑light scenes suffer noticeable noise and motion blur. Gaming and heavy apps stumble because the Dimensity 6400 chipset cannot keep up, and the 30 W charger feels slow for a 2026 mid‑range device. Software runs near‑stock Android 16 with useful Moto gestures, but update support is limited to one to three versions.

Read full reasoning

The phone arrives as a gray import, so there is no official warranty and any repair costs are paid by the buyer.
