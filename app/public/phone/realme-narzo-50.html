Is the Realme Narzo 50 worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz refresh rate display
  • 33W fast charging
  • Includes a headphone jack

What is not

  • Poor build quality
  • Very short battery life
  • No official brand warranty
  • Lack of software updates

Buy it if You need a cheap phone with a 120Hz screen and a headphone jack.

Skip it if You want a durable phone with long battery life and official warranty.

Why we say that

Owners report serious build quality issues where volume buttons feel dampened and the phone hangs open by itself. The battery life is a major concern, with some users claiming it drains from 100% to 0% in just 4 hours. You will also face performance drops under heavy use and a display that can become non responsive under water.

The camera lacks EIS video recording and has limited features. Since this phone is 4 years old, it has a lack of updates and owners report that no updates are available. It is an unofficial import, meaning you get shop warranty instead of brand support.

You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying.
