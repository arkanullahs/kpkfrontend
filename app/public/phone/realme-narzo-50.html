Is the Realme Narzo 50 worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz refresh rate display
  • 33 W fast charging
  • 50 MP main camera sensor
  • Headphone jack included

What is not

  • Battery drains in four hours
  • Poor build quality and loose buttons
  • Limited camera features, no EIS
  • No official warranty or updates

Buy it if Buy it if you need a large, fast‑refresh screen and quick charging at a low price and can accept short battery life and no brand warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you rely on long battery endurance, official warranty support, or regular software updates.

Why we say that

The Realme Narzo 50 offers a 6.6 inch 120 Hz IPS LCD, a 50 MP main sensor and 33 W fast charging, but owners report poor build quality, a screen that can become unresponsive when wet, and limited camera features such as missing EIS video. Performance is acceptable at light use but drops under heavy multitasking, and the Helio G96 chipset shows its age after four years with no further OS updates expected. The phone arrives as a gray‑import with only a shop‑provided warranty, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

Software includes Android 11 with Realme UI 2.0 and medium‑high bloatware, which may affect the experience. Overall it works for basic tasks if you can tolerate short battery life and lack of official support.
