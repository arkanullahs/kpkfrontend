Is the Realme Narzo 50 worth buying?

What is good

  • Helio G96 chipset performance
  • 120Hz display refresh rate
  • 33W fast charging support
  • Includes a headphone jack

What is not

  • Extremely short battery life
  • Poor build quality and durability
  • Display issues and non-responsiveness
  • No remaining software update support

Buy it if You are looking for a very cheap secondary device and are comfortable managing a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty.

Skip it if You need a reliable daily driver that can handle heavy use, receive software updates, and maintain a solid physical build.

Why we say that

Living with this phone is difficult because users report that the battery can drain from 100 percent to 0 percent in just 4 hours. The build quality is a major concern, with owners noting that volume buttons feel dampened and the phone casing can hang open by itself. Performance is inconsistent, as the device tends to drop in speed under heavy use.

You should be aware that the display may become non-responsive if exposed to water. Since this model is now 4 years old, you should expect no remaining software update life. Owners have expressed frustration over the lack of available updates and the limited camera features, specifically noting that EIS video recording is not available.

Because only gray-import stock is currently available, you will receive a shop warranty rather than a brand warranty. You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before making any payment.
