Is the Honor Magic 8 worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 7000 mAh battery endurance
  • Stable gaming with Snapdragon 8 Elite
  • Very bright 6000 nits peak display
  • Top-tier eye protection and PWM

What is not

  • Slow USB 2.0 data transfer speeds
  • Messy software with notification issues
  • Thermal throttling in performance mode
  • Lack of eSIM support in some versions

Buy it if You prioritize raw battery endurance and gaming performance above all else and are comfortable managing an unofficial device.

Skip it if You require reliable notification delivery, fast data transfer speeds, or the peace of mind that comes with an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Owners consistently praise the massive 7000 mAh battery and the stable gaming performance provided by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset. However, the experience is marred by a messy software interface that suffers from notification delivery inconsistencies and an aesthetic that mimics iOS. While the display is bright and offers great eye protection, the build quality is criticized for the inclusion of a slow USB 2.0 port.

Users also report thermal throttling during intense gaming sessions, where performance drops significantly after five minutes. The camera system captures realistic stills and offers great zoom, yet some find the main sensor output insincere. Because this device is only available through unofficial channels, you must be prepared to rely on shop warranties rather than official brand support.

Does the Honor Magic 8 have a reliable battery for heavy users?

Yes, the phone features a massive 7000 mAh battery. Owners report that it offers massive endurance and often outlasts rival devices that have similar or even larger battery capacities.

How does the Honor Magic 8 perform during long gaming sessions?

The Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset provides very stable frames for gaming. However, owners report that the device suffers from thermal throttling in performance mode, where it puts out high numbers for about five minutes before dropping off.

Is there an official warranty available for this phone in Bangladesh?

No, the Honor Magic 8 is currently only available as unofficial gray-import stock. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, so you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

How long will the Honor Magic 8 receive software updates?

The device runs Android 16 and is promised up to 7 major OS updates. While the software is feature-rich, users should be aware that the current MagicOS 10 experience includes medium levels of bloat and ads.
