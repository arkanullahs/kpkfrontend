Why we say that

In real use the phone lasts well beyond a full day even with gaming and video streaming, and the 100 W charger refills it quickly. The Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset delivers flagship speed, making games run smoothly at high settings and apps feel instant. The 6.58 inch LTPO OLED panel offers 120 Hz refresh and very high peak brightness, and reviewers note comfortable eye care and no PWM flicker.

Camera results are modest; the 50 MP sensor struggles in low light and does not match the price tag. Build feels modern but the USB type C port is limited to USB 2.0 and the device lacks eSIM support. Because every unit is a gray import, there is no official Honor warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s limited warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status.

The Honor Magic 8 is an under-the-radar powerhouse that delivers outstanding battery life, top-tier gaming performance, and a comfortable flat screen. While its main camera hardware isn't industry-leading, significant algorithm improvements and clever software features make it a highly capable all-rounder.

Best for Gamers and power users wanting a flat screen and huge battery.

  1. TTechXReviews

    Honor Magic 8 Review: Why Is No One Talking About This? (opens YouTube)

    3K views · Dec 2025

    TechXReviews highly praised the Honor Magic 8 for its exceptional battery efficiency and robust gaming performance, noting that it effortlessly maintains high frame rates with excellent stability. The reviewer loved the display, pointing out its class-leading eye protection features. While they felt the physical main camera sensor was a bit underwhelming for a modern flagship, they were incredibly impressed by Honor's new image processing algorithms, which produce highly realistic, natural photos without aggressive digital sharpening. They concluded it is a highly competitive, overlooked option.

  2. VVia Tech

    Via Tech (opens YouTube)

    Via Tech found the Honor Magic 8 Standard Edition to be a remarkably well-rounded device with no obvious shortcomings. They praised its comfortable 6.58-inch flat screen, massive battery, and the inclusion of fast wireless charging. The reviewer was particularly impressed by the software, highlighting the versatile new physical AI button and the system's unique ability to seamlessly clone extensive data from both iOS and HarmonyOS devices. Ultimately, they highly recommend it for anyone who doesn't need absolute top-tier professional photography hardware.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 7,000 mAh cell gives the phone more than a full day of use for most people, and it still holds up when you add heavy gaming or video streaming, thanks to its efficient chipset.

Is the Honor Magic 8 good for heavy gaming?

Yes, the Snapdragon 8 Elite processor scores 9.8 for performance and 9.6 for gaming, delivering smooth frame rates and quick load times even in demanding titles, making it a solid gaming phone.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with a 1/1.56" size and f/1.9 aperture does not capture enough light, so low‑light photos are softer and lack detail, which many owners find disappointing for a flagship price.

Will I get an official Honor warranty in Bangladesh?

No, every unit is a gray import and carries only the shop’s limited warranty; there is no official Honor service support in Bangladesh, so you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
