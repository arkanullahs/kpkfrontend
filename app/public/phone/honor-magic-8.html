Is the Honor Magic 8 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000‑nit peak brightness for sunny days
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 with 12 GB RAM
  • 7000 mAh battery with 100 W fast charging
  • 50 MP main and selfie cameras with optical zoom

What is not

  • USB‑C 2.0 port only
  • Thermal throttling in performance mode
  • Inconsistent app notifications
  • No eSIM support in some versions

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize long battery life, a bright high‑refresh display and strong gaming performance over the latest USB speeds and flawless software.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable USB‑C 3.x data rates, stable notifications, or guaranteed eSIM compatibility.

Why we say that

Its 7000 mAh battery is rated 9.4 by owners and, paired with 100 W fast charging, gives massive endurance that often beats rivals with similar capacities. The 6.58‑inch LTPO OLED screen reaches a peak 6000‑nit brightness, making sunlight readability easy, and the 120 Hz refresh rate feels smooth. Powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and 12 GB RAM, it holds stable frames in games, though the chip throttles after about five minutes in performance mode.

The 50 MP main camera with OIS and 3× telephoto produces realistic photos, yet some users find the images a bit artificial, especially in low light. MagicOS 10 runs Android 16 with up to seven major updates, but the UI feels cluttered and notifications can be unreliable. The phone arrives as a gray‑import, so you only get a shop warranty and must confirm IMEI/BTRC status.

USB‑C is limited to 2.0, and certain versions lack eSIM support.

Via Tech found the Honor Magic 8 Standard Edition to be a remarkably well-rounded device with no obvious shortcomings. They praised its comfortable 6.58-inch flat screen, massive battery, and the inclusion of fast wireless charging. The reviewer was particularly impressed by the software, highlighting the versatile new physical AI button and the system's unique ability to seamlessly clone extensive data from both iOS and HarmonyOS devices. Ultimately, they highly recommend it for anyone who doesn't need absolute top-tier professional photography hardware.

TechXReviews · Honor Magic 8 Review: Why Is No One Talking About This? ↗

TechXReviews highly praised the Honor Magic 8 for its exceptional battery efficiency and robust gaming performance, noting that it effortlessly maintains high frame rates with excellent stability. The reviewer loved the display, pointing out its class-leading eye protection features. While they felt the physical main camera sensor was a bit underwhelming for a modern flagship, they were incredibly impressed by Honor's new image processing algorithms, which produce highly realistic, natural photos without aggressive digital sharpening. They concluded it is a highly competitive, overlooked option.
