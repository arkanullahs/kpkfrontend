Is the Vivo Y22s worth buying?

What is good

  • Reliable 5,000 mAh battery life
  • Includes a dedicated headphone jack
  • Features a built-in FM radio
  • Solid 6GB RAM and 128GB storage

What is not

  • Poor display and gaming performance
  • Low camera and video quality ratings
  • Limited remaining software update life
  • Outdated compared to newer models

Buy it if You prioritize having a headphone jack and FM radio on a budget and do not mind using an older device.

Skip it if You want a phone that performs well in games, takes high-quality videos, or receives current software updates.

Why we say that

Owners report significant dissatisfaction with the display quality and overall performance, which are rated quite low. While the 5,000 mAh battery provides decent longevity, the device falls short in gaming and video recording capabilities. You should be aware that this model is four years old, meaning it has reached the end of its software support cycle.

Because the newer Vivo Y27s is available for nearly the same price of 21,999 taka, most buyers will find that newer model to be a much better value. If you choose this device, expect a basic experience with medium levels of bloatware and ads in the Funtouch OS interface.
