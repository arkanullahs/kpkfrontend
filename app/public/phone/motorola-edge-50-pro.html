Is the Motorola Edge 50 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright and sharp 144Hz P-OLED display
  • High quality 50MP main and selfie cameras
  • Light and compact IP68 rated build
  • Fast 125W charging capability

What is not

  • Battery drains quickly during normal use
  • Unreliable WiFi connectivity issues
  • Only gray-import stock with shop warranty
  • Historically weak software update support

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a high-quality display and camera system and are comfortable managing potential battery and connectivity quirks.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you need reliable battery life, stable WiFi connectivity, or the security of an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Owners report that the battery drains quickly, with some describing the life as terrible even during normal use. You will enjoy a sharp 144Hz P-OLED screen and capable 50MP cameras, but the daily experience is marred by frustrating WiFi disconnections that occur every few minutes. While the phone is light, compact, and carries an IP68 rating, the software support remains a concern due to a history of weak updates.

You are currently limited to gray-import stock, meaning you must rely on shop warranties rather than brand support. Always confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your money. This device offers high-end hardware specifications, but the real-world reliability issues reported by current owners make it a risky purchase for anyone needing a dependable daily driver.
