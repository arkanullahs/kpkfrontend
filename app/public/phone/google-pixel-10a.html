Is the Google Pixel 10a worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 3000 nit OLED display
  • Strong 5100 mAh battery
  • Clean stock Android with seven year updates
  • 48 MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • 30 W charging feels slow
  • Tensor G4 ages under heavy load
  • Plastic back feels cheap
  • Manual brightness dimming outdoors

Buy it if Buy if you value a bright screen, long battery and long term software support.

Skip it if Skip if you need fast charging, top gaming performance or a premium metal build.

Why we say that

The 6.3 inch POLED panel can peak at 3000 nit, making outdoor use easy, though the bezels are thick and manual brightness may dim unexpectedly in sunlight. The 5100 mAh cell is praised for lasting a full day and scores 8.5 in owner ratings. The 48 MP main sensor with OIS captures sharp stills, but the ultrawide lacks autofocus and there is no telephoto lens.

The Tensor G4 chipset handles daily apps well, yet it throttles during heavy gaming, reflected in a six out of ten gaming rating. Charging at 30 W is an improvement but still feels slow compared with newer mid range phones. Software stays pure Android 16 and promises seven years of updates.

Official warranty is only on the ৳67,999 unit; cheaper gray imports have no manufacturer cover.
