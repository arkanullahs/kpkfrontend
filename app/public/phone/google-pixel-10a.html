Google Pixel 10a Is it worth buying?

If you want a solid daily driver with a bright screen and good camera but can live without fast charging or a strong gaming performance, the Pixel 10a fits the bill.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value a bright screen, solid camera and long software support and don’t need fast charging or high‑end gaming performance.
Consider alternatives if
You need a fast‑charging phone, strong gaming hardware, or an official manufacturer warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Bright 120 Hz OLED display
  • 48 MP main camera with good processing
  • Clean stock Android with long updates
  • Large 5100 mAh battery lasting a day

Reported trade-offs4

  • 30 W charging feels slow
  • Tensor G4 struggles with heavy gaming
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Thick bezels and manual brightness dimming

Why we say that

The 6.3‑inch 120 Hz OLED can hit 3000‑nit peaks, making outdoor use easy, and the 48 MP main sensor delivers best‑in‑class stills for the price. Battery endurance is reliable, lasting a full day for most users, though the 30 W wired and 10 W wireless charging feel sluggish compared with newer mid‑range rivals. The Tensor G4 handles everyday apps smoothly, but heavy gaming exposes its limits and performance scores dip.

Software is clean stock Android 16 with up to seven major OS updates, giving long‑term peace of mind. Build quality is sturdy and IP68 rated, yet the plastic back, thick bezels, manual brightness dimming and lack of magnetic accessories are noticeable drawbacks. Overall, it feels like a modest upgrade over the Pixel 9a rather than a fresh step forward.
