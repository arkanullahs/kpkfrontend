Buying verdict

Redmi Turbo 5 Max Is it worth buying?

The Redmi Turbo 5 Max is a massive device built for China that brings significant compromises for users in Bangladesh.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
Buy this phone only if you are an advanced user who can manage China ROM limitations and prioritize extreme battery life over camera quality and network stability.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable notifications, a functional camera for photography, or a device with a standard manufacturer warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Exceptional 9000 mAh battery life
  • Bright 3500 nit AMOLED display
  • Flagship level Dimensity 9500s performance
  • Solid premium metal frame build

Reported trade-offs4

  • Significant notification delays on China ROM
  • Poor camera sensor tuning and ultrawide
  • Incompatible 5G bands outside China
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

While the 9000 mAh battery is exceptional and easily lasts 1.5 to 2 days, the software experience is hindered by the China ROM. Owners frequently report notification delays on social media apps, which many consider a deal breaker. The phone is physically bulky despite its premium metal frame and IP68/69 rating.

You will face challenges with 5G band incompatibility outside of China, and the camera performance is mediocre with a poor ultrawide sensor and no telephoto lens. Because these are gray-import units, there is no manufacturer warranty, and you must rely solely on the shop for any repairs. The lack of Google services and Bangla language support on the China ROM makes this a difficult device to use for the average buyer.

Does the Redmi Turbo 5 Max come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import built for the Chinese market. You must rely on the specific shop for any repair promises, and all repairs will be paid services.

How does the battery perform during daily use?

The battery performance is exceptional due to the 9000 mAh capacity. Owners report that the phone easily lasts between 1.5 and 2 days on a single charge. It is the highest-rated feature of the device according to user feedback.

Can I use this phone for heavy gaming?

Yes, the Dimensity 9500s chipset provides flagship-level performance that handles heavy gaming with minimal throttling. While the device may get warm during intense sessions, the heat remains manageable with cooling.

Are there any issues with the software or notifications?

Yes, the China ROM causes significant notification delays for social media apps, which many users find to be a major deal breaker. Additionally, the software lacks Google services and Bangla language support, making it difficult to use for many local buyers.
