Redmi Turbo 5 Max Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day without charging, the Redmi Turbo 5 Max delivers that.

What works well4

  • 9000 mAh battery with 100 W fast charge
  • 6.83‑inch AMOLED panel at 120 Hz
  • Dimensity 9500s chipset gives strong performance
  • IR blaster for remote control

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Camera lacks sharpness and low‑light detail
  • Notification delays and heavy bloatware on HyperOS
  • Phone can get hot under sustained load

Why we say that

The 9000 mAh battery is rated as industry‑leading and owners give it a 9.8 rating, so most heavy users can expect the phone to stay on for well over a day without needing to recharge. Fast 100 W charging restores it quickly. The Dimensity 9500s chipset provides strong performance and a smooth 6.83‑inch AMOLED display at 120 Hz, but the Chinese HyperOS ROM suffers from notification delays, heavy pre‑installed bloatware and limited update cadence.

The 50 MP main camera has decent hardware but owners report mediocre processing and lack of sharpness, especially in low light. Under sustained heavy load the device can warm up to about 50 °C despite a vapor chamber. There is no official manufacturer warranty; only the shop’s own warranty applies and repairs are paid.

How does the phone perform in gaming and does it overheat?

Is the camera good for low‑light photography?
