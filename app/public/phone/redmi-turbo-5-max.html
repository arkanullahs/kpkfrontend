Redmi Turbo 5 Max Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day without charging, the Redmi Turbo 5 Max delivers that with its 9000 mAh battery.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value massive battery life and fast charging more than camera perfection and official support.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable Google apps, low light photography, or a brand warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 9000 mAh battery lasts many days
  • 100 W charger refills quickly
  • 6.83 inch AMOLED panel with 120 Hz smoothness
  • Dimensity 9500s gives strong performance

Reported trade-offs4

  • Chinese ROM lacks Google services
  • Camera produces soft images
  • Phone gets hot under heavy load
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

The massive cell is paired with a 100 W charger that can refill the huge pack in a few minutes. The 6.83 inch AMOLED screen runs at 120 Hz and shows bright colors, while the Dimensity 9500s and 12 GB RAM give strong performance for most apps and games. Owners love the endurance but complain about the Chinese ROM that lacks Google services, shows notification delays and carries a lot of pre installed bloatware.

The 50 MP camera is decent on paper but produces soft images and struggles in low light. The phone also warms up under sustained heavy load and there is no official manufacturer warranty, only the shop's limited promise.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 9000 mAh cell is rated as industry leading, owners report it can easily cover a full day of heavy use and often stretch to two days without needing a charge, especially with the 100 W charger handy.

Will the phone handle demanding games smoothly?

The Dimensity 9500s paired with 12 GB RAM gives high performance scores and most games run at high settings, but some titles show occasional lag because the Chinese ROM is not fully optimized for every game.

Is the camera good for low light photos?

The 50 MP main sensor has a large 1/1.95" size and f/1.5 aperture, yet owners say the image processing is mediocre, resulting in soft details and lack of sharpness in low light scenes.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

All units are gray import Chinese ROMs, so there is no official Xiaomi warranty in Bangladesh; the shop provides its own limited warranty and any repairs cost extra. Software updates are limited to two to four major Android releases and include a lot of pre installed bloatware.
