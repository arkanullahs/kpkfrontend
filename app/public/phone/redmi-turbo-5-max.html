Redmi Turbo 5 Max Is it worth buying?

The Redmi Turbo 5 Max is a massive device built for China that brings significant compromises for users in Bangladesh.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You are a power user who prioritizes battery life and gaming performance above all else and can manage the limitations of a China ROM.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable phone with official warranty, stable notifications, and a high-quality camera for daily photography.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Exceptional 9000 mAh battery life
  • Powerful Dimensity 9500s gaming performance
  • Bright 3500 nit AMOLED display
  • Solid IP68 and IP69 build quality

Reported trade-offs4

  • Significant notification delays on China ROM
  • Mediocre camera sensor tuning
  • Inconsistent 5G network support
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

While the 9000 mAh battery is exceptional and easily lasts up to two days, the software experience is hindered by the China ROM. Owners frequently report frustrating notification delays on social media apps, which many consider a deal breaker. The device is physically bulky and the camera performance is mediocre, lacking sharpness and a telephoto lens.

Because these are gray-import units, you get no manufacturer warranty, and you must rely on whatever limited support the shop provides. You will also face challenges with 5G band incompatibility outside of China and potential issues with Google services or Bangla language support. It is a powerful machine for gaming, but the daily software experience and lack of local support make it a difficult recommendation for the average user.

Does the Redmi Turbo 5 Max come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import built for the Chinese market. You must rely solely on the repair promises made by the specific shop where you purchase the device.

How is the battery performance of this phone?

The battery performance is exceptional due to the massive 9000 mAh capacity. Owners report that the phone easily lasts between 1.5 and 2 days on a single charge. It supports 100W charging, though it may get warm during the process.

Can I use 5G networks in Bangladesh with this device?

You may face significant issues with 5G connectivity. There are many reports from owners stating that the phone lacks 5G support when used outside of China. You should confirm the network compatibility before making a purchase.

Is this phone suitable for photography?

The camera performance is considered mediocre. Owners complain about a lack of sharpness, poor ultrawide results, and the absence of a telephoto lens. Additionally, the front camera is limited to 1080p 60fps, which may disappoint those expecting 4K video.
