Is the Realme C85 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 7000 mAh battery lasts two days
  • 6.8‑inch AMOLED 120 Hz display
  • IP69‑rated plastic body
  • 45 W fast charging

What is not

  • Snapdragon 685 is obsolete for the price
  • eMMC storage causes noticeable lag
  • Low‑light photos are grainy and soft
  • No 3.5 mm headphone jack

Buy it if Buy it if you value long battery life and a bright screen over raw speed.

Skip it if Skip it if you need smooth gaming or high‑quality night photos.

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh battery easily lasts 1.5 to 2 days for most users. The 6.8‑inch AMOLED panel is bright and runs at 120 Hz, but reviewers question its sharpness. The plastic body carries an IP69 rating and can survive drops, yet it feels cheap.

The Snapdragon 685 chipset is dated and, together with eMMC storage, causes lag and slow app launches. Daylight photos from the 50 MP camera are okay, but low‑light shots turn grainy and soft. There is no 3.5 mm headphone jack and the Realme UI includes medium‑high bloatware.

Owners feel the phone is overpriced for the performance it offers. Overall it suits those who value battery life and a vivid screen more than gaming speed or night photography.
