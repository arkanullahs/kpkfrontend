Is the Samsung Galaxy S25 Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz LTPO OLED display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite performance
  • Long‑lasting 4900 mAh battery
  • Up to seven Android upgrades

What is not

  • No official warranty in Bangladesh
  • 45W charging feels slow and no charger included
  • Ultrasonic fingerprint sometimes fails
  • Ultrawide camera lacks autofocus

Buy it if Buy it if you want flagship speed, a great screen and long battery and can accept gray‑import warranty limits.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable fingerprint unlocking, fast charging or the latest camera hardware.

Why we say that

The 6.7‑inch 120Hz LTPO OLED panel shines with 2600‑nit peaks and smooth motion. Snapdragon 8 Elite with 12GB RAM delivers top‑tier performance and gaming scores above nine, while the 4900 mAh battery often outlasts a day of heavy use. Owners praise the clean One UI experience and seven years of Android updates.

The main 50MP camera produces solid photos but feels dated and the ultrawide still lacks autofocus. The ultrasonic fingerprint sensor can be finicky, and 45W charging is slower than expected with no charger in the box. Gray‑import status means any repair costs are out‑of‑pocket.

The newer S26 Plus sells for a similar price, so consider that if you want the latest hardware.

GSMArena Official · Samsung Galaxy S25 Plus full review ↗

GSMArena considers the Galaxy S25 Plus a solid, iterative flagship that benefits greatly from its new Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset, delivering excellent battery life and snappy performance. However, they note that Samsung held back on hardware upgrades this year. The design is only slightly thinner, it misses out on the Ultra's anti-reflective screen glass, and the aging camera hardware is noticeably beginning to trail behind competitors in pure sharpness.

Just Josh · Samsung Galaxy S25 Plus: Long Term Review ↗

Just Josh found the S25 Plus to be a highly polished and premium device that is impressively lightweight for its size, but admitted it felt like an unnecessary upgrade from his two-year-old phone. While he appreciated the brighter display, improved fingerprint reader, and better battery efficiency, he was frustrated by the remarkably slippery screen coating and Samsung's persistent, un-installable duplicate apps. Ultimately, he recommends it mostly for those upgrading from much older devices.

Tech Spurt · Samsung Galaxy S25 Plus Review | A Cheaper Ultra Alternative? ↗

Tech Spurt praised the massive performance boost and stable cooling capabilities brought by the new processor, particularly for users upgrading from older Exynos models. He was highly impressed by the all-day battery life and the vibrant, eye-searingly bright display. On the downside, he criticized the recycled camera hardware that struggles with low-light video, the lack of proper display dimming for sensitive eyes, and the messy AI features cluttered throughout the software.

Lim Reviews · Samsung Galaxy S25 Plus Review: MASSIVE Power Upgrade! 🔥 ↗

Lim Reviews highlighted the massive power upgrade and incredible efficiency of the new Snapdragon processor, noting it provides a night-and-day difference from the previous generation's chip in his region. He found the battery life to be fantastic and enjoyed the fluid refresh of the new software interface. While he felt the photos remained color-accurate and pleasing, he strongly criticized the phone's tinny, below-average stereo speakers and wished Samsung had finally included larger camera sensors.

How many Android updates will the Galaxy S25 Plus receive?

Samsung promises up to seven major Android upgrades for its flagships. The S25 Plus will start with Android 15 and can expect yearly updates for several years, keeping the software fresh and secure.

Is the battery life good enough for a full day of heavy use?

The 4900 mAh battery is rated higher than the S24+ and many owners describe the endurance as mind‑blowing. In real‑world tests it comfortably lasts a day of gaming, streaming and browsing.

How does the camera perform in low‑light situations?

The 50MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures decent low‑light shots, but reviewers note the hardware feels stagnant since 2022. The ultrawide lacks autofocus, which can hurt detail in dim environments.
