Why we say that

The 6.7 inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X panel runs at 120Hz and can reach 2600 nit peak, making every visual pop. Snapdragon 8 Elite and 12GB RAM deliver top‑tier speed for gaming and multitasking, and the 4900 mAh battery is praised as mind‑blowing, easily lasting a full day of mixed use. Build quality feels refined and lightweight, and the phone will receive up to seven major Android upgrades with a clean One UI experience.

However, charging is limited to 45W without a charger in the box, the ultrasonic fingerprint sometimes fails, and the ultrawide camera still lacks autofocus. The newer S26 Plus sells for a similar price and usually offers a better overall package.

The Samsung Galaxy S25 Plus delivers a massive leap in performance and battery life thanks to its new processor, but its recycled camera hardware and lack of an anti-reflective screen make it a very minor update overall.

Best for Users upgrading from a three- or four-year-old phone

  1. GGSMArena Official

    Samsung Galaxy S25 Plus full review (opens YouTube)

    420K views · Feb 2025

    GSMArena considers the Galaxy S25 Plus a solid, iterative flagship that benefits greatly from its new Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset, delivering excellent battery life and snappy performance. However, they note that Samsung held back on hardware upgrades this year. The design is only slightly thinner, it misses out on the Ultra's anti-reflective screen glass, and the aging camera hardware is noticeably beginning to trail behind competitors in pure sharpness.

  2. JJust Josh

    Samsung Galaxy S25 Plus: Long Term Review (opens YouTube)

    305K views · Mar 2025

    Just Josh found the S25 Plus to be a highly polished and premium device that is impressively lightweight for its size, but admitted it felt like an unnecessary upgrade from his two-year-old phone. While he appreciated the brighter display, improved fingerprint reader, and better battery efficiency, he was frustrated by the remarkably slippery screen coating and Samsung's persistent, un-installable duplicate apps. Ultimately, he recommends it mostly for those upgrading from much older devices.

  3. LLim Reviews

    Samsung Galaxy S25 Plus Review: MASSIVE Power Upgrade! 🔥 (opens YouTube)

    168K views · Jan 2025

    Lim Reviews highlighted the massive power upgrade and incredible efficiency of the new Snapdragon processor, noting it provides a night-and-day difference from the previous generation's chip in his region. He found the battery life to be fantastic and enjoyed the fluid refresh of the new software interface. While he felt the photos remained color-accurate and pleasing, he strongly criticized the phone's tinny, below-average stereo speakers and wished Samsung had finally included larger camera sensors.

The 4900 mAh battery is rated 8.6 by owners and many call its endurance mind‑blowing. It comfortably powers a full day of browsing, video, and moderate gaming, though heavy gaming will reduce the runtime.

Is the Galaxy S25 Plus good for mobile gaming?

Yes, the Snapdragon 8 Elite paired with 12GB RAM scores 9.6 for performance and 9.2 for gaming. The 120Hz display and well‑managed heat let you play demanding titles smoothly.

Does the camera work well in low‑light situations?

The 50MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures decent low‑light shots, but reviewers note the hardware feels outdated since 2022. The ultrawide lens still lacks autofocus, limiting its usefulness in dim environments.
