Honor 200 Is it worth buying?

If you can live without an official Bangladeshi warranty and accept possible software hiccups, the Honor 200 offers a flagship‑class display and fast charging at a gray‑import price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if

What works well4

  • 4000‑nit OLED display with 120 Hz refresh
  • 100 W fast charging for rapid top‑ups
  • 50 MP main camera with optical image stabilization
  • 12 GB RAM and up to 512 GB storage

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official warranty in Bangladesh
  • China ROM may lack Google services
  • Video recording lags severely
  • Software bugs cause stutters and delayed notifications

Why we say that

The 6.7‑inch OLED panel reaches a peak brightness of 4000 nits and runs at a smooth 120 Hz, making everyday use feel premium. Battery life is decent thanks to a 5200 mAh cell, but you will need to tweak settings for the best endurance and rely on the 100 W charger for quick top‑ups. The Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 with 12 GB RAM handles most apps well, yet random stutters appear during gaming or heavy multitasking due to software bugs.

The 50 MP main camera with OIS delivers sharp photos and good portrait shots, but video recording suffers severe lag. The phone arrives as a China ROM unit, so Google services may be missing and need extra steps to install. After‑sales support is limited, with many service centers refusing to service Honor devices.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5200 mAh battery provides good endurance for regular use, but owners report needing to adjust power settings for optimal life. Fast charging at 100 W restores the charge quickly when needed.

Is the Honor 200 suitable for mobile gaming?

The Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 processor and 12 GB RAM give decent performance, and gaming scores are around 6.8. However, software bugs can cause random stutters, so the experience is not always smooth.

Will the phone have Google services and receive updates?

The phone sold here is a China ROM unit, so Google Mobile Services are not pre‑installed and must be added manually. Global versions include Google, and the device is expected to receive three to four major Android updates, though support is slower than on flagship models.
