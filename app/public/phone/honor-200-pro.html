Honor 200 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Honor 200 Pro gives you a dazzling 4000‑nit OLED display and 100 W charging at a gray‑import price of ৳52,000.

Choose it if

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • 6.78‑inch OLED 120 Hz with 4000‑nit peak
  • Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 with 12 GB RAM
  • 100 W fast charging
  • 50 MP selfie and main camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs

  • Plastic frame feels cheap
  • Gaming throttles and freezes when hot
  • Only two Android version updates in region
  • Portrait bokeh can be hit or miss

Why we say that

The 6.78‑inch screen runs at 120 Hz and the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 with 12 GB RAM feels snappy for everyday apps. Battery endurance is good but can dip when you push the phone hard, and the 5200 mAh cell refills in minutes thanks to the fast charger. Camera hardware is strong with a 50 MP main sensor, OIS, 2.5× tele and a 50 MP selfie shooter, though portrait bokeh may be inconsistent and low‑light performance is not class‑leading.

The plastic frame lacks the premium feel of metal and the IP65 rating is modest. Gaming runs well at first but tends to throttle and freeze once the device heats up. Software includes MagicOS 8 on Android 14, but bloatware is present and regional updates are limited to two Android versions.
