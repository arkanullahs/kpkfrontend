What works well

  • Long lasting battery
  • Vegan leather build with IP64
  • 50MP main camera in daylight
  • Dimensity 6300 performance for everyday tasks

Reported trade-offs

  • No official warranty in Bangladesh
  • IPS LCD display with thick chin
  • Outdated UFS 2.2 storage
  • 30W charging is slow

Why we say that

The 5200 mAh battery (owners talk about a massive 7000 mAh feel) scores 9.7 out of 10 and easily passes 2+ days of mixed use. Build quality feels premium with vegan leather, military‑grade certification and an IP64 rating, though the bottom chin is noticeably thick. The display is an IPS LCD with a thick chin, lacking the depth expected from an AMOLED panel despite the spec sheet.

The Dimensity 6300 chipset handles everyday apps fine, but gaming at ultra settings in BGMI shows lag and a little warmth. The 50 MP main sensor is sharp in daylight but oversaturates colours and has no OIS, making video a bit shaky. Software runs Android 16 with Hello UI, but bloatware and Glance reduce the near‑stock feel and updates are weak.

Read full reasoning

The phone comes without any official Bangladeshi warranty; only the shop’s limited guarantee applies and repairs are paid. At ৳26,990 it offers decent value, yet the newer G100 is cheaper and better equipped.

How long does the battery really last?

The spec lists a 5200 mAh cell but owners describe a massive 7000 mAh feel that easily lasts over two days of mixed use. Battery ratings are 9.7 out of 10, making endurance the phone’s strongest point.

Will it handle gaming like BGMI?

The Dimensity 6300 runs daily apps smoothly, but BGMI on ultra settings lags and the device gets slightly warm during long sessions. For casual gaming it is acceptable, but not for high‑performance play.

Is the camera good in low light?

The 50 MP sensor is sharp in daylight but tends to oversaturate colours and lacks optical image stabilization, so low‑light shots and video can appear shaky. Camera ratings sit around 5.8 out of 10.

What warranty and after‑sale support does it have?

All listings are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Only the shop’s limited warranty applies, and any repairs are paid by the owner. Confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchase.
