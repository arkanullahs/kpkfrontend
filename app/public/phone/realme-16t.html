Buying verdict

Realme 16T Is it worth buying?

The Realme 16T gives you an 8000 mAh battery that can stretch two to three days, but you pay for it with a low‑resolution screen and dated performance.

Choose it if
You need a rugged phone with massive battery and can accept a low‑resolution display and slower performance.
Consider alternatives if
You expect a high‑refresh, high‑resolution display, top‑tier gaming speed, or an official brand warranty.

What works well

  • 8000 mAh battery lasts 2‑3 days
  • IP69K and military‑grade durability
  • 50 MP main camera works for daylight social media
  • 45 W charger included

Reported trade-offs

  • HD+ resolution on 6.8‑inch screen
  • Dimensity 6300 feels outdated for gaming
  • Virtual gyro delay hurts gameplay
  • Price far above value

Why we say that

The phone is built to survive drops and water with IP69K and military‑grade protection, and the 50 MP main camera is acceptable for bright‑light social media posts. However, the 6.81‑inch IPS LCD is only HD+ and looks washed out on a large panel, and the Dimensity 6300 chipset shows lag, especially with the virtual gyroscope in games. Charging at 45 W is slow for an 8000 mAh cell, so a full top‑up takes several hours.

The device arrives as a gray‑import from India, so there is no official Realme warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop's limited warranty applies and any repair costs are yours. Software runs Realme UI 7.0 on Android 16 with medium‑high bloat and about two to three OS updates.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 8000 mAh battery is rated to last two to three days of normal use, even with moderate screen time and 5G. Charging at 45 W is slow for such a large cell, so expect several hours to refill.

Can the Realme 16T handle modern mobile games smoothly?

Gaming performance is limited by the Dimensity 6300 chipset, which is considered outdated in 2026. Users report noticeable input lag from the virtual gyroscope and a gaming rating of only 4.5, so fast‑paced titles feel sluggish.

Is the camera good for low‑light or night photography?

The 50 MP main sensor with an f/1.8 aperture is adequate for daylight social‑media posts, but it lacks versatility in low‑light situations. Reviewers give the camera a 5.5 rating, indicating modest performance after dark.
