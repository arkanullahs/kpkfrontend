Is the Realme 16T worth buying?

What is good

  • Huge 8000 mAh battery lasts 2‑3 days
  • IP69K rugged build protects against drops
  • 50 MP main camera works in daylight
  • 144 Hz refresh rate on large screen

What is not

  • HD+ resolution on 6.8‑inch display
  • Outdated Dimensity 6300 causes lag
  • Virtual gyroscope delay in games
  • 45 W charging too slow for capacity

Buy it if Buy it if you need a rugged phone with massive battery life and can accept a low‑resolution screen and modest performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you want a smooth gaming experience, a sharp high‑resolution display, or official brand warranty support.

Why we say that

Its 8000 mAh battery easily stretches to two or three days of normal use, and the IP69K rating means it survives drops and water splashes. The 50 MP main sensor captures decent daylight photos for social media, but it lacks versatility and struggles in low light. The Dimensity 6300 chipset feels dated, and the virtual gyroscope adds noticeable lag in games, making the 144 Hz panel feel underused.

Charging at 45 W is slower than expected for such a large battery, and the phone comes without any official Realme warranty in Bangladesh, only a shop‑provided guarantee. Overall, owners love the endurance and ruggedness but feel the display, performance and price are far below what they expected.
