Is the Realme 16T worth buying?

What is good

  • 1200 nit bright 144 Hz display
  • 50 MP main camera
  • 8000 mAh battery lasting long
  • Dimensity 6300 handles 5G gaming

What is not

  • Only shop warranty no brand warranty
  • Medium high ads in UI
  • Only gray import stock available
  • Software updates limited to two or three versions

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large battery bright screen and decent camera without needing a brand warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you rely on official brand warranty or want a phone with minimal ads.

Why we say that

The 8000 mAh cell keeps the device running well beyond a full day even with video streaming and social media. The 6.81 inch IPS LCD shines at 1200 nit and refreshes at 144 Hz, making scrolling and gaming feel smooth. The Dimensity 6300 chipset handles 5G games without major lag, though the Realme UI shows medium high ads that can be distracting.

The 50 MP main sensor captures detailed photos in good light, but low‑light performance is only average. The device runs Android 16 with Realme UI 7.0 and will receive about two or three major OS upgrades. The main complaint is the lack of an official brand warranty, relying instead on a shop warranty that may vary in coverage.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 8000 mAh battery is large enough to last more than a full day with moderate use such as browsing, social media and video playback. Heavy gaming may reduce the time but it will still exceed a day for most users.

Can this phone handle gaming smoothly?

The Dimensity 6300 chipset together with a 144 Hz IPS LCD provides smooth performance for most mobile games. 5G connectivity is supported, though occasional UI ads may appear during gameplay.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP sensor with an f/1.8 aperture captures detailed images in good lighting. In low‑light situations the results are acceptable but not exceptional, as the sensor size limits how much light is gathered.

What warranty and software update support does it have?

The phone is sold as a gray import with a shop warranty only, not an official brand warranty. Realme UI promises about two or three major Android updates, and users should verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.
