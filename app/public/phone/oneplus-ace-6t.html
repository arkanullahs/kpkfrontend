Is the OnePlus Ace 6T worth buying?

What is good

  • 8300 mAh battery lasts two days
  • 100 W fast charging restores power quickly
  • 165 Hz AMOLED display is bright and smooth
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 offers strong performance

What is not

  • Plastic back feels cheap
  • Camera quality is low
  • Thermal throttling during heavy gaming
  • China ROM causes notification delays

Buy it if Buy it if you need massive battery life, fast charging and high‑refresh gaming performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you rely on a high‑quality camera or an official manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

Its 8300 mAh silicon‑carbon battery delivers genuine 2‑day endurance even with heavy use, and the 100 W charger restores full power in under an hour. The Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset and 165 Hz AMOLED panel make gaming smooth until the device heats to 40‑45 °C, at which point performance may throttle and some demanding titles crash after about 25 minutes. The metal frame feels solid, yet the plastic/fiberglass back feels cheap for the price.

The 50 MP main sensor is decent on paper but produces budget‑grade images, especially in low light, and the ultrawide lens lacks macro capability. Software runs on ColorOS 16 without Google services, leading to notification delays and occasional bloat. There is no official OnePlus warranty; only the shop’s limited warranty applies, and any ROM conversion voids that coverage.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 8300 mAh silicon‑carbon battery provides about two days of screen‑on time for typical daily tasks. Heavy gaming or video playback will reduce that to roughly a full day, but the endurance remains impressive for a phone in this price range.

Will the phone overheat while gaming?

Yes, under sustained heavy gaming the device can reach 40‑45 °C, causing the chipset to throttle. Users report crashes after about 25 minutes in demanding titles, so long gaming sessions may be unstable.

Is the camera good for low‑light photos?

The 50 MP main sensor is budget‑grade and struggles in low light. Images appear reddish and the ultrawide lens lacks macro capability. Overall camera ratings are low, so it is not ideal for low‑light photography.

Does the phone come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

All units are gray‑imported China‑ROM models, so there is no OnePlus manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop provides its own limited warranty, and any ROM conversion to a global version voids that coverage.
