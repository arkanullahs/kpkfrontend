Buying verdict

Tecno Camon 30S Is it worth buying?

This phone offers a bright screen and a decent camera, but daily usability is inconsistent.

Choose it if
You prioritize a good display and camera over gaming performance.
Consider alternatives if
You play heavy games or need a long lasting battery.

What works well

  • Bright and smooth AMOLED display
  • Good camera quality with OIS
  • Solid build quality
  • Includes an IR blaster

Reported trade-offs

  • Fast battery drain
  • Laggy gaming performance
  • Unstable WiFi connections
  • High amount of bloatware and ads

Why we say that

Owners report that the device feels laggy and stutters during gaming sessions like CODM and Mobile Legends. You will likely deal with a battery that drains fast and unstable WiFi connections that disconnect frequently. The software experience is hindered by high amounts of bloatware and ads.

While the build quality is solid and the AMOLED display is smooth, the actual performance does not match the hardware specs. It is a device where the visual appeal and camera quality are offset by software sluggishness and connectivity issues.

How is the gaming performance on this phone?

Gaming performance is poor. Owners report that the phone gets laggy and stutters when playing titles like Mobile Legends and CODM. It has a performance rating of 5.5 and a gaming rating of 4.5.

Does the battery last a full day?

How many software updates will it get?

The phone runs on Android 14 with HIOS 14. It is expected to receive up to 2 major Android upgrades.

Is the camera good for photography?

The main camera is 50MP with OIS and a 1/1.56 inch sensor. Owners have given the camera a rating of 7.5 and describe the overall camera quality as good.
