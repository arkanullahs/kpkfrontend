Is the OPPO A60 worth buying?

What is good

  • Solid build quality
  • Good 90Hz display
  • Decent battery life
  • Includes a headphone jack

What is not

  • Poor camera quality
  • Lag and performance issues in games
  • Medium amount of bloatware and ads

Buy it if You need a durable phone with a good screen for basic tasks.

Skip it if You want a good camera or a smooth gaming experience.

Why we say that

Owners report that the camera quality is poor and describes it as trash compared to other phones in the same budget in Bangladesh. Gaming is a weak point with reports of lag in the first few seconds and longer loading times. While the battery life is generally described as decent, one user reported a severe issue where they could not unlock the phone after shutdown.

The Snapdragon 680 4G chipset does not provide a high performance experience. You get a 90Hz IPS LCD screen that reaches 950 nits peak brightness, which is a highlight. However, the actual daily use experience is marred by these performance gaps and poor photo quality.
