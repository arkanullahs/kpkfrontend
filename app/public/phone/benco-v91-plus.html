Why we say that

It easily lasts over a day with 15 to 25 percent remaining. However, daily use reveals several frustrations. The Unisoc T606 chipset is slow and causes delays when opening apps.

Owners complain that the plastic body scratches easily and the screen is hard to see under direct sunlight. The software is outdated, running Android 13, and users have expressed a desire for Android 14. Video quality is poor due to a lack of stabilization.

Charging is a slow process, taking nearly 2 hours to reach full capacity. It feels like a basic device where the high RAM and 90Hz screen are the only highlights against a struggling processor and old software.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

Yes, this is an official BD unit. It comes with the brand's own warranty and access to their official service network.

Can I play PUBG or Free Fire on this phone?

The Unisoc T606 chipset is slow. Owners report that it is only suitable for 2D games, and the gaming rating is low at 2.8.

How is the camera for taking photos at night?

The camera performs poorly in low light. While it has Pro and Macro modes, the overall video stabilization is lacking.

How long does the battery last and how fast does it charge?

The 5000 mAh battery lasts over a day. However, the 18W charging is slow and takes nearly 2 hours for a full charge.
