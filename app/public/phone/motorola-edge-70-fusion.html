Is the Motorola Edge 70 Fusion worth buying?

What is good

  • 144 Hz AMOLED with 5200 nits peak brightness
  • Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 delivers smooth daily use
  • 68 W fast charging fills battery quickly
  • Premium IP68/69 design feels solid

What is not

  • Random reboots and freezes
  • Audio cuts out unexpectedly
  • Wi‑Fi drops frequently
  • Blue vertical lines may appear

Buy it if Buy it if you want a bright 5G phone with fast charging and can tolerate occasional software glitches.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a rock‑solid camera or reliable long‑term software stability.

Why we say that

It runs on a Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 chipset, 8 GB RAM and up to 256 GB storage, delivering smooth daily tasks and fast 68 W charging for the 5200 mAh battery that owners rate 9.8 out of 10 and say can last several days. The premium IP68/69 build feels solid, yet many users report random reboots, screen freezes, audio dropouts, Wi‑Fi drops and occasional blue vertical lines on the display. The 50 MP main camera with OIS captures decent photos, but focus hunting and inconsistent processing lower its rating to 6.5.

The phone arrives as a gray‑import unit built for India, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s limited warranty applies and any repairs are paid. Prices range from 36,999 to ৳47,999 depending on configuration.
