Is the Motorola Edge 70 Fusion worth buying?

What is good

  • 5200 mAh battery with good endurance
  • 6.78‑inch AMOLED 144 Hz display with 5200 nits peak
  • Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 provides decent performance
  • 68 W fast charging

What is not

  • Frequent freezes and random reboots
  • SIM‑2 drops connection after short time
  • Wi‑Fi stability problems
  • Camera tuning feels mediocre

Buy it if Buy if you value a bright large screen and long battery life and can tolerate occasional software instability.

Skip it if Skip if you need reliable gaming performance, stable dual‑SIM operation, or an official Motorola warranty.

Why we say that

The Edge 70 Fusion shows off a 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel that can hit 5200 nits, making outdoor use easy, and its 5200 mAh cell lasts through a full day of normal use. The Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 chipset handles everyday tasks without lag, and 68 W charging refills the battery quickly. However, owners report random freezes, sudden reboots and stuttering in apps, which makes multitasking frustrating.

The second SIM slot often drops out after a short period, and Wi‑Fi connections can become unstable. The glass back scratches more easily than expected and the camera software feels under‑tuned, so photos are not a strong selling point. Software is a near‑stock Hello UI but comes with some bloatware and Lenovo’s update support is weak.

The phone ships as gray‑import stock, so only a shop warranty is available and you must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
