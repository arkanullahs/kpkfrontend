Motorola Edge 70 Fusion Is it worth buying?

The Edge 70 Fusion feels like a premium‑looking phone that can stay bright all day but it often misbehaves when you need it most.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
If you want a bright high‑refresh screen and fast charging at a mid‑range price and can live with occasional software hiccups, consider this phone.
Consider alternatives if
If you need a phone with reliable software, official warranty, or flawless multitasking, look elsewhere.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 144 Hz AMOLED display with very high brightness
  • 5200 mAh battery and 68 W fast charging
  • Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 handles daily tasks well
  • Premium IP68/69 design

Reported trade-offs4

  • Random freezes and reboots
  • Audio stops and needs restart
  • Screen may show vertical line defects
  • Second SIM can shut down

Why we say that

Its 6.78 inch AMOLED panel reaches a peak 5200 nits and runs at 144 Hz, making scrolling and media look smooth. The Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 chipset handles everyday apps without lag, and the 5200 mAh battery with 68 W charging scores a 9.8 rating from owners, giving multi‑day use for most. However, the software is unstable; users report random freezes, reboots and audio cutting out, which only a restart can fix.

The main camera is decent in good light but struggles with focus and low‑light processing. Some units arrive with two thin blue vertical lines on the screen, and the second SIM slot may stop working. There is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh, only the shop’s limited cover, so repairs will cost extra.
