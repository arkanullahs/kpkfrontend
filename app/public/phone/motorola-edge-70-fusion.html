Buying verdict

Motorola Edge 70 Fusion Is it worth buying?

Expect a bright 144Hz AMOLED display and fast charging, but be ready for frequent software freezes and hardware quirks.

Choose it if
Buy if you want a bright, fast‑charging 5G phone and can tolerate software glitches.
Consider alternatives if
Skip if you need reliable software, official warranty, or a flawless display.

What works well

  • 144Hz AMOLED screen
  • 68W fast charging
  • Strong 5200 mAh battery
  • 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs

  • Random freezes and reboots
  • Audio cuts out unexpectedly
  • Wi‑Fi and second SIM instability
  • Vertical line screen defects

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch screen can reach 5200 nits peak brightness, making outdoor use easy. Battery capacity is 5200 mAh and owners rate endurance at 9.8, so you can get through a day without a charge. The Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 processor with 8GB RAM handles daily tasks, yet random reboots and freezes are common and can interrupt gaming or video playback.

Audio may stop suddenly and only a restart brings it back. Wi‑Fi connections drop and the second SIM often shuts down on its own. Some users see two blue vertical lines on the screen edges.

Read full reasoning

The phone ships as a gray import built for India, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s limited warranty applies and repairs are paid. Confirm the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The phone has a 5200 mAh battery and owners rate its endurance at 9.8 out of 10. Most users report multi‑day use on a single charge, and the 68W charger refills the battery quickly.

Will the phone run games smoothly?

The Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 processor with 8GB RAM provides decent performance for casual games, but the device is known for random freezes and reboots that can interrupt gameplay and affect overall stability.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The main 50MP sensor has optical image stabilization, but reviewers note focus hunting and inconsistent processing, especially in low‑light scenes, resulting in average pictures compared with the rating of 6.5.
