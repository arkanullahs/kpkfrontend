Is the Realme Note 60 worth buying?

What is good

  • Rugged die cast aluminum body
  • Large 5,000 mAh battery
  • 90 Hz LCD display
  • Headphone jack included

What is not

  • 10 W charging is very slow
  • Screen brightness struggles in sunlight
  • Laggy multitasking and gaming
  • Camera lacks sharpness

Buy it if Buy it if you want a durable phone with long battery life for basic apps and do not need fast charging or high performance gaming.

Skip it if Skip it if you rely on fast charging, need a bright outdoor screen, or want smooth gaming and heavy multitasking.

Why we say that

The aluminum body feels solid and the 5,000 mAh battery gives decent endurance, yet the 10 W charger will keep you waiting for hours. The 6.74 inch LCD runs at 90 Hz which feels smoother than many budget phones, but its 560 nits peak brightness makes outdoor use a struggle. The Unisoc Tiger T612 chip and 8 GB RAM handle basic apps, but multitasking becomes sluggish after software updates and gaming is barely playable.

The 32 MP main camera captures acceptable photos in good light but lacks sharpness and low‑light performance. Software is light but the medium‑high bloat can tax the RAM. Overall it is a rugged, affordable device for simple daily tasks, not a performance or photography flagship.
