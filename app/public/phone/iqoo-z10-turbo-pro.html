Buying verdict

iQOO Z10 Turbo Pro Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day and charge in minutes, the iQOO Z10 Turbo Pro delivers that.

Choose it if
You want long battery life, high refresh display and gaming power at a gray‑import price.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable camera, premium build or official warranty.

What works well

  • 7000 mAh battery with 120 W fast charge
  • 144 Hz AMOLED screen with 4400 nits peak brightness
  • Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 gives flagship performance
  • IR blaster for remote control

Reported trade-offs

  • Plastic body feels cheap and IP65 rating is low
  • Main camera is average and ultrawide is poor
  • Device gets hot around 43 °C and may throttle
  • No manufacturer warranty, only shop warranty

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh battery lasts well beyond a full day and the 120 W charger refills it in roughly half an hour. The Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset keeps most games at a smooth 60 fps, though heavy titles may throttle as the device warms to about 43 °C. The 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel is bright, fast and works well outdoors, but the plastic rear feels cheap and the IP65 rating is modest.

The main camera produces decent daylight images, yet colors are over saturated and the ultrawide lens adds little value. Software runs Android 15 with OriginOS or Funtouch, bringing some bloatware and occasional notification delays. There is no official warranty, only the shop’s limited coverage, so repairs will be out‑of‑pocket.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 7000 mAh cell can easily last a day and a half of normal use, and the 120 W charger fills it from zero to full in about half an hour, according to owner reports.

Will the phone overheat while gaming?

During intense games the phone can reach about 43°C, which owners notice as noticeable heat and occasional throttling, especially in titles like Genshin Impact, so long sessions may drop frame rates.

Is the camera good in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor records decent daylight shots, but colors tend to be over saturated and the 13 MP ultrawide behaves like a toy, offering little detail, so low light photography is not a strong point.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

All units are gray imports, so there is no official Vivo warranty in Bangladesh; the shop you buy from will give its own limited coverage and any repair will cost extra, and software updates follow the China or India ROM schedule.
