Is the Nokia FLIP 2660 worth buying?

What is good

  • Very easy to use
  • No bloatware or ads
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • Has FM radio

What is not

  • Very poor camera quality
  • Weak display and performance
  • No manufacturer warranty
  • Poor gaming experience

Buy it if You need a simple, easy to use phone for basic calling.

Skip it if You want a camera, gaming performance, or official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Owners find it very easy to use, but almost everything else scores poorly. The display and performance are weak, and the camera is nearly useless. You will struggle with gaming or video tasks as owners rated these very low.

Since it was released in 2022, it is now 4 years old. You should expect little to no software updates. It is a gray import, which means you get no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh.

Any repairs will be paid, and you only have whatever promise the shop gives you. Be sure to check the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying.
