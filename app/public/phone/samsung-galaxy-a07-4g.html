Samsung Galaxy A07 4G Is it worth buying?

If you can live with a sluggish UI, the Galaxy A07 4G gives you a big screen, solid battery and a 50 MP camera for under 17 k taka.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a large battery, headphone jack and a decent camera at a sub‑20 k taka price and can tolerate some UI lag.
Consider alternatives if
You expect smooth gaming or a fast, bloat‑free Android experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 5000 mAh battery lasts a day
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • 50 MP main camera for the price
  • 25 W fast charging

Reported trade-offs4

  • 4 GB RAM causes severe lag
  • One UI feels heavy on low‑end hardware
  • Camera app opens slowly
  • Gaming stutters badly

Why we say that

The 6.7‑inch 90 Hz PLS LCD is bright enough for everyday use but the notch looks dated. The 5000 mAh cell paired with a 6 nm Helio G99 chip and 25 W charger provides a full day of mixed usage, though heavy apps drain it faster. Android 15 runs One UI 8, which many owners call a heavy, bloated layer that struggles on the 4 GB RAM version, causing noticeable lag and gaming stutters.

The camera produces decent photos in good light, but the app opens slowly and there is no optical image stabilization for low‑light shots. Headphone jack and FM radio are still present, and Samsung promises up to six major Android upgrades, which is a strong point for the price. Overall the phone feels usable for media and basic tasks but falls short for multitasking and gaming.
