Is the Infinix Hot 60 worth buying?

What is good

  • Capable Dimensity 7020 5G chipset
  • Large 5200 mAh battery capacity
  • Smooth 120Hz display refresh rate
  • Includes headphone jack and IR blaster

What is not

  • Low resolution display quality
  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • Only gray-import stock available
  • Slow software update delivery

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize 5G connectivity and a faster chipset over display resolution and official brand support.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you want a high-quality screen, a clean software experience without ads, or the security of an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

While the performance is considered an improvement over older chips, the user experience is hampered by high levels of bloatware and notification ads within the XOS 15.1 software. Owners are particularly vocal about the display, noting that it is only an IPS LCD panel rather than AMOLED and suffers from a low resolution that many find disappointing. The camera system, despite the 50MP main sensor, is not considered top-tier by those who use it daily.

You should also be aware that this phone is currently only available as gray-import stock. This means you will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, and you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before handing over your 17,990 taka.
