Is the POCO F7 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • 120W wired fast charging
  • 6.67 inch 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite performance
  • 12GB RAM with up to 512GB storage

What is not

  • No official warranty in Bangladesh
  • Thermal throttling under long gaming sessions
  • Ultrawide and selfie video limited to 1080p
  • Pre-installed ads and bloatware

Buy it if Buy it if you need raw power, fast charging and can accept a shop warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need eSIM, a flawless camera video experience, or a clean software environment.

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Elite and 12GB RAM make apps and games feel instant, and the 120W charger fills the 5300 mAh cell in minutes. The 6.67-inch 120Hz AMOLED panel reaches peak brightness around 3200 nits, so outdoor use is clear. Camera performance shines on the 50 MP main sensor and 2.5× telephoto, yet the ultrawide struggles and both ultrawide and selfie video stop at 1080p.

Thermal throttling appears during long stress tests, which can cause occasional frame drops in intense games. Software runs HyperOS 3 on Android 15 with ads and bloatware, and you can expect two to three major OS upgrades. Overall it feels powerful but unpolished.
