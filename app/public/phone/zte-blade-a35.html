Buying verdict

ZTE Blade A35 Is it worth buying?

The Blade A35 will give you a big screen and long battery at a rock‑bottom price, but expect sluggish performance and weak photos.

Choose it if
You need a very cheap phone for calls, texts and occasional web browsing.
Consider alternatives if
You expect smooth gaming, fast app performance or good low‑light photos.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • Large 6.75‑inch 90 Hz display
  • 5000 mAh battery lasts up to 1.5 days
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included
  • Official warranty at the cheapest price

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe lag and freezing in apps
  • Poor low‑light camera performance
  • Unstable Wi‑Fi and slow internet
  • Android Go lacks widgets and freezes

Why we say that

At ৳9,099 it offers a 6.75‑inch IPS LCD with 90 Hz refresh, a 5000 mAh cell that can stretch to about one and a half days for basic tasks, and a headphone jack plus FM radio. The Unisoc SC9863A1 processor with 4 GB RAM struggles to open apps quickly and freezes during games, earning a low performance rating. The 8 MP rear camera is only decent in bright light and fails in low‑light scenes.

Android 14 Go edition feels stripped, lacking home‑screen widgets and showing occasional bugs. Wi‑Fi can be unstable and the flashlight turns off after three minutes. If you need a cheap device for calls, texts and occasional browsing, it may suit you, but it will frustrate anyone looking for smooth multitasking or quality photography.
