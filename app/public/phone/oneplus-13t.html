Why we say that

The OnePlus 13T delivers a vivid 6.32‑inch LTPO AMOLED panel that reaches 1600 nits and runs at a smooth 120 Hz, making everyday scrolling and media consumption pleasant. Powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite and 12 GB RAM, it handles apps and games with high performance, and the 80 W charger refills the 6260 mAh battery in under an hour. Owners praise the efficient battery life and the bright display, yet they note the camera's 2× telephoto is modest and low‑light photos lack detail.

The phone ships with a China‑ROM ColorOS version that omits Google services and Bangla language support, and it only offers USB 2.0, limiting fast file transfers. There is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop's limited warranty and must pay for any repairs. Software updates follow the OxygenOS roadmap with up to four major Android upgrades, but the China variant may face fragile update paths.
