iQOO 15 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can game for hours and charge fast, the iQOO 15 delivers, but expect compromises in camera and software bloat.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize gaming, long battery life and fast charging.

What works well4

  • 7,000 mAh battery lasts two days
  • 6.85 inch 144 Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance
  • 100 W wired fast charging

Reported trade-offs4

  • Heavy pre installed bloatware
  • Ultra wide camera blurry on moving subjects
  • Speakers sound tinny
  • No official manufacturer warranty

Why we say that

The 7,000 mAh battery gives two day endurance and the 100 W wired charger refills it in under an hour. The 6.85 inch LTPO AMOLED screen is bright, smooth and accurate, making media and games look great. Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and up to 16 GB RAM keep performance high even in demanding titles, though the device can get hot during long sessions.

Build quality is solid with IP68 rating and a customizable LED ring, but the speakers sound tinny and the ultra wide lens struggles with moving subjects. OriginOS 6 runs Android 16 and promises up to five major updates, yet heavy pre installed bloatware slows the experience. There is no official manufacturer warranty, only the shop's limited coverage, so repairs will be paid out of pocket.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The phone has a 7,000 mAh cell that owners say gives about two days of normal use. Battery ratings are 9.7 out of 10, and the 100 W charger can refill it quickly.

Is the iQOO 15 suitable for heavy gaming?

Yes, the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chip with up to 16 GB RAM scores 9.6 for gaming. It runs games smoothly, though the frame can become hot during very long sessions.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor is decent, but the telephoto lens adds a lot of noise in low light and the ultra wide lens is blurry on moving subjects. Overall camera rating is 6.5.

Will the phone get software updates and is there a warranty?

It ships with Android 16 and OriginOS 6 and promises up to five major Android upgrades. The software includes noticeable bloatware. There is no official manufacturer warranty; only the shop's warranty applies and repairs are paid.
