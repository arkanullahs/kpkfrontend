iQOO 15 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run games for hours without charging, the iQOO 15’s 7000 mAh battery and Snapdragon 8 Elite chip deliver that.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize battery life, gaming power and a bright display over a clean software experience and official warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need a low‑price phone, pristine camera performance or official brand support.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 7000 mAh battery gives multi‑day endurance
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite provides elite gaming performance
  • 144 Hz LTPO AMOLED with 6000 nits peak brightness
  • IP68/IP69 water and dust resistance

Reported trade-offs4

  • Aggressive bloatware and intrusive ads
  • Ultrawide camera weak in low light
  • Speaker quality sounds tinny
  • Price is high for its generation

Why we say that

The 6.85" LTPO AMOLED panel runs at 144 Hz and can peak at 6000 nits, so outdoor visibility is excellent. Performance scores are high, and the device handles demanding games with elite speed, though a bit of thermal throttling may appear after long play. The camera setup is decent for everyday shots, but the ultrawide lens struggles in low light and the selfie sensor is not as sharp as expected.

Software runs on OriginOS 6 with up to five major Android upgrades, yet it is loaded with bloatware and ads that many owners find intrusive. Audio from the speakers is underwhelming, and the price is noticeably higher than previous iQOO generations. Remember that every unit is a gray‑import, so you only get the shop’s limited warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 7000 mAh cell is rated for industry‑leading endurance, so most users can expect two full days of moderate use before needing to recharge, and a 100 W charger refills it in about half an hour.

Is the iQOO 15 suitable for heavy gaming?

The Snapdragon 8 Elite processor together with up to 16 GB RAM gives elite gaming performance, and the 144 Hz display keeps motion smooth, though some users notice minor thermal throttling after long sessions.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS handles low light reasonably well, but the 13 MP ultrawide lens and the 32 MP selfie camera are mediocre, producing softer details and more noise in dim environments.

Will I get any official warranty or software updates?

All iQOO 15 units in Bangladesh are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty; the shop provides its own limited coverage and any repairs are paid. The phone ships with OriginOS 6 and promises up to five major Android upgrades.
