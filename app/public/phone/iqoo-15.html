Buying verdict

iQOO 15 Is it worth buying?

Expect two days of heavy use on a single charge, but plan for extra storage and software cleanup.

Choose it if
Buy if you need long battery life, a bright high‑refresh display, and top gaming performance.
Consider alternatives if
Skip if you rely on an official warranty or need a strong all‑round camera.

What works well

  • 6000‑nit peak brightness
  • 100W wired fast charging
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance
  • IR blaster for remote control

Reported trade-offs

  • Heavy pre‑installed bloatware
  • Ultra‑wide camera is soft
  • Moving subjects come out blurry
  • Speakers sound tinny

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh cell gives the iQOO 15 a two‑day endurance that most users praise. Its 6.85‑inch LTPO AMOLED panel reaches a 6000‑nit peak and a smooth 144 Hz refresh, making bright outdoor viewing and fast scrolling easy. Powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 with up to 16 GB RAM, the phone stays responsive in demanding games and multitasking.

Charging at 100 W fills the battery in about half an hour, while the 40 W wireless option adds convenience. The IR blaster lets you control TVs without extra accessories. However, the device arrives as a gray‑import with no manufacturer warranty, so repairs are paid and you must rely on the shop’s limited cover.

Read full reasoning

Software includes OriginOS 6 or Funtouch OS with medium‑level bloatware that can slow the experience. Camera scores are mixed; the 50 MP main sensor is solid, but the ultra‑wide lens is soft and moving subjects often blur. Speakers are thin and low‑light video from the telephoto lens shows noticeable noise.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Is the iQOO 15 suitable for mobile gaming?
