Is the iQOO 15 worth buying?

What is good

  • 7000 mAh battery lasts two days
  • 6000 nit peak brightness on 6.85 inch AMOLED
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 handles games smoothly
  • 100 W wired charging fills quickly

What is not

  • Heavy preinstalled bloatware slows the UI
  • Ultra wide camera is poor and blurry
  • Speakers sound tinny and weak
  • No official warranty in Bangladesh

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize gaming performance and long battery life over camera perfection.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a reliable official warranty or top tier photography.

Why we say that

It packs a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and a 7000 mAh battery that most owners say lasts two days, and the 100 W charger refills it in about half an hour. The 6.85 inch LTPO AMOLED screen reaches a 6000 nit peak, giving bright, color accurate visuals that earn a 9.8 display rating. Gaming performance scores 9.6, with the Q3 chip keeping frame rates stable, though the chassis can feel hot during marathon sessions.

Build quality is solid with IP68/69 protection and a customizable LED ring, but the phone carries heavy preinstalled bloatware that slows the UI and a speaker system that sounds tinny. Camera results are mixed: the 50 MP main sensor with OIS is decent, the 3x telephoto works, but the ultra wide is weak and moving subjects often blur, and low light video from the telephoto is noisy. Because every unit is a gray import, there is no official warranty; you must rely on the shop’s limited cover and confirm the IMEI status.

Overall the device feels like a high end gaming machine with excellent battery and display, but compromises on software polish, audio and photography.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 7000 mAh cell gives about two days of normal use, and owners rate battery life at 9.7 out of 10. A 100 W wired charger refills the pack in roughly half an hour.

Is the phone good for gaming?

The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 paired with the Q3 chip delivers top tier gaming performance, earning a 9.6 rating from owners. It stays stable, though the frame can become hot during very long sessions.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS handles low light stills reasonably, but the ultra wide is weak and moving subjects often turn out blurry. Low light video with the telephoto lens shows noticeable noise, reflected in a 6.5 camera rating.

Will I get an official warranty and software updates?

All iQOO 15 units in Bangladesh are gray imports, so there is no manufacturer warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited cover and paid repairs. The phone runs Android 16 with OriginOS 6 and promises up to five major Android upgrades, though it ships with medium level bloatware.
