Is the Apple iPhone 16e worth buying?

What is good

  • Powerful Apple A18 chipset
  • Clean iOS software experience
  • Compact and durable build
  • Long term software support

What is not

  • Dated 60Hz display refresh rate
  • Lacks an ultrawide camera
  • Poor value for money
  • Battery drain after software updates

Buy it if You are a dedicated Apple user who specifically wants a compact device with the A18 chip and does not mind a 60Hz screen.

Skip it if You want a modern display, versatile camera options, or better value for your money compared to the iPhone 17e.

Why we say that

While the A18 chip provides strong performance, owners frequently complain that the device lacks the versatility expected at this price point. The lack of an ultrawide camera is a major point of contention for users who feel the hardware does not match the cost. Battery life is generally solid due to the efficient chip, but many owners report significant drain issues following software updates.

The build quality is compact and durable, yet it feels like a recycled design from older models. Most buyers find the value proposition poor when compared to other options like the iPhone 17e, which often sells for a similar price. If you prioritize modern display smoothness or camera flexibility, this device will likely leave you disappointed.

Tech Burner · Apple iPhone 16e Unboxing - Most Confusing iPhone ! ↗

Tech Burner finds the iPhone 16e an oddly positioned device that offers a mixed experience for its price. While praising the sleek matte build, USB-C convenience, Action button, and decent 4K video from the 48MP main sensor, he points out noticeable cutbacks including the 60Hz notch screen, binned GPU, lack of an ultrawide camera, and missing Cinematic video mode. He concludes that unless it receives substantial price cuts, buyers are generally better off purchasing older models like the iPhone 14 or iPhone 15.

GSMArena Official · Apple iPhone 16e full review ↗

GSMArena concludes that the iPhone 16e is a competent entry point into modern iOS and Apple Intelligence, offering strong CPU performance, great stereo speakers, capable 4K video recording, and IP68 water resistance. However, these cost savings come with clear drawbacks, including an older 60Hz display with limited outdoor brightness boost, a 4-core GPU that trails standard models in graphics tests, and the lack of camera versatility. They recommend it only to buyers who insist on a brand-new, future-supported iPhone rather than a previous-generation flagship.

ATC Android ToTo Company · iPhone 16e : Apple এর নতুন স্ক্যাম নাকি মাস্টারপ্ল্যান? ↗

ATC views the iPhone 16e as a modernized replacement for the older iPhone 14, offering major advantages in longevity through its A18 chip, 8GB of RAM, Apple Intelligence support, and USB-C port. Although the phone compromises on hardware by omitting an ultrawide lens, Cinematic video mode, and fast MagSafe charging while keeping an older 60Hz notch screen, the reviewer argues that for buyers in Bangladesh seeking long-term software support and solid day-to-day performance over camera versatility, it makes more sense than buying aging predecessors.
