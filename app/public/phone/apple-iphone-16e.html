Why we say that

The A18 3nm chip and 8GB RAM give a smooth performance score of nine point four and keep high frame rates in demanding titles. iOS stays clean and will receive up to six major updates, so software stays fresh. The 48MP main sensor captures decent photos in good light, and the 12MP selfie camera is reliable.

However the 6.1 inch OLED panel is limited to 60Hz and has thick bezels, which many owners call outdated. Battery life sits at six point eight rating and drops noticeably after the iOS 26.0.1 update. The phone arrives as a gray import, so there is no manufacturer warranty and any repair costs are out of pocket.

What reviewers said on YouTube

4 reviews · 5.3M views

The Apple iPhone 16e is a capable entry-level option that offers modern A18 performance, long-term software support, and USB-C at a lower cost. However, heavy hardware compromises like a 60Hz notch display, missing video modes, and a single rear camera make older discounted models or standard flagships more versatile alternatives.

Best for Buyers wanting a future-proof iOS device on a budget

  1. GGSMArena Official

    Apple iPhone 16e full review (opens YouTube)

    446K views

    GSMArena concludes that the iPhone 16e is a competent entry point into modern iOS and Apple Intelligence, offering strong CPU performance, great stereo speakers, capable 4K video recording, and IP68 water resistance. However, these cost savings come with clear drawbacks, including an older 60Hz display with limited outdoor brightness boost, a 4-core GPU that trails standard models in graphics tests, and the lack of camera versatility. They recommend it only to buyers who insist on a brand-new, future-supported iPhone rather than a previous-generation flagship.

  2. AATC Android ToTo Company

    iPhone 16e : Apple এর নতুন স্ক্যাম নাকি মাস্টারপ্ল্যান? (opens YouTube)

    435K viewsBangla

    ATC views the iPhone 16e as a modernized replacement for the older iPhone 14, offering major advantages in longevity through its A18 chip, 8GB of RAM, Apple Intelligence support, and USB-C port. Although the phone compromises on hardware by omitting an ultrawide lens, Cinematic video mode, and fast MagSafe charging while keeping an older 60Hz notch screen, the reviewer argues that for buyers in Bangladesh seeking long-term software support and solid day-to-day performance over camera versatility, it makes more sense than buying aging predecessors.

    2.8M views

  4. TTech Burner

    Apple iPhone 16e Unboxing - Most Confusing iPhone ! (opens YouTube)

    1.7M views

    Tech Burner finds the iPhone 16e an oddly positioned device that offers a mixed experience for its price. While praising the sleek matte build, USB-C convenience, Action button, and decent 4K video from the 48MP main sensor, he points out noticeable cutbacks including the 60Hz notch screen, binned GPU, lack of an ultrawide camera, and missing Cinematic video mode. He concludes that unless it receives substantial price cuts, buyers are generally better off purchasing older models like the iPhone 14 or iPhone 15.

Will the iPhone 16e receive software updates for many years?

The device runs iOS 18.3.1 and can be upgraded to iOS 26.6, giving it about five to six major version updates, and the system stays free of ads and bloat.

How does the battery perform after the latest iOS update?

Owners report mixed endurance and a rating of six point eight, but many notice a noticeable capacity drop after updating to iOS 26.0.1, so battery life may be shorter than expected.

Does the iPhone 16e include an ultra wide lens?

No, the phone only has a 48MP main sensor and a 12MP selfie camera, and reviewers point out the lack of an ultra wide camera as a clear limitation.

Is there an official Apple warranty for this model in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray imports built for the US and Australia, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s own promise applies and any repair cost must be paid by the owner.
