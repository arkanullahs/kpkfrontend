Why we say that

The 6.85 inch AMOLED panel reaches 2000 nit peak brightness and refreshes at 144 Hz, making everyday scrolling and gaming feel smooth. Snapdragon 8 Elite paired with up to 24 GB RAM delivers strong performance and a gaming rating of nine out of ten. Camera hardware is solid with a 50 MP main sensor, but basic tuning leaves low‑light photos underwhelming and the overall camera rating sits at six.

Build quality is rated medium and the phone carries an IP68 rating, yet ZTE has almost no official service network in Bangladesh. The unit you will find is a gray‑import with only a shop warranty, so confirming IMEI and BTRC status before purchase is essential.

ZTE 10S Pro: what buyers ask us

What is the ZTE 10S Pro price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for ZTE 10S Pro in Bangladesh was ৳1,15,990, checked 24 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

The 7050 mAh cell is described as efficient and owners give it an eight out of ten rating. In real life it can comfortably last two days of heavy use, and longer with moderate usage.

Is the phone good for gaming?

Yes, the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset together with up to 24 GB RAM and a 144 Hz AMOLED display makes games run smoothly. Owner ratings give gaming a nine out of ten, indicating strong performance.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The main camera uses a 50 MP 1/1.55 inch sensor with f/1.9 aperture and OIS, but reviews note only basic tuning. Low‑light shots are decent but not exceptional, reflected in a six out of ten camera rating.

Will I get an official warranty or service support?

The phone is only available as a gray‑import unit, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. You will rely on the shop warranty and must confirm IMEI and BTRC status, while ZTE service centres are virtually absent.
