Is the ZTE 10S Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Strong gaming and performance ratings
  • Large 7050 mAh efficient battery
  • 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset

What is not

  • Basic camera tuning
  • Medium build quality
  • No official ZTE service presence
  • Medium amount of bloatware and ads

Buy it if You want a high performance gaming phone with a very large battery.

Skip it if You need a reliable official brand warranty and a great camera.

Why we say that

Owners give gaming a 9.0 and performance an 8.5, which is backed by the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset. The 7050 mAh battery is efficient and lasts a long time. However, the build quality is only rated 6.5 and the camera is a weak point with basic tuning and a 6.0 rating.

You must be careful with the purchase because only gray import stock is available. ZTE has little to no official service presence in Bangladesh. If the phone breaks, you are entirely dependent on the shop warranty.

There is also a contradiction in reports regarding the headphone jack, as some owners mention its removal while other data says it is present.
