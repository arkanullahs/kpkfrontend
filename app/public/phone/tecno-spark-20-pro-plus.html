Buying verdict

Tecno Spark 20 Pro Plus Is it worth buying?

If you need a large, bright screen and a high‑resolution camera without breaking the bank, the Tecno Spark 20 Pro Plus delivers that at ৳24,499.

Choose it if
You want a big, vibrant screen and decent daylight photos while staying within a tight budget.
Consider alternatives if

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • 6.78 inch AMOLED 120Hz display
  • 108MP main sensor for daylight shots
  • 5000 mAh battery with 33W fast charging
  • 8 GB RAM and 256 GB storage

Reported trade-offs

  • No 5G connectivity
  • Software updates are rare
  • Sunlight boost feature does not work
  • Low‑light portrait quality is weak

Why we say that

Its 6.78 inch AMOLED panel with 120Hz refresh and 1000‑nit peak brightness looks vibrant even in bright rooms. The 108MP main sensor produces sharp daylight photos, though portrait and low‑light processing fall short. Battery life is solid, lasting over a day, and the 33W charger refills it quickly.

Performance feels smooth for everyday tasks and can hold about 40 FPS in PUBG, but it is not meant for heavy gaming. The phone lacks 5G and suffers from Wi‑Fi instability. Software is a pain point with scarce Android updates and a buggy Sunlight boost feature.

Read full reasoning

At three years old, expect little future OS support. Overall it offers a premium feel with a vegan‑leather finish, but compromises on software and low‑light imaging.

How long does the battery last on a normal day?

Can I play PUBG or other heavy games smoothly?

How does the camera perform in low‑light or portrait mode?

The 108MP sensor captures sharp daylight images, but in low‑light or portrait situations the processing is weak, resulting in noisy backgrounds and less detail, which many owners find disappointing.
