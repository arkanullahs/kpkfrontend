Is the Tecno Spark 20 Pro Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.78 inch AMOLED display with 120 Hz refresh
  • 108 MP main camera for bright‑day photos
  • 5000 mAh battery with 33 W fast charging
  • 8 GB RAM and 256 GB storage

What is not

  • No 5G connectivity
  • Software updates are rare and slow
  • Sunlight boost often does not work
  • Wi‑Fi can be unstable

Buy it if Buy it if you value a large AMOLED screen, big storage and decent battery at a budget price.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable 5G, frequent OS upgrades or heavy gaming performance.

Why we say that

The 6.78 inch curved AMOLED panel with 120 Hz refresh and 1000‑nit peak brightness looks vibrant even in bright rooms, and the 8 GB RAM plus 256 GB storage keep everyday apps running smoothly. Battery life is solid, lasting over a day, and the 33 W charger refills it quickly. Gaming runs fine at around 40 FPS in titles like PUBG, though it will struggle with more demanding games.

The 108 MP main sensor captures sharp daylight photos, but low‑light and portrait shots are weak. Owners frequently complain about the lack of Android updates, with only one or two OS upgrades expected, and the Sunlight boost feature often fails. Wi‑Fi stability can be erratic and the phone does not support 5G, limiting future network performance.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery typically lasts more than a full day of mixed use, including browsing, video streaming and social media. When it does run low, the 33 W charger refills it in about an hour.

Can I play heavy games like PUBG on this phone?

For everyday games the phone feels smooth and can hold around 40 FPS in PUBG, which is acceptable for casual play. It is not built for demanding titles that require high frame rates or heavy graphics.

Does the camera work well in low light?

The 108 MP main sensor delivers sharp photos in bright daylight, but low‑light scenes and portrait processing are weak, resulting in noisy or less detailed images. Expect decent results only when lighting is good.

Will I get Android updates for this phone?

The Spark 20 Pro Plus is three years old, so Tecno is unlikely to provide many more Android updates; only one or two OS upgrades are expected. It does carry the brand’s official warranty and service network in Bangladesh.
