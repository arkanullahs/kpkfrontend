Is the Realme P1 Speed 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright and vibrant 120Hz OLED display
  • Snappy performance for daily tasks
  • Includes a 3.5mm headphone jack
  • Stable 60fps gaming in PUBG

What is not

  • Significant thermal throttling and overheating
  • Poor low-light camera performance
  • Noticeable bloatware and ads in software
  • Lack of stabilization in 4K video

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a vibrant display for media consumption and only perform light to moderate daily tasks.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a heavy gamer or if you require a reliable camera and a clean software experience without ads.

Why we say that

While the Dimensity 7300 Energy chipset handles daily tasks well, the phone suffers from thermal throttling and even shutdowns during intense sessions. Users specifically note that the device stops working after 25 minutes of heavy gaming because the battery cannot handle the rising temperature. The camera performance is average in daylight, but it struggles with low-light detail and artificial portrait processing.

You will also encounter noticeable bloatware and ads within the Realme UI 5.0 interface. Since this device is only available through unofficial channels, you must rely on shop warranties rather than official brand support. Always verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before finalizing your purchase.

Does the Realme P1 Speed 5G have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is currently only available through unofficial gray-import channels. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, so you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before buying.

Is this phone good for playing heavy games like Genshin Impact?

Owners report that the phone struggles with heavy games like Genshin Impact. It experiences significant thermal throttling and can even shut down after 25 minutes of intensive gaming because the battery cannot manage the high temperatures.

How is the camera performance in low light conditions?

The camera performance is average during the day, but it is poor in low-light settings. Users have reported that the device lacks detail in dark environments and produces artificial-looking results when using portrait mode.

How many years of software updates will this phone receive?

The device runs on Android 14 with Realme UI 5.0. It is expected to receive between 2 to 3 OS updates throughout its lifecycle, though you should expect to deal with pre-installed bloatware and advertisements in the interface.
