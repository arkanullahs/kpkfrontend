What works well

  • 7,000 mAh battery lasting two days
  • IP69 rugged and shock‑proof design
  • 144 Hz high refresh rate display
  • 50 MP selfie camera

Reported trade-offs

  • 720p resolution on large screen
  • eMMC storage causes noticeable lag
  • rear camera weaker than front
  • no official manufacturer warranty

Why we say that

The 7,000 mAh cell pairs with 60 W fast charging, though the included 80 W brick cannot charge faster than the phone allows. Its IP69 rating and military‑grade shock resistance make it suitable for rough handling and outdoor work. The 6.81‑inch IPS LCD runs at 144 Hz, but the 720p resolution gives only about 260 ppi, so text and videos look less sharp than on FullHD panels.

Performance feels basic; the Dimensity 6300 chipset with eMMC storage leads to noticeable lag in heavy apps and games, and the rear 50 MP sensor is outperformed by the front camera. Software runs Android 15 with Realme UI 6.0, but medium‑high bloatware and ads add clutter, and updates are limited to two or three major versions. Because every unit is a gray import, there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh, only the shop’s limited guarantee, and any repair will cost extra.
