Is the Realme 15x worth buying?

What is good

  • Huge 7000 mAh battery
  • 60W fast charging
  • IP69 water‑dust protection
  • 1200 nits peak brightness

What is not

  • 720p resolution on 6.8‑inch screen
  • Mediocre camera quality
  • Outdated Dimensity 6300 for 2025 gaming
  • Software hangs after a month

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life and can tolerate a low‑resolution display.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect sharp visuals, strong gaming performance, or an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Owners love the massive 7000 mAh battery that easily lasts beyond a full day of browsing, streaming and light gaming. The 60W charger refills the huge cell quickly, and the IP69 rating protects the phone from rain and dust. However, the 6.81‑inch IPS LCD is stuck at HD+ resolution, giving a pixel density of only about 260 ppi, which makes text and videos look soft.

The Dimensity 6300 chipset feels dated for 2025 and struggles with demanding games, while the software can start hanging after a month of use. The phone is sold as gray‑import stock, so you only get a shop warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 7000 mAh battery receives a rating of 9.6 from owners and provides insane endurance, easily lasting more than a full day of normal browsing, video streaming and light gaming before needing a recharge.

Can the Realme 15x run modern mobile games smoothly?

The Dimensity 6300 chipset is adequate for basic titles but is considered outdated for 2025. Owner ratings show gaming performance around 4.2, so heavy or graphics‑intensive games will feel sluggish.

Is the camera usable in low‑light conditions?

Both rear and selfie cameras are 50 MP, but the rear sensor is smaller and reviews describe the overall camera quality as mediocre. Low‑light shots are especially disappointing, reflected in a camera rating of 4.8.

Will I get an official Realme warranty and regular software updates?

Only gray‑import stock is available, which comes with a shop warranty, not an official Realme warranty. You must confirm IMEI and BTRC status with the seller. The phone runs Android 15 with Realme UI 6.0, but users report hanging after a month and expect only 2‑3 OS updates, with medium‑high bloat and ads.
