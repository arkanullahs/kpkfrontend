Is the POCO C75 worth buying?

What is good

  • Efficient battery life
  • Large 120Hz display
  • Decent camera quality
  • Includes headphone jack and FM radio

What is not

  • Overheating during gaming
  • Slow performance and RAM issues
  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • No official brand warranty

Buy it if You need a cheap phone for basic tasks and value a large screen.

Skip it if You want to play games or need a device with official brand warranty.

Why we say that

While the large 120Hz screen and battery life are positive, owners report serious stability issues. The phone often auto restarts or reloads recent apps when you try to open them. Performance is slow and it is easy to run out of RAM.

When the RAM is full, the screen touch can become unresponsive. Gaming is a struggle as the device gets very hot after 30 minutes of play. You also have to deal with high amounts of bloatware and ads in the software.

Some users claim there are no updates for this phone. Since only gray imports are available, you will get a shop warranty instead of brand support.

What is the POCO C75 price in Bangladesh?

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there are no confirmed official units in stock. The listings we track are gray imports, which means you only get a shop warranty. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

Is the POCO C75 good for gaming?

It is not recommended for gaming. Owners report that the phone gets very hot after 30 minutes of gameplay. It also has a very low owner rating of 2.0 for gaming and suffers from slow performance.

How is the battery life on this device?

The battery life is a strength of this phone. It has a 5160 mAh battery that owners describe as efficient, and it received a rating of 7.5 from users.

Will I get software updates for this phone?

The software is HyperOS based on Android 14. While the specs mention 2 to 3 OS updates, some owners report that there are no updates for this phone.
