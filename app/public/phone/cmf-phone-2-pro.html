CMF Phone 2 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can survive a full day of heavy use without hunting for a charger, the CMF Phone 2 Pro delivers.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value long battery life, bright display and clean software more than flagship camera or fast charging.

What works well4

  • 5,000 mAh battery lasting over a day
  • 6.77‑inch AMOLED 120 Hz with 3,000 nits peak
  • Clean, bloat‑free Nothing OS
  • Strong 5G and network reception

Reported trade-offs4

  • 33 W charging feels slow
  • Main camera lacks OIS and shows pink cast
  • Overheating and occasional app crashes under load
  • Single mono speaker and glued back

Why we say that

The 5,000 mAh battery provides stellar endurance and easily lasts beyond a day, though the 33 W charger is slower than many rivals and some listings omit the charger. The 6.77‑inch AMOLED panel with 120 Hz refresh and 3,000 nits peak brightness makes media consumption pleasant, and Nothing OS feels clean and responsive. Daily performance on the Dimensity 7300 Pro is smooth, but heavy gaming or sustained workloads cause heating and occasional app crashes, and the gaming rating sits at a modest six out of ten.

The 50 MP main camera captures detail but suffers from pink color shifts, no optical image stabilization, and HDR settings that reset, limiting low‑light results. Audio is limited to a single mono speaker and the back is glued, making repairs harder. As a gray‑import, there is no official CMF warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s limited warranty and paid repairs.

How long does the battery last on the CMF Phone 2 Pro?

The 5,000 mAh battery gives stellar endurance and easily lasts a full day of typical use, even with video and browsing. Charging is limited to 33 W, which many reviewers find slower than competing phones in this price range.

Can the CMF Phone 2 Pro handle heavy gaming?

For everyday apps the Dimensity 7300 Pro chipset feels smooth, but heavy gaming pushes the phone and it can lag and heat up. Reviewers gave the gaming experience a modest 6 out of 10, so expect occasional frame drops.

How good is the camera in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor captures detailed photos but suffers without optical image stabilization, leading to blur in low light. Colors often shift pink and exposure can be uneven, and HDR settings reset after reopening the app.

Does the phone get official warranty and software updates in Bangladesh?

All units are gray‑import, so there is no official CMF warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s limited warranty and paid repairs. The device runs Nothing OS 3.2 on Android 15 and promises up to three major OS updates.
