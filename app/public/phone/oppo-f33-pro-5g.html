Is the OPPO F33 Pro 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 1400 nits AMOLED screen
  • Huge 7000 mAh battery
  • 80W fast charging
  • 5G connectivity

What is not

  • Camera quality low
  • Performance feels average
  • Build feels average
  • No official brand warranty

Buy it if Buy it if you value long screen time and a vivid display more than top tier camera performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need flagship level speed or high quality video recording.

Why we say that

The 6.57 inch AMOLED panel shines at 1400 nits and runs at 120Hz, making everyday scrolling feel smooth. The 7000 mAh cell combined with 80W charging means you can go long without a plug and top up quickly. The Dimensity 6360 Max chipset handles basic apps well, but owners report average performance in demanding games.

The 50MP main sensor and selfie lens are advertised as high resolution, yet real world tests describe mediocre results and no 4K video capability. Build quality receives average scores and there is no clear brand warranty, only shop warranty that requires IMEI verification. Software runs Android 16 with ColorOS 16, offering a few years of updates similar to other mid‑range devices.

Overall the phone feels like a solid media companion if you can tolerate its camera and speed limits.
