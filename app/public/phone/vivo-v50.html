Is the Vivo V50 worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 6000 mAh battery capacity
  • Extremely bright 4500 nits peak display
  • Durable IP69 rated build quality
  • Excellent Zeiss portrait and night mode

What is not

  • Outdated UFS 2.2 storage bottleneck
  • Video recording limited to 30fps
  • Virtual proximity sensor failure during calls
  • Medium bloatware and background audio bugs

Buy it if You prioritize extreme battery endurance and a highly durable, water-resistant build above all other technical specifications.

Skip it if You are a mobile gamer or a content creator who requires fast storage speeds and high frame rate video recording.

Why we say that

While the 6000 mAh battery offers top-tier endurance and the IP69 build quality feels premium, owners are vocal about significant drawbacks. The inclusion of outdated UFS 2.2 storage creates a noticeable performance bottleneck, and the lack of 60fps video recording is a major disappointment for a phone at this price point. Users also report a persistent issue with the virtual proximity sensor failing to turn off the screen during calls.

While the Zeiss camera system performs well for portraits and night shots, the software experience is marred by medium bloat and background audio bugs. Given that the newer Vivo V60 is available for a similar price of 50,500 taka, many buyers find the V50 difficult to justify. It is a phone that excels in raw battery capacity but falls short in the technical refinements expected by modern users.

How does the battery perform in real-world usage?

The phone features a massive 6000 mAh battery that provides top-tier real-world endurance. Owners rate the battery performance highly with a score of 9.2 out of 10, making it one of the most reliable aspects of the device.

Is the Vivo V50 suitable for heavy gaming?

While the Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 chipset is stable, the phone is not ideal for heavy gaming. Owners rate the gaming experience at only 5.5 out of 10, largely because the outdated UFS 2.2 storage acts as a major performance bottleneck.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 15 with Funtouch 15. It is promised to receive up to 3 major Android upgrades on the upper models, though budget variants are limited to 2 major OS updates.

Does the camera perform well in low light conditions?

The camera system is capable of excellent night mode photography and Zeiss portraits. However, users should be aware that some owners have reported focus blur issues, and the overall camera rating from users is 7.8 out of 10.
