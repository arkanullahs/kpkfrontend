Is the itel A200 Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • Long lasting 6000 mAh battery
  • IP65 dust and water resistance
  • Smooth 120 Hz display
  • Headphone jack and FM radio

What is not

  • Slow 18 W charging
  • Poor camera quality
  • Intrusive ads in apps
  • Laggy performance for games

Buy it if Buy it if you need a rugged phone with excellent battery life for basic calling, messaging and media playback.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect a sharp camera, fast gaming or an ad‑free Android experience.

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh battery is the star, easily lasting up to 48 hours on light use, while the IP65 rating and military‑grade certification protect it from dust and rain. The 6.75‑inch IPS LCD runs a smooth 120 Hz refresh, yet it looks dim in bright sunlight and the notch feels dated. Performance feels sluggish; multitasking and modern games lag, and the 13 MP camera struggles with dynamic range and sharpness, especially in low light.

Android 15 Go brings many pre‑installed apps and frequent intrusive ads, making YouTube and other services feel cluttered. Charging at 18 W is noticeably slow for such a large battery. Overall, it suits basic users who value endurance and ruggedness over speed, camera quality, and a clean software experience.

How long does the battery really last on a typical day?

Owners report the 6000 mAh battery can last up to two days with light usage such as calls, messaging and occasional video. Heavy gaming or streaming will reduce that time, but it still outlasts most budget phones.

Can the phone handle modern mobile games?

The device uses a Unisoc T7250 chipset with 4 GB RAM, which struggles with heavy titles. Reviews note lag in multitasking and inability to run demanding eSports games smoothly, so it is best for casual games only.

Is the camera usable in low‑light conditions?

The 13 MP main sensor has poor dynamic range and low sharpness, especially in dim environments. Users find photos lack detail and appear noisy, making it unsuitable for low‑light photography.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

The phone comes with the brand’s official Bangladesh warranty and can be serviced through the listed network. Software runs Android 15 Go with itelOS 15.1, but updates are rare and the system includes high‑level bloatware and ads.
