OnePlus Nord 5 Is it worth buying?

If you can live without a manufacturer warranty and need a fast 5G phone, the OnePlus Nord 5 gives flagship level speed at a gray import price.

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset delivers smooth performance for everyday tasks and most games, while the 6.83 inch Swift AMOLED panel offers 144 Hz refresh and 1800 nit peak brightness that stays readable in bright sunlight. The 5200 mAh battery paired with 80 W charging keeps the phone alive through a full day and refills quickly, and the 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures sharp photos in good light. Owners praise the build quality and display but note network connectivity problems, a weak ultrawide lens, and the fact that software updates have stopped.

Because every listing is a gray import, there is no official brand warranty; repairs must be paid to the shop that sold it. Overall the phone feels premium for its price but requires acceptance of these trade offs.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The Nord 5 uses a 5200 mAh battery that owners rate 8.2 out of 10 for endurance. It lasts through a full day of mixed use and recharges quickly with the 80 W charger, so most users find it sufficient.

Will the phone handle heavy gaming smoothly?

The Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset and 8 to 12 GB of RAM give the Nord 5 a performance rating of 8.0. Gaming scores are around 7.6, so most modern games run smoothly at medium settings.

Is the camera good in low light and how is the ultrawide lens?

The main 50 MP sensor with a 1/1.56 inch size, f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures sharp stills and videos in good light. In low light it performs reasonably, but owners say the ultrawide lens is meh and not reliable.

Does the Nord 5 come with an official warranty or future software updates?

All listings are gray imports, so the phone has no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop may offer its own limited cover, but repairs are paid. OxygenOS promises up to four major Android upgrades, yet owners report updates stopped since November.
