Is the OnePlus Nord 5 worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright and smooth 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Efficient and long lasting battery
  • Excellent main camera stills and videos
  • Low amount of bloatware and ads

What is not

  • Reports of stopped software updates
  • Network connectivity problems
  • No eSIM support
  • Mediocre ultrawide camera

Buy it if You want a high performance 5G phone with a great screen and fast 80W charging.

Skip it if You need a reliable software update schedule or eSIM support.

Why we say that

Owners appreciate the bright 144Hz AMOLED display and the efficient battery life. The Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset handles tasks well, and the main camera takes excellent stills. However, the experience is marred by reports of network connectivity problems and a lack of eSIM support.

Most concerning are the reports from owners who say their devices stopped receiving software updates since November. While the hardware is capable, these software and connectivity issues make it a risky purchase. You also have to choose between a cheaper unofficial unit or a more expensive official one.
