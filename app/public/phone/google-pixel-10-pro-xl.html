Is the Google Pixel 10 Pro XL worth buying?

What is good

  • Stunning 6.8 inch LTPO OLED display
  • Excellent camera processing and natural skin tones
  • Premium build with IP68 water resistance
  • Clean stock Android with long term updates

What is not

  • Thermal throttling and heating after short use
  • Network modem shows instability
  • Charging is much slower than 45W rating
  • Battery drains faster than flagship rivals

Buy it if Buy it if you value a gorgeous display, strong camera and clean software more than fast charging or heavy gaming.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable fast charging, heavy gaming performance or rock solid network stability.

Why we say that

The 6.8 inch LTPO OLED panel shines with 120 Hz refresh and peak brightness of 3300 nits, making outdoor viewing easy. The 50 MP main sensor paired with Google’s software gives natural colors and good skin tones, while the 42 MP selfie camera is also strong. Build quality feels premium and the IP68 rating adds peace of mind.

Software is clean stock Android 16 with up to seven major upgrades, so the phone stays fresh for years. However, the Tensor G5 chip is not power efficient, leading to faster battery drain than other flagships and a modest battery rating of six. Thermal throttling appears after fifteen minutes, with the device reaching 40 °C and sometimes feeling hotter, which also hurts gaming performance.

The network modem can be unstable and the 45W charger charges far slower than expected. Overall the phone feels high end but shows its limits in endurance, heat and fast charging.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

The 5200 mAh battery gives decent capacity but owners report average endurance and faster drain than other flagships because the Tensor G5 is not very efficient. Expect a full day of moderate use, not a long weekend.

Can I play demanding games without issues?

Heavy gaming is a weak point. The device heats up quickly, reaches around 40 °C after fifteen minutes, and throttles performance. Most owners find it unsuitable for demanding titles, though casual games run fine.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS and Google’s processing produces good low light photos, especially skin tones. Portrait edge detection can miss some subjects, but overall image quality remains high for everyday use.

Will I receive Android updates for many years?

Pixel 10 Pro XL ships with Android 16 and promises up to seven major Android upgrades. The same update policy that gave Pixel 8+ seven years of support applies, so you can expect long term software freshness.
