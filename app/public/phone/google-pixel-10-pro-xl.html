Is the Google Pixel 10 Pro XL worth buying?

What is good

  • Stunning 6.8 inch LTPO OLED display
  • Excellent camera processing and natural skin tones
  • Premium build with IP68 water resistance
  • Clean stock Android with long term updates

What is not

  • Thermal throttling and heating after short use
  • Network modem shows instability
  • Charging is much slower than 45W rating
  • Battery drains faster than flagship rivals

Buy it if Buy it if you value a gorgeous display, strong camera and clean software more than fast charging or heavy gaming.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable fast charging, heavy gaming performance or rock solid network stability.

Why we say that

The 6.8 inch LTPO OLED panel shines with 120 Hz refresh and peak brightness of 3300 nits, making outdoor viewing easy. The 50 MP main sensor paired with Google’s software gives natural colors and good skin tones, while the 42 MP selfie camera is also strong. Build quality feels premium and the IP68 rating adds peace of mind.

Software is clean stock Android 16 with up to seven major upgrades, so the phone stays fresh for years. However, the Tensor G5 chip is not power efficient, leading to faster battery drain than other flagships and a modest battery rating of six. Thermal throttling appears after fifteen minutes, with the device reaching 40 °C and sometimes feeling hotter, which also hurts gaming performance.

The network modem can be unstable and the 45W charger charges far slower than expected. Overall the phone feels high end but shows its limits in endurance, heat and fast charging.

Marques Brownlee · Pixel 10/Pro Review: Good News and Bad News! ↗

Marques concludes that the Pixel 10 Pro XL is a familiar mixed bag that retains both the magic and the flaws of its predecessors. While he highly praises the smooth Android 16 software and the brilliant addition of MagSafe-style magnetic charging, he expresses disappointment in the phone's raw performance and slipping camera dominance. He notes that the new Tensor G5 chip still acts more like a mid-range processor, and the camera's zoom capabilities fall surprisingly short compared to current flagship rivals.

Mrwhosetheboss · Google Pixel 10 / 10 Pro Hands on - Too Much Google? ↗

Arun feels that Google's latest offering is a safe but frustratingly incomplete device that leans too heavily on unfinished software. He appreciates hardware bumps like the brighter screen, bigger battery, and magnetic charging, but is highly critical of the buggy AI features, poor portrait mode, and a base storage of just 128GB on the standard Pro. Ultimately, he argues that the phone's hardware significantly underdelivers for its premium price tag.

Technology Gyan · Google Pixel 10 Pro XL Unboxing & Test - The Best Android Phone? ↗

Manoj highlights the phone's undeniable premium feel, stunningly bright display, and incredibly smooth software experience that remains unmatched in the Android world. However, he is let down by the persistent thermal throttling issues with the new Tensor chip, noting that the phone still heats up significantly during gaming despite the move to TSMC manufacturing. He views it as a great device for everyday users who value a refined UI and capable cameras, but questions its high asking price.

ATC Android ToTo Company · Pixel 10 Pro XL impression - এটাই বাস্তব 🤣 ↗

Tushar issues a strong warning for buyers in Bangladesh to avoid the eSIM-only US variants and opt for international models instead to retain physical SIM support. He finds the phone's general interface smooth and the display excellent, but harshly criticizes its terrible gaming performance, noting that the device gets physically uncomfortable to hold within minutes. He remains skeptical of the heavily marketed AI features—which are largely unusable in the region—and concludes that it fails to justify its flagship expectations.

GSMArena Official · Google Pixel 10 Pro XL review ↗

Will points out that despite minor hardware additions like magnetic wireless charging, the Pixel 10 Pro XL struggles to keep up with the competition in crucial areas. He praises the dependable main camera and excellent display, but is heavily disappointed by the shockingly poor battery endurance and slow charging speeds. With severe thermal throttling limiting its performance in stress tests, he concludes that buyers can easily find better value from other brands in this price bracket.

Trakin Tech English · Pixel 10 Pro XL Detailed Review - Underrated? Ft. Comparison vs Galaxy S25 Ultra ↗

Ashad concludes that the Pixel 10 Pro XL is a refined but polarizing alternative to the Galaxy S25 Ultra, shining in subtle areas but dropping the ball in others. He praises its superior haptics, richer speakers, and natural HDR processing on the main camera, but harshly criticizes its underpowered gaming performance and terrible portrait mode. Ultimately, he feels that with many AI features region-locked at launch, it struggles to offer the comprehensive value of its Samsung rival.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

The 5200 mAh battery gives decent capacity but owners report average endurance and faster drain than other flagships because the Tensor G5 is not very efficient. Expect a full day of moderate use, not a long weekend.

Can I play demanding games without issues?

Heavy gaming is a weak point. The device heats up quickly, reaches around 40 °C after fifteen minutes, and throttles performance. Most owners find it unsuitable for demanding titles, though casual games run fine.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS and Google’s processing produces good low light photos, especially skin tones. Portrait edge detection can miss some subjects, but overall image quality remains high for everyday use.

Will I receive Android updates for many years?

Pixel 10 Pro XL ships with Android 16 and promises up to seven major Android upgrades. The same update policy that gave Pixel 8+ seven years of support applies, so you can expect long term software freshness.
