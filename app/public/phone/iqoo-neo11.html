iQOO Neo 11 Is it worth buying?

If you can live without a Bangladeshi manufacturer warranty and need a phone that can run games for hours, the iQOO Neo 11 delivers that with a massive battery and flagship chipset.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life, high‑refresh display and gaming performance and are comfortable with a gray‑import warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need an official warranty, fast USB data transfer, or a top‑tier camera for low‑light photography.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 7,500 mAh battery lasts all day
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite gives flagship performance
  • 144 Hz LTPO AMOLED display stays bright outdoors
  • 100 W fast charging refills quickly

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official Bangladeshi warranty, only shop warranty
  • USB‑C port limited to USB 2.0 speed
  • Notification delays and software bloat
  • Heating during long gaming or camera use

Why we say that

The 7,500 mAh cell gives industry‑leading endurance, and the 100 W charger refills it quickly. Snapdragon 8 Elite paired with 12 GB RAM makes heavy games feel smooth and the 144 Hz LTPO AMOLED panel stays bright outdoors. The metal frame feels solid, and the IR blaster adds a handy remote control option.

However, the device arrives as a gray‑import with only the shop’s limited warranty, and repairs are paid. The USB‑C port is limited to USB 2.0 speeds, and owners notice notification delays and noticeable heating during long gaming sessions. The 50 MP main camera is capable but lacks a telephoto lens and the ultrawide is basic, while OriginOS (CN) brings bloatware and Chinese‑market restrictions.
