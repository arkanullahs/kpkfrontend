Why we say that

The 6.78 inch AMOLED panel shows vivid colours at 120Hz and can peak at 4500 nits, making outdoor viewing easy. The Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset handles everyday apps smoothly and keeps temperatures steady, but it falters when you push high FPS games. The 50MP main sensor with optical image stabilization delivers sharp daylight photos and a useful 3.5x telephoto, yet indoor motion shots and low light scenes are disappointing.

Nothing OS 4.1 feels clean and fast, with no ads or bloat, though you will not receive any official service support in Bangladesh. All units are gray imports built for India, so there is no manufacturer warranty, only the shop’s limited cover and paid repairs. Confirm the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

Owners rate the battery at 8.2 out of 10 and say the 5080 mAh cell gives strong real‑world endurance. The 50W charger refills the phone quickly, though it is not the fastest on the market.

Can I play high FPS games smoothly?

The Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 handles daily tasks well and keeps temperatures stable, but owners report the phone struggles with high FPS gaming. Gaming performance is rated around 6.2 out of 10.

How does the camera perform in low light or indoor settings?

The 50MP sensor with OIS and 3.5x telephoto works well in daylight, but indoor motion shots and low light images are poor. Users describe indoor camera results as terrible for moving subjects.

Will I get official warranty and service support in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray imports built for India, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Only the shop’s limited warranty applies and any repairs must be paid. Nothing has little official service presence locally.
