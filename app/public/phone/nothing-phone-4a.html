Nothing Phone 4a Is it worth buying?

If you can live without an official Bangladeshi warranty, the Nothing Phone 4a offers a smooth display and clean software at a mid‑range price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a stylish Android with a smooth display, decent camera and all‑day battery at a mid‑range price and can accept gray‑import warranty limits.
Consider alternatives if
You need strong gaming performance, reliable indoor photography, or official brand service in Bangladesh.

What works well4

  • 120 Hz AMOLED display with high peak brightness
  • Clean Nothing OS with no bloatware
  • 50 MP main camera with telephoto and ultrawide lenses
  • 5080 mAh battery lasting all day

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Thick bezels and plastic frame feel dated
  • Indoor motion blur on camera
  • Gaming performance only acceptable

Why we say that

The 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 4500 nits, making everyday use bright and fluid. Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 and 8 GB RAM deliver steady performance for apps and casual games, though heavy gaming will feel limited. The 5080 mAh battery comfortably lasts a day, but the 50 W charger is only average for the price tag.

Camera results are decent in daylight with a 50 MP sensor, 3.5× telephoto and ultrawide lens, yet indoor motion blur is noticeable and low‑light video scores low. The design is distinctive but the plastic frame, IP64 rating and thick bezels feel dated for a phone above 40 k taka. Because every unit is a gray import, you rely on the shop’s warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How does the phone perform for gaming?
