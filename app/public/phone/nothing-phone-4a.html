Is the Nothing Phone 4a worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz AMOLED display with 4500 nits peak
  • Clean Nothing OS with no bloatware
  • Distinctive Glyph back design
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Severe shutter lag on indoor moving subjects
  • Thick body and only IP64 rating
  • Performance feels modest for price
  • Shop‑only warranty, no official service

Buy it if Buy if you want a striking look, vibrant screen, and clean software and can live with modest performance and limited warranty.

Skip it if Skip if you need top‑tier gaming, reliable indoor camera or official brand service in Bangladesh.

Why we say that

It ships with a 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel that runs at 120 Hz and can peak at 4500 nits, so outdoor visibility and scrolling are excellent. The Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 paired with 8 GB RAM gives acceptable day‑to‑day performance but many owners notice the chipset feels underpowered for heavy gaming or multitasking, especially at its price. Battery life scores 7.2 out of 10; the 5080 mAh cell lasts a full day for most users, yet the phone’s thickness and the modest 5,000 mAh capacity compared to its size draw criticism.

Camera reviewers praise the 50 MP main sensor and 3.5× telephoto zoom, but indoor moving subjects suffer severe shutter lag and artifacts, making low‑light video practically unusable. The Glyph back is distinctive, though the IP64 rating feels insufficient for some. Because the unit is only available as a gray‑import, warranty is limited to the shop you buy from and official service is practically absent in Bangladesh.
