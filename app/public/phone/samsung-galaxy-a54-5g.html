  • Severe 5G and signal drop issues
  • Phone heats up during gaming or video streaming

Why we say that

The phone feels premium with glass back and IP67 protection, and the 50 MP main sensor captures sharp photos in good light. Battery endurance is strong, yet the supplied 25 W charger is missing and the charge speed feels sluggish. Owners report random restarts after the Android 13 update and frequent 5G signal drops that can interrupt calls.

Gaming and continuous video streaming cause the device to heat up and throttle performance. Software updates are promised for up to four Android versions, but at three years old the remaining update window is short. Overall it offers a solid camera and display for its price, but the network reliability and charging experience may disappoint.
