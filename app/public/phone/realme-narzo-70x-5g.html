Buying verdict

Realme Narzo 70x 5G Is it worth buying?

If you want a 5G phone with a bright 120 Hz screen and fast charging without spending a fortune, the Realme Narzo 70x 5G fits the bill.

Choose it if
You need a budget 5G phone with a smooth everyday experience and a bright high‑refresh display.
Consider alternatives if
You require reliable low‑light photography or an official brand warranty.

What works well

  • 120 Hz display with 950 nits brightness
  • 45 W fast charging
  • Dimensity 6100+ handles daily tasks
  • Large 5000 mAh battery

Reported trade-offs

  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Camera lacks OIS and low‑light performance
  • High‑graphics games lag
  • Battery endurance varies

Why we say that

The 6.72‑inch IPS LCD reaches 950 nits, so outdoor use feels clear even in sunlight. The Dimensity 6100+ chipset and 6 GB RAM handle everyday apps, social media and video streaming smoothly. Battery life is mixed; many users enjoy a full day, but a few report it dropping after 24 hours of moderate use.

Charging at 45 W refills the 5000 mAh cell in about two hours, which is quick for this price range. The 50 MP main camera captures decent daylight shots but lacks OIS and struggles in low light. Gaming is acceptable for casual titles but high‑graphics games show lag.

Read full reasoning

The phone arrives as a gray‑import, so there is no official Realme warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited warranty and paid repairs. Overall it offers strong value for its display and charging speed, but you must accept the warranty limitation and modest camera performance.
