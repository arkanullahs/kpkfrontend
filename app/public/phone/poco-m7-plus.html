Is the POCO M7 Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • 7000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 50 MP main camera for bright scenes
  • 144 Hz smooth display
  • 5G connectivity

What is not

  • No gyroscope hurts gaming
  • HyperOS 2 heavy with ads
  • Thick bezels and large size
  • Dim panel despite high refresh

Buy it if Choose this phone if you value long battery life, a high refresh display and basic 5G use over top tier gaming or photography.

Skip it if Avoid it if you need premium camera performance, a sleek design or a clean, low‑ad software experience.

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh battery gives industry‑leading endurance and the 33W charger tops up reasonably fast. The 6.9‑inch IPS LCD runs at 144 Hz, so scrolling feels smooth even if the panel is not very bright at 850 nits. Performance comes from a Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 chip and 6 GB RAM; it handles everyday apps fine but shows its limits in newer games, especially without a gyroscope.

The 50 MP main camera captures decent images in good light but lacks OIS, making low‑light shots basic. HyperOS 2 adds many pre‑installed apps and ads, which many owners find intrusive. Build quality feels solid but the thick bezels and large footprint make the phone bulky, and speakers are reported as weak.
