What works well4

  • 7000 mAh battery lasts over two days
  • 6.9‑inch 144 Hz bright LCD screen
  • Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 handles daily tasks
  • IR blaster for remote control

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty
  • Heavy and bulky design
  • Low‑light camera produces noisy images
  • No gyroscope and no 3.5 mm jack

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh silicon‑carbon battery is the strongest point and it easily stretches beyond 48 hours of mixed use. The 6.9‑inch IPS LCD with 144 Hz refresh and 850 nits peak brightness makes media consumption comfortable, though the lack of AMOLED contrast is noticeable. Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 runs Android 15 smoothly for everyday apps, but gaming is limited to low settings and the missing gyroscope hurts motion‑based titles.

The 50 MP main sensor captures decent daylight photos but struggles in low light, showing visible noise. HyperOS 2 feels fluid but is cluttered with ads and bloatware. The phone arrives as a gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty; only the shop’s limited warranty applies and repairs are paid.

Read full reasoning

The IP64 rating protects against splashes, yet the device feels heavy and bulky, making one‑hand use difficult.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 7000 mAh battery provides more than two days of mixed usage without charging. Light browsing and messaging may stretch to three days, while heavy video streaming will bring it closer to a full day.

Can I play modern games smoothly on this phone?

Casual games run fine, but the Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 struggles with demanding titles. You will need to lower graphics settings and the missing gyroscope limits motion‑controlled games.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

Daylight photos are decent with the 50 MP sensor, but low‑light shots show noticeable noise and lack detail. Owners rate the camera around five out of ten.

Will I get any official warranty or software updates?

All listings are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop offers its own limited warranty and the phone receives up to two major Android upgrades under HyperOS 2.
