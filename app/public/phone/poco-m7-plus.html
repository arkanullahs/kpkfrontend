Is the POCO M7 Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • Exceptional two-day battery endurance
  • Large 6.9 inch 144Hz display
  • Useful 18W reverse wired charging
  • IP64 dust and water resistance

What is not

  • Missing gyroscope and headphone jack
  • Excessively heavy and bulky design
  • Poor speaker quality with crackling sounds
  • High amount of ads and bloatware

Buy it if You need a phone that lasts over two days on a single charge and you do not mind a heavy device.

Skip it if You are a mobile gamer or someone who values high-quality audio and a compact, lightweight phone.

Why we say that

With a massive 7000 mAh battery, it offers exceptional two-day usage, but this comes at the cost of a heavy, bulky frame that many users find difficult to handle with one hand. While the 144Hz display is large and bright, it lacks the contrast of AMOLED panels. Performance is strictly for casual use, as the Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 struggles with gaming and lacks a gyroscope.

Owners frequently complain about the muffled speaker quality, the absence of a 3.5mm headphone jack, and the intrusive ads found within HyperOS 2. The camera setup is limited to decent daylight shots, with low-light performance suffering from significant noise. If you prioritize battery life above all else, this phone delivers, but the lack of essential sensors and the heavy build make it a polarizing choice for daily use.
