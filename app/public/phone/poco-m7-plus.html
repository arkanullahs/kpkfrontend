Buying verdict

POCO M7 Plus Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run for two days on a single charge, the POCO M7 Plus delivers that endurance at a low price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize massive battery life, a large bright screen, and 5G connectivity and can accept bulk, limited gaming and no official warranty.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 7000 mAh battery lasts over two days
  • 6.9‑inch 144 Hz screen is bright and smooth
  • 50 MP main camera captures decent daylight photos
  • 5G support and IR blaster add extra utility

Reported trade-offs4

  • Very heavy and bulky, hard to use one‑handed
  • No gyroscope limits motion games
  • No 3.5 mm headphone jack and speakers sound muffled
  • 33 W charging is slow for 7000 mAh battery

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh silicon‑carbon battery easily exceeds 48 hours of normal use, and the 6.9‑inch 144 Hz IPS LCD stays bright even in sunlight with 850 nits peak. The Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 and 6 GB RAM keep everyday apps smooth, but gaming is restricted to low settings and the missing gyroscope prevents motion‑based titles. Photos are acceptable in daylight thanks to a 50 MP main sensor, yet low‑light images are noisy and lack detail.

The phone feels heavy and bulky, making one‑handed use uncomfortable, and it lacks a 3.5 mm jack while the speakers sound muffled. Charging at 33 W is slower than expected for such a large battery, and HyperOS 2 adds noticeable ads and bloatware. Because every unit is a gray‑import, there is no official manufacturer warranty; only the shop’s limited coverage applies, and any repair costs are out‑of‑pocket.

How long does the POCO M7 Plus battery last in real use?

The 7000 mAh silicon‑carbon battery gives more than two days of normal use, and many owners report lasting over 48 hours on a single charge. Heavy use will drop the time to around a day.

Can I play games on the POCO M7 Plus without lag?

The Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 with 6 GB RAM handles everyday apps smoothly, but gaming is limited to low graphics settings. Lack of a gyroscope and modest GPU mean fast‑paced titles will feel constrained.

Is the camera good for night or low‑light photos?

The 50 MP f/1.8 main sensor produces decent photos in daylight, but low‑light shots show noticeable noise and lack detail. Reviewers rate the camera around five out of ten, so expect modest performance at night.

Will the phone come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray‑import, so the phone arrives without any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop may offer its own limited warranty, but repairs are paid and you must verify IMEI and BTRC status.
