Is the Realme P3 Lite worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • 45 W fast charging fills in about 75 minutes
  • 5G connectivity at an ultra‑budget price
  • IP64 build with headphone jack and FM radio

What is not

  • HD+ 720p screen looks fuzzy at 120 Hz
  • Camera produces reddish skin tones and feels cheap
  • Heavy bloatware forces you to uninstall many apps

Buy it if Buy it if you need 5G and long battery life on a tight budget.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect a high‑resolution display or a camera for serious photography.

Why we say that

In everyday use the 6.67" IPS LCD shows smooth 120 Hz motion but the HD+ resolution makes text and circles look fuzzy. The 32 MP main sensor captures decent daylight shots, yet skin tones turn reddish and the camera bump feels deceptive. Performance feels fine for social media and light apps, but multitasking and gaming feel sluggish despite a 4.8 rating.

The 45 W charger refills the 6000 mAh pack in about 75 minutes, though the first boot is cluttered with medium‑high bloatware that must be removed one by one. Build quality is solid with IP64 protection, a headphone jack and FM radio, but the phone arrives as gray‑import stock with only a shop warranty, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
