Buying verdict

Realme C100i Is it worth buying?

The Realme C100i is a battery-focused device that prioritizes longevity over performance.

Choose it if
You prioritize extreme battery life for basic daily tasks and do not mind slow charging speeds.
Consider alternatives if
You play games, care about camera quality, or expect a fast and modern user experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well

  • Massive 7000 mAh battery capacity
  • Smooth 120Hz display refresh rate
  • Durable IP64 rating and Armor Shell
  • Includes a convenient headphone jack

Reported trade-offs

  • Extremely slow 15W charging speed
  • Dated 720p display resolution
  • Slow eMMC storage performance
  • Poor low light camera quality

Why we say that

Owners consistently praise the massive 7000 mAh battery, which earns a high 9.2 rating, but they are frustrated by the painfully slow 15W charging speed that takes a long time to fill such a large capacity. While the 120Hz display offers smooth scrolling, users frequently complain about the dated 720p resolution and the notch design. The Unisoc T7250 chipset combined with eMMC storage results in a sluggish experience for anything beyond basic tasks, reflected in a low 3.5 rating for gaming and 3.8 for performance.

The 8MP camera is strictly for daylight use, as it loses significant detail in low light. You will also encounter medium to high levels of bloatware and ads within the Realme UI. If you need a phone that lasts all day and do not mind waiting hours for it to charge, this might serve your needs, but power users will find the hardware limitations difficult to live with.

How long will the Realme C100i receive software updates?

The device is supported by Realme UI and is scheduled to receive between 2 to 3 OS updates. You should expect a medium to high amount of bloatware and advertisements pre-installed on the software.

Is the Realme C100i suitable for mobile gaming?

This phone is not recommended for gaming as it holds a low 3.5 rating from owners. The combination of the Unisoc T7250 chipset and slow eMMC storage makes the device strictly suitable for basic tasks only.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The 8MP main camera is considered basic and is only usable in daylight. Owners report that the camera loses significant detail when you try to take photos in low light environments.

Why is the official warranty important for this phone?

The brand warranty applies only to the official unit purchased at the official price. Cheaper listings are gray imports that have no manufacturer cover, meaning any service center will only accept them for paid repairs.
