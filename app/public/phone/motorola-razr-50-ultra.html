Is the Motorola Razr 50 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 165Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • Durable hinge and IPX8 rating
  • Near stock software with low bloat
  • Strong performance and build quality

What is not

  • Heavy thermal throttling
  • Battery efficiency is lower than expected
  • No ultrawide camera
  • No official brand warranty in Bangladesh

Buy it if You want a foldable with a high quality screen and a durable build.

Skip it if You prioritize official brand warranty or need a phone for heavy gaming without throttling.

Why we say that

It features a very bright 165Hz LTPO AMOLED display and a durable hinge with an IPX8 rating. Owners generally like the performance and build quality. However, there are serious concerns regarding heat.

Users report heavy thermal throttling during use. The battery is not as efficient as expected, which is a common complaint. You get a near stock software experience with low bloatware, but Motorola has a history of weak updates.

While it supports up to 3 major Android upgrades, the actual delivery may be inconsistent. You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No official brand backed warranty is available. The units found in shops we track are imports. You will only get a shop warranty, so you must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

How is the battery life and charging?

The phone has a 4000 mAh battery with 45W charging. Owners rate the battery 7.5 out of 10 and report that it is not as efficient as they expected.

Is this a good phone for gaming?

It uses the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset, but owners report heavy thermal throttling. Gaming is rated 7.0 out of 10 by users because of these heat issues.

How many software updates will it get?

The phone comes with Android 14 and is promised up to 3 major Android upgrades. However, the evidence notes that Motorola has been historically weak with software updates.
