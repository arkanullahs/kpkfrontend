Samsung Galaxy S26 Plus Is it worth buying?

If you want a flagship that handles games and multitasking without lag, the Galaxy S26 Plus delivers.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a powerful, large‑screen phone for gaming and heavy multitasking and can accept slower charging and a premium price.
Consider alternatives if
You prioritize fast charging, advanced display color support, or a lower‑priced flagship with similar specs.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 120Hz LTPO AMOLED display with 2600‑nits peak
  • Exynos 2600 delivers top‑tier performance
  • 12 GB RAM with up to 512 GB storage
  • Clean One UI with up to 7 years updates

Reported trade-offs4

  • 45W charging feels slow
  • No 10‑bit color or Dolby Vision
  • 4900 mAh battery seen as modest
  • Night mode is unintuitive

Why we say that

The Exynos 2600 SoC and 12 GB RAM keep performance smooth even under heavy loads. The 6.7‑inch LTPO AMOLED panel shines with 2600‑nits peak brightness and a fluid 120 Hz refresh rate, though it lacks 10‑bit color depth and Dolby Vision. Battery endurance is praised despite the 4900 mAh cell, but many owners note the capacity feels modest for 2026 and the 45 W charger feels slow compared to rivals.

Build quality feels premium and the IP68 rating is now considered basic. Camera hardware is solid with OIS and a 3× telephoto lens, yet night mode can be confusing and overall image quality trails the S25 Ultra/Plus. Software is clean One UI 8.5 with up to seven years of updates, but the price is high for the feature set.

TTrakin Tech · Samsung Galaxy S26 & S26+ Unboxing & Quick Review ⚡ Exynos 2600 Tested! ↗

Trakin Tech views the S26 Plus as an incremental but solid update, praising the highly capable Exynos 2600 chipset which rivals top-tier competitors in raw performance. They appreciate the bright display, dependable battery, and smart new AI features, though they note it misses out on premium Ultra features like the privacy display and major camera hardware upgrades.

TTech Spurt · Samsung Galaxy S26 Plus Review | Upgrade to Ultra? ↗

Tech Spurt finds the phone perfectly fine but heavily criticizes the high price tag given the lack of meaningful hardware changes. While praising the gorgeous display and new software additions like call screening, they were disappointed by the recycled cameras struggling with moving subjects, the lack of an anti-reflective screen, and the rapid battery drain during intense gaming.

VVersus · Galaxy S26 vs iPhone 17: We didn't expect that! ↗

Versus was genuinely shocked by the Exynos chipset's ability to thoroughly beat the latest iPhone in multi-core and graphical stress tests. Despite this major performance win and delivering better telephoto portraits, they advise waiting for a discount, concluding that the recycled camera lenses, lack of built-in magnets, and higher price tag make it a poor value at launch.
