Samsung Galaxy S26 Plus Is it worth buying?

The Samsung Galaxy S26 Plus offers minimal generational improvements, making it a difficult recommendation for those seeking value.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You are a dedicated Samsung user who prioritizes long-term software support and stable gaming performance above all else.
Consider alternatives if
You want a device that offers a noticeable upgrade over the previous generation or better value for your money.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Stable performance under heavy gaming loads
  • Polished One UI 8.5 software
  • Excellent 7-year update support
  • Gimbal-like OIS and Horizon Lock

Reported trade-offs4

  • Underwhelming 4900 mAh battery capacity
  • Thick display bezels and polarizing design
  • Extremely poor value for money
  • Minimal performance gains over previous generation

Why we say that

While the Exynos 2600 chipset provides stable performance and gaming, owners report that the device is only 1 to 5 percent faster than the previous model despite the high price. The 4900 mAh battery is widely considered insufficient for a 2026 flagship, leading to significant user frustration. Aesthetics are another point of contention, as many owners dislike the camera bump and note that the display bezels appear thicker than the previous generation.

While the software experience is polished with 7 years of updates, the overall consensus is that the S25+ and S25 Ultra remain better financial choices. You are essentially paying a premium for a device that feels like a very minor update over its predecessor.

Trakin Tech · Samsung Galaxy S26 & S26+ Unboxing & Quick Review ⚡ Exynos 2600 Tested! ↗

Trakin Tech views the S26 Plus as an incremental but solid update, praising the highly capable Exynos 2600 chipset which rivals top-tier competitors in raw performance. They appreciate the bright display, dependable battery, and smart new AI features, though they note it misses out on premium Ultra features like the privacy display and major camera hardware upgrades.

Tech Spurt · Samsung Galaxy S26 Plus Review | Upgrade to Ultra? ↗

Tech Spurt finds the phone perfectly fine but heavily criticizes the high price tag given the lack of meaningful hardware changes. While praising the gorgeous display and new software additions like call screening, they were disappointed by the recycled cameras struggling with moving subjects, the lack of an anti-reflective screen, and the rapid battery drain during intense gaming.

Versus · Galaxy S26 vs iPhone 17: We didn't expect that! ↗

Versus was genuinely shocked by the Exynos chipset's ability to thoroughly beat the latest iPhone in multi-core and graphical stress tests. Despite this major performance win and delivering better telephoto portraits, they advise waiting for a discount, concluding that the recycled camera lenses, lack of built-in magnets, and higher price tag make it a poor value at launch.
