Is the Samsung Galaxy S26 Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • Elite 2nm Exynos 2600 performance
  • Seven-year software update promise
  • Premium build quality
  • Very good battery endurance

What is not

  • Slow 45W charging speeds
  • Unintuitive camera Night mode
  • Lacks 10-bit display depth
  • Minor iterative upgrade

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize long-term software support and a reliable, cool-running flagship experience from Samsung.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you want fast charging speeds or the latest display technologies like 10-bit color depth.

Why we say that

Living with the device reveals a premium build and elite performance from the Exynos 2600 chipset, which runs cooler than previous generations. However, owners frequently express frustration with the 45W charging speed, which lags significantly behind Chinese competitors and the S26 Ultra. While the 4900 mAh battery provides very good endurance, some users feel the capacity is low for a 2026 flagship.

The camera system features a refreshed GNG sensor that performs well, but the Night mode interface is unintuitive to use. The display is bright and responsive, yet it disappoints power users by lacking 10-bit depth and Dolby Vision support. Ultimately, while the seven-year update promise adds long-term value, the current market consensus is that this phone does not offer enough new features to justify its high price tag.

The reviews

Samsung Galaxy S26 Plus review: Déjà vu?GSMArena Official · 52K views

Trakin Tech · Samsung Galaxy S26 & S26+ Unboxing & Quick Review ⚡ Exynos 2600 Tested! ↗

Trakin Tech views the S26 Plus as an incremental but solid update, praising the highly capable Exynos 2600 chipset which rivals top-tier competitors in raw performance. They appreciate the bright display, dependable battery, and smart new AI features, though they note it misses out on premium Ultra features like the privacy display and major camera hardware upgrades.

Tech Spurt · Samsung Galaxy S26 Plus Review | Upgrade to Ultra? ↗

Tech Spurt finds the phone perfectly fine but heavily criticizes the high price tag given the lack of meaningful hardware changes. While praising the gorgeous display and new software additions like call screening, they were disappointed by the recycled cameras struggling with moving subjects, the lack of an anti-reflective screen, and the rapid battery drain during intense gaming.

GSMArena Official · Samsung Galaxy S26 Plus review: Déjà vu? ↗

GSMArena highlights the surprisingly improved battery life and solid sustained CPU performance of the new Exynos chip. However, they ultimately question the phone's value proposition, noting that since the design, display technology, and cameras are virtually identical to last year's model, buyers are paying a premium for very few tangible upgrades.

Versus · Galaxy S26 vs iPhone 17: We didn't expect that! ↗

Versus was genuinely shocked by the Exynos chipset's ability to thoroughly beat the latest iPhone in multi-core and graphical stress tests. Despite this major performance win and delivering better telephoto portraits, they advise waiting for a discount, concluding that the recycled camera lenses, lack of built-in magnets, and higher price tag make it a poor value at launch.
