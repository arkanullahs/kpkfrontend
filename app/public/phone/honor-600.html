Honor 600 5G Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day without charging, the Honor 600 5G delivers that.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 7,000 mAh battery with 80 W fast charge
  • 200 MP main camera with OIS
  • 6.57 inch AMOLED display with high peak brightness
  • IP68/69K water and dust protection

Reported trade-offs4

  • Software updates are inconsistent across regions
  • USB C data speed is slow
  • Performance feels modest for the price
  • No telephoto lens for distant shots

Why we say that

The 7,000 mAh battery paired with 80 W fast charging lets you stay unplugged for a full day and then top up in minutes. The 200 MP main sensor with optical image stabilization captures detailed photos and the 6.57 inch AMOLED panel offers bright colors and a peak brightness that can outshine most indoor lighting. The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset runs everyday apps smoothly, and the device is protected by an IP68/69K rating and even includes an IR blaster.

Owners praise the endurance and screen but complain about the slow USB C 2.0 transfer speeds, a software update schedule that varies by region, and performance that feels modest for the price. The lack of a telephoto lens also limits distant photography.

How long will the battery last on typical daily use?

The 7,000 mAh cell provides industry leading endurance, and owners rate battery life at 9.4 out of 10. Most users can get through a full day of mixed usage without reaching the charger.

Will this phone handle modern games smoothly?

The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset is stable for everyday tasks, but gaming scores are around 6.5 and performance is described as modest for the price. Expect decent frame rates at medium settings, not ultra high.

How does the camera perform in low light situations?

The 200 MP sensor with f/1.9 aperture and OIS captures good portraits, but the phone lacks a telephoto lens and edge detection can struggle. Low‑light photos are acceptable but not exceptional.

What warranty and software update support can I expect?

Official units priced from ৳59,999 include the brand warranty, while cheaper gray imports have no manufacturer cover and only paid repairs. Software receives three to four major Android upgrades, but update timing is inconsistent and varies by region.
