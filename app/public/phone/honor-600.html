Is the Honor 600 worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 7000 mAh battery capacity
  • Top-tier IP68 and IP69K resistance
  • Extremely bright 8000 nits display
  • High-resolution 200MP main camera

What is not

  • Inadequate Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset
  • Poor MagicOS 10 user experience
  • Lacks a dedicated telephoto lens
  • Conflicting software update promises

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize extreme battery life and a durable, water-resistant build above gaming performance.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a mobile gamer or expect a smooth, bloat-free software experience for your investment.

Why we say that

Owners appreciate the thin and light build that includes IP68 and IP69K water and dust resistance. However, the daily experience is hampered by a MagicOS 10 interface that many users find difficult to navigate. While the 200MP main camera captures high-quality images, the lack of a telephoto lens limits versatility.

Performance in gaming is rated poorly, and the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset is widely considered inadequate for a device costing up to 67,999 taka. Software support remains a major point of contention, as owners report regional disparities where users outside the EU receive fewer updates than promised. If you prioritize battery longevity over raw processing power, this device might suit your needs, but most buyers will find the software experience and performance lacking for the money.
