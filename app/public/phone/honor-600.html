Why we say that

The 7000 mAh battery easily exceeds a full day of mixed use and the 6.57 inch AMOLED screen reaches a peak brightness that remains visible under direct sunlight. The 200 MP main sensor produces detailed pictures in good light, and the 50 MP selfie camera is sharp enough for social media. Daily performance feels stable thanks to the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4, but owners note that gaming frames drop on demanding titles and the USB C 2.0 port feels slow when moving large files.

Software updates arrive irregularly, especially outside the EU, and the global build includes some bloatware that can be confusing. Official units carry a brand warranty, while cheaper gray imports rely on paid repairs at service centres. Overall the phone feels premium but its price is high for the performance level.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

Is the Honor 600 suitable for gaming?
