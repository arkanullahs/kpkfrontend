Is the Xiaomi 13T worth buying?

What is good

  • Stunning 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Excellent Leica-tuned main camera
  • Premium build with IP68 water resistance
  • Fast charging capabilities

What is not

  • Frequent virtual proximity sensor failures
  • Thermal throttling during heavy gaming
  • High amount of pre-installed bloatware
  • Only unofficial gray-import stock available

Buy it if You prioritize a high-quality display and Leica camera performance over gaming stability and official warranty support.

Skip it if You are a heavy gamer or someone who requires a reliable proximity sensor and official brand warranty coverage.

Why we say that

Living with this phone means dealing with a virtual proximity sensor that frequently fails during calls and significant thermal throttling during long gaming sessions. While the 144Hz AMOLED display is stunning and the Leica-tuned main camera performs well, the software experience is hampered by excessive pre-installed bloatware. Owners report average battery endurance, though the fast charging remains a highlight.

Because this is a 2023 release, you should expect limited remaining software support. Since only unofficial gray-import stock is available, you must rely on shop warranties rather than official brand support. Always verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before finalizing your purchase to avoid future connectivity issues.

What is the Xiaomi 13T price in Bangladesh?

Xiaomi 13T costs ৳65,990 in Bangladesh, in stock at 1 shop we track as of 3 Sep 2026.

Does the Xiaomi 13T have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as unofficial gray-import stock. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, so you must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

Is the Xiaomi 13T good for long gaming sessions?

Owners report that the device suffers from thermal throttling during long gaming sessions. The phone gets warm and drops frames in demanding titles like Genshin, resulting in a gaming rating of only 6.5 out of 10.

How long will the Xiaomi 13T receive software updates?

The phone was released in 2023 and is now three years old. You should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point, despite the initial promise of 3 to 4 major Android upgrades.

What is the battery life like on this device?

The 5000 mAh battery provides average endurance for daily use. While the battery life itself is not exceptional, the fast charging feature is considered a highlight by owners who rated the battery performance at 7.0 out of 10.
