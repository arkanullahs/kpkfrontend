Is the Xiaomi 13T worth buying?

What is good

  • 144Hz AMOLED display with 2600‑nit peak brightness
  • 50MP Leica‑tuned main camera with telephoto lens
  • 5000 mAh battery with 67W fast charging
  • Dimensity 8200 Ultra provides solid performance

What is not

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Proximity sensor often fails during calls
  • Glossy back attracts fingerprints easily
  • Heavy pre‑installed bloatware

Buy it if Buy it if you want a high‑end display and camera at a mid‑range price and can accept a gray‑import warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need official warranty support or the latest software updates.

Why we say that

The 6.67‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 144 Hz and can reach 2600 nits, making every video and game look sharp and bright. The Dimensity 8200 Ultra chipset with up to 12 GB RAM handles daily tasks and most games without noticeable throttling, and the 5000 mAh battery lasts through a full day of mixed use. Charging at 67 W is quick but not the fastest you will find.

The 50 MP main sensor tuned by Leica delivers natural colours and a useful 2× telephoto lens, while the 20 MP selfie camera is adequate. Owners love the build quality and IP68 rating, but the glossy back attracts fingerprints and the virtual proximity sensor often fails during calls. The phone runs Android 13 with HyperOS and promises up to four major Android upgrades, yet at three years old the remaining update window is short.

Excessive pre‑installed bloatware is a common complaint, and any repair will be paid out of pocket because there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh.

Tech Spurt · Better than iPhone 15 Pro Max, Half the Price! | Xiaomi 13T ↗

Tech Spurt concluded that the Xiaomi 13T is a massive bargain that provides almost everything found on the pricier Pro model for significantly less money. He praised its bright 144Hz AMOLED screen, cool and steady gaming performance, and natural-looking stills from the Leica-tuned cameras. Although he was annoyed by the smudge-prone glossy back, plastic sides, and absence of wireless charging, he found its comprehensive feature set, fast wired charging, and extended software support easy to recommend over pricier flagship alternatives.

GSMArena Official · Xiaomi 13T full review ↗

GSMArena concluded that the Xiaomi 13T is an excellent upper mid-ranger that delivers flagship-grade amenities—such as IP68 water resistance, an outstanding 144Hz OLED panel, and versatile Leica-branded cameras—at a reasonable price point. Although its Dimensity 8200 Ultra chip trails top-tier flagships and charging was reduced to 67W compared to last year's 120W, the phone showed superb thermal stability and reliable battery endurance. Aside from a soft selfie camera and plastic frame, they deemed it a well-rounded device worth recommending.

SamZone · Xiaomi 13T । Another Mid Budget player! ↗

SamZone highlighted the Xiaomi 13T as a capable and balanced contender in the mid-budget tier, focusing on its vibrant 144Hz high-refresh display and snappy everyday performance across multitasking and social media apps. He viewed the phone as a well-rounded device that brings premium screen technology and versatile camera capabilities within reach of budget-conscious consumers looking for smooth daily reliability.

ATC Android ToTo Company · Xiaomi 13T : The Underrated Balanced Phone ! ↗

ATC concluded that the Xiaomi 13T is an underrated, highly balanced smartphone that punches well above its price tag. The review praised its sharp 1.5K 144Hz display with Dolby Vision, solid Dimensity 8200 Ultra performance, and official IP water resistance. The reviewer was particularly impressed by the Sony IMX707 main sensor and 2x telephoto lens with Leica color tuning, noting that they produce authentic skin tones and textures rarely seen in this category.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery is rated as reliable for all‑day use. Most owners report it lasting through a full day of mixed usage, and the 67W charger refills it quickly, though it is not the fastest charging available.

Will this phone handle mobile games smoothly?

Yes, the Dimensity 8200 Ultra with up to 12 GB RAM delivers smooth daily use and stable gaming. Reviews note no throttling thanks to the built‑in voltage controller, and the 144 Hz screen adds fluid visuals.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor with a large 1/1.28" size, f/1.9 aperture, and OIS captures natural colours even in dim light. Leica tuning and the telephoto lens help retain detail, though it is not marketed as a dedicated night‑mode champion.
