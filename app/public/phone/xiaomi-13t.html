Reported trade-offs

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Frequent virtual proximity sensor failures
  • Excessive pre-installed bloatware
  • Glossy back attracts fingerprints

Why we say that

Living with this phone means dealing with a glossy back that acts as a magnet for fingerprints and grease. Owners frequently report that the virtual proximity sensor fails during calls, which can be frustrating. While the Leica-tuned camera and 144Hz display are highlights, the software experience is hampered by a high volume of pre-installed bloatware and ads in system apps.

Since this is a 2023 model, you should expect a limited remaining lifespan for software updates. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. You must rely entirely on the shop for any repairs or coverage, so verify the IMEI and BTRC status before handing over your money.

Does the Xiaomi 13T come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as a gray import. There is no manufacturer warranty provided by the brand in Bangladesh. Any repair coverage is limited to what the specific shop promises, and you will have to pay for repairs.

How is the battery life and charging speed for daily use?

Owners report that the 5000 mAh battery provides reliable all-day use. The 67W charging is fast, though it is not considered elite compared to other options. It is sufficient for most users who need to top up during the day.

Will this phone receive software updates for a long time?

The phone was released in 2023 and is now three years old. You should expect a short remaining life for software updates at this point. It supports up to 4 major Android upgrades and 4 to 5 years of security updates.

Is the Xiaomi 13T good for gaming?

The Dimensity 8200 Ultra chipset provides stable gaming performance without throttling thanks to the vapor chamber cooling system. While it is capable for daily use and gaming, owners rate the gaming experience at 6.8 out of 10.
