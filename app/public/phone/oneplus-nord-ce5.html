OnePlus Nord CE5 Is it worth buying?

The OnePlus Nord CE5 offers a powerful Dimensity 8350 Apex chipset that delivers smooth performance, but it comes with significant compromises that owners frequently highlight.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize raw performance and a high-quality display for daily tasks and are comfortable with a mono speaker setup.
Consider alternatives if
You are a mobile photographer who needs reliable low-light performance or a gamer who requires native high frame rates.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Bright 120Hz Fluid AMOLED display
  • Smooth sustained performance
  • Reliable day-long battery endurance
  • Fast 80W charging capability

Reported trade-offs4

  • Mono bottom speaker setup
  • Poor low-light camera performance
  • No microSD card slot
  • Annoying forced software pop-ups

Why we say that

While the 120Hz Fluid AMOLED display is bright and excellent for outdoor use, the experience is marred by a mono speaker that many users find frustrating. Gaming performance is generally stable, though owners report that the 120 fps claim is misleading as it often uses up-scaling rather than native frame rates. Battery life is reliable for a full day, yet heavy gaming sessions can cause noticeable heat.

The camera system struggles in low light, with the ultra-wide lens becoming nearly useless in dark conditions. Software is clean, but users are annoyed by persistent update pop-ups and permission requests. The build quality is functional with an IP65 rating, but the lack of a microSD slot and weak haptic feedback are notable downgrades.

Does the OnePlus Nord CE5 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

The official unit comes with a brand warranty, but the cheaper gray import units do not have manufacturer cover. If you purchase an unofficial unit, any service center that accepts it will treat the repair as a paid service.

How is the battery life and charging speed on this phone?

The phone provides good day-long endurance with its 5200 mAh battery. It supports fast 80W charging, which helps keep the device powered. Some owners have reported that the battery can get hot during heavy gaming sessions.

Is the OnePlus Nord CE5 good for gaming?

The device offers smooth sustained performance with low throttling. However, owners report that the 120 fps gaming claim is misleading because the phone often up-scales frames using a polarization method rather than providing native high frame rates.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

While the 50MP main camera takes decent daylight shots, it struggles with color casts and softness in low light. Owners specifically report that the ultra-wide camera becomes almost useless when shooting in dark environments.
