OnePlus Nord CE5 Is it worth buying?

Why we say that

The phone runs Android 11 with OxygenOS 13 and upgrades to Android 13, offering a smooth UI and low bloat. Its 6.43‑inch Fluid AMOLED panel is bright, color‑accurate and supports HDR10+, which owners rate 9 out of 10. The 4500 mAh battery paired with 80 W charging feels quick, yet some users notice drain issues and the battery rating is only 8.0.

The Snapdragon 750G handles everyday tasks well, but sustained gaming triggers thermal throttling and heavy performance drops, reflected in a gaming rating of 5.5. The 64 MP main camera gives decent low‑light shots, though the lack of a strong ultrawide and selfie lens limits versatility. Official warranty is only on the ৳41,999 listing; cheaper gray imports have no manufacturer cover and rely on paid repairs.
