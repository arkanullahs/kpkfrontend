Is the OnePlus 15 worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 7300 mAh battery life
  • Top-tier Snapdragon 8 Elite performance
  • Extremely scratch-resistant ceramic build
  • Smooth 165Hz LTPO AMOLED display

What is not

  • Noticeable camera hardware downgrade
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Potential software issues with China-market units
  • Reports of heating and brightness drops

Buy it if You are a mobile gamer who prioritizes battery life and raw performance over professional-grade photography.

Skip it if You rely on your phone for high-quality photography or require a device with a local manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

Owners report that the massive 7300 mAh battery is the standout feature, often lasting two to three days on a single charge. While the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset delivers top-tier gaming performance with 165fps support, the camera hardware has seen a noticeable downgrade compared to previous models. Users frequently complain about smaller sensors and the lack of Hasselblad branding, resulting in photos and videos that fall short of expectations.

The build quality is excellent with a scratch-resistant ceramic coating and an IP69K rating. However, buyers must be cautious as many units in the local market are China-market versions running ColorOS, which lacks Google services and Bangla language support. Repairs are paid out of pocket since these are gray imports without manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh.

What is the OnePlus 15 price in Bangladesh?

OnePlus 15 costs ৳85,999 to ৳1,12,499 in Bangladesh on unofficial listings, in stock at 3 shops we track as of 4 Sep 2026. The official price starts at ৳1,03,999.

Does the OnePlus 15 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, every listing for this phone in Bangladesh is a gray import. There is no manufacturer warranty support regardless of the brand's service score. Any repairs must be paid for by the owner, and coverage is limited to whatever the individual shop promises.

How does the battery perform during daily use?

The phone features a massive 7300 mAh silicon-carbon battery that is highly rated by owners. Many users report that the battery lasts for two to three days on a single charge, making it one of the most reliable aspects of the device.

Is the OnePlus 15 suitable for heavy gaming?

Yes, the device is excellent for gaming due to the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset. It supports up to 165fps in compatible games and includes bypass charging to manage heat. Owners have given the gaming performance a high rating of 9.6 out of 10.

How many years of software updates will this phone receive?

The OnePlus 15 is a flagship device that is scheduled to receive 4 OS updates. Buyers should be aware that units running the China-market ColorOS may have a different software experience compared to the global OxygenOS version.
