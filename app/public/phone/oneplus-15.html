OnePlus 15 Is it worth buying?

If you want a phone that can game for hours and survive drops, the OnePlus 15 delivers that but its camera and warranty fall short.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs

What works well4

  • 7300 mAh battery lasts multiple days
  • 165 Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance
  • IP69K ceramic coated metal build

Reported trade-offs4

  • Camera hardware feels underwhelming
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Some units run ColorOS without Google services
  • eSIM not available in many regions

Why we say that

The 7300 mAh battery easily lasts two to three days of normal use and the 120 W charger tops up in half an hour, so you rarely hunt for a plug. Performance is flagship level thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and a 165 Hz LTPO AMOLED panel with a dedicated touch response chip, giving gaming scores near perfect. The ceramic coated metal body with IP69K rating feels solid and can handle rain or accidental spills without worry.

The software runs Android 16 with OxygenOS 16, which is smooth but essentially a ColorOS skin, so you lose the pure Android feel and some users miss Google services on China market units. Owners complain that the 50 MP camera uses a smaller sensor and aperture, resulting in noisy low light shots that rely heavily on software, and that the lack of an official warranty means any repair costs come out of your pocket.

How long will the battery last in daily use?

The 7300 mAh cell gives the OnePlus 15 enough power for two to three days of typical use, and the 120 W charger can refill it from zero to full in about thirty minutes.

Is the OnePlus 15 good for gaming?

Gaming feels smooth thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chip, the 165 Hz panel and a dedicated touch response processor; owners rate performance and gaming at 9.8 and 9.9 out of ten.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor is smaller than typical flagships and lacks a large aperture, so low light photos are noisy and rely heavily on software processing, which many owners find disappointing.

Will I get official warranty and software updates?

There is no manufacturer warranty for gray‑import units in Bangladesh; shops may offer their own limited cover and repairs are paid. The phone receives up to four Android OS upgrades, and OxygenOS is essentially a ColorOS skin with low bloat.
