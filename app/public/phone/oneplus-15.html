Is the OnePlus 15 worth buying?

What is good

  • Exceptional 7300 mAh battery endurance
  • Exceptionally smooth 165Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • Powerful Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance
  • Durable IP68 and IP69K build quality

What is not

  • Camera sensor downgrade and tuning issues
  • Confusion between ColorOS and OxygenOS versions
  • Regional lack of eSIM support
  • Underwhelming glass build quality

Buy it if You prioritize extreme battery life and raw performance above all else and are comfortable verifying the software version before purchase.

Skip it if You rely on a high-end camera experience or require a phone that works perfectly with Google services and Bangla language out of the box.

Why we say that

Owners report that the 7300 mAh battery is the standout feature, yet the device suffers from a sensor downgrade compared to competitors like the iQOO 13. You must be careful when buying because many units in Bangladesh are China-market versions running ColorOS instead of the global OxygenOS. This means you might face issues with Google services and the lack of Bangla language support.

While the 165Hz display is exceptionally smooth, the camera tuning and the loss of the traditional OxygenOS identity have left many long-time fans disappointed. The build quality is durable with IP68 and IP69K ratings, but the glass finish feels underwhelming to many users. Before spending your money, verify if you are getting the global version to avoid the risks associated with software conversion.

What is the OnePlus 15 price in Bangladesh?

OnePlus 15 costs ৳85,999 to ৳1,12,499 in Bangladesh, in stock at 6 shops we track as of 2026-08-30.

Does the OnePlus 15 support eSIM in Bangladesh?

No, the device lacks eSIM capabilities in this region. Owners have reported that this is a frustrating limitation for those who prefer using digital SIM cards.

How long will the OnePlus 15 receive software updates?

The global version of the phone running OxygenOS is promised four OS updates. Please note that converting a China-market unit to global software can make the device update-fragile.

Is the camera on the OnePlus 15 good for photography?

While the main and telephoto cameras are decent, owners report a sensor downgrade compared to predecessors and competitors. Users have noted specific issues with camera tuning and video quality.

What is the difference between the China and Global versions?

The China version runs ColorOS, which lacks Google services and Bangla language support. The global version runs OxygenOS. Many gray-market units in Bangladesh are China versions, so you must check the software before buying.
