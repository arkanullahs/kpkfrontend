Does the HMD 150 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every listing is a gray import, meaning you must rely on the specific shop for any coverage. Repairs are paid and not backed by the brand.

Can I play games or use social media apps on this phone?

No, you cannot use apps or play games on this device. It uses a Unisoc 6531F chipset which is designed for a feature phone. Owners report that the hardware is incapable of running any smartphone software.

How is the battery life on the HMD 150?

The phone features a 2500 mAh battery. While this is a small capacity, the device has a low power draw which helps the battery last during basic usage. Owners have rated the battery performance as 5.0 out of 10.

Who is this phone best suited for?

This phone is best suited for someone who wants a simple device for calling. It is rated highly for ease of use, but it is not suitable for anyone who expects smartphone features, a good camera, or a high quality display.
