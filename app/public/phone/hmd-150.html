Is the HMD 150 worth buying?

What is good

  • Very low price at ৳4,999
  • Headphone jack for wired audio
  • Simple interface easy for basic calls
  • Battery lasts enough for light use

What is not

  • No official warranty
  • Tiny low‑resolution screen
  • Very weak performance, cannot run apps
  • Camera almost unusable

Buy it if Buy it if you only need a cheap phone for calls, texts and occasional music.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect a smartphone that can run apps, games or take decent photos.

Why we say that

At ৳4,999 it feels like a basic feature phone rather than a smartphone. The 2.4 inch TN TFT screen is tiny and low‑resolution, so reading messages or browsing feels cramped. Battery capacity is only 2,500 mAh but the low‑power chipset keeps it alive for a day of light use.

The Unisoc 6531F chip cannot run modern Android apps or games, and owners rate performance and gaming near zero. The camera is practically unusable, especially in low light. There is no official manufacturer warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited guarantee and any repair will cost extra.

Software updates are limited to about two Android versions and there is no bloatware. Overall it is a simple, cheap device for voice calls, SMS and occasional music via the headphone jack.
