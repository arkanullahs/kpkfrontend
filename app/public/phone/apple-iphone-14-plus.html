Buying verdict

Apple iPhone 14 Plus Is it worth buying?

If you want a large iPhone that still lasts through a full day without hunting for a charger, the iPhone 14 Plus delivers that reliably.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large iPhone with solid battery life, comfortable weight and smooth iOS for everyday use and casual gaming, and you can accept an imported unit without official warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need a high‑refresh‑rate screen, fast charging, official Apple warranty or a phone with a telephoto zoom lens.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Long battery endurance
  • Lightweight solid build
  • Clean iOS with updates
  • Strong gaming performance

Reported trade-offs4

  • 60 Hz display feels sluggish
  • Slow charging and no charger included
  • Proximity sensor may keep screen on
  • No telephoto zoom lens

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch OLED screen is bright and color accurate, but its 60 Hz refresh feels sluggish compared with newer models. Battery endurance is a strong point and the phone feels lighter than the Pro Max, making it comfortable to hold. iOS remains clean and ad‑free, and the A15 Bionic handles everyday apps and most games without heating up.

The main camera is solid in normal light but lacks zoom and can over‑process fine details. Charging is slow and the box does not include a charger. The device is an imported gray‑stock unit, so there is no official Apple warranty and you must check IMEI status with the seller.

Read full reasoning

At four years old the software update window is closing, and a known proximity sensor glitch may keep the screen on during calls.
