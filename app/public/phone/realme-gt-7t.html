Why we say that

The 7,000 mAh cell paired with 120 W fast charging means you can refill the huge pack in under an hour. The 6.8‑inch AMOLED panel shines at a 6,000‑nit peak and 120 Hz refresh, so outdoor visibility and smooth scrolling feel premium. Under the hood, the Dimensity 8400 Max delivers stable, cool performance and easily sustains high‑frame‑rate gaming, earning high owner scores.

The build feels solid with an IP68/69 rating despite a plastic frame. The camera setup is only average; the 50 MP main sensor lacks a telephoto lens and video suffers from noticeable EIS jitter, which owners mention as a disappointment. Realme UI runs Android 15 but includes medium‑high bloatware and only a few major OS updates.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 7,000 mAh cell typically provides 1.5 to 2 days of screen‑on time even with moderate to heavy usage. When it runs low, the 120 W charger can refill the pack in under an hour.

Will this phone handle high‑frame‑rate games smoothly?

Yes. The Dimensity 8400 Max chipset stays cool and delivers stable performance, allowing most high‑frame‑rate titles to run smoothly. Owner ratings give gaming a 9.0 out of 10.

Is the camera good for low‑light and does it have a telephoto lens?

The 50 MP main sensor is only average and lacks a telephoto lens, so zoomed shots are not possible. Video recording suffers from EIS jitter, and low‑light performance is not a strong point.

What warranty and software update support does the gray‑import model have?

All units are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop provides its own limited warranty and any repair costs are paid by the owner. Realme promises about two to three major Android OS updates, but the UI includes medium‑high bloatware.
