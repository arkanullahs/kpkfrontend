What works well

  • 7000 mAh battery with two‑day endurance
  • 120 W fast charging under 40 minutes
  • 6000‑nit peak AMOLED display
  • IP69 water‑dust protection and grippy vegan leather

Reported trade-offs

  • Poor camera calibration and weak ultrawide
  • Noticeable EIS jitter in 4K video
  • Medium‑high bloatware and ads, no official warranty

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh cell paired with 120 W charging fills the huge pack in under 40 minutes, so you rarely worry about power. The 6.8‑inch AMOLED panel shines with a 6000‑nit peak, delivering crisp images even in bright sunlight. Under the vegan‑leather back the device feels grippy and the IP69 rating protects it from water and dust, a rare combo at this price.

Daily performance feels smooth thanks to the Dimensity 8400 Max and 12 GB RAM, and games run at high frame rates without stutter. Software runs Android 15 with Realme UI 6.0 but you will see medium‑high bloatware and ads, and the UI receives only two to three major OS updates. Camera quality is mixed; the 50 MP main sensor is acceptable, but the ultrawide is soft, calibration is poor and 4K video shows noticeable EIS jitter.

Read full reasoning

Heat stays moderate but the chip may throttle under long stress. The phone arrives as a gray‑import with only the shop’s warranty, so you must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 7000 mAh cell gives about two days of mixed usage, and the 120 W charger refills the pack in under 40 minutes, so you can go a full day and a night without hunting for an outlet.

Is the Realme GT 7T good for gaming?

Powered by the Dimensity 8400 Max and 12 GB RAM, the phone runs games at high frame rates with smooth performance, and the large battery prevents sudden shutdowns during long sessions.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor is acceptable in low light, but the lack of proper tuning makes images look soft, the ultrawide is especially weak, and video suffers from noticeable EIS jitter.

Will I get official warranty and software updates?

All units are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s limited warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status. Realme promises two to three major Android updates.
