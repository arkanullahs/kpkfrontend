Is the Realme GT 7T worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz bright AMOLED display
  • 7000 mAh battery with 120W fast charge
  • Dimensity 8400 Max performance
  • IR blaster for remote control

What is not

  • Only gray‑import stock, no brand warranty
  • Battery level shown only half of real capacity
  • Camera calibration is poor in most scenes
  • Realme UI carries noticeable bloat

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize smooth gaming and long battery life over flawless cameras and official warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable low‑light photography or a brand‑backed warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.8‑inch AMOLED screen is bright and fluid at 120 Hz, and the Dimensity 8400 Max chipset feels like a flagship killer in everyday use. Battery life feels huge thanks to 7000 mAh, yet the software often shows only half the charge, confusing many owners. Camera performance is polarising; the 50 MP main sensor can capture decent low‑light shots but poor calibration leads to inconsistent colours.

Realme UI brings Android 15 but includes medium‑high bloat and limited update promises. The phone arrives only as a gray‑import unit, meaning you get a shop warranty rather than Realme’s official coverage. Overall it feels powerful and long‑lasting, but you must accept the software quirks and warranty limitations.

Will the battery last a full day despite the reporting error?

The phone has a massive 7000 mAh cell that can easily cover a day of typical use. However the software often reports only half the charge, so the displayed percentage may look low even though capacity is still available.

Is the Realme GT 7T suitable for high‑frame‑rate mobile gaming?

The Dimensity 8400 Max paired with 12 GB RAM makes games run very smoothly at high settings. Owner ratings give gaming an 8.4 and performance an 8.6, so it feels like a flagship killer for gamers.

How does the camera perform in low‑light situations?

Low‑light results are mixed. Some owners appreciate the 50 MP sensor with OIS, yet many report zero calibration which leads to colour and exposure issues. Overall camera ratings are low, around 5.8 out of 10.

Will I get an official Realme warranty and how many Android updates?

Only gray‑import units are available, which come with the shop’s warranty instead of Realme’s official coverage. The phone ships with Android 15 and Realme UI 6.0 and is promised 2‑3 major OS upgrades and four security patches.
