Is the Vivo Y05e worth buying?

What is good

  • Large 5150 mAh battery capacity
  • Includes a dedicated headphone jack
  • Features built-in FM radio
  • Smooth 90Hz display refresh rate

What is not

  • Poor performance and thermal throttling
  • Dim IPS LCD display panel
  • Slow 15W charging speed
  • Medium level of ads and bloatware

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a basic device for simple tasks like calls and light web browsing.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you plan to play games or expect a high quality camera experience.

Why we say that

Owners report that the Unisoc T7225 chipset suffers from thermal throttling during sustained loads, making it a poor choice for gaming or heavy multitasking. The 6.74 inch IPS LCD screen is described as dim, with a peak brightness of only 570 nits. While the 5150 mAh battery offers a large capacity, users note that efficiency is modest.

The software experience is hindered by a medium amount of ads and bloatware. Build quality is another concern, as the plastic chassis lacks any official IP rating for water or dust resistance. With an 8MP main camera providing only basic image quality, this phone is best suited for very light tasks rather than demanding applications.
