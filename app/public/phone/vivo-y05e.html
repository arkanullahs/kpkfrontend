Is the Vivo Y05e worth buying?

What is good

  • large 5150 mAh battery
  • 15W fast charging
  • headphone jack and FM radio
  • official Vivo Bangladesh warranty

What is not

  • 8 MP camera produces noisy low detail photos
  • LCD screen is dim and colors are off
  • CPU and GPU are slow gaming suffers
  • price is high for the offered specs

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large display, headphone jack and official warranty at the lowest official price.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a strong camera, bright display or smooth gaming performance.

Why we say that

The 6.74 inch IPS LCD runs at 90Hz and the 5150 mAh cell can last a day with light use, yet it drains quickly when you push apps. The Unisoc T7225 chipset struggles with everyday tasks and games, and the ultra game mode is missing. Photos from the 8 MP rear sensor are noisy and lack detail, especially in low light.

The LCD is dim and colors appear off, despite a claimed 570‑nit peak. Software carries medium ads and bloat, and the plastic build feels cheap even though the phone carries an official Vivo Bangladesh warranty. Overall it feels like a budget device priced at the higher end of the market.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 5150 mAh battery can last a full day with light tasks, but owners report noticeable drain when the phone is under heavy use. Average endurance is considered moderate.

Can I play games smoothly on the Vivo Y05e?

Gaming is not smooth because the Unisoc T7225 CPU and GPU are slow and often throttle. The phone lacks a dedicated ultra game mode and receives a low gaming rating from users.

Is the camera good for low light photos?

The 8 MP rear sensor struggles in low light, producing noisy images with little detail. Users describe the photos as low‑detail and noisy, making it unsuitable for dim environments.
