Is the Vivo Y05e worth buying?

What is good

  • Includes a 3.5mm headphone jack
  • Features a built-in FM radio
  • Large 6.74 inch 90Hz display
  • Official warranty support in Bangladesh

What is not

  • Poor camera detail and noisy photos
  • Slow performance and gaming throttling
  • Dim display with inaccurate colors
  • Heavy bloatware and advertisements

Buy it if You need a basic official-warranty device for simple tasks and prioritize having a headphone jack and FM radio.

Skip it if You care about gaming performance, camera quality, or getting the best value for your money.

Why we say that

Living with this phone involves managing a display that owners describe as dim with inaccurate colors and a camera system that produces noisy, low-detail images. While it features a 5,150 mAh battery, users report that it drains quickly under heavy use. The software experience is hindered by pre-installed bloatware and advertisements.

Gaming performance is notably weak, with owners noting the absence of an effective game mode and frequent CPU throttling. The build quality is primarily plastic, and while there is an IP64 claim, users have expressed skepticism regarding its actual water resistance. Overall, owners feel the device is overpriced for the specifications provided, noting that cheaper alternatives exist in the market that offer better value for money.

Does the Vivo Y05e have good battery life?

Owners report that the 5,150 mAh battery provides only average endurance. It is known to drain quickly when the phone is under heavy use, which may be disappointing for users who rely on their device throughout the entire day.

Is the Vivo Y05e suitable for gaming?

The phone is not recommended for gaming. Owners report that the Unisoc T7225 chipset suffers from slow performance and throttling. Additionally, the device lacks a dedicated ultra game mode, leading to a weak gaming experience.

How is the camera performance on this phone?

The camera performance is considered poor by owners. The 8MP main sensor and 5MP selfie camera produce photos that lack detail and contain significant noise. Users have expressed frustration with the image quality for a phone released in 2026.
