Is the Tecno Camon 40 worth buying?

If you need a big AMOLED screen on a tight budget, this phone delivers. The 6.78 inch panel runs at 120Hz and shows vivid colors, while the 50MP main sensor with OIS captures decent photos in good light. Memory is generous with 8GB RAM and up to 256GB storage, and the 5200 mAh battery promises long use. In practice owners complain about the phone heating up quickly, especially during games, and the battery draining faster than expected. Charging in Hyper mode feels slower than advertised and software updates arrive late, leaving the device with older HiOS versions and many ads. Audio issues also appear, with some users hearing nothing unless they connect Bluetooth. Overall it feels like a mixed bag: strong display and camera but weak thermal management and software support.

What is good

What is not

  • Battery drains quickly and charges slowly
  • Delayed Android and HiOS updates
  • Volume issue, no sound without Bluetooth
