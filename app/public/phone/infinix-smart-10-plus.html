What works well4

  • 6000 mAh battery lasts two days
  • 120 Hz IPS LCD smooth for basic use
  • Premium look with IP64 and drop resistance
  • Headphone jack, FM radio and IR blaster

Reported trade-offs4

  • Very slow 18 W charging
  • Terrible internet and Wi‑Fi connectivity
  • 8 MP camera poor in low light
  • No NFC for contactless payments

Why we say that

The 6000 mAh cell gives you enough juice for calls, messaging and light apps without hunting for a charger. Inside, the Unisoc T7250 with 4 GB RAM handles everyday tasks, and the 6.67‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, making scrolling feel smooth indoors. The build feels premium, has an IP64 rating and can survive a 1.5 m drop, while the headphone jack, FM radio and IR blaster add useful extras.

Android 15 on top of XOS 15.1 brings the latest OS, though the skin adds noticeable bloat and ads. The 8 MP camera is acceptable in daylight but struggles in low light. Connectivity is a pain point; users report very poor internet and Wi‑Fi performance.

Charging is also slow, with 18 W taking about two hours for a full top‑up. No NFC means you cannot use contactless payments.

How long does the battery last on a day-to-day basis?

The 6000 mAh battery can easily last two days of typical use, covering calls, messaging and light apps. When it finally runs out, the 18 W charger needs about two hours to fill it completely.

Can I play games like PUBG on this phone?

The phone can run basic games, but PUBG or Free Fire feel laggy and drop frames. Its Unisoc T7250 chipset and 4 GB RAM manage simple titles, yet heavy gaming is a stretch.

How is the camera performance in low-light conditions?

The 8 MP rear sensor with f/2.0 aperture captures decent photos in good lighting, but colours wash out and detail disappears in dim environments. Low‑light shots are generally unsatisfactory.

Does the phone come with an official warranty and how many OS updates will it receive?

Yes, the Infinix Smart 10 Plus sold through official channels includes the brand’s warranty and access to its service network in Bangladesh. It is expected to receive one or two Android version upgrades, delivered slowly.
