Is the Vivo x80 worth buying?

What is good

  • Strong ZEISS-powered camera
  • Excellent 6.78 inch AMOLED display
  • Fast 80W charging
  • Solid build quality

What is not

  • Main camera focus hunting in low light
  • Battery drains quickly
  • Limited remaining software update life
  • Mediocre gaming performance

Buy it if Buy this phone if you are a photography enthusiast who values the ZEISS camera system and can manage with a fast charger to offset the quick battery drain.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you want long-term software support, reliable low-light photography, or a device capable of high-end gaming performance.

Why we say that

While the ZEISS-powered camera is a highlight for many, owners frequently report that the main sensor struggles with focus hunting in low-light conditions, leading to blurry images. Daily usage is defined by a solid build and an excellent display, but the experience is hampered by a battery that drains quickly. Owners rate the gaming performance at a low 5.5, suggesting it is not the best choice for heavy mobile gamers.

Because the phone is now 4 years old, you should expect little to no remaining software update life. While the 80W charging helps, the overall performance ratings from current owners reflect a device that is beginning to struggle with modern demands. If you prioritize camera quality over battery longevity and software longevity, this phone remains a specific niche option.

Does the Vivo X80 perform well for mobile gaming?

Owners have rated the gaming performance of this device at 5.5 out of 10. Given this rating and the age of the Dimensity 9000 chipset, it is likely not the ideal choice for users who prioritize heavy gaming.
