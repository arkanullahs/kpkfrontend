Why we say that

Living with this phone means dealing with a device that is well past its prime regarding software support. While the ZEISS-powered camera is a highlight, owners frequently report that the main camera fails to lock focus in low-light conditions, leading to blurry images. The battery life is another point of contention, as users report it drains quickly, which aligns with the average battery rating of 7.5.

Performance and gaming scores are mediocre, making it a difficult recommendation for power users. Since every unit is a gray import, you will have no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh. Does the Vivo X80 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every listing is a gray import, meaning you will have to pay for all repairs yourself. You should only rely on whatever limited coverage the specific shop provides.

How is the camera performance in low light conditions?

While the phone features a strong ZEISS-powered camera, it suffers from a widely reported issue called camera focus hunting. The main camera frequently fails to lock focus in low-light conditions, which often results in blurry photos.

Will this phone receive future software updates?

This phone was released in 2022 and is now four years old. You should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point, as the device has reached the end of its typical support cycle.

Is the battery life good enough for daily use?

Owners report that the battery drains quickly on this device. The battery has an average rating of 7.5 out of 10, and users have expressed frustration regarding how fast the power depletes during regular usage.
