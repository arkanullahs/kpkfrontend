Is the Redmi K90 worth buying?

What is good

  • 7100 mAh battery lasts all day
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite delivers top performance
  • 100 W charger fills the battery in under an hour
  • 6.59‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED is bright and smooth

What is not

  • Heat builds up around camera bump
  • CN ROM lacks Google services and Bangla support
  • Standalone 5G only, often falls back to 4G
  • HyperOS adds noticeable bloat and notification lag

Buy it if Buy it if you want flagship speed, a massive battery and can live with software quirks and no official warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable 5G, an official warranty or a clean Android experience.

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Elite and 12 GB RAM keep apps and games running smoothly, and the 100 W charger refills the 7100 mAh cell in under an hour. The 6.59‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED is bright enough for outdoor viewing, though its warmer tone may not please everyone. Owners love the fast performance but complain about the phone getting very hot around the camera bump during intensive tasks, making it uncomfortable to hold.

The Chinese HyperOS ROM brings a lot of pre‑installed apps and occasional notification delays, and it lacks Google services and Bangla language out of the box. 5G works only on standalone networks, so many users fall back to 4G. Because every unit is a gray import, there is no official Xiaomi warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited cover and paid repairs.
