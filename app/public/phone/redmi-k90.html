Buying verdict

Redmi K90 Is it worth buying?

The Redmi K90 gives you flagship performance and a massive 7100 mAh battery, but expect serious heat and no official warranty.

Choose it if
You want flagship speed and huge battery at a gray‑market price and can live with heat and software quirks.
Consider alternatives if
You need an official warranty, reliable 5G or a clean Android experience.

What works well

  • 100W fast charging
  • 12GB RAM with Snapdragon 8 Elite
  • 7100 mAh battery lasting over a day
  • 6.59‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED display

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe heat near camera bump
  • China ROM lacks Google services
  • Notification delays in HyperOS
  • Only SA‑only 5G gives weak signal

Why we say that

It runs on a Snapdragon 8 Elite chip with 12 GB RAM, delivering fast app launches and smooth multitasking. The 6.59‑inch AMOLED panel offers 120 Hz refresh and peak brightness that can reach 3500 nits, making outdoor use easy. Charging at 100 W fills the huge cell in about 45 minutes, so downtime is short.

The camera package includes a 50 MP main sensor with OIS and a 2.5× telephoto lens, but the ultrawide struggles and the telephoto lacks stabilization. Software is the China‑only HyperOS 3, which adds bloatware and occasional notification lag. 5G works only on standalone networks, so you may see weaker signal compared to full‑band devices.

Owners love the battery life and raw speed, but they complain about the hot spot near the camera bump and the lack of Google services.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 7100 mAh silicon‑carbon battery gives owners about nine hours of screen‑on time, and most users report it easily lasts a full day of mixed usage. Battery rating is 9.6 out of 10.

Is the phone suitable for gaming?

Performance scores are high at 9.2 and gaming gets a 7.5 rating, so games run smoothly. However, intensive sessions make the camera bump area very hot, which can be uncomfortable to hold.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures decent low‑light shots, but the ultrawide lens is weak and the 2.5× telephoto lacks OIS. Overall camera rating is 6.5 out of 10.

Does the phone have an official warranty or software updates in Bangladesh?

All units are gray‑import China ROMs, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s limited warranty applies. Software updates are limited to two to four Android versions depending on the tier, and the ROM includes noticeable bloatware.
