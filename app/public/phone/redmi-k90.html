Redmi K90 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day on a single charge, the Redmi K90’s 7100 mAh battery delivers that, but expect the device to get uncomfortably hot during heavy use.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value long battery life and top tier performance over official warranty and flawless heat management.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable 5G, low light camera quality, or official Xiaomi service in Bangladesh.

  • 7100 mAh battery lasts over a day
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite gives strong performance
  • 100 W fast charging fills the huge battery quickly

Reported trade-offs4

  • Phone gets very hot near camera bump
  • China ROM lacks Google services and Bangla
  • Standalone only 5G may drop to 4G
  • Shop warranty only, no official support

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset provides elite speed for gaming and multitasking, and the 100 W fast charger refills the large battery in under an hour. The 6.59 inch AMOLED panel is bright enough for outdoor use, though its LTPS panel leans toward a warmer hue. The camera setup offers a solid 50 MP main sensor and a useful 2.5× telephoto, but the ultrawide lens is weak and the telephoto lacks stabilization.

The phone runs a China HyperOS 3 ROM that includes a lot of bloatware and can delay notifications. It only supports standalone 5G, so you may often fall back to 4G. Warranty is limited to the shop’s promise, with any repair costs paid by you.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

The Redmi K90 uses a 7100 mAh silicon carbon battery that owners rate 9.6 out of 10. In typical mixed usage it can stay alive for more than nine hours of screen on time, easily covering a full day without charging.

Will the phone overheat while gaming?

During intensive gaming the Snapdragon 8 Elite runs fast but heat concentrates at the camera bump. Users report the phone becoming very hot to touch and sometimes hard to hold, so long sessions may be uncomfortable.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The main 50 MP sensor with f/1.9 aperture and OIS captures decent low light shots, but the ultrawide lens is weak and the 2.5× telephoto lacks OIS, so overall low light performance is only average.

Is there any official warranty or software support?

All units are gray import China ROMs, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop provides its own limited warranty and any repair will be charged. Software updates are limited to 2‑4 Android versions and include noticeable bloatware.
