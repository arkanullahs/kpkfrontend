What works well4

  • Large 5700 mAh battery
  • Useful bypass charging
  • Bright 120Hz AMOLED display
  • 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs4

  • Poor water resistance
  • Slow UFS 2.2 storage
  • No NFC or Infrared
  • Limited software update promise

Why we say that

Owners highly praise the 5700 mAh capacity and the bypass charging feature. However, the build is a major worry as users report water entering the front camera during tests. Performance is mixed because the efficient Dimensity 7400 chip is slowed down by UFS 2.2 storage.

You will also find medium bloatware in Funtouch OS and a limited promise of only two major Android upgrades. Many buyers feel this is an overpriced rebrand of the iQOO Z10R. The lack of NFC and Infrared ports has caused strong frustration among users.

Vivo T4R: what buyers ask us

What is the Vivo T4R price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Vivo T4R in Bangladesh was ৳27,990, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No official brand backed warranty is available. All recorded listings are imports. You will only get a shop warranty, so you must confirm the BTRC status and IMEI with the seller before buying.

How is the gaming performance on the Vivo T4R?

The phone uses an efficient 4nm Dimensity 7400 chip. However, owners report that the UFS 2.2 storage slows down the performance. It received a gaming rating of 6.5 out of 10 from owners.

How long will I get software updates?

The phone comes with Android 15 and Funtouch 15. It is promised up to 2 major Android upgrades. Some users consider this a poor update promise for a 2025 release.

Is the battery life good for heavy use?

Yes, the battery is a strong point. It has a huge 5700 mAh capacity and owners gave it a 9.2 rating. The bypass charging feature is also highly praised by users.
