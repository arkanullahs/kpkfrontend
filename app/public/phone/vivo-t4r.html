Is the Vivo T4R worth buying?

What is good

  • Huge 5700 mAh battery lasts all day
  • Bright 1800‑nit AMOLED display
  • Fast 44W charging
  • Dimensity 7400 chip gives solid performance

What is not

  • No NFC or infrared ports
  • Poor water resistance, front camera leaks
  • Only gray‑import stock, shop warranty only
  • UFS 2.2 storage slows gaming

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life and a bright screen and can accept limited connectivity and warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you rely on NFC, need strong water resistance, or want a flagship‑level gaming experience.

Why we say that

It packs a 5700 mAh cell that easily lasts a full day of heavy use and supports fast 44W charging, which owners praise. The 6.77‑inch AMOLED panel is bright enough for outdoor viewing, reaching a peak of 1800 nits and offering a smooth 120 Hz refresh rate. Under the hood, the Dimensity 7400 4nm chipset delivers decent performance, but the UFS 2.2 storage slows gaming and multitasking, reflected in modest gaming scores.

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS and f/1.8 aperture captures decent photos, yet low‑light results and overall camera ratings remain average. Build quality is a concern; the phone lacks NFC and infrared, and water can seep into the front camera due to poor sealing. Only gray‑import units are available, meaning you receive a shop warranty and must verify IMEI/BTRC status.

The device feels like an overpriced iQOO Z10R rebrand with limited software support beyond two Android upgrades.
