Is the Vivo Y05 worth buying?

What is good

  • Huge 6500 mAh battery
  • 120 Hz 1200‑nit display
  • IP65 water‑dust protection
  • Headphone jack and FM radio

What is not

  • 15 W charging is very slow
  • Unisoc T7225 struggles with apps
  • 8 MP camera lacks detail
  • Gaming performance is poor

Buy it if Buy it if you need long screen‑on time, a rugged phone and can tolerate slow charging.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect fast gaming, high‑quality photos or quick charging.

Why we say that

Daily use feels comfortable thanks to the 6.74‑inch IPS LCD that can hit 1200 nits and refresh at 120 Hz, making scrolling and video watching pleasant. The 6500 mAh cell lets you stay unplugged for more than a day, yet the 15 W charger fills it at a snail’s pace, which owners find frustrating. Build quality is solid with an IP65 rating and anti‑drop design, so the phone survives drops and splashes.

Performance is limited by the Unisoc T7225 chipset; multitasking feels sluggish and games stutter after a short time. The 8 MP rear camera captures basic shots but lacks detail, especially in low light, and the software adds noticeable bloatware and ads. Overall, it suits users who value endurance and durability over speed and photography.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 6500 mAh battery is rated high by owners and can easily last over a full day of mixed use, including browsing, video streaming and calls. Charging, however, is slow because the charger is only 15 W.

Can I play modern games smoothly on the Vivo Y05?

The phone uses the Unisoc T7225 chipset, which owners describe as underpowered. Gaming scores are low and you will notice lag and frame drops in most modern titles, making it unsuitable for heavy gaming.

Is the camera good for low‑light photos?

The rear camera is an 8 MP sensor with f/2.0 aperture. Reviews say it lacks detail and struggles in low‑light conditions, and the flash is considered more of a gimmick than a solution.

Does the phone come with an official warranty and software updates?

Yes, the Vivo Y05 sold through official channels includes the brand’s warranty and access to its service network in Bangladesh. Budget models receive about two major Android updates and the software contains moderate bloatware and ads.
