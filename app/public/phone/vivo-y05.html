Vivo Y05 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can survive a day without charging, the Y05’s 6500 mAh battery delivers that, but everything else feels compromised.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value long battery life and rugged basics over camera quality and speed.
Consider alternatives if
You need fast charging, strong photography or smooth gaming.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 6500 mAh battery lasts over a day
  • 120 Hz bright IPS LCD
  • IP65 water and dust resistance
  • Headphone jack and FM radio

Reported trade-offs4

  • 15 W charging takes over two hours
  • 8 MP camera is disappointing
  • Unisoc chip feels laggy in games
  • Notch display feels dated

Why we say that

The 6.74" IPS LCD is bright and runs at 120 Hz, yet the notch design and low resolution feel dated. Performance is sluggish because the Unisoc T7225 cannot keep up with basic multitasking or gaming, and the 8 MP camera produces disappointing photos even in good light. Charging is painfully slow at 15 W, taking more than two hours to fill the large cell.

Software runs OriginOS 6 on the global version or a heavier Chinese build, both limited by the weak hardware. Build quality is solid with an IP65 rating, and the phone keeps useful extras like a headphone jack, FM radio and IR blaster. Overall it is a battery‑first device that sacrifices speed, photography and future‑proofing.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 6500 mAh cell gives exceptional endurance, owners report it easily lasts more than a full day of mixed use. With moderate screen brightness and normal app usage you can expect a day without reaching low battery.

Will the phone handle mobile games well?

The Unisoc T7225 struggles with basic tasks and games, owners notice frame drops and lag. Simple apps run okay but any demanding game feels sluggish, so it is not suited for heavy gaming.

Is the camera usable in low light?

The main sensor is only 8 MP with f/2.0 and the selfie lens is 5 MP. Reviews call the photos a major disappointment, especially in low light scenes where images become noisy and lack detail.

Does the phone come with an official Bangladeshi warranty and how many updates will it receive?

An official Bangladeshi unit is listed, so the brand’s own warranty and service network apply. The device ships with Android 16 and OriginOS 6, but budget models receive only two major OS updates, limiting long‑term software support.
