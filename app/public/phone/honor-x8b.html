Why we say that

The 6.7 inch AMOLED panel runs at 90 Hz and can peak at 2000 nits, making outdoor use easy. Battery life is decent thanks to good efficiency of the 4500 mAh cell and 35 W charging refills it quickly. The 108 MP main camera and 50 MP selfie unit look impressive on paper, yet owners notice noticeable lag when taking photos and the video recordings suffer from low audio quality.

The Snapdragon 680 chipset struggles with heavier apps and games, reflected in low performance and gaming scores. Software runs Android 13 with MagicOS 7.2, but at three years old the phone is unlikely to receive further major updates. Finally, the device arrives as a gray import, so there is no manufacturer warranty and any repair will be paid out of pocket.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

With moderate use such as browsing, social media and occasional video, the 4500 mAh battery usually lasts around 10 to 12 hours. Heavy gaming or bright screen settings will reduce that time noticeably.

Will this phone run games smoothly?

The Snapdragon 680 is a budget chip and struggles with demanding titles. Simple games run okay, but newer 3D games feel laggy and frame rates drop, matching the low gaming rating from owners.

Is the camera good in low light and for video?

The 108 MP sensor captures detailed photos in good light, but in low light the images become noisy and slower to focus. Video recordings have poor audio quality and overall low video scores from users.

Will I get an official warranty or future software updates?

The phone is sold as a gray import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh and any repair will cost you. At three years old it is unlikely to receive any major Android updates.
