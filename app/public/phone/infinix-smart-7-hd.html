Infinix Smart 7 HD Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that simply makes calls, texts and plays videos without breaking the bank, the Infinix Smart 7 HD fits the bill.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a cheap phone for basic calls, messaging and occasional media playback.
Consider alternatives if
You expect smooth gaming, fast multitasking or long‑term software support.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 6.6 inch bright IPS LCD
  • 5000 mAh battery lasts a day
  • headphone jack for wired audio
  • budget price under ৳12,000

Reported trade-offs4

  • Only 2 GB RAM feels slow
  • Android Go with many ads
  • Shop warranty only, no brand guarantee
  • Software updates likely ended

Why we say that

The 6.6 inch IPS LCD is bright enough for indoor use and the 5000 mAh battery can easily last a full day of moderate usage. Performance feels sluggish because only 2 GB RAM and Android Go are paired with a heavily loaded XOS skin that shows many ads. The camera captures decent daylight shots but struggles in low light, producing noisy pictures.

You will not get a brand warranty; only a shop warranty is offered and you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying. Software updates are unlikely as the device is already three years old, so future security patches may be scarce. Overall it works for basic tasks but does not satisfy power users.

Infinix Smart 7 HD: what buyers ask us

What is the Infinix Smart 7 HD price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Infinix Smart 7 HD in Bangladesh was ৳11,990, checked 24 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

How long does the battery last on the Infinix Smart 7 HD?

The 5000 mAh battery can easily cover a full day of moderate use such as calls, messaging and video streaming. Heavy gaming or bright screen settings will drain it faster.

Can I play games smoothly on this phone?

The device has only 2 GB RAM and runs Android Go with a heavy XOS skin, so most modern games will lag or stutter. Simple casual games run okay.

How good is the camera in low light?

The 8 MP main sensor with f/2.0 aperture captures acceptable daylight photos, but in low‑light conditions the images become noisy and lack detail. The 5 MP selfie camera shows similar limitations.

Will I get a manufacturer warranty and software updates?

No official brand warranty is listed; only a shop warranty may be offered and you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller. The phone was released in 2023 and is already three years old, so further Android updates are unlikely.
