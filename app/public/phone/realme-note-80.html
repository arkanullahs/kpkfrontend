Realme Note 80 Is it worth buying?

If you need a big battery and smooth screen without breaking the bank, this phone fits the bill.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Large 6300 mAh battery
  • 90 Hz LCD display
  • Headphone jack included
  • Affordable official warranty

Reported trade-offs4

  • 8 MP camera struggles in low light
  • 15 W charging feels slow
  • Medium‑high ads in UI
  • Unisoc chipset limits heavy gaming

Why we say that

The 6.74‑inch LCD runs at 90 Hz and the 6300 mAh cell keeps the device alive through a full day of mixed use. The Unisoc T7250 with 4 GB RAM handles everyday apps well but struggles with heavy games and high‑resolution media. The 8 MP camera is adequate in daylight but produces noisy pictures when light is low.

Realme UI 5.0 on Android 15 brings medium‑high ads and bloat, which some users find intrusive. Charging at 15 W is noticeably slow compared to faster standards. Overall, it offers solid core features for its price, but the compromises in camera, charging speed and software polish are the main sources of owner complaints.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 6300 mAh battery can easily last a full day of mixed use such as browsing, video streaming, and messaging, and may stretch to two days with lighter usage. Charging at 15 W will take several hours to refill.

Can this phone handle modern mobile games smoothly?

The Unisoc T7250 chipset together with 4 GB RAM can run casual games and older titles without major lag, but newer demanding games may show frame drops or lower settings. Expect modest performance rather than flagship speed.

How does the 8 MP camera perform in low‑light situations?

The 8 MP main sensor with f/2.0 aperture captures decent photos in good light, but in low‑light environments the small sensor and lack of advanced processing result in noisy, dim images. The 5 MP selfie camera shows similar limitations.

What warranty and software updates does the official unit provide?

The official Bangladesh unit comes with the brand’s warranty and can be serviced at any authorized Realme center. Realme promises about two to three major Android OS upgrades, so the phone should receive updates through Android 16 or 17.
