iQOO Z10 Turbo Plus Is it worth buying?

The iQOO Z10 Turbo Plus gives you flagship‑level gaming and a massive 8000 mAh battery at a gray‑import price of around ৳49,000.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want top gaming performance, huge battery life and a bright high‑refresh display at a gray‑import price.
Consider alternatives if

What works well4

  • 8000 mAh battery lasts up to two days
  • 90 W fast charging with bypass mode
  • 144 Hz AMOLED display with 5500 nits peak brightness
  • Dimensity 9400+ delivers flagship‑level gaming

Reported trade-offs4

  • Plastic build feels cheap
  • Camera colors oversaturated and no telephoto
  • Chinese ROM delays notifications
  • Lens may fog inside

Why we say that

The 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 144 Hz and can peak at 5500 nits, so the screen stays bright and responsive even in sunlight. The Dimensity 9400+ chipset together with 12 GB RAM keeps most games above 60 fps, and the 90 W wired charger with bypass mode refills the huge battery in under an hour. Owners love the endurance, the fast charging and the smooth display, but they complain about the plastic body, oversaturated photos and the lack of a telephoto lens.

Lens fogging has been reported, and the Chinese OriginOS 5 ROM can delay WhatsApp or Messenger notifications. The phone arrives as a gray import, so there is no manufacturer warranty; only the shop’s limited warranty applies and any repair costs are out‑of‑pocket.
