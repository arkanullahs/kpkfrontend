What works well

  • 8000 mAh battery lasts one to two days

Reported trade-offs

  • Plastic build feels cheap

Why we say that

Its 8000 mAh battery and 90 W wired charger keep it alive for one to two days even with heavy use, which owners rate as 9.8 out of 10. The 6.78 inch AMOLED panel runs at 144 Hz and can peak at 5500 nits, giving bright colors and smooth touch response that gamers love. Under the hood the Dimensity 9400+ chipset with 12 GB RAM pushes high FPS in titles like PUBG, though a few optimization gaps remain.

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures detail, but users report oversaturated colors, no telephoto lens and occasional fogging inside the lens. The phone runs Android 15 with OriginOS 5, a Chinese ROM that many find slows notifications for WhatsApp and Messenger. Because every unit is a gray import, there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s limited warranty and must pay for any repairs.
