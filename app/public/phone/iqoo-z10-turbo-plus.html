Is the iQOO Z10 Turbo Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 8000 mAh battery capacity
  • Elite Dimensity 9400+ gaming performance
  • Vivid 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Convenient bypass charging for gamers

What is not

  • Severe notification delays on Chinese ROM
  • Recurring lens fogging and condensation issues
  • Oversaturated camera colors and no telephoto
  • Plastic build lacks premium feel

Buy it if You are a mobile gamer who prioritizes raw performance and battery life above all else.

Skip it if You rely on instant social media notifications or expect a reliable, high-quality camera experience.

Why we say that

Owners report that the massive 8000 mAh battery is the standout feature, easily lasting one to two days of heavy use. While the Dimensity 9400+ chipset delivers elite gaming speeds, the experience is hampered by significant software issues. Users frequently complain about severe notification delays on the Chinese OriginOS 5, meaning WhatsApp and Messenger alerts often fail to arrive instantly.

The camera system is another weak point, with owners noting oversaturated colors and a recurring issue where condensation forms inside the lens. The build quality feels balanced but relies on plastic materials rather than premium alternatives. Because this is an unofficial import, you must be prepared for shop-only warranties and potential BTRC registration concerns.

It is a device built for gamers who want maximum power for their money, provided they can tolerate the software quirks and camera limitations.

Does the iQOO Z10 Turbo Plus have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available through unofficial gray-import channels. You will receive a shop warranty instead of a brand warranty, so you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

How does the battery perform during daily use?

The phone features a massive 8000 mAh battery that provides top-tier endurance. Owners report that it comfortably lasts between one and two days on a single charge, making it one of the most reliable devices for long-term usage.

Is the iQOO Z10 Turbo Plus good for gaming?

It is excellent for gaming due to the Dimensity 9400+ chipset and bypass charging support. While it sustains high frame rates, some users have noted minor optimization gaps in PUBG that may affect the experience.

What should I know about the software before buying?

The Chinese version runs OriginOS 5, which suffers from severe notification delays for apps like WhatsApp and Messenger. If you prefer a more standard experience, you should ask the seller if they are providing an India-market unit with Funtouch OS.
