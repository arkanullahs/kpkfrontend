Is the Samsung Galaxy S26 worth buying?

What is good

  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 delivers top performance
  • 12 GB RAM keeps multitasking smooth
  • 6.3 inch LTPO AMOLED reaches 2600 nits brightness
  • 50 MP main camera offers versatile shooting

What is not

  • 4300 mAh battery is modest for a flag
  • No 10 bit color depth or Dolby Vision
  • 25 W charging is slower than rivals
  • Green line issue can appear on the panel

Buy it if Buy it if you want a fast phone with a bright screen and a capable camera and you can tolerate moderate battery life.

Skip it if Skip it if you need long lasting battery or the latest display tech like Dolby Vision.

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset and 12 GB RAM keep apps and games responsive. The 6.3 inch LTPO AMOLED can hit 2600 nits, so outdoor visibility is excellent. The 50 MP main camera provides versatile shooting options.

Owners note that the 4300 mAh cell is modest for a flag and that the screen lacks 10 bit depth and Dolby Vision. Charging at 25 W is slower than many rivals and some units develop a faint green line over time.

What is the Samsung Galaxy S26 price in Bangladesh?

Samsung Galaxy S26 costs ৳80,499 to ৳88,499 in Bangladesh, in stock at 13 shops we track as of 2026-08-30.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 4300 mAh cell gives about a full day of mixed usage, but heavy screen brightness or gaming will need a charge before evening. Owners say it is better than the S25 but still modest for a flag.

Does the phone get hot while gaming?

Gaming performance is strong thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and the Exynos variant is noted for barely any heat. In most titles the phone stays comfortable, though long sessions can raise temperature slightly.

How good is the night mode on the camera?

The 50 MP sensor with OIS and 3x telephoto works well in daylight, but night mode feels unintuitive and does not match the leap of earlier models. Low light shots are acceptable but not outstanding.

How many Android updates will the device receive?

One UI 8.5 runs Android 16 and Samsung promises up to seven major Android upgrades for flagships. That means you can expect software updates for about seven years from launch.
