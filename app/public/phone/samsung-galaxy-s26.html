What works well

  • Fast Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chip
  • Bright 2600‑nit LTPO AMOLED display
  • Premium aluminum and glass build
  • One UI 8.5 with long support

Reported trade-offs

  • Mediocre 4300 mAh battery life
  • Only 25 W charging, slow
  • Thermal throttling during heavy gaming
  • Weak Wi‑Fi connectivity

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chip delivers top‑level performance and the 6.3" LTPO AMOLED screen reaches a peak 2600 nits, making everyday use bright and smooth. Build quality feels premium with aluminum and glass, and One UI 8.5 promises stable software and up to seven years of updates. Owners complain about the 4300 mAh battery lasting only average time, 25 W charging that feels slow, and Wi‑Fi signals that are weaker than expected.

Heavy gaming can trigger thermal throttling, and the camera hardware shows little innovation. The cheaper gray‑import listings lack Samsung’s official warranty, so repairs become a paid service. Choose the official version if warranty matters, otherwise the lower price comes with trade‑offs.
