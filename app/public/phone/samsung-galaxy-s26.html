Is the Samsung Galaxy S26 worth buying?

What is good

  • 6.3 inch LTPO AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance
  • 12GB RAM with up to 512GB storage
  • One UI 8.5 with seven years of updates

What is not

  • 25W charging is slow
  • Battery life only average
  • Thermal throttling in heavy games
  • No IP69 water resistance

Buy it if Buy it if you value a bright screen and flagship speed and can accept modest battery endurance.

Skip it if Skip it if you need fast charging, long battery life, or a water‑proof design.

Why we say that

The 6.3 inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED screen reaches a peak brightness of 2600 nits, making outdoor use easy. Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 paired with 12GB RAM delivers smooth multitasking and gaming, though heavy sessions can trigger thermal throttling. The 50MP main camera records clear photos, yet reviewers note it lacks the innovation of newer Chinese rivals.

Battery endurance sits at an average rating of six out of ten and the 25W charger is slower than many competitors. Software runs One UI 8.5 on Android 16 with promised seven years of updates, but occasional back gesture and WiFi bugs appear. Overall the phone feels premium but you must accept modest battery life and limited water resistance.
