Vivo V70 FE 5G Is it worth buying?

The Vivo V70 FE 5G offers a massive 7000 mAh battery that lasts well over a day, but the hardware struggles to justify its high price tag.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize extreme battery longevity and a bright display over high-end gaming performance or professional camera features.
Consider alternatives if
You are a mobile gamer or a photography enthusiast who needs reliable low-light performance and a dedicated telephoto lens.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Massive 7000 mAh battery life
  • Bright 1900 nits AMOLED display
  • Smooth software experience
  • Solid IP68/69 build rating

Reported trade-offs4

  • Underpowered chipset for the price
  • Weak zoom and low-light camera performance
  • No micro-SD card slot or eSIM
  • Plastic frame feels cheap

Why we say that

Owners frequently report that the Dimensity 7360 Turbo chipset feels underpowered for the cost, leading to a mediocre gaming experience. While the 1.5K AMOLED display is bright at 1900 nits, the camera system is a major point of contention. Despite the 200MP sensor, users find low-light performance inconsistent and zoom capabilities weak due to the lack of a telephoto lens.

The build includes an IP68/69 rating, yet the plastic frame feels cheap in the hand. Software on OriginOS 6 is smooth, though you should expect a medium amount of ads. Many buyers are frustrated by the lack of an eSIM and the absence of a micro-SD card slot.

The significant price gap between RAM variants also makes the higher-tier models feel like poor value for money.

Vivo V70 FE 5G costs ৳44,490 to ৳49,499 in Bangladesh on unofficial listings, in stock at 8 shops we track as of 21 Oct 2026. The official price starts at ৳51,999.

Does the Vivo V70 FE 5G have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

The brand warranty applies only to the official unit. The cheaper gray-import listings have no manufacturer cover, and any service centre that accepts them will treat the device as a paid repair.

How does the camera perform in low-light conditions?

Owners report that the 200MP sensor is good on paper but struggles in practice. The main camera performance is inconsistent in daylight and specifically weak when shooting in low-light environments.

Is this phone suitable for heavy gaming?

The Dimensity 7360 Turbo chipset feels underpowered for the price point. Owners have rated the gaming experience at 6.5 out of 10, suggesting it is not the best choice for demanding mobile games.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 16 and OriginOS 6. The update promise includes three OS updates for the upper models and two OS updates for the budget variants.
