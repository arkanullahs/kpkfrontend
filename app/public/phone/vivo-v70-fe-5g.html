Is the Vivo V70 FE 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 7,000 mAh battery with 90W fast charge
  • 6.83‑inch AMOLED display up to 1,900 nits
  • 200 MP main camera with OIS
  • IR blaster for remote control

What is not

  • Plastic body feels cheap
  • Main camera inconsistent in daylight
  • Chipset underwhelming for price
  • No microSD or eSIM support

Buy it if If you need long battery life, a bright screen and can accept a plastic feel, this phone may suit you.

Skip it if If you prioritize top camera performance, strong gaming power, or expandable storage, look elsewhere.

Why we say that

The 6.83‑inch AMOLED panel hits 1,900 nits and 120Hz, making outdoor viewing easy. The 200 MP main sensor has OIS but daylight photos are inconsistent and zoom is weak, and the plastic chassis feels less premium despite an IP68/69 rating. Performance is limited by the Dimensity 7360 Turbo, which feels underwhelming for the price range.

It lacks a microSD slot and most markets do not support eSIM, which many buyers see as missed opportunities. Overall, it is a solid media and battery device for users who can ignore the camera quirks and chipset limits.
