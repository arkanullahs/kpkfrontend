Realme 15 Pro 5G Is it worth buying?

Battery life is the biggest plus, lasting up to two days on a single charge thanks to the massive 7,000 mAh silicon‑carbon cell and 80 W fast charging.

3 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need long battery life and a bright high‑refresh display and can accept a plastic back.
Consider alternatives if
You expect premium build, telephoto camera, or top‑tier gaming performance.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well3

  • 7,000 mAh battery with 80 W charging
  • 6.8‑inch 144 Hz OLED display
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs4

  • Plastic back feels cheap
  • LPDDR4X RAM limits heavy gaming
  • No telephoto lens
  • Official warranty only on official units

Why we say that

The 6.8‑inch OLED panel delivers 144 Hz refresh and peak brightness that can reach 6,500 nits, making outdoor use very clear. The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 paired with 12 GB RAM handles daily apps smoothly, but the LPDDR4X memory and chipset show noticeable warming during long gaming sessions and occasional frame drops in demanding titles. The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures decent photos, yet the missing telephoto lens leaves a gap for zoom shots.

Build quality feels mixed; the IP68/69 rating is good but the plastic back panel disappoints at this price. Realme UI 6.0 runs Android 15 with up to three major OS upgrades, though bloatware and ads are noticeable. Overall the phone offers strong battery and display but falls short on premium feel, camera versatility, and high‑end gaming performance.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

Can this phone handle heavy games smoothly?
