Buying verdict

Realme 15 Pro 5G Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can last two days on a single charge, the Realme 15 Pro 5G delivers that.

Choose it if
You value long battery life, a bright high‑refresh display and 5G connectivity and can accept a plastic body and missing telephoto.
Consider alternatives if
You need a premium metal feel, a telephoto camera or top‑tier gaming performance.

What works well

  • 7,000 mAh battery with 80 W fast charge
  • 6.8‑inch OLED panel with 144 Hz refresh
  • 50 MP main sensor with OIS and 4K60 video
  • Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset with 12 GB RAM

Reported trade-offs

  • Plastic back feels cheap for price
  • No telephoto lens on the camera module
  • LPDDR4X RAM instead of LPDDR5
  • Heavy bloatware and ads in Realme UI

Why we say that

The 7,000 mAh silicon‑carbon battery paired with 80 W fast charging gives you up to 48 hours of normal use and a quick top‑up. The 6.8‑inch OLED panel shines with 144 Hz refresh and a peak brightness of 6,500 nits, making media consumption comfortable even in bright light. The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset with 12 GB RAM handles everyday apps smoothly, but heavy games cause noticeable warming and occasional frame drops.

The 50 MP main camera with OIS captures good low‑light shots, yet the lack of a telephoto lens limits zoom options. Build quality feels cheap because the back is plastic, and the device runs Realme UI with noticeable bloatware and ads. Overall it feels like a solid mid‑range phone with a standout battery and display, but compromises on premium feel and gaming performance.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.
