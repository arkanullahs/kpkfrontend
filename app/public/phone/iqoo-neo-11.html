iQOO Neo 11 Is it worth buying?

The iQOO Neo 11 gives you a massive 7500 mAh battery that can easily last a full day of heavy use, but you must accept that it arrives as a gray‑import with only the shop's warranty.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need massive battery life and high‑end gaming performance and accept a gray‑import with shop warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on fast data transfer, need reliable notifications, or want official brand support.

What works well4

  • 7500 mAh battery lasts all day
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite handles games smoothly
  • 144 Hz LTPO AMOLED screen is bright outdoors
  • IR blaster adds remote control option

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • USB‑C port limited to USB 2.0 speed
  • Notifications often delayed or missing
  • Phone gets hot during long gaming

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset delivers flagship‑level speed and makes demanding games run smoothly, though the device can become noticeably hot during long sessions. The 6.82‑inch LTPO AMOLED panel refreshes at 144 Hz and stays bright outdoors, while the IR blaster lets you control TVs and air conditioners. The main 50 MP camera captures decent photos but lacks a telephoto lens and the ultrawide is basic, and owners report delayed notifications and a USB‑C port limited to USB 2.0 speeds.

OriginOS CN brings Chinese‑market apps and medium‑level bloatware, and software updates are limited to three OS versions. Overall, it is a strong performer for battery life and gaming, but it falls short on software polish and official support.

Will the iQOO Neo 11 receive software updates in Bangladesh?

How long does the battery last with normal daily use?

Is the iQOO Neo 11 good for heavy gaming?
