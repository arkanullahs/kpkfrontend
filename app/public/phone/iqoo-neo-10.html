Is the iQOO Neo 10 worth buying?

What is good

  • Monster gaming performance and thermal management
  • Elite 144Hz AMOLED display with 4400 nits peak brightness
  • Blazing fast 120W charging
  • Massive 7000 mAh battery capacity

What is not

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Inconsistent battery endurance reported by owners
  • Lacks a dedicated proximity sensor
  • Plastic build construction

Buy it if You are a mobile gamer who prioritizes raw performance and display quality above all else and are comfortable managing gray-import software hurdles.

Skip it if You require a reliable manufacturer warranty, a premium metal build, or a dedicated telephoto lens for photography.

Why we say that

Owners report monster performance and stable 120FPS in titles like PUBG and COD, backed by excellent thermal management. However, living with this phone requires navigating gray-import realities, as there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. You must confirm if you are buying an India unit with Funtouch OS or a China unit with OriginOS, as the latter requires manual Play Store setup.

While the 7000 mAh battery is massive, users report inconsistent daily drain, sometimes needing a midday charge. The lack of a dedicated proximity sensor means the phone relies on software tricks, which some users find less reliable. The plastic build feels slim but lacks the premium materials found in other devices at this price point.

What is the iQOO Neo 10 price in Bangladesh?

Does the iQOO Neo 10 have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer or brand-backed warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you only receive whatever limited coverage the specific shop provides. All repairs must be paid for out of pocket.

How long will the iQOO Neo 10 receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 15 and is promised to receive up to 3 major Android upgrades. You should confirm with your seller whether you are receiving the global Funtouch OS version or the Chinese OriginOS version before purchasing.

Is the battery life on the iQOO Neo 10 reliable?

While the phone features a massive 7000 mAh battery, owners report that endurance is inconsistent. Some users experience random drain patterns that may require a midday charge, despite the large capacity and fast 120W charging capabilities.

Who is the iQOO Neo 10 best suited for?

This phone is best suited for users who prioritize gaming performance and display quality. With a 144Hz panel and the Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset, it is built for high-frame-rate gaming, provided you are comfortable with the lack of official warranty support.
