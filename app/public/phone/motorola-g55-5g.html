Motorola G55 5G Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day without hunting for a charger, the Motorola G55 5G delivers.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large battery, smooth display and decent camera at a sub 30 k taka price.
Consider alternatives if
You need a guaranteed brand warranty or fast software updates.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

  • 5000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 120 Hz IPS LCD feels smooth
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS
  • Headphone jack and FM radio included

Reported trade-offs3

  • Only shop warranty, no official brand warranty
  • Software updates historically weak
  • Gray import stock may need IMEI check

Why we say that

The 6.49 inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, making scrolling and casual gaming feel fluid. The Dimensity 7025 chipset with 4 to 8 GB RAM handles everyday apps well, though it may struggle with the most demanding games at high settings. The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture and optical image stabilization captures clear photos in good light and is usable in low‑light, while the 16 MP selfie camera is adequate for video calls.

Android 14 is present and can be upgraded to Android 15, but the brand’s update history is weak, so future OS upgrades may be slow. The phone includes a headphone jack and FM radio, which are rare these days. The only warranty comes from the shop, not the manufacturer, so verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
