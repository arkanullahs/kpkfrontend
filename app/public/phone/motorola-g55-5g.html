Why we say that

The 6.49‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, making scrolling feel smooth and games look fluid. Will the phone receive future Android updates?

The device ships with Android 14 and can be upgraded to Android 15. Historically Motorola provides only one or two major updates, so you should not expect long‑term support beyond Android 15.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5000 mAh battery usually lasts a full day with moderate use, including browsing, social media, and occasional gaming. Using the 120 Hz refresh rate heavily may reduce endurance slightly.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures clear images in bright environments. In low light the results are decent but not as strong as higher‑end phones, with some noise and loss of detail.
