Is the Nokia 150 worth buying?

What is good

  • No bloatware or ads
  • Includes FM radio
  • Has a headphone jack

What is not

  • No camera
  • Very old 2016 hardware
  • Limited software update life

Buy it if You need a simple phone for basic calls and radio.

Skip it if You want a camera or a device with modern software updates.

Why we say that

It is a 2016 device with a 2.4 inch TFT display and a Unisoc 6531F chipset. You will find no camera on this phone. Because it is 10 years old, you should expect little to no remaining software update life.

The software is a clean mix of Stock Android and S30+ without ads or bloat. Be very careful with where you buy it. The official unit costs ৳4,499 and includes a brand warranty.

Cheaper listings are gray imports. These gray units have no manufacturer cover. If you take a gray import to a service centre, they will only treat it as a paid repair.
