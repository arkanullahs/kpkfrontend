Is the iQOO Z11i worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz IPS LCD display
  • 6500 mAh battery lasting two days
  • Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 performance for everyday tasks
  • Headphone jack and IR blaster

What is not

  • 15W charging is slow for the big battery
  • 13MP main camera struggles in low light
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Medium amount of pre‑installed ads

Buy it if Buy it if you want a big, fast‑refresh screen and long battery at a low price.

Skip it if Skip it if you need fast charging, strong low‑light photos, or official warranty support.

Why we say that

The 6.74 inch IPS LCD panel runs at 120Hz and can reach 1200 nits, so outdoor visibility is solid. The Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 chipset handles daily apps and casual games without noticeable lag, but it is not a flagship level processor. The 6500 mAh cell gives you close to two days of mixed usage, yet the 15W charger takes many hours to refill the tank.

The 13MP main sensor and 5MP selfie lens are adequate in daylight but produce noisy pictures when light is scarce. The phone runs Android 16 with OriginOS 6, which includes medium‑level ads and bloat. Because it is a gray‑import, there is no official brand warranty; you rely on the shop’s limited coverage and paid repairs.

Owners often mention the slow charging speed and the modest camera as the main complaints.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

Is the iQOO Z11i suitable for mobile gaming?

The Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 chipset provides smooth performance for most casual and mid‑range games, and the 120Hz display helps reduce motion blur, though very demanding titles may show occasional frame drops.
