Buying verdict

ZTE V80 Design Is it worth buying?

The ZTE V80 Design gives you a big bright screen and lots of storage for just ৳20,100, but expect a fragile build and sluggish performance.

Choose it if
If you want a large bright display and ample storage while staying within a tight budget.
Consider alternatives if
If you need reliable gaming, low‑light photography, or an official warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well

  • Bright 1,000 nit 6.75 inch LCD
  • 50MP daylight camera
  • 8GB RAM with 256GB storage
  • Headphone jack

Reported trade-offs

  • Fragile rear panel with gaps
  • Slow eMMC storage limits multitasking
  • No gyroscope for motion games
  • Low‑light camera performance poor

Why we say that

The 6.75 inch IPS LCD reaches 1,000 nits, which makes outdoor use easy, and the 8GB RAM with 256GB internal space lets you keep many apps and media. The 50MP main sensor produces decent photos in daylight, yet it struggles with dynamic range and low‑light scenes. Battery life is average; the 5,000 mAh cell lasts a day of normal use but does not match phones with larger cells.

The device runs Android 16 with MyOS, but medium level bloatware and ads remain, and updates are limited to one or two versions. Storage is eMMC, creating noticeable slowdowns when multitasking or loading heavy games. There is no gyroscope, and the rear panel has visible gaps and scratches easily.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 5,000 mAh battery provides enough power for a full day of regular use such as browsing, social media and video playback, but it will not last through heavy streaming or gaming and it is lower than phones with 6,000 mAh cells.

Can this phone handle modern games smoothly?

Gaming performance is weak; the Unisoc T7280 with 8GB RAM and eMMC storage struggles with demanding titles, frame rates drop and the lack of a gyroscope prevents motion based games from working properly.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50MP sensor captures clear images in bright daylight, but in low‑light it shows poor dynamic range, noisy pictures and limited detail, so you should rely on additional lighting or avoid night photography.

Is there any official warranty or software support?

All listings are gray imports, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop may offer a limited cover, but any repair will be paid. Software updates are expected for only one or two Android versions.
