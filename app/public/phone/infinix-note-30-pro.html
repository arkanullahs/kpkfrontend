Infinix Note 30 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Infinix Note 30 Pro is a budget device that requires you to accept significant compromises in software and performance for its display and camera specs.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize a high-quality display and wireless charging on a strict budget and do not mind dealing with software ads.
Consider alternatives if
You need a reliable device for gaming, require official brand warranty support, or dislike software that lags during daily use.

Why we say that

While the 120Hz AMOLED screen is a highlight, owners frequently report that the phone lags during basic scrolling tasks. You should be prepared for a high volume of preloaded apps and notification ads within the XOS 13 software. The device also tends to heat up after long periods of use, which suggests the cooling system struggles under pressure.

Since this is a 2023 release, you should expect very little remaining software support. Every unit available in Bangladesh is a gray import, meaning you will not receive any manufacturer warranty support. Any repairs required will be paid out of your own pocket, and you must rely solely on the limited coverage provided by the specific shop where you purchase the device.

How is the battery life and charging performance?

The phone features an efficient 5000 mAh battery that owners rate at 8.0 out of 10. It also includes wireless charging capabilities, which is a notable feature for a device at this price point.

Is this phone suitable for gaming and heavy performance?

This phone is not recommended for heavy gaming or high-performance tasks. Owners rate the gaming experience at 4.0 and general performance at 4.5 out of 10. Users report that the device lags during basic scrolling and heats up after long use.
