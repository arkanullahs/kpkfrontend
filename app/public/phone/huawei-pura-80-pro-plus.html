Huawei Pura 80 Pro Plus Is it worth buying?

If you can live without Google Play and accept a shop only warranty, the Huawei Pura 80 Pro Plus offers a large bright display and strong performance for its price.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value a bright fast screen, strong performance and can manage workarounds for missing Google services.
Consider alternatives if
You need official warranty support or rely on Google Play for daily apps.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 6.8 inch LTPO OLED display with 120Hz refresh
  • Peak brightness up to 3000 nits
  • Kirin 9020 chipset with 16GB RAM
  • 5700 mAh battery for long use

Reported trade-offs4

  • No Google Play services at all
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Underdisplay fingerprint scanner often unreliable
  • Service limited to shop repairs

Why we say that

The 6.8 inch LTPO OLED screen runs at 120Hz and can peak at 3000 nits, making outdoor use easy. Kirin 9020 with 16GB RAM and up to 1TB storage handles multitasking and gaming smoothly, reflected in the high performance and gaming ratings. Battery life is solid thanks to a 5700 mAh cell, though heavy use will still need a charge before night.

The 50MP main camera with OIS and 4x telephoto is decent, but owners note the under‑display fingerprint sensor is unreliable. The biggest drawbacks are the lack of Google services and the fact that any repair depends on the shop’s willingness, as there is no official warranty in Bangladesh.

Will the phone work with my favorite apps without Google Play?

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50MP main sensor with f/1.6 aperture and OIS handles low light better than average, but it is not class‑leading. Expect decent but not spectacular night shots.
