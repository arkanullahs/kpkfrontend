Is the POCO M8 worth buying?

What is good

  • Stunning 3D curved AMOLED display
  • Excellent battery endurance with 5520 mAh
  • Durable build with IP66 and military-grade ratings
  • High peak brightness of 3200 nits

What is not

  • Lack of an ultrawide camera
  • Heavy bloatware and ad-heavy software
  • No 3.5mm headphone jack
  • 4K video lacks stabilization

Buy it if You want a durable phone with a high-quality display and long battery life for daily tasks.

Skip it if You need a versatile camera system, a clean software experience, or official brand warranty support.

Why we say that

Living with this device means dealing with a heavy HyperOS interface that is packed with bloatware and ads, which many owners find frustrating. While the 5520 mAh battery easily lasts all day, the camera setup is limited by the lack of an ultrawide lens and poor video stabilization at 4K resolution. Gaming performance is restricted to 60fps with occasional frame drops, making it better suited for general tasks than intensive play.

Because these units are gray imports, you must rely entirely on the shop for repairs rather than any manufacturer support. The build quality is impressive with IP66 and military-grade ratings, but the absence of a 3.5mm headphone jack is a notable inconvenience for many users. You are getting a powerful screen and solid battery life, but you must be prepared to manage the software experience and accept the lack of official warranty coverage in Bangladesh.

Does the POCO M8 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you must rely on the specific shop for any repair promises and pay for all services yourself.

How is the battery life for daily usage?

The 5520 mAh battery provides excellent endurance for users. Owners report that the efficient Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 chip ensures the phone lasts through the entire day without needing a charge.

Is this phone good for gaming?

The device is capable for daily tasks, but gaming is limited to 60fps. Owners report that you will experience occasional frame drops during gameplay, so it is not ideal for heavy mobile gamers.

How long will the POCO M8 receive software updates?

The phone runs on Android 15 and is promised up to 4 major Android upgrades. However, users should be aware that the HyperOS interface comes with a high amount of bloatware and advertisements.
