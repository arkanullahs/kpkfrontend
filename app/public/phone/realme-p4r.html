Buying verdict

Realme P4R Is it worth buying?

The Realme P4R will keep you powered for days, but you pay for that endurance with a cheap feel and weak camera.

Choose it if
You need long battery life and can accept a basic screen and camera.
Consider alternatives if

What works well

  • 8000mAh battery lasts multiple days
  • 144Hz refresh rate feels smooth
  • 1200 nits peak brightness for bright outdoors
  • 5G connectivity for fast data

Reported trade-offs

  • Plastic build feels cheap
  • HD+ LCD resolution is low
  • Charging 45W is slow for large battery
  • Camera struggles in low light

Why we say that

Its 8000mAh battery is the standout feature, giving multi day endurance that owners rate at 9.8 out of 10. The 6.81 inch IPS LCD runs at 144Hz, but the low HD+ resolution and LCD panel limit sharpness. The Dimensity 6300 chipset handles basic tasks but struggles with modern games, reflected in a 4.5 gaming rating.

Charging at 45W fills the large cell in over an hour, which feels slow for the capacity. The plastic build and polarising pulse light design feel inexpensive, and the 50MP camera suffers from poor colour science and low‑light performance. Software comes with heavy bloatware and ads, requiring cleanup.

The phone arrives as a gray import with no official warranty, only the shop's limited promise, and any repair will be paid by the buyer.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The 8000mAh cell gives multi day endurance, and owners rate battery performance at 9.8 out of 10. With moderate use you can expect two to three days between charges, but heavy gaming will reduce that.

Will the Realme P4R run modern games smoothly?

The phone uses a Dimensity 6300 chipset which is entry level. It can handle casual titles, but owners report it struggles with modern games and the gaming rating is only 4.5 out of 10.

Is the camera usable in low light situations?

The 50MP main sensor is mediocre and colour reproduction is weak. Low light performance is poor and owners note fake cut outs on the camera module. The camera rating is 4.2 out of 10.

Does the phone come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray imports, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop may offer its own limited warranty, but any repair will be paid by you. Verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.
