Realme P4R Is it worth buying?

The Realme P4R is a niche device defined entirely by its massive 8000 mAh battery, which is the only feature that truly stands out.

3 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
Buy this phone only if your absolute priority is multi-day battery life and you do not mind a low-resolution screen.
Consider alternatives if
You care about camera quality, gaming performance, or having a valid manufacturer warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well3

  • Massive 8000 mAh battery capacity
  • Durable and shock-resistant build
  • IP65-rated water and dust resistance

Reported trade-offs4

  • Low resolution 720p display
  • Heavy bloatware in software
  • Deceptive camera design with fake cutouts
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

While the battery offers multi-day endurance, the rest of the phone struggles to justify its price point. Owners frequently complain about the low-resolution 720p display, which makes the 144Hz refresh rate feel wasted. The Dimensity 6300 chipset is entry-level and fails to provide smooth performance for modern gaming or long-term responsiveness.

You will also need to spend time cleaning up the heavy bloatware found in Realme UI 7.0. The camera setup is deceptive, featuring fake secondary cutouts and a sensor that often produces over-exposed images. Because this phone is only available through gray-import channels, you will not receive any manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh.

Read full reasoning

Repairs must be paid for out of pocket, and you should verify the BTRC status with your seller before purchasing.

Does the Realme P4R come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is only available as a gray import. There is no manufacturer warranty support in Bangladesh regardless of the brand's service score. Any coverage is limited to what the individual shop promises, and all repairs are paid.

Is the Realme P4R suitable for gaming?

The phone is not recommended for gaming. Owners report that the Dimensity 6300 chipset is entry-level and struggles with modern gaming titles. It also faces issues with long-term responsiveness during daily use.

How long will the Realme P4R receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 16 with Realme UI 7.0. It is expected to receive 2 to 3 OS updates over its lifespan. Be aware that the software comes with medium to high levels of bloatware and ads.

How does the camera perform in real-world conditions?

The camera performance is poor according to owner ratings. Users report over-exposure issues in videos and photos. Additionally, the phone features a deceptive design where two of the three camera cutouts are fake and non-functional.
