Is the Realme P4R worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 8000 mAh battery capacity
  • Large 6.81 inch 144Hz display
  • Bright 1200 nits peak brightness
  • Capable 50MP main camera

What is not

  • Medium-high levels of ads and bloatware
  • Only unofficial gray-import stock available
  • Low ease of use rating
  • No official brand warranty

Buy it if You need the longest possible battery life and do not mind managing ads and bloatware on your device.

Skip it if You want a clean software experience and the security of an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Living with this phone means managing a user interface that owners describe as having medium-high levels of ads and bloatware. While the 8000 mAh battery is the standout feature, the overall ease of use is rated at a modest 5.5 out of 10 by actual owners. You are getting a large 6.81 inch display with a 144Hz refresh rate, but you must be prepared for the reality of unofficial gray-import stock.

This means you will rely on a shop warranty rather than a brand warranty. Always confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your money. The software experience is hampered by the pre-installed applications, which can be frustrating for users who prefer a clean interface.

If you prioritize raw battery capacity over a polished software experience, this device might serve your needs, provided you are comfortable with the limitations of unofficial imports.
