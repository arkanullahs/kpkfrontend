Is the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 worth buying?

What is good

  • Exceptional 9.8 performance rating
  • Stunning 8.0 inch 120Hz display
  • Powerful Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset
  • High quality 200MP main camera

What is not

  • Poor 6.0 battery life rating
  • Visible and worsening screen crease
  • Only unofficial gray-import stock available
  • No official brand warranty support

Buy it if Buy this phone if you are a power user who demands top-tier gaming performance and a massive display for multitasking.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you prioritize long battery life or require the security of an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Owners rate the performance at 9.8 and the display at 9.7, making it a top choice for power users and gamers who prioritize a large screen experience. However, the device is currently only available through unofficial channels, meaning you will rely on shop warranties rather than official brand support. You must be prepared for concerns regarding the screen crease, which users report becomes more visible over time.

The 4400 mAh battery is a weak point, receiving a low rating of 6.0 from owners. While the 200MP camera system performs well, the overall experience is defined by its premium hardware and the inherent fragility of the folding mechanism. Ensure you verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before finalizing any purchase.
