Is the Samsung Galaxy S26 FE worth buying?

What is good

  • 1900 nit 6.7 inch AMOLED display
  • 50MP main camera with OIS
  • Up to seven years Android updates
  • 45W wired charging

What is not

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • 8GB RAM struggles with 2026 AI tasks
  • 8MP telephoto feels outdated
  • Overheating can dim screen in heat

Buy it if Buy it if you value a vivid display, solid main camera and long software updates and can live with a gray import warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need strong gaming performance, reliable battery longevity or an official Samsung warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X panel reaches 1900 nits and runs at 120 Hz, making everyday use feel smooth and visible even in sunlight. The Exynos 2500 3 nm processor delivers decent performance, and the 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures clear photos in good light. Samsung promises up to seven years of Android updates, which is rare for a mid range device.

However, the phone is a gray import, so there is no official Samsung warranty and you rely on the shop’s limited cover. Owners report the 8 GB RAM feels thin for current AI workloads and gaming scores are modest. The 8 MP telephoto lens is considered a toy, and the device can overheat, causing the screen to dim in hot weather.

Battery capacity is average and charge‑cycle count is lower than expected, leading to faster degradation.
