Samsung Galaxy S26 FE Is it worth buying?

Expect a bright, smooth screen and decent day‑to‑day speed, but don’t be surprised if the telephoto feels cheap and the charging feels slower than newer flagships.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large, bright screen and solid software support and are okay with a modest camera and slower charging.
Consider alternatives if
You need top‑tier gaming performance, a strong telephoto lens, or a phone with a fast USB‑C data port.

Why we say that

The 6.7 inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X panel reaches 1900 nits and refreshes at 120Hz, making media and scrolling pleasant. Under the hood, the Exynos 2500 delivers solid performance for apps and multitasking, though it still lags behind Snapdragon flagships in heavy gaming. Battery life is okay thanks to efficiency gains, but the 4900 mAh cell is unchanged from previous models and the 45W charger is not as fast as competitors.

The camera setup uses a recycled 50MP main sensor and an 8MP telephoto that many owners call unacceptable for the price. Software is a strong point with Android 17, One UI 9 and up to seven years of updates. The biggest complaints are the USB‑C port being limited to USB 2.0, the glossy back attracting fingerprints, and the overall value being poor compared to older S‑series models.
