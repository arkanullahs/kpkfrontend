What works well

  • High performance with Snapdragon 8 Gen 2
  • Bright LTPO AMOLED display
  • Premium ceramic design with IP68 rating
  • Good photos and decent telephoto

Reported trade-offs

  • Battery drains quickly
  • Reports of MIUI bugs and instability
  • No official brand warranty
  • Limited remaining software update life

Why we say that

It offers strong performance and a bright, smooth display that owners rate highly. However, the software experience is a mixed bag. Early versions had widespread reports of crashes, lag, and occasional reboots.

Battery life is a major pain point for users. Owners report that the battery drains quickly, with some noting capacity drops to around 4000 mAh after three years. Since the device was released in 2022, it is now four years old.

Read full reasoning

You should expect very short or no remaining software update life. You will need to verify the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before paying.

Xiaomi 13 Pro: what buyers ask us

What is the Xiaomi 13 Pro price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Xiaomi 13 Pro in Bangladesh was ৳89,999, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, every listing we have recorded is a gray import. You will only get a shop warranty instead of brand backed support. You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller.

How is the battery life on the Xiaomi 13 Pro?

Owners report that the battery drains quickly. Some users mention that after three years of use, the battery capacity drops to around 4000 mAh.

Will this phone get Android updates for a long time?

The phone was released in 2022 and is now four years old. You should expect short or no remaining software update life at this point.

Is this a good phone for gaming?

Yes, it is a strong performer. It uses the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset and owners have given it a gaming rating of 8.0 out of 10.
