Is the Realme 14 pro lite worth buying?

What is good

  • Vivid 6.7 inch AMOLED with 120Hz refresh
  • Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 delivers solid performance
  • Large 5200 mAh battery lasts a day
  • Sharp 50 MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Heavy bloatware needs manual removal
  • Water resistance claims are unrealistic
  • Selfie camera feels outdated
  • Branding is confusing

Buy it if If you want a bright display and decent performance at a sub‑33 k taka price and can tolerate extra cleanup, this phone may fit your needs.

Skip it if If you need reliable water resistance, a strong selfie camera, or an official Realme warranty, look for another model.

Why we say that

The 120 Hz panel reaches 2000 nits, so outdoor use feels clear and smooth. Under the hood the Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 and 8 GB RAM handle everyday apps without lag, yet gaming scores stay modest and you may need to lower settings for newer titles. Battery life is decent thanks to a 5200 mAh cell, though heavy use will still need a nightly charge.

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures sharp photos, but the lack of periscope zoom and an older 32 MP selfie lens limit versatility. Owners complain about the heavy pre‑installed apps, the unrealistic water‑resistance claim and the confusing “pro lite” name. The phone arrives as a gray‑import with shop warranty only, so verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
