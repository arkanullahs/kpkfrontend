What works well4

  • Bright 2000‑nit curved AMOLED display
  • 5200 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 45 W fast charging
  • Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 handles daily tasks

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official brand warranty, only shop warranty
  • UI filled with bloatware and ads
  • No microSD slot or headphone jack
  • Thermal throttling reduces long gaming performance

Why we say that

The 6.7‑inch curved AMOLED panel peaks at 2000 nits and runs at a smooth 120 Hz, making everyday scrolling and video watching feel fluid. A 5200 mAh cell paired with 45 W charging keeps the phone alive through work, travel and light gaming, and the Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 chipset handles most apps without stutter. Owners complain that the UI is loaded with bloatware and ads, that the plastic frame feels cheap despite the glass back, and that the phone lacks a microSD slot and headphone jack.

The 50 MP main sensor is decent in daylight but struggles with portrait detail and low‑light shots, while the 32 MP selfie camera over‑softens faces. During long gaming sessions the device can heat up and throttle, so sustained high‑frame rates are not reliable. Because every unit is a gray import, you only get the shop’s limited warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
