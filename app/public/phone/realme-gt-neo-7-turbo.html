Why we say that

The phone runs realme UI, which feels familiar but includes medium‑high ad load typical of budget devices. You will get roughly two to three Android version updates, which is modest for a mid‑range phone. Owners often complain about the need to verify the device’s legal status and the lack of a manufacturer warranty, and some find the pre‑installed ads intrusive.

If you are okay with a shop‑issued warranty and can confirm the phone is cleared for local networks, the device can be a decent everyday handset, but it may not satisfy users who demand official support or a clean software experience.
