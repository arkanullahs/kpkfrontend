Why we say that

The 6.59 inch AMOLED panel reaches 1200 nits and runs at 120Hz, making everyday scrolling feel smooth. Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 delivers solid performance with an 8.0 rating, and the 80W charger fills the 6500 mAh cell quickly. The 50MP main sensor and matching selfie camera give decent photos, though low‑light results are only average.

Owners complain about the phone heating up quickly and the battery draining faster than expected, despite the large capacity. Network stability is another issue with frequent SIM drops. The device lacks a microSD slot, eSIM support and uses a lower‑grade protection glass.

There is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s limited warranty applies, and repairs are paid. Software runs Android 16 with ColorOS 16, but ads and missing work‑profile icons are noted.
