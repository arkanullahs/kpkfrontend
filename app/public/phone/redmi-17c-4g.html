Is the Redmi 17C 4G worth buying?

What is good

  • Large 6.88-inch 120Hz display
  • Large 5160 mAh battery capacity
  • Includes a dedicated headphone jack
  • Equipped with 8GB of RAM

What is not

  • China-ROM lacks Google and Bangla
  • Only shop warranty available
  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • Slow 18W charging speed

Buy it if Buy this phone only if you are an advanced user who knows how to handle China-ROM software limitations and accepts the risks of gray-import units.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you need a device with official brand warranty, pre-installed Google services, and full Bangla language support out of the box.

Why we say that

You are getting a large 6.88-inch display with a 120Hz refresh rate, but the device runs on a China-ROM version of HyperOS 3. This software version lacks Google services and Bangla language support, and converting it has become difficult. Because these are gray-import units, you will not receive a brand warranty, only a shop warranty.

You must verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your money. The budget nature of this device means you should expect a high amount of bloatware and advertisements in the software. While the 5160 mAh battery is a decent size, the 18W charging speed is quite slow for a phone in this category.

Think carefully about whether the lack of official support and the software limitations are worth the 23,200 taka price tag.
