Buying verdict

Vivo V60 Is it worth buying?

The Vivo V60 gives you a day plus battery and a very bright screen, but its mid range internals feel overpriced.

Choose it if
You need a bright screen, long battery life and fast charging at a mid range price.
Consider alternatives if
You expect flagship level gaming performance or the latest storage speeds.

What works well

  • 5000 nit bright AMOLED display
  • 90W fast charging under 1 hour
  • Massive 6500 mAh battery lasting two days
  • 50 MP dual cameras with good portrait quality

Reported trade-offs

  • UFS 2.2 storage slower than rivals
  • Weak 8 MP ultrawide camera
  • Awkward bottom fingerprint sensor
  • Gaming struggles on AAA titles

Why we say that

Owners love the 6.77 inch AMOLED panel that reaches 5000 nit and stays smooth at 120Hz. The 90W charger fills the 6500 mAh cell in under an hour, so you rarely wait for power. Daily performance feels snappy thanks to the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 and 12 GB RAM, yet heavy games heat up and cannot run AAA titles at high settings.

Camera results are solid for portraits and telephoto, but the 8 MP ultrawide is low detail and lacks autofocus. The plastic frame is slim and IP68 rated, though the fingerprint sensor sits low and feels awkward. Software runs fine but is cluttered with medium bloatware and ads.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

Can the Vivo V60 handle heavy gaming?

The phone feels snappy for everyday apps, but it gets toasty during heavy gaming and cannot run AAA titles at high settings without lag.
