Is the Vivo V60 worth buying?

What is good

  • Huge 6500 mAh battery lasts two days
  • Bright 5000‑nit AMOLED display works outdoors
  • 90 W fast charging under one hour
  • 50 MP main and selfie cameras capture detail

What is not

  • UFS 2.2 storage slower than rivals
  • 8 MP ultrawide camera lacks detail
  • Optical fingerprint sensor placed low
  • AAA games lag and cause heating

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, fast charging and a bright large screen and can accept slower storage and no official warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect flagship‑level gaming performance, fast UFS 3 storage, or an official manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

With a 6.77‑inch AMOLED panel that can reach 5000 nits, outdoor visibility is excellent and the 120 Hz refresh feels smooth. The 6500 mAh cell easily stretches to two days of normal use and the 90 W charger fills the battery in under an hour. The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 and 12 GB RAM handle everyday apps without hiccup, yet AAA titles stumble at high settings and the device gets warm during long sessions.

The 50 MP main and selfie sensors produce detailed photos, while the 8 MP ultrawide lens is soft and lacks autofocus. Storage is limited to UFS 2.2, which feels dated compared to rivals. The plastic frame is slim and IP68/69 rated, but the optical fingerprint sensor sits low and feels awkward.

Software runs on Funtouch OS with noticeable bloatware and only a few major Android updates.
