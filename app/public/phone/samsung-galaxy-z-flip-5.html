Is the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 5 worth buying?

What is good

  • Gapless hinge with smooth folding
  • 6.7‑inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X inner display at 120 Hz
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 provides strong everyday performance
  • One UI foldable UI with useful cover‑screen widgets

What is not

  • 3700 mAh battery limits endurance
  • 25 W charging feels slow for a premium phone
  • Screen flicker and hinge wobble appear after weeks
  • Thermal throttling reduces performance in sustained gaming

Buy it if Buy it if you value the foldable form factor above raw specs and can accept shorter battery life.

Skip it if Skip it if you need long gaming sessions, fast charging, or a phone that will receive updates for many more years.

Why we say that

The inner 6.7‑inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X panel feels premium with 120 Hz refresh, and the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 handles daily tasks smoothly. The gapless hinge folds neatly, yet the cover screen stays at 60 Hz and 720p, limiting its usefulness. Battery life is modest because of the 3700 mAh cell, and 25 W charging feels slow for a flagship.

Owners report occasional screen flicker, hinge wobble after weeks, and thermal throttling that cuts gaming performance. Software is solid with One UI foldable features, but being a 2023 model, only a few years of updates remain. Overall, it suits those who prize the flip design above raw specs.

How long will the Galaxy Z Flip 5 receive software updates?

Samsung promises 4 to 7 years of Android updates for its flagships. Since the Z Flip 5 launched in 2023, only about two to three years of updates remain, so you should not expect long‑term software support.

Is the battery life good for daily use?

The 3700 mAh battery is helped by the efficient Snapdragon 8 Gen 2, but owners rate battery life only 5.8 out of 10. Expect a full day of moderate use and need to charge daily with 25 W.

Can I play games smoothly on this phone?

Everyday tasks feel snappy thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2, but during long gaming sessions the phone throttles heavily because of its compact foldable design. Gaming performance is therefore moderate and not ideal for heavy gamers.

Is the official warranty reliable in Bangladesh?

An official Bangladesh unit comes with Samsung’s own warranty and can be serviced through the brand’s network. This means you are covered for hardware defects and can get official support at authorized service centers.
