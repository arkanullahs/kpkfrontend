Is the Realme 16 worth buying?

What is good

  • 7000 mAh battery lasts multiple days
  • Bright 6.57‑inch AMOLED 120 Hz display
  • IP69 water and dust resistance
  • 60 W fast charging

What is not

  • Dimensity 6400 chipset underperforms for price
  • Camera lacks ultrawide, telephoto and 4K video
  • Fingerprint sensor sits too low
  • Realme UI includes noticeable bloatware

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, a bright screen and can accept modest performance and camera features.

Skip it if Skip it if you want strong gaming performance, excellent low‑light photos or an official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Its 7000 mAh battery and 60 W fast charge let you go multiple days without hunting for a charger, and the 6.57‑inch AMOLED panel at 120 Hz stays bright even in sunlight. The slim body is IP69 rated, so rain and dust won’t be a problem. However, the Dimensity 6400 Turbo chipset feels dated for a 2025 release and cannot deliver flagship‑level gaming or heavy multitasking.

The 50 MP main sensor lacks ultrawide, telephoto and 4K video, and low‑light shots are mediocre. Realme UI 7.0 still carries noticeable bloatware and only receives about two to three Android updates. There is no official Bangladeshi warranty; you must rely on the shop’s warranty and verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
