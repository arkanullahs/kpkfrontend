Is the Samsung Galaxy M17 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 1100 nits Super AMOLED display
  • OIS provides decent camera stability
  • Long software support with Android 15
  • Capable Exynos 1330 chipset

What is not

  • Disappointing battery life for an M-series phone
  • Dated design with large bezels and old notch
  • Slow performance for gaming
  • Lacks stereo speakers

Buy it if You prioritize a bright AMOLED screen and long-term software support for light daily usage.

Skip it if You are a gamer or someone who expects reliable all-day battery life from a modern smartphone.

Why we say that

Owners report that the device feels outdated due to its bulky design, large bezels, and an old-style notch that many find laughable in 2025. While the Super AMOLED display is a highlight, the 90Hz refresh rate and overall build quality receive low marks from users. Performance is adequate for light daily tasks, but the phone struggles significantly with gaming and feels slow.

Battery endurance is a major point of contention, with many users noting it drains quickly and struggles to last 12 hours. You should also be aware that this device is only available through gray-import channels, meaning you will rely on a shop warranty rather than an official brand warranty. Given that the newer Samsung Galaxy M36 5G is available for a similar price, most buyers will find better value elsewhere.

Does the Samsung Galaxy M17 5G have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is currently only available as gray-import stock in Bangladesh. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, so you must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

How long will the Samsung Galaxy M17 5G receive software updates?

The device comes with Android 15 and One UI 7 out of the box. Samsung promises up to 6 major Android upgrades, ensuring the software remains current for several years.

Is the Samsung Galaxy M17 5G good for gaming?

Owners report that the phone is only fine for light tasks. It feels outdated and slow when running games, resulting in a low gaming rating of 4.5 out of 10.

Why do users complain about the battery life on this phone?

Despite being part of the M-series, users report disappointing endurance. Many owners state that the battery drains fast and finds it hard to last 12 hours on a single charge.
