Is the Redmi 15 4G worth buying?

What is good

  • Huge 7000 mAh battery lasting up to two days
  • Large 6.9‑inch bright IPS display
  • IR blaster for remote control
  • Snapdragon 685 with 6 GB RAM

What is not

  • UI lag and stuttering in everyday use
  • Heavy, bulky plastic build
  • Noisy, grainy photos in low light
  • 33 W charging feels slow for large battery

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, a big screen and can tolerate slower performance and ad‑heavy software.

Skip it if Skip it if you want smooth gaming, a clean UI, or a strong low‑light camera.

Why we say that

Its 7‑inch IPS panel is bright and smooth at 144 Hz, though the high refresh rate feels more like a marketing claim. The 7000 mAh battery truly stands out, easily lasting 1.5‑2 days with moderate use, but the 33 W charger needs about two hours to fill it, which feels slow for such a large cell. The Snapdragon 685 with 6 GB RAM handles basic apps, yet owners report noticeable lag and stutters even in simple tasks.

The 50 MP main camera produces noisy, grainy pictures, especially in low light, and the software layer, HyperOS, is heavy on ads and bloatware. Build quality is solid plastic but the phone is bulky and heavy, and it lacks a 3.5 mm headphone jack. Official warranty applies only to the official unit priced at ৳21,399; cheaper gray imports have no manufacturer cover.
