Motorola Edge 60 Fusion Is it worth buying?

If you value a premium look and a battery that easily lasts a day, the Edge 60 Fusion can fit your needs, but expect software hiccups and weak gaming performance.

What works well

  • Bright 6.67 inch P‑OLED display
  • Long‑lasting 5200 mAh battery
  • Premium vegan‑leather body with IP69 rating
  • Fast 68 W charging

Reported trade-offs

  • Software is buggy and has ads
  • Gaming performance is weak
  • Camera app lags and focus hunts
  • Touch sensitivity drops with screen protector

Why we say that

The 6.67 inch P‑OLED panel shines at 4500 nits peak, making outdoor use comfortable. The vegan‑leather back feels lightweight and the IP69 rating protects against water and dust. The 5200 mAh cell paired with 68 W charging gives you a full charge in under an hour and still survives heavy use.

The Dimensity 7300 chipset handles everyday tasks but stumbles on demanding games and shows occasional UI lag. The camera captures decent daylight photos, yet the app can be slow and focus hunting is common, especially in low light. Android 15 runs a near‑stock Hello UI, but updates are limited to three major versions and early Android 16 builds bring bugs and pre‑installed ads.
