Is the Motorola Edge 60 Fusion worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 4500‑nit P‑OLED display
  • 5200 mAh battery with full‑day endurance
  • Premium vegan‑leather body with IP69 protection
  • 68 W fast charging

What is not

  • Software bugs and bloatware
  • Camera app lag and focus hunting
  • Dimensity 7300 struggles with heavy games
  • Touch sensitivity drops with screen protector

Buy it if Buy if you value a sturdy, bright phone with long battery life and can tolerate occasional software glitches.

Skip it if Skip if you need top‑tier gaming performance, reliable software updates, or an official manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.67‑inch P‑OLED panel reaches a peak 4500‑nit brightness and runs at 120 Hz, making outdoor viewing clear and colors accurate. Its 5200 mAh battery paired with 68 W fast charging easily gets you through heavy use without a recharge. The premium vegan‑leather body feels lightweight yet offers class‑leading IP69 protection, and the 50 MP main sensor captures decent daylight photos.

However, the Dimensity 7300 chipset shows its age in demanding games and the UI can stutter. Software updates are buggy, with bloatware and occasional VPN or Dolby issues. The phone is sold as a gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty; only the shop’s limited cover applies.

At a price starting from ৳32,999, it competes closely with the newer Edge 70 Fusion, which may be a better overall value.

Is there an official manufacturer warranty for the Edge 60 Fusion in Bangladesh?

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

Will this phone handle demanding games well?

The Dimensity 7300 chipset is under‑powered for heavy gaming; users notice stutters and lower frame rates, and the gaming rating is only 5.5, so it is not ideal for intensive titles.
