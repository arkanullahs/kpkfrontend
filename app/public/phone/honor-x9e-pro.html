Honor X9e Pro Is it worth buying?

If you can live without a local warranty, the Honor X9e Pro gives flagship level performance for a gray import price.

Why we say that

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 paired with 8 GB RAM handles multitasking and modern games smoothly, while the 6.8 inch AMOLED panel at 120 Hz feels fluid and bright. The 108 MP main sensor with f/1.7 aperture and optical image stabilization captures detailed photos, and the 16 MP selfie camera is adequate for video calls. A massive 11000 mAh battery lasts well beyond a day of typical use and refills in under an hour with the 80 W charger.

The device runs Android 16 with MagicOS 10, offering three to four major OS upgrades like other flagships, though it carries a medium amount of preinstalled ads. It also includes an IR blaster for TV control. The only downside is the lack of an official brand warranty, so any repair will be paid through the shop’s own service.
