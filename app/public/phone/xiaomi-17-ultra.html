Xiaomi 17 Ultra Is it worth buying?

If you want a flagship display and raw speed, the Xiaomi 17 Ultra delivers, but expect trade‑offs.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize a bright, fast screen and flagship‑level gaming performance and are comfortable handling a gray‑import warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable all‑day battery life, a clean software experience, or official brand support.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 3500‑nit LTPO AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance
  • 90W fast charging
  • Up to 1 TB storage

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official warranty in Bangladesh
  • Battery drains quickly under heavy use
  • HyperOS is cluttered and over‑processed
  • Main camera video stabilization poor

Why we say that

The 6.9‑inch LTPO AMOLED panel reaches a peak of 3500 nits and stays fluid at 120 Hz, making everyday use feel premium. Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 paired with 16 GB RAM gives top‑tier performance and smooth gaming even in demanding titles. The 90W charger refills the 6000 mAh battery quickly, yet the large cell does not last a full day under heavy use and drains fast when the screen is bright.

HyperOS feels cluttered and applies aggressive, non‑removable image processing that oversharpens photos and colors, while the main camera’s video stabilization struggles with even slight hand movement. The massive camera bump feels intrusive in the hand. Because every unit is a gray import, there is no official Xiaomi warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s limited cover and must verify IMEI status.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

The 6000 mAh cell charges quickly with 90W, but owners report it struggles to last a full day under heavy use, especially when the bright 3500‑nit display is active.

Is the phone good for high‑end mobile gaming?

Yes, the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and 16 GB RAM provide industry‑leading sustained performance, and the 120 Hz LTPO panel keeps games smooth and responsive.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with a large 1/1.4" size captures detail, but aggressive sharpening and color processing can make low‑light shots look over‑processed, and video stabilization is weak.

Will I get official Xiaomi warranty and updates?

All units are gray imports, so there is no official Xiaomi warranty in Bangladesh. The shop may offer limited cover. Software updates follow the global roadmap with three to four OS upgrades and four to five security patches.
