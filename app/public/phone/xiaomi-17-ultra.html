Is the Xiaomi 17 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • 120 Hz LTPO AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset
  • 50 MP Leica‑tuned main camera
  • 90 W fast charging

What is not

  • Battery drains fast under heavy use
  • Camera processing oversharpens and colors look off
  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Video stabilization is poor

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize raw performance, high‑refresh display and fast charging and accept a gray‑import warranty.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable battery life, official warranty or clean software.

Why we say that

The 6.9" LTPO AMOLED panel hits 3500 nits peak and stays smooth at 120 Hz, making outdoor use pleasant. Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 keeps games and apps running without throttling, and the 90 W charger refills the 6000 mAh cell quickly. The 50 MP main sensor with Leica tuning captures detailed photos, yet aggressive sharpening and color shifts disappoint many users.

Video stabilization struggles, and the camera app often resets its settings. HyperOS offers many features but feels cluttered and shows ads in system apps. Battery life is inconsistent, draining fast under heavy use despite the large capacity.

The phone arrives as a gray‑import with only the shop’s warranty, so repairs are paid and you must verify the global ROM version before buying.

Will the Xiaomi 17 Ultra receive regular software updates in Bangladesh?

The phone ships with Android 16 and HyperOS 3 and can be upgraded to Android 17. Xiaomi promises about three to four major OS upgrades and four to five security patches, but the system includes noticeable bloat and ads.

How long does the 6000 mAh battery last in everyday use?

Although the capacity is large, owners report inconsistent endurance. Heavy gaming or camera use drains the battery quickly, and many find the overall runtime shorter than expected for a flagship with a 6000 mAh cell.

Is the camera suitable for low‑light photography?

The 50 MP sensor with a 1/1.4" size and Leica colour tuning captures detailed images, but aggressive processing oversharpens and distorts colours. Video stabilization is weak and the camera app often resets its settings, affecting low‑light results.

What warranty and repair support are available for this phone?

All listings are gray‑import units, so there is no official Xiaomi warranty in Bangladesh. You rely on the shop’s warranty, and any repairs are paid. Verify the IMEI and that you receive the global ROM version before purchase.
