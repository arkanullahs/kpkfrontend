Buying verdict

Xiaomi 17 Ultra Is it worth buying?

The Xiaomi 17 Ultra delivers flagship performance and a blinding display, but its battery and camera software quirks can be frustrating.

Choose it if
You want top‑tier gaming, a super bright screen, and can accept a shop‑only warranty and occasional battery quirks.
Consider alternatives if
You need long, reliable battery life, stable video recording, or official Xiaomi after‑sales support.

What works well

  • 6.9" LTPO AMOLED 3500‑nit display
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 gaming performance
  • 50 MP Leica‑tuned main camera hardware
  • 90W fast charging fills 6000 mAh quickly

Reported trade-offs

  • Battery drains fast under heavy use
  • Only shop warranty, no official support
  • Camera processing oversharpened and colors off
  • Large camera bump feels heavy and awkward

Why we say that

The 6.9‑inch LTPO AMOLED panel reaches 3500 nits, making outdoor use easy, and the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 keeps games running at high frame rates without throttling. 16 GB of RAM and up to 1 TB of storage feel spacious, and the 90 W charger refills the 6000 mAh cell quickly. In real life, the battery drains noticeably fast when you push the phone with gaming or heavy camera work, and owners report inconsistent endurance.

The main camera hardware is excellent, yet aggressive image processing produces oversharpened pictures, color shifts, and shaky video because stabilization is weak. Software runs HyperOS 3, which is feature‑rich but cluttered and shows ads in system apps. The device arrives as a gray‑import with only a shop‑provided warranty, so you must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

How long does the battery last in everyday use?

The phone packs a 6000 mAh cell, but owners say it underperforms and drains quickly during gaming or heavy camera work. Battery life is inconsistent and rated only 6 out of 10, so expect shorter endurance than the capacity suggests.

Is there an official Xiaomi warranty for this model in Bangladesh?

No. All listings are gray‑import units, so the phone comes without any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The only protection is the shop‑provided warranty, and any repairs must be paid for by the owner.

Will the phone receive Android updates and for how long?

It ships with Android 16 and HyperOS 3 and is upgradable to Android 17. Xiaomi promises about three to four major OS upgrades and four to five security patches, but the software layer is heavy with ads.

How does the camera perform in low light and video?

The 50 MP sensor and Leica colour tuning give strong hardware results, but aggressive processing makes images oversharpened and colour‑shifted. Video stabilization is weak, so even slight hand movement appears in the footage, and the app often resets settings.
