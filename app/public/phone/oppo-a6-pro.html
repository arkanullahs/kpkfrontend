Is the OPPO A6 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 7000 mAh battery with 80 W fast charging
  • 120 Hz AMOLED display with 1400 nits brightness
  • IP68/69 water‑resistant military‑grade build
  • Smooth ColorOS 15 experience

What is not

  • Dimensity 6300 underperforms for the price
  • Camera weak in low light, no ultrawide
  • Video lacks stabilization, results are shaky
  • No microSD slot for extra storage

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, a bright high‑refresh display and can tolerate modest performance.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect flagship gaming, strong low‑light photos or an ultrawide camera.

Why we say that

It packs a 7000 mAh cell that can last two to three days on moderate use and refills in under an hour with 80 W fast charging. The 6.57‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 120 Hz and reaches 1400 nits, so it stays vivid even in bright sunlight. Build quality is solid with IP68/69 water resistance, and the ColorOS 15 skin feels smooth despite some bloat.

However the Dimensity 6300 chipset struggles in games and everyday multitasking, and the 50 MP main sensor delivers only average daylight shots with poor low‑light performance, no ultrawide lens and shaky video. The lack of a microSD slot limits storage expansion. Official warranty is available through OPPO’s Bangladesh service network, but owners consider the overall value far below the 30 k price tag.
