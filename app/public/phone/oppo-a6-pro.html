Is the OPPO A6 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • 7000mAh battery lasts two to three days
  • 80W fast charging fills quickly
  • 120Hz AMOLED display is vibrant
  • IP68/69 water resistance and solid build

What is not

  • Dimensity6300 feels underpowered for price
  • No ultrawide camera
  • Low‑light photos are poor
  • Videos lack stabilization

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, a bright smooth screen and can accept modest performance at this price.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect strong gaming, good low‑light photography or an ultrawide lens for the cost.

Why we say that

The 7000mAh cell gives you two to three days of use and the 80W charger refills it in minutes. The 6.57 inch AMOLED panel runs at 120Hz and stays bright even in sunlight thanks to 1400 nits peak brightness. The build feels solid with military‑grade durability and IP68/69 water resistance.

However the Dimensity 6300 chipset struggles with games and everyday multitasking, making the experience feel sluggish for a device priced at ৳30,699. The camera setup lacks an ultrawide lens, performs only average in daylight and fails in low light, while video recordings are shaky because there is no stabilization. Software runs on ColorOS 15, which is smooth but carries noticeable bloat.

Overall owners feel the phone is overpriced for the performance and camera compromises, even though the battery and display are commendable.
