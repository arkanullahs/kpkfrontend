Is the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 6 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Best-in-class multitasking software
  • Top-tier 7.6 inch display quality
  • Lighter and more refined build
  • Seven years of software support

What is not

  • Glacial 25W charging speeds
  • Severe thermal throttling issues
  • Poor under-display camera quality
  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize advanced multitasking software and a large foldable display for productivity.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a heavy gamer or need reliable, fast charging and a worry-free warranty.

Why we say that

While the 7.6 inch display is top-tier for brightness and quality, the persistent crease and reports of the inner screen protector bubbling remain major concerns for long-term owners. Performance is driven by the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, yet users report severe thermal throttling that can cause apps to fail or the screen to dim during heavy use. The 4400 mAh battery is decent for light tasks but causes anxiety during heavy tablet usage.

Charging is glacial, taking nearly 2 hours to reach full capacity. The under-display camera is poor, often making subjects look sick in photos. You are paying ৳1,29,799 for an unofficial unit with no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh, meaning any repairs are paid out of pocket.

While the software multitasking is best-in-class, the high price is difficult to justify given these hardware limitations.
