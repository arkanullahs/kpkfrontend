Is the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 6 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • Top-tier 7.6 inch foldable display
  • Best-in-class multitasking software
  • Seven years of Android updates
  • Lighter and more refined build

What is not

  • Severe thermal throttling and heating
  • Glacial 25W charging speeds
  • Poor under-display camera quality
  • Inner screen protector bubbling issues

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a large, high-quality foldable display and advanced multitasking features for light to moderate daily tasks.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a heavy gamer or power user who cannot tolerate thermal throttling, slow charging, or the lack of a manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

While the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset is powerful, owners report severe thermal throttling that causes apps to fail and the screen to dim during heavy use. The 4400 mAh battery is decent for light tasks, but it creates anxiety during heavy tablet usage. Charging is another major pain point, as the 25W speed is glacial and takes nearly 2 hours for a full charge.

The inner screen protector is prone to bubbling and breaking in the middle. Additionally, the under-display camera quality is poor, often making subjects look sick in photos. While the multitasking software is excellent, the high price of ৳1,29,799 makes it difficult to justify without trade-in credits.

You must remember that this is an unofficial gray import, meaning there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh and all repairs are paid.

What is the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 6 5G price in Bangladesh?

Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 6 5G costs ৳1,29,799 in Bangladesh on unofficial listings, in stock at 1 shop we track as of 9 Sep 2026.

Does the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 6 5G come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is an unofficial gray import. There is no manufacturer warranty available in Bangladesh regardless of the brand's service score. Any repairs required will be paid for by the owner, and coverage is limited to whatever the specific shop promises.

How long will the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 6 5G receive software updates?

This device is supported by a seven-year Android update promise. It runs on Android 14 with One UI 6.1.1 out of the box and is eligible for up to seven major Android upgrades, ensuring long-term software support for the hardware.

Is the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 6 5G good for heavy gaming?

While it features a powerful Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset, it suffers from significant thermal throttling. Owners report that the device gets hot, which leads to screen dimming and apps failing to open, making it a difficult choice for sustained heavy gaming sessions.

What is the battery life like on the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 6 5G?

The 4400 mAh battery is considered decent for light use. However, owners report that it causes anxiety during heavy tablet tasks. Combined with the glacial 25W charging speed that takes nearly 2 hours to reach full capacity, the battery experience is a notable weakness.
