  • Heavy ad‑filled software
  • Weak Unisoc T7250 performance
  • Low brightness screen
  • Gaming struggles on sustained load

Why we say that

The 7000 mAh battery and 100 W fast charging keep you running even when power is scarce. The 6.78‑inch IPS LCD with 120 Hz refresh feels smooth, but its brightness and contrast are modest, so outdoor use can be dull. The Unisoc T7250 chipset provides basic performance for messaging, browsing and light apps, yet it falters under sustained load and gaming, which owners rate poorly.

The software runs itelOS on Android Go, packed with ads and bloatware, and updates are infrequent. Build quality is acceptable for the price, and the official Bangladesh warranty covers service at authorized centers. Overall, the phone is suited for users who value battery life above speed, but expect a lag‑free gaming or premium display experience and will have to tolerate frequent ads.
