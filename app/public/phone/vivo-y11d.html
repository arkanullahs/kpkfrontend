Vivo Y11d Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that lasts a full day on a single charge, the Vivo Y11d delivers that without breaking the bank.

3 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life and a low official price.
Consider alternatives if

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well3

  • 6500 mAh battery with 44 W fast charge
  • 120 Hz bright 6.74‑inch LCD
  • 50 MP main camera sensor

Reported trade-offs4

  • Only 4 GB RAM causes multitask lag
  • Gaming suffers frame drops and heating
  • Single mono speaker limits audio
  • Front camera only 5 MP gives low selfies

Why we say that

The 6500 mAh battery and 44 W fast charger give you all‑day use and a one‑hour top‑up. The 6.74‑inch LCD with 120 Hz refresh feels smooth and the 1200‑nit peak brightness keeps the screen visible outdoors. The 50 MP main camera can capture detailed shots but the tuning is weak, especially in low light, and the 5 MP selfie camera is underwhelming.

Performance suffers from only 4 GB RAM, causing app lag and UI stutter, and gaming triggers frame drops and heating. Audio comes from a single mono speaker and there is no punch‑hole for the front camera. The phone runs Android 16 with Origin OS that shows a moderate amount of ads.

Overall it offers solid battery value at a low official price but compromises on speed, gaming and selfie quality.
