Samsung Galaxy A37 5G Is it worth buying?

If you value a big, bright AMOLED screen and a solid main camera more than raw speed, the Galaxy A37 5G will feel decent but not spectacular.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a large AMOLED screen, a decent main camera and official warranty and can accept modest performance and slower charging.
Consider alternatives if
You need fast gaming, ample storage, or a cheap price with warranty coverage.

What works well4

  • 6.7 inch Super AMOLED 120 Hz display
  • 50 MP main camera with optical image stabilization
  • IP68‑rated Victus+ glass build
  • 5G connectivity

Reported trade-offs3

  • Exynos 1480 struggles with gaming and heavy apps
  • 45 W charging feels slow and no wireless option
  • Gray‑import units lack official Samsung warranty

Why we say that

The 6.7‑inch Super AMOLED panel with 120 Hz refresh and 1900‑nit peak brightness makes everyday use pleasant, and the 50 MP main sensor with OIS captures clear photos in good light. Battery life is reliable thanks to the 5000 mAh cell, though the 45 W charger feels slower than expected and there is no wireless option. Performance suffers with the Exynos 1480, especially in gaming and heavy multitasking, and the 128 GB storage variant feels cramped with Android 16 and Samsung UI bloat.

The virtual proximity sensor is less accurate than a physical one. Official‑warranty units start at ৳51,499, while gray‑import models drop to ৳35,990 but lack manufacturer support. Overall, it is a middle‑ground phone that works for media consumption but may disappoint power users.
