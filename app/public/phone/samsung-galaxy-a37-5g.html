Samsung Galaxy A37 5G Is it worth buying?

If you want a bright 5G phone with a strong main camera and fast charging, the Galaxy A37 5G delivers, but expect modest performance and limited storage.

4 strengths · 3 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a bright 5G phone with a solid main camera, fast charging and can live with modest performance and limited internal storage.
Consider alternatives if
You expect flagship‑level gaming, high‑resolution HDR display, or need expandable storage.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 1900‑nit Super AMOLED display
  • 50MP main sensor with OIS
  • 45W fast charging
  • IP68 water resistance

Reported trade-offs3

  • Exynos 1480 feels outdated for 2026 games
  • 128GB storage too small, no SD slot
  • Virtual proximity sensor is unreliable

Why we say that

The 6.7‑inch Super AMOLED panel can hit 1900 nits, making it easy to see outdoors, and the 50MP main sensor with OIS captures clear photos in good light. Battery life is solid for everyday tasks thanks to the 5000 mAh cell and 45W charger. However, the Exynos 1480 chipset feels dated in 2026 and struggles with newer games, reflected in low gaming and performance scores.

The 128 GB variant quickly fills up because Android 16 is heavy and there is no microSD slot. The virtual proximity sensor often fails to turn off the screen during calls. The ultrawide lens is weak and the display lacks HDR and high resolution, which may disappoint media lovers.

Overall it suits users who value display brightness and camera basics over high‑end speed.
