Buying verdict

Samsung Galaxy A37 5G Is it worth buying?

The Samsung Galaxy A37 5G is a device that struggles to justify its price tag when compared to 2026 mid-range rivals.

Choose it if
You prioritize a long-term software update promise and can find the device at a significant price drop.
Consider alternatives if
You are a gamer or someone who needs reliable storage for many apps and media files.

What works well

  • Reliable 5000 mAh battery endurance
  • Strong main camera sensor performance
  • Durable Victus+ glass and IP68 rating
  • Bright 1900 nits Super AMOLED display

Reported trade-offs

  • Outdated Exynos 1480 chipset performance
  • Restrictive storage with no SD card slot
  • Unreliable virtual proximity sensor during calls
  • Cheap feeling plastic frame construction

Why we say that

While the 5000 mAh battery provides reliable endurance for daily tasks, the Exynos 1480 chipset feels outdated and underperforms during gaming sessions. Owners frequently report frustration with the virtual proximity sensor, which fails to turn off the screen consistently during calls. The lack of an SD card slot combined with the heavy bloatware on Android 16 makes the 128GB storage variant feel extremely restrictive.

Although the build includes Victus+ and IP68 protection, the plastic frame feels cheap in the hand. The display is bright at 1900 nits, but users are disappointed by the low resolution and lack of HDR support. Given that the older Galaxy A53 5G is available for ৳32,999, this phone is currently poor value for money.

Does the official warranty cover all Samsung Galaxy A37 5G units?

No, the brand warranty applies only to the official unit. The cheaper gray import listings have no manufacturer cover, and any service centre that accepts a gray import unit will only perform repairs as a paid service.

How long will this phone receive software updates?

The Samsung Galaxy A37 5G comes with an excellent 6-year update promise. This is part of the standard support for the A-series, which typically receives between 4 and 6 years of updates depending on the specific model release.

Is the Samsung Galaxy A37 5G good for gaming?

Gaming performance is a weak point for this device. Owners have rated the gaming experience at 5.8 out of 10, noting that the Exynos 1480 chipset feels outdated and struggles to keep up with other mid-range rivals released in 2026.
