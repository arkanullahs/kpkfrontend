Is the POCO X7 worth buying?

What is good

  • High-res AMOLED display with Dolby Vision
  • Reliable day and night camera stills
  • Premium build with IP68 rating
  • Strong value compared to Redmi Note series

What is not

  • HyperOS 2 app reloading bugs
  • Slower charging than the X6 model
  • High amount of bloatware and ads
  • Below par web browsing battery efficiency

Buy it if You want a high-quality display and a reliable camera system for a competitive price and are comfortable managing unofficial warranty terms.

Skip it if You require a phone with a brand warranty or need a device that handles heavy multitasking without app reloading issues.

Why we say that

Owners report that the device feels premium in the hand thanks to its improved build and IP68 rating. While the 50MP camera with OIS provides reliable results for day and night photography, the overall user experience is hampered by software bugs. Specifically, users have complained about severe app reloading issues when using HyperOS 2, though this has seen improvements in OS 3.

The battery offers good endurance, yet web browsing efficiency is lower than expected and the 45W charging speed is slower than the previous X6 model. It is important to note that this phone is currently only available through unofficial channels, meaning you will receive a shop warranty rather than a brand warranty. Always confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before making a purchase.

Does the POCO X7 have a good battery for daily use?

The phone features a 5110 mAh battery that provides good overall endurance. However, owners have noted that efficiency during web browsing is below par, so your mileage may vary depending on your specific usage habits.

Is the POCO X7 suitable for mobile gaming?

The Dimensity 7300 Ultra chipset handles daily tasks smoothly and is decent for occasional gaming. It is not a flagship device, so users should manage their expectations regarding high-end performance in demanding games.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The main 50MP camera includes OIS and a Sony IMX sensor. Owners report that it provides reliable results for both day and night stills, making it a solid choice for photography in various lighting conditions.

What is the software update situation for this phone?

The device runs on Android 14 with HyperOS. You can expect to receive 2 to 3 OS updates over the lifespan of the phone. Be aware that the software comes with a high amount of bloatware and ads.
