What works well4

  • Bright 3000‑nit AMOLED display
  • Strong 5110 mAh battery endurance
  • Durable IP68/69 build with Victus 2 glass
  • Fast 45 W charging (takes over 1 hour)

Reported trade-offs4

  • UFS 2.2 storage causes app jitters
  • HyperOS adds heavy bloat and ads
  • 45 W charger slower than expected
  • Proximity sensor often fails

Why we say that

The 6.67" AMOLED panel reaches 3000 nits and runs at 120 Hz, making everyday use look sharp. The 5110 mAh cell provides about 7 to 8 hours of screen‑on time, which owners call strong endurance. Build quality is high with IP68/69 protection and Gorilla Glass Victus 2, so the phone feels durable.

The 50 MP main camera captures decent daylight shots, but the ultrawide lens is weak and selfies are only average. Performance is smooth for daily tasks and the phone stays cool while gaming, yet the UFS 2.2 storage leads to occasional app launch jitters and the HyperOS skin adds noticeable bloat and ads. Charging at 45 W is slower than expected, taking over 75 minutes for a full charge.

Read full reasoning

Proximity sensor failures and a misleading Always‑On Display also bother users. Overall it works well if you value display and battery more than premium software and storage speed.
