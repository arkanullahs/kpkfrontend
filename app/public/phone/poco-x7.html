What works well

  • 6.67 inch 120 Hz AMOLED display
  • 5110 mAh battery with strong endurance
  • IP68/69 rating and Gorilla Glass Victus 2
  • 50 MP main camera works well in daylight

Reported trade-offs

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • 45 W charger needs over 75 minutes for full charge
  • UFS 2.2 storage causes app launch jitters
  • HyperOS adds high bloat and frequent app reloads

Why we say that

The 6.67 inch AMOLED panel reaches 3000 nits peak brightness and runs at 120 Hz, making media consumption pleasant. Build quality is solid with IP68/69 protection and Gorilla Glass Victus 2, so drops are less of a worry. The 50 MP main camera performs well in daylight, though the ultrawide lens is weak and low‑light shots are average.

Performance feels smooth for daily tasks, but the UFS 2.2 storage introduces occasional app launch jitters. HyperOS adds noticeable bloat and frequent app reloads, and the 45 W charger needs over 75 minutes for a full charge. Overall, it is a value‑focused phone if you accept the gray‑import warranty and software quirks.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

Owners report the 5110 mAh cell gives about 7‑8 hours of screen‑on time, which is strong for a 6.67 inch phone. The endurance stays good even with the 120 Hz panel, but heavy use will still need a daily charge.

Will the phone handle gaming without overheating?

The Dimensity 7300 Ultra keeps the device cool during most gaming sessions and the build’s IP68/69 rating helps with heat dissipation. Some users notice occasional slow‑downs because UFS 2.2 storage jitters, but the phone does not overheat.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.5 aperture and OIS captures clear daylight photos, but owners say low‑light performance drops and the ultrawide lens is weak. Selfies from the 20 MP front camera are average.

What warranty and software update support does the POCO X7 have?

All listings are gray imports, so there is no official POCO warranty in Bangladesh. The shop provides its own limited warranty and any repair will be charged. Software updates are limited to two or three Android versions and HyperOS includes noticeable bloat and ads.
