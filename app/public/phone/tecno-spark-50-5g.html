Is the Tecno SPARK 50 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 6500 mAh battery lasting all day
  • 45 W fast charging
  • 5G connectivity at a low price
  • 120 Hz smooth display

What is not

  • 720p screen resolution feels low
  • Base RAM feels insufficient for heavy apps
  • HiOS adds noticeable bloatware and ads
  • Camera struggles in low light

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize long battery life, 5G and a cheap price.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a high‑resolution screen, strong low‑light camera or plenty of RAM.

Why we say that

Fast 45 W charging refills it quickly, and 5G connectivity works reliably on the Dimensity 6400 platform. The 6.78" IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, giving smooth motion, but the 720p resolution feels blurry compared to higher‑end screens. Memory is listed as 6 GB, yet many owners complain the base RAM feels tight for heavy apps and gaming.

HiOS 16 brings Android 16 features but also a lot of bloatware and ads, and software updates arrive slowly, usually one or two versions. The 50 MP main camera captures bright shots, but low‑light performance is mediocre and the 8 MP selfie lens is modest. Build quality includes a matte finish and a camera bar, though it scores only average in owner ratings.

Overall, it offers strong battery and connectivity at a budget price, but compromises on display sharpness, RAM headroom and software polish.
