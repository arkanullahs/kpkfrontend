Is the Nokia 220 4G 2024 worth buying?

What is good

  • Sturdy classic plastic construction
  • Very easy to use
  • Battery lasts due to low power draw
  • Includes FM radio and headphone jack

What is not

  • Small and dim TFT screen
  • Poor camera and video quality
  • Extremely limited performance

Buy it if You need a simple, sturdy official phone for calls and texts.

Skip it if You want to play games or need a good screen.

Why we say that

This phone is a basic tool for calls and texts. Owners appreciate the sturdy plastic build and find it very easy to use. However, the experience is limited.

The screen is small, dim, and low resolution. Performance is very weak, and the camera and video quality are poor. While the battery capacity is low, it lasts a while because the phone uses very little power.

It is not a device for entertainment or apps. You will mostly find complaints about the display and the lack of performance.
