Is the Apple iPhone 12 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Powerful Apple A14 Bionic chipset
  • Bright 1200 nits Super Retina XDR display
  • Versatile 12MP camera system with OIS
  • Reliable 5G connectivity support

What is not

  • Small 2815 mAh battery capacity
  • No official brand warranty available
  • Limited remaining software update life
  • Only gray-import stock options

Buy it if Buy this phone if you want an affordable entry into the Apple ecosystem and are comfortable with the risks of a gray-import device.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you need a device with a long-lasting battery or guaranteed future software updates.

Why we say that

While the A14 Bionic chipset remains capable, the 2815 mAh battery is small by modern standards and will likely struggle to last through a full day of heavy use. You are limited to gray-import stock, which means you lose the security of a brand warranty and must rely on a shop warranty instead. It is essential to verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before handing over your money.

Because the phone is 6 years old, you should not expect any significant future software updates. This device is strictly for those who prioritize the Apple ecosystem on a budget and are comfortable managing the risks associated with older gray-market hardware.
