What works well

  • Bright 6.1 inch Super Retina XDR OLED display
  • A14 Bionic chip handles apps smoothly
  • 5G support for faster data
  • 12 MP triple camera with optical image stabilization

Reported trade-offs

  • Small 2,815 mAh battery limits endurance
  • Limited future iOS updates after six years
  • Only gray import stock, shop warranty only

Why we say that

The 6.1 inch Super Retina XDR OLED panel reaches 1,200 nits, making outdoor use easy. The A14 Bionic chip runs iOS smoothly and handles most apps without lag. The triple 12 MP camera with optical image stabilization gives good photos in daylight and decent low‑light results.

Battery capacity is only 2,815 mAh, so a full day of heavy use may require a charge before night. The phone is six years old, so the remaining iOS update window is short and you may not see many future features. Only gray import stock is available, meaning you get a shop warranty rather than an official Apple guarantee.

Apple iPhone 12 Pro: what buyers ask us

What is the Apple iPhone 12 Pro price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for Apple iPhone 12 Pro in Bangladesh was ৳54,990, checked 11 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Will the battery last a full day of typical use?

The 2,815 mAh battery is modest for a phone of this size. Light to moderate use will usually get you through the day, but heavy browsing or gaming may need a charge before evening.

Can the iPhone 12 Pro run modern games smoothly?

The A14 Bionic processor is still capable of handling most current games at playable frame rates. Graphics‑intensive titles will run, but you may need to lower settings to keep temperatures comfortable.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The main 12 MP sensor has an f/1.6 aperture and optical image stabilization, which helps in dim environments. Results are decent but not as bright or detailed as newer models with larger sensors.

Will the phone receive iOS updates for many more years?

The device launched in 2020 and has already received five to six major iOS versions. Being six years old, Apple is likely to stop major updates soon, so future support will be limited.
