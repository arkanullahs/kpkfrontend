iQOO Z11 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can last two days on a single charge, the iQOO Z11 delivers that but you will feel its limits in heavy gaming and low‑light photography.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You value long battery life and a smooth high‑refresh screen more than top gaming performance.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable high‑end gaming or a strong low‑light camera.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 9020mAh battery lasts two days
  • 90W fast charging fills in about an hour
  • 144Hz AMOLED display with high brightness
  • IP68/69 water and dust resistance

Reported trade-offs4

  • Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 struggles in heavy games
  • Noticeable heating during long sessions
  • Camera dynamic range is inconsistent
  • No official brand warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

The 9020mAh battery and 90W charger keep you online for 48 hours and refill in just over an hour. The 6.83‑inch AMOLED panel runs at 144Hz, shows vivid colors and reaches a peak of 5000 nits, making media consumption pleasant. The Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset handles everyday tasks well yet struggles with demanding titles like Genshin Impact, often heating up and dropping frames.

The single 50MP rear sensor with OIS captures decent photos but suffers from inconsistent dynamic range and lacks an ultrawide lens. OriginOS 6 runs smoothly but you will notice notification delays and medium‑level ads. The phone arrives as a gray‑import with no manufacturer warranty, so any repair will be paid and you must verify the ROM version before buying.
