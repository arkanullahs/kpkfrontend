Buying verdict

Xiaomi 17T Is it worth buying?

The Xiaomi 17T offers a massive 6500 mAh battery that provides exceptional endurance, but the overall experience is held back by significant software and build quality issues.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize long battery life and stable gaming performance over premium build materials and software refinement.
Consider alternatives if
You are sensitive to UI stutters, dislike ads in your system apps, or expect a premium metal build quality.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Exceptional 6500 mAh battery endurance
  • Stable and cool gaming performance
  • Great 5x telephoto lens
  • Very bright 3500 nits peak display

Reported trade-offs4

  • Cheap feeling plastic frame
  • Inconsistent 60Hz display drops
  • Bloatware and ads in HyperOS
  • Main camera exposure and skin tone issues

Why we say that

While the Dimensity 8500 Ultra chipset keeps gaming stable and cool, owners frequently report micro-stutters because the 120Hz display inconsistently drops to 60Hz. The plastic frame feels cheap for the price, and the camera system struggles with overexposure and aggressive skin smoothing despite the inclusion of a 5x telephoto lens. HyperOS 3 is feature-rich but frustrating due to persistent bloatware, ads, and UI bugs.

You will also find the Always-On Display limited to a 10-second duration. If you prioritize battery life above all else, this device performs well, but the inconsistent display refresh rate and build quality make it a difficult recommendation for those expecting a premium experience at this price point.

Is the Xiaomi 17T good for mobile gaming?

The Dimensity 8500 Ultra chipset provides stable and cool performance during gaming sessions. While it lacks flagship-tier raw power, owners rate the gaming performance at 8.2 out of 10, making it a reliable choice for most mobile games.

What are the main concerns regarding the camera performance?

Owners report that the main sensor often struggles with exposure and skin tones. Pictures can appear overexposed with muted colors, and the software applies aggressive skin smoothing to portraits, despite the high quality of the 5x telephoto lens.
