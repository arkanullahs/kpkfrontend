Xiaomi 17T Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day on one charge, the Xiaomi 17T delivers.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want long battery life, a bright screen, and decent gaming performance at a mid‑range price.
Consider alternatives if
You need a premium build, flawless camera output, or a clean software experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • 6500 mAh battery lasts all day
  • Bright 3500 nits AMOLED display
  • 5× telephoto lens on 50 MP camera
  • IR blaster for remote control

Reported trade-offs4

  • Plastic frame feels cheap
  • Screen often drops to 60 Hz
  • Camera over‑exposes skin tones
  • HyperOS includes bloatware and ads

Why we say that

Its 6500 mAh battery easily lasts a full day of heavy use and the 67 W charger refills it in a reasonable time. The 6.59‑inch AMOLED panel reaches a peak of 3500 nits, making outdoor viewing clear, but the refresh rate often falls back to 60 Hz even when set to 120 Hz, causing noticeable stutters. The Dimensity 8500 Ultra chipset stays cool and handles most games well, though it does not match flagship raw power.

The 50 MP main sensor offers a 5× telephoto lens that is useful, yet many owners complain about over‑exposed photos and muted skin tones. The plastic frame feels cheap despite an IP68 rating, and HyperOS 3 is packed with features but also brings bloatware, ads, and occasional UI bugs. Official units carry a brand warranty, while cheaper gray‑import listings do not.
