Xiaomi 17T Is it worth buying?

The Xiaomi 17T offers a massive 6500mAh battery and a stunning display, but it struggles to justify its premium price tag due to build quality and software frustrations.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a long-lasting battery and a high-quality display for media consumption and photography.
Consider alternatives if
You demand a premium metal build, clean software without ads, or consistent high-refresh-rate gaming performance.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Outstanding 6500mAh battery endurance
  • Stunning 1.5K AMOLED display with high brightness
  • Excellent 5x telephoto camera with Leica tuning
  • Capable Dimensity 8500 Ultra performance

Reported trade-offs4

  • Plastic frame feels cheap for the price
  • Inconsistent 120Hz refresh rate causes micro-stutters
  • Ad-heavy software with bloatware issues
  • Slow 67W charging speed for the segment

Why we say that

While the 5x telephoto lens provides excellent reach, owners frequently report issues with overexposure and aggressive image processing. The plastic frame feels underwhelming for a device costing up to ৳77,599, and the 67W charging speed is considered slow for this segment. Users also face persistent micro-stutters because the 120Hz refresh rate inconsistently drops to 60Hz.

HyperOS provides a smooth experience, but the presence of bloatware and system ads remains a significant annoyance. The low placement of the fingerprint sensor further complicates daily use. If you prioritize battery endurance and screen quality over premium materials and software cleanliness, this device might suit your needs, but be prepared for these specific compromises.

Does the Xiaomi 17T come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

Official warranty applies only to units purchased through official channels, which start at ৳74,999. Cheaper gray-import units have no manufacturer cover, and service centers will only accept them for paid repairs.

How long will the Xiaomi 17T receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 16 and HyperOS 3. It is scheduled to receive 3 to 4 OS updates and 4 to 5 years of security updates to keep the software current.

Is the Xiaomi 17T suitable for heavy gaming?

While the Dimensity 8500 Ultra is capable for daily tasks, owners report that the phone throttles under sustained load. Gaming performance is rated at 7.2 out of 10, suggesting it may struggle with demanding titles.

What should I know about the camera quality before buying?

The camera features a 50MP main sensor and a 5x telephoto lens with Leica tuning. However, users report that photos often suffer from overexposure and processing artifacts, making images look too bright at times.
