Is the Xiaomi 17T worth buying?

What is good

  • 6500 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 6.59‑inch AMOLED with 3500 nits peak
  • 50 MP main camera with 5× telephoto
  • Dimensity 8500 Ultra handles games coolly

What is not

  • Plastic frame feels cheap
  • Screen often drops to 60 Hz
  • Main sensor overexposes and mutes colors
  • HyperOS shows bloatware and ads

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large battery, bright screen and decent camera at a mid‑range price and can tolerate a plastic body and software ads.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a premium metal build, flawless UI refresh rate, or a clean Android experience.

Why we say that

The 6.59‑inch AMOLED is extremely bright with a 3500‑nit peak, yet the refresh rate often falls back to 60 Hz even when set to 120 Hz, causing visible stutter. The Dimensity 8500 Ultra chipset runs games smoothly and stays cool, though it does not match flagship raw power. The 50 MP main camera offers a useful 5× telephoto lens, but the sensor frequently overexposes scenes and mutes skin tones, especially in low light.

HyperOS 3 brings many features but is cluttered with bloatware and ads, and the always‑on display is limited to ten seconds. Overall, the phone feels like a solid mid‑range device with standout battery and display, while its build, software polish, and camera consistency hold it back.
