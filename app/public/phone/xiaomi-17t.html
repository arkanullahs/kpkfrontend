Xiaomi 17T Is it worth buying?

Expect a huge, bright screen and a battery that can survive a full day of heavy use, but be ready for a plastic feel and occasional UI hiccups.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs

What works well4

  • 6.59‑inch 120 Hz AMOLED display
  • 50 MP main camera with 5× telephoto
  • 6500 mAh battery with 67 W fast charging
  • Dimensity 8500 Ultra stable performance

Reported trade-offs4

  • Plastic frame feels cheap
  • Screen often drops to 60 Hz
  • HyperOS includes bloatware and ads
  • Main camera overexposes skin tones

Why we say that

The 6.59‑inch AMOLED panel reaches 3500‑nit peaks and offers 120 Hz refresh, yet it often falls back to 60 Hz, causing visible stutter in menus. The Dimensity 8500 Ultra chipset keeps games stable and cool, though it does not match flagship raw power. The 50 MP main camera provides a useful 5× telephoto lens, but many users complain about over‑exposed shots and muted skin tones.

HyperOS 3 adds many features but is cluttered with bloatware and ads that can be disabled only with effort. The 6500 mAh battery scores 9.3 for endurance, while 67 W charging is merely average for the price. Official units start at ৳60,999 and include a brand warranty; cheaper gray‑import models start at ৳53,990 and lack manufacturer coverage.
