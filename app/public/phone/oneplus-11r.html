Is the OnePlus 11R worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz Fluid AMOLED display
  • Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 performance
  • 100W wired fast charging
  • 50MP main camera with OIS

What is not

  • Battery drains before a full day
  • Phone may hang in bright sun
  • Only shop warranty, no brand warranty
  • Software updates likely ended

Buy it if Buy if you want flagship speed, great display and can handle limited battery life.

Skip it if Skip if you need full day battery or official OnePlus warranty.

Why we say that

The OnePlus 11R runs on Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 and offers 16GB RAM with up to 512GB storage, so apps open instantly and games stay fluid at 120Hz. The 6.74 inch Fluid AMOLED panel reaches 1450 nits peak, making outdoor use bright and clear. Charging is rapid with 100W wired speed, filling the 5000 mAh cell in minutes, yet owners report the battery rarely lasts a full day and often needs two charges.

The main 50MP sensor with OIS delivers decent photos in daylight, but low light results are only average. OxygenOS 15 provides a clean Android 13 experience and can be upgraded to Android 15, though the three year old device likely has few updates left. The phone arrives as a gray import unit, so only a shop warranty is offered and you must verify the IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.
