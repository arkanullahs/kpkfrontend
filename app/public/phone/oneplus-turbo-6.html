Is the OnePlus Turbo 6 worth buying?

What is good

  • 9000 mAh battery lasting up to two days
  • Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 performance for gaming
  • 1800 nits peak brightness on AMOLED display
  • IP68/69 water dust protection

What is not

  • No ultra wide or telephoto lens
  • Main camera struggles with exposure and highlights
  • Plastic frame feels cheap
  • ColorOS includes Chinese bloatware and ads

Buy it if Buy it if you value long battery life, strong gaming performance and can live without Google services or a premium camera.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a high‑quality camera, official OnePlus warranty or OxygenOS experience.

Why we say that

The device runs on a Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset with 12 GB RAM, delivering strong performance and smooth gaming at 165 Hz on a 6.78 inch AMOLED panel that can reach 1800 nits peak brightness. IP68/69 rating protects against water and dust, yet the plastic frame feels cheap for a phone priced at 49,490 taka. The main 50 MP sensor lacks ultra wide and telephoto lenses and often mishandles exposure and highlights, earning a low camera rating.

The China‑ROM version ships with ColorOS 16, bringing Chinese bloatware and ads, and it does not include Google services or Bangla language support. Warranty is only from the shop, not the brand, and converting to a global ROM can void it and make updates fragile.
