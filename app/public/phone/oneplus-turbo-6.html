Buying verdict

OnePlus Turbo 6 Is it worth buying?

Expect a phone that can run games at high frame rates while lasting a full day and a half on a single charge, but don’t expect a polished camera or official support.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need massive battery life, a bright high refresh screen, and strong gaming performance at a gray‑import price.
Consider alternatives if
You rely on a strong camera, official warranty, or native Google and Bangla support.

  • 9000 mAh battery lasting up to two days

Reported trade-offs4

  • No official manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh

Why we say that

The 9000 mAh battery gives a rating of 9.8 and owners report up to two days of use before needing a recharge. The 6.78 inch AMOLED panel reaches 1800 nit peak brightness and refreshes at 165 Hz, making outdoor viewing easy. Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 keeps performance and gaming scores above 8, and the thermal system stays stable under load.

The plastic frame feels cheap despite an IP68/69 rating, and the Chinese ColorOS lacks Google services and adds unwanted apps. Camera quality suffers from missing ultrawide and telephoto lenses and poor exposure control, earning a low rating. Repairs are paid and warranty is limited to the shop’s promise.

How long does the battery last in real use?

The 9000 mAh battery is rated 9.8 by owners and provides about one and a half to two days of endurance before needing a recharge, even with moderate gaming and screen use.

Can the phone handle heavy gaming without heating up?

Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 delivers performance scores above 8 and gaming scores of 8.2. Reviews note effective thermal management that stays stable under heavy loads, so long gaming sessions remain comfortable.

Is the camera good for low‑light photography?

The 50 MP main sensor has f/1.9 aperture and OIS, but owners complain about poor exposure control, limited dynamic range, and the lack of ultrawide or telephoto lenses, resulting in low ratings for camera quality.

Will I get an official warranty or regular software updates?

All units are gray imports with no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop offers its own limited warranty and repairs are paid. The phone runs a China ROM of ColorOS 16, which may receive fragile updates and conversion to global software voids any warranty.
