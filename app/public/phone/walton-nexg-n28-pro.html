Why we say that

The 6000 mAh battery easily lasts over a day, which is great for heavy users. However, charging that large battery with 18W is slow. Owners frequently complain about the display, noting that the HD+ resolution and 480 nits brightness make it hard to see in direct sunlight.

The software experience is hindered by ads and bloatware that require manual cleanup. While the 120Hz refresh rate feels smooth, the overall performance is limited. The Helio G81 chipset handles basic tasks but struggles with gaming.
