Is the Walton NEXG N28 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Excellent battery endurance from 6000 mAh
  • Smooth 120Hz display refresh rate
  • Premium look with IP64 rating

What is not

  • Low HD+ resolution and brightness
  • Slow 18W charging speed
  • Heavy bloatware and ads
  • Plastic build feels hollow

Buy it if Buy this phone if you need a device that lasts all day on a single charge and you only perform basic daily tasks.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you are a gamer, want a sharp display for media, or dislike dealing with pre-installed ads and bloatware.

Why we say that

Living with this phone means relying on the 6000 mAh battery to last over a day, but you will have to manage significant bloatware and ads that clutter the software experience. While the 120Hz refresh rate makes scrolling feel smooth, the low HD+ resolution and limited brightness make it difficult to see the screen clearly in direct sunlight. The Helio G81 chipset is strictly for basic tasks, and owners find that gaming requires low settings to remain functional.

The plastic build feels hollow despite the IP64 rating, and the 18W charging speed is quite slow for such a large battery. You should expect to spend time cleaning up the software to improve performance. If you prioritize long battery life above all else, this phone serves that purpose, but it falls short for users who value display clarity, fast charging, or a clean software interface.
