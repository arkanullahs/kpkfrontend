Is the Redmi 15C 4G worth buying?

What is good

  • 6000 mAh battery with 33 W fast charging
  • 50 MP main camera for daylight shots
  • Headphone jack and FM radio
  • 120 Hz refresh on large display

What is not

  • 720p resolution looks pixelated on 6.9‑inch panel
  • Severe UI lag and frequent app crashes
  • Battery can drop 20‑30 % overnight
  • No manufacturer warranty on gray‑import units

Buy it if If you need a big screen, long battery and basic camera and can tolerate slow performance.

Skip it if If you want smooth gaming, fast UI or reliable warranty coverage.

Why we say that

The phone packs a 6000 mAh cell and 33 W charger, so it can survive a day of light use, but software bugs often cut several percent off overnight. The Helio G81 Ultra with 4 GB RAM manages simple apps, yet the UI is noticeably laggy and many apps crash. The 50 MP sensor delivers acceptable daylight photos, but lack of stabilization and poor low‑light handling limit its usefulness.

The 720p panel on such a large display looks pixelated, and the 120 Hz refresh does little to hide the blur. HyperOS adds many ads and runs heavy on the modest hardware. The phone includes a headphone jack, FM radio and a 120 Hz refresh, but it lacks modern comforts like dual speakers or an IR blaster.

Official units carry a brand warranty, while gray‑import listings do not.
