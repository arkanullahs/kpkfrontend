Is the Redmi 15 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 7000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 6.9‑inch 144 Hz display feels smooth
  • 50 MP main camera works well in daylight
  • IR blaster adds remote control capability

What is not

  • System UI filled with intrusive ads
  • No gyroscope limits gaming performance
  • Low‑light photos are poor
  • 33 W charging is slow for large battery

Buy it if Buy it if you prioritize long battery life and a large, smooth screen over gaming and low‑light photography.

Skip it if Skip it if you need strong gaming performance, a gyroscope, or a clean ad‑free software experience.

Why we say that

Its 7000 mAh battery and efficient Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 chipset give you more than a day of mixed use, and the 33 W charger can top it up overnight. The 6.9‑inch IPS LCD runs at 144 Hz, so scrolling feels fluid, and the 50 MP sensor produces decent photos in bright light. However, the software layer is cluttered with system‑wide ads that appear everywhere, and the lack of a gyroscope makes gaming feel sluggish, with PUBG or CODM dropping to about 20 fps on low settings.

Low‑light photography is weak and video options are limited. The phone arrives as a gray‑import unit, so you only get a shop warranty and must verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The Redmi 15 5G houses a 7000 mAh cell that owners rate 9.8 for endurance. In mixed daily use it can easily exceed a full day, and it even supports reverse charging for other devices.

Can I play games like PUBG or CODM smoothly?

Gaming on this phone is weak. It lacks a gyroscope and the Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 struggles, so PUBG or CODM run at about 20 fps on the lowest settings with noticeable stutters.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP sensor captures decent images in bright conditions, but owners report poor low‑light performance and limited video capabilities, making night shots noisy and lacking detail.

Will I get a brand warranty and regular software updates?

Only gray‑import units are available, so you receive a shop warranty rather than an official Xiaomi guarantee. The phone ships with Android 15, can be upgraded to Android 16, and HyperOS promises 2‑4 major OS updates, though it carries many ads.
