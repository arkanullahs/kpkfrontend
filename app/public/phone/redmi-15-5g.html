Buying verdict

Redmi 15 5G Is it worth buying?

The Redmi 15 5G is a battery-focused device that prioritizes longevity over performance, making it a niche choice for specific users.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize extreme battery life for basic tasks and do not care about gaming or high-end camera performance.
Consider alternatives if
You are a mobile gamer, dislike system ads, or require a reliable manufacturer warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Industry-leading 7000 mAh battery capacity
  • Large and smooth 144Hz IPS display
  • Premium-feeling polycarbonate and metal hybrid build
  • Includes a functional IR blaster

Reported trade-offs4

  • Weak gaming performance with frequent stutters
  • Intrusive system-wide ads throughout the interface
  • Poor low-light camera and video capabilities
  • Lack of a hardware gyroscope

Why we say that

Owners report that the 7000 mAh battery is industry-leading, but the trade-offs are significant. You will deal with a weak Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 chipset that struggles with gaming, often hitting only 20fps in titles like PUBG or CODM. The software experience is heavily burdened by intrusive system-wide ads that users find constant and frustrating.

While the 144Hz IPS display is large and smooth, it lacks the vibrancy of OLED panels. The camera performance is serviceable in daylight but fails in low-light conditions, and the lack of a hardware gyroscope limits its utility for many. Because these units are gray imports, you must rely entirely on shop-provided support rather than manufacturer backing.

Read full reasoning

If you value battery life above all else, this phone delivers, but the poor gaming performance and heavy ad presence make it a difficult daily driver for most.

Does the Redmi 15 5G come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone does not have any manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. Every unit available is a gray import, meaning you must rely on the specific shop for any repair promises. You should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before purchasing.

Is the Redmi 15 5G good for playing games like PUBG?

The phone is not recommended for gaming. Owners report weak performance with the Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 chipset, noting that intensive titles like PUBG or CODM run at only 20fps on the lowest settings with noticeable stutters.

How long will the Redmi 15 5G receive software updates?

The device runs Android 15 and is upgradable to Android 16 with HyperOS 3. Depending on the specific tier of the device, it is expected to receive between two and four operating system updates over its lifespan.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The 50MP main camera is described as serviceable for daylight photography. However, owners report poor performance when shooting in low-light environments and note that the video capabilities are quite limited.
