Redmi 15 5G Is it worth buying?

The biggest advantage of the Redmi 15 5G is its massive 7000 mAh battery that easily lasts a full day of heavy use, but the phone comes with several trade‑offs that owners notice quickly.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a huge battery and a large smooth screen without caring about high end gaming or official warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You expect strong gaming, low light photography, or official warranty support.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 7000 mAh battery lasts all day with heavy use
  • 6.9‑inch 144 Hz IPS panel feels smooth
  • 50 MP main sensor gives decent daylight photos
  • 33 W charger refills the large battery reasonably fast

Reported trade-offs4

  • No manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh
  • Low light camera performance is poor
  • Gaming stalls at low frame rates
  • System UI filled with intrusive ads

Why we say that

The 6.9‑inch IPS panel runs at 144 Hz, giving smooth scrolling, yet it lacks the vibrancy of OLED and HDR. The 50 MP main camera produces decent daylight photos, but low light performance is poor and video features are limited. Gaming is a weak point; the Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 struggles, there is no gyroscope and titles like PUBG run at about 20 fps with stutters.

The software runs HyperOS on Android 15 and can be upgraded to Android 16, but it is cluttered with system‑wide ads. Finally, because every unit is a gray import built for India, there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh, and any repair will be paid by the owner.

Is there any official warranty for the Redmi 15 5G in Bangladesh?

No, every unit is a gray import built for India, so the phone has no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop you buy from may offer a limited cover, but any repair will be paid by you.

How long does the battery last in real use?

The 7000 mAh cell is rated as industry leading and owners give it a 9.8 rating. In daily use it easily survives a full day of browsing, video and calls, and it also supports reverse charging.

Can I play demanding games like PUBG or CODM smoothly?

Gaming performance is weak. The phone lacks a gyroscope and the Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 struggles, delivering about 20 fps with stutters even on the lowest settings. It is not suited for intensive titles.

How does the camera perform in low light and video?

Daylight photos are serviceable with the 50 MP sensor, but low light shots are poor and video capabilities are limited. Expect noisy pictures and lack of detail when lighting is weak.
