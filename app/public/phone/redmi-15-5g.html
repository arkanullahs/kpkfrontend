Buying verdict

Redmi 15 5G Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run all day without charging, the Redmi 15 5G will keep you online for many hours.

Choose it if
You value long battery life and a smooth screen more than top tier gaming or camera performance.
Consider alternatives if
You need official warranty, strong low light photos, or a phone that handles demanding games without stutter.

What works well

  • 7,000 mAh battery lasting over a day
  • 144 Hz IPS display feels smooth
  • 50 MP main camera captures decent daylight photos
  • 5G connectivity and IR blaster included

Reported trade-offs

  • Intrusive system ads appear everywhere
  • No hardware gyroscope for motion games
  • Gaming stalls at low frame rates
  • 33 W charging is slow for large battery

Why we say that

The 7,000 mAh battery lives through a full day of browsing, video and calls, and the 144 Hz IPS panel feels smooth on the 6.9 inch screen. Daylight photos from the 50 MP sensor are acceptable, and the device supports 5G and an IR blaster. However, the system is filled with ads that appear in most screens, and the phone lacks a hardware gyroscope, which hurts motion based games.

Gaming performance is weak; popular titles drop to about 20 fps with stutter even on the lowest settings. Charging at 33 W is slow for such a large cell, and low light photography is disappointing. Because the unit is a gray import, there is no official warranty; only the shop’s limited coverage applies.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

The 7,000 mAh cell is rated at 850 nits peak brightness and, according to owners, can easily cover a full day of mixed use such as browsing, video streaming and calls without needing to recharge. Reverse charging is also supported.

Will this phone run modern games smoothly?

Owners report that the Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 chipset struggles with demanding titles. PUBG or CODM at the lowest settings often drops to around 20 fps and shows noticeable stutter, so expect weak gaming performance.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP sensor with f/1.8 aperture delivers serviceable photos in daylight, but owners say low light shots are dark and lack detail. Video recording is also limited, making it unsuitable for night photography.

Is there any official warranty and how many Android updates will I get?

All units are gray imports built for India, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop offers its own limited warranty and any repairs are paid. Software runs Android 15, upgradeable to Android 16, with HyperOS receiving two to four OS updates depending on tier.
