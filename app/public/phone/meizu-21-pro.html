Meizu 21 Pro Is it worth buying?

If you can accept a gray‑import phone without Google services, the Meizu 21 Pro gives you a flagship‑class screen and fast performance for ৳74,990.

Why we say that

The 6.79‑inch LTPO AMOLED panel reaches 1250 nits and runs at 120 Hz, making everyday scrolling feel smooth. Under the hood, the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 on a 4 nm process delivers strong sustained performance and stays cool even during long gaming sessions. The 50 MP main camera with f/1.9 aperture, OIS and 3× telephoto captures decent low‑light shots, and the 32 MP selfie sensor is adequate for video calls.

Owners rate the display and performance highly, but they complain that the 5050 mAh battery is not efficient and often needs a charge before the day ends. The phone runs Flyme on Android 14, shows medium ads, and will not receive further OS updates. Because it is a China‑ROM gray import, there is no official Meizu warranty in Bangladesh and any repair cost falls to the shop’s limited warranty.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

Is the Meizu 21 Pro good for gaming?

How does the camera perform in low‑light?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.9 aperture, OIS and 3× telephoto handles low‑light better than many phones, though the lack of Google HDR processing may limit detail.
