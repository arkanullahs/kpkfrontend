Is the Infinix Note 12 G88 worth buying?

What is good

  • Bright 1000 nits AMOLED display
  • Capable 50MP main camera sensor
  • Includes a headphone jack and FM radio

What is not

  • Poor battery efficiency
  • High levels of bloatware and ads
  • Low performance and gaming ratings
  • No remaining software update life

Buy it if Buy this phone if you prioritize a bright AMOLED screen and a basic camera for simple tasks on a strict budget.

Skip it if Skip this phone if you need reliable battery life, smooth gaming performance, or a device that receives current software updates.

Why we say that

While the 6.7 inch AMOLED display reaches 1000 nits of peak brightness, users rate the overall display experience at only 4.0 out of 10. The Helio G88 chipset struggles with modern demands, reflected in low owner ratings for gaming and general performance. You will encounter high levels of bloatware and notification ads within the XOS 10.6 software.

Owners specifically mention that the 5000 mAh battery is not efficient despite its large capacity. Photography is handled by a 50MP main sensor, though users feel the lack of an ultrawide lens limits the device. Since this model was released in 2022, you should expect little to no remaining software support.

The build quality and ease of use also receive low marks from those who have used the phone daily.
