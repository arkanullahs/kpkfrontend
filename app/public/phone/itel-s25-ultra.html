What works well

  • Bright 1400 nit curved AMOLED display
  • Extremely slim 6.9mm build
  • Excellent battery endurance
  • Good selfie camera

Reported trade-offs

  • Slow 18W charging speed
  • Weak gaming performance
  • Proximity sensor malfunctions
  • High amount of bloatware

Why we say that

It feels great in the hand due to the 6.9mm thickness and the 1400 nit curved AMOLED display is highly praised for visibility. However, living with it reveals significant flaws. The Unisoc T620 chipset is basic and struggles with heavy games.

Owners frequently complain about a malfunctioning proximity sensor, especially after OS updates. You will also deal with high amounts of bloatware and ads in the software. While the battery lasts a long time, the 18W charging is outdated and slow.

Read full reasoning

It is a device that looks more expensive than it performs, and some users feel it was overpriced at launch.
