Is the itel S25 Ultra worth buying?

Expect a vivid, large screen but be ready for slow speed and storage tricks. The 6.78 inch AMOLED panel shines with 120Hz refresh and up to 1400 nits peak brightness, making outdoor use comfortable. The 50MP main sensor can capture images, yet most owners describe the photos as mediocre, especially in low light. Battery capacity is 5000 mAh, but real‑world tests show it drains quickly and does not last a full day of heavy use. The Unisoc T620 chipset paired with 8 GB RAM feels sluggish, and gaming scores are low. Storage numbers are unreliable; some units advertised as 256 GB actually contain only 16 GB. The software layer adds many ads and bloat, and updates are rare beyond the promised two major Android upgrades. Overall the phone feels like a budget display device with compromises in performance, battery endurance, and storage integrity.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?
