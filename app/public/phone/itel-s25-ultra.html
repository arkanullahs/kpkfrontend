itel S25 Ultra Is it worth buying?

The itel S25 Ultra is a visually striking device that prioritizes display quality over raw performance.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a premium-looking curved display for watching videos on a budget.
Consider alternatives if
You are a gamer or someone who needs a reliable, bloat-free software experience.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Stunning curved AMOLED display
  • High 1400 nits peak brightness
  • Extremely slim and durable build
  • Good daily battery endurance

Reported trade-offs4

  • Slow 18W charging speed
  • Heavy bloatware and ad-filled software
  • Poor performance in heavy gaming
  • Proximity sensor bugs after updates

Why we say that

Owners consistently praise the 6.78 inch curved AMOLED screen for its 1400 nits peak brightness, making it excellent for media consumption. However, living with the phone reveals significant frustrations. The Unisoc T620 chipset struggles with heavy tasks and gaming, leading to frame drops.

Users report that the software experience is marred by heavy bloatware and intrusive ads. There are also specific technical complaints regarding proximity sensor malfunctions following software updates. While the build is slim and durable, the 18W charging speed feels outdated for a 5000 mAh battery.

Read full reasoning

You must also be cautious of counterfeit units that misrepresent storage capacity. If you prioritize a premium screen look and feel, this device delivers, but it falls short for users who demand smooth performance or reliable software stability.
