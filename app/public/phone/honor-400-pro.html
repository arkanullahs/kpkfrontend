Buying verdict

Honor 400 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Honor 400 Pro gives you a bright 6.7 inch AMOLED display, a 200MP main camera and 100W fast charging but its plastic frame and limited official warranty reduce its overall appeal.

Choose it if
Buy if you want a bright large screen, fast charging and a high resolution camera and can accept a plastic body and limited warranty.
Consider alternatives if
Skip if you need reliable USB speeds, consistent gaming performance, or a fully covered manufacturer warranty.

What works well

  • 6.7 inch 120Hz AMOLED display
  • 200MP main camera with OIS and 3x tele
  • 100W wired charging under one hour
  • 6000 mAh battery lasting up to two days

Reported trade-offs

  • Plastic frame despite metal claims
  • USB 2.0 makes data transfer slow
  • Video recording shows jitter
  • Full Android updates only for EU region

Why we say that

Battery life is strong with a 6000 mAh cell, lasting up to two days for most users and surviving eight to nine hours of screen on time. Performance feels flagship thanks to Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 and 12GB RAM, yet stress tests show throttling to about 62 percent and occasional frame drops in demanding games. Camera quality shines in well lit scenes and portraits, but the 50MP selfie sensor is softer and video recording can appear jittery.

Software runs on Android 15 with MagicOS 10 and promises up to six major updates, though only EU devices receive the full schedule. Charging reaches full in under an hour, but USB 2.0 makes file transfers painfully slow and curved screen protectors are hard to find locally.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 6000 mAh battery provides elite endurance, giving most users up to two days of use and eight to nine hours of screen‑on time, so you can comfortably get through a workday and still have charge left.

Will the phone handle heavy gaming without issues?

Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 and 12GB RAM deliver flagship speed, but stress tests show throttling to about 62 percent and occasional frame drops in demanding titles such as Genshin Impact, so performance is good but not flawless.

Is the camera good in low light and for video?

The 200MP main sensor with OIS and 3.0x telephoto produces sharp photos in bright light and excellent portraits, while the 50MP selfie camera is softer; video recording can be somewhat jittery, especially in low‑light scenes.

What warranty and software updates does the Honor 400 Pro receive?

Only the official unit sold with a brand warranty receives manufacturer coverage; grey‑import listings have no official warranty and repairs are paid. Software runs Android 15 with MagicOS 10 and promises up to six major Android upgrades, but full update support is limited to EU region devices.
