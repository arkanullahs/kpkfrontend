Honor 400 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Honor 400 Pro offers a massive 6000 mAh battery and a powerful Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset, but it comes with significant compromises that owners frequently highlight.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want a high-performance device with a massive battery and do not mind managing bloatware or using a plastic-framed phone.
Consider alternatives if
You need fast file transfer speeds, a premium metal build, or guaranteed long-term software support outside of the EU region.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Exceptional 6000 mAh battery endurance
  • Powerful Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 performance
  • Very bright 5000 nits peak display
  • Fast 100W charging capability

Reported trade-offs4

  • Plastic frame on a premium device
  • Slow USB 2.0 data transfer speeds
  • Heavy bloatware and pre-installed junk
  • Deceptive software update policy claims

Why we say that

While the display is bright and the main camera performs well, the device feels less premium due to its plastic frame and slow USB 2.0 data transfer speeds. Users are particularly frustrated by the heavy amount of bloatware pre-installed on the device. There is also significant controversy regarding the software update policy, as owners report that the promised 6 major Android upgrades are restricted to the EU region, leaving other markets with fewer updates.

The camera system is capable, yet it struggles with HDR processing and highlight clipping. If you prioritize battery life and raw performance, this phone is a strong contender, but you must be prepared to deal with software clutter and hardware limitations that feel out of place at this price point.

Honor 400 Pro costs ৳65,990 to ৳77,499 in Bangladesh on unofficial listings, in stock at 6 shops we track as of 30 Oct 2026. The official price starts at ৳74,990.

Does the Honor 400 Pro come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

The official unit comes with a brand warranty, but the cheaper gray-import listings do not have manufacturer cover. If you buy an unofficial unit, any service centre that accepts it will only perform repairs as a paid service.

How long will the Honor 400 Pro receive Android updates?

While the phone is marketed with up to 6 major Android upgrades, owners report that this policy is deceptive. These 6 major updates are only available for the EU region, while other regions receive significantly fewer updates.

Is the Honor 400 Pro good for heavy gaming?

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset is powerful, but owners report that the phone throttles under sustained heavy load. This means that while it can handle games, performance may drop during long gaming sessions.

How does the camera perform on this phone?

The 200MP main camera and telephoto lens are strong, but the camera system has notable weaknesses. Owners report that HDR processing and highlight clipping remain issues, which can affect the quality of your photos.
