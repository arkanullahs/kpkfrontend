Why we say that

The 5200 mAh battery gives reliable all‑day life, yet the included 10W charger needs about three hours to fill. The 6.78‑inch IPS LCD runs at 120Hz and reaches 700 nits, but the 720p resolution feels soft and sunlight visibility is limited. The Helio G81 Ultimate paired with 4 GB RAM often reloads apps, making gaming possible only on low settings such as FreeFire.

The 8MP rear sensor with f/2.0 aperture produces poor exposure and low‑light softness, a step down from earlier 13MP models. Build looks like an iPhone but the plastic scratches easily and the chin is thick. Android 16 with XOS 16 is current, yet XOS adds many pre‑loaded apps and ads, and only one or two OS upgrades are expected.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 5200 mAh battery is rated for reliable all‑day use, and most owners report it lasts through daily tasks. However, the slow 10W charging means you will need to plan for a three‑hour charge when it runs low.

Can I play games like FreeFire smoothly?

Gaming is limited to low settings because the Helio G81 Ultimate with 4 GB RAM often reloads apps. FreeFire can run, but you will notice lag and may need to lower graphics to keep it playable.

How does the camera perform in low‑light conditions?

The 8MP rear sensor with f/2.0 aperture produces soft images and bad exposure when lighting is poor. Reviewers describe low‑light shots as blurry and lacking detail, a downgrade from previous 13MP versions.

Will the phone receive software updates and is the warranty reliable?

The device ships with Android 16 and XOS 16, and Infinix promises one or two OS upgrades. The official Bangladesh warranty applies, giving you brand support and access to the service network.
