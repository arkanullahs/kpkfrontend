Infinix Smart 20 Is it worth buying?

The Infinix Smart 20 is a budget device that struggles with basic multitasking and photography, making it a difficult choice for daily use.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You need a basic device for simple tasks and prioritize a large screen and long battery life over performance.
Consider alternatives if
You play games, multitask between apps, or care about taking clear photos.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Solid 5200 mAh battery endurance
  • Large 6.78 inch 120Hz display
  • Includes headphone jack and IR blaster
  • Stylish slim design with IP64 rating

Reported trade-offs4

  • Severe multitasking lag with 4GB RAM
  • Poor 8MP camera quality
  • Slow 10W charger included in box
  • High amount of bloatware and ads

Why we say that

While the 5200 mAh battery provides solid endurance, the experience is hampered by a slow 10W charger included in the box despite the phone supporting 15W speeds. Owners report severe lag when running multiple apps due to the 4GB RAM limit, and the 8MP camera produces soft, mediocre images that many find frustrating for a 2026 device. The 6.78 inch display offers a 120Hz refresh rate, but the low 720p resolution and thick bezels are noticeable.

You will also encounter significant bloatware and notification ads within the XOS 16 software. Outdoors, the screen brightness is insufficient, often requiring you to shield the display from direct sunlight to see clearly. The plastic build is stylish and slim with an IP64 rating, but it is prone to scratching easily.

If you prioritize performance or camera quality, this device will likely disappoint you.
