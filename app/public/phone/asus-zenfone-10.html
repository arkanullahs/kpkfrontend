Why we say that

The 5.92 inch Super AMOLED panel runs at 144Hz and can peak at 1,100 nits, making everyday use feel smooth and bright. Battery life is decent with a 4,300 mAh cell and 30W charging, though the phone does not support fast wireless charging despite some reports. The 50MP main sensor with OIS captures clear photos in good light, while the 32MP selfie camera is adequate for social media.

Build quality is solid with an IP68 rating and a lightweight plastic back, but the lack of an official Asus service centre means any repair depends on the shop’s warranty. Owners report the device gets hot during long gaming sessions and may throttle, and a few have seen the phone brick after about two years. Software updates are limited to two major Android upgrades, so future OS support is unlikely.

How long will the battery last on a typical day?

The 4,300 mAh battery usually lasts a full day with moderate use, thanks to the efficient Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chip. Heavy gaming or high brightness will reduce endurance, and the phone charges at 30W wired.

Will the phone overheat while gaming?

Owners report noticeable heat during long gaming sessions, which can cause the processor to throttle. The 144Hz display makes games look smooth, but you may need to lower settings or take breaks to keep temperatures manageable.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50MP sensor with optical image stabilization performs well in bright environments, but low‑light results are average. The ultrawide lens adds flexibility, yet you should not expect the same quality as dedicated photography phones.

What warranty and service options are available in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray imports, so there is no Asus manufacturer warranty. The shop you buy from may offer a limited warranty, but any repair will be paid for by you and depends on the shop’s policies.
