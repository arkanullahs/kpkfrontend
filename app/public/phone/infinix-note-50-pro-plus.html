Is the Infinix Note 50 Pro Plus worth buying?

What is good

  • 100W fast charging
  • 1300‑nit bright AMOLED display
  • Dimensity 8350 performance
  • 12 GB RAM with 256 GB storage

What is not

  • Fingerprint sensor often unresponsive
  • XOS preloads many ads
  • Software updates are slow
  • Build rating only moderate

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large, high‑refresh screen and strong performance for gaming and media at a mid‑range price.

Skip it if Skip it if you rely on a flawless fingerprint reader or want a clean, fast‑update Android experience.

Why we say that

The 5200 mAh battery feels efficient and lasts through a normal day, while the 12 GB RAM and 256 GB storage keep apps and media flowing. Gaming feels smooth on the 144 Hz panel and the 50 MP main camera delivers solid photos with good zoom and close‑up detail. Owners praise the bright 1300‑nit display and the glass‑metal body with IP64 protection.

The main complaints are an unreliable fingerprint sensor, a heavily ad‑laden XOS skin and slow software updates that only bring one or two Android versions. Overall it feels like a capable mid‑range device that works well for media and games but requires patience with software quirks.
