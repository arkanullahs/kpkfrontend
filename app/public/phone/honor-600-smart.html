Is the Honor 600 Smart worth buying?

What is good

  • Massive 7700 mAh battery capacity
  • Bright 120Hz IPS LCD display
  • Supports 5G connectivity
  • Includes Google services

What is not

  • Only available as gray-import stock
  • No official brand warranty
  • Basic 5MP selfie camera
  • Medium level of bloat and ads

Buy it if You want a phone with a massive battery and a large display for media consumption.

Skip it if You require an official brand warranty or a high-quality selfie camera for social media.

Why we say that

Living with this phone means carrying a large 6.87 inch display that reaches 1020 nits of peak brightness, making it usable outdoors. You get the Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 chipset which supports 5G connectivity. The software experience is powered by Android 16 and MagicOS 10, though users should expect a medium amount of bloat and ads in the interface.

Because this device is currently only available through gray-import channels, you will not receive a brand warranty. Instead, you must rely on the shop warranty provided by the seller. It is essential to verify the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before finalizing the purchase to ensure the device is registered correctly.

While the 50MP main camera handles standard photography, the 5MP selfie camera is quite basic for a phone at this price point.

Does the Honor 600 Smart come with an official brand warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is currently only available through gray-import stock. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, so you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

How long will the Honor 600 Smart receive software updates?

The device runs on Android 16 and MagicOS 10. While the provided information notes that flagships receive 3 to 4 OS updates, you should clarify the specific update policy for this model with your seller.

Is the battery life on the Honor 600 Smart good for heavy usage?

The phone features a 7700 mAh battery, which is significantly larger than most standard smartphones. This capacity is designed to provide extended usage time, supported by 45W charging to help replenish the power.

Can I use this phone for gaming and photography?

The phone uses the Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 chipset and a 50MP main camera. It is capable of handling daily tasks and 5G connectivity, but the 5MP selfie camera is limited in its performance for high-quality photography.
