Why we say that

The Honor 600 Smart packs a massive 7700 mAh battery that owners rate 9.5 for endurance, and it supports 45 W fast charging. Its 6.87 inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz but only offers a 720p resolution, which many find disappointing on such a large panel. Powered by a Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 chipset and 8 GB RAM, everyday tasks run okay, yet owners complain the memory feels thin for 2026 software.

The 50 MP main camera records basic photos but lacks optical or electronic stabilization, resulting in poor video quality. 5G connectivity works, but the phone is priced between 38,999 and ৳39,499 as a gray import with no official warranty; only the shop’s limited warranty applies and repairs are paid. Overall it delivers excellent battery life at a high price for modest performance.

How long does the battery really last?

The 7700 mAh cell gives the phone about two days of moderate use, which owners rate 9.5 for endurance. It can be refilled in roughly an hour with the supplied 45 W charger, so you rarely need a plug.

Will this phone handle gaming well?

Gaming scores are low, with a performance rating of 4.5 and a gaming rating of 3.8. The Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 chipset and 8 GB RAM manage casual titles, but demanding games will feel laggy and frame drops are common.

Is the camera good for low light photos and video?

The 50 MP sensor captures decent daylight shots, but it lacks optical or electronic image stabilization, so low light photos are noisy and video is limited to 1080p30 without stabilization. Reviewers rate the camera around 5.2 out of 10.

What warranty and software updates can I expect?

All units are gray imports, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh. The shop provides its own limited warranty and any repair costs are paid by the owner. MagicOS 10 runs on Android 16 and typically receives three to four major OS upgrades on flagship models, with medium level bloat and ads.
