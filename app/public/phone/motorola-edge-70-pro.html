Buying verdict

Motorola Edge 70 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Edge 70 Pro gives you a huge bright 144Hz AMOLED screen and a 90W charger, but you must accept a gray import warranty and some gaming hiccups.

Choose it if
You want a large bright screen, fast charging and a strong camera and can live with a shop warranty and occasional gaming slowdowns.
Consider alternatives if
You need an official brand warranty, reliable gaming performance or fast data transfer.

What works well

  • 6.78 inch 144Hz AMOLED display
  • 90W wired fast charging
  • 6500mAh battery lasting a full day
  • 50MP periscope camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs

  • No official warranty in Bangladesh
  • USB 2.0 limits file transfer speed
  • Curved screen causes accidental touches
  • Heavy gaming throttles and drops frames

Why we say that

With a 6.78 inch panel that can peak at 5200 nits, everyday use feels vivid and smooth, and the 6500mAh battery easily survives a full day even for power users. The 50MP periscope camera with OIS captures detailed shots, though the software often over sharpens images and can look saturated. The phone runs on a Dimensity 8500 Extreme chipset, delivering snappy performance for apps, yet heavy games push the device to 40 to 43°C and cause frame drops into the 30s.

The curved edges look premium but sometimes register accidental touches. Data transfers are limited by a USB 2.0 port. You will receive only the shop’s warranty and up to three Android version upgrades.

Read full reasoning

Overall it feels premium for its price but requires compromise on warranty, gaming stability and software polish.

Will the battery last a full day with heavy use?

The 6500mAh cell is rated 9.6 out of 10 for endurance and owners report it easily makes it through a full day even for power users, so you can expect a day long run without charging.

How does the phone perform in games?

For everyday apps the Dimensity 8500 Extreme feels snappy, but during heavy gaming the device heats to 40 to 43°C, throttles and often drops the frame rate into the 30s, making the experience uneven.

Is the camera good in low light?

The 50MP periscope sensor with OIS and a 3.5x tele lens captures a lot of detail, yet the software tends to over sharpen and saturate images, so low light photos may look harsh and inconsistent.

Does it come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

All listings are gray imports built for India, so the phone has no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; you only get the shop’s warranty and must verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
