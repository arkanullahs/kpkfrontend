Motorola Edge 70 Pro Is it worth buying?

The Motorola Edge 70 Pro is a high-end device that excels in battery endurance but struggles with inconsistent software processing and thermal management.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You prioritize exceptional battery life and a clean software experience for daily tasks.
Consider alternatives if
You are a serious mobile gamer or if you require a reliable manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • Industry-leading 6500 mAh battery life
  • Stunningly bright 144Hz AMOLED display
  • Fast 90W wired charging support
  • Premium IP68/69 rated build quality

Reported trade-offs4

  • Curved edges cause frequent accidental touches
  • Over-sharpened and inconsistent camera processing
  • Significant thermal throttling during heavy gaming
  • Slow USB 2.0 data transfer speeds

Why we say that

Living with this phone means enjoying a massive 6500 mAh battery that easily lasts a full day, even for power users. However, owners frequently report frustration with the curved display, which causes constant accidental touches and input misses. While the 144Hz AMOLED screen is stunningly bright, the software processing for the 50MP camera is often over-sharpened and saturated, leading to unnatural results.

Gamers should be aware that the Dimensity 8500 Extreme chipset suffers from significant throttling and frame drops during heavy sessions, with temperatures reaching 43°C. The device uses a slow USB 2.0 port, making large file transfers tedious. Since all units in Bangladesh are gray imports, you must rely on shop-provided coverage rather than a manufacturer warranty.

Read full reasoning

With only 3 major Android upgrades promised, the long-term software support feels underwhelming for a phone at this price point.

Does the Motorola Edge 70 Pro come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is not available through official channels. Every unit currently in the market is a gray import, meaning there is no manufacturer warranty support. You must rely on the specific warranty terms provided by the shop where you purchase the device.

Is this phone suitable for heavy gaming?

The phone is not ideal for heavy gaming. While it handles daily tasks well, owners report significant thermal throttling and frame rate drops into the 30s during intense gaming sessions. The device can reach temperatures between 40°C and 43°C under these heavy loads.

How long will the Motorola Edge 70 Pro receive software updates?

The device is promised to receive up to 3 major Android upgrades. Many users find this update policy underwhelming given the current market standards for high-end smartphones.

How does the camera perform for photography?

The camera features strong periscope hardware, but the software processing is a major drawback. Owners report that images are often over-sharpened and heavily saturated, which can make photos look unnatural or like an acid trip.
