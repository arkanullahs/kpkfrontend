Buying verdict

OnePlus Nord 6 Is it worth buying?

If you need a phone that can run games and last through a day, the OnePlus Nord 6 delivers that with a huge battery and fast display.

Choose it if
Buy if you want strong gaming, long battery and fast display and can accept a gray‑import without manufacturer warranty.
Consider alternatives if
Skip if low‑light photography or a fully covered warranty are top priorities.

What works well

  • 7500 mAh battery with 10‑12 hour screen time
  • 165 Hz AMOLED display bright in sunlight
  • 80 W wired charging
  • Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 handles games smoothly

Reported trade-offs

  • Screen flickers while charging
  • SIM and microphone holes placed confusingly
  • Low‑light camera performance is poor
  • Gray‑import units have no official warranty

Why we say that

The 7,500 mAh cell gives about 10‑12 hours of screen time and refills quickly with 80 W charging. The 6.78‑inch 165 Hz AMOLED stays bright even in strong sunlight. Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 keeps PUBG and Genshin Impact smooth, though it is a bit slower than the OnePlus 15R.

Owners love the clean OxygenOS and the handy IR blaster. The main camera is well tuned in daylight but struggles in low light, and the ultrawide is only average. The plastic body passes an IP69K rating but the SIM tray and microphone hole are placed too close together, and the screen flickers while charging.

Read full reasoning

Full‑screen always‑on display is missing. Gray‑import units are cheaper but lack official manufacturer warranty; only the official listings (starting at ৳62,999) include brand coverage.

How long does the battery really last in daily use?

The Nord 6 packs a 7,500 mAh battery that owners report lasting 10 to 12 hours of screen on time. It can be topped up from zero to full in about 40 minutes with the 80 W charger that comes in the box.

Will the phone handle heavy games like PUBG or Genshin Impact?

Powered by Snapdragon 8s Gen 4, the Nord 6 runs PUBG and Genshin Impact with stable thermals and smooth frame rates. It is a bit slower than the OnePlus 15R but still provides a solid gaming experience.

Is the camera good in low‑light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor is well optimized for daylight shots, but owners note that low‑light performance is poor. The ultrawide lens is average and does not compensate for the weakness in dim environments.
