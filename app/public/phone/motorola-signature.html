Is the Motorola Signature worth buying?

What is good

  • 165 Hz LTPO AMOLED display with 6200‑nit peak
  • 50 MP main and selfie cameras with OIS
  • Ultra‑slim 6.9 mm body with IP69 rating
  • 90 W wired fast charging

What is not

  • Heavy thermal throttling during long gaming sessions
  • Battery 5200 mAh feels small for a day
  • Charger often missing from the box
  • Daylight video looks overly contrasty

Buy it if Buy it if you value a top‑tier display and camera and accept a gray‑import warranty with possible heat under heavy use.

Skip it if Skip it if you need long‑lasting battery life, reliable gaming performance, or an official manufacturer warranty.

Why we say that

The 6.8‑inch LTPO panel reaches a 6200‑nit peak, making outdoor viewing easy. The 50 MP main sensor with OIS and a 50 MP selfie lens deliver excellent stills, though video can appear overly contrasty in daylight. Battery life is adequate for a typical day but feels small compared to newer 7000 mAh rivals, and the fast 90 W charger is often missing from the box.

Build quality is high with a 6.9 mm ultra‑slim frame, IP69 rating and military‑grade durability. Software runs a near‑stock Hello UI on Android 16 and promises up to seven major OS upgrades. Remember the phone is a gray‑import, so only the shop’s limited warranty applies and you must confirm IMEI and BTRC status before purchase.

Will the Motorola Signature get official warranty in Bangladesh?

No official Motorola warranty is available in Bangladesh because the phone is a gray‑import built for India. The shop provides its own limited warranty, so you should verify the IMEI and BTRC registration before buying.

How long will the phone receive Android updates?

The device ships with Android 16 and Motorola promises up to seven major Android version upgrades, which is among the longest update commitments in the market. You can expect software support for several years.

Is the battery enough for a full day of use?

The 5200 mAh battery is rated reliable for a day of typical use, but it is smaller than the 7000 mAh+ batteries seen in many 2026 flagships. Heavy use may require charging before nightfall.

How does the phone perform for gaming?

Everyday apps run smoothly, yet sustained gaming causes the phone to heat up and throttle performance, lowering frame rates. Reviewers gave the gaming experience a 6.8 out of 10 rating.
