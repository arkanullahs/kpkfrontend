POCO C51 Is it worth buying?

The POCO C51 is a budget device that functions as a rebadged Redmi A2+ and currently lacks any official brand-backed warranty support in Bangladesh.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
Buy this phone only if you need a basic, low-cost device for simple tasks and are comfortable with a shop-provided warranty.
Consider alternatives if
You want a reliable camera, gaming performance, or official brand warranty support.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

What works well4

  • Large 5000 mAh battery capacity
  • Includes a dedicated headphone jack
  • Features built-in FM radio support
  • Affordable entry price of ৳9,790

Reported trade-offs4

  • Poor camera quality and no gyroscope support
  • High levels of bloatware and advertisements
  • Slow 10W charging speed
  • Low owner ratings for gaming and performance

Why we say that

Living with this phone involves managing significant performance limitations, as owners report poor camera quality and a lack of gyroscope support. The device runs on Android 13 Go edition with a heavy presence of bloatware and ads. While it features a 5000 mAh battery, users have noted issues with battery drain over time.

With the phone being released in 2023, you should expect very little remaining software update life. Owners have rated the gaming performance and display quality quite low, making it a difficult choice for anyone expecting a smooth experience. Since only gray-import stock is available, you must rely on shop-provided warranties rather than official manufacturer support.

POCO C51: what buyers ask us

What is the POCO C51 price in Bangladesh?

The last price we recorded for POCO C51 in Bangladesh was ৳9,790, checked 24 Sep 2026. No shop we track has it in stock right now.

Does the POCO C51 come with an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, there is no official brand-backed warranty support for this phone in Bangladesh. All units currently found in the market are gray-imports, meaning you must rely solely on the warranty provided by the specific shop where you purchase the device.

How long will the POCO C51 receive software updates?

The phone was released in 2023 and is already three years old. You should expect very short or no remaining software update life at this point in time.

Is the POCO C51 suitable for mobile gaming?

Owners have rated the gaming performance of this device very poorly, giving it a score of 2.0 out of 10. It is not recommended for users who prioritize gaming as a primary function of their smartphone.

What are the common complaints regarding the battery life?

While the phone includes a 5000 mAh battery, owners have reported issues with battery drain. Some users have expressed concerns about the long-term health of the battery and the need for frequent recharging.
