Buying verdict

iQOO Z9s 5G Is it worth buying?

This is a strong all rounder that excels in battery life and display quality.

Choose it if
You want a reliable all rounder with great battery life and a bright screen.
Consider alternatives if
You need a professional camera for 4K 60fps video or official brand warranty.

What works well

  • Long lasting 5500 mAh battery
  • Bright 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Smooth daily performance
  • Includes OIS on main camera

Reported trade-offs

  • No 4K 60fps video recording
  • Software bugs and medium bloatware
  • Mixed build quality reviews

Why we say that

Owners highly praise the 5500 mAh battery for its endurance and capacity. The 120Hz AMOLED screen is generally well received, although one user mentioned a lack of sharpness. Daily use is smooth with the Dimensity 7300, but some users have reported stuttering after software updates.

The camera is viewed as average, with some users noting it simply has OIS without standout quality. You should be aware that the software has medium levels of bloatware and ads, and some users have found bugs in Funtouch OS. Video recording is a weak point because the phone does not support 4K 60fps, limiting you to 30fps.

Does this phone have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, every listing we have recorded is a gray import. You will only get a shop warranty instead of brand backed support. You must confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with the seller before buying.

It uses the Dimensity 7300 chipset and is smooth for daily use. Some owners reported stuttering after updates, and the gaming rating from owners is 6.5 out of 10.

How many software updates will this phone get?

The phone comes with Android 14 and Funtouch 14. It is expected to receive up to 3 OS updates, though some users have reported bugs in the software.
