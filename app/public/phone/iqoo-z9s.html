Is the iQOO Z9s 5G worth buying?

What is good

  • 5500 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 1800 nits AMOLED screen is very bright
  • 44 W charger refills quickly
  • Dimensity 7300 handles everyday tasks smoothly

What is not

  • No official brand warranty
  • 4K video limited to 30 fps
  • Post‑update stutter reported
  • Camera quality only average

Buy it if Buy it if you want a bright, long‑lasting phone with decent performance at a low gray‑import price.

Skip it if Skip it if you need an official warranty or high‑end video recording capabilities.

Why we say that

Owners love the 5500 mAh cell that easily lasts a full day of mixed use and the 1800 nit AMOLED panel that stays visible even in bright sunlight. The Dimensity 7300 chipset and 120 Hz display handle everyday apps and casual games without major lag, though a few users notice stutter after recent software updates. The 50 MP main sensor with OIS delivers decent photos but does not stand out in low‑light situations.

Software runs Android 14 with Funtouch 14 and promises up to two major Android upgrades, yet update‑related bugs have been reported. The phone lacks an official brand warranty, so you must rely on a shop warranty and verify the IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
