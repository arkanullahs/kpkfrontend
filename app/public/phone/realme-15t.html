Is the Realme 15T worth buying?

If you need a phone that can stay on all day without hunting for a charger, the Realme 15T delivers that with its massive 7000 mAh battery and a bright 120 Hz AMOLED display. The screen looks good in most lighting and the 5G modem keeps you connected fast. However, the camera struggles to produce usable photos, especially in low light, and owners say the performance feels dated compared to newer models. Gaming is limited and the device lacks NFC, which some users miss. Software includes Android 15 with Realme UI 6.0 and promises up to three major Android upgrades, but the budget UI carries medium to high ads. Build quality is decent but not as premium as the Realme 14T. Overall, it works well for basic tasks and long battery life, but falls short for photography, heavy gaming, and future‑proof performance.

What is good

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The 7000 mAh battery can comfortably last about 24 hours of mixed use, including browsing, video streaming and occasional gaming, according to owners. Realme rates it as an efficient battery, so you rarely need a daily charge.

Is the Realme 15T good for gaming?

Gaming on the Realme 15T feels average; the Dimensity 6400 Max chipset and 8 GB RAM manage casual titles but struggle with demanding 3D games, reflected in a low gaming rating of 2.0 from owners.

How does the camera perform in low light conditions?

The 50 MP main sensor does not deliver good low light results; owners report grainy, dark images and describe the camera as poor, giving it a rating of only 2.5 out of 10.

Will the phone receive Android updates and how long is the warranty?

The Realme 15T ships with Android 15 and Realme UI 6.0 and promises up to three major Android upgrades, so you can expect support for a few years. Official warranty starts at 31,499 taka for the 8 GB/256 GB model.
