Is the iQOO Z10 Turbo worth buying?

What is good

  • 7620 mAh battery with 90 W fast charge
  • 6.78‑inch 144 Hz AMOLED with 4400‑nit peak
  • Dimensity 8400 delivers high FPS gaming

What is not

  • Low‑light photos are weak
  • 5G bands often not supported
  • Device gets hot during heavy use

Buy it if Buy it if you need long battery life, a high‑refresh bright screen and strong gaming performance and can accept limited 5G and camera compromises.

Skip it if Skip it if you rely on strong low‑light photography, stable 5G everywhere, or a premium metal build.

Why we say that

The 7620 mAh silicon‑carbon cell gives 8 to 10 hours of screen on time and the 90 W charger reaches full charge in about 35 to 45 minutes. The 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel is bright enough for sunlight and runs at a smooth 144 Hz, making everyday scrolling pleasant. The Dimensity 8400 chipset feels very snappy and can push high frame rates in most games, though PUBG is capped at 60 FPS and the phone gets noticeably warm during long sessions.

Camera quality is acceptable in daylight but struggles in low light and lacks useful zoom, a trade‑off many owners mention. 5G support is limited and many users cannot connect to local 5G bands. Software runs OriginOS 5 or Funtouch OS, which is smooth but includes some bloat and occasional notification delays.

Build quality is basic plastic with an IP65 rating, and the fingerprint sensor sits low on the frame.

How long will the battery last in daily use?

The 7620 mAh silicon‑carbon battery provides about 8 to 10 hours of screen‑on time and can easily last a full day of moderate use. A 90 W charger refills it from zero to full in roughly 35 to 45 minutes.

Is the phone good for gaming?

The Dimensity 8400 chipset is very snappy and delivers high frame rates in games like Call of Duty and Genshin Impact. PUBG is limited to 60 FPS, and the device can become warm during long gaming sessions.

How does the camera perform in low light?

Daylight shots are decent with the 50 MP main sensor and OIS, but low‑light and zoom results are poor. Owners say the camera was sacrificed to keep power consumption low.

Will I get an official brand warranty and software updates?

Most units in Bangladesh are gray‑import stock, so they come with a shop warranty, not a Vivo/iQOO brand warranty. Software is OriginOS 5 or Funtouch OS with about three major Android updates and medium bloat.
