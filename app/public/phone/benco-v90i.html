Is the Benco V90i worth buying?

What is good

  • 5000 mAh battery lasts a full day
  • Premium look and fingerprint resistant build
  • 6.9 inch IPS LCD with 120 Hz refresh
  • Lightweight Android 13 UI with low bloatware

What is not

  • Screen hard to read in direct sunlight
  • Camera exposure often over bright and lacks detail
  • Only a 10 W charger is included
  • Gaming performance struggles with multitasking

Buy it if Buy it if you want an affordable large screen phone for basic apps and media.

Skip it if Skip it if you need a strong camera, fast charging, or heavy gaming.

Why we say that

The 6.9 inch IPS LCD runs at 120 Hz, giving smooth scrolling for everyday apps. Its 5000 mAh cell provides a full day of mixed use, though the included 10 W charger makes refilling slow. Build quality feels premium and resists fingerprints, which is impressive at the price.

Performance is modest; the Unisoc T606 with 4 GB RAM handles basic tasks but shows lag when multitasking or playing heavier games. The 13 MP rear camera suffers from poor exposure control and lacks detail, especially in low light and portrait mode. The display is bright enough indoors but becomes hard to read under direct sunlight.

Software runs Android 13 with a light skin and medium ads, but updates are rare. Overall it is a budget friendly media device for casual users.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

Can I play games smoothly on this phone?
