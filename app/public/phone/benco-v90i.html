Buying verdict

Benco V90i Is it worth buying?

If you need a big screen and a battery that can survive a full day, the Benco V90i delivers that at a very low price.

Choose it if
You want a large, cheap phone with long battery life and can accept basic camera and light gaming.
Consider alternatives if
You need a good camera, fast charging, or strong gaming performance.

What works well

  • Premium look and feel
  • 5000 mAh battery lasts all day
  • 6.9‑inch 120 Hz display feels smooth
  • Light Android UI with little bloatware

Reported trade-offs

  • Sunlight makes screen hard to read
  • Camera exposure is poor
  • 10 W charger charges slowly
  • Gaming performance is weak

Why we say that

The phone feels premium and resists fingerprints, and the 6.9‑inch 120 Hz panel is smooth for everyday scrolling. Battery life is solid, but the included 10 W charger makes refilling slow. The camera struggles with exposure and produces blurry portraits, and the display is hard to read under direct sunlight.

Performance is adequate for basic tasks but lags when multitasking or playing games, though it stays cool. Software is a lightweight Android 13 skin with moderate bloatware and infrequent updates. Overall, it works for simple use cases but falls short where camera quality, fast charging, or strong gaming performance matter.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 5000 mAh battery provides full‑day endurance for typical browsing, messaging and video playback, but the included 10 W charger refills the battery slowly, so you will need to plan charging time.

Can I play games smoothly on the Benco V90i?

Only very light games run without noticeable lag. Multitasking and heavier titles cause slowdown, although the phone remains cool during use, making it unsuitable for serious mobile gaming.
