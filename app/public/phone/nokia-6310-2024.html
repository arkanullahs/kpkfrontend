Is the Nokia 6310 2024 worth buying?

What is good

  • Sturdy classic Nokia build
  • No ads or bloatware
  • Long standby battery life
  • Easy to use

What is not

  • Low resolution TFT display
  • Poor camera quality
  • Not suitable for gaming

Buy it if You need a durable and simple phone for basic calling.

Skip it if You want to use apps or take good photos.

Why we say that

It feels sturdy with a classic Nokia build that owners appreciate. You will find the software clean because there are no ads or bloatware. However, the hardware is very limited.

Owners complain heavily about the display and camera, both of which receive very low ratings. Performance is only suitable for basic tasks. You cannot use this for apps or gaming.

The battery provides the long standby time typical for this kind of device. It is a reliable tool for communication, but it lacks any modern smartphone capabilities.
