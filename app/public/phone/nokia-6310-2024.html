Is the Nokia 6310 2024 worth buying?

What is good

  • Sturdy classic Nokia build
  • Very long standby battery
  • Simple ad‑free software
  • Headphone jack and FM radio

What is not

  • Tiny low‑resolution screen
  • Very weak camera
  • No gaming or app performance
  • No 5G connectivity

Buy it if Buy it if you want a reliable, low‑cost phone for calls, texts and long standby without needing apps or high‑end features.

Skip it if Skip it if you expect a modern smartphone experience with good camera, fast performance or gaming capability.

Why we say that

The device feels solid in hand and the classic Nokia design gives a sense of durability. The software is stripped down to basics, so you get a clean experience without ads or unnecessary apps. Battery standby is long enough for a typical feature‑phone user, and the inclusion of a headphone jack and FM radio adds practical extras.

However, the 2.8 inch TFT screen is small and low‑resolution, making reading or browsing uncomfortable. The camera struggles in any light and the phone cannot handle games or modern apps, as the Unisoc 6531F chipset is very limited. Overall it works well for calls, texts and simple tasks, but you will quickly notice its inability to run demanding software.

Owners appreciate the sturdiness and battery life but complain about the tiny display and poor camera performance.
