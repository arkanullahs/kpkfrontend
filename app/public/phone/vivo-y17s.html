What works well

  • Large 5000 mAh battery
  • Includes a headphone jack
  • 50MP main camera
  • 700 nits peak brightness

Reported trade-offs

  • Slow 15W charging
  • Medium amount of bloatware
  • Limited remaining software updates

Why we say that

It uses the Helio G85 chipset and a 5000 mAh battery to handle simple tasks. The 6.56 inch screen reaches 700 nits peak brightness. You will find a headphone jack and FM radio here.

However, this phone was released in 2023. It is now 3 years old. You should expect very short or no remaining software update life.

Read full reasoning

The Funtouch OS also comes with medium levels of bloatware and ads. Charging is slow at 15W. It is not a device for long term software support.
