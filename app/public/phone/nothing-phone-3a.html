Is the Nothing Phone 3a worth buying?

What is good

  • 120Hz AMOLED display with high brightness
  • Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 delivers smooth performance
  • 5000 mAh battery with 50W fast charging
  • Clean Nothing OS with no ads

What is not

  • Camera quality feels cheap
  • No eSIM support
  • Ghost touches reported
  • No official warranty in Bangladesh

Buy it if Buy it if you want a large, fast Android phone at a mid‑range price and can accept gray‑import risk.

Skip it if Skip it if you need reliable camera performance or official brand warranty.

Why we say that

Daily use is smooth thanks to the Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 chipset and 8 GB of RAM, and the 5000 mAh battery with 50W charging keeps the phone alive through most of the day. The display is a standout with high peak brightness and fluid refresh rate, making media consumption enjoyable. However, owners report that photos look cheap, especially in low light, and some experience ghost touches.

The essential key moves the power button to an unfamiliar spot, which takes time to adjust. There is no official brand warranty in Bangladesh; you rely on the shop’s warranty and must verify IMEI and BTRC status before buying.
