POCO M7 5G Is it worth buying?

If you need a cheap 5G phone that can survive a day without charging, the POCO M7 5G delivers that but brings many compromises.

4 strengths · 4 trade-offs
Choose it if
You want an affordable 5G phone with long battery and can tolerate software bloat and limited charging speed.
Consider alternatives if
You need reliable low‑light photography, fast charging, or official brand warranty.

Based on published reviews and owner reports.

Review sources ↓

What works well4

  • 6.88 inch 120 Hz LCD screen
  • 5160 mAh battery with full‑day endurance
  • Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 handles daily apps
  • 33 W charger included (though limited to 18 W)

Reported trade-offs4

  • Only 18 W fast charging despite 33 W charger
  • Low‑light photos are noisy and soft
  • HD+ LCD lacks full‑HD sharpness
  • Random reboots and hangs after a month

Why we say that

The large 6.88‑inch 120 Hz LCD keeps the experience smooth, yet its HD+ resolution feels modest and brightness peaks at 600 nits. Battery life is a strong point, lasting a full day for most users, but the included 33 W charger only supplies 18 W, so fast charging is slower than advertised. Performance is adequate for everyday apps thanks to the Snapdragon 4 Gen 2, but heavy games often lose frames.

The camera is sharp in daylight, but low‑light shots are noisy and lack detail. Software runs on HyperOS with noticeable ads and bloatware, and users report random reboots after a month. There is no official brand warranty; only the shop’s limited warranty applies and any repair costs are out of pocket.

How long does the battery last on a typical day?

The POCO M7 5G packs a 5160 mAh cell and owners report excellent endurance that easily covers a full day of mixed use, though heavy gaming or screen‑on time will drain it faster.

Can the POCO M7 5G run modern games smoothly?

The Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 chipset and 6‑8 GB RAM give smooth UI performance, but owners notice frame drops in demanding titles, so casual games run fine while heavy games may stutter.

Is the camera usable in low‑light situations?

The 50 MP main sensor captures sharp daylight images, but in low light it struggles, producing soft, noisy photos and the depth sensor adds little benefit, according to user feedback.
