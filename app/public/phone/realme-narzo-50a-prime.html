Is the Realme Narzo 50A Prime worth buying?

What is good

  • Large 6.6 inch IPS LCD display
  • 50MP main camera sensor
  • Includes a dedicated headphone jack
  • 128GB storage variant available

What is not

  • Constant lagging and performance issues
  • Battery drains quickly and is difficult to charge
  • Frequent screen blackouts and freezing
  • Significant thermal issues during use

Buy it if You only need a basic device for very light tasks and are willing to accept significant performance limitations.

Skip it if You need a reliable phone for daily use, gaming, or multitasking without constant freezing and battery issues.

Why we say that

Owners frequently report that the device suffers from constant lagging, screen freezing, and unexpected blackouts on the 6.6 inch IPS LCD display. The battery life is a major concern, as users describe it as draining quickly and being difficult to charge. Thermal issues are common, and the software experience is hindered by bloatware and ads.

With the phone released in 2022, you should expect little to no remaining software update support. Owners have expressed significant dissatisfaction, rating the performance at 2.5 and gaming at 3.0 out of 10. If you rely on your phone for daily tasks, the reported freezing and charging difficulties make this device a risky choice for your money.
