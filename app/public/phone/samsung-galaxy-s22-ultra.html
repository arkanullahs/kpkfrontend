Is the Samsung Galaxy S22 Ultra worth buying?

What is good

  • Large 6.8 inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display
  • High 1750 nits peak brightness
  • 108MP main camera with OIS
  • Fast 45W charging

What is not

  • Reported vertical green line screen defect
  • Camera autofocus struggles in low light
  • Short or no remaining software updates

Buy it if You want a high resolution screen and 12GB RAM at a low price.

Skip it if You cannot risk a screen defect or need long term software support.

Why we say that

You get a high quality 6.8 inch screen and a 108MP camera, but owners widely report a vertical green line appearing on the AMOLED display after a few weeks. The camera also struggles to focus in low light conditions. Since this device was released in 2022, it is now 4 years old.

You should expect very little or no remaining software update life. While the price is lower than the cheapest gray import at ৳84,999, the risk of screen failure and autofocus issues makes it a risky choice for a primary device.
