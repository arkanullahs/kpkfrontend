Is the Realme GT8 Pro worth buying?

What is good

  • Stellar 7000 mAh battery endurance
  • Extremely bright 7000 nits peak display
  • Very fast 120W wired charging
  • Unique interchangeable camera caps

What is not

  • Severe GPU throttling under load
  • Nagging software notifications and bloat
  • No LTPO display technology
  • USB-C 2.0 lacks video output

Buy it if You prioritize multi-day battery life above all else and do not mind managing software notifications.

Skip it if You are a serious gamer who needs sustained performance or someone who requires external display connectivity.

Why we say that

While the 7000 mAh battery easily lasts two days, owners report that the phone aggressively limits GPU power during long gaming sessions, losing nearly 70 percent of its initial burst speed to stay cool. The display is incredibly bright at 7000 nits, yet the lack of LTPO technology means it cannot drop below 60Hz to save power. You will also deal with nagging notifications and bloatware in the Android 16 interface.

The camera system offers unique Ricoh modes and a 3.0x telephoto lens, but the absence of autofocus on the ultrawide and selfie cameras limits its versatility. Since this is only available as gray-import stock, you must rely on shop warranties rather than official brand support. The USB-C 2.0 port also prevents any external display output, which is a significant limitation for a device at this price point.

Does the Realme GT8 Pro have an official warranty in Bangladesh?

No, this phone is currently only available through gray-import channels. You will receive a shop warranty instead of an official brand warranty, so you should confirm the IMEI and BTRC status with your seller before purchasing.

How long will the Realme GT8 Pro receive software updates?

The device runs Android 16 and is promised up to 4 major Android upgrades. You can expect the software to be supported for several years, though you should be prepared for medium to high levels of bloatware.

Is the Realme GT8 Pro good for heavy gaming?

While it features the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset, owners report severe GPU throttling during long sessions. The phone stays cool by aggressively limiting performance, which can result in losing 70 percent of its initial burst speed.

What should I check before buying a unit in Bangladesh?

You must verify if the unit is a China-market version or a global version. China-market units lack Google services and Bangla language support. Always confirm the BTRC status and ensure the shop provides a reliable warranty.
