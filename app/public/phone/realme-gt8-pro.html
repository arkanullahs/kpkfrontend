Realme GT8 Pro Is it worth buying?

Expect a phone that can stay lit under direct sun and keep you online for two days, but be ready for throttled gaming and a lack of official after‑sales support.

What works well

  • 7,000 nits peak brightness
  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 performance
  • 7,000 mAh battery lasts two days
  • 50 MP main camera with OIS

Reported trade-offs

  • Severe GPU throttling in long games
  • No LTPO, cannot drop below 60 Hz
  • USB‑C 2.0 no video out
  • No official brand warranty

Why we say that

The 6.79‑inch AMOLED panel reaches a peak brightness of 7,000 nits and runs at a smooth 144 Hz, making media consumption pleasant. The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 paired with 12‑16 GB RAM delivers strong single‑core performance and quick app launches, yet sustained GPU load drops up to 70 percent, which hurts long gaming sessions. The 7,000 mAh battery easily stretches to a second day of moderate use and refuels in minutes thanks to 120 W charging, though the supplied cable feels dated.

The 50 MP main sensor with OIS and 3× telephoto captures sharp stills, while the ultrawide and selfie lenses lack autofocus. The phone arrives as a gray‑import unit, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s limited warranty applies. Software runs Android 16 with Realme UI 7, offering up to four major OS upgrades but includes medium‑high bloat and ads.

How long does the battery last in daily use?

The 7,000 mAh cell is rated to give about two days of normal use. Owners report it easily makes it through a full day without charging and can stretch to a second day with moderate usage.

Is the phone good for gaming?

It reaches high peak speeds thanks to the Snapdragon 8 Elite, but owners notice severe GPU throttling after a short time, losing almost 70 percent of its initial burst performance. Gaming scores are around 6.2 out of 10.

How does the camera perform in low light?

The 50 MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS captures good low‑light stills, and Ricoh mode helps street photography. However, the ultrawide and 32 MP selfie lenses lack autofocus, which can reduce sharpness in dim conditions.

Will I get an official warranty and software updates?

All units are gray‑import, so there is no manufacturer warranty in Bangladesh; only the shop’s limited warranty applies and repairs are paid. The phone runs Android 16 with Realme UI 7 and promises up to four major OS upgrades, though it carries medium‑high bloat and ads.
