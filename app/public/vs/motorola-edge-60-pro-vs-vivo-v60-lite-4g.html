Choose the Motorola Edge 60 Pro if…

The Motorola Edge 60 Pro is in a completely different league than the Vivo, offering a near-flagship Dimensity 8350 chip that destroys the entry-level Snapdragon 685 in every performance metric. Since you need a reliable device, this phone provides 5G connectivity, a superior telephoto lens with OIS, and a much more powerful processor that will keep the phone feeling fast for years.

Choose the Vivo V60 Lite 4G if…

This phone is significantly underpowered for its price, using an entry-level chip that will struggle with modern apps and multitasking. It lacks 5G and a dedicated telephoto lens, and the absence of OIS makes its camera performance inconsistent compared to the Motorola.
