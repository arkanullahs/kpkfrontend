Choose the Motorola Edge 60 Pro if…

The Motorola Edge 60 Pro is in a completely different league than the Vivo, offering a near-flagship Dimensity 8350 chip and a 3x optical telephoto lens that the Vivo lacks entirely. Since you need a reliable daily driver, the inclusion of 5G and a significantly more powerful processor makes this the only logical choice for your budget.

Choose the Vivo V60 Lite 4G if…

This phone is severely underpowered for the price, relying on an entry-level Snapdragon 685 chip that will struggle with modern apps and lacks 5G. With no OIS and no telephoto lens, it cannot compete with the Motorola's camera capabilities, making it a poor investment.
