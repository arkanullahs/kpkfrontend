Choose the Google Pixel 9 Pro XL if…

Consider the Pixel 9 Pro XL only if camera quality and clean software are your absolute top priorities, as it has the best camera tuning in the business and guarantees 7 years of updates. However, it's an older 2024 model with a significantly weaker, less efficient processor that has a history of heat and modem drain complaints, and it has zero official service or warranty support in Bangladesh.

Choose the Xiaomi 15T Pro if…

For your budget, the Xiaomi 15T Pro is the smarter buy because it gives you a much newer and vastly more powerful flagship chip that will handle everything faster and stay relevant for years longer. You also get a brighter screen, a bigger battery, and it's from a brand with good service support in Bangladesh. While the Pixel has a slightly better camera reputation, the 15T Pro's camera hardware is strong and the overall package is more future-proof.
