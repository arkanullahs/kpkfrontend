Choose the Samsung Galaxy A07 4G if…

The Galaxy A07 4G is the better long-term investment because it offers a superior software support promise of six major Android updates, ensuring it stays relevant much longer than the M06. Choose the Samsung Galaxy M06 5G if…

The Galaxy M06 5G is only worth considering if 5G connectivity is an absolute requirement for your area, as it otherwise falls behind the A07 in software longevity. It uses a newer Dimensity 6300 chip, but the shorter update cycle makes it a less compelling value for long-term ownership.
