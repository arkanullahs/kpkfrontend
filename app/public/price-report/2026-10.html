On the same configuration, official units cost a median 5.6% more than imports, and 7 recent launches got cheaper. What that means if you are buying, from 7 reads of Bangladeshi shops.

The short version

  1. 78% of the 2,560 phone listings we read between 6 and 18 October 2026 ended at the price they started at; 229 were cut and 332 raised, for a median change of 0%.

    Share card

  2. On the identical configuration, the official unit cost a median 5.6% more than the import across the 63 phones sold both ways between 6 and 18 October 2026.

    Share card

  3. The cheapest 5G phone in stock between 6 and 18 October 2026 was the ZTE A76 (4GB/128GB) at ৳13,500.

    Share card

  4. The steepest fall on a phone released in the last 6 months: the Google Pixel 11 Pro (12GB/256GB, import) went from ৳147,999 to ৳134,999, down 8.8%, between 6 and 18 October 2026.

    Share card

  5. Of the 566 handsets in stock at a Bangladeshi shop at some point between 6 and 18 October 2026, 90% were in stock somewhere on every date we read.

How the figures are made

Every figure is computed from the listings we recorded on the nights a shop read succeeded, compared within one listing or one configuration and channel. We have not held these phones; this is a record of what Bangladeshi shops published.

Price changes
Each listing is one shop's offer of one exact configuration. Its first in-stock price of the month is compared with its last; listings read in stock on fewer than two dates are left out, as are listings whose memory size is not stated. One listing, one vote; the band is the listing's first price. Computed from offer_observations.price_bdt, offer_observations.stock_state, offer_observations.quality_status, observation_batches.observed_at, observed_offers.configuration_id, observed_offers.model_id.
Official vs import
Same phone, same configuration, both channels. Each channel's monthly price is the median of its daily lowest in-stock price, and needs 3 read dates. Premium = (official - import) / import. Popular = carried in stock by the most shops this month; we have no sales figures. Computed from offer_observations.price_bdt, observed_offers.channel, observed_offers.configuration_id, observation_batches.source, observation_batches.observed_at.
Recent launches
A box is one exact configuration on one channel. Its lowest in-stock price at any shop on the month's first read is compared with the same on its last. Recent = released, per the spec sheet, in the 6 calendar months up to this one. A move under 4% or ৳700 is not counted, nor is a box under ৳8,000. Computed from offer_observations.price_bdt, observation_batches.observed_at, observed_offers.configuration_id, observed_offers.channel, phones.specs:specs_raw.status.
Cheapest 5G
Every 5G phone (per its spec sheet) is priced at its cheapest box's monthly price: the median of that box's daily lowest in-stock price, over at least 3 read dates, so one night's typo or flash sale is not the answer. Computed from offer_observations.price_bdt, observation_batches.observed_at, observed_offers.configuration_id, phones.specs:net_5g.
Value by band
Value measured one way: per band, the phone with the highest measured AnTuTu score for its chipset, at its cheapest box's monthly price. Chipsets with no measured run are not ranked (no estimate stands in), and a band needs three measured phones. Computed from offer_observations.price_bdt, observed_offers.configuration_id, phones.specs:chipset, knowledge/soc_db.json:antutu.total.
Stock
A handset is in stock on a date when any shop read that date reports a usable in-stock listing for it. The rate is over handsets in stock on at least one date, and its denominator is the dates their shops were read, not calendar days. A shop that does not publish stock counts for nothing either way. Computed from offer_observations.stock_state, offer_observations.quality_status, observation_batches.observed_at, observed_offers.model_id.
